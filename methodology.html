Methodology & Gazetteers

Reference documentation for trade flow classification, corridor normalisation, and primary filings provenance.

1. Core Principles & Governance

The Cargo & Trade Flows module adheres strictly to primary observational and regulatory data integrity:

  • Primary Source Precedence: Figures are sourced directly from regulatory customs authorities (ComexStat, MAGyP, USDA FAS), port authorities (Pilbara Ports Authority), or official corporate regulatory filings (SEC EDGAR Form 6-K, ASX Announcements).
  • Empirical vs. Derived Accounting: Discrete fixture observations record actual commercial agreements and charter contracts. Physical export tonnage reflects statutory customs declarations and terminal scale weights. These series represent separate operational dimensions and are maintained on distinct bases.
  • Zero Unverified Estimates: No simulated values, synthetic interpolations, or illustrative placeholders are published. When a primary filing does not report a metric, the field remains null.

2. Fixture Matrix & Multi-Field Classification

The fixture ledger aggregates chartering contracts across dry bulk, liquid tankers, and gas carriers. Records are classified using a deterministic multi-field matching engine:

Taxonomy Normalisation

Commodity classification processes five fields simultaneously: department, segment, charterer, route, and comment.

  • Dry Bulk Hierarchy: Major Bulks (Iron Ore, Coal, Grains, Bauxite) and Minor Bulks (Fertilizers, Steel Products, Cement/Clinker, Industrial Minerals, Salt, Gypsum).
  • Liquid Tanker Hierarchy: Crude Oil (VLCC, Suezmax, Aframax trades) and Clean Petroleum Products (CPP: Gasoline, Diesel, Jet Fuel, Naphtha).
  • Gas Carrier Hierarchy: LPG (VLGC, LGC, MGC, Coaster) and LNG.

3. Trade Corridor Gazetteer

Fixture origins and destinations are normalised from raw port and terminal strings against a standard maritime trade corridor gazetteer spanning over 300 global marine loading and discharge hubs.

Corridor Resolution Rules

  • Load Terminals: Specific berths (e.g. Ponta da Madeira Pier IV, Herb Elliott Port Hedland Berth 3, San Lorenzo Pier 2) are resolved to their parent marine terminal and macro export region.
  • Discharge Options: Charter contracts featuring contractual discharge options (e.g., "OPTS CHINA/SPORE", "SK/JPN OPTS") are resolved to explicit regional option corridors (e.g., options — Far East, options — Continental Europe).
  • Direct Bilateral Corridors: Corridors with explicit loading and discharge destinations are assigned to standard bilateral trade lanes (e.g., Australia → China, Brazil → China, US Gulf → East Coast Mexico).
