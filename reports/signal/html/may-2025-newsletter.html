US Corn Trade Flows

 

U.S. corn exports are on the rise, boosting vessel demand and offsetting weaker soybean trade. With steady demand from Mexico and potential growth in Vietnam, U.S. corn could see continued export momentum in 2025 and beyond.

Soybean Trade Flows

 

China's pivot away from U.S. soybean imports amid renewed tariffs is boosting South American exporters and shifting vessel demand from U.S. ports to Brazil and Argentina—reshaping Panamax and Supramax activity in global dry bulk.
