Container Report - Week 48

Container

Container freight rates were a mixture of fortunes in the past week. The FBX Weighted Average decreased by 32 points from 3,419 at the end of last week, ending the week at 3,387.

The main trade lane from Asia into the US West Coast (FBX01) saw rates easing off by USD 236, from USD7,643 at the end of last week, ending this week at USD7,407/FEU. The other major trade into the states, US East Coast from Asia (FBX03) also declined since the end of last week by USD367 ending the week at USD9,054 from USD9,421/FEU last Friday. Trade into the North Continent, Asia to North Europe (FBX11), saw rates creep up slightly over the week from USD3,406 to USD3,522/FEU by the end of the week. The trade from the Far East into the Mediterranean (FBX13) saw rates increase by USD214 from USD5,357 to USD5,571/FEU at the end of the week. All other FBX routes remained flat, with no significant movements.
