Tanker report - Week 47

Clean

LR2

LR2 performance was mixed this week, with a sharp rise in eastbound Middle East Gulf rates contrasting with softer westbound and Mediterranean routes. TC1 (75kt MEG/Japan) climbed 36 points to WS1049, lifting the Baltic round-trip TCE by $11,400 to $308,200/day. Conversely, TC20 (90kt MEG/UK-Continent) eased by $378,600 to $18.64 million, corresponding to a Baltic round-trip TCE of $287,600/day. TC15 (80kt Mediterranean/East) also softened, declining $100,000 to $7.66 million, with the TCE slipping $1,300 to $60,100/day.

LR1

LR1 rates were comparatively stable, particularly on the eastbound Middle East Gulf trade. TC5 (55kt MEG/Japan) remained broadly unchanged at WS993, equating to a Baltic round-trip TCE of $206,800/day. Westbound, TC8 (65kt MEG/UK-Continent) edged $78,600 lower to $14.56 million, with the corresponding TCE easing $1,000 to $222,200/day.

MR

MR markets weakened across the board, with the most pronounced losses centred on the US Gulf and the wider Atlantic market. TC17 (35kt MEG/East Africa) declined around 12 points to WS913, reducing the TCE by $1,900 to $117,600/day. On the UK Continent, TC2 (37kt ARA/US Atlantic Coast) fell 19 points to WS184, with the Baltic round-trip TCE declining $3,400 to $11,300/day.

Pressure was considerably stronger in the US Gulf. TC14 (38kt US Gulf/UK-Continent) fell sharply by 121 points to WS237, cutting the round-trip TCE by $24,100 to $23,700/day. TC21 (38kt US Gulf/Caribbean) recorded a similarly substantial decline, falling $470,800 to $762,500 and reducing the Baltic TCE by $27,300 to $20,100/day. The broader deterioration in Atlantic earnings was reflected in the MR Atlantic Triangulation Basket TCE, which fell from $60,600/day to $37,000/day.

Handymax

Handymax performance diverged between the Mediterranean and UK Continent markets. In the Mediterranean, TC6 (30kt Cross-Mediterranean) strengthened by 30 points to WS399, lifting the Baltic round-trip TCE by $9,300 to $76,100/day. In contrast, TC23 (30kt Cross UK-Continent) declined 23 points to WS394, with the corresponding TCE falling $6,500 to $72,700/day.

VLCC

VLCC rates on the Baltic Exchange strengthened across most routes this week. TD3C (270,000 mt Middle East Gulf/China) rose 21.25 points to WS315.63, generating a daily round-trip TCE of $1,079,794 for the standard Baltic VLCC. TD34 (Gulf of Oman/China) was comparatively stable, edging just over a point higher to WS309.38, equating to a round-trip TCE of $305,337/day.

In the Atlantic, sentiment was more mixed. The 260,000 mt West Africa/China route (TD15) eased 4.29 points to WS293.57, corresponding to a round-trip TCE of $272,771/day. Conversely, the US Gulf/China route (TD22) advanced by $2.5 million to approximately $34.5 million, lifting the daily round-trip TCE to $253,509/day.

Suezmax

The Suezmax market firmed broadly across the Baltic Exchange routes this week. The 130,000 mt Nigeria/UK Continent route (TD20) advanced 22.56 points to WS330.56, equating to a daily round-trip TCE of approximately $165,677. TD27 (130,000 mt Guyana/UK Continent) strengthened by 14.73 points to WS332.06, returning just under $163,800/day. The most pronounced increase was recorded on the 145,000 mt US Gulf/UK Continent route (TD33), which gained more than 37 points to WS306.67, with the corresponding TCE rising to just over $184,800/day.

In the Black Sea, the 135,000 mt CPC/Augusta route (TD6) softened to WS416.33 midweek before recovering to WS426.11 at the time of writing, equating to a daily round-trip TCE of just over $306,900.

Aframax

The Aframax market strengthened sharply this week, with exceptional gains across the Atlantic routes accompanied by firming in the North Sea and Mediterranean, while Vancouver exports were comparatively subdued.

In the North Sea, the 80,000 mt Cross-UK Continent route (TD7) advanced 40.83 points to WS288.33, equating to a daily round-trip TCE of just under $183,937, basis Hound Point/Wilhelmshaven.

In the Mediterranean, TD19 (80,000 mt Cross-Mediterranean) posted a stronger gain of 84.44 points to WS352.22, returning approximately $134,100/day on a Ceyhan/Lavera basis.

The most pronounced moves were seen across the Atlantic, where Aframax rates surged significantly higher. TD26 (70,000 mt East Coast Mexico/US Gulf) climbed almost 310 points to WS631.11, generating a daily round-trip TCE of just over $219,200. TD9 (70,000 mt Covenas/US Gulf) recorded a similarly sharp increase, gaining 311 points to around WS623 and returning just over $197,400/day. TD25 (70,000 mt US Gulf/UK Continent) was also assessed 283 points higher at just over WS596, equating to approximately $180,100/day on a Houston/Rotterdam basis.

By comparison, the Vancouver export routes were considerably more stable. TD28 (80,000 mt Vancouver/China) eased by $200,000 to $8.17 million, corresponding to a round-trip TCE of approximately $162,000/day. TD29 (80,000 mt Vancouver/Pacific Area Lightering point off the US West Coast) slipped four points to WS683.
