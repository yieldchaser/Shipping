MARKET COMMENTARY 
 
Bulkers: The dry cargo market underwent a five-day decline before recovering towards the end of the 
week. The Baltic Dry Index (BDI) closed today at 3,370 points, reflecting a decrease of 3.91% compared 
to last Friday's closing value of 3,507 points. 
 
Baltic Dry Index           3370  +34 
Baltic Capesize Index  5768 +112 
Baltic Panamax Index    2251  -31 
Baltic Supramax Index  1767   +5 
Baltic Handysize Index    988   +8 
 
The bulker S+P market remains strong, though it was somewhat quieter this week, with a total of 15 
transactions recorded, comprising 2 kamsarmaxes, 2 panamax, 1 ultramax, 5 Supramaxes, 1 Handymax 
and 4 Handysize bulk carriers.  
 
Tankers: Emirati buyers (clients of Onex) are reported to have scooped up this week four VLCCs - 
ASHOKA, NISSOS HERACLEA, XI XIU  and DENNIE – as well as two Suezmaxes, EVRIDIKI 
and ORFEAS at firm levels. 
 
For a detailed review and additional information regarding these week’s transactions, kindly refer to our 
“representative sales” section.
