 Capesize 
Iron ore rates West Australia/China fluctuated, with Dampier and Port 
Hedland to Qingdao settling between USD 12.45/13.50 fio. In the Atlantic, 
Tubarao to Qingdao routes achieved USD 34.85 fio, while runs from 
Saldanha Bay and Nouadhibou to Qingdao secured  USD 23.49 fio and USD 
35.75 fio respectively. Transatlantic runs from Narvik to Rotterdam made 
USD 10.90 fio, and Tubarao to Eren fetched USD 29.85 fio.

and South China with Singapore/Japan or worldwide redelivery secured

USD 14,750/21,000 pd to China/India ranges. Indonesian coal runs from

between USD 11,500/22,000 pd, with further runs to South Korea,

Asia via ECSA to Singapore/Japan range settling between USD 17,000/22,000 
pd. In the Atlantic, Continent departures fixed v ia US Gulf to Continent at

Ultramax / Supramax 
In the Pacific, NoPac rounds and local trips from North China with South East 
Asia and Singapore/Japan redelivery secured USD 16,000/22,000 pd. For 
longer runs from North China, a trip to the Med reached USD 26,000 pd, a 
run to West Africa achieved USD 23,000 pd and a trip to the US Gulf fixed at 
USD 14,500 pd. From South China and South East Asia, coal and nickel ore 
runs to China and South East Asia fetched USD 15,000/18,000 pd, while a 
barites trip from South China to the US Gulf achieved USD 15,500 pd. From 
India, a trip to China fixed at USD 17,500 pd, while PG and India departures 
to local ranges settled between USD 15,500/22,750 pd. In the Atlantic, US 
Gulf departures to NCSA and ECSA secured USD 25,000/27,000 pd, a USEC 
run to East Med made USD 31,50 0 pd and ECSA trips to NCSA fetched USD 
26,000 pd. ECSA backhauls to Continent, India and Singapore/Japan ranges 
achieved USD 28,000/39,000 pd. From the Black Sea/Med, a Black Sea run 
to South East Asia settled at USD 28,000 pd and an East Med trip via Bla ck 
Sea to South Korea made USD 21,000 pd. From South and East Africa, runs 
to China and Far East ranges obtained USD 18,000/24,000 pd. On period, an 
Ultramax fixed from South East Asia for 4/6 months duration at USD 22,000 
pd.

In the Atlantic, ECSA departures secured between USD 18,500/27,000 pd for

pellets runs settled at USD 22,000 pd to the Continent and USD 23,000 pd to
