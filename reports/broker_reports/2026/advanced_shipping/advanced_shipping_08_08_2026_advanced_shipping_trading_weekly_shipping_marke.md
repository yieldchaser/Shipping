comparison, the “Africa Hope” 53/2009 Yangzhou, China (SS 01/2029, DD 
12/2026) was sold back in May at high USD 10 mill. 
 
Hsin Chien Marine have committed their Handysize “New Journey” 36/2015

19,8 mill . Remind you that the one -year-older sister vessel “KS Grace” 
36/2014 Shikoku, Japan (SS 01/2029, DD 01/2027) was sold back in April at

WEEKLY SHIPPING MARKET REPORT - pg. 2 
 
Week 32 (31st Jul to 7th Aug 2026) 
 Capesize 
Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route strengthened, with Port 
Hedland and Dampier to Qingdao fixtures settling between USD 15.00/16.30 
fio. In the Atlantic, Tubarao departures to Qingdao, including West Africa 
options, fetched between USD 34. 25/36.10 fio, while a Boffa run to Yantai 
and Longkou reached USD 36.80 fio. Transatlantic activity saw a Tubarao run 
to Rotterdam fixing at USD 15.50 fio. 
Kamsarmax / Panamax 
In the Pacific, Australian rounds with redelivery in China, Japan, Taiwan, 
India and South East Asia fetched between USD 16,500/21,000 pd. NoPac  
trips with Singapore/Japan and South East Asia options settled between USD 
16,250/17,500 pd, while a North America West Coast run to Malaysia made 
USD 15,000 pd. North China trips to Japan were agreed between USD 
17,500/19,750 pd and Indonesian coal runs to South China, Japan, Taiwan 
and South East Asia ranges achieved USD 13,750/20,000 pd. From East Coast 
India, West Coast India and South East Asia, trips via ECSA and NCSA to Far 
East destinations obtained USD 18,500/24,500 pd, while a West Africa 
departure via NCSA secured USD 21,500 pd plus USD 550,000 ballast bonus. 
In the Atlantic, transatlantic and fronthaul fixtures via USEC and NCSA 
obtained between USD 24,000/35,000 pd. On period, short-term activity for 
6 to 13 months duration settled between USD 19,950/23,000 pd, while a 
newbuilding vessel fixed for a longer 22 to 24 months period at USD 19,500 
pd.  
Ultramax / Supramax 
Out of India and the Persian Gulf, coastal and regional runs from West Coast 
India to East Coast India settled between USD 19,000/21,000 pd, while a 
long-haul trip via South Africa to China made USD 17,500 pd and a PG trip to 
ECSA fixed at USD 13,000 pd. O ut of South Africa, coal trips to Far East and 
China options secured between USD 18,500/24,500 pd, alongside ballast 
bonuses from USD 185,000 to USD 245,000. In the Atlantic, an ECSA grain 
run to Singapore/Japan range achieved USD 29,000 pd, while a West M ed 
trip via Continent to India obtained USD 24,000 pd. On period, an Ultramax 
fixed for 5 to 7 months duration at USD 21,000 pd with Atlantic redelivery.  
Handymax / Handysize 
In the Pacific, a local South East Asia trip with sugar fixed at USD 16,000 pd. 
In the Atlantic, transatlantic runs from USEC and US Gulf to Continent range 
fetched between USD 17,000/18,500 pd, while trips from ECSA and NCSA to 
US Gulf and West Africa ach ieved USD 18,250 pd and USD 16,500 pd 
respectively. Out of Continent, runs to WCSA and East Med settled between 
USD 15,250/17,000 pd. In the Med, transatlantic departures from East Med 
to USEC and US Gulf ranges were agreed between USD 12,000/14,000 pd, 
alongside extra ILOHC bonuses where applicable, while a return trip from 
USEC to East Med made USD 17,000 pd. On period, a Handysize open in 
South East Asia fixed for 4 to 6 months duration at USD 17,250 pd.
