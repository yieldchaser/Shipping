MARKET COMMENTARY 
 
Bulkers: The Baltic Dry Index closed today to a peak of 3,089 points, reflecting a 13% increase 
compared to last Friday's closure of 2,732 points. Despite August summer holidays kicking in, the S+P 
activity remains strong, with another busy week recording 10 transactions (mainly larger tonnage), 
comprising 2 capes, 4 kamsarmaxes, 1 Panamax, 2 Supramaxes & 1 Handysize bulk carriers. For a 
detailed review and additional information regarding these week’s transactions, kindly refer to our 
“representative sales” section.  
 
Tankers: VLCC spree this week as we recorded eight units changing hands, mainly to Emirati buyers. 
 
Demolition: The Subcontinent markets are overall healthy; Pakistan is the hottest market due to local 
steel market improvement. Indian local steel market is stabilizing while Bangladesh is softening due to 
local steel market getting slower. For an overview of demolition market rates, please check our
