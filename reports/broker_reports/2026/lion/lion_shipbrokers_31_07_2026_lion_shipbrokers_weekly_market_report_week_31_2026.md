MARKET COMMENTARY 
 
Bulkers: The charter market experienced a period of notable volatility this week, hitting a four-week 
low mid-week before staging a strong rebound at the end of the week. Despite the freight rates’ short-
term decline, the secondhand asset market continues to remain strong, as we recorded another busy 
week with 12 transactions, comprising 3 “baby” capes, 1 Panamax, 1 Ultramax, 6 Supramaxes & 1 
Handysize bulk carriers.
