## Market Report Content

 
   
 
Clarkson Hellas Ltd. 
 
Desk Talk 
Even with the expected seasonal slowdown we are seeing healthy activity in the S&P market, with 
several transactions recorded and ongoing negotiations. 
 
With the Strait of Hormu z tensions continuing to shape  market sentiment. Transits through the St rait 
remain near record lows , and viole nce has resurfaced, with further Houthi attac ks on commercial 
vessels in Bab  el-Mandeb and U.S. forces striking a container ship that attempted to breach 
Washington’s blockade on Iranian ports in the Gulf of Oman , u nderscoring the security risk  and ri sk 
premium pricing across Gulf routes. 
 
Drybulk sentiment remains firm, led by Capesize gains, but strong across sizes, supported by steady iron 
ore and coal demand. Capesize Buyers are still active for vessels around 15 years old and, at the same 
time we have recorded a number of recent Kamsarmax sales. The majority of enquiries we are seeing, 
however, are concentrated in the geared segment, predominantly modern Handy Bulkers, 15-20 years 
old Supramaxes and modern Ultramaxes. 
 
On the tanker side, Suezmax spot rates jumped to an assessed global average of USD 108,500/day (from 
USD 81,000/day last week) after a burst of USG activity pulled tonnage away from W.Afi ca and Med. 
Activity is al so rotating between segments , VLCCs, quiet last week, are now more active in the US G, 
while Suezmax demand is shifting Worldwide. 
This is also re flected in the increased purchase enq uiries for VLCC, Suezmax and Afra max tonnage, 
although in general, sentiment continues to suggest that Buyers are not yet willing to pay the premium 
Sellers are asking. On the product tankers side, Buyers are actively looking for MR tonnage of around 15 
years of age and younger.

As mentioned, the Kamsarmax sector has seen particularly strong activity recently, with a n umber of 
around 10 years old vessels changing hands. The MEDI POSITANO (ABT 82K DWT, 2015, TSUNEISHI ZOSEN, 
SCRUBBER) was sold to Greek Buyers for around USD 32m. Meanwhile, the PRESINGE (ABT 82K DWT, 2015 
TSUNEISHI ZHOUSHAN) has been acquired by Indian interests for USD 29.25m. 
The BBG WUZHOU (ABT 82K DWT, 2016, TSUNEISHI ZHOUSHAN) was sold via auction at xs USD 29m, which 
was the opening price, as Buyers did not face any competition. Meanwhile, the ROYAL HOPE (ABT 81K 
DWT, 2015, JMU TSU), was sold at levels around USD 31m after inviting offers on the 5th of August. 
 
On the Supramax side, the GLOBAL ORIOLE (ABT 59K DWT, 2012, NACKS) was sold to Chinese interest for 
USD 19.5m, w hilst the vintage GLORY BRIDGE  (ABT 50K DW T, 2001, MITS UI CHIBA) was acquired by 
Chinese interest for USD 7.5m.
