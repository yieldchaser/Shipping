## Capesize/Newcastlemax

The fundamentals look good for the next couple of months, so we do not expect a major spot market correction within that period. 
However, the spot market might be close to reaching a ceiling, as the Capesize/Kamsarmax ratio is above 2, which, since 2018, has meant further upside is limited. That said, we expect the Kamsarmax spot market to improve in the next month or two as the second ECSAM grain wave should kick in.

## Panamax/Kamsarmax

The second ECSAM grain wave should be incoming, and the market is likely to be lifted as a result. 
Coal trades in the Pacific will likely remain weak, however. China's inventories are high, and India's demand is weighed down by a weaker macroeconomic backdrop. The lower year-on-year market is likely to continue.

## Supramax/Ultramax

Around this time of year, the market usually settles and then trends sideways in Q2.  We find it likely that the BSI-63 index will settle in the low teens. 

The correlation to the average seasonal movement suggested the market would be a bit higher now. The market could still climb slightly due to ECSAM grains and a slightly positive indication from industrial metals prices and manufacturing PMIs. However, fundamentals do not seem supportive of an index climb much higher than 13.000 USD pd.
