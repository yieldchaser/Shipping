# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 20 August 2024

*Published on 20 August 2024*

For the week ending 16 August, all Drewry Indices showed an increase. Crude and Port Indices were top performers, increasing by 4.8% and 4.5% respectively. The Crude Equity Index grew due to increased spot charter rates for VLCC vessels. LPG, Dry Bulk and Container rose 3.3%, 2.6% and 2.2% respectively.
