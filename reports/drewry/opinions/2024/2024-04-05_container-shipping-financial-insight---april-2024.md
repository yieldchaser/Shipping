# Container Shipping Financial Insight - April 2024

*Published on 05 April 2024*

The recent incident at the Baltimore port has caused an increase in the stock prices of carriers that frequently call there. Consequently, the Drewry Container Equity Index rose by 5.1% between 26 March 2024 and 4 April 2024.
