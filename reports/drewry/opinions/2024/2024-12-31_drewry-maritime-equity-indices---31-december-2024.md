# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 31 December 2024

*Published on 31 December 2024*

For the week ending 27 December, all Drewry Equity Indices were on a rise (vs S&P up 0.7% and Russell up 0.1%). LPG and Dry Bulk indices were up 5.3% and 3.0% due to rise in TCE rates. Meanwhile, Product and Crude Equity Indices were up 5.5% and 4.8% as the World Bank raised its forecast for China for both 2024 and 2025. Container Index increased 2.2% following escalating tensions in Yemen.
