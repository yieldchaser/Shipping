# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 01 October 2024

*Published on 01 October 2024*

For the week ending 27 September, Drewry Indices were on the rise except for Product Tanker, which declined 0.3% due to a fall in TCE rates.

The Container Index rose 10.5% due to the labour strike on the US East Coast, signalling investor optimism regarding disruption-led improved carrier earnings. The Dry Bulk Index increased 8.2% as stimulus measures from China are expected to boost demand for the dry bulk sector. Meanwhile, the Port Index gained 4.1% as RTOs experienced a substantial rise of 9.0%.
