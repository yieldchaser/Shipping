# Intra Asia Container Index - 01 Oct

*Published on 01 October 2026*

Drewry’s Intra-Asia Container Index (IACI) edged up 2% this week to $1,518 per 40ft container.

The IACI Composite Index reached an all-time high for the sixth consecutive week, as typhoon disruption and port congestion tightened capacity. Carriers used blank sailings and port omissions to limit capacity, while higher operating costs added to upwards pressure on rates. See detailed commentary below.

Drewry’s Intra-Asia Container Index (IACI) reports actual spot container freight rates for major intra-Asia trade routes. The Index, which will be updated weekly (from 02 Jan 26), consists of 18 route-specific indices representing individual shipping routes and a composite index. All indices are reported in USD per 40ft container.
