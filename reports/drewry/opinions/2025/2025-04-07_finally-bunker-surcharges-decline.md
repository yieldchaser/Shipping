# Finally, Bunker Surcharges Decline

*Published on 07 April 2025*

Oil prices are highly volatile, but Drewry tracking of average marine bunker prices by quarter for Very Low Sulphur Fuel Oil shows a downwards trend which has now lasted for nearly three years.

**Source:** Drewry LSFO Bunker Tracker, BAF and bunker policy support services

Taking the Asia-Mediterranean route as an example, typical “Bunker Adjustment Factors” (BAF) had surged in 2022, as Russia commenced its attempted invasion of Ukraine, but have since been mainly falling for nine quarters.

For shippers, current best practice is to have a standard BAF policy, under which the BAF charges of all providers are indexed on an independent fuel price. Drewry recommends this technique to all medium and large shippers.

A more advanced practice is to be able to forecast future BAFs, for whichDrewry has also developed a model.
