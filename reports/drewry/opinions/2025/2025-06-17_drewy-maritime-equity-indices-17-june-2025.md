# Drewy Maritime Equity Indices - 17 June 2025

*Published on 17 June 2025*

For the week ending 13 June 2025, all the Drewry Equity Indices were on an upward trend (vs S&P 500 down 0.4% and Russell 2000 down 1.5%) due to escalating tensions between Iran and Israel.

The Crude and Product sector led the gains, rising 7.6% and 7.5%, respectively, driven by the potential threat to tankers and oil refineries.

On a YTD basis, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index leads with a gain of 20.9%, followed by the Container Equity Index, up 15.9%. The steepest YTD decline was seen in the Drewry Product Tanker Equity Index, which is down 10.9%.
