# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 07 January 2025

*Published on 07 January 2025*

For the week ending 3 January 2025, Drewry Equity Indices recorded up moves across sectors.

LPG and LNG indices led the gains with 7.8% and 3.8% WoW increases, respectively, while the Port index trailed, due to underperformance in regional terminal operators. However, in 2024, the Drewry Equity indices showcased mixed trends - LNG index leading with a 27.6% rise and Crude index trailing with a 29.3% decline.
