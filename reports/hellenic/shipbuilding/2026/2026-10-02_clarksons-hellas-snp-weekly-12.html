CLARKSONS HELLAS – SnP WEEKLY

Tanker rates remain at or near record highs as hopes of a reopening faded. President Trump rejected Iran’s proposalto reopen the Strait, at least five tankers were struck in Hormuz this week, and the US is reported to be sending a third aircraft carrier to the region.

VLCC earnings are averaging over USD 700,000 p/d on the Baltic Exchange’s assessment, with Middle East Gulf-China voyages at USD 1.2 million p/d. The strength has spilled into Suezmaxes, where average earnings jumped by nearly USD 130,000 on Thursday to around USD 448,000 p/d, as charterers downsizing from VLCCs to cut freight costs ran into limited supply.

Product tankers continue to climb, with average clean earnings estimated near USD 140,000 p/d, led by the LR segments, though a possible US diesel export ban and China’s reported suspension of October product exports are risks to the segment.

S&P activity on crude carriers remains firm, with several players moving on tonnage that can be delivered promptly, particularly units positioned close to the Middle East Gulf. Sellers’ strong price ideas are being met by willing buyers, keeping modern secondhand VLCCs and Suezmaxes priced well above Newbuildings, a record premium in the case of VLCCs.

On dry, the period market appears to be at a healthy standstill, with fixtures continuing to be concluded at like last-done levels, while spot earnings have taken a bit of a breather, but may logically pick back up once people are back from the holidays, primarily the Chinese Golden Week.

For reference, the BDI settled at 3,148 points, whereas last week, had been trading in the 3,400-3,500 area.
Overall, we continue to see a healthy number of enquiries across the board of sizes, with a slight spike notable for modern eco Ultramax, as well as Kamsarmax tonnage, on the gearless size.

Dry Cargo

In the gearless space, Capesize BULK JOYANCE (ABT 175,636 DWT, 2012, JINHAI HEAVY IND) was concluded at USD 31.1m, while the Post-Panamax LC MILADY (ABT 93,758 DWT, 2011, SHANHAIGUAN SB) was sold at a price in the high USD 14m’s.

Further down the size-range, the modern Eco Kamsarmax GCL HAZIRA (ABT 81,986 DWT, 2021, NANTONG COSCO KHI) was acquired by German interests for a price of USD 38.8m, basis delivery October/November 2026.

Meanwhile, BABITONGA (ABT 81,600 DWT, 2019, TSUNEISHI ZOSEN) changed hands for USD 38.5m, with the new owners being C/O Sincere Navigation.

In the Supramax space, YC AEQUOR (ABT 56,785 DWT, 2012, NINGBO LANTIAN SB, C:4X30T) has achieved a price in the high USD 13m’s.

Tanker

On Tankers, the VLCC SEAKING (ABT 317,000 DWT, 2013, HD HYUNDAI (HII)) was sold to clients of ADNOC at USD 175m.

Elsewhere, in the product tankers space, the MR2 ATLANTIC CROWN (ABT 47,128 DWT, 2007, HD HYUNDAI MIPO) was sold for USD 19.2m.
