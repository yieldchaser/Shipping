Weekly Vessel Valuations Report, August 04 2026

Hellenic Shipping News  |  05 August 2026  |  https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/weekly-vessel-valuations-report-august-04-2026

Bulkers

Values held broadly stable this week, with clean sales clustering close to VV Value across sizes, and the 15-year-old 60k DWT Supramax benchmark firming 1.9% as buyer appetite for midage Japanese-built Ultramax tonnage picked up notably.

Capesize Attikos (178,900 DWT, 2012, HSG Sungdong) sold SS/DD Due by Capital Axis Maritime for USD 37 mil, VV Value USD 38.0 mil.

Kamsarmax Mont Fort (82,300 DWT, 2012, Tsuneishi Zosen) sold to Greek buyers for USD 22 mil, VV Value USD 22.1 mil.

Ultramax CMB Jordaens (63,400 DWT, 2019, Tadotsu Imabari) sold to Bangladeshi buyers for USD 35.2 mil, VV Value USD 35.7 mil.

Supramax Capt Eugene (55,500 DWT, 2010, Mitsui Ichihara) sold by United Shipping Lines for USD 16.6 mil, VV Value USD 16.7 mil.

Handy African Harrier (37,800 DWT, 2014, Imabari) sold by MUR Shipping for USD 20.1 mil, VV Value USD 20.2 mil.

Tankers

ADNOC L&S’s push into secondhand VLCCs reads as part of its broader wartime fleet-building spree, paying a clear scarcity premium to lock in owned crude-carrying capacity as the Strait of Hormuz and Red Sea disruptions squeeze available tanker supply.

VLCC Seapassion (299,300 DWT, 2017, Hyundai Heavy Ind Ulsan Shipyard), Delta Angelica and Delta Glory (319,900 / 319,800 DWT, 2012, Hyundai Heavy Ind), and Delta Apollonia and Delta Amazon (319,700 / 319,900 DWT, 2015, Jinhai Intelligent Manufacturing) sold enbloc by Thenamaris and Delta Tankers to ADNOC Logistics and Services for USD 597 mil combined, VV Value USD 522.3 mil combined.

Containers

Container values remain stable this week.

Sub Panamax Container Montpellier (2,824 TEU, Dec 2006, Hyundai Mipo) sold SS/DD Due by Wilhelmsen Ahrenkiel Bulk to undisclosed buyers for USD 27.2 mil, VV Value USD 25.9 mil.

Panamax Containers Spirit of Melbourne, Spirit of Sydney, Spirit of Singapore, & Spirit of Auckland (3,750 TEU, 2007, Daewoo) sold enbloc (Inc TC) by Capital Containers Ship Management to undisclosed buyers for USD 40 mil each, VV Value USD 30.2 mil each.
