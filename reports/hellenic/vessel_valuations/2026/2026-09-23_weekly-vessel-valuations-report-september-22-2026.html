Bulker S&P values held broadly stable across the board this week, with recent Panamax and Ultramaxresale activity trading at modest premiums.

Panamax BW Japan (81,600 DWT, May 2019, Tsuneishi Cebu) sold to Great Eastern Shipping for USD 38.25 mil, VV Value USD 36.56 mil.

Ultramax Hull 297 (63,500 DWT, Jan 2027, Jiangsu Haitong) sold to an undisclosed buyer for USD 39.0 mil, VV Value USD 37.52 mil.

Panamax Osaka Star (84,900 DWT, Mar 2016, Sasebo) sold to an undisclosed buyer for USD 34.0 mil, VV Value USD 31.63 mil.

Handysize Boston Harmony (38,500 DWT, Aug 2015, Shin Kurushima Toyohashi) sold to an Unknown Greek buyer for USD 24.0 mil, VV Value USD 22.21 mil.

Tankers

Tanker S&P values, specifically VLCC and Suezmax, have surged since last week, continuing the trend of strong Middle Eastern buying interest driving some of the highest prices ever seen for VLCC and Suezmax tonnage amid the ongoing regional disruption. Aframax and MR2 values held broadly stable by comparison.

VLCC Atherina (319,500 DWT, Jan 2011, Hyundai HI Ulsan Shipyard) sold to an undisclosed buyer for USD 146.0 mil, VV Value USD 124.74 mil.

VLCC Kallista (317,400 DWT, Feb 2010, Hyundai HI Ulsan Shipyard) sold to an Unknown Chinese buyer for USD 132.0 mil, VV Value USD 129.27 mil.
