Bulkers

Bulker S&P activity remained busy this week, with a steady flow of Capesize,Supramax and Handysize transactions changing hands amid broadly resilient sentiment. Values were broadly stable with the exception of mid age Handy BCs; values dipped in response to the China Spirit’s soft pricing.

Capesize Navios Pollux (180,700 DWT, 2009, STX Offshore) sold DD Due to Chinese buyers for USD 30.75 mil, VV Value USD 30.8 mil.

Supramax Harvest (58,800 DWT, 2008, Tsuneishi Zhoushan) sold DD Passed to Chinese buyers for USD 13.8 mil, VV Value USD 14.2 mil.

Supramax Kanchana Naree (56,900 DWT, 2011, Taizhou Sanfu) sold SS/DD Due to Chinese buyers for USD 14.6 mil, VV Value USD 14.6 mil.

Handysize China Spirit (35,300 DWT, 2013, Dongzhe) sold by BoComm Leasing for USD 13 mil, VV Value USD 13.5 mil.

Tankers

Tanker values firmed across the board this week, with owner appetite for modern tonnage continuing to build against the backdrop of ongoing Strait of Hormuz disruption. Evidence of persistently elevated war-risk premiums comes in the form of the recently reported Pinios resale for USD 200m, basis prompt Middle East delivery through the Strait.

VLCC Pinios (306,000 DWT, Feb 2026, Hengli Shipbuilding) sold by Dynacom Tankers for USD 200 mil, VV Value USD 179.4 mil.
