Handysize bulker values firmed at the older end of the curve this week, while Capesize, Panamax, Supramaxand the younger Handysize bands remained in a stable range.

Capesize Erato (180,100 DWT, Feb 2010) sold to an undisclosed South Korean buyer for USD 36.75 mil, VV Value USD 35.71 mil.

Panamax KM MT Jade (81,200 DWT, Oct 2008) sold to an undisclosed Chinese buyer for USD 16.0 mil, VV Value USD 15.69 mil.

Ultramax IVS Dunes (62,700 DWT, Sep 2020) sold inc. TC to an undisclosed Chinese buyer for USD 36.8 mil, VV Value USD 36.34 mil.

Handysize Wooyang Cles (39,200 DWT, Jul 2014) sold to undisclosed buyers for USD 18.8 mil, VV Value USD 18.22 mil.

Tankers

Tanker S&P values pushed sharply higher this week, with owner appetite for VLCC and Suezmax tonnage building fast against the backdrop of ongoing Strait of Hormuz disruption and a wave of fresh buying activity from a single active Middle Eastern buyer across both segments. The recently reported Ashoka VLCC sale at USD 130.0m underscores the persistently elevated risk premium following the Yanbu pipeline drone attack.

VLCC Ashoka (302,600 DWT, Mar 2010) sold to an Unknown Middle Eastern buyer for USD 130.0 mil, VV Value USD 108.36 mil.

VLCC Norns (310,000 DWT, Oct 2009) sold to Onex DMCC for USD 112.0 mil, VV Value USD 104.01 mil.

Containers

Older Sub Panamax values came up a little this week off the back of the sale of the Erasmus Ninja, highlighting the demand for vessels of this size.

Sub Panamax Container Erasmus Ninja (2,546 TEU, Nov 2007) sold fwd dely to MSC for USD 30.5 mil, VV Value USD 28.37 mil.
