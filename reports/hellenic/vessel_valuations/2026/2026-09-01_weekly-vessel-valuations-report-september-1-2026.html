Bulker values remain stable this week.

Capesize Jian Fa (175,100 DWT, 2004, SWS) sold to South Korean buyers for USD 18.5 mil, VV Value USD 18.8 mil.

Panamax Ivestos 9 (75,100 DWT, 2008, Hudong) sold to Chinese buyers for USD 11.9 mil, VV Value USD 12.1 mil.

Supramax Marianna (55,800 DWT, 2010, IHI) sold by Byzantine Maritime for USD 17 mil, VV Value USD 17.9 mil.

Supramax Spar Scorpio (53,600 DWT, 2006, Chengxi Shipyard) sold to Chinese buyers for USD 11.5 mil, VV Value USD 10.9 mil.

Handysize Devbulk Sinem (38,000 DWT, 2013, Pha Rung) sold to Turkish buyers for USD 14.8 mil, VV Value USD 15.0 mil.

Tankers

Tanker values were mostly flat this week. Resale and 5-year-old Suezmax were the only segments to see any real uptick.

Suezmax Suez Ice Supreme (146,500 DWT, 2007, Universal) sold to undisclosed UAE buyers for USD 56.0 mil, VV Value USD 58.7 mil.

Aframax Seasenator (105,700 DWT, 2007, Namura) sold SS/DD due by Valles Steamship for USD 38.0 mil, VV Value USD 39.8 mil.
