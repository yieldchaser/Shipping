Weekly Vessel Valuations Report, August 25 2026

Bulkers

Supramax values firmed this week following evidence of more strong pricing, as seen in the sales of the Gramos and Global Oriole.

Kamsarmax BBG Wuzhou (81,900 DWT, 2016, Tsuneishi Zhoushan) sold Auction, SS/DD Due to Indian buyers for USD 29 mil, VV Value USD 30.8 mil.

Ultramax Gramos (61,000 DWT, 2019, Nantong COSCO KHI) sold to Greek buyers for USD 34.5 mil, VV Value USD 33.3 mil.

Supramax Global Oriole (58,700 DWT, 2012, Nantong COSCO KHI) sold to Chinese buyers for USD 19.5 mil, VV Value USD 19.1 mil.

Tankers

Tanker S&P values firmed further for VLCC tonnage across the age curve this week, with ADNOC Logistics & Services sustained buying interest continuing to underpin scarcity premiums for older units.

VLCC Hellstugutinden (299,100 DWT, 2003, Universal) sold by Hermes Ship Management for USD 57.0 mil, VV Value USD 56.93 mil.

Suezmax Sonangol Namibe (158,400 DWT, 2007, Daewoo) sold by Sonangol for USD 49.7 mil, VV Value USD 50.05 mil.

Suezmax Bristol (156,900 DWT, 2024, Hyundai Samho Heavy Ind) sold by CMB Tech to Naftomar Shipping and Trading for USD 123.0 mil, VV Value USD 109.8 mil.

MR2 On Promise and On Prosper (50,000 DWT, 2026, Hyundai Mipo) sold en bloc by Onex DMCC for USD 59.0 mil each, VV Values USD 59.1 mil and USD 59.01 mil.

Containers

Second-hand Handy Container values came up this week on the back of recent handy sales sold for notably high prices.

Handy Container Nordpuma (1,730 TEU, 2015, Zhejiang Ouhua) sold for USD 31 mil, VV Value USD 25.9 mil.

Handy Container Fitz Roy (1,740 TEU, 2011, Guangzhou Wenchong) sold to MSC for USD 26 mil, VV Value USD 23.4 mil.
