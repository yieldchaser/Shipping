Weekly Vessel Valuations Report, August 11 2026

Bulkers

Values climbed this week. There was more evidence for strengthening buyer appetite formidage Japanese-built Ultramaxes, which resulted in values firming.

Kamsarmax Royal Hope (81,000 DWT, 2015, JMU) sold by Tokei Kisen for USD 30.8 mil, VV Value USD 28.6 mil.

Ultramax Amis Wisdom II (61,600 DWT, 2010, Oshima) sold by Wisdom Marine Lines for USD 22 mil, VV Value USD 19.8 mil.

Supramax Agios Nektarios I (56,700 DWT, 2010, Jiangsu Hantong HI) sold to Chinese buyers for USD 13.3 mil, VV Value USD 13.6 mil.

Handysize Sakura Dream (38,200 DWT, 2013, Imabari) sold by Shunzan Kaiun for USD 18.5 mil, VV Value USD 17.8 mil.

Tankers

Tanker S&P values held broadly stable across most tanker segments this week, though modern VLCC tonnage continued to firm as ADNOC L&S’s secondhand buying spree keeps a scarcity premium on owned crude-carrying capacity.

VLCC Front Humber (298,800 DWT, 2017, Hyundai Samho Heavy Ind) and Front Vefsna (297,400 DWT, 2017, Hanjin Subic) sold by Frontline to ADNOC Logistics and Services for USD 135 mil each, VV Value USD 133.4 mil and USD 134.4 mil respectively.
