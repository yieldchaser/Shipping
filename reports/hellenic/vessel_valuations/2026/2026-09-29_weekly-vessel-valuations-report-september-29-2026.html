Bulkers

Bulker S&P values held stable across the board this week. Handy resales drifted downwardsin response to soft pricing for the Sider Bellezza.

Capesize Houheng 5 and Houheng 6 (262,000 DWT, Feb/Sep 2017, Guangzhou Shipyard International) sold to an undisclosed buyer for USD 144 mil, VV Value USD 151.28 mil.

Ultramax Indigo Breeze (60,400 DWT, Feb 2017, Mitsui Tamano) sold to Greek buyers for USD 30.5 mil, VV Value USD 31.61 mil.

Handysize Sider Bellezza (40,000 DWT resale, 2026, Naikai) sold by Libera Corporation for USD 38.5 mil, VV Value USD 38.63 mil.

Tankers

Larger crude values continued to firm sharply this week, extending last week’s surge as Middle Eastern buying interest for modern crude tonnage persisted amid the ongoing regional disruption.

Suezmax Suez Protopia (160,400 DWT, May 2008, Samsung) sold by Imperial Petroleum for USD 82.0 mil, VV Value USD 74.34 mil.

Suezmax Almi Galaxy (157,800 DWT, Feb 2012, Daewoo) sold by Almi Tankers for USD 88.0 mil, VV Value USD 83.48 mil.

Aframax TP Affinity (114,100 DWT, Jul 2016, Hyundai Samho) sold by TransPetrol Maritime Services for USD 73.0 mil, VV Value USD 80.72 mil.

Containers

Handy and Sub Panamax container values moved up this week following the sale of the EF Emma. Panamax and Post Panamax values remain stable.

Handy Container EF Emma (1,698 TEU, Feb 2008, Nordic Yards Warnemunde) sold for USD 24 mil, VV Value USD 19.6 mil.
