MMI Daily Iron Ore Index Report November 20 2026

Hellenic Shipping News  |  20 November 2026  |  https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/mmi-daily-iron-ore-index-report-november-20-2026

Iron ore futures and most port spot prices rose modestly today, while seaborne brands and domesticore softened slightly. DCE most-traded closed at 652.5 yuan/mt, up 0.69% from the previous close. Six Qingdao spot products including PB Fines, Newman Fines and Mac Fines rose 3 yuan/wmt, PB Lump gained 1 yuan and Carajas Fines dipped 2 yuan; port 61%, 58% and 65% indices advanced to 640, 597 and 802 yuan/wmt. Seaborne 61% and 65% composite indices held flat at USD 89.10 and 105.00/dmt while 10 of the 12 seaborne brands edged down USD 0.05-0.80/dmt; the Friday-updated Domestic Ore Composite Price Index fell 1.06% from Nov 19 to 792.40 yuan/mt. On the fundamental side, total port inventory continued to build while demand remained relatively soft. Latest ten port inventory climbed to 109.2582 million mt on Nov 19, up 1.3027 million mt (+1.21%) WoW, as builds in concentrates and lump outweighed draws in fines and pellets. Surveyed daily hot metal stood at 2.3479 million mt on Nov 18 (down 15,000 mt WoW) with the BF operating rate at 86.86% (down 0.37 percentage point). On Nov 19, C3 ocean freight dipped 0.26% to USD 35.04/mt while C5 rose 3.65% to USD 14.20/mt. A cautious, range-bound near-term view remains appropriate. Firmer futures and mainstream medium/low-grade port spot prices provide interim support, but elevated ten-port inventory, lower hot metal output and slight easing across seaborne brand quotes continue to limit upside elasticity. Mill margin changes and the timing of winter restocking remain key to watch; predominantly needs-based purchasing could keep ore prices fluctuating within a narrow range.
