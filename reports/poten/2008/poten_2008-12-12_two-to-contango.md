# Two to Contango

The continued decline in the price of crude oil has created opportunities for market players. Crude oil forward price structures generally occur in two forms: backwardation is when the physical spot price is higher than the future price and contango is when the physical spot price is lower than the future price. Although the market has experienced varying degrees of contango in 2008, the differential has been more pronounced in recent weeks. 3 Mo. Storage 6 Mo. Storage Instead of spot chartering a ship and exercising a storage option, charterers will look to hire a ship for short-term time charter to serve the same purpose. Charterers will pay in the range of $55,000 per day in today's market for a storage period of approximately three to six months. At $55,000 per day the physical storage costs are approximately $2.45 per barrel and $4.95 per barrel for three or six months respectively, based on a 2 million barrel cargo.

Additionally, charterers must consider the financial costs associated with holding the barrels for the duration of their position. Assuming a 10% cost of capital, the monthly carrying costs are approximately $.38 per barrel on the basis of a $45 per barrel spot market price. For a six month period, would total about $2.25 per barrel. Add in the freight element for a similar period and the total carrying cost would be roughly $7.20 per barrel.

Traders in today's market could conceivably gain $8.00 on the price of crude over the next six months - or a net spread of $.80 per barrel. Consider the gain on a 2 million barrel cargo and the charterer could effectively gross $1.6 million. Certainly other costs will be a limiting factor on the gross spread, such as bunkers used during the storage period, insurance and cargo loss. If near-term physical oil prices continue to stay weak relative to future prices, there will likely be more opportunities available to market participants. However, it is difficult to see the window staying open too long as people pile into the game.
