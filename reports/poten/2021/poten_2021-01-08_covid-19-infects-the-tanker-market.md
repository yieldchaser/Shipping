# Covid 19 Infects The Tanker Market

### Top Reported Dirty Spot Charterers for 2020*

For many people, it will be a relief to put 2020 in the rear-view mirror. It was a year like no other, with many extraordinary events, some highs, but mostly lows. Some of these events reinforced the rankings, while others changed them. Because China was less impacted by Covid-19 than Europe and the Americas, Unipec strengthened its position on top of the leader board (despite a lower fixture count). In 2020, they controlled 18% of total dirty spot cargoes, versus 17% in 2019. Overall, spot market activity was down 16% due to Covid-19.

### Top 10 VLCC Spot Charterers

It remains lonely at the top of the VLCC segment. In 2020, Unipec did more (reported) spot fixtures that the next twelve largest VLCC charterers combined. There were some notable changes relative to the previous year: Petrobras moved up from fifth to third and Equinor moved into the top ten (from 14 th in 2019). Another new entry is Bahri, due to the unusually high Saudi spot fixture activity in March/April. We recorded a total of 2,266 reported VLCC spot fixtures in 2020 (down 7% from 2019).

### Top 10 Suezmax Spot Charterers

In the Suezmax segment, several of the top players switched position. ExxonMobil came out on top (they were 4 th the previous year and 9 th in 2018), while Shell moved in opposite direction (from 1 st to 4 th ). The Indian Oil Company (Ioc) jumped from 6 th to 2 nd . Petrobras entered the top 10, a testament to the continued growth of Brazilian crude oil production and exports in 2020. The total number of reported Suezmax spot fixtures in 2020 (2,055) was 23% lower than in 2019 (2,675).

### Top 10 Aframax Spot Charterers

Vitol, Shell and BP maintained their position on top of the rankings in the Aframax segment. Chevron dropped back a few places, while Unipec gained one spot. Trafigura entered the top ten in 6 th spot. The total number of reported Aframax spot fixtures fell 20% in 2020 to 2,983 fixtures. * The above report has been assembled from market intelligence and reported spot market activity. As such, it may not provide a complete picture of the market due to the private nature of many spot market fixtures.
