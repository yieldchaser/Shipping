About Poten & Partners

For nearly 80 years, Poten & Partners has been providing our clients with valuable insight into the international oil, gas and shipping markets. We are a team of experienced brokers, commercial and technical consultants, all of whom specialize in the energy and ocean transportation industries. We have over 180 professionals located on four continents, with offices in Houston, New York, London, Athens, Singapore, Perth, Guangzhou, and Long Island. Our global services include: ship and commodity brokerage, research, business intelligence, consulting, commercial advisory and business training.
