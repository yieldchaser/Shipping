# Giving Thanks

For many, the Thanksgiving holiday in the United States is characteristically a time for gathering with friends and family and hopefully reflecting upon a successful year. 2006 is certainly another year that has exceeded many peoples' expectations. For those in the tanker industry, there is much for which to be thankful. The chart below shows the 2006 Poten Tanker Freight Index (PTI) and Poten Tanker Equity Index. Both of these indices are currently experiencing upward trends.

### Reasons to Give Thanks

As we sit in front of our televisions tomorrow and watch football (American style) and eat copious quantities of turkey and trimmings, we can reflect on the following reasons to be thankful:

### ¡   Relatively strong worldwide economic growth driving increased

demand for crude oil and products ¡ A crude oil price that seems to be in a range that has not stifled economic growth, and kept oil company profits high ¡ Tanker rates that have allowed owners to invest in new ships and pay down debt, making it easier to sustain weaknesses in the market ¡ Continued investor interest from Wall Street to Main Street in shipping and oil company stocks ¡ An expected outcome for 2007 that should keep us all thankful
