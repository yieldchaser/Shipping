A decree pronouncing for the vali- dity of a bottomry bond is conclusive as to the validity of the bond, and unless it is appealed from the bond must be con- sidered as valid. At the same time it determines nothing with respect to the amount of money the bondholder is en- titled to recover from the owners of the vessel. That is to be ascertained by the subsequent reference to the registrar and merchants. By this mode of proceeding the party promoting the suit obtains at once the realization of his security, whe- ther it be ship, cargo, and freight, or the value of these as represented in the bail, and the party proceeded against is no less benefited in having the real extent of his liability ascertained by persons competent to the investigation, and that at a comparatively small expense. The Catherine, 3 TV. Eob. 3. 9a.
