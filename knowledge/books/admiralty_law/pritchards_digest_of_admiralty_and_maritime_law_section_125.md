The plaintiff after- wards entered the berth, and was removed by the defendant. Evidence was also given that, according to the usage on board the steamer, the rights of passen- gers inter se to berths during the voyage were to be determined by the way-bill as finally settled, and sent abroad, and that disputes during the voyage, with respect to passengers' accommodation, should be decided by the captain or steward.
