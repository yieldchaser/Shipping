In all eases of bottomry, sal- vage, and wages, the Court of Admiralty possesses an undoubted power to decree a sale of the vessel proceeded against, un- less the demand of the successful suitor is satisfied. The jurisdiction of the court therein is confirmed by the municipal law of this country, and by the general prin- ciples of the maritime law ; and the title conferred by the court in the exercise of this authority is a valid title against the whole world, and is so recognized by the courts of this and of all other countries. The Tremont, 1 W. Eob. 164. 2054.
