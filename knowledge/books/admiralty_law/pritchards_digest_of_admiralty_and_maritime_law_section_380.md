By Masters. (a) Generally. 2358. A master of a vessel is not de- barred from suing for his wages by the fact that he is a joint mortgagee of the ship. The Repulse, 2 W. Bob. 399. 2357. The master of a vessel has the same right to sue for and recover wages due to him as an ordinary seaman, but no more, and therefore he is not entitled to recover in an action for wages until he has been discharged, or the service has been otherwise terminated.
