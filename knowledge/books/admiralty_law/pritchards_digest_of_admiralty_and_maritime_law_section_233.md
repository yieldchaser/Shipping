Ibid. r. 17, No. 338. 139. Nothing in this order shall pre- vent a solicitor from taking out a warrant for the arrest of any property, notwith- standing the entry of a caveat in the "Caveat Warrant Book;" but the party at whose instance any property in respect of which a caveat is entered shall be ar- rested, shall be liable to have the warrant discharged and to be condemned in costs and damages, unless he shall show, to the satisfaction of the judge, good and sufficient reason for having so done.
