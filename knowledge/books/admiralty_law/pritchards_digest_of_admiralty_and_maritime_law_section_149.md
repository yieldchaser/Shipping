In an action by shipowners to limit their liability in respect of a colli- sion with their vessel, and where it ap- peared that the master, who was on board at the time of the collision was a part owner, and the collision occurred with- out the negligence or privity of the re- mainder of the owners, they have a right to have their liability limited, with a reservation of any right of action there may be against the master personally in respect of his negligence. The Crieket, The Endeavour, 48 L. T. 585 ; 5 Asp. 53. 1424. In a cause of limitation of lia- bility under the 54th section of the Mer- chant Shipping Act Amendment Act, 1862, arising out of a collision, the plain- tiffs alleged that the collision occurred without their fault or privity. It was proved that the master, who was also part owner of the vessel sued, was on board at the time of the collision, but not on deck, the vessel being then in charge of the second mate and a coasting pilot. Held, that under these circumstances there was no obligation upon the master to be on deck; that the fact of his being part owner was no reason for charging the other owners with blame ; and the fact of his being on board at the time of the collision was not in itself sufficient to show that it occurred " with his fault or privity." The Obey, L. E. 1 A. & E. 103. 1425.
