But the law cannot well apply such nice distinctions in practice; and the cases which hold an unconditional purchaser not in possession liable for supplies, although the furnisher had no knowledge that he was owner, or a mortgagee who has taken possession liable although the furnisher did not know of his title or possession, may rest, either upon the ground that he holds the benefit and must therefore admit his request for it, or that his ownership •confers a constructive agency and authority on the person — usually the master — who ordered the supplies or repairs.
