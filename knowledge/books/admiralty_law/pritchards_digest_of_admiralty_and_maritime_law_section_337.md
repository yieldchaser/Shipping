Within fourteen days after a consent for taking evidence by affidavit as between the parties has been given, or within such time as the parties may agree upon, or the court or a judge may allow, the plaintiff shall file his affidavits and deliver to the defendant or his solici- tor a list thereof. Ord. XXXVIII. r. 25, No. 545. 1636. The defendant, within fourteen days thereafter, or such other time as aforesaid, shall file his affidavits and deliver to the plaintiff or his solicitor a list thereof. Ibid. r. 26, No. 546. 1637. Within seven days thereafter, or such other time as aforesaid, the plain- tiff shall file his affidavits in reply, which are to be strictly confined to matters in reply, and shall deliver to the defendant or his solicitor a list thereof. Ibid. r. 27, No. 547. 1638.
