(501) When the examination-in- chief has been completed, the examiner reads over to the witness the answers he has taken down, and procures his signature at the end of that part of the deposition, and, according to the old practice, also at the end of each preced- ing sheet thereof. (501a) The same course is pursued on the cross-examination and re-examination. (502) In the cross-examination the exa- miner, in taking down the evidence, embodies the question in the answer.
