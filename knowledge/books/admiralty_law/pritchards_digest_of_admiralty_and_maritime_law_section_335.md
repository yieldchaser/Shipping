In a cause of damage by collision, before appearance of defendant -witnesses were allowed to be examined on proof that the ship was about to sail for Syria. She sailed in fact, and no opportunity was given to cross-examine. The Chance, Swabey, 294 ; 30 L. T. 219 ; 6 W. R. 221. 1590.
