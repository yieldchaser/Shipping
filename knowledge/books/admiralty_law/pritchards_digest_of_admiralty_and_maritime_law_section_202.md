An express authority is necessary from a part owner of a ship to the ship's husband to order works not necessary as repairs, such as lengthening a ship ; but such authority once given cannot be re- voked after it has been acted upon, and it is for the part owner when sued for contribution to prove that it was revoked before the works were commenced, or a contract for them entered into.
