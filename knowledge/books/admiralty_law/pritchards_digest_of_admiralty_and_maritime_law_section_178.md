The jurisdiction of the Trinity House of Leith under their charter of incorporation of the 27th of July, 1797, and under 1 Geo. 4, c. xxxvii.,f and 5 Geo. 4, c. xxxix., does not extend to the district between Orfordness and the mouth of the Thames, which district is subject to the jurisdiction of the London Trinity House. For the bye-laws issued by this Trinity House, see P. R. No. 325 of 1882, p. 132. 1952.
