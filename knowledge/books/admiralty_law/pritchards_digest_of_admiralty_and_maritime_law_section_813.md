For provisions enabling the har- bour master to cause any vessel laid by or neglected as unfit for sea service, and lying within the limits of the harbour, dock, or pier, to be removed therefrom at the expense of the owner and laid upon any part of the strand, sea-shore, or other place where it may be placed without injury to any person, and to recover the expense of such removal from the owner by summary complaint in Eng- land or Ireland before a justice, and in Scotland before the sheriff, and, in case of refusal or neglect to pay such charges within seven days after they are awarded by such justice or sheriff, empowering the harbour master to levy such charges by distress and sale of the property or any part thereof, and the justice or sheriff to issue his warrant accordingly, see the Harbours, Docks, and Piers Clauses Act, 1847 (c. 27), s. 57.
