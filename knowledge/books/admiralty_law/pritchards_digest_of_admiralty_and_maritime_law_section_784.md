The master or owner of every ship shall pay to every seaman his wages in the case of a home-trade ship within two days after the termination of the agree- ment or when such seaman is discharged, whichever first happens ; and in the case of all other ships (except ships employed in the southern whale fishery or on other voyages for which by the terms of their agreement they are compensated by shares in the profits of the adventure) within three days after the cargo has been de- livered, or within five days after the sea- man's discharge, whichever first happens; and in all cases the seaman at the time of his discharge is entitled to be paid on account a sum equal to one-fourth part of the balance due.
