A master has no authority to bind his owners by writing forward to a broker in a foreign port, prior to the ship's arrival there, authorizing the broker to charter his ship. The authority of a master to bind his owners by charter-party arises when he is in a foreign port, and his owners are not there, and there is difficulty in communicating with them.
