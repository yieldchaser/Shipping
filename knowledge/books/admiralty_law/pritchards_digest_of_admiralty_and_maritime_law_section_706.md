If any such expenses as are to be borne by the owner are paid by any con- sular officer or other person on behalf of her Majesty, or if any other expenses in that respect are so paid, and the man's wages are not accounted for to such func- tionary as required, such expenses shall be repaid to him by the master of the ship, and if not so repaid, the amount, with costs, shall be a charge upon the ship, and be recoverable from the master or owner as a debt due to her Majesty, and by ordinary process of law or in the same manner as wages.
