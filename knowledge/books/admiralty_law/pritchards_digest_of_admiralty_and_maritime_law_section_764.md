Every officer of the Board of Trade, every commissioned officer of any of her Majesty's ships on full pay, every British consular officer, the registrar-general of seamen and his assistant, every chief officer of customs in her Majesty's do- minions, and every superintendent, may, in cases where he suspects the law has not been complied with, exercise the fol- lowing powers : — He may require the owner, master, or any of the crew, of any British ship to produce any official log- books or other documents relating to such crew or any member thereof in their pos- session or control; he may require any such master to produce a list of all per- sons on board his ship, and take copies of such official log-books, or documents, or of any part thereof ; he may muster the crew of any such ship ; he may sum- mon the master to appear and give any explanation concerning such ship or her crew or the said official log-books or documents. Penalty for refusing or ne- glecting to obey any such requirements, not exceeding £20. Ibid. s. 13. 13. Chain Cables and Anchors Acts. 1 . Generally* 90.
