For it is a term which is used with various meanings. In a technical sense, it denotes the having taken the benefit of an insolvent law ; in the popular sense, a general inability to pay debts ; and, in a mercantile sense, a stoppage of payment, or failure in one's circumstances, as evinced by some overt act.
