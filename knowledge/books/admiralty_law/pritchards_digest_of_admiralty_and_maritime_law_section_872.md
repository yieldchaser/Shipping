Except as by this act excepted, all persons shall have in the province of Ontario the like rights and remedies in all matters (including cases of contracts and tort, and proceedings in rem or inpersonam) arising out of or connected with naviga- tion, shipping, trade, or commerce on any river, lake, canal, or inland water of which the whole or part is in the province of On- tario, as such persons would have in any existing British vice-admiralty court, if the process of such court extended to such province. See the Maritime Jurisdiction Act, 1882 (c. 34), s. 1. 174.
