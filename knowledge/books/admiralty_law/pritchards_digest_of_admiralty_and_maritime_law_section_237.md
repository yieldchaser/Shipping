(81) On taking out the summons the prac- titioner procures a blank form of order from the registry, endorses it with the name of the cause, and with his name and address, for the purpose of identification, and places there- on an adhesive stamp of five shillings in ad- dition to the stamp of three shillings on the summons. This order is handed in to the registry on obtaining the summons. (82) When the order has been made by the judge it is drawn up in the registry on the stamped form previously left there, and it is handed to the practitioner or his clerk on his application for it. A copy of this order is served on the adverse solicitor.
