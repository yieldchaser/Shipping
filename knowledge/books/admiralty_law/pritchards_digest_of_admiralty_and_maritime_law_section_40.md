The Board of Trade may make modifications and alterations from time to time in the rules for measurement of tonnage. See M. 8. Act, 1854 (c. 104), s. 29, as amended by M. 8. Act, 1872 (c. 73), s. 3. 86. In the rules for measurement of tonnage, the tonnage deck is to be taken to be the upper deck in ships which have less than three decks, and to be the second deck from below in other ships. All measurements are taken in feet and fractions of feet, fractions of feet being expressed in decimals. See the M. 8. Act, 1854 (c. 104), s. 20. 87. The tonnage of every ship to be registered, with the exceptions mentioned in the next section ; and the tonnage of every ship to which such rule can be applied, whether about to be registered or not, is to be ascertained according to the measurements, deductions and divi- sions in rule 1 and the table therein set forth as to transverse areas, computation from areas, poop and other closed-in space, and cases of two or more decks.
