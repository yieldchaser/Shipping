(410) The law invests the master with a necessary control over his passengers. He may make proper regulations for their government, such as may ensure their safety, promote the general comfort, and preserve decent order ; and these regula- tions he may enforce by all temperate and needful exercise of power. But here his authority over his passengers finds its limit, and he is a trespasser if he goes beyond it. Oonkling's United States Admiralty Practice (2nd ed.j, p. 439.
