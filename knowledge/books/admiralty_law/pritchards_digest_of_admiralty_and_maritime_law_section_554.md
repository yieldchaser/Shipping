By order of the master of the P. an anchor and chain, weighing thirteen tons, was obtained at Eamsgate, and put on board the luggers C., thirty-four tons, and theL., eighteen tons, which, with the lifeboat's crew, in addition to their own on board, were towed out by the A., and anchored near the P. At daylight of the 5th the A. assisted two other tugs in endeavouring to tow the P. off the sand, but her services not being longer required, she returned to Eamsgate at about midday. In the evening the gale increased, and the luggers were ordered to proceed to the Nore, where they arrived on the 6th, and subsequently on the 12th they conveyed the anchor and chain to London, and returned to Eams- gate on the 14th.
