Draught of Water.* 151. The Board of Trade may, in cases in which they think it expedient, direct any person appointed by them for the purpose, to record in such manner and with such particulars as the Board may direct, the draught of water of any sea- going ship as shown on the scale on her stem and stern post, upon leaving any dock, wharf or harbour, for sea; and such person shall thereupon keep such record, and from time to time forward it or a copy to the Board, and such record or copy produced out of the custody of the Board shall be admissible in evidence of the draught of water of the ship at the time specified. See the M. 8. Act, 1871 (c. 110), s. 5. 152. The master of every British sea- going ship, on proceeding to sea, shall record her draught of water in the official log, and produce the same to any prin- cipal officer of customs when required. Penalty for breach not exceeding £20. Ibid. 153.
