Eemuneration is due to a vessel for putting its second officer on board another vessel to navigate it, in conse- quence of the death of the master, besides the remuneration to that officer for his services. Havre, July 17, 1872; J. H. 1872:1:165. 1223. Held, that the remuneration to the salving vessel was to be propor- tionate to the risk run by that vessel from being so deprived of its second officer, and to the benefit to the ves- sel so assisted. The rule of one-third salvage applies only in cases of derelict found at sea. Douai, 20 May, 1863; J. H. 1864: 2 : 120 ; Bordeaux, 7 March, 1865 ; J. H. 1865 :2: 142. 1228. The property derelict must have been deserted by the ship's company without intention to return, it must have been discovered on or in the open sea, that is, out of sight of land, and must have been effectually salved.
