An owner of a ship, in possession of her, and having both the legal and equitable title, is liable for all supplies furnished and all repairs made to her, and all work and service rendered for her benefit, by order of the master, and in general, for all the contracts made by him for the benefit of the vessel ; because, from the necessity of the case, the master is invested with a very wide authority to do and provide whatever is requisite for his ship.
