If the master or owner of any British ship does or permits to be done any thing, or carries or permits to be carried any documents, with intent to conceal the British character of such ship from any person entitled by British law to inquire into the same, or to assume a foreign character, or with intent to de- ceive any such person, such ship shall be forfeited to her Majesty ; and the master, if he is privy to the commission of the offence, is guilty of a misdemeanour.
