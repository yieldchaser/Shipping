In January, the barque C, laden with lead and grass, was within 1£ mile of the Broad Sand, Torbay, with the wind driving violently on to the sand and a heavy sea on. She had been exposed to a terrific storm the previous night and was water- logged with seven feet of water in her, and her mizenmast was gone. She had two anchors down, her crew were exhausted and unable to work, and she was in great danger of parting with her anchor and being driven upon the Broad Sand. The steam-tug P., having been telegraphed for, about three p.m. left Dartmouth Harbour, having nine men on board in addition to her crew, and with all hatches battened down she succeeded in rounding Berry Head through a tremendous sea and reaching Torbay. She placed five of her additional crew on board the barque, and then pro- ceeded to the assistance of another vessel. She afterwards returned to the barque and towed her into Dartmouth.
