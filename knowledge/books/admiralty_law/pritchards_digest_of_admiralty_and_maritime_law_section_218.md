To report Loss of Anchors or Chains. 183. When anchors or cables are lost from any vessel, the pilot in charge is at once to report the occurrence in writing to the secretary of the Trinity House, London, specifying the place and time of the occurrence, the state of the weather, the direction of the wind, time of tide, quantity of cable riding by, whether the cable was slipped or parted, and the quantity lost; see Regulations of the Trinity House, London, of November, 1853, and July, 1862. 9.
