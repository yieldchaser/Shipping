In estimating the value of a snip lost by collision, the best evidence is the opinion of competent persons who knew the ship shortly previous to the time it was lost. The second best evidence is the opinion of persons conversant with shipping, and the transfers thereof. Many other circumstances may be called in aid, — as the original price of the vessel, the amount of repairs done to her, the sum at which she was insured, and the like ; but these facts have a slighter bear- ing upon the case.
