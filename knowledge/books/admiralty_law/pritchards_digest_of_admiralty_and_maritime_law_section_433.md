When damage is done by a ship both to persons and goods, the liability of the ship is to be estimated at not less than £15 per ton for the purpose of ad- justing the compensation to be paid to claimants in respect of loss of life or personal injury. Nixon v. Roberts, 1 Johns. & H. 739 ; 7 Jur. N.S. 820 ; 30 L. J. Oh. 844 ; 9 W. E. 890 ; 4 L. T. N.S. 679. 381. The defendants were the owners of a ship which came into collision with and sunk a China clipper called the 0., of which Captain M. the master, and his wife, the father and mother of the plaintiff, a boy three years of age, suing by his guardian, lost their lives at Shanghai, through col- lision caused by negligence of the defen- dants' servants.
