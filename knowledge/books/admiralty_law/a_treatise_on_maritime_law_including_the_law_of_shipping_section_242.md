In order to decide this question, it is necessary for the jury to attend to what is the punishment of flogging referred to in that law ; and my instruction is, that it is corporal punishment by stripes inflicted with a cat, or any punishment whicl^ in substance and effect, amounts thereto. The particular form of the instrument is not material ; what you must look to is the effect produced. If the man was punished by stripes, inflicted with a rope, and this, in substance and effect, is the same kind of pun- ishment as the punishment of flogging with a cat, then it is prohibited by this law. The degree of severity of the punishment is not material. It is the kind, and not the degree, of punishment which is important.
