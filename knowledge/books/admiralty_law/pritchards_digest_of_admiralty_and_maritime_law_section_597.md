In November, the Eussian barque E., 375 tons, from the Baltic to Cette with deals, was dragging her anchors in the Downs, and in danger of collision in a heavy gale from the S.S.W. with rain and a heavy sea. The lugger L. C, of ten tons, value £500, came up, put three of her crew on board, and made fast a hawser, which, how- ever, very soon parted. The E. driving astern, nearly came into collision with the lugger, and drove out into the open sea without any anchors or chains. The tug P., of 240 h.-p., value £500, came up, and with a hawser got the head of the E. round.
