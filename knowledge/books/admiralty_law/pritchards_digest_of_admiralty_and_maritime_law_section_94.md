The district of the court of survey for London includes the City of London, and the districts of all the Metropolitan county courts, the districts of the county court of Kent holden at' Gravesend, Dartford, Greenwich and Woolwich, the districts of the county court of Essex holden at Brentwood and Bomford, and the district of the county court of Surrey holden at Wandsworth. The dis- trict of any other court of survey in Eng- land is the district of the county court of the place at which the court of survey is held. Ibid. 749. The wreck commissioner is judge of every court of survey in the United Kingdom, and the other judges are those described in the second column, Ibid. 750. A court of survey for a port or district shall consist of a judge sitting with two assessors. See M. S. Act, 1876 (c. 80), s. 7. 751. The courts are held at the places whose names they bear, or at any place within their respective districts, and may, by the permission of the judge, be ad- journed to any place out of those districts.
