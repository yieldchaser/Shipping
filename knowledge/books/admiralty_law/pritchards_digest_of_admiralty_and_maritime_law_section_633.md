She had dragged her anchor, and had eight feet of water in her. She hoisted a signal of distress, which was observed by six salvors who manned the pilot-boat and went to her assistance, and after considerable danger and difficulty five of them boarded the H., leaving one in the pilot-boat, which was in great danger from the state of the weather and sea.
