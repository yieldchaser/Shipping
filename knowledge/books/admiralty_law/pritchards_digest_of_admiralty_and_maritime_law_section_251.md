In a suit instituted under the 4th section of the Admiralty Court Act, 1861, for building and equipping a vessel, the defendants pleaded that the alleged causes of action became vested in a trustee for the plaintiffs' creditors under a composi- tion deed. The plaintiffs in reply alleged that they assigned the causes of action prior to the execution of the deed, and that they were suing as trustees for their assignees. Held, that they might sue as trustees, although they had, but subse- quently to the assignment, executed the composition deed. Held, also, that though the assignment of the causes of action was prior to the arrest of the vessel and the institution of the suit, it carried with it the inchoate right to proceed against the vessel.
