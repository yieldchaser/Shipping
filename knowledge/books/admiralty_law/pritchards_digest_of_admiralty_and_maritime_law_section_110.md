Unless it is the docking time only that limits his declaration, or the latest date for which an excursion certificate can be granted in accordance with the regulations, he is not to grant a certificate for a home- trade, excursion, river, or smooth-water steamer for a shorter period than one month, nor for a foreign-going steamer for less than a period of three months, without the written p. authority of the Board of Trade. In such cases, and also in cases in which the surveyor refuses the certificate altogether, he is to give a statement in writing of the repairs he con- siders necessary to enable him to grant a certificate for the time asked. By this para- graph the surveyor is to understand that a foreign-going steamer that is not fit for a declaration for three months is not to be deemed by him as fit for a certificate at all. The same rule is to apply to other steamers that are not fit for a month's certificate.
