In cases where such death has happened, and the consul finds after inquiry that the vessel is bound direct, or will shortly return to the United Kingdom, he will not demand the wages and effects unless special circumstances render it expe- dient; but when the consul finds that the settlement of the claims of relatives will be unduly retarded if delivery of the wages, &c. by the master is delayed until the arrival of the ship in the United Kingdom, he will require from the master the payment of the wages of the deceased either in cash or by bill on the owners, and the delivery of his effects or of the proceeds of sale of them.
