The purpose of this alteration undoubtedly was to restore the bill of lading to the meaning and operation which it was supposed to have had before that case. We think, however, though this case (which we shall consider more fully in another connection) be law, that the term " dangers of the seas " includes substantially all that is comprehended in the latter part of the new phrase.
