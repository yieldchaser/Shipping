If a minor is shipped on a whaling voyage by his father, and, after serving several years, deserts after having become of age, the father is entitled to his wages earned during bis minority. Coffin v. Shaw, U. S. D. C, Mass., 19 Law Reporter, 146. So the minor, if he ships after the death of bis father, may avoid the contract by deserting, and recover on a quantum meruit Vent v.
