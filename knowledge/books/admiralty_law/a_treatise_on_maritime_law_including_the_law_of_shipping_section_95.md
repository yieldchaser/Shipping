The latter rule is that which we think is favored, and will generally be adopted in this country ; and the simplest method of applying it would be, for the shipper to pay the whole freight, deducting what would be the ordinary or usual cost of carrying the cargo from the port at which he received them to that of their original des- tination.1 The questions relating to pro rata freight have often arisen in cases of capture and recapture, or release and return of the ship ; or condemnation and sale of the ship and cargo and an ultimate recovery of the proceeds ; or sale under decree for salvage, and payment of proceeds to the shipper or his representatives, after deducting salvage. We do not consider these cases by them- selves, for the general rules and principles, we have already stated, are perfectly applicable to them.
