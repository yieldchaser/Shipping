The 56th section of the Harbours Act, 1847, enables a harbour-master to remove any obstruction from a harbour, and recover the expenses of such removal from the " owner." A vessel belonging to W. was wrecked at the mouth of, and obstructed, a harbour. The harbour- master removed it, and brought an action to recover the costs of such removal against W., and also against E., with whom the vessel had been insured, and who had paid as on a total loss. No notice of abandonment was given to E. Held, by the Court of Appeal (affirming the decision of the Queen's Bench Divi- sion), that W. was liable under the statute, and that he was not entitled to recover over against E.
