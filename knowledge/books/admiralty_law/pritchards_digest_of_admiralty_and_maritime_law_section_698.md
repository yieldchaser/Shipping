(115) Capture does not dissolve the con- tract for wages ; at most it is but suspended during the prize proceedings, for the event of which the parties have a right to wait : and by the subsequent restoration of the vessel, the contract revives in its full force, and the parties are remitted to their former character and rights. The Saratoga, 2 Gallison's Eep. 178.
