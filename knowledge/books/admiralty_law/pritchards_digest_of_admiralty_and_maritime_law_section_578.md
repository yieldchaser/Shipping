The N. was derelict and in a miserable state of confusion, with 1 3 inches of water in her hold, both maintopsails hanging to the yard-arms, the ends towing alongside, her wheel unshipped, no rudder wheel ropes, compass, chronometer, sextant, or flag on board, and only her long-boat left.
