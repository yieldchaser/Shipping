But all the reasons for the rule are not so obvious. The effect of this rule is, that whatever is sacrificed, becomes by being sacrificed, the property, and the loss, equally, of all who are benefited by it And in order that this rule should have this effect, it is obvious that it must be so applied, that if A's goods are jettisoned and B's saved, A shall lose as much and save as much, and B" shall lose as much and save as much, as if B's goods had been jettisoned and A's saved. It follows from this that the owner of the goods sacrificed is not repaid their full value, for then he would be in a better posi- tion than they who pay him.
