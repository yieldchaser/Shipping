Orr v. Dickenson, supra. 81. Shipbuilders in America mortgaged a ship, and then, with the consent of the mortgagee, sent her to England for sale. The mortgage was duly registered in America, but by agreement with the mortgagee was not indorsed on the cer- tificate of registry, and the purchaser had no notice of such mortgage. Held, by the Court of Appeal, that the right to the ship acquired under American law must be recognized, but that the pur- chase, having taken place in this country, must be governed by English law.
