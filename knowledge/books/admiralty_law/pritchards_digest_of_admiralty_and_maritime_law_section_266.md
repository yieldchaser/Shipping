On what Days. 602. In Admiralty actions no instru- ment except a warrant shall be served on a Sunday, Good Friday, or Christmas Day. Ord. LXVII. r. 12, No. 1023. 3. Time of Day. 603. Service of pleadings, notices, sum- monses, orders, rules, and other proceed- ings, shall be effected before the hour of six in the afternoon, except on Saturdays, when it shall be effected before the hour of two in the afternoon. Ord. LXTV. r. 11, No. 971. 604. Service effected after six in the afternoon on any week-day except Satur- day shall, for the purpose of computing any period of time subsequent to such service, be deemed to have been effected on the following day. Service effected after two in the afternoon on Saturday shall for the like purpose be deemed to have been effected on the following Monday. Ibid. 4. Within what Period. 605.
