MARKET COMMENTARY
Bulkers: The Baltic index has corrected, closing today at 2752 points, 6.5% lower than last Friday’s
2944 points. Regardless the s+p market continues to show strength, maintaining healthy volumes of
secondhand transactions, although activity has slightly declined compared to earlier in the summer.
Asset values remain firm overall, with modern and Japanese built ships in high demand. This week we
have recorded seven deals comprising 2 capes, 2 post-Panamax, 1 Supramax & 2 Handysize bulk
carriers. We have also gathered from market insights that:
- Japanese sellers invited offers on 15th July 2026 for their panamax bulker YARRA (78,184
dwt, blt 2015 Sasebo/Japan, KR, ss due 05/2030 dd due 05/2028, 7 ho/7 ha, B&W 6S60ME-C8) which
is committed for a price region $ 27 mill – $ 28 mill.
- The ultramax INCE BEYLERBEYI (61,429 dwt, blt 2012 Iwagi/Japan, NK ss/dd due 01/2027, 5
ho/5 ha, 4 x 30.7t CR, B&W 6S50MC-C8) is committed on off-market basis for region $ 22 mill, a
softer price compared with two-week old sale of HAATO (61,472 dwt blt 2011 Shin Kasado/Japan,
surveys freshly passed) for $23.5 mill.
- Offers were invited on 14th July 2026 for the supramax IVS CRIMSON CREEK (57,945
dwt, blt 2014 Shin Kurushima/Japan, NK, ss due 07/2029 dd due 06/2027, 5 ho/5 ha, 4 x 30.5t
CR, B&W 6S50ME-B9) and the vessel is committed at region $ 23.5 mill.
- Vietnamese sellers invited offers on 10th July 2026 for their supramax HPC ATLANTIC (56,064
dwt, blt 2013 Minaminippon/Japan, NK, ss due 10/2030 dd due 10/2028, 5 ho/5 ha, 4 x 30t CR, B&W
6S50MC-C, Tier II) and we understand that they have received levels at region $ 19.5 mill basis charter
free delivery 25 July – 30 September 2026 at Singapore-Japan range.
For a detailed review and additional information regarding these week’s transactions, kindly refer to
our “representative sales” section.
