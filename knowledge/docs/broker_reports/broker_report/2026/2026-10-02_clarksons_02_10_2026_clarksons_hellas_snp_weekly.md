Desk Talk
Tanker rates remain at or near record highs as hopes of a reopening faded. President Trump rejected
Iran's proposal to reopen the Strait, at least five tankers were struck in Hormuz this week, and the US is
reported to be sending a third aircraft carrier t o the region. VLCC earnings are averaging over USD
700,000 p/d on the Baltic Exchange's assessment, with Middle East Gulf-China voyages at USD 1.2 million
p/d. The strength has spilled into Suezmaxes, where average earnings jumped by nearly USD 130,000 on
Thursday to around USD 448,000 p/d, as charterers downsizing from VLCCs to cut freight costs ran into
limited supply. Product tankers continue to climb, with average clean earnings estimated near USD
140,000 p/d, led by the LR segment s, though a possible US diesel export ban and China's reported
suspension of October product exports are risks to the segment.
S&P activity on crude carriers remains firm, with several players moving on tonnage that can be
delivered promptly, particularly units positioned close to the Middle East Gulf. Sellers' strong price ideas
are being met by willing buyers, keeping modern secondhand VLCCs and Suezmaxes priced well
above Newbuildings, a record premium in the case of VLCCs.
On dry, the period market appears to be at a healthy standstill, with fixtures continuing to be concluded
at like last-done levels, while spot earnings have taken a bit of a breather, but may logically pick back
up once people are back from the holidays, primarily the Chinese Golden Week. For reference, the BDI
settled at 3,148 points , whereas last week, had been  trading in the 3,4 00-3,500 area . Overall, we
continue to see a healthy number of enquiries across the board of sizes, with a slight spike notable for
modern eco Ultramax, as well as Kamsarmax tonnage, on the gearless size.
In the  gearless space,  Capesize BULK JOYANCE  (ABT 175,636 DWT, 2012, JINHAI HEAVY IND) was
concluded at USD 31.1m, while the Post -Panamax LC MILADY (ABT 93,758 DWT, 2011, SHANHAIGUAN
SB) was sold at a price in the high USD 14m's. Further down the size-range, the modern Eco Kamsarmax
GCL HAZIRA (ABT 81,986 DWT, 2021, NANTONG COSCO KHI) was acquired by German interests for a
price of USD 38.8m, basis delivery October/November 2026. Meanwhile, BABITONGA (ABT 81,600 DWT,
2019, TSUNEISHI ZOSEN) changed hands for USD 38.5m , with the new owners being C/O Sincere

a price in the high USD 13m's.
