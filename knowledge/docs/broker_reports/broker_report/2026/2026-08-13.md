Rates

Activity continues to build across August and September dates in the West. While
vessel availability remains reasonable on paper, the tonnage to cargo ratio continues

In addition, September CPC stems are expected to be released imminently. With rates

prioritise CPC cargoes over adjacent markets.
North Sea
The stems through the 2nd decade have been depleted for Aframaxes with some
Suezmax activity and a few relets in the mix. With a limited amount of fixtures the
market has steadily fallen over the last week by around 20 points. US markets are
strengthening and pulling tonnage out of the North Sea, so we may have started to
bottom out, and heading into the 3rd decade date wise we could start to see some
pinch points which will see rates bounce.
Mediterranean
A steady week activity wise so far but rates have been eroded. Natural window
pushing into 3rd decade with a list that has been trimmed but still looks relatively
healthy for charterers. With the issues in the Black Sea, tonnage that wouldn’t
normally look X-Mediterranean is recalibrating. Early West Mediterranean positions
are looking at turning right and heading for the land of opportunity across the pond.
