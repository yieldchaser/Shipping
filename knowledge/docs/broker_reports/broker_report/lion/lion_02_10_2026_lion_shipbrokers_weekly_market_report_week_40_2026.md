## Bulkers
Our Chinese friends are currently away for Golden Week (October 1st-8th), celebrating the
Mid-Autumn Festival with traditional mooncakes and lanterns. With multiple holidays across the Far
East - including South Korea (October 3rd-9th) - the s+p market has a quieter feel (and
significantly fewer emails!). That said, a notable wave of transactions emerged as numerous buyers
rushed to conclude business ahead of the break at firm levels.
Uncertainty prevails in the chartering market this week, as Charterers are holding back most cargoes in
the hopes of securing lower rates. The exception to this is the US Gulf, where there is more activity,
along with cautious optimism for East Coast South America cargoes within the coming weeks.
