The top spots continue to be dominated by major integrated oil companies. Trading companies also make their presence known in the market as several of the largest ones appear in the top 20. A few nationally affiliated companies have significantly grown in the ranks, such as Statoil and PetroChina. Refiners such as Sunoco and Valero still remain within the top 20, although their ranks have slipped somewhat on lower reported fixture activity.
Overall reported VLCC spot market activity is down 8 percent from 2007. The big news in 2008 is that after a three year run in the top VLCC chartering spot, ExxonMobil has slipped to the number two slot.

## The Year Ahead
The spot market remains an important part of the information flow in the tanker industry as well as a critical part of the energy supply chain. While many of the same company names continually appear at the top of the spot chartering list, the changes often reflect trends such as the rising importance of India and China as well as oil company consolidation. Although it will take another 12 months to sort out this year's champions, 2009 promises to be an exciting year in the tanker market as charterers scramble to retain their positions.
