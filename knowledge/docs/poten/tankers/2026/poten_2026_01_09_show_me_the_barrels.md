## Top Reported Dirty Spot Charterers for 2025*
It is that time of the year again. Who comes out on top in our annual ranking of dirty spot charterers? It is no surprise that Unipec remained the lead spot charterer in 2024, taking care of 15.4% of the reported fixtures. However, below the Chinese behemoth, we saw some changes last year. ExxonMobil leapfrogged Chevron and Shell to 2 nd place (up from 4 th ), while Petrobras jumped from 9 th to 4 th on the strength of growing Brazilian production and exports. The large oil traders also did well, with Trafigura showing particular strength (from 22 nd to 11 th ). Overall cargo volumes and fixtures were down slightly compared to 2024.

## Chart: Top 10 VLCC Spot Charterers
Unipec's VLCC spot charters were slightly higher in 2025 (up 8 relative to 2024). The fixture volume of Petrobras, the No. 2, jumped from 89 to 141 (+58%). India's IOC, on the other reduced its activity in the VLCC sport market, falling from 2 nd to 5 th , reporting 13 less fixtures. Chinese charterer PetroChina re- entered the top 10, at the expense of Thailand's PTT, who came in 12 th . Overall volume of reported VLCC fixtures was up from 2,147 in 2024 to 2,345 in 2025 (+9%). This resurgence of activity was also reflected in the VLCC freight market.

## Chart: Top 10 Suezmax Spot Charterers
In the Suezmax segment, Total took the top spot from ExxonMobil, which dropped two places. Chevron also surpassed ExxonMobil to settle in 2 nd place. Glencore dropped out of the top 10 (to 12 th ) and was replaced by Trafigura (up from 19 th in 2024). Total Suezmax spot fixtures increased by 40 (2.5%) compared to 2024 (1,672 versus 1,632).

## Chart: Top 10 Aframax Spot Charterers
There were some major changes in the Aframax segment. Vitol reclaimed the top spot after dropping to #10 in last year's ranking. Chevron, last year's No. 1 dropped to 5 th . SOCAR, the State Oil Company of Azerbaijan moved up one spot to 2 nd . Overall, reported Aframax spot fixture activity was down 12% in 2025, compared to 2024 (1,983 versus 2,262).
