## Atlantic Coast
Whatever lies ahead for the volume of US gasoline demand, the trade of gasoline stocks from across the Atlantic will remain interlinked with supply. So far, the pull back in demand has had minimal impact on vessel fixtures or earnings. Perhaps the US Atlantic Coast's tight gasoline manufacturing capacity as well as limitations on bring more domestic supply into it promote this trade. With gasoline inventories near seasonal lows, currently high distillate inventories may constrain refiners' flexibility to restore gasoline inventory. The recent drop in gasoline prices may also prompt an unexpected late summer spurt in demand. A potentially perfect storm might form if low inventories and a constraint to fill them collide with a spurt in demand. This would be a tempest that only more trans-Atlantic gasoline supply could quell.
