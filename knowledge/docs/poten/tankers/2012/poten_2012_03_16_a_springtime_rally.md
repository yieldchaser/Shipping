## A Springtime Rally
Anyone following the tanker market during the last few years knows that bright spots have been few and far between. This week, however, VLCC spot market rates took upward strides bringing time charter equivalent earnings to levels not seen in quite some time. The combined uptick in fixing activity and waning vessel availability in the Arabian Gulf is a perfect recipe for shipowners in the spot market.
Freight rates for the benchmark VLCC trade from the Arabian Gulf to Far East increased by 10 Worldscale points over the past week bringing time charter equivalent (TCE) earnings into the $38,500 per day range. The knock on effect in the West African market is even more pronounced. West Africa to Far East rates have jumped nearly 20 Worldscale points equating to time charter equivalents in excess of $50,000 per day!
The chart below shows Worldscale rates for both Ras Tanura to Chiba and Bonny to Chiba voyages versus VLCC availabilities on a 30 day forward basis.

## Vessel Availablity
VLCC Availability - Next 30 Days WS Rate (AG - FE) WS Rate (WAF - FE) The March Arabian Gulf lifting program developed slowly putting more pressure on the back-end of the month. As a result, VLCC availabilities which have averaged 92 vessels year-to-date have dropped to below 66 vessels on a thirty-day forward basis. The increased activity in the last decade of the month combined with the strong interest for westbound cargoes snapped up a large portion of swing tonnage and increased ton-mile demand.
Overall fixing activity in the Arabian Gulf has increased significantly year-on-year. By this point in 2011, 254 spot fixtures were reported ex-Arabian Gulf. This year reported spot fixture volumes increased 25% to 319. The distribution amongst the types of charterers remains consistent from year to year. Chinese charterers account for about 35% of ex-Arabian Gulf activity while oil companies continue to dominate concluding 62% of the fixtures.

## Rate Rollercoaster Ride
Although conspicuously absent for the past few years, the VLCC market is no stranger to volatility. The chart below shows time charter equivalents for Ras Tanura to Chiba and Bonny to Chiba on 260kt, as well as the one-year time charter rates on a weekly basis.

## VLCC Spot & Term Charter Rates
1-Yr TC Ras Tanura - Chiba Bonny - Chiba Spot market rates for both trades have averaged around $53,000 per day over the historical period shown above, while the one-year time charter rate has averaged $49,000 per day. The average disparity between one-year term charter and spot rates is minimal, the relative volatility is not. The standard deviation for the Ras Tanura to Chiba voyage is $46,200 per day and $35,300 per day for the Bonny to Chiba voyage. It is $17,400 per day for the one-year time charter rates highlighting the more stable nature of the term charter market.
If spot rates continue their upward climb, term charter rates will likely rally in tandem. When the ship supply in the Arabian Gulf rebalances at some point in the future, spot charter rates will soften accordingly. But as history has shown, term charter rates demonstrate more resistance. Optimism from a spot market spike tends to provide lingering support for term charter rates. There is reason to believe the spot run-up could persist due to increased movements from factors like Chinese strategic stocking. Those interested in securing term charters for one year should strike while the iron is hot.
