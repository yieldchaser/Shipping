## Summary
weak demand difficult
During the first quarter of 2012, the dual closure of Valero's 235 kbpd Aruba refinery and the remaining 350 kbpd of capacity at Hovensa's St. Croix facility offered the potential that the loss of this Caribbean refining capacity would provide a boost to product tanker demand. Long a source of clean product imports to the USAC, the loss of this short-haul supply would require imports from longer-haul locations for a region shedding its own refining capacity, so the hypothesis went.

## weak demand difficult
During the first quarter of 2012, the dual closure of Valero's 235 kbpd Aruba refinery and the remaining 350 kbpd of capacity at Hovensa's St. Croix facility offered the potential that the loss of this Caribbean refining capacity would provide a boost to product tanker demand. Long a source of clean product imports to the USAC, the loss of this short-haul supply would require imports from longer-haul locations for a region shedding its own refining capacity, so the hypothesis went. Although tonne-miles have risen sequentially, the continuing US product demand malaise has muted the effect.

## Europe/FSU
Canada/Mex Lat Amer Africa AG/Asia The initial data has supported the market hypothesis -- as the loss of Caribbean supply provided a sequential boost in tonne-mile demand -- but weak product demand has still left tonne-miles 3.9% lower yoy during the first quarter, despite a rebound from 4q11. PADD1 gasoline consumption - the key import demand driver - was 69 kbpd yoy lower during 1q12, and 80 kbpd lower yoy in April. PADD1 clean product imports rose from 705 kbpd in March to 816 kbpd in June, according to the EIA's weekly data, suggesting incremental demand, but TCE earnings on the Baltic TC2 route for 37,000 MT UKC-USAC have slid below zero in July.

## MR earnings are
languishing despite refinery closures With the IEA reporting that PADD1 gasoline inventories slid to 52.2 MB last week, or 10.3% below the five-year average, market observers have suggested that required restocking will continue to feed a surge in TC2 fixtures. Refiners may bring inventories back to more normal levels, but with gasoline demand hitting its seasonal peak in July and with Delta scheduled to restart its 185 kbpd Trainer refinery in September, refiners may have other avenues for inventory restocking.
