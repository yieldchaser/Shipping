## Summary
European diesel prices
A series of refinery outages in the Atlantic Basin and anticipated seasonal maintenance have sharply tightened regional gasoil balances and have sent European diesel prices soaring. Typically, this would open up the trans-Atlantic arbitrage and encourage US Gulf diesel cargoes into Northwest Europe or the Med, stimulating MR demand. Unfortunately, the explosion and fire at Venezuela's 645 kbpd Amuay refinery, temporary USG refinery closures from Hurricane Isaac and the August fire at Chevron's Richmond refinery have all combined to remove supply and push diesel prices higher in the USG.

## European diesel prices
A series of refinery outages in the Atlantic Basin and anticipated seasonal maintenance have sharply tightened regional gasoil balances and have sent European diesel prices soaring. Typically, this would open up the trans-Atlantic arbitrage and encourage US Gulf diesel cargoes into Northwest Europe or the Med, stimulating MR demand. Unfortunately, the explosion and fire at Venezuela's 645 kbpd Amuay refinery, temporary USG refinery closures from Hurricane Isaac and the August fire at Chevron's Richmond refinery have all combined to remove supply and push diesel prices higher in the USG. With a closed arb and plentiful tonnage, MR freight rates on the Baltic TC14 route for 38 USG-UKC have languished. As Europe heads into its Autumn refinery maintenance season and as supplies come back on line in the USG, an reopening arb should stimulate activity.

## European Crude Runs
These heightened crack spreads reflect the loss of European refining capacity, including the permanent closure of the UK's 220 kbpd Coryton refinery in July, as well as the earlier shutdown of France's 105 kbpd Berre L'Etang refinery. Anticipated refinery closures are also weighing on the product market, as ExxonMobil plans to shut one 80 kbpd crude distillation unit at its 330 kbpd Fawley plant in the UK this month, while ERG will be closing its 89 kbpd Rome refinery this autumn. Combined with scheduled European refinery maintenance in September and October, European crude runs should experience a more pronounced seasonal decline this year, with October crude runs hitting 11.5 mbpd, as shown in the following chart.
