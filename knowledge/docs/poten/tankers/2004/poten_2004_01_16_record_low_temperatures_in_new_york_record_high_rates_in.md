## Record Low Temperatures in New York, Record High Rates in the Caribbean
Record Low Temperatures in New York, Record High Rates in the Caribbean While even the most thick-skinned and fur-clad New Yorkers seek refuge from ever-plunging temperatures, Aframax vessel rates skyrocketed to record levels, briefly rising this week to a rate of WS 402.5 for the Caribbean/USA trade. This translates into daily earnings of about $72,000 per day for owners.

## WS Rates
Aframaxes Next 20 Days As you can see on the previous chart, as vessel availability goes down, rates go up. This relationship, however, is not particularly strong. Rates reached nearly WS 400 early last year, but were unable to go any higher. But this week two fixtures were concluded at WS 402.5. Interestingly, fixture activity has not picked up as shown below. In fact there seems to be no relationship between fixture activity and rates.

## There's More to Come
Two factors are at play here. One is the Bosphorus Effect where Aframaxes left the Caribbean to enjoy higher rates in the Mediterranean. As owners chase the end of the rainbow, ship supply dropped off in the Caribs, as seen in the number of available vessels. That came full circle when charterers seeking the fewer available ships caused rates to escalate. The second factor is greater lightering activity offloading VLCCs. That's why we think that there is more momentum left in this market.
