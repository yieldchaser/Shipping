## Can Fuel Oil Be Turned into Silver?
The fuel oil market in 2004 has been characterized by increased demand, volatile prices, changing regulations and new restrictions on tanker transportation. In this dynamic environment, the timing for the Silver Anniversary of the international Fuel Oil/Energy Buyers' Conference would seem to be quite appropriate. As it has for the past several years, Poten & Partners is proud to assist with this year's conference that will be held at the Eden Roc Renaissance Resort & Spa in Miami Beach, Florida, on October 24-26, 2004. A wide range of topics will be addressed from differing perspectives at the 25th meeting of the Fuel Oil/Buyers' Conference. These include: § President Bush's energy plan and its impact on international oil markets; § An overview of BP's role in fuel oil, heavy feedstock, and bunkers around the world; § Four power companies (with activities in Malta, Cyprus, Florida, and Nova Scotia) will assess the role of fuel oil in power generation; § The long-term outlook for oil prices; § Fuel oil storage in a worldwide context and its impact on the logistics chain; § Worldwide cat cracking overview and the properties of slurry oil; § The changing transportation market of fuel oil in world markets. The program has been designed to be of interest to fuel oil buyers and sellers from around the world. Tanker owners, storage owners, and investment banks will also find the conference to be of value. We look forward to seeing you there. For further information and registration information visit www.fswierz.com or contact Frank Swierz at 603-645-6001.

## October 24-26, 2004
Eden Roc Renaissance Resort & Spa, Miami Beach, Florida

## SUNDAY, OCTOBER 24, 2004 - 5:00 PM REGISTRATION AND RECEPTION
MONDAY, OCTOBER 25, 2004 - 7:30 AM REGISTRATION AND CONTINENTAL BREAKFAST GREETINGS AND INTRODUCTION Don Bonett , Conference Chairman

## KEYNOTE ADDRESS
Mark Maddox , Acting Assistant Secretary for Fossil Energy, Department of Energy will discuss the President's energy plan and in particular how it affects the international oil markets, supply and demand, prices and production, reserve additions as well as petroleum refining.

## BP OIL INTERNATIONAL PRESENTATION
Sandi Braun , Product Trading Manager, Supply & Trading, Europe and Africa, BP Oil International Ltd., will provide an overview of BP Oil's role in fuel oil, heavy feedstock and bunker markets around the world.
FUEL OIL IN THE MIX OF FUELS Organized and moderated by Larry Axelrod , Senior Consultant, Poten & Partners, Inc. Tancred Tabone, Chairman, Enemalta Corporation, Malta Dr. Adonis Kellas , Assistant Manager (Generation) Electricity Authority of Cyprus Marcus Pourteau , Central Puerto of Argentina Doug Max , Fuel Oil Manager, Florida Power & Light Colin Thomson , Commercial Lead-Heavy Fuel Oil, Nova Scotia Power Inc. Panelists will provide presentations on the role of fuel oil in power generation at their respective companies.
TANKER MARKET AND FUEL OIL TRANSPORT Jeffrey Goetz, Manager, Marine Projects, Poten & Partners, Inc. will discuss the changing worldwide tanker-transportation trade patterns for fuel oil and the evolution of the trade onto larger vessels with more diverse destinations.

## October 24-26, 2004
Eden Roc Renaissance Resort & Spa, Miami Beach, Florida

## TUESDAY, OCTOBER 26, 2004
SHORT- AND MEDIUM- TERM OUTLOOK FOR OIL PRICES John Brunton , Manager, Trading Innovation & Application Cargill, Geneva Office will provide insight into the new OPEC long-term outlook and the importance of coal prices to residual oil.

## VOPAK PRESENTATION
Hari Dattatreya, Divisional Director, sales & Marketing, Vopak, Netherlands will discuss Vopak's historical evolution, their terminal activity, worldwide fuel oil storage activity including shipping logistics and blending capability.

## SLURRY OILS SUPPLY/DEMAND
Vincent J. Guercio, Consultant , CTC International will present an overview of cat cracking around the world and discuss slurry properties, slurry production, outlets and worldwide slurry consumption by region.
