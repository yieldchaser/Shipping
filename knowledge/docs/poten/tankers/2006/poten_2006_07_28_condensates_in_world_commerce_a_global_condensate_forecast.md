## Condensates in World Commerce: A Global Condensate Forecast
Condensates in World Commerce:

## A Global Condensate Forecast
Some believe that the world is running out of oil. The informed consensus is that this is not so for some time, that more liquid hydrocarbons can be extracted from the ground to meet growing demand over the next decade or so. But the composition of what is produced will in all likelihood change. It will move more heavy as more heavy oils are produced and refined; and it will move more light as more condensate is produced as a by- product of new gas production.
Poten & Partners is pleased to announce the publication of Condensates in World Commerce , A Global Condensate Forecast , 2006 edition . The scope of this publication is particularly relevant as this study concentrates on condensates and how these streams will be produced, used, traded and priced over the next decade. As condensate is a non-uniform product, one of the challenges in the market today is being able to anticipate how and where it will be traded. This report looks at these issues in relation to the expanding condensates supply.
The condensate market is divided generally between an East and West market. Given this clear distinction, the report has been designed to discuss the prevailing supply/demand picture in each of these markets. A final section of this report provides a comprehensive list of detailed condensate specifications for all the streams that are traded.
As shown on the chart above, the outlook through 2010 is for significant increases and the outlook through 2015 while lower, is still relatively strong.

## Forecast
Currently most of the condensate in the market works on a program basis, either an oil company supply chain, or with contracted customers. Future changes in supply present an opportunity for traditional outsiders, specifically traders, which may lead to an evolution in traditional trade flows.
For those involved in crude oil, condensates, gas and/or tankers, this will prove to be a valuable planning resource.
