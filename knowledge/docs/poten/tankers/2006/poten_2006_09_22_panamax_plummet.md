## Summary
Panamax Plummet
While most sectors in the tanker market have been experiencing firm rates for the summer and continuing through September, the earnings in the Panamax market have taken a tumble. Within a period of less than one week, worldscale rates fell from WS 225 to WS120 and corresponding earnings fell from $27,500 per day to $8,700 per day on the 50,000-ton on the Caribbean - USA trade. While a sustained market at these levels is unfathomable for the long-term, there are a few unique drivers in the Panamax market that will contribute to continued downward pressure on rates.

## Panamax Plummet
While most sectors in the tanker market have been experiencing firm rates for the summer and continuing through September, the earnings in the Panamax market have taken a tumble. Within a period of less than one week, worldscale rates fell from WS 225 to WS120 and corresponding earnings fell from $27,500 per day to $8,700 per day on the 50,000-ton on the Caribbean - USA trade. While a sustained market at these levels is unfathomable for the long-term, there are a few unique drivers in the Panamax market that will contribute to continued downward pressure on rates.

## Finding the Cause
The high concentration of fuel oil trading on Panamaxes makes demand for these vessels highly subject to the demand of this commodity. Prices for #6 fuel oil, like prices for crude oil, have been at historically high levels for the past two years with prices climbing from about $100 per ton to well over $300 per ton. Primarily, utilities and electricity providers that require fuel oil for power generation drive demand. For many utilities, the ability to switch between fuel oil-based and natural gas-based power generation contributes to the price competition between the two commodities. Getting More Gas Even at $300 per ton, fuel oil was a relative bargain compared to natural gas. This is no longer the case. As shown below in the past year, the price of natural gas has declined from over $14.00 to under $5.00 per million btu with a two year low reached last Friday of $4.86 per million btu.

## Natural Gas leading rates?
The chart on the previous page compares the Henry Hub natural gas spot price versus Panamax daily earnings - with a twist. The Panamax rates are actual rates that occurred 30 days later. What the chart tells us is that with a nearly 80% statistical correlation, the movement in natural gas prices will predict Panamax rates 30 days later. As the price for natural gas continues its decline, it is likely that there will be increased downward pressure on Panamax rates in the near term as the demand for fuel oil declines. Looking ahead through the fourth quarter, however, history indicates that rates will increase substantially from September levels. Follow the gas market for the clues.
