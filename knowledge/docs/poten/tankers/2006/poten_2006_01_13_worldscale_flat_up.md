## Carib/USGulf
AG/Sing UKC/USAC AG/LOOP $0.00 The 2005 edition of the Worldscale Book had an assumed bunker price of $173.00 per ton, which was revised upward to $231.50 per ton for the 2006 edition, an increase of 34%. Bunker prices in the Worldscale Book are the average bunker prices for the previous year. The revision of the assumed bunker price is responsible for the increase of 15% on the flat rate for the long haul AG/LOOP voyage and 11% on the short haul Carib/USGulf voyage.
What Does the Market Say? It is no small task to adjust the flat rates for changes in port charges, bunker prices, and exchange rates. But is it necessary? At the end of the year when the new flat rates apply to voyages with a load date on or after January 1, the spot market fixtures are based on a Worldscale percent for the new or old rates. Guess what happens? When fixing on the 2005 rates, the Worldscale percent was higher to reflect the lower flat rate and vice versa. This adjustment therefore provides the owner with the same revenues for a given voyage no matter which flat rate is used. No doubt the discipline imposed by the annual process and constant updates keeps the system from developing inconsistencies over time. But not to worry - if and when inconsistencies develop, the market will respond accordingly.
