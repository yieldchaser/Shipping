## The Down and Dirty: 2009's Top Charterers
With refinery utilizations low and volumes on long‐haul trade routes sapped by a 2 million barrel per day drop in worldwide demand, it comes as no surprise that 2009 saw a marked slowdown of activity in the tanker market. The recently completed year was one in which the tanker market was beset by forces both specific to the industry and completely out of its control. An examination of the changes between total fixtures reported in 2008 and 2009 reveals some of the results of the forces that shaped the tanker market over the past year.
As shown in the chart below, the total number of reported spot market fixtures last year failed to reach the levels achieved in 2008 in all but one of the tanker classes discussed. In total, 8,800 fixtures were captured by the Poten & Partners Fixture Database last year, an almost nine percent decrease from the 9, 587 fixtures captured in 2008.

## Chart: 2009 - Top Dirty Charterers - What a Difference a Year Makes!
In terms of total fixtures across vessel classes, Shell maintained its position as top charterer in 2009, with five more reported fixtures than in 2008, for a total of 691. As shown, the top ten overall charterers have remained essentially the same group over the past two years, while some jockeying for position among them led to minor changes this year. Petrobras fell to number 11 after breaking into the top ten last year, while Unipec moved up one place from the 10 th to the 9 th most active charterer.

## 2009 - Top 10 VLCC Charterers
The fact that all but two of the top ten VLCC charterers for 2009 are Asian based companies speaks to the central role that developing nations of that region have played in supporting ton‐mile demand this year. The reduction of westward bound fixtures from the Arabian Gulf explains the reduced position of ExxonMobil and Shell as well as the exit of BP and Chevron from the top ten this year. The reduced movement of cargoes bound west was the driving force behind the seven percent decrease in overall VLCC spot market activity observed between 2008 and 2009. Unipec blew the competition out of the water by almost 40 percent and bucked the trend by increasing their total activity by 63 fixtures.

## 2009 - Top 10 Suezmax Charterers
Suezmaxes sustained a 15 percent drop in reported spot market activity from 2008 levels last year. Among top ten Suezmax charterers, CSSA and Shell swapped the first and third positions on the list from their positions on last year, while Sun moved from the fourth to the second most active on the list, with nine less fixtures.

## 2009 - Top 10 Aframax Charterers
A review of reported spot fixtures reveals a three percent reduction in activity on Aframax tankers in 2009. Shell maintained its position as top charter of Aframax vessels in 2009, and as has been the case in years past, the top ten charterers remained the same, although there were some changes in their relative positions.

## 2009 - Top 10 Panamax Charterers
The only market segment to see an increase in activity last year, Panamax fixtures grew slightly from 944 in 2008 to 958 last year and saw more changes in the top ten than other market segments. While Valero, Citgo, and Trafigura, respectively 5 th , 8 th , and 9 th on last year's list, dropped from the top ten in 2009, Vitol, Hess and PMI replaced them in the ranks of top Panamax charterers last year.

## PMI
While reported spot market activity presents only a portion of total fixture volume, this overview of 2009 spot market activity conforms to the dominant narratives of the tanker market over the past year. The continually rising profile of Asian charterers revealed in the lists above is a natural extension of the strength of demand growth displayed in emerging eastern markets last year. The growth of floating storage fixtures, brought on by a combination of vessel deliveries and reduced crude oil prices earlier this year, accounts for the increased presence of trading companies on the lists above. Looking forward to the year ahead, the direction of the market could well be determined strength of these two trends.
