## Strait Flush
As a bunkering port near the Arabian Gulf, Fujairah already plays an important role in international tanker markets. It has long served an anchorage point for ships awaiting nomination for cargoes in the Arabian Gulf, but coming years are likely to see Fujairah's role expand. The United Arab Emirates (UAE) is attempting to utilize Fujairah's strategic location in a development plan it hopes will enhance the emirate's status as a maritime hub rivaling Rotterdam and Singapore. A number of ongoing projects related to the development effort in Fujairah have potential to influence regional and international tanker trade flows.
Most significant among the UAE's many plans for Fujairah is the addition of a pipeline designed to heighten the country's versatility as a crude oil supplier. The UAE's estimated 98 billion in oil reserves are the world's seventh largest and allow the collection of seven emirates to be the world's third largest crude oil exporter, with exports of 2.5 kbd in 2008. The Habshan area, currently serves as the collection point for onshore crude oil production in Abu Dhabi, where the majority of the UAE's reserves are located. Construction is already underway on the Abu Dhabi Crude Oil Pipeline (ADCOP), linking the Habshan crude oil collection point to the port at Fujairah. Initiated by the government‐owned International Petroleum Investment Company (IPIC) in 2006, the 250 mile, 1.5 million barrel per day pipeline will allow the majority of exports from the UAE to avoid the Strait of Hormuz, as shown in the map below.
The pipeline is being built by a subsidiary of the China National Petroleum Corporation (CNPC) and will be operated by Abu Dhabi National Oil Company (ADNOC) and Abu Dhabi Company for Onshore Oil Operations (ADCO). Construction is reportedly on schedule and the pipeline is expected to reach full operation in 2011. Production and consumption of crude oil in the United Arab Emirates are shown in the chart below.

## Bypassing a Strait
The diverted marine traffic the ADCOP pipeline is expected to bring to Fujairah has inspired a significant expansion of terminal facilities there. The deepwater port at Fujairah, which has already seen a steady increase in shipping traffic over recent years, will reportedly be outfitted with a number of loading facilities capable of receiving vessels with deadweights up to 180,000 dwt. An oil terminal is currently being built on land between Fujairah's port and the nearby Hajjar Mountains.
The expansion of export facilities at Fujairah will be accompanied by other developments designed to strengthen the emirate's infrastructure and to support the emirate's overall economic development. Desire to expand the port at Fujairah is also fed by the expectation of increasing demand in the region, which some expect will translate into increased fuel oil imports. Fuel oil is already stored, blended and redistributed at Fujairah, and the pipeline and port development plans have inspired a significant expansion of storage capacity by both government‐affiliated and private companies.
After the port's expansion, Fujairah's role in the international tanker market will continue to evolve along with the demands of its customers. In the short term, the principle significance of the ADCOP pipeline will be the creation of an alternative shipping route that would allow exporters to bypass the Strait of Hormuz. While only a few days transit will be saved, larger economic benefits could be realized in a time of distress.
Iranian threats to block the passage have hovered in the background of the recent escalation in tensions between Tehran and the West and development of the ADCOP pipeline has been presented as a key strategic project for the UAE. The opportunity to avoid any possible increased insurance premiums, would be realized by owners who are able to avoid the region.
This pipeline's impact on ton‐mile demand and total export volumes are likely to be small, from a ton‐mile prospective. In the event that political tensions increase, export more projects to come online that will look to achieve similar goals.
