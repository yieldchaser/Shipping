## The 27
Since its inception over a generation ago, the Fuel Oil/Energy Buyers' Conference hosted by Poten & Partners and Frank Swierz Associates has grown into the premier gathering of the world's fuel oil and energy buyers as well as energy service providers. This year's conference will be held October 29-31 and has maintained its traditional Miami Beach, FL location.
While dwarfed by crude oil movements, fuel oil is still a pretty big business. To date in 2007, nearly 75 million tons of fuel oil has been reported transported on large tankers to and from various international locations.

## A Global Business
This year's conference will open with an update on United States Energy Policy. Dr. Carmen Difiglio, Deputy Assistant Secretary for Policy Analysis, Office of Policy and International Affairs, U.S. Department of Energy will enlighten the audience on Presidential policies and energy legislation that could affect oil markets, and will discuss programs to improve U.S. energy security by reducing the domestic dependence on imported petroleum.
Biofuels, including ethanol, are among the fastest growing commodities. Mr. Steve Levy of Sprague Energy plans to tell attendees about the latest developments for biofuels supply, distribution, and quality. Storage for commodities is always a challenge. Nordic Storage's Mr. Ulf Vleeshouwers will delve into fuel oil storage issues such as tank heating, insulating and emptying and provide insight to tackling these challenges.
Given the uncertainty surrounding global fuel oil demand and supply, Mr. James Osten of Global Insights will present his view on the world's oil supply/demand balance and then explore several scenarios of how demand might evolve. As China's demand for fuel oil has become a key part of the world market, Mr. Guoquiang Zheng, Managing Director of Jifeng Petroleum Consulting Company, will provide a perspective on this market.
Rounding out the conference is a discussion of fuel oil hedging and risk management by Mr. Hartley Connett, Executive Director, Marketing, Mitsui & Co. Energy Risk Management (USA) and finally some insights into the oil refining process will be presented by Mr. Gary Wright, Optimization Manager, ConocoPhillips.
For full details on the conference's events and program at South Beach Miami Beach, Florida, please visit www.fswierz.com . We look forward to seeing everyone in Miami.
