## The Knife Edge
In the past week the spot tanker market experienced a sizeable upward swing. Spot VLCC rates increased significantly creating some momentum to put the fourth quarter in motion. With strong crude oil demand prevailing worldwide, the total volume of VLCC fixture activity has seen a significant increase in 2007 to date. The chart below shows total worldwide reported fixture activity on a weekly basis for 2006 and 2007, as well as the high and low range of activity since 2001. This week's 56 fixtures represented the maximum amount of reported spot VLCC fixtures worldwide since 2001.

## Spot Market Activity - VLCC
The increase in worldwide VLCC activity contributed to less VLCC supply in the Arabian Gulf, a key demand area for VLCCs. The chart below shows the daily Worldscale (WS) rate for VLCCs Arabian Gulf - Far East compared to the availability of vessels in the Arabian Gulf the next 30 days.

## Next 30 Days
VLCC WS (AG - FE) A tightened supply of vessels available to meet cargoes creates an environment in which rates can become quite volatile, and subject to significant upswings.

## Run-Up in Rates
Last week saw the largest one-day increase in WS rates in the past five years- a 50 WS point jump! Day rates on VLCCs in the Arabian Gulf - Far East are achieving $150,000 per day on a timecharter equivalent (TCE) basis. Those vessels fixed Arabian Gulf - US Gulf are earning near $100,000 per day, up from $25,000 per day a week ago.
Although WS rates have demonstrated significant one-day upside growth, it should be noted that the largest one-day drop in the VLCC spot market was 40 WS points seen in 2004. What goes up may eventually come down!
