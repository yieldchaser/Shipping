## Get 'Em While They're Hot
Although it is only the first official week of summer, the time charter market is already hot. Over the past several months, time charter rates have trended upward. With an element of uncertainty always present in the tanker market, charterers are seeking the shelter of term coverage. The time chartering of vessels generally assures the charterer of quality tonnage for moving cargoes and can act as a financial hedge against the volatility of spot market rates.

## Time Charter -1 Year
Time Charter -3 Year Over the last five and a half years, the spot time charter equivalent (TCE) has averaged $56,200 per day, while the time charter market rates have averaged $51,800 and $39,400 for the one and three year charters, respectively.

## Number of Time Charters
Of the nearly 160 time charters reported year-to-date in 2007, oil companies, trading companies and independent shipowners/operators have each chartered about one-third of the total. With only 130 large tanker newbuilding deliveries in 2007, the number of double-hull vessels available for term charter has actually declined. Interest remains high for quality tonnage so expect the period market to remain strong for the foreseeable future.
