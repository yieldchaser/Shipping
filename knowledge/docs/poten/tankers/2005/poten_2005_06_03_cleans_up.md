## Cleans Up!
The clean up position on a baseball team is where the fourth batter has the opportunity to clean up the bases by hitting a home run. Product carriers may be hitting a home run with U.S. refineries operating at full utilization in the face of growing oil demand. Under these circumstances, not only should clean product imports be growing but their rate of growth should also be accelerating.

## Accelerating Growth!
The above chart shows that this observation is true- the rate of growth of product imports is indeed accelerating. The next chart shows the volume of spot clean fixtures for small, handymax, and panamax tankers (2005 figures are annualized).

## Voyage Days
The 10% increase in the number of handymax fixtures since 2000 translates to a 12% increase in the number of voyage days over the same period of time. The rise would have been more pronounced were it not for the fall-off in the number of Far East fixtures.
So What Can We Conclude? With U.S. clean product imports projected to rise, we can only conclude that demand for product carriers will continue to grow. Of course, there will be fluctuations along the way and spare gasoline refinery capacity in Europe will some day be exhausted. Then the United States will have to look even further afield for its import needs, which will benefit larger sized clean products carriers. As an example of longer-haul clean fixtures, on May 4 th , a LR2 tanker was fixed to move 75,000 tons of gasoil from the Arabian Gulf to the U.S. Gulf

It looks to us that the clean up batter is about to hit a home run!
