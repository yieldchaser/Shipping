## Bunkers Going Bonkers?
Yes and no. Yes, in absolute terms the cost of bunkers has escalated; no, in relative terms compared to spot WS rates at today's robust levels. Owners are still making plenty of money and giving up a few extra WS points to the bunker suppliers can't be hurting that much. Considering the general direction of crude oil prices and continuing demand for fuel oil in China, we expect that owners will be giving up more WS points to pay for the extra cost of bunkers. Again it won't be hurting owners unless, G_d Forbid, spot rates were to come down!
