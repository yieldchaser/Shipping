## (2006 - 2010).
Poten & Partners is pleased to announce the publication of Tanker Prospects (2006 - 2010). The 192-page report covers our assessment of the prospects for VLCCs, Suezmaxes, Aframaxes, Panamaxes, and Handymaxes through 2010. For this publication, we have had to take a new approach to assessing VLCC demand. In the past, VLCC demand was based on, for the most part, minimizing distance. We do not believe that this model is appropriate any longer. China has made it abundantly clear that it will seek a diverse oil supply. Distance, therefore, appears to have taken a backseat to diversification of sources to enhance energy (oil) security. Our approach is then to assess VLCC demand not on minimizing shipping distances, but on China's maximizing imports of Atlantic basin crude; namely from West Africa. The replacement of this West Africa crude in the Atlantic basin by Middle East crude increases VLCC demand because of greater voyage distances. From this analysis arises our assessment of the VLCC market going forward. Similar techniques have also been utilized for the smaller vessel sizes.
