## MARKET SUMMARY

## INDIA

The Indian market strengthened during the week due to the limited availability of steel plates, as slow cutting activity and several vessels remaining away from shore restricted supply. This improvement is expected to be temporary, with conditions likely to change over the coming weeks. Demand for steel remains very weak at the end-product level. Buyers remain keen to acquire vessels but continue to face difficulty competing with offers from other recycling destinations. Dark-fleet vessels continue to be recycled at Indian shores. As India remains the only destination accepting such vessels, it holds a significant advantage over competing markets.

## BANGLADESH

Local market conditions weakened over the week, although healthy inquiry for recycling candidates remains. Buyers are showing greater interest in bulkers and other vessels without compliance- related concerns. Compliance risks remain a major challenge after a tanker was sanctioned following its arrival and completion of clearances, just before beaching. Such developments place significant pressure on both ship recyclers and cash buyers.

## PAKISTAN

Market conditions remained largely unchanged, with recyclers actively seeking vessels but very few candidates being offered. Local markets remain short of scrap, supporting healthy conditions and good demand from buyers. Pakistani buyers remain unable to compete with Bangladesh but are currently in a position to compete with India.

## TURKIYE

Aliaga’s ship recycling market remained largely unchanged over the past week, with sentiment continuing to be affected by the weak Turkish lira and the limited availability of attractive recycling candidates. There has been little indication of any meaningful recovery, and the market continues to lag behind the Indian subcontinent in terms of competitiveness. Most conventional recycling candidates are expected to favour South Asian destinations. However, Aliaga continues to maintain its established role as a specialised market for vessels requiring EU-approved recycling facilities and Basel Convention compliance.
