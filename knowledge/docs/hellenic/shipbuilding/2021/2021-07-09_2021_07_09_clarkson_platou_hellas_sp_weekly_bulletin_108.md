## NEW BUILDING

In dry bulk this week, Aston Enterprise are reported to have ordered two firm 82k dwt Kamsarmax at Chengxi Shipyard, with deliveries set for 4Q 2022 and 1Q 2023. Chengxi Shipyard confirmed taking order for a firm 82k dwt Kamsarmax from Chellaram Shipping, with delivery expected within 2023. In the gas carrier market, Samsung announced taking order for three firm 180k CBM LNG carriers from an unknown buyer, with the first vessel to be delivered in 4Q 2023 and the remaining two in 1Q 2024. Sovcomflot declared options to add two further vessels in their series of 174k CBM LNG carriers at Hyundai Samho. The vessels, which are set to be delivered in 4Q 2023 and 1Q 2024, bring the series to three overall. Surely, it is only a matter of time for the much anticipated USD 600 per ldt level to be reached for a conventional ship which would be a remarkable recovery from this time last year where the market was in a depressed state due to the Covid restrictions in place.
