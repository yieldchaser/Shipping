# New Building

A quiet week in the newbuilding market with no new orders reported across the conventional Dry, Tanker, Container or Gas sectors. The only order to surface has been placed by Neptune Co., a joint venture of Aman Group and the Cruise Saudi for a 183M, 23,000 GT passenger cruise vessel at T.Mariotti Shipyard in Italy. The vessel is expected to deliver in 2026.

# Recycling Chattogram Reprieve?

There have been rumours circulating this week that the financial restrictions that have been imposed on the recyclers in Bangladesh may be eased in March or April, 2023. The problems surrounding the issuance of letters of credit over the last several months do appear to be improving, with more units reportedly being resold to the local recyclers, albeit the smaller ldt size. However, some optimism has reverberated from the area this week, although there is still no genuine/official report from the domestic banks to authenticate this, but a light could be around the corner.

With Pakistan almost non-existent, India remains the most reliable market at this current time with price levels being indicated around the USD 600/ldt level for containers and back towards the mid 500’s for dry bulk tonnage. Hunger and appetite remain aggressive from these recyclers to procure tonnage whilst the steel demand locally outweighs the supply, although we are certainly witnessing more of a steady stream into the marketplace.

Meantime, our thoughts go to all those in Turkey and Syria who have been tragically affected by the devastating earthquake that occurred this week. Our sincere prayers are with all those that have suffered this week.
