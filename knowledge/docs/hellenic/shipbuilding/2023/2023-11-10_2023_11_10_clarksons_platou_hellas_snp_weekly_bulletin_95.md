## New Building

In tankers this week, Hyundai Samho announced contracting two firm 158k dwt Suezmaxes with an unnamed owner, with both vessels set to deliver in 1H 2026. Xiamen Shipbuilding announced orders for four firm 41k dwt MR1 product tankers with an unnamed owner, with the vessels expected to deliver in 2025 and 2026. Ningbo Xinle contracted six firm 7.5k dwt Chemical Tankers with Mila Ltd., with the vessels expected to deliver from 1Q 2027 and into 2028. In dry bulk, Fujian Shipping group announced ordering two firm 64k dwt Ultramaxes at Nantong Xiangyu, with the vessels expected to deliver in 2025. In general cargo, Royal Bodewes announced contracting three firm 9.4k dwt General Cargo vessels with Aasen Shipping, with the first vessel set to deliver in December 2025 and the final vessel in September 2026. Ningbo Xinle, in addition to their chemical tanker order, signed twelve firm 8.3k dwt general cargo vessels with Mila Ltd. The vessels will deliver throughout 2027 and 2028.

## Recycling Part time!

The title certainly does not apply to the current market conditions but to the significant events that have taken place this week (Bahri week in Dubai and Eisbein in Hamburg) and the forthcoming Diwali holidays during the next 7 days, resulting in a very quiet week except for glasses clinking together. What we can say is that whilst the domestic markets in all three Indian sub-Continent regions have seen some stability return, we certainly have not seen the usual ‘pre-Diwali’ boost in prices from India. There are reports of a few more ships trickling into the marketplace, however the majority are being withdrawn or temporising when Owners see that the price levels are not reaching their expectations. We understand that clients of MSC continue to drip feed some of their aging container units into India with every vessel giving prompt delivery, but generally the lack of tonnage continues. Overall, a quiet period has descended upon the market, which is expected to carry on during next week’s Diwali festivities.
