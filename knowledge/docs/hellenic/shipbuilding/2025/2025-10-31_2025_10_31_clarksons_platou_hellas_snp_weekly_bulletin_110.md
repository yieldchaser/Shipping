Another 15-year-old Capesize has changed hands this week, highlighting the continued strong demand for vessels of this vintage over the last couple of months. The **mv Rosemary** (abt 179k dwt, built 2010 Daewoo) was inviting offers earlier this month and, following healthy competition with more than ten bids received, has now been sold for around USD 28.5m.

In the geared segment, a couple of Supramax sales were concluded.
