## RECYCLING Holiday mode!

As we entered Ramadan this week, there was a subdued feeling across the sub-continent as end recyclers started to observe the holy month and retreat home. This has been a familiar theme of a quiet market across sectors in all regions for most of the year with a distinct lack of tonnage continuing to starve the ship recyclers. However there was an impressive price received for the below reported Capesize, which has been a rarity this year with the firm freight markets on offer.

This price follows an announcement from the Central Bank in Pakistan that imposed a 100% cash margin on the importation on 177 items including Steel products. By not including Ships in this new banking margin, prices are expected to increase further in Pakistan with this being one of the sole ways to import Steel into the country with no cash restrictions. The Government seems to have taken this step to discourage imports and support the balance of payments due to the struggling Pakistani rupee (PKR) which had depreciated to an all-time low of 185.4 against the dollar this week.
