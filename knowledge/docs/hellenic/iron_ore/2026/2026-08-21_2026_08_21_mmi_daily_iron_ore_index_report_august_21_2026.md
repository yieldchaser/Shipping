## Main
Iron ore futures traded steady today. The DCE most-active contract I2701 settled at RMB 707.5/mt, up 0.21% from the previous trading day.Spot prices at Qingdao Port rose by RMB 1-4/mt. Traders largely followed the market, while mills purchased on an as-needed basis, leaving overall spot transaction volumes moderate. Both supply and demand for iron ore saw notable shiŌs this week. The latest statistics show total iron ore inventories across China's 35 major ports at 145.54 million mt, down 1.24 million mt week-on-week - a modest decline overall. Average daily port outbound volumes rose 48,000 mt week-on-week to 3.136 million mt. A typhoon is expected to make landfall next week, which may disrupt iron ore arrivals, and this phased contraction on the supply side should lend support to iron ore prices. In addition, expectations of a coke price hike are building, while gains in steel prices have stalled and mill margins are broadly under pressure - factors that may cap the upside for iron ore prices. On balance, absent further impetus from the news flow, iron ore prices are expected to hold a rangebound pattern in the near term.
