# Market Commentary · Imported Ore

Futures & spot DCE most-traded I2701 closed at 682.0 yuan/mt, up 1.49% from the previous session. Qingdao PB Fines were 650, Newman Fines 650, Mac Fines 657, BRBF 700, Newman Lump 825 and SSF 512 yuan/wmt, each up 10 yuan; Carajas Fines rose 7 yuan to 822 and PB Lump rose 5 yuan to 820. SGX official current-month FEFV26 was last priced at USD 90.75/dmt, timestamped Oct 29, 17:14:08 (UTC+8). SMM’s daily review described consecutive price gains but still limited overall trading.

Drivers Domestic futures, port indices and seaborne quotations strengthened together, extending the previous recovery. The seaborne 61% index rose more than the 65%, narrowing the grade spread, while the port high- low spread widened slightly. Structural performance therefore differs across markets. Firmer prices may encourage offers, but durability still depends on mill purchases, transaction volumes and resource availability.

Demand The latest 242-mill survey showed daily hot metal output of 2.3621 million mt on Oct 28, up 3,700 mt from Oct 21, and a blast furnace operating rate of 87.30%. Two consecutive increases support baseline consumption, while discretionary restocking remains constrained by orders, profitability and existing mill stocks. Environmental restrictions and maintenance schedules may affect subsequent production, leaving actual demand in focus.

Inventory & Ten-port stocks were 104.0353 million mt on Oct 29, down 618,100 mt (-0.59%) from Oct 22. Fines at 81.8182 supply million mt, concentrates at 7.5304 million mt and pellets at 1.6715 million mt fell by 261,100, 203,800 and 157,500 mt, respectively. Lump rose 4,300 mt to 13.0152 million mt. The drawdown may reflect arrivals, unloading and mill collections together rather than one demand factor. Latest global shipments and Chinese arrivals, for the week to Oct 23, were 33.4494 and 27.6076 million mt.

Cost & margin Oct 28 freight was USD 34.17/mt for C3, up 1.49% from the previous reading, and 14.03 for C5, up 0.72%. Higher ore prices and freight may raise procurement costs for some cargoes, while stronger port selling prices support receipts. Net margins still depend on contracts, exchange rates and settlement timing; one day’s price direction does not establish a broad return to profit for mills or traders. SMM’s assessment published Oct 29 showed the period’s average import margin turning positive at 1.53 yuan/mt from -4.89, an improvement of 6.42 yuan. This import trading measure does not establish a return to profit for mills or coke producers.

# Market Commentary · Domestic Ore

Prices The Domestic Ore Composite Price Index held at 797.44 yuan/mt on Oct 29. SMM’s current Tangshan brief quoted 66% concentrate at 885–890 yuan/mt on a dry-basis, tax-inclusive ex-works basis, down 5–10 yuan. The regional quotation and national composite measure different markets.

Supply & SMM’s Tangshan brief described low mining and beneficiation activity, little progress in private-mine restarts demand and tight supply. Mill losses and pressure on bids remained strong. The purchasing response to marginal production improvement still needs to be assessed against regional resources, orders and blending costs.

Outlook SMM expects Tangshan concentrate prices to remain range-bound near term. Persistent resource tightness alongside stronger buying could support offers, while limited recovery in mill margins and orders could constrain upside. Heating-season environmental maintenance remains a prospective risk rather than evidence of widespread restrictions already in effect.
