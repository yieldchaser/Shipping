## Main
Today, the DCE iron ore futures consolidated and rose today. The most-traded contract, I2701, closed at 716 yuan/mt, up 1.27% from the previoustrading day. Spot prices at Qingdao port rose by 6-10 yuan/mt, with traders actively selling while steel mills adopted a wait-and-see sentiment, resulting in limited overall spot trading volume. According to SMM shipping data, global iron ore shipments totaled 33.17 million mt last week, edging down 1% MoM, with a cumulative YoY growth of 1%. Meanwhile, as the impact of the typhoon faded, port unloading efficiency improved. Last week, China's iron ore arrivals totaled 30.78 million mt, rebounding sharply by 65% WoW, with a cumulative YoY increase of 4.6%. This week, supply pressure has increased significantly, posing some pressure on ore prices. However, driven by expectations of demand during the traditional "September-October peak season", market sentiment turned positive, coupled with rising expectations of macro-level fiscal and financial policies to boost domestic demand. Iron ore futures prices followed the strength in steel products. However, considering that iron ore's own fundamentals remain weak, and rising coke prices further squeeze steel mill profits, ore prices face significant upward resistance. Although prices may hold up well in the short term, the upside is expected to be limited.
