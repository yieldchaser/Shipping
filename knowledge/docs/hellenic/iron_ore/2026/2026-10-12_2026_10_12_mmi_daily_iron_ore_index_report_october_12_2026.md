### Key View

Today’s market showed a seaborne recovery alongside softer port prices rather than broad-based strength. The 61% seaborne index rose to USD 89.80/dmt and the 65% to 109.50, while all 12 brand quotations gained USD 0.55–0.60. Qingdao PB Fines at 648 and Newman Fines at 651 yuan/wmt each fell 2 yuan; Mac Fines rose 2 yuan to 652, Carajas Fines rose 1 yuan to 831, and PB Lump fell 11 yuan to 834.

### Market Commentary · Imported Ore

Futures & spot DCE most-traded I2701 closed at 683.0 yuan/mt, up 0.07% from Oct 9. Qingdao PB Fines were 648, Newman Fines 651, BRBF 698, Newman Lump 843 and SSF 510 yuan/wmt, each down 2 yuan from the previous valid quotation; Mac Fines were 652 (+2), Carajas Fines 831 (+1) and PB Lump 834 (-11). SMM’s daily review reported low trader activity, mills buying to immediate needs, limited enquiries and thin transactions. SGX official delayed data showed current-month FEFV26 at USD 90.75/dmt, timestamped Oct 12, 17:34:59 (UTC+8).

Drivers Higher seaborne indices and brand quotations contrasted with lower composite port indices. The seaborne rebound may reflect a short-term recovery in offers and buying schedules, but thin port trading is not sufficient to establish a broad improvement in demand. The 61% seaborne index rose more than the 65%, narrowing their spread from USD 20.20 to 19.70/dmt. In contrast, the 65%-58% port spread widened from 201 to 202 yuan/wmt, so structural changes in the two markets should be assessed separately.

Demand SMM’s daily imported ore review judged that coke price cuts had eased mill costs, but weak end-user demand had prevented this from generating meaningful additional ore purchases. The latest 242-mill survey remained the Sep 30 reading of 2.3882 million mt of daily hot metal output and an 88.57% blast furnace operating rate. If mills continue controlling raw-material stocks and restocking only as needed, the seaborne rebound may feed slowly into port transactions. Further margin repair could improve buying appetite at the margin.

Inventory & In the week to Oct 9, global iron ore shipments were 34.1393 million mt, down 1.8031 million mt (-5.02%) WoW; supply arrivals in China were 27.9147 million mt, down 1.1796 million mt (-4.05%). Brazilian shipments fell 18.31% to

7.0226 million mt, while Australian shipments edged up to 19.5081 million mt. Lower supply flows may ease subsequent arrival pressure, but existing port stocks and mill demand remain important to the balance. The latest available 10-port inventory was 103.0165 million mt as of Sep 30.
Cost & margin The second-round coke purchase-price cut took effect at midnight on Oct 12, reducing wet-quenched and dry- quenched coke by 100 and 110 yuan/mt, respectively. This offers mill cost relief, while further cuts remain an expectation. SMM’s imported ore cost-and-margin table put the period’s average import margin at 15.37 yuan/mt, up from 14.22; this measures import trading rather than steelmaking profitability. Oct 9 freight was USD 33.68/mt for C3 (-2.21%) and 14.39/mt for C5 (-2.04%), potentially improving cost conditions for some cargoes.

### Market Commentary · Domestic Ore

Prices The latest Domestic Ore Composite Price Index was 821.58 yuan/mt on Oct 9, down 0.39% from the previous reading. Imported ore prices and mill margins remain influences on domestic concentrate bargaining.

Supply & SMM’s mine production and sales survey published on Oct 12 reported capacity utilisation of 57.3% for the demand survey week, down 0.8 percentage point WoW. Concentrate output fell 28,000 mt to 870,000 mt, while mine concentrate stocks increased 15,000 mt to about 263,000 mt. Lower output alongside rising inventory suggests that reduced supply does not necessarily improve sales support immediately.

Outlook Concentrate supply may recover as some operations resume after holiday maintenance. If mill buying remains cautious, mine destocking could face pressure. The demand benefit from lower coke costs depends on the extent of improvement in finished-steel margins, leaving domestic ore potentially soft and differentiated across regions in the near term.
