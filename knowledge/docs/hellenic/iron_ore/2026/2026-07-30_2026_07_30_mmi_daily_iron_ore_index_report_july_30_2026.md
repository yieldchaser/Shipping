## Main
The iron ore market showed a lackluster performance on the spot today. The DCE main contract I2609 closed at 715 yuan/ton, down 3.31%from the previous trading session. Spot prices at Qingdao Port fell by 15-20 yuan/ton from the previous trading day. Traders showed relatively low willingness to quote, while steel mills' procurement was mostly for rigid demand, resulting in generally poor spot transaction sentiment. On the fundamental side, iron ore inventories continued to show structural divergence. The total inventory at 10 ports tracked by SMM stood at 106.92 million tons, up 1.29 million tons month-on-month, with stockpiles mainly accumulating in fines and lump ore, though pellet ore inventories remained at four-year lows. At the macro level, escalating U.S.-Iran tensions and their spillover effects triggered panic in the broader market, which transmitted to the iron ore futures market as a risk asset, leading to selling pressure and dragging overall prices lower. In the near term, iron ore prices are likely to continue declining amid panic sentiment.
