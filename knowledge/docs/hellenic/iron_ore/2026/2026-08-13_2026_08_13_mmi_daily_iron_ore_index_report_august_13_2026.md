## Main
Iron ore futures edged lower today. The DCE front-month contract I2609 seƩled at RMB 705/tonne, down 0.21% from the previous trading session.Spot prices at Qingdao Port were broadly unchanged from the prior trading day. Traders largely followed the market, with mills purchasing on an as-needed basis; overall spot transaction volumes remain modest as of this point. SMM's latest ten-port inventory survey shows total portside stocks at 106.95 million tonnes, up marginally by 360,000 tonnes week-on-week. Within the breakdown, fine concentrate and lump ore continued to draw down, while fines and pellets recorded a slight build. Fine concentrate prices remain firm, having rebounded after the pullback seen in recent weeks. Fines prices, by contrast, have held steady, weighed by elevated inventory levels and relatively stable - albeit subdued - demand. On the macro sentiment front, most market participants are currently cautious given lingering uncertainty over downstream newsflow, and are inclined to adopt a waitand-see stance. In the near term, iron ore prices are expected to trade within a narrow range from a fundamentals perspective, with limited upside and downside alike.
