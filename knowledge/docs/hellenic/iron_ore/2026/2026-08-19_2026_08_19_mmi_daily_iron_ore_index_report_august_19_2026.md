## Main
Iron ore futures traded firmer today. The DCE most-active contract I2701 settled at RMB 712/mt, up 0.78% from the previous trading day.Spot prices at Qingdao Port declined by RMB 2-3/mt. Traders were reasonably active in offering, but mill purchases remained largely restocking on a just-in-need basis, leaving overall spot transactions fairly subdued. According to SMM research data, the blast furnace capacity utilisation rate across the 242 sample steel mills stood at 88.84% this period, up 0.24 percentage points week-on-week. Average daily pig iron output came in at 2.4069 million mt, up 6,400 mt week-on-week - a modest rebound after two consecutive weeks of decline, lending phased support to iron ore prices on the demand side. In terms of market structure, demand for medium- and low-grade ore is already showing signs of picking up. That said, against a backdrop of rising coking coal and coke prices, mills' raw material procurement costs have moved higher and their acceptance of high-priced iron ore remains limited. On balance, iron ore prices are expected to maintain a rangebound pattern in the near term.
