## Main
Today, the DCE iron ore futures trended weaker today, with contract 12609 closing at 735.5 yuan/mt,down 0.47% from the previous trading day. Port spot prices fell about 1-2 yuan/mt from the previous trading day. Traders were moderately active in offering, and steel mills mostly restocked as needed.
As of now, spot transaction volumes were moderate.
Iron ore demand is expected to continue declining this week. According to SMM statistics, the hot metal impact from blast furnace maintenance between July 4 and July 10 was 1.2637 million mt, up 77,800 mt WoW, and the impact is expected to increase by another 64,500 mt next week to 1.3282 million mt. In the short term, iron ore demand is expected to keep contracting, price support will gradually weaken, and overall ore prices may fluctuate within a range in a bearish trend.
