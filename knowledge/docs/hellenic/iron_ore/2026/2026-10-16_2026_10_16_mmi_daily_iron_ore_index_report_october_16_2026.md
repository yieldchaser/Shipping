### Market Commentary · Imported Ore

Futures & spot DCE most-traded I2701 closed at 672.5 yuan/mt, up 0.15% from Oct 15. Qingdao PB Fines were 642, Newman Fines 645, Mac Fines 646, BRBF 692, Newman Lump 837 and SSF 504 yuan/wmt, each up 2 yuan; Carajas Fines fell 8 yuan to 817 while PB Lump held at 826. SGX official current-month FEFV26 was last priced at USD

91.00/dmt, timestamped Oct 16, 17:30:47 (UTC+8). SMM’s daily review reported reasonable trader willingness to offer, needs-based mill restocking and thin overall trading.
Drivers Seaborne composite indices and brand quotations recovered and domestic futures edged higher, but port products did not all rise together, showing structural differences in the recovery. The rebound may reflect adjustments in offers and buying schedules; its durability requires confirmation from actual transactions. The seaborne 61% index rose more than the 65%, narrowing their spread from USD 18.70 to 18.35/dmt. A one-day spread change alone is insufficient to establish a shift in blending demand.

Demand SMM’s Oct 16 coke briefing described cautious, needs-based mill purchases and controlled arrivals. Cost relief has not translated broadly into discretionary restocking. The latest 242-mill survey showed daily hot metal output of 2.3520 million mt on Oct 14, down 1,200 mt from Oct 7. A narrower decline may ease concerns about further demand contraction, but a recovery still depends on orders, margins and maintenance implementation.

Inventory & Ten-port stocks were 104.0212 million mt on Oct 15, up 1.0047 million mt from Sep 30, with increases in fines supply and lump and declines in concentrates and pellets. This comparison spans the holiday, and product trends may reflect both arrivals and mill collection patterns. Global shipments of 34.1393 million mt and Chinese arrivals of

27.9147 million mt in the week to Oct 9 both declined from the preceding survey week. Whether lower flows ease inventory pressure still depends on subsequent trading and collections.
Cost & margin SMM’s Oct 16 imported ore cost-and-margin assessment put the period’s average import margin at 6.01 yuan/mt, up 4.91 yuan from 1.10. This measures import trading rather than steelmaking profitability. Oct 15 freight was USD 31.39/mt for C3 (-3.50%) and 11.60 for C5 (-8.23%), offering potential cost relief. Expectations for the next coke price move remain contested; changes not yet implemented should not be treated as realised cost improvements.

### Market Commentary · Domestic Ore
