## Main
Iron ore futures and seaborne quotes firmed notably today, while most Qingdao port spot products posted modest gains.DCE most-traded closed at 663.0 yuan/mt, up 0.91% from Nov 25. Seven of the eight Qingdao spot products advanced while one declined: PB Fines, Newman Fines, Mac Fines, BRBF, Newman Lump, SSF and Carajas Fines each rose 3 yuan/wmt, whereas PB Lump edged down 2 yuan to 780 yuan/wmt. Port 61% and 58% indices climbed to 642 and 603 yuan/wmt (each +2 yuan), while the 65% port composite index eased 3 yuan to 792 yuan/wmt. Seaborne 61% and 65% composite indices rose to USD 89.90 and 105.75/dmt (+0.70 and +0.30/dmt) and all 12 seaborne brands advanced USD 0.70-1.20/dmt; the SMM Domestic Ore Composite Price Index stood at 792.24 yuan/mt on Nov 26, unchanged from Nov 25. On the fundamental side, ten-port inventory recorded a phased drawdown and ocean freight extended gains, while hot metal output remained relatively low. Latest ten-port total inventory fell to 107.4695 million mt on Nov 26, down 1.7887 million mt (-1.64%) from Nov 19 as fines, lump, concentrates and pellets all destocked. On Nov 25, C5 freight rose another 2.61% to USD 16.10/mt and C3 gained 0.57% to USD 35.18/mt. On the demand side, surveyed daily hot metal across 242 mills was 2.3359 million mt on Nov 25 (down 12,000 mt WoW, -0.51%) with the BF operating rate at 87.09% (+0.23 percentage point); on the supply side, global iron ore shipments stood at 35.2588 million mt (+10.88% WoW) and Chinese port arrivals at 27.0447 million mt (+1.04% WoW) in the week to Nov 20. Iron ore prices may maintain a range-bound pattern in the short term. Phased port destocking and consecutive freight gains support futures and seaborne offers, but lower hot metal output amid northwest mill winter maintenance, cautious mill inquiries and upcoming arrivals from prior high overseas shipments continue to constrain spot upside. Continuity of port inventory drawdowns and the start of winter raw material restocking remain key to track.

Seven of the eight Qingdao spot products advanced while one declined: PB Fines, Newman Fines, Mac Fines, BRBF, Newman Lump, SSF and Carajas Fines each rose 3 yuan/wmt, whereas PB Lump edged down 2 yuan to 780 yuan/wmt. Port 61% and 58% indices climbed to 642 and 603 yuan/wmt (each +2 yuan), while the 65% port composite index eased 3 yuan to 792 yuan/wmt. Seaborne 61% and 65% composite indices rose to USD 89.90 and 105.75/dmt (+0.70 and +0.30/dmt) and all 12 seaborne brands advanced USD 0.70-1.20/dmt; the SMM Domestic Ore Composite Price Index stood at 792.24 yuan/mt on Nov 26, unchanged from Nov 25.

On the fundamental side, ten-port inventory recorded a phased drawdown and ocean freight extended gains, while hot metal output remained relatively low. Latest ten-port total inventory fell to 107.4695 million mt on Nov 26, down

1.7887 million mt (-1.64%) from Nov 19 as fines, lump, concentrates and pellets all destocked. On Nov 25, C5 freight rose another 2.61% to USD 16.10/mt and C3 gained 0.57% to USD 35.18/mt. On the demand side, surveyed daily hot metal across 242 mills was 2.3359 million mt on Nov 25 (down 12,000 mt WoW, -0.51%) with the BF operating rate at 87.09% (+0.23 percentage point); on the supply side, global iron ore shipments stood at 35.2588 million mt (+10.88% WoW) and Chinese port arrivals at 27.0447 million mt (+1.04% WoW) in the week to Nov 20. Iron ore prices may maintain a range-bound pattern in the short term. Phased port destocking and consecutive freight gains support futures and seaborne offers, but lower hot metal output amid northwest mill winter maintenance, cautious mill inquiries and upcoming arrivals from prior high overseas shipments continue to constrain spot upside. Continuity of port inventory drawdowns and the start of winter raw material restocking remain key to track.

# Market Commentary · Domestic Ore

Prices The SMM Domestic Ore Composite Price Index stood at 792.24 yuan/mt on Nov 26, unchanged from Nov 25 (down 0.16 yuan or 0.02% from 792.40 on Nov 20). The national composite index and regional iron ore concentrates such as those in Tangshan and Liaodong differ in grade, wet/dry basis and tax treatment, so absolute price levels are not directly comparable.

Supply & Overall operating rates across mines and beneficiation plants in Tangshan and other producing areas demand remained relatively low, keeping local concentrate availability steady; on the demand side, with surveyed daily hot metal down to 2.3359 million mt on Nov 25 and mills facing cost-control pressure, procurement remained predominantly needs-based with strong bid-cutting sentiment, leaving spot trading subdued. With most imported port spot prices rising 3 yuan/wmt today, local domestic-imported price spreads still require evaluation alongside grades and delivered plant costs.

Outlook The domestic ore market may remain range-bound with regional divergence in the near term. Steady local supply under low beneficiation operating rates paired with baseline blast furnace demand lends floor resilience, whereas subdued hot metal output and mill cost-control pressure continue to cap upside room. Beneficiation plant operations in key regions and winter restocking pace remain key to monitor.
