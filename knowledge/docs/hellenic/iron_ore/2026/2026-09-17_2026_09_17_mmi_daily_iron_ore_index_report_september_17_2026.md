## Summary
Main
The biggest change this period is on the cost side. The average imported ore margin rose from -13.18 to -3.97 yuan/mt,a 9.21- yuan improvement, and none of the three reasons SMM gives sits in demand - lower Australian freight, a stronger yuan against the dollar and a narrower seaborne premium. C5 Australia→China freight has fallen for three straight sessions, from 18.34 to USD 16.37/mt; lower landed costs improve import economics directly and explain why USD quotes rose broadly today (Indian fines +2.59%, BRBF +1.08%) while most yuan spot prices held flat.
