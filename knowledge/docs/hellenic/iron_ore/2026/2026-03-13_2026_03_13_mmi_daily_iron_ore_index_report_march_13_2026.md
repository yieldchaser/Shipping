The main contract, I2605, eventually closed at 811.5 CNY/ton, up 2.33% from the previous trading session. Meanwhile, spot prices increased by 5-10 CNY/ton compared to the previous trading day. Traders' enthusiasm for quong was average, while steel mills restocked based on immediate needs, with fewer inquiries. Overall, spot market transac ons were sluggish. Looking ahead, the modera ng pace of iron ore inventory accumula on suggests a pick-up in demand.
