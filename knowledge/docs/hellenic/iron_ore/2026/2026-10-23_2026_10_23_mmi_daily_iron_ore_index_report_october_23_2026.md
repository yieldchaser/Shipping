# Market Commentary · Imported Ore

Futures & spot DCE most-traded I2701 closed at 675.0 yuan/mt, up 0.90% from the previous session. Qingdao PB Fines were 643, Newman Fines 643, BRBF 696, Newman Lump 833, SSF 505, Carajas Fines 818 and PB Lump 823 yuan/wmt, each up 4 yuan; Mac Fines rose 3 yuan to 649. SGX official current-month FEFV26 was last priced at USD 90.75/dmt, timestamped Oct 23, 17:54:19 (UTC+8).

Drivers Following the previous broad decline, domestic futures, port spot and seaborne quotations recovered together. The seaborne 61%-65% price differential narrowed slightly from USD 17.65 to 17.60/dmt, while the low-grade port index posted a relatively larger gain. Higher prices may encourage buying, but durability still requires transactions and restocking; a one-day rebound does not establish a trend reversal.

Demand The latest 242-mill survey showed daily hot metal output of 2.3584 million mt on Oct 21, up 6,400 mt from Oct 14, and a blast furnace operating rate of 87.16%. Improved production supports baseline consumption, but mill stocks, end-user orders and margins still determine purchasing intensity. Persistent buying could improve downside support, while purchases limited to immediate needs could constrain the recovery.

Inventory & Ten-port stocks were 104.6534 million mt on Oct 22, up 632,200 mt (+0.61%) from Oct 15. Fines fell 1.2085 supply million mt to 82.0793 million mt; concentrates, lump and pellets reached 7.7342, 13.0109 and 1.8290 million mt, increasing by 416,900, 1.3362 million and 87,600 mt, respectively. Different product trends may reflect both arrival composition and collection schedules. Global shipments and Chinese arrivals in the week to Oct 16 were

32.5276 and 24.5464 million mt, down 4.72% and 12.07% WoW.
Cost & margin Oct 22 freight was USD 33.50/mt for C3, up 3.78% from the previous reading, and 14.20 for C5, up 6.93%. Higher freight may increase costs for some cargoes, while firmer spot selling prices support receipts. Actual trading margins still depend on contracts, settlement timing and exchange rates; import trading, steelmaking and coking profitability should be assessed separately. SMM’s assessment published on the morning of Oct 23 put the period’s average import margin at 1.03 yuan/mt, down 8.76 yuan from 9.79. This is an import trading assessment, not post-close mill profitability.

# Market Commentary · Domestic Ore

Prices The Domestic Ore Composite Price Index was 797.36 yuan/mt on Oct 23, up 0.13% from Oct 22. SMM’s current west Liaoning brief quoted concentrate at 640–650 yuan/mt on a wet-basis, tax-exclusive ex-works basis, distinct from the national index in region and pricing terms.

Supply & SMM described subdued trading in west Liaoning. Earlier environmental checks constrained some mining and demand beneficiation operations, and independent concentrators struggled to source ore, leaving buyers and sellers at an impasse. Supply constraints may support some offers, but actual buying still depends on mill margins and orders.

Outlook SMM expects west Liaoning concentrate prices to remain range-bound near term. Persistent buying improvement could strengthen sellers’ bargaining power amid resource constraints, while limited trading could restrain recovery. Regional moves should be assessed against transactions rather than extrapolated into nationwide gains.
