## Main
Today, the DCE iron ore futures consolidated on a subdued note. The most-traded I2701 contract on DCE closed at 713.5 yuan/mt, down 0.28%from the previous trading day. Spot prices at Qingdao port fell by 0-2 yuan/mt, traders sold at market prices, and steel mills restocked as needed. The overall spot trading sentiment was sluggish, with trading volume slightly up from yesterday. According to SMM statistics, this week (August 22 to August 28), blast furnace maintenance affected a total of 1.4337 million mt of hot metal production, an increase of 67,400 mt WoW. Looking ahead to next week (August 29 to September 4), the hot metal production impact is expected to pull back to 1.2633 million mt, down 170,400 mt WoW. Affected by the rise in coke prices squeezing steel mill profits and the still uncertain recovery of traditional peak-season demand, some steel mills chose to postpone their production resumptions. It is expected that this week's hot metal production will edge down, steel mills' raw material purchasing sentiment will become cautious, thereby puƫng pressure on futures and spot prices, and the iron ore market may maintain a fluctuating trend in the short term.
