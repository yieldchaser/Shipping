However, softer imported ore and mills’ cost-control priorities kept bargaining pressure strong. Regional supply constraints coexist with cautious buying, making actual trading conditions more informative than individual quotations alone.

Outlook SMM expects near-term Liaodong prices to remain range-bound. Conservative mill buying could limit a recovery, while continued local supply tightness may cushion declines. Domestic ore should be assessed by region and pricing basis rather than treating concessions by some sellers as a nationwide decline.
