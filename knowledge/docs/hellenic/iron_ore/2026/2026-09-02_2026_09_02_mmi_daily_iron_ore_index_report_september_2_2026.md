## Main
Iron ore futures traded softer today. The DCE most-active contract I2701 settled at RMB 714.0/mt, down 1.11% from theprevious trading day.Spot prices at Qingdao Port fell in step by around RMB 10-13/mt. The pullback in futures dragged spot offers lower, while mills showed stronger willingness to restock on dips, lifting overall trading activity slightly. SMM research data this week show the blast furnace capacity utilisation rate at 88.88%, up 0.47 percentage points week-on-week, while average daily hot metal output at sample steel mills came in at 2.4080 million mt, up 12,700 mt week-on-week. Driven by a modest recovery in overseas steel demand, iron ore demand has warmed at the margin, providing firm support to the floor for ore prices. Although the coke market continues to dominate sentiment in the near term, there are as yet no signs of mills cuƫng production due to tight coke supply. On balance, iron ore prices are likely to extend a pattern of a rising floor and a rangebound-to-firmer trend in the near term.
