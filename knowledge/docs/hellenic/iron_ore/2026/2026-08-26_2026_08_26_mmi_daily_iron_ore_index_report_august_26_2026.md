## Main
Today, the DCE iron ore futures consolidated on a strong note. The most-traded I2701 futures contract on DCE closed at 720 yuan/mt,up 0.49% from the previous trading day. Spot prices of mainstream varieties rose by 2-7 yuan/mt, with traders adjusting to market conditions and maintaining a stable pace of shipments. Steel mills adopted a cautious wait-and-see aƫtude, and their purchasing enthusiasm pulled back. Overall spot market transaction sentiment was sluggish, with trading volume lower than yesterday. According to a SMM survey, on August 26, the blast furnace operating rate of 242 steel mills surveyed by SMM was 89.13%, down 0.06 ppt MoM. The blast furnace capacity utilization rate stood at 88.41%, down 0.43 ppt MoM. Daily average hot metal production at sample steel mills was 2.3953 million mt, down 11,600 mt MoM. Hot metal output has hit a boƩom, and a slight uptick may be seen next week. Market expectations have improved, providing some support for ore prices. Going forward, close attention should be paid to the squeezing effect of consecutive coke price hikes on steel mill profits, and whether hot metal output can achieve growth as expected. These will be key factors influencing ore price trends.
