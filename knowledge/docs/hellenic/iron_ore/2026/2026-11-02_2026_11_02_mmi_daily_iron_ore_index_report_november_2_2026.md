# Key View

Prices recovered in November’s first session, with futures, port and seaborne indices moving higher together. The seaborne 61% and 65% indices reached USD 90.90 and 106.90/dmt, with most brand quotations firmer, indicating improved short-term price support. Supply flows offer some cushion. Arrivals in China fell 15.73% WoW in the week to Oct 30, potentially easing near- term incoming pressure, although global shipments rose 2.58%, leaving room for later changes in arrivals. Two consecutive increases in the latest hot metal readings support baseline consumption, but production recovery is not the same as discretionary restocking; orders and margins determine purchasing persistence.
