Higher output is not the same as discretionary restocking: mill stocks, end-user orders and margins still shape purchasing schedules. Higher freight adds a cost variable, making selling prices, procurement contracts and exchange rates important to margin assessment rather than extrapolating one day’s production or price move into a trend reversal.
