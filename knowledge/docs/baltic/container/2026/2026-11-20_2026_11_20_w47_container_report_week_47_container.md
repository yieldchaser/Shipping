Container

## Container
Container freight rates were mixed over the past week, with the FBX Weighted Average declining 57 points from 3,476 to 3,419. The main Asia-US West Coast trade lane (FBX01) recorded a significant correction, falling $809 from $8,452 to $7,643/FEU by week's end. Similar downward pressure was evident on the Asia-US East Coast route (FBX03), which declined $427 from $9,848 to $9,421/FEU. In contrast, Asia-North Europe (FBX11) edged higher, increasing from $3,366 to $3,406/FEU. The largest upward movement was recorded on Asia-Mediterranean (FBX13), where rates surged $1,076 from $4,281 to $5,357/FEU. Elsewhere, North Europe-US East Coast (FBX22) softened by $107, from $2,746 to $2,639/FEU.
