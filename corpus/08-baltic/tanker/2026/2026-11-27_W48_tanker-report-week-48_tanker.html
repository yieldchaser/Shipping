Tanker report - Week 48

Clean

LR2

The TC1 75kt MEG/Japan index edged up by three points this week to WS1053, with the corresponding Baltic round-trip TCE rising by $1,000 to $309,400/day. For the TC20 90kt MEG/UK-Continent run, the index remained relatively flat, gaining just $13,100 to $18.59 million, corresponding to $288,600/day on the Baltic round trip. The TC15 80kt Mediterranean/East index climbed by $80,000 to $7.73 million, with the Baltic round-trip TCE increasing by $1,200 to $61,200/day.

LR1

The TC5 55kt MEG/Japan index gained five points this week to WS994, translating to $206,900/day on Baltic round trip. For a trip westward on the TC8 65kt MEG/UK-Continent route, the index slipped by $29,800 to $14.44 million, while the Baltic round-trip TCE edged up by $800 to $221,900/day.

MR

The TC17 35kt MEG/East Africa index climbed by around nine points this week to WS921, with the TCE rising by $1,300 to $118,800/day. On the UK-Continent, the TC2 37kt ARA/US Atlantic Coast index shed 25 points to WS153, with the corresponding Baltic round-trip TCE falling by $3,900 to $6,600/day. In the US Gulf, the TC14 38kt US Gulf/UK-Continent index jumped by 101 points to WS336, with the round-trip TCE gaining $21,100 to $44,800/day. The TC21 38kt US Gulf/Caribbean run climbed from $750,000 to $1.11 million, with the corresponding Baltic TCE more than doubling from $19,600 to $41,200/day. The MR Atlantic Triangulation Basket TCE increased from $36,400/day to $52,300/day.

Handymax

In the Mediterranean, the TC6 30kt Cross-Mediterranean index dropped by 57 points to WS342 this week, with the corresponding Baltic round-trip TCE falling by $15,600 to $61,100/day. The TC23 30kt Cross UK-Continent index shed 61 points to WS308, while the run’s TCE declined by $17,300 to $48,700/day.

VLCC

Baltic Exchange’s VLCC rates firmed again this week across Middle East routes. The TD3C (270,000 mt Middle East Gulf/China) climbed 10 points to W1033.75, generating a daily round-trip TCE of $1,100,100 for the standard Baltic VLCC. TD34 (Gulf of Oman/China) also edged up 8.44 points higher to WS336.94, equating to a round-trip TCE of $336,098/day.

In the Atlantic, rates rose early to fall back down again. The 260,000 mt West Africa/China route (TD15) ultimately eased a couple of points to WS300 following a 10 point jump on Wednesday, with the corresponding round-trip TCE is $279,851/day. Conversely, the US Gulf/China route (TD22) rose by $1.43 million to the $36.5 million level, pulling up the daily round-trip TCE to $270,834/day.

Suezmax

The Suezmax market showed continued strength this week across Baltic routes. The 130,000 mt Nigeria/UK Continent route (TD20) jumped another 22.78 points to WS380, equating to a daily round-trip TCE of approximately $197,600. TD27 (130,000 mt Guyana/UK Continent) elevated by 45.55 points to WS389.44, returning just over $206,400/day. The TD33 145,000 mt US Gulf/UK Continent route (TD33) was less notable, gaining a modest 7.78 points to WS328.89, with the corresponding TCE rising to just over $203,100/day.

In the Black Sea, the 135,000 mt CPC/Augusta route (TD6) resurged gently to WS454.78 (+14.78), equating to a daily round-trip TCE of just over $334,900.

Aframax

The Aframax market was varied this week, with steam lost across the Atlantic routes, accompanied by firming in the North Sea and Mediterranean, while Vancouver exports were unperturbed.

In the North Sea, the 80,000 mt Cross-UK Continent route (TD7) advanced 35.83 points to WS352.5, generating a daily round-trip TCE of about $253,300, basis Hound Point/Wilhelmshaven.

In the Mediterranean, TD19 (80,000 mt Cross-Mediterranean) posted a gentler gain of 15.28 points to WS397.5, returning approximately $163,100/day on a Ceyhan/Lavera basis.

Across the Atlantic, Aframax rates came off sharply this week prior to Thanksgiving. The TD26 (70,000 mt East Coast Mexico/US Gulf) dumped 40.28 points to WS961.11, generating a daily round-trip TCE of just over $318,700. TD9 (70,000 mt Covenas/US Gulf) recorded a similarly aggressive drop, losing 75 points to around WS796 and returning just over $263,750/day. TD25 (70,000 mt US Gulf/UK Continent) took the heaviest hit, being marked 116.67 points lower than last week at just over WS501, equating to approximately $147,489/day on a Houston/Rotterdam basis.

The Vancouver export routes were also weaker this week. The TD28 (80,000 mt Vancouver/China) eased by another $295,000 to $7.57 million, corresponding to a round-trip TCE of approximately $147,900/day. TD29 (80,000 mt Vancouver/Pacific Area Lightering point off the US West Coast) shed nine points to WS674.
