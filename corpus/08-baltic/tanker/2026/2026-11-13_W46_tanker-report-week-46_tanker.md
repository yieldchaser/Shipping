# Tanker Report - Week 46

**Date:** 13 November 2026  
**Publisher:** Baltic Exchange | **Category:** Tankers  
**Original URL:** [http://www.balticexchange.com/en/data-services/WeeklyRoundup/tanker/news/2026/tanker-report-week-46.html](http://www.balticexchange.com/en/data-services/WeeklyRoundup/tanker/news/2026/tanker-report-week-46.html)

### Clean

*LR2*

The TC1 75kt MEG/Japan index ultimately ticked up by 8 points this week to WS1016, while the corresponding Baltic round-trip TCE edged up by $3,061 to $298,000/day. For the TC20 90kt MEG/UK-Continent run, the index rose by $264,000 to $19.23 million, corresponding to $297,300/day on Baltic round trip. The TC15 80kt Mediterranean/East index shed a further $820,000 to settle at $7.9 million, with the Baltic round-trip TCE falling by $10,100 to $63,400/day.

*LR1*

The TC5 55kt MEG/Japan index eased by 13 points this week to WS1005, converting to $209,900/day on Baltic round trip. For a trip westward on the TC8 65kt MEG/UK-Continent route, the index fell by $60,700 to $14.63 million, while the Baltic round-trip TCE return declined by $498 to $223,200/day.

*MR*

The TC17 35kt MEG/East Africa index strengthened by 27 points this week to WS924. Additionally, the TCE increased by $4,700 to $119,500/day. On the UK-Continent, the TC2 37kt ARA/US Atlantic Coast index firmed a modest 4 points to WS198, with the corresponding Baltic round-trip TCE climbing by $1,500 to $13,800/day. In the US Gulf, the TC14 38kt US Gulf/UK-Continent index was shot down by 37 points to WS397 and the round-trip TCE declined by $7,200 to $55,700/day. The TC21 38kt US Gulf/Caribbean was cut down by $201,000 to $1.31 million, with the corresponding Baltic TCE falling by $11,500 to $48,100/day. The MR Atlantic Triangulation Basket TCE dropped from $72,700/day to $67,100/day.

*Handymax*

In the Mediterranean, the TC6 30kt Cross-Mediterranean index fell off by 54 points to WS363 this week, with the corresponding Baltic round-trip TCE dropping by $15,000 to $65,200/day. The TC23 30kt Cross UK-Continent index dipped down to WS382 then back up again this week to WS406, while the run’s TCE followed this to finally end back up at $76,100/day.

### VLCC

VLCC rates on the Baltic Exchange routes headed downwards again this week. The TD3C route (270,000 mt Middle East Gulf/China) has fallen 188 points since last Friday to WS1014.38, producing a daily round-trip TCE of $1,078,714 for the standard Baltic VLCC. TD34 (Gulf of Oman/China) fell more sharply, losing 234 points to WS310.71, giving a round-trip TCE of $307,160/day.

In the Atlantic market, the rate for the 260,000 mt West Africa/China route (TD15) lost 210 points to WS288.57, producing a round-trip TCE of $267,748/day. Meanwhile, the US Gulf/China route (TD22) fell by over $19.6 million to about the $33.8 million mark, resulting in a daily round-trip TCE of $247,973/day.

### Suezmax

In the Suezmax sector, the market has fallen again this week across all the Baltic Exchange routes. The rate for the 130,000 mt Nigeria/UK Continent voyage (TD20) fell by 92 points to WS307.22, giving a daily round-trip TCE of about $151,300. The TD27 route (Guyana/UK Continent, basis 130,000 mt) dropped 47 points to WS313.33, producing a daily round-trip TCE of just under $157,600. The 145,000 mt USG/UKC route (TD33) fell over 20 points to WS267.22, giving a round-trip TCE of a little over $155,600/day.

In the Black Sea, the 135,000 mt CPC/Augusta route (TD6) tumbled another 192 points to just below WS425, producing a daily round-trip TCE of just below $305,000.

### Aframax

In the North Sea, the rate for the 80,000 mt Cross-UK Continent route (TD7) fell 78 points to WS247.5, giving a daily round-trip TCE of just below $141,300 basis Hound Point/Wilhelmshaven.

In the Mediterranean, the rate for the 80,000 mt Cross-Mediterranean route (TD19) fell by 15 points to WS267.78. Basis Ceyhan/Lavera, this produces a daily round-trip TCE of about $86,000.

Across the Atlantic, rates fell again but not as heavily as a week ago. The 70,000 mt East Coast Mexico/US Gulf route (TD26) fell almost 36 points to WS310, giving a daily round-trip TCE of just over $83,000. The 70,000 mt Covenas/US Gulf route (TD9) dropped 34 points to about WS302, producing a daily round-trip TCE of just over $77,300.

The 70,000 mt US Gulf/UK Continent route (TD25) dropped 33 points to just below WS295, giving a round-trip TCE, basis Houston/Rotterdam, of about $71,700/day.

On the Vancouver export routes, rates weakened again. The TD28 route (80,000 mt crude oil Vancouver to China) fell by $1.115 million to $8.565 million, producing a round-trip TCE of under $171,300/day. The TD29 route (80,000 mt crude oil Vancouver to Pacific Area Lightering point off the USWC) fell 55 points to WS686.
