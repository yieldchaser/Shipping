# Tanker Report - Week 44

**Publisher:** Baltic Exchange | **Category:** Tankers

### Clean

*LR2*

The TC1 75kt MEG/Japan index shed 99 points this week to WS1003, with the corresponding Baltic round-trip TCE falling by $33,200 to $288,700/day. For the TC20 90kt MEG/UK-Continent run, the index edged up by $43,800 to $21.86 million, corresponding to $342,700/day on Baltic round trip. The TC15 80kt Mediterranean/East index dropped by $830,000 to $11.12 million, with the Baltic round-trip TCE declining by $12,600 to $101,800/day.

*LR1*

The TC5 55kt MEG/Japan index fell by 94 points this week to WS1021, translating to $208,900/day on Baltic round trip. For a trip westward, the TC8 65kt MEG/UK-Continent index dropped by $303,600 to $15.99 million, while the Baltic round-trip TCE return decreased by $5,600 to $245,200/day.

*MR*

The TC17 35kt MEG/East Africa index edged up by around 4 points this week to WS901, although the TCE slipped by $500 to $112,900/day. On the UK-Continent, the TC2 37kt ARA/US Atlantic Coast index shed 5 points to WS226, with the corresponding Baltic round-trip TCE falling by $1,000 to $16,600/day. In the US Gulf, the TC14 38kt US Gulf/UK-Continent index climbed by 46 points to WS496 and the round-trip TCE gained $9,300 to $73,500/day. The TC21 38kt US Gulf/Caribbean run rose by $232,100 to $1.78 million, with the corresponding Baltic TCE increasing by $13,500 to $77,900/day. The MR Atlantic Triangulation Basket TCE climbed from $77,900/day to $85,500/day.

*Handymax*

In the Mediterranean, the TC6 30kt Cross-Mediterranean index eased by 6 points to WS523 this week, with the corresponding Baltic round-trip TCE dropping by $1,700 to $108,900/day. The TC23 30kt Cross UK-Continent index also shed 6 points to WS503, while the run’s TCE fell by $1,900 to $102,000/day.

### VLCC

VLCC rates on the Baltic Exchange routes appear to have found a floor at the back end of last week, with rates rebounding this week. The TD3C route (270,000mt Middle East Gulf/China) has risen 48 points since last Friday to WS1262.5, producing a daily round-trip TCE of $1,345,710 for the standard Baltic VLCC. TD34 (Gulf of Oman/China) rose more sharply, gaining 89 points to WS659.29, giving a round-trip TCE of $699,257/day.

In the Atlantic market, the rate for the 260,000mt West Africa/China route (TD15) climbed 82 points to WS633.75, producing a round-trip TCE of $632,191/day. Meanwhile, the US Gulf/China route (TD22) gained over $4.2 million to almost $61.5 million, resulting in a daily round-trip TCE of $477,013.

### Suezmax

In the Suezmax sector, the weaker trend continued across the Baltic Exchange routes. The rate for the 130,000mt Nigeria/UK Continent voyage (TD20) fell almost 14 points to WS713.89, giving a daily round-trip TCE of over $396,100. The TD27 route (Guyana/UK Continent, basis 130,000mt) dropped by 25 points to WS712.5, producing a daily round-trip TCE of just over $401,000. The 145,000mt USG/UKC route (TD33) fell by almost 37 points to WS621.94, giving a round-trip TCE of over $411,600/day.

In the Black Sea, the 135,000mt CPC/Augusta route (TD6) tumbled 116 points to WS958.33, producing a daily round-trip TCE of about $769,800. There are currently CPC cargoes being worked, but suitable tonnage is plentiful. With vessel availability outweighing current demand, further downward pressure on rates is likely over the coming days.

### Aframax

In the North Sea, the rate for the 80,000mt Cross-UK Continent route (TD7) fell back about 20 points this week to around WS570, giving a daily round-trip TCE of just over $466,000 basis Hound Point/Wilhelmshaven.

In the Mediterranean, the rate for the 80,000mt Cross-Mediterranean route (TD19) tumbled 137 points to around WS703. Basis Ceyhan/Lavera, this produces a daily round-trip TCE of a little over $323,300.

Across the Atlantic, rates were comparatively steady on the shorter-haul routes but weakened further on the transatlantic run. The 70,000mt East Coast Mexico/US Gulf route (TD26) rose 2 points to WS720, giving a daily round-trip TCE of almost $251,400. The 70,000mt Covenas/US Gulf route (TD9) also gained 2 points to WS710, producing a daily round-trip TCE of just over $226,400.

The 70,000mt US Gulf/UK Continent route (TD25) fell 25 points to almost WS707, giving a round-trip TCE, basis Houston/Rotterdam, of just under $216,800/day.

On the Vancouver export routes, rates weakened again. The TD28 route (80,000mt crude oil Vancouver to China) fell by $2 million to $12.26 million, producing a round-trip TCE of under $253,400/day. The TD29 route (80,000mt crude oil Vancouver to Pacific Area Lightering point off the USWC) fell a further 11 points to WS904.
