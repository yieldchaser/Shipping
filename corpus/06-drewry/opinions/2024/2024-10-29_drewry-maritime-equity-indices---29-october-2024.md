# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 29 October 2024

*Published on 29 October 2024*

Similar to the previous week, most of the Drewry Equity Indices declined in the week ending 25 October except the Container Shipping Index.

The Container Index rose 3.4% driven by the expectation of strong 3Q24 results. Conversely, falling spot rates led to a decline in the bulk shipping and port sectors. The Crude and Product Tanker Index plummeted by 7.7% and 7.5%, respectively. Similarly, the LPG, Dry Bulk, and LNG Indices declined by 4.5%, 4.2%, and 2.1%, respectively.
