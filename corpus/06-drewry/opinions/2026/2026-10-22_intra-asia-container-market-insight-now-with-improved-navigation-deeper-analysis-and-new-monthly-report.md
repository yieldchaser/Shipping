# Intra-Asia Container Market Insight - Now with Improved Navigation, Deeper Analysis and New Monthly Report

*Published on 22 October 2026*

London, UK, 22 October 2026: Drewry, the maritime industry’s leading independent research and advisory firm, is pleased to announce the launch of an enhanced version of itsIntra-Asia Container Market Insight(IACMI) featuring improved navigation, deeper country-to-country and port-to-port analysis, and new monthly PDF report to support more informed commercial decision-making.

Delivered through the Drewry OnDemand platform, IACMI provides integrated intelligence on intra-Asia container shipping markets, helping shippers, freight forwarders, shipping lines, port authorities and terminal operators monitor market developments, benchmark performance and assess carrier strategies.

The refreshed service retains IACMI’s established regional coverage, including trade route fundamentals, now-casted demand analysis, carrier strategies, port performance and freight-rate monitoring, alongside interactive access to detailed market data. Existing subscribers will benefit from the enhancements through their current subscription, with no changes to access arrangements or pricing planned.

The improvements further strengthen IACMI as a practical market intelligence resource for monitoring the evolving intra-Asia container market and supporting operational and strategic decisions.
