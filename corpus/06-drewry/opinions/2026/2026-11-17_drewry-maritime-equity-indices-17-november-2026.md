# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 17 November 2026

*Published on 17 November 2026*

Drewry Equity Indices delivered mixed returns for the week ending 13 November 2026, compared to 2.5% and 1.3% rise in the S&P 500 and Russell 2000, respectively. Both infrastructure-related indices and bulk shipping indices showed a mixed trend.

The Drewry Port Equity Index (-4.9%) was the weakest-performing segment during the week, primarily due to a 9.6% decline in ICTSI’s share price amid concerns over rising operating costs and the sustainability of its acquisition-led growth strategy. In contrast, the Drewry Logistics Equity Index gained 2.7%, supported by a 7.3% WoW rise in CH Robinson’s share price following management’s positive commentary on the RXO acquisition. The Drewry Product and Crude Tanker Equity Index was the top-performing segment, advancing 4.1% and 4.0%, respectively on the back of strong forward bookings and robust 3Q26 earnings. The Drewry LPG Equity Index rose 3.5%, supported by elevated freight rates and BW LPG’s announced VLGC sale at an attractive valuation. Meanwhile, the Drewry LNG Equity Index fell 2.0% as softer LNG shipping spot rates weighed on investor sentiment.

On a YTD basis, infra-focused indices have delivered positive returns (Container +19.2%, Ports +18.2%, Logistics +9.9%). Within bulk shipping, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index remains the top performer, up 94.5%, outperforming other segments (Product: +75.3%, LPG: +58.7%, Dry Bulk: +44.5%, LNG: 1.4%).
