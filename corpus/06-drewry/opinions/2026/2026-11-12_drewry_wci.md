## Market Commentary & Analysis

The Drewry World Container Index (WCI), the benchmark widely referenced by procurement teams, decreased 2% to $4,305 per 40ft container this week, driven by declining rates on the Transpacific and Asia–Europe/Med trade routes. The Transpacific trade route followed the downward trend seen on the Asia–Europe/Med trade route over the past three weeks.

On the Transpacific trade route, rates from Shanghai to New York decreased 3% to $9,020 per 40ft container, the lowest since August 2026, while rates from Shanghai to Los Angeles fell 2% to $7,416 per 40ft container, the lowest since September 2026. To support freight rates, carriers are attempting to manage capacity through blank sailings.

On the Asia-Europe/Med trade route, rates from Shanghai to Rotterdam decreased 3% to $3,504 per 40ft container, while rates from Shanghai to Genoa remained stable at $4,168 per 40ft container. Carriers are continuing to manage capacity through blank sailings.
