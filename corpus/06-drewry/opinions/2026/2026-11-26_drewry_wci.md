## Market Commentary & Analysis

The Drewry World Container Index (WCI), the benchmark widely referenced by procurement teams, decreased 3% to $4,032 per 40ft container this week, driven by lower rates on the Transpacific trade route amid weak demand.

On the Transpacific trade route, rates continued their downtrend for the eighth consecutive week. Spot rates from Shanghai to New York decreased 7% to $7,615 per 40ft container, and those from Shanghai to Los Angeles fell 8% to $6,437 per 40ft container. Amid weak demand, carriers are reducing capacity through blank sailings.

On the Asia-Europe/Med trade route, spot rates from Shanghai to Genoa increased 8% to $4,802 per 40ft container, while Shanghai to Rotterdam rates remained the same for the second consecutive week at $3,485 per 40ft container. Carriers are managing capacity through blank sailings.
