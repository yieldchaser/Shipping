# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 13 October 2026

*Published on 13 October 2026*

Drewry Equity Indices delivered mixed returns for the week ending 9 October 2026 (vs. the S&P 500 up 1.2% and Russell 2000 down 0.9%, respectively). Infrastructure-related indices were broadly flat, whereas the bulk shipping indices showed a mixed trend.

Our infrastructure-related sectors were broadly flat, except for the Drewry Logistics Equity Index, which declined 0.9%, mainly due to a 9.6% drop in C.H. Robinson’s share price amid investor concerns over the pause in share buybacks, as well as increased leverage and execution risks associated with the company’s proposed USD 5.8bn acquisition of RXO. The Drewry Product Tanker Equity Index (+6.2%) was the top performer of the week, driven by an 8.6% increase in Hafnia’s share price following Brookfield-controlled OCM Njord’s plans to divest its remaining stake in Torm. It was followed by the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index and Drewry LPG Shipping Equity Index, which gained 5.2% and 2.6%, respectively, due to elevated freight rates. In contrast, the Drewry LNG Shipping Equity Index declined 2.6%, led by a 2.7% WoW fall in Nakilat’s share price.

On a YTD basis, infra-focused indices have delivered mixed returns (Container +27.8%, Ports +26.9%, Logistics -1.5%). Within bulk shipping, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index remains the top performer, up 130.1%, outperforming other segments (Product: +89.4%, LPG: +87.0%, Dry Bulk: +58.5%, LNG: +3.9%).
