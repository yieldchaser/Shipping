# Ports & Terminals Financial Insight - October 2023

*Published on 05 October 2023*

The sector continues to trade in the undervalued zone with EV/EBITDA multiple of 9.2x (vs the industry’s 10-year average: 10.7x). Considering that interest rates are already high, we believe that upside risk is limited from the mid-to-long-term perspective.  However, with the sluggish demand outlook, investors should remain patient and search for pockets of opportunities instead of investing in the sectorial theme.
