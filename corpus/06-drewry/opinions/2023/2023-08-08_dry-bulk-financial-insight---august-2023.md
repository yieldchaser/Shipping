# Dry Bulk Financial Insight - August 2023

*Published on 08 August 2023*

In June and July, the Drewry Dry Bulk Equity Index increased 5.8% but underperformed the S&P 500, which gained 9.8%. The improvement during June and July (after declining 28.5% during March and May) is primarily because of seasonal demand. The index is down 15.2% YTD. Dry bulk shipping companies have thus far reported weak 2Q23 results, which is also putting pressure on their stock prices. Pacific Basin and Star Bulk revenues declined sharply in 2Q23, mainly due to lower freight rates.
