# King of the Clean Hill

The annual reviews of the most active charterers in the tanker market are widely read by many different market participants. It is important to keep perspective on what the fixture information truly represents- the fixtures that are reported openly in the market. As was pointed out in last week's Opinion, The Top Bananas , comprehensive fixture information is becoming more opaque as the market sees increases in contracts of affreightment, consecutive voyages and term business.

Similar to the dirty spot tanker market, rates in the clean market continued to remain above long-term averages in 2006. It is not surprising then that the total amount of reported spot fixtures has increased almost 10% from 2005 to 2006, as shown below.

### Top Charterers

Shell retains the top position with the greatest number of reported clean spot market fixtures (recall that they are number one in dirty fixtures as well.) Vitol climbed to the number two slot, as BP slid to number three. Lukoil rose to the number six position, from number 11 in 2005.

The top 10 charterers accounted for 37% of the total reported spot market activity in the clean market.

In 2006, the total amount of reported clean spot market Aframax fixtures declined by 9% as compared to 2005. The clean market accounts for only 8% of the total spot market activity concluded on Aframaxes. The largest clean trade for Aframaxes continues to be Arabian Gulf - Far East with 65% of the total reported fixtures. The next largest trade is Arabian Gulf - UK Continent with 14% of the total fixtures.

Based on reported fixtures, clean activity accounts for a sizeable percentage of total Panamax fixtures, over 40%. The total amount of reported fixtures in 2006 increased by 35% as compared to 2005. The majority of clean Panamax fixtures are from the Arabian Gulf, but movements from the UK Continent and the Far East also comprise a large percentage of the market. The largest charterer of clean Aframaxes and Panamaxes in 2006 was Vitol, taking the majority of these cargoes from the Arabian Gulf and the Mediterranean

### Titan

Clean activity significantly outweighs dirty activity in the Handymax sector, accounting for 88% of all reported spot fixture activity. There was an increase of 6% in reported spot fixtures from 2005 to 2006. A large percentage, 41% of the Handymax fixtures are bound for the United States, loading primarily in the UK Continent and the Caribbean

### PMI

The total amount of reported clean spot fixtures increased 9% from 2005. The total number of reported spot fixtures in 2006 was 1,313 with Lukoil as the largest charterer. Most of the activity in this sector occurs in the Mediterranean.

### Projector

### Less Transparent

As more and more traders charter-in to build fleets of clean product carriers, the amount of reported spot fixtures will not reflect the amount of clean products actually moving on tankers. However, our assessment is that the information that is summarized in the preceding pages is still an accurate reflection of who are the most active charterers in the market. With worldwide product demand expected to remain high in 2007, anticipate that demand for product carriers will stay strong, but we will have to wait until January 2008 to find out who was 2007 "King of the Clean Hill."
