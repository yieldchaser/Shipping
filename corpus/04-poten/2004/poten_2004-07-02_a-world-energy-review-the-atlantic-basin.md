### Conclusion

In the short run, the narrowing gap for Middle East oil is expected to continue with rising production in Russia, the Caspian Sea, North and West Africa amply meeting expected growth in North American import demand. Considering the situation in the Middle East, reduction of U.S. dependence on non-Atlantic basin oil would be an understandable objective of the U.S. government. However the counter-movement of primarily Nigerian crude to Asia does provide incremental cargoes for VLCCs in the form of replacement cargoes from the Middle East. Thus it is not a foregone conclusion that westbound VLCC cargoes will decline in the future. But it is apparent that the Middle East will have to look further beyond the Atlantic basin for incremental business.
