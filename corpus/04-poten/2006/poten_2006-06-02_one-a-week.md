### Spot Market Action

The total amount of reported spot market activity has been on the rise overall for the past ten years and has declined somewhat in the past three. The total number of reported fixtures was 3,866 in 1996 and peaked in 2002 at 5,283, as indicated by the chart on the next page. The past three years have seen a total number of fixtures in the 4,700 ranges. With the fleet growing by 210 vessels (or 43%) it is clear that a larger percentage of the total amount of cargoes moving are doing so under contracts. ### Up Doesn't Mean Down

The theory that the increase in fleet will lead to a decline in rates has certainly not proven itself in the Aframax sector. The increasing demand for tonnage has outpaced the orderbook and contributed to a positive rate trend over the past ten years. Even if the "one a week" fleet development will continue through 2008, history suggests that the Aframax sector will remain buoyant.
