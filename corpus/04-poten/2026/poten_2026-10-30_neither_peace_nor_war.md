## Analysis & Commentary

Neither Peace Nor War

What if the U.S.-Iran conflict never ends? The recent war between the United States and Iran started 8 months ago and there is no end in sight. The conflict is in a stalemate. Many people expect that there will be a diplomatic solution at some point within the next 3-6 months that will reopen the Strait of Hormuz and (gradually) bring Iran back into the international community. However, there is an alternative scenario that is also possible. The next several years could look like the last 3-4 months (since the Islamabad Memorandum of Understanding effectively fell apart in early July): The conflict remains unresolved, with intermittent attacks, continued sanctions, unsuccessful negotiations and temporary arrangements to keep the oil moving. What would the oil and tanker markets look like under such a scenario? That is the topic of this week’s Tanker Opinion.
