## Analysis & Commentary

The Gift That Keeps On Giving

ExxonMobil approves further Guyana expansion The first time we dedicated a Tanker Opinion to Guyana was in October 2019 before any oil had reached the market. First oil started to flow in 2020. Since those early days, Guyana has become a major non-OPEC producer and exporter. Thursday, ExxonMobil announced that it made a final investment decision on the Longtail development offshore Guyana after receiving final government and regulatory approvals. This is the eighth development on the Stabroek block, and it is expected to start up in 2030. Because all of Guyana’s oil is being exported, these developments have been a boon for the tanker market. In this Tanker Opinion, we will go back to the early days, what has happened since and what can we expect for the future, with a particular focus on the impact on the tanker markets.
