**EuroDry Ltd. Announces Order for the Construction of a Third Modern 82,000 DWT Kamsarmax Bulk Carrier**

**Athens, Greece – October 14, 2026** – EuroDry Ltd. (NASDAQ: EDRY, the “Company” or “EuroDry”), an owner and operator of drybulk vessels and provider of seaborne transportation for drybulk cargoes, announced today that it has signed a contract with Hengli Shipbuilding (Dalian) for the construction of a third 82,000 DWT Kamsarmax bulk carrier. The vessel will be built to an eco-design specification compliant with EEDI Phase 3 standards and is scheduled to be delivered during the third quarter of 2029.

The total consideration for the newbuilding contract is approximately \$37.0 million and will be financed with a combination of debt and equity. The contract is conditional upon receiving a refund guarantee from a bank acceptable to the Company.

**Aristides Pittas, Chairman and CEO of EuroDry, commented** **:** “We are pleased to announce the order of a third 82,000 DWT eco Kamsarmax vessel. With modern five-year-old Kamsarmaxes being valued at or above newbuilding prices, we consider that ordering new tonnage is a better economic proposition, as we get a brand-new, more fuel-efficient ship for the same or even lower capital investment. This order brings our newbuilding program to five vessels, lowering the average age of our fleet and positioning us competitively in a market environment that increasingly values fuel efficient and environmentally friendly vessels.”

**About EuroDry Ltd.**

EuroDry Ltd. was formed on January 8, 2018 under the laws of the Republic of the Marshall Islands to consolidate the drybulk fleet of Euroseas Ltd. into a separate listed public company. EuroDry was spun-off from Euroseas Ltd on May 30, 2018; it trades on the NASDAQ Capital Market under the ticker EDRY.

EuroDry operates in the dry cargo, drybulk shipping market. EuroDry's operations are managed by Eurobulk Ltd., an ISO 9001:2008 and ISO 14001:2004 certified affiliated ship management company and Eurobulk (Far East) Ltd. Inc., which are responsible for the day-to-day commercial and technical management and operations of the vessels. EuroDry employs its vessels on spot and period charters and under pool agreements.

The Company has a fleet of 11 vessels, including 3 Panamax drybulk carriers, 5 Ultramax drybulk carriers, 2 Kamsarmax drybulk carriers and 1 Supramax drybulk carrier. EuroDry’s 11 drybulk carriers have a total cargo capacity of 766,420 dwt. After the delivery of two Ultramax vessels in 2027 and the delivery of the three Kamsarmax vessels in 2028 and 2029, the Company’s fleet will consist of 16 vessels with a total carrying capacity of 1,139,420 dwt.
