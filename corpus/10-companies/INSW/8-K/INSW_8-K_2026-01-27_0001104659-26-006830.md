**Section 8 – Other Events**

Item 8.01 Other Events.

On January 27, 2026, a wholly-owned subsidiary of International Seaways, Inc. (the “Company”) acquired sole ownership of Tankers International, a leading shipping pool founded in 2000, providing commercial and administrative management of modern, independent VLCC tonnage. At the same time Tankers International announced the formation of a new pool to commercially manage Suezmaxes and that the Company will contribute its spot trading Suezmaxes to such new pool.

A copy of the press release issued by Tankers International on January 27, 2026 announcing the creation of the Suezmax pool is attached hereto as Exhibit 99.1.
