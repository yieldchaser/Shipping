**INTERNATIONAL SEAWAYS REPORTS**

**THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS**

**New York, NY – November 5, 2026–** International Seaways, Inc. (NYSE: INSW) (the “Company,” “Seaways,” or “INSW”), one of the largest tanker companies worldwide providing energy transportation services for crude oil and petroleum products, today reported results for the third quarter 2026.

**HIGHLIGHTS & RECENT DEVELOPMENTS**

**Strategic Fleet Expansion:**

· Acquired Bruton Limited (“Bruton”) and its four newbuilding VLCCs primarily for total consideration of approximately three million shares and cash.

· Took delivery of two LR1 newbuilds with the remaining four on order expected to deliver in the second half of 2028.

· Sold a 2012-built Suezmax for proceeds, net of commissions and fees, of \$74 million, recognizing a gain of \$53 million.

**Quarterly Results:**

· Record net income of \$344 million, or \$6.90 per diluted share, including \$53 million gain on sale.

· Near-record adjusted net income (1) of \$291 million, or \$5.84 per diluted share, and adjusted EBITDA (1) of \$345 million.

· Strong quarterly free cash flow (1) of \$234 million.

**Returns to Shareholders:**

· Declared dividend of \$5.05 per share, matching record set in the previous quarter.

· Fourth consecutive quarter with a payout ratio of at least 85% of adjusted net income.

· Declared dividends of \$16.80 per share over the last twelve months represent a 22% yield.

**Healthy Balance Sheet:**

· Total liquidity was approximately \$964 million as of September 30, 2026, including cash and short-term investments of \$454 million and \$510 million undrawn revolving credit capacity.

· Net loan-to-value (1) less than 6% as of September 30, 2026.

Lois K. Zabrocky, International Seaways President and CEO commented, “We delivered record quarterly net income, reflecting both exceptional tanker market conditions and the benefits of our long-term approach to fleet investment and capital allocation. While tanker markets were already strong earlier in the summer, rates surged to remarkable levels toward the end of the quarter. Our ability to capitalize on this extraordinary market reflects more than just favorable conditions; it is the result of years of disciplined capital allocation, fleet renewal and growth, all while strengthening our financial position and returning substantial capital to our shareholders. As we approach our tenth anniversary, we could not have predicted the extent of the disruptions that have created such a powerful earnings environment, but we have always focused on positioning Seaways to capture opportunities throughout the cycle. We continue to execute that strategy every day by optimizing our fleet across crude and product tanker markets, maintaining a healthy balance sheet and sharing the upside with our shareholders, while preserving the flexibility to pursue future growth and create long-term value.”

Jeff Pribor, the Company’s CFO stated, “Our strong cash generation and financial position provide Seaways with considerable optionality in capital allocation. The completion of the Bruton transaction demonstrates our disciplined approach to deploying capital, using our shares as currency for growth while maintaining significant financial resources for future investments. Importantly, our financial position allows us pursue additional investment opportunities while sustaining substantial returns to shareholders.”

**THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS**

Net income for the third quarter of 2026 was \$344 million, or \$6.90 per diluted share, compared to net income of \$71 million, or \$1.42 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily driven by higher TCE revenues (1) from spot earnings that increased an average of approximately \$38,300 per day across the fleet and higher profit-sharing results on applicable time charters by approximately \$75 million.

Shipping revenues for the third quarter were \$483 million, compared to \$196 million for the third quarter of 2025. Consolidated TCE revenues (1) for the third quarter were \$443 million, compared to \$192 million for the third quarter of 2025.

Adjusted EBITDA (1) for the third quarter was \$345 million, compared to \$108 million for the third quarter of 2025.

**Crude Tankers**

Shipping revenues for the Crude Tankers segment were \$359 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to \$96 million for the third quarter of 2025. TCE revenues (1) were \$321 million for the third quarter, compared to \$93 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in TCE revenues (1) was driven by higher average spot earnings of over \$78,000 per day and higher average time charter earnings of approximately \$274,000 per day, reflecting higher profit-sharing results.

**Product Carriers**

Shipping revenues for the Product Carriers segment were \$124 million for the third quarter, compared to \$100 million for the third quarter of 2025. TCE revenues (1) were \$121 million for the third quarter, compared to \$99 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in the third quarter of 2026 was attributable to higher TCE revenues (1) from spot earnings of approximately \$12,400 per day compared to the third quarter of 2025.

**FLEET OPTIMIZATION**

During the fourth quarter, the Company executed a series of transactions to acquire Bruton for approximately three million shares and cash. The Company expects to complete the settlement of the total consideration with Bruton shareholders on or about November 11, 2026. Bruton has one 2026-built, scrubber-fitted, dual-fuel (LNG) VLCC on the water and three VLCC newbuildings scheduled for delivery in November 2026, August 2027 and October 2027. As of October 31, 2026, the remaining construction installments total \$279 million with sale leaseback financing agreements in place for up to 90% of the vessels’ construction price. Gross debt outstanding under these agreements is approximately \$170 million as of October 31, 2026. The VLCC currently on the water is contracted at \$95,000 per day for a firm period ending April 2027. The second scrubber-fitted, dual-fuel (LNG) VLCC scheduled for delivery in November 2026 has secured a 12 month time charter at a rate of \$107,000 per day.

During the third quarter, the Company took delivery of *Seaways Magellan* and *Seaways Delgada* , completing its six-vessel LR1 newbuilding program at an aggregate contract price of approximately \$359 million. The Company drew approximately \$73 million under its Korean export agency-backed facility (the “ECA Credit Facility”) in connection with these deliveries. The Company has also contracted for four additional scrubber-fitted, dual-fuel (LNG) ready LR1 vessels, scheduled for delivery in the second half of 2028, at an aggregate contract price of \$244 million. The Company expects to finance these vessels through a combination of long-term financing and available liquidity and had made no installment payments as of September 30, 2026. All ten vessels are expected to trade in the Panamax International Pool, which has consistently outperformed the market.

During the third quarter, the Company entered into two time charter agreements with an average duration of three years. As of October 1, 2026, the Company has 11 vessels on time charter agreements with an average duration of 2.0 years and total future contracted revenues through expiry of approximately \$305 million, excluding any applicable profit share.

During the third quarter, the Company sold a 2012-built Suezmax for \$74 million, net of commissions and fees. In the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company sold eight vessels for aggregate proceeds of approximately \$290 million, net of positioning, commissions and fees. The vessels were among the oldest remaining in the fleet with an average age of 17 years. The Company recognized gains of approximately \$141 million in connection with the sale of these vessels.

**RETURNS TO SHAREHOLDERS**

In September 2026, the Company paid total dividends of \$5.05 per share of common stock. The Company paid total dividends of \$11.75 per share of common stock for the nine months ended September 30, 2026.

On November 3, 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors declared a quarterly dividend of \$5.05 per share of common stock. The dividend will be paid on December 23, 2026, to shareholders with a record date at the close of business on December 9, 2026.

In November 2026, the Company’s Board of Directors increased its share repurchase program to \$100 million and extended its expiry from the end of 2026 to the end of 2028.

**HEALTHY BALANCE SHEET**

During the third quarter of 2026, the Company drew \$73 million under the ECA Credit Facility in connection with the deliveries of *Seaways Magellan* and *Seaways Delgada* .

During the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company made \$20 million in scheduled principal repayments in connection with all of its debt arrangements.

**ABOUT INTERNATIONAL SEAWAYS, INC.**

International Seaways, Inc. (NYSE: INSW) is one of the largest public tanker companies in the world, providing seaborne transportation services for crude oil and refined petroleum products. The Company owns and operates a fleet across the principal tanker asset classes, including vessels on order. The Company focuses on the safe and reliable operation of its fleet and primarily employs its vessels in commercial pools, most of which it has an ownership interest, enhancing scale and market access. The Company is headquartered in New York City, N.Y. Additional information is available at https://www.intlseas.com.

**EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA**

EBITDA represents net income before interest expense, income taxes, and depreciation and amortization expense. Adjusted EBITDA consists of EBITDA adjusted for the impact of certain items that we do not consider indicative of our ongoing operating performance. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA do not represent, and should not be a substitute for, net income or cash flows from operations as determined in accordance with GAAP. Some of the limitations are: (i) EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA do not reflect our cash expenditures, or future requirements for capital expenditures or contractual commitments; (ii) EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA do not reflect changes in, or cash requirements for, our working capital needs; and (iii) EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA do not reflect the significant interest expense, or the cash requirements necessary to service interest or principal payments, on our debt. **Net Loan-to-Value**

Net loan-to-value represents gross debt less cash and short-term investments divided by the aggregate market value of the Company's fleet as of September 30, 2026, based on third-party vessel valuations provided by VesselsValue. Management uses net loan-to-value as a measure of financial leverage because vessel financing is generally secured by individual tanker assets and the secondhand tanker market provides transparent and highly liquid market valuations.

**Time Charter Equivalent (TCE) Revenues**

Consistent with general practice in the shipping industry, the Company uses TCE revenues, which represents shipping revenues less voyage expenses, as a measure to compare revenue generated from a voyage charter to revenue generated from a time charter. Time charter equivalent revenues, a non-GAAP measure, provides additional meaningful information in conjunction with shipping revenues, the most directly comparable GAAP measure, because it assists Company management in making decisions regarding the deployment and use of its vessels and in evaluating their financial performance.
