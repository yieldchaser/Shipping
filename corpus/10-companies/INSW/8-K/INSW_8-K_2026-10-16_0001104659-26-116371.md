Item 1.01 Entry into a Material Definitive Agreement.

*Exchange and Support Agreements*

On October 16, 2026, International Seaways, Inc., a corporation formed under the laws of the Republic of the Marshall Islands (“ Parent ”), and International Seaways Operating Corporation Ltd., an exempted company limited by shares incorporated under the laws of Bermuda and direct wholly owned subsidiary of Parent, entered into Exchange and Support Agreements (each, an “ Exchange Agreement ”) with certain shareholders of Bruton Limited, an exempted company limited by shares incorporated under the laws of Bermuda (“ Bruton ” and, such exchanging Bruton shareholders, the “ Exchanging Holders ”), collectively holding Norwegian depositary receipts (“ NDRs ”) representing approximately 50.5% of Bruton’s outstanding common shares, \$0.10 par value per share (the “ Bruton Common Shares ”). Pursuant to the Exchange Agreements, each Exchanging Holder has agreed, among other things, to exchange each of the Bruton Common Shares underlying its NDRs for 0.05190 shares of Parent common stock, no par value per share (the “ Parent Common Stock ,” and such exchange ratio the “ Per Share Stock Consideration ”) in a private transaction (collectively, the “ Exchanges ”), to attend a shareholder meeting of Bruton pertaining to the Merger (defined and described below) and to vote the Bruton Common Shares subject to its Exchange Agreement in favor of the Merger.

The completion of the Exchanges is not subject to any conditions to closing, other than execution and public announcement of the Transaction Agreement (defined and described below), which have been satisfied. The Exchanges are expected to close on or about October 20, 2026.

*Transaction Agreement*

Also on October 16, 2026, Parent, Bravo Ltd., an exempted company limited by shares incorporated under the laws of Bermuda and indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (“ Merger Sub ”), and Bruton entered into a Transaction Agreement (the “ Transaction Agreement ”), pursuant to which, upon the consummation of the transactions contemplated therein, Merger Sub will merge with and into Bruton, with Bruton surviving the merger as an indirect wholly owned subsidiary of Parent (the “ Merger ”).

The Transaction Agreement provides, among other things, that (i) each outstanding Bruton Common Share (other than certain excluded shares) held by a holder of more than 10,000 Bruton Common Shares will be converted into the right to receive the Per Share Stock Consideration, and cash in lieu of any fractional shares of Parent Common Stock, and (ii) each outstanding Bruton Common Share (other than certain excluded shares) held by a holder of 10,000 or fewer Bruton Common Shares will receive cash consideration of \$6.3044 per share.

The consummation of the Merger is subject to customary closing conditions, including approval of the Merger by Bruton’s shareholders, the absence of any applicable law, order, injunction, decree or other requirement preventing the consummation of the Merger and approval for listing of the shares of Parent Common Stock to be issued in the Merger on the New York Stock Exchange, subject to the completion of the Merger and official notice of issuance. The Transaction Agreement contains customary representations and warranties for stock-for-stock transactions, covenants governing business operations pending the completion of the Merger, non-solicitation obligations and other customary covenants, as well as customary termination rights, including if the Merger has not been consummated on or before November 15, 2026 or upon an uncured material breach by the other party.

Bruton is expected to hold its special general meeting of shareholders to vote on the proposed Merger on or about October 30, 2026. Subject to the satisfaction of customary conditions to closing, the Merger is expected to close promptly following the Bruton special general meeting of shareholders.

*Statutory Merger Agreement*

As contemplated by the Transaction Agreement, on October 16, 2026, the parties also entered into a Statutory Merger Agreement under Bermuda law (the “ Statutory Merger Agreement ”) to effect the Merger under Bermuda law.

The foregoing description of the Exchange Agreements, the Transaction Agreement and the Statutory Merger Agreement does not purport to be complete and is qualified in its entirety by reference to the full text of such documents, copies or a form of which are filed as Exhibits 2.1, 2.2 and 2.3, respectively, to this Current Report on Form 8-K and incorporated herein by reference.

**International Seaways Expands VLCC Fleet Through Strategic Combination with Bruton Limited.**

*Agrees to Acquire 50.5% Interest Directly from Bruton Shareholders*

*Executes Transaction Agreement to Acquire Remaining Shares of Bruton Limited by Merger*

*Announces Preliminary Third Quarter 2026 Results*

NEW YORK--(BUSINESS WIRE)-- International Seaways, Inc. (NYSE: INSW) (the “Company,” “Seaways,” or “INSW”), one of the largest tanker companies worldwide providing energy transportation services for crude oil and petroleum products, today announced the execution of agreements to acquire Bruton Limited (OSL: BRUT) (“Bruton”), in a series of transactions that values Bruton at approximately \$370 million.

The transaction combines Bruton’s 4 VLCC vessels, including newbuildings, with Seaways diversified fleet of approximately 70 vessels across both the crude and product segments.

Seaways has entered into exchange agreements with Bruton shareholders holding approximately 50.5% of Bruton’s outstanding common shares, to immediately acquire those shares in exchange for 0.0519 INSW shares for each Bruton share held (the “First-Step Exchange”). The First-Step Exchange transactions are expected to settle on or about October 20, 2026.

Seaways has also entered into a definitive transaction agreement with Bruton, pursuant to which Company will acquire all of the Bruton shares that remain outstanding following the completion of the First-Step Exchange in a cash and stock merger (the “Merger” and, together with the First-Step Exchange, the “Transactions”). Seaways expects to issue approximately 3.12 million INSW shares in the Transactions, representing approximately 96% of the aggregate consideration payable in the Transactions, with the remainder settled in cash.

Bruton will hold a special meeting of shareholders on or about October 30, 2026, to vote on the proposed Merger. The Bruton shareholders party to the First-Step Exchange have agreed to vote their shares in favor of the Merger. The Merger is expected to close promptly following the Bruton special meeting of shareholders, subject to the satisfaction of other customary closing conditions. Additional details with respect to the structure of the Transactions will be included in the Company’s current report on Form 8-K, expected to be filed today with the SEC.

Lois K. Zabrocky, International Seaways President and CEO commented, “We're excited about the opportunity to expand and modernize our fleet through this accretive transaction with Bruton. The transaction underscores our disciplined approach to growth. Following completion of the transaction, Seaways will remain well-positioned to continue executing on our growth strategy, generating attractive returns and creating long-term value for our shareholders.”

**Preliminary Third Quarter 2026 Results**

The Company also today announced selected preliminary financial results for the third quarter of 2026.

Based on information currently available, the Company expects:

· Shipping Revenues and Other Operating Revenues of approximately \$480-\$490 million

· Net income of approximately \$340-\$350 million, which includes a gain on the sale of a 2012-built Suezmax of approximately \$50 million

· Total Liquidity was approximately \$965 million as of September 30, 2026, including cash (and short-term investments) of \$455 million and \$510 million of undrawn revolving credit capacity.

Results for the third quarter were driven primarily by strong market conditions that resulted in blended spot earnings of approximately \$68,000 per day compared to \$79,000 per day in the second quarter of 2026 and \$29,000 per day in the third quarter of 2025. During the first three weeks of the fourth quarter, the Company has fixed blended spot earnings of approximately \$180,000 per day on about 37% of expected fourth quarter revenue days. Blended spot earnings exclude revenues associated with profit-share components of the Company’s time charters.

These preliminary results are based on management's initial review of operations for the quarter and are subject to completion of the Company's financial closing procedures. Actual results may differ from these preliminary estimates and will be reported in the Company's full earnings release on November 5, 2026.
