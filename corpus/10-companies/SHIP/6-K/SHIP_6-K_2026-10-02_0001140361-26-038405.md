TO THE SHAREHOLDERS OF SEANERGY MARITIME HOLDINGS CORP.

We are an international shipping company specializing in the worldwide seaborne transportation of dry bulk commodities, primarily iron ore, bauxite and coal. Our fleet consists of seventeen modern-design Capesize vessels and two Newcastlemax vessels with a total transportation capacity of 3.46 million deadweight tons. Upon our upcoming newbuilding deliveries, acquisitions and sale, our fleet is expected to grow to 26 vessels and approximately 4.8 million deadweight tons. We are a prominent pure-play Capesize shipping company listed in the U.S. capital markets.

Since our 2025 Annual General Meeting, we have continued to capitalize on the strong fundamentals of the Capesize market, delivering robust financial and operating performance. During the first half of 2026, we achieved record financial results, with net revenues of \$97.8 million and net income \$35.9 million, while maintaining our consistent capital return policy by extending our dividend track record to 19 consecutive quarters. We also expanded our fleet renewal program to eight modern vessels, representing an aggregate investment of approximately \$591 million, and opened a new source of long-term capital through our debut €100 million corporate bond on Euronext Athens. Together, these steps are reshaping Seanergy into a larger, younger and more efficient pure-play Capesize company, built to create value for shareholders through the cycle. They reflect our continued focus on fleet modernization and disciplined capital allocation.
