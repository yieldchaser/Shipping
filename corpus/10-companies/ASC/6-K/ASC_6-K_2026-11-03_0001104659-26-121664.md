Ardmore Shipping Corporation Announces Financial Results For The Three and Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026

HAMILTON, Bermuda, November 3, 2026 - Ardmore Shipping Corporation (NYSE: ASC) (“Ardmore”, the “Company” or “we”) today announced results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

Highlights and Recent Activity

● Reported net income attributable to common stockholders of \$16.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, or \$0.40 earnings per basic and diluted share, compared to net income attributable to common stockholders of \$12.1 million, or \$0.30 earnings per basic and diluted share, for the three months ended September 30, 2025. We reported Adjusted earnings of \$16.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, or \$0.40 Adjusted earnings per basic and diluted share, compared to Adjusted earnings of \$12.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025, or \$0.31 Adjusted earnings per basic and diluted share. (See reconciliation of net income to Adjusted earnings in the Non-GAAP Measures section).

● Reported net income attributable to common stockholders of \$100.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, or \$2.46 earnings per basic share and \$2.45 earnings per diluted share, compared to net income attributable to common stockholders of \$26.7 million, or \$0.66 earnings per basic and diluted share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. We reported Adjusted earnings of \$88.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, or \$2.16 Adjusted earnings per basic share, and \$2.15 per diluted share, compared to Adjusted earnings of \$27.2 million, or \$0.67 Adjusted earnings per basic and diluted share for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. (See reconciliation of net income to Adjusted earnings in the Non-GAAP Measures section with the main driver of the variance being the \$12.2 million gain on the sale of the Ardmore Engineer in June 2026).

● MR tankers earned an average spot TCE rate of \$27,031 per day for the three months ended September 30, 2026. Chemical tankers earned an average spot TCE rate of \$23,266 per day for the three months ended September 30, 2026. Based on approximately 45% of total revenue days currently fixed for the fourth quarter of 2026, the average spot TCE rate is approximately \$57,600 per day for MR tankers; based on approximately 60% of total revenue days currently fixed for the fourth quarter of 2026, the average spot TCE rate for chemical tankers is approximately \$30,100 per day.

● Consistent with the Company’s variable dividend policy of paying out dividends on its shares of common stock equal to two-thirds of Adjusted earnings, the Board of Directors declared a cash dividend on November 3, 2026, of \$0.27 per common share for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. The dividend will be paid on December 15, 2026, to all shareholders of record on November 27, 2026.

● During the third quarter of 2026, the Company agreed to the opportunistic sale of the Ardmore Endeavour (2013-built, STX, no scrubber) for \$34.3 million. The vessel was delivered to the buyer in October 2026.

● As previously announced, the Company exercised its options for a further two 40,500 dwt Handysize tankers to be built at Wuhu Shipyard. This expands the original order to six vessels in total on similar terms, and the Company has no further newbuilding options outstanding. The deliveries of these six vessels are scheduled from late 2028 and onwards.

Gernot Ruppelt, the Company’s Chief Executive Officer, commented:

*“ Already-strong market momentum has accelerated into the fourth quarter, with MR spot bookings at a large multiple of our cash breakeven. Against a backdrop of positive long-term demand fundamentals and an aging fleet, tankers continue to play a vital role in bridging global energy markets at a time when geopolitical disruption is driving scarcity premiums for key commodities.*

*We continue to execute our capital allocation policy in a disciplined manner; returning capital to shareholders equal to two-thirds of Adjusted earnings while simultaneously investing in value-driven fleet renewal and growth. Our decisions remain grounded in our long-term strategy and our strong governance .”*

**Geopolitical Conflicts**

Geopolitical tensions cause volatility in the market. The ongoing conflicts in the Middle East have significantly disrupted shipping transits via the Strait of Hormuz, a major oil and gas trade route. This increases security concerns and uncertainty. In addition, the conflict in Ukraine has significantly increased tanker demand and rates by reordering global oil trading patterns. Changes in or resolution of these conflicts may lead to a reversal of these trading patterns or other effects that could significantly decrease tanker demand, rates and values.

Since mid-December 2023, Houthi rebels in Yemen have carried out numerous attacks on vessels in the Red Sea. As a result of these attacks, many shipping companies have routed their vessels away from transiting the Red Sea, which has affected trading patterns, rates, and expenses. Following a pause in late 2025, Houthi attacks on commercial vessels in the Red Sea resumed in 2026 and maritime security concerns in the region remain elevated.

Further escalation or expansion of international hostilities could continue to affect the price of crude oil and the oil industry, the tanker industry, demand for our services and our business, results of operations, financial condition, and cash flows.

**Geopolitical and Economic Uncertainty**

Governments continue to take actions to implement new or increased tariffs on foreign imports and port fees. These activities have resulted in tariffs being levied on various goods and commodities, which may trigger an escalation of trade wars. These actions have been disruptive to global markets, resulting in significant volatility in stock and commodity prices and an increase in general global economic uncertainty, including the risk of economic recessions. As a result of the rapidly changing and unpredictable geopolitical climate, the shipping industry is experiencing uncertainty as to future vessel demand, trade routes, rates and operating costs.

Management’s Discussion and Analysis of Financial Results for the Three Months Ended September 30, 2026 and 2025

***Revenue.*** Revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was \$87.7 million, an increase of \$6.5 million from \$81.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The Company’s average number of operating vessels was 24.6 for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to 26.8 for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

The Company had 1,715 spot revenue days for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to 1,938 for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The Company had 19 vessels employed directly in the spot market as of September 30, 2026, compared to 22 vessels as of September 30, 2025. In addition, the Company had four product tankers and one chemical tanker employed under time charters as of September 30, 2026, consistent with four product tankers and one chemical tanker as of September 30, 2025. Revenue days derived from time charters increased to 502 for the three months ended September 30, 2026, from 378 for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

The increase in revenue during the three months ended September 30, 2026 was primarily driven by higher spot charter rates, which increased revenue by \$11.1 million. This was partially offset by a reduction in spot revenue days, which decreased revenue by \$8.4 million. Increased employment of vessels under time charters resulted in a further \$3.8 million increase in revenue. Together, these factors resulted in a net increase in revenue of \$6.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026.

***Voyage Expenses.*** Voyage expenses were \$34.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of \$5.5 million from \$28.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase was primarily driven by higher bunker prices, partially offset by a decrease in spot revenue days.

***TCE Rate.*** The average TCE rate for the Company’s fleet was \$25,358 per day for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of \$1,883 per day from \$23,475 per day for the three months ended September 30, 2025. TCE rates represent net revenues (a non-GAAP measure representing revenue less voyage expenses) divided by revenue days. Net revenue utilized to calculate TCE is determined on a discharge-to-discharge basis, which is different from how the Company records revenue under U.S. GAAP.

***Vessel Operating Expenses.*** Vessel operating expenses were \$18.5 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of \$2.1 million from \$16.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase is primarily due to a full quarter of expenses for the three owned vessels added to the fleet late in the third quarter of 2025. Vessel operating expenses can be prone to fluctuations between periods.

***Charter Hire Costs.*** Total charter hire expenses were \$1.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, a decrease of \$3.3 million from \$4.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. This reduction is a result of having one chartered-in vessel for the majority of the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to four for the majority of the three months ended September 30, 2025. Total charter hire expenses in the third quarter of 2026 were comprised of an operating expense component of \$0.7 million and a vessel lease expense component of \$0.6 million (September 30, 2025: \$2.4 million and \$2.2 million, respectively).

***Depreciation.*** Depreciation expense for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was \$9.2 million, an increase of \$0.4 million from \$8.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. This increase is primarily due to a full quarter of depreciation expense for the three owned vessels added to the fleet late in the third quarter of 2025. This is partially offset by the sale of the *Ardmore Engineer* , classified as held for sale in March 2026 and sold in June 2026, and the *Ardmore Endeavour* , classified as held for sale in September 2026.

***Amortization of Deferred Drydock Expenditures.*** Amortization of deferred drydock expenditures for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was \$1.7 million, generally consistent with \$1.6 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The deferred costs of drydockings for a given vessel are amortized on a straight-line basis to the next scheduled drydocking of the vessel.

***General and Administrative Expenses: Corporate*** general and administrative expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were \$4.6 million, a decrease of \$0.7 million from \$5.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. This is driven by lower legal fees (related to last year’s debt refinancings) in the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025.

***General and Administrative Expenses: Commercial and Chartering*** expenses are the expenses attributable to Ardmore’s chartering and commercial operations departments in connection with its spot trading activities. Commercial and chartering expenses for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were \$1.1 million, consistent with \$1.1 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

***Interest Expense and Finance Costs.*** Interest expense and finance costs for the three months ended September 30, 2026 were \$1.0 million, a decrease of \$0.7 million from \$1.7 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease was primarily due to a lower average balance drawn down across the quarter on the Company’s revolving credit facilities. Included in the amount for the three months ended September 30, 2026 is amortization of deferred finance fees of \$0.2 million, consistent with \$0.2 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

***Loss on* *Extinguishment of Debt.*** The Company did not record any loss on extinguishment of debt during the three months ended September 30, 2026, as compared to a loss on extinguishment of debt of \$0.5 million recorded during the three months ended September 30, 2025. This was related to the partial write-off of deferred finance fees associated with the Company’s previous revolving credit facility.

About Ardmore Shipping Corporation

Ardmore delivers energy, mobility, and essential commodities, supporting global trade through the seaborne transportation of refined petroleum products, chemicals and other liquid goods. Operating as a fully integrated shipping company, all core commercial, technical, operational, and corporate functions are conducted within the Ardmore company structure. Through its global platform, Ardmore maintains direct control over asset management, operations, and commercial execution, promoting consistent standards, efficiency, and accountability across the fleet.

Ardmore’s core strategy is centered on the continued development and operation of a modern, high-quality fleet of product and chemical tankers, while continually evolving and innovating across the business to position the Company optimally for the future, leveraging its fully integrated model to build long-term customer relationships and maintain a sharp focus on cost, safety, and performance optimization.

Ardmore provides its services through voyage and time-charter arrangements, delivering reliable and efficient transportation services to its first-class customer base — all guided and coordinated by our team members at sea and ashore.

(2) Time Charter Equivalent (“TCE”) rate, a non-GAAP measure, represents net revenues (a non-GAAP measure representing revenues less voyage expenses) divided by revenue days. Revenue days are the total number of calendar days the vessels are in the Company’s possession less off-hire days generally associated with drydocking or repairs and idle days associated with repositioning of vessels held for sale. Net revenue utilized to calculate the TCE rate is determined on a discharge-to-discharge basis, which is different from how the Company records revenue under U.S. GAAP. Under discharge to discharge, revenue is recognized beginning from the discharge of cargo from the prior voyage to the anticipated discharge of cargo in the current voyage, and voyage expenses are recognized as incurred.

***EBITDA + vessel lease expense component (i.e., EBITDAR) and Adjusted EBITDAR***

EBITDAR is defined as EBITDA ( *i.e.,* earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization) plus the vessel lease expense component of total charter hire expense for chartered-in vessels. Adjusted EBITDAR is defined as EBITDAR before certain items that Ardmore believes are not representative of its operating performance, including gain or loss on sale of vessels.

For the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company recognized total charter hire expense of \$1.3 million in respect of time charter-in vessels under operating leases. The total expense includes (i) \$0.6 million in respect of the right to use the leased assets (i.e., vessel lease expense component), and (ii) \$0.7 million in respect of the costs of operating the vessels (i.e., operating expense component). Under U.S. GAAP, the expense related to the right to use the leased assets (i.e., capital component) is treated as an operating item on the Company’s consolidated statement of operations, and is not added back in its calculation of EBITDA. The treatment of operating lease expenses differs under U.S. GAAP as compared to international financial reporting standards (“IFRS”). Under IFRS, the expense of an operating lease is presented in depreciation and interest expense.

Many companies in Ardmore’s industry report under IFRS; the Company therefore uses EBITDAR and Adjusted EBITDAR as tools to compare its valuation with the valuation of these other companies in its industry. The Company does not use EBITDAR and Adjusted EBITDAR as measures of performance or liquidity. EBITDAR and Adjusted EBITDAR, as presented, may not be directly comparable to similarly titled measures presented by other companies. In addition, EBITDAR and Adjusted EBITDAR should not be viewed as measures of overall performance since they exclude vessel rent, which is a normal, recurring cash operating expense related to the Company’s in-chartering of vessels that is necessary to operate its business. ***EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted Earnings and Adjusted Earnings (for purposes of dividend calculation)***

EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted earnings are not measures prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP and are defined and reconciled below. EBITDA is defined as earnings before interest, taxes, depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA is defined as EBITDA before certain items that Ardmore believes are not representative of its operating performance, including gain or loss on sale of vessels, gain or loss on extinguishment, unrealized gains/(losses) on derivatives and profit/(loss) on equity method investments. Adjusted earnings excludes certain items from net income attributable to common stockholders, including gain or loss on sale of vessels and write-off of deferred finance fees (i.e., loss on extinguishment) because they are considered to not be representative of the Company’s operating performance.

EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted earnings are presented in this press release as the Company believes that they provide investors with a means of evaluating and understanding how Ardmore’s management evaluates operating performance. EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA increase the comparability of the Company’s fundamental performance from period to period. This increased comparability is achieved by excluding the potentially disparate effects between periods of interest expense, taxes, depreciation or amortization, which items are affected by various and possibly changing financing methods, capital structure and historical cost basis and which items may significantly affect net income between periods. The Company believes that including EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and Adjusted earnings as financial and operating measures assists investors in making investment decisions regarding the Company and its common stock.

For purposes solely of the quarterly common dividend calculation, Adjusted earnings represents the Company’s Adjusted earnings for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, but excluding the impact of unrealized gains / (losses) and certain non-recurring items.

These non-GAAP measures should not be considered in isolation from, as substitutes for, or superior to, financial measures prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP. In addition, these non-GAAP measures may not have a standardized meaning and therefore may not be comparable to similar measures presented by other companies.
