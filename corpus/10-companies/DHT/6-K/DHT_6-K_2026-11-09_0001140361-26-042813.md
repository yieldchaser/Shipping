The third quarter of 2026 was another exceptionally strong quarter for the Company, with profit after tax amounting to \$270.9 million, reflecting continued robust tanker market conditions and strong commercial performance. Profit after tax for the first nine months of 2026 reached \$633.7 million, further extending the Company’s record earnings and underscoring the strength of the market environment and the Company’s operating platform.

QUARTERLY HIGHLIGHTS:

• In the third quarter of 2026, the Company achieved average combined time charter equivalent earnings of \$141,300 per day, comprised of \$167,100 per day for the Company’s VLCCs operating in the spot market and \$114,200 per day for the Company’s VLCCs on time charter.

• Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was \$269.5 million. Net profit for the quarter was \$270.9 million, equating to \$1.68 per basic share. After adjusting for the \$34.2 million gain on sale of DHT Bauhinia and the non-cash fair value gain related to interest rate derivatives of \$2.2 million, the Company had ordinary net income for the quarter of \$234.5 million, equating to \$1.45 per basic share.

• In the third quarter, the Company entered into 3-year time charter agreements for the VLCCs DHT Jaguar, built in 2015, and DHT Panther, built in 2016. The vessels were fixed at rates of \$75,000 per day and \$100,000 per day, respectively, under contracts concluded with global energy companies. The charter for DHT Jaguar commenced in September 2026, while the charter for DHT Panther commenced in October 2026.

• On July 20, 2026, DHT Bauhinia, built in 2007, was delivered to its new owner. The vessel was debt free, and the transaction generated net cash proceeds of \$51.0 million. In connection with the sale, the Company recognized a gain of \$34.2 million in the third quarter of 2026.

• On July 24, 2026, the Company took delivery of DHT Impala, a VLCC newbuilding from Hyundai Samho Heavy Industries. The vessel entered into the spot market and represented the fourth and final vessel in a series of newbuildings delivered in 2026.

• For the third quarter of 2026, the Company declared a cash dividend of \$1.45 per share of outstanding common stock, payable on November 30, 2026, to shareholders of record as of November 23, 2026. This marks the 67 th consecutive quarterly cash dividend and is in line with the Company’s capital allocation policy to pay out 100% of ordinary net income. The shares will trade ex-dividend from November 23, 2026.

■ The freight market remains strong due to ongoing logistical disruptions resulting in operational inefficiencies, in combination with shifting crude procurement patterns expanding transportation distances.

■ Geopolitical risks persist because recent events demonstrated for the first time in history that a full or partial closure of the Strait of Hormuz is possible, altering long-term risk perceptions even if regular traffic resumes.

■ Supply chain resilience is a priority for Middle Eastern oil producers, but expanding their logistical capabilities will not automatically restore the tanker fleet's historical productivity levels.

■ Energy security concerns should drive a major inventory restocking cycle by governments and corporations, which is expected to boost transportation demand above actual consumption levels.

■ Private consolidation is tightening market dynamics following a structural fleet consolidation by a private aggregator earlier in the year, resulting in fragmented spot supply and restricted information flows.

■ Limited effective fleet supply growth due to an aging fleet, a growing share of vessels operating outside mainstream trades, and a limited near-term newbuilding delivery schedule.

■ News flows and US midterm election results move commodity and capital markets, and fuels volatility, including significant deltas between physical and derivative markets for freight as well as creating large bid-ask spreads in term contracts.

THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIALS

The Company reported shipping revenues for the third quarter of 2026 of \$332.1 million compared to shipping revenues of \$107.2 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase from the 2025 period to the 2026 period includes \$219.3 million attributable to higher revenue per day and \$5.7 million attributable to an increase in total revenue days.

Other revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were \$0.3 million compared to \$0.2 million in the third quarter of 2025 and relate to technical management services provided. The decrease is due to a reduction in the fleet size for which the Company provides third-party technical management services.

Other income for the third quarter of 2026 was \$2.1 million, compared to \$1.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. The income related to the distribution of equity received from The Norwegian Shipowner’s Mutual War Risk Insurance Association.

The Company recorded a gain of \$34.2 million in the third quarter of 2026 related to the sale of DHT Bauhinia, compared to a gain of \$15.7 million in the third quarter of 2025 related to the sale of DHT Peony.

Voyage expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were \$41.8 million, compared to \$28.0 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was primarily due to an increase in bunker expenses of \$7.2 million, an increase in port expenses of \$3.5 million, an increase in broker commissions of \$2.5 million and an increase in other voyage-related costs of \$0.5 million. Voyage expenses generally vary depending on trading patterns during a quarter.

Vessel operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were \$19.0 million, compared to \$18.4 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was mainly related to a higher number of operating days resulting from an increase in the average number of vessels in the fleet.

Depreciation and amortization, including depreciation of capitalized survey expenses, was \$28.8 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to \$26.0 million in the third quarter of 2025. The change was mainly due to higher vessel depreciation of \$4.6 million following the delivery of four newbuildings during 2026, partially offset by a \$1.9 million decrease in depreciation due to retrofitted exhaust gas cleaning systems becoming fully depreciated and lower depreciation resulting from the sale of vessels.

General and administrative (“G&A”) expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were \$4.3 million, consisting of \$3.4 million cash and \$0.9 million non-cash charges, compared to \$4.1 million in the third quarter of 2025, consisting of \$3.4 million cash and \$0.7 million non-cash charges. Non-cash G&A expense includes accruals for social security taxes.

Net financial expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were \$3.8 million compared to \$2.5 million in the third quarter of 2025. The increase was mainly due to \$4.1 million higher interest expenses resulting from increased debt following the delivery of four newbuildings, partially offset by a non-cash gain of \$2.2 million related to interest rate derivatives, compared to a non-cash loss of \$0.4 million in the corresponding period of 2025.

As a result of the foregoing, the Company had a net profit in the third quarter of 2026 of \$270.9 million, or earnings of \$1.68 per basic share and \$1.68 per diluted share, compared to a net profit in the third quarter of 2025 of \$44.8 million, or earnings of \$0.28 per basic share and \$0.28 per diluted share. The increase from the third quarter of 2025 to the third quarter of 2026 was mainly due to a \$227.4 million increase in operating income, reflecting stronger operating performance during the period, partially offset by a \$1.3 million increase in net financial expenses.

Net cash provided by operating activities for the third quarter of 2026 was \$171.4 million compared to \$60.9 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase was due to a net profit of \$270.9 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to a net profit of \$44.8 million in the third quarter of 2025, partially offset by a \$99.9 million change in operating assets and liabilities and a \$15.7 million decrease in non-cash items included in net income.

Net cash used in investing activities was \$44.7 million in the third quarter of 2026, comprised of \$91.1 million related to investment in vessels under construction and \$4.6 million related to investment in vessels, partially offset by \$50.9 million related to proceeds from the sale of DHT Bauhinia. Net cash provided by investing activities was \$11.5 million in the third quarter of 2025 and was mainly related to proceeds from the sale of DHT Peony of \$50.1 million, partially offset by \$26.2 million related to investment in vessels under construction and \$12.3 million related to investment in vessels.

Net cash used in financing activities for the third quarter of 2026 was \$133.6 million, comprised of \$196.7 million related to cash dividend paid and \$11.8 million related to scheduled repayment of long-term debt, partially offset by \$75.2 million related to issuance of long-term debt. Net cash used in financing activities for the third quarter of 2025 was \$73.8 million, comprised of \$38.6 million related to cash dividend paid, \$22.1 million related to prepayment of long-term debt, and \$12.7 million related to scheduled repayment of long-term debt.

As of September 30, 2026, the cash balance was \$154.7 million, compared to \$79.0 million as of December 31, 2025.

The Company monitors its covenant compliance on an ongoing basis. As of September 30, 2026, the Company was in compliance with its financial covenants.

As of September 30, 2026, the Company had 161,235,573 shares of common stock outstanding compared to 160,799,407 shares as of December 31, 2025 .

The Company declared a cash dividend of \$1.45 per common share for the third quarter of 2026 payable on November 30, 2026, for shareholders of record as of November 23, 2026.

As a result of the foregoing, the Company had net profit for the first three quarters of 2026 of \$633.7 million, or earnings of \$3.93 per basic share and \$3.93 per diluted share, compared to net profit of \$144.9 million, or earnings of \$0.90 per basic share and \$0.90 per diluted share in the first three quarters of 2025. The increase was primarily attributable to a \$489.1 million increase in operating income, reflecting stronger operating performance during the period. Operating income in the first three quarters of 2026 included a \$94.2 million gain on the sale of DHT China, DHT Europe and DHT Bauhinia, compared to a \$52.9 million gain on the sale of DHT Scandinavia, DHT Lotus and DHT Peony in the first three quarters of 2025.

RECONCILIATION OF NON-GAAP FINANCIAL MEASURES

The Company believes that these non-GAAP measures provide useful supplemental information for its investors and, when considered together with the Company’s IFRS financial measures and the reconciliation to the most directly comparable IFRS financial measure, provide a more complete understanding of the factors and trends affecting the Company’s operations. The Company does not regard these non-GAAP measures as a substitute for, or as superior to, the equivalent measures calculated and presented in accordance with IFRS. Additionally, these non-GAAP measures may not be comparable to other similarly titled measures used by other companies and should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of the Company’s operating results as reported under IFRS.

Fair value measurement is based on Level 2 in the fair value hierarchy as defined in IFRS 13 *Fair Value Measurement* . Future cash flows are estimated based on forward interest rates (from observable yield curves at the end of the reporting period) and contract interest rates, discounted at a rate that reflects the credit risk of various counterparties.

Note 5 – Vessels

A vessel’s recoverable amount is the higher of the vessel’s fair value less cost of disposal and its value in use. The carrying amounts of vessels held and used by us are reviewed for potential impairment whenever events or changes in circumstances indicate that the carrying amount of a particular vessel may not accurately reflect the recoverable amount of a particular vessel. Each of the Company’s vessels have been viewed as a separate CGU as the vessels have cash inflows that are largely independent of the cash inflows from other assets. In instances where a vessel is considered impaired, it is written down to its recoverable amount. For the quarter ending September 30, 2026, the Company performed an assessment using both internal and external sources of information and concluded that there were no indicators of impairment.
