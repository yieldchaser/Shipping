Vale pays semi-annual remuneration on its participating debentures Rio de Janeiro, September 22nd, 2026 – Vale S.A. (“Vale”) informs that it will pay the semi-annual remuneration on its participating debentures (“debentures”) on September 30th, 2026, in the gross amount of R$ 1.712064114 per debenture, totaling R$ 512,161,695.811 to holders of debentures with a position registered in custody with B3 S.A. - Brasil, Bolsa, Balcão (“B3”) and/or with Banco Bradesco S.A. (“Bradesco”), on the closing of September 29th, 2026. The financial settlement will be on October 1st, 2026, through B3 or Bradesco, according to the debentures’ custody agent. Withholding income tax will be charged on the amount paid to holders of the debentures, at the rate applicable to fixed income from financial investments. The tax rate will vary according to each investors’s individual situation, with exemption exclusively for those who can provide unequivocal, legal proof of their tax-exempt status.
