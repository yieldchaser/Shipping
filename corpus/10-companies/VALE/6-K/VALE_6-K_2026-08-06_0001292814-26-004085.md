Vale clarifies press reports Rio de Janeiro, August 5, 2026 – In attention to news reports published today regarding charges of mining royalties (“CFEM”, the Financial Compensation for Mineral Exploration) by the Brazilian National Mining Agency, Vale S.A. (“Vale” or the “Company”) clarifies that it regularly pays the CFEM, complying with both the applicable regulations and the constitutional limits in force. Vale believes that the aforementioned charges are unfounded and will respond in due course before the competent authorities. Further information on the matter under discussion is available in item 4.7.I.iii of the Company’s 2026 Reference Form and in note 27.b to the Company’s 2025 Consolidated Financial Statements.
