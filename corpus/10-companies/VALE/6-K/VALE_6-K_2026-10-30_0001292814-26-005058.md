This metric is disclosed consistently and in compliance with CVM Resolution 156. For the reconciliation of EBITDA Proforma to Net Income, please refer to the table above. •Proforma net income attributable to Vale's shareholders - a metric that provides a clearer view of earnings performance across periods.
