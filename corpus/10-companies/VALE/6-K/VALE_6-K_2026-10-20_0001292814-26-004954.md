**Vale files application for Panda Bond Program in China**

Rio de Janeiro, October 20, 2026 - Vale S.A. (“Vale” or “Company”) informs that Vale Overseas Limited, its wholly owned subsidiary, has filed an application with the National Association of Financial Market Institutional Investors (“NAFMII”) for the registration of a 2026-2028 Renminbi-denominated debt financing instruments program in an aggregate principal amount of up to RMB 10 billion (the “Panda Bond Program”). Any debt financing instruments issued under the Panda Bond Program (the "Panda Bonds") will be fully and unconditionally guaranteed by Vale.

The registration of the Panda Bond Program is subject to approval by NAFMII, and the filing of the application does not constitute an issuance of Panda Bonds under the Panda Bond Program. Any future issuance under the Panda Bond Program will be subject to, among other factors, prevailing market conditions.

This press release is provided solely for informational purposes and in accordance with applicable regulations, and does not constitute an offer to sell, or a solicitation of an offer to buy, any securities. Any offer and sale of the Panda Bonds is subject to market and other conditions and will be made solely in China’s Interbank Bond Market in accordance with applicable laws and regulations of the People's Republic of China. The Panda Bonds and the related unsecured guarantee by Vale have not been and will not be registered with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission under the U.S. Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”) or state securities laws, and may not be offered or sold in the United States absent registration or an applicable exemption from the registration requirements of the Securities Act and applicable state securities laws. The Panda Bonds and related guarantee will not be registered with the Brazilian Securities and Exchange Commission ( *Comissão de Valores Mobiliários – CVM* ) and may not be offered or sold in Brazil, except in circumstances that do not constitute a public offering or unauthorized distribution under Brazilian laws and regulations.

The Company will keep the market informed of any material developments in connection with the Panda Bond Program.
