Vale reports on the Vice Chairman of the Board of Directors and the Lead Independent Director Rio de Janeiro, July 30, 2026 – Vale S.A. (“Vale” or the “Company”) hereby informs that, on this date, the Company's Board of Directors approved the following changes to the Board composition, effective as of August 1, 2026, and until the end of the current term of office, pursuant to Article 11, Paragraphs 6 and 8 of Vale's Bylaws. &squarf; Mr. Reinaldo Duarte Castanheira Filho, an independent Board member, was elected by the Board to serve as Vice Chairman of the Board of Directors; &squarf; Mr. Wilfred Theodoor Bruijn, an independent Board member, was appointed by the independent members of the Board to serve as Lead Independent Director. Vale's Board of Directors reaffirms its commitment to the highest standards of corporate governance, transparency, and sustainable value creation for its shareholders and other stakeholders.
