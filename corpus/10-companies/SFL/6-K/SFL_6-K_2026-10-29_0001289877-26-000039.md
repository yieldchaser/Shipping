**EXHIBIT 1**

**SFL – Newbuild agreements and long-term charters of two 8,800 TEU Container Vessels and one Very Large Ammonia Carrier**

SFL Corporation Ltd. (NYSE: SFL) (“SFL” or the “Company”) today announced that it has agreed to acquire two 8,800 TEU Container Vessels with scheduled delivery in 2027 and one 93,000 cbm dual-fuel Very Large Ammonia Carrier (VLAC) with scheduled delivery in 2028. Aggregate acquisition cost is approximately \$365 million.

SFL has agreed minimum 8-year time charters to a leading liner company from delivery for the two container vessels. The charters may be extended up to another four years, and will have purchase options at the end of year 8, 10 and 12, including a profit share feature.

The VLAC has been chartered out on time charter basis for a minimum of 5 years from delivery to a global leader in the commodities industry, and may be extended up to another two years. In addition, the charterer will also have the option to develop a sale of the vessel during the last two years of the fixed period, subject to a profit share mechanism with SFL.

These charters will add a minimum of approximately \$333 million to our fixed-rate charter backlog, which may become higher if extension options are declared.

Ole B. Hjertaker, CEO of SFL Management AS, said in a comment: “We are pleased to continue building the asset portfolio, combining newbuilds with early delivery and charters to existing high-quality counterparties. With the acquisitions and new charters announced so far this year, we have added more than \$1.6 billion to our fixed rate charter backlog.”

**About SFL**

SFL has a unique track record in the maritime industry and has paid dividends every quarter since its initial listing on the New York Stock Exchange in 2004. The Company’s fleet of vessels is comprised of tanker vessels, bulkers, container vessels, car carriers and offshore drilling rigs. SFL’s long term distribution capacity is supported by a portfolio of long term charters and significant growth in the asset base over time. More information can be found on the Company's website: www.sflcorp.com.
