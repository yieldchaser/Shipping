On May 1, 2025, Star Bulk Carriers Corp. (the “Company”) mailed an amendment (the “Amendment”) to the Company’s Proxy Statement (the “Proxy Statement”) to reduce the quorum requirement for a stockholders’ meeting from a majority to one-third of the issued and outstanding shares entitled to vote at the meeting, which was approved by the Company’s Board of Directors on April 28, 2025.
