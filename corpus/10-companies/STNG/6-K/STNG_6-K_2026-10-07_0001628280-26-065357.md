**INFORMATION CONTAINED IN THIS FORM 6-K REPORT**

Effective as of October 7, 2026 (the “Effective Date”), Ms. Auste Adelborg resigned as secretary of Scorpio Tankers Inc. (the “Corporation”). Her resignation was not the result of any dispute with the Corporation.

Ms. Charlotte Wheatcroft has been appointed as secretary for the Corporation as of the Effective Date.

Ms. Wheatcroft is a Chartered Company Secretary and senior corporate governance executive.

Prior to joining Scorpio Tankers Inc. Ms. Wheatcroft served as Company Secretary and Head of HR of a London-based sovereign wealth investment group. She has extensive experience advising Boards, Committees and senior leadership teams across multinational and listed organizations. She began her career at international law firms.

Ms. Wheatcroft is a Fellow of the Chartered Governance Institute UK & Ireland and she holds an LLB (Hons) and an LLM from The University of Manchester.
