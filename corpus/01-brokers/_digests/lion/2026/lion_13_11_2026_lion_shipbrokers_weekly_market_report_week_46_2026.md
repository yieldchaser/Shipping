Market Commentary 
Sale & Purchase: The second-hand market remained active, with a further forty-four vessels reported sold 
across all sectors (20 bulkers, 15 tankers, 7 gas carriers and 2 containerships), and values held firm despite 
the sharp correction in tanker freight rates that followed the US mid-term elections. Buying interest remained 
strongest for geared tonnage and for Panamaxes from Far Eastern buyers, while appetite for Capesizes 
appeared slightly more subdued.  
In the bulker sector, 20 vessels changed hands: 2 Capesizes, 2 Post-Panamaxes, 2 Kamsarmaxes, 1 Panamax, 
1 Ultramax, 4 Supramaxes and 8 Handysizes. Clients of Seanergy Maritime of Greece paid region low USD 70 
mill for the 2019-built eco Capesize NIGHTSKY (183,017 dwt, Namura/Japan) from compatriots Alcyon 
Shipping, basis delivery in January 2027. Further down the age scale, the scrubber-fitted PAN ADVANCE 
(179,185 dwt, 2009 Hyundai/Korea) went to clients of Lila Global for region USD 35.4 - 35.8 mill, also basis 
delivery in January 2027, in line with the USD 35 - 36.5 mill paid last week for the two-year-older Setouchi-
max CAPE ALLIANCE (206,190 dwt, 2007 Imabari/Japan).  
In the Post-Panamax segment, the INDUS TRIUMPH (92,967 dwt, 2012 Taizhou Sanfu/China) also went to 
Greek buyers for region USD 16.5 - 16.6 mill, in line with the USD 16.5 mill paid for the one-year-older CARL 
OLDENDORFF (93,028 dwt, 2011 Taizhou CATIC/China) two weeks ago, while the 2010 Yangfan-built SHUN 
CHANG (93,322 dwt) achieved USD 15 mill.  
In the Kamsarmax segment, the 2019 Oshima-built TAHO AUSTRALIA (81,788 dwt), for which offers were 
invited on 6th November 2026, is rumoured to be trading at region USD 38 mill and the 2012 Jinhai-built 
ZHEN NING (79,463 dwt) achieved USD 20.39 mill, while Chinese buyers paid region USD 9 mill basis prompt 
delivery Turkey for the 25-year-old Japanese Panamax TARIEL (76,623 dwt, 2001 Imabari/Japan).  
In the geared sizes, the 2013 Shin Kasado-built Ultramax DIAMOND QUEEN (61,414 dwt) achieved region 
USD 24 mill, while the 25-year-old Supramax YIN HE HAI NAN DAO (48,983 dwt, 2001 IHI/Japan) was sold at 
online auction for USD 7.94 mill. In between, the VINCA (52,447 dwt, 2006 Tsuneishi Cebu/Philippines) 
fetched USD 12.5 mill basis delivery in January 2027, a firm level against the USD 9 mill paid last week for the 
three-year-older HV ALICE (52,425 dwt, 2003) from the same yard. Chinese-built Supramaxes also changed 
hands, with the TONY SMITH (56,498 dwt, 2011 Qingshan/China) fetching excess USD 15.5 mill and the older 
KANG CHENG (55,541 dwt, 2004 Nantong COSCO KHI/China) USD 11.55 mill.

On the tanker front, 15 sales were reported. Clients of ADNOC acquired two units from Trafigura: the 2017-
built VLCC OLYMPIC LADY (approx. 299,000 dwt, Hyundai Gunsan/Korea) for USD 185 mill and the Suezmax 
SPONDE (158,500 dwt, 2012 Samsung/Korea) for USD 110 mill, matching last week's sale of the same-age 
FLEVES (158,517 dwt, 2012 Hyundai/Korea). Trafigura is understood to have realised a gain of about USD 36 
mill on the SPONDE, having bought her for USD 74 mill in September. ADNOC was also behind the purchase 
of the non-eco VLCC EUROSPIRIT (320,105 dwt, 2011 Daewoo/Korea, SS/DD due 11/2026) for USD 153 mill, 
a vessel her sellers had acquired for just USD 48 mill in 2020.  
In the MR segment, four methanol-ready Qidong Qianyao resales (49,800 dwt, 2027 - 2028 delivery) were 
sold at USD 50 mill each, slightly below the USD 53 - 55 mill reported for the four Chengxi-built CS units last 
week. In the smaller sizes, the stainless steel TARUCA (19,983 dwt, 2005 Fukuoka/Japan) was sold for region 
mid-high USD 12 mill.  
The gas sector provided the headline deal of the week, with Mitsui OSK selling four ice-class 170,000 cbm 
LNG carriers, the NORTH VALLEY, NORTH LIGHT, NORTH OCEAN and NORTH MOON (2024 - 2025 
Daewoo/Korea), to Novatek Gas & Power for USD 897.5 mill en bloc, or about USD 224 mill each. Clients of 
Singapore-based Sinogas Maritime acquired the 155,000 cbm tri-fuel diesel-electric GASLOG SHANGHAI and 
GASLOG SALEM (2013 & 2015 Samsung/Korea), both damaged in separate drone attacks in the Middle East 
earlier this year, at online auction for region USD 100 mill each, and Chinese buyers paid USD 80 mill for the 
VLGC FRITZI N (80,676 cbm, 2009 Hyundai/Korea), just below the USD 81 mill paid last week for the one-
year-older GAS WARICH (82,026 cbm, 2008 DSME/Korea).  
For a detailed review and additional information regarding this week’s transactions, kindly refer to our 
“representative sales” section. 
Demolition: The recycling market remained starved of tonnage, with owners continuing to keep older vessels 
trading in the firm freight markets and most of the week's limited business involving small dry units and 
sanctioned tonnage. Bangladesh remained the strongest destination and firmed further, with buyers keen 
for candidates at around USD 540 - 550 per LT for bulkers and USD 560 per LT for tankers; the small 1991-
built cement carrier SHINAKI MARU (2,621 ldt) was sold there at USD 460 per LT. India firmed, with recyclers' 
sentiment positive despite softer local steel prices during the Diwali holidays, and levels improved to around 
USD 470 - 480 per LT for bulkers and USD 490 - 495 per LT for tankers. Pakistan remained subdued, with 
ample steel supply limiting recyclers' appetite, while Turkey held steady, with damaged candidates in the 
Black Sea difficult to tow to Aliaga.
