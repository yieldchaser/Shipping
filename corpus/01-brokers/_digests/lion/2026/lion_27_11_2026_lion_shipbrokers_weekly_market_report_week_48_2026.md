Market Commentary 
Sale & Purchase: The second-hand market stayed busy, with a further thirty-one vessels reported sold (20 
bulkers, 7 tankers and 4 containerships), on top of the deals covered in last week's report. 
Dry bulk again dominated, with buyers active across the size range from Capesize down to Handysize and 
values holding firm. Tanker activity was quieter, with four MR newbuilding resales and the MR pair IDI and 
KALINKA the main priced deals. 
In the bulker sector, 20 vessels changed hands: 3 Capesizes, 6 Post-Panamaxes, 2 Ultramaxes, 4 
Supramaxes and 5 Handysizes.  
Following last week's sale of the EVER ABILITY at USD 39.7 mill, Everest Shipping also sold her sister EVER 
BEAUTY (180,156 dwt, 2011 Qingdao Beihai/China, scrubber fitted) at high USD 39 mill to Chinese buyers, 
basis delivery January - April 2027, confirming the level for older Chinese-built Capesizes. The one-year-
younger ASL OTSL 2 (180,643 dwt, 2012 Tsuneishi Cebu/Philippines) is understood to have achieved USD 48 
mill, a premium of some USD 8 mill over the Qingdao Beihai-built sisters. Elsewhere in the segment, the 
Japanese-built FRIENDSHIP (abt 177,000 dwt, 2009 Namura/Japan) is understood to have been sold for 
USD 33 mill. 
The Post-Panamax segment was the busiest. Clients of Hayfin acquired the Oshima-built trio HARVEST 
TIME, HARVEST RAIN and HARVEST FROST (95,263 dwt each, 2014 - 2015 Oshima/Japan) en bloc for USD 
88 mill, or about USD 29.3 mill each. The Chinese-built NEW HALO (87,450 dwt, 2011 Hudong-
Zhonghua/China) fetched USD 16 mill, while the veteran CORONA JOYFUL (88,233 dwt, 2003 
Imabari/Japan) achieved a firm USD 11.8 mill. The MARINA (abt 87,000 dwt, 2006 IHI/Japan) is understood 
to have been sold for USD 14.9 mill. 
In the geared sizes, the modern Japanese Ultramax SIDER MADEIRA (64,061 dwt, 2020 Mitsui/Japan) 
commanded USD 37.4 mill, while Middle Eastern buyers paid USD 22.8 mill for the ISEACO GRACE (61,683 
dwt, 2011 Oshima/Japan, SS/DD due 10/2027). 
Among the Supramaxes, the SUPER RICE (abt 56,000 dwt, 2013 Jiangsu Hantong/China, scrubber ftd) is 
understood to be committed to Greek buyers at a price in the region of low-mid USD 19 mill, while the one-
year older JIN BI (56,361 dwt, 2012 Jiangsu New Hantong/China, SS/DD due 09/2027) achieved USD 17.8 
mill. The Indonesian flagged CHLOE (56,770 dwt, 2010 Jiangsu Hantong/China) got a discounted USD 12 
mill, most likely due to her Indonesian inter-island coal trade. The discount is evident when compared with

In the MR segment, UK buyers acquired four 50,000 dwt newbuilding resales at Qidong Qianyao (hulls 
YZJF2024-013 to -016, delivery November 2027 - November 2028) en bloc for USD 50 mill each. The MR 
pair IDI (abt 46,000 dwt, 2007 Hyundai Mipo/Korea) and KALINKA (abt 47,000 dwt, 2008 Onomichi/Japan) 
is understood to have been sold en bloc for USD 51 mill. 
In the container sector, the pair of 2,340 TEU newbuilding resales JIANGSU HUATAI HT-290 and HT-291 
(delivery April and July 2027) were resold for USD 36 mill each. In addition, the NORDMAAS (abt 1,800 TEU, 
2019 Jiangsu/China) is understood to have been sold for USD 35.4 mill and the veteran AS FREYA (abt 1,200 
TEU, 2004 Peene Werft/Germany) for USD 12 mill. 
For a detailed review and additional information regarding this week’s transactions, kindly refer to our 
“representative sales” section. 
Demolition: Recycling activity remained thin, with only one priced sale reported, as freight earnings stay 
attractive across all sectors and conventional candidates remain scarce.  
The only priced deal was the 1974-built mining vessel CORA (7,065 ldt), sold to India at USD 548 per LT, a 
level that reflects her specialised type rather than the general market.  
Price indications held broadly steady, but sentiment cooled. Bangladesh remains the best-paying 
destination, although political unrest, weaker steel plate prices and sluggish domestic demand have made 
recyclers more cautious.  
Indian recyclers eased their offers by USD 20 per LT on a slow domestic steel market, although the coming 
months are traditionally the busiest for Indian steel consumption.  
Pakistan stayed steady, with buying capacity limited as only eleven yards hold DASR approval.  
Turkey was unchanged, with production at a major Izmir steel mill still disrupted following last week's 
accident.
