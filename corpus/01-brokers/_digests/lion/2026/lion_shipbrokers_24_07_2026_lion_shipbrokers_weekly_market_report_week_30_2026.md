## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

The dry bulk sale and purchase market remained stable with asset values largely 
unaffected, despite a minor decline in the overall freight market. The Baltic Dry Index (BDI) fell to a 
multi-week low on Monday, but rebounded over the week and closed today at 2743 points, more or less 
the same level as last Friday (2752 points). Buyers are refusing to go on summer holidays, as 
demonstrated by another busy week with 16 transactions recorded, comprising 2 capes, 1 post-
Panamax, 1 kamsarmax, 1 panamax, 5 Ultramaxes, 3 Supramaxes & 3 Handysize bulk carriers.  
 
We have gathered from market insights that post-panamax PONT ROUGE (99,992 dwt blt 
2021/Tsuneishi Zhoushan P.R.C) is trading firm at region $ 36 million, while hearing scrubber fitted 
kamsarmax MONT FORT (82,113 dwt blt 2012/Tsuneishi Japan in under firm negos at region $ 22 
million. We also picked up that Greek controlled supramax CAPT EUGENE (55,499 dwt blt 
2010/Mitsui, Japan is negotiating firm close to region of $16.6 million.  
 
For a detailed review and additional information regarding these week's transactions, kindly refer to 
our "representative sales" section.

### Demolition

The Subcontinent markets remain stable. Pakistan and Bangladesh continue to 
demonstrate a strong and positive outlook, whereas India maintains stability but with a weaker forecast.
