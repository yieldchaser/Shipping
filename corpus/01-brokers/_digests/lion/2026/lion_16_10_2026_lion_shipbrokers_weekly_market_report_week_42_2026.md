MARKET COMMENTARY 
 
Bulkers: The charter market experienced a significant downturn this week as the Baltic Dry Index (BDI) 
declined for 4 th consecutive week closing today at 2,511 points, reflecting a slump of almost 14 % 
compared to last Friday's closing value of 2,917 points: 
 
Baltic Dry Index   2511 -34 
Baltic Capesize Index  3249 -73 
Baltic Panamax Index  2314 -42 
Baltic Supramax Index  1865 +9 
Baltic Handysize Index  1011 +4 
 
Although charter rates hit an over-one-month low, the bulker S+P market is particularly active with a 
total of 16 transactions recorded, comprising 2 panamax, 4 ultramaxes, 3 supramaxes, 1 handymax and 
6 handysize bulk carriers.  
 
For a detailed review and additional information regarding these week’s transactions, kindly refer to our 
“representative sales” section. 
 
Demolition: The Subcontinent markets remain stable this week, with minimal availability of new 
tonnage. Despite a decline in local steel prices, demo rates are largely unchanged. Indian market 
sentiment is positive, with a notable demand for specialized vessels, including stainless steel tankers and 
RoPax vessels. Cash buyers are willing to offer a significant premium for these types of ships. For an 
overview of demolition market rates, please check our comprehensive demometer below:
