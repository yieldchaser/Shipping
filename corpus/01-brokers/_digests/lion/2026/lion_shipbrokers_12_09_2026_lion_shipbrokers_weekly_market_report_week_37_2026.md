## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

The dry cargo market continues to demonstrate strength, maintaining firm levels. The Baltic 
Dry Index (BDI) reached a multi-year peak of 3,628 points last Friday, before slightly declining to 3,507 
points today: 

The S+P market remains very firm and active with 21 recorded transactions, comprising 2 kamsarmaxes, 
3 panamax, 3 ultramax, 4 Supramaxes & 9 Handysize bulk carriers. For a detailed review and additional 
information regarding these week's transactions, kindly refer to our "representative sales" section.
