Desk Talk 
 
In the aftermath of the US midterm elections, oil prices have softened and freight rates have started to 
ease, at least for the time being. Asset values, however, remain relatively firm, as shipowners, traders 
and charterers seek to assess the evolving market dynamics and anticipate what lies ahead, particularly 
in relation to the operational inefficiencies that have driven tanker freight rates to extraordinary levels 
since March. 
 
Even if an agreement is reached between the US and Iran, a swift return to normality in tanker 
operations is unlikely. Security concerns, insurance requirements and the gradual unwinding of shuttle 
tanker operations and ship-to-ship (STS) transfers will continue to disrupt established trading patterns and 
constrain operational efficiency. Consequently, the normalisation of tanker markets is likely to be a 
gradual process, rather than an immediate response to any political breakthrough. 
 
Meanwhile, in the dry bulk segment, Capesize earnings have strengthened further, reaching 
approximately USD 43,900/day and their highest levels in six weeks. Improved Atlantic activity, 
increasing Guinean bauxite exports and the faster-than-expected ramp-up of the Simandou project 
are providing additional support to the segment. With Simandou's first-phase capacity of 60 mtpa 
potentially being reached ahead of schedule, further increases in iron ore volumes could provide a 
positive demand driver for Capesize tonnage. Recent market momentum continues to underpin buyer 
appetite for geared tonnage aged around 10–15 years, spanning from Handysize upto Ultramax 
segments. Meanwhile, within the non-geared sector, we have observed increased interest in Post-
Panamax vessels. 
 
 
 
 
 
In the Ultramax space CARAVOS LIBERTY (ABT 63,301 DWT, 2013, DAYANG S.B., C:4X36T) has been 
committed at USD 24.5m. 
 
Meanwhile, in the Handysize sector, DANAE (ABT 40,015 DWT, 2022, SK KOCHI JYUKO, C:4X30T) was sold 
at USD 34.5m. 
 
 
 
 
Although we understand various negotiations to be ongoing this week, we cannot report any new 
tanker sales.
