Week 38 (11th Sep to 18th Sep 2026) 
 Capesize 
Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route eased slightly, with 
Dampier and Port Stanley departures to Qingdao settling between USD 
15.95/17.20 fio. In the Atlantic, long-haul business from Tubarao to Qingdao, 
including West Africa options, mainta ined a firm tone between USD 
41.90/43.00 fio, alongside a Seven Islands run reaching USD 46.65 fio.  
Kamsarmax / Panamax 
In the Pacific, NoPac  trips with China and Singapore -Japan options settled 
between USD 19,000/25,000 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, 
Japan, India and South East Asia ranges fetched between USD 19,250/26,000 
pd, alongside a South China trip via China to India fi xing at USD 22,750 pd. 
Indonesian coal runs to South China, Japan and India achieved between USD 
14,000/23,000 pd, while a fertilizer trip from North China to India reached 
USD 20,000 pd. From East Coast India and West Coast India, trips via ECSA 
to Singap ore-Japan range obtained between USD 18,750/25,000 pd, 
alongside an ECI coastal trip to WCI securing USD 32,500 pd. Out of South 
East Asia, a run via South Africa to Singapore-Japan made USD 24,000 pd. In 
the Atlantic, a Continent departure via NCSA to Chi na reached USD 34,750 
pd, while a West Africa trip via NCSA to Continent and Mediterranean range 
fetched USD 23,500 pd. On period, vessels secured between USD 
17,000/22,500 pd for durations ranging from 2 laden legs up to 14 months.  
Ultramax / Supramax 
In the Pacific, Indonesian coal trips to ECI and Thailand ranges settled 
between USD 22,000/23,500 pd, while steel and regional runs from North 
China to Singapore and ECI range obtained between USD 17,000/23,500 pd. 
Far East departures via PG to Mediterran ean fixed between USD 
22,500/25,000 pd, alongside a North China trip to USG securing USD 17,000 
pd. Out of West Coast India, trips to Far East and West Africa range settled 
between USD 12,500/18,500 pd, while an ECI run via Indonesia back to India 
achieved USD 13,500 pd. From South Africa, business was active with runs 
to WCI, India and Far East ranges agreed between USD 21,000/26,750 pd, 
accompanied by ballast bonuses between USD 210,000 and USD 270,000. 
On period, an Ultramax fixed for 6 to 8 months duration at USD 23,500 pd. 
Handymax / Handysize 
In the Atlantic, Baltic departures via ECSA options fixed at USD 22,000 pd, 
while a trip to Caribs range achieved USD 17,000 pd. Out of Continent, a 
steel run to US Gulf settled at USD 15,000 pd. From US Gulf and USEC, grain 
and wood pellet runs to NCSA an d Continent range reached USD 22,000 pd 
and USD 20,000 pd respectively, while a local US Gulf trip secured USD 
21,000 pd.
