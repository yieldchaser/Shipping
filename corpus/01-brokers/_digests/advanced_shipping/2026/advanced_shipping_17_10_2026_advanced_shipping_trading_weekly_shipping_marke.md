 Bulkers 
 
The Capesize “Golden Myrtalia” 178/2011 SWS, China (SS/DD 10/2026) was 
committed to Norwegian interests at USD 40 mill  basis SS/DD passed. For 
reference, the sister vessel “Pigassos” 176/2011 SWS, China (SS/DD 
07/2026) was sold back in May at USD 31,7 mill.

12/2027, Ice Class 1C) back in April at USD 16,5 mill, Harren Bulkers have 
now committed the sister “Pabal” 76/2012 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 10/2027, 
Ice Class 1C) at rgn USD 17 mill. 
 
On the Handysize sector, Japanese owners committed their “ Locomotion” 
38/2013 Shimanami, Japan (SS 12/2030, DD 12/2028) at USD 19,8 mill . 
Remind you that back in July, the “Sakura Dream” 38/2013 Imabari, Japan 
(SS 12/2030, DD 12/2028) was sold at USD 18,5 mill basis delivery Nov -
Dec’26. Moreover, ZBB Bulkers committed their “ ZBB Serenity ” 37/2010 
Huatai, China (SS 10/2030, DD 10/2028, Ice Class 1C) to Turkish compatriots 
at USD 12 mill . By way of comparison, the one -year-younger “CS Calla” 
37/2011 Tianjin, China (SS/DD 10/2026, Ice Class 1C) was sold in September 
at low-mid USD 11 mill. In addition, Carsten Rehder committed the “ Lima 
Strait” 37/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 07/2027) at USD 15,2 mill . 
Note that in June, the sister vessel “Maple Marina” 37/2012 Hyundai Mipo, 
Korea (SS/DD 10/2027) was sold at USD 14,3 mill.

WEEKLY SHIPPING MARKET REPORT - pg. 2 
 
Week 42 (9th Oct to 16th Oct 2026) 
 Capesize 
Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route softened further, with 
Dampier to Qingdao fixtures settling between USD 11.25/13.40 fio. In the 
Atlantic, long-haul business from Tubarao to Qingdao, including West Africa 
options, fixed between USD 30.00 /31.50 fio. Out of South Africa, Saldanha 
Bay departures to Qingdao achieved between USD 23.25/24.08 fio, while a 
TRMT run to Qingdao made USD 9.85 fio. 
Kamsarmax / Panamax 
In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore -Japan and West Coast India 
options settled between USD 20,000/23,500 pd. Australian rounds with 
redelivery in China, Japan, India and Singapore -Japan ranges fetched 
between USD 19,000/27,000 pd. Indonesian coal runs to South China, China 
and Japan ranges achieved between USD 16,000/23,500 pd. From West 
Coast India and East Coast India, trips via South Africa and ECSA to India and 
Singapore-Japan ranges fixed between USD 25,500/28,750 pd. In the 
Atlantic, a Continen t trip via USG back to Continent/Med range made USD 
21,500 pd, an East Med run via NCSA achieved USD 27,250 pd, and a 
Continent departure via NCSA to Singapore -Japan reached USD 32,000 pd. 
On period, fixtures for 2 laden legs up to 7 months duration were s ecured 
between USD 19,000/27,000 pd.  
Ultramax / Supramax 
In the Pacific, Indonesian coal runs to South East Asia and ECI settled 
between USD 23,000/24,000 pd and USD 15,250 pd respectively. NoPac trips 
from North China and Japan to South East Asia fetched USD 25,000 pd, while 
South China clinker runs to ECI achi eved strong levels between USD 
30,000/31,000 pd. A long -haul departure from North China to West Africa 
fixed at USD 24,000 pd. Out of the Persian Gulf, a limestone trip to India 
obtained USD 16,500 pd, while a South Africa coal run to ECI reached USD 
26,000 pd alongside a USD 260,000 ballast bonus. In the Mediterranean, salt 
to US Gulf and clinker to West Africa made USD 14,500 pd and USD 22,000 
pd respectively. In the Atlantic, a US Gulf front -haul petcoke trip to China 
secured USD 34,000 pd. 
Handymax / Handysize 
In the Pacific, regional departures from North China to South East Asia fixed 
at USD 19,000 pd, while a South East Asia coal run to Far East settled at USD 
12,750 pd. A trip from South East Asia via Australia to China made USD 
16,000 pd and a steel run fro m East Coast India to Continent obtained USD 
13,500 pd. In the Atlantic, a US Gulf grain run to WCCA achieved USD 17,500 
pd, an ECSA departure to Continent fixed at USD 15,000 pd alongside a USD 
150,000 ballast bonus and an East Med trip to NCSA reached USD 11,000 pd.
