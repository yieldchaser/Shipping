WEEKLY SHIPPING MARKET REPORT - pg. 2 
 
Week 47 (13th Nov to 20th Nov 2026) 
 Capesize 
Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route softened slightly, with 
Dampier and Port Hedland fixtures settling between USD 13.40/14.30 fio. In 
the Atlantic, long-haul business from Tubarao to Qingdao, including West 
Africa options, held steady levels between USD 35.00/35.50 fio, while a Port 
Cartier departure to Qingdao hit USD 48.50 fio. Transatlantic trade saw a 
Seven Islands run to Rotterdam fix at USD 15.50 fio. In regional trades, Teluk 
Rubiah to Qingdao achieved USD 11.20 fio, while coastal Indian business 
from Visakhapatnam to Hazira made USD 7.00 fio. 
Kamsarmax / Panamax 
In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore-Japan options settled between 
USD 19,000/21,000 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, Japan, 
India and Singapore-Japan ranges fetched between USD 21,500/26,000 pd. 
Indonesian coal runs to China, SE Asia and India ranges achieved between 
USD 13,000/20,000 pd, alongside coastal Indian iron ore trips from ECI to 
WCI fixing between USD 21,000/22,000 pd. From India, trips via ECSA to 
Singapore-Japan and Persian Gulf ranges fixed between USD 17,000/21,000 
pd, while a direct ECSA front-haul reached USD 21,500 pd alongside an 
$800,000 ballast bonus. In the Atlantic, Continent departures via USEC made 
USD 28,000 pd to India and USD 17,250 pd on a transatlantic round. On 
period, two Kamsarmaxes delivering in North China fixed for 9 to 12 months 
duration between USD 19,260/21,500 pd.  
Ultramax / Supramax 
In the Pacific, Indonesian coal trips to China, SE Asia and ECI ranges settled 
between USD 15,500/21,000 pd, while an Australian round within SE Asia 
fetched USD 23,250 pd. A South China departure to ECI reached USD 30,000 
pd and a two-laden-leg trip from North China with Singapore-Japan 
redelivery achieved USD 24,000 pd. Long-haul runs from North China to 
West Africa fixed between USD 25,100/25,250 pd, whereas a North China 
trip to US Gulf made USD 19,000 pd. Out of West Coast India, a trip via 
Persian Gulf to ECI settled at USD 14,000 pd. In the Atlantic, Continent scrap 
to East Med obtained USD 32,000 pd, while a West Africa front-haul 
departure to North China fixed at USD 17,000 pd. 
Handymax / Handysize 
In the Pacific, a long-haul trip from Japan to US Gulf fixed at USD 22,000 pd. 
In the Atlantic, ECSA grain runs to WCSA achieved strong levels between USD 
32,600/35,000 pd, while departures to USEC and US Gulf made USD 32,000 
pd and USD 25,000 pd respectively. Out of US Gulf, grain trips to NCSA 
settled between USD 19,000/24,000 pd, alongside a transatlantic run to 
Continent at USD 21,750 pd. From Continent, grains to West Africa reached 
USD 26,000 pd, whereas a lumber trip to US Gulf fixed at USD 19,000 pd.
