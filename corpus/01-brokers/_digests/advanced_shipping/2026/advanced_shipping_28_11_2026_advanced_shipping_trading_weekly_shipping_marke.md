 Bulkers 
 
The Post Panamax “New Halo” 87/2011 Hudong-Zhonghua, China (SS 
11/2030, DD 11/2028) was committed to undisclosed interests at USD 16 
mill. Remind you that the sister vessel “Constantinos G.O.” 87/2011 Hudong-
Zhonghua, China (SS 03/2031, DD 06/2028) was reported sold in September 
at the same price. 
 
On the Ultramax sector, the “Sider Madeira” 64/2020 Mitsui, Japan (SS 
02/2030, DD 01/2028, Scrubber fitted) was committed at USD 37,4 mill. For 
reference, the “Benjamin Oldendorff” & “Britta Oldendorff” 62/2020 
Oshima, Japan (SS 03/2030, DD 06/2028 & SS 04/2030, DD 04/2028, 
Scrubber fitted) were sold back in July at low USD 37 mill each. Moreover, 
Iseaco Shipmanagement committed their “Iseaco Grace” 61/2011 Oshima, 
Japan (SS/DD 10/2027) to Middle Eastern interests at USD 22,8 mill. For 
reference, the “Amis Wisdom VI” 61/2011 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS/DD 
09/2026) was sold in July at rgn USD 22 mill. 
 
Blue Fleet Management have committed their Supramax “Super Rice” 
56/2013 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 07/2028, Scrubber fitted) to undisclosed 
interests at low USD 19 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel “Jin Bi” 
56/2012 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 09/2027) of Jinhui Shipping & Trading was 
committed at USD 17,8 mill. Remind you that back in July, the sister vessel 
“Lila Frostburg” 56/2013 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 08/2028) was sold at high 
USD 16 mill. 
 
The Handysize “Christina” 32/2011 Jiangmen, China (SS/DD passed) of Y-
Know Ship Management was committed at USD 11,55 mill. By way of 
comparison, the two-years-younger sister vessel “Bao Shine” 32/2013 
Jiangmen, China (SS 01/2028, DD 10/2027) was sold a month ago at USD 13 
mill. In addition, LA Maritime committed the “East Wind I” 28/2002 Imabari, 
Japan (SS/DD 10/2027) at USD 5,85 mill, while the sister vessel “ABK Tiger” 
28/2002 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 02/2027) was sold earlier this month at USD 
5,3 mill.

WEEKLY SHIPPING MARKET REPORT - pg. 2 
 
Week 48 (20th Nov to 27th Nov 2026) 
 Capesize 
Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route rallied significantly, with 
Dampier and Port Hedland fixtures to Qingdao settling between USD 
15.80/16.50 fio, while a Dampier run to Kaohsiung fixed at USD 14.95 fio. In 
the Atlantic, long-haul business from Tubarao to Qingdao stood steady at 
USD 35.10 fio, whereas West African departures from Freetown and 
Nouadhibou to Qingdao achieved between USD 35.60/36.50 fio. In the 
transatlantic market, a Morebaya run to Gijon or Dunkirk made USD 13.10 
fio. 
Kamsarmax / Panamax 
In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore-Japan options settled between 
USD 17,000/19,000 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, India, SE 
Asia and Singapore-Japan ranges fetched between USD 17,500/23,000 pd. 
Indonesian coal runs to South China, Japan and India ranges achieved 
between USD 14,500/21,250 pd. Out of West Coast India and Persian Gulf, 
coal trips via South Africa to India fixed between USD 24,000/27,500 pd. In 
the Atlantic, front-haul departures from East Coast India and West Africa via 
ECSA to Singapore-Japan range obtained between USD 21,750/26,000 pd, 
with the West African delivery also securing a USD 475,000 ballast bonus. 
On period, a Kamsarmax delivering in North China fixed for 11 to 13 months 
duration worldwide at USD 17,500 pd.  
Ultramax / Supramax 
In the Pacific, Indonesian coal trips from East Coast India and South East Asia 
settled between USD 14,000/19,000 pd, while an ECI iron ore run to China 
made USD 14,500 pd. A NoPac grain trip from North China to Singapore-
Japan range reached USD 22,000 pd, whereas a South China bagged cement 
departure to WCSA fixed at USD 19,250 pd. In the Atlantic, US Gulf and USEC 
transatlantic grain, petcoke and wood pellet runs to Continent and 
Mediterranean ranges surged between USD 31,500/39,000 pd, while front-
haul departures to China and Japan achieved USD 31,750 pd and USD 30,750 
pd respectively. Continent scrap to Med obtained USD 34,250 pd. Out of 
ECSA, sugar trips to West Africa and Red Sea fixed at USD 32,000 pd and USD 
20,500 pd, alongside a front-haul to Singapore-Japan at USD 19,500 pd plus 
a USD 950,000 ballast bonus. On period, two Ultramaxes in Asia fixed for 8 
to 13 months on index-linked rates between 103% and 104% of BSI63. 
Handymax / Handysize 
Limited reported activity was recorded in the sector this week, centered 
exclusively in the Atlantic. Continent scrap via the Baltic to East Med 
achieved USD 32,000 pd, while ECSA grain departures to West Africa and 
coastal North Brazil settled firmly between USD 30,000/32,000 pd. On 
period, a Handysize delivering in West Africa fixed for 5 to 7 months duration 
worldwide at USD 14,500 pd.
