WEEKLY SHIPPING MARKET REPORT - pg. 2 
 
Week 44 (23rd Oct to 30th Oct 2026) 
 Capesize 
Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route strengthened, with 
Dampier and Port Hedland fixtures settling between USD 13.80/15.10 fio. In 
the Atlantic, long -haul business from Tubarao  to Qingdao, including West 
Africa options, pushed higher to USD 36.00/36.60 fio. Transatlantic and long-
haul departures from Seven Islands to Far East destinations achieved strong 
levels between USD 39.25/39.75 fio, while a Nouadhibou run to Qingdao 
made USD 34.15 fio. 
Kamsarmax / Panamax 
In the Pacific, NoPac  trips with Singapore -Japan and Persian Gulf options 
settled between USD 17,500/19,250 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in 
China and Singapore-Japan ranges fetched between USD 17,000/24,500 pd. 
Indonesian coal runs to South China achieved between USD 13,000/17,000 
pd, alongside a coastal ECI trip fixing at USD 16,900 pd. From East Coast 
India, Japan and South East Asia, trips via ECSA to Singapore -Japan range 
fixed between USD 17,000/21,000 pd, while a direct ECSA front -haul 
reached USD 18,000 pd along side an $800,000 ballast bonus. Out of West 
Coast India, runs via South Africa to India range obtained between USD 
18,500/19,500 pd. In the Atlantic, a West Med bauxite trip via West Africa 
to Continent made USD 22,500 pd, while a Continent departure via U SEC to 
Singapore-Japan reached USD 28,350 pd.  
Ultramax / Supramax 
In the Pacific, Indonesian coal trips to SE Asia, China and India ranges settled 
between USD 20,000/25,000 pd, while limestone runs to ECI achieved 
between USD 27,000/32,000 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, 
Japan, ECI and SE Asia ranges fetc hed between USD 21,000/26,500 pd. 
NoPac and clinker runs to SE Asia obtained USD 21,000 pd and USD 22,750 
pd respectively. Long -haul trips from China to West Africa reached USD 
28,000 pd, while a Japan departure via China to Mediterranean made USD 
25,500 pd. In the Atlantic, US Gulf front -haul petcoke and grain trips to Far 
East and India fixed between USD 28,500/32,000 pd, alongside transatlantic 
wood pellets to Continent at USD 31,000 pd. Out of WCSA and ECSA, front -
haul departures to Far East range achieved between USD 19,750/30,250 pd. 
On period, several Ultramaxes fixed for 3 to 7 months duration between USD 
23,000/24,500 pd. 
Handymax / Handysize 
Limited reported activity was recorded in the sector this week. In the 
Atlantic, a US Gulf departure to NCSA range fixed at USD 16,000 pd. On 
period, a Handysize delivering in ECSA was secured for 5 to 7 months 
duration in the Atlantic at USD 20,800 pd.
