Week 43 (16th Oct to 23rd Oct 2026) 
 Bulkers 
 
The Post Panamax “Maritime King” 95/2011 Imabari, Japan (SS 02/2031, DD 
04/2029) of Shoei Kisen was committed to undisclosed interests at USD 19,2 
mill. Note that a month ago, the two -years-younger sister vessel “HC 
Wisdom” 95/2013 Imabari, Japan (SS 06/2031, DD 09/2029, Scrubber fitted) 
was sold at USD 24,5 mill. 
 
Atlantic Bulk Carriers have committed their Ultramax “ Desert Namuli ” 
63/2021 Tadotsu, Japan (SS 06/2031, DD 03/2029) at xs USD 39 mill. Remind 
you that back in July, the two-years-older “CMB Jordaens” 63/2019 Tadotsu, 
Japan (SS 09/2029, DD 08/2027) was sold at USD 35,5 mill. 
 
On the Supramax sector, Uglands Rederi committed their “ Kristinita” 
58/2011 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 07/2031, DD 05/2029) to clients of 
ADNOC at xs USD 20 mill. For reference, the three -years-older sister vessel 
“Harvest” 58/2008 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 02/2028, Scrubber 
fitted) was sold in late August at USD 13,8 mill. Moreover, Ya Sa Shipping 
committed their “Yasa Unsal Sunar” 55/2007 Mitsui, Japan (SS 06/2031, DD 
04/2029) at USD 15,1 mill , while the “ Jin Xing ” 55/2007 Oshima, Japan 
(SS/DD 07/2027) of Jinhui Shipping & Transportation was committed to 
clients of Heng Fa Shipping at USD 14,6 mill . By way of comparison, the 
“Plate Queen” 55/2007 Oshima, Japan (SS 08/2031, DD 08/2029) was sold 
earlier this month at USD 15,3 mill. 
 
Taylor Maritime have committed the OHBS Handysize “IVS Kestrel” 32/2014 
Kanda, Japan (SS 05/2029, DD 05/2027) at USD 17,4 mill. Note that a month 
ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel “Crimson Wyoming” 33/2015 Kanda, 
Japan (SS 03/2030, DD 04/2028, OHBS) was sold at low USD 18 mill. 
 
Tankers 
 
Following the sale of the Suezmax “ Tataki” 150/2010 New Times, China (SS 
01/2030, DD 03/2029, Scrubber fitted) earlier this month at USD 96 mill , 
Dynacom Tankers have now committed the sister vessel “ Zouzou N. ” 
150/2010 New Times, China (SS 01/2030, DD 05/2029, Scrubber fitted) at 
USD 103 mill. 
 
Norwegian owners have committed the LR2 “Afra Mare” 105/2008 Hyundai, 
Korea (SS/DD 09/2028, Scrubber fitted) to undisclosed interests at USD 59 
mill. By way of comparison, the one-year-younger “Eurointegrity” 105/2009 
Hyundai, Korea (SS 02/2029, DD 03/2027) was committed a month ago at 
USD 60 mill.

Week 43 (16th Oct to 23rd Oct 2026) 
 Capesize 
Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route showed mild gains, with 
Dampier and Port Hedland fixtures settling between USD 12.85/14.25 fio. In 
the Atlantic, long-haul business from South Brazil, including Tubarao, Itaguai 
and Sudeste to Qingdao, fi xed steadily between USD 31.00/31.90 fio, while 
a Ponta Da Madeira run to Isdemir achieved USD 16.75 fio. Out of South 
Africa, Saldanha Bay departures to Qingdao and Dangjin  obtained between 
USD 23.25/25.75 fio. 
Kamsarmax / Panamax 
In the Pacific, NoPac  trips with Singapore -Japan options settled between 
USD 18,000/26,000 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, Japan and 
South East Asia ranges fetched between USD 19,750/24,500 pd. Indonesian 
coal runs to South China, Japan and India ranges achieve d between USD 
14,500/23,250 pd. From West Coast India and South East Asia, trips via ECSA 
to Singapore-Japan range fixed between USD 18,750/21,000 pd. A long-haul 
fertilizer trip from North China to Mediterranean range made USD 17,500 
pd. In the Atlantic, transatlantic runs from Continent via NCSA to Europe 
range settled between USD 22,500/23,500 pd, while a US Gulf departure to 
Continent/Med range achieved USD 26,000 pd alongside a USD 600,000 
ballast bonus. Front -haul trips from Gibraltar via USEC to Indi a obtained 
strong levels between USD 31,000/35,000 pd.  
Ultramax / Supramax 
In the Pacific, Indonesian coal trips to West Coast India and East Coast India 
achieved USD 27,000 pd and USD 33,000 pd respectively, while a run to 
Japan range fixed at USD 19,500 pd. Long -haul departures from China 
maintained firm levels, with trips to M editerranean from South China and 
North China securing between USD 27,500/31,000 pd, alongside a North 
China run to West Africa at USD 27,500 pd. Out of West Coast India, a coastal 
limestone trip to ECI range made USD 12,500 pd. In the Atlantic, an ECSA 
front-haul departure to South East Asia was agreed at USD 20,500 pd 
alongside a USD 1,000,000 ballast bonus. 
Handymax / Handysize 
In the Pacific, regional departures from Japan and North China to South East 
Asia and ECI settled between USD 16,500/22,000 pd, while a South China 
trip to WCI fetched a strong USD 30,000 pd. An ECSA front-haul run to South 
East Asia reached USD 22,750 pd.  In the Atlantic, Continent scrap to Black 
Sea hit USD 41,000 pd, while steel and grain runs  to USG and West Africa 
ranges fixed between USD 19,500/25,000 pd. Out of US Gulf, trips to NCSA, 
West Africa and India ranges obtained between USD 16,000/19,000 pd, 
while an ECSA run to East Africa achieved USD 25,000 pd. An East Med 
cement trip to Caribs  made USD 12,500 pd. On period, a Handysize 
delivering in ECSA fixed for 5 to 7 months at USD 19,500 pd.
