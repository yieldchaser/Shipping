WEEKLY SHIPPING MARKET REPORT - pg. 2 
 
Week 30 (17th Jul to 24th Jul 2026) 
 Capesize 
Iron ore rates on the West Australia/China route were under pressure, with 
Dampier to Qingdao fixtures settling between USD 11.05/11.10 fio. In the 
Atlantic, departures from Tubarao and Acu to Qingdao, including West Africa 
options, secured between USD 32. 00/34.30 fio. Further East, runs from 
Gladstone and HPCT to East Coast India settled at USD 20.40 fio and USD 
17.40 fio respectively. 
Kamsarmax / Panamax 
In the Pacific, NoPac rounds with Singapore/Japan redelivery fixed at USD 
14,000 pd, while trips via Australia to China and Singapore/Japan range 
settled between USD 15,000/19,500 pd. Indonesian coal runs from South 
East Asia and South China to South China, India and local ranges fetched USD 
13,000/18,000 pd. Out of India and South East Asia, trips via ECSA with 
Singapore/Japan and South East Asia redelivery achieved USD 
17,500/22,750 pd. On period, activity was firm with vessels fixing between 
USD 16,000/20,500 pd for 10 to 13 months, a short 2/3 laden legs option 
securing USD 21,000 pd, and a 12-month deal settling at USD 18,750 pd.  
Ultramax / Supramax 
In the Pacific, NoPac trips from North China with Singapore/Japan redelivery 
fixed at USD 18,000 pd. From South China and South East Asia, local, clinker, 
and coal runs to China, South East Asia and East Coast India fetched between 
USD 14,500/25,000 pd, wh ile multi -laden legs to Singapore/Japan range 
reached up to USD 24,000 pd. Departures from India and PG ranges for local 
and China trips settled between USD 15,000/21,500 pd, while a trip from 
West Coast India to Continent made USD 14,000 pd. In the Atlantic, a US Gulf 
run to Continent achieved USD 23,000 pd. Out of South Africa, coal trips to 
East Africa and West Coast India ranges obtained between USD 
17,250/28,500 pd. On period, Ultramaxes fixed for 4/6 months duration 
between USD 22,500/22,750 pd, while another achieved USD 18,350 pd for 
12/14 months. 
Handymax / Handysize 
It was a quiet week for the Handymax/Handysize segment with limited 
reported activity. From the Black Sea and East Med, trips to Continent and 
Med ranges settled between USD 15,000/16,800 pd. In the Atlantic, an ECSA 
grain run to Singapore/Japan range achi eved USD 21,500 pd. From East 
Africa, a prompt trip to South East Asia fixed at USD 14,000 pd.
