# DRY BULK COMMENTARY

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route maintained an active presence, with Port Hedland and Dampier departures to Qingdao settling between USD 14.15/15.30 fio. In the Atlantic, Tubarao fixtures to Qingdao, including West Africa options, achieved between USD 37.25/37.50 fio. Out of South Africa, a Saldanha Bay run to Qingdao fetched USD 26.14 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore-Japan options settled between USD 18,500/20,500 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in South China and Singapore-Japan ranges fetched between USD 16,000/21,500 pd. Indonesian coal runs to South China, India and regional destinations achieved between USD 12,100/17,500 pd, while a South East Asia trip via South Africa to Singapore-Japan made USD 20,250 pd. From East Coast India, East Africa and regional origins, trips via ECSA to Far East destinations obtained between USD 16,500/25,000 pd, alongside an ECSA departure securing USD 21,000 pd plus USD 1,000,000 ballast bonus. In the Atlantic, transatlantic and fronthaul fixtures via USEC and US Gulf range ranged from USD 26,100 to USD 32,750 pd. On period, activity was brisk with vessels securing between USD 17,500/22,250 pd for durations ranging from 5 to 14 months.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, a North China trip via NoPac to Australia settled at USD 19,000 pd, while a South China run to WCSA made USD 19,000 pd. Indonesian coal trips with options to India, China and regional destinations achieved between USD 15,000/23,000 pd, whereas steel, slag and clinker runs from North China to East Coast India range secured between USD 18,500/23,000 pd. Out of West Coast India, trips to East Coast India and Singapore-Japan range obtained USD 24,000 pd and USD 16,600 pd respectively. In the Atlantic, transatlantic grain and coal fixtures from US Gulf to Continent and East Med reached between USD 27,000/32,000 pd, while a petcoke run to South East Asia made USD 31,000 pd. Out of Continent, trips to East Med and East Africa settled between USD 21,000/21,500 pd. On period, vessels secured between USD 19,000/22,750 pd for durations ranging from 5 to 13 months.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Pacific, Indonesian clinker runs to South China and Taiwan settled at USD 15,000 pd, while a South East Asia trip via Australia to Singapore-Japan range also fetched USD 15,000 pd. A South China trip with Singapore-Japan options for 2 to 3 laden legs made USD 15,800 pd. In the Atlantic, Continent trips via Murmansk and Baltic options to Continent and West Africa ranges obtained between USD 15,850/17,000 pd, while a prompt Continent run to West Africa fixed at USD 16,500 pd. Out of South America, a West Coast South America coastal trip secured USD 16,750 pd.
