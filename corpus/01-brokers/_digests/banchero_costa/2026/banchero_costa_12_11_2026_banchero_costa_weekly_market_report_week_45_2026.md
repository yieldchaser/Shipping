CHINA BAUXITE IMPORTS
Bauxite is one of the most important 
dry bulk commodities, accounting 
for 4.4 percent of all seaborne dry 
bulk trade in 2025, rising to 4.9 
percent so far in 2026.
Total seaborne exports of bauxite 
increased in Jan-Dec 2025 by +20.3% 
year-on-year to a record 246.4 mln 
tonnes, according to vessel tracking 
data from AXS Marine.
This followed on the +13.6% y-o-y 
growth recorded in 2024, and the 
+5.2% y-o-y growth in 2023.
In Jan-Oct 2026, global loadings 
increased by a further +11.7% y-o-y 
to 226.2 mln tonnes, from 202.6 mln 
t in Jan-Oct 2025.
The largest exporter of bauxite by far 
is Guinea, which accounted for 
72.5% of global bauxite exports in 
Jan-Dec 2025, rising to 78.2% in Jan-
Oct 2026.
In Jan-Oct 2026, shipments from 
Guinea increased by +21.9% y-o-y to 
176.9 mln tonnes, from 145.2 mln t 
in Jan-Oct 2025.
Guinea’s exports in the first ten 
months of 2026 alone already 
exceeded the 102.8 mln tonnes it 
exported in the whole of 2022.
In second place is Australia, which 
accounted for 14.9% of global 
bauxite exports in Jan-Oct 2026.
In Jan-Oct 2026, bauxite exports 
from Australia declined by -11.9% y-
o-y to 33.7 mln tonnes.
This followed a +2.4% y-o-y increase 
in Australian exports in 2025, and a 
+12.8% y-o-y jump in 2024.
Far in third place is Brazil, with just a 
2.1% share in Jan-Oct 2026.
Exports from Brazil declined by -7.9% 
y-o-y in Jan-Oct 2026 to 4.8 mln t, 
after surging +27.2% y-o-y in 2025.
A recent development is Guyana, 
whose bauxite exports surged by 
+280% y-o-y in 2025 to 2.9 mln t, 
although volumes have since 
flattened, down -3.3% y-o-y to 2.4 
mln t in Jan-Oct 2026.
Indonesian exports are still down at 
zero, due to their policies in recent 
years to ban exports of unrefined 
ores and push for investment in 
smelters in the country.
Let’s remember that as recently as in 
2022 Indonesia exported 18.2 mln 
tonnes of bauxite, amounting to 11% 
of global bauxite exports.
In terms of imports, by far the top 
destination for bauxite so far in 2026 
is Mainland China, accounting for 
88.6% of global bauxite imports.
Imports to China increased by 
+13.2% y-o-y in Jan-Oct 2026 to 
201.8 mln tonnes, from 178.3 mln t 
in Jan-Oct 2025.
This followed a +23.9% y-o-y 
increase in Jan-Dec 2025 to 213.1 
mln tonnes, which was over 50 
percent above the 139.2 mln t 
imported by China in Jan-Dec 2022.
Outside China, the largest importer 
was the European Union, accounting 
for 4.0% of global bauxite imports in 
Jan-Oct 2026.
Imports to the EU increased by 
+3.3% y-o-y in Jan-Oct 2026 to 9.1 
mln t, continuing the recovery from 
the recent trough of 7.6 mln t 
imported by the EU in full-year 2023.
In third place was India, accounting 
for a 3.2% share and 7.4 mln t in Jan-
Oct 2026, a surge of +82.3% y-o-y.
The United Arab Emirates were in 
fourth place with 2.2 mln tonnes in 
Jan-Oct 2026, down -51.0% y-o-y, a 
0.9% share of global imports.
Let’s now focus specifically on China, 
which as we mentioned above is the
world’s largest importer of bauxite 
with an 88.6% share.
Top bauxite discharging ports in 
China in Jan-Oct 2026 were:
Yantai New West Port (34.3 mln t in 
Jan-Oct 2026), Fangcheng (29.1 mln 
t), Yantai (16.9 mln t), Huanghua 
(16.7 mln t), Caofeidian (14.7 mln t), 
Longkou (14.6 mln t), Qingdao (13.7 
mln t), Rizhao (13.6 mln t), Tianjin 
(8.2 mln t).
77.3% of bauxite imported into 
China in Jan-Oct 2026 was loaded on 
Capesizes, 5.2% on VLOCs, 11.7% on 
Post-Panamaxes or Baby-Capes, 
3.4% on Panamaxes/Kamsarmaxes, 
2.4% on Supramaxes.
Obviously, the main source of 
bauxite for China is Guinea, 
accounting for 80.7% of China’s 
bauxite imports in Jan-Oct 2026.
Shipments from Guinea to China 
increased by +21.9% y-o-y in Jan-Oct 
2026 to 162.9 mln tonnes.
This follows a +32.8% y-o-y increase 
in 2025, a +13.6% y-o-y increase in 
2024, and +35.4% y-o-y in 2023.
In second place as a source was 
Australia, which accounted for 16.1% 
of China’s imports in Jan-Oct 2026.
In Jan-Oct 2026, shipments to China 
from Australia declined by -3.1% y-o-
y to 32.4 mln tonnes.
A new entry since 2025 is Guyana, 
which shipped 2.6 mln tonnes to 
China in 2025 and 1.9 mln t in Jan-
Oct 2026.
