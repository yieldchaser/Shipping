UNITED STATES LPG IMPORTS
Global LPG (liquified petroleum gas) 
trade had been growing steadily for 
four consecutive years, but 2026 has 
broken the trend. 
Total seaborne exports of LPG in 
2025 increased by +4.8% year-on-
year to 154.7 mln tonnes, according 
to vessel tracking data from LSEG. 
This follows the +3.4% y-o-y growth 
recorded in 2024, and the +2.4% y-o-
y growth in 2023. 
In Jan-Sep 2026, however, global 
seaborne LPG exports declined by      
-11.7% y-o-y to 102.7 mln tonnes. 
These totals mask significant 
differences in export volumes from 
different exporters, as well as in 
demand from the various importers. 
In Jan-Sep 2026, LPG exports from 
the USA increased by a further 
+6.3% y-o-y to 56.9 mln tonnes, and 
now account for 55.4% of all global 
seaborne LPG exports. 
LPG exports from the GCC, on the 
other hand, collapsed by -57.0% y-o-
y in Jan-Sep 2026, to 12.1 mln 
tonnes, including 3.5 mln t from the 
UAE, 2.8 mln t from Saudi Arabia, 2.4 
mln t from Qatar, 2.2 mln t from 
Oman, 1.1 mln t from Kuwait. 
Shipments from the GCC accounted 
for just 11.8% of global exports in 
Jan-Sep 2026, down sharply from 
24.2% in the same period of 2025. 
Exports from the EU increased by 
+5.9% y-o-y in Jan-Sep 2026 to 4.3 
mln t. 
In terms of imports, the top 
destination for LPG in Jan-Sep 2026 
was still Mainland China, accounting 
for 18.6% of global imports. Imports 
to China declined by -28.1% y-o-y in 
Jan-Sep 2026 to 18.8 mln tonnes. 
Imports to the EU declined by -3.3% 
y-o-y in Jan-Sep 2026 to 16.3 mln t. 
The EU accounted for 16.0% of 
global seaborne LPG imports. 
Volumes into India declined by            
-26.4% y-o-y to 12.2 mln tonnes. 
Imports to Japan were essentially 
unchanged at -0.1% y-o-y at 7.2 mln 
tonnes in the same period. Imports 
to South Korea in Jan-Sep 2026 
declined -15.5% y-o-y to 5.3 mln t.
Let's now focus on the United 
States, which is now the largest 
exporter of LPG in the world, 
accounting for 55.4% of global 
volumes in Jan-Sep 2026. 
As already mentioned above, LPG 
exports from the USA in Jan-Sep 
2026 increased by +6.3% y-o-y to 
56.9 mln tonnes. This followed a 
+6.1% y-o-y increase in 2025, a 
+11.1% y-o-y increase in 2024, and a 
+15.0% y-o-y increase in 2023. 
Top LPG loading ports in the United 
States in Jan-Sep 2026 were: 
Houston (24.4 mln t in Jan-Sep 
2026), Beaumont (16.2 mln t), 
Freeport (6.9 mln t), Marcus Hook 
(5.1 mln t), Corpus Christi (1.6 mln t), 
Ferndale (1.1 mln t), Geismar (0.5 
mln t), Paulsboro (0.3 mln t). 
79.6% of volumes in Jan-Sep 2026 
were loaded on VLGCs (70+ cu.m.), 
with 4.8% on LGCs (25-70k cu.m.), 
11.2% on MGCs (13-25k cu.m.), and 
4.3% on SGCs (0-13k cu.m.). 
Surprisingly, given the ongoing 
political tensions, the major 
destination of American LPG is still 
Mainland China. 
Mainland China accounted for 16.4% 
of the USA's total seaborne LPG 
exports in Jan-Sep 2026, well down 
from the 29.5% peak of 2024. 
Volumes on this route went through 
the roof in the first half of the 
decade, but have corrected sharply 
since. 
Whilst LPG trade from the USA to 
China grounded to a halt in 2019 (it 
was just 0.3 mln tonnes!), already in 
2020 the United States emerged as 
the number one supplier of LPG to 
China with 6.2 mln tonnes. 
This went up to 6.9 mln t in 2021 
(+11.9% y-o-y), 9.2 mln t in 2022 
(+32.7% y-o-y), 16.3 mln t in 2023 
(+77.2% y-o-y), and a peak of 19.7 
mln t in 2024 (+20.5% y-o-y). 
In 2025, however, volumes from the 
USA to China fell by -39.9% y-o-y to 
11.8 mln t, and in Jan-Sep 2026 they 
declined by a further -7.2% y-o-y to 
9.3 mln t. 
In second place is the European 
Union, which accounted for 12.0% of 
USA LPG exports in Jan-Sep 2026. In 
Jan-Sep 2026, the USA exported 6.8 
mln tonnes of LPG to the EU, down      
-13.7% y-o-y. This followed a +24.8% 
y-o-y increase in 2025, and a +20.7% 
y-o-y increase in 2024. 
The USA also exported 6.8 mln t to 
Japan in Jan-Sep 2026, up +17.8% y-
o-y, and 4.8 mln t to South Korea, 
down -14.3% y-o-y. Shipments to 
India surged by +282.1% y-o-y to 4.7 
mln tonnes, whilst volumes to 
Indonesia rose by +20.8% y-o-y to 
4.4 mln t.

fresh tonnage.

approval procedures for new yards

encouraging investment and 
expansion within the industry.

recycling market.
