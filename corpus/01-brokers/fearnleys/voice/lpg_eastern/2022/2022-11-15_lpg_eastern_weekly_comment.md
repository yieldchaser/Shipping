## Market Commentary

MEG Dec C3 CP was traded at $640 and C4 at $5 less. Dec/Jan was traded at about $10 backwardation, reflecting the strong front market. Qatar Petroleum was reported to award their sale tender to a Chinese Major for 2-9th Dec lifting 3/1 cargo at Dec CP minus 20s. And ADNOC was said have rich inventory and have advanced some nominations for term liftings in Dec.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium for propane delivered in Dec strengthened this week, as supply especially for 1h Dec delivery looks tight when sellers are unable to firm up their delivery schedule unless transit slots booked. Earlier this week, 23kt propane parcel for 2h Dec delivery was traded at Dec FEI plus very low teens. And subsequently, similar cargo for 2h Dec delivery was bid at Dec FEI plus mid-teens but no offers seen on Thurs. Off window, several spot buy tenders were discussing. One Vietnam based PDH user issued tender to buy heavy propane cargo for 2h Dec delivery into Vung Tau. And Taiwan CPC issued tender to buy 11/11 for last decade Dec delivery. Other than spot tenders, focus of the week is still the term negotiation for next year into China. Two SChina traditional importers are reported to secure monthly 11/11 at CP plus nearly $40 and some portion using FEI link. One NChina PDH user also secured several propane cargoes for delivery in mainly 2H of the year at FEI plus low mid-single digit and partially CP link price. Another EChina petchem player also said to award their monthly 46kt propane buy tender at FEI plus mid-single digit and CP at low 40s. Last but not least, another two EChina PDHs are negotiating for monthly 23kt or 46kt propane, respectively. And we are still expecting a few more petchem players to term up for next year.
