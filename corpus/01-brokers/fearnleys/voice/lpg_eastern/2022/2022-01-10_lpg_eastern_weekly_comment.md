## Market Commentary

MEG Followed the strong gains in crude, Feb C3 CP rallied to $757. Feb C4 CP also surged up surprisingly and the spread to C3 narrowed down to only $3 from last week’s $20ish, driven by the strong C4 price in US for winter blending demand and several butane buy inquiries from EAST. Qatar Petroleum issued a tender to sell 16-21th Feb lifting cargo at a flexible ratio.

**FAR EAST**

 Demand for Feb arrival propane stay robust with a flurry of tenders and uncovered trader shorts seen in the market. Cash premiums for such period have been bid up from FEI plus mid teens to mid 20s earlier but softened to low 20s later this week in the window. For propane market, Taiwan FPCC awarded their tender to procure 23kt propane for Feb delivery at Feb FEI plus low 30s. Another two E.China PDH users also awarded their buy tender, with one reported to secure a 23kt parcel for 1h Feb delivery at Feb FEI plus 30ish while the other was said to award a 46kt parcel for similar delivery range at Feb FEI plus mid/high 20s. And there is still another NChina PDH user buy tender for 23kt and/or 45kt parcels during mid/2h Feb delivery. Other than tenders, some 1h Feb delivered full cargo parcel was reported to conclude at FEI plus mid 20s. For butane market, HTC didn’t award their buy tender for 2h Feb delivered 23kt butane as offers are not attractive compared to Naphtha reportedly. One SChina cracker was reported to inquire privately for a heavy butane cargo on 2h Feb delivery, also for cracking use.
