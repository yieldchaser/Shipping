## Market Commentary

MEG Despite the crude rise, Oct C3 CP slumped to $602 and C4 at $569 at the time of writing. Oct C3/C4 narrowed down from last week’s close to $40 to low $30s this week. However, evenly split cargo is still oversupplied, especially we’ve seen a handful of spot cargos available from middle east producers.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium for Oct delivered have continuously improved for propane parcels. Several cargos for 2h Oct delivery were changed hand, with full cargo lot of 46kt traded at Oct FEI minus mid-teens, or Oct FEI minus mid high single digit into Echina, and half cargo lot of 23kt at Oct FEI minus $8ish. Subsequently, 23kt propane for 2h Oct delivery was offered at FEI minus $4. Nevertheless, butane cargos are less favored. We’ve seen trader offering 23kt butane for 2h Oct at Oct FEI minus high 30s in the window, and several Oct delivered evenly split cargos are struggling to find home into EAST. Off window still quite active with several tenders been awarded. Taiwan CPC was reported to buy the last decade Oct delivered 11/11 parcel from a Chinese Major at Oct CP plus low 40s via tender. And one Vietnam PDH user was said to procure an Oct delivered full propane cargo from another Chinese Major at CP plus 30s. One NChina importer didn’t award their buy tender for 46kt propane for 10-31st Oct delivery reportedly. One European trader just tendered to buy a evenly split cargo for 2h Oct delivery into India. And outside tender, we’ve hearing another EChina importer are inquired privately for a 23kt propane for 2h Oct delivery.
