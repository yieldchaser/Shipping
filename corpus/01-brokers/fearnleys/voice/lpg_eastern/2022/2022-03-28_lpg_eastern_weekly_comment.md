## Market Commentary

MEG Following the oil price hike, Saudi raised Apr C3 CP at $940 and C4 CP at $960, up by $45 and $40 respectively, which is slightly higher than swap level but within expectations. Backwardation into May enlarged further, and May C3 CP was traded at $867.

**FAR EAST**

 23kt propane for 2h Apr arrival was traded at Apr FEI plus $39 and $44 this week, and similar cargo for 1h May arrival at May FEI plus $48. Off window a couple of 2h May delivered propane was traded at Apr FEI minus small digits. Main activities this week are still buy tenders from petchem users. One EChina PDH was reported to cover two propane cargo for May delivery at May FEI plus 30s. The PDH was said to resell their Apr term cargos due to ongoing turnaround program. Another EChina PDH also issued tender to secured 23kt propane parcel for end Apr/early May delivery. One Ningbo-based cracker procured a 1h May delivered 46kt propane reportedly at May FEI plus high 40s via tender. Other PDHs are keeping lower rates mostly, and one 600kt/y PDH in EChina is planning one-month turnround as of eroding margins. One Vietnam importer issued tender to buy 1-17th May delivered 3/1 or 2/2 ratio cargo. One Australian origin evenly split cargo for late May early Apr arrival is available in the market.
