## Market Commentary

MEG A week of volatility as oil is on a roller coaster. Mar C3 CP followed the trajectory and traded at $784 at the time of writing. Interestingly Mar C4 was traded $10 higher than C3, due to the improved butane demand as traders bidding to cover shorts into India and SEA region against a tightness in butane supply in front market. Saudi Aramco released their Mar lifting with no plus tolerance for butane. Additionally, both KPC and QP was reported to sell evenly split FOB cargos, one for mid Mar lifting and the other for end Mar lifting.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium softened this week, with 23kt parcel for 2h Mar delivery bid at Mar FEI plus mid high teens only while offers hover at plus mid-20s. Off window a handful of tenders are floating in the market. Two Korean players issued buy tenders to secure upto 92kt LPG for 2h Mar and 1H Apr delivery for petchem use. One EChina cracker was reported to cover a 2h Mar delivered 46kt propane at Mar FEI plus mid 20s only. Another EChina PDH user issued tender to buy 1 upto 5 parcels of 23kt propane monthly from Mar to Jul delivery. One Schina traditional importer also called a buy tender for monthly 6kt or 12kt butane from Apr to Dec delivery. One Taiwan Petchem plant was also reported to buy a 2h Mar delivered propane cargo at Mar MOPJ minus high 30s for cracking feedstock.
