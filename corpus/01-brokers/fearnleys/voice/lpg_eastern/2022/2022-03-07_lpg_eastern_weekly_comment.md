## Market Commentary

MEG A week of volatility with CP swings from $880 to $1000, along with the wild Oil. At the time of writing, Apr C3 CP was traded at $916 and C4 at $20 higher. Apr/May C3 CP was traded at $30 backwardation. Saudi Aramco was reported to sell two heavy propane cargos for late Mar lifting at fixed price. And Qatar Petroleum tendered to sell 19-29th Apr lifting 3/1 or 4/0 cargo.

**FAR EAST**

 Slow start this week and window appears thin, as 23kt propane was bid at Apr FEI plus $40 while seller asking plus $60s for a 46kt parcel. Outside window, tenders swarmed up especially after the dramatic drop on Wednesday. Japanese and Korea importers are actively buying cargos, with two Canada origin propane cargos delivered in Apr and May respectively reported sold into Japan, and one Ferndale origin evenly split cargo into Korea for May delivery. Another two Korean importers issued tender to buy in total 69kt LPG for Apr delivery into Petchem segments. One Ningbo-based cracker user tendered to buy 23kt or 46kt propane parcel for 2h Apr delivery. Taiwan FPC withdrew their buy tender for 2h Apr arrival 46kt LPG at flexible ratio due to unattractive price as overnight FEI/MOPJ spread shrank. Indonesia PERTAMINA issued two swap tenders this week to sell their 3-4 Apr and 12-13 Apr US FOB 2/2 parcels and buy back same volumes for 11-15 May and 22-24 May delivery. Apart from tenders, several buyers are exploring Apr delivered evenly split parcel into SEA or Schina.
