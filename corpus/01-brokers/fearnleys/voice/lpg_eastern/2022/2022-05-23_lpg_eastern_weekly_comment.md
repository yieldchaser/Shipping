## Market Commentary

MEG Jun Propane CP swap fell at $736 and Butane was traded at flattish level. Meanwhile, Jun/Jul CP was traded at $15 contango, reflecting the front length. Qatar Petroleum was reported to sell two 22-24th June lifting FOBs at CP minus 20s level. Discounts deepened as of weaken summer demand against the soaring freight. 1st round June CP recommendation ranged from $725 to $770 for C3 and C4 was recommended at $5 less to flattish to C3.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium edged up slightly this week. 23kt propane for 2h Jun delivery was concluded over window at FEI plus $9. And similar cargo for 1h Jul delivery was bid at Jul FEI plus mid-single digit. Off window a handful of buying tenders floating in the market, mainly driving by the petchem. Taiwan FPCC tendered to buy last decade Jun delivered 23kt to 46kt LPG at flex ratio, and awarded one 23/23 cargo at Jun MOPJ minus high 70s. Two Korean importers also issued buy tender to secure 23/0 and 0/23 for Jul delivery respectively, with one reported to secure 23kt propane for 1-5 Jul delivery at Jul FEI plus mid high single digit into Daesan. Apart from such, one European trader issued tender to buy 23kt or 46kt evenly split cargo for Jun to 1st decade Jul delivery into Malaysia, and it was reported to award one 23/23 at CP plus 20ish.
