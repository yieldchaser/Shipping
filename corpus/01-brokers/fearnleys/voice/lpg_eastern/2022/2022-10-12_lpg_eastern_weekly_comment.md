## Market Commentary

MEG ADNOC released their Nov lifting acceptance with some advancement up to 5-7 days reported. KPC refinery production was suspended unexpectedly but was reported at a limit impact to exports. Swap market experienced a volatile week with Nov C3 CP once soared up to $600 and declined to $577 at the time of writing, C4 CP was assessed at $22 less. Nov/Dec was traded at 6-7$ contango.

**FAR EAST**

 Active market with a handful of deals were concluded this week. 23kt propane for 2h Nov delivery was changed hand at Nov FEI minus $2/2.5 during mid-week but softened on Thurs with bids for similar cargo show at minus $5.88 while offer at Flat to FEI. Off window a flurry of tenders is in the market, especially from importers’ feedstock procurement tender on term basis for next year. Two Chinese petchem users issued propane or heavy propane buy term tender for next year delivery. Taiwan CPC also issued a swap tender to sell their Saudi FOB for monthly 11/11 and buy back 8-12 cargos on des basis at similar grades. One Korean importer also issue two term tenders to secure monthly 23kt butane in different lots for cracking and was reported to award only one. One EChina PDH procured a Nov delivered 46kt propane via tender at 50% Nov FEI minus low-mid single digit and 50% Nov CP plus low 40s reportedly. Another EChina cracker also issued buy tender to secure 23kt to 46kt propane for 2h Nov delivery.
