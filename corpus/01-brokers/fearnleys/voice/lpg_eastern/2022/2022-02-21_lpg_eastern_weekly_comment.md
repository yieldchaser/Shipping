## Market Commentary

MEG Following the soaring oil, Mar C3 CP surged to $865 at the time of writing, and C4 CP traded at $35 higher, driven by prompt demand from India and SEA against limited butane supply from AG producers. Mar/Apr CP was traded at $16 backwardations. 1st round CP recommendation was at below $800 range only, which shall be overwritten in 2nd round CP to catch up the rise in swap market.

**FAR EAST**

 Active bids shown in window this week, mainly for 2h Mar delivered propane and equal ratio split cargos both. 23kt propane for 2h Mar delivery was traded at $902 on Thurs, equates to FEI plus low 30s. Early Mar lifting 2/2 ratio was bid at $885 for C3 and $920 for C4 respectively but no offers seen. Demand for evenly split cargos from India and SEA appeared robust with both Indian and SEA importers out to seek cargos, against tight supply on AG butane and less ARB butane cargo available from US. Two Indian PSUs issued prompt tenders to secure more than 260kt (in total) evenly split cargo for late Feb/Mar arrival. One traditional SChina importer failed to award their previous annual buy tender and reissued a new tender to secure 23kt evenly split cargo in two lots for Apr delivery. Indonesia PERTAMINA also tendered to buy one evenly split cargo for mid Apr delivery. Apart from such, two Korean players also tendered to buy 23kt LPG each, one for propane one for butane for 1h Apr delivery.
