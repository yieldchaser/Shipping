## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco cut Jan CP by $55 to $740 for C3 and by $40 to $710 for C4. Despite the decline, the set CP is still higher than expectations, with most expecting at $710-730 range for propane only. Subsequently, Feb C3 CP rapidly caught up with the rise and was traded at $735 at the time of writing, and C4 CP was at $24 less. Feb/Mar CP was traded at over $30 backwarded structure, reflecting the bearish outlook after winter season. One AG producer was said to have a 2h Jan lifting evenly split cargo available for sale.

**FAR EAST**

 With the opening of new year, cash premium for 1h Feb delivered market edged up, as a plenty of buying inquires emerging whereas few offers seen. 23kt lot for 1h Feb delivery was bid at Feb FEI plus $17.5 and 46kt lot at Feb FEI plus $14. Half cargo parcel for 2h Feb delivery was also bidding at similar level compared to 1h Feb. Outside window a flurry of tenders is floating in the market. Three Chinese petchem user have issued buy tenders to secure up to 115kt propane in total for 1h Feb delivery, with some parcels concluded at the level of FEI plus 20ish reportedly. There are another two Chinese PDH users were said to explore propane for Feb delivery as well. Taiwan FPC also issued tender to procure 23kt up to 46kt LPG with flexibility in grades for mid Feb delivery.
