## Market Commentary

MEG A handful of Jul lifting cargos were sold at heavy discounts this week. KPC awarded their tender to sell one 19/20th Jul lifting 2/2 at CP minus high teens reportedly. Qatar Petroleum was also reported to sell one 9/10th Jul lifting 2/2 at CP minus around $20 and one 14/15th Jul lifting 3/1 at CP minus low-mid teens. Other than producers’ spot sales, some Jul lifting 2/2 cargo was also reported changed hand at CP minus $18. July Propane CP was traded at $742, and Butane was slightly higher at $744. Jul/Aug CP was traded at $9 contango structure.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium for Jul arrival cargos continued to soften this week. We’ve seen 23kt propane parcels for 1h Jul delivery concluded at FEI minus $6.5 and $7 respectively on Thursday window, whereas similar cargo for 2h Jul delivery was traded at Jul FEI minus $4.5-5. One SChina importer was reported that they failed to award their buy tender for 11.5/11.5 parcel delivered during 6-23rd Jul into Shenzhen, as the offer at CP plus 60s is higher than expectation. Several PDHs are also exploring July arrival propane yet to issue tender to secure feedstock, as seeing downstream margins still under pressure and waiting premiums to decline further.
