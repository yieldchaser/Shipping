## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco set Feb Propane and Butane CP both at $775, up by $35 and $65 from Jan respectively, which fell within market’s expectation. However, some petchem players are worrying that downstream products value failed to catch up the rise, and squeezed or even negative margin might lead to the operation deratings. Mar CP was traded at $774 at the time of writing and Mar/Apr CP backwardation deepened to nearly $40.

**FAR EAST**

 Market is quiet this week as of lunar new year holiday. 23kt propane parcel for 1h Mar arrival was bid in window at Mar FEI plus $18 while no offers seen. Off window discussions are also muted but we expect more buying inquires for 2h Mar arrival will emerge once players returns. Despite the thin activities, front Mar FEI has maintained the strength at $770 and Mar/Apr FEI was traded at $30, due to a combination of unexpected coldness, delays caused by Panama congestion and fog, together with the 4.3mil big draw in US propane inventory.
