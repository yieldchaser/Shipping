MEG Following the crude rally, Nov Propane CP was traded at $600 and Butane at $582. Nov/Dec CP was traded at $7 contango as entering winter. **FAR EAST**

Similar cargo was offered in window at FEI minus $6 for 1h Nov delivery, whereas bid for 2h Nov delivery cargo is at FEI minus $6.5, in view of slight contango on 1h/2h Nov. And we are expecting more demand coming out next week when China back from holidays.
