## Market Commentary

MEG May C3 CP dropped to $808 as oil slumps to three-week low, and C4 CP was at $15 higher than C3 as butane more favourable into summer season. May/Jun C3 CP was traded at $30 backwardation. Two evenly split cargos for May lifting changed hand at CP minus 10 level reportedly.

**FAR EAST**

 This week in window 23kt propane for 1h May delivery was traded at May FEI plus $35, while similar cargo for 2h May delivery was offered from mid 30s to high 20s on FEI link. Off window one Korean importer was reported to buy 23kt propane for 15-21 May delivery into Dasean at May FEI plus 20s. One EChina PDH issued tender to buy 23kt or 46kt propane for May delivery, and 23kt propane monthly from Jun to Dec delivery. Another EChina PDH also covered 23kt propane for May delivery reportedly. Despite the recent decline in cost, PDH margins are still not healthy and two EChina PDHs, each with 600kt/y capacity, are reported to have turnround plan for 30-40 days from 2h Apr and May respectively.
