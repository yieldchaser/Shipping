## Market Commentary

MEG Apr C3 CP surged to $930 and C4 was traded at $15 higher, buoyed by the higher oil prices. Due to front strength, Apr/May CP was traded at 30ish backwardation. Saudi released their Apr acceptance with no cuts but some delays reported. **FAR EAST**

 Apr discussion was limited in window, 23kt propane parcel for 2h Apr delivery was bid at Apr FEI plus around $40. Off window 46kt propane for 2h Apr delivery was reported traded at Apr FEI plus high 30s and 23kt parcel for 1h May delivery was reported done at CP plus high 20s level. One Korea cracker user tendered to buy 3-9th May delivered 23/23 or 23/0 or 0/23. Two PDHs in China has started their turnaround for 15 days and 45 days respectively this week, which is expected to reduce min 165kt propane demand in total. At the time being, four Chinese PDHs are offline and the majority of remaining PDHs fail to run at full capacity as of squeezed margins. One EChina PDH is expected to resume operation by May and they’ve just issued buy tender to procure a 1h May or 1-25th May delivery full propane.
