## Market Commentary

Cash premium for evenly split on an Feb AG Fob, stands firm in low $30s to Feb CP, underpinned by tightening spot availability. Earlier, Indian spot buy tenders were awarded a couple on FOB basis at CP plus $30s for Feb and plus mid-high single for March, and some on CFR basis at CP plus $50s–60s for Mar and below $50s for April, according to market sources. Demand remained robust, with another Indian PSU issuing a tender to secure one evenly split monthly cargo for Feb to April delivery, on either FOB AG or CFR basis. At the time of writing, Feb CP was traded at $528 for C3 and $514 for C4, while the Feb/Mar CP C3 traded at $18 backwardation, reflecting the front strength. US FOB values for Feb lifting surged amid widening ARB to EAST. At the time of writing, Arb to East was traded at $209 for Feb and $190 for Mar. Evenly split ratio for Feb lifting was reported traded at 10cpg, as players actively procured such for Indian and SEA market, driven by better economics with declining MB butane price. While 4/0 for Feb lifting was assessed at 9cpg. U.S. propane/propylene stocks fell by 2.4 million bbl to 95.7 million bbl on the week ended January 9, leaving inventory 23% higher on YOY basis. Cash differentials for Feb delivery soared up further with acute supply tightness, partly due to the previous delays caused by fog closures in late dec/early Jan loadings ex US. 23/0 parcel for 2h Feb delivery basis Chiba was bid at Feb FEI plus $36 while similar cargo with narrower laycan and specific origin/region was bid upto plus $50, yet to attract offers. 46/0 lot for 2h Feb delivery basis Ningbo was bid at Feb FEI plus $33. Outside window, Pertamina was said to award their buy tender for Mar delivery evenly split cargo at Mar CP plus 50$. Befar also issued buy tender for similar ratio on Mar delivery. Chinese spot purchase slowed down this week, as most have already secured minimal tons for feedstock/consumptions for Feb delivery in last two weeks, and stepped back from the intense bids in window. PDH rates declined further this week to below 70%, due to glitches, scheduled maintenance and weak margins. Other than petchem, residential prices in SChina/EChina spiked up as of shortage in supply. Up to 4 VLGCs of Iranian stems are floating outside SChina, as vessels having issues for berthing due to sanction and old ages, etc.. This being said, over 1mmt tons is expected to arrive in China for Jan delivery. However, as disruptions on supply due to Iran turmoil, we might see a significant decline on exports into China for Feb and even into 1h Mar delivery.
