## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco announced Aug C3 CP at $470 and C4 at $460, up by $70 and $85 respectively, which is in line with curve value and market expectation. The decrease in exports and increased demand from India lend support to the strong CP, and at the time of writing, Sep CP was traded at $517 for C3 and $511 for C4. Butane is in favored due to seasonal demand. Earlier one end Aug lifting 2/2 was said to be concluded at CP plus high single digit. Similar grade for Sep lifting was bid in window at $525 for C3 and $520 for C4 respectively, which equates to CP plus high single digit. And outside window mid-teens bid was heard as well.

**FAR EAST**

 23kt propane parcel for 1h Sep delivery was bid at Sep FEI minus mid-single digit and outside window low single digit discount was traded reportedly. In addition to this, a handful of 46kt propane parcels were reported to change hand at Sep Fei minus low single basis EChina or plus small premium basis NChina. One EChina PDH user was reported to only award their tender for 2h Sep delivered propane at FEI plus teens, paying the higher premium into river port and dead freight. Other PDHs yet to call procurement tender for Sep delivery, particularly seeing the eroding margins on soaring costs. One China Major importer issued term tender to secure monthly one propane cargo from Q4 2023 to Q3 2024 delivery. Similarly, another Indian Major also tendered to buy max 2335kt evenly split or heavy butane cargo for AG FOBs in 2024. Previously one Indian major is reported to award their 2024 annual term tender on some AG FOB volumes at CP minus teens level. One European Major also tendered to buy evenly split cargo for CFR Port Klang during late Aug to Sep delivery or FOB AG in Sep lifting, but was said not awarded eventually. Another SChina traditional importer tendered to buy a 11/11 or 0/23 for mid/2h Sep delivery.
