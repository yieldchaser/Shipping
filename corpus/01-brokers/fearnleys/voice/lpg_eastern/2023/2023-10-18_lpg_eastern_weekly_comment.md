## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco released their Nov acceptance and ATC was seen to fix a couple of vessels for Nov liftings, with one heavy butane reported to be placed into India. A week of volatility in paper swap, following the crude. At the time of writing, Nov C3 CP was traded at $580 and C4 is assessed at $13 higher. Nov/Dec CP was at flattish level. Cash premium for AG evenly split cargos was assessed at teens to $20 level due to the robust demand from India and Indonesia.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials for Nov delivered propane softened as of muted discussions. 23kt propane for 2h Nov delivery was offered at Nov FEI minus $3.5 in the window. Outside window, spot inquires remained quiet as petchem importers refrained from buying due to negative margins. Several PDHs were reported to swap or defer their feedstock for Oct delivery to later period. This being said, one trader issued buy tender to secure a full or heavy propane cargo for late Nov to Dec delivery into Ningbo. Another trader was reported to procure a full propane cargo for 1h Dec delivery into Echina at Dec FEI plus mid single digit. Unlike propane, demand for evenly split cargos remained strong. One Indian major issued tender to procure two evenly split cargos from Dec to Feb delivery. And PERTAMINA was also reported to secure similar for early Dec arrival. Other than spot, at least four Chinese Importers have issued term tenders to secure next year’s LPG supply, minimum 2mmt in total. Market players reckoned that it could be a good timing as recent decline in freight might lead to cheaper premium, and which might not sustain for long.
