## Market Commentary

MEG Jun propane CP was traded at $508, and butane assessed at $500. One producer trading house was reported to fix over 14 vessels for May lifting, which lent support to the freight whereas exerted pressure on CFR June market with plentiful supply. Qatar Petroleum was reported to award their 8-9 Jun lifting evenly split cargo, which assessed at CP minus mid high 20s against the soaring freight. ADNOC released their Jun lifting acceptance, and it is believed that the freight rates will gain further in view of the front tightness in shipping.

**FAR EAST**

 Window is muted with limited bid and offers. Off window a couple of 23kt propane for 2h Jun delivery were changed hand at FEI plus $1-2. Apart from that, a handful of 46kt propane parcels into China for similar period were reported concluded at June FEI plus $6-7, mostly for PDH use. Cracking wise, one China major managed to cover a late Jun delivered propane cargo into Hainan reportedly. And another Korean importer also tendered to buy a late Jun delivered 23kt butane. Other than petchem use, a couple of players in Schina are also inquiring for evenly split cargos. Additionally, offers for Iranian cargo into China is reported to edge higher and some traditional users shifted purchase from such origin to normal stems, which help absorbed the abundant supply from both AG and US. With limited vessel availability and unstable program, we believe importers will keep eyes on non-Iran origins at a competitive price.
