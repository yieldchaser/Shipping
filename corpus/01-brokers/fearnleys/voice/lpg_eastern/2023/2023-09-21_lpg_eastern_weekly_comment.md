## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi released their acceptance with no cuts but also no plus tolerance for term liftings. KPC sold a 12/13 Oct lifting full propane via tender. Evenly split cargos are still in tight supply and was bid at the equivalence of CP plus mid 30s in the window. At the time of writing, Oct C3 CP was traded at $569 and C4 at $10 higher.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium for 23kt propane parcels for Oct delivery inched up this week as less offers available, together with the US loading delays and the soaring freight. Such parcel was traded in window during mid-week at Oct FEI plus $0.5. 46kt propane for 1h Nov delivery was reported concluded at Nov FEI plus low single digit basis NChina. Off window a flurry of tenders ongoing for both propane and butane. Last Friday FPC awarded their buy tender for an evenly split cargo delivered in 2h Oct at Oct MOPJ minus high teens. And Pertamina tendered to buy two cargos of such grade for Nov delivery, while IOC also reported to procure same for both Nov and Dec delivery. Other than butane, three PDH users issued tender to secure both Oct and Nov delivered propane as feedstock. Apart from spot, one Chinese Major also issued term tender to procure monthly 46/0 for 2024 delivery and we believe more importers will start to renew their negotiate on new terms soon.
