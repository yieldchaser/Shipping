## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco announced Sep CP at $550 for C3 and $560 for C4, up by $80 and $100 respectively. The CP surge is resulted by the tight supply especially on butane grade, one reason owing to the oil cuts, and the other on Indian import increments. Oct C3 CP was assessed at $570 at the time of writing, $20 higher than Sep, reflecting the strong forward market. One Indian importer was reported to cancel their buy spot tender for Oct lifting due to high offers.

**FAR EAST**

 Muted discussions on 23/0 for 2h Sep and 1h Oct delivery in the window. Off window 46/0 for 1h Oct delivery was concluded between traders at Oct FEI minus low single digit. In contrary to the quiet window, a handful of tenders are floating in the market this week. Despite the squeezed margins, petchem players emerge to secure feedstock for late Sep and Oct or even Q4 delivery. One NChina PDH secured a 46/0 for Oct delivery at Oct FEI plus high single digit via tender reportedly. Another EChina PDH user also managed to secure some propane parcels for Q4 delivery into Yantze river at FEI plus double-digit premiums. One SChina PDH also tendered to buy 23/0 for late Sep/early Oct delivery into Qinzhou, which is due next week. Taiwan FPCC issued two buy tenders to secure one 44/0 parcel des Ningbo in Oct and one flexible ratio on C3/C4 delivered to Mailiao during late Sep to early Oct, which was reported only awarded the propane parcel at Oct FEI plus high single digit. With soaring costs, some PDH users might consider rate cuts but again new capacity released in Q4 might somehow offset the demand loss.
