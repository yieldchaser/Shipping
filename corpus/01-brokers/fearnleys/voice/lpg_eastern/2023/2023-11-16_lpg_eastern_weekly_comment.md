## Market Commentary

MEG Qatar Petroleum was reported to sell a 4-15 Dec lifting full propane cargo to a Japanese importer. Evenly split cargo for Dec lifting was assessed at CP plus $10 though yet to hear any deal concluded. At the time of writing, Dec C3 CP was traded at $609 and C4 was traded at $12 higher. Dec/Jan CP was traded at $6 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials continued to fall on weak sentiment. A couple of 23kt propane parcels for 2h Dec was offered in window at Dec FEI minus 1, while off window earlier such parcels were changed hand at Dec FEI plus small digit. Outside window trades still in slow motion, as of depressing petchem demand against the negative margins, coupled with uncertainties on route diversion for US loadings. One cracker issued buy tender to secure 23/0 des Ningbo during 21-30 Dec, while the rest petchem players remain quiet and wait-and-see. On the other hand, butane demand remains robust with both Indian and Indonesian importers buying. Indonesia PERTAMINA reissued tender to secure an evenly split cargo for 16-20 Dec FOB ex AG or CFR 1-5 Jan. Indian BPCL also issued buy tender for monthly two and a half cargo from Dec to Feb delivery.
