## Market Commentary

MEG Premium for evenly split cargos rebounded this week, with Feb lifting FOBs traded at CP flattish level reportedly. The reason being was the tighter supply due to crude cuts and some planned maintenance from AG producers, which volume affected yet to be known. At the time of writing, Jan C3 CP was assessed at $617 and C4 at $15 higher. Jan/Feb CP was traded at $2 contango.

**FAR EAST**

 Spot discounts deepened amid muted discussions. Off window 23kt propane for 2h Jan delivery was reported traded at Jan FEI minus mid 20s, and bids or offers were barely seen in the window, perhaps due to the coming holiday. Outside window several petchem players emerged to procure feedstock propane for Jan to early Feb delivery. One SChina PDH user secured 46kt propane for Jan delivery at Jan CP plus high 40s, while another two Echina based players procured 23kt propane each at Jan FEI minus mid-teens. One Korean importer also tendered to buy 23kt propane for early Feb delivery. To the contrary of spot discussion, term negotiations in China are stagnant as of the high cash differentials caused by the rising freight and potential supply deficit. The poor margins in both petchem and residential segment have refrained the buying interests and we might see active spot inquiries for later Feb delivery (after CNY period).
