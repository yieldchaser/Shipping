## Market Commentary

MEG Discussing level for evenly split cargo softened as Indian and Indonesia demand were covered gradually. Latest BPCL was heard to have awarded Jan and Feb liftings at small premium to CP. However, similar grade sold into Indonesia was reported done on CFR basis, at a equivalence of discounts to CP. Dec C3 CP was assessed at $600 at the time of writing. And due to front butane inquiries, Dec C3/C4 still maintained at -$15. Dec/Jan C3 was traded at $8 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 23/0 parcel for 2h Dec was heard changed hand at Dec FEI minus mid-single digit, while some Cananda origin parcels for same period were sold at minus low-mid teens basis Chiba reportedly. Similar cargo for 1h Jan delivery was bid at Jan FEI plus $6 in window and was heard traded at plus $8 off window. A handful of tenders are floating this week. PERTAMINA was reported awarded their Jan procurement tender at Jan CP plus $90s to $100 on CFR basis. One Vietnam-based PDH plant issued tender to buy 23/0 or 46/0 for Dec to 1st decade Jan delivery. One EChina petchem player tendered to buy 0/23 for 1st decade Jan delivery. One EChina cracker issued new tender to procure 23/0 or 46/0 for 3-10 Jan delivery into Ningbo, which had secured 46/0 for 2h Dec delivery at Dec FEI minus mid-teens via previous tender. Taiwan FPCC tendered to procure 0-22kt C3 and 22kt C4 for 2h Dec delivery. One 33/11 for end Dec lifting ex Australia was reported placed into Japan. As limited term been signed for next year, we will expect active spot inquiries from petchem players, on the premise of maintaining basic operating rates.
