## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco set Feb CP at $790 for both C3 and C4, hiked up by $200 for C3 and $185 for C4, respectively. Such increment was resulted by the supply cuts due to port maintenance and the delays caused by fog weather in US, together with the robust buying for Feb arrival volumes driven mainly from petchem. Swap collapsed after the Feb CP rally, and Mar CP was traded at $673 for C3 and C4 at $10 less. Spot discussions for evenly split cargos remained active as traders need to cover shorts into India/SEA and currently bids for Mar loaders were reported at minus mid high single digit to double digits discounts, while offers heard at small discounts or even small premium over CP.

**FAR EAST**

 Strong bids for propane delivered in 1h Mar this week as of limited supply. Till Thurs, 46kt propane parcel was still bid at Mar FEI plus 90ish but offers yet to see. Off window such parcel was reported concluded at Feb FEI plus 40s which equated to Mar FEI plus 130s level by then. Premium for 2h Mar softened, as offer shown at Mar CP plus mid 50s equating to Mar FEI plus 90s. A couple of 46kt propane for 2h Mar delivery were changed hand at Feb FEI plus teens reportedly. Due to the negative margins, PDHs in China started to cut rates or advanced their scheduled maintenance to avoid the costly feedstock purchase, and we expected a min 200kt loss in propane demand. However, shall the flat price come off more to generate marginal profits, PDHs might improve their run rate afterwards accordingly, especially with the expectation of economic recovery from post-Covid. One EChina PDH user cancelled buy tender for a 1st decade Mar delivery propane into Ningbo due to high offer. Another Chinese also tendered to buy a 15-28th Mar delivered 22kt propane parcel into Taixing which due next week.
