## Market Commentary

MEG A handful of FOB spot sales from producers emerged this week, hence the July CP dropped to $431 and Jul C3/C4 spread widened to $25. KPC issued a couple of tenders for heavy butane cargos, lifting at 2-3 Jul and 9-10th Jul respectively, among which one was reported done at CP minus 50s. QP also tendered to sell evenly split or heavy propane ratio cargo for 17-21th July loading. Players are waiting for Saudi’s acceptance to have the full scheme on AG supply.

**FAR EAST**

 Nonstop buying tenders bring the hustle and bustle back; however, the awarded cash premiums declined this week, reflecting on the abundant supply in either propane or butane. 23kt propane for Jul delivery into Ningbo was concluded at Jul FEI plus low single via tender, and 46kt parcel into Yantai was also reported done at Jul FEI plus small premium, while similar parcel into Chiba was said at Jul FEI minus high single digit. By the time of writing, we are still waiting for one Echina PDH to close their procurement tender for 23kt propane for late July early Aug delivery. And we are expecting a few more PDH users to procure more feedstock, perhaps for stockpiling. Apart from propane, one Chinese also tendered to buy 66-88kt LPG mainly evenly split or heavy butane cargo for their cracking feedstock for Jul delivery, yet to know the result. There are other inquiries for 11/11 parcels into SEA or Schina but the spread quite wide, as of the flooded supply from Iran and some AG producer, against high freight costs. On window, 23kt propane for 2h Jul delivery changed hand at Jul FEI minus $1 and 11/11 parcel at Jul FEI minus $14. Propane parcel for 1h Aug delivery was bid at Aug FEI minus $5 yet to see offers. Indian BPCL finally called spot tender to procure one or two evenly split cargos for Aug delivery. Indian spot demand on import remains slow in first half of the year. Hopefully, the curtailed price on cylinder and extended subsidy can promote some residential demand.
