## Market Commentary

MEG Value for evenly split cargo on Jan lifting has dived into negative territory at CP minus high single, as of subdued interests from India and Indonesia. Jan C3 CP was traded at $600 at the time of writing and C4 assessed at $14 higher. Jan/Feb C3 CP was traded at $14 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials plunged further this week as limited buying interests on 23kt propane parcel basis Chiba. Such parcel for 1h Jan delivery was offered at Jan FEI minus low 20s, while similar parcel for 2h Jan is in better contango, with bid at minus mid teens versus offer at minus mid single. To the contrary, active inquiries from China this week as of improved margin against the declined flat price on feedstock coupled with good discounts. Several full propane cargos for Jan delivery were changed hand and placed into East and North China at FEI minus from high teens to low/mid 20s this week. And there are another two spot buy tenders ongoing for in total 96kt C3 during Jan delivery into Ningbo. With shortage of term supply, we might expect active spot inquiries from Petchem players on spot market, on the premise of normal operation. Other than spot, the descent in freight have triggered importers to negotiate on next year term supply. We have seen three Chinese importers emerged this week for term inquires, mainly to secure petchem feedstock.
