## Market Commentary

MEG Though crude rallied up, Jun C3 CP was still traded below $500 and at the time of writing at $493, and C4 was assessed at $5 less as long supply out of AG. FOB discounts deepened further, against the lukewarm demand and soaring freight. Evenly split ratio for Jun lifting was assessed at CP minus 30s even into 40s. Qatar Petroleum was reported to award their sale tender for 19/20th Jun lifting heavy propane at FEI netback value. And one Oil major also tendered to sell an evenly split cargo for mid Jun lifting. Saudi Aramco finally released their Jun lifting acceptance, and again players have concerns over their surplus spot sales.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium slightly inched up in window and 23kt propane for 2h Jun delivery was bid at Jun FEI plus $5, partially due to the rising freight. However off window buying inquiries appear thin, as most have finalized June program and domestic consumption slowed down for the coming slack season amid high inventory for residential sector . One producer trading arm was said to have placed a mid-Jun arrival propane this week at CP plus less than $30, and it was seen to have fixed at least three vessels for Jun liftings ex AG. One full propane parcel for 2h Jun delivery was placed into Echina at Jun FEI plus high single digit. PDH margins seems healthy for now, and the run rates in China have improved to 80% in general. Two new PDHs each 600kt/y, located in SChina and EChina respectively, have started trial run and produced on spec propylene this week. However, one Schina PDH accidentally shut down and might need one month for maintenance and it was reported reselling their propane for Jun arrival.
