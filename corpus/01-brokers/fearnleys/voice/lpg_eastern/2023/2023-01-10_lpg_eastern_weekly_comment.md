## Market Commentary

MEG Feb C3 CP surged up to $627 and C4 was traded at $15 higher, reflecting the front strength, partially resulted by some reported maintenance plans from Yanbu and ADNOC terminals. Feb/Mar was traded at $37 backwardation. Spot discounts for Feb lifting evenly split cargo was traded at minus $13 recently. Qatar Petroleum tendered to sell one cargo for 20-23rd Feb lifting in either 4/0 or 3/1 or 2/2 ratio.

**FAR EAST**

 Several 23kt propane parcels for 2h Feb delivery were actively traded, with one reported concluded at FEI plus $33 earlier this week. Similar bids were shown in window but offers unseen. Supply for 1h Feb delivery appeared tight and 1h/2h Feb spread was assessed at $20 backwardation. After last week’s tender fiesta, this week the premium continued to soar up as of another flurry of tenders emerging. Unfortunately, cargos for 1h Feb delivery were relatively tight and three PDH buy tenders seeking 1h Feb volumes were reported cancelled. Only one N.China PDH user was reported to buy outside tender for a 23kt propane parcel for 1h Feb delivery at Feb FEI or CP plus 80s or 90s level. And one Vietnam-based importer managed to secure 46kt propane for end Jan to 1h Feb delivery into Vung Tau reportedly. One EChina PDH user was reported to procure one 46kt propane parcel for 2h Feb delivery at Feb CP plus 50-60s. One NChina petchem user tendered to buy flexible ratio for Feb delivery. Another EChina Cracker also tendered out to buy one to two lots of 46kt LPG for Feb delivery. Additionally, Taiwan CPC tendered to buy 11/11 for 19-28th Feb delivery, which still in validity. Apart from spot, one NChina PDH user also secured a mini term for 46kt propane monthly from Feb to Apr delivery via tender.
