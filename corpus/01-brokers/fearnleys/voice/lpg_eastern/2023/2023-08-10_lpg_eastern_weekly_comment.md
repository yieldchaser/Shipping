**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials turns into positive territory as of traders shorts in window against the delays caused by Panama congestion. 23kt propane parcel for 2h Sep delivery was bid at minus small single digit while off window plus small premium was reported done. Sep FEI/Cp continued to enlarge and by the time of writing it was traded over $100. One Vietnam based PDH user issued buy tender for Sep delivery propane but the result was not clear yet, as the previous procurement tender cancelled due to high offers. 46kt propane for Sep delivery into Ningbo was placed at Sep FEI plus low single digit and discussing level increased furthermore due to limited offers as well. Other than such, there were three 1h/mid Sep arrival Iranian cargos available via tender this week and some Chinese players are eyeing on such instead of looking at non-Iranian origins which priced at $40 higher easily.
