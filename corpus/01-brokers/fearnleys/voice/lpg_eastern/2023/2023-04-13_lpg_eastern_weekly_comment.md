## Market Commentary

MEG May CP rallied up following crude gains, and C3 was traded at $578 at the time of writing, while C4 at $6 cheaper. ADNOC released their May lifting program with some delays reported. And players are waiting for Saudi acceptance to know the full scheme. Discussions for evenly split cargo are active especially KPC was reported to sell two cargos at CP minus mid-teens to mid-20s respectively for 1-8th May lifting via tender. QP also tendered to sell end May lifting 3/1 ratio.

**FAR EAST**

 Busy week for Asian players after Easter Holiday. A handful of 23kt parcels for 2h May delivery were changed hand from flattish to small discounts to May FEI. Off window a flurry of tenders is floating in the market, for both petchem and cracking users. With marginal profits for PDHs, several Chinese plants have emerged to secure feedstock. 46kt propane for 1h or mid May delivery were traded at May FEI plus teens as of delays caused by Panama congestion, while similar parcel for 2h May delivery at low mid single digit only. Cracking wise, Taiwan FPCC awarded their buy tender for 2h May delivery at May MOPJ minus around $105. Demand for evenly split cargos remained robust as well entering into summer, coupled with talks for disruptions in May supply from Iran due to shipment delays, which lent support to butane. 11/11 for May were traded at May CP plus 40s into SEA and SChina. And CPC also tendered to procure 11/11 DES Shenao and/or Kaohsiung 22-31 May.
