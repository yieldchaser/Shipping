## Market Commentary

MEG Mar C3 CP was traded at $709, rallied up again with the front robust demand. Mar C4 was traded at parity to C3. However, Apr C3 was traded at wider backwardation at $65 to Mar CP. Market players are waiting for Adnoc and Saudi Aramco acceptance with expectations on cuts from Adnoc. Evenly split cargo for Mar lifting was traded at Mar CP minus mid-single digit and seller are asking small premium this week.

**FAR EAST**

 A handful of propane for 2h Mar delivery were changed hand and mostly placed into Japan at Feb FEI plus teens or Mar FEI plus 80s range. Supply looks tighter with most cargos are absorbed into far east and there are only bids seen in window for 2h Mar delivery parcels. One evenly split cargo for early Mar lifting ex Australia was placed into SChina at Mar FEI plus 80s basis Chiba equivalence. Another oil Major was also reported to have procured a 11/11 parcel for 2h Feb to 1h Mar delivery into Malaysia via tender. Taiwan CPC also tendered to buy 22-31st Mar delivered 11/11 parcel. Despite the poor margins, China petchem players, including both PDH and cracker users are inquiring propane for Mar delivery. 23kt propane for 1h Mar delivery was traded into EChina at Mar FEI plus over $110 and 46kt parcel for 2h Mar delivery into Ningbo was concluded at Mar FEI plus high 90s reportedly. However, another two EChina based PDH users cancelled their buy tender for Mar arrival propane due to the high offers.
