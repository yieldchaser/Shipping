## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco set Mar CP at $720 for C3 and $740 for C4, declined by $70 and $50, respectively. C3 was almost in line with expectation but C4 is slightly higher than recommended. Apr C3 CP was traded at deep backwardation at $640, and C4 CP at $10 higher. Evenly split cargo discussions quiet down with few inquiries into India, and value for Apr loaders was expected at double digit discounts to Apr CP. Qatar Petroleum released their Apr term lifting acceptances with some delays reported, as some players nominated early dates to avoid backwardations for 40$ into May.

**FAR EAST**

 Lack of activities in window this week as players away for IE week in London. Off window some sellers are still reported to have unsold Mar delivery tons, perhaps will roll into Apr delivery instead. One AG producer was reported to offer two 2h Mar lifting evenly split cargos for Apr delivery. Another evenly split cargo for 4-5th Apr lifting ex Darwin was also offered via tender. Despite weakening sentiment this week, we’ve seen a handful of buy tenders for petchem use. One EChina cracker user procured 23kt propane for 1-10th Apr delivery at Apr FEI plus high 30s. Another EChina PDH user also issued buy tender to secure 3 lots of 23kt propane for Apr and May delivery. Taiwan FPCC also tendered to procure flexible ratio LPG for any Apr delivery.
