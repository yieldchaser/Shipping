## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco today announced September Propane CP at $350, this being $20 less than August, Butane September CP came at $360, flat to August. Paper swaps had been traded higher this week, due to prompt demand from both India and Indonesia for September liftings of equal ratio cargos. After the September CP announcement, October CP traded at up to $357, October FEI/CP quickly narrowed to $21 from a previous $30ish. One European trader reportedly sold a 20-25 September lifting of full cargo C3 to a Chinese importer at discount to Oct CP.

**FAR EAST**

: A few deals were concluded in the window this week as traders had to cover shorts. 23k Propane were traded at September FEI minus $11ish. A similar cargo for 1h October delivery were offered at Oct FEI minus $16, buyers are still to come forward. Indonesia’s Pertamina awarded an evenly split 2/2 delivery into Kalbut during 4-6th October, reportedly at October CP plus high 30s. And new tender for same ratio cargo at 8-10th October delivery into Teluk Semankga has closed but result is yet to be known. India’s HPCL also issued prompt tender for end Aug and Early Sep delivered equal ratio cargoes, which they didn’t manage to award. Later they reissued buy tender for 45kt equal ratio cargo to be delivered during 11 Sep to 5 Oct, and a 21k equal ratio cargo for 21-31 Sep delivery. Comparatively, the Chinese market appeared quiet this week as most Buyers have already covered their September positions and waiting for September CP announcement before taking October positions. One end September/early October arrival of full Propane is reported to have been sold to China at October CP plus low mid-40s. One E.China petchem user is reported to have purchased 23kt Propane for 2h September delivery at September CP plus low 50s level. China custom data shows August imports at close to 2mmt, which is the highest monthly import level in 2019 so far.
