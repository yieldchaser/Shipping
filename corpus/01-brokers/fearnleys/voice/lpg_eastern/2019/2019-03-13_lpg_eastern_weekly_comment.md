## Market Commentary

**MEG:** April CP swap’s have seen volatility throughout this week with $10 up and down swings on daily basis, reflecting continued uncertainty in the front market and differing views with regard to future CP value. April MEG liftings continue being traded at double digits premium to CP and still inquires remain outstanding. Spurred by demand from India and Indonesia especially. Term lifters are still awaiting Saudi acceptances, which should come over the weekend, before taking additional decisions. At the time of writing, April propane CP is traded at $488 and butane at around $20 higher.

**FAR EAST**

: There is big a big gap in price between cargoes delivered in 1h April versus 2h April. This Thursday 23kt propane for 2h Apr was concluded at balance March FEI flat, and other bids for similar cargo are at April FEI plus $12. However, the CFR market for 1h April delivery continue being very active, with bidding at April FEI +$34 while offering stands at April CP +$47, this equates to April FEI plus $60 at current values. The delays of US origin tonnes saw no relief this week, causing further backwadation between 1st and 2nd half arrival cargoes. India’s BPCL issued buy tenders totaling 85kt in total volume for April delivery. Indonesia’s Pertamina tendered to buy a 44kt split cargo for delivery in 1h April, the result is unknown at time of writing. FPC withdrew their buy tender for 22k or 44k propane for delivery 6-20 April as no suitable offer was received for this period. A couple of propane and heavy propane cargos have been sold into China, with premium concluded reported to be above April CP +$40’s. There are still outstanding inquires for 2h April delivery but buyer’s sentiment is weaker because of slow domestic distribution and eroding margins with surging costs.
