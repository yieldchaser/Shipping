## Market Commentary

MEG Inquiries for FOB cargoes of equal ratio split has prevailed in the market during this week but few October liftings are available. One Asian Major was reported to have resold their November equal ratio cargo at November CP plus $3. On Thursday one Chinese player was out trying to sell their early November lifting of 2/2 ratio at November CP plus $8, but nothing has been concluded yet. Because to the tightness of MEG supply amidst strong demand, October FEI/CP has been narrowing rapidly. In fact, October CP has surpassed FEI by $5-7 during Thursday’s trading. October CP stands firm at $420, despite crude softening throughout this week. November’s CP is traded at $9-10 below October but we believe it might catch up if the tightness of MEG supply continues.

**FAR EAST**

 The aftermath of the Saudi incident has continuously been supporting the CFR East market price. Traders have been busy securing US origin cargoes to cover their shorts in the SE Asia markets and active bids and trades have been shown up in the window all of this week. FOBs ex MEG for specific dates are being bid at October CP plus from small single digit to teens premium, depending on the ratio and loading dates. 23kt Propane for 2h October delivery has been traded a couple of times at Oct FEI minus $3 to $5. India’s IOC and BPCL have issued buy tenders consecutively to cover front shorts for October delivery but it seems they were unable to secure prompt availabilities. Indonesia’s Pertamina is reported to have secured an October delivery of equal ratio at October CP plus low mid 40s and one similar cargo for November delivery at high 40s, an additional tender for an evenly split cargo for 1h November delivery into Teluk Semangka remain uncovered. Taiwan’s FPCC is reported to have awarded their buy tender for a full propane cargo with delivery mid Nov at Nov MOPJ minus high 60s, Hanwha Total is also reported to have award their private tender at November MOPJ minus 80s for 23k propane delivered early November. Another hot issue this week is that on Wednesday US levied more sanctions against several Chinese companies and individuals. It has aroused players’ attentions in the forward market into China, especially when China is the biggest single destination for absorption of Iranian oil and gas.
