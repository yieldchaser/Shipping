## Market Commentary

**MEG:** There are plenty of FOB spot cargoes available from producers and traders as resales, some of which have already been concluded. Qatar Petroleum reportedly awarded a full Propane for 31th July to 2th Aug lifting at Aug CP minus high single digit. Kuwait closed their sale tender for a Butane heavy cargo for lifting 11-12 August today with result yet to be known. ADNOC released their August acceptances with some liftings being advanced. Considering front high freight rates and forward contango market, no wonder lifters like to nominate and get latter liftings. Trader resale are also being actively discussed for cargoes across the whole month. One trader is offering early Aug lifting 2/2 at minus 10$ in window as well. At the time of writing, August CP has risen by nearly $20 to close to $390, and September at $8 higher.

**FAR EAST**

: Supply appear to be in abundance. We have seen offers for 2h August Propane at AFEI minus $5-6, while bids are at minus $10. The Origin spread has enlarged dramatically from barely zero to small digits three weeks ago, to back around $20s, mirroring the length of US tons in the front. With large stocks and slow domestic consumption in July, Chinese Buyers have remained quiet for 1h August, that was until end this week. One domestic importer and one PDH user were reported to have covered with some spot purchases. PDH users are now mainly exploring 2h Aug delivery tons. Despite the high freight and with limited buyer interests, price discussion levels have come off significantly from 70s in last two weeks to 50s for Propane, while equal ratio is even lower. China’s 2nd biggest LPG importer has issued term tender to purchase one 2/2 or 3/1 cargo monthly from October 2019 to June 2020. The result is yet to know. FPCC awarded their buy tender to secure a 11/11 for mid August delivery at Aug MOPJ minus around $105. Following this, FPCC issued term tender to cover 22/0 or 44/0 monthly with delivery from October 2019 to September 2020, which closes this Friday. Last time they’ve awarded one 44/0 monthly from July 2019 to June 2020 at MOPJ minus low 70s.
