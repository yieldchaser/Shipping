## Market Commentary

**MEG:** After the large price falls throughout this week, Crude rebounded on Thursday afternoon, trickered by the two tanker explosions in the Gulf of Oman. Following this, LPG Propane prices rose to $348, reviving a bit from overnight’s slide to below $340. Butane maintains a discount of about $20 against Propane, which is (still) unusual for a typical summer season. This is a testament to the fact that Butane’s length from producers continue. With ample supply from Middle East and sluggish demand from end users, July CP is still under pressure. Add to this the seemingly ever increasing freight rates, this has killed many buyers’ appetite, mid-teens discounts appear insufficient to trigger speculative trades as well. Adnoc released their July acceptances with some liftings being advanced. Qatar Petroleum issued a sales tender for 7-9th July lifting on full propane cargo basis. Saudi Aramco is reported to still have a late June 2/2 cargo available for sale.

**FAR EAST**

: Quiet week with only a few physical deals being concluded this week. Off window a 23kt Propane cargo for 2h July delivery changed hands at July FEI plus $6-$8, which equates to July CP plus mid high 30s. Non-US origin equal ratio cargoes are assessed at a similar value, while Propane is still at a premium of plus 50s level, this mainly driven by PDH demand which still enjoy substantial profit margins in an otherwise sluggish market. The forward traded market into August is currently at a slight backwardation, as the front still gains support due to delays caused by US freeport FM and Nederland turnarounds. Some players expect the premiums to come off further with the anticipation of plentiful arbitrage cargoes reaching Eastern markets in August, along with cheaper MB price and a perceived weaker demand in the East.
