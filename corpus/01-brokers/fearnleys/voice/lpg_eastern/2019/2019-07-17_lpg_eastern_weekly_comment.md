## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco released their August acceptances with several equal ratio cargoes for early month lifting while the predominant heavy Propane at end month. KPC is reported to have awarded their 13/14th August FOB sale tender at August CP minus mid/high teens. FOB values have now been under pressure for a while with freight standing firm and stagnant demand from end user Buyers. However, with contango into forward market and possibly less supply from Iran, we believe the situation might improve. At time of writing, August CP is traded at $3. Contango into Sep has narrowed but still maintained at $3, compared to last week’s $7-9.

**FAR EAST**

: The market is recovering from the quietness of last week with a handful of deals concluded, off window mainly. Full cargo Propane for 2h Aug delivery is reported to have been traded at FEI minus low teens, while half cargo at minus high single to $10. 23k propane for 1h Sep delivery is reported to have done at FEI minus $5.5. With front length being almost absorbed, this has enhanced the discussing levels gradually. Nevertheless, the FEI discount levels may stay for the time being with continued abundant arb tons from US and still limited outlets. For Non-Us cargo, one E.China PDH Buyer have tendered to buy 2h August Propane, result is yet to be known. A few other Chinese petchem users are also looking for Propane for similar arrival period. One Chinese traditional domestic importer is currently inquiring for an equal ratio cargo with 2h August delivery. In addition, one SEA importer explores for heavy Butane or half cargo butane for 2h August delivery. FPC awarded their term tender for one full cargo Propane per month from Oct 2019 to Sep 2020 at MOPJ minus about high 70’s.
