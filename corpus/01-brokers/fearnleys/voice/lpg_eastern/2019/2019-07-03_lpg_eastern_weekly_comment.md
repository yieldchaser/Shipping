## Market Commentary

**MEG:** As far as deals are concerned, it has been a quiet week in the MEG. The big news was the announcement of the Saudi Aramco Contract Price for July of USD 375/MT for Propane and USD 355/MT for the Butane. The Yanbu discount was set at USD 13 pmt, reflecting the strong VLGC freight market. Forward numbers have been dropping throughout the week, with a small rise at the end of the week, basically shadowing the Crude futures. Most MEG based producers have additional cargoes for sale, with 2 of them having floated sales tenders. One being 3 x 22+22 kt loading 30-31 July, 25-26 Aug and 15-19 Sept, respectively. The other being for 45 kt c3 loading 31 July/02 Aug. It will be interesting to the outcome of these. In addition, India based importers are trying to delay their contractual MEG FOB volumes, because of the slowing down of import requirements.

**FAR EAST**

: It is not, nor has it been a busy week in the Eastern CFR markets. With the HK LPG conference having completed last week, there was hopes for an active return but this was quickly squashed. The petchem demand is very low with cracker turnarounds in August, so no reprieve from that side. The prices in the East continue to be under pressure, which will likely continue with Indian slowdowns, excess MEG supply and US origin cargoes still arriving at pace. Strong VLGC freight’s doesn’t make life easier for Sellers or Buyers and the tariff’s just continue the inefficiencies. Propane still enjoy strong demand from PDH operators, which is expected to continue for the near term, at least.
