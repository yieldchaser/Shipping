## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco’s Jan lifting acceptance has astonished the whole market, with cancellations/delays/cuts/grade changes happening. This being said, the front supply has tightened up further. Due to this, swap value has shot up again this week. At the time of writing, Jan propane CP is traded at $507, $67 higher than Jan CP, and butane rose up to $531 with continuous $25 gap. Feb CP chased up, traded at $487 with $20 backwardation, and we believe the spread might narrow down further as Feb supply doesn’t look abundant either. Qatar Petroleum was reported to award their sell tender for 27/29th lifting full C3 to one Chinese importer at CP plus high single premium.

**FAR EAST**

: Very eventful market this week before Christmas, with various bids in/off window on either US/non-US and tenders floating. Propane for 2h Jan delivery is bid at Jan FEI plus mid 30s or flat price $564 while no seller seen. Mid-Jan lifting equal ratio cargos is being bid at $515/545 but again nothing concluded. Equal ratio demand appears strong with SEA inquiries. One Vietnam importer has issued buy tender to secure two parcels of equal ratio cargos delivered in 21st Dec till 1h Jan and 25th Jan to 10th Feb, and the result is yet to know. Meanwhile, Indonesia Pertamina has issued new tender to secure two evenly split cargo as well, but delivered during early and mid Feb, which valid till tomorrow. However, demand into China is still mainly driven by propane for petchem use. One SChina Importer has bought 2h Jan arrival 23/0 at mid-80s, luckily we would say, since apart from this, still several rigid demand haven’t been covered. Despite the squeezed margins on PDHs, some plants with downstream complex still have profits and keep exploring cargos. However, several failed to award their buy tenders for propane delivered from 2h Jan till 1h Feb as in lack of offers. Some readjust the program and try deferring into Feb purchase, otherwise might need to lower down run rate before running out of feedstock.
