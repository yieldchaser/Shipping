## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Another volatile week as flat values continued sliding, following the crude. June propane CP is now trading at $424 at the time of writing, almost $100 less than May CP. Butane is traded at $18 less. KPC is reportedly awarded their 4/5th June 2/2 at June CP minus mid high single digit. Qatar tendered to sell a 3/1 for 26/28th June lifting at the pricing on BL dates, with the backwardation to July CP being at more than teens this doesn’t look attractive to buyers unless it comes with an attached substantial discount, more so considering the high freight and sluggish demand in Eastern CFR markets.

**FAR EAST**

: Premiums has come off quickly this week as US export resumes operation and supply tensions getting eased. On Thursday a handful of deals was concluded in the window. 23k propane for 2h June delivery was traded at June FEI plus $11 and July FEI plus $12, while similar cargo for 1h July was done at July FEI plus $6, which reveals the forward length. There are some cargos being placed into China at a lower premium this week. Market appears long with abundant equal ratio cargos, some of which being surplus from India importers. Propane is maintaining it’s higher value with requirement from PDH buyer. 23k propane was sold at June CP plus low 50s while 11/11 was done at $15-20 lower. One domestic importer is reported to have resold a 2/2 cargo for 2h June delivery at June CP plus 30s level. Indonesia’s Pertamina appear to have awarded their two 2/2 cargoes for July delivery at June CP plus high 20s to low 30s. FPC awarded their spot tender for a 33/11 cargo delivered during 1st half July at July MOPJ minus around $105. Previously they’ve awarded their term tender reported at MOPJ minus 70s for 44k C3 monthly from July 2019 to June 2020.
