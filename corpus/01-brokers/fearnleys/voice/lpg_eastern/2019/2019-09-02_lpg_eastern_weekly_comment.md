## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Another volatile week with Oct CP initially plunging to $346, only to rebound to $357 today, following the crude rise. With several buying tenders into SE Asia, more or less promptly, FOB buying interests surged but few deals have been traded as lifters are awaiting the acceptances to get full picture. There was one trader bidding Oct lifting at flexible dates for a 2/2 FOB cargo at Oct CP minus $1. Butane have undoubtedly gained support as most inquiries are mainly for equal ratio split. At the time of writing, Oct butane is traded $372.

**FAR EAST**

: Seemingly slim interest with limited bids/offers in window. Outside window 23k propane for 1h Oct delivery was reported to have been traded at Oct FEI minus $18/19 CP, this equates to Oct CP plus small digit premium only. 1h/2h Oct maintains at $3 contango but it may easily be erased as additional US exports are scheduled for arrival in Q4. Otherwise it’s a week with tenders being floated. India’s IOC issued buy tender for two evenly-split cargoes for delivery during mid Oct and last decade Oct respectively. Indonesia’s Pertamina have also issued tender to secure two evenly-split cargoes for delivered into Kalbut during late Sep and late Oct respectively, considering both FOB and CFR alternatives. Taiwan’s FPCC issued a buy tender to secure an early Oct 11/11 or 22/0 arrival. And it was reported to have been awarded at Oct MOPJ minus mid 70’s One China’s largest Importers issued a buy tender for one full or Propane heavy cargo to be delivered monthly from Oct 2019 to Sept 2020, FEI pricing to be used. Other Chinese importers are exploring Oct arrival cargoes, with equal ratio discussed at Oct CP plus 40s, and Propane or Propane heavy possibly at higher premia as this include PDH users.
