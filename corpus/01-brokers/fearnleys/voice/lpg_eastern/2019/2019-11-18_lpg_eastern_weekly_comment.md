## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Another volatile week with flat price rolling up and down by $15 on daily basis. At the time of writing, Dec CP is traded at $417, while butane at $15 higher. Forward month is in backwardation as Dec/Jan CP maintains at $5/6. Despite such, Jan market is expected to improve as real winter demand kicks in. Fob discussions for 1h Dec are still at discounted level, with last mid Dec FOB sale from KPC reporting at Dec CP minus mid-single digit. One Producer is said to have spot availability in 1h Dec lifting.

**FAR EAST**

: Window is not very exciting with sellers asking Dec FEI plus $8 for 2h Dec 23/0, while off window deal level for similar cargo at plus $3-3.5 only. Most players have balanced their Dec position and Japan/Korean importers are not keen on speculative buying considering year-end stock. Off window market is quite active with tenders flowing and Chinese covering shorts especially for petchem use. Indonesia Pertamina was reported awarded one equal ratio cargo for 1h Jan delivery at Dec CP plus 50s, and following this, they’ve reissued new buy tender to purchase four evenly-split cargos delivered from early to 2h Jan. However, other evenly-split cargo for 2h Dec delivery into SEA is reported done at Dec CP plus high 40s only, as no more buyers can take such early laycan. Spot deals into China this week is more propane heavy related. Last Friday one heavy propane was sold into SChina at Dec CP plus high 50s. And this week, a couple of pure propane were sold at Dec CP plus 60s to around $70, depending on delivery destination, for petchem use. Other than spot, one SChina importer has issued term tender to secure 11/11 monthly, either 1st decade delivery from Jan to Dec, or 2nd to 3rd decade delivery from Mar to Dec.
