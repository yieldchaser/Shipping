## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Despite the crude rise and fall, Propane CP has been quite stable at 340ish until Thursday, it dived to $335 at the time of writing and still traded down. Butane CP maintains $14/15 higher and it’s likely to sustain as of better demand from India. Limited lifting inquiries from last decade Aug into early Sep, amid ample shipping length, have exerted pressure on freight. FOB discussions have been improved to smaller discounts, especially for later Sep lifting cargos, due to weakening freight and slight contango into Oct. Players are still waiting Saudi Aramco’s acceptance to reveal a clearer picture on Sep FOB market.

**FAR EAST**

: Market remains quiet until Thurs, a flurry of offers appear in window with delivery ranging from 1h Sep into 1h Oct. 23k propane for 1h Sep delivery is offered at Sep FEI minus $23 which equates to Sep CP plus $4! 2h Sep delivery cargo is offered at minus $16 while bid at minus $25. And sellers with 1h Oct cargo are asking Oct FEI minus $9/$10. Off window, 2h Sep propane was reported traded at Sep FEI minus $15 earlier this week. As Us arb to east is still open and weak demand from Europe, we believe lifters will continuingly bring tons and east discussion will still be heavily discounted. Other than window, some tenders are flowing in the market. One E.China PDH is reported to issue a buy tender for 23k propane delivered in late Sep, while another E.China petchem user is reported to issue a buy tender for 23k propane monthly delivered from Oct to Dec. There are a few more propane demand in late Sep from other petchem users, but traditional importers are cautious and hesitant to stockpile in such bearish market. India demand is slowly recovering, with one importer have purchased 23k butane for 2h Sep delivery via tender, and some other inquiry for Sep lifting cargos emerge.
