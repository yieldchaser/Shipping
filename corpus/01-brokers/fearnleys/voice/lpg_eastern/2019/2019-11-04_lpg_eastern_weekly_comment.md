## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Despite the crude rise, the Dec CP plunged to $398 at the time of writing, whilst Dec FEI/CP spread fluctuates from $29 to $36. QP has released their Dec lifting nomination with some delays by 5 days or so being reported.

**Baltic**

 continues to rise until mid-week stabilizes at $75ish. This being said, few fixtures happening in MEG and the soaring freight has erodes buyer’s appetite to explore FOB spot.

**FAR EAST**

: This week front market is weakening. Propane for 1H Dec delivery was traded at Dec FEI plus $5 and dropped further to $3 reportedly. 2h Dec also traded in similar level earlier this week, with slight backwardation only. Some importers are closely monitoring year-end stock and avoiding over purchase. Off window there are some traders’ inquires for prompt delivery into India and Indonesia which supports butane price a bit, despite the bids are in discounts on FOB netbacks. One reason being is less butane from US available as arbitrage window closed now, therefore players has turned to explore AG origin. Other than such, last Fri one South China PDH user has awarded their buy tender for 23/0 1st decade delivery in Dec at Dec CP plus around $60, and they’ve issued another buy tender for similar cargo delivered in mid Dec. And one more propane cargo reported sold into North China at Nov CP plus around/below $70, which equates to CP minus mid-single digit discount. Other Chinese also exploring heavy propane cargos in 2h Dec delivery. CPC issued buy tender for 11/11 or 0/11 delivered in late Dec, result is yet to know.
