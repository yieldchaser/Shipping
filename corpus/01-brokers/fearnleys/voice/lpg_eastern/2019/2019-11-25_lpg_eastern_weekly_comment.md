## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco has set Dec Propane CP at $440 and Butane CP at $455, up by $10 respectively, which some players reckon above expectations. Therefore, the contango structure into Jan has changed and Jan CP slides to $438. It’s still early to judge how will Jan CP turns, but we believe the Jan swap will edge up, as forthcoming winter demand will lend support fundamentally.

**FAR EAST**

: Front market appears to be strong this week. Off window Propane for 2h Jan delivery was reported traded at Jan FEI plus high single prem to $10. Dec FEI/CP enlarged to 50ish this week and Jan FEI/CP maintains at high 30s to $40. The unexpected explosion in Texas has roused some concerns on Jan delivery so far limited effect being shown, but players are still closely monitoring the situation. Apart from window, market is floating with all tenders. Indonesia Pertamina was reported to award their last tender for three evenly split cargos delivered from early Jan to late Jan, price ranging from high 40s to high 50s depends on delivery date. And they’ve still inquiring via new tender for another early Jan delivery cargo, which result is yet to know. India IOC is reported to award their buy tender for 2h Jan delivery equal ratio cargo at Jan CP plus 50ish. Taiwan FPCC also secured an early Jan delivery full propane at Jan MOPJ minus around $40 via tender. Other Chinese importers are actively seeking supply for next year via term tender or private discussions. Cash premium negotiation is in line with freight as of non-Us origin request, but more players are keen to discussing FEI link as well, instead of 100% CP link. On spot side, importers start to explore Jan cargos especially 1h Jan arrival, with one SChina PDH user tendered to buy 6-15th Jan delivery propane.
