## Market Commentary

**MEG:** The continuing high freight is killing buyer’s appetite to lift FOBs and we see AG lifters reoffering their FOBs instead of chartering in vessels. One early November lifting 2/2 is being reported sold at November CP minus small digit to a China major who had shipping length available meeting the laycan. Saudi acceptances was finally announced and so far only minor delays being reported. November CP has been sliding most of this week $417 at the time of writing. Reasons for this include high freights and crude softening as well.

**FAR EAST**

: Premium still edged up for Propane with delivery in November in the window. We have seen bids for 23kt Propane to bedelivered in 2h November climing up to high single digit based on November FEI while offers haven’t appeared yet. A couple of 2h November delivery for full Propane have been traded into Japan and Korea at flat to small premium levels. Players reckon that although ample supply from US at such wide arbitrage window, latest loading of equal ratio tons destined for South and South East Asia has alternatively restricted the full Propane liftings, given existing export capacities. Demand from South and SE Asia continue to be robust, with Indian and Indonesian tenders flowing in the market. BPCL issued buy tender for one equal ratio cargo for 1h November delivery, and Pertamina issued a buy tender to secure another two equal ratio cargoes for early Dec delivery. At the same time, trades into China are also very active. Several PDH Buyers have secured Propane for November at the premium of over CP + $70 since late last week. One newly started PDH plant in S.China secured one full Propane cargo for 1H November delivery at November CP plus high $60s last week and issued another tender to purchase 23kt Propane for end Nov delivery.
