## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco has announced October CP at $420 for Propane and $435 for Butane, up by $20 respectively from September, which was in line with market expectations. However after the October CP was set, November CP slid by over $25 to $385 only, as compared to previous Thursday. Crude dipped below $60 this week, amidst the recovery of Saudi production and concerns over global demand. Qatar petroleum has released their November acceptances with no major delays being reported. Players are now awaiting other producers’ further acceptances, with a couple of November lifting FOBs Sellers already being seen and asking mid to high single digit premium.

**FAR EAST**

: It’s been a quiet week in the Asian market, mainly due to the ongoing China national holidays. Sellers of 23kt propane for 1h November delivery are asking November FEI minus $3 to minus $7, while bids heard at minus $10 level. The FEI/MB Arb is still wide open, this despite the rising freight and Far East discounts. This week both India’s IOC and Indonesia’s Pertamina have retendered to buy equal ratio cargos, one is for full month Nov delivery into Ennore and Haldia, and the other is for 6-8th Nov into Teluk Semangka. The result is yet to know but we trust the IOC tender will have high chance of getting awarded because of the greater flexibility in delivery timing. Despite their holiday’s, Chinese importers are still sniffing for November delivery cargoes, mainly heavy propane ratio as this is for winter heating and petchem use. Late last week one full Propane cargo for early Nov arrival was reported sold at November CP plus low mid 60s basis 1 port E.China, one major China based importer was reported to have resold a 23/0 2h October delivery at October CP plus close to $70 to a PDH user. One new S.China PDH plant operator was out with a buy tender last Friday for a near prompt delivery of 23kt propane for 20-31 Oct delivery, it didn’t award in the end. Players suspect the buyer will defer its demand into November because of the wide backwardation of over $35 on October to November CP.
