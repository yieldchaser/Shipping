## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco announced August propane CP at 370$ down by 5$ while butane CP at 360$ up by 5$, in line with market expectation. Few fobs available from producer on Aug lifting, apart from one trader is reported bought a mid Aug lifting equal ratio cargo from Saudi at Aug CP flat level, to utilize their vessel. Paper swap is volatile after Aug CP set. Sep CP surged up to 380$ on Wed but came off quick on Thursday, traded flattish to Aug CP at 370$ only. Sep FEI/CP also narrowed down to mid-30s and still under pressure.

**FAR EAST**

: Nothing was concluded in Window this week despite active bids/offers in window. On window 1h Sep propane seller asking Sep CP plus $29, or Aug Balance FEI minus $1, whereas buyers are not seen. 2h Sep seller offers at Sep FEI minus 7.5$ while bid at minus 11$. Off window 1h Sep was reported traded at Sep FEI minus 8.5 to 9$ mid-week. Although forward market in contango structure, abundant front length still exerts pressure on CFR value and buyers are expecting bigger discounts. Chinese buyers have started exploring Sep delivery cargos. One Nchina PDH buyer has issued tender to secure one full propane cargo during 1h Sep delivery. At the time of writing the result is yet to know. Off window sellers is heard targeted at high 60s to 70s for such cargo. Indonesia Pertamina is reported to have awarded their latest tender for 10-14th Sep delivered into Kalbut at Aug CP plus low 50s, which alternatively reflected less discounts on FOB netback.
