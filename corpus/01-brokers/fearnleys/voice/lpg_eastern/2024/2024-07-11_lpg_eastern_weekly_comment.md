## Market Commentary

MEG With the declining freight, FOB premiums improved further this week. We’ve heard seller asking teens premium for Aug lifting evenly split cargo however yet to hear deals concluded. Indian majors have started to procure term supply next year, with IOC asking for one cargo monthly in Q1’25, and BPCL looking for 62 VLGC cargos in total for next year delivery, for evenly split ratio. Aug CP was traded at $587 at the time of writing, and Aug C3/C4 was traded at $15. Aug/Sep CP was traded at $2.5 contango.

**FAR EAST**

 Delay on supply caused by the hurricane doesn’t lend much support to the CFR market, due to the lackluster demand and the softening freight. 23kt propane for 1h Aug was rumored changed hand at Aug FEI plus mid single digit, and similar cargo for 2h Aug delivery was bid at plus $3.5-4.5. 46kt propane parcel for Aug delivery was bid at Aug FEI minus $4 basis Chiba and plus $5-6 basis NChina. Outside window market appears quiet this week, especially the high flat price cost discouraged petchem buying interests. Till Thursday one SChina PDH awarded tender to secure 23kt upto 46kt C3 des Qinzhou in 1-25 Aug delivery at Aug FEI plus low single reportedly. One NChina PDH was said to procure an Aug delivery propane basis Panjin at Aug FEI plus low teens. Other bids for similar cargos basis Ningbo were heard at Aug FEI plus low mid-single this week. With current margins, China PDHs are unlikely to ramp up rates further, which was reported at around 75%.
