## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco set Jan C3 and C4 CP at $620 and $630, up by $10 respectively. Some end users reckoned the price is lower than expectation with the anticipation of less supply due to oil cuts and rumored maintenance ex Yanbu liftings. At the time of writing, Feb C3 CP was assessed at $617 and C4 at $10 higher. Feb/Mar was traded at $10 backwardation. Evenly split cargo for Feb lifting was assessed at CP flattish level without fresh deals reported since last done before new year holiday.

**FAR EAST**

 Muted activities in window as most players are away from holidays. 23kt C3 parcel for 2h Feb delivery was bid at the equivalence of Feb FEI minus mid/high single digit, while outside window similar parcel for 1h Feb delivery was heard changed hand at Feb FEI minus small single digit. 46kt parcel was reported sold into Ningbo at Feb FEI minus 5$ with sketchy details, while other bids for similar parcel at high single discount. Outside window one EChina petchem player issued buy tender for 46kt C3 delivered during 4-10th Feb, which is believed shall receive aggressive offers as limited players willing to import during/before CNY period. We have a couple of new PDHs expected to start up in Jan, hopefully can run stable and import regularly from 2h Feb or Mar onwards, with sustainable margins, if any.
