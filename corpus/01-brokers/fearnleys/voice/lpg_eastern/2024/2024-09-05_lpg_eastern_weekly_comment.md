## Market Commentary

MEG Premium for Oct lifting evenly split cargos still stands firm at mid-teens level, with vigorous demand from India and Indonesia. IOC was reported award their buy tender for Oct and Nov lifting evenly split cargos at CP plus low-mid teens and continued to secure monthly one cargo for Q4 delivery. Meanwhile, BPCL also tendered to buy monthly 20kt heavy butane cargo and 45kt evenly split cargo for Q4 delivery. At the time of writing, Oct C3 CP was traded at $607, lagging crude losses. Oct C3/C4 was at $5 but it’s expected to narrow down further. Oct/Nov CP was traded at flattish level.

**FAR EAST**

 Active bids in window this week, with 23/0 parcels for 1h Oct delivery traded at Oct FEI plus $5.5 and $6. Similar parcels for 2h Oct delivery was bid at plus $5 while offer at $7.5. 46/0 lot for 2h Oct delivery was bid at plus $2.18 basis Dongguan, SChina and plus $5.68 basia Tianjin, NChina. Outside window a handful of 46/0 parcels were placed into China at Oct FEI plus mid-single digit basis Ningbo reportedly, majority for trader shorts. Despite the talk on rate cuts caused by squeezed margins, we are still seeing PDHs emerge to explore Oct spot or Q4 supply. One SChina PDH user re-issued buy tender for 23/0 for Sep to Oct delivery or 46/0 for mid/2h Oct delivery. Other than spot, another NChina PDH player have issued term procurement tender for 46/0 monthly delivery in 2025. Apart from propane, butane demand was unexpectedly strong, given few demands on cracking. One Korean importer tendered to buy 11kt butane for 1h Oct delivery. One European trader also issued buy tender for 23kt butane in Oct delivery into EChina but canceled in the end. PERTAMINA also issued buy tender to secure two evenly split cargos for early and mid Oct delivery but was reported only award one parcel at Oct CP plus around hi 50s.
