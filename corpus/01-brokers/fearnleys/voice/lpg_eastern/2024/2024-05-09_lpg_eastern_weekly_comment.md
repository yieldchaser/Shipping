## Market Commentary

MEG Inquiries slowed down with deeper discounts requested owing to the increasing freight. Compared to last week, buying ideas for Jun lifting evenly split cargo have declined from CP minus mid teens to minus 30s range. KPC awarded their sale tender for a 12/13 Jun lifting 2/2 to a producer trading company reportedly, which is out of surprise as market players expect such buyer shall have ample supply. At the time of writing, Jun C3 CP was traded at $540 and Jun/Jul CP at $9.

**FAR EAST**

 The premium for Jun delivery propane have soared up due to the increasing Panama transit costs. 23kt C3 for 2h Jun delivery was changed hand at Jun FEI plus high teens level, while similar parcel for 1h Jun delivery was bid at Jun FEI plus 20ish but no offers seen. We believed the market will come off once the panama situation eased and seller program sorted out. Off window 46kt lot for 2h Jun delivery into NChina was also traded at Jun FEI plus high teens reportedly. One SChina PDH awarded their buy tender for 23kt C3 at Jun CP plus 50s for late May to early Jun delivery. Another PDH user canceled their propane buy tender for Jun delivery into EChina and NChina due to high offers, which heard at Jun FEI plus high teens/20 level reportedly. Apart from such, some traditional special origin receivers have explored on normal origin this week, due to the uncertainty on supply and higher premiums asked. HTC also issued tender to procure 23kt butane for mid Jun delivery on MOPJ link.
