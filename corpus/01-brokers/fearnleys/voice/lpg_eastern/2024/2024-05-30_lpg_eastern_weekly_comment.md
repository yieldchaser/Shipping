## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco announced Jun CP at $580 for C3, rollover from May; and $565 for C4, down by $20 MOM. This is in line with curve value but C3/C4 spread is wider than expected, assuming due to the propane shortage from Saudi for Jun liftings. Qatar Petroleum have sold two cargos via last tender for late Jun lifting, with one reported sold to ATC and another to an oil major at the price reported at a premium to July CP or Jun CP minus teens discounts. By the time of writing, Jul C3 CP was traded at $560 and Jul C4 CP at $15 less.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium for 1h Jul delivery surged up amid robust demand. 23/0 for 1h Jul CFR FAR EAST was bid at Jul FEI plus mid teens but no offers seen. Outside window a handful of 46kt propane cargos have been placed into China for feedstock use, at the price from Jul FEI plus high teens to mid 20s depending on locations. The cracking demand on butane slowed down due to narrowing spread however propane for PDH use are still in good shape amid healthy margins to downstream PP. However the soaring flat price and premium have eroded the margins and suppressed some spot demand. One EChina PDH issued tender to secure Jul delivery heavy propane cargo but was reported to cancel due to high offers asking over 20s premium to Jul FEI. Different from strong premium for propane, butane discussion yet to improve. Bids in general are in FEI plus singles range or less, while offers asking above 20s. One SChina traditional importer reported to cancel their buy tender for evenly split cargo for 1h Jul delivery, hearing offers asking CP plus 70s. One Korean petchem player was reported to award 23kt butane for end Jun early Jul delivery at Mopj minus 60s. Pertamina is also reported to buy a evenly split cargo for early Jul delivery at Jul CP plus 50s.
