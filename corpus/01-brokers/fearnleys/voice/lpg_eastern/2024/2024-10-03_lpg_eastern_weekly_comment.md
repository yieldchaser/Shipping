## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco raised up Oct CP by $20 for C3 at $625 and by $25 for C4 at $620, reflecting the strong demand in Oct butane especially from India. Entering Nov, the buying inquiries for Nov lifting slowed down but still assessed at teens premium to Nov CP. Following the Middle East conflicts, Nov CP traded firmer alongside the rising crude. By the time of writing, Nov CP was traded at $630 and Nov C3/C4 at $8.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials for Nov inched higher with active trader’s bids in window. 23/0 parcels for 1h Nov delivery and 2h Nov delivery was bid at Oct FEI plus $6/6ish, and similar cargo for 1h Dec delivery was traded at Nov FEI plus $1.88 which equates to Dec FEI plus hi single digit. During the Golden week holiday, Inquiries from China are muted. However, the volatile swap price, especially the soaring FEI flat price, up by roughly $20 , might raise concerns on motivated rate cuts for PDHs and some resales. At the time of writing, Nov FEI was traded at $670 and offers for full propane cargo basis Echina were reported at the premium of teens level but barely heard bid range. Other than propane, Indonesia PERTAMINA reissued their buy tenders for two 2/2 cargos delivered during mid and late Nov respectively, seeking better offers. Hyosung Vena also issued buy tender for 11/11 split CIF in Nov delivery.
