## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco raised Sep C3 CP to $605, up by $15 and C4 CP to $595, up by $25. This is in line with the swap value, reflecting the tightness for Sep supply against strong demand for evenly split cargo from India and Indonesia. This being said, some end receivers are discontented about the high flat price during summer season. Cash premium for such 2/2 FOB Oct is offered at CP plus teens level. By the time of writing, Oct CP was assessed at $606 for C3, and C3/C4 traded at $10. Oct/Nov CP was traded at flat level.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials softened on Oct delivered tons during the week. 23/0 for 1h Oct CFR FAR EAST was reported concluded at Oct FEI plus mid-single digit, and similar parcel for 2h Oct was offered at Oct FEI plus $5 vs bid at $3. Apart from propane, one 0/23 parcel for end Sep early Oct delivery was offered at Sep propane FEI minus $5. Outside window one 23/0 2h Oct delivery was traded at Sep FEI plus $2 reportedly. Discussions for Oct trades into China slowed down also. A handful of 46/0 for 1h Oct delivery was traded at Oct FEI plus mid/high single basis North and East China. However, the surging propane costs and squeezed petchem margins refrained some players from buying. Several PDHs unexpectedly shut down due to glitch and we expect it extend the maintenance due to negative margin, while some others are considering cutting rates. Meanwhile, cash premium for special origin stems on Sep delivery still hovered at CP plus 40s basis Schina, which has driven some Nchina players to procure normal stems instead.
