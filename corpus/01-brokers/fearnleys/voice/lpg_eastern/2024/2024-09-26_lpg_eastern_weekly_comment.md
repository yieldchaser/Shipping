## Market Commentary

MEG Despite the recent decline in crude oil price, Oct CP still maintained the strength and traded at $618 for C3 and $616 for C4, by the time of writing. This is in line with the 1st round recommendation which submitted mid-week at $610-625 for C3 and $620-635 for C4 reportedly. In window one trader was bidding evenly split cargo for any Oct lifting at $615 for C3 and $645 for C4.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium slightly strengthened compared to last week, with 23/0 parcel for 2h Oct delivery basis Far East traded at Oct FEI plus low mid single digit level. And a couple of 46/0 parcels for 2h Nov delivery basis Ningbo was offered at Nov FEI plus $14. Importers refrained their buy interests, especially seeing swap value surging up on Thurs due to ET planned maintenance and potential impact under new hurricane. Some resales for Oct delivery were heard from NChina players but volumes have been well absorbed and barely see the premium declined much. This being said, we yet to see any new major shutdowns apart from scheduled maintenances. One SChina PDH player just issued spot tender to procure propane for Nov delivery, and meanwhile they’ve awarded their term tender for monthly 46/0 at odd and even month to two different sellers. FPC was also reported to secure a 23kt propane for 2h Oct delivery into Mailiao at Oct MOPJ minus small digit via tender, which equates to Oct FI flat at the time of conclusion. HTC also issued tender to purchase a 23/0 parcel for late Nov delivery.
