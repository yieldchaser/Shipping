## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco set Apr CP at $615 for C3 and $620 for C4, down by $15 and $20 respectively, which is lower than expectation, partly owing to the spot availability on FOB cargos. KPC was reported to sell two evenly split cargos recent, with one for 14/15 Apr prompt loading at CP minus 50s and the other at minus 20s for a later laycan. Qatar Petroleum released May lifting schedule with no cuts but some minor delays.

**FAR EAST**

 A handful of deals concluded in window this week. 46/0 for 2h May delivery was changed hand at Bal Apr FEI minus $7 which equates to May FEI plus mid-single digit on the date. 11/11 and 0/23 for same period delivery were concluded at May FEI plus $6.9. 46/0 for 1h May delivery was bid at May FEI plus $11 basis Yantai and 23/0 parcel for 2h May delivery bid at $7 but few offers seen in window. Before the Qingming festival, a flurry of tenders was awarded this week. Taiwan FPC was reported to award two full propane cargos at May MOPJ minus high 60s into Mailiao and May FEI plus high teens into Ningbo via tenders. Another three Chinese petchem players also awarded 23/0 parcels at May FEI (or mix of May and Jun FEI) plus low-mid teens respectively, for prompt 2h Apr to 1h May delivery. Apart from propane, one SChina traditional player also tendered to buy 11/11 for 1-20 May delivery but was reported cancelled. However, one Iranian origin 3/1 cargo for late Apr delivery was placed into China at May CP plus $10 reportedly. HTC also tendered to buy 23kt butane for 10-19 May delivery.
