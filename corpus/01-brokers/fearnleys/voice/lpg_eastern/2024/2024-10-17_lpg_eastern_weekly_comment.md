## Market Commentary

MEG Cash premium hovers above $20 for Nov lifting evenly split cargos, as in lack of spot availability. Earlier this week one trader also bid for similar cargo in window at Nov CP plus $20 but no offers seen. By the time of writing, following the crude drop, Nov C3 CP was traded at $625 but still resilient to drop further.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials inched up and 23kt C3 for 2h Nov delivery was bid at Nov FEI plus $3.5 while similar cargo for 1h Dec at Dec FEI plus $7.5. Outside window a couple of 46kt C4 for 2h Nov delivery was changed hand at Nov FEI plus $5-6 basis Echina. However petchem inquiries from end users are limited, owing to the waning margins. A handful of PDHs were reported to cut rates or consider shutdown in Nov. This week so far only one SChina PDH user was heard to procure two 46/0 parcels for 1H and 2H Nov delivery, at Nov FEI plus 7ish and CP plus 40s respectively. Other than that, Taiwan FPCC issued tender to buy a Dec lifting Us 4/0 into Mailiao. A couple of Iranian 3/1 parcels for early Nov delivery basis SChina, were reported to trade at Nov CP plus teens. Given the increasing tensions, recent Iranian LPG programs/offers are delayed until vessels loaded for sail and for sale. However, Oct delivery into China is estimated to rebound over 700kt, from 594kt in Aug and 660kt in Sep. Other than spot, One NChina Major importer issued term tender to secure monthly supply propane. Taiwan CPC also issued a FOB/DES swap tender as usual to secure the logistics supply next year.
