## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco announced Dec propane CP at $635 and butane at $630, a rollover from Dec. Propane is in line with swap value, but butane is higher than most expectations, especially in view of ampler butane supply compared to Nov liftings. Evenly split cargo for Dec was assessed at CP flattish level amid limited discussions. By the time of writing, Jan CP was traded at $630 and Jan C3/C4 was traded at $7.

**FAR EAST**

 A couple of 23/0 parcels on 2h Dec delivery was changed hand at Dec FEI plus $2/2ish last Friday, and discussions have shifted to Jan program. In window several 23/0 parcels for 1h Jan delivery was traded at Jan FEI plus mid-single digit. However, outside window the sluggish demand has exerted further pressure on FEI, with Jan FEI/CP was traded depressingly at -$13. Chinese petchem players hold off on spot procurement, with bearish outlook on downstream consumption before CNY (starting end Jan) and uncertainties on delays of restart up from maintenance. We’ve only seeing one Vietnam based importer issue buy tender for one 3/1 or 2/0 or 1/1 for 1h Jan delivery. Other than normal stems, one 2/2, special origin, for 1h Dec arrival basis Schina was reported done at Dec CP minus $20. And three more heavy propane cargos for Dec arrival are reported unsold amid thin trades. Till Nov we’ve seen over 8mmt tons been placed into China, similar vs last year, whereas the trades are more spot tender related, causing more volatility in cash differentials than before.
