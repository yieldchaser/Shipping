MEG Saudi Aramco released their Mar lifting acceptance with no cuts heard. Qatar Petroleum issued tender to sell an evenly split cargo for end Mar lifting. AG supply looks ample and discussion softened but still in premium level. At the time of writing, Mar C3 Cp was traded at $618 and C4 at $626. Mar/Apr was traded at $40 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 Active offers seen in window for sale on 23/0 for 2h Mar and 1H Apr delivery. During mid week, 23/0 for 1H Apr delivery was traded at Apr FEI plus $10. After the CNY Holiday, a handful of buying inquiries emerged. One EChina cracker procured 23/0 for late Mar arrival at Mar FEI plus high single digit. Another petchem user was also reported to buy a 4/0 for Mar delivery into Yangtze river port at FEI plus teens level. One NChina PDH tendered to buy two 46/0 for 2h Mar and Apr delivery. One EChina PDH user also tendered to secure monthly 46/0 basis Chiba for 2H of the year. Apart from Chinese, Indonesia Pertamina tendered to buy one 22/22 for 3-7 Apr delivery. There are also two Iranian stem sale tenders floating, to sell upto 8 cargos for Mar arrival, at 2/2 or 3/1 ratio. And still a couple of Feb arrival cargos remained unsold.
