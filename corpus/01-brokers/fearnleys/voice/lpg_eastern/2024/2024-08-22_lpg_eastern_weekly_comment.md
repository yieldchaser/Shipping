## Market Commentary

MEG Bids for evenly split cargos for Sep liftings softened due to higher freight however offers still maintained at mid-teens premium to lifting month CP, as of strong demand from India and Indonesia. Despite the loss in oil price, Sep CP was traded resiliently at $595, and Sep/Oct CP traded at flat to small contango.

**FAR EAST**

 Earlier in window 23/0 parcel for 2h Sep delivery was traded at Sep FEI plus $5 and bids continue to edge up. Similar cargo for 1h Oct was bid at Oct FEI flattish to $1, yet to entice sellers’ interest. Off window a handful of 46/0 lots for 2h Sep delivery was traded into EChina and NChina at Sep FEI plus mid high single last Friday, and a couple of similar cargo for 1h Oct delivery was also been placed into East and NChina, at Oct FEI plus mid single digit basis Ningbo reportedly. This being said, still quite a few tenders were cancelled due to high offers. One SChina cracker user cancelled their buy tender for evenly split or heavy butane cargo for Sep delivery, another SChina PDH didn’t award their tender for Sep to Oct delivery propane either, with both offers reported at delivery month CP plus 50s range. FPC also cancelled their 3rd buy tender for 23/0 or 46/0 late Sep delivery into Mailiao. PERTAMINA issued another buy tender to procure two evenly split cargos for mid Sep and early Oct delivery. Special origin for Sep delivery was reported done at CP plus low 40s basis Schina, which barely have any price advantage vs normal stem and some players already diverted to normal cargo purchase. Other than spot inquiries, Chinese importers started to secure next year term supply in view of recent soft freight. One China traditional importer have concluded their annual term tender and secured monthly cargo and quarterly cargo reportedly. Another China major petchem player also issued term tender to secure monthly cargo (heavy propane) basis Ningbo and Maoming and even Chiba.
