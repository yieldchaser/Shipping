## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco announced Jul CP at $580 for C3 and $565 for C4, a rollover from Jun, which is cheaper than swap values. Following crude, Aug CP surged up to $592 and C3/C4 maintains $15. Owing to the softening freight and decent CFR price, Aug FOB discussions also inched up and turned into premium, with seller asking Aug CP plus $3 for Aug lifting evenly split cargos in window while bids outside window in discounted level.

**FAR EAST**

 The upcoming hurricane have raised up concerns on current US Gulf loadings, which affects on the Aug delivery supply. 23/0 for 1h Aug delivery was traded earlier at Aug FEI plus $7, higher than curve value, then weakened to $5 on the time of writing. 46/0 parcel for Aug delivery basis Ningbo was bid at Aug FEI plus $5, and similar cargo basis Chiba was bid at Aug FEI minus $3.5. Outside window FIC awarded their buy tender for 46/0 1-10 Aug delivery at Aug FEI plus mid high single and they issued another tender for 46/0 2h Aug delivery. Similarly, Huatai also reported to award 23/0 for early Aug delivery at Aug FEI plus mid single. Tianjin Bohai also tendered to buy 23/0 or 46/0 for 1h Aug delivery, which is still within validity. Despite the fact that there are still some more feedstock procurement demand, the soaring flat price has eroded the margins and we are expecting some new PDHs or units under maintenance might defer startup/re-startup. Apart from regular stems, there are still two special origin evenly split cargos, for end Jul delivery basis Schina are for sale via tender.
