## Market Commentary

MEG Qatar Petroleum was reported to award their sale tender for a 25-28th Feb lifting split cargo at CP minus mid-teens level. Saudi Aramco released their Feb acceptance with details remain sketchy, but we don’t expect producer to have spot avails as its trading arm was heard to buy Feb spot previously. At the time of writing, Feb C3 CP was traded at $580 and C4/C3 at $10, Feb/Mar CP was assessed at $9 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 The market seems more balanced this week after the hectic buyings from Chinese last week, which quite well absorbed the excess tons. Less offers were seen in window but more bids shown for 23kt propane parcels delivered in 2h Feb and 1h Mar. Cash differentials have improved from last week’s discount to premium level, with a couple of 23/0 parcels for 2h Feb delivery changed hand at Feb FEI plus $3 to $5 level. Feb FEI/CP narrowed down further this week to $23 only, down by 18$ vs last Monday, partly due to the decline in freight. Fei/MOPJ widened recent and some cracker are seen switching to LPG from Napatha for feedstock, however subject to run rates and ethylene margins. One SChina based cracker has issued tender to secure 11kt upto 45kt propane for late feb/1h Mar delivery. The drastic drop in freight have also enticed some term discussion interests. Indonesia PERTAMINA called new term tender for split grade cargo monthly delivery from Apr to Dec. Last week a couple of PDH buy term tenders have heard awarded with details yet to know, but premium edged down as of cheaper logistics costs.
