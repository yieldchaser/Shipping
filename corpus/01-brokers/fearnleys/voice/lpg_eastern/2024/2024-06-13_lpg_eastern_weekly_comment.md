**FAR EAST**

Similar cargo for 1h Aug delivery was traded at Jul FEI link plus $1.68. Outside window a handful of 46kt propane for 2h Jul delivery was traded, at the price of Jul FEI plus mid single digit basis chiba, or FEI plus low mid teens basis Ningbo. There are still a couple of Jul feedstock demand uncovered and shall call out buy tender next week. Other than normal stems, three Iranian cargos for end Jun/1h Jul delivery of evenly split or heavy propane ratio were reported sold at Jul CP plus 40s basis SChina, and we expect more cargos for 2h Jul delivery shall be available soon.
