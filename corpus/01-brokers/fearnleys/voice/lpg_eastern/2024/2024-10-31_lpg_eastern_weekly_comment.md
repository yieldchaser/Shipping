## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco announced Nov CP at $635 for C3 and $630 for C4, up $10 each. Such is above expectation, especially recommended price ranged at $620-625 in view of the declining crude. Despite the slim discussion on fobs due to high premium against tight supply, evenly split cargos for Dec lifting are assessed in teens premium.

**FAR EAST**

 Spot discussions muted before CP announcement. On window 23kt propane for CFR 1h Dec basis Chiba was offered at Dec FEI plus $3/4. And similar parcel for 2h Nov delivery was changed hand at Nov FEI minus $3 and bid inched up to minus $1 mid-week. Outside window some 46kt propane for 1h Dec delivery into Echina and NChina was changed hand at Dec FEI plus low mid single digit. Nov FEI/CP was narrowed down further to $9 only, vs $25 WOW, resulted by the weakening spot demand on CFR east against tight supply on AG supply. The squeezed margin continued to refrain buy interests from petchem players. Moreover some Chinese are reselling their Nov/Dec cargos. One NChina based importer was reported to sell two propane cargos, with one 23/0 2h Nov delivery basis Chiba traded at Nov FEI minus $3 and a 46/0 1h Dec delivery basis Liaoning at Dec FEI plus mid high single. One Chinese major importer issued term buy tender to secure more 46/0 monthly delivery basis Ningbo or Maoming. Another SChina importer also issued buy term tender for 11/11 selective month delivery basis Shenzhen.
