## Market Commentary

Cash differentials for Nov evenly split FOB cargos ex AG hover at teens level to Nov CP, driven by the strong butane demand, especially with Indian importer like IOC and HPCL calling tender for Nov and/or Dec cargos. Additionally, the tightening sanction on Iranian fleets and tensions over US-China tariff talks have diverted more buy interests on normal AG stems, which consequently lends further support to cash premiums. By the time of writing, Nov C3 CP was traded at $450 and C4 traded 5$ higher to C3. US FOB netbacks for Asian market slightly improved as of the softening freight. However, the deep discounts on CFR East market on front market have dampened the lifting incentives, and a couple of late Oct and Nov liftings are reported cancelled when arbitrage deteriorated earlier this week. Bids for Nov delivered propane parcels basis Chiba are shown at Nov FEI minus $20s on the window. The uncertainties over US-China tariff talk and the additional imposed rival port fees move, have disrupted the traditional trade operation and leave the market unsettled. Outside window a handful of tenders are floating this week. One NChina PDH awarded their spot tender by securing two US 4/0 cargos for earlier Nov delivery at Nov FEI minus low 20s and also issue term tender to secure monthly 4/0 including Us origin, for next year. FPC also issued similar term tender to procure monthly 4/0 des Mailiao. Another two importers only tendered for non-Us 2/0 or 0/2 or 1/1 parcels for next year. A couple of Korean importers issued spot tender to buy LPG with butane grade for Nov delivery. Outside tender, non-Us 4/0 or 2/2 parcels were reported placed into China at Nov CP plus 80s range.
