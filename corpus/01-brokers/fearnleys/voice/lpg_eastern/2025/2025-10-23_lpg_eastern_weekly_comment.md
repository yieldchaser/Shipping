## Market Commentary

Cash premium for AG 2/2 FOB cargo soared up further, with one Nov FOB being traded at Nov CP plus high teens reportedly. Dec parcels are also in heated discussion, with IOC reported to award a Dec cargo at Dec CP plus $7 via tender. By the time of writing, Nov C3 CP was traded $460, following the crude, and Nov/Dec C3 CP in $10 contango. FOB trading activities ex US for Nov slightly improved, as some players try to utilize their ships and procure spot cargos. ARB to EAST stabilized this week at $140ish and $150 for Nov and Dec respectively, whilst netback to Asia edged up to 5cpg with softening freight. Us Propane and propylene stock rose by 800k barrels, and inventory increased by 1.4% versus last year. Asian price surged up with Nov FEI being traded at $500, and Nov FEI/CP enlarged by $15 to $40 versus last week. 23/0 parcel for 2h Nov delivery basis Chiba was bid at Nov FEI minus $15 and similar parcel for 1h Dec was bid at Dec FEI minus $9 in the window. Outside window FPCC was reported awarded their buy tender for two Dec lots, with 4/0 concluded at Dec FEI minus mid/high single and 2/0 at Mopj linked price. Shenghong was also reported to award their buy tender for non-Us flexible ratio LPG delivered during 10/Nov to 20/Dec, but details remained sketchy. A couple of non-Us stem for Nov delivery was reported placed into China, with 1/1 parcel at Nov CP plus 70 basis SChina and 2/0 heard at 80 range.
