## Market Commentary

Saudi Aramco reduced Nov CP to $475 for C3 and $460 for C4, down by $20 and $15 respectively. Given they are still in the process of term renewal, market participants expect such a decline on CP, but the announcement is slightly set on high end versus swap value. Cash premium for evenly split cargos remained firm with robust Indian and Chinese demand, coupled with tighter US butane supply to EAST in winter typically. By the time of writing, Dec CP rallied up to $490 for C3 and $ for $473 for C4. Spot sentiments for US FOBs strengthened with arbitrage into Asia widening from $150s to $160s for both Nov and Dec lifting. Notional FOB netbacks into EAST improved to 5ish cpg and some late Nov lifting FOB was reported changed at 5.75 cpg. US propane/Propylene inventory increased by 2.5mml barrels, much higher than expected. Asian price rebounded, following the stronger CP price announced and relief on trade tensions after US/China talk. 46/0 parcels for 1h Dec delivery basis Chiba was offered via window at Dec FEI minus $7 and 23/0 for 2h Dec was bid at $518, which equates to Dec FEI minus $4. Outside window a handful of term tenders are under negotiations. One Chinese petchem players was reported awarded their buy propane tender for US stems at FEI minus high single, and non US stems at CP plus $60s. Another two Chinese importers also issue similar tenders to procure open origin propane. Indonesia Pertamina was reported to award some evenly split cargos via new term tender at CP plus 20s range. Similarly, some Philippines importers award their term tender for 1/1 split at CP plus 30s. Spot activities slowed down as players are awaiting CP, however one 2/2 for Dec delivery was reported sold into SEA at CP plus 30s, which reckoned to be US stem. Whereas another Non US 2/2 was placed into China at Dec CP plus 80s reportedly. Huayi cancelled their propane buy tender for Nov/Dec delivery, hearing offer at the premium of 80s as well.
