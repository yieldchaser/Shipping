## Market Commentary

MEG Spot discounts for Mar lifting FOBs are assessed at CP minus 20, reflecting the sluggish Indian demand against a plenty of spot availabilities. One producer trading arm was offering both 2/2 and 4/0 cargos in window, and Qatar Energy also tendered to sell 3/1 or 4/0 for 20-28 Mar lifting. At the time of writing, Mar CP was traded at $602 for C3, and $592 for C4.

**FAR EAST**

 A couple of buyers are bidding 23/0 for 2h Mar delivery at Bal Feb FEI minus small single, which equates to Mar FEI plus mid high single, while sellers offered at Bal Feb FEI plus $3 or Mar FEI plus $13. 4/0 parcel for 2h Mar delivery into NChina was bid at Mar FEI plus 10ish but yet to entice sellers. Mar FEI/CP finally turned into positive after been traded in minus teens for a long while, as front CP under strong selling pressure in both physical window and paper swaps. However, in lack of consistent and substantial buying demands, the FEI/CP fluctuates rapidly. Outside window spot inquiries are limited as in lack of incentive to buy speculative cargos. Only one EChina based PDH issued tender and bought only 23/0 parcel for Mar delivery at Mar FEI plus mid teens level from another NChina player.
