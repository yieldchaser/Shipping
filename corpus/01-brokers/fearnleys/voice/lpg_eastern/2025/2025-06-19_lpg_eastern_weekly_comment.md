## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi acceptance was released late but no cuts or restrictions on propane reported. However, uncertainties over potential disruption on Middle East supply lent support to the front CP, coupled with the soaring crude. By the time of writing, Jul CP for C3 was traded at $603 and C3/C4 at $30. Slim buying interests for Jul liftings FOBs seen this week, as freight soared up since the Israel-Iran conflict. Evenly split cargo was offered at ard Jul CP minus 40s but no bids heard. HPCL tendered to buy upto three evenly split grade for Aug and Sep lifting or delivery. Earlier this week we’ve heard Iranian suppliers expediated the loadings and tried to deplete the storage. Our shiptrack shows over 900kt LPG on the water now and some prompt cargos for late Jun/early Jul arrival basis Schina are still on sale.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium for 23/0 2h Jul delivery basis Chiba softened this week, traded from plus $4 to $2. A similar parcel for 1h Aug delivery was traded at Jul FEI plus $1.5. 46/0 for 2h Jul delivery basis Ningbo was changed hand over window at Jul FEI plus $3. Outside window demand has dampened by soaring flat prices and negative petchem margins, especially Jul FEI surged up by $40, which refrained Chinese buyers from taking any more US stems given additional 10% tariff. Additionally, there are prompt propane cargo was offered into China at Jul CP plus single digit. FPC cancelled their buy tender for propane on Jul to Aug delivery and butane on 2h Aug delivery into Mailiao, as offers not attractive. HTC just issued buy tender for 23kt butane delivered 3-9 Aug.
