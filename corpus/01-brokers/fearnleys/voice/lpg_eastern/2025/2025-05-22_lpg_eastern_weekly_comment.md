## Market Commentary

MEG Butane supply is pretty long especially plenty of evenly split cargo available for sale on Jun liftings. KPC was reported to sell another 0/4 cargo for 18-23 Jun lifting, which is the 2nd pure butane cargo sold via tender for Jun loader. Qatar Energy was also said to award their sell tender for a 2/2 or 3/1 cargo on 26-29 Jun lifting, at CP minus 40s reportedly. On Thur window, one oil major offered a 19-20 Jun lifting 2/2 at a fixed price $555 for C3 and $525 for C4 as well, which equates to Jun CP minus $30s. By the time of writing, Jun CP was assessed at $587 for C3 and $562 for C4. Jun/Jul CP was assessed at $30 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 Active bids for propane delivered from 2h Jun to 2h Jul surged in window, but few offers seen. Earlier this week 23/0 parcel for 2h Jun was traded at Jun FEI plus $4.5 and similar parcel for 1h Jul was traded at Jun FEI plus $1.5. A couple of 46/0 for 2h Jun delivery basis NChina were changed hand at Jun FEI plus mid-high single digit. Similar cargo for 1h Jul delivery basis Ningbo was bid at Jun FEI link plus low single digit in window, and bid for 2h Jul 4/0 basis Yantai was at Jun FEI flattish. Indian IOC was reported to buy monthly one 2/2 for Q3 at CP plus 20s-30s range, which help relieved butane selling pressure for Jul onwards but not front market. A couple of 0/4 cargos been loaded for late Jun/Jul delivery into Far East, which exerted pressure on butane sales. 2/2 for Jun delivery into China was traded at Jul CP plus 20s reportedly this week. Comparatively, propane is still discussed in better premium. However, importers are evaluating costs between FEI link US stem with additional tariff and CP link Non-Us stem. One NChina PDH player issued a tender to procure 33kt or 46kt C3 excluding US origin for late Jun to Jul delivery. Despite the eroded margin, we are expecting some PDHs return from scheduled maintenance in late May/Jun, which might incite some buying inquiries, but at a weakening premium.
