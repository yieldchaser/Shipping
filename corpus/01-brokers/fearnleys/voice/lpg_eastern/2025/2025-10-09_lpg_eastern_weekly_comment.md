## Market Commentary

Spot differentials for Nov FOBs ex AG are evaluated at mid high single digit premium to Nov Cp. Qatar Energy was reported to sell two mid Nov lifting fobs, one 2/2 and one 4/0 at such level. By the time of writing, Nov Cp was traded at $470 and Nov C3/C4 at $2. Spot discussions for Nov Fobs ex US are subdued as economics to EAST still at cancellation level with narrowing arbitrage, among plentiful propane supply against bearish demand. Terminal fee for Nov liftings was heard being offered at below 4cpg but little buying interests. CFR Asian window fell further with offers of 23/0 parcels for 2h Nov delivery basis Chiba being shown at Nov FEI minus $19 while bid at minus $23. Outside window similar parcel for 1h Nov delivery was said changed hand at minus low 20s. Nov FEI swap dropped further to $494 at the time of writing. The widening FEI/MOPJ however stimulated cracking demand. Lotte Chemical was reported to procure a 2/2 for Nov delivery at Nov MOPJ minus 30s. With Chinese back from long holidays, market participants are expecting the demand revived as petchem margins improved by cheaper feedstock prices. However with the uncertainty on US-China tariff talk, Chinese buyers are still cautious on cargo origins. Huayi issued buy tender for non Us 23/0 delivered for late Oct to Nov delivery basis Schina.
