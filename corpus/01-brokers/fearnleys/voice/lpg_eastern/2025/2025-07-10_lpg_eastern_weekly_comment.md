## Market Commentary

**MEG:** FOB discounts for Aug lifting FOBs dampened as of ample supply against lukewarm demand and weak netback on CFR basis. One evenly split cargo was reported traded at Aug CP minus high 30s/$40. By the time of writing, Aug CP for C3 was traded at $565, and C3/C4 narrowed down to $20 from last week’s $26 as seasonal demand improved for butane and additional cracking demand.

**FAR EAST**

: Cash differentials slide further this week with limited propane buying inquiries. Several parcels for 23/0 for 2h Aug delivery basis Chiba were changed hand at Aug FEI minus $5 or Jul FEI minus $0.5 to $1.5. And offers are still under downward pressure. 46/0 for similar delivery range was assessed at Aug FEI minus mid-teens. Outside window PDHs are hesitant to move, with tentative turnaround schedule in Aug and bearish outlook on downstream demand. Only one PDH major issued tender to buy non-Us propane for Aug delivery so far. Despite the slim interest on propane, cracking inquiry surged up as FEI/MOPJ widened, following the crude rise. Two Korean importers are reported to award their butane buy tender for Aug delivery at Aug MOPJ minus around $40. One more Korean company also issued tender to buy upto 44kt flexible ratio for Aug delivery into their Indonesia cracker. Several petchem players didn’t award their Aug butane buy tender as offers not attractive. CPC also issued tender to buy 1/1 split for last decade Aug delivery.
