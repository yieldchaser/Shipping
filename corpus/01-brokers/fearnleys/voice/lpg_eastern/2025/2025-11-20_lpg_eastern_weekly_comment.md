## Market Commentary

Cash premiums for Dec lifting evenly split cargoes have surged further to CP plus $30s range, driven by tight supply and recent active spot purchases. A Middle East producer has cut tolerance on propane for term liftings and canceled one cargo reportedly. By the time of writing, Dec CP was traded at $486 for C3 and Dec C3/C4 at $15. Dec/Jan CP C3 narrowed to flattish vs last week’s $9, reflecting front strength. US FOBs remain strong, with December FOB cargos changed hand at 7-8 cpg, supported by the strong CFR Asian market for front December and January delivery. ARB to Asian market for Dec and Jan were traded at around $161 and $155 respectively. Sentiments for Asian market strengthened, with cash differentials firmer for both Dec and Jan discussions. In window 2/0 parcel for 2h Dec delivery basis Chiba was once traded at Dec FEI plus $9. 4/0 parcel for earlier Jan delivery was bid at Dec FEI plus $2, equates to Jan FEI plus $7 basis Yantai, and Jan FEI plus $5 basis Ningbo, respectively. Outside window 4/0 parcel for 2h Dec delivery into Ningbo was changed hand at Dec FEI plus $12 reportedly. One Chinese PDH procured a late Dec delivered Canadian propane at Dec CP plus 70s. Pertamina was reported to award their buy tender for 1/1 or 2/2 for last decade dec delivery at Dec CP plus $60s. One Schina importer tendered to buy 1/1 or 2/2 for Jan delivery, result is yet to be known. Similar offers into N.China ranged from Jan CP plus $90s to $100, yet to hear any deal concluded. Other than spot, a couple of PDH users have issued buy term tenders to secure next year term supply, which offers are expected to edge up in view of front supply tightness.
