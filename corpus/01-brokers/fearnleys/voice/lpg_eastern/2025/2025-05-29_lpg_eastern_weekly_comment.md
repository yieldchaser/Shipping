## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco announced Jun CP at $600 for C3 and $570 for C4, down by $10 and $20 respectively, which unsurprisingly higher than expectation. Jun C3/C4 enlarged to $30, reflecting long supply on butane. Evenly split cargo for Jun lifting was assessed at Jun CP minus $60s or even lower, in view of thin discussions, heavy backwardation into Jul and downward CFR bids. After Jun CP announced, Jul C3 CP surged to $575.

**FAR EAST**

 Slim window after CP set, with 23/0 for 1h Jul delivery bid at Jul FEI plus $3 and yet to see offers. Earlier this week a handful of buyers are trying to cover shorts for 23/0 2h Jun delivery basis Chiba and bid once surged to Jun FEI plus $9. Outside window a flurry of tenders floating. Indonesia Pertamina awarded their buy tender for mid Jul delivered evenly split cargo at Jul CP plus $10 reportedly. Lotte Chemical cancelled their buy tender on heavy butane cargo for Jul delivery as price not attractive versus napatha for cracking reportedly. One SChina cracker user awarded their buy tender for 11-22 kt C4 before 10/Jun delivery at Jun CP minus single digit, while another Chinese reported to buy 23 kt C4 for Jul delivery at the equivalence price of Jul CP minus single digit. CPC also issued tender to procure a 1/1 parcel for Jul yet to hear result. Despite active butane inquiry, a handful of heavy butane cargos lifted ex AG are reported unsold and buying appetite inhibited as of slow consumptions. Propane discussions are healthier but with softened premium on non-US stems. HTC tendered to buy 23/0 for last decade Jul delivery. A couple of PDH users have issued tenders to secure Jul delivery propane (excluded US stems), which awarded at Jul CP plus high 20s to mid-high 30s range based on different location. We are expecting a few more petchem players buy tender next week to secure Jul feedstock. However importers refrained from speculative buying and higher operation motivation due to negative margins. Other than normal stems, we’ve seen over 900kt special origin cargos arrived in China for both Apr and May, 20% up than average in Q1 2025.
