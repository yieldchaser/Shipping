## Market Commentary

Saudi Aramco cut Oct Cp to $495 for C3 and $475 for C4, which fall shorts of market expectations as earlier recommendation reported at over $540 for C3. The surprising price reduction is reckoned as strategic move to spark term buyer interest, especially when Indian NOCs issued buy term tender for US stems. Forward month Cp swaps immediately dived and at the time of writing, Nov Cp C3 was only traded at $468. The price drop stimulated the buying interests and cash differential for AG FOB Nov rapidly changed from minus mid single digit to teens premium. Comparatively, US fobs remained illiquid as of the narrowing ARB. Cp link AG cargos are more affordable especially to Chinese players. Notional netback to CFR Asia is assessed at 3ish cpg which is close to cancellation level and we’ve heard a couple of late Oct lifting parcels were reported cancelled. CFR Asia market is in great volatility with spot price extend losses as of dull demand over uncertainties. 23/0 for 1h Nov delivery basis Chiba was offered at Nov FEI minus $13 yet to attract bid. HTC was reported to award their buy tender for 23/0 for late Nov delivery at Oct FEI plus small digit. E1 also issued spot tender to procure a 2/2 for Nov delivery.
