## Market Commentary

MEG Spot differentials for evenly split cargo maintained at teens premium level. Qatar Energy issued sale tender to offer 3/1 or 2/2 ratio for 22/26 May lifting. By the time of writing, May CP was traded at $575 for C3 and C3/C4 at $20. Indian PSU HPCL issued buy tender for May and Jun evenly split cargos for either FOB or CFR, which was reported concluded at delivered basis.

**FAR EAST**

 Despite the US-China trade friction, price differentials improved amid strong bids for 2h May propane. 23/0 for 2h May delivery basis Chiba was changed hand at May FEI minus $1.5, vs last thurs’ bid at minus $10.88. Japanese and Korean importers were reported seeking Us stems and freed up their non-US tons to China market. May FEI surged up surprisingly by over $60 vs last Thur, with anticipation on potential tariff negotiations, and paper hedging on FEI/CP and FEI/MB. ChinaGas was reported to award a 23/0 for May delivery at May CP plus mid high 50s. Similarly, FIC was also said to procure a 23/0 parcel for May at a fixed price which is equivalent to similar level. Huayi also tendered to buy propane for May to 1H Jun delivery. With current flat price and premium, the landed costs just barely breakeven for PDH to maintain operation. The overall PDH run rate have dropped to around 65% and might continue to drop as of more tentative turnarounds in May. One 3/1 Iranian stem for 1h May delivery was heard sold via tender at May CP plus 30s, while the other two cargos via tender are not awarded and expected to be re-tendered.
