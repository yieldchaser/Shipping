## Market Commentary

MEG Spot differentials for Jul liftings are under pressure as of subdued interests. BPCL was reported to award their buy tender for a Jul evenly split FOB cargo at Jul CP minus 40s. At the time of writing, Jul CP C3 was traded at $585 and C3/C4 was traded at $30.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium for Jul delivery propane softened this week. Despite some hiccups in Us loadings, cargos for Jul delivery are still in ample supply as importers reselling in window. A couple of 46/0 cargos for 2h Jul delivery basis Ningbo was traded at Jul FEI plus mid-single digit. And several parcels of 23/0 for 2h Jul delivery basis Chiba were changed hand at Jul FEI plus $3 and afterwards $1.5. Outside window one PDH importer was reported to secure two lots of 46/0 for end Jul delivery into Echina and NChina at Jul FEI plus mid-high and high single digit respectively, which cargos are reckoned to be US origin. With additional 10% tariff into China, US stems are still affordable to import compared to non-Us stem, with minimal price advantages. Other than propane, butane demand restored for petchem cracking, together with some speculative purchase. HTC was reported to secure 0/2 for late Jul delivery at Jul MOPJ minus 30s, which is the 3rd butane parcel procured for Jul delivery. Similarly, FPC was reported to award similar cargo at Jul MOPJ minus 40s. One Jul delivery butane cargo was heard traded into China at fixed price of $510, equates to Jul CP minus 40s roundabouts, has stimulated other Chinese players’ buying interests.
