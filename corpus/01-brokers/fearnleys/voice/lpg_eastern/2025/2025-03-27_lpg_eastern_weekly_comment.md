## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco announced Apr CP at $615 for C3 and $605 for C4, a rollover from Mar, which is in line with swap market. For term liftings in Apr, Aramco was reported to cut the plus 10% tolerance, which naturally lent support to the CP, together with the rising crude price. However as in lack of spot buying inquiries from India etc, evenly split cargos are still assessed at mid high teens discounts to CP. By the time of writing, May CP was traded at $600 for C3 and C3/C4 at $10.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium soared up further as supply tightness extended from 2h Apr to 1h May delivery. 23/0 for 2h Apr delivery into Far East was changed hand at Apr FEI plus low 20s, and similar parcel for 1h May delivery was concluded at Apr FEI plus low-mid single, which also equates to May FEI plus low 20s. Bids for 2h May parcels softened relatively but still at plus $17 on the window n yet to elicit offers. Outside window discussions also strengthened with limited offers seen. HTC was reported to procure a 23kt butane parcel for 1-20 May delivery at May MOPJ minus $30, and subsequently issued another tender to secure 2/2 or 0/2 or 2/0 for last decade May delivery, reflecting the strong cracking demand. ChinaGas was also said to award their buy tender for one 4/0 parcel on 1h May delivery into Quanzhou at May FEI plus mid high 20s. FPCC cancelled their buy tender for flex ratio on 1h May delivery due to limited offers and unattractive price. Pertamina also retendered to procure early May delivery even split cargo, which is still in validity. CPC also issued tender to procure one 1/1 or 2/2 for 1h May delivery. With the soaring flat price and cash premiums, petchem buyers are revaluating the narrowing margins and might adjust the run rate accordingly.
