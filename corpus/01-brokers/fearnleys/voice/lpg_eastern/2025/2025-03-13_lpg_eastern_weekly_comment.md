## Market Commentary

MEG Spot differentials for Apr lifting remained stable and one Middle East trading house is reported to sell two evenly split cargo for Apr lifting at Apr CP minus high teens. Despite the discounts, buying inquiries are still limited as of muted Indian spot demand. By the time of writing, Apr propane CP was traded at $595 and butane at $585. Apr/May CP was traded at $20 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium soared up with surging demand especially for full propane cargo on Apr delivery, due to the delays from US terminals caused by fog, and prompt buyings to cover position before next week China conference. Two 23/0 parcels for 1h and 2h Apr delivery were traded in window, with one done at $614 equating to Apr FEI plus $28, and the other at plus $19. Outside window a number of 46/0 cargos were also placed into China from Apr FEI plus hi teens to low mid 20s, varying by regions and conditions. A handful of tenders are floating this week also. FPCC cancelled their buy tender for two propane cargos delivered in Apr and May as offers’ discounts not satisfactory. Sinobenny was reported to procure one 11kt C3 only for Apr delivery at Apr CP plus 30s reportedly. Tianjin bohai issued buy tender for two 46/0 for 1H and 2H May delivery respectively. And HTC issued tender to buy 23kt butane for 1st decade May delivery. At the same time, four heavy propane cargos of special origins for mid Apr delivery was offered via a sale tender.
