## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Jun Propane CP swaps surged up to $340, in tandem with the crude rise. June Butane CP was assessed at $15 lower at $325. Saudi Aramco has released acceptance for June liftings, with 9 to 10 cargos deferments/cuts reportedly and half for Indian market. This being said, supply is still overwhelmed demand and FOBs discussed at heavy discounts. Qatar Petroleum was said to sell another late June lifting heavy propane cargo at minus 10 to Jul CP, similar price level to their last sale tender for 18/20th June lifting evenly split cargo . Jun/Jul CP still traded at 10$ backwardation. We expect Jul CP might dive deeper after June CP set, as front length hasn’t been absorbed fully and might roll into July.

**FAR EAST**

: Cash premium on June delivery cargos fell sharply amid bearish demand in EAST this week. 23k Propane for 2h Jun delivery was traded at June FEI plus $2 earlier this week, and on Thursday offer plunged to Jun CP minus $9, equates to Jun FEI minus $5. It’s indeed a very tempting price for North East Asian importers, seeing a delivered cargo offered at discount to CP. But majority are stuck with high inventory and expect lower import costs due to backwardation. Spot cargo for Jun arrival has been offered at CP plus teens into China but yet to hear interest either. China import in May likely to hike up to 2mmt, up by over 30% compare to April. One EChina PDH user issued semi-term tender to secure 5 full cargo propane delivered from Jul to Dec. Undoubtedly it’s a pretty good timing to secure term amid such oversupply market.
