## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco set April CP at $230 for Propane and $240 for Butane, slashed down by nearly 50% respectively. Despite it is lowest level in recent 20 years, some players were expecting lower numbers, especially seeing swap traded below $200 for last two weeks. The prompt demand from India due to lockdown, and the slow recovery from China demand, both somehow lends supports to it. May CP is traded at $235 at the time being, but it is yet too early to say whether it can stand firm amid this volatile market. But due to the prompt demand on evenly split cargos, C3/C4 spread is traded at $18 now, wider than Apr and expected to enlarge continuously.

**FAR EAST**

: The week is dominant by tenders from India and Indonesia. India PSUs were reported to only cover 66-110k tonnes via tenders this week, and three majors still tendered to buy nearly 500k tonnes evenly split cargos in total, delivered from Apr to Early June. Indonesia Pertamina also tendered to buy one equal ratio cargos for 2h Apr or May arrival. On Thursday one parcel of 23k propane for 1h May delivery was traded at May FEI plus $8 on window, which equates to May CP plus high teens. Late last week a couple of propane cargos were sold at May CP plus 40ish into China. But this week sellers target for higher level, as other market demand spiked up with anticipation for less supply on May arrival due to some cancellations for Apr liftings ex US.
