## Market Commentary

MEG Nov Propane CP surged up by nearly $25 to $407 this week, following the oil rebound, coupled with the active inquiries on evenly split cargoes. Sellers are targeting for premium in view of less butane available from US, while buyers bid at teens discounts. The strong Nov swap turned Nov/Dec into backwardation at $3.

**FAR EAST**

 Far East value extended gains this week, as traders covering shorts caused by previous US delays and concerning on coming Hurricane Delta. FEI Nov boosts up to $431 and FEI/CP Nov enlarged to $26 from last week’s $18. Cash premium for 1h Nov delivery propane was finally turned into positive territory, with 23k propane parcel concluded at Nov FEI plus low-mid single digit, which hasn’t been seen since this June. We believe the potential delays will still lend support to far east market, until terminals clear all the backlogs. Despite the China holiday, a couple of Chinese importers managed to cover some parcels for Nov delivery and still explore to buy more.
