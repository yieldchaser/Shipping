## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Oct Propane swap fell to $350 but Butane traded at $6 higher. Contango into Nov widened to $10 from last week’s $3, due to lackluster demand for the front. Qatar Petroleum is reported that it didn’t award sale tender for 6/7th Oct lifting propane, understanding the netback into FAR EAST market is dreadful for such ratio. KPC issued tender to sell one early Oct lifting heavy butane cargo, which is still under validity.

**FAR EAST**

: The oversupply situation is still not eased. Propane for 1h Oct delivery was traded under downward pressure, at high 20s to low mid 30s discounts to Oct FEI. Similar parcel for 2h Oct delivery is assessed at more than $10 contango with expectations on recovery from heating demand into winter and new petchem use. Taiwan FPCC awarded its buy tender to secure 23k propane for end Oct delivery at MOPJ minus 30. Discussions for other regions focus on evenly split cargo mainly. BPCL awarded its tender to purchase one cargo for Oct delivery at CP plus high 20s based on two disports. Similar cargo for 2h Oct delivery was sold into SEA at Oct CP plus teens. In addition, one China petchem importer is reported to cover such ratio for later Oct delivery but at discounts to Oct FEI.
