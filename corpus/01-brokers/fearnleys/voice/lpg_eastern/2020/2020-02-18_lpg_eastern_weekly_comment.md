## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco released Mar acceptance with three to four cargos reported being cancelled/deferred. Therefore the front CP firmed by $10 to $414 compared with last week, while Mar/Apr Cp backwardation widens further to around $50, reflecting the bearish sentiment on forward market due to uncertainties on general demand. KPC was reported to award their 6/7th Mar lifting 2/2 at mid-single digit discount to Mar Cp.

**FAR EAST**

: Active bids in window on Thursday for propane delivered in 2h Mar and 1H April into Japan/Korea, with buying ideas’ at premium of $16 basis delivered month Fei. Discussions into China remains thin as demand still yet to recover. However China on Tuesday announced will accept applications from some China companies for tariff exclusions on some Us imports. Importers are considering to submit applications to acquire such exemptions and have more diversified source and more competitive import costs, especially for petchem buyers located in east and north China. One Chinese Echina PDH has awarded some parcel of their buy tender at 60s, and would like to wait and see the Tariff exemptions results in Mar before purchasing more for forward months. Pertamina also issued buy tender for two equal ratio cargos delivered in early and mid Apr respectively.
