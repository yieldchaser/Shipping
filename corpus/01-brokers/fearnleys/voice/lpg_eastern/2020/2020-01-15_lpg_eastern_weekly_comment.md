## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Swap price still stumbles this week. At the time of writing, Feb Propane CP weakened to $510 while Mar at deep backwardation at $452. However, butane CP has edged up due to tight MEG supply amid robust demand from India and Indonesia, currently traded at $550. Qatar Petroleum was reported to award their propane sale tender for 24/27th Feb lifting but the price is yet to know. A bundle of Q2 lifting FOBs for equal ratio split were changing hand at loading month CP plus mid-single digit.

**FAR EAST**

: Far East market is strengthened due to the delay caused by foggy weather in US. Feb FEI/CP kept narrowing down from previous minus $20ish to minus $10. Bids on propane for 2h Feb were at Feb FEI plus mid-high 20s in window, while offers off window pegged at high 30s to $40. Several Chinese PDH users are reported to have secured propane feedstock delivered in 2h Feb at the premium ranging from low-mid 80s to $90ish on Feb CP link, which equates to $20ish premium on FOB netback. Feb purchase has almost finished, and players started exploring on Mar arrival tons. Importers specialized in residential markets are quieter relatively, as domestic prices weakening with refinery destocking before CNY. FPCC cancelled their buy tender for 22kt propane or 22/22 during end Feb delivery, and is expected to come out for Mar for better economy as widen spread between FEI and MOPJ in Mar than in Feb. Indonesia PERTAMINA has issued tender to secure three equal ratio cargos delivered from end Feb to 1h Mar.
