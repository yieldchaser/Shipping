## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Sep Propane CP stabilized at $368 at the time of writing, and Butane traded at $13 less. CP spread from Sep to Nov is traded at nearly flattish, yet to reflect the seasonal demand changes. ADNOC released their Sep lifting acceptances with advancements reported as well. Supply ex MEG still appears long as producers reported with spot availability for 2h Sep liftings.

**FAR EAST**

: Discussions for 1h Sep delivery propane were muted, and players attempt to swap cargo for later arrival. Propane for 2h Sep delivery was traded at FEI minus mid-teens for half parcel, and minus high teens respectively for a full lot. Despite the potential 10-11 cancellations from Aug to Sep liftings ex US, the Arb volume was not expected to decline much due to terminal resales and recording new high exports for Jul and Aug. Taiwan FPCC issued buy tender to secure 23kt LPG for 2h Sep delivery, either 11/11 or pure propane or butane. Other East buyers are exploring cargos for similar period or end Sep/Early Oct delivery, but not rush to move with current high inventory.
