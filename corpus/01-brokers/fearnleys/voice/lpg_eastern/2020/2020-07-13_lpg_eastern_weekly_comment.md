## Market Commentary

MEG Aug swap CP surged strongly to $372, up by $24 and backwardation into Sep enlarged to $15. ADNOC acceptance is released with advancements reported, as lifters prefer to nominate for later month loading. KPC finally sold their 4/5 Aug lifting 3/1 cargo at Aug CP minus around $30 via tender. Netback on heavy propane FOBs are still under heavy discounts. To the contrary, equal ratio FOB discussion improves with the increasing demand on butane.

**FAR EAST**

 Asian market improved this week with far east players emerged to cover Aug demand. Earlier this week, 23kt propane parcels have been traded at Aug FEI minus mid-teens for 1h and low teens for 2h Aug delivery respectively. On Wed similar parcel for 2h Aug delivery was traded at Aug FEI minus $7 only, amid the stronger paper swap. In addition to this, several equal ratio cargos have been placed into China and some Chinese major is keen to swap by taking in heavy butane ratios. Two PDHs located in EChina with total capacity exceeding 1mmt/y propylene are under trial run. And one 1.1mmt/y ethylene cracker located in Panjin NChina is expected to come online next week, which now receiving their first VLGC cargo for feedstock.
