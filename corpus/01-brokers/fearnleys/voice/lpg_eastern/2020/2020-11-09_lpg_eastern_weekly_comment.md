## Market Commentary

MEG Dec Propane CP surged up to $450 at the time of writing, gaining supports from front strong demand. Butane was traded at $10 higher. Dec/Jan CP traded at $12 backwardation, and it’s likely to enlarge further, with anticipation of forthcoming ARB volume into EAST. ADNOC released their Dec acceptances with some delays being reported, since majority tend to nominate early liftings to avoid backwardation into Jan. KPC issued tender to sell one 11/11 loading 6/7th Dec. While QP tendered to sell one 21/25th Dec lifting 4/0 or 3/1. FOB discussions for Dec lifting equal ratio splits were at Dec CP minus around $10 while heavy propane cargo was bid at heavier discounts.

**FAR EAST**

 A flurry of bids was shown in/off window this week. Several 23kt propane parcels for 2h Dec delivery were traded Dec FEI plus $9/10. And outside window traders and importers are actively inquiring Dec delivery cargos, propane mainly. Evenly split cargos were slightly less favorable into NEA. Other than spot discussions, intensive term negotiations are going on this week. Indian BPCL was reported to secure 90% of their term volumes at CP minus mid high single digits. One Schina traditional importer awarded their annual tender for 11/11 monthly at CP plus low 30s reportedly. Following this, another two petchem importers also issued term tender to secure heavy or full propane cargos for feedstock use.
