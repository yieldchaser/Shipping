## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Despite the crude falls to the lowest in last 12-month, Mar Propane CP stands firm at $420, with Butane CP at $50 higher. However, Mar/Apr backwardation further widens to $85, reflecting the concerns on declining demand due to COVID-19 outbreak globally. Saudi has deferred the announcement on Mar CP till this Sunday.

**FAR EAST**

： A couple of cargos for 2h Mar and 1h Apr delivery were changing hands this week. Deals for propane in 2h Mar delivery were concluded at Mar FEI plus $17, and 1h Apr reportedly at Apr FEI plus mid-teens as well. With potentials for Chinese to import US origin in April, Mar/April FEI has narrowed down by $5 WOW and April FEI/CP maintains at above $22, whilst Mar FEI/CP at -$51 only. This week low risk areas in China has slowly resumed work, yet to fully recover. One EChina PDH still has appetite in Mar arrival propane but other majority have finished Mar purchase and waited for tariff exemption news before further programing for April. Some Schina importers are speeding up distribution to resolve high inventory, as so far still a handful of cargos sitting on the water… Indonesia Pertamina has reportedly awarded one equal ratio cargo for early April arrival into Kalbut and retendered to buy another cargo for mid-April arrival.
