## Market Commentary

**MEG:** 1st round June CP recommendation ranged widely from $300-$370 for Propane with majority at $330-340, in line with swap value. Butane was recommended at parity to propane or $10-15 less. Saudi Aramco is reported to have a heavy butane spot cargo for June lifting. And other AG producers including QP and ADNOC are also said to have spots avail. This adds on more volume to an oversupplied market, which revealed a reason why June/Jul CP backwardation has enlarged from $10 to $30 now.

**FAR EAST**

: With a bunch of June arrival cargos unabsorbed, front still appears long amid lukewarm demand and Jul price discussion is edging down. One full cargo propane for 1h Jul arrival was reported sold at Jul FEI minus $4 while another equal ratio for Jul arrival was at even lower minus $7 reportedly. Asian importers are still hesitant to move for July spot amid high inventory. Despite the narrowing Arb, there is no June cancellations so far. And lifters are said now consider shipping cargos via CAPE instead of Houston Channel to avoid the depressed Jul market and capture the contango into Aug.
