## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Despite the crude slumped this week, swap value came up drastically with anticipation on Saudi cuts for May term liftings. May Propane CP has traded at $314 at the time of writing and the spread between Butane narrowed down to flattish or $2 only. Due to front strength, May/June backwardation has enlarged to around $20.

**FAR EAST**

: Active bids in window this week again but only one parcel of 23kt propane for 2h May delivery has been traded at May FEI plus $19. Comparatively, a flurry of deals were concluded into China this week. One full cargo propane for 2h May delivery reported sold into China at May FEI plus 20ish. And several evenly split cargos were sold at May CP plus 40ish. Despite the premium softening to 30ish level into India and Indonesia this week, the household demands from both regions continuously lend support to the EAST market. India has announced further lockdown and players expect its June purchase requests. Indonesia Pertamina issued several tenders this week to secure evenly split cargos from End May to July delivery, with requests on fixed price for June/July tons. Taiwan CPC issued buy tender for end May/early June 11/11 but limited offers received.
