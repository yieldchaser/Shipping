## Market Commentary

MEG A couple of Jan lifting FOBs from producers emerged this week. Qatar Petroleum was reported to sell one 12/13th Jan lifting evenly split cargo at CP minus 30ish. This is their 2nd spot cargo sale for Jan lifting, and it is said to sell the 3rd one for mid-Jan lifting, either 2/2 or 3/1 cargo. Followed the crude and the relatively tightness on pure propane supply for Jan delivery basis, Jan Propane CP gained the support and jumped to $482, rallied up by $24 compared to last week. However, Butane is comparatively weaker and was traded at $2-4 less. Saudi Aramco is yet to release their Jan lifting schedule by the time of writing.

**FAR EAST**

 The shipping delay situation in Panama Canal doesn’t get eased and continuously affects the Dec and even Jan delivery. Jan FEI/CP has expanded further to $63 due to the front tightness on propane and the soaring freight. 23kt propane lot for 2h Jan delivery was bid in window at Jan FEI plus $21 while offer at $35. Off window, Taiwan FPCC secured 22k propane for 1-10th Feb Delivery at Feb FEI plus high 30s via a tender, which equates to Feb CP plus low 70s. Other than this, spot discussions are stuck in stalemate as buyers not yet ready to afford the current premium. India IOC issued term buy tender to secure 1 or 2 lots of 9.5/10.5 cargo for monthly delivery into Kandla in 2021.
