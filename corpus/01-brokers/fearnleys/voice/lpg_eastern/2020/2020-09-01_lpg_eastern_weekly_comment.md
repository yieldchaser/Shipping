## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco has maintained Sep Propane CP at $365 and raised up Butane CP by 10$ to $355, which is below most expectations, and even lower than swap value. Propane Oct CP edged up to $382 but dropped to $366 on Thursday, reflecting the length in the front market. And FOB propane netback seems miserable as delivered value is traded under heavy discounts. Comparatively, butane is more favorable. KPC awarded its 27/28th Sep lifting equal ratio cargo tender at Oct CP minus mid-teens reportedly, which significantly improved from previous FOB sales. QP issued sale tender to offer one 6/7th Oct lifting 45/0, which is due to close by 8th Sep.

**FAR EAST**

: Propane for 2h Sep market appears dreadful this week, with one 2h Sep 23/0 parcel traded at Sep FEI minus $30. Similar cargo for 1h Oct delivery is offered at Oct FEI minus high teens discount but barely attract buying interests. In contrary, butane discussion is more active. Indian IOC secured two evenly split cargos at CP plus 30s for 2nd and 3rd decade Oct delivery respectively. Similarly, BPCL issued buy tender to secure one evenly split cargo for 7-25th Oct delivery for 2 disports basis, which is yet to close. PV Gas also purchased one 11/11 parcel for early Oct delivery promptly at CP plus high 20s.
