## Market Commentary

MEG Dec Propane CP surged up to $445, up by $30 this week, reflecting the strength in front market. Butane CP was traded at $8 higher than propane at the time of writing, slowly narrowed down as being less favorable in winter season. Dec/Jan CP spread was at $11 backwardation and might enlarge further. Qatar Petroleum released the Dec lifting with not cuts being heard. AG fobs discussions are in a stalemate as buyers exploring at double digit discounts or more, while sellers targeting at single digit only.

**FAR EAST**

 Another hectic week with active inquires for Dec delivery cargos, especially on propane. Cash premium stands firmer as better winter demand against continuous delays on US cargos via panama. A plenty of buyers are bidding propane for Dec delivery in and off window but only a few sellers emerged. Half cargo propane parcel for 2h Delivery was bid at mid-high single digit on FEI link while sellers asking at double digits premium. One full cargo propane for 2h Dec delivery was sold into E.China at Dec FEI plus teens, and half cargo was bid at Dec CP plus from 20s to 30s subject to different conditions. Other than this, a handful of tenders are running in the market. Korean HTC awarded their buy tender for 23kt propane in 1h Dec delivery at FEI plus high single digit reportedly. Taiwan FPCC was reported to secure 23kt propane for 1h Dec delivery at Nov FEI plus high single digit, while CPC’s buy tender for 11/11 last decade Dec delivery was still yet to award. CPC also issued a FOB/DES swap tender by selling 176k-264k FOBs and buying back 8 to 12 lots of 11/11 on delivery basis for next year.
