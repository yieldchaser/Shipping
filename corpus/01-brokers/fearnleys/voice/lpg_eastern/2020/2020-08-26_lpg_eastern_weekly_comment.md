## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Sep CP inched up this week to $372 and Sep/Oct CP traded at $2 contango. Regardless the bullish expectation on Q4 demand, Q4 CP contango appears narrow and might not widen until real demand kicks in. 1st round CP have been recommended from $375 to $390 for propane, and $10 less or parity for butane. Netbacks for FOB AGs are discussed at minus 20s but is improving with the fresh Indian demand on preparation for Deepavali.

**FAR EAST**

: A couple of propane parcels from 2h Sep to 1h Oct delivery have been traded this week. 2h Sep 23/0 was done at Sep CP minus high single digit, equates to Sep FEI minus mid high teens. And similar cargo for 1h Oct was reported done at Oct FEI minus high single digit. Despite that some US loadings are delayed due to the hurricane, FAR EAST price stabilized and even softened later this week. One of the reasons being is the overlength for 2h Sep arrival cargos, against thin buying interests due to high inventory. Indian IOC issued tender to purchase two evenly split cargos for 2nd and 3rd decade Oct delivery, which is yet to close.
