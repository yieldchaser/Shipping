## Market Commentary

MEG Despite the deteriorating FOB netback, Aug CP surged up to $348, up by $14 compared to last week. C3/C4 spread has narrowed down to $10 from previous $20, due to the improving butane cracking demand. QP has released their Aug acceptance with some advancements. It is also reported to award its sale tender for 4-7th Aug lifting 3/1 cargo at Aug FEI minus mid-30s, which equates to Aug CP minus 40ish. KPC failed to award the sale tender of 1/2nd Aug lifting 3/1 and retendered to 4/5th Aug lifting, which is due by this Friday. One Chinese importer is offering a mid Aug lifting full propane at Aug CP minus high teens but yet to attract bids.

**FAR EAST**

 Aug delivered propane value is still under heavy discounts to Aug FEI, with offers for 1h Aug parcel at minus $17 while bid at minus $20. Off window Aug buying inquiries are quite thin this week, except for Korean cracking use. One Korean petchem user purchased a late Aug delivery 23/0 at Aug FEI minus low teens reportedly. Another Korean importer tendered to buy monthly 23kt butane delivered from Oct 2020 to Mar 2021. One NChina importer issued a mini buy tender to secure two to four full propane cargo delivered during Aug 2020 till Dec 2021. Although US FOB netbacks are into cancellation territory, some players still rather to lift tons and sit for contango with anticipation on recovery into Sep and Q4.
