## Market Commentary

MEG Aug CP has been volatile, with highest being traded to $390 earlier this week and slashed by $20 to $370 on Thursday. Hence, 1st round CP idea ranged widely from $350 to $400. With the decline on front CP, Aug/Sep turned into a slight contango. Saudi Aramco released Aug acceptance for with some liftings being advanced. KPC awarded their CFR tender to sell one 3/1 cargo delivered during end Aug early Sep into East, at the price of Sep CP minus mid-teens reportedly.

**FAR EAST**

 The cash discounts for Aug delivery propane has narrowed to mid low single digit to Aug FEI. One 23kt propane for 2h Aug delivery was changing hand at Aug FEI minus $3. Aug FEI/CP fluctuates wildly from -$29 to +$2.5 by the time of writing, partly back by the robust freight rate. One Korean petchem user issued a semi tender to secure monthly 23k propane delivered from Sep to next Feb. One EChina PDH user issued annual term tender to purchase monthly 23k to 46k propane delivered into Ningbo in 2021. Indonesia PERTAMINA has issued buy tender for monthly one evenly split cargo lifting ex US from Sep to Dec, which is still in validity.
