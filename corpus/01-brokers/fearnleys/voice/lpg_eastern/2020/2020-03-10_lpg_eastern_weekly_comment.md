## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Oil price has slashed down by over 25% in this unprecedented oil war. Swap number fluctuates in heavy decline, as April C3 CP plummeted by more than $100 at the start of this week and at the moment traded at $255. C3/C4 spread has rapidly narrowed down from previous $45 to $10, as demand mainly driven by propane for petchem especially into China. Producers are boosting their oil production, and on mid-week Saudi Aramco was reported to sell 4-5 spot cargos loading in Later Mar and April, and still have spot volume avails. Additionally, Qatar Petroleum also issued sale tender for one 23-25 Apr loading heavy propane or equal ratio cargo.

**FAR EAST**

: Market is oversupplied with a handful of spot cargos available from AG out of a sudden. Cash premium came down from 20ish to teens and now bids at discounted level only to Apr FEI. Chinese Importers very actively purchased Apr cargos, with more than 3 full propane cargos being sold from late last week and price ranging from 40 to mid-40s on Apr CP link. Since more sellers seen this week, remaining buyers lowered down or held back bids. This being said, PDHs and crackers have seen attractive margin amid lower feedstock price than before. And it might be a good timing to stockpile before entering into contango in forward months, if with storage allowance.
