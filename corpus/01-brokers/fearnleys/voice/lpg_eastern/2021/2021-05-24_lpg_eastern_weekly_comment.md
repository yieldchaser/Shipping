## Market Commentary

MEG An unexpected surge this week on Jun swaps. At the time of writing, June C3 CP was traded at $522 and C3/C4 at $5 differentials. Jun/Jul C3 CP entered a slight contango structure at $2-3. First round June CP recommendations ranged from $500 to 515 for propane, but we believe with the rise in paper swap, 2nd round idea will edge up accordingly. KPC awarded another tender of 33/11 cargo for end Jun lifting reportedly at June CP minus high single digit, which was also their 5th spot sales for Jun loading.

**FAR EAST**

 Far East Index spiked up with FEI/CP Jun enlarged to $43, from last week’s $27.5. There are concerns over tightness of supply in the front, due to the June cancellations ex US, together with the temporary delays from Panama congestions. One 23k propane for 2h Jun delivery was traded at Jun FEI plus $6.5 on Thursday in window. India IOC issued buy tender to secure one evenly split cargo for 1h Jul delivery. One EChina Petchem user have secured 23k propane for late Jun delivery at Jun FEI plus low single and subsequently issued another tender to buy 46k propane for 1h Jul delivery for their cracking feedstock.
