## Market Commentary

MEG Oct Propane CP swap rallied up to $752, increased by nearly $60 compared to last week. However, Butane was traded at $6 cheaper to Propane, due to muted inquires in the front for evenly split cargos. Nov CP was traded at $4 contango, reflecting the good expectations on winter demand for forward months.

**FAR EAST**

 Discussion level for Oct delivered propane remained stable amid limited buying inquires. 23kt Propane parcel for 2h Oct delivery was traded at balance 16-30th Sep FEI, which equates to Oct FEI minus $5. Comparatively, paper swaps on FEI were buoyed up, following the rise in Crude, other light ends in upstream, and gained an unexpected support from Chinese funds who are hedging for domestic DCE futures. Off window a handful of tenders are flowing in the market this week. HTC issued buy tender to secure 23kt propane for end Oct early Nov delivery. One SChina importer tendered to procure a 11/11 parcel for Nov delivery. Taiwan CPC issued buy tender for one 11/11 parcel during 2h Oct delivery. Apart from spot tenders, end users emerged with their term procurement tenders. One EChina PDH user issued annual term tender to secure monthly 23kt propane from Jan to Dec 2022 delivery. Indian IOC was reported to award their last term tenders for monthly one evenly cargo at CP minus low single digit to double digits, depending on which month loading. And they’ve again issued two more tenders to secure similar volume for Q1 and Q2 delivery. Two Philippine importers issued term tender as well, with one inquiring for monthly 30-40kt LPG and the other for monthly 40-46kt.
