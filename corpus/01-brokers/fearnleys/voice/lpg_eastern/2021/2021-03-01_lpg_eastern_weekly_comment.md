## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco raised up Mar propane CP by $20 to $625 and Butane CP by $10 to $595, which is more or less within expectations. Apr propane CP was traded at $575, while spread to butane narrow to $20, expecting on Butane demand recovery due to summer. Evenly split cargos for Apr lifting was shown at low teens discount while buyer expects more discounts as netback into EAST market still not pleasant, and also the deep backwardation into May for around $37, despite the weakening freight.

**FAR EAST**

 A flurry of bids in the window this week. Bid idea for 1h Apr delivery was at Apr FEI plus mid 20s while offer at low 30s, and 2h Apr discussing idea at $3-4 lower. One SChina Importer awarded via tender to secure one 2h Apr delivery 11/11 split at partially Apr CP plus low teens and partially Apr FEI plus 20s. Another SChina PDH user issued tender for 23k C3 for 1-10 Apr delivery into Dongguan. And one Korean Importer also tendered to buy 11/11 split delivered into Daesan delivered in 1h Apr.
