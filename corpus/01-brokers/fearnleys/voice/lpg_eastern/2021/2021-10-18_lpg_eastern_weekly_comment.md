## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco released Nov acceptance with no cuts but some advanced liftings reported, and they were said to have ample butane supply and sold one heavy butane cargo for end Oct loading. ADNOC LNG was also reported to sell one mid Nov lifting evenly split ratio at CP plus small premium. Nov C3 CP was traded at $859 at the time of writing, and C3/C4 spread narrowed down further to $23.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials for Nov delivery cargos have improved as winter demand picking up against some potential delays caused by Panama prolonged waiting. 23kt propane for 2h Nov delivery were traded at fixed price of $905 on Wed and $897 on Thurs, equates to Nov FEI plus small digits on the day of trading. Similar cargo for 1h Dec delivery was bid at Nov FEI minus $1 while offer at plus $1.88. There are a few Australia evenly split cargos available in the market, with one is offered basis loading mid Nov ex NWS and the other on CFR Japan basis during mid/2h Dec. At least four Chinese PDH users have issued term tender to secure monthly 23kt or 46kt propane for feedstock. And one EChina petchem user called spot tender to secure 46kt to 69kt for late Nov to 1h Dec delivery into Ningbo.
