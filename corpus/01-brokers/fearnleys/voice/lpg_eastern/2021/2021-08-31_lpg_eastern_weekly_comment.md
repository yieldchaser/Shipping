## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco raised up Sep propane CP by $5 to $665 and butane CP by $10 to $665, which is slightly lower than curve trading value. Oct Propane CP surged up to $690 at the time of writing, with Butane CP was assessed at parity. Oct/Nov CP was traded at a slight contango for $1-2. QP released their Oct acceptance with most in line with nomination reportedly.

**FAR EAST**

 Discussions are slow this week, with big gaps between bids/offers. 23kt propane for Sep arrival was still shown in window this week at Oct FEI minus mid-single digit, while no buyers emerged as most are bidding for Oct program. During mid-week, one 23kt propane parcel for 1h Oct delivery was traded at Bal (2-30) Sep FEI minus $2. Following this, similar parcel was offered at Oct FEI minus 2 while buying idea at 3-4 bucks cheaper. Full propane cargo was offered at Oct FEI minus 3.5 in window while outside window minus 6 was reported done earlier. Off window, two Chinese Petchem users issued buy tenders to purchase 23kt propane each for Oct delivery, with price reported at Oct FEI minus small digits and Oct CP plus 30s respectively. Indian IOC also issued buy to secure at least 9 evenly split cargos for Q1 to Q3 2022 delivery, and demand for Q4 is expected to come later on.
