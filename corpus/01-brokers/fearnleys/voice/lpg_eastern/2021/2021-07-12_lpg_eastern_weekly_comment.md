## Market Commentary

MEG Despite the oil price slumped, Aug C3 CP was traded stably at $652, and C4 was traded at $10 less. Aug/Sep CP was traded at $4 backwardation. Market players are waiting for Saudi’s acceptance on Aug liftings, before taking further move. Qatar Petroleum was reported to sell their 12/13th Aug lifting full propane cargo.

**FAR EAST**

 A flurry of bids for 2h Aug delivered propane are shown in window this week. One 23kt propane parcel for 1h Sep delivery was concluded at Aug FEI plus $2. Off window one 23kt propane for 2h Aug was traded at Aug FEI plus $3 reportedly, and several full cargo propane for Aug delivery were changing hands at Aug FEI minus 3-4$ for 1h delivery period, and minus around $1 for 2h period. Although off window spot discussions are muted, several tenders are floating in the market. One EChina PDH user procured monthly 23kt propane for Sep to Nov delivery into Ningbo via tender. FPCC awarded their buy tender for 23kt propane in Aug delivery at Aug MOPJ minus $10. HTC secured 23kt propane for end Aug to early Sep delivery at FEI plus mid-single digit reportedly. However, supply on evenly split cargo looks tight relatively. CPC was reported did not award their buy tender for end Aug delivered 11/11 parcel. Similarly, Sinobenny did not award their tender for 11k upto 22k evenly split cargo either reportedly.
