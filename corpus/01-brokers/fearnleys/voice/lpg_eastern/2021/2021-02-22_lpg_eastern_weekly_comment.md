## Market Commentary

MEG Following the crude, Mar C3 CP surged up to $622 and C4 to $594 at the time of writing. 1st round recommendation ranged widely from $590 to $625 for propane, and butane at $20-30 less. Mar/Apr CP backwardation narrowed down to $38. Saudi Aramco was reported to cut 4-5 cargos for Mar lifting. However, with the coming OPEC+ meeting, market participants anticipate on production increase and expect Saudi exports to resume partially. Qatar Petroleum sold one late Mar lifting heavy propane cargo reportedly, and previously they’ve also sold one equal ratio cargo for 20/24th Mar lifting at around 40$ discount to Mar CP.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premiums for Mar arrival propane deteriorated as US terminals gradually resumed exports, however players reckon that it might still need 10 days or even more to clear the backlogs, plus the delay caused by the foggy weather. 23k propane parcel for Apr delivery was bid at Apr FEI plus $20ish this week, while no offers shown in window yet, as sellers are waiting for clear US loading schedules over the weekend. Chinese returned to market this week, and a couple of propane parcels for 2h Mar arrival were placed into China, at Mar FEI plus high 20s or Mar CP plus low teens reportedly. However, buying interests are slim, partly due to the negative margin in residential sector, and partly because a handful of PDHs have scheduled turnarounds or maintenances due to glitches. Apart from these, one Korean importer tendered to buy 3 to 6 lots of monthly 23k Butane parcels for delivery from Apr to Sep.
