## Market Commentary

MEG Due to the unexpected supply disruption from one Middle East producer, July C3 CP soared up to $587 at the time of writing, up by nearby $50. July C3/C4 was traded at flattish, reflecting the shortage on butane supply for front prompt lifting. A couple of traders were actively exploring equal ratio cargos to cover their short, caused by the unexpected delays or cancellations on their term liftings. KPC was reported to award their tender to sell three FOB cargos for Jul to Sep lifting, two of which are heavy propane ratio, and only one evenly split ratio. Qatar Petroluem also issued tender to sell one full propane cargo for 19/21th Jul lifting.

**FAR EAST**

 Failing to catch up with strong CP, Jul FEI/CP has narrowed to $18, down by $20 compared to last week. Cash premium discussion has inched up from low single to mid single digit on FEI link for CFR 2h Jul propane parcels. On mid-week, one 23k propane for 2h Jul delivery was traded at Jul CP plus $28 in the window. And similar cargo for such period was bid up to Jul FEI plus $6.5. Outside window, one SChina petchem importer has purchased half cargo propane parcel for 1h Jul delivery outside their tender. One Vietnam based player issued buy tender to secure a heavy propane cargo for mid Aug delivery.
