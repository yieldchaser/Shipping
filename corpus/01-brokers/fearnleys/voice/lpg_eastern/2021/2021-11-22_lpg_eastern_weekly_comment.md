## Market Commentary

MEG Following the crude, Dec C3 CP was tumbled to $770 earlier and rebounded to $810 later this week. Dec C3/C4 maintained at $33, despite that a couple of evenly split cargos have been consumed and placed into India this week. 1st round CP recommendation ideas vary widely from lowest $740 to highest $830 for Dec C3 reportedly, while C4 at $30-40 less. Dec/Jan CP still traded at over $30 backwardation structure, regardless of the forthcoming winter demand.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials weakens further, seeing 23kt propane for 2h dec delivery was traded from flattish to minus 3.5$ on Dec FEI link. 46kt propane parcel for 1h Jan delivery was offered at Jan FEI plus $8.8 while half cargo was offered at Dec FEI plus $2. Paper market is full of volatility this week. Dec FEI fluctuates from $20 to $50 daily, shadowing the movement on MB export price. Off window discussions are slow as of holiday timing and players are waiting for CP announcement. One Korean Importer issued buy tender to secure monthly 23kt butane from Jun to Dec delivery in 2022, in order to capture the good discounts for cracking butane to Napatha. One EChina PDH user didn’t award their buy tender for monthly 23kt propane from Dec to Mar delivery into Ningbo. Another EChina PDH was exploring only 23kt propane for Dec delivery. Due to the squeezed margin, 5 or 6 PDHs in China are reported to schedule or advance their turnarounds to Dec and Jan, and we expect a minimum loss of 190kt propane for feedstock demand.
