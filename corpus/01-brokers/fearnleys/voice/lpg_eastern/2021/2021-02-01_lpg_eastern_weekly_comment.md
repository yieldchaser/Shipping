## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco raised Feb CP at $605 for C3 and $585 for C4, up by $55 respectively. Mar C3 CP swap soared up and traded at $588 at the time of writing, and C3/C4 spread narrowed down to $7$, as more equal ratio buying interests emerged this week. Qatar Petroleum released their Mar acceptance with some minor delays reported. Despite the freight is dropping dramatically, Netback on FOBs are still discussed in heavy discounts, especially when Mar/Apr backwardation assessed at $50.

**FAR EAST**

 Less activities this week in the window. One 23kt propane for 1h Mar delivery was done at Mar FEI plus $15, and off window similar level was concluded into Japan. There are some concerns on the tighter supply for 2h Mar arrival tons as the result of Feb lifting US cancellations. Together with the coming China Lunar New Year, receivers are moving ahead of time to explore cargos for 2h Mar and even 1h Apr arrival. Hence we’ve seen a flurry of buying tenders this week. A new cracker located in Ningbo issued tender to purchase one full propane cargo for end Mar early Apr arrival. Similarly, another NChina Petchem user also tendered to secure such propane for feedstock. One SChina importer cancelled their buy tender for 1-2 lots of 11/11 delivered in Mar and Apr respectively, as offers reported at FEI plus 40s were yet to attract interests. Taiwan FPCC secured 22kt propane for 2h Mar delivery at Mar FEI plus 20 via tender, meanwhile CPC tendered to buy a parcel of 11/11 for 2h Mar to 1h Apr delivery.
