## Market Commentary

MEG Despite the crude drop, Jun C3 CP stands firm and by the time of writing, it was traded at $503. June C3/C4 CP narrowed down further to $5, reflecting the improved demand on butane in summer season. Jun/Jul CP was traded at slight backwardation at $2. Qatar Petroleum was reported to award their full propane cargo for 19/20th Jun loading at CP minus teens level. KPC tendered to sell another fob, which is heavy propane ratio for 20/21th Jun loading. This is their 4th spot sale for June lifting.

**FAR EAST**

 Active buying inquires, especially driven by petchem demand, lent support to FAR EAST price. A couple of cargos were traded in window. Full cargo propane for 2h Jun delivery was traded at Jun FEI flat and 23/0 parcel for 1h Jul delivery was done at Jun FEI plus $2. One Korean petchem importer awarded their buy tender for one evenly split cargo of late Jun delivery at Jun FEI plus high single digit reportedly. One Indian importer was said to cancel their buy tender for one equal ratio cargo for 2h Jun delivery, due to the unfavourable price and high freight rates. One EChina petchem user purchased a full propane cargo for mid/2H June delivery at Jun CP plus low-mid 40s. One Schina traditional importer secured a 11/11 parcel for Jul delivery at Jul CP plus high 40s reportedly. Another EChina player also tendered to buy one 23kt propane parcel for late June delivery.
