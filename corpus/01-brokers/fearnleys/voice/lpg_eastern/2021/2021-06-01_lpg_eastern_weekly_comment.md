## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco announce Jun Propane CP at $530, up by $35 and Butane at $525, up by $50. Jul swap rallied up this week, following the crude rise. At the time of writing, Jul C3 CP was traded at $540 and C4 at $532. QP released their July acceptance with no cuts or cancelations reported.

**FAR EAST**

 As the Panama congestion situation eased, cash premium for Jul delivery parcels softened compared to earlier this week. 23k propane parcels for 2h Jul delivery was bid at FEI flattish to $1 in the window. Off window a couple of deals were concluded. Two parcels of 23k propane for 1h Jul delivery were traded at Jul FEI plus $3.5-4. A flurry of cargos was placed into E.China this week via tenders. At least 92k propane for 1h Jul delivery were purchased by 3 different petchem players for feedstock use, with price ranging from Jul FEI plus high single to low teens. Another SChina PDH user also tendered to secure 22/0 delivered in 1st decade July.
