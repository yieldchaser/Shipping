## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Following crude's rollercoaster volatility, Sep C3 CP once dived to $650ish from last week’s $680 and now rebounded close to $670, at the time of writing. First round CP idea on Sep C3 was recommended at the range from $650 to $675, and C4 at $5 cheaper or at parity reportedly. Qatar Petroleum was reported to sell one mid Sep lifting full propane cargo.

**FAR EAST**

: Spot discussion for 2h Sep delivery range was mute this week. However a handful of Sep arrival cargos were reported unsold and likely to be rolled into Oct delivery, as most receivers already move into Oct program. 23kt propane parcel for 1h Oct delivery was bid at Sep FEI minus $1, yet to lure offers in the window. Off window Japanese importers are reported to buy Oct delivered cargos for stockpiling, and one 3/1 cargo for 1h Oct delivery was said to be placed into Japan earlier this week. And one Vietnam-based PDH importer awarded their buy tender for one full propane cargo on end Sep to Oct delivery at CP plus low 20s reportedly. Additionally, HPCL issued term tender to secure 14 evenly split FOB cargos from Jan to Dec 2022. However, BPCL cancelled their buy tender to one to two evenly split cargos for Oct delivery.
