## Market Commentary

FAR EAST A couple of deals were concluded in window this week. 23k propane parcel for 1h Jun delivery was traded at May FEI plus $5 earlier, and then cash premium dropped to $1. Similar cargo for 2h Jun delivery was bid at June FEI plus $1 while offer at $6. Off window a handful of tenders floating in the market. One Korean Petchem user was said to secure one 23kt C3 for 6-12 June delivery at June FEI plus low teens via tender. Another Korean Major issued term tender to secure 7 upto 12 lots of 23k butane parcel monthly from June delivery onwards. Indian IOC was reported to purchase one equal ratio split cargo on FOB scheduled for 2h June delivery at June CP minus mid-low teens. And Indonesia Pertamina reissued their term buy tender to purchase 1-2 evenly split cargos monthly from July to Dec delivery.

**MEG**

 Saudi Aramco set May Propane CP at $495, down by $65 and Butane CP at $475, down by $55. Such is more or less in line with swap value. And backwardation into June has narrowed down to almost flattish. We expect C3/C4 spread to narrow down further as better demand for butane into summer season. KPC is reported to sell their 4/5 Jun lifting equal ratio cargo.
