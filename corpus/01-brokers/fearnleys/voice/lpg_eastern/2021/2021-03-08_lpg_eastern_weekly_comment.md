## Market Commentary

MEG Apr CP has plunged to $580 from $600 earlier this week, reflecting the overlength on FOBs. FOB discussions are under heavy discounts, as limited buying interests against ample supply. KPC is reported to award its sale tender for three evenly split cargos from Apr to Jun lifting at CP minus high teens discounts. And following this, another trader tendered to resell similar cargos. Qatar Petroleum also tendered to sell one mid Apr lifting full propane, which shall attract more buying interests due to more propane outlets in the EAST for the time being.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premiums for Apr delivery cargos slid down. Bids for 23k propane parcel of 2h Apr delivery were shown in window at Apr FEI plus low teens, while sellers’ target at high 20s. Apr FEI/CP was traded at minus 30s, which is quite frustrating for some sellers with non Us origin cargos. This being said, petchem demand slowly rebounded as players emerging to seek feedstocks either for PDH use or for replacement of Naphtha in cracking, which will relatively absorb some surplus volume. Taiwan FPCC tendered to buy flexible ratio within 46kt for Apr delivery and secured one 11/11 parcel at Apr MOPJ minus low 30s. Another NChina petchem player also issued buy tender for similar cargo with such flexibility. One SChina PDH importer tendered in advance to seek a 23/0 parcel for May delivery, and its last tender for Apr delivery did not award. Apart from these, one Korean player tendered to buy 23k C3 for 2h Apr delivery, and its last tender for 11/11 was reported not awarded either.
