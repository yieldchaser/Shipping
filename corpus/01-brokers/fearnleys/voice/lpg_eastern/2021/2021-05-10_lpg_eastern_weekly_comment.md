## Market Commentary

MEG Swap value retreated after last surge. At the time of writing, Jun CP was traded at $496 and C3/C4 traded at $8-10. Jun/Jul CP was traded in $2 contango. KPC is reported to sell their 15/16th June lifting evenly split cargo at Jun CP minus mid/low teens. This is the 3rd spot stem they’ve marketed for June loadings. With freight soaring up, FOB netbacks are discussed at deeper discounts.

**FAR EAST**

 23k propane for 1h Jun delivery was bid at Jun FEI plus mid-single digit, and similar parcel for 2h Jun delivery was at low-single digit. Arbitrage window is closed against rising freight and we’ve seen cancellations happening for Jun liftings. Off window a handful of cargos have been consumed, mainly by petchem players. One Korean petchem player issued term tender last Friday to secure 3 lots of 23k propane monthly from Jul 2021 to Jun 2022.
