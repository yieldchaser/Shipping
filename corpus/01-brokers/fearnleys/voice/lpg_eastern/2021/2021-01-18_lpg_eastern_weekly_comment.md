## Market Commentary

MEG Very volatile on paper swaps this week. Feb Propane CP was traded from lowest at $585 to highest $625, and backwardation into Mar enlarged from last week’s $20s to nearly $50. At the time of writing, Feb C3 was traded at $600 and Mar assessed at $552 relatively. Saudi Aramco was reported to cut 4-5 cargos for Feb liftings, which alternatively lent support to Feb CP. However, with limited butane buying interest, Feb butane was valued at $40 cheaper. Qatar Petroleum still has a late Feb lifting evenly split cargo to sell.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premiums for both Feb and Mar arrival cargos have been traded down this week. 23k propane for 1h Mar delivery was traded at Mar FEI plus low-mid 30s only on Thursday, while earlier this week bids hover above $50 in the window. Outside window sellers with FEB arrival cargos also lowered down offers to attract buying interests, plus the freight is falling sharply as of fewer liftings due to Saudi cuts and US cancellations. One Chinese PDH secured two parcels of 23kt propane for Feb and Mar delivery respectively, which reported done at 50% FEI pricing plus the 40s and 50% CP pricing plus teens only. Another Chinese major also tendered to buy one full cargo with the flexible ratio for 2h Feb to early Mar delivery, which is due on Friday. Indonesia PERTAMINA secured one early Mar arrival evenly split cargo and following this, another SEA importer also issued buy tender to purchase similar cargo for 1h Mar delivery.
