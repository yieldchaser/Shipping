## Market Commentary

MEG Swap value is falling this week, despite the crude rebounding. Mar propane CP plunged to $570 and Mar C3/C4 spread widened to $25 as a handful of evenly split FOB cargos are available against slim buying interests. FOB discussions are still in heavy discounts especially deep backwardation into Apr at above $45. Apart from lifters’ resales, Qatar Petroleum also issued a tender to sell one evenly split or heavy propane cargo for late Mar lifting.

**FAR EAST**

 A couple of propane parcels for 2h Mar delivery were concluded in the window. But off window market is in lack of activities as of the coming Lunar New Year holiday. Chinese importers/terminals are busy destocking before CNY. Several PDHs in China have scheduled turnarounds in Mar, therefore we’ve seen some inquiries deferred to Apr rather than Mar arrival cargos. Several Chinese tenders for both Mar and Apr delivery have been canceled as prices yet to trigger interests. However, we do expect them to reexplore again after CNY, with a clear scheme of domestic consumption.
