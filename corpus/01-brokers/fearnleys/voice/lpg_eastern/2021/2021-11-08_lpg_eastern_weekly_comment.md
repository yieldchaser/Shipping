## Market Commentary

MEG Following the crude, Dec C3 CP was retreated to $865 at the time of writing, from highest at $887 earlier this week. Dec C3/C4 spread widened to $40 as weaker seasonal demand on butane. Dec/Jan backwardation also enlarged further to $30 from last week’s $20, reflecting the front strength, partly caused by US cargos delays due to Panama congestion. Saudi Aramco was said to have one end Nov lifting heavy butane cargo available to sell. Qatar Petroleum also issued tender to sell one 45kt propane for 15-24th Dec lifting.

**FAR EAST**

 Due to the continued waiting at Panama Canal, a handful of cargos was rolled into 2h Dec delivery but cash differentials improved into positive territory. 23kt propane for 1h Dec delivery was changed hand at Dec FEI plus $4. And similar cargo for 2h Dec was concluded at $890 on Thursday. Outside window plenty of tenders are running this week. Indonesia PERTAMINA was reported to buy one evenly split cargo for Nov delivery at Nov CP plus high 30s via tender, but did not award their latest buy tender for Jan delivery. One SChina PDH user didn’t award its buy tender for 66kt propane on dec delivery but was said to cover some volumes outside tender. One EChina petchem user secured 23kt propane for 2h Dec delivery into Ningbo at Dec FEI plus mid-high single digit. Similarly, another EChina PDH buyer award their buy tender for 46kt propane on Dec delivery at Dec FEI plus low single digit. Other than spot tenders, One new PDH user located in Lianyungang was reported to award their term buy tender for monthly 23/0 at CP plus less $40. Another SChina traditional importer was said to award their term tender for monthly 11/11 parcel at CP plus less/around $40 and FEI plus less/around $10.
