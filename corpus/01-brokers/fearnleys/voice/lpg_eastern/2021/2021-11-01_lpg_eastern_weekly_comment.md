## Market Commentary

MEG Despite the increased supply in Nov, Saudi Aramco raised up their Nov Propane CP by $70 to $870, and Butane by $35 to $830. This is much higher than most players’ expectations and the swap value traded previously. Dec Propane CP was traded at $850ish and Butane at $20 cheaper during the mid week, amidst uncertainties over crude and lukewarm demand for the front.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials weakens this week in lack of buying interests. Off window 23kt propane parcel for 1h Dec delivery was traded at Dec FEI minus low single digit reportedly. Similar cargo was offered on Wed window at FEI flat but was bid at FEI minus $6. Regardless of the delays caused by Panama waiting, Dec supply still appears ample as a couple of unsold cargos rolled from Nov into 1h Dec. FEI swaps still under downward pressure as Chinese DCE-PG continued a slump this week. However, despite the eroding PDH margins, one SChina PDH plant issued spot tender to purchase in total 66kt propane for Dec delivery into Dongguan but requested for Jan or Feb CP link pricing. Indonesia PERTAMINA also tendered to buy one evenly split cargo for either on CFR basis for late Nov-early Dec delivery or on FOB for mid Nov loading ex AG.
