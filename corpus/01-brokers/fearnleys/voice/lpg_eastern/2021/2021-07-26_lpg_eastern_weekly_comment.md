## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco raised up the Aug Propane CP by $40 to $660 and Butane by $35 to $655, which is above some players’ expectations. However, it reflected the tightness in the front market, resulted by cuts from one producer’s Aug liftings against robust demand on evenly split cargos. We have seen active bids for such ratio loading during late Aug to early Sep this week. Following such, Sep Propane CP was traded at $666, and butane at $5 less at the time of writing. We believed with OPEC boosting the oil production, more LPG outputs in forward months are expected and the tightness situation might be eased.

**FAR EAST**

 Other than bids for AG FOBs, inquires for Aug and Sep delivered Propane are less active comparatively. 23kt propane for 2h Aug delivery was bid at Aug FEI plus small premium while no offers seen. Whilst seller is offering similar cargo for 1h Sep arrival at Aug FEI plus $2.5. Off window spot discussions are muted, but still a couple of deals were concluded, mainly through tenders. One late Aug lifting evenly split cargo ex Australia was sold via tender to one SEA importer at Sep CP plus mid 30s reportedly. And Indian BPCL was reported to secure similar cargo for Sep lifting ex AG as well. One SChina importer was reported to sell a 11/11 parcel for 1h Sep delivery at Sep CP plus 20s. Apart from spot discussion, one Chinese Major issued a semi tender to secure monthly 46k propane from Oct to Mar delivery into Ningbo.
