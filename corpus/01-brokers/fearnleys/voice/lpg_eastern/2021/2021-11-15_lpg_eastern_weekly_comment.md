## Market Commentary

MEG Swap price has been on a roller coaster this week. At the start Dec CP plunged to $800 from last week’s $860 and rebounded to $820ish during mid-week, but quickly retreated below $800 as crude declined below $80. Dec C3/C4 spread furthered widened to $33, as a plenty of butane available in the front market against lukewarm demand. Dec/Jan CP still traded at $30 backwardation. Saudi Aramco released their Dec acceptance, and majority received in line with their nominations. It is expected to export at 700kt in Dec, similar to Nov exports. Qatar Petroleum was reported to award their sale tender of 15-24th Dec lifting full propane to one Chinese player at FEI netback link.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium weakens this week as buying inquires subdued, together with easing congestion in Panama . 23kt propane parcel for 2h Dec delivery was offered at Dec FEI plus $3.5 while bided at plus $1. 23kt butane was offered at fixed price $762 on Thursday, yet to entice interests. Off window a few tenders are running. One Korean petchem user issued buy tender to secure an evenly split cargo for 1-7th Jan delivery into Daesan for cracking. One EChina PDH user issued buy tender for monthly 23kt propane from Dec to Mar delivery.
