## Market Commentary

MEG Flat price is weakening this week. May Propane CP plunged to $480, and Butane was traded at $25 less. Backwardation into June narrowed down to $5 from last week’s mid10s. ADNOC released their May lifting acceptance with some delays being reported. However, it doesn’t lend much support to CP value. FOB discussions of evenly split cargos are still under mid-high teens discounts. Qatar Petroleum issued tender to sell one late May lifting full propane cargo, which is due next week.

**FAR EAST**

 A handful of deals were concluded in window this week. 23k C3 parcels for 1h May delivery was traded at May FEI plus low 20s, and similar cargos for 2h May delivery was done at May CP plus $43/44. However, cash premium softens as US delay situation is getting eased. On Thursday similar cargo for 2h May delivery was bid at May FEI plus mid-teens, while offer equates to high teens level. Off window a flurry of tenders floating this week. One Korea Petchem user tendered to buy one 22/22 or 23/0 for end May early June delivery. One SChina traditional importer awarded their buy tender to secure one 11/11 parcel for 2h May into June delivery, at a mixed price of CP plus low 30s and FEI plus teens. Another SChina PDH user also procured one 23/0 parcel for 2h May delivery via tender at May CP plus mid 30s reportedly. And we are expecting more cracking demand emerge next week, as LPG economy improving as feedstock. At the time of writing, May FEI/MOPJ was traded at around minus $70.
