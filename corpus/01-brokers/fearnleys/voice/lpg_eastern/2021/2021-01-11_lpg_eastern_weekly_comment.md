## Market Commentary

MEG Feb CP continues to rally up this week, with the expectation on Saudi cuts on Feb liftings. At the time of writing, Feb C3 CP was traded at $620 while C3/C4 spread deepened further to $38, as of limited buying inquires for butane. Qatar Petroleum was reported that it didn’t award its sale tender for 24/25th Feb lifting evenly split cargo. Other similar cargo for 2h Feb lifting was offered at Feb CP minus teens but yet to attract interest, especially with the prevailing high freight rates.

**FAR EAST**

 Despite active inquires in window this week, cash premium for 2h Feb delivered 23/0 has declined from high 30s to low 30s on FEI link, and full cargo lot was assessed at $10 less or even cheaper. Outside window a handful of cargos are seeking home, which exerted pressure on Feb FEI and FEI/CP narrowed down sharply from last week’s $40 to $20 accordingly. Seeing the backwardation for over $50 into Mar FEI, importers with comfortable inventory intent to defer their buying inquiries into Mar instead. Similar parcel for 23/0 delivered in 1h Mar was bid at Mar FEI plus at $53, which equates to Feb FEI plus low single, however sellers are reluctant to offer their Feb arrival tons at the time being. Other than the spot activities, Indian HPCL issued tender to buy from 6 upto 10 equal ratio cargos for Mar to Dec delivery.
