## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco released Sep acceptance with several cuts reportedly. This undoubtedly lent support to Sep CP, which resiliently traded at $675-680 earlier this week, but dropped towards $660 on Thursday as Crude slumped more than 3%. Discussion for equal ratio split cargos are active as Indian majors call on buy tenders one after another. And FOB netback improves to premium level for Oct liftings. Sep C3/C4 also narrowed down to $5 consequently.

**FAR EAST**

 Discussions for Sep delivery parcels were subdued this week as most players have finished their program. Cash premium sink to discount level this week with 23kt propane for 2h Sep delivery was traded at Sep FEI minus $2. Indian IOC awarded their new tender to secure one Oct delivered evenly split cargo at CP plus 40s reportedly, right after their Sep purchase last week. HPCL tendered to buy one and half cargo for Oct delivery but result is yet to know. Similarly, BPCL issued tender to secure one to two cargos for Oct. One EChina cracker issued buy tender to procure 23k propane for 2h Sep delivery. And one Schina traditional importer tendered to buy one 11/11 parcel for end Sep to early Oct arrival. Korean HTC also procured 23kt butane for similar timing delivery via tender.
