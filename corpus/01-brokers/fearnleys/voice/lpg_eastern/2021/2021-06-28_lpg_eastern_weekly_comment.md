## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco raised Jul C3 CP by $90 to $620 and C4 CP by $95 to $620, more or less in line with market expectations. Aug CP surged up on Thursday to $655, alongside the crude rise, and reflecting the overall strength. Aug C3/C4 is traded at parity and Aug/Sep CP backwardation was once narrowed down to $1 but widened to $4 at the time of writing, mirroring the front strength.

**FAR EAST**

 Foreseeing reduce on US exports incurred by closed Arb to EAST and potential cancellations, a handful of buyers are bidding actively in window this week. Earlier 23k propane for 1h Aug delivery was traded at Aug FEI plus $3. And there are still a flurry of bids for such parcel at similar level while seller hefted cash premium of their offer to plus $6. Similar cargo for 2h Aug delivery was discussed slightly cheaper or flattish level. Petchem margins have been squeezed against the soaring feedstock cost, whereas the rise of end products price are refrained due to new releasing capacities. We might expect some petchem plants lower down rates or turn into maintenance if needed. Other than this, quite a handful of tenders are floating in the market. One EChina cracker plant issued tender to buy two lots of 23k propane parcel for mid and late Aug delivery respectively into Ningbo. One Vietnam Petchem player tendered to procure one full cargo propane for late Aug to early Sep arrival for their PDH feedstock. Indonesia Pertamina issued buy tender for one evenly split ratio cargo, either on FOB for mid Aug loading ex AG or CFR for late Aug delivery.
