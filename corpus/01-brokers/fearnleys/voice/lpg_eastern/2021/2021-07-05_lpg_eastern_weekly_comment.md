## Market Commentary

MEG A week of volatility as Aug C3 CP once soared up to $685 earlier this week and fell sharply to $630 by the time of writing, following the crude. Aug C4 CP was traded at $5 less, however evenly split ratio is still more in favoured compared to other ratios, as of less availability relatively. Qatar Petroleum issued tender to sell 12/13th Aug loading 45kt propane which due next week.

**FAR EAST**

 Expecting less late Jul/Aug loadings ex US, we have seen a flurry of bids for Aug delivery propane in the window this week. 23kt propane for 1h Aug delivery was traded at Aug FEI plus 1.5 and bid up continuously Same cargo for 2h Aug delivery was bid at flattish level or slightly cheaper. 45kt propane parcel was bid at minus single digit discount to FEI subject to delivery range. Off window discussions were muted except for petchem rigid demand. Despite the squeezed margin against high cost in feedstock, quite a handful of tenders from petchem plants have been awarded reportedly. One EChina plant procured two parcels of 23kt propane for mid/2h Aug delivery at Aug CP plus high 20s to 30 for cracking use. One Vietnam-Based plant secured in total 46kt propane for late Aug to early Sep delivery for PDH feedstock. Meanwhile, one EChina PDH user issued a mini term tender to secure monthly 23kt propane for Aug to Nov delivery, which due next week. In terms of residential use, Indonesia PERTAMINA was also reported to purchase one end Aug delivery evenly split cargo at Aug CP plus mid-30s. And one Indian Importer also tendered to buy two evenly split ratio parcel, with one is 20kt for Jul delivery and the other 45kt or Oct loading ex AG, but the result is sketchy.
