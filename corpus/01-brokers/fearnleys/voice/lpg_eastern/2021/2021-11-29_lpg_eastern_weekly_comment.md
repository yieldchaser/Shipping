## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco set Dec CP at $795 for Propane and $750 for Butane, down by $75 and $80 respectively compared to Nov. Previously in 1st and 2nd recommendation, some term lifters have recommended at above $800 for both grades. Jan CP for propane tumbled to $630 and C3/C4 narrowed down to $5-10, partly caused by the volatile crude over uncertain impacts by the new variant. Qatar Petroleum announced their Jan acceptance with some delays reported.

**FAR EAST**

 A handful of deals been concluded in window this week, but cash differentials weakened in Jan delivered market. 23kt propane for 2h Jan delivery was traded from Jan FEI plus $4 to minus $2, reflecting the bearish sentiment amid high volatility. Outside window, One Korean petchem player tendered to buy 23/23 or 23/0 or 0/23 parcel for mid Jan delivery into Daesan and awarded for 23/0 parcel at FEI plus single digit premium. Another Korean importer also tendered to buy 23kt propane for 6-10 Jan delivery into Daesan which is due on Friday. One EChina petchem plant issued buy tender to secure in total 46kt propane for 1h/Mid Jan delivery for cracking use.
