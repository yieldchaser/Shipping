## Market Commentary

The VLCC market, as with everything else, is all about perception, what you think you see versus what it really is. The perception the last couple of days is that we are in for a downturn and charterers are going to play the Golden Week holiday, starting today, for all it’s worth in trying to soften owner’s resolve. And yes, the market has shed 10-15 points MEG/east, but then again China's largest charterer - knowing more than most - reportedly repeated a WS 90 MEG/China without much further ado yesterday. We’ve seen very little concluded mid-October onwards and if we are to end up with 155 or more fixtures for the month it could prove to be a restless holiday. The west/east arb momentarily opening up with a couple of corresponding fixtures has added some optimism in the Atlantic too. We might of course see a bit more shaved off rates both east and west before it levels off, but there’s always more to it than meets the eye.
