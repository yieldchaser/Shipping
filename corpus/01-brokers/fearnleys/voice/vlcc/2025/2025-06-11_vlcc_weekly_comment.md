## Market Commentary

The VLCC market remains in a lethargic state with little change to speak of from the MEG in the week gone by. A sputter of activity is keeping the flame from being extinguished, but rates are going sideways - at best - and held up more from stubbornness on the owners’ part than supply and demand. The Atlantic, however, has seen a bit of a tonnage clear-out, albeit on falling rates, but with two third of the US export program yet to be tackled, a continues flow from Brazil and a bit of West African seasoning, there could possibly be potential for slight a turnaround as we move further out on the curve. Hope is eternal.
