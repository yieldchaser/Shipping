## Market Commentary

The owning community has done well keeping rates at elevated levels, but the resolve is starting to show cracks as volumes have subsided and are unlikely to see much improvement for the remainder of the 60-day MOU between the US and Iran. Arguably we have seen a bit more production emerging in the MEG, but a far cry from pre-war levels and transit through the Strait is still precarious. A rumour suggest that WS 250 has been done for an inside MEG loading for east. Whether true or not there’s seldom smoke without fire and direction of travel is evident. However, TD3C paper for July is currently pegged each side of WS 250 which could indicate a floor. Rates for “outside” loading continue to be under pressure as well with WS 159 last done for a Korea destined cargo, and the enquiry in question had 10 punters competing for it, the balance of which are left looking for employment. The Atlantic remains quiet barring almost daily Brazilian export requirements, but competition for same is naturally also tightening. All said and done however, a summer market in the WS 100-150 range is not something the tanker market has been used to historically. As always, it’s all in the eye of the beholder.
