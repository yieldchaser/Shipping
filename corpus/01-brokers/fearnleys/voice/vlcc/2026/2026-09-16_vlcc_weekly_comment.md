## Market Commentary

VLCC rates for a Fuj/East run are now 3/4 on the way to WS 1000! Arguably charterers and trades alike also have themselves to “blame” as they have relet ships at increased intervals as well. The Atlantic market is also firing on all cylinders although mainly left for Suezmaxes now as VLCC supply is running out. All in all access to physical crude trumps freights at the moment as refineries are scrambling for supply. The big concern ‌is ⁠the disruption to Saudi Arabia's East-West pipeline and Yanbu export infrastructure, following attacks on Saudi energy facilities. Saudi Arabia is reportedly now offering more loadings of crude oil to Asian refiners via ship-to-ship transfers off Oman's Sohar port. However, visible vessel transits through the Strait of Hormuz remain in the single digits.
