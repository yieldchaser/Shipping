## Market Commentary

After a hectic week in Dubai, most charterers return with a more relaxed feel, with plenty of deals having been done under the radar/over the dinner table. TD3C is wobbling, with WS 115 being the last done at present. With a number of oil co. relets pushed and we close in on the quieter 10–20-decade laycans, more downward pressure is possible. Looking to the Atlantic for strength and plenty to be found on the smaller ships. For the VL’s rates are holding, with a number of deals done USG-Ningbo at the USD 14.5m level. However, laycans in Wafr and the USG within range of Eastern ballasters will be under pressure. Charterers have played the “off-market” game very well to remove what was some considerable heat. Big TCE’s remain however, and any major rapid drop in rates looks unlikely, if owners hold their nerve.
