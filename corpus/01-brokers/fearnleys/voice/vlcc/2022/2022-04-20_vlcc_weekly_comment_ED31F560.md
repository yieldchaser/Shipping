## Market Commentary

The April MEG program closed out with a healthy 140+ fixtures, so we watch to see what May has to offer. There is only 10 days left of the current month, so May should be gathering speed. As such, plenty of ships on subs, with no details attached as charterers seek to work from the shadows to keep activity private. The position list remains well stocked, and as a result, owners are struggling to keep hold of the pre-Easter gains, with rates slipping to the WS 50 level for modern well badged MEG/China. Activity remains in the Atlantic with a number of cargoes looking ex USG at the time of writing. However, Suezmaxes are wobbling a bit as VLCC’s have been stepping on their toes for West Africa runs. With an armada of modern units ballasting west to seek their fortune, logic dictates that rates are capped in the short term.
