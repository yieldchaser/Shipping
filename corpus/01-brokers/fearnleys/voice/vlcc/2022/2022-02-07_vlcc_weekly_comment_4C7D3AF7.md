## Market Commentary

Last week provided little cheer for owners, but what little there was, ebbed further away this week. Lows of WS 16 for MEG/West and low WS 30’s for East doesn’t make for healthy returns. A glance down the position list shows the scrubber/eco vessels taking most of the available cargoes, as simply making more sense of the returns. Meanwhile, the older (although under 15 years) unscrubbered ships are building up. For these vessels to make the same returns, it would need a 10 point ish swing in their favour, which in the short term remains unlikely. However, with the US attitude to Iran warming, albeit more to do with angry voters at the petrol pump, we could see a shift in the VL market as we know it. Also, with Chevron proposing Venezuelan possibilities, it could be time for the shadow market to come in to the light.
