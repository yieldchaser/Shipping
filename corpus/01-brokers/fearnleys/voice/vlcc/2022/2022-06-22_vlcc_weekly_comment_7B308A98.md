## Market Commentary

Quite a turn around this week. Owners have the bit between their teeth and are looking to continue market gains over the last week or so. Driven by an active Atlantic market, with rates creeping up USD 200k for USG/East on Monday, it set the tone and the West Africa market followed suit (jumping to WS 50 Wafr/China), as only modern approved tonnage would fit the requirements. What had been a sluggish MEG market burst into life Tuesday, with Unipec grabbing what they could at the WS 45-46 level, leaving other charterers entering the market and owners looking to push (at the time of writing owners talking the WS 50’s). Those charterers that can escape on handicapped/old can do so, but it remains only a few points discount. Plenty of cargoes still to work, so it remains all to play for during the remainder of the week.
