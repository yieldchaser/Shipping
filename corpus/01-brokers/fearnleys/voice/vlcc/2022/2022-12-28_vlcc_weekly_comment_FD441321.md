## Market Commentary

A week shortened by the festive period, with London back in play today. Last week finished on a slow slide, as rates for MEG/East slipping down to the low WS 70’s for a Taiwan run. Breaking WS 70 for China will likely remain only possible on a handicapped vessel for the time being, as unconfirmed reports WS 72.5 paid on a relet TD3C. With early January slipping away (almost 50 fixtures done), any cargo influx in the short term may remain an owner’s dream. A similar pattern in the Atlantic market, where WS 70 was broken on a Brazil/East run (WS 69.99), and USG/East remains around USD 9.2m. We predicted a busy period between Christmas and New year, and Petrobras export yesterday and a few cargoes in today suggests owners will have some meat to chew. However, a lot of bearish market talk out there and the lists remain lengthy.
