## Market Commentary

With a holiday in the US yesterday and London off Thursday and Friday for the Golden jubilee, expectations were for a busier period ahead of the Posidonia event that begins next week. While activity has been on the rise and bunkers have continued to firm, any resultant uptick in Worldscale rates has been somewhat subdued, as charterers fight for every quarter point over last done. Therefore, the result barely covers the higher bunkers costs to owners, and contributing to an overall negative on a TCE basis at the time of writing. With some added political tensions in the Middle East (Iranian seizure of two Greek vessels) and a shortened work week ahead of a week that has many travelling, the question is will we see some further upward pressure develop, despite sufficient supply to cover demand?
