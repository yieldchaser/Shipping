## Market Commentary

On the surface, a slowly softening market with a drip of enquiry, as MEG/East hovering just north of WS 40 for modern ships which tick all boxes, and high WS 30’s for those which do not. TCE’s return on the cusp of loss making, even for the most economical of units. But, there remains a flow of ‘off market’ deals, with owners happy to fix as close to last done as they can. A line in the sand will be drawn at some point, so further softening will be strongly resisted. At the time of writing, the Atlantic market has a couple of enquiries from both Brazil and the USG, and with Suezmaxes slowly turning upwards, there is hope for VLCC owners heading for the Cape, or indeed already hunting those waters. Reports of a 130 mt stem Wafr-UKCM doubling up to a VLCC as economies of scale kicked in to play will bring some cheer.
