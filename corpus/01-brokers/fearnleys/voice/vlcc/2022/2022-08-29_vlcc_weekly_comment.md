## Market Commentary

It may have been a summer of discontent for most other things, but not for tankers. There is currently a bit of a downward pressure in the VLCC market, and a couple of points were chopped off from last done yesterday, but if anyone had predicted that MEG/East would be trading in the mid-high WS 70's only a few months ago few would have believed it - May closed out with rates trading in the very low WS 40's. We could probably still see a point or two off still, as things become more of a date- than a rate game, but we do not believe that the bottom will fall out, by any means - sentiment is just too strong.
