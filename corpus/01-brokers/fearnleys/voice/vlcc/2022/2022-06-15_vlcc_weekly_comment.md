## Market Commentary

With the dust settled after another Posidonia, we catch up on deals done during the week. Plenty of AG ships on subs no details as rates struggle to make any headway, despite increasing fuel costs. AG/Korea sits at the ws43.5 level, a couple of points discount if you find yourself handicapped in any way. With June closing around 140 fixtures, volume is decent and Unipec already exploring July dates. Other sizes showing improvement, albeit heavily Atlantic driven, VL’s may follow suit, as a number plucked from the list in Rotterdam, Cape of Good Hope etc, with no details attached. Therefore a Charterer may find that as they go to the list for a ship, it will likely be thinner than it first appears.
