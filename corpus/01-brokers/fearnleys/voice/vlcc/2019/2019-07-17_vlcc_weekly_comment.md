## Market Commentary

There’s been very little change in the VLCC segment over the last week.

**MEG**

 rates remain stagnant, ranging in the mid ws40’s eastbound for modern unrestricted tonnage, and in the upper ws30’s to very low ws40’s for the more vintage fraternity, depending on the voyage, terms and conditions. Despite somewhat increased activity for USG export cargoes, rates remain static at USD 4.0/5.0 million for Singapore/Korea, respectively. The same picture is evident for the West Africa/East market, similarly hovering in the mid ws40’s. The only glimmer of optimism has been a couple of Brazil/East runs paying up on last done levels, but overall tonnage remains plentiful in all areas so even with the August MEG program coming into the picture, little change is expected short term.
