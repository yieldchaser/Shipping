## Market Commentary

The VLCC market took a proper beating this week, trading in the mid ws30’s MEG/eastbound at the time of writing – barely covering OPEX. Although dramatic, it is not unusual for this time of year, and on a “positive” note, it probably can’t go much lower. Alas, we do not foresee a pick-up this side of the summer, although market players are full of optimism for second half 2019.
