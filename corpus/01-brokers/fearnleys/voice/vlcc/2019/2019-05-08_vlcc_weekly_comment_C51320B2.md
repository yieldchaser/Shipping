## Market Commentary

Another lacklustre week in the VLCC market, with ships piling up in Fujairah and Galle. Owners are currently facing returns well below OPEX for most cargo combinations, with rates currently trading in the high W30’s from both MEG- and West Africa/eastbound for modern ships – and a 2.5-5 point discount for older units.
