## Market Commentary

VLCC rates have surged this week and show no signs of stopping any time soon. A combination of increased seasonal volumes, the recent ban on certain Chinese ships for breaching Iran sanctions, and charterers being reluctant to take ships with Venezuelan trading history, has significantly affected tonnage supply. As a result, charterers are reaching forward date-wise to secure tonnage, fuelling an already red-hot market. All other segments, from Afra- to Suezmaxes, are working in tandem, leaving little wiggle-room for alternative freight options. Daily VLCC earning currently return upwards to USD 130k/day, and falling bunker prices will contribute further. In short; a perfect storm.
