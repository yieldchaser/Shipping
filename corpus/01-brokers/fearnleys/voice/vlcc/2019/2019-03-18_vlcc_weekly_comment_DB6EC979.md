## Market Commentary

With the March VLCC MEG program behind us the Owning community was looking forward to a sprout of activity for early April with ambition to push rates up in to the ws60 eastbound. However, activity has thus far been disappointing. Ws59 is last done for a MEG/China run, although still about USD 30k per day in t/c returns on a non eco modern ship - well above historic returns for time of year.

**USG**

 activity has also halted, and a handful of Owners having ballasted their ships from the east on spec are nervously awaiting fresh export cargoes to take them back east.
