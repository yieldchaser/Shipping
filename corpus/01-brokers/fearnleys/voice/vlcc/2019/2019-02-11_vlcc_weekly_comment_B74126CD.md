## Market Commentary

Another week with rates for the VLCCs under pressure and therefore corrected down somewhat more. There is however a feeling that there is support at present level and the ‘bottom’ may have been found. Those with modern ships, spurred by in influx of fresh business ex USG, have taken a slightly firmer stand. Therefore, WAfr/East rates may edge up slightly, though not yet done.

**MEG**

/East remains another story as the list is still overpopulated by ‘mature’ ladies or ex drydock. Therefore a turnaround here is probably a bit down the road and a very active start of the March program required.
