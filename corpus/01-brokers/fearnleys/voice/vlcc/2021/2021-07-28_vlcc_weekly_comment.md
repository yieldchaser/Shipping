## Market Commentary

Great Scott! It’s been 36 years since "Back to the Future" blasted into cinema theaters and became a pop culture phenomenon, and with the current tanker market continuing to trudge along at a sluggish pace we have to look back to the same era to find its equivalent. The "floor" has arguably been lifted from the year low, with the current conference rate for MEG/East runs holding either side of the ws30 mark depending on a ship’s age and trading background, but still a far cry form covering OPEX even for the best of them - and few signs of a major uptick any time soon. As Marty Mcfly said a number of times throughout the trilogy; “This is heavy”!
