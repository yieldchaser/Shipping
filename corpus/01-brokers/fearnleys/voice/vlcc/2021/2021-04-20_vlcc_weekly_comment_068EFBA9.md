## Market Commentary

On the surface, pretty busy. Deals quietly done under the radar remain in fashion, as charterers pick a target and owners feel special they were chosen. Difficult for owners to really drive the market upwards. However, in a much promised busier 2nd half to the year, owners will hope this tactic will be harder to achieve. Rates on the whole have remained steady through the week. WS34.5 for modern MEG/East and handicapped have been able to up rates closer to the WS30 level, despite a quieter MEG market falling between the April and May stems. At the time of writing, the Atlantic looks busy – 4 cargoes rptd in the North Sea and another 4 in the USG. With owners given the luxury of choice, rates may move in their favour. In mitigation however, these cargoes are seeing a bumper number of offers (9 into Korean USG stem).
