## Market Commentary

Step by step the market moves up, with MEG-East now reportedly at the WS 42.5 level at the time of writing.

**West Africa**

 also holds last done at similar levels at WS 42 to test, attracting modern ballasters around the Cape. Likewise, prompter dates in the USG/Caribs - rumours of USD 5m+ heading East. However, we are a little light on enquiry at present, and with Golden week in a few days, we will need a pre-holiday rush to keep pushing rates upwards, certainly in the MEG. And it is worth noting, as rates go up, charterers may have the tactic to let standards slip a bit. Suddenly an over 15-year vessel becomes workable, dry docks are not so offensive, and remote or bunker sires are overlooked. It is easy to get carried away. TCE's are still awful for owners, barely covering Opex, but the improvement is certainly welcome.
