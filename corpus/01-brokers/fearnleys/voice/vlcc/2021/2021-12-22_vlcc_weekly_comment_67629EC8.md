## Market Commentary

The VLCC market failed to capitalize on the positive momentum from end of last week. Last Friday saw an influx of cargoes hit the market and MEG/east rates inched into the low W40, with expectations of further gains in the pipeline. Alas, this week proved a disappointment, with a number of internal Chinese COA deals and ditto number of ships having disappeared quietly under the radar, bringing the fixture count in line with the curve and squashing hopes of a pre-Christmas rush. A scatter of enquiry is keeping the flame burning, but only enough to support current levels at best, and leaving players longing for the holidays.
