## Market Commentary

The MEG market remains a frustrating hunting ground for owners, as the expectation of any improvement in rates remains on the horizon. Charterers able to source tonnage at last done levels in the low WS 30's MEG/East, and avoiding any rush to fix. There remains much of the 3rd decade to cover however (25-30 cargoes?) and we already over 100 cargoes for September, so plenty of fixing to be done in the short term. The Atlantic remains busy, but again, rates yet to move as ballasters content to escape a stagnant East market if possible. Some owners preferring to keep things short, as they realize that if you fix long now, that is your Q4. Much debate among market players as to when the much vaunted rate improvements will come, but we still a little early for that right now.
