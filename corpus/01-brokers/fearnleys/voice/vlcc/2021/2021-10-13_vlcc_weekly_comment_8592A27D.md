## Market Commentary

The VLCC market has been threatening to improve for the past month or so, but with a number of false dawns so far. Charterers have found a cheaper vessel, a relet, or a date re-arrangement and the market has continued sideways. At the time of writing, we are seeing quite a build up of Atlantic cargoes. Some may be tender business or rumour, but there is no smoke without fire. The MEG, quieter by comparison, is still unfinished for October and we are closing in on a healthy 120 cargoes for the month. We understand 8 offers in to a Formosa cargo, but modern 'unhandicapped' vessels are likely to steer well clear. With bunkers heading North of USD 600/mt, owners will need to push the market just to maintain current TCE’s. Higher numbers look likely in the near term, but charterers will be using all the tricks in their armoury.
