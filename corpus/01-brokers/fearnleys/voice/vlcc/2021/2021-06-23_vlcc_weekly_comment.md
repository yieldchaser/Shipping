## Market Commentary

A little more was expected of this week, as the July MEG stems hit the shelves. However, under the radar and drip-feed tactics continue. OPEC+ remain in discussion for further easing on oil output cuts, as prices rise on the expectation of continued demand recovery. These price increases of course having a detrimental effect on VLCC owners bottom lines, as bunkers soared to close to the USD 540/mt level in Singapore. A grim reality is that some owners on ships lacking scrubbers can only make sense of voyages if they already have cheaper bunkers on board. The result is perhaps a glimmer of improvement when we look at worldscale rates, as MEG/East improves a point to ws32.5, Wafr/East at the ws34 level, but the TCE’s on a particular voyage likely lower, as owners battle to keep voyage calculations in the black.
