## Market Commentary

The VLCC market has moved up a little, but only dragging rates from loss making levels to almost covering OPEX, depending on the voyage in question. The MEG continues to be the quietest area, full of mysteries and ongoing Chinese “COA” commitments. The Atlantic has been the main focus of attention, with a stronger bias and rates currently in the mid to upper W30’s to the east. Brazil has been particularly busy, with upwards to a dozen export cargoes concluded in the week gone by. As rates for USG export cargoes have dropped to $4.2 mill lumpsum for China, the USG has seen more questions for the last decade of February, but have yet to see the same push as from Wafr and Brazil.
