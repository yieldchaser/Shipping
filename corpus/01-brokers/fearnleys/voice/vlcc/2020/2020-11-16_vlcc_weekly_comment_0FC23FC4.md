## Market Commentary

The December MEG program has kicked off with a yawn. The Chinese are “programming” Chinese ships under so called “COA” terms, so far needing little aid from third party VLCC owners. The position list, albeit well stocked, has thinned somewhat of modern unrestricted units. One owner “scored” yesterday, fixing a W28.5 for a replacement job MEG/China off a prompt laycan. Alas, it was the exception that confirms the rule, as later laycans are likely priced closer to W25 – with a couple of points off for older and/or ex d/d types. In the Atlantic our own Equinor also got a premium for one of their relets, fixing a USG/China run at $5 million lumpsum, well up from last done - but again off a laycan where the competition was limited. Time will tell, but we doubt it is going to move the market in any significant manner.
