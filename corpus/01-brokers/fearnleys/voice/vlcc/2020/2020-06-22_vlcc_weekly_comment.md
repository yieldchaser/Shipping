## Market Commentary

The dam has burst, and VLCC rates have taken a - overdue - nosedive. The recent lack of activity, with the June MEG fixture count at an 18-year low, and July has kicked off with a rate slide.

**MEG**

/East rates are now realistically in the WS 30’s for all classes, and West Africa/East has followed suit. Daily earnings are still in the USD 20’s, but with increased bunker prices and further downward potential OPEX levels could come under threat moving forward. By all accounts it’s going to be a hot, long summer of discontent.
