## Market Commentary

This week started off on a positive note with news of a Covid-19 vaccine spurring all markets. Half a dozen VLCC cargoes hit the market from the get go, an unusual occurrence regardless of market conditions. Alas, with the recent tonnage build up, and no visible united front from the owning community, they were quickly absorbed at last done levels. Each to his own seems to be the prevailing mantra, whether an excuse on age or vetting status, or the larger outfits being awash with tonnage and mopping up.

**MEG**

/East runs remain just north of WS25, translating to TCE’s well below OPEX, and further hit by increased bunker prices. The Atlantic market is arguably a tad better, but for much longer commitments and still barely breaking even. We do not expect any significant change short term.
