## Market Commentary

The VLCC market failed to capitalize fully on the upward momentum from Friday when a W65 was logged MEG/China. Numerically activity has been healthy, but most have been Chinese “COA” liftings and a few program deals, leaving little to choose from for independent owners. The owning community’s fighting spirit still remains high, however, and a W60 concluded midweek – on an oil company relet to bout – was against the tide and shows strength. There are still discounts to be had from the vintage fraternity, but a W53.25 replacement fixture MEG/Vietnam on a ’03 built unit is not a giveaway either. Rumours of a mass US sanction listing of owners having traded from Venezuela failed to materialize, but the threat of same has cautioned players and partly aided current rate levels. Continued OPEC+ cuts through July does not indicate a turnaround any time soon, but daily VLCC earnings in the high $40k’s per day is nevertheless miles ahead of those of their smaller sisters, struggling to cover OPEX.
