## Market Commentary

A new week, a new month, alas the same poor VLCC market, with the coming winter looking more Game of Thrones than gain in freights. The second Covid-19 wave has hit hard, leaving little hope for oil demand, emphasized further by leading OPEC countries and Russia suggesting further production cuts – on the back of which oil prices have recouped most of last week’s losses. Adding insult to injury bunker prices have followed suit, with daily tanker earnings now into single digits per day. However, owners are becoming increasingly reluctant to commit to these cuts to the bone earnings, which has curbed the downward spiral somewhat.

**MEG**

/eastbound voyages are now priced in the mid WS20’s with a few more points added for longer West/East voyages, and we expect more of the same moving forward.
