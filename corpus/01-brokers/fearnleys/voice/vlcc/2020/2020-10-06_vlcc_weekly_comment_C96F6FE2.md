## Market Commentary

This week has been characterized by Golden Week in China. Oil giant Unipec tied up a chunk of the local fleet under so called COA terms to cater for some peace and quiet over the holidays, leaving little for third party owners to bite into. Apart from the odd Korea/Taiwan bound cargo, a few Indian ones and a smattering of Atlantic activity, it’s been very quiet. However, those charterers who did dip their feet in met some resistance from a very bored and disinterested audience. Yet biding their time, the results were in line with last done levels, or a smidgeon less. The tonnage list is like a box Liquorice allsorts, plenty to choose from no matter your taste, keeping a lid on MEG/East rates in the WS20’s - returning TCE’s below OPEX. The USG export market held steady, paying in the region of USD 4.6 mill for China.
