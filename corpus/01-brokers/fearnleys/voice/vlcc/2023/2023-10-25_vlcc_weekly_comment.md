## Market Commentary

VLCC has taken a turn for the worse in the week gone by, down some 10 WS points for a MEG/East run since same time last week. In the Atlantic, recently the main catalyst for an upswing the VLCC sector, their smaller sisters have dominated the headlines as Suez- and Aframax rates have gone from strength to strength: However, given a chance VLCCs will put a spanner in the wheels. Even on a part cargo basis freight now supports going the V-route all things equal but thus far the fixing window is out of sync. A domino effect of fixing and failing from the USG has added to the list of ships committed to the area looking for a home. Until VLCCs come back into flavour vis a vis the smaller segments we expect more of the same short term.
