## Market Commentary

The VLCC market has gone from strength to strength in the week gone by. A couple of fixtures have now been logged at WS 79.5 MEG/East, and those charterers still exposed with June MEG bbls will risk having to face something starting with an 8 to be taken out. Some charterers have started reaching into early July, although stem dates are confirmed, and with demurrage rates now well into the USD 70’s/day it can prove an expensive exercise. Things will settle down at a stage of course, but not before June is a wrap.

**USG**

 exports have also sprung to life, knocking on USD 9 million in freight for the benchmark Ningbo discharge, and spiced with continued enquiries from both Brazil and Wafr, now starting with a 7, there’s plenty to keep the fire burning. This will in turn tempt more owners to turn their ships left at Galle, further depleting the MEG position list. Come what may of production cuts we can’t see this going south in the near future.
