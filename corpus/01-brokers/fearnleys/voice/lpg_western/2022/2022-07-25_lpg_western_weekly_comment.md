## Market Commentary

AMERICAS As of the middle part of this week, few deals have been done in the US Gulf FOB market attributed to unpredictable, sharp crude swings along with a general summer holiday market. At present, bids for US GULF FOB cargos sit at 4 cpg, with August FOB sellers targeting slightly higher in the mid 4's. Numbers in line with netback levels to the East, but failing to account for costs canal delays etc. The stagnant resale market has left freight feeling long, and predictably lead to notable drops (close to 5 bucks in past couple days) in freight rates on a Houston/Chiba basis. As of today, the Baltic assessed Houston/Chiba freight at $92 pmt. On Wednesday, the EIA showed a larger than anticipated build of 2.4 million barrels for the week ending 22nd of July. As it stands total stocks now sit at 62 million barrels, (4 percent lower than the same period last year) Though propane exports increased week on week, so has propane production- which now sits at roughly 2.37 million barrels per day. NWE Propane market feels particularly week at the moment, following a record month of US origin cargos arriving in the region. The total imported volume exceeded storage capabilities alluding to the high run rates at flexible steam crackers both in and out of the ARA. In eastern Europe, the ban on Russian origin LPG cargos has been lifted into Poland, however, there have yet to be deals done as supply current supply seems adequate to meet summer demand.

**MED**

 In the med, the spot market has continued to sit idle as demand has been more than adequately fulfilled by term contracts. Very dead summer market.
