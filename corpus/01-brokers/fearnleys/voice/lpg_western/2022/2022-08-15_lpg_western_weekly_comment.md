## Market Commentary

AMERICAS LPG market activity levels in the U.S. ground practically to a halt early this week with very little accomplished by a largely complacent and mostly disinterested trading community. The EIA data release on Wednesday appears to have spurred interest as a larger-than-expected build of 2.3mmbbls was reported and brought overall stocks to nearly 102% of last year at an overall total of 68 mmbbls. Unless the newly released forecasts for a cold winter turn out to be incorrect, this should effectively set aside concerns that stocks might be insufficient as crop drying is likely going to be limited. That, in turn, is due to poor crop yield and oppressively hot and dry growing conditions over the summer. Meanwhile, poor arb conditions, softening freight rates and limited end-user demand have kept pressure on terminal fees, which were this week quoted for 1H Sep at between 4cpg and NWE The market continues to be well supplied with record US arrivals continuing and covering all demand. However, the market has begun to show signs of demand recovery with petchems and end users beginning to review their 4th quarter demand. Recent rains in Germany and Switzerland have given hope that Rhine levels will start to recover.

**MED**

 Lackluster market continues. Algerian exports continuing to look East, to take the place of Med demand, until the market recovers.
