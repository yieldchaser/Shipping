## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Pricing volatility is the key factor in the domestic U.S. propane market, as Mont Belvieu values have declined over the course of April but seen vast pricing swings in the meanwhile. The combination of reactive crude and natural gas prices - which are tightly linked to both the lingering Covid-related issues in the East and the war in Ukraine – has allowed April MB propane to conclude in the high $1.20’s/gal. According to the EIA, US propane inventory levels as of 22 April increased by 2.2mmbbls from the previous week, though some observers cast doubt on the accuracy of the data and are already anticipating a set of corrective data in the weeks to come. The build was larger than expected but the market nonetheless gained a penny on the day, confounding many. Domestic production levels reached their highest levels ever, but robust exports in April (and going forward) have left the impression that a series of pushes and pulls – a ‘tug of war’, in essence - will be seen through the summer in a battle pitting production levels versus export volumes. NWE May CIF ARA propane was last marked at $752/t (up by $4/t) as the European market looked to buy May propane and butane due to multi-dimensional supply constraints in the North Sea. The first due to lagging output in Norway due to maintenance at gas processing centers, in addition to notable decrease in exports availability pertaining to disruption in Russian product. OPIS reported a decrease nearing 35% in April exports compared to the same period last year. That said, propane has continued its weakness in relation to naphtha- The spread has averaged -$136/t in April, compared to -$78/t same time last year, and presently sits at -$138/t. For this reason, LPG cargos loading from US East Coast, continue to garner heavy interest as European buyers look to maintain equilibrium due to the aforementioned supply disruptions in the North Sea.

**MED**

 The Med continues to be tight, butane which has been hard to find in the region allowing the arb from the US to the Med to widen- acting as a valve to mitigate supply pressure -as vessels STS into coasters in the region. Continued maintenance on a pair of West-Med crackers has been extended slightly, however the news did little to exacerbate current tightness.
