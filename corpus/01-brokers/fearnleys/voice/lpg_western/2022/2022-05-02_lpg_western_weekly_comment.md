## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The EIA reported a 1.6mmbbl week-over-week increase in domestic US propane stocks for the reporting period ending 29 April. This brings the overall total stock level to 40.8mmbbls, or 98% of last year’s total and 66% of the total achieved in 2020. In order for inventories to reach the desired 80mmbbl level by end Oct 2022, and with 26 weeks remaining in the traditional ‘build’ period, an average weekly improvement of 1.51mmbbls will be required. For the past five years, and for the period from May through Oct, the weekly build rate has averaged 2.064mmbbls. Therefore, the desired level of approximately 80mmbbls should be achieved with relative ease. This is in spite of generally robust export volumes but because of the rising production levels. Meanwhile, current terminal fee market levels for June range from around 5cpg to 6cpg, depending on timing, grade(s) and level of desire (from both sides). A number of offers – particularly 50/50 parcels - have recently populated the market, but buyers note the ongoing backwardation and remain cautious. NWE The spot market in the region has been active, and is trading end May. The bid/offer level in the TOT window has been wide, with many bidders today seeing an inflated market value in the window. Today the bottom line is that the arb from the US is closed and that production in Europe remains 30-40% below usual. Some spring maintenance is being carried out by petchems reducing demand at the same time as mild weather in the region further reducing demand. Cargoes remain well bid and offered, and physical value we assess at a small premium to May CIF ARA. That said cargoes continue to flow to the region from the US and find homes at premiums to CIF ARA outside of the window. So it looks like netback values are not painting the whole picture.
