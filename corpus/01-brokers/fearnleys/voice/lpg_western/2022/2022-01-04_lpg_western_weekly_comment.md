## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The first cold fronts of the season have finally swept across the nation, but this is only causing normal winter demand. Market levels, however, have managed to creep up once again , and are now in the $1.10/gal level for Jan, perhaps because the true winter period commenced only a couple of weeks ago, because export volumes remain healthy, and because of the strength of Brent and WTI. The EIA this week reported an 800,000bbl decline in stocks to the 65.70mmbbl level as of 31 Dec, which places propane inventories at just over 90% of the same time last year. In that the data revealed a 200,000bbl increase in Gulf Coast stocks, however, there exists a general agreement that additional corrective data is likely to be released in the coming weeks or that increased production is showing up. With regards to terminal fees, buy-side interest is generally at 5.75 cpg while sellers have shown themselves at 6.25cts. An early Feb transaction was noted at 6cts/gal. NWE Traders in the region planned supply over the holiday period well, and the market remains balanced for January. Terneuzen will receive its first VLGC since mid-December and is expected to be back to full demand before the end of the month. This is will add to the region’s demand, which is already near max. The January propane naphtha spread of +$35pmt is keeping Chem demand up, and retail demand remains high with average winter temperatures. Europe continues to command a strong netback, the Mt Belvieu, CIF ARA arb is wide open for the whole of Q1 for cargoes arriving in the same month as loading. Which has many traders holding 1H Month US loading cargoes looking for short in Europe.

**MED**

 The region’s demand has not yet surpassed term supply. Though a butane deal done was heard to have been done this week. December import volume from the US was 600KT, the highest since January 2021. One of the big questions hanging over the region today is: Are all VLGCs heading to Gibraltar are for the Med, or are they set to transit the Suez Canal to go to Eastern destinations?
