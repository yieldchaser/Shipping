## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The US showed another large build of 3.3 million barrels in the weekly propane inventories putting the total above 80 million barrels for the first time this year. The 81.1 million barrels now in inventories should end any concern of having enough product to cover the upcoming winter demand. Unfortunately for US exporters the big price drop went hand in hand with NWE and Asian prices, keeping the export market pegged to the 4 cent per gallon level for October. Going forward the ever increasing freight market combined with the lack luster Chinese market is keeping a floor on terminaling fees. In South America, Gasmar covered their November CFR requirement from a Turkish Trader at what was labeled “A very competitive offer.” NWE The sentiment in the NWE large cargo market has tightened some in the last week and many traders anticipate more is to come - sighting petrochemical demand and colder weather. The increased demand has allowed European arb economics to beat levels shown by far east participants- as Asian PDH margins continue to flounder. The Butane price in Northwest Europe still sits at 100 percent of Naphtha- but has softened some week on week as Mediterranean buyers ( present in the market last week) have gone away.

**MED**

 October interest in them med has picked up steam- at least directionally- as Europe's limited refinery supplies will likely not be enough to satisfy all around demand as winter looms. However, it's understood that if and when the region does tighten up- US origin VLGC's will quickly fill the void, limiting how tight the market can really get.
