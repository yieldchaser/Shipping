## Market Commentary

Mt Belvieu prices are still affected by the weekly stock numbers. This week Mt Belvieu rose after the, 1 million barrel less than expected 1.4million barrel build. International traders cannot ignore the Mt Belvieu price reactions to the weekly inventories, but the feeling is that whether inventories get to 90million or 100million barrels, stocks will be plentiful for winter. Export discussions have been quiet due to an industry conference in Hong Kong and FOB buying ideas have been declining by the day in line with the declining arbs and stagnant freight.
