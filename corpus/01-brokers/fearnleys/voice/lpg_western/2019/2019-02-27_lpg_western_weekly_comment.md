## Market Commentary

This winter in North America has been a monster. The cold weather started early and it has lasted long. None the less the propane production has been an even bigger monster. Averaging just over 2 million barrels a day, the question of whether the US could handle record exports and cold weather has been answered with a resounding YES. Export Terminals and mid-stream companies are gearing up with expansions, new fractionators, and new pipelines to handle the production going forward, but the question will no longer be is there enough product, but where will the product go? This week US C3 inventories decreased by 1.2 million barrels which was less than expectations. This and declining freight rates has allowed the arb to widen to levels not seen in several months and is pointing towards stronger terminalling fees. It certainly seems that this who locked in terminalling under 6 cpg for 2019 are currently on the right side for now.
