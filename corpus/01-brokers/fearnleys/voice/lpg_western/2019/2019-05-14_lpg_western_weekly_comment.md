## Market Commentary

Delays , delays, delays. Seems the Houston Ship Channel cannot rid itself from the huge magnitude of delays that have occurred this year. First it was fog, then two terrible explosions at two different locations, and last week a collision between a tanker and several barges. The effects of these delays had huge ramifications in the Asian markets and the effects of which are still being felt. Asia is now very dependent on US exports and delays in the US Gulf result in large premiums being paid in Asia. The Asian market is also complicated by the 25 percent tariffs on US product going into China which has in turn put a premium on non US origin product. On the domestic front the US Propane market showed a weekly build of 2.7 million barrels the majority of which was in the US Gulf. Production came in well over 2 million barrels per day and domestic demand showed an all time low. Going forward we expect lower US prices and the arb to remain wide.
