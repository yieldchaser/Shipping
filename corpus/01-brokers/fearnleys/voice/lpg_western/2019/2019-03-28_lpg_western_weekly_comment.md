## Market Commentary

The winter is over, the absolute price of propane is falling, and this combined with loading delays is allowing the arbs and freights to reach the best levels in over a year. Delays in the US Gulf has caused near panic in Asia forcing premiums to FEI to sky rocket. The US is exporting roughly 2.5 million tons per month of LPG and out of this roughly 50 percent is going to Asia . Hence 15 day delays in loading translates into aproximately 750,000 mts per month into Asia and thus it is not hard to understand the large premiums being seen for second half May arrivals. The question going forward is, will arbs last at reasonable levels. Fog season will end but as stated before production is too strong and US propane must price to move. Already today propane is down to 45 percent of WTI and we expect it to drop further. Historically, propane below 45 percent of WTI makes for a good Arb and frieght market.
