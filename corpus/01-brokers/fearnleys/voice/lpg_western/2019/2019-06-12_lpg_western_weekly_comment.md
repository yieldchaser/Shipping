## Market Commentary

Despite strong and increasing freight, the arb remains open from the US to Europe and Asia. Production and exports remain at record levels and we expect this to continue going forward. In fact it is hard to see the scenario changing anytime soon unless demand begins to waver. However, with prices so low compared to the alternatives, this is not something we expect to happen. Propane inventories rose 2.8 million barrels for the week ending June 7th which slightly above trader expectations, and puts the total number on track to reach over 100,000,000 barrels by October 1st , 2019.
