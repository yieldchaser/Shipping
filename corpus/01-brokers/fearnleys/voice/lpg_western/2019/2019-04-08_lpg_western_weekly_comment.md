## Market Commentary

FOB discussions in the USGC remain thin. The fog and benzene spill caused havoc and delays which will affect USGC loadings well into May. Traders with cargoes loading from terminals on the Houston ship channel are still unsure of the exact number of days delay they will incur. Halting all FOB trade discussions in the area due to laycan uncertainty. This has led to Marcus Hook going from the ugly duckling, during the well documented ME2 commissioning problems, to the belle of the ball today. Demand for East Coast US tons is at a premium and Marcus Hook has not disappointed. Set to export 9 VLGCs in April and we believe 7 VLGCs per month onwards. Marcus Hook is having a global impact on export cargo flows. The build of 1.2 million barrels although expected surprisingly pushed MT Belvieu up to 42% WTI from 39% the week prior. Latin American demand has been strong, with 80 000 MT more imported into Mexico alone in the 1st quarter 2019 compared with 1st quarter 2018. South American incremental seasonal demand continues, a recent US VLGC tender is rumoured to be heading that way.
