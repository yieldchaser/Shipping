## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: The 300,000 barrel draw in US inventories was slightly below expectations and is not expected to have a big effect on the Mt Belvieu price. US inventories remain above 100 million barrels, which is keeping the Mt Belvieu price well around 36% of WTI. With cooler than average temperatures on the way for much on the US we expect to see more draws in PADD 1 as product is moved from secondary to tertiary storages. These draws are unlikely to change the plentiful US supply picture with the 4 week average of production at 2.172 million barrels per day. Most traders expect with over 100 million barrels in inventories and domestic production above 2 million barrels per day. There will be enough product to see winter through, including the additional export capacity built this year. For this reason the arb from the US is maintaining it’s strength as USGC FOB terminalling fee discussions are still in double figures.
