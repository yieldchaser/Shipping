## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: The market was battered down after inventories on Wednesday after showing a higher than expected build of 3.2 million barrel build for the week ending August 9th 2019. This has left US inventories at 86.5 million barrels, 24% above the same week last year. The way US production is going has lead traders to believe US inventories will exceed 100 million barrels of propane before draw season begins. It has also lead to traders believing that draw season will begin later than the historic October 1st date. US FOB discussions are still above cancellation levels, but with delivered markets looking very weak and discounted, traders are wondering where they can place the tons. The question is if Mt Belvieu propane continues to be cheap, around or below 30% of WTI, will the good value manage to sustain 1.3 million barrels per day or above of exports from the US through winter?
