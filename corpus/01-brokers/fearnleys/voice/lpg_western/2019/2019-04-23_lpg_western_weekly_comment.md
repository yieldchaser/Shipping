## Market Commentary

We are starting to see a clearer picture of the volume of exports lost to the delays on the Houston ship channel. Exports were curbed by approximately 200,000 barrels per day for 45 days. Keeping 9 million barrels of LPG in the domestic US market. With this in mind some traders found it surprising to see Mt Belvieu prices rise after a slightly smaller than expected, but still a build of 1 million barrels in US inventories. We expect to see maximum exports from US terminals for the next few weeks as the catch up continues, with the normal lifting schedule to be resumed at all terminals by the beginning of June. South American demand continues its seasonal increase. Importers are having to pay hefty premiums to draw tons to the region to compete with the CFR market in the East. Regarding the Gasmar tender it is still uncertain if they were able to buy anything for second half June arrival.
