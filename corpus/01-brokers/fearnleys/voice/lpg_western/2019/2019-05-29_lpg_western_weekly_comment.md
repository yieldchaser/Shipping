## Market Commentary

The US propane inventories showed an unexpected drawdown today of 100,000 barrels. This drawdown was completely unexpected and very far from the consensus view of a build of 2 million barrels. Under normal circumstances, this would have caused the propane market to spike upwards but with crude falling so fast and so hard it meant that propane would not fall as much and propane did firm compared to WTI closing today at 39 percent of WTI. General belief is that that this is a correction from prior weeks and that next week should once again show a build of close to 2 million barrels. Bottom line is that there is enough inventory to support, domestic cracking, retail and exports for the foreseeable future. In other news US terminals are continuing their catch up from last months delays. Enterprise expects to be caught up in early June and Targa by late June. In Canada, Alta Gas completed their first export from their new facility at Prince Rupert Island, British Colombia. They had a few normal start up delays but overall the terminal performed well and the spec was perfect. All going well Alta gas hopes to have 30 VLGC propane cargos a year from the new terminal.
