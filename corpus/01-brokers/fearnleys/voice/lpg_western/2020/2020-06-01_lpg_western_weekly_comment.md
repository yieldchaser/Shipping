## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: A higher than expected US propane inventory build of 3.1 million barrels was the fifth straight build in inventories. Arbitrageurs hoped this would improve the curve, however Mt Belvieu remained strong as US traders focused more on production declines than inventory builds. Current production dipped slightly to 1.989 million barrels per day and we believe the days of production over 2 million barrels per day is now a thing of the past. Netbacks remain around 4 cpg however deals continue to be done above these levels as buyers seem to be more content on moving their vessels as opposed to netback econs. Discussions on export cancellations is continuing but it seems to us the number is fewer than believed, although the net amount of cargos from the US is dropping and this is really taking its toll on the shipping market.
