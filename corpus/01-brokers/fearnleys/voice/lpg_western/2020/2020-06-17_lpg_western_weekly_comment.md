## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: A larger than expected build of 2.7 million barrels in US propane inventories helped improve the arb slightly Wednesday. The underlying positive note from the EIA stats was the production level. This has risen for the third week in a row to 2.142 million barrels a day. This rise in production is already having an effect as export volumes are beginning to ramp from the US East Coast. With global demand starting to recover and US production rising traders are reluctant to cancel US cargoes. Lets hope both these trends continue and keep cancellations at bay. In South America Gasmar awarded the second tender of the season and are expected to have at least one more before their Winter ends.
