## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Another good build of 1.1million barrels occurred in US inventories. There was little immediate Mt Belvieu price reaction however today propane is trading above mid-winter levels. Traders noted a second consecutive week of production around 1.935 million barrels per day and exports around 830,000 barrels per day. Production below 2 million barrels per day was expected. Exports on the other hand were surprisingly low and likely to increase. The mood regarding 2H June US FOBs being traded at the moment is that once the overhang of US cargoes loaded in May has been cleared in the East market strength will return to a market trading around cancellation levels today. Some predict it will return with a bang! Due to global waterborne LPG volume being cut to far, and demand reviving.
