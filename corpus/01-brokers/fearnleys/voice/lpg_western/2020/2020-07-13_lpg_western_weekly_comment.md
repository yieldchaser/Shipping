## Market Commentary

AMERICAS A sizable inventory build of 3.5 million barrels, due in part to a decrease in exports (901,000b/d on the week), softened the Mt. Belvieu propane price, and effectively widened LST/FEI arbitrage. Netbacks for August cargos are back above 4 cents and discussions in the spot market are bid at 4.5 and offered at 5.5cpg for C3 cargoes. The ramp up of US Crude and Natural Gas production has continued its bullish momentum, as the WTI price has stabilized above $40/ barrel- allowing for a return of US East Coast exports which were cut into sharply back in April/ May as producers scaled back volume in the Marcellus/ Utica regions. It is estimated now that Marcus Hook exports are back up to the equivalent of 10/11 or so VLGC's/ month. That said, US traders are still feeling the covid induced demand lapse in Asia -however economics are decent enough to stave off any cancelation rumblings, especially when supported by large product builds and the subsequent hope of cheaper Mt. Belvieu.
