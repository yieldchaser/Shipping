## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: On Wednesday, the EIA reported a build of 660,000 barrels, the bulk of which came from the East Coast. While exports decreased this week compared to last, it’s reported that 1.15 million barrels /day found their way to the water, furthering the notion that propane and butane has been far less susceptible to coronavirus demand interruptions than rest of the barrel. In the recent weeks, the propane price along with Spot cargo discussion has been kept afloat by considerable demand from China, Indonesia, Korea, Japan and India. Indias demand for LPG has increased as much as 20 percent, and will keep exports firm if it continues. As it turns out, the US is no different. Argus reported that domestic propane marketers saw 10 percent increase in usage this month compared to last- contributing to balance. In the US Gulf, terminals and third party sellers are looking to market the remaining 2-3 May cargos, before looking ahead to June. As it stands levels for these cargos are being valued at 6-7 cpg , with ship availability proving the difficult variable.
