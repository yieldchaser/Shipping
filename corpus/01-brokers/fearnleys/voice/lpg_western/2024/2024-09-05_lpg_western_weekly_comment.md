## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The month of August 2024 has seen very little in the way of propane pricing changes, as on Aug 1 non-TET c3 at Mont Belvieu was posted at 76.875cts/gal and on Aug 30 values were marked at 76.0625, a net decline of a little more than three-quarters of a cent. However, a loss of over 2.5cpg has been seen in the first days of September, much of which was realized today after the EIA posted its inventory findings for the week ending 30 Aug. And where the market was anticipating an approximate 1mmbbl build, the EIA data revealed a surprising 2.6mmbbl propane stock increase which brought overall US to 95.70mmbbls, or 99.7% of last year. It is anticipated that the flat-price decline will continue to incentivize exports, which are still at (effectively) maximum levels. Meanwhile, production of propane in the US remains robust, with a YTD average of 2.648mmbbls/day which compares to this week’s 2.689mmbbl/day production level.
