## Market Commentary

AMERICAS A pricing standoff currently exists between FOB buyers and sellers, and both – it seems – are correct in their assessment of value. Buyers are pointing to the Panama Canal risks (and costs) as well as rising freight rates and placing July terminal fee prices in the upper single digits. Sellers, on the other hand, reference the arb and limited Middle East avails and are holding out at double-digits. The Mont Belvieu market has seen declines through the month of May – until today, when a bit of a rally has brought propane prices up to the ~72.00cts/gal level, or about a penny increase from earlier in the week. This is initially believed to be a result of recently relaxed Panama Canal fees and contracting arbs. As for the inventories, the EIA this week reported overall stock levels at 64.20mmbbls, an increase of 2.2mmbbls over the previous week, and which brings current stocks to 96.25% of the levels recorded a year ago. Those in the US market are generally comfortable with the rate of build, and most estimates bring inventory levels to around 100mmbbls by the first week of Oct, which is mostly accepted as being sufficient for the following winter and for ongoing overwater exports.
