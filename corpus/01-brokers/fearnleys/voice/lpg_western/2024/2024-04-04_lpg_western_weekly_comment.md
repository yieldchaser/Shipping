## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Domestic US propane inventory expectations for the week ending 29 March ranged from a draw of around a half-million barrels to as much as a build of over a million barrels. On yesterday the EIA reported a 400,000bbl decline in stocks which brought the overall total to 51.80mmbbls. This is 93% of last year, but 150% of two years ago. One of the components to the Mont Belvieu pricing structure – and one of the several reasons that the MB complex has retained it strength – is the concern that robust exports would limit the ability of domestic stocks to reach suitable levels later in the Fall. However, calculations are presently indicating that stock levels will – in the first week of October – range somewhere between 94-98mmbbls which is well within the comfort zone. Nonetheless, MB propane has enjoyed the support from the crude arena, which from the front of March to now has increased by $5/bbl (and rising). The fact that domestic US crude inventories rose by 3.2mmbbls last week has had little (or no) impact, as geopolitical concerns continue to apply upward pressure. With regards the US terminal fees, a couple of transactions were noted for end-Apr and (separately) early-May, each at 7cpg. A couple of other parcels are on offer in the first decade of May in the low 7cpg range, with buy-side interest beginning to collect in the upper 6cpg range.
