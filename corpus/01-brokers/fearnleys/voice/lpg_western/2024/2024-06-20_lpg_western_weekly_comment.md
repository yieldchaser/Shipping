## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The Mont Belvieu propane market remains robust at ~80cpg for July, and is being bolstered in tandem by both the strong crude market (which this week pushed through resistance levels of $81.55/bbl and is today at $82.34/bbl), as well as recent domestic US inventory levels which have revealed less-than-expected builds. In this week’s data, the EIA reported that stock levels increased by 1.6mmbbls to a total of 71.4mmbbls, where the average build for Week 24 is 2.2mmbbls and expectations were calling for upwards of 2.5mmbbls. Those predictions were based on the notion that export levels were off a bit after a series of storms over the past month battered the City of Houston and caused periodic and temporary power outages. It is believed that July cargoes are at this point sold out, save for one potential cargo which is offered at 14cpg. Most of the activity which occurred for July took place at between 10.00 and 12.50cpg.
