## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Activity levels have dulled in recent days as many regular market participants have either retreated to the sidelines in an effort to preserve any profits achieved in 2024, or have begun to depart for the holidays. However, a few transactions are known to have been achieved including a recently concluded 17.50cpg 4:0 January FOB deal which has effectively set market levels. Meanwhile, many await the results of a term tender which may well set the forward path. December is now concluded and only a handful of January offers are known to exist, but depending on arb levels (which continue to hover around the $200/mt level) – and freight to the East (which seems to be waffling at just over $100/mt) – this could change. Little is anticipated in the coming days – even weeks, however – due to the holidays. Otherwise, the EIA yesterday reported a 3.0mmbbl decline in overall propane stock levels for an updated total of 90.10mmbbls, which is 96% of last year and 103% of two years ago. The draw was not surprising as several industry contacts accurately assessed the decline prior to its release. Now the Days of Supply stands at 66 days (down from 75 days at last week’s report), production remains robust at 2.667mmbbls/day (against a 2024 average of 2.668mmbbls/day) while export levels were reported at 1.987mmbbls (versus a YTD average of 1.751mmbbls/day).
