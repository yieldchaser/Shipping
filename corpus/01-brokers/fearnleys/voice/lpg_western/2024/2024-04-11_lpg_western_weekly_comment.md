## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Terminal fee levels for 2H April and 1H May are pretty firmly entrenched at the 7cpg level – the rate at which a series of transactions are known to have occurred within that timeframe. The 2H May market ranges a bit more widely as buyers and sellers ideas diverge – but only by a half-cent, or so. The EIA propane inventory data for the week ending 05 April 2024 was generally anticipated to reveal a build of a half-million or so barrels of propane – which would have brought overall stocks well in to the 52mmbbl range. However, a draw of 100,000bbls was recorded and stock levels now stand at 51.7mmbbls. Not only did the overall total raise a few eyebrows, but a secondary surprise was noted with a 600,000bbl build on the Gulf Coast in PADD III. Exports have been extremely robust and so to see a build is noteworthy. From the higher vantage point, US propane stocks at the first week of October have ranged anywhere from 90-102mmbbls as of the first week of October. This year should see little difference, as current calculations put the first-week Oct inventory level at between 94-98mmbbls … “right down the fairway”, as it is said. NWE Competing factors have left participants puzzled when trying to conceive direction in the European LPG market- Following a month of seemingly unworkable arbitrage econs (US /NWE) already 430,000 tons have pointed toward NWE from various US export terminals, which, given warm weather and balanced stocks, would cover any and all demand. However, Propane remains the preferred feedstock to Naphta, both of which are off around $10 from their April high’s. Scheduled maintenance at Karsto (April 12-18) and Mongstad (April 5-17), further compounded by an unplanned outage in the run up, will likely cause a disruption of north sea supplied LPG supplied to NWE, all of which could theoretically leave NWE LPG tighter than appears.
