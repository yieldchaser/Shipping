## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The US LPG market has been very subdued this week due to the U.S Thanksgiving holiday on Thursday. Many regular market participants have been away from their desks and little has occurred. The EIA inventory report, however, was released yesterday amid earlier predictions of a 1.10-1.40mmbbl draw. Where the 5-yr average for Week 47 shows an average draw of 224,000bbls, for the week ending 22 Nov the EIA reported a draw of 1.0mmbbls, bringing the overall propane total stock level in the U.S. to 96.70mmbbls, or about 98% of the same time last year but 107% of two years ago. There are presently 87 Days of Supply, versus a 2024 average of nearly 83 Days of Supply and a Q4 average of nearly 99%. Terminal fees, meanwhile, are in the mid teens at this point, though an 18cpg 4:0 transaction was reported earlier in the week, though most buyers balk at such lofty levels and point to calculated levels at 14-15cpg.
