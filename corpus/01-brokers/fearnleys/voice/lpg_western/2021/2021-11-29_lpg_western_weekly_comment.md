## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The sky is probably NOT falling but it sure feels like it. Lack of weather across the US has caused propane prices to crash from a 1.41 per gallon a month ago to 97 cents per gallon today. The inventory situation, which a month ago looked as though the US would not have enough product to cover first quarter export demand, is no longer an issue. Talk of 10 percent production growth, the lack of weather and falling Asian demand, have all contributed, but the bottom line is, we are not running out of product. The weekly number showed a draw of 1 million barrels which had little effect on the market. Terminal fees have continued to fall with Jan cargos bid at 4.50 cpg and sellers at just under 6. NWE The European market seems to be the only one which knows what winter is! CIF ARA continues to show relative strength in the context of FEI losing over $50pmt today and Mt Belvieu propane dipping under the dollar mark for the first time in months. Europe is feeling winter weather already and is forecast to have coldest winter in 30 years. But that is not the whole story as PDHs started buying again in December and January, ramping up imports from the US. And local supply continues to be around 36% less than usual as LPG is spiked into the natural gas stream. The arb East coming in has the December East/West spread at +$32 and more US loading VLGCs looking to come to the region. A seen by today’s window offer of a full propane VLGC cargo for end December arrival at December CIF ARA flat. This offer was basis 1 port with EU qualifications for buyer and equivalent to an offer of CIF ARA +$5pmt for a TOT stem.

**MED**

 The market is calm in the region and it seems buyers are waiting to see offers before showing their hand. Cold weather is being felt and demand is increasing, although this is yet to translate to action in the market.
