## Market Commentary

NWE CIF ARA propane fell further have been falling further than crude oil values.. A cargo TOT window continues to be offered and value is now assessed at a slim premium of USD 0.5 per metric ton. The spot market has been quiet. Traders are waiting to see the full affect of the region’s supply maintenance and are well covered for the time being. CIF ARA values will have to increase if they want to continue to attract US product, as a small build in inventories on the other side of the pond resulted in Mt Belvieu strengthening vs WTI and the arb narrowing.

**MED**

 Multiple butane offers are seen in the region causing bearish sentiment and leaving mixed butane at 71.3% naphtha. Demand in the region has quiet. AMERICAS Following the EIA’s tepid report of a 400,000 barrel build, the propane price at Mt. Belvieu softened slightly, and then stabilized at around 82 cpg. Presently, US propane stocks sit at levels 30 percent lower than this time last year (44.4mn bl), and thus a handful of market participants foresee strong US propane price on the back of product tightness, for the balance of 2021. As of Thursday morning, the June LST/FEI arbitrage on paper is yielding netbacks definitively below cancelation economics, though no new cancellations have been reported- as the Far East continues to actively search for product on the back of petrochemical demand. Participants with product sourced from the US are opting to load cargos with the hope that the FEI spot price rises on the journey over, instead of cancelling. In June, a handful of cargos remain on offer from the gulf, though at these levels it would take a very competitive offer in order to yield a deal. In Latin America, Brazil continues to buy to cover seasonal demand
