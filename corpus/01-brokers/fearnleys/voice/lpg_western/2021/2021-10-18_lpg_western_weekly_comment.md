## Market Commentary

AMERICAS A primary U.S. propane inventory increase of 1.9mmbbls was recorded by the EIA for the week ending 15 Oct, surprising most of the market which had been anticipating either a modest draw or a small build. Total stocks have reached 73.6mmbbls, or 75% of both last year and two years ago. On a Days of Supply basis, there continues to be a deficit against last year, and stocks on that basis are still below the range of the last 5 years, hence the ongoing concerned focus related to the winter demand period as it relates to the continuing robust export volumes. Although there has begun to be cooler weather in North America, there have yet to be any real storm systems which might mark the true commencement of winter. Cargo availability continues to be split, with offers being seen from the trading community, while still being limited from the terminals themselves. Terminal fees reflect this and for Nov spot trade is presently focused at just under 6cts/gal. NWE The well documented news that LPG is being spiked into the LNG stream by producers has rapidly improved CIFA ARA values. Though TOT window offers have been generating the most discussions. Dow and Vilma both offering in the window was seen as bearish. Dow is expected to receive term tons and is not running at 100% so are looking to make an achievable profit on tons they have in the region today. For traders the arb is wide open, netback values from the US are around 9cpg for November for US liftings arriving in North West Europe in the same month. Raising the question whether selling in the TOT window can make more sense than waiting in the Panama canal to sell at a discount in the East. The question is whether these offers will push the window into a discount, or if the European market with less locally produced tons, can absorb the offered tons and more heading into this winter market?

**MED**

 The quiet market and prevailing tight supply environment continues. Moroccan public holidays adding to the lack of trade discussions. No winter weather in the Mediterranean region aiding the wait and see trader attitude.
