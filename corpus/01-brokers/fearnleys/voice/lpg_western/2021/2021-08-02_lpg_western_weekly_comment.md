## Market Commentary

NWE An offer was seen in the window which equated to September CIF ARA -$2.5pmt. This was the first bit of action seen in over a week. The offer changed the view that the market is leaning towards being balanced rather than tight. On the back of VLGCs loaded in the US heading to the region in early Sept. Butane saw a bid in the window and is at 95% September naphtha, though this is a moving target due to crude volatility. Petchem demand has come off significantly due to planned maintenances being carried out and importantly forward pro/nap values go from a meagre -$18pmt September moving to an unfavorable +$2pmt by December. The temperatures are cooler than seasonal averages though this is not expected to create enough demand to change the market outlook in the short term.

**MED**

 The region has been starved of tons with the arb from the US remaining closed. This has helped drive demand for locally sourced tons, FOB Lavera values particularly strong for summer. Both the East and West Mediterranean are tight of LPG Mix and butane. AMERICAS With many out of the office on holiday, the MB propane market continues to trudge along in the 1.08-1.09/gal range, though all the while the level of concern related to propane stock levels in the U.S. is increasing. Wednesday’s EIA report showed a 1.5mmbbl build, putting overall total US propane inventories at an even 66mmbbls. This includes, though, a 300,000bbl decline in PADD III (Gulf Coast) stocks and puts this year at 76% of the 86.7mmbbls recorded at the same time in 2020, and 81% of the 79.3mmbbls achieved in early August 2019. There are around 10 weeks remaining in the traditional stock ‘build’ season, and to reach 80mmbbls another 14mmbbls of inventory growth is needed – or 1.4mmbbls (on average) per week. The overarching question – and the source of the concern – is whether this will be a sufficient volume to accommodate domestic needs (crop drying + res/com + petrochemical) and overwater export requirements. Meanwhile, terminal fees continue sitting between 5.00 and 5.25cpg, depending on location, timing, and ratio. A host of mixed-ratio re-sellers have begun to emerge, and while there are full-propane cargoes available, the Ethane/Propane pricing differential issue continues to dissuade the terminals themselves from entering the fray at competitive price levels.
