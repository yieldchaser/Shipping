## Market Commentary

NWE Window bidding failing to find seller has raised CIF ARA paper value as well as pushed the physical window premium to near CIF ARA July +$10pmt. All tons arriving to the region are accounted for by crackers, leaving 1H July tight. The arb from the US remains unworkable as Mt Belvieu strength continues off the back of strong crude and lower than expected inventory build. So although the netback has increased and CIF ARA July is getting close to 3cpg, it is still more than 1cpg below the FEI netback. For the time being the market is set to remain tight.

**MED**

 There has been butane selling interest in the region, though buyers are keeping quiet. Petchem demand in the West Med is strong, VLGCs coming in and no surplus product being resold. AMERICAS Market values for July propane at Mont Belvieu have risen to $102.75/gal (as of this writing), riding the combined wave of support from both the crude oil sector as well as the most recent propane inventory data released by the EIA. In that report the government revealed a 1.046mmbbl increase in primary propane stocks in the U.S., bringing the overall total to 56.240mmbbls, which compares to last year’s total figure of 71.331mmbbls, or a 15.091mmbbl deficit YOY. WTI, meanwhile, was marked at $73.08/bbl for August, having been spurred by U.S. gasoline futures which – in turn – were being pushed by a bullish inventory drawdown. Terminal fees in the U.S. are presently in the 4.50-5.00cts/gal range, with offers seen on the higher end of the range and bids at the lower end, and more than a penny lower on the East Coast. Meanwhile, in an interesting turn of events not seen in well over a decade, Enterprise this week marked itself as a buyer of August Enterprise cargoes (any dates, any ratio) and is now known to have purchased a cargo from a term customer. Additional purchases of similar variety are anticipated.
