## Market Commentary

AMERICAS A welcomed lower than expected 1.6 million barrel draw in US propane inventories. Although there is a cold front in the North East we do not expect more large draws in US inventories this winter. Cancellations occurred in February and early March which will bring the export volume down. We are starting to see the effect of these cancellations and Mt Belvieu has come down to just above 60% WTI. Improving the arbs East and West, both FEI and CIF ARA have been strengthening while Mt Belvieu comes off. Let’s hope this trend continues and hauls US netbacks out of cancellation territory. US FOB bids are beginning to rise back up to levels sellers are willing to consider. The question is, are FOB bids rising fastest to prevent any further cancellations? Our prediction. Yes!
