## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The 4.1mmbbl build in propane stocks for the week ending 28 May was instantly met with both elation and contempt, and resulted in an almost immediate decline in Mont Belvieu propane values (to 89.75cts/gal for Jun), but also speculation as to whether next week’s data release would include corrected data. On the former point, and after some weeks of disappointing build levels, propane stocks in the U.S. were in rather desperate need of showing signs of improving. In any case, at 48.2mmbbls as of the 28 May, domestic US propane stocks still pale in comparison to levels of a year ago when 67.3mmbbls were reported, a 19.1mmbbl deficit YOY. The generally accepted comfort level for pre-winter propane stocks is in the 65-70 mmbls range, which will require an average weekly build over the next 21 weeks of sbout 1 million barrels per week which we believe is very achievable. Meanwhile, terminal fees have been seen and reported in the 3.5-4.0 cpg range for June, with asking levels in July tentatively within the same range for the Gulf Coast and a penny cheaper for the USEC.
