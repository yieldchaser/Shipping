NWE An end October TOT window bid at CIF ARA October flat saw an early November TOT offer surface at November CIF ARA minus $2/MT. Term imports to the region continue to satisfy demand as petrochemical demand remains low whilst unseasonably warm temperatures are keeping incremental winter demand at bay. **MED**

 Large trans-Atlantic volumes continue to be imported to the region. While the hottest September on record is keeping demand subdued. Leading to a quiet week in the spot market. AMERICAS Mont Belvieu propane levels have so far not succumbed to the weight of domestic inventory levels, but instead have been more swayed by crude, which until late September was on a slow but steady upward march that concluded in the low $90’s/bbl range – which propane values followed. As of the start of October, though, there have only been declines in the crude arena and, as such, the trajectory of propane has mostly mirrored WTI, remaining all the while at a ratio in the 33-34% range. As for the inventories themselves, most of the market this week was anticipating a correction last week’s data, which revealed a reported build that was well below expectations. This week the EIA data confounded even more, as a 0.00mmbbl build (or draw) was reported, leaving overall stocks at 101.4mmbbls when an average of +1.75mmbbls was predicted (and historically has seen a 2.4mmbbl rise). Whether or not this marks the conclusion of the traditional build season – or whether a large correction is now in order - remains to be seen, but up until now an overall total had been pegged at the 105mmbbl level as of end-Sep. As for the terminal fee market, things had been cooling off and a Nov transaction was reported in the low 10cpg range with buyers retreating to single digits. More recently, though, there was an about-face and an end-Oct was this week rumored at 13cpg.
