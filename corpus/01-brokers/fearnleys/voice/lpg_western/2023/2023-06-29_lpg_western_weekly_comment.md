## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Spot FOB market activity is generally muted despite the relatively wide arb due to what seems to be ever-rising freight rates. At least a couple of cargo cancelations have been noted as a result, as those with ships and FOB volumes are focusing on reletting their vessels in place of lifting themselves. Terminal fees continue in the low 5cpg range but are presently in contango through the balance of the year, culminating in the mid-single digits by Q4 2023. Term contract talk for 2024 is centered around the 7cpg levels for full propane, with levels below that expected if butane is included. LatAm demand also remains limited despite the counter-seasonal requirements which might normally emerge by now. Targa Resources is preparing to engage the final sequences of its refrigeration expansion at Galena Park, and as such there are not expected to be any additional spot avails will be available through August. On completion the expansion will allow for an additional monthly pair of full-propane VLGCs. NWE The TOT window continues to be active with a matching bid and offer, and while these have not come together the physical premium is assessed at July CIF ARA +$3/MT. July imports to Europe are looking thin with the majority of US spot cargoes heading East. Which is one of the reasons the continuous bidding in the window has not been hit. Though European values will soon need to start competing with the strong East netback before the market becomes thin in the second half of July. Propane values dropped more rapidly than Naphtha pushing the July Propane vs Naphtha spread to $109/MT. Butane remains the most favourable feedstock as outright value is $120.5/MT below that of Propane.

**MED**

 Sonatrach will post July SP prices on Friday, June prices of $405/MT on both propane and butane is likely to decrease as Saudi CP was set low at $400/MT propane and $375/MT butane for July. In the Med propane value have not come off significantly although butane is likely to be set low.
