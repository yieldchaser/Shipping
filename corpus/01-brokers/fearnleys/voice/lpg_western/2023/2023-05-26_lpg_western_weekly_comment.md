## Market Commentary

NWE TOT window saw some activity with a 1st decade June offer which equated to CIF ARA +$2.75/MT. Early June buying looked to be almost done in the region and the offer level was seen as more hopeful than actual market level. The main issue is that the petrochemical buyers are well supplied during that timeframe and retail buyers are adding minimal demand. The June propane naphtha spread is $175/MT, and continues to improve through propane’s weakness, so petchem buying will remain at a maximum. The East vs West spread of $85/MT is side so US spot cargoes will head East. Butane remains assessed at 75% naphtha with little buying interest.

**MED**

 The region had seen a rise in butane buying interest early week due to naphtha’s strength propping up butane values. With crude oil values off today discussions are likely to stall until these settle. US Mont Belvieu propane prices declined overnight on the weight of rapidly growing inventory levels, which the EIA yesterday reported to have reached 66.7mmbbls as of 19 May, 2023, or 144 and 151% more than the third-week of May 2022 and 2021, respectively. The 3mmbbl build this week is the second largest since 2019 and well ahead of the 1.4mmbbl 5-year average. In short, stock levels of propane are increasingly rapidly, which is then having a predictable impact on the pricing structure. That said, the value of propane at Mont Belvieu has been – with a couple of notable exceptions - in decline since the start of 2022, loosely mirroring (but modestly outpacing) the softening crude oil market. The propane-to-crude ratio has been descending all the while and has been hovering on either side of 37%, which is below the 2023 YTD average of 43%, and well off the 54% average established since Jan 2021. In order to incentivize exports, propane prices will need to continue the existing downward trend, and thus the ratio to crude will continue to decline, perhaps to unprecedented levels. With regards terminal fees, offers are on the table in June in the low 5cpg range, with July being offered in the mid-5cpg range. August offers currently exist at between 5.50 to 6.00cpg
