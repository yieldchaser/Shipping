## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Mont Belvieu propane market levels are in retreat after the EIA inventory data for the week ending 15 Sep showed a 1.6mmbbl build which pushed overall stocks to 100.7mmbbls – which was entirely expected, but nonetheless is an important milestone, if only from a psychological perspective. In any case, there are a couple of weeks remaining in the traditional propane inventory build season and it is pretty widely anticipated because of the high production levels and simultaneously reduced export levels that stocks will in the next couple of weeks broach the 105mmbbl level. As such, the pricing structure – which has lately been aligned with the strength of the crude markets – has reversed course and is now back in the low/mid 71cts/gal range. Terminal fees, meanwhile, appear to have crested (at least for the moment) as offers for 1H Nov are presently seen in the 11-12cpg range and buy-side interest exists in the 9-10cpg range. Shipping remains the stumbling point, however, as delays (and therefore costs) associated with the Panama Canal have forced a number of shipowners to transit via the Cape of Good Hope and/or Suez Canal. Those routes require additional sailing days which, thus, further exacerbates shipping avails. NWE The TOT window has been quiet since a 9-13 October offer pushed assessed physical values to -$11/MT to October CIF ARA. US tons continue to head to the region even though petrochemical demand remains low, tipping the market to long. The October East/West spread $116/MT will continue to see US spot cargoes head East. Many spot VLGC fixtures not offering West options to lock in sky high freight rates on longer voyages. Which could make it difficult for European buyers to attract cargoes to the region if demand rises. Butane remains at 88% September Naphtha, though trading has been quiet as crude oil & naphtha price swings have made it difficult to pin down a northwest European butane value.

**MED**

 The region continues to import large volumes of LPG, with 110,000MT set to arrive in the region in early October. Turkish demand has stayed strong and mixed ratio VLGC demand continues.
