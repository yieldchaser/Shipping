## Market Commentary

AMERICAS In a rather classic case of the market becoming “overdone”, spot terminal fees for 1H October rocketed upwards in late August and early September, reaching heights not seen for some years as a couple of transactions were noted above 15cpg. Asking levels went even higher but the headwinds that accumulated alongside such levels became too great – particularly in the face of rising freight costs and delays associated with the lack of rain at the Panama Canal. As such terminal fees for 2H have declined into what is now deemed to be the low ‘teens’ range. The timing of shipping (and availability), though, remains a concern. Meanwhile, 1H Nov is presently offered at 11cpg and bid in the 10s – still rather high from a historical perspective, but generally supported by the Eastern arb. All the while the EIA reported on Wed that propane stock levels as of 08 Sep increased by 3.1mmbbls to 99.1mmbbls – well above the 5-year high point of 97.380mmbbls, 127% of last year, and 140% of two years ago. There exists at this point no reason to believe that overall US propane inventory levels won’t reach 105mmbbls by the end of the month, and well positioning the market for what is increasingly expected to be a colder-than-average winter. Otherwise, the persistent high heat in the US South and Midwest regions may have finally begun to abate, but nonetheless the production and transportation systems across the nation continue to struggle, perhaps most notably a recent power outage which caused several days lifting delays at one of the terminals. Current estimates are calling for a full roster within the month of September, though. NWE A TOT window deal was done between BP and ENI as BP raised their 3-8 October buying idea to a fixed price of $588/MT where it was booked. 26-30 September TOT cargo continued to be offered by ENI which pushed end September physical premium down $0.50/MT to September CIF ARA +$4/MT. September Northwest European import volumes look set to be above 450,000MT. It is not yet know if petchem demand will rise in October as some plants are set to go on maintenance while others come back online. Butane demand is starting to see the seasonal demand increase and although values are stable at 83.5% Naphtha outright value has increased with rising naphtha and crude oil.

**MED**

 The full effects of the devastating earthquake in Morocco and floods in Libya are not yet known. Mediterranean imports have been steady at a large volume of over 400,000MT for the past 11months. Flat price gains continue in the region due to rising crude.
