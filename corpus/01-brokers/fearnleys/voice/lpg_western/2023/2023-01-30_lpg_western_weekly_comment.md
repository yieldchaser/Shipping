## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The domestic U.S. market seems to have come to the realization that at nearly 150% of last year’s end-Jan propane stock level, 73mmbbls as of 27 Jan is simply too much inventory. As such, propane prices at Mont Belvieu have declined by more than a nickel over the course of this week and appear poised for further losses. It is noteworthy that crude values have also declined over the period, but talk of summertime brine containment issues at Mont Belvieu has now also been raised. Fundamentally, the declining PDH margins in the East are also at the root of the issue, as buyers there returned from holiday and found that a large number of cargoes are scheduled to arrive while – at the same time – downstream petchem margins in the East have been shrinking. Meanwhile, spot FOB Gulf Coast terminal fees – recently touted in the mid-teens – have simultaneously been softening in the wake of recently robust freight values. Asking levels last week were in the 12cpg range (some notably higher, but also attached to a freight component), but then 11cpg was seen, followed by 10cpg, and now – most recently – talk of 9cpg has been circulating, all for March. There was this week a rather impromptu spot FOB tender conducted for late-Feb which was reportedly fixed in the low/mid teens. LatAm demand continues sporadically, though a recent two-cargo propane tender was confirmed as not awarded. Whether any additional requirements will surface publicly remains to be seen NWE The TOT window has been very active this week, with some Monday night madness as physical window bids reached February CIF ARA +$47.5pmt. Since global propane values have seen a rapid decline in value, and the TOT window has seen a $30pmt drop to February CIF ARA +$17.5pmt. CIF ARA had been keeping up with FEI Market values, and as soon as a sign of weakness was seen, it followed suit. This has shifted the propane naphtha spread in the region from a meagre minus $30pmt to minus $101pmt and widening. Four VLGCs are heading to North West Europe from the US by mid-February, which has calmed the market. Butane has been relatively quiet compared with the propane market and values lie at 90% February Naphtha.

**MED**

 A 4KT butane offer FOB Algeciras failed to find interest, and leaves butane in the region at $800MT. indicating low demand from Morocco, the obvious home for the cargo.
