## Market Commentary

AMERICAS At the cusp of Nov and Dec the prompt MB propane market took a sharp step upwards, having risen from the mid 60cpg range to over 71cpg in just a couple of days. This is attributed to a couple of things, but most prominently to investor short positions which were in need of being covered. By now the market has begun to subside and has retreated back to the 67-68cpg range after those positions were covered. Meanwhile, though, LPG market sentiment continues to feel cautious, and many regular participants remain in retreat on the sidelines. Ongoing delays (and costs) associated with the lack of rain in Panama persist and do not appear to be abating soon. Crude oil values have seen losses of more than $20/bbl since the start of Q4 which, when coupled with the recently rising propane values have given cause for the propane/WTI spread to rise to above 40%. Looking at the Jan cargo roster, there are presently believed to have been two cancellations, with two more which are pending decision. Terminal fees have softened and for 2H Jan are notionally in the 5-6cpg range NWE The TOT window was not bid for the first time on over a month, reducing the assessed premium for TOT deliveries from a $6/MT to a $3/MT premium to December CIF ARA. VLGC have been transiting the Panama Canal with more ease in December so far compared with previous months, bringing the January East West spread down to +$154/MT. Butane has risen to 95.5% to December Naphtha through strong buyside interest, and remains attractive after the large drop in crude oil and naphtha values seen Wednesday. The butane rise coupled with fewer aggressive offers from transiting VLGCs has seen European netback values improve. With US Gulf FOB cargo values assessed between 5-6cpg the arb to Europe is open for both propane and butane. On paper the European market is an attractive home for US tons, though the low petchem demand and slow rise in winter demand continues to make it a challenge to place tons in the region.

**MED**

 Crude oil price volatility saw trade discussions take a back seat. Fewer forecast LPG transits through the Suez Canal helped support values in the region while seasonal demand is beginning to support values in the region.
