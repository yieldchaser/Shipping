## Market Commentary

US After having been fixed in the upper 4cpg range (twice), a low 5cpg transaction was recorded – all for Jun 4:0 parcels – the FOB USGC terminalling fee market seems to be very slowly edging upwards. Calculated netbacks as of this writing are in the 6cpg range, though trade is thin as the number of offers vastly exceeds willing buyers. That, combined with an undercurrent of strength in the near-term freight market may be keeping a lid on things, though. The EIA this week revealed a surprisingly large propane inventory build – one that was much needed, as it has been hoped that a wider arb could help activity levels improve. As of 30th May stock levels increased by 6.8mmbbls to an overall total of 61.90mmbbls. This is on the heels of record-level production levels of 2.897mmbbls and a soft weekly export level of just 1.231mmbbls/day (against a maximum of 2.335mmbbls/day). This, in turn, is likely the result the reshuffling that came about on the news of tariffs, which when announced gave cause for a host of lifters to swap from 4:0 parcels to 2;2 cargoes in their attempts to deliver to non-Chinese destinations.
