## Market Commentary

US: The EIA this week recorded a 2.2mmbbl primary propane inventory gain which brought overall stocks to 50.40mmbbls, or just over 81% of the same time last year. Current calculations, which are based on 5yr average weekly builds/draws, project that propane inventories will peak in late October in the 87-88mmbbl range – or just below the 90mmbbl average level for the 49th Week of the year. Aside from potential weather-related production disruptions over the pending summer there is little impeding the lofty production rates in the U.S., which at more than 2.83mmbbls/day as of this week’s EIA report continues to push up against the 2.85mmbbl/day maximum. Export volumes, meanwhile, are presently departing at 2.2mmbbls/day (against a maximum recorded rate of 2.335mmbbls/day). Spot FOB USGC terminal fees are pretty squarely established at 6cpg, as a small handful of activity has been recorded at just above and below that level for June. The tariff relief – temporary though it may be – brought freight back to life which is thus keeping a lid on spot terminal rates.
