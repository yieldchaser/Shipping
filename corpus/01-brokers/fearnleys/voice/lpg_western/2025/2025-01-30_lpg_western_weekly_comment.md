## Market Commentary

US Over the month of January 2025 U.S. Gulf Coast FOB terminal fees have declined from the mid teens to single digits, only to surge back to the low double-digits and then – as of this writing – land at just over 10cts/gal for March FOB propane cargoes. The volatility within the Mont Belvieu propane market is not to be overlooked as the primary factor in this, as extreme cold weather pushed a generally soft market from the mid 80cts/gal range in early Jan to over 95cts/gal, only then to retreat to the upper 80s as of the close of the market this week. Furthermore, the strength of the WTI market enhanced the volatility as crude values this month have moved from a starting point of ~$73/bbl to over $80/bbl and then back to its current level in the mid $72/bbl range. During all of this time the market began looking towards U.S. stock level data for market direction, and for the week ending 24 Jan predictions generally ranged from a draw of 4 to 5mmbbls. When the EIA reported a surprise decline of 7.9mmbbls, the market was again taken aback and MB levels rose by 2cts/gal on the day. Volatility is key – but with the weather now beginning to subside, a softer MB market is anticipated, particularly since the C3/WTI ratio remains above 50%.
