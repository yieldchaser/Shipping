## Market Commentary

US Asking levels for FOB terminal fees took a sharp turn to the upside over the past week or so; Where there was plenty of talk of the upper single digits early on, the tide has turned, and sellers are offering April FOB in the low teens – which is supported by a late-March transaction at 12cpg for a 4:0. The arb / netback, though, doesn’t necessarily seem to support those levels but sentiment seems to have overtaken. Meanwhile, the EIA yesterday reported its findings for the week ending Feb 24 and revealed a lower-than-anticipated draw of 3.8mmbbls, bringing overall US stock levels to 51.50mmbbls, which is almost exactly the same as last year, but 85% of two years ago. On a Days of Supply basis there are 34 days of inventory, where at the same time last year there were 40 days, and in 2023 there were 44 Days of Supply. This may be – in part – contributing to the relative strength of propane values at Mont Belvieu. At more than 88cpg propane is pegged at around 55% of crude, which has bounced around since the start of the year from a high of over $80/bbl to a current value at below $70/bbl.
