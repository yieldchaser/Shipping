## Market Commentary

US With only a couple of recent transactions on which to base a terminal fee price assessment, it is clear – despite calculated netbacks - that the Gulf Coast market ranges from 5.50-6.00cpg for September. October values are likely just on the higher end of that range and are seen at 6.00cpg and above. Aside from those few deals, very little is occurring as holidays, vacations, and events have many regular market participants away from their desks. As for the EIA statistics, the 2.6mmbbl build was a surprise to many, but in any case an overall total of 91.20mmbbls now resides in primary storages, which is 99% of the same time last year. Production levels in the U.S. remain entirely robust at 2.851mmbbls/day – 98% of the historical maximum level. Export volumes at 1.719mmbbls/day continue at just below the 2025 YTD average of 1.835mmbbls/day, but are expected to increase as the newly installed Flex capacity has by now been engaged.
