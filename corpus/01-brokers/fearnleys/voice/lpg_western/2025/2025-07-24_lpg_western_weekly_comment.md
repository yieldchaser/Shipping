## Market Commentary

US: The freight market has adeptly captured the widened US/Asia arb and simultaneously managed to retain much of its strength, even in the face of its narrowing. This has kept US spot FOB terminal fees in the 5.00-5.00cpg range for August, though trade has been thin. While the Sep market has yet to fully develop, asking-levels at present are mildly higher and in the 6cpg range. The EIA yesterday released its propane inventory findings and its data surprised the market with a half-million barrel decline for an overall US stock level of 82.30mmbbls – still within striking distance of the 100mmbbl mark at the end of Oct, but nonetheless revealing the strength of the US export market. Where the highest export month is pegged at 2.335mmbbls/day, the EIA reported that last week’s outbound volumes totaled 2.095mmbbls/day. Conversely, production levels have not reacted to waning crude prices and at 2.871mmbbls/day is just off the high-water mark of 2.897mmbbls/day.
