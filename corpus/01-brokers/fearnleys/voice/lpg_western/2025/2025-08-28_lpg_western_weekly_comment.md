## Market Commentary

US Despite generally softening WTI values, and despite the healthy propane inventory builds that have been seen in recent weeks – those which have bolstered stock levels in the US to an overall total of 93mmbbls – MB propane values continue to firm, perhaps on the heels of gains that have most recently been achieved in the Asian markets or, perhaps, simply an end-of-the-month spike in the pricing structure. The EIA data shows that overall US production levels remain strong at 2.835mmbbls/day, where conversely the export volumes were sub-average (YTD 2025) at 1.732mmbbls/day. That combination – if it continues – should allow propane stocks to reach beyond 100mmbbls by the conclusion of the traditional ‘build’ season. Terminal fees for spot FOB USGC transactions have subsided from the upper 5s cts/gal range to not much more than 5cts/gal at present.
