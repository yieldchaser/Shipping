## Market Commentary

US The market seems to be settling – for the moment – but uncertainty still reigns with regards to the implementation of trade tariffs. There does exist hope that the US and China can come to some kind of agreement on both ethane and LPGs since those commodities have become so crucial for both parties; in 2024 LPG was the second-largest value commodity shipped from the US to China and it is presently being anticipated that the issues at hand may be ironed out sooner than later. Meanwhile, the EIA recorded a very welcomed propane inventory build of 2.2mmbbls, where a more modest increase of ~.500mmbbls was generally anticipated. This brought overall stocks to 46.60mmbbls, or 82% of last year. Based on 5-yr average weekly build rates, it is calculated that US propane stocks will peak at below 90mmbbls at the end of October. This is based on domestic production levels (which should likely remain robust, save for a nosedive in crude values), and export levels (which are poised to rise in the next 60 days or so as Energy Transfer’s expansion is enabled). As for Terminal Fees, the market now for May 4:0 cargoes is likely on either side of 6cpg, with June perhaps just a bit higher. Very little is occurring, though, as participants seem content to wait as they seek greater clarity on the tariff(s) issue.
