## Market Commentary

US Mont Belvieu propane prices remain stubbornly robust, as the close on Wednesday afternoon was marked at 83.25cts/gal ($434/mt). Crude, meanwhile, rose only 20cts/bbl on the day – but at the conclusion of winter it might have been anticipated that propane values would be softening by now. The EIA data for this week, however, showed a slightly higher-than-expected draw (of 2mmbbls, versus 1.0-1.5mmbbls anticipated), which then gave cause for the 2ct/gal gain on the day. The EIA data revealed an overall total of 43.30mmbbls, which is 83% of last year. Production figures, though, were solid at 2.715mmbbls/day, the seventh consecutive week of growth which is now outpacing the YTD average of 2.640mmbbls/day. Exports, too, were strong, and at 2.194mmbbls/day are a bit more than 300,000bbls/day above the YTD average of 1.865mmbbls/day. As to the market itself, the draw recorded yesterday was one component, but rising freight rates are having an impact, as well. Recent fixtures at $105, $106 and (most recently) $110 for the Hou/Chi route have caused terminalling fees to suffer, and where the market earlier this week was on either side of 11cts/gal, a deal was fixed on Wednesday at 10cts/gal but at present bids are in the 8 (plus) cpg range, indicating that perhaps a 9cpg deal might be on the horizon.
