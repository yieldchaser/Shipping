## Market Commentary

Despite sustained period of healthy earnings in the tanker chartering markets, it seems the upcoming seasonal holidays are gradually slowing down potential S&P interests. The firm spot markets continue to support Sellers quest for premiums over last dones in order to conclude any potential sales. Saying that, a couple of Buyers are willing to go and meet sellers expectations in order to secure prompt spot tonnage, particularly on the region 10 year and older vintages. This is well illustrated by the sale of the Zodiac controlled JUPITER SUN (ABT 115K DWT SASEBO 2007) which was sold at USD 28.7m. This is the second vessel sold by Zodiac to this buyer, worth noting that the Jupiter Sun is scrubber-fitted hence the premium against the same age sistership Virgo Sun which was sold last month. Regardless both levels represent a significant jump from last concluded transactions. With signs of ongoing negotiations for tanker units of similar age we expect a number of Buyers to take positions before year end. The majority of eyes are now however, focused on 2020, patiently waiting to assess the actual impact that the new regulatory constrains will have on the markets. New revised benchmarks are being set in the Dry SnP markets with three deals being done across segments. The Capesize GOTIA (ABT 178K DWT SHANGHAI WAIGAOQIAO 2012) was sold at Usd 23m, the Kamsarmax MENARO (ABT 81K DWT JIANGSU JINLING 2016) achieved Usd 22.75m while the Dolphin 57’ design Supramax BAOJI (ABT 56K DWT XIAMEN 2013 C4x36 T) was sold at Usd 11.6m. These levels illustrate particularly well the subdued sentiment we have been witnessing from potential Bulker Buyers which, as indicated by the abovementioned sales, has driven values down by 5 to 10% since the summer. This negative trend can also be demonstrated by the lack of Japanese built vessels changing hands with Sellers preferring to withdraw their units rather than following this downward direction.
