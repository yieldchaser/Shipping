## Market Commentary

Resolution of the conflict in the Middle East seems no closer despite some encouraging headlines this week. That said, there appears to be a steady supply of buyers willing to transact at current levels, while giving owners attractive exit points across the size and age curve. All the confusing ongoing diplomacy aside, values show no signs of softening with suezmax tankers this week setting new levels for mid aged ships as 15-year-old STELLA (164K DWT Hyundai 2011) sold for USD 67M. The BDI crossed the 3000-point mark for the first time since 2023 on the back of the recent run, whilst contending with perhaps a quieter week than usual due to holidays in China. There appears to still be room for even higher levels on the larger sizes, with some predicting Capesize will hit the USD 50K/day mark in the coming weeks. The majority of second-hand activity is limited as usual to the rgn 15-year-old tonnage, with the exception of 85K DWT wide beam Kamsarmax pair SEACON NOLA/HAMBURG (85K DWT Huangpu Wenchong 2023) begin sold for USD 36M each.
