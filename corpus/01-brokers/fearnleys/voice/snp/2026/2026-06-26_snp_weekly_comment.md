## Market Commentary

Tanker rates are grabbing headlines in the aftermath of the confusing yet tangible progress which has been made on opening the Strait of Hormuz. The market is offering some levels of correction to the extraordinarily high levels that we have seen in the last months, however at numbers that will satisfy owners. In the shadow of continued newbuild ordering, a quieter week in the second-hand space as buyers observe how the next phase of the Middle East saga plays out. In the specialised market, STST J19 GREENWICH PARK (19k dwt Fukuoka 2011)* SSDD due 06/26 was sold to Vietnamese buyers at USD 22.8M. Easing, though fragile, conditions in the Strait of Hormuz are a positive for the dry market. Lower and more consistent crude pricing will give confidence for the general macro demand outlook, while an open Strait will release trapped cargoes and allow countries within the Gulf to resume dry trading. Rates though appear to be looking on the toppy side, with the market understandably cautious about 2H 2026. Still, plenty of liquidity in the second hand space, most notably scrubber fitted capesize LADY DEENA (182k dwt JMU 2020) selling to Chinese buyers at USD 70M. Scrubber fitted 2020 blt capesize units have increased in value by 10% in the past year, seen against the sale of last done unit ex BULK GINZA (182k dwt Imabari 2020) which sold to GENCO at USD 64M in May 2025.
