## Market Commentary

The Sinokor machine continues to gather momentum this week as we enter a new era for the VLCC space. Such a singular concentration of vessel ownership has not been seen in the history of this market, and the South Korean outfit are showing no signs of letting up as 11 Dynacom VLCCs were confirmed this week. TC rates are quickly surpassing last done levels, from USD 90k/day to USD 100K/day reported for one year, which is justifying the historically high valuations for VLCCs. It’s not just the larger ships that are benefitting, sentiment is also engulfing most tanker segments, where sellers are increasingly confident to hold their positions. Predictably, with Lunar New Year celebrations in full swing this week, the dry market has taken a breather. It is a rosy picture otherwise across the sector, as rates are expected remain strong throughout 2026. While not having the dramatic movement of the tanker market, dry is being seen as a safe bet and provides buyers with a more accessible entry point. This week ultramax ONLY YOU (60K DWT Sanoyas 2017) was sold for USD 28.3M.
