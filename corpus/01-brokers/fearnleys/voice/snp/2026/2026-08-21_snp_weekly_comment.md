The prevailing factors are tightening lists and causing rates to surge. Incredible returns are there to be made, seen in the confidence shown from an unknown buyer rumored to have bought the VLCC HELLSTUGUTINDEN (299k dwt Universal 2003) for a sensational USD 57M. While the tanker market keeps making headlines, the dry space is bringing in consistent returns, keeping prices firm. Capesize rates have strengthened in recent weeks, which will attract buyers at both ends of the age curve. Pricing the older end of the curve, we have JIAN FA (175k dwt SWS 2004) sold this week at USD 18.5M. A quiet week, otherwise, due to holiday season.
