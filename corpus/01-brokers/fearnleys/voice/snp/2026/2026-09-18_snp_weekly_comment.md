## Market Commentary

With no real end in sight for this relentless tanker bull run, buyers are still willing to pay the ever-increasing price asks from owners. Huge premiums are being paid for prompt tonnage, with good reason, as new owners can expect to write down large portions of the investment over a small number of voyages. For that reason, already cash rich owners will need record levels to part with these immensely profitable assets. Despite plenty of ongoing VLCC negotiations at astronomical levels, the suezmax sector is writing the headlines this week with SPEEDWAY (158k dwt Hyundai 2017) selling for USD 99M. Activity in the dry sector is extensive this week despite the general firm pricing across the sector. This period could be a prudent time to sell, given the current elevated pricing and the air of uncertainty over rising oil prices and subsequent costs potentially hampering macro demand in 2027. In a usually illiquid market we are seeing new transactions with RTM DIAS/FLINDERS (89K dwt NAMURA 2013) being sold to Chinese buyers for an en bloc price of USD 42.8M.
