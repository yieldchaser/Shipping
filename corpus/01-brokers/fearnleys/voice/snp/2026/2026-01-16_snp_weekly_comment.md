## Market Commentary

The frenzied start to the year continued in the tanker market this week, not only due to the emergence of further Sinokor deals but other transactions cementing these new price levels. The Sinokor activity is impacting values across the crude space, and sellers are now more confident than ever that their price asks will be matched. For anything 5 years or older, this provides an attractive exit for owners. Scrubber fitted VLCC pair HUNTER/SERENDIPITY (299K DWT Hyundai Samho 2021) were bought by Trafigura for USD 125M each, while Scorpio continue take advantage of these favourable prices with the sale of scrubber fitted LR2 STI KINGSWAY (109K DWT Sungdong 2015) for USD 57.5M to Paragon Mobility. On the dry bulk side, the excitement seems more measured. By this we mean seeing the usual premiums on more modern ships with a long trading life ahead of them, and a busier, more opportunistic environment at the other end of the age curve. Buyers have been in hot pursuit of Newcastlemaxes over the past 6 months and have had to pay premium levels for modern ships, seen in the sale this week of scrubber fitted NORD PALLADIUM (209K DWT SWS 2021) at USD 76.25M to Zhejiang Shipping. Further to this, ultramax STARRY NIGHT (61K DWT NACKS 2022) received a firm USD 32.5M from undisclosed buyers.
