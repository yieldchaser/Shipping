## Market Commentary

A busy week in the tanker S&P markets with a continued string of older tankers changing hands. We are reporting three older VLCC’s sold at storage induced prices, however we understand the sale of GRAND LADY was concluded some time ago. A large number of older Aframax and MR tankers are for sale, but transactions taking place underpin that values for same are not seeing any gain and are best case moving sideways. Not since Euronav’s acquisition of VLCC newbuilding resales at Usd 93.5 mill in early March have we seen any modern/brand new tankers being transacted. Talk of the week has been the massive surge in the share price for NAT aka “the come-back kid”. Following an airing on CNBC a week ago by its CEO Herbjørn Hansson, a massive 110 million shares were traded on Tuesday worth about Usd 807 mill. Its market cap broke the Usd 1 bn mark but is now at Usd 880 mill. The dry S&P market is still kind of alive, albeit we have yet to see any noteworthy modern units being sold. Chinese Dolphin 57 Supramaxes see prices drop; AMAZONIT DOLPHIN 57k built 2011 Hantong invited offers earlier this week with best bid at low Usd 7 mill. This is in line with the sale of CONTI PERIDOT built 2011 Taizhou which this week achieved low Usd 7 mill in a bank controlled sale.
