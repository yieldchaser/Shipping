## Market Commentary

This week continues to see most sale and purchase activity take place in the dry market where a number of vessels have changed hands. This increased liquidity in the sector has enabled the relative downward correction that most market players expected in light of the global disruption. However the consensus seem to be that we are near the bottom in terms of values with a relatively positive market expectation for the quarters to come. Saying that values are on average down between 15 to 20% since pre-covid transactions therefore still representing an attractive play from a buying perspective. We expect to see more deals take place in the coming weeks which should provide a clear indication as to what direction this SnP market will take. No change in the tanker market where we witness very little activity with the few exceptions on the overaged front with sellers willing to transact. Past sellers of more modern units have now most definitely taken a step back unwilling to entertain discussions at today’s levels.
