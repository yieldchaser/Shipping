## Market Commentary

There is increased talks of floating storage again as crude oil prices fell region 10% this week, primarily at the front-end of the curve. This combined with Trafigura taking in 14 VLs for region 6 months TC, the last 1-2 weeks has created positive momentum and lifted rates from opex levels to Usd 20,000-25,000/day. The sale of ADS STRATUS (300,000DWT, Hitachi 2002) at Usd 25.5 mill demonstrates that market players still are able to find some middle ground in what has been a widening bid/ask spread on vintage VLs. There is a flurry of deals being done in dry, but with a steady flow of vessels coming for sale we have not seen bidding rounds taking off. Buyers are selective and not easily carried away, albeit many in numbers. We continue to see action in the Kamsarmax segment - a notch down on price from last week when corrected for DD position and scrubber - with IKAN BAWAL (83,500DWT, Sanoyas 2012) and KEY CALLA (83,500DWT Sanoyas, 2010) sold at Usd 16 mill and Usd 12.75 mill, respectively.
