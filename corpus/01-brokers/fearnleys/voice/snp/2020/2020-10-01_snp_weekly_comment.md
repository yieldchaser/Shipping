## Market Commentary

In what we understand was a deal concluded a couple of months ago, Dynacom is reported to have purchased a nine year old Japanese owned/built VLCC for Usd 45 mill. This is as such the second VLCC of this age sold at more or less same levels so we can safely conclude that values have taken another tumble. Values for vintage VLCC are moving sideways with SEAWAYS MULAN built 2002 reported gone at levels in line with last week’s sale of PANTARISTE, also built 2002. We see more and more vintage available for sale with further deals expected to materialize soon. As expected, more tanker newbuilding contracts are coming to light with Atlas Maritime this week reported to have signed for two Aframax tankers at Daehan for mid Usd 40 mill. Additional Suezmax and VLCC contracts are also being done at levels in line with expectations. This, together with dismal spot rates are certainly not lending support to any positive development for crude values. Following inspections by around 10 buyers, offers are invited on Monday for LR2 tanker CHAMPION PRINCESS built 2012 which, together with the Xihe tanker sell-off, will be the ones to watch in the near future. The increased activity out of Brazil has seen Capesize earnings skyrocketing, the BCI has more than doubled in under 3 weeks. This marks another dramatic market move in a year that has been the most volatile ever for Capes. In general, we find fundamentals to be more than sound and expect it to be so for another year and a half at least. For the smaller segments things are also moving in the right direction, albeit slower. We see demand coming back by varying degrees in all major discharge regions (Europe, Japan, Korea, India, South East Asia), whereas China remains strong notwithstanding the last month and a half’s slowdown. Fresh YTD spot market highs for Supramax are highly likely during Q4, but we are less certain of that for Kamsarmax. Vessel deliveries will fall markedly the next year and a half which, coupled with a continued economic recovery and massive stimulus measures, could make for very interesting times ahead. The renewed dry optimism is evident in the very significant number of second-hand transactions every week. Values are at a robust floor and from here any movement should be north.
