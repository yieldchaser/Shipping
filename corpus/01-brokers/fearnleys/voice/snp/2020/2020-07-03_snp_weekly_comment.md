## Market Commentary

Another week that has seen a flurry of transactions in the dry snp markets with a number of vessels changing hands across all sectors. In the Capesize segment the Japanese controlled “GRACEFUL MADONNA” (2010 Koyo 180kdwt) has been sold to clients of Eastern Pacific at Usd 20.7m with DD passed and BWTS installed. The deal is setting a well needed benchmark for Japanese built Cape units of this vintage considering that the last comparable sale dates back to February. Elsewhere the majority of units being sold remain of early to mid-2000 built tonnage with the Handysize sector seeing increased activity on the more modern side of the spectrum. On the tanker side activity remains subdued to say the least with only two sales to report.
