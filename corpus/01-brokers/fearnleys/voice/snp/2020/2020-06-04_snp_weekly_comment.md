## Market Commentary

The sense of urgency to buy tankers is waning and values are slowly correcting downwards. Two 2000 built VLCC’s have been sold for region Usd 24 mill each which is Usd 6 mill lower than the recent peak at Usd 30 mill for the same age LUCKY TRADER concluded at the end of April. However it should be noted that LUCKY TRADER had her Special Survey passed and gave prompt delivery. Values for vintage tankers will be most exposed going forward but across the board we see the bid/ask spread widening in line with falling newbuilding prices and expectations. Judging by history we are likely to enter a period of limited sales activity with a wait-and-see attitude prevailing. For bulkers we see a clear buying momentum building up and we may indeed be getting closer to a more robust floor on values which should trigger increased S&P activity. Sales volume YTD has been dominated by 10 year olds and older which is where we expect such a floor first to be established. The more modern ones should then follow suit, perhaps with another 10-15% correction downwards.
