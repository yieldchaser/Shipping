## Market Commentary

We have a strong start to the second half of the year for all dry segments and the bottom for period rates and asset values have likely passed for all segments and vintages. We now have a robust floor on values and the dry S&P activity is increasingly on fire. Kamsarmax and Supra-/Ultramax aged eight to twelve years are in particular demand. Following inspections by nine buyers, the Japanese controlled Kamsarmax KM YOKOHOMA built 2011 Sanoyas was promptly sold at region USD 15m, levels which fall in line with expectations. BW Dry’s recent deal on subjects for the sale of its three ten year old; Japanese built Kamsarmax BW ACORN, BW BARLEY and BW EINKORN failed to materialize, and they were instead quickly sold to new buyers this week. We understand Centrofin is the buyer of the latter two and Orion the buyer of BW ACORN. This vessel was in fact contracted as newbuilding by Orion and sold to BW Dry in 2018. Any movement in dry values will be north in the near to medium term and we shall soon be seeing more modern tonnage being offered for sale. It is time-out in the tanker S&P market. Values remain under pressure and there is no sense of urgency to acquire assets unless a particularly attractive deal comes along.
