## Market Commentary

A number of sales to report across all sale and purchase markets with a notable pickup in activity on the tanker front. The week began with Japanese owners inviting offers on the clean trading LR2 unit “CHAMPION PRINCESS” (Abt 105k dwt built 2012, HHI). The vessel had been inspected by up to 10 Buyers and is believed to have received up to 6 offers to finally end up in the hands of Great Eastern Shipping at a price of Usd 26.4m. The substantial buying interest can in part be explained by an extensive period of inactivity in the sector with virtually no transactions, which, coupled with a falling chartering market has lead buyers to chase a considerable correction in asset values. A correction that becomes particularly apparent when looking at the last comparable sale of the Aframax “NANYANG STAR” (Abt 105k dwt built 2012, Sumitomo) sold at Usd 29.8m in March this year. We expect further activity in the Aframax/LR2 sector with a number of vessels inviting offers over the coming days. NYK has also disposed of its VLCC “TAKAOKA” (Abt 300k dwt built 2011, Universal) which was sold at Usd 45m earlier in the week. This follows the sale of “TOKITSU MARU” (Abt 300k dwt built 2011 , Mitsubishi) which was sold from the same owners at Usd 45m last week. In the dry sector Scorpio Bulkers has sold the sisterships “SBI SOUSTA” & “SBI CONGA”(Abt 81k dwt built 2016 &2015, Hudong) for region Usd 18.5m each. This brings the tally to three vessels sold over the past two weeks and we expect the selling exercise to continue in line with the company’s strategy of shifting capital and focus to its recently announced offshore & renewable venture.
