## Market Commentary

As anticipated values in both the tanker and dry S&P markets have largely stabilised with a healthy and steady amount of ships being sold across most segments at prices falling in line with recent transactions. However a softer sentiment is certainly more evident for older tankers than for bulkers. Sales activity on the modern tanker side is negligible against a stream of modern bulk tonnage changing hands. Scorpio Bulkers continues unabated its selling spree of its modern fleet with the sale of SBI ZUMBA (81k DWT, 2016, Hudong) at Usd 20mill, which is in line with the sale of SBI Reggae (81k DWT, 2016, Hudong) last month. We also have a benchmark sale on Post-Panamax with the sale of SANGEET ( 95k DWT, 2012, Imabari) at Usd 15.5mill to Polembros. The sale illustrates the decline in values since last done, which was SUNNY SMILE (95k DWT, 2013, Imabari) sold at Usd 21.5mill to the same buyer back in the summer of 2019.
