## Market Commentary

Tanker sale and purchase activity remains subdued with most players now having taken a step back from potential acquisitions in light of weaker charter markets. Owners of modern tonnage are still hopeful in achieving yesterdays benchmark rendering the bid offer spread hard to bridge, we do however see sellers of more vintage units gradually correcting their expectations in order to dispose of their older vessels in what can still be considered a relatively strong exit environment. With an increased level of buying enquiry for dry tonnage it comes to no surprise that most transactions this week are found in the Dry SnP Markets. This pick up in liquidity is driven by the Supramax sector where four units have changed hands this week at levels that clearly reaffirm the current downward trend in values. The lack of sales of medium/large sized units is in no case reflective of the current appetite that potential buyers have for Kamsarmax and Capes, however we have yet to see enough candidates made available for sale. We do however expect to see a pick up in activity in those larger segments once momentum picks up and sellers decide to go meet these renewed bids.
