## Market Commentary

The dry S&P activity continues to pick up with a large number of vessels reported sold. Whilst older units still account for majority of sales we now have an interesting bench-mark set by the sale of the Japanese controlled Oshima Kamsarmax resale which was sold to Greek interests at $ 29m. Furthermore three 2010 Japan built Kamsarmaxes are also reported to be committed on subjects at region Usd 40 mill en-bloc. The buying momentum now shaping up leads us to conclude that the downside risk on values is limited. Buyers hoping for a re-run of 2016 values will wait in vain. Tanker values are sliding and vintage vessels remain most at risk.
