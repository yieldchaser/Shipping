## Market Commentary

Another week for the history books in the crude and clean markets with latter now also on fire with spot rates hitting all-time high levels. The S&P activity is mostly unaffected, except for older VLCC's which remain hot commodity. Four such vessels are sold this week alone of which two 2005 built at a reported Usd 38 mill each. This is approximately Usd 5 mill more that same age YUGAWASAN fetched only three weeks ago. The price for the LR1 tanker ETERNAL DILIGENCE built 2006 Onomichi is surprisingly low, but we expect it’s a function of few buyers having been able to inspect the vessel.
