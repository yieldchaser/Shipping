## Market Commentary

With much of the market convening in Oslo for Norshipping, the tanker market is excused for having a quieter week on the SNP front, that said, outlook and values remain firm. MR2 CELSIUS RIGA (46K DWT HMD 2010) affirmed the strength of this sub sector, achieving $28.5M to US based buyers. The dry market appeared to somewhat stabilise this week after recent volatility, however general sentiment remains uncertain as we approach the summer period. This could provide an opportunity for buyers to pick up some discounted tonnage in the short term, with price ideas generally softening across the sector. Elsewhere, Capesize HERUN ZHOUSHAN (181K DWT SWS 2017) sold at $42M, the first sale of a modern Capesize since March.
