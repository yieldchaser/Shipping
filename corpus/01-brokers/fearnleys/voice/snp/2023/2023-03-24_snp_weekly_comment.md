## Market Commentary

Tanker Snp markets were relatively subdued with only a handful of transactions to report this week. Older tonnage is still fetching significant premiums with the Suezmax EUROVOYAGER (159K DWT DAEWOO 2005) reported sold at USD 38M and LR2 STAR PROSPERITY (115K DWT SASEBO 2009) was sold for USD 41M. While interest remains high in the MR space, only one transaction was registered this week as OCEAN III (45K DWT BOHAI 2007) was sold for USD 21.5M. A busy week in the dry sector sees transactions taking place all across the size and age curve. Tomini splashed out on 2x scrubberfitted Newcastlemax units MP THE BRUSCHI/MP THE VRABEL (208K DWT JIANGSU NEWYANGZI 2020/2021) for a total of USD 121M. They weren’t the only ones to pick up large tonnage as Norden affirmed their commitment to Capesizes with the acquisition of AQUAHAHA/AQUATONKA (179K DWT HHIC 2012) for USD 28M each. Scrubber fitted Capesize MOUNT FABER (176K DWT NAMURA 2008) was sold at USD 22.7M, providing valuable insight to those after a potential asset play at the higher end of the age curve. Despite soaring demand in the Supramax sector, only one transaction was regsitered this week with GF ORIENT (57K DWT STX DALIAN 2011) bought by Chinese buyers for USD 13.5M.
