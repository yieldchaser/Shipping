## Market Commentary

The dry market rally continued this week as Capesize earnings saw their biggest daily jump since 2010, this was particularly prevalent in the North Atlantic market where tonnage is most scarcely available. Scrubber fitted Capesize duo CHOW/COMANCHE 181K DWT SWS 2016 are primed to take advantage of this rally, being bought by Genco for USD 41.3M each. Smaller sizes are also seeing a welcomed push in rates as a result, after a fairly uneventful Q3. This bump in rates, if sustained, will begin to justify the comparatively firm price expectations across the bulk market. This could also entice more cash rich owners looking to diversify, such as Pyxis, who bought scrubber fitted Kamsarmax PEDHOULAS CHERRY 82K DWT JIANGSU NEWYANGZI 2015 at USD 26.5M. News of OPEC+ production cuts rather dampened the festive sprit in the Tanker market as rates will likely see a dip in the short term, but in the medium to longer term result in higher ton miles. In reality, these cuts won’t drastically change the overwhelmingly positive overall tanker story. MR tankers have been making headlines this week as some units have reported TCE earnings in excess of USD 100K/day, no wonder there is a swell of interest here with a number of ongoing discussions behind the scenes, surely to set new price benchmarks once completed.
