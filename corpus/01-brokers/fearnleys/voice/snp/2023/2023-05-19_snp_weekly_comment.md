## Market Commentary

Scrubber-fitted Aframax SOUTHERN GLORY (108K DWT TSUNEISHI 2019) is the standout transaction in the wet market, purchased by Eastern Pacific for USD 66M, thus affirming value for Aframax units of this age following the recent sales of SPERCHIOS and ARISTOKLIS. Otherwise, it was the familiar tale of older tonnage achieving firm prices, often at huge premiums to the previous purchase price. No transaction represents this better than WONDER POLARIS/BELLATRIX (115K DWT SAMSUNG HI 2005/2006) selling en bloc for USD 72M, compared to their respective purchase prices of USD 13.6M (06/22) and USD 18.15 w/TC attached (12/21). In the only VLCC transaction of the week, having failed previously at USD 53M, BALITC SUNRISE (309K DWT HHI ULSAN 2005) sold to Chinese buyers at USD 51.5M. Units at the top end of the size curve are the flavour of the week in the Dry market, making up the majority of transactions. There appears to be a run on vintage Capesize units, with levels on 2004 built vessels exceeding last done on a 2006 built. VICTORIUS/OLYMPIUS (171K DWT HYUNDAI SAMHO 2004) sold en bloc for USD 17M per vessel to Dubai based buyers, which surpasses the USD 16.3M spent earlier in May on the younger C H S SPENDOR (170K DWT IHI 2006). TASIK MELATI (180K DWT KOYO 2004) sold for USD 15.5M, perhaps a slightly lower price than the VICTORIUS/OLYMPIUS due to the manner of sales process (sealed bids/outright offers). Elsewhere in the dry sector owners around the market are reporting disappointing results largely due to the lower earnings environment for the first half of 2023, adding to a widely softening sentiment.
