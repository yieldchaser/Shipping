## Market Commentary

The unfolding geopolitical turmoil in the Middle East is generating headlines and stirring concerns about contagion risks in the region. Although the events haven't yet impacted commodity pricing or shipping rates, the concerns are evident. This week’s Tanker transactions seem like an echo from last week, but this week focused on 15 year old tonnage. We’ve seen the sale of the LR1 CHEMTRANS AEGEAN (76k dwt DALIAN 2007) for $22m and the sale of the MR2 ELIZABETH M (50k dwt SLS 2007) for $22m and MR2 CONSTANCE (50k dwt SPP 2008) for $23.5m. Although the dry spot market appears to have taken a breather, the secondhand market appears to persist with the volume of transactions. We have recorded the sale of 5 Capesize units built between 2007 and 2010 with pricing pretty much flat from last week demonstrated by the sale of the Capesize AQUA BONANZA (180k dwt IMABARI 2010) sold for $23m. Other noteworthy dry sales are the Ultramax CP SHANGHAI (63k dwt CHENGXI 2015) sold for $23.5m and the Supramax XING HONG DA 8 (58k dwt YANGZHOU DAYANG 2012) sold for $12.7m.
