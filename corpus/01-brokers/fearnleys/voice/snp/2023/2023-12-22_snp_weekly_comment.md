## Market Commentary

The unfolding situation in the Red Sea, coupled with the upcoming festive break, meant the S&P market had a rather quiet week. Sellers can be forgiven, as the ramifications of the Red Sea disruption could yet dramatically change price expectations when the market returns to full capacity in the New Year. In a sub-sector that has largely struggled for liquidity in the latter half of the year, scrubber fitted Suezmax SERENEA (158K DWT SAMSUNG HI 2009) sold this week for USD 45.5M. Elsewhere, a trio of 2008-2009 blt MRs sold, helping price other units circulating in the market. Modern tonnage in the dry market has been hard to come by for most, and this week’s transactions are symptomatic of this. That said, there remains much optimism in the sector going into the New Year, with Capesize rates ticking along around USD 20k/day. The hope will be that momentum can be maintained despite the upcoming Chinese New Year celebrations in early February.
