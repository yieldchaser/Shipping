## Market Commentary

A flurry of activity in the Dry market this week, as players tie up loose ends from the winter break and execute before the inevitable slowdown in S&P activity over the Chinese New Year period. The market will be paying keen attention to the fallout from the first CNY under China’s more relaxed COVID stance, hopeful there is no U turn in policy after the festivities which pre covid represented the biggest migration of people on earth. Transactions of note include Capes AQUANAVIGATOR & AQUASALWADOR (ABT 180K DWT 2011/12 DAEHAN) at USD 25m and USD 27m respectively, widely reported to have been snapped up by Greek interests, a transaction that could indicate a floor has been reached on dry bringing additional comfort to market players that have been patiently timing their next move in the space. Elsewhere the tier II Supramax SUNRISE RAINBOW (ABT 56K DWT 2012 JIANGSU HANTONG) sold for USD 15.25m, piling pressure on the many Supramax sellers in the market. A lacklustre Tanker charter market hasn’t discouraged the S&P space, as asset values continue to rise. Those with an Ice class notation remain the key beneficiaries, shown in the USD 47.5m received for Suezmax ICE TRANSPORTER (ABT 146K DWT 2006 UNIVERSAL). VLCC COSGREAT LAKE (ABT 298K DWT 2002 NACKS) sold to Middle Eastern buyers for an eyewatering USD 39m, showing the run for older large tankers is not yet done. In the MR space TEN unloaded 6 Ice class 1A units APOLLON/ARIS/AJAX/AFRODITE/ARTEMIS/ARIADNE (ABT 53K DWT 2005 HYUNDAI MIPO) for a price in the region of USD 21m each.
