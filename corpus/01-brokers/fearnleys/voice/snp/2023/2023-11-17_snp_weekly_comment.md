## Market Commentary

Tankers have taken a breather this week as the Dry bulk segment dominates the S&P market. Headlines were stolen by Transmed sale of their AGIS (182k dwt Namura 2023) to Arcelor Mittal, what is considered a very firm $67m. Among other Capesize transactions was the sale of the FRONTIER BRILLIANCE (181k dwt Imabari 2013), which was sold for $30.6m with SS/DD due. Elsewhere, benchmarks are also being set on modern Kamsarmax front with the resale of the AP LOVRIJENAC (82k dwt Hantong 2024) to NNC for $37.62m. In the Ultramax segment, the GLORY HARVEST (63k dwt Hantong 2014) was sold for $21.5m. This sale includes a 1-year time charter to Pan Ocean, explaining the slightly higher than expected price. There have been several Handysize sales, with the most noteworthy being the sale of the APRILIA (36k dwt Jiangdong 2017) to Greek buyers for $20.2m. In the wake of last week’s numerous tanker transactions, we have recorded 3 Aframax sales supporting last week's activity. One of these was the IRIDESCENT (112k dwt New Times 2009) which was sold for $40m. Following last week's purchase of 8 LR2’s, Torm has this week sold 2xLR1’s, the TORM ESTRID and scrubberfitted TORM ISMINI (74k dwt HHI 2004) en bloc for $40m. Lastly some activity in the MR segment with the sale of the SEAWAYS LORAIN (51k dwt STX 2008) for $24.5m and the FALCON SEXTANT (50k dwt STX 2009) for $25.5m.
