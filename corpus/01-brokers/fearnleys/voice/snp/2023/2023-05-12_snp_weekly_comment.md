## Market Commentary

Recent sluggishness seen in the tanker charter market seems to have spilled into the S&P space, as the sector registered an unremarkable week. Asset values remain strong however, with Suezmax CLASSIC (159K DWT HHI 2005) transacting for USD 36.8M, more than double what Seven Islands paid for the unit back in December 2020. Aframax PS PISA (108K DWT Hudong Zhonghua 2010) was sold for USD 36.5M, issues with the vessel condition explaining the lower than market price on the unit. With an enticing rates environment, vintage MRs continue to be in high demand, MR SYMPHONY (46K DWT STX 2004) fetching just under USD 18M. Tanker owners across the size curve are likely asking themselves the same question; how far can this market go? The dry market remains a target for most market players, seen in the competitiveness for the Capesize DENSA COBRA (180K DWT STX 2011) which had a rumoured 6-7 buyers. This, however, did not prevent the sale from failing, with the unit being circulated around the market once again. There seems to be a run on older Capesize units, seen in TASIK MELATI (180K DWT KOYO 2004) taking offers this week and attracting levels of xs USD 16M, which is well over the last done for a ship of this vintage (C H S Splendor). In the even more competitive Ultramax space, scrubber fitted VOKARIA (63K DWT COSCO YANGZHOU 2020) sold for USD 32M.
