## Market Commentary

The roaring tanker S&P market has turned to a whisper as buyers take a backseat amid feelings of toppy pricing, and a general lull in activity. That said, there is still a trickle of transactions to report this week, concentrated in the MR sector. DONG-A TRITON 50K DWT HMD 2015 sold for USD 38.5M, levels which will not excite other sellers of similarly aged tonnage, who have been marketing their units with expectations of xs USD 40M in mind. Of course, owners will not be too phased, with earnings still healthy in this part of the size curve. Sellers in the dry market will not be hurried by the increased level of enquiry, despite the dwindling earnings environment. A shared positive outlook for end Q3/Q4 by owners means that lowball offers are being duly ignored. Mostly older ships changing hands this week with NYK shedding capesize OCEAN COBALT 180K DWT IMABARI 2008 for USD 19.4M, and Lomar picking up JOY 79K DWT JINHAI HI 2011 for USD 14M.
