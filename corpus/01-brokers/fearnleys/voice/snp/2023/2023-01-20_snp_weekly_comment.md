## Market Commentary

The beginning of Chinese New Year looks to prolong the Tanker charter market’s subdued sentiment, while activity on S&P remains high. No changes from the theme of the last few quarters as ice class units and older large tankers remain the darlings of the S&P market, seen in Ice class 1A Aframax SEAMAGIC (116K DWT 2007 HHI) sold for a hearty USD 47M, and scrubber fitted VLCC FPMC C HONOR (298K DWT 2008 UNIVERSAL) changing hands for USD 55M. In further Aframax activity, KYTHIRA WARRIOR (115K DWT 2006 SANOYAS) and KRITI GALAXY (110K DWT 2006 MITSUI) sold for USD 38M each, showing that even non-Ice class tonnage can benefit from this rising tide. The Dry market too will be impacted by CNY festivities, but this did not stop Pacific Basin from dominating the headlines this week as they spent USD 125M on 6x Ultrabulk units (4x Japanese blt Ultramaxes, 1x Tsuneishi Cebu Supramax and 1x Imabari blt Handysize). Taking advantage of the recent value correction in c.10 yo Ultra/Supramax units, they have taken position at a time where others are still sat debating whether the bottom of the market has been reached or not. Elsewhere, Greek buyers have seen opportunity in PPMAX AM LIBERIA ( 98K DWT 2013 TSUNEISHI ZHOUSHAN), swooping in at USD 20.50M.
