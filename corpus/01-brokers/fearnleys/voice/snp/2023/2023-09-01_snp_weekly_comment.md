## Market Commentary

A slow week across the sale and purchase market sees only one sale reported in the tanker sector with CAPT THANASIS 40K DWT HMD 2004 selling at just under USD 18M. Across the larger units, despite much of the market returning from holidays, S&P discussions are yet to regain momentum given a limp sentiment in the spot market. The dry market is similarly quiet with only three sales to report. The most notable being NORD PACIFIC 61K DWT SHIN KURUSHIMA 2018 selling for USD 27.15M. The last transaction of a 2018 blt Japanese unit was in July when KK PROGRESSION 64K TSUNEISHI 2018 achieved USD 28.5M which reaffirms the gradual softening in values for these younger Japanese built units.
