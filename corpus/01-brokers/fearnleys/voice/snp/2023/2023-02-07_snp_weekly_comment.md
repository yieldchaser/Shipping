## Market Commentary

The ageing tanker selloff continued this week with MARAN AQUARIUS (320K DWT 2005 DSME) and SEAHERO (306K DWT 2006 DSME) each fetching over USD 50M. It is easy to see why, when ships of this size and age have almost doubled in value over the last eighteen months. Clear premiums for Ice class units are still very visible in the market with the sale of scrubber-fitted Ice 1A LORELEI (73K DWT 2007 STX JINHAE) for USD 33.5M, compared to both HAFNIA COLUMBIA/KRONBORG (76K/74K DWT 2007 DALIAN/NEW CENTURY) each selling for USD 22M. Meanwhile in the dry market, timing appears to be key as market players look to position themselves as values seem to be bottoming out. China’s post CNY recovery has been slower than usual, in part to people enjoying their first in person CNY since the pandemic began, but also due to decreased liquidity. This will likely be remedied by additional stimulus in March, which could lead buyers into action. In the highly competitive Japanese Ultramax space, scrubber-fitted NORD POTOMAC (63K DWT I-S SHIPYARD 2016) sold for USD 27.75M setting well needed benchmark in the segment with the most buying enquiries. Over in the Supramax sector, MILOS (56K DWT 2010 COSCO Zhoushan) sold in the high 13s, and Navios unloaded the SERENITAS N (56K DWT 2011 YANGZHOU GUOYU) for USD 12.5m.
