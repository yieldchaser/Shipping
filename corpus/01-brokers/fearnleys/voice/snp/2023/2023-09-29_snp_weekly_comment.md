## Market Commentary

The standout transaction in the Tanker market is CRUDESUN/CRUDEMED (115K DWT DAEHAN 2018) sold for USD 70M each. We understand the vessels are fixed to Unipec for 3 years at USD 40K/day. A further transaction in the Aframax/LR2 sector is PS VENEZIA (108K DWT HUDONG ZHONGHUA 2010) at USD 37.5M (DD Due) to Vietnamese buyers Fgas, notably fetching a lower-than-expected price in what is quite an in-demand part of the Afra/LR2 age curve, even with surveys due. Elsewhere SHANDONG ZIHE (40K DWT SHINA 2004) sold via online bidding platform at USD 14.45M. Volume in the dry sector remains high, with sales across the size curve this week. This comes despite murmurs in the market of a possible slight further softening in the space before the anticipated recovery, only time will tell when we hit the bottom of this dry cycle. JP Morgan acquired 3x large handysize vessels FENGNING/FUNING/FOOCHOW (39K DWT CHENGXI 2015) for USD 19.5M each, around the same levels as last done on a 2015 unit (CIELO DI VALPARAISO) at USD 20M in March 2023. Offers were due this week on Capesize SUIGO (174K DWT NAMURA 2011), and she was eventually sold at USD 23.5M to Greek buyers.
