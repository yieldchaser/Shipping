## Market Commentary

Despite a lack of headline grabbing transactions this week, the tanker market fundamentals remain consistent. Spot market players would likely disagree, as sentiment wobbled on the back of Chinese New Year festivities. Ice class 1C Aframax MARATHON (105K DWT 2005 HHI) sold for USD 32M, with premiums for ice class tonnage still being realised by sellers. In keeping with the norm of the last few months, Aframax LILA FUJAIRAH (113K DWT 2007 DSME) was bought by Middle Eastern buyers for USD 37M. Navios made for the bulk of the story in the dry market, shedding three of their older units; Panamax NAVIOS LIBERTAS/PROSPERITY I (75K DWT 2007 STX JINHAE) and Supramax NAVIOS AMARYLLIS (58K DWT 2008 TSUNEISHI ZHOUSHAN). The sale of Tess 58 BONITA (58K DWT 2008 TSUNEISHI ZHOUSHAN) at USD 15.8M shows that despite widely reported hammering of values, there is still money in well regarded yards and designs. As the Dry purchase enquiries continue to mount up, some owners are revaluating their selling positions and putting previously circulated candidates on the backburner, holding out for brighter days ahead.
