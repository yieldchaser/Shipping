## Market Commentary

Following last weeks momentum the tanker charter markets levelled as global financial and oil markets were shaken on news of potential banking crisis. In the S&P space, older units continue to receive healthy premiums with VLCCs KASSOS I (319K DWT 2007 HYUNDAI SAMHO) commanding USD 61M and VLCC YUFUSAN (311K DWT 2005 MITSUI) selling for USD 52.3M. Offers were invited on SUEZMAX LILA HONG KONG (159K DWT 2003 BOHAI), eventually seeing Chinese buyers secure the unit for USD 31M. Interest remains high in the MR sector, with rates displaying significant volatility particularly in the Atlantic. Only one transaction though was registered in this sector this week, with MRK Shipmanagement picking up SAFESEA NEHA III (46K DWT 2003 SHIN KURUSHIMA) for USD 13.75M. Increased activity in the dry S&P market is providing is providing little relief for the ever-swelling number of purchase enquiries in the sector. Seen as the next potential asset play, and an outlet for tanker owners’ increased cash supplies, the demand for dry ships is beginning to push asset values perhaps north of where recent market returns would usually denote. In the headline size category, CAPESIZE pair GOLDEN FENG/SHUI (169K DWT 2009 DAEHAN) were snapped up for USD 23M each by Greek buyers. Elsewhere, another Chinese Ultra sale in the form of ULTRAMAX SOHO PRINCIPAL (63K DWT 2016 YANGZHOU DAYANG), going for USD 26.5M.
