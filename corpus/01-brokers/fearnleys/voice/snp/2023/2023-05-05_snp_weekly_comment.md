## Market Commentary

Tanker values showing no signs of abating. Another week with pricey Aframax sales; WONDER AVIOR (108K DWT 2004 HHI) achieved USD 30.1m and the one year younger and scrubber-fitted SONGA CORAL (107k 2005 KOYO) is reported sold for USD 35m. To put this into perspective, WONDER AVIOR was purchased by the Sellers in May 2021 for just below USD 10m. Expecting further activity in the sector with the modern scrubber fitted Aframax SOUTHERN GLORY 2019 Tsuneishi seeing a number of buyers getting outright in view of the upcoming offers which are invited on 10th May. Dry values are moving sideways as seen by the sale of Capesize DENSA COBRA 2011 STX at USD 27.7 which, adjusted for yard, falls is line with the sale in mid-April of STELLA ADA 2011 Dalian at USD 25.6 mill. Japanese owners remain notably absent from the sales lists.
