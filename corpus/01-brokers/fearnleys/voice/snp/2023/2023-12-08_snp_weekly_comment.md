## Market Commentary

The Dry market, despite the recent uptick in earnings, still lacks liquidity. Market players will need to see this earnings surge sustained for at least a few months before we see any significant changes in buying impetus, and with Chinese New Year swiftly following the Christmas holidays, this seems unlikely. The few sales that are reported this week are useful in the sense they help benchmark a number of candidates on the market, such as Supramax ERMIONE (56K DWT IHI 2008) sold by Byzantine for USD 14M, and handy VULLY (35K DWT Shinan 2011) selling for USD 13.2M. VLCCs are making the most noise on the tanker front, with three sales reported this week. Scrubber fitted VLCC ATHENIAN FREEDOM (299K DWT HHI 2013) was bought by Greek interests at USD 73.5M, however it is the behind the scenes candidates that have been igniting conversations at festive industry get togethers. One thing is for sure, should the modern candidates being discussed transact at their rumoured levels, the modern VLCC market will witness a significant jump in values. At the other end of the age curve, HARAD (303K DWT Samsung 2001) sold for USD 30M to Chinese buyers, still well above scrap levels and a staggering delta of USD c.100M to current newbuild price ideas. Elsewhere, Greek interests picked up LR1 LEON APOLLON (75K DWT HMD 2009) for USD 28.5M.
