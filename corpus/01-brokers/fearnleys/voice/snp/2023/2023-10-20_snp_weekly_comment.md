## Market Commentary

Much of the market has been left scratching their heads as LR1s AESOP/SIENA (74K DWT 2012 HMD) were purchased by Naftomar for a very firm price of USD 41.5M each. Details are yet to emerge on the transaction, so pricing on existing market candidates of that age remain unchanged for now. With the ongoing situation in the Middle East on a knife edge, it would appear that tanker sentiment will remain firm for some time. NAT purchased Suezmax CHEROKEE (158K DWT 2016 HYUNDAI SAMHO) for USD 72M from CM Lemos, the firm price highlighting the relative lack of available candidates in the market. Liquidity continues to be a feature of the dry market, as market players jostle for position. Despite a recent uptick, the market is still relatively low in terms of asset values, so there is much opportunity for those looking to divest into Dry. In the rather traditional pattern for this stage of the dry cycle, a number of Japanese ships went into Greek buyers – ROYAL FUKUYAMA (82K DWT 2013 TSUNEISHI) was sold for USD 23M, and Ultramax SANTA FRANCESCA (61K DWT 2016 SHIN KASADO) sold for USD 26.8M. Elsewhere, scrubber-fitted Capesize CYMONA IRON (176K DWT 2011 SWS) was sold for USD 26M.
