## Market Commentary

Asset players are driving a surge in genuine buying interest across the Dry market. Where perhaps purchase enquiries over the last few months seemed like more of an information gathering exercise, buyers are now ready to capitalise on the recent market price correction. Despite this surge in buying interest, many subsectors are still facing weakened returns at least in the short term, thus justifying lower price levels. Capesize continues to dominate in terms of liquidity with 5 sales reported this week. Of those, IANTHE (180K DWT DSME 2009) sold for USD 21M while YUAN FU STAR (176K RONGSHENG 2011) sold to Middle Eastern buyers at USD 22.3M. Elsewhere lower down the size curve, ongoing deals are the talk of the market, likely to have significant impact on prices once completed. Discussions on Tankers at the upper end of the size curve appear to be resuming after the summer hiatus, however, at levels lower than initially proposed. That said, general outlook across the sector remains bullish especially as the winter months approach. Activity was restricted to the smaller sizes this week with Union selling MR BRUNSWICK (45K DWT SHIN KURUSHIMA 2010) to PV TRANS for USD 24M, and Maritime Asset Partners selling Handy Tanker ACAMAR (37K DWT HMD 2011) for USD 23.4M.
