## Market Commentary

Older crude tankers are still achieving record premiums, but one cannot ignore a slight toppy feeling as prices stabilize, rather than continue to rise as they have done over the last 12 months. Of course, the USD 42.5M achieved for VLCC LULU 316K DWT HHI 2003 is still extremely healthy, but it feels as though a ceiling has been reached at the older end of the age curve. Elsewhere long-time sales candidate Aframax MINERVA ZOE 105K DWT HHI 2004 was sold for USD 31M, a good price for sellers given many buyers are now pricing these older Afras at sub USD 30M. A flurry of Kamsarmax transactions dominated the dry sector this week. JY HONGKONG 81K DWT CHENGXI 2019 was sold via commercial auction at USD 30M, showing a softening in values from last done AQUAVITA SUN 81K DWT JIANGSU HANTONG 2019 at USD 32M in March. The optimistic argument in the dry sector is that sluggishness seen in the China recovery story is behind us, and the outlook is positive for the rest of the year. However, in reality, question marks remain over the immediate summer period, and how the market will react. Some sellers are open to adjusting their expectations as underlying values soften, which opens the door for potential asset players, seen in Thenamaris’ purchase of Kamsarmax LORD STAR 82K DWT SANOYAS 2013 at USD 23.2M. Capesize values continue to soften, seen in the sale of AQUAENNA 175K DWT JINHAI 2011 for USD 22M to Costamare.
