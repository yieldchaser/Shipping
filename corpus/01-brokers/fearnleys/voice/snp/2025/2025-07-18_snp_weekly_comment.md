## Market Commentary

KMG of Kazakhstan are reported to have bought scrubber fitted LR2 resale pair LAMU / DUNE (113K DWT COSCO Yangzhou 2025) at USD 72M each, in line with non scrub LR2 HESPERIA TIDE (115K DWT Zhoushan Changhong 2025), bought last week by Polembros for USD 70M. In the MR sector, despite the less than exciting rates on offer, buyers are still seeing value in 15–17-year-old units. There has been a large amount of liquidity in this age bracket with buyers taking a chance that those bumper spot earnings seen last year will return. The dry market is showing resilience, with healthy returns across the sector. These elevated rates are already fuelling an increase in buying interest, though sellers will be keen to extract as much value as possible and units will be priced accordingly. It was a busy week in the Kamsarmax sector, seeing five sales. Scrubber fitted ULTRA LION (81K DWT Tsuneishi Zhoushan 2015) was sold for a better than anticipated price of USD 24.8M, signalling a potential recovery in pricing for this sector, which corrected so sharply towards the end of 2024.
