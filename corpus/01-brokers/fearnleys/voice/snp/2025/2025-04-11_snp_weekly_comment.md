## Market Commentary

In a turbulent week across global markets, at least tanker S&P remains predictable. There seems to be no end in sight for Chinese appetite for older VLCCs, with eye watering sums being paid for ships close to 20 years old. Mid USD 40Ms in a normal market would typically buy you a c.10 year old unit, let alone 18 year old DD due EUROHOPE (306K DWT Daewoo 2007). Chinese buyers are leading the way this year in terms of S&P buying activity, however with the emerging USA-China trade war one can only speculate how the next few months will pan out. Elsewhere MR values appear to have stabilised following a rapid correction over the last few quarters, with PS ATENE (49K DWT HMD 2018) selling in line with her sisters at USD RGN 37M. Extremely high levels of activity this week in the Dry sector, possibly as a result of recent rallies causing buyers to act, sensing an opportunity. Subsequently though, these rallies in general have cooled off. Furthermore, escalation of the Trump tariffs and the emergence of a trade war involving the leading global economies will potentially leave these buyers with an anxious few months ahead. That said, at least in terms of port calls, the largest dry bulk carriers will be sheltered from the proposed USTR, minimising the impact on this sector. Unfortunately, the 90-day delay announced by President Trump rather kicks the can down the road on what impact USTR and tariffs will have on the market. In the standout transaction of the week, Indonesian buyers Jholin bought modern Ultramax trio OAKGATE/MAPLEGATE/DIONISIS (60K/63K DWT Oshima/Iwagi Zosen/I-S 2018/2019) for USD 32M each.
