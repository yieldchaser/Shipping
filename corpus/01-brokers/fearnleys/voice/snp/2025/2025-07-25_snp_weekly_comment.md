## Market Commentary

These summer periods tend to give time for reflection for those in the tanker markets, and, generally speaking, this summer is no different. Crude earnings have dropped off since a spike in June, and product rates are languishing. This has resulted in a well-documented reshuffling in the products market, with very healthy liquidity in the MR space, particularly in the c.15-year-old range where values appear to have hit a floor. This sentiment seems to have spread into the Aframax/LR2 space which is attracting added attention from opportunist investors. Scrubber fitted BLUE PRINCE (109K DWT Hudong Zhonghua 2009) was sold this week for USD 32.25M, as part of three vessels sold in this size range. Dry cargo charter brokers hoping to enjoy their summer break have been glued to their desks this week in a flurry of activity in the charter market. Healthy returns are spurring owners into action to capture these earnings in what has mostly been an uninspiring sector over recent times. The supramax – ultramax sector has seen a bump in SNP activity this week, as pricing has fallen into line for investors. CP CHONGQING (63K DWT Chengxi 2016) was sold this week for USD 21M, in line with other similar units sold. Elsewhere, scrubber fitted Capesize PACIFIC WEST (175K DWT Jinhai 2012) sold for USD 23.2M, in line with last done similar unit scrubber fitted GOLDEN ZHOUSHAN (175K DWT Jinhai 2011) sold at USD 22M earlier this month.
