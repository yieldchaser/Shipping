## Market Commentary

The modern transactions this week have taken the tanker market by surprise, setting fresh valuation benchmarks across several segments. The scrubber fitted ECO BEL AIR/BEVERLY HILLS (157K DWT Hyundai 2019) were sold en bloc to Performance for USD 152M, marking the first modern suezmax sale since the NORDIC THUNDER (157K DWT Hyundai 2017) achieved USD 66M in June 2025. Scrubber fitted VLCC LANDBRIDGE PROSPERITY (308K DWT Dalian 2016) changed hands for USD 85M. In the dry bulk market, liquidity remains strong for both modern and older tonnage. The scrubber fitted MINERAL SHOU GANG INTERNATIONAL (206K DWT Qingdao Yangfan 2019) was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 65.5M, adding to the recent wave of modern newcastlemax sales. Demand for modern ultramaxes continues to stay high with scrubber fitted IMABARI QUEEN (60K DWT Sanoyas 2016) being sold at USD 23.7M, a price that appears favourable for buyers British Bulkers. In the handysize sector, BEN 1 (35K DWT Jiangdong 2015) changed hands for USD 14.7M.
