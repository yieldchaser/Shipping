## Market Commentary

Tanker headlines this week were stolen by scrubber fitted VLCCs LANDBRIDGE GLORY/WISDOM 307K DWT Dalian 2019/20, which sold to Oman Shipping co (Asyad) for USD 205M. This timing is seen as somewhat curious for Chinese built VLCCs given uncertainty over USTR and the evolving, yet potentially easing, tariff situation. MRs it appears, are close to bottoming out, seen in SS/DD due TAMIAT NAVIGATOR (46K DWT HMD 2010) at USD 18M, a unit that would have transacted closer to USD 30M this time last year. Easter holidays are likely to have had an impact on the lower-than-average number of dry sales this week, although this is coinciding with fewer firm enquiries in the market on the back of the much-reported growing uncertainty and general geopolitical instability. That said, rates remain consistently above opex levels, though doing little to excite potential investors. In a rare transaction of a modern handy, NORD ABIDJAN (37K DWT Minaminippon 2020) *OHBS was sold for USD 25.5M.
