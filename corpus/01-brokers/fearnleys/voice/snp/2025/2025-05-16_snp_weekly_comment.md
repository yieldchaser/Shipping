## Market Commentary

Sellers of tankers are not pressed to sell, and it seems that eventually one will find a buyer in spite of current high asset values. Long time sales candidate NEW NAXOS (299K DWT Universal 2003) has changed hands this week for USD 33M, beating last done AUSTRALIS (299K DWT Universal 2003) which sold earlier in the year at USD 28M. Despite the healthy returns available in the earnings market, these levels provide an attractive exit point for owners, exhibited by Teekay selling suezmax pair ASPEN SPIRIT/CASCADE SPIRIT (156K DWT Rongsheng 2009) for USD 37.5M each to Nigerian buyers. The story is not quite so rosy in the products market, where we continue to see price correction. SEAWAYS CITRON/SEAWAYS FRONTIER (49K DWT HMD 2007) sold for USD 14M each, a far cry away from the USD c.25M they were seeking last summer. The dry market continues its unremarkable run this week, that said in these uncertain times, owners will be very satisfied with the reliable returns they are seeing. HMM are back in a purchasing mood with the acquisition of scrubber fitted Newcastlemax LUISE OLDENDORFF (207K DWT Hyundai 2015) for USD 51M, otherwise it appears that the most likely buyers for second hand bulk carriers are located in China. In the capesize sector THALASSINI AVRA (182K DWT Universal 2011) sold for USD 29M, to Chinese buyers.
