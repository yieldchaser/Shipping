## Market Commentary

Buyers expecting modern tanker values to drop have their hopes firmly dashed by now. The recent rush of modern tanker transactions has continued with a headline deal between Metrostar and Delta Tankers, the latter purchasing suezmax sisters CRUDE ZEPHYRUS/LEVANTE (156K DWT New Times 2021) for USD 156M. This is interesting as it represents an age of asset which would have been ordered for substantially less than it is worth today, while having had almost 5 years of trading in mostly strong tanker markets. Representing a very understandable decision to take money off the table on desirable assets, this sale could encourage others to do the same. Elsewhere, PNSC have been busy acquiring Scrubber fitted STAVANGER POSEIDON (50K DWT Hyundai Vietnam 2020) for USD 44.15M, and scrubber fitted aframax pair NAFSIKA (112K DWT Sumitomo 2022) / LORAX (109K DWT Sumitomo 2022) for USD 75M each. While the China-USA port fee battle has caused more than its fair share of headaches this week, it is unlikely to have a notable impact on the overall market. Sales in the dry market appear to have taken a brief pause, perhaps while the market makes sense of the new rulings, but it is worth remembering that overall sentiment is high and many sales candidates are reflecting this in their price ask.
