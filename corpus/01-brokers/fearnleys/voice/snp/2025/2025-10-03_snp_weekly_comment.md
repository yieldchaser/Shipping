## Market Commentary

Mixed sentiment this week across the larger crude sizes, and a range of opinion on the strength of Q4 earnings. On the back of the recent rally, where VLCCs have seen spot rates of around USD 100K/day, it seems bizarre to question earnings but softer FFAs and a quiet Chinese market with the beginning of Golden Week has people thinking on the resilience of this crude market. On second hand tonnage, modern, large transactions remain at a premium, but it is the clean space that is picking up once again. MRs enjoyed plenty of liquidity at the beginning of the year, and this looks to be returning as prices appear to have bottomed out. T MATTERHORN (47K DWT Iwagi Zosen 2010) sold for USD 20M, with SSDD due in Dec 25 and TC attached until Q1 26. The dry market appears to be flourishing; values have corrected upwards across the sector very rapidly and there is a healthy amount of buying interest across each subsector. While there are some submarket fundamentals that warrant this buoyant outlook, other subsectors are seeing values increase despite potentially testing times ahead. To this point, the ultramax sector has a reported 179 ships to be delivered in 2026, the highest number in a decade, but values and demand for candidates are both still very strong. This week ANDIAMO (63K DWT Shin Kasado 2019) sold for USD 30.5M, against last done 2019 ultramax MONA LISA (63K DWT Imabari 2019) at USD 29.5M back in August.
