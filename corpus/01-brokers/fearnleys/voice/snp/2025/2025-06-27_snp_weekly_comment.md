## Market Commentary

The tanker market has absorbed recent news developments and geopolitical tensions with little disruption and volatility. There was a brief spike in VLCC spot rates, however this has since fallen away with the market normalising. This week, Yasa acquired scrubber fitted VLCC C.SPIRIT (313K DWT HHI Gunsan 2013) for USD 67.5M, against last done 2013 scrubber fitted VLCC CAPTAIN X KYRIAKOU (300K DWT HHI Ulsan 2013) sold in September 2024 for USD 80M. Elsewhere MRs continue to change hands given the recent correction on pricing with 4 further sales this week. Most notably, NORD OCEANIA (49K DWT Onomichi 2018) sold for USD 36.5M, levels that this time last year restricted buyers to non eco tonnage. Emerging news of a US-China trade deal being struck will have not only the global stock markets rallying but could also provide some life to the steady yet unexciting dry market. Earnings have been stable for the most part, aside from the usual capesize rollercoaster, with many touting a stronger 2026. This trade agreement could be the first green shoots of the next prosperous period for dry owners. Brave maritime continue their run on Japanese tonnage this week as they picked up ARIES SAKURA (39K DWT Shin Kurushima 2020) for just under USD 26M. Elsewhere, scrubber fitted capesize PACIFIC EAST (176K DWT SWS 2012) sold for USD 27.5M.
