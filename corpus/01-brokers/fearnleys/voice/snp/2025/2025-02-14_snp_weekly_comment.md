## Market Commentary

Very good - slightly rephrased The en bloc transaction of two scrubber fitted Enesel LR2s ELYITS/KAVAFIS (113K dwt SWS 2024/2023) to Centrofin for USD 143M grabbed the attention of the market this week. The levels paid, despite being well above historical average, represent a sharp correction in value given the last done scrubber fitted LR2 STI LILY (109K dwt New Times 2019) was transacted at similar levels in October 2024. This does show however, that would be sellers are increasingly willing to transact in light of growing uncertainty and increasing unpredictability across the tanker market. Older afras are too proving popular, with handsome returns available on loading Urals crude in the Baltic. The much-anticipated post Lunar New Year bounce has yet to materialise on dry, as capesize spot rates hover close to two-year lows. This will not panic owners for now, who are busy assessing a daily changing environment as talk of tariffs dominate the political landscape. Transactions are for the most part limited to older tonnage, with ECO units still highly desirable across the market. In the largest sector by size, Berge Bulk bought scrubber fitted ore carrier NSU INSPIRE (250K DWT NAMURA 2011) for USD 37M.
