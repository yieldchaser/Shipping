## Market Commentary

The search for renewed benchmarks in the tanker market has been a fruitless task for a while now, however, this looks to be changing at least in the MR space. An Increased liquidity is driving the ongoing value correction, indicating more activity in the segment as these corrected levels continue to attract the attention of opportunistic players. Pumproom MR ANNA M (47K DWT Iwagi Zosen 2010) sold in xs of USD 17M, almost 35% less than what she could have expected this time last year. There remains healthy activity across the dry market despite a somewhat dreary outlook for the rest of 2025. Most will be well placed to ride out this current storm, with rates remaining comfortably above OPEX for the time being. The next few months could see an increase in buying interest, as buyers look to position themselves to catch potential predicted upside in 2026-2027. That said, modern tonnage remains at a premium and these buyers will likely need to beat last done on these units in order to secure tonnage. In the modern ultramax space, scrubber fitted AQUAVITA LIME (63K DWT Nantong Xiangyu 2021) sold for USD 30.5M.
