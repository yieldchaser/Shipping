## Market Commentary

Headlines in the tanker market have been stolen by two rare modern transactions this week. On the VLCC front DHT reported their acquisition of the the scrubber fitted VLCC PAPALEMOS 320K DWT HYUNDAI 2018 for USD 107 million while Delta Tankers have reportedly purchased the NORDIC THUNDER 157K DWT HYUNDAI 2017 for USD 65 million. Both transactions setting some welcomed benchmarks across both crude segments in an ever-evolving geopolitical landscape. In the product sector ADNOC have sold their LR1 sisters BANI YAS and YAMILAH-III 75K DWT STX 2010/2011 for USD 22.2 million each. A notable decrease from the last comparable transaction of FLAGSHIP WILLOW 75K DWT SPP 2012 at USD 27.8 million in February 2025. Dry markets were equally active this week with CMB selling their Newcastlemax MINERAL HIROSHIGE 208K DWT IMABARI 2019 basis a forward delivery for USD 65 million while the scrubber fitted Capesize BULK GINZA 182K DWT IMABARI 2020 was sold to Genco for USD 64 million. Lastly In the handysize sector the NY TRADER III 39K DWT JIANGMEN NANYANG 2016 was sold by clients of Nisshin to a European buyer for USD 16.8 million.
