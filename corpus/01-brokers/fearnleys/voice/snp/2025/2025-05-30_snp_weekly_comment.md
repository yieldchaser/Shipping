## Market Commentary

MR sales continue to provide liquidity to the tanker second hand market. Despite some recent stability on values basis last dones, it would not be surprising to see values correct further amid significant deliveries to come throughout the rest of 2025. At the older end of the spectrum, PS CAPRI (49K DWT STX Jinhae 2011) sold for USD 18M, in line with last done GLENDA MELODY (49K HMD 2011) at USD 17.9M. Younger units NORD JEWEL*/JOY (49K JMU 2018) *scrubber fitted, sold for just under 74M en bloc to Paragon Mobility, in line with last done en bloc transaction PS ATENE/MILANO (49K DWT HMD 2018). Further question marks over the legality and implementation of President Trump’s sweeping tariffs this week mean a similar story of uncertainty in the shipping markets. That said, the dry market remains consistent and active, although unexciting. Competition is still fierce for large handy bulkers, seen in BUNUN ORCHID (37K DWT I-S Shipyard 2021) bought by Stealth for USD 25M. Elsewhere, Indian buyers acquired the Kamsarmax trio CL GRACE/MONA/TIFFANY (81K DWT Yangfan 2012/2013) for USD 45M en bloc.
