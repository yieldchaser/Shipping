## Market Commentary

The tanker market finds itself in a strange position. There is bullish sentiment on large crude tonnage, however it is yet to manifest itself into values and S&P activity. It makes a good investment proposal at a time where confusion reigns across the market, however it is hard to see those with modern ECO tankers surrendering their positions to buyers. Non-ECO tonnage on the other hand, is plentiful in supply with many motivated sellers in the market. Across products it appears that the recent boom is very much over, something that will be crystallised should ongoing second-hand discussions materialise. The Lunar New Year is a milestone many in the dry market have been waiting for. There is a lot riding on the next few months, which many feel will set the tone for the rest of the year. Despite the holidays, Chinese buyers were still active on Capes, buying CAPE HERON/HAWK (177K DWT MITSUI/NAMURA 2005/2006) en bloc for USD 30M. Earnings in general are still sluggish, even with the panamax market showing signs of life, but there is some way to go if it is to have a meaningful effect on values. For now, the dry story remains as it was with an overall bearish sentiment for 2025.
