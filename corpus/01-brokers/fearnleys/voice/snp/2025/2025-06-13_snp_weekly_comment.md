## Market Commentary

Almost a clean sweep for MRs this week as the sector remains the focus of second-hand tanker buyers. This has been the story on tanker S&P throughout the last months primarily due to the recent correction in pricing, but also the anticipated deliveries to take place this year. Most of the MRs sold this week are SS due, indicating sellers will take market offerings in order to avoid spending more on SS due units. Scrubber fitted NORDTOKYO (50K DWT Samsung HI Geoje 2020) was sold for USD 38.2M, against last done scrubber fitted EDEN (49K HMD 2020) at USD 40M back in March of this year. A much quieter than usual week on dry, where liquidity has not been an issue so far this year. The underlying story, however, remains the same where majority older ships are transacted amongst the rare modern sale. Values have been sliding gradually against the consistent yet uninspiring earnings market, seen in scrubber fitted BRIGHT PEGASUS (82K DWT Tsuneishi Zhoushan 2013) which sold for USD 17.8M this week, against scrubber fitted BRIGHT GEMINI (82K DWT Tsuneishi Zhoushan 2013) from same sellers which sold for USD 23M back in September 2024.
