## Market Commentary

Despite a number of markets still in the midst of seasonal holidays, the dry snp market continue to draw sustained interest with a flurry of transactions to report this week. The Ultramax sector has seen three 2014 Jinling built units change hands for a price of Usd 67 m enbloc marking a further step up in values for Chinese built vessels. In comparison, the "SANTA BARBARA ( 2013, Iwagi, 61k dwt) was sold at Usd 24.5m this week underlying the premium priced in for Japanese designed units. Notable activity in the Handysize sector as well which has seen 8 vessels sold this week. With buying enquiry remaining strong and signs of ongoing negotiations across all sectors we expect the volume of transactions to remain.  Tanker markets remain subdued in terms of concluded business with pockets of activity on the vintage crude carrier front with owners driven to sell by high residual values off the back of continuous strong rate offered by cash buyers for demolition, however with expected decline going forward.
