## Market Commentary

The 25 vessels changing hands this week across all dry segments are to say the least a clear indicator of the sentiment engulfing the dry markets. As expected the Capesize sector has followed suit with three sales this week leading values upwards. The Japanese controlled “UNITED BREEZE” (2012, Imabari, 180k dwt) has achieved Usd 29.5mill being the 5th Cape sale this month. To put levels in perspective, the one year older “COLOGNY” (2011, Tsuneishi Cebu, 180k dwt) was sold last month at Usd 22.9mill which, even when taking into consideration the discount carried by the Cebu design against Imabari, illustrates particularly well the appreciation in pricing. The Kamsarmax/Panamax sector has seen 12 sales this week with notable activity on the modern Kamsarmax front with the “KARLOVASI” (2016, Oshima, 82k dwt) being sold to Norden at Usd 27mill. The last comparable sold unit of similar design and vintage dates back to December last year when the “SAKIZAYA NOBLE” (2017, JMU, 80k dwt) was sold at Usd 25.5mill. The Ultramax and Supramax sectors continue to joy of very strong levels of buying enquiry with a consistent stream of sale candidates being placed on the market by owners looking to extract the value currently offered by competing buyers.
