## Market Commentary

The continued depression over the sport markets has slowed down the S&P activity even further in the tanker space. However, we have seen a new benchmark on modern VLCCs in a deal between two listed entities. Frontline added two more VLs with the purchase of M/T Nissos Antiparos and M/T Nissos Santorini from Okeanis Eco Tankers – both vessels are 318k dwt, built at Hyundai and with scrubbers installed. The price of USD 90 m shows a clear upturn and that there is willingness for the right ships of good specs. An obvious comparison and indicator is Hunter Group’s sale of M/T Hunter Atla (blt 2019 at DSME) back in February at USD 84.5 m. In the Dry space, we have a new benchmark of around 10 year old Chinese Capesize with the sale of “SPRINGBANK” (2010, New Times, 177k dwt) at USD 26.5 m. Except for few auction sales recently, the most comparable one is the “PACIFIC CANOPUS” (2012, Dalian, 180k dwt), sold for USD 21 m end March. Taking the age difference into consideration, we would estimate an increase of about 50% in value the past 3 months for this vintage. The lack of modern Japanese units available along with the steep development in pricing on these units have increased the focus on Chinese built tonnage. Charter hires paid are not justifying sufficient premium for the prices asked for Japanese built tonnage lately. Same is illustrated with the sale of “ELINDA MARE” (2010, New Century, 80k dwt) at USD 18.5 m. Just a month ago, sister vessel “XIN FENG” was sold for USD 16.5 m. We expect new benchmark in the Supramax sector with the “BULK COSTA RICA” (2010, NACKS, 58k dwt) inviting offers today. Reportedly over 10 buyers have purchased the report and the expected sales price is in the very high teens.
