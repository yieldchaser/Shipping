## Market Commentary

Momentum continues to build in the tanker SnP markets with an increasing number of transactions materialising this week. The Japanese controlled LR2 “CHAMPION PROSPERITY” (115k dwt, built Sasebo, 2009, Coated) invited offers after having north of 20 buyers inspecting. The vessel is now committed at a price of Usd 19.8m to Greek buyers. The strong interest is not only a clear indicator of the growing appetite for quality tanker tonnage but when compared to the sale of the one year older “FALCON EXPRESS” (115k dwt, built Sasebo, 2009, Coated) sold last month at Usd 14.5m; the price achieved this week points to a clear upward trend in values which should remain unhindered with a number of buyers finding themselves looking for alternative opportunities in a market with a thin supply of sale candidates. Also increasing activity across most product segments as seen by East Med’s purchase of SOCATRA’s two HMD resales. Both vessels deliver in 2022, and the price is reported at Usd 35.5 mill each – presumably on novation basis. Even for LR1s which normally is a pretty illiquid class we see more deals surfacing. The dry markets seem to have met some relative stability following the steep appreciation in values witnessed over recent weeks. Most reported transactions are now falling in line with last done, consolidating benchmarks across sectors. Saying that, buying enquiry remains strong with sign of ongoing negotiations however a constant stream of tonnage being placed for sale seems to be keeping a lid on competition and contributing to the abovementioned flattening in prices – famous last words.
