## Market Commentary

Boom! A surprisingly strong dry market has caught most off guard, and we have this week seen the highest rates for both Panamax and Supramax since 2010. The surge is on back of strong demand for grains and coal in the Far East, but also a general pick up in demand all over. We continue to see a flurry of deals for region 10yrs old Kamsarmax with the KINOURA (82k dwt, Tsuneishi, 2012) sold at USD 18mill, the EVERGLORY (81.5k dwt, Qingdao Beihai, 2012) sold at USD 16mill and the GLOBE ELECTRA/DANAE (81k dwt, STX, 2010) sold at USD 15.75mill each. All in all the sales point towards rising values, with the KINOURA being an exception. The market being most bullish on the Japanese built tonnage which is expected in an upturn, and in the next phase we expect that re-pricing of the Chinese built tonnage will outperform. Tanker sale and purchase markets remain subdued with limited liquidity. The ever deepening bid/offer spread renders transactions hard to materialize with potential sellers holding back in view of better times. Only three sales to report this week with new downward benchmarks being set in the MR sector with the sale of the Japanese controlled pump-room unit BRIGHT FORTUNE (48k dwt, Iwagi, 2020) at USD 13.75mill.
