## Market Commentary

Despite Tanker chartering market seeing signs of recovery we continue to see subdued snp activity, with a growing list of buying enquiry failing to be met by any realistic sale candidates. In terms of concluded business, the M/T Starlight Venture (291k dwt, blt HHI 2004) is reported sold to Nigerian buyers at USD 30 mill. Lot of talks as well this week concerning the troubled MR M/T Marvin Independence (50k, blt Hyundai Mipo 2018). The vessel is a high spec, LNG-ready, IMO II, 20 grade MR that eventually went for auction at USD 17.1 mill to Greek buyers. We understand there are still several ongoing legal outstandings surrounding the vessel and its sale that may impact the transaction. In the other hand, activity remains high in the Drybulk space with headlines being stolen by the Capesize sector this week which has seen Turkey’s Ciner emerge as the buyer of 3 x 2016 built units. The SWS and Dalian built vessels, M/V Stella Naomi, M/V Stella Hope and M/V Stella Nora – were sold at an enbloc price of Usd 132 mill including the balance of index-linked TCs attached. The sale provides a clear target and benchmarks for the owners that we have seen place fresh candidates in the market this week. Elsewhere, Malaysian Bulk Carriers joyed of the recent appreciation in values witnessed in the supramax front by selling its two Shin Kurushima built sisters M/V Alam Madu and M/T Alam Molek (both 58k dwt, blt 2014). The vessels were sold to Meghna of Bangladesh who is reported to have paid 26 mill each.
