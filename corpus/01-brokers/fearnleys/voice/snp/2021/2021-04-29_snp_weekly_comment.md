## Market Commentary

We have seen healthy activity levels across the board as owners are looking to place their chips for the long awaited upswing in the tanker market. Ridgebury stole the headline this week and have bought 4 x 2007 and 1 x 2006 Dalian built VLCC’s at USD 120m. The vessels all have a BB attached to Euronav until 2021, giving them downside protection before the chartering market is expected to recover. Furthermore, Thenamaris bought the 2 year younger sister ship “NEW CREATION” for USD 37.5m. International Seaways have sold their CAP 1 rated “SEAWAYS TANABE” (2002, HITACHI, 300K DWT) to Chinese buyers for USD 24m, which falls in with last week’s sale of the “MARAN CARINA” (2003, DSME, 306K DWT) at USD 24m when we take survey positions into account. Liquidity in the vintage Suezmax space remains healthy and we understand that Tsakos have sold the “SILIA T” (2002, HHI, 164K DWT) for USD 15.8m. The deal falls in line with pricing achieved on the “EUROFAITH” (2004, HHI, 151K DWT) which also changed hands this week for USD 18m, once relevant discounts for age applied. In the product market, Cido are rumoured to have committed the “LR2 POSEIDON” (2009, SASEBO, 110K DWT) and “LR2 POLARIS & LR2 PIONEER”(2008, SASEBO, 110K DWT) on subject finance terms for low 60s enbloc to undisclosed buyers. Pricing looks attractive compared to last week’s sale of the uncoiled “CHAMPION PROSPERITY” (2009, SASEBO, 115K DWT) which achieved a price of USD 19.8m after seeing a hefty amount of interest. With the Golden week coming up in China and the Easter holidays in Greece, we have seen good activity across all the segments in dry although expect this to come off next week. Notable sales on the dry side include the widebeam Kamsarmax resales ex-Chengxi Shipyard from Foremost Group with delivery this year to Yasa Shipping of Turkey at USD 28.5m each. With newbuilding prices at region USD 27 – 28m in China, we regard the deal as a favourable one for the buyers considering delivery in the strongest dry market we have seen in a decade. Scrubber and BWTS-fitted “EMERALD STAR” (2014, TESS CEBU, 82K DWT) have been reported sold for USD 25.1m after receiving initial offers at region 23-24m. “PACIFIC BRIGHT” (2013, Hantong, 57K DWT) sold for USD 14.8m illustrates a substantial increase (about 50%) in value of Chinese built Supramaxes comparing to same age Dolphin 57 “GLOVIS MADONNA” sold for USD 9.7 m early this year. The sale is also a good indicator for the discount to Japanese design, when comparing to the sale of “LOWLANDS PATRASCHE” (2013, TESS CEBU, 58K DWT) at USD 18.2m.
