## Market Commentary

We are witnessing a sentiment shift this week in the SnP markets with a loss of momentum in Dry cargo following a substantial correction in rates which resulted in an immediate weakening of buying interest. A loss of momentum which is particularly apparent in the volume of reported sales, but which has yet to transcribe into lower values. A resilience in pricing that can be attributed to sellers choosing to show composure in what many consider to be a temporary dip. Therefore, this week comes to a close with eight vessels changing hands at levels in line with their respective "last dones". A stark contrast to the Tanker markets where the growing anticipation of a market upturn is leading an increase in buying enquiry. In terms of concluded business, the product sector is once again capturing most of the activity with five of the eight reported sales. Norden have committed their scrubber fitted "NORD SKATE" and "NORD STINGRAY" (51K DWT 2009 STX) at Usd 17m each. A sale falling in line with Norden's effort to renew its MR fleet considering that earlier this month they acquired the two modern sisterships ECO LOS ANGELES and ECO CITY OF ANGELS (50K DWT HMD 2020) at Usd 36.5m each.
