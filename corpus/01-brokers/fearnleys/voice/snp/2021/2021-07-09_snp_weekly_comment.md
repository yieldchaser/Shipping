## Market Commentary

This week comes to a close with yet another flurry of transactions in the Dry cargo markets with up to twenty sales to report. Weekly benchmarks barely have time to set before being broken by ever increasing values making any potential purchase a constant moving target for buyers eager to capture the returns offered by fruitful freight markets. The Supramax and Ultramax segments have captured the vast majority of transactions with twelve vessels changing hands this week resulting in substantial gains on asset values. Notably we have seen 2017 built SDARI 64s achieving Usd 25m while the "BULK PATAGONIA" (2012, Kawasaki, 58k dwt) was sold at a firm Usd 20.5m. Elsewhere the Greek controlled "SERENE SUSANNAH" (2010, STX Dalian, 58k dwt) was sold at Usd 15m. Worth noting in this context that the same owners sold the exact sistership "SERENE LYDIA" at Usd 8.5m in February this year. These renewed values are already on the brink of becoming a short lived history with buyers putting forward levels above last done in a bid to secure tonnage in an increasingly competitive environment.  After a spike of niche VL activity last week the tanker market has seen close to zero transactions this week; however with skyrocketing dry values we are witnessing a shift of enquiry into the tanker segments with several buyers preferring to chase tonnage in illiquid tanker market that have seen values flatten over the past weeks. Notable activity in the product sector with whispers of a number of mid 2000 built MRs in the process of being sold.
