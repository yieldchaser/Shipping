## Market Commentary

Subdued freight rates in the Tanker space are keeping S&P activity limited with only a handful of deals to report this week. The Judicial Managers of Xihe Holding have sold another VLCC - namely the M/T Chang Bai San (318k dwt, blt SWS 2012)  which achieved USD 41,50 mill – USD 2,65 mill down compared to the sister M/T Qi Lian San which was sold back in late April. On the older units we have seen M/T Katsuragisan (311k dwt, blt 2005 Kawasaki) sold for USD 30.3 mill. Fairly in line with expectations given current demolition rates but substantially lower than M/T Olympic Legend (309k dwt, blt 2003 Samsung) which got USD 31,5 mill in July. The latter was seen as very strong. Dry cargo markets continue to joy of a number of transactions with a steady flow of sale candidates being placed on the market to meet the ever-increasing demand for tonnage. Supported by the solid footing provided by the charter markets sellers are raising their price expectations driving potential buyers above last done levels in order to secure a deal. Supramax and Ultramax segments continue to capture the bulk of the enquiry with vessels across all vintages regularly changing hands.
