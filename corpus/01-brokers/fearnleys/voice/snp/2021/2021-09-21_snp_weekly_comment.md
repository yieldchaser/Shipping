## Market Commentary

The dry market continues on the same pace and we note another week with stronger prices paid. The kamsarmax M/V Lowlands Nello (82k dwt, blt 2015 Sanoyas) has seen USD 32,2 mill in a deal with Chronos – this compares to the M/V Peak Liberty (82k dwt, blt 2015 Tsuneishi) which was reported last week at USD 30 mill. Suitably named M/V Million Bell and M/V Glad Mark (both 58k dwt, blt 2012 Kawasaki) are concluded at USD 22 mill each – half a million below Trans Oceanic (58k dwt, blt 2012 Tsuneishi) that was reported last week, but with deliveries in Q1 2022 there should be a good few months of earnings before handing over, so in total a sweeter deal for the sellers. The handies still receive their fair share of attention as well and based on the amount of enquires in the market we could expect a further lift on values going forward. The tanker side is still fairly quiet, but with the increased activity in the spot markets and general optimism for a recovery to take place soon, we expect more interest. It seems older vessels are now sensibly adjusting closer and more related to scrap values, which is probably a sign that the support from sanctioned cargoes is easing off. Most activity is on the product segments with MRs being the most liquid asset – in an illiquid market. Prices have no clear direction, and flat is probably the best comment to make.
