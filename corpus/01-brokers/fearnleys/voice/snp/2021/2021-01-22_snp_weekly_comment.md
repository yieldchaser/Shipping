## Market Commentary

On the tanker market we have seen notable activity with three modern VLCC’s sold this week setting some much needed benchmarks for units of similar vintage. Following its purchase in November of TRF HORTEN built 2018 at HHIC-Phil at Usd 72m, Delta Tankers of Greece has now acquired the one year older sistership HUDSON for Usd 71.5m. Both vessels were sold by TRF who is also a 50% shareholder of the sisterships ECO QUEEN and ECO FUTURE built 2016 DSME which are sold at Usd 68m each to DHT Holdings. We expect liquidity on the modern front to pick up momentum in the near future supported by an increasing amount of enquiry with a market consensus anticipating a recovery for the second half of this year. Newbuilding prices have certainly passed their bottom spurred by a combination of increased steel prices, strengthening local currencies vs USD and a strong influx of large container vessel projects. Dry SnP markets continue to joy of high volumes of transactions with vessels changing hands across all sectors. Pricing-wise it seems that most market players are still looking to digest the recent appreciation in values that we witnessed last week with buyers looking to figure out whether this is indeed the start of a sustained upward trend in values rather than a one off glitch in what has been until now a relatively flat market.
