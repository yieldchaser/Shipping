## Market Commentary

The holidays are over in most regions, and activity is picking up again. Zodiac have scored on their 4th VLCC from Xihe, with the purchase of M/T JIN HUA SAN (317k dwt, blt 2009 SWS) at USD 36.8 mill. This is $2,5 mill up from the sister M/T TAI SAN (also SWS 2009) that was done back in October last year. Still – these forced sales are not representative for the general market, and with the growing interest we expect prices to continue climbing. As we all know, there is still not much support to get from the physical markets but increasing steel prices have an impact both on the NB prices and demolition values, and as such pulling everything up. These tendences are evidenced with two 2004 built VLCCs sold - M/T STARLIGHT VENTURE (291k, blt 2004 HHI) at USD 30.6 mill and M/T NEW CENTURY (299 dwt, blt 2004 Universal) at USD 31 mill which clearly show the trend. An easy reference here is DHT’s sale of their three 2004 models only a month ago. Despite their scrubbers and Cap 1 rating the price obtained was a fair bit lower at USD 29,6 mill (on average) each. We would guess the buyers of these could score a nice profit by flipping as the scrubbers should give a further premium compared to the most recent deals. The fuel spread seems to continue to widen. Okeanis Eco Tankers’ LR2s – all 115k, built Hyundai 2015 and with scrubbers have been widely discussed in the market. SOCAR was pointed out as the buyer, but this week it surfaced that Torm managed to pull it together at the end. A nice deal for both parties it seems, and the official price is USD 40,25 mill each. With the shipyards leaning back, and the general good interest, we expect prices to keep moving north. Another week with dry sales of all sizes and new benchmarks set. Notable sales include the 2012 Japanese built Kamsarmaxes “SPRING AEOLIAN” and “TANGERINE ISLAND”, both sold for region USD 21m each. This compares with the 2012 Japanese built Kamsarmax “KINOURA” which was sold for USD 18m end of February, and illustrates clearly the development. With the increased prices and competition on Japanese vessels, we also witness a firming trend on the value of Chinese tonnage. Sister vessels “PEDHOULAS FARMER” and “PEDHOULAS BUILDER” (2012, Zhejiang Ouhua, 81k dwt) have been reported sold for USD 22m each. The scrubbers and BWTSs fitted on both have a clear effect, but this is still a strong substantial jump compared to the previous 2012 Chinese built Kamsarmax sold, “EVERGLORY” end February at USD 16m. We see an increased amount of interest from buyers for around 10 – 15-year-old bulkers, and with the steep increase of the demo prices along with the firm dry market, we expect a continued rise across all sectors and age in dry.
