## Market Commentary

Activity level remains high in most tanker segments. Even on LR1s the tension increases, and a few deals being negotiated and/or placed on subs. Whilst not many new firm sales to report in the larger segments, there are ongoing discussions, and new deals and probably new benchmarks will surface soon. There is a general upwards trend for asset values in most segments, and with the sale of Aframax STAVANGER BLISS (105k dwt, blt Sumitomo 2008) at USD 19.5 mill, we have a clear indicator when compared to the LR2 CHAMPION PROSPERITY (115k dwt, blt Sasebo 2009) at USD 19.8 mill last week. Even though reported not to be in perfect condition – at least on paper, CHAMPION PROSPERITY is a more attractive vessel with her size, coating and age in her favor. Higher transaction volume and increasing values inevitably makes for wider bid/ask spreads, and we may see deal-making dragging out a bit in time as the underlying earnings are not showing any positive signs as yet. The dry market continues the upward path and the BDI is now at 2788, the highest level since 2010. The capes are now leading the charge and there are rumours that EIBHLIN (182k dwt, Univeral, 2011) is done at xs USD 30mill, with some even reporting USD 31-32mill. If true, this would be a huge step up from UNITED BREEZE (181k dwt, Imabari, 2012) which was sold only a month ago for USD 29.5mill. Genco has picked up SERENA R (64k dwt, Yangfan, 2016) for USD 20.5mill. This sale adds to a good volume of 5yrs old Chinese Ultras, which have consolidated around last done price levels and created a strong benchmark.
