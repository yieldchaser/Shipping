## Market Commentary

Further holidays this week could be blamed for the lack of output on the tanker second hand sales front however, the story very much remains in keeping with recent market trends. Overall lack of supply of modern sales candidates, and fair liquidity on the older tonnage. Those who are genuine sellers of modern ships in this market are in no rush and can continue to enjoy the bumper earnings while their suitors fight it out. New York listed Scorpio announced they sold STI MANHATTAN 50K DWT SPP 2015 for USD 40.8M to KSS Line, only a tick higher than the sister units they sold to International Seaways back in February of this year, showing that despite the sharp gains in MR values over the recent run, pricing has remined consistent in the last 6 months. A subdued week by recent standards in the dry market, but not one without notable headlines. Asset values, for anything younger than 10 years, continue to strengthen. Sellers of modern units are few and far between, and this scarcity is contributing to an emerging disconnect between asset prices and charter rates. Pangaea bought Belships supra sisters BELFRIEND/BELTIDE 58K DWT TSUNEISHI CEBU 2016 at USD 28.3M each, comfortably eclipsing the last sale of a 2016 Tess Cebu 58 (EMMANOUELA 57K DWT Tess Cebu 2016) sold at USD 22M back in November 2022. Elsewhere in a rare modern ultramax transaction, FLORENTINE OETKER 63K DWT IMABARI 2017 was bought by Ince of Turkey for USD 32.85M.
