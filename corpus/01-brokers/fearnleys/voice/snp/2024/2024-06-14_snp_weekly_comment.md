## Market Commentary

Headlines about the tanker second hand market have provided little in terms of variance in the past few months (slowing activity amid high prices), however, the reported sales this week confirm there are still those willing to buy and sell at these levels. Prices in general have continued to firm despite few transactions to prove it, shown here with scrubber fitted FRONT THOR 159K DWT JIANGSU RONGSHENG 2010 selling in the low USD 48Ms. Suezmax transactions around this age bracket have been hard to come by, however, Frontline’s ability to dispose of their 2010 blt Rongsheng series at better than last done on each occasion is a prime example of the underlying positivity in the tanker space. MRs also continue to gain in value, with ALHENA 52K DWT GSI 2012 selling to Greek buyers at USD 32.2M. Quite an unremarkable week in the dry market with the usual activity of players jostling for position on older units. Capes remain strong, with scrubber fitted NYMPHE 180K DWT DSME 2009 sold to Hayfin at USD 29.3M, just slightly up on last done 2009 CORINTHIAN PHOENIX 179 K DWT HHI 2009 at USD 29M. Otherwise, it is tough to see where the potential asset play is in the dry bulk space. Modern eco ships are scarcely available and priced to represent that, and older bulkers are often priced in excess of any justifiable calculation from an investment standpoint.
