## Market Commentary

Lack of liquidity is a distant memory for dry market players as the run on second hand bulkers continues this week. Perhaps a cause for all this excitement is that despite the seemingly buoyant market, there is still some way to go before historical highs are reached, at least from a TC point of view. There is work to be done even to eclipse the 2022 TC market, though judging by the ever-increasing competition on any new market candidate, there is no shortage of confidence that this will not be achieved. Modern Scrubber fitted Tess 82 Kamasarmax SANKO HAWKING 82K DWT TSUNEISH 2021 sold this week at a notable USD 41.5M. At the other end of the age curve where values are likely to fluctuate significantly as players look to take advantage of the spot market, Capesize CASTILLO DE VALVERDE 173K DWT BOHAI 2005 sold at USD 15M. The second hand wet market is happy to stay out of the headlines this week, with more ink being devoted to the strong demand for Newbuilidng tanker slots. There is still demand for older tankers, albeit softer than we’ve seen in recent times, shown in aframax SEA HAZEL 106K DWT TSUNEISHI TADOTSU 2004 being sold at USD 29M.
