## Market Commentary

The Tanker market moved on in relentless fashion this week with a flurry of transactions in the product space, against an ever-tightening crude sector. Scrubber fitted Suezmax RAPTOR (159K DWT Universal 2007) was sold to Chinese buyers at USD 46.5M. Worth noting that she is surveys freshly passed but nevertheless this transaction shows just where the market has reached, given this is a 17-year-old ship. Across the product sector, an abundance of buyers are giving owners of ageing ships the option to cash out at a very healthy level, such as in the case for TORM selling TORM PLATTE (46K DWT HMD 2006) for USD 22M. No such thing as a quiet week it seems, as an abundance of transactions were registered this week in the Dry market. Scrubber fitted Newcastlemaxes BULK SHANGHAI/SEOUL (207K DWT New Times 2019) were bought by Neda Maritime, a deal which will have others looking upon it in envy as modern dry tonnage seems increasingly tough to come by. Lunar New Year has not impacted the market quite as it used to, with Chinese buyers still active and an overall bullish feel to the market, giving impetus to those still in the office.
