## Market Commentary

A subdued week for the tanker S&P market with a number of deals on hold behind the scenes. The unfolding situation in the Red Sea raises the question whether Sellers of large Crude tonnage could hold out and receive premium rates/price levels. This is coupled with the fact that without a very prompt delivery, Buyers would miss out on the best of the winter returns and would be unwilling to pay a premium to do so – resulting in a standoff. Smaller product carriers were the movers this week with ice 1A Handy Tankers DINAH/PLUTO 37K DWT HMD 2008 being sold by Tufton for USD 41.75M en bloc, booking them a sizeable profit on the units. Elsewhere, MR SOCHRINA 46K DWT SUNGDONG 2008 was bought by Besiktas for USD 24M. A busy week in the Dry sector but in general the story remains the same, which is one of a significant lack of liquidity on modern tonnage. Rates are experiencing a seasonal dip, however, this does not translate to lower values. Quite the contrary for those available modern market units, with some owners taking advantage of the scarcity of candidates and indeed increasing their price expectations. With that in mind, in the one standout modern transaction of the week, scrubber fitted Newcastlemax MINERAL QINGDAO 206K DWT Qingdao Yangfan 2020 was bought by Winning for USD 54.25M. Otherwise Kamsarmax SUPER LUNA 81K DWT JINHAI HI 2016 sold to Modion at USD 22.6M, part of a very active week for ships of this size. Further down the size curve activity thrived with a number of Supramax units blt 07-12 transacted, helping price other candidates in which is a very competitive corner of the market.
