## Market Commentary

Despite the usual seasonal slump and lacklustre earnings environment, there remains a healthy amount of activity on the dry S&P front. This only amplifies questions on the disconnect between asset values and earnings, particularly on modern ships. It hasn’t however, deterred Alpha Bulkers acquiring DD due capesize HERUN GLOBAL SWS 2016 at USD 49.5M, in line with recently done COURAGEOUS (181K DWT SWS 2016). Kamsarmax trio THERESA HEBEI/JINLIN/GUANGDONG (81K DWT SAINTY YANGHZHOU / JIANGSU EASTERN HI 2012) were sold en bloc at USD 19M each to a Chinese buyer, a premium versus the USD 17.5M paid for YU QIANG (81K DWT PLA4807 2012) by a Greek buyer. MRs continue to dominate activity within tanker S&P market as GREEN SEA (50K DWT DAE SUN 2014) was sold this week at just under USD 39M, with SS/DD passed. A price that when compared with recent transactions is Illustrating particularly well the premium that HMD built units still command over their Korean counterparts. Liquidity for large mid aged tankers remains resilient as EMERALD I (104K DWT SAMSUNG HI 2007) sold for USD 38M, this is in contrast to older c.20yo afra and suezmax tankers where the number of available candidates is growing steadily.
