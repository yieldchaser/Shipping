## Market Commentary

A low volume environment seems to be the new norm on tanker SNP, despite healthy levels of buying enquiry within the market. The comfort for owners joying the sustained high returns, means they are not in a rush to dispose of tonnage and will certainly not accept anything below last done. This is shown with EPS offloading pumproom MR PACIFIC DIAMOND 47K DWT IWAGI ZOSEN 2010 for a healthy $26.7m. The same vessel was acquired for $18m back in 2022, an eye-watering 48% increase. The dry market continues to steal headlines in the second-hand markets, despite a growing disconnect between earnings and values. A rise in pricing particularly well illustrated by the sale of the Kamsarmax GIA AMBITION 84K DWT CSSC TIANJIN 2022 which was picked up this week for USD 38.5M by Egyptian buyers, roughly 17% more than the last 2022 Chinese sold in October 2023 KUNO OLDENDORFF 82K DWT JIANGSU 2022.
