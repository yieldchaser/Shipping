## Market Commentary

While the story on modern tanker tonnage remains steady, older units are once again in the spotlight as geopolitical turmoil continues to pose questions to investors. There is a real sense of unpredictability over escalating conflict in the Middle East, and the US elections getting ever closer. Record prices on modern MRs are being crystalised in the form of scrubber fitted MARITIME VERITY/INSPIRATION (49K DWT GSI 2021), where not long ago, NB prices were in line with these levels now seen on almost 4 year old ships. A quieter week in terms of reported transactions in the usually busy dry sector has been interestingly coupled with what has felt like an avalanche of new candidates on the market. The 10-15 y.o. Capesize sector seems particularly active, where confidence in the iron ore demand story is waning in the wake of a strong period for owners and charterers. Values and rates on the smaller sizes however, seem fairly consistent for the time being, despite a recent slight softening on the increasingly liquid Ultramax market where OCEAN AMBITIOUS (63K DWT China Shipbuilding 2016) was sold for USD 25.5M.
