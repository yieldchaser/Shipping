## Market Commentary

Despite the market entering the usual summer lull, capesize bulkers remain an attractive investment as 2 further units were reported sold this week. Pricing remains firm, with CAPE MATHILDE 178K DWT MITSUI 2010 achieving close to USD 30M and scrubber fitted GENCO HADRIAN 169K DWT SUNGDONG 2008 being sold for USD 25M. This price environment seems for good reason too, with Vale reporting this week they increased iron ore production in Q2 and expect to reach the upper end of their 2024 guidance. Scrubber fitted kamsarmax KRISTIAN OLDENDORFF 82K DWT JIANGSU NEW HANTONG 2024 saw the first transaction of a Chinese kamsarmax in xs of USD 40M, sold at USD 40.85M, showing the value of a new ship on the water. Scrubber fitted sisters PACIFIC JEWELS/TREASURES 115K DWT DAEHAN 2016 were reported sold to Vietnamese buyers in the high USD 60Ms, this represents a strong firming in the market following from two sales in February of scrubber fitted 2018 built sisters POLAR BRIGHT/ACE 109K DWT DAEHAN 2018 for 65.5M USD. NAVE EQUATOR 49K DWT SPP 2009 was bought by Soechi at USD 26M, riding the high of the increasingly strong market with a near 20% rise in values since last year. Clearly buyers are backing this market, with TORM further establishing market position in a cash plus shares deal for 8x 2014-2015 MRs valued at USD 340M.
