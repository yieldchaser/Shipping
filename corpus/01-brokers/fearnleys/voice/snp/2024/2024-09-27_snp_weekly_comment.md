## Market Commentary

In the standout transaction of the week, Evalend took 2x Hengli scrubber fitted VLCC resales due for delivery in 2026 for USD 127M each. While modern ship values are holding fairly steady, there is much debate on the fate of older units and whether buyers are still there to pay yesterday’s prices. That said, undercutting the last done on c.10-year-old tanker could provide owners with a straightforward way to take some money off the table at extremely healthy historical levels. This is just what Advantage tankers did, with VLCC ADVANTAGE VIRTUE (296K DWT BOHAI 2009) being sold to Chinese buyers at USD 51M. There are a number of stories to contend with on dry bulk this week. Extreme weather has spurred the capesize market onward, where a single day jump of 8% was registered on Thursday this week. This is while supramax values hinted that there may be an imminent correction. ZEN-NOH GRAIN PEGASUS (55K DWT OSHIMA 2010) sold for USD 15M, this is at a sizeable discount to last done similar VISAYA (56K DWT MITSUI 2010) at USD 16.8M. Levels that will excite buyers in what is a very liquid sector. Elsewhere, Newcastlemax MINERAL CLOUDBREAK (205K DWT HHIC-Phil 2012) was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 38.5M.
