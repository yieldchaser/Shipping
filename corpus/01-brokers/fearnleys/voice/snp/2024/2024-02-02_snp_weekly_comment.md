## Market Commentary

Shipping stocks were topic of discussion this week as Al Jazeera reported, and then retracted, that a ceasefire had been brokered between Israel and Hamas. This sent a number of stocks spiralling down over investor fears that this latest period of disruption to shipping would be over. Otherwise, it was a generally uneventful week, with 3 tanker sales reported. Scrubber fitted MILTIADIS JUNIOR 320K DWT SWS 2014 was sold for USD 80M, setting a new benchmark for 10 year old VLCCs, while Ardmore has kickstarted their fleet renewal with the acquisition of MR tanker ST. PAULI 49K DWT HMD 2017, the unit now the most modern of their fleet. Impending Lunar New Year could be the reason for the healthy liquidity this week, with participants rushing to complete deals before the market with inevitably takes a breather during the holidays. The Supramax subsector demonstrated why it is such a competitive space, with a total of 7 ships changing hands this week. SOLAR AFRICA 58K DWT TESS CEBU 2011 was the standout transaction in the surpas as it sold for a healthy USD 17.8M. Elsewhere, Agricore have purchased their first Capesize unit, buying scrubber fitted SEAMATE 177K DWT SWS 2010 for USD 26M.
