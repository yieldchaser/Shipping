## Market Commentary

The situation across the tanker second hand market has remained largely the same over the past months, despite the usual volatility in the charter space. The climb in asset values show no sign of respite with very strong level of buying enquiry for modern eco tonnage being met by an ever-thinning list of available candidates. Newbuildings become therefore the avenue of choice where despite the continuous headlines of placed orders, the relative percentage of fleet renewal is still historically low. A flurry of 2009 blt Aframax tanker sales this week, showing the high demand from the Far East for ships of this type and vintage. GSTAAD GRACE II/AFRAGOLD 115K DWT New Times 2009, sold for USD 42M and 41M respectively with the formed freshly surveys passed, this will help price other units in the market. Despite a fall in earnings on Capes this week, it seems buyers are not being discouraged by the resilient price targets from owners, with the overall vessel supply story trumping doubts of the longevity of this run. This is shown in scrubber fitted FRONTIER UNITY 181K DWT Koyo Dockyard 2012, selling to Greek buyers at USD 35M . A particularly busy week on the Supramax front, where Navios sold two Tess 58s NAVIOS CELESTIAL/CHRISTINE B 58K DWT Tsuneishi Zhoushan 2009 for USD 30M en bloc.
