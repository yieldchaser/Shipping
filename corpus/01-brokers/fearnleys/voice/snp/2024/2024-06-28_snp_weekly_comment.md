## Market Commentary

Softer sentiment across the tanker charter market won’t be of too much concern to owners as the overall picture remains strong. Tanker SNP has lagged behind dry this year in terms of liquidity, and it seems unlikely to change anytime soon particularly as we enter the summer months. The Japanese controlled scrubber fitted Aframax PUSAKA JAVA 108K DWT TSUNEISHI 2018 invited offers on 25.06.24 and is now understood to have been committed at rgn USD 68M to EPS. Elsewhere, Kyklades Maritime bought the scrubber fitted C. PROSPERITY 313K DWT HHI ULSAN 2009 at USD 53M, a slight discount against last done given the ship has her special surveys due. Despite a slower week on the spot market, dry values continue to push upwards. Belships announced this week that along with declaring a purchase option on BELMAR 63K DWT SHOEI KISEN 2021, they have agreed to also purchase an unnamed Japanese built 2024 64K DWT Ultramax at USD 41M, setting the pace for the rest of the market and showing that there are genuine buyers at levels only previously speculated. In other news, Pioneer have followed up their recent supramax purchase with the acquisition of two Newcastlemax sisters MINERAL CHARLIE/MAUREEN 205K DWT HHIC PHIL INC 2012 at USD 81.6M en bloc.
