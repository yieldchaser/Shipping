## Market Commentary

The tanker story remains unchanged from recent trends, with relatively modern (younger than 15 yo) sales candidates hard to come by, resulting in low liquidity. Money continues to flood the newbuilding yards giving an idea of the future make up of the global fleet, where we are seeing advancements in designs and fuelling, this week seen in the first ammonia dual-fuel aframax tankers being ordered by MISC (through shipping arm AET). In the second-hand market, Tsakos sold IZUMO PRINCESS (105K DWT SUMITOMO 2007) for USD 37.1M. The bulk market continues to see fairly strong liquidity despite prices making many buyers in the market feel uneasy, even with the general positive market sentiment. Modern tonnage is still very popular with YMK QUARTET (82K DWT Jiangsu Yangzi-Mitsui 2021) being sold at USD 35M to Greek buyers. FEDERAL ISLAND (63K DWT TADOTSU SHIPYARD 2017) was sold for USD 32M, a firming against the last done 2017 blt unit SANTA JOHANNA (61K DWT SHIN KURUSHIMA 2017) at USD 30.5M back in February.
