## Market Commentary

An unsurprisingly slow week in terms of SnP transactions as markets are slowly finding their post-holiday rhythm. Subdued summer freight markets are consolidating a relatively cautious sentiment with a number of players anticipating a possible value correction on the short term especially on older tonnage. A dynamic that would see owners exploring potential sales in hope to capture last done levels that remain historically attractive. A consensus that however remains far from negative with a widely shared positive long-term sentiment. On the Tanker front only one sale to report this week with the 20 year old Aframax “SILVERA” (Abt 115k dwt Hyundai 2004 ) being sold at Usd 27m. Levels that remain in line with recently concluded business.
