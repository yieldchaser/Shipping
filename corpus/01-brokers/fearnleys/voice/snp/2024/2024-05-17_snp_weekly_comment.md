## Market Commentary

Another quiet week in terms of volume on second hand tanker sales, rather in keeping with recent trends. In the standout transaction, Dee4 sold MR DEE4 LARCH 49K DWT HYUNDAI VINASHIN 2016 at USD 41M, comfortably eclipsing the last comparable Vinahsin MR transaction of scrubber fitted TRF BERGEN 49K DWT HYUNDAI VINASHIN 2015 which sold for USD 36M back in October 2023. This shows yet again how far MR prices have firmed in the recent run. Elsewhere, NIPPON PRINCESS 105K DWT SUMITOMO 2008 was sold at USD 41M, this transaction likely will be a comparable for the numerous units on the market in this age/size range. It will also be interesting to see whether prices continue to firm over the summer, usually a quiet time for tanker SNP. Modern ultramax units continue to be in high demand and are priced accordingly. HMM bought SSI PRIVILEGE 63K DWT JINLING SHIPYARD 2019 from Densay for USD 32.5M, with SS/DD due, the Turkish outfit flipping the unit they bought earlier this year for USD 30.3M, an unusually quick turnaround on such a modern unit. Elsewhere Agricore paid USD 35.75M for EL GRASSO 181K DWT IMABARI 2012, priced in line with recent Japanese capesize sales of this age.
