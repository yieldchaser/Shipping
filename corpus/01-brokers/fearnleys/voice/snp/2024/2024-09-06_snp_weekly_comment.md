## Market Commentary

There is a ‘back to school’ feel in the market this week, with most players returning from their seasonal holidays. In the tanker space, MRs are continuing to hold value against last done deals in the market, with Scorpio selling the scrubber fitted sisterships STI SAN ANTONIO / TEXAS CITY (50K DWT SPP 2014) at an enbloc price of USD 85M. Levels that are becoming increasingly attractive for owners that so far have resisted the temptation of these historical highs. A sentiment particularly well illustrated by the increase in availability on older large tankers, with some owners looking to exit positions they likely fought aggressively for 18 months ago. The dry space continues to dominate in terms of S&P activity, with the sector being the preferred avenue second only to newbuildings, for profit reinvestment for owners. While COSCO made the headlines revealing a 42-ship newbuilding order, there appears to be an increase in Japanese controlled bulkers, which will help set pricing benchmarks going forward for the rest of the year. Much of the liquidity remains in the older units, particularly this week on the capesize front. Surveys due AZURE OCEAN (180K DWT IMABARI 2007) was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 25M.
