## Market Commentary

No tanker sales to report this week, further reinforcing our previous comments on lower liquidity being the new norm in the sector. Despite this lower liquidity environment, there are sure to be smiles across the faces on all stakeholders throughout get-togethers at Posidonia next week. A more eventful week across the dry SNP market before an inevitable slowdown as the industry unites in Athens. We are still seeing limited modern sales, with the majority of transactions happening around the 15-year-old mark. Pricing remains at a disconnect with earnings, despite overall general bullish feeling towards the charter market, thus proving the overall vessel supply story is the dominant factor in the current run on asset values. Large handysize YASA VIOLET 40K DWT JIANGMEN NANYANG 2024 was sold to Pan Ocean for USD 34M, showing the premium buyers are willing to pay for a brand-new ship on the water. There looks to be a sizeable uptick in 15-year-old capesize values as EASTERN WINDFLOWER 175K DWT NEW TIMES 2010 sold to Chinese Buyers for USD 28M, eclipsing the last done on a New Times cape with 1-year younger ex MINERAL BRUGGE 175K DWT NEW TIMES 2011 selling in March for USD 26M, representing a near 10% increase in merely three months.
