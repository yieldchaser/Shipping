## Market Commentary

After some optimism last week tanker rates again face some overall pressure. That is, however, not out of the ordinary for this time of the year, and everything looks set for normal seasonality to the upside in coming weeks and months. TC rates have not been immune to these summer doldrums but hold at overall solid levels – especially for longer durations. This bodes well for values in the near to medium term, however any disappointment in earnings will put values for the older ones firmly at risk. Values for modern tankers are, and will remain resilient but we see rather limited upside potential from present levels. Tangible deals for the very modern ones are few and far between but following its acquisition of three modern VLCC’s from Korean owners in May, Bahri made another swoop this week for nine modern, scrubber-fitted units from Capital Group reportedly for abt Usd 1 bn en-bloc. Prices agreed are in line with our value estimates. Earnings in the Capesize and Ultramax segments are at solid levels, whereas Kamsarmax earnings continues to slide. However, the imminent US and Canadian grain season could reverse this trend. Coal demand in Asia will eventually improve in Q4, so earnings in the Ultramax and Kamsarmax segments will likely average in the mid or even high teens through the remainder of this year. In the Capesize segment, the weakening fundamentals in China could result in a near-term correction in earnings. However, seasonal factors like Indian coal demand and increased Guinean bauxite shipments should support the market in Q4. We have seen a bumper dry SNP actitivity YTD and with this expected back-drop on earnings, the briskness should continue once the holiday season has passed.
