## Market Commentary

Bahri week provided a welcome distraction for the tanker market this week as owners find themselves waiting for what would be a very welcome seasonal boost. The second-hand market has struggled for liquidity throughout this year, and it looks like the final six weeks of 2024 will be no different. The list of sales candidates is growing as owners look to capture historical highs, but investors are uneasy given a lull in rates and ongoing geopolitical uncertainty. The incoming Trump administration could shake up the market with a combination of increased exports and stringent tariffs, however, it is too early to tell and is difficult to predict. In the small chem tanker space, KIISLA/SUULA (14k DWT 2004/5 VIANA DO CASTELO)*Ice 1A sold en bloc for USD 12M. On dry, rallying Capesize spot rates are providing a ray of positivity against the overall softening sentiment. Liquidity remains consistent, and pricing continues to slide, although there will be no rapid correction until rates fall further. While this is not headline news, the reality is that the majority of dry owners are sitting on debt free units which are comfortably clearing opex and returning a healthy profit, this means that while there are buyers, these owners will not allow themselves to be lowballed. In a segment where the value correction has been more pronounced, CL RIZHAO/SINGAPORE/TANJIN (81k DWT JIANGSU JINLING 2015-16) sold via auction en bloc at USD 68M.
