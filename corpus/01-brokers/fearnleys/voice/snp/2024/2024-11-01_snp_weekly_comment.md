## Market Commentary

Tanker sales have stuttered throughout the year and continue to do so despite the ever-growing list of candidates. A slight spike though was seen this week courtesy of George Procopiou’s Dynacom selling 3x ice 1A Panamax Tankets ICE ENERGY/FIGHTER/VICTORY (70K dwt Onomichi 2006) to Middle Eastern buyers at USD 72M. Sellers of J19 JAL SIDDHI (19K dwt Kitanihon 2006) managed to catch a buyer in a market space where interest is shrinking rapidly against falling TC rates, selling the unit for USD 20.1M. The dry market was less active than usual this week as values begin to noticeably slide, in what could be seen as the first move in levelling out the well documented imbalance between asset values and charter rates. That said, rates for the most part aren’t yet in a position where they are causing hurt to owners, sitting comfortably above opex. Ultramax ERIN MANX (63k dwt Tess Zhoushan 2020) sold at USD 32.5M, helping price other eco Chinese candidates of similar age. The dry sector is likely to be the first avenue for investors should asset values continue to correct, with many buyers watching this space intently.
