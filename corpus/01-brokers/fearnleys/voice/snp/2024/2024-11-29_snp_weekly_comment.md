## Market Commentary

Slow week again for second hand tankers, though there is increased appetite being shown from Far Eastern buyers, particularly on larger tonnage. Whether or not this translates into increased liquidity is yet to be seen, however, with the winter market noticeably absent, it is not expected that these buyers will rush to transact. On smaller sizes, handy tanker MAERSK BERING (29K DWT GSI 2005)*ice 1C was bought by Chinese buyers for USD 15.55M. A clear direction for Dry cargo markets with consensus among most market players that there is still some way to go for this ongoing correction. Sentiment which is keeping liquidity high with some Sellers deciding to catch the falling knife at what remain historically attractive levels, while Buyers seeing increasing value in these correcting benchmarks. This week has seen flurry of capesize activity with scrubber fitted capesize sisters K.CONFIDENCE/K. VICTORY (181K SASEBO 2012-13) inviting offers this week and being traded around USD 32-33M each, while the scrubber fitted CAPE DREAM (179K DWT HHI ULSAN 2011) was sold at USD 28M to Chinese buyers.
