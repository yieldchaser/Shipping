## Market Commentary

Activity remains subdued in the tanker SnP markets with a resilient bid/offer spread still too wide to bridge. Some movement must be noted however, with a number of potential sellers gradually breaking rank and recalibrating their price expectations. It remains to be seen how much of a correction will be enough to entice the number of buyers patiently sitting on the sidelines. One sale to report, with VLCC TAIGA 311K DWT MITSUI 2007 sold to Chinese Buyers at USD 44.5m. In the Dry segment, Capesize rates have some tailwind at the end of this week, with average spot rates moving up approx 25%. This should give some breathing space to owners speculating on their next move. CAPE LAUREL 180K DWT IMABARI 2010 has been sold after a number of parties showed interest and in the end fetching mid USD 28m from Chinese buyers. For vintage tonnage, owners continue to cash in on healthy levels such as for sisters CAPTAIN MIKE & IOANNIS M 87K DWT IHI 2005 which were sold at USD 25m en-bloc to ever-present Chinese buyers.
