## Market Commentary

Steady activity in the Dry Cargo sectors with healthy amount of transactions despite a relative weakening in sentiment. The Kamsarmax sector has seen a handful of vessels changing hands with values continuing to show resilience against an increasingly challenging environment. As mentioned in last week's wrap up, pressure on values seems to be most felt on the Supramax front. A correction partly due to the heighten liquidity usually seen in the sector which makes this specific asset class more reactive to price volatility. Notable pick up in buying enquiry on the Capesize front where we expect further activity in the near future. Despite the limited amount of sales to report on the Tanker front, this week continue to see positive momentum driven by increased levels of buying enquiry. We are indeed witnessing a strong buying appetite with signs of a number of ongoing negotiations; an appetite however that is met with a fast-moving target by sellers who have been long awaiting for market rebound and who are now revising their ideas upwards in light of what seems to be an unanimous consensus of even better days to come.
