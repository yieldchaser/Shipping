## Market Commentary

Dry asset values are gradually aligning to the lower earnings across the board with sellers willing to take a 10-15% reduction in price compared to recent highs. Notable sale this week is the scrubber-fitted 2015 Japan built Ultramax ULTRA TRUST at Usd 27.8m to Eagle Bulk. This compares with the sale end July of the non-scrubber fitted GOLDEN CECILIE and GOLDEN CATHRINE also 2015 built Japan at USD 31.5m each. In the near future, non-eco Chinese built vessels will take the biggest hit in values. The next benchmark deal for Capesize will be FRONTIER TRIUMPH (2012/Imabari/181k dwt), which is inviting inspections in Singapore on 12th September. Last comparable sale was the two year older MINERAL HAIKU (2010/Koyo/180k dwt) reported sold for Usd 34m in May. Tankers with ice class 1A remain in hot demand as seen by the sale of Aframaxes ALBA and ARISTODIMOS built 2005 and 2006 respectively at a firm Usd 65m en-bloc. Both vessels are scrubber-fiited.
