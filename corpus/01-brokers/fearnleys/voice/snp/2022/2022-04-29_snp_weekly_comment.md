## Market Commentary

Deal of the week being Delta Tankers’ purchase of Suezmax STENA SUPREME built 2012 Samsung at Usd 37.5 mill basis prompt delivery FE with SS and BWTS due by the end of June. Last comparable sale was in March when DENSA ORCA also built 2012 Samsung was sold for Usd 32.5 mill with her SS imminently due as well. Even allowing for the fact that the Stena vessel has electronic main engine, this represents a hike of 12-14% in a short time. Aframax, Suezmax and MR are all hot commodity and VLCC values will also be following suit. Last done prices are becoming less relevant with sellers gaining the upper hand. Euronav has confirmed the acquisition of two VLCC's from Hartree, both scrubber fitted DSME units - GHILLIE built in 2019 and CHELSEA in 2020 and the en-bloc price is Usd 179 mill; a tick down compared to what has been speculated in advance. Euronav also confirmed the sale of four non eco VLCCs built 2011 and 2012 for an en-bloc price of Usd 198 mill. The confirmed price is in line with what we reported as being done to Sinokor in March. Transactions in the 33k dwt stainless steel segment are rare indeed but Stolt Tankers is now reported have acquired three such vessels from K-Line built 2012 and 2013 at an en-bloc price of Usd 76 mill. The last deal in this segment was in early 2021 when EGD Shipholding bought two vessels built 2009. Bulkers continue to change hands across all segments. Values are moving sideways with continued focus on the smaller sizes. However two Capesize sales to report this week. The limited supply of modern Capesize for sale and sellers unwilling to reduce asking levels, bode for new higher benchmark deals in the near future. To date, the last modern Capesize deal was in April for RED SAGE 2015 JMU built at Usd 47.5.
