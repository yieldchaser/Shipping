## Market Commentary

Another busy week in the tanker SnP market with vessels across the age curve transacted. Sentek Marine offloaded NEPTUNE M & PACIFIC M, 2019 HHI for Usd 90m each to HMM. The price reflects a below-market charter attached. Older VLCC tonnage continues to make strong gains as demonstrated by the sale of BRILLIANT JEWEL, 2002 DSME for a whopping Usd 40m to Chinese buyers. Two interesting Suezmax sales as well, both at prices slightly above our reference values. The dry market is beginning to feel the widely anticipated downward pressure in earnings, with some chartering managers seeing rates as low as Usd 6k/day for Supramax tonnage in the Far East. This has resulted in a somewhat quieter week on the sale and purchase front. There is no sense of urgency to move for sales candidates and buyers are as such increasingly adopting a wait and see attitude. In a notable transaction GREAT GLEN and GREAT AMBITION, 93K DWT 2010/2011 JIANGSU JINLING were sold en bloc for Usd 37m by Sinotrans.
