## Market Commentary

Dry cargo markets continue to capture most of the SnP activity with vessels changing hands across all segments. The sisterships “DARYA JYOTI & DARYA MOTI (2010/STX/80k dwt)”, have been sold to Greek buyers at USD 24m each representing a tick up in values for 1st generation Kamsarmax units of Korean design. Notable pick up in activity in the Panamax front with four mid 2000 built units changing hands; prices achieved continue to fall in line with most recent transactions. Buying enquiry remains particularly strong in the Ultramax segment where a number of vessels are being placed on the market for sale suggesting further activity in the near future. It has been a subdued week in terms of concluded business for Tanker markets which is however not particularly reflective of the buying appetite witnessed in most segments. A momentum in values seems to be getting hold with signs of ongoing negotiations suggesting a tick up in levels put on the table by potential buyers. Increased levels that should soon be supported by a higher volume of vessels changing hands providing consolidated benchmarks for future transactions.
