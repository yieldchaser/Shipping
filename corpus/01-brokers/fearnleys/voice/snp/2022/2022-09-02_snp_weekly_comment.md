## Market Commentary

Product tanker values are continuing their gradual climb with each reported sale exceeding their relative last done. Celsius Shipping has disposed of its older BWTS fitted LR2 tankers -  “CELSIUS ESBERG” & “CELSIUS EVERETT”  built 2009 and 2008 at New Times – respectively achieving Usd 33m and Usd 31m. The sale is marking yet another substantial jump considering that the last comparable sale of a Chinese built unit was that of the 2006 Dalian built “JAG LYALL” which was sold at Usd 26.2m end of July this year. This momentum is partly shared by crude segments, where the overall trend in values remains positive with DHT Tankers selling their 2008 built VLCC “DHT EDELWEISS” at Usd 37m, despite some fragility seen in earnings. The vessel was sold basis survey due with BWTS to be fitted at next docking. Despite having no sales to report on the MR front this week, activity remains high with signs of a number of negotiations suggesting further sales in the near future. Following a relative show of resistance from Dry cargo sellers we are definitely witnessing a number of owners caving to the ever lower bids shown by buyers who are looking to capitalise on the most recent correction. With every new deal reaffirming this downward trend, we expect an increased amount of sellers opting to pull the trigger sooner rather than later in hope to achieve what is still broadly considered as a high price point on the curve. A sentiment well illustrated by the sale of the 2010 Hyundai Vinashin built “CLARKE QUAY” which achieved Usd 17m.
