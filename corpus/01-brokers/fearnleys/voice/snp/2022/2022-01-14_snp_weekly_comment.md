## Market Commentary

While SnP markets are gradually finding their rhythm post the holiday season, this new year opens with no particular surprises with unchanged sentiment across both sectors. Dry cargo continues to draw healthy amounts of buying interest on the second hand front with freight continuing to provide support for potential buyers looking to take further positions in order to joy of these sustained returns. Supramax sector captures the bulk of activity with a handful of vessels changing hands at values which for now remain broadly in line with the end of year transactions. Saying that there have been signs of ongoing negotiations where bids have come at a discount to last done with potential buyers hoping to take advantage of the slight corrections in the chartering markets and this quieter seasonal period. Sellers however remain committed to their targets leading to a flat pricing curve. With Tankers closing the year with a widely shared sense of disillusion about a market recovery that seems to become increasingly evasive; most market players have started the year with caution. This subdued sentiment can be felt in part in the thinning level of enquiry with buyers taking a wait and see approach rather than going to meet an ever-stagnating offer. This being particularly apparent on the modern front as opposed to older tonnage where pockets of activity can be seen with a number of region 10 year old product carriers changing hands.
