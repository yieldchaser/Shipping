## Market Commentary

Dry cargo markets continue to see a flurry of SnP activity with up to 15 vessels changing hands this week. Notable developments on the Kamsarmax front where new benchmarks are being set. The 2007 built “OCEANIC” (Abt 82k Tsuneishi Fukuyama) was sold at Usd 21m while the 2010 built “MBA LIBERTY” (Abt 82k dwt built Tsuneishi Zhoushan) achieved Usd 22m. A comparison highlighting the relative discount carried by the Tess 82 built at the Zhoushan facility against a straight Japanese built unit. A discount however that remains minor compared to straight Chinese designs with the 2 year younger “YANGTZE XING HUA” (Abt 82k built Guangzhou Longxue 2012) also achieving Usd 22m. Elsewhere the Supramax market continues to joy high liquidity with strong levels of enquiry being met with a steady stream of sale candidates. Following the most recent pick up in activity it has been a quieter week in the tanker sector with most of the activity captured by the product sector. With strengthening chartering markets we expect a number of Buyers to come out the wood work in an effort to secure additional tonnage. It will be no easy feat though, with sellers expecting this gradual rate recovery to translate into higher asset values in the near future.
