## Market Commentary

Following weeks of escalation - the growing fears shared by most over the past few weeks have unfortunately become a reality with Russia’s military operations currently underway in Ukraine. A volatile geopolitical environment with undeniable ramifications across all markets which have met Russia’s aggression with large scale economic sanctions. Measures that have yet to be fully announced and which implications remain to be seen; an uncertainty that has left the tanker markets with an already tighter list of available tonnage. Adding to an already skewed fleet positioning east of Suez leading to rate gains in the Atlantic.

**Baltic**

 Aframax rates have also surged with most owners deciding to hold back unless they see a considerable premium from charterers. We expect these firming chartering markets to soon translate into a pick in SnP activity with potential buyers finding enough support from these improved rates to pull the trigger on future acquisitions. Dry cargo players are of course also endeavouring to digest the effect that these recent events will have on their market; direct impact is as expected felt in the Black Sea where Ukraine has officially halted its operations in several of its ports including from Yuznhy where force majeure has been declared. A disruption in exports for several key commodities coming out of the Black Sea that will have to be substituted should the situation persists. The tonnage list in the region is of course following suit with owners shying away from Black Sea fixtures. Similarly, we expect this tension on rates to provided support to an already steady value environment which could be mean further gains for Dry cargo assets across all segments.
