## Market Commentary

Compared to recent activity, the tanker market had a rather subdued week on the S&P front. However, this is not to be mistaken for a cooling market, quite the opposite. There seems to be no foreseeable end to the tanker bull run with charter markets and asset values remaining red hot, often leaving last done in the rear-view mirror. This is shown in Performance Shipping paying USD 44m for DPP trading LR2 FOS HAMILTON blt 2013 HHI, compared to recent comparable transaction RAFFLES HARMONY blt 2013 HHI which sold for USD 41.80m in October. Values in the dry space continue to feel downward pressure, following a notable squeeze in earning sentiment. Scrubber fitted Capesize HL SHINBORYEONG blt 2010 SAMHO HI was bought by Stealth Maritime for USD 23.8m, having invited offers across the market and following a number of recent Capesize transactions. In the Supra/Ultra sector, some rates are being reported at below OPEX levels in places indicating it could be a tough winter to come for market players. In an area of the size curve which will be carefully watched as the values correct, Ultramax ACHILLEAS blt 2012 Yangzhou Dayang sold for USD 22.30m to Eastern buyers basis ss/dd freshly passed and BWTS installed.
