## Market Commentary

Despite it be a quiet week in the snp market with a shrinking number of sales to report, headlines were stolen by the reported ongoing discussions between Scorpio and Hafnia for the acquisition of Scorpios LR1 fleet. The deal would comprise of 12 LR1 product carriers built in 2016 and 2015 with all units being scrubber fitted. It would mark a continuous consolidation in the Product space which has been led by Hafnia over the years through a number of large scale transactions. A move that seem to be welcomed by most market players who see it as a stamp of confidence in a market that has been falling short of expectations. In terms of concluded transactions this week, the dry markets have seen a handful of vessels changing hands notably in the Supramax segment which continues to capture the bulk of activity. Momentum seem to be subdued compared to last quarter’s frenzy, mainly due to market players finding their bearings in this new year and identifying the next investible sector.
