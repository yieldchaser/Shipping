## Market Commentary

Tankers in all asset classes remain in hot pursuit and sellers are clearly gaining the upper hand. SFL Corp swooped for four modern Suezmax tankers from Ciner. The vessels were on TC to Kock with one year left which have been cancelled in exchange for new 6 years TC contracts to Koch, adding approximately Usd 250 million to SFL’s fixed-rate backlog. The 2019 DSME built VLCC “C.GUARDIAN” invited offers this week with 7 buyers understood to having shown their cards and the vessel is now reported sold for Usd 98 mill. Last comparable sale was Hunter’s 2020 DSME built VLCC’s at Usd 96 mill. This week’s sale represents as such about 6% increase and the implied value for resale/2022 VLCC is now Usd 110 mill. Aframax/LR2 built 2009-2011 is a sweet-spot for many buyers with several deals expected to materialize soon. The same goes for MR where UOG this week added another two 2011 built units to its fleet. Suezmax tankers with ice class 1A are few and far between which, together with the vessels having open loop scrubbers, goes a long way in explaining the firm price Ridgebury achieved for its 2006 and 2007 built vessels. Dry asset values are under pressure. SNP volumes are low with buyers increasingly applying a wait-and-see attitude. Sellers will have to readjust price expectations or take cover for a while. Latter being the case for two 2015 JMU built Kamsarmaxes which have been withdrawn from the market as best on offer was region Usd 31.5 mill. In May, two 2015 sister vessels were sold for Usd 35.5 mill.
