## Market Commentary

Values for tankers are moving north. Our take is that values are up by 5-10%. Unconfirmed reports that Euronav has purchased from Hartree 2 x 2019/2020 scrubber-fitted DSME built VLCCs at USD 182-184 mill en-bloc, possibly with an element of shares as payment but the trend is clear. Suezmax STENA SUPREME 2012 SHI invited offers yesterday. The vessel is expected to be sold next week and price to be north of USD 35 mill. Delivery will be basis SS due in June. Last comparable sale is DENSA ORCA also 2012 Korean built which was sold for USD 32.5mio with her SS/DD promptly due early March. Purchase interest for MRs and Aframax is picking up and ice classed older tonnage is seeing a premium.   Dry market is moving sideways with focus on Panamax and Supra/Ultramax, however Capesize is again back in fashion. Our expectations are for healthy rates until end of Q2, with a more gloomy 2H of this year. Notable sales are the 2 x 2016 Japanese built Ultramaxes “NAVIGARE BACCA” and “TOMINI INTEGRITY” at USD 32.5 m and USD 33 m respectively. Worth noting that TOMINI reportedly purchased the “TOMINI INTEGRITY” at USD 19 m July 2020. We expect the values of modern tonnage to retain steady, with a potential upside in the near future with lack of quality tonnage available for sale.
