## Market Commentary

A ground-breaking week for tanker asset values, with Bahri paying USD 112m each for 2 x 2020 built VLCC’s from Vitol which in our book translates to about USD 120m for a brand-new Korean built vessel with scrubber. This is up about 16% from mid this year. Vitol also reported to be selling a resale LR2 delivering 2023 which we understand comes with a three-year TC back. Another flurry of transactions across the dry cargo segments which are illustrating the underlying market correction experienced over the recent weeks. Notably the Japanese controlled Capesize NAVIOS OBELIKS built 2012 Koyo which has been sold to Synergy at USD 29m, this follows the two vessels sold by JP Morgan last week. The Supramax segment continues to feel the downwards pressure with a number of sellers in the market looking to catch an ever sharper falling knife. Tess-58 BULK CARINA 2016 Cebu was reported sold at USD 22m, while lower down the age curve the AZZURA built 2004 IHI sold at USD 13m. There is an ever-increasing number of vessels for sale within the Supramax sector, with owners and opportunists making plans to take advantage of the potential bargains to be had.
