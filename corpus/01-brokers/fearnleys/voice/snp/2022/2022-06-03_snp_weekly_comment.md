## Market Commentary

Crude asset values are stabilizing at newfound higher levels, except for modern Aframax where the sale of RUMFORD (107k 2012 Tsuneishi) at Usd 32.8 mill represents another uptick. The vessel has SS passed and BWTS fitted. Certainly more buyers than sellers around with the sweet spot being Aframax/LR2 and MR built 2007-2012. Dry asset values are moving sideways except for Supramax where values continue to gain. Handysize are still a favorite with buyers whereas Capesize deals are few and far between.
