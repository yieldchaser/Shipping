## Market Commentary

As widely anticipated, the dry cargo markets have witnessed a number of corrective sales this week. Navios have sold the Supramax NAVIOS ULYSSES 2007 Oshima at a price of USD 14.5m providing a much needed benchmark for a market that is seeing a number of similar candidates being placed for sale. What remains to be seen is whether this will spur on the number of transactions or encourage scepticism as we head into a potentially challenging Q4. More modern tonnage is proving desirable, with scrubber-fitted Ultramax NORD BARENTS 2019 Oshima selling at USD 33.3m. The tanker market is alive with excitement and rumour, leaving market players wondering whether this buoyant sentiment can translate into previously unthinkable asset prices. Sales of older ships at firm levels such as VLCC VIKI 2000 Samsung HI at USD 29.5m are causing a conundrum as assets further up the age curve fail to entice buyers into chasing owner’s price ideas. Ridgebury Tankers though, have cashed in with 3 sales concluding this week MRs RIDGEBURY BIRCH & ACACIA 2006 Shin Kurushima at USD 17.5m each, and RIDGEBURY VOYAGER 2008 HMD at USD 19.5m.
