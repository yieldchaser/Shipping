## Market Commentary

As the tanker charter market adjusts to new Russian crude regulations, the S&P space remains strong. VLCC SEAKING (318K DWT 2005 HHI) was sold by Thenamaris for USD 51.5m, proving there is still no shortage of suitors for ageing large tankers. Modern scrubber fitted Aframax SOUTHERN ROUSE (108K DWT 2018 TSUNEISHI) sold for USD 62m, having invited offers at the end of November, which should help price the number of Aframax units in the market. Timing will be crucial in the dry market as players expect a run in Q1/Q2, but also debate on the impact of new regulations, and the reliability of China easing their COVID restrictions. This week’s S&P activity shows that owners must be busy pondering their options as only a handful of sales were seen. Open Hatch Supramax RIO CHOAPA (50K DWT 2012 OSHIMA) sold for USD 16.5m which is basis SS due. Kamsarmax NORD POLLUX (81K DWT 2016 TSUNEISHI CEBU) sold for USD 25.2m which we understand reflects delivery in the Sellers' option until May 2023.
