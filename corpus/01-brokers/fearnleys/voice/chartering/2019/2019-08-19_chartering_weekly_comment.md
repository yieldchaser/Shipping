## Market Commentary

West This week has seen a weakening in the Western premium which built up previously. In the west, this was driven mostly by an abundancy of relets through which players looked to benefit from the higher Western rate. Despite limited vessel availability in 1H Sep, there are a larger amount of ships in the second half of the month. It will be interesting to see whether or not this leads to a further reduction of Western freight rates. East There were dynamic movements in the Baltic this week. It weakened quickly from $60 to low $50’s, before strengthening to $60 again. This sudden change was driven by two relets deals which pushed down the market. As those relets were gone and many vessels headed West to seek a premium, the East market turned firm again. Moreover, massive Indian demand finally returned – we expect 5-6 spot inquiries in 1H Sep, which could help tighten the Eastern vessel position list. Beside the 1H Sep market change, several Saudi FOB cuts haven't affected anything in the market yet.
