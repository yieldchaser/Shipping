## Market Commentary

WEST: The West VLGC market has not really seen much activity so far this week. September liftings ex USG are seemingly more or less done, and the vessel overhang going into October is rather limited with a handful of vessels available. Towards the end of this week there have been a number of tenders in WAF and Marcus Hook, offers for which are due on Friday. Indicative levels being discussed so far show a slight premium to the Baltic. EAST: Vessels currently discharging in the Far East are being watched carefully as delays due to ullage issues have led to a backlog at disports. This could potentially leave early October vessel supply relatively tight in the USG and elsewhere. Otherwise, the AG has been relatively quiet, with a few Indian cargoes expected to be floated at the end of this week.
