## Market Commentary

Subdued activity in the VLGC market this week as the world anxiously awaits further developments around the Straight of Hormuz. The vessel balance in USG/USEC remains largely unchanged, however, a narrowing arbitrage is halting new activity unless freight drops significantly from last done levels of mid USD 260’s basis HCvP. On the back of the shear volatility in the product market seen in the recent month or so and the arbitrage movements parallel to a potentially opening of the Straight of Hormuz, it is hard to be adamant of short term freight movements should an opening delay further.
