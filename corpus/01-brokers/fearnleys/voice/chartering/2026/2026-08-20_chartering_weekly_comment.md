## Market Commentary

Fixture activity continued this week, with several concluded, all for September dates. The position list has tightened further, down to 14 ships from yesterday's 17. Positions are stacking as: 5 ships second decade September, 6 last decade September, and 3 first decade October. FOB interest is reported for first decade September, but with no ships currently sitting in that window, it may get pushed into the second decade, adding further pressure to an already thin 5-ship count there. Fixing levels have been choppy, ranging between USD 255 and 280pmt this week, (subject to vessel type and routing options) though levels have firmed back toward USD 270pmt more recently. With fresh fixtures and a tightening list, we believe freight will stay flat. Panama Canal costs remain a live factor in routing decisions: yesterday a northbound slot auction cleared at USD 4.2m, with a southbound auction at USD 851k.
