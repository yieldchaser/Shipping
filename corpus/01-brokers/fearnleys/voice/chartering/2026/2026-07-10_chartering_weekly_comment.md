## Market Commentary

Sentiment in the VLGC sector improved markedly during the week, pushing USG–East TCEs above USD 100,000/day after a sharp rebound from the lows recorded last week. In the West, an active fixing pace has significantly reduced prompt tonnage availability, leaving only a handful of July positions open. As a result, freight levels have moved higher, with H/C rates climbing from the low USD 160s to around USD 210. Owners remain optimistic on the near-term outlook, supported by improving MEG demand and an increasing preference for Cape routing over Panama. At the same time, the firmer MEG outlook continues to shape decisions, with vessel positioning increasingly reflecting expectations of stronger Middle East activity and sustained support for freight levels.
