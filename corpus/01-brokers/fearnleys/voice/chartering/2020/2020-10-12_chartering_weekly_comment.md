## Market Commentary

EAST Shipping activity East of Suez has been rather slow this week with most eyes on the West. However, one Indian major is still in the market for a ship off early November, and another fob tender for mid-November load in the Middle East has also received some attention. Last done deal was to a Chinese trading company reported at usd 63 baltic, but otherwise there is not much else to report. We are still waiting for next month acceptances from Aramco which will provide final clarity for the November lifting program. Meanwhile, turnaround time in some southern and northern Chinese ports are still very slow making ship schedules more uncertain, and it is expected that freight rates will remain firm for the weeks to come. WEST After a slow start to the week owing to large parts of the US being off due to the Columbus Day, the market activity indeed picked up the pace. Uncovered cargoes versus open vessels seeking employment has been largely in favour of the latter and as such we saw rates jumping from mid/low USD 90’s H/C fixed last week to USD 103 H/C within a matter of days. The market remains tight on vessel supply for November, however, we expect chartering activity to keep going albeit at a slower pace and that owners’ and trader relets’ freight ideas will keep north of last done.
