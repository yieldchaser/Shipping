## Market Commentary

In spite of several holidays around the world, the VLGC market has seen a handful of fixtures being concluded both East and West this week. Non-booked southbound Panama delays are still causing a painful headache (14 days waiting at the time of writing) for both owners and charterers alike, adding on largely to total freight cost as well as making forward planning more difficult. The main fixing activity out of the US is currently focused around 1H Feb dates, while the MEG market still focusing on last decade Jan. We have started to see some delays in the East where one oil major already has taken in a replacement ship on subjects at a healthy premium to last Baltic print. We would not be surprised if we were to see more delays going forward adding fuel to the already burning hot market.
