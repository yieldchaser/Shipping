## Market Commentary

WEST: Little has changed from last week in terms of market sentiment in the VLGC market West of Suez. The majority of players remain bearish with regards to freight rates, while bunkers have continued to rally some 10% at time of writing. These things together have put pressure on earnings for those with tonnage. However, there is a glimmer of hope within the product market, where netbacks are looking slightly better and there seems to be healthy interest on both the buy and sell side of FOB cargoes. In theory, this could mean greater demand for tonnage and may be the start of a shift towards a more balanced supply and demand picture on the VLs. EAST: In Middle East the Adnoc acceptances were posted Tuesday, and in addition to their term cargoes there are talk that they may also have some spot fob availability. Meanwhile there have also been a couple of fob tenders discussed from other suppliers for loading within 1st decade July. But with ample freight availability dominated by trader relets, freight rates remain under pressure. There is an overhang of about 15 vessels from June which are likely set to take some waiting time as fixing window is now slowly moving towards July. In terms of activity thus far this week one Indian major booked a ship for end June loading, while a trader was reported fixing a ship for mid June load in the MEG.
