## Market Commentary

EAST With the final release of Saudi acceptances coupled with a FOB tender the MEG market has been rather active this week. Together with a tightening market in the West, we have started to see freight ideas strengthening throughout the week. In addition to a handful of relets still available, all three owners may still have one or two ships that could make January dates, subject to them not being absorbed into the West market. Looking at the volumes already fixed for the month, the remainder of January seems more or less balanced and we expect freight to remain strong. WEST The West market saw a bit of a Christmas rush during the last few days prior to the holiday. The Houston/Chiba via Panama rates are set around high USD 150’s per ton and looks set to stay put for the next handful of deals to be done for the first half of February loading. Vessel supply is anything but ample and we expect the market strength to persist for the time being.
