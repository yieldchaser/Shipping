## Market Commentary

EAST With China off on longer holidays this week the market has been fairly inactive. We saw a couple of ships being fixed for October laycans at the beginning of the week, but apart from these deals there is not much to report. We are still left with some possible length from traders during end Oct/early Nov, but some of these ships might eventually be absorbed into own programs or sent back West later. However, owners and traders would be inclined to wait until the Adnoc acceptances are out from early next week before making any decisions on where to send their open ships, as these acceptances could potentially create more interest for freight from early November in the MEG. WEST Activity has indeed been looming rather than booming this week in the West. A widening arb and healthy FOB appetite (though lack of FOB sellers right now) will in all likelihood increase activity on the shipping side sooner rather than later. Vessel availability is largely controlled by only a couple of owners and as such we expect the market to remain firm around mid/low USD 90’s Houston/Chiba before potentially even increasing again. A West Africa cargo early November and a USG cargo tender ex Nederland 6-7 Nov seems to be the talk of the town and would indeed provide a good gauge for the market.
