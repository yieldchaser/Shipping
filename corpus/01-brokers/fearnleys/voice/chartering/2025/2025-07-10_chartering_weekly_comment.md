## Market Commentary

EAST A total of five ships, East and West, were placed on subs yesterday. Three of these were done in the Middle East, bringing the total spot count in the region up to 23 for July. Six of these are so far Indian PSU cargos, and we do hear some talks that at least one more Indian July cargo might come to market at a later stage. With an already high spot count in July, there might not be a whole lot of cargos left for this month, but given what’s left of ships just a few more cargos is enough for the balance to remain tight. WEST Spot fixing activity out of the US Gulf (USG) remains steady, even as traders continue to describe the market as challenging. July positions are largely covered, while August has already seen 17 spot fixtures concluded in the mid-to-high $120s to $130 HCvP range—9 in the first decade and 8 in the second. The emergence of an anticipated market cargo has helped stimulate further activity. Vessel availability appears balanced for the remainder of the month, with major owners currently dominating the position list. Looking ahead, the market is expected to remain firm.
