## Market Commentary

EAST Although activity saw a slight increase this week, freight rates have failed to maintain previous levels. A total of four Indian PSU tenders and a few additional cargoes have been fixed, bringing the fixture count to seven at the time of writing. However, with a still extensive and relet-heavy position list, overall market sentiment remains subdued. WEST This week has seen increased activity out of the US Gulf, with four fixtures reported - two for the last decade of September and two for the first decade of October. One fixture was also seen out of WAF with the Soyo tender. Freight has softened, fixing levels between $149.5 and $150.5. A significant portion of the VLGC fleet is either sailing or expected to sail via Cape (laden and ballast respectively) which in turn supports freight for prompt activity given that the market is tight on vessel supply and confined to few players controlling the available tonnage. Additionally, concerns over Panama Canal congestion are contributing to the preference for Cape routing.
