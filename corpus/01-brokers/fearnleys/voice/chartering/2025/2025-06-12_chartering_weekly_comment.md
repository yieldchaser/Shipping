## Market Commentary

EAST June spot fixing is already on par with March (25 spot deals ex MEG including Yanbu) – the strongest spot lifting month(s) so far this year. Market expects a few more deals towards the very end of the month before July fixing takes the focus. The open end June ships in the East might eventually be absorbed by US cargos, meaning although there’s a selection of open ships still off June dates – freight is set to remain stable. WEST USG/East freight is currently trading at parity with MEG/East rates. As of today, 16 spot fixtures have been concluded for July. Based on historical monthly trends, this implies that approximately 14 cargoes remain to be fixed for the month. U.S. gas production data released yesterday showed a build of 4 million barrels—the second consecutive week of higher-than-expected inventory growth. Despite this, the East arbitrage has not widened as anticipated, largely due to hedging activity in the paper market. As a result, freight levels are holding just a few dollars below the last done rate of USD 122/mt
