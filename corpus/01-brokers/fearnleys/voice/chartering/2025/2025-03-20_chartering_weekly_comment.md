## Market Commentary

EAST Substantial delays on nomination dates from Saudi this month together with an industry gathering in Guangzhou, China have kept the East market relatively slow so far this week. Despite low activity levels we’ve seen big gains on freight with last done at $58 RT/C and $62.5 equivalent for destination India. April fixing is just getting started in the Middle East with 6 spot fixtures done so far. Going forward freight is set to remain firm WEST Available tonnage for April is becoming increasingly scarce. With over 30 spot fixtures already concluded, the month is nearly fully booked. This tightening market has pushed freight rates upward, rising from the low $90s to around $110 per metric ton for a Houston/Chiba via the Panama Canal voyage. However, a few unfixed cargoes remain for late April, leading to higher asking rates compared to last done . It has been months since we saw a balanced to tight freight market in the US, and theoretical earnings are now yielding above $1.2 mill per month.
