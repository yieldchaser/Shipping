## Market Commentary

EAST The Middle East market is taking a drop as it looks like freight climbed too much too quickly. The gap in TCE earnings between East/West have seemingly become too big while the Arb is shut ex US, and we are seeing $ ideas coming into the mid-60s RT/CH from a peak of low 80s RT/CH at the beginning of the week on an India bound fixture. WEST Most of the attention on freight has been East of Suez with no deals reported in West this week. We have been hovering around cancellation levels in the US lately, and two players were reported to have cancelled liftings in May and June earlier today. Nevertheless, a couple of players have tested the market for freight in end June and early July, but it remains to be seen where the next deal is at.
