## Market Commentary

EAST Activity in the East is beginning to show signs of recovery, with several fixtures reported in the MEG and one out of Australia for November loading. The regional position list is gradually rebalancing, though sentiment remains subdued. A more pronounced shift is unlikely unless a surge of Indian PSU tenders materializes as so far, only one has emerged for early November. Freight levels appear to be stabilizing, but the overall tone remains soft. WEST The VLGC market in the West has seen a noticeable uptick in activity over the past week, with 11–12 fixtures concluded. This signals a modest recovery in momentum, although monthly fixing volumes in both hemispheres remain slightly below the year-to-date average. In the US Gulf, the landscape has shifted. Vessels are now arriving open and ready to load on prompt dates, a scenario not observed for some time. This development points to a loosening of the previously tight tonnage situation, granting charterers greater flexibility. Despite the increase in fixture activity, the market continues to face downward pressure, with freight rates softening and sentiment remaining cautious. TCE earnings are currently approximately USD 43,000–45,000/day, offering owners a respectable return amid an otherwise uncertain environment. For now, the market appears to have found a temporary floor, supported by the recent burst of hiring activity.
