## Market Commentary

EAST One market tender bound for India was fixed yesterday in the mid-$80s and an FOB tender towards the end of August is due later today, also attracting shipping interest. Freight is rising in both basins with the East currently leading the way with higher earnings. With cargo dates being relatively widely spread we do see a date dependent freight market. WEST Shipping activity west of Suez remains robust. A total of nine cargoes have been fixed for the third decade of August, bringing the total number of August USG fixtures to 29. Notably, this week also saw the first September fixture concluded, coming in slightly ahead of July levels. Currently, only two ships remain open for August laycans. Over the past 24 hours, market rates have risen from USD 135 to above USD 140, reflecting tightening tonnage and sustained demand. Looking ahead, market conditions are expected to remain firm, underpinned by limited vessel availability and continued chartering interest.
