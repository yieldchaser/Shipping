## Market Commentary

EAST The BLPG1 index continue to come off, and as a result nobody seems to be in a rush to move on upcoming shipping requirements. The only players that have tested freight since last week has been a handful of Indian majors, but each of these deals have all been concluded at a discount to the previous deal. We are however seeing stronger upstream production, and many of the Middle Eastern suppliers are dealing with higher inventory of LPG. We therefore expect to see more spot fob cargos in the market over the coming weeks. WEST We have seen the same for the West market. BLPG2 and 3 have been coming off rapidly and we had to enter double digits before freight resumed. We have seen a good handful of fixtures at the end of this week with last done concluded at USD mid 100s Houston/Chiba and low 60s Houston/Flushing. With the latest activity we count approximately 15 spot fixtures out of the USG/USEC so far in July. As the market finally seems to have found a floor and with several uncovered cargoes out there is plenty of room for more spot activity, albeit at rates somewhere in between capex and opex.
