## Market Commentary

EAST Shipping ex Middle East was moving slow until the release of Aramco acceptances on Tuesday. Several fixtures are on subs or concluded for end August and beginning September laycans at freight rates hovering around USD mid 50s RT/C per ton, and there are reports of more still uncovered in and around the first half of September. Looking ahead it should not be surprising that some owners already aim higher than last done numbers and that we'll see the market coming up slowly but surely. WEST This week we have seen approximately five deals done in the Western market, both for August and September dates at levels around low 90s H/C and 50 H/F. We saw the first August fixture over 50 days ago and with just a notch above 20 spot fixtures for the month August fixing finally looks to have come to an end as the last remaining ships have been absorbed. Moving into September we expect an uptick in both exports and spot activity as July and August have not been the strongest.
