## Market Commentary

EAST With many of the market participants East of Suez out of office most of this week things are moving slow so far. Direction for the remainder of May ex MEG will take shape in the coming days - consensus around freight looking long and should come off a bit further. While the remaining spot volumes for May is yet to be seen, even with a healthy ending to the month, market tonnage is looking to be oversupplied. WEST Players slowly but surely coming back from holidays. The Western freight market still looks strong and shows downward resistance in spite of missing activity. Last done fixture was concluded at mid/low USD 120s Houston/Chiba which results in a USD 10 west premium versus the BLPG1 benchmark route. At the time of writing there have been a lack of product trade with Far East due to various holiday observances, but for now the most important question is how much demand there will be for vessels 1st decade in June to tell where freight is going. Freight activity ex US has been quite subdued, only five US spot deals done so far in June, however, there is action looming.
