The fixing window is still mainly concentrated around mid-January, and the sentiment on freight continue to come off which is mainly due to lack of activity previously. For last decade January ex USG, would-be charterers are holding back on any fixing with the expectation that freight continue falling - the BLPG3 index has come off over 10 dollars the last 2 days. The position list for the rest of January is dominated by pure shipowners – specifically one owner with 5 available ships. We count 22 spot fixtures ex USG so far for January – only 4 fixed in 2H Jan. Last done January fixture concluded at high USD 190s H/C.
