## Market Commentary

EAST After QP and Petco product tenders were awarded the activity on freight has calmed down in the East. We currently count 11 fixtures concluded ex Middle East in November, whereas 8 deals done for 1st decade and 3 deals done for 2nd decade. We expect to see more demand for ships from mid-2H November, but as most ships for this window are still waiting on discharge orders for current voyages there are few ships with clear itinerary as of today. East Baltic is currently at a 6-7 dollar premium to West. One could expect that East market is about to top out unless there are cargos on the front that needs to be covered last minute. WEST While seeing the East moving fast and the premium growing bigger the West is currently not keeping the same pace. So far for November we have seen 27 spot fixtures out of USG/USEC and there should still be a handful of more deals to be done for 2H Nov. This period is looking very tight as we only see a couple trader relets available and with the arb still wide we should expect today's strong freight rates to maintain going forward.
