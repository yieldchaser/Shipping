## Market Commentary

EAST The fixing window has shifted to mid-2H August in the MEG. After seeing several deals concluded last week the market has calmed down, and there has not been too much to report thus far. A couple of Indian Majors have tested the market, but as the lineup of ships looks well supplied we don’t expect charterers to rush in for uncovered cargos. East freight is still trading at a premium to West, but we expect this gap to be further narrowed in the days to come. WEST This week we have seen deals done both in the USG and West Africa, but fixing activity still remains slow in the West. Last done deal is now concluded at USD mid/low 90s Houston/Chiba range, but it is likely freight has to drop even more before we see an uptick in activity unless the arb soon gives some support. If neither of this happens it is a possibility we soon will see prompt open ships in the USG as the month of August are just a few days away.
