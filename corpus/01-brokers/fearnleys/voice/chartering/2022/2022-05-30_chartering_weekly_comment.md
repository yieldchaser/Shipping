## Market Commentary

EAST One producer has been driving much of the activity in the Middle East lately, and they still keep active into the beginning of this week. After a rapid increase in freight rates recently we sooner or later had to see a correction, and that we got as the Baltic index dropped over USD 9 in a day. But there is still a small East premium at around USD 5,000 per day in market earnings compared to the West. Last done deal concluded out of the AG is around mid 90s Ras Tanura/Chiba. WEST The shipping balance in the West remains relatively tight and in control of few players and this is likely to continue as July fixing moves on. With the observance of a US holiday at the start of this week and UK off on Thursday and Friday the recent fixing rush has slowed down, but we expect it to pick up its pace again next week when all market players are back. There are still some uncovered cargoes left 1H July and it is expected that the players in need for shipping coverage this period will bring some further action to an already hot market. At the end of last week we saw deals done at USD 140 Houston/Chiba and around USD 80 Houston/Flushing, but this week we are closer to USD mid 130s H/C.
