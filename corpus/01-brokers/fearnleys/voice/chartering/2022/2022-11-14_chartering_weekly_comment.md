## Market Commentary

EAST Market expect Aramco acceptances to be released later today after which more 1H December cargos should appear. Any early cargos ex Saudi could potentially put further fuel to the already burning hot market. Last done ex Middle East is at 145, but most Owners holding for closer to 150. Once the full December program is known some additional relets could potentially be made available, but comparing the availability for December with expected spot volumes freight remains tight and is expected to remain strong. WEST Latest Baltic print is now showing close to a 20 dollar discount in the West before waiting in Panama as we see the Eastern freight rates climb even further. Three vessels have been put on subs so far this week with last done out of the USG concluded at around USD 200 H/C. In total we have seen a 21 spot fixtures so far for December ex USG, 7 of them in the first half and 14 in the second. There are 6-7 vessels left in USG in Dec and half of them might end up in MEG as Owners could catch a big East premium while also avoiding the heavily congested Panama Canal. Vessel supply for 2H December is looking tight.
