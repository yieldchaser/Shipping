## Market Commentary

EAST Aramco acceptances for June was finally released on Wednesday and there’s reports much of the tons being allocated in the second half of the month. A couple of FOB spot cargos have also been discussed due to some excess production. Latest ship currently on subs are reported in the low 80s ex Middle East, a somewhat 5 dollar jump from last done. With a diminishing West premium one could expect freight in the East to soon top out. However, the position list is quickly getting slimmer and as such we expect some upside still remains. WEST The western freight rates continue to rise and have now broken the 130-dollar mark for Houston/Chiba which we have not seen since the beginning of 2021. We do not see the rates decrease quite yet as the tight position list is currently dominated by pure shipowners and the arb remains wide. Fixing focus has now shifted towards the last decade of June with the majority of fixtures this week done with 20-31 June laycans as the last remaining vessels in mid June and end May recently got absorbed. There have been approximately 30 spot fixtures out of the USG/USEC so far in June, same as May, and we can expect this number to climb closer to 40.
