## Market Commentary

EAST With reports of substantial cuts in February Middle East volumes many have been expecting freight to stay weak East of Suez. Following a rush of fixtures in the West and increased demand for volumes in Far East Asia, the sentiment has shifted also in the Eastern loading region. Reports of a couple of deals in the 70s rt/c are already being circulated, and although second half February positions still looks lengthy – the option of sailing West into a hot freight market will push the floor upwards in the East despite the expected shortage in volumes. WEST After having seen a handful of spot fixtures getting done a couple days ago at low 100s H/C out of the USG we are now seeing last done concluded at high 120s H/C. In total we count a dozen spot fixtures in the west this week for March dates. At the time of writing it seems difficult finding an open position in ely March, but there could still be a couple more appearing as we await full discharge rotation.
