## Market Commentary

EAST November fixing in AG seems to be nearing an end with year to date lowest monthly spot count so far (16 fixtures). December has kicked off early with four confirmed deals at the time of writing - two of which are from Indian PSUs. This even before Adnoc acceptances for December has been published point to a continuously strong market. While the Panama situation evolves more and more vessels will pass by Middle East ports on the way to USG via Suez or Cape and as such we will see a longer theoretical position list in the East which could mean we will more often than not see East trading at a discount to the West going forwards. WEST At the time of writing, three spot fixtures have been observed in the West this week, bringing the total count to 18 deals out of the US Gulf/US East Coast for December dates. On the Western position list, only one vessel remains open for 1H December. Between 20-30 December, only 2 spot fixtures have been concluded, and the vessel supply this period is looking relatively tight. Every vessel on the West list is currently ballasting via Suez or Cape. The last done Neo-VLGC ex US Gulf was a few dollars below 240 H/C, while the latest Panamax fixture closed at 270 H/C. January fixing is already underway.
