## Market Commentary

EAST A good number of cargos fixed out of both main loading regions triggered by the correction seen this week. There are some signs of the market now finding its feet again – but AG in particular looks relatively lengthy for the remainder of July and some vessels will likely be kept in the region due to restricted trade. Alas, the fixing level going forward will rely upon volume throughput and it could start to look like July is getting close to finished meaning we should expect an overhang into August. WEST After seeing the first spot fixture this week concluded at mid 160s H/C we have now seen rates climb to mid 170 H/C as per last done ex USG. With that said we count 17 spot fixtures out of the USG/USEC so far in August. The vessel supply for the rest of the month is looking tight and with several uncovered cargoes ex USG, especially in the last decade, we should see the current strong rates in the West maintain as long as the arb supports.
