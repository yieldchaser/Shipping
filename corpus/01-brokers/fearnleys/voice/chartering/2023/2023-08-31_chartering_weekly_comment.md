## Market Commentary

EAST At the time of writing only one Indian PSU cargo has been fixed out of AG so far on September dates versus an average just above 5 the previous past three months. A second India cargo was quoted today flushing out a couple of other cargos which led to a race between charterers to secure the few ships that was left for their cargo laycans. With few vessels and a fierce competition for vessels freight have now spiked up by more than 20 dollars from last done previously this week. WEST We have seen a good amount of spot activity in the western market this week and now we count 23 spot fixtures for October ex USG/USEC – all done in 1H of the month. The first open position we find from 6 Oct onwards which also is the only position left for 1H Oct. For the vessels coming up in 2H Oct dates are still a bit uncertain for most of them and by the looks of it the vessel supply is very tight, but this period is still far ahead and we can therefore expect a couple more vessels to appear on the list when schedules are firm. Last done fixture concluded at low 180s H/C.
