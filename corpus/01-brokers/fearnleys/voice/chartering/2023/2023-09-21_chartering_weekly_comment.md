## Market Commentary

EAST After a small handful of late Indian cargo quotes hitting a very tight shipping market Owners have basically named their price and last done India deal now at above 200 RT/C equivalent and latest non-Indian done at low 180s RT/C. The regular fixing window in the East is now well into last decade of October where sentiment from both a strong US market and the record levels concluded for India deals have spilled over to the Ras Tanura - Chiba route. This means we are fixing more than a month ahead where itineraries are somewhat blurry for most vessels and the asking price and last done is reflecting same. WEST Starting off the week we found the BLPG3 index at low 220s H/C and in just a few days we have seen it jump to about 250 H/C - this rate gives a daily TCE of approximately 150k. Although seeing these high rates the West is still trading at a discount of about 30 pmt compared to the East. At the time of writing we count 6 spot fixtures out of the USG/USEC for November dates and the position list for 1H Nov is already looking extremely slim showing only a few workable positions left for the uncovered cargoes out there. With that said there should be more room for western freight rates to continue increasing.
