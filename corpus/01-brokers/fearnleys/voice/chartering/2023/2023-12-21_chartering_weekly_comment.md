## Market Commentary

EAST Christmas is just around the corner which usually slow things down for a bit, and the last Baltic print for the year will be this coming Friday the 22nd of December. Yesterday proved to be a day of heightened activity, and currently, a few cargoes remain uncovered. The prevailing sentiment indicates a growing momentum that suggests an upward trajectory in freight rates today. While there's a cargo on End December dates in the works, the primary focus has swiftly shifted to the middle of January. At the time of writing, we have recorded 9 spot fixtures - 3 of these concluded by Indian PSU. WEST This week we have seen a good amount of spot fixtures in the west, and we can now count 30 deals for January dates ex USG/USEC. Recent challenges in the Red Sea, coupled with draught issues in Panama, have forced most Owners to send their vessels via Cape from the East to the US. Freight rates are on the rise and the vessel supply is looking tight before shifting focus towards February fixing and Christmas Holidays.
