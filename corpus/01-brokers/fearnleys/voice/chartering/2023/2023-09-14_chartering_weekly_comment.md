## Market Commentary

EAST The list of available positions for first half of October in the AG is getting slimmer now as a small handful of ships have been taken out both in the East and in the West. There are still some cargos in the works in this period, and more cargos are expected to hit the market later on as well for 1H October dates. So far this week freight remains stable in both regions and with the current activity level the threat of any imminent drop in freight seams bleak. WEST Although we have seen as much as 38 spot fixtures in October ex USG/USEC only 13 of them have been done in 2H Oct. Compared to the YTD average for 2H of a month we find October three spot fixtures behind. We have already seen the first fixture in November, but October fixing does not seem completely finished just yet as there are still a couple open positions left in October and a few Charterers still out looking. The Western market is currently trading at a discount of about 15 dollars to the East before waiting in the Panama Canal as we see last done fixture ex USG at mid-low 220s H/C and mid-low 120 H/F.
