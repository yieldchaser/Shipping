## Market Commentary

EAST Freight market is staying active despite not even Adnoc dates being published for next month. Although last done for a standard AG voyage at this very moment is just below yesterday baltic print – numbers shown today and last night in the East are now climbing up to the 150s for the early part of October. Charterers with firm cargo dates in the first half, or at least first 10 days, of the month are therefore seemingly rushing to fix in before less ships are available and freight potentially just even higher than as of now. WEST Entering this week we found the BLPG3 index at mid 190s H/C. Today we find it 25 dollars higher after having seen the last couple deals done at 220 H/C. At the time of writing we have seen about 30 spot fixtures for October dates ex USG/USEC and only a handful of them have been fixed in 2H Oct where we still see several uncovered cargoes left, but the vessel supply is currently very limited this period.
