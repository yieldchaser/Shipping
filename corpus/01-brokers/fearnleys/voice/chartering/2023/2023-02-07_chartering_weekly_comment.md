## Market Commentary

EAST Even with two more rounds of acceptances to go we have had quite a few cargoes already fixed on March dates in the Middle East. At the same time, we have seen some East candidate vessels fixed for cargoes in the West finally making the very front of March rather tight. A bit further into March there is at the time of writing more tonnage to choose from. Looking at the cargo side, maintenance at Ruwais is expected to last during the whole of March, actual impact in terms of decreased volumes remain speculative. Ras Tanura volumes are reported to come back to pre-maintenance levels while Yanbu is expected to remain less active for now. WEST So far this week 5-6 spot fixtures done in the Western market. At the time of writing, we count a total of 31 spot fixtures (9 first decade, 16 second decade, 6 last decade) out of the USG/USEC in March. There are currently 7-8 ships (4 relets) left in USG for end March, and there could still be a couple more appearing on the list as some ships with February laycans loading in USG/USEC might discharge in the NWE/Med/Waf. Last done concluded at high USD 130s H/C.
