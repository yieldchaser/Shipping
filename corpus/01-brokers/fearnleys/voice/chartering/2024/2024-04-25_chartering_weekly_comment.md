## Market Commentary

EAST A freight tender in the Middle East managed to break the 60’s yesterday and another deal was done at very low 60’s. At the same time we’re seeing a more optimistic freight environment in the West which makes some people wonder if market has come off a bit too much in the region. Now most Owners are aiming to hold for 60’s and above. But we can’t ignore the fact that there’s still a decent length in the East and with Panama getting more congested, the long way back to the West via Cape makes earnings there less attractive than it looks at first sight. WEST In the Western market we have seen a handful of spot fixtures out of the US Gulf so far this week, and we now count 31 deals in total for May dates – the year-to-date average is at 30 spot fixtures. There are still 3-4 open vessels (1 relet) left in May and with the currently slim FOB availability that month we could expect some waiting time for the ships still available. June fixing is already underway and we find last done fixture concluded in the high 120s H/C.
