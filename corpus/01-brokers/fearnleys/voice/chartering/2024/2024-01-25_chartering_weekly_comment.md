## Market Commentary

EAST A spur of optimism in the West seeing freight coming up from last done levels and earnings yielding more than $10kpd above the Eastern market. At the same time the East market is suffering from oversupply of ships coupled with, so far, way too few cargos. At current discount it’s tempting to start hoping East freight may come up from here considering the somewhat more optimistic environment in the US – but with 10 spot fixtures so far confirmed on February dates and close to 20 potential open ships for the remainder of the month, it still doesn’t look like we’re out of the mud just yet. WEST This week we saw western freight rates fall to its lowest point since March 2022 and it is now trading 90-100 H/C v Pan range. March fixing USG is already underway although we still see about 12 open positions left for Feb dates – 4 of them being relets. It remains to be seen how many cargoes there are left in February before we shift all focus towards March.
