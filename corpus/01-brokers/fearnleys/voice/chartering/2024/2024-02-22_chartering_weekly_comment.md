## Market Commentary

EAST A combination between premium voyages and prompt laycan cargos helps push freight to new heights in the East. We maintain our view that the second half of March is still showing signs of length and as such we believe freight may have come up a bit too much too quick. Again this is evident in the large gap between East and West freight. Direction ahead will by and large be decided by how and when end March cargos will approach the market going forward. WEST Western freight rates have recently seen an improvement, now trading at a TCE of 40-45k pd, albeit still at a significant discount compared to the East. In total we count 27 spot fixtures for March ex USG/USEC and only 10 of them have been done in the second half of the month so we should expect some more cargoes to appear in the end. The position list is looking tighter showing about 5 vessels left in March before moving into April.
