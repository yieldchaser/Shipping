## Market Commentary

EAST Over halfway through the week, the East has so far failed to bring much excitement in the spot market. There are reportedly one or two Indian cargoes remaining on September dates – and statistically we could hope for one or two additional cargoes before we look to October. Even with an optimistic view on remaining volumes it looks like freight has more downside, and with the APPEC conference still in full swing in Singapore, it’s rather unlikely that activity will pick up just yet. WEST A few of deals have been concluded in the West so far this week bringing the total to 7 spot fixtures out of the USG/USEC for October dates – 3 first decade and 4 second decade. There are still 14 available ships before 15th October in the US Gulf. That said, shipping is currently looking a bit long for the time being with few available spot cargoes in the market. Freight rates are on its way down with last done deals at 105-108 H/C range.
