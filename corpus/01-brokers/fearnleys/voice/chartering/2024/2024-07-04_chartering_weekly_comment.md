## Market Commentary

EAST July has been strong in terms of spot volumes especially in the first two decades of the month. The next couple of weeks will reveal if we will see an equally strong third decade and a month performing above the typical month. The average monthly spot count from Middle Eastern ports (inc. Yanbu) for the first half of 2024 stands at just below 23 spot fixtures vs July so far at 23 (10 in first, 9 in second and 4 in third decade). We expect more to come on July dates still, especially considering we only have seen four spot deals in the last decade (one of which rep still on com subs). WEST In contrast to last week the West has been more active than the East in terms of fixing activity with 7 spot deals done. So far we count 4 spot fixtures for August dates ex USG/USEC with the last few deals done around 110 H/C. Although August fixing in the USG is well underway, we still find a chunk of July vessels seeking employment. In addition, the position list for 1H August is starting to build up which could limit the upside.
