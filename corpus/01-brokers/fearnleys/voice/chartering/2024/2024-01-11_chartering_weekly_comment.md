## Market Commentary

EAST The East market took a nosedive yesterday following the trend in the West. Considering East is still trading at a premium the risk remain on the downside. Adnoc dates was published yesterday with reports of some advancements. February fixing would likely commence next week. With the risk of sounding repetitive, although most of the ships on the East position list could look west, the down trending market coupled with excess tonnage is bound to keep freight in a negative spiral. WEST With the arb narrowing a lot lately we have also seen freight in free fall as a result. On Tuesday we recorded the biggest ever drop on the BLPG3 route at 33 dollars while we yesterday found western freight rates at its lowest since August 2023. In total we have only seen 8 spot fixtures for February dates ex USG/USEC - same time last year we had seen over twice as many for Feb.
