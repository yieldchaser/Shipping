## Market Commentary

EAST: Seemingly more than half way through August fixing in the Middle East we now stand at 14 concluded spot fixtures. Available ships for the second half of the month has been building up, not necessarily because of a lack of spot cargos but, simply due to too many vessels now likely ending up in the region. This can partly be explained by Owners preference for shorter voyages at current freight levels - although USG is now being fixed at a theoretical premium of about 2-3 dollars. WEST: August fixing gained some momentum this week, albeit at rates steady around low USD 90s basis Houston/Chiba via Panama and low USD 50s basis Houston/Flushing. The current fixture count is up to 24 spot deals which remains more or less at par with the last few months' development. At the moment, the product market is stronger relative to shipping and the market is in need for a drying up of shipping length in order to gain momentum. The Panama Canal Authority announced that it will be back to normal within the next couple of months, however, in terms of shipping outlook, we remain positive for US activity once the terminals catch up any backlog and start exporting at full capacity again.
