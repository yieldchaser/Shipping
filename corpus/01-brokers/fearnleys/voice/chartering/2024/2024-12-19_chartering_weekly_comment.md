## Market Commentary

EAST With the late release of Saudi acceptances yesterday the East market remained surprisingly inactive at the beginning of the week - especially considering Christmas is right around the corner. We have yet to see any spot fixtures concluded for January dates ex Middle Eastern ports if we exclude Yanbu (three deals done), but at the time of writing we observe two uncovered cargoes out there for early Jan – one being an Indian PSU. WEST A spot tender out of the US Gulf was concluded earlier this week several dollars below last done, which has pushed Western freight rates slightly lower, and now we are seeing spot deals being done around USD 110 H/C. Fixing activity in the first and second decade of January is seemingly finished with currently no available vessels left this period. In the last decade there still remain 7-8 open positions before the market shifts focus into February where we already have seen one vessel fixed for early dates.
