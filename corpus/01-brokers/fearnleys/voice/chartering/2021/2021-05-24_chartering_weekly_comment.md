## Market Commentary

EAST With the preceding week failing to revive any real momentum this week has so far followed suit with less than a handful of fixtures so far concluded. Thus, freight has followed in a downward direction. Hopes are that with a couple of Indian cargoes together with a few more end June stems, supply/demand will be more or less in equilibrium and as such making sure the drop in freight will close and flat out from here. WEST As we are approaching the end of May, the number of VLGC liftings in total ex US and Canada is approaching about 90 VLGC equivalents (including some LGCs loaded). June looks set to follow suit. At the time of writing there are only a handful of vessels left for the balance of June and these vessels are largely controlled by only one owner. A few fixed rate bids from potential charterers have been shown for last decade June loading ex USG, however, the tight vessel supply makes it somewhat difficult to strike due to a large difference between bids and offers. Freight for second half June will find its feet again at some point, but the list of open vessels is not getting any longer.
