## Market Commentary

EAST CP was announced earlier this week but we are yet to see any increase in activity on the back of it. Time of writing we have seen approx. 11-12 spot deals concluded in the East for July, so we anticipate that there is at least a handful of uncovered cargos left before we close the month off. But as options on shipping still looks pretty healthy nobody seems to be in a rush to move. One Indian major booked a relet in the high usd 40s Baltic earlier, but sentiment is expected to weaken for next deal(s) provided non India voyage. WEST In the Western VLGC market, the slowdown in the rate increase we observed and reported last week has kept on this week and seemingly more-or-less come to an end for now. This is related to a market in which only can be described as next to inactive, whereas most negotiations, with the exception of a couple, has so far failed to materialize into any clean deals. A less than favourable trading environment on the product market combined with a growing positions list, at least for the first half of August, indicates the direction of the market may be sideways to downward. At the rates most recently concluded, VLGCs in the Western hemisphere is trading at around 30k per day for the transpacific freight route.
