## Market Commentary

EAST Currently there is a premium of about three US dollars in the western hemisphere compared to the East. It seems there are too many relets in the MEG for second half July dates, and thus the West premium will likely sustain in the short term. As the first half of July is more or less done in terms of fixing, we anticipate that focus will shift to the second half of July very soon. WEST A handful of vessel enquiries have been worked during the end of July ex USG and the uptick in rates seen last week is continuing, albeit at a slower pace. Offers from owners are currently in the mid/high USD 80's per ton H/C basis, and it is likely that we'll see rates hovering around this level for a little while. Vessel availability seems relatively balanced throughout the first decade of August and some owners are withholding vessels from the spot market in order to meet potential COA commitments. Thus, there may be a vessel or two more, or less, than what it looks like in this time period.
