## Market Commentary

EAST With Diwali coming up next Thursday, Indian PSUs seemingly have been keen to sort out most of their November programs in advance of the festivities, and 6 out of the 9 spot fixtures for November so far has been Indian fixtures. As freight ideas in the West approaching mid 90s on the back of a flurry of fixtures ex US last night the East/West diff is widening. East freight should eventually also see the effects of a tightening market West of Suez as those owners who have the option to seek cargoes in US are more likely to do so now. A theoretical 50 RT/C (almost 3 dollars above last Baltic) versus a 95 H/C still results in a more than 6 dollar West premium before factoring in Panama waiting. WEST The West VLGC market has been very active so far this week, and first decade December fixing is well underway with still uncovered cargoes early in the month. End November only has a couple of vessels left open, and in all likelihood, these will be absorbed rather soon. Early December is tight! Freight rates are steadily increasing from mid/high USD 80’s and up towards mid USD 90’s if all sub-fixtures are confirmed. Yet again and for the market to sustain, we are reliant upon not overcooking the goose.
