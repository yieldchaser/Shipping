## Market Commentary

EAST A few vessels came back open on the positions list over the Easter long weekend and with little or no more cargoes expected from either Indians or other Charterers for April, we might get a small overhang into May after all which should, if not ease the market, at least slow down the spike a little bit. That said, one Charterer fixed out a relet at around 60 dollars for a distressed cargo. For May laycans we are yet too see much cargoes at play apart from a FOB tender being worked ex Kuwait. WEST Shipping availability looks tight for May out of the US. This is mainly on the back large amount of liftings for March and April ex US, in combination with a heavy DD schedule planned for in Q2 and also more delays for the VLGCs passing southbound in the Panama Canal. However, at the moment there is no arbitrage to support freight starting with an $8 handle USG/East, so it remains to be seen if we are entering a period whereby freight rates starts dictating the arb.
