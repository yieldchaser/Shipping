## Market Commentary

EAST February was overall quite dull in terms of spot activity in the MEG. With a weak start to March fixing and reportedly no Saudi cargoes nor any Indian requirements for the first 10 days of March, spot freight is still tumbling downwards. We have seen a few cargoes being mentioned for second decade March dates, but with a market getting a little bit weaker by the day and availability still remaining long, charterers seem to be in no rush for now. WEST US Gulf is experiencing delays at the load terminals and uncertainties with more fog approaching is keeping freight markets on hold. We are fast approaching OPEX earnings for the owners, but even at these earnings, there are arguably not enough cargoes to be lifted ex-US in March to employ all the ships coming open.
