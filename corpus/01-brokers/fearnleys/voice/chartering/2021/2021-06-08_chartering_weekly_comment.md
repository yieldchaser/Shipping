## Market Commentary

EAST Yet another uneventful week in the East so far, and talk of production issues at Ras Tanura is certainly not helping the cause. As freight remains long for balance of June and rates continue to be under pressure, it is likely that players will continue to wait until we have more acceptances for July. One trader was marketing a relet at sub Baltic levels earlier, but no takers so far and the Baltic has dropped below USD 50 for the first time since April. WEST The uncertainty around how resilient the freight market actually is coupled with how much more downward 'theoretical' pressure from the product market freight would sustain, has been the talk of the town for the last week or so. It seems that the build in the US stocks seen yesterday aided the trading environment enough to create some momentum whereby freight would find its feet and freight fixtures could be developed. A handful of cargoes has been fixed during the last week or so, and the hope is that there are more to come.
