## Market Commentary

EAST The Saudi acceptances were published over the weekend, but we are yet to see any real activity on the back of it. Meanwhile, we are still seeing some length on shipping from the traders, and as such the climb in Baltic from earlier weeks has turned (for now). We have seen a couple of cargo and freight tenders in the market for last decade of June this week, and last done deal on shipping was concluded around usd 60 baltic. We also expect to see more Indian cargos quoted in the market later for June loading, which is partly due to some of the Indian majors reported having covid related issued on their own t/c ships. WEST In the Western hemisphere the activity level has been considerably reduced compared to the last couple of weeks, much owing to large parts of the world observing national holidays. Nevertheless, the arbitrage remains relatively stable and so does freight ideas from both relets and pure shipowners. A good old count of open/available vessels in the USG for that of June is largely in favour of the market remaining where it is for the foreseeable future. There should still be some uncovered cargoes end June ex USG, thus it is likely that we will see another example of little or no overhang going into July. NB Another Newbuilding order emerged this week, bringing the total orderbook up to 64 ships, whereas 15 for delivery within this year, 18 next year and 31 ships in 2023.
