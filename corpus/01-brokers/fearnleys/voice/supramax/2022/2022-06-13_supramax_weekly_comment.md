## Market Commentary

Currently it seems the market will be in for a rebound, but there is no change to the fundamental story that demand growth remains below the rate of supply growth. The market usually climbs at this time of year, so the last couple of weeks’ drop should serve as a clear sign and warning that fundamentals are headed in the wrong direction for owners. The macro-picture is too weak. Just skim through the last months’ manufacturing and steel PMI readings across all major countries/regions and it seems very unlikely that demand will rise in the coming months.
