## Market Commentary

The share of the fleet east of Suez has dropped noticeably the last few weeks, which perhaps could trigger upside as Asian coal demand is set for a seasonal upturn in the coming months. China’s demand has been low the last weeks but rising industrial metal prices could indicate that demand is about to rise going forward. If the above scenario plays out remains to be seen, but what is clear is that the market will be supported in the near-term by the high ECSAM grains activity and the upcoming USG grain season.
