## Market Commentary

The Supra/Ultra vessel market remains subdued globally, with limited progress in the Atlantic region and strong tonnage availability in the North Atlantic. South Asia faces tightened tonnage availability in early August, and notable transactions include Iseaco Grace and Federal Indus securing new ventures. There are glimpses of potential recovery in South Asia due to ongoing activity, but cargo inquiries remain limited, prompting some owners to consider reduced bids to secure business.
