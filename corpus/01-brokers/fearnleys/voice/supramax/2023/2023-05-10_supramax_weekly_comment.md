## Market Commentary

The effect of the Easter Holidays kept markets at low activities and rates softened further. The Atlantic market in main loading areas from USG and ECSA is well positioned and rates for FH pay a premium, though trips within the Atlantic are reduced slightly. An Ultramax loading Mississippi River was fixed close to USD 23,000 pd for a trip to Japan with grains. While Ultramax still getting premium to load in draft-restricted ports, a smaller tonnage of 56 000 dwt is fixed at USD 15,000 pd for a trip across the Atlantic. The Black Sea and Continent markets are very quiet due to a lack of fresh cargo and supply of prompt tonnage. Cont-Med trade rates are ranging from USD 14,000 pd to USD 16,000 pd depending on trip duration and delivery port. Activity from Russian ports was slowing due to a lack of cargo at Russian ports due to the inland logistic challenges. A Supramax was reported at USD 16,500 pd basis delivery Atlantic Spain via Russian Baltic to India with fertilizers. The Far East market still lacks coal demand thus rates remain low. In Asia, we see rats only at low USD 10,000 pd no matter of vessel size or direction. Pretty gloomy market. The 1 mill USD question – do we believe in dramatic improvement during the upcoming summer?
