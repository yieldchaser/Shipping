## Market Commentary

The Supramax FFA market has been up all week long despite the holiday season in China. However, the physical market remains at a low with barely any activity on the spot market. Traders in China had covered their positions well before the mid-autumn festival. There has been a dip in the PG-WCI rates for Supra/Ultras and heard couple’ vessels open spot and awaiting next employment. Meantime, effective November 01st, 2023 and until further notice, Panamax Plus transits are suspended. Panamax size vessels must transit the Panamax Locks complying with the corresponding maximum TFW draft for the Old Locks. We can expect to see a potential ripple effect of this new regulation on geared vessel sizes in the coming months. Hope we can see activity pick up next week once the traders are back in office in China.
