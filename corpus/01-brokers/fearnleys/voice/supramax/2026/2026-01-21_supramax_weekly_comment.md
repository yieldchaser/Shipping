## Market Commentary

The Handy and Supra markets have continued to improve, and charterers are losing their leverage - strongly supported by a healthy flow of fresh enquiries and slimmer tonnage lists. Overall conditions remain mixed in terms of wide ranges of hire and freight ideas; however the spread-gap has continuously become smaller and negotiations clearly showing owners getting back on the horse. In the Handy segment, sentiment has become firmer in Atlantic and Pacific, providing better support to rates. Owners continue to test higher levels where cargo flow allows, though some resistance persists from charterers. The Supramax/Ultramax market was notably more busy throughout the week. Owners continued to stand their ground and holding out on negotiations and trading until reasonable numbers were seen. Charterers continue to resist the upward pressure, but seems to have been given in to owners demands as LC’s have been more spot and non-negotiable. Overall sentiment is firm, but the market remains sensitive to regional cargo availability.
