## Market Commentary

The Handy and Handymax market remained active this week, supported by a healthy mix of spot and short-period opportunities across both the Atlantic and Pacific basins. Cargo flow remained steady, with active tonnage circulation across key regions. Demand was primarily driven by grain, coal, fertilizer, and petcoke trades. The Atlantic basin, particularly South America, continued to generate the majority of activity, while Asia provided consistent short-haul employment opportunities. The Supramax and Ultramax markets remained firm, although spot cargo demand has started to ease, with market attention shifting toward late July and August positions. East Coast South America (ECSA) continues to offer significantly stronger returns than West Africa, and despite the widening spread between the two regions, the U.S. Gulf market remains broadly balanced. Owners have remained selective in their voyage choices, particularly when considering repositioning opportunities as the USG continues to rise.
