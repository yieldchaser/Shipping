## Market Commentary

The Handy and Supra markets have improved in terms of spot activity across both basins this week, supported by steady demand and a consistent supply of tonnage. Overall conditions remain firm but mixed, with freight and hire ideas showing a wide range. While activity levels have improved, negotiations continue to reflect charterers upper hand and owners holding vessels to the very last minute. In the Handy segment, sentiment has edged slightly firmer in Atlantic and Pacific, providing some underlying support to rates. Owners continue to test higher levels where cargo flow allows, though resistance persists from charterers on shorter voyages and lower-quality stems, keeping gains in check. The Supramax/Ultramax market was notably busy and broadly balanced throughout the week. Owners maintained a constructive stance and continued to push rate ideas, particularly on longer-haul business. Charterers, however, remained disciplined and resisted upward pressure on shorter or less attractive cargoes. Overall sentiment is cautiously firm, with pockets of strength emerging, but the market remains sensitive to regional fundamentals and cargo availability.
