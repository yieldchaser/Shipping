## Market Commentary

The Supramax/Handysize market remained mixed, with the Atlantic showing signs of improvement while Asia continued to soften. In contrast, the Pacific market remained under pressure as a lack of fresh cargo demand, particularly from South Asia, led to increasing prompt tonnage.
