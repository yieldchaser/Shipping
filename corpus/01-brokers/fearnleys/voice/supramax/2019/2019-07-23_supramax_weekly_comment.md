## Market Commentary

The sentiment has remained the same over last 7 days , although the pace of rate increases slowed down . In the Atlantic the upward trend stopped today and USG to Continent pay again around 18500 . Back-hauls voyages to USA/SAM were slightly higher , average USD 500 more by comparison to last Wednesday levels . On the other hand North Continent /Baltic tonnage was in search of cargoes direction East Med/Black Sea , so spot cargoes to USG/SAM had to pay premium rates .

**Pacific**

 rates stayed very healthy and rose last week by average of USD 1000, whilst Nopac voyages are paying now more or less USD 1000 a day more comparing to last Wednesday reports . The overall BSI index rose from 928 to 1019 , therefore all in all the bullish sentiment prevails in the market .
