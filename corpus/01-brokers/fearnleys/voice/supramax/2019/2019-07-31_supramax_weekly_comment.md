## Market Commentary

The market for Supramaxes has seen weakening sentiment the last week following from the drop of the bigger sizes. The lack of fresh interest from charterers in the Atlantic region have put some pressure on the rates in USG and East Med. ECSA is stable, with owners able to obtain good rates for direction Med USD 17,000 and FH USD 15,500 + 550,000 BB on Tess58. PG-India, as well as Asia markets despite the negative indexes, remains active and well supported with long list of cargo orders.
