## Market Commentary

The market turned green in most areas apart from ECSA, where there is still a lack of inquiries, and Ultras fixing around USD 13,500+350k gbb for fh to ECI/Bangladesh.

**USG**

 has showed a rapid improvement with vessels fixing around USD 25,000 for fh to India, while TA to Med in the high teens, and from the Continent, scrap cargoes to Emed is paying in the mid-teens. In the Indian Ocean, South Africa RV's paying around USD 115k+115k gbb, while MEG to China paying around USD 12,000. Further East, Indo/China coal is paying around USD 9,500.
