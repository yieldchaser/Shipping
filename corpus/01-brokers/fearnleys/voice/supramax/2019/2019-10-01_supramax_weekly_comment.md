## Market Commentary

The poor activity related to the Golden week holidays in Asia added pressure on the market with the Baltic Supramax Index losing ground.

**Atlantic**

 basin suffered from a lack of fresh enquiry, both ex-US Gulf and East Coast South America. The surplus supply in the area of the larger size Panamax/Kamsarmax vessels made owners to accept lower rates. Ultramaxes from ECSA were fixing $ 20,000. MV CP Shenzhen 63’ dwt reported to be fixed $ 20,000 APS Up River to Turkey. However Mediterranean markets was holding good momentum where demand for prompt positions remained strong.
