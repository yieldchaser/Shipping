## Market Commentary

Another positive week for the market with BSI routes rising up. More activity from key areas such as the US Gulf and ECSA pushed rates up. The fixtures reported from the US Gulf showed good gain in rates. TCT from Miss River to Med-Cont being fixed at USD 27,000 pd on Ultramax and TCT to Far East was fixed mv Grande Island Tess 58 at USD 31,000. Trips with clinker from Med to West Africa also showed improvement in rates by gaining USD 2,000-3,000 per day from last done. Scrap from Continent followed the same trend fixing in hi USD 20,000’. Indian Ocean and Asia remain strong with stable flow of cargoes and shortage of tonnage. A 56,000 open west coast India was fixed for a trip from Persian Gulf to Bangladesh around USD 32,000. MV Unity Discovery 60’ dwt open Pakistan rumoured to be fixed for a trip to Chittagong at USD 40,000. Inter-Pacific coal voyages paying around USD 31,000.
