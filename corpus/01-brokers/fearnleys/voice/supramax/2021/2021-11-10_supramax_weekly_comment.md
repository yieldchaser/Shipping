## Market Commentary

The increasing concerns over the recent market development continues to dominate owners' minds. The fact of slower demand of the key commodities such as iron ore, coal together with strict environmental measurements in China added to the deepest index dive during the last 9 months. The period market took a complete break as we saw the decrease from the highest rates paid on Tess 58 of USD 30,000 pd for 1 year charter to the USD 19,000 pd that rumoured was fixed this week. The most suffered market appears to be in the East where we saw drop from solid USD 35,000 pd in most of the trading routes to below USD 20,000 pd on Supra as recently reported. BSI dropped over 20% last week, which is still understating the actual market drop as per done fixtures. The Atlantic market compared to Asian markets fell as well but not as hard.

**USG**

 and ECSA are still paying above USD 30,000 pd for TA destination and front hauls are around low/mid USD 40,000 pd.
