## Market Commentary

Holiday mode across most of the areas leading to a limited fresh information. Rates were dropping in most of the regions. From Atlantic only firm activity seen from USG for Atlantic runs, where grain business to Continent was paying USD 21,000. Fronthauls were covered closer to USD 24,000.

**Continent**

 was also slowing down, where trip to USG would pay USD 12,000 now. Furthermore, lackluster activity from Black Sea where fronthauls paying close to 20k now, otherwise little reported. Supra from ECSA to Australia was reported to be fixed at USD 13,300+300k ballast bonus. Same sentiment from Pacific basin. Supra from Indonesia to Philippines was covered at USD 11,200 and from same position to China at USD 8,000. Vessel open in Koh Sichang with clinker to Cebu concluded at low USD 11,000 and Indo-WCI runs were paying USD 14,000 this week. Period market remained flat.
