## Market Commentary

The Supramax sector continued to firm, supported by ongoing demand from the US Gulf and South Atlantic alongside limited prompt tonnage, allowing owners to maintain higher rate ideas across the Continent and Mediterranean while Asian markets remained firm despite a growing bid-offer gap on certain routes. In the Indian Ocean, steady inquiry and a tightening tonnage list underpinned sentiment, adding upward pressure to rates. The Handysize market also sustained a positive tone, with the Atlantic benefiting from fresh demand and improving confidence, while in Asia, a shift in the cargo-to-tonnage ratio supported gradual gains.
