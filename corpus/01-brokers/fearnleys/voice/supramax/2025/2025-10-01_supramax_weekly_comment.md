## Market Commentary

The Supramax/Ultramax market ended September on a relatively muted tone as holidays in Asia slowed activity, leaving sentiment in the East soft with charterers maintaining the upper hand. The Atlantic, however, retained a firmer footing, though some questioned whether the recent push from the US Gulf had already peaked. The Continent and Mediterranean were seen as steady, while the South Atlantic stayed finely balanced. In Asia, prompt tonnage continued to weigh on sentiment, with rates slipping lower across both regional and Indian Ocean positions. As the monsoon season comes to an end, we however do see more cargoes volumes ex West Coast India. Period interest surfaced, though with mixed levels compared to previous reports and the bid/offer spread is too wide in present levels. Overall, the index average started correcting, reflecting softer conditions despite steady demand from the Atlantic.
