## Market Commentary

After the rally of chartering activity over the past couple of weeks, there are now becoming noticeable signs of softening emerging as we move into summer. Prompt interest has eased, save for optimisations or speculative opportunities. Looking a month out, ship availability is increasing, but most of the tonnage emerging is controlled by subletters. Generally, their length is too short or have limitations to accommodate fully flexible periods/trades, but they are impacting market sentiment. Term fixing activity is also slowing as Charterers pause to assess what vessels are left and how the market plays out; however, Owners stance remains unchanged - at least for now - as they weigh the cost (and potential benefit, in winter) of spot exposure versus fixing for term. On the newbuilding front, there have been no new orders recorded, however prices are steadily rising for delivery in 2024.
