## Market Commentary

Firstly, to those celebrating Eid – Eid Mubarak. National holidays have been plentiful recently and these “interruptions” could be seen to affect the rhythm of the market, yet LNG prices continue to rise throughout the curve and point to continued improvement in the underlying market. It’s impact on freight is very noticeable, owners remaining bullish with this bullishness being built on the evidence of persistent multi-month enquiries. As mentioned last week the activity in the multi-month market is likely to reduce due to the lack of supply, unless we see a step up in levels to encourage further sublets into the market as owners. Looking at availability generally, there appears to be a systemic shortage of availability within the independent owners fleets. Although some vessel available is starting to appear in all basins from portfolio players, the prompt available remains stable with adequate yet not overbearing availability. Looking further out to June and Q3, availability is far improved and adds pressure to rate increases. Despite this the start of Q3 looks stronger than usual for LNG Prices and shipping activity. In the newbuild market - no new orders have been seen this week yet the newbuild price continues its steady climb.
