## Market Commentary

LNG freight levels continue their rally as we move further into the winter season. There are a handful of requirements targeting December and January laycans as Charterers have started hitting the market for tonnage much earlier than in the past. As a result, we see rate levels aligning in both basins, in contrast to the disconnection seen in recent weeks. On the vessel supply side, availability remains very tight with most independent tonnage being sold out and portfolio tonnage remains subject to uncertainties around scheduling or are restricted on period. Those willing to take the risk are seeking higher levels of compensation, thus pushing the market higher. The term market also remains busy with Charterers focusing on securing coverage for 2022 and beyond. There are no official LNG newbuild orders recorded so far this week but the shipbuilding market remains firm.
