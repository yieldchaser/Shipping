## Market Commentary

The record-breaking start to the year continues as both the LNG and shipping markets gain strength week-on-week. Availability remains tight, and our reported headline rates combined with full three-way voyage economics are pushing TCE levels above $300,000 per day for some routes. This seasonal cycle has several factors at play, from uncertainty in demand due to Covid-19 through to cold weather, but the full examination will follow in due course; for now, buyers, sellers, charterers and owners are focussed on their exposures or opportunities. There is perhaps a glance now away from “how high will it go?” towards “how long will it last and how hard will it fall?”, but while there is backwardation in the forward curve the commodity price sits materially higher through 2021 than it did just weeks ago. Rebuilding inventories and a more conservative approach to next winter should support a more stable market once the current dynamics have played out: we should see more utilisation and less volatility on the shipping side this year than last.
