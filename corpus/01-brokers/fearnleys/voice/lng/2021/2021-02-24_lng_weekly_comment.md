## Market Commentary

It has been a flat week for the LNG Shipping markets. Sentiment continues to dip further with limited activity and soft demand for shipping. There were requirements to be worked, however the impact on rates remain minimal. The continuous increase in prompt availability across both basins, potential cargo cancellations from US projects, constant decline in product prices as we move closer to Q2 and a closed West – East arbitrage window did contribute to the lack of shipping opportunities. Multi month and short term inquiries seem to be put on hold as Charterers take the “wait and see” approach to further evaluate their shipping portfolio. Nonetheless, we do expect some interest in the coming weeks or months as Charterers look to take advantage of the current market conditions. Utilization is imperative and this could mean more inclination from Owners to work competitive rates now for longer duration to brace the shoulder months. On Newbuilding activities, Maran Gas seem to have added to their order tally at Samsung.
