## Market Commentary

LNG shipping rates, especially for term, continue to firm up with week-on-week gains across both basins. While the Pacific is fairly quiet on the spot market, demand for shipping remains high in the West with several requirements to work on. Owners with vessel availability are still very focused on either 1-2 spot laden legs or term period very close to 1 year + but nothing in between. Charterers, on the other hand, prefer to secure vessels up till end Q1 – early Q2 next year. Hence, it remains challenging to bridge that gap between Charterers preference to cover winter and Owners preference to secure longer period. Furthermore, the current spot cargo prices have also fueled strong demand for ships which in part explains Owners stance at the moment. So the question remains on everyone’s mind: To fix at the current rates and duration or take “the wait-and-see approach”? There are no new orders reported this week and newbuilding prices remain stable, with a firm undertone.
