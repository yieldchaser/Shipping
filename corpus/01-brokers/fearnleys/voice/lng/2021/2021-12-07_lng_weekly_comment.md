## Market Commentary

Market participants with shipping length were hoping that the shipping activity would pick up on the back end of the CWC Rome conference. However, the week has so far been quiet. The arbitrage has fluctuated around the turning point to support inter basin trades, but the underlying story of the year 2021 (limited cargo availability) has put some restraints on the activity. The shipping rates came off a touch due to the lack of cargo activity and a slight increase in sublet shipping availability. Yard activity in terms of enquiries and orders is high and this week we’ve logged 1xCelsius and 2xJPM. Newbuilding prices are in general firming with limited slot availability.
