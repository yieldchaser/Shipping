## Market Commentary

After a couple of relatively quiet weeks in the spot market, we are seeing more requirements for August and September laycans both East and West. Nevertheless, fixing levels have continued their slight soft trend as sublet tonnage competes for these cargoes in an effort to optimise their portfolios. Cargo market remains volatile at firm levels with European and Asian markets competing for the cargoes and destination changes coupled with some production issues out of the US may encourage more sublet opportunities on the short term. For the ones keeping an eye for term tonnage, despite the slow down in activity this week, freight levels remain robust as Independent Owners with remaining availability are weighing different opportunities. Important to note again that tonnage options are slimming as most tonnage has shifted hands and been incorporated into portfolios, which will play a key factor on how the market develops in the months to come. In the Newbuilding markets, prices keep firming for all types of tonnage, including LNG ships. For new orders, Capital Gas has exercised another option and added one more vessel to their latest pair of speculative orders a couple of weeks ago. Vessel is expected for delivery late 2023.
