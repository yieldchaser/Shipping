## Market Commentary

LNG prices continue to firm as competition for cargoes globally is driving the price per MMBtu delivered into Asia above $32’s. After the recent lack of activity, business now being concluded is showing all signs of firmness, with rates increasing across most sectors. As we see the fixing window move out into November, Charterers are seeing freight for winter now being priced into these discussions. There is also a lack of visibility on winter tonnage, adding to the firmer outlook. We may see gains in rates over the next few weeks as the prompt sublet tonnage retreats back into schedules and interest for spot cargoes for late November/December mature, however Golden week in China next week might put the brakes on a little. No newbuild orders have been recorded placed at yards this week, but market talk is around the expected Qatar orders and for how many vessels they will do in this round.
