## Market Commentary

It has been another week of relatively low spot activity in the LNG shipping market. Portfolio sublet positions keep “flooding” the market at both basins, where the ones looking at securing tonnage can find some seasonal bargains at very competitive levels; yet period flexibility and promptness of available tonnage doesn’t always necessarily allow for these deals to get concluded. Looking at the cargo market, LNG prices keep climbing due to continuous supply disruptions and uncertainties, having JKM and TTF at a constant battle. Charterers looking for tonnage for winter opportunities seem to struggle to get much traction from the few Independent Owners with available tonnage or from subleters managing their future schedules. As a result, we are witnessing a halt in near term charter activity, but with cargo market being volatile, this could quickly turn either way. NB prices continue their ascending trend, however no new large LNG orders have been recorded this week.
