## Market Commentary

It has been yet another week of surging gas prices both in Europe and in Asia, causing a lot of speculations around how long this gas shortage can last and what will be the ultimate ramifications out of it. Naturally, this has been the hot topic this week in Gastech, as it is the first time in a very long time since we have seen a large (physical) industry gathering! Regardless of the striking headlines that the LNG cargo market has been causing lately, it has not reflected into more LNG shipping activity. On the contrary, it has been another lackluster week in the spot market, with only a handful of fresh requirements. We can perhaps partly blame the Gastech or multiple public holidays in the Asia region for this week's quietness, but the fact is that the surprisingly high gas prices in Europe have led trade-flows to be more intra-Atlantic heavy than anticipated causing majority of industry players to have sufficient length in their current LNGC fleet. However, looking past the relative weakness of the prompt shipping market, the view of the winter market still remains tight or at least extremely volatile, with no certainty on how availability might look like during the peak winter. On the newbuilding side, market is still keeping active and shipyards have their hands full with various ongoing discussions.
