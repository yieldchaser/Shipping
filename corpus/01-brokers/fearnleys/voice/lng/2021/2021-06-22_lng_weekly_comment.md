## Market Commentary

Although the maintenance and drydocking season is well underway it doesn’t appear to affect the shipping market much. Spot rates continue to build on the previous weeks’ gains, supported by term discussions and a strong product market due in part to warm weather across Europe and some problems with European pipeline supply. There are available vessels particularly in the east, but it must be noted that most of those offering today are subletters and the tonnage could quickly be withdrawn if the cargo market strengthens further or the East/West arbitrage increases. We’ve seen two orders for multiple newbuildings this week: one from COSCO and PCI firming up three options at Hudong-Zhonghua, and one from Capital Gas at HHI. The Cosco tonnage is due for delivery at the end of 2024 and early 2025, whiles the Capital Gas tonnage is due for delivery by the end of 2023.
