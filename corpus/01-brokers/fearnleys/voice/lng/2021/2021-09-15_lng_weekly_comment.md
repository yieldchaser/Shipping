## Market Commentary

While cargo prices strengthened and remained strong this week, the high LNG prices have so far not translated to any strengthening of the spot charter rates - hence, the correlation between cargo price movements and charter rates is yet to be seen. However, a vast majority of the available LNG vessels for spot charters are controlled by portfolio players and traders. As sellers’ cargoes are offered with the requirement to procure spot shipping, they are more often than not, lifted by buyers with available vessels for further trading and filling up short positions given the ideal LNG trading market conditions. The market is also not flushed with a long selection of vessels as the availability list is relatively short. Meanwhile, the term charter rates for 1 year period remain steady. Charterers’ interest in security over vessel availability for a term period continue to be a priority for various players. Newbuilding prices continue to climb as Mitsui OSK Lines (MOL) confirmed the orders for upto four (4) LNG vessels at Daewoo Shipbuilding & Marine Engineering (DSME) on the back of charters with Novatek Gas & Power Asia. The 174,000m3 LNG vessels, fitted with ME-GA engines for propulsion, along with a shaft generator and an onboard reliquefaction unit, are expected to be delivered in 2024.
