## Market Commentary

We pass another week with relatively few spot fixtures, and the running total for the year has now fallen some way behind the high watermark of 2020. Will the second half of the year generate enough spot activity to catch up? It’s unlikely, and in fact current trends suggest the fixture count may even slip below the 2019 tally. However, with very few ships under independent owners’ control and with the cargo market remaining buoyant, the rest of 2021 is set to be a fascinating game of chess, in which novel structures and creative deals on both cargo and freight will help charterers make the most of their tonnage - there is plenty of opportunity for win-win scenarios. The multi-month market is again more active, and the limited pool of independent owners and those charterers with enough length to sublet are being kept busy. There haven’t been any fresh newbuilding orders placed this week.
