## Market Commentary

The exponential increase in LNG charter rates seen in recent weeks, slowed down slightly this week. However, we are still on an uptrend where rate levels may not have peaked yet and we are barely into full-blown winter. The strong European gas demand is still keeping more Atlantic cargoes than usual within the basin. This may change in the near future if Asian LNG demand strengthens against European demand. Asian inter-basin cargo deliveries would only lead to further tightening of an already very limited open tonnage list which could put further pressure on the current high charter rates and voyage economics. The availability of modern vessels are almost a rarity in today’s spot & short term market as most are on charter and utilised for the charterers' scheduled trades. The firm newbuilding prices are not deterring new LNG ship orders. Capital Gas has reportedly confirmed another three orders with one at Hyundai Samho Heavy Industries (HSHI) and two at Hyundai Heavy Industries (HHI). Not to be outdone, QatarEnergy also secured their first set of Korean-built newbuildings of 174,000m3 LNG vessels with four at Daewoo Shipbuilding & Marine Engineering (DSME) and two at Samsung Heavy Industries (SHI). Expect more to come in the near future from QatarEnergy.
