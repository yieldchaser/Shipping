## Market Commentary

The LNG shipping market has been relatively quiet this week with few new firm requirements in either basin, although there are a fair number of cargo tenders. Taking the Atlantic, the lacklustre mood might be a fair reflection of a weakened prompt cargo market, as TTF slipped due to minimal demand off the back of storage levels reaching near capacity in Europe. It was a similar story East of Suez, with a lack of demand from Asian buyers keeping the cargo market flat. Thus, without huge cargo revenues or a strong contango, tendered volumes are mostly going to those with excess shipping length. This leaves the shipping market at the mercy of weather conditions in both Asia and Europe for the weeks ahead, and perhaps this represents a return to normality! On the newbuilding front, it was reported that Celsius have declared two options for 180,000 cbm LNG carriers at China Merchants Heavy Industry Jiangsu with delivery of the vessels scheduled for Q1 and Q4 of 2027.
