## Market Commentary

The LNG shipping market in the East has seen a uptick in demand for vessels for March loadings, resulting in a shortage of available vessels in the spot market. Last week ended with a surge in fixtures, with almost all available vessels in the Pacific being chartered. This sudden spot demand has caused daily rates in the East to firm. This firmer trend is expected to continue this week, as the prompt availability of vessels in the Asian spot market remains limited for March and into April loadings. In the West, sentiment is more steady and ship availability is sufficient to cover demand. There were no orders for large LNG newbuildings made this week, however shipyard slots remain generally tight and orders from other segments continues to fill up any extra yard capacity.
