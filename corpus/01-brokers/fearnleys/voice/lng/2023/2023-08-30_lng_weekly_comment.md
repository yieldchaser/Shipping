## Market Commentary

As the market gets ready to descend on Singapore for what is looking like being a very busy Gastech, the past week hasn’t seen the usual “pre-conference” surge in activity although there remains a number of enquiries to keep people occupied between meetings. Uncertainty still rules the market, with the Australian labour dispute edging closer and the potential impact of El Nino set against high storage levels in both basins and a relatively flat forward curve. As such and unsurprisingly, many enquiries have fallen or will fall away as optimisations and tenders and cargo swaps play out. So, what next? This uncertainty may have resulted in less activity but it hasn’t stopped fixing levels rising earlier this year than we have seen in recent years, and although few expect rates to spike as they did last year, we are now on the cusp of the floating window. Let’s hope there are enough breakout areas at Gastech, just in case… There were no newbuilding orders placed last week.
