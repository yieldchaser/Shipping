## Market Commentary

With a surge of vessel availability becoming more apparent across both basins, though predominantly in the West, we see LNG freight rates continue to come off across the board this week. The delta between vessels in the East vs those in the West increases in view of recent fixing levels in the Atlantic and the seemingly minimal number of 160,000cbm+ availability in the Pacific. That said, there is some potential upside for the spot market with the cold snap hitting Northern China and Europe that could lead to cargo requirements to supply both landmasses, as well as the prospect of a Freeport restart. In the meantime, though, requirements are typically optimisations and tenders and these are not sufficient to keep everyone busy, nor rates high. On the Newbuilding front, it was reported that Hyundai Heavy Industries has awarded a contract to build three 200,000cbm LNGCs for delivery in 2026. Capital has also secured a further two slots for 174,000cbm MEGA vessel for delivery in Q3 2026.
