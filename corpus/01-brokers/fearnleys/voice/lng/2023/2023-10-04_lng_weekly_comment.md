## Market Commentary

We typically expect a strengthened freight market in LNG as we head into the Northern Hemisphere’s winter months. However, this week has shown no signs of that, as headline rates are lower and ballast bonuses remain unchanged at round trip. The prospects of any profitable contango play appear bleak now, yet gas markets refuse to provide participants with any sustained direction to date. European inventory levels are currently high, and TTF has fallen this week which may in turn further solidify decisions to redirect laden vessels towards the East. In the Atlantic, requirements that were previously lingering have been fixed at lower levels compared to previous deals. In the Pacific, many major players have been inactive due to holidays in China and Korea, coupled with high inventory levels in Japan. However, as the week progresses and requirements start to firm up, we anticipate increased activity in this region, which may stem the decline. While the signs point to a weaker freight market, it's noteworthy that Charterers are attempting to extend the fixing window into December. There haven't been any new build orders placed in the past week, however, QatarEnergy has taken steps to reserve 17 slots at HHI.
