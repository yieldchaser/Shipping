## Market Commentary

As we roll into 2H August, we find ourselves with a quieter spot market to start the week with no new requirements or spot fixtures recorded as of yet. Although the case, we still do see momentum with ample activity in the background as market participants continue to test levels for various winter periods and attempt to narrow the still gaping bid-ask spread. Most notably in the East, albeit a healthy degree of availability on the prompt and otherwise, we understand that end-September / beg-October laycans are still struggling to attract the needed tonnage, prompting an increase in our rates this week accordingly. On the term side of the equation, a 22-month charter on a 2-Stroke delivering in November has been concluded circa the mid-$120k region, further softening and pulling term rates down across the board. Otherwise, newbuild prices remain flat this week and we have no new orders to report.
