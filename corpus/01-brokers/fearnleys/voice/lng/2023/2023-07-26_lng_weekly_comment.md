## Market Commentary

We are now almost at the end of July, and it has in actual fact felt like a proper summer (holiday) week in the markets. Aside from the many industry players reading this week’s reports from their hammocks, the relatively quiet 7 days can be attributed to the general lull in the cargo market. The Southern Europe heatwave has caused some increases in European gas pricing, but this seems to have been relatively well controlled. We have had a few spot requirements concluded in the Atlantic with levels indicating a “balanced” market, whereas the Pacific (where people, paradoxically, aren’t on holiday yet) has stayed very quiet. Surprisingly, we have seen a few short voyages out of the Middle East emerge, with some charterers looking to capitalise on summer sentiment for Q4 liftings in order to optimise freight. Term and multi-month discussions continue in the background, as the market tries to price the contango into winter. Vessel availability has also stayed more or less balanced in both basins whilst on the newbuilding side there are some discussions on new orders, which should be announced out of China soon. Furthermore, it was reported that NYK has found lengthy term charters on their recent orders.
