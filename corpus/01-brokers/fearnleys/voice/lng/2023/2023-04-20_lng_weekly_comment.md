## Market Commentary

Despite national holidays being enjoyed around the globe, activity in the LNG cargo market and subsequently the shipping market has remained buoyant, with a combination of anticipated shorts and FOB tenders leading to a number of fixtures being registered this week. Activity in the Atlantic saw a marked improvement with a flurry of fixtures for 1H May being concluded and prompt vessels continue to see interest with ships being worked firm as we issue this weekly publication. Although the shoulder months still have some weeks to run, rates appear to have found a floor. Should activity continue in the same vein, we can expect rates to stabilise and tick upwards in the comings weeks, yet it is perhaps important to reiterate that there is a big discrepancy in the level of activity and interest between the desirable 174,000m3 tonnage and anything smaller. It is not to say that smaller tonnage is irrelevant, but its marketability needs careful consideration in the Atlantic whilst in the Pacific, owners are offered more relief due to a greater variance in parcel sizes. The newbuilding price sees another small increase, yet no new buildings were reported this week.
