## Market Commentary

With the buzz of Posidonia behind us, we are rattled back to reality by the recent Freeport LNG explosion and a 40% cutback of LNG flows from NordStream 1, due to technical issues, further tightening an already stretched LNG market and releasing a flurry of prompt LNG vessels, many only until recently committed, back unto the market. In turn, while LNG prices across the forward curves of Asian and European indexes rise in the wake of these events, vessels’ spot rates across both basins plummet, bringing us back to levels not seen for some weeks. However, with a fair few requirements still out there in the Atlantic and Pacific, and in anticipation of increasing demand from the East as we inch towards winter, we can expect spot rates to maintain some level of support and buoyancy going forward. On the term side of the equation, a similar story can be told as 1-Year term rates soften off the back of current events. That said, this is driven by prompt availability discounting the coming weeks. Subject to Freeport start-up, the market remains structurally tight and the prompt discount vanishes for later deliveries. On newbuildings, two new orders have been reported this week from a Greek Head Owner as vessel prices continue to tick upwards, and with the rise in US interest rates the impact on newbuild charter rates is likely to be significant.
