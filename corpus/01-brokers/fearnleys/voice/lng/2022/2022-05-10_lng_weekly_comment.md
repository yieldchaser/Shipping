## Market Commentary

Rates continue their upward trajectory and owners are increasingly including positioning payments in their offers. This is not to say that winter has arrived, rather the market is wrestling with the innate inefficiencies of Europe being the premium market for the 6th month in a row. Conventional metrics for assessing utilisation are no longer relevant as tonne-time has soared in the battle to secure slots into NWE (with the DES LNG discount to TTF reported at around $10/MMbtu yesterday). Portfolio tonnage is increasingly tied up in operational storage (i.e., seeking a home) meaning that a handful of new shorts have emerged both sides of Suez. These spot requirements have added to the already firm term market which has in turn dragged up short-term rates (in the Pacific especially). Charterers who were originally targeting cold 2-strokes are now more willing to accommodate older and warmer tonnage, as the list of available vessels to meet a growing number of laycans diminishes. This will be welcome news to “owners” of older tonnage who should be increasingly popular in this age of energy and shipping security. Turning to Newbuilds, it’s been a quiet week for new orders, but prices continue their ascent.
