## Market Commentary

Market activity is relatively muted as participants are enjoying various celebrations around the world. LNG prices have shown a slight drop as Asian demand remains weak due to Covid restrictions predominantly in China, while in the West there is also a softening due to gas supplies currently meeting demand in Europe and prompt DES pricing is now at a significant discount to TTF. In a boost to those markets that will be seeking to refill storages, Hammerfest may restart operations by the end of the month. In the spot market, most requirements still appear primarily in the East, likely in response to South Asian buyers looking at spot cargoes. Spot availability remains ample at both basins, but with markets opening up next week and activity picking up, we may see some reshuffling in portfolios and a subsequent decrease especially in modern tonnage. Term activity remains of focus as sentiments and rate levels keep rising. There has been no New Building orders reported so far this week but prices are steadily increasing. To those celebrating in the Middle East we are wishing Eid Mubarak and to those in the East, we hope everyone is having an enjoyable Golden Week!
