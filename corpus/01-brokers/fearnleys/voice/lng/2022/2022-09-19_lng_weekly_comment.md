## Market Commentary

LNG prices remain elevated on the prompt but further down the curve, the contango rewards those with sufficient length (and appropriate tonnage) to float, whilst those without such length are left wondering how to obtain it! In the Atlantic, a couple of 2-strokes were hotly contested. Whatever transpires over the next week, it is clear that in the West, both size and boil off management capability really matters. In the Pacific, availability has taken a hit this week on the back of some frantic chartering activity. Most fixtures have been on 155,000m3 vessels and below, primarily because this is the only fit for purpose tonnage in the region (other than the occasional large vessel with truncated availability). Cold vessels remain scarce and vessels that were previously topping up the availability list are now propping up the availability list. As a consequence of all the activity, the number of uncovered cargos has reduced significantly, but with more cargoes anticipated over the next couple of weeks, there may be some very interesting fixtures just around the corner. Newbuilding activity remains muted, with the last announcement relating to Qatari business yet with so many new projects in the US anticipated, this segment remains healthy.
