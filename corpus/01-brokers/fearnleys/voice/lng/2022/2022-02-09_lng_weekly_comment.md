## Market Commentary

The current spot LNG shipping market continues its soft trend from last week. The present lack of requirement and limited arbitrage opportunities have further increased the vessel availability list, putting spot rates under pressure. There seem to be no shortage of ships for February – March delivery and although a couple of spot fixtures were recently concluded, we do not anticipate any significant or positive changes to the current levels. Due to a quieter spot market, rate ideas for multi months and up to one year term periods with early start-up have also softened. Despite the recent bearish sentiments, there are still potential inquires for multi months and term charters being discussed. In the newbuilding market, it was reported that Venture Global LNG is linked to a 3+3 potential LNG Carriers orders at DSME for delivery in 2025/2026. Maran Gas have also increased their orders at DSME for 2025 deliveries.
