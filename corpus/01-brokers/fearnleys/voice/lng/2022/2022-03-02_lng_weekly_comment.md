## Market Commentary

Cargo prices continue their rally with TTF hitting $50/MMBtu as uncertainties over gas supplies continue. Arbitrage is currently shut with all cargoes pointing to Europe. There are several factors contributing in the shipping market turning towards either direction in the long run, however on the prompt, we are witnessing an upward trend on levels both in the spot and in the term market. There have been no new building orders this week but prices keep increasing.
