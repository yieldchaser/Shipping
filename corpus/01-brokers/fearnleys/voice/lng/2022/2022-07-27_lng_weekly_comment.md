## Market Commentary

With the summer holiday period in full flow across most of Europe, spot shipping activity has remained light with a focus still towards multi-month business, as indicated by a growth of requirements from off takers seeking coverage across the upcoming winter period and the front months of 2023. Consequently, spot rates continue to stagnate and there has not been a material shift in the number of available vessels in either basin as various subletters seek employment in the near term. With the divergence between spot and multi-month interest, there continues to be a disconnect between spot and terms rates, as has been the theme for much of this year. It was announced this week that two 174,000 m3 were ordered at DSME shipyard, as newbuild prices continue to firm up.
