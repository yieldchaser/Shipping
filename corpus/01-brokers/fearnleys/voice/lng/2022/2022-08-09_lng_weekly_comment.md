## Market Commentary

We observed last week that an unusual feature of this current market is the sheer number of prompt spot cargoes. Well, for better or for worse, this behaviour has now been normalised and so it is only natural that we released another August loading cargo requirement today. Of course, some traditional Asian Owners still adhere to the four to six week fixing window and accordingly they have stoked the fire in the September spot market with a brace of Australian cargoes loading on the same day (albeit from opposite ends of the country). Whilst this activity is welcome news for Owners, one noteworthy complication at present is the difficulty that off-takers are having securing cool down slots. The spread between warm and cold tonnage is unsurprisingly attractive at these LNG prices but with terminals holding firm to their presentation requirements (cold and ready to load) Charterers are faced with a limited pool of suitable vessels to pick from and in turn we have now comfortably moved into the 6-figure territory (Round Trip) for inter-basin cargoes. With a handful of term requirements still being pursued, sentiment has definitely turned bullish across the board and we are witnessing a narrowing of the delta between spot and term rates. No new orders to report this week; in fact, Q3 has been pretty lacklustre in this department, with a grand total of 4 new contracts inked to date. Notwithstanding this, Qatargas slot allocation is keeping concerned parties busy.
