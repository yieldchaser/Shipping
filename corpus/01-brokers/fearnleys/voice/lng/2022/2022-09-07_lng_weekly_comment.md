## Market Commentary

Gastech is back in full force as the LNG industry players gather in Milan perhaps for the first time in over two years. While the much awaited gatherings and networking sessions are all happening this week, ship chartering activities have not slowed down with various spot and term requirements circulating in the market. There have been few straightforward vessel availabilities as the opportunity to charter out mainly involves disponent owners. Spot charter rates are climbing exponentially over the anticipated shortage of shipping capacities for the upcoming winter despite only being in early September and it may be expected that this trend will continue as long as the high demand for LNG develops over the next few months. On newbuildings, it was reported that seven 174,000 m3 have been contracted at DSME for delivery by February 2026 and four at SHI shipyard. The orders were against previous reserved slots for Qatar Energy and ExxonMobil. Separately, we are pleased to announce that Moss Maritime, DNV, Fearnleys and Siemens Energy have partner up to develop the first-of-its-kind LNG vessel.
