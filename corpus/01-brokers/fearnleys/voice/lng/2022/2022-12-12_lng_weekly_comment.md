## Market Commentary

The headlines this week have been all about the cold weather that has persisted in the Northern Hemisphere, corresponding to the draw down in LNG stocks especially in Europe. It has been a good reminder that Mother Nature tends to switch the thermostat rather quickly and although most of the countries have been prepared for this, it has still caused sudden spikes especially on electricity prices across the old continent. This has in turn killed the LNG arbitrage for now, but at least the contango is looking rather firm into Q1 2023. On the LNG shipping side, most of the subletters that have tonnage have been carefully assessing the situation during the last few days, and consequently the spot market looks rather balanced, although it is to be said that all the spot opportunities are now for loadings past the new year. On the other hand, the term market seems to be holding firm with further reports emerging on term fixtures concluded with deliveries as far as Q3 next year. On the newbuilding side, it was a quiet week with no new orders confirmed, but the yards report tender activity and continue to be comfortable with the current orderbooks and future outlook.
