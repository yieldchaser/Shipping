## Market Commentary

It is the first week of 2022 and the LNG market is slowly finding its pace after the Christmas break. Small developments so far, as market participants enjoy their last days of Holidays. The spot market has resumed where it left off, with very few requirements looking into January and February. There are a handful of available vessels to meet these requirements, consisting mainly of excess sublet tonnage. The cargo market remains volatile, though, as TTF and JKM prices are in constant battle. For now, the West-to-East arbitrage is closed but should the volatility persist, we may see some more requirements emerging in the coming weeks. The term market, on the other hand, has continued to be active throughout the end of the year and we anticipate 2022 to be another year with focus on securing term coverage from the various portfolios. In the New Building segment, NYK is understood to have contracted another vessel at SHI following their recent order at the yard. From Fearnley LNG we wish you all again a Happy New Year and here’s to a successful 2022!
