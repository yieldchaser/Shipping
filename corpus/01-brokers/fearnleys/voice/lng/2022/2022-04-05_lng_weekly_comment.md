## Market Commentary

Over the last week we’ve seen a steady flow of spot shipping requirements coming to the market with a shift of availability, neatly matching the new requirements. The overall activity has left the spot rates unchanged. Overarching all spot discussions is the term availability of + 170k CBM vessels and remaining and/or pending requirements to be filled until the end of 2023. The overall availability is apparently scarce, with some occasional subletters making a short appearance before their tonnage is either fixed or withdrawn. We believe that the overall focus on term coverage will persist as the market adopts to the new normal. NB prices continues to increase and does the activity at Yard’s where we’ve recorded 3 NB’s booked this week.
