## Market Commentary

The spot market remains firm this week with a steady amount of fixtures concluded. Rates continue to hold at relatively ”healthy” levels for spot voyages, with a lack of prompt vessels both East and West of Suez. Availability further out in the Pacific remains high but the fixture window is extending closer to these vessels. This tonnage is unlikely to be open for long as the majority of sublets have a backstop and may be needed internally. European LNG storage levels remain high and we may see more vessels head East if discharge slots can’t be secured in Europe. Along with an active spot market, the term shipping market also remains on the gas (excuse the pun) with plenty of interested parties still in or coming to seeking tonnage, and term rates are firming as a consequence. Tonnage from head owners for term requirements still remains elusive. Security of tonnage for the future, predominantly modern tonnage, remains at the forefront of term discussions. Newbuild activity remains high, 6 more orders placed this week and newbuild prices may continue to increase. The first larger LNG event since Covid restrictions were lifted is currently underway as the World Gas Conference in Korea kicked off this week, drawing in crowds from across the industry. For those attending the event in Daegu we hope it has been a great success.
