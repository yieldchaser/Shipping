## Market Commentary

As we slowly make our way towards the end of the summer season, the market continues to apprehensively bubble and churn out a slew of spot and multi-month requirements, predominantly from the East, ahead of everyone’s return to desk. In tandem, this week a small but consistent arbitrage window has opened up off the back of a strengthening JKM (at mid-high $50’s) while a steepening discount to TTF (at high $60’s) prevails, further hinting at an anticipated pickup in activity in the coming weeks. With cool down slots still hard to come by and Charterers holding firm in their demands for cold ships, with the number of them relatively few and far between, we’ve come to a stand-off of sorts between Owners and Charterers. However, as LNG prices mount ever-higher, availability of cold vessels begin to deplete and perhaps even the prospect of a wider arb looms, the creeping pressure to fix might just have Charterers give way and take the best of what they can get while the gettings good. Otherwise, no newbuilding orders reported this week.
