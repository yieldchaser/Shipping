## Market Commentary

A week in which the major global festivals of Easter Passover and Ramadan converge was always going to be a little quieter, but despite a drop off in activity we haven't seen a drop in levels. Enquiries that cooled down last week have re-emerged together with loadings further into May and June. The various holidays have produced volatility in the cargo markets, but the fundamentals remain strong. The term market continues to see more activity, and levels remain strong. The newbuild sector remains heated, with a further two newbuildings from MOL due for delivery in 2026.
