## Market Commentary

Prompt demand from Japan for LNG cargoes continues, as the nation is looking at restocking inventories after the heatwave of last week. Importers from Europe and Asia keep battling for cargoes amidst the global natural gas supply shortage, but current record high seasonal prices have deterred some buyers. The West to East arbitrage is still closed, hence there are few signs of reduction of the sublet tonnage oversupply in the near future. Spot market activity in Atlantic remains subdued, as portfolio players with tonnage keep winning prompt cargo tenders. In the East, there has been some more activity but interest has mainly been focusing on smaller Steam Turbine tonnage putting further pressure on rates. Looking further ahead, the Freeport outage continues to be a concern over the next months, but for the time being most subletters are reluctant to release any modern tonnage over winter in anticipation of potential cargo opportunities or arbitrage openings. There have been no new NBs orders reported so far this week, however both NB and SnP markets remain active with prices firming.
