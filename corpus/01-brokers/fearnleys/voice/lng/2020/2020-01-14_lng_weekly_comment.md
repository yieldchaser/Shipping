## Market Commentary

As we hit mid-January, we are starting to see more action in the spot market; however, this increase in enquiries is counterbalanced by more ships redelivering into the market, widening availability in both basins. The Pacific leads the way in activity, but prompt owners are swallowing this up quickly and rates are edging lower. The fixing window is now well into February, and although prompt vessels continue to gather in the basin we’ve not seen many Owners lured west for the prospect of better numbers in the Atlantic, perhaps because the lower availability is currently matched by fewer requirements. Portfolio players remain in charge of the tonnage situation in the basin, holding it stable and leaving room for optimization should they have the space to do so. With the Lunar New Year festivities starting at the back end of next week we could see a flux of demand pop up as market players hoping for some rest over the period look to cover themselves before the holidays begin. Yard prices remain unchanged since last week, and no new orders have been reported in the market.
