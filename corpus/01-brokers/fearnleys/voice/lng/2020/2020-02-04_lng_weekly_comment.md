## Market Commentary

The past week has been subdued for LNGCs as they follow the wider energy markets, coming under more scrutiny than normal. The LNG cargo market has fallen close to record lows, with weak sentiment being reinforced by the extended public holidays in China and the impact on demand, with uncertainty ongoing. In the west, a prompt Atlantic requirement that kept bearish sentiment at bay last week has vanished. The region remains untested and although rates are reassessed lower, Atlantic availability looks ominous and we could see further falls. The East is currently in the midst of a competitive spot tender and whilst we await the results the amount of interest generated among "owners" again adds some downwards pressure to rates.
