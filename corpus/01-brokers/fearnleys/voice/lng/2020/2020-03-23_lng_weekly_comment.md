## Market Commentary

The commodity and financial markets across the globe have been nothing less than volatile, no different to what the LNG shipping markets have experienced of late. The LNG shipping spot market activities went into overdrive in the Atlantic with a series of vessel requirements issued, along with a flurry of spot fixtures and vessel swaps, as sentiment spiked amongst the shipowners and players with relets. These are spilling over to the Pacific market where intra-Asia chartering requirements began to emerged. Vessels are available for shipowners to propose to these requirement, however with increased rate expectations as compared to just a week ago. As many of us work from homes during these unprecedented times, the lockdowns, quarantines and border closures have proved to provide a challenge to the shipping operations and crew management efforts. We spare a thought to all seafarers who have been affected during this period. No LNG vessel newbuild orders or 2nd hand transactions were reported last week.
