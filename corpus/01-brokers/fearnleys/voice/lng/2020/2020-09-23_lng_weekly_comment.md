## Market Commentary

As we leave behind a difficult summer, we move forward into an autumn showing cautious signs of the usual seasonal recovery. The shipping market is still grappling with the fallout from recent and potentially upcoming US Gulf storms and a revival of the Covid-19 threat, but the immediate impact of relets flooding the Atlantic basin has eased. Any such tonnage is only available for a limited period, so while there may be some bargains for very prompt charterers, the typical fixing window looks brighter for owners. The continuous bubbling of cargoes and requirements in the East picked up in recent weeks and is now cutting into the availability list, and rates are moving up as a result. There were no fresh orders placed at the yards this week
