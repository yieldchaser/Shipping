## Market Commentary

As we publish the energy major Total is performing its remote investor day with a very central focus on the environment and a transition to a less carbon intensive future. At the same time prominent news stories portray that traders are also accelerating their transition to renewables. It’s this transition that relevance to LNG really starts to take hold and should give some comfort to the average reader of this weekly publication - LNG currently has a very significant role to play. Global LNG demand might be taking a pause whilst celebrations are enjoyed for China’s Golden week but weather systems such as ‘La Nina’ are predicted to bring colder temperatures in due course adding to increasing market demand. On the flip side, supply is subject to significant interruptions across the global with the latest in Norway at the Hammerfest plant, thankfully no injuries, but production is not expected to return until November. More specifically and to LNGC’s, interruptions, increased demand and illiquidity of vessels combines to create a very intriguing market, consequences could result in muted activity but could also cause significant opportunity for freight to rise to year highs as schedules change and ton time increases as vessel head further afield. Naysayers will point to high European storage levels and the lack of global industrial production as everyone tousles with the pandemic. It won’t change overnight and we don’t expect great changes in levels this week but next week may bring more clarity and improved owner returns. In the meantime we would like to wish all those who are celebrating Golden week a pleasant and restful break.
