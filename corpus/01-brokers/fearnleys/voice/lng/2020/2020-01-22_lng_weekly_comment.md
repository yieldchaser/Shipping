## Market Commentary

The seasonal improvements in terms and market sentiment in the “Winter period” is now easing, with headline rates tapering off as vessel availability grows. The charter market faces backwardation in the coming weeks, and shipowners are looking to secure charters ahead of the shoulder months of March, April and May. The past week saw a handful of spot fixtures in both basins, mainly addressing a firm need of vessels due to fleet scheduling constraints rather than potential charters arising from trading opportunities; cargo optimization is creating more FOB sales but these are being picked up by players with their own shipping, leading to less chartering activity. Likewise, several opportunities that were initially for shipping instead resulted in FOB sales with no incremental spot shipping demand. This trend may continue as a significant number of the vessels available in the market for the next two months are sublets from projects, traders and portfolio players rather than those owned by independent shipowners, and the requirements that do lead to firm charters will be keenly fought over.
