## Market Commentary

As prompt LNG trading and shipping conditions continue to underwhelm, most market players are busy making the best of things today while looking ahead for hints of change. The bare facts are that LNG pricing remains low across the globe, US cargo cancellations are biting and vessel availability is creeping up. But if these pessimal conditions are the harshest for survival they’re also a great driver of ideas and innovation, and as we enter the second half of the year there are increasing signs of clever thinking and smart trades. High inventory levels and low pricing in Europe combined with fewer US cargoes is creating a novel dynamic in the north Atlantic, and demand continues to return to markets in the east as economies gain momentum emerging from lockdown. The prospect of European reloads to capture value either in localised contangoes or in inter-basin trade are encouraging for tonne-time and tonne-mile demand, and in turn ship owners are entertaining creative ideas on rates such floating structures. There will be some fascinating moves in the months ahead. Although no newbuildings have been ordered this week, Qatar Petroleum have moved to reserve berths for more than 100 LNGCs at the three main Korean yards.
