## Market Commentary

Shipping rates are continuing their descending trend in both basins - quicker than one would expect, as lacklustre demand goes on. Cargo prices remain under pressure, offering minimal trading margins. In the West, increasing availability sees Independent Owners and Portfolio Players with excess length “fighting” for requirements. In such a market, utilisation for Owners is key, hence recent tests on levels have shown further drops. In the East, halt in demand and delays in Chinese ports on the back of the Corona virus see more diversions as well as floating storage situations, the levels of which, however, are not high enough to the extent we could see some recovery in rates. Nevertheless, should floating storage continue, we should not disregard the case of few extra requirements to emerge in this basin. Term tender activity is still active, but there were no NB orders recorded this week on conventional tonnage. In the midsize segment, PETRONAS signed a 12-year time charter party for two 79,960m3 newbuilding LNG vessels with “K”Line, on the back of orders at Hudong-Zhonghua Shipbuilding (Group) Co. Ltd. The twin sister vessels, dubbed the “Wuhaogou-Max” or “W-Max” are specifically constructed to be fully optimised for the delivery of LNG supply commitments to Shenergy’s Wuhaogou LNG terminal in Shanghai, China.
