## Market Commentary

It has been one of the most dramatic weeks in the history of oil & gas industry, of which we and other market players are still trying to grasp what the real ramifications might be for the LNG markets. The oil price crash and wildly spreading Covid-19 virus have left many wondering what the rest of the year might entail and how one can adjust their forward views amidst this volatility and uncertainty. That said, the LNG shipping market had a surprisingly active week, with multiple spot and multi-month opportunities emerging, the latter being clearly incentivized by softening rate levels for 6-12 month time charters. One could describe this “multi-month hunting” almost an annual phenomenon taking place in around March-May when the final winter activity has bypassed and owners are struggling get good utilization in the spot market. However, this year it all feels a bit different, as the unpredictability of LNG prices, cargo flows and shipping rates seems to be influencing market players to hedge their bets and choose utilization over speculation. To sum up, we are keeping spot rates mostly unchanged this week, but have adjusted our term rates across the line. From the newbuilding front, we have no new orders to report this week, but yard prices in general are slightly softening as US dollar strengthens.
