## Market Commentary

The current market remains rather bearish with shipping rates seen dipping week on week. At present, there is quite a handful of requirements firmed up this week which did help ease some rate pressure. We do, however, not anticipate much changes to the current rate levels seen. Moreover, there is limited improvement in the LNG prices, which kept the West-to-East arbitrage opportunities low. The continued lack of cross basin trades/arbitrage has also reduced ton-miles significantly, which in turn, would create relatively lengthy short-term supply of shipping in both basins as vessels fixed for a single voyage would quickly become available again. Some charterers were also seen using this opportunity now to inquire for multi month charters. Should the rates be fairly competitive, we do envisage Charterers securing vessel(s) for a longer duration, possibily into Q1 next year. This could very well prove to be a win-win for both parties, especially for Owners who value and target to achieve maximum vessel utilization.
