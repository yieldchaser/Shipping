## Market Commentary

Firm market conditions remain but with many expected to take some well-earned rest after a long year, we could see activity wane in the last two weeks of 2020. Bids continue to flood in for vessels with prompt availability and the drag from the Pacific into the Atlantic continues to be the preference for Owners. Those with Pacific requirement remain at battle against strong duration at high levels in the Atlantic. Tonnage opening in late January could be a cause of concern as current backwardation on the cargo side shortens opportunities for trade return at current freight levels. However, the prompt market is likely to continue to hold until we see the fixing window shift into play with increase of tonnage expected. Rates are likely to hover around current levels for now, but sublet availability could put a slight soft tone on rates towards end of winter period. The newbuilding market remains active: at least four new 174,000cbm have been inked in this week, two will be built at Samsung Heavy, and two at HSHI. Despite a recent influx in orders, we are currently still down from the 19 orders placed in December last year. For those who celebrate, a very merry Christmas from all of us here at Fearnley LNG.
