## Market Commentary

It has been another monotonous week for the LNG Shipping market. Sentiments continue to dip further amid signs of limited demand in both basins. The vast number of cancellations on cargoes to be delivered in June from US projects and what seems like an unending closed arbitrage trading window have led to a lack of opportunities for shipping and rather, more sublets seen in the market. The shipping rates is still under pressure as “last done” were reportedly concluded lower week on week. Nevertheless, we do see some premium on the larger sizes, as more end users look to take advantage of the low LNG spot prices to procure larger quantities. Furthermore, there are discussions with some players interested in securing multi months at the current levels, for prompt to Q3 2020 delivery and for periods up till the end of next winter season. While the present environment is challenging, if the ongoing lock-downs and restrictions in most parts of the world eases, we could see the global energy demand pick up and LNG demand to follow. On the Newbuilding front, there hasn’t been any firm deals concluded last week and sentiment remains steady to soft.
