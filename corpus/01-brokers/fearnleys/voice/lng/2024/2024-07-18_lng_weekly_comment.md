## Market Commentary

Market activity remains high this week and all eyes are on spot rates as requirements for August firm up. The imbalance between rates in the East and West persists, with the West still seeing higher levels than the East although the delta has narrowed. On vessel availability, more 2-Stroke vessels are expected to become open starting from the end of July in the West. Conversely, the East sees little emergence of these vessels. Although the list of prompt Steamers is growing in the East, the challenge lies in finding suitable requirements for these vessels. Additionally, Charterers have started seeking tonnage to cover their Winter needs. While there are some emerging requirements for September in both basins, most of these are tentative and expected to firm up soon. Meanwhile on the cargo front, Freeport LNG is expected to restart its first train this week, following disruptions by Hurricane Beryl. There have been no new orders this week.
