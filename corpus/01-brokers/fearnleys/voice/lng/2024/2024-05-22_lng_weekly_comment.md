## Market Commentary

Following a busy close to the previous week all eyes have been on rate levels, but terms remain stable in both basins. Prompt availability remains quite high particularly East of Suez, and opportunities for older vessels is challenging especially with many vessels either warm or likely to warm up. There is a little more liquidity for modern 2-strokes and TFDEs, and the level of activity is enough to support current market levels without threatening to nudge higher. The arbitrage for taking cargoes East is a little more pronounced than it has been and there are more ships plying the usual trade out of the Atlantic, and this may result in upward pressure in the coming weeks. The term charter market remains lively as Charterers look to secure tonnage ahead of winter, with further requirements expecting to firm up in the next few weeks. Meanwhile on the cargo front, ongoing maintenance and supply uncertainties are causing some disruption although Freeport production is rebuilding. There have been no new orders this week.
