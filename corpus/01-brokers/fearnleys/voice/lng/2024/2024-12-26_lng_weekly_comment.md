## Market Commentary

As the holiday season continues for many, the LNG shipping market has remained relatively stable since our last update. Spot fixtures have been recorded in both the Atlantic and Pacific basins, but without significant movements in charter rates, despite the seemingly tightened availability of two-stroke tonnage in the US Gulf towards the end of January. In the East of Suez, firm opportunities have been limited, with the majority of shipping requirements appearing out of the Atlantic basin for intra-AB trades with arbitrage opportunities remaining scarce. Meanwhile, multi-month and term charter discussions have taken more of a focus, with several deals being executed in the past week, as players look to capitalise on attractive rates that are becoming more achievable across market. With the next seven days expected to be relatively quiet as the market slowly returns from the holiday period, we will see if any end-of-year surprises emerge. No newbuilding orders for conventional LNG carriers have been reported this week, with newbuilding prices holding.
