## Market Commentary

Last week saw a good number of fixtures, although they were insufficient to support rates, which continue to be under pressure. This week commenced with a relatively quiet Monday due to the UK bank holiday. However, over the past few days, several spot enquiries emerged for both basins, with cargoes requiring ships for end September and October. There is an ample supply of ships available promptly and in the upcoming months, and we can anticipate fixtures going forward. Owners are generally holding out for longer-term charters of at least one year, rather than fixing only for the winter months, to avoid being idle during the “shoulder months” of March, April, and May. Regarding multi-year charters, there are ongoing enquiries, of which owners would be eager to secure long-term charters for their modern and newbuilding vessels. Meanwhile, there are more steam turbine vessels being circulated for sale, coinciding with the end of their current long-term charters. There have been no newbuilding purchases this week, as prices have not softened despite having no orders of LNG vessels at the shipyards for some time now.
