## Market Commentary

After the tumultuous activity that erupted in the market last week, it has been a different story so far this week with the majority of Asia away on public holidays and the Atlantic players catching their collective breath. Activity has been muted, and rates are holding at similar levels to last done yet there are still cargoes out there to fix so this lull may be only a brief respite. Gas prices continue to soften, which is expected as we transition towards the latter part of winter, and demand in both regions is not expected to rebound significantly. French strikes at terminals are effecting scheduling but are unlikely to have an impact on pricing as inventory levels remain high, with the arb opening slightly we may see some of these cargoes head out East if there are takers. Although no newbuilding orders were placed this week, it was yet another significant week for the QatarEnergy project, firming up an additional 25 vessels with fellow compatriots, Nakilat.
