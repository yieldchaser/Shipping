## Market Commentary

The shipping industry is enjoying a brief slowdown during Posidonia, although the LNG sector remains busy and continues to build on the high levels of activity seen so far this year. Spot market rates are finally showing signs of change particularly in the Atlantic, with notable rises for deliveries into July. There is also further interest in multimonth tonnage through to early 2025, but the lists are getting quite short for such tonnage and rates continue to climb for winter business. Vessel availability is balanced between the West and East, due to tight cargo arbitrage, with both indexes reaching year-to-date highs. Record heatwaves, maintenance and congestions are likely to have a rollercoaster impact on shipping, albeit with a trendline upwards. On the new building front, Adnoc has announced plans to build six LNG carriers in two South Korean yards.
