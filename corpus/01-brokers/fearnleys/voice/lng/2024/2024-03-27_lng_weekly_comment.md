## Market Commentary

As we approach the end of the first quarter, the market can reflect on a very busy few months in which players have adapted well to the changing dynamics. With both canals essentially out of action, with the implementation of the EU ETS scheme, with engine power limitation restricting some speeds and with LNG supply surges and disruptions, there has been a lot to deal with! Perhaps unsurprisingly given these uncertainties, the LNG freight market did not drop as fast or as far as some had predicted, and the past weeks had seen a broadly flat market with spot rates for 2-strokes staying above the $50k per day mark; however, ongoing LNG production issues and vessel over-supply are now weighing on rates, and this important benchmark has been breached. There continues to be enough demand to keep owners and sub-letters busy and there is life in the arbitrage for US Gulf cargoes to go East, although many of the emerging requirements are tenders or optimisations and the spot market is now softening. On multi-month and longer term charters, there has been a slight lull as owners and charterers are a little out of alignment on periods and rates right now, but this is temporary and activity will pick up again as we approach the summer months. There have been no new orders this week.
