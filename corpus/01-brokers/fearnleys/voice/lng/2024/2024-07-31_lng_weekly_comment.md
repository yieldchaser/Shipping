## Market Commentary

As we move into a new month, the market has been busy firming up and working spot and multimonth requirements. The end of last week saw a number of clean fixtures or vessels get placed on subjects in both basins, with the Atlantic basin remaining at a premium to the Pacific, while this week the spot market has quietened down and attention has been more on the longer term opportunities, with several ongoing requirements seeing indicative offers due. Thus, LNG players will be eager to see where rate levels will settle for the winter months. On the availability front, the list of prompt Steam vessels continues to grow, presenting a challenge in finding suitable charter requirements for these vessels as they warm up and await opportunities. Additionally, a number of 2-stroke vessels are expected to become available in upcoming months in both basins. On the cargo side, Freeport has resumed operations at all three of its liquefaction trains and this will draw down some of the prompt tonnage. Meanwhile, there have been no new orders this week.
