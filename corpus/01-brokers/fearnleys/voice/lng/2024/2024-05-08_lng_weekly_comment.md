## Market Commentary

Following Golden Week, the current working week has seen more public holidays around the world and thus it has been a relatively quiet start to the week in the LNG market. There has been an uptick in enquiries and there will be more fixing in the coming days. High inventory levels in certain regions have led to stagnant prices though, and with surplus prompt tonnage in all basins spot charter rates are expected to remain stable. In contrast, there has been more activity in multi-month periods as cargo players are looking for winter shipping security. HHI's announcement of two new LNG carriers ordered at $266 million each sets a new benchmark, indicating a strong outlook for the shipbuilding industry.
