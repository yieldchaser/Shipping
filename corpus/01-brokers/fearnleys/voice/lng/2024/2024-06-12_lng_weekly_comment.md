## Market Commentary

As players in the shipping industry return to their normal work pace following Posidonia, the LNG shipping market has also reverted to the levels of activity observed in previous weeks, predominantly in the West. So far this week, various spot requirements have emerged to cover July loadings out of the Atlantic basin, and several fixtures are currently being worked. The demand for both two-stroke tonnage and mid-sized vessels, combined with a tightening availability list, has increased pressure on charter rates in the Atlantic and strengthened market sentiment. Conversely, East of Suez, activity has slowed due to a limited number of firm requirements, with the focus so far this week being directed to FOB cargo tenders and the growing number of developing requirements for the second half of July and August loadings. In view of the current market conditions in the East, owners may begin to consider positioning their vessels to the Atlantic with a potentially improved chance to reduce idle time and to capitalize on potential arbitrage opportunities. On the newbuilding front, prices remain stable as no new orders have been reported so far this week.
