## Market Commentary

The LNG market remains flat, with few transactions as bearish fundamentals and minor index fluctuations continue to keep netbacks unattractive. Spot demand is generally limited to optimisations and cargo tenders, and the margins available for shipping are not very generous, especially with ample vessel supply. While a winter upturn is still expected, it’s unlikely to match the peaks of previous years. The approach of a major conference is often associated with a quieter spell in the spot market, and we will see whether the present lull persists through Gastech. In contrast to the spot market, longer-term requirements are keeping owners occupied and it’s likely that discussions in Houston next week will see more enquiries emerge or firm up. Six more QC-Max LNG carriers have been placed on order by QatarEnergy. And while prices remain stable, the heavy 2024 delivery schedule could face delays later in Q4.
