## Market Commentary

Following recent softening, rates have stabilised in the spot and period markets as national holidays globally have limited levels of activity. The main source of requirements has arisen from market cargo tenders across all basins that seek vessels subject to award, yet most of these opportunities are being covered through portfolios with available tonnage to utilise. A consequence is that prompt availability remains well stocked, yet owners are unwilling to lock in a large idle periods at current levels, and with most requirements a few weeks out, the misalignment is causing protracted negotiations. From the yards, four newbuilding's confirmed this week with a JV between K Line and Hyundai Glovis being awarded part of the QE Phase 2 requirement. Delivery August 2027 and a price in the region of $230M USD. For unallocated slots, prices remain flat. The second-hand market also continues to be active with robust prices as further sales are reported this week, although the details of these sales consisting of two mid-size carriers is not yet fully known.
