## Market Commentary

It has been a bit of a mixed bag when it comes the sentiment of this week’s LNG shipping market. There has been robust chartering activity with multiple new spot requirements in both basins, which coupled with the partial restart of Freeport would usually mean a “full-steam ahead” in our ship order telegraph. However, on the other hand we have seen a flurry of new vessels been shown as available for various periods, especially from Asian subletters, causing some to question whether shipping demand is going to hold firm through the winter. But as ever, no one has the crystal ball to see how things will play out, and like so many previous cycles we must brace ourselves for some rate volatility in the months to come. As we currently stand, the Pacific spot rates have almost caught-up with the Atlantic rates; however, the exception remains for inter-basin rates, since most subletters are reluctant to show tonnage with worldwide flexibility from the Atlantic. On the newbuilding front, it has been a quiet week with no new orders, but we are almost at the end the “holiday month” so expect to see more activity going forward.
