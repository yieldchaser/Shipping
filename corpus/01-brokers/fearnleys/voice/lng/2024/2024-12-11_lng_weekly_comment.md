## Market Commentary

Spot chartering activity continues apace as we head into the final weeks of 2024, across a fixing window stretching from the out through to the end of January. There has been tightness on the front end in the Atlantic but rates are remaining stubbornly flat for TFDE's and 2-strokes. A little further out, charterers with January requirements have looked to get ahead of their remaining Christmas shopping and a good number of ships have been placed on subjects. In spite of this forward fixing, last week’s marginal rate increase does not yet feel embedded into the market and may be tested again. In the same vein, Pacific requirements seeking modern tonnage for end January have revealed healthy availabilities with levels under pressure as portfolios complete deliveries into Asia on effectively sunk shipping.
