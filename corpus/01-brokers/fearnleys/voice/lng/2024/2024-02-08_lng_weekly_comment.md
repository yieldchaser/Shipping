## Market Commentary

It's starting to feel like the Red Sea might be having an impact on the market after all - or everyone was just so excited about the potential for spot shipping that it has now become a self-fulfilling prophecy? Either way, this past week has been one of the busiest we've seen in years. Whether it's summer term coverage or single voyage charterers, all three basins have been buzzing and despite a surplus of tonnage being shown for each LayCan, rates feel like they're firming. This has translated into higher term fixtures and, in a few instances, a decent spot premium too. As ever it's anyone's guess as to how long this will last but it's given the usual short-term protagonists sufficient cause to stop and reassess their next steps. On the newbuilding side, SHI has secured a large order to build 15 LNG carriers for Qatar for $3.45 billion with an average price of about $230 million per LNG vessel.
