## Market Commentary

Where did October go? A handful of spot fixtures have been concluded since our last report, with a similar number of requirements on the horizon or being worked at the time of writing. Charterers continue to pile pressure on headline rates on the prompt, although with sustained activity there has been some resistance from subletters and levels appear to have bottomed out in the high-teens for modern 2-strokes in the Atlantic, at least for the time being. With sentiment at its weakest, the fixing window is moving right out to cover periods into January and throughout the first quarter 2025 in some cases. Whilst we would typically expect a healthy premium for forward fixing, particularly at this time of year, rate increments appear minimal despite requirements effectively locking away vessels for the remainder of the traditional Winter period. Competition for similar requirements is likely to remain strong. Yearly highs on European LNG prices and minimal arbitrage opportunities continue to ensure the bulk of flexible volumes remain in the Atlantic, skewing already weakening fundamentals in favour of oversupply. That said, with freight at such depressed levels there are plenty of fixing opportunities on spot and an increase in 1-2 year charters may soon follow. Whilst most discussions thus far have been on index-linkage, a correction in fixed price charter rate ideas for 2025 onwards could spur additional demand for the safe haven of period coverage.
