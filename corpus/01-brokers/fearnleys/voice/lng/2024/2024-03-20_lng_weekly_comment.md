## Market Commentary

As we approach the end of Q1 2024, the LNG shipping market maintains a steady course, a typical scenario for this time of year characterized by lower demand and moderated LNG prices. There is some uncertainty on the cargo side, with traders keeping a close eye on supply concerns such from potential cyclone disruptions in Australia, unplanned maintenance of Norway’s Aasta gas field and some lower production out of Nigeria. In spite of the above, there have been signs of a reviving freight market with several requirements firming up in both basins and firming rates for modern tonnage in the Atlantic. The first half of this week saw some anticipated spot requirements firm up in the East, accompanied by a couple of new opportunities for tonnage holders out of the Atlantic. With the high vessel competition in the east, owners and charterers will be keen to see how rates will move in the wake of this activity. Furthermore, although cargoes have been traded within their respective basins, there is a small arbitrage flickering to life and charterers are seeking flexibility to head East. As well as increased spot activity, multi month and term business continues to be discussed although the specific periods being sought by charterers or offered by owners are not always aligned. There were no newbuilding orders reported this week; however, prices remain stable.
