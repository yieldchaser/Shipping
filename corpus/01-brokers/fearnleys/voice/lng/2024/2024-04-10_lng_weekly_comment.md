## Market Commentary

Eid celebrations have slowed down activity as many people are enjoying holidays around the world, causing a slight softening on spot rates. Vessel availability has increased on the prompt, as ample sublet tonnage is being pushed into the market, coupled with independent owners’ tonnage redeliveries from their previous term charters. More requirements are expected to emerge for May cargoes next week and this should see tonnage being absorbed. On the term side, Charterers interest has turned to multimonth terms covering summer as well as for autumn and into winter and periods discussed are around a year, but the bid/ ask spread is still a little wide for now. There were no newbuild orders reported this week. Prices at the yards remain stable, with activity mainly for larger tankers.
