## Market Commentary

It is the first week after the Chinese New Year, and as expected we have seen more activity in the market, especially in the Middle-East & Pacific regions. The spot market has really kept everyone busy, and the amount of requirements in recent weeks have brought back some memories from a few years ago, when spot fixing was the dominant activity, before the crazy days of term fixing experienced in the last two years. Furthermore, with the recent fixtures done at distinctly firmer levels than two weeks ago (at least in the Pacific), the spot market may have “bottomed out” at least for now. However, the LNG cargo market has a very shoulder month-esque feeling, and prices continue to track down with muted demand both in Asia and Europe. This is despite the fact that this year’s “black swan” event at the Red Sea is keeping everyone with waterborne cargoes on their toes, and unfortunately, as every week goes by, there hasn’t been any added glimmer of hope of things getting better anytime soon. On the newbuilding front, it was a relative quiet week for LNG carriers, albeit the biggest headlines were definitely yet another extension of the Mozambique project’s newbuilding deal of 17 LNG carriers, this time pushing the deadline of confirmation until later July this year.
