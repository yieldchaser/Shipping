## Market Commentary

There has been little change to rates and sentiment in spot LNG shipping over the past week, with a handful of fixtures spread between the two basins at levels largely unchanged week-on-week. Although as a word of caution for those claiming winter has already passed us by, storage levels in Europe are being drawn down quickly and Korea is experiencing record snowfall for this time of year. Traction on multi-month and mid-term charters remains limited though we could see some moves for periods in the range 12-24 months before the end of the year. There are more positive notes for long-term LNG growth as President Trump and his team continue to outline plans for their return to the White House. The rapid restart of new U.S. export permit approvals featured prominently in a broad energy package floated this week, which is likely to support new long-term offtake agreements next year. There has also been some moves on the FSRU front, with reports that the Energos Eskimo is heading to Egypt for a 10yr charter to EGAS once she finishes up duty in Aqaba, Jordan in mid-2025. EGAS is also looking for another FSRU in the near-term to support a widening gap between domestic gas production and expected growth in power consumption.
