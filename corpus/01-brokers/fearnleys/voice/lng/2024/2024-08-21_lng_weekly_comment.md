## Market Commentary

It’s counterintuitive to be dropping rates as we charge ahead into the Q4 fixing window, but the conservative world of LNG appears to be shaking off the shackles of conventional wisdom these days. The main focus this week has been down the curve, with a handful of Charterers looking for big ships with laycans in the second half of this decade. This has kept the smiles on the faces of the fortunate owners who have Newbuild tonnage (or slots) on offer, and helps distract from the paucity of demand in the short term market. The latter is completely justifiable when the fundamentals are examined, but we’ve become so used to sentiment calling the shots that it seems unfaithful to write the rest of 2024 off too quickly. What we do know is that volumes on the water always spike in Q4 and this means that shipping utilisation will also go up. Will it be enough to turn a five-figure market into a 200k per day bonanza? Maybe not. But this certainly looks to be a buying opportunity for modern tonnage and if too many savvy charterers pick up on this, anything is possible. No newbuilding orders this week, but this will need to change soon also.
