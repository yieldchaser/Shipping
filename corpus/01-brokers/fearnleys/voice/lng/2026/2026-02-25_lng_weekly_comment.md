## Market Commentary

The LNG shipping markets East and West of Suez have been moving in different directions lately, and the trend has continued this past week with rates softening in Asia and the Middle East while firming in the Atlantic. In the East, there continues to be a steady flow of requirements but competition among owners and subletters is giving charterers the edge in negotiations especially for those that can work with smaller vessels. The basis for the rising market in the Atlantic is well understood, with off-takers working hard to mitigate weather-related disruptions and delays. The market has climbed considerably week-on-week, and with portfolios holding on to tonnage there are some windows showing no availability at all. While there is speculation over whether the spot market will correct as these disruptions unwind, the multimonth market for periods through summer suggests a higher floor than we saw in January.
