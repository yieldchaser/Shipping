## Market Commentary

Looser market fundamentals are reasserting influence on the spot side this week, as despite a slightly gentler decline in rates than some had predicted the downward pressure is growing. There is still an impressive amount of volume being produced and at the time of writing, there remains a healthy handful of requirements to be worked in both basins; however, despite the fixing frenzy continuing any fresh requirements are matched with fresh availabilities. Rising tensions between Iran and the US, along with colder weather across Europe, have led to a narrower TTF/JKM spread and a firmly closed Arb for Atlantic cargoes to the East. Uncertainty on scheduling in Egypt and the ongoing risk of bad weather in the Atlantic remain potential disrupters, but portfolios are confident enough to show the length that had all but disappeared through parts of Q4. Discussions for longer period remain active, with the soft front months blurring into concerns of a repeat of last winter on both owners and charterers minds. The busier streak in the newbuilding space continues with multiple orders being finalised, whilst several owners are leaning towards more speculative plays in anticipation of large-scale projects applying further upward pressure on prices.
