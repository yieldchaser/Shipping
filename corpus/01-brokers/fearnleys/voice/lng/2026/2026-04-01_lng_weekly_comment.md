## Market Commentary

Just when we thought this market had run out of surprises, the world of LNG Shipping was flipped on it's head (again) this week when Qatar Energy opened the top drawer and pulled out a playbook from the past. In an innovative move that would make Nobu Su proud, the world's biggest LNG Charterer launched their Qatar Max Coal Carrier project this week, to tap into the skyrocketing black diamond demand from their largest Asian customers. Dubbed the "Coal-Max" for short, a source in Doha (who spoke on the condition of anonymity) described how this latest masterpiece was born out of a particularly intense brain-storming session on Monday. "Basically, the shipping team said 'we're 20 ships long', the short-term marketing team said 'all our customers are switching to coal', and I said: 'let's just open up the tanks and fill them with Newcastle's finest?' Carbon is carbon, hey"? The upgraded vessels should start delivering their newest fossil fuel in Q3 this year, although at the time of print, neither Qatar Energy nor any Class Society were returning our calls.
