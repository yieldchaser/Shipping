## Market Commentary

With Gastech drawing to a close, the rush of fixtures concluded at the tail-end of last week has been followed by a quieter week in fixing terms but a very active few days of meetings and discussions. The ships that have been placed on subjects are at higher levels than we have seen for some weeks, and a broad consensus emerged during Gastech that the market has likely passed through the bottom and will continue to strengthen as we approach the fourth quarter. That said, there was not as much consensus on how far the recovery will take rates or even what would constitute a bullish or bearish winter market. High LNG prices remain and this typically correlates with higher freight and may yet define the winter, but thus far we have seen an inverse relationship as owners and sub-letters have fixed lower rates to mitigate the higher boil-off costs of idling. While this dynamic remains and as LNG prices push higher, it is likely to act as a ceiling on freight until (or unless) traders and portfolios begin pulling tonnage back into their own fleets.
