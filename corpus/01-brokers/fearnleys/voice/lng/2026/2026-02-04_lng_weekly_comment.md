## Market Commentary

The LNG shipping market appears to have paused for breath after the highs and lows of winter volatility, with enough activity to keep owners and charterers busy but with rates across all three major regions broadly steady. The status quo is for very low rates in the Atlantic and there doesn’t seem to be much respite in the near future for owners or sub-letters seeking to capture value in their open positions; there may be some more opportunities as US producers get back on track after the recent weather-related disruptions, but competition among 2-stroke tonnage remains fierce. The markets East of Suez still command a premium for now despite some softening sentiment, and players will be watching the ongoing spot tenders with interest. If there is a light on the horizon for owners, it is the multimonth/multiyear market for which recent fixtures price in a strengthening market later in the year. For those visiting Doha for LNG 2026, we hope you all enjoy a successful trip among old friends, with particular credit to our Qatari hosts for once again providing a fabulously warm and friendly welcome!
