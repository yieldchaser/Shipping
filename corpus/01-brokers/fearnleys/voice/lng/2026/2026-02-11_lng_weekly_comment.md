## Market Commentary

After a highly successful LNG2026 in Doha, it is London’s turn to be in the spotlight with International Energy Week and it feels appropriate that the focus shifts to the Atlantic. After a rollercoaster winter that saw rates surge well into the six digits before crashing down below $10,000 per day just weeks later, the region is enjoying a bounce in charter rates for early March with levels around double what they were this time last week. Turbulent weather is still impacting schedules and is leading to a tightening availability list, and with several uncovered cargoes right in the fixing window there is more likelihood the renaissance will continue than it will stall. The Middle East and Pacific regions are holding steady and are still at a premium to the Atlantic, although it there is a fair chance the situation will be reversed within days if current momentum holds. All this will give owners some comfort, yet the landscape remains challenging and will not be helped by the number of sublets being show for multimonth periods. We wouldn’t unbuckle the seatbelts just yet.
