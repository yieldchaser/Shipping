## Market Commentary

The LNG and LNG shipping markets are suffering seismic shocks in the wake of military actions in the Middle East. The geopolitical situation is very fluid and information is flowing fast and is not always subject to validation, but it is increasingly apparent that there is no quick resolution on the horizon and the disruptions we are seeing to the LNG trade are likely to persist for some time. How long will Middle Eastern production remain offline? How will Asia adapt to reduced supply? When will Europe seek to replenish low storage levels? How will US volume be distributed between importers? It is too early to answer these questions with much certainty, but these past days have shown charterers are willing to pay rates of 10 times the levels from last week to secure prompt tonnage, and there is interest for multi-month periods up to a year in the six figure range – unthinkable as we prepared our report last week. Underpinning the frantic trading, chartering and operational activity that is keeping us all very busy, we are grateful to the brave seafarers working in increasingly challenging situations across all sectors.
