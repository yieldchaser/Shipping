## Market Commentary

The momentum reported last week has continued in both basins, albeit in fits and starts, with spot rates in the Atlantic basin now edging higher than in the Pacific and Middle East. The primary driver in what is still a fundamentally over-supplied Atlantic market has been scheduling disruptions, mainly linked to cold weather impacting cargo operations as well as storm and adverse sea conditions. The result is several mis-matches between cargo dates and vessel arrival times, which has added to the usual steady stream of requirements and pushed rates back above $30,000 per day. The tight spell is currently within the middle decade of March, but if disruptions continue and if more requirements emerge this is likely to spread through towards the end of March. The markets East of Suez are a little quieter as expected through the holidays, although recent fixing levels are struggling to hold steady due to a healthy availability list. Multimonth activity is also a little more muted this past week, although with the interest shown in recent candidate vessels this is surely just a pause. And for those celebrating, we hope you have enjoyed the holidays!!!
