## Market Commentary

The market appears to have entered a more stable, albeit still fragile phase. Volatility on commodity pricing has subsided somewhat, perhaps in protest in response to weeks of wild swings at the news and non-news dominating the public discourse around closure or opening of the Straits of Hormuz. Paper markets have grown increasingly resistant to new "news" with the reality already priced in on the physical. In the Atlantic basin, activity remains subdued, with several participants describing the market as quiet and lacking liquidity. Despite this, underlying concerns around European storage and forward balances persist, providing some support to sentiment. Vessel availability is still sufficient, but increasingly fragmented, and there is a growing expectation that tonnage could tighten further toward end-May.

**East of Suez**

, buyers remain largely absent with an eye on softer prices down the curve and prompt supply well covered. Looking ahead, the key question remains whether late-May demand materialises and translates into fixing activity. For now, the market remains finely balanced, with limited downside but no clear catalyst for upside unless activity picks up.
