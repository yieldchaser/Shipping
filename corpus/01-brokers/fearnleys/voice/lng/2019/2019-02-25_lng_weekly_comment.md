## Market Commentary

We are almost done and dusted with February and that also means the official end of the Northern Hemisphere “winter months”. From a market perspective there is little change, as the cargo demand has stayed relatively flat for a while now. There are however signs of improved cargo activity, which has in turn rippled into new shipping requirements for March and April. Still, LNGC spot rates remain under pressure, since the availability list is rather long and includes sublets that in general can fix at competitive levels to compensate for their reduced flexibility or period availability. It’s IP week in London, and among other discussions, there are still a wide range of views on how the rest of the 2019 will pan out on commodity pricing. The only thing certain is that no one has a reliable crystal ball. On the newbuilding side, it was another active week with Maran Gas ordering two more 174,000cbm LNGC newbuilds from DSME for late 2021 delivery. More to come next week?
