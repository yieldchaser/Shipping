## Market Commentary

Summer months are upon us, traditionally a period where incremental demand for LNG depends bit more on how hot the weather is.

**Far East**

 DES prices remain low, at around $4 per mmbtu, hardly a conducive environment for spot trading. During last weeks, demand pattern and shorter trades led to increased availability of sublet vessels from various projects, importers and exporters alike. Hence, the recent firming charter rate in both basins came to a halt. This should all be temporary as the market is expected to firm again as we start working the more autumn dated cargoes leading up to winter. On the newbuilding side, Maran Gas Maritime hit the headlines yet again with another LNG vessel order at DSME for delivery within the first half of 2022. Samsung Heavy Industries also announced last Friday that they secured another two orders for LNG vessels, to be delivered in 2022.
