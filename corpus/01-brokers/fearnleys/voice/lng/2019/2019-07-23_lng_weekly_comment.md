## Market Commentary

It has been another active week on the shipping side, as we have witnessed a number of fresh requirements from both the East and the West of Suez. Various cargo tenders have been keeping Charterers and Owners busy especially in the Pacific, although these tenders usually yield only shorter one voyage spot fixtures. There are still a handful of open prompt vessels competing for the next business and thus rates have remained steady this week. Looking forward, we still see a few Charterers trying to find the perfect vessel to cover the upcoming winter period and thus multi-month rates have been holding strong but only a few deals have been concluded. On the cargo side, the aforementioned sell tenders have kept the supply high and thus LNG prices low, a tricky situation to many given the relatively firm shipping market. The uncertainty of the shipping outlook, together with the comparatively bearish cargo market, has created a lot of discussion around freight derivatives and the possibility to adopt them in wider perspective into LNG shipping – something that will be very interesting going forward. On the newbuilding front, SK Shipping has been confirmed placing an order with HHI for one 180,000cbm LNGC with planned delivery in 2021.
