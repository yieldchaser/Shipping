## Market Commentary

We are only in the second week of December but with the reduced activity it feels as though the holiday mood may have already hit the market. As overall availability has increased slightly, spot rates continue to soften from recent highs although so far both headline levels and voyage economics remain broadly positive. Therefore, the delicate market balance continues to hold, and could yet tilt further towards charterers or swing back in owners’ favour, especially if the build-up of vessels waiting to discharge at certain terminals leads to prolonged floating storage. There is more action on the long term side, with continuing newbuild tenders for charterers looking to secure slots prior to the anticipated ordering boom for Qatari and Mozambique volume, among others. Maran has contracted two new MEGI 174k CBM vessels at DSME for delivery in 2022.
