## Market Commentary

It’s been yet another monotonous start to the week for the LNG shipping market with limited requirements seen. The national holiday(s) in Asia earlier this week did contribute to a slow start, however trade deals may have also not materialize due to the current softening of LNG prices. There were a few sell tenders which could have developed into shipping requirements, but again they were mostly awarded to Traders/ Portfolio player with length in shipping. The continued limited cross basin arbitrage has also decreased ton/ miles and ensure a relatively decent short-term supply of shipping as vessels fixed on spot for a single voyage are mostly trading within the basin and would return on the availability list within the span of 2-3 weeks. Despite this, sentiment and rate expectation remain stable as we move into September. We do feel that traders/ portfolio players are slightly cautious with regards to subletting their tonnage during this period as the majority of market participants expect rates to pick up. As for multi month charters, we did hear favorable numbers on the last deal concluded and we still do encounter some strong multi month interest from a handful of charterers looking for coverage this winter. On the Newbuildings, Tsakos Energy Navigation have order up to two vessels (1+1) from Hyundai Heavy industries last week.
