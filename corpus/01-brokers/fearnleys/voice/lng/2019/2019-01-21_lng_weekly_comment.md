## Market Commentary

Another week of a relatively soft shipping market as the lack of intensity in trading activities has led to various project and trader-controlled vessels being available for sub-let. This is in addition to vessels being redelivered this and next month due to charterers not declaring the optional periods, where they had previously anticipated an active trading market in January. As a result the current sentiment is far from what most have predicted a couple of months ago. Every fixture heard in the market recently seems to have been done at terms lower than the previous fixture as headline rates dropped approximately 70% since the peak levels established in November. As reported last week, the correlation between the LNG shipping and cargo market has been restored but It may not take long for vessel availability to drop significantly as soon as there is an uptick of trading activities. As project and trader-controlled vessels contribute to almost half of the available tonnage list, expect these ships to be the first to go, to be utilized by their own charterers when LNG trading and demand increases.
