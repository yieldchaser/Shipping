## Market Commentary

IOC terminal commissioning was hotly contended this week and is welcome demand and marks the opening of the first LNG Terminal on the East coast of India. This new arrival occurs just as OLT announces that the LNG Toscana Regasification Terminal in Italy is working at almost 100% after less than one year from the introduction of the new allocation mechanism via auction, a very clear indication that trade routes has shifted significantly. Signs of Spring can be seen in Europe which is welcome relief from the dark days of winter but offers less rest bite for owners. The position list could be described as blossoming but that doesn’t give correct connotations for its effect on the market and charter rates which continue to soften. Its weak across the board – all basins and all duration take a hit except for 3 years and longer. Its clear that the prompt market is being underpinned but lackluster demand but the bigger picture remains relatively stable, more LNG due to be produced, the only question mark here is will it be in time for winter 2019/2020. Second-hand prices and NB prices remain stable, for now, but ongoing discussions between the Korean yards could see a significant change in the ownership structure of the 1st tier LNG yards, which may impact NB prices and in turn Charter rates.
