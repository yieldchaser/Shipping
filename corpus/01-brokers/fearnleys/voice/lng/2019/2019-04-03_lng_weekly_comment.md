## Market Commentary

LNG 19 in Shanghai is well underway and there is some exciting news being released in connection with the conference. In terms of shipping, it has been announced that TMS Cardiff secured a 10yrs contract with Vitol. In the product market Shell has signed a SPA with NextDecade for 2 MTPA from the Rio Grande project and Novatek has signed preliminary supply agreements with both Vitol and Repsol for volumes from their Arctic 2 project. With a lot of the market participants in Shanghai for LNG 19 the spot market is quiet, with only sporadic requirements surfacing. Spot rates are stable from last week, but the outlook continues to look positive.
