## Market Commentary

As August comes to a close with the LNG cargo market continuing to underwhelm, owners and charterers circle the dancefloor waiting for each other to make the next move. Portfolio players and traders with length in their fleets, either through trading shorter routes or with pre-secured winter coverage, are growing reluctant to sub-let but continue to be well-placed to pick up the cargoes that do come to market. The result is a general lack of demand for independent tonnage; however, the good news for owners is that vessel availability remains tight particularly in the west, and the east is not awash with vessels despite the usual seasonal build-up. With the imminent start-up of Freeport and the approaching winter demand, the enduring strong sentiment is readily understood. On the newbuilding front, there were two further orders last week: one fresh order placed by NYK at SHI and an option exercised by Maran Gas at DSME. This brings the YTD total for 2019 to 31 conventional LNGCs, versus 37 for the same period in 2018 and 62 for the full year.
