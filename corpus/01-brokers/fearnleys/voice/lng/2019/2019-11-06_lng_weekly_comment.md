## Market Commentary

LNG rate market phrenic seems to be slowly easing, as the effect of US/China sanctions eliminates, however there are still a couple of upcoming requirements that need coverage especially in the East. Charterers looking at securing a vessel today for late November or December loadings, still have to pay six-digit figures as prompt availability remains tight. Looking at the spot availability going forward it is expected that a few vessels that caught the Oct-Nov arbitrage will start redelivering. That might put pressure on rates. However, we might see some of those vessels looking at business West of Suez as West to East arbitrage is very slowly reviving. In the Atlantic, prompt availability has dried up, however a few opportunistic sublets appear here and there from portfolio players, as FOB cargo optimisations look like a preferred option for them creating some potential length, but with limited flexibility. Despite high tender activity, newbuilding prices remain stable for now....
