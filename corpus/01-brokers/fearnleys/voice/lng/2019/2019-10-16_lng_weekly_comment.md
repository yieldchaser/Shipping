## Market Commentary

Rates are firm, but stable from last week, except from some minor adjustments to reflect the most recent discussions. Prompt availability is tight, but at the same time, the high rates has lured some sub-letters to the surface. However, its proven difficult to match these positions with outstanding shipping requirements due to the sub-let vessels often restricted window of availability. Market is expected to remain tight over the coming weeks as multiple factors are impacting the balance in the shipping market. We’ve had four newbuilds added to the orderbook by BW and MISC for delivery in 2022. BW may be speculative, MISC is basis term contracts with Exxon.
