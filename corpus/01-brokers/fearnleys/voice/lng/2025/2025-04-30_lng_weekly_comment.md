## Market Commentary

Another active week in the spot market on LNG shipping where the crunch on tonnage for end May dates in the USG remains the topic of conversation since our last report. Charter rates have appreciated more quickly than has been seen at any point this year with 2-strokes in the Atlantic around the $40,000 pd mark on certain date ranges. Requirements continue to crop up on the back of cargo diversions away from Europe. Several lifters of U.S. volumes are strategically short whilst portfolios are also redelivering ships and replacing on a weak spot market, the resulting rush for tonnage highlights the potential for pockets of tightness to appear in the right trading environment, despite the overarching weaker fundamentals. The Pacific is yet to make up the widening gap in charter rates due to a lack of firm requirements of late, though with several ships sure to ballast back to the Atlantic we may see some rebalancing further forward. On the period side, further interest in multiyear charters is being worked in the market this week although the story remains that whilst there are plenty of willing sellers for 2026, offers for longer period are more limited and priced accordingly.
