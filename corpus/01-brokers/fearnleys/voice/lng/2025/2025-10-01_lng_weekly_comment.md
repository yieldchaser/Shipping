## Market Commentary

The steady flow of requirements in the Atlantic appears to have absorbed much of the modern tonnage willing or able to show flexibility off October dates, with the remaining time- or price-sensitive availabilities held back for now. The most recent fixing levels suggest a floor may have been reached and also interest in multimonth requirements imply some charterers are now ready to snap up tonnage for winter, but the year has taught us not to get ahead of ourselves… either way, an immediate rebound in rates is unlikely given the overall supply imbalance. Requirements further out are yet to garner much interest at the lower end of the rate range of recent weeks, but we shall see how the market shapes up as we approach the fixing window. The Pacific continues to lag somewhat with lower liquidity, as challenges for owners remaining cold seem more likely to curb oversupply as opposed to a resurgence of requirements. There is still a lot of interest in the medium- and long-term space, with some interesting pricing signs for existing and newbuild tonnage emerging for the remainder of the decade.
