## Market Commentary

Another bumper week for spot fixtures worldwide, with the focus shifting back towards the Atlantic. Apart from a few discounted deals on outstanding prompt positions, charter rates for intra-Atlantic spot voyages have held steady in the mid $30s. The crowded requirement list for end-August should imply a firming market, but depending how each individual requirement matches with some tight restrictions on vessel availabilities there are still some trades being discussed at lower rates. That said, the pricing of worldwide flexibility and even longer periods through October/November remains flat in the low-mid $40s and we are entering what is typically the seasonal uplift. The gap for modern 2-stroke coverage through Q4 and into Q1 still requires some bridging, although this may be challenging considering a growing consensus that spot will remain accessible and range-bound through the Winter months, thanks to ample vessel supply on paper. It's been another good week for the U.S. Energy sector and LNG producers, following Trump's deal with the EU, which has also seen the announcement of successful FID by Venture Global on their CP2 project. Whilst this creates additional demand for long-term shipping, current sentiment sees very limited new orders on the Newbuilding side as the market grapples with a large orderbook and stagnated demand growth in the immediate years.
