## Market Commentary

We have seen less liquidity in the Atlantic spot market this week with several requirements sitting in the window for longer than usual in 2025. Ongoing strikes in Europe and deferrals in Egypt continue to create uncertainty in discharge schedules and subsequent vessel availabilities, resulting in limited firm candidates showing for outstanding first decade November cargoes. Whilst charterers’ expectations have increased week on week leading to a couple of fixtures, the gap between bid and offer remains wide, with owners’ ideas firming amid a thin availability list. However, fundamental vessel oversupply and weak sentiment is casting a shadow over hopes of a winterish bump into November, as portfolios still see value in keeping their fleet utilised in the low $20s RT. Meanwhile, market participants are evaluating the potential implications of recent geopolitical developments and their possible impact on LNG shipping. No newbuilding orders have been reported this week, with prices remaining stable.
