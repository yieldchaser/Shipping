## Market Commentary

The ebb and flow of a soft summer market continues across all basins. In the Atlantic, after the record-breaking July followed by a much quieter August, the market is now beginning to pick up again although rates remain stubbornly range-bound. The Pacific basin had taken up a bit of the slack offered by the Atlantic but without coming close to matching the hectic pace of fixing in the West, while the Middle East has also been a little quiet of late following the surge of activity earlier in the summer that emphasised how important the region has become. For periods a little longer, the bid (and to an extent, the offer) suggests a fairly flat winter and one in which charterers will likely feel comfortable with a little more spot exposure… so we may be in for a busy winter. After a quiet few months at the shipyards, this week felt like a bonanza with six orders reported by Samsung on behalf of two owners, for delivery between November 2027 and September 2028. The specifications remain under wraps for now so direct comparisons to previous orders are a little unclear, but all the indications are that pricing resists downward pressure despite relative inactivity this year.
