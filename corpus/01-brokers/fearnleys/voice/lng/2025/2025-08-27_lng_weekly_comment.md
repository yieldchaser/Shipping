## Market Commentary

Activity levels in the Atlantic spot market have been somewhat lacklustre of late, though a slower August can certainly be forgiven in a year when the number of spot fixtures to date already totals close to 300. The resulting sentiment in the West has been a little mixed, with laycans moving out beyond marketed availabilities. Prompter enquiries for end September continue to emerge, though corresponding availabilities are largely untested with many requirements optimisation-led or linked to cargo purchases. All in all, this makes for a rather sideways trajectory for charter rates, as supported by the levels reported for an interbasin fixture for October implying minimal upside priced through the balance of Q4. Rates have begun to soften in the Pacific despite a small number of fixtures. Tonnage continues to build up on TFDE’s and whilst new volumes are expected online in the next couple of months, current conditions are challenging with additional length brought about by maintenance at one Australian export terminal and limited activity out of the Middle East. New volumes from Barossa expected online in the next couple of months and further LNG Canada ramp up are needed to bring more balance.
