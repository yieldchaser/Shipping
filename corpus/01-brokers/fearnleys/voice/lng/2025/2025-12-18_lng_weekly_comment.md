## Market Commentary

As we race closer to the end of the year, the rush to cover requirements for the first half of January is starting to slow down and there is a corresponding softening in rates. The flagging momentum may suggest the winter spike is over, but there is still some shape to the curve and considerable volatility, particularly for earlier laycans in the Atlantic for which rates remain very much in the six-figures. Further out into the second half of January and into February, fixing levels are lower but is this due to a general market easing or have charterers fixed well-ahead, before the upcoming holidays? Regardless, the feeling remains that we will see a gradual decline in rates rather than an abrupt fall. Longer periods remain in limbo as independent owners and subletters seek to capture the recent strength while charterers hold off for the excitement to fade, but it is likely that levels will settle a little higher for multi-month and multi-year charters next year than they were through much of this year.
