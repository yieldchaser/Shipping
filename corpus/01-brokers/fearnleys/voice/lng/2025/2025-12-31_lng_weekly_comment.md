## Market Commentary

As we put down A Christmas Carol for another year, we can pick up another Dickens novel to see in the New Year. 2025 initially appeared to be a year of lows for LNG shipping, until a late surge in Q4 served as a timely reminder of the market’s capacity for sharp swings. The uptick, driven primarily by additional LNG supply, suggests there may be more opportunities ahead, though the path forward will be far from smooth. A 50% orderbook-to-fleet ratio highlights the growing pains of rapid expansion, while uncertainty over a potential medium-term LNG glut adds further complexity. In this multidirectional, technologically tiered, and fast-evolving market, volatility remains the only constant. So whether a charterer enjoying low rates but stung by this winter spike or an owner suffering the soft market but locking in historically high numbers of fixtures, 2025 really did see the best of times and the worst of times, and we all look forward to 2026 with Great Expectations.
