## Market Commentary

Fewer requirements have been seen in the market over the past week with holidays across most of Asia a contributing factor. On that note, we take this opportunity to wish readers a Happy Lunar New Year and hope it will be full of happiness and prosperity for all. The Year of the Snake is said to signify rebirth, and the LNG shipping market is in great need of shedding some of its older skin this year. The removal of vintage vessels, which the market requires for rebalancing, will not occur at a rate to match the steady stream of newbuildings arriving in advance of delayed project start-ups. However, the charter rates observed on the most modern tonnage in today’s spot market are piling pressure on owners. Recycling is picking up and lay-up may be under consideration for some trifuels. If the Atlantic spot market continues to see 2-stroke tonnage fixing in the single digits and at implied negative earnings, this rebirth will surely be accelerated. The market will also look to call upon the creativity and adaptability associated with this year in the Lunar calendar, as optional structures remain the most likely method of bridging the current gap on rate ideas for periods ranging from 1 to 3 years, prior to the longer term tightness forecast at the end of the decade.
