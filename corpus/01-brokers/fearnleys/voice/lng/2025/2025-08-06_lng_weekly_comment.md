## Market Commentary

A continuation of last week’s themes as spot activity in the Atlantic remains high, with the fixing window stretching out to mid-September, although there has been a notable lack of movement on charter rates as underlying length amongst portfolios continues to absorb the bulk of requirements. On paper the market would appear tight with more enquires than there are marketed vessels, but with the fixing window stretched much further out it is likely that ships redelivering from charter or shaken out of schedules will emerge and competition should keep rates in check. The situation is somewhat reversed in the Pacific since there are more vessels available and few requirements to be worked, so the end result is the same. The term market continues to draw in resources with ongoing requirements for multi-year periods, and although pricing suggests a fairly flat winter this year the curve does trend upwards thereafter.
