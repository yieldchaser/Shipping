## Market Commentary

The bubbling momentum in the spot markets for loadings through the end of November and early December has continued through this week, despite some uncertainty following news of some lost cargoes out of the US Gulf; however, the resulting prompt length does not really come into play for later laycans in the Atlantic and so rates have continued to firm. With many of these requirements now covered, focus shifts a little further back and we enter into speculative territory: how many of the FOB sales will take in tonnage? How many of the optimisations will play out? How many fresh firm requirements will hit the market? In short, owners and charterers are assessing how long will the current freight imbalance last and when the market will start to soften. The firming market in the East is trailing that in the West, and the dynamic quite different. Named positions are few and far between but equally, open requirements remain scarce.
