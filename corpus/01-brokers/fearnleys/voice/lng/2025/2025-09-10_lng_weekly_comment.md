## Market Commentary

With the party makers and Gastech shakers currently plying their trade throughout Milan, the market can be forgiven for its somewhat lacklustre performance in the first half of this week. Of course, seasonally a lull is expected at this time of year, and one assumes that this annual gathering of LNG protagonists is timed accordingly to combat depressing rate levels with some industrywide bonhomie. Rejuvenated and reseated at their desks next week, the hope is that the powers that be will ensure that trading volumes recover in time to provide Owners with the Christmas present they so justly deserve. This might sound like wishful thinking from a shipbroker who is wading through the market doldrums, but with Corpus Christi, LNG Canada and Barossa all set to push additional volumes onto the water in Q4, all that’s needed is a prolonged cold snap to flip the current market sentiment on its head. It’s also worth noting that spot shipping is just the very visible tip of the market in which we all operate and as observant readers of Tradewinds will have noted, vessel recycling, LNG bunkering and long-term tendering activities are all continuing apace. In short, whilst those of us operating in the short-term chartering market might be agitating for increased levels of activity, when the LNG shipping market is considered as a whole, we’ve probably all never been busier. Enjoy the rest of your week!
