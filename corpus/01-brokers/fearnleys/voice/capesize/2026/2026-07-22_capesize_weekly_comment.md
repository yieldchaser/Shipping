## Market Commentary

This week on Cape, we saw quieter activity in the Pacific and Atlantic basins. All markets overall have fallen since last week levels with some signals of improvement.

**Pacific**

 C5 sees limited miner activity and growing competition from owners. West Australia stems are limited with owners seeing low USD 11s and others claiming to see low USD 12s for first half August. Latest fixture from West Australia/Qingdao was done at USD 11.1 for August 7-9 dates. C3 Brazil/China and West Africa stems also saw slow activity this week so far, and rising bunker prices may further affect freight rates going forward. Today's offers so far are in the low-mid USD 33s for mid August dates, and ideas in the high USD 32s for end August laycans. Yesterday we heard rumors of a West Africa stem fixed in the USD 31s. North Atlantic is also quiet and we see growing tonnage lists building. The North is mostly confined to a couple transatlantic stems, and a few fresh fronthaul stems. Last night we heard of a fixture for early August in mid USD 42 levels ex Sept-Iles/Qingdao for 6-15 August on a standard Cape.
