## Market Commentary

Slowdown for the big ships on key routes, consequent average daily earnings losing 20 pct w-o-w to come in at bit over USD 17k. World steel production lower than for same period last year - and dominating West Australia/China and Brazil/Feast trades both remain at far from full speed. A Europe in need for substantial long haul coal volumes to fill the void of Russian material only partly compensates, mildening a soft tendency by taking ship capacity out of the ordinary Pacific and front haul trades. Growing concern over Mediterranean tonnage build-up as Black Sea exports, normally absorbing some 10 units/month, have come to a complete stop - transatlantic market in deep need of increased mineral volumes to maintain levels. Forward expectations and thus period interest nevertheless remain positive and far above spot - representative fixtures include 180,000 dwt, built 2015, done for close to 2 years at a handsome USD 32k.
