## Market Commentary

Capesize rates are increasing in the East and in the West, with greater expectations as well as actual activity out of Brazil being the main driver. The West Australian iron ore rates are consequently following up day by day. C5 is presently at 14,50, up from USD 12 pmt one week ago. The overall index is up from 19,000 to 31,000 over the same period. This is happening after a long period of time where the Brazilian exports have been affected by heavy rains in the beginning of the year. There is still room for a further positive trend in rates.
