## Market Commentary

It’s been an extreme week as oil and bunkers been tossing voyage numbers left, right, and centre, hour to hour, minute to minute. The North Atlantic has been suffering as the Russian invasion of Ukraine has taken out loading areas either directly caused by war or by self-sanctions. Further linked to the war we see much more coal heading towards Europe from destinations which is atypical - replacing Russian coal. In other news, though voyage rates mostly are up due to bunkers, we see hire rates improving as well as we are heading towards a more active season out of Brazil.
