## Market Commentary

We see scarce volume for mid November C5, East Australia, South Africa, West Africa, and Indian business. On C3 ex Brazil to China, large majority of November stems seem to be covered. Miners and operators are out in market with late November and December stems, but we see high resistance for bids and taking of ships. On the bright side, we see several fresh stems and enquiries in the Atlantic. On a whole, market optimism has further diminished from last week.

**Far East**
