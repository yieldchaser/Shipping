## Market Commentary

Big gains, admittedly from very poor levels, for the big ships. Volumes of major commodities iron ore, coal, and bauxite significantly up on most relevant routes, jacking average daily earnings for standard units up 67 pct w-o-w to come in at a still modest USD 13k. Period interest very high but fixing activity still limited as both sides await strike prices to match justifiable levels and forward expectations/FFA values.
