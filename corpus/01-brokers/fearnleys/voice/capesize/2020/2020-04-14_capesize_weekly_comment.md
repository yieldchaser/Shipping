## Market Commentary

The market in the South Atlantic has improved over the last week, with the iron ore route from Brazil to China being up about 15% on voyage basis, and 40% on time charter basis. This is due to decreased congestion at load-ports and in general more activity indicating that export is picking up after the wet season. The Pacific has experienced some positiveness, however over the last 2 days it has come down to where it was the previous week, with C5 - the West Australia-China route - fixing at USD 4.40-4.45 levels. At the time of writing this report the market for Brazil is improving, but participants are far from convinced this is the beginning of a spring rally and the Pacific is a bit of a question mark.
