## Market Commentary

Last week the market sentiment turned positive with expectations of having reached a bottom. Forward contracts traded up and spot fixtures was concluded above last done, though only marginally. However, this week started negative as every other market. The weighted time charter average is down 11% from last report, with the Atlantic round voyage suffering the most with a decrease of 22%. There are very limited amount of good news around, but the period market has at least remained somewhat active.
