## Market Commentary

Year-high and positive Q4 expectations for the big ships despite a moderate correction taking place as we speak. General Atlantic and fronthaul iron ore volumes being the main drivers, with Brazilian miners facing a possible lack of early loaders.

**Far East**

 activity is moderate in comparison, with West Australian iron ore trade making up almost all demand. Average daily earnings up more than 30 pct w-o-w to come in at USD 33,500. Market however still in need of positives to gain self-confidence, as value for long- vs short rounds have gone from premium to discount. The glaring discount also for forward values gives little room for long period fixing, with most conclusions limited to short duration - typically talk of index type tonnage done for 5-7 months at USD 20,500 and around 12 months struggling to exceed USD 16k.
