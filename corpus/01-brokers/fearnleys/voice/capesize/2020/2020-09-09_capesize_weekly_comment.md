## Market Commentary

The market has come off over the last week with the basket of all routes being marked down by 12% to USD 15,482. This is mostly due to slower activity from miners and tonnage being freed up after bad weather in the Far East. On a positive note we see that the coal segment starting to find the current pricing interesting and the economy of scale is making sense compared to Panamaxes.
