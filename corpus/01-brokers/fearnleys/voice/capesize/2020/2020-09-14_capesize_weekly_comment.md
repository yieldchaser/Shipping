## Market Commentary

There is a good supply of cargoes in the Pacific, in particular C5 has been active although freight level is more or less unchanged from last weeks' USD 6.85 pmt to present low 7s pmt. India has been buying more coal, resulting in more active Indonesia/India and Richards Bay/India trades, but also here rates are not moving. Period activity is low, explained by the relatively flat and uneventful FFA market, but expected to pick up soon.
