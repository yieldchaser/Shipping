## Market Commentary

Market activity in general has calmed down after we reached close to USD 40,000 per day for the 5TC basket. With less activity, levels have softened down 6% week to week with both the major iron ore routes West Australia to China and Brazil to China. However, all the majors in Pacific BHP, FMG and Rio Tinto are still booking tonnage. Period rates have also seen a correction more or less in line with spot levels, down 6% from last week.
