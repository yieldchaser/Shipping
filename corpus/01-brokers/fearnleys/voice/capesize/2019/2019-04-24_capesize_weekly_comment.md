## Market Commentary

After c5 bottomed at USD 5.35 last week, the market has improved to 6.15 this week on the back of fresh enquiries out of Australia. There also been more period activity with modern 180,000 units being paid 16,300 and 17,000 for one year. In the near term we expect the market to improve a bit further, but the big gains have to wait until we see stronger activity out of Brazil. In that respect, things are of course hinging on how quickly Vale can bring capacity back online.
