## Market Commentary

The average index value has dropped USD 2,000 since last week This is not a dramatic drop, but rather a result of relatively lower demand. C5 has moved from USD 10,7 pmt to 10, 4 pmt whilst c7 is unchanged. This trend is a result of many owners will keep their ships in the pacific in the months to come for scrubber installation prior to IMO 2020. Period rates have not been too much affected though, and value for one year is still excess 20,000.
