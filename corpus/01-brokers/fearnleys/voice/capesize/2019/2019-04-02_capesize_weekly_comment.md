## Market Commentary

Still short-term doom and gloom for the big ships, as market is only slowly absorbing an abundance of prompt units left without employment following some 8 mio tons of West Australia iron ore exports falling out due to cyclone. Apart from certain bright spots on fronthaul, spot is completely on its knees - tc-equivalents of the WAust/China milk route are negative and Brazilian rounds are not much better. Period interest nevertheless remain healthy for quality tonnage, with levels presently bid by major miners at some USD 14,500 for about 1.5 years on modern 180,000-tonners being a positive contrast to average daily spot earnings of less than USD 4000.
