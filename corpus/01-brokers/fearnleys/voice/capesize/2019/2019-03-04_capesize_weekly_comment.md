## Market Commentary

The smaller ships are continuing to improve, so we reiterate our view from last week that the bottom is in and that an upturn should materialize very shortly. The Capesize to Panamax market ratio is now at 58%, which is the lowest level since early 2016. Every time previously this ratio was at such low levels a market rebound followed.
