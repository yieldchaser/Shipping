## Market Commentary

With the Chinese New Year celebrations taking place this week, the dry bulk market is as expected extremely quiet. Freight rates are presumably at the lowest for the year over all, and number of spot ships is increasing every day. On top of this, the dry bulk community is watching the developments in Brazil carefully, and what the actual consequences will be in terms of reduced output. Time being it is estimated that 40 million tons of iron ore pellets will be impacted, but this is an estimate only and the length of the period is unknown.
