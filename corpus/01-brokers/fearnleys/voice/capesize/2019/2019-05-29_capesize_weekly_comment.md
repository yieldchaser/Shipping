## Market Commentary

Despite a rather inactive Brazilian market, rates have been up this week mainly led by the Australian demand; the West Australia to China route is up, approaching USD 8 pmt level, and average TC has just reached excess USD 13,000 level. With Vale still not in the market, it is however difficult to assess whether this trend will continue after the holidays, but sentiment is healthy, and last week’s Brazil fixtures have given some positive surprises.
