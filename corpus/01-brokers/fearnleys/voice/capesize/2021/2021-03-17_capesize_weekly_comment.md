## Market Commentary

Balanced with positive undertone. Steady flow of iron ore West Australia/Far East, with already healthy demand from major miners supplemented by relets from numerous operators. Substantial and growing coal volumes heading for India, with China now expected to also step up imports - Capes eating into rocketing Panamax territory for both. Increased fronthaul activity Brazil-Wafr-USEC-Colombia/Far East coupled with falling number of ballasters. Spot earnings for the big ships flat w-o-w, stable at around USD 18k/day, but market in clear contango thus period deals discussed+done substantially higher. Representative fixtures include vintage 180,000 dwt fetching USD 20,500 for about 12 months, modern Newcastlemaxes closer to USD 25k.
