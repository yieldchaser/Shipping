## Market Commentary

Still correcting, arguably at high levels. Average daily earnings for the big ships down another 6 pct w-o-w, coming in at just over USD 29k. Transatlantic doldrums and fronthaul imbalance for June being mostly to blame - ballasters presently having a hard time achieving last done or close on the Brazil/China route as demand side pretty much limited to one major miner. Present gap between 100 days China-Brazil-China and 50 days Pacific round is roughly USD 10k in favour of the latter, meaning no flow of "new" ballasters as long as Pacific iron ore and coal trades keep absorbing prompt tonnage. Main players waiting for market to find a floor and again improve, as major commodities much in demand and overall fleet efficiency moderate.
