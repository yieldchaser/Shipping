## Market Commentary

Rates for the big ships not quite living up to expectations, although at very healthy levels and fair volumes. Average daily earnings pretty much unchanged w-o-w, standing at usd 29k for standard 180000 dwt units. Iron ore remains main driver. Volume shift from Australia to Brazil, as the china-brazil-china round voyage route is improving to almost close the gap and par with pacific rounds at around usd 26k/day – providing hope and promise for the weeks to come. General outlook bright, both due waust/china volumes expected to pick up after a slow period, but even more so as major miners very busy picking ships for the tonne/mile-crucial Brazil/Far East trade. Commodity prices indicate further strong demand. Period activity moderate, with recent fixtures limited to modern standard units concluded on index-basis.
