## Market Commentary

Capes saw further improvement in the Pacific with c5 route up 13% from last week. This was also resulting in less ships ballasting towards Brazil, and from Friday there were signs indicating freight rates would move up. Present c3, Tubarao to China route, is at USD 20 pmt, which is up 21% from last week. Over the past few years a c3 market excess USD 20 has been considered strong, but perhaps this will be the new normal for this year?
