## Market Commentary

Last week’s market took a breather and some market participants suggested this was it before we head downwards towards Q1. However, the fundamentals still prevail, and the fact is that there is a very limited amount of available tonnage in all basins and the demand still very healthy. The biggest increase we see is in the Atlantic with the round voyage fixing 23% higher than last week. But all other segments also up and the average of all routs is up by 16%. Though we are approaching holiday season and can sense owners’ willingness to cover it still seems too tight for this market to have seen its peak just yet, thus we expect a further increase in levels for the week to come.
