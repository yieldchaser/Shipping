## Market Commentary

After last week push market calmed down and started to slide. Big part of the explanation is that there is currently some planned maintenance work at West Australia limiting exports and a “sudden” release of vessels from discharge ports in China and India. However, the last couple of days the North Atlantic has been much more active, and rates improved significantly. This has infected the other segments sentiment as well, thus levels seem to increase over the coming days.
