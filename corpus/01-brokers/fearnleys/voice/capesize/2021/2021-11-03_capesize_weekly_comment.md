## Market Commentary

Market not yet fully done adjusting from October's somewhat overheated sentiment for the big ships, although last 3 weeks have looked much like a landslide. With sentiment nervous and paper players leading the way, average daily earnings shredded by another USD 13k w-o-w to come in at USD 27k. Pain is particularly felt on the Brazil/China fronthaul run, where adjusted iron ore demand prospects and lack of spot activity have knocked the 100-days China-Brazil-China round voyage value down by another 27 pct to USD 22k. Inter-Pacific spot demand is healthy and stable in comparison, but Pacific traders have nevertheless seen it coming off close to 30 pct over last 5 trading days to stand at USD 23k. Considerable period interest, but little or nothing presently being concluded as eroding spot and paper values translates into justified target levels constantly sliding.
