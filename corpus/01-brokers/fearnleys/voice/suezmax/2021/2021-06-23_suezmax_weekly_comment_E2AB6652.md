## Market Commentary

An air of moderate optimism pervades the market this week. Neighboring vessel segments, namely Aframax and VLCCs, could potentially provide some further support. Let's be clear, the fundamentals are sketchy at best, but there has been a distinct change in sentiment and with the added impetus of a firming Mediterranean and North Sea Aframax market, trading at well over crossover levels, there are indeed positives for owners to latch onto. This will likely hold vessels on the Continent and away from West Africa, somewhat limiting the pool of ships available to charterers. So, on the back of this Td20 could touch ws55 or possibly more this week, whilst in the East Td23 trades low ws30's with potential upside.

**MEG**

/East will challenge high ws50's/low ws60's, but will need further cargoes to support those levels.
