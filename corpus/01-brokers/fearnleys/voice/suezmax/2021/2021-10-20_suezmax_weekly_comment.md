## Market Commentary

All load zones are set for further rates rises this week due to a combination of circumstances that will catch some participants off guard. We are seeing Black Sea and Td20 laycans converging, which is nothing out of the ordinary during the Northern Summer months, but at this time of year, it has traditionally been a bellwether of volatility, especially when combined with delays in the straits. This forces charterers to pick from the same pool of FOC ships which can easily stress the list. The East is also offering up similar distractions for owners, as a firmer outlook may well hold Eastern tonnage away from West Africa, to some degree at least. With VLCCs and Aframaxes also looking firmer, this market is exhibiting all the signs of further volatility and next done is likely to be higher than last done.
