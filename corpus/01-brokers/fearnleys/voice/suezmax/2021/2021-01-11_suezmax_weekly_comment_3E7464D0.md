## Market Commentary

After nearly a year of negative news stories, we are delighted to bring you a positive spin, well, sort of positive. Despite the best efforts of the chartering community to apply downward pressure, rates, for the most part, appear to be trading flat. Given present returns and rising bunker prices, it's no surprise that owners have tried their utmost to resist further falls. However, in a market where owners and charterers are happy to repeat last done, there is always the danger that complacency can set in and if these derisory enquiry levels persist, it is not inconceivable that rates could be traded down a few more points.
