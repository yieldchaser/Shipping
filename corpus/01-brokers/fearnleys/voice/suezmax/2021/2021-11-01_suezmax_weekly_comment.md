## Market Commentary

As we cautioned last week, there were likely to be regrets from owners who threw in the towel too early in West Africa, and this proved to be correct. Despite a relatively quiet week in most load zones, Suezmax owners managed to reverse losses in West Africa with Td20 climbing back up WS 77.5. This unfortunately comes with an obvious caveat; rates might be tested marginally downwards unless we see a marked increase in global cargo volumes, and it needs to happen this side of the weekend. With Black Sea delays hovering around 8 days north bound, charterers are now fixing Td6 deep into the third decade with owners eyeing this as a demurrage trade rather than an opportunity to push rates up.

**MEG**

/East is likely to trade mid WS 70's on modern whilst Td23 has a long list of ships to choose from and may threaten mid WS 40's.
