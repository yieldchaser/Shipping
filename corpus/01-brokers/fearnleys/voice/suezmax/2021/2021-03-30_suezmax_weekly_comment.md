## Market Commentary

Whilst the Suez Canal drama has come to end, it’s worth pointing out that the origins of last weeks firmer west market have been lost in the Suez Canal narrative. This event simply added to what was already a more confident market. Hence why some of the steep falls we’ve seen in West Africa over the last twenty four hours, appear very hasty, ignoring an active cargo menu in neighbouring regions. Fundamentally, the Td20 list is still competing with a busy Black Sea and Libya program, whilst Aframax’s, albeit softer, remain in Suezmax owners crosshairs. For now, Wafr/Ukcm will trade circa W70 whilst a lack or enquiry in the Middle East Gulf will apply some downward pressure with an East run likely to fall back into the w60’s on modern tonnage. Td23 will look to mop up some of the slack on the list at mid/high W20’s
