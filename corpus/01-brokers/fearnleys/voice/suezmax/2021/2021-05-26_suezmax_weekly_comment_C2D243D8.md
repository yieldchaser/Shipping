## Market Commentary

Signs of life emerging in the East as the list for modern, 2x20T cranes vessels have tightened significantly for the first decade with owners pushing for more than last done levels. Hitherto, charterers have been able to fix off-market with zero noise, but now, reduced tonnage availability is likely to smoke a few more cargoes out into the open market, as the perceived bottom has been reached.

**MEG**

/East trades WS55 on modern with possible upside and Td23 looks susceptible to adding a little more weight. In the Atlantic, charterers still have the luxury of attaining in and around last done without too much trading. With V's stalling and possibly coming off a tad, support for Td20 is lacking and it's going to need a further reduction of Eastern ballasters to lift rates, should trade WS47.5 with potential 1-2 points upside.
