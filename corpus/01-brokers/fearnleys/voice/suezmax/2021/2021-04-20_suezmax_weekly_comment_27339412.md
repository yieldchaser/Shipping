## Market Commentary

Wafr/TA stems have, until recently, traded at a discount to Td20, but that differential appears to have shutdown today as Nidas is reported to have booked 130KT at WS57.5 for a USAC run. We evidently have a firmer Caribbean/TA Aframax market where rates are presently trading at crossover levels which will now hold American seaboard tonnage away from West Africa. North West Europe is also showing signs of life which will further distract tonnage on the Continent. In the East, there's a flatter feel with a long list that will see rates trade in and around last done levels. Td23 has traded a few times at WS22.5, which now appears to be the conference rate for this week. Basrah/East trades at WS62.5 flat with owners and charterers happy to repeat.
