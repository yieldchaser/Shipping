## Market Commentary

The writing appears to be on the wall for Suezmax's this week, especially in West Africa, where Vlcc's have swooped on a number of stems that were initially earmarked for their little sister. This release valve hasn't immediately softened rates, however there is some negative pressure in the offing as the East list is long , especially prompt tonnage, of which a large number have missed the natural MEG window and have now committed to West Africa. Whilst moderate VLCC sentiment and high bunkers will prevent rates from falling off a cliff, Td20 is unlikely to trade above W60 as we eat into the third decade of February. Likewise, this will leave Meg/East with a similarly weakened outlook.
