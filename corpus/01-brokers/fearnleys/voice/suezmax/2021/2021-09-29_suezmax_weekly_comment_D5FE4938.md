## Market Commentary

Whilst there's a general consensus that the market will continue to improve, the pace of improvement remains pedestrian and dull. The East Suezmax market is unfortunately more VLCC-sensitive than the West, and at the time of going to print, we are way off from seeing any meaningful interplay between the two segments . In the West, the main potential drivers are the highly nuanced regional Aframax markets that have recently been well into crossover territory, especially in the Mediterranean. This potentially holds Suezmax tonnage away from West Africa which could create more bottlenecks.
