## Market Commentary

Over the last week there have been some gaping opportunities for owners to push on and capitalise, but instead, they dropped the ball. They may get another bite at the cake this week as the Mediterranean Aframax market has seen a number of parcels find a home on Suezmax. With the Black Sea barely touched for April dates and the list of CPC suitable ships getting tighter, Aframax/Suezmax crossover, may yet yield some upside. The majority of Suezmax are weighted in the Western Mediterranean/UKC and ballasting in for Min flat Augusta may not be appealing at present levels. Td20 may be a beneficiary, as this could hold vessels away from West Africa whilst Eastern ballasters, with half an eye on proceedings, may sniff out a point or two more. Td23 trades WS20-22 and MEG/East on modern is steady at WS60-62.5.
