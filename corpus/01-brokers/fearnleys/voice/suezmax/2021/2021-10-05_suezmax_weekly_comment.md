## Market Commentary

The Suezmax market looks to have found a firmer footing with most load zones experiencing solid chunks of enquiry for the first time in well over a year.

**MEG**

 enquiry, and most notably Basrah, looks to have gone into overdrive with Bot/West firming significantly. Last done Td23 jumped five points to WS 35 with owners so bullish it would be unwise to bet against rates pushing on again. The key factor that has helped balance out supply and demand has been the Atlantic where significant gains have been made in the Caribbean and West Africa with Td20 now comfortably in the low WS 60's and likely to add further weight. It now remains to be seen if NWE and the Mediterranean will have a further injection of Aframax/Suezmax interplay which could fuel further volatility. Outlook is firm in all regions.
