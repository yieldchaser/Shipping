## Market Commentary

The beginning of the end? Is the so called 'summer market' soon to be behind us? It's showing signs is the answer. Guyana cannot take VLCCs from September until April. This is going to add 10 or so more cargoes a month and it's worth adding that the VLCC list in general in the West is not long.

**USG**

 rates have already started to slowly rise and that will only assist. In the Mediterranean and West Africa there is still early tonnage but you can sense a bit of optimism from owners.

**MEG**

 has been busier. For Basrah liftings the list is shorter so watch this space. The Suezmax market is showing signs of improvement.
