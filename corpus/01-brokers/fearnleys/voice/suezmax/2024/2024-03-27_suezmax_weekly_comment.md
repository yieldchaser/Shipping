## Market Commentary

A short week which will be mostly remembered for... Sorry, let me start again. This week will likely not be remembered in Suezmax history. On the large part rates have stayed where they were on Monday. Most the fixing has been off market.

**Med**

 suezmaxes are being propped up by Aframax cargoes but even those rates are under pressure. W.Africa has threatened to notch up since 130 x w110 has been done for TD20 but hasn't yet. Really it should do but there's enough early tonnage which hasn't managed to find an employer. Guyana/Ukcm fixed at 145 x w92.5 - this was more than expected with the reason being that any USG-USAC or ECC position is likely to favour Usg/Ukcm at 145 x w75. This leaves the Guyana cargoes to look at Cont/W.Med ballasters only. The MEG has seen a bit of activity today after nothing all week. But really it's been flat before the weekend and W.Africa/MEG will probably tick up a touch but it's been largely uneventful. The good news is, Suezmaxes have flatlined since mid-January and these are still good earnings for Owners.
