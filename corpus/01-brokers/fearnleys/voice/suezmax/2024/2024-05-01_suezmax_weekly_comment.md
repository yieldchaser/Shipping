## Market Commentary

Last week, there were smoke signals emanating from the USG Aframax market, suggesting growing support for the wider Suezmax Atlantic market, but ultimately, availability of afra tonnage has thus far quashed this momentum and capped Suezmax (for now). In West Africa, we should be about to close the books on the second decade, save for the odd straggler cargo but it's the third decade that could throw up a little volatility. Reports on the trading side suggests that end month Wafr stems are not the easiest to sell, which could make it a healthy Suezmax decade. When you also factor in the limited availability of Vlcc tonnage in the Atlantic for those dates, there is room for optimism, In the East, whilst the list is fairly tight, there has been a lack of surface-fixtures which will maintain a flat feeling in the region for the balance of the week.
