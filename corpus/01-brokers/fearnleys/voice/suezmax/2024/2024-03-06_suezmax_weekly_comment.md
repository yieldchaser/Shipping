## Market Commentary

Slow start to the week, but the main area of interest has been the US Gulf. After seeing some rate levels down into the mid 80’s it’s now back up to around the WS 92.5-95 level for transatlantic business, also the lightering sector has been very active.

**West Africa**

 has been very slow so far this week with only one replacement and one cargo working for a Massongo/Rotterdam voyage. We suspect that rates should be close to WS 100-102.5 levels for a standard TD20 run. Mediterranean/Black Sea region has seen a few ships disappear, both from the Black Sea and the Mediterranean, but once again the tonnage list within the area remains long and with little enquiry due to oil company relets being in position which hasn’t helped. Long volumes from Libya remain low. Middle East Gulf has been ticking over with Indian business and a few charterers covering for western destinations. The latest rate levels have been concluded at below last done; WS 67.5 via Cape and WS 97.5 via Suez from Basrah to Augusta. Eastern runs should command WS 120 levels.
