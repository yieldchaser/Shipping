## Market Commentary

Q4 has a distinctly Q3 feel to it which is rather unnerving considering the optimism that pervaded this segment less than a month ago. Where do we go from here? The bane of the market remains that much vilified class of vessels, well, vilified by some in the owning community who ironically TC their vessels out, that being the humble relet. With an enormous chunk of the Suezmax fleet now in the hands of oil companies, there is a natural cap on freight whilst charterers are content to profit from the delta between chartering in and out, which caps the downside to some extent (a silver lining of sorts). For now, TD20 will do well to stay in the WS 90's whilst MEG/East will trade WS 112.5-115 with a softer underbelly.
