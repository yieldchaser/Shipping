## Market Commentary

The arb for West/East moves is more or less shut which will see the majority of Atlantic basin barrels stay local. Focusing on West Africa, there remains a glut of unsold barrels which suggests there will be some prompt requirements that will help support rates with TD 20 currently trading last done at WS 112.5. Across the pond, the softening oil price will not bring about an SPR release anytime soon, therefore any uptick will have to come from other sources. In the East, despite a flurry of enquiry, tonnage is beginning to build and on that basis we are calling MEG/East WS 115-120 and BOT/UKCM WS 60-65 via Cape.
