## Market Commentary

Some participants have been slow to react to fast changing developments in the Suezmax market and were caught up in a "last done" mind set which has proved costly on some deals as the market sped passed them. With the risk of stating the obvious, we have seen non-Urals freights increasing by over a third on TD20, whilst cross-Med rates have nearly tripled. Kozmino Aframax cargoes continue to fix with little to no issues on the selling side, suggesting urals West of Suez will soon find ways to market. Cheap crude always moves, no matter what the perceived risks are. At the time of going to print, we may see some profit taking from some early ships, but next done is more likely to be in excess of last.

/East will trade somewhere in low WS 100's whilst TD20 will share the same price point.
