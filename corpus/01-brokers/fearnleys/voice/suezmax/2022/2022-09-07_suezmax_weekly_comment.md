## Market Commentary

It’s been a dreadfully quiet market across all load zones. If MEG/China was tested on modern tonnage today we would expect it to trade maximum WS 120’s, purely down to dire enquiry levels, whilst Td23 should settle at about WS 60. TD20 is on life support and if we don’t see 3-4 cargoes enter the market today it will fall through the WS 120 mark. Td6 will steadily trade down into the low WS 180’s and will stay there for the remainder of the week although by our count CPC will be done for the month by the end of this week at the latest (bar the odd straggler). Cpc/Korea should trade about USD 5-5.25m.

**USG**
