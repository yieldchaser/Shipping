## Market Commentary

A sense of modest optimism pervades the Atlantic Suezmax market this week, but we have to emphasize the word "modest" as the tonnage supply looks fairly adequate, especially in West Africa, suggesting this recent upturn is based on sentiment, not fundamentals. Reports of a couple of TD20 cargoes struggling to repeat last done levels has brought the prospect of VLCC co-loads into view, which may cap proceedings at WS 95 level. A further limiting factor remains the East Suezmax market which has been the least exciting region of late. There is a lot of expectation that we will see increased MEG/West requirements in the medium term, but until that translates into real world fixtures TD23 is going to trade high WS 40's/low WS 50's.
