## Market Commentary

Last week, TD20 had the look and feel of a market that could trade well past WS 100, but by the time Monday rolled around there was a hint of a stall. A number of Wafr/long Mediterranean voyages traded up in dollar per day terms (over a TD20 run), but the market tends not to focus on numbers/maths and it was the lower WS rates that grabbed the headlines, ultimately forging market direction. The US Gulf remains the prime driver behind Atlantic rates and as long as cargoes and ships continue to trade in a concentric Atlantic-manner, we will continue to see further volatility. The East remains poor hunting ground for this segment, save for TD23, which provides a free ticket back to the Med perfectly positioning those owners who trade the Russian market.

**MEG**

/East needs testing but should trade circa WS 100.
