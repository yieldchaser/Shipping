## Market Commentary

In the Atlantic, Aframaxes continue to lend physical/psychological support to Suezmaxes whilst VLCCs could, conversely, cap proceedings. For now, the bulls are winning as it's moving in the right direction with TD20 poised to make further gains. Another support mechanism for the Atlantic comes in the form of charterers choosing to pothole the market with multiple laycans being spread out over a wide date-range. This allows momentum to build and imbues owners with confidence to ask for more. In the East, MEG/West trade flows continue to increase which has naturally benefited freight rates, particularly as both 15 and 20 tonne crane vessels are in demand which helps thin the list for eastbound requirements. TD23 is likely to hit WS 70's whilst a China run won't give you much change from WS 102.5-107.5 on a modern ship.
