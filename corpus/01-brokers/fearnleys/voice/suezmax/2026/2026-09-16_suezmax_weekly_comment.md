## Market Commentary

Split Stems ex BRZ & West Africa along with an influx of USG/EAST serves to populate the Atlantic basin heavily with enquiry. Rates, whilst astronomical, did not price in the huge demand upside this market had & still has amidst an dearth of VLCCs. VLCC availability for Brazil upto end October could be as low as 4-5 ships incl projections and with USG/West Africa markets drawing down on the same pool that leaves collossal volumes for Suezmaxes. 36MBBLS ex West Africa alone are outstanding upto 20th October, vs 38 logical ships either FOC, ex local positions or ETS within 7 days. Again these must be portioned across both sides of the Atlantic. CPC has failed to keep pace. In fact the recent fixtures at 500 level are more an indication that some owners, in spite of market momentum, are prioritising minimising waiting time to optimise TCEs. This is characteristic of the market in general and, along with the tonnage taken on Short TC cheaper than market TCEs, has stopped the market in general from roofing for now. Rather than fixing forward as usual, CPC, partially due to a delay in stem releases is working prompter than the Atlantic basin, but once it shifts to 8-10 onwards the expectation is that TD6 ticks up. Meanwhile the MEG, bolstered by VLCC rates, has climbed to 130x690 levels FUJA/EAST - there are availabilities east of suez, but a good amount of these won't go near the region, instead they are set to head to COGH. Given the severely limited availability on VLCC solid demand is expected here also.
