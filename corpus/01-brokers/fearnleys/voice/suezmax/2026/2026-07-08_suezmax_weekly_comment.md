## Market Commentary

Outstanding West Africa volume for the third decade is likely nearing 10 mbbls, with 4–5 Guyana cargoes still to cover and likely a similar number from CPC. Further Brazilian volumes may also emerge, with VLCC availability remaining short in the Atlantic for July laycans. The front end of the Suezmax Atlantic list is fairly balanced; however, renewed Middle East tensions could see vessels waiting on MEG business ballast towards the Cape, offering charterers more end-July/early-August options. Conversely, if strikes persist, Asian buyers may turn to the Atlantic again, renewing forward buying and helping absorb any excess tonnage.
