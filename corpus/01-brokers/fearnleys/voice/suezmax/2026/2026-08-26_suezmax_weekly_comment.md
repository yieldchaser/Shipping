## Market Commentary

There has been reasonable activity to start the week. However, the market remains very much oversupplied, with 17 FOC and 7 ETS 5 day window ETA West Africa by 10 September. On the flip side, there are plenty of cargoes to move in the second decade from both Guyana and West Africa. For the time being, however, charterers do not appear to be under any pressure to work much beyond two weeks forward from these regions. In addition, the Aframax market stateside appears to be heavily oversupplied, with Aframaxes pricing Suezmaxes out of USG/TA moves on a USD/bbl basis, which further incentivises charterers to sit on their hands.
