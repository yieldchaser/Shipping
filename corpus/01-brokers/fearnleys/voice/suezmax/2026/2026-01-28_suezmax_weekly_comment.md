## Market Commentary

Surface activity in West Africa remains limited, however, the USG continues to pay well for UKCM openers. With Aframaxes also in good shape stateside, the wider strength in the Americas has helped underpin Suezmax ideas. This should keep TD20 sentiment supported and continue drawing potential West Africa contenders away. Supply is now short enough that an influx of 2nd‑decade cargoes in West Africa could give owners the impetus to push, and while cargo counts suggest more should still be left to move, it remains to be seen whether it materialises this side of the weekend. The MEG has also seen supply trimmed through recent enquiries and vessels heading to the Cape of Good Hope. However, the relet‑heavy VLCC list remains a concern and, with their rates taking a hit, sentiment on adjacent sizes may be affected in the medium term.
