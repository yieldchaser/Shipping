## Market Commentary

Stronger rates reported from both the USG and West Africa yesterday/this morning. We still have healthy volumes to cover in West Africa for 1st decade Feb, in addition multiple February cargoes expected from Guyana, Brazil and the USG. Supply stateside is incredibly short in addition to thin tonnage in Europe with more poor weather inbound - the tug of war for tonnage will continue and heat up further. Expect owners in the East to keep options open for both MEG and West Africa fixing, and off the back of VLCC sentiment above us, owners will have wind in their sails. Expect Venezuelan, Iranian & Black Sea volatility to bolster sentiment further.
