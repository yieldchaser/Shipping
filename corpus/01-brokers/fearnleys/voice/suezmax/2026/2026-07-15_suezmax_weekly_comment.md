## Market Commentary

The mystery list is piling up again, much like last week. This implies that the majority of rates have been in and around last-done levels. Despite the eased supply at the week's outset, healthy demand has counteracted this and kept the market in equilibrium. The list has now tightened slightly but unless charterers have very prompt dates in hand, owners on the front end are expected to remain pragmatic, as there are a reasonable number of vessels currently on berth in the West, as well as tonnage arriving from the East for the forward window. In isolation, Suezmaxes appear steady. However, there are reports that East-bound enquiries have reignited in the Atlantic in response to heightened tensions in the Middle East, while the Aframaxes are firming in the West - this is bringing Suezmaxes closer to being competitive, albeit not quite there yet.
