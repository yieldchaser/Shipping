## Market Commentary

The healthy gains experienced by the Atlantic last week have now stalled due, in part, to the lull in market activity created by a UK Bank Holiday (Monday). This has created a false impression of underlying softness, which could be very misleading for those not paying close attention. The West African list has been trimmed by a steady procession of west Med'/Cont' ships that continue to ballast transatlantic. A number of these vessels are privately on subjects or fully fixed which further distorts the picture. Therefore, we are calling the Atlantic close to the bottom with little downside. In the East, it's active but soft for now. A couple of Kuwait fuel oil cargoes will help to keep MEG/East in and around WS 90 levels, whilst TD23 is likely to trade down to low/mid WS 50'S.
