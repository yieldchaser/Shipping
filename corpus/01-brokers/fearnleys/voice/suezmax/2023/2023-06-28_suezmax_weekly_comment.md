## Market Commentary

On the surface, it feels like a typical summer slumber, but this market is anything but typical. How many northern summers can you recall TD20 trading in the low WS 100's or TD 6 pricing at similar numbers. But we live in a world of comparisons, and yes, this week, the market has corrected downwards in most western load zones with the exception of Nigeria (FIRS tax issue still impacting rates). For now, at least, the East market appears semi-impervious to exogenous factors in the Atlantic basin and has a steady feel. Participants are well accustomed to the opacity of the majority of trades in this region, therefore rates don't appear as susceptible to the same volatility that the Atlantic market exhibits.
