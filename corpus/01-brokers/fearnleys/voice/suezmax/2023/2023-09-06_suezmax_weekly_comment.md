## Market Commentary

With VLCCs and Aframax in the doldrums, one would have logically presumed that the Suezmax market would exhibit similar frailty. Not so. Charterers have been very quick to swoop on early third decade ships for their TD20 requirements which has stabilized rates in the low WS 70's. Balmo paper has curiously been trading at WS 76, so make of it what you will. This is all the more bizarre considering how quiet the general Atlantic market has been, therefore we will have to put this down sentiment. The East saw a replacement TD23 cargo trade at WS 62.5, but for the natural window, we are pinning mid/high WS 50's on this run with a flat feel.
