## Market Commentary

The Suezmax market has spent the best part of eight months peaking in and around the WS 130's for TD20, before steadily coming off again into WS 90-100 levels. This pattern is on the cusp of being repeated this week, with last done rates reported at the WS 115 level. Whether the fall is as precipitous as previous crashes comes down to whether owners buy the pattern or fundamentals. If they're looking at the fundamentals, then there's a case for the brakes to be applied but sentiment is more likely to triumph. The main support base in the USG has all but dried up for now and the Black Sea continues to be pinned down by a continuous stream of vessels coming through the Suez Canal, either on spec' or discharging off the back of TD23 voyages.

**MEG**

/East has repeated WS 127.5 several times, but we expect this to be tested downwards as softer sentiment from the Atlantic infects the East. TD23 will find a home in the WS 60's with a softer trend.
