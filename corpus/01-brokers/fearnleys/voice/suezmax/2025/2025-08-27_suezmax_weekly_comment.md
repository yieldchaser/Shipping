## Market Commentary

Despite Baltic rates easing slightly yesterday and tonnage increasing over the long weekend, the West remains in good shape. In West Africa, both VLCCs and Suezmaxes remain light on second-decade September fixing. Although the tonnage profile may appear to lean on the softer side, prospective demand should be sufficient to counterbalance this. Additionally, CPC, USG, Brazil, and Guyana are expected to draw from the same pool of tonnage, meaning charterers cannot rest on their laurels. The market has the potential to turn quickly, especially with three days remaining before the weekend. The East, on the other hand, despite showing signs of promise, has a reasonable amount of supplementation of vessels able to make laycans prior September 15th. This has left the market with a steadier feel.
