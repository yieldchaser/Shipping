## Market Commentary

A few mysteries coming to light today as charterers continue to pick off vessels for 2nd decade West Africa. Availabilities remain reasonable albeit extension over the weekend isn’t huge. The balance between available tonnage, remaining stems and how forward we are working indicates a bit of mounting pressure albeit not substantial. If we had demand impetus from CPC and USG we would argue on the contrary but with strong coverage in the Black Sea and cheaper Aframaxes in the states these regions may not offer support right away. The VLCCs in the MEG continue to fix at healthy levels despite some ‘cheaper’ rumours earlier this week. Expect this heightened sentiment to trickle down to Suezmaxes, despite a healthier list for the smaller ladies.
