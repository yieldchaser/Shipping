## Market Commentary

The Suezmax market remains steady, but there might be a small downside to this. Coronavirus, Libya still closed down all export, and the lack of demand for oil in the east are some of the factors.

**Wafr**

-market still trading at w75-80 depending on voyage, which still give Owners above $20k/day. All the uncertainty makes people hesitant to take any action at all, and the market is in general very quiet. We dont expect to see any major changes going forward, but fundamentals are ok, so positive news could have a big impact on this market.
