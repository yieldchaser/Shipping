## Market Commentary

The Suezmax market in the Atlantic is still quite active, but ratewise it remains steady. A balanced tonnage-list is one factor, but even with a tight list it would be hard to move this market as the slow activity on the VLCC's is putting a lid on the whole Suezmax market. In the MEG we have much of the same problem, market is at bottom, or at least close to it, but it will take some time for this market to rebuild. All eyes on the VLCC's for now!
