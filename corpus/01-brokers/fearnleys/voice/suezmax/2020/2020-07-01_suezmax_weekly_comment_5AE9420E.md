## Market Commentary

The Suezmax market have had decent activity for the last couple of weeks. Going into this week, the tonnage list in the West was looking healthier, counting less than 120 ships next 30 days in West Africa. Owners have picked up on this, but unfortunately for them, we are missing the activity to tip the market in their direction. We are still looking at low WS40's for TD20, but heading into the 20-30 window, a small increase in cargo activity could have a bigger effect on the market than we have seen lately. In the East on the other hand, it’s basically status quo. Tonnage list is still looking awful in owners’ perspective, and for the time being something extraordinary will have to happen to move MEG/East anywhere close to WS50 again.
