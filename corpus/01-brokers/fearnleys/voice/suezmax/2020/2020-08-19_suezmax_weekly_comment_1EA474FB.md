## Market Commentary

Unfortunately for the owners, the market both in the east and in the west have been flat since we last spoke. Owners lack fundamental support to take the fight to the charterers and accept variations over last done in fear of losing the cargo. The activity level, albeit for the western hemisphere, have been decent but with the eastern market being in the doldrums, the Atlantic is getting a steady supply of ballasters from the east looking for employment and ,as such, we see little chance of immediate change for the balance of this week
