## Market Commentary

For Suezmaxes in the Atlantic it’s all about catching the falling knife. Market is coming off in chunks in all areas, and the only thing question asked is how low will this go. Fundamentals are not supporting this massive drop, but at the same time, there are enough ships in position. With VLCCs dropping as they have done, Suezmaxes will follow, and we are not done yet. Some positive new about the possible cure for Corona-virus might help break the fall, but going forward we still expect this segment to soften further, at least for the rest of the week.
