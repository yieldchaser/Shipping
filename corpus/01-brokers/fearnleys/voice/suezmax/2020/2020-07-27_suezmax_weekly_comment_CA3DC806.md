## Market Commentary

Suezmax rate-support has been underpinned by a mixture of sentiment and stresses to the list. Whilst general enquiry levels have suffered due to production constraints, resistance has been established by dint of many of the modern ships on the list being in the hands of fewer owners. When continued delays in China are added to the mix, the net affect of this owner-concentration is that they are more willing to test the market upwards and less willing to give ground when market activity subsides. This pattern is likely to be repeated as charterers bite deeper into the second decade.
