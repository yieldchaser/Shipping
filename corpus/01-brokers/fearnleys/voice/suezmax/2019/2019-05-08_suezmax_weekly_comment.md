## Market Commentary

Suezmaxes had a slow start after the holiday period as Charterers took advantage of the steady accumulation of tonnage, rates ticked down accordingly. However owners resistance started to build in West Africa with some preferring to stand off cargoes preferring to sit and wait for the right voyages, this has had a knock on effect with ws 80 now having been paid for waf/east up a handful of points but purely being sentiment driven. Elsewhere the Med and Black sea have ben low on fixing volume with TD6 currently steady at ws 80 no too much will change here for the balance of this week.
