## Market Commentary

Sporadic action has allowed tonnage lists to build and further erosion of rates across the board has been the flavour of the past week. It has been a slow decline rather than a collapse in levels which has allowed earnings to remain close to 20 k per day in the Atlantic, there has been some notable fixing out of the Usg and this is one area that has shown some life, the WTI- Brent spread has been wide enough to allow imports into the ukcm and that is an attractive voyage for a select few. Some owners have preferred to sit ships spot, seemingly satisfied to wait for the right voyage giving the best TCE returns rather than fixing less attractive options at lower money. Still further pain ahead for the foreseeable future but the Atlantic basin tonnage lists will need watching if Usg exports continue.
