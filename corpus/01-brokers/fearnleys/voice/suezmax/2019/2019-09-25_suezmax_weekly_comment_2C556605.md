## Market Commentary

Suezmaxes firmed up significantly in West Africa end of last week, with rates nearing three-digit numbers for West Africa/UKCM. The weekend gave charterers a chance to re-group and we have seen a slight softening tone in the Atlantic so far this week, which would be expected after rates rose from ws60 to ws95 for West Africa/UKCM in one week. While USG-CBS and West Africa have been rather quiet on the surface this week, Mediterranean/Black Sea and MEG have been busy and this in turn could help owners make this small downturn a short one.
