## Market Commentary

Owners went in to the weekend with their hopes high after an active last week. So far this week, rates in West Afrika have kept steady with charterers having plenty of early tonnage to choose from.

**MEG**

 and MED-Black Sea have been busy for several weeks now and continues to be busy which absorbs ship that would elsewhere face a ballast to WAFR. Couple that with increased activity in Americas and little natural supply to choose from and rates could very soon be on the move again. Time will tell if the weekend once again will provide charterers with some breathing space or if next week will set fire to rates in the Atlantic.
