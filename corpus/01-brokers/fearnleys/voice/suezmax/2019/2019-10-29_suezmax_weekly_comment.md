## Market Commentary

The Suezmax market has been in a stand-off for almost two weeks now. Very little activity in all areas, building up the position list and getting some breathing space for the charterers. And as so many times before, charterers came out on top and the market has started to come off in chunks.

**West Africa**

/UKCM is now down to ws125, but still have some downside potential even though we are closing in on the bottom. The East market is looking to come off quite a bit for the rest of the week as well. Going forward, we could easily see the activity picking up a bit, on the back of this quiet period. TCE wise market is still good, and if this is the bottom, there is no point for owners to be unhappy.
