## Market Commentary

Aframaxes trading in the Nsea and Baltic continues to suffer from the lack of available market cargoes. This is mainly due to the production cuts especially from the strategic crude export terminals in the Baltic. Another main factor is that relets fix a huge portion of the few available cargoes on a private basis, hence leaving very little to fix for other owners. Going forward the market will move sideways around current bottom levels. Much of the same can be said for the Mediterranean and Black Sea where the market has continued to move sideways yet another week. Although little activity from charterers, rates have bottomed out at around WS60 for a straight cross-Med voyages. This gives owners a return of about USD 1,500 pd in TCE. We expect this to continue in the week to come.
