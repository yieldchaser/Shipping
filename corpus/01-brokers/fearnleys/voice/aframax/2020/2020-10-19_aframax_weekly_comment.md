## Market Commentary

Aframax owners trading in the Nsea/Baltic have shown great resistance in the past week with some owners having refused to fix at current low levels. On the back of this we saw a minor uptick in rates. However, going forward we need to see a bigger volume or at least other areas showing a sign of life to see a significant upward correction. Until that happens we expect rates to slide sideways. In the Med/Bsea a long awaited increase in activity across the board has resulted in a slight pickup in rates for the first time since the summer. Recent activity is also supported by more barrels being exported from Libya after FM being lifted in several ports. Owners will be eager to see how long the current activity level will be maintained and to what extent it will shorten the long list of prompt open tonnage in the area.
