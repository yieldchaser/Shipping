## Market Commentary

Rates in the Mediterranean and Black Sea moved sideways this week, while in the North Sea and Baltic they took another hit in the southward direction. Each quoted market cargo in the area attracted between 9 to 12 offers. Going forward there are still plenty of available positions and we expect rates to stay around current low levels.
