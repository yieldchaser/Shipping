## Market Commentary

Aframax rates in the North Sea came off slightly as expected due to lack of activity and dates moving forward.

**Baltic**

 rates ex Russian ports is also under downward pressure as more tonnage willing to call Russian ports are emerging while awaiting further news about any possible further sanctions on Russian oil. Going forward we expect the market to move sideways with a softer undertone if we don’t see an uptick in activity. In the Med/Bsea we have seen a quiet start to the week following the Easter holidays. The political diversion in Libya has resulted in several oil fields and ports closed as Force Majeure has been introduced. As expected, tonnage is also building up in the area and we have seen a downwards correction in rates. We expect the market to remain soft in the natural fixing window.
