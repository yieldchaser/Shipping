## Market Commentary

The Nsea market moved more or less sideways in the last week with earning around USD 55k pd. We do see more owners, who earlier was only concentrating on Russian cargoes, now also looking at loading non-Russian North Sea grades. How this will affect rates remain to be seen. However, we expect the market to remain at current levels as the supply and demand scenario looks fairly balanced at the time of writing.

**Med**

/BSea market slowly but steadily moved up to higher levels last week. Returns on a cross-Med run right now are even better than voyages ex CPC with owners trying to earn a tad more on each fixture. 1st decade of November might be a bit slower later this week, but overall, we expect rates to remain strong as supply of vessels is very balanced due to surrounding areas offering good opportunities as well.
