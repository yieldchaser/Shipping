## Market Commentary

The North Sea/Baltic market been moving sideways this week with rates for a cross UKC paying around WS 185 level. However, with quite a few Panamax enquires looking at Aframax tonnage due to a tight tonnage list on Panamax this we could see an upward pressure on rates moving into the 3rd decade fixing window. The Med/Blsea market continued the downward spiral throughout last week. There’s been some under the radar activity this week, but not sufficient to support the prompt and available tonnage hence freight levels taken a further dip this week. We don’t foresee any real improvements for the balance of the week unless activity bounces back. However, some owners now ballasting away eyeing more promising returns in the USG.
