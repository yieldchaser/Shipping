## Market Commentary

Aframax rates in the North Sea declined significantly the last week. The current fixing window is in the 3rd decade of September, and the tonnage list looks quite healthy from a charterer’s perspective. We expect rates to move sideways give or take a couple of points till we reach October fixing dates.

**Med**

/Bsea market continued trading at low levels with charterers taking advantage of CPC inactivity. It’s a tough market for owners as they seek for alternatives away from the area with USG market being the only attractive one in the West. Moving forward we expect rates to remain soft and move sideways, a trend which might persist for the following weeks as well.
