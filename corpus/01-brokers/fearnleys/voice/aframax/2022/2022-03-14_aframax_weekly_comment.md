## Market Commentary

We still see large premiums being paid for Russian port calls despite less reported activity over the last couple of days. Everyone is waiting to see if any stems get cancelled and how this will affect the export program for both March and April.

**North Sea**

 crude spot earnings came off significantly last week and have been hovering around WS 120 levels due to erratic bunker prices. Going forward we might see rates in the North Sea improve as we expect even more VLCC stems ex WCN being lifted on Aframaxes. Although the rates ex Bsea have come off this last week, voyages ex Russian ports are still giving owners a healthy return. In the Mediterranean, we have seen the market soften further and we expect this to continue going into the weekend.
