## Market Commentary

Aframax owners in the North Sea have had a week with very few cargo enquiries. A great portion of cargoes in the North Sea basin have been covered with own tonnage. On the back of this charterers tested the market by quoting the market and managed to push down the rates for an Ecuk load off 17-18 May dates. Going forward we expect rates to move sideways till activity picks up again. The Med/Bsea market has been trading sideways all week with a softening trend. This is due to a lack of cargo activity and a tonnage list which is getting longer. The status is quo in Libya for the time being, and since surrounding markets are not offering any firmer alternatives, we expect the market to remain soft going into next week.
