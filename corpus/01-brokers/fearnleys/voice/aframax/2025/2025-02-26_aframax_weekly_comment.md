## Market Commentary

With IE week in full swing market activity on the surface has been limited. Natural dates are pushing into the end of the first decade and we have seen some stems taken on bigger ships which is limiting available cargo for Aframax. Ballasting out of the area is still an attractive prospect for the early tonnage. Limited activity being reported in the region. Vessels still showing interest in the Mediterranean from the North Sea adding to a long list of vessels that need to be employed before owners can hold some ground here. With action in the USG we might lose some vessels to ballasting stateside, but at this point it is unlikely to support this market without increased activity here.
