## Market Commentary

NORTH SEA After a busy couple of weeks with the North Sea rates pushing up over 20 points, this week started slowly. The loading schedules for the first decade September look a little light on volume, and with relets and Suezmax coming into play we could see a slow down in market activity. Available tonnage is still on the tighter side which even with a slow down in activity may mean the downside potential on rates is somewhat limited. MEDITERRANEAN Busy start to the week in the Mediterranean with rates correcting and charterers have taken tonnage at the lower rates for the 1st decade. Tonnage list has cleared up but dates are also pushing forward for now. Surrounding areas are holding strong so we don’t foresee further correction on the rates for local Mediterranean runs for now.
