## Market Commentary

North Sea After a flurry of activity at the end of last week clearing the last of the September requirements October has started on the slower side. The available tonnage remains thin but with relets available and at least so far a lack of activity the market is likely to remain steady as we push into the 2nd decade fixing window. US markets continue to pull tonnage away but this is relatively balanced with tonnage coming back laden. Mediterranean Market has been active under the radar with vessels being picked up quietly by Charterers at more or less last-done levels. We are approaching mid October dates for all ports. Tonnage list is still balanced considering the amount of cargoes and the fixing window being forward now. Rates are voyage / date sensitive with Owners able to be selective on cargo choice.
