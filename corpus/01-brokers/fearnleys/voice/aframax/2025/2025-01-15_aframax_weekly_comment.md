## Market Commentary

With the first full week of 2025 in full swing markets are adjusting to the new Worldscale schedule. Activity has been limited so far which is adding pressure on rates with fixing window already pushing into the end of the 2nd decade. US markets are picking up which may bring opportunities, but so far the year remains on the softer side rate wise. Inactivity through the start of the week along with Suezmaxes absorbing Afra parcels created a very long list of prompt vessels and a steady stream continually coming back around. Summer fundamentals in the middle of Winter has had a negative impact on sentiment and it seems that it will take a while before we see any change to the current situation.
