## Market Commentary

NORTH SEA Steady activity so far this week and dates are pushing out towards the end of the 1st decade. With surrounding markets looking firmer tonnage will be pulled out of the area to look at alternative business which in turn should put pressure on North Sea both availability and rates levels. MEDITERRANEAN Continued activity, rates have been moving sideways in the early part of the week. Tonnage list has shrunk on the front end but fixing window is late 1st/early 2nd decade for most of the ports. As USG market has jumped 25p since Monday we expect to see some ballast from the West Mediterranean. Which again will put pressure on the Mediterranean rates. Like the North Sea likely to firm.
