## Market Commentary

North Sea A relatively slow week so far activity wise but vessels have been clearing the area which has kept the rates steady. Relets have come back into the frame taking various stems out into the third decade. List is slowly building again, but if US markets remain activity, more will ballast, keeping the North Sea availability balanced. Owners are able to be a bit more selective on which cargoes they want to look at. Early March window is not expected to be that busy on paper so we don't expect big swings on the rates however some delays here and there might change things pretty quickly. Mediterranean After the busy end of last and start of this week we are done now for February dates in the Mediterranean and March dates will be worked from today. Feeling top-ish? I would say so, as owners repeated the rates on the last busy window and if 01-05 March isn't that busy they should be happy to do it again or even leaving a few points on the table. Weather expected to clear out from this weekend onwards and more vessels will come back open next week.
