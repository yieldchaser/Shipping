## Market Commentary

North Sea The North Sea list has been greatly reduced with vessels heading to the US. The amount of vessels in ballast to the US so far in August is the largest amount we have seen in the same period in any previous month. The North Sea market has remained steady even with the clearing of the list holding around the WS 200 level. Dates are starting to push towards the end of the month which should see some increase in activity and itineraries will be important for B/L dates which may allow owners to chase a slight premium. Mediterranean Activity in the Mediterranean has been consistent with a few replacement requirements also coming into the mix. The US has attracted vessels from the West Mediterranean along with some compromised vessels has kept the tonnage available to charterers interesting. Rates have firmed around WS 15 points from last week and heading to the end August/early September fixing window we may still see some more firming.
