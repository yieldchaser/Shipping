## Market Commentary

As North Sea Aframax loading dates are now moving into the third decade of September, the list of available tonnage continues to thin. With neighbouring markets such as the US Gulf and Mediterranean offering attractive alternatives, owners are increasingly ballasting away from the North Sea. While rates remain steady for the time being, tightening tonnage availability could support a modest upward correction in the near term. Activity in the Mediterranean remains robust, with cargoes being quoted from virtually every export area and loading port. Rates continue to gain momentum, and with charterers actively seeking to secure tonnage for forward-loading programmes, the market is expected to remain firm with further upside potential.
