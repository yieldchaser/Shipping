## Market Commentary

North Sea rates have held relatively flat over the past week with last-done levels being repeated at WS 145. Activity has been healthy and a steady flow of ballasting has helped thin out the list, although positions are gradually pushing into end/early fixing windows. We expect the traditional TA players to continue heading West, with sentiment leaning towards a firmer US market, in turn lending some support to TD7 going forward. The Mediterranean market has had a softer week, with charterers capitalising on a long tonnage list to push rates lower, while some deals have been concluded quietly under the radar. Despite overall healthy activity, availability has remained too high to be fully absorbed, keeping downward pressure on rates. A handful of prompt vessels remain, and the balance looks weak as we settling into July stems. Owners, now, may look to resist further erosion of current levels, although without a fresh wave of stems it is difficult to see a meaningful recovery.
