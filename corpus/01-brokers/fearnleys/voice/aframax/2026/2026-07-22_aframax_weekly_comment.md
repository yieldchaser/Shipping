## Market Commentary

North Sea North Sea and Mediterranean markets have settled down activity wise in the early part of the week after what had been a very active couple of weeks. The North Sea list remains on the thin side but natural dates now pushing into August with the potential for relets to come back into the frame. The list will be further depleted especially the early part with tonnage heading off to the very firm US markets. For a Summer market rates remain strong giving owners very healthy returns. Mediterranean Rates in the Mediterranean continue in the high WS 300s with a bit of underground activity so far this week but slower compared to the chaos of last week. At first look tonnage list is long however delays in Trieste, vessels ballasting from the Red Sea-East and potentially ballasting towards USG might keep it more balanced. Returns are too good for owners to say 'no', if a small discount is required but sentiment remains strong.
