## Market Commentary

North Sea The main headline this week has been vessels ballasting. Tonnage list in the North Sea has been trimmed with tonnage heading mainly to USG but by the middle of the week heading to the Mediterranean with the busy firming market becoming the destination of choice.

**North Sea**

 has remained steady so far with not enough activity to be affected by the smaller tonnage list. Natural dates should start to push towards 3rd decade. Mediterranean We are slowly heading into 3rd decade of July and rates for local runs are close to the WS 200 mark. Multiple factors are positively affecting the market and the owners have built some proper summer sentiment for now. There are still outstanding cargoes for the 2nd decade and rates are expected to remain under further upward pressure.
