## Market Commentary

Aframax rates in the Nsea market dropped a few points as activity slowed down and most cargoes been taken out on relets in the current fixing window. The USG market is the most interesting right now in the Western hemisphere, and we might see ballasters from the Nsea and Wmed turning their bow in that direction and ballasting transatlantic. We expect the market to move sideways with a softish feel to it. In the Med/BSea rates dropped by 30 points for local runs even though earthquake didn’t affect loadings in Ceyhan that much. Tonnage list had built up the last 10 days as there wasn’t enough activity to absorb sufficient number of vessels and inevitably charterers managed to push rates lower. We except market to remain weak for the rest of the month unless firming USG market will attract more ballasters from Europe.
