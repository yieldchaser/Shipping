## Market Commentary

It has been yet another quiet week for Aframax Owners trading in the North Sea. Lack of activity still keep rates at bottom levels. Some Owners have ballasted away from the area to seek better opportunities elsewhere. We can only expect a recovery in rates once activity picks up.The past week proved to be a bit more interesting in the Mediterranean, and rates ticked up on the back of increased activity. However, sentiment is now changing and there is a softer feeling to the market. Although, the cap on the Med Aframax market from the Suezmaxes has become so ever apparent, the natural Aframax tonnage is still relatively tight in the region. The backlog of traffic in Trieste and the consistency of volume will be two key factors in keeping the pressure on current levels. The limits been put on rates in this region have caused concerns for Owners, but we cannot see rates falling significantly much below where we are now going forward.
