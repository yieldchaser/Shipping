## Market Commentary

Market activity in the Nsea has been rather limited as we have seen relets being fixed between oil companies or being programmed for own cargoes. However, rates this week have been maintained at similar levels as last week. Going forward we expect rates to move sideways as the tonnage list looks rather balanced compared to the expected cargo volume for second half of February. Last week, activity slowed down in the Med and as a result rates for local runs lost some more ground while the rates for CPC-Med runs also moved lower despite the long waiting days in Turkish Straits. Liftings of Azeri crude are uncertain now after the earthquake in South Turkey, something that could put rates under further downward pressure moving forward.
