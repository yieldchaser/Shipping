## Market Commentary

NORTH An active end to last week, and the start of this week has seen the natural North Sea tonnage with safe itineraries being picked off, tightening the list and pushing dates out towards end month. We have seen some weather delays, and with surrounding areas looking attractive and firming tonnage is likely to ballast out of the area, this combination will further tighten the list and put some more pressure on freight rates. MEDITERRANEAN A flurry of activity has firmed Mediterranean Aframax throughout this week. With the activity and lists at least off prompter dates beginning to tighten, owners look to keep the pressure on for higher numbers though that being said, next week could be quiet and this time of year always leaves room for a ship to be wrapped up at last done rates in time for Christmas. Continued consistent supply has potential for higher numbers as rates are only moving in one direction.
