## Market Commentary

The whole European Aframax market has been under downward pressure for more than two weeks with returns for owners being as low as 20k/day right now. Premium players cannot find Russian cargoes as Urals are trading above price cap, supply of oil in general in the West is lower the last couple of weeks and a bunch of vessels looking for employment has sent rates tumbling all across the board. Short-term future will be tough for the owners as all signals are bearish and the summer market is here to stay.
