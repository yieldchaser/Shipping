## Market Commentary

With stormy weather hitting the North Sea we have seen some weather delays over the Xmas and New Year’s period. Charterers have searched the tonnage list for vessels with safe itineraries to avoid delays. This combined with a firming USG market with tonnage likely to ballast out of the area, will maintain rates at current levels, at least up to mid-month January dates. It’s a tough start to 2024 in the Mediterranean with supply coming slow. Tonnage is top heavy with a mass of prompt ships that will look for opportunities elsewhere with the Mediterranean yet to spark excitement. Not much hope for owners this side of the weekend with rates to remain steady with downward pressure whilst demand continues to outweigh supply, making a rising USG market all the more enticing for owners.
