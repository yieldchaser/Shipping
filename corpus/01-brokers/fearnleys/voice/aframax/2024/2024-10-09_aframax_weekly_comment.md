## Market Commentary

Freight levels have moved up from last week but earnings are still lagging behind the Mediterranean and US markets. Natural dates are now into the 15-20 window. We are still seeing prompter vessels ballasting out of the area which is reducing the front end of the position list but so far not enough to affect levels unduly. The window for some key ports in the Mediterranean is now fixing well-ahead but with Libyan exports returning to the market and with tonnage in the region shortened on the front end, Owner’s sentiment is positive as they push for higher returns on earlier cargoes. Suezmax are watching on closely for any opportunity with the two segments working in tandem for some routes.
