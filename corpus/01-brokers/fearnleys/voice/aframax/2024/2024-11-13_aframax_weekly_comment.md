## Market Commentary

North Sea market continues with little market activity, just a few off market private deals. Dates are pushing into the 3rd decade, and we still have relets and prompt tonnage to take up any cargoes that may come to market. The trend is steady, and it looks likely to remain as such for the near future. US markets have softened but due to the lack of activity in the North Sea the States has continued to attract ballasters. Sharing in similarity to the North Sea activity in the Mediterranean has been extremely slow compared to last week. The tonnage list remains somewhat balanced but vessels are expected to come back into position. Rates keep hovering around last done levels and with said tonnage coming around and neighboring markets lacking in alternatives, another influx of cargoes is needed in order to turn the tide.
