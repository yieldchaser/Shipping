## Market Commentary

Some steady activity in the North Sea with dates pushing into the 2nd decade. A lot of programming of tonnage but placing Bbls still proving tricky for charterers so we could well see some delays. Rates are flat at the moment but with USG firming we will see pressure on availability as tonnage heads out of the region. List now looking well established and relets are in the region. Prompter vessels are locking in on opportunities in front of them allowing charterers to squeeze some points out of last done rates. CPC somewhat lacks suitable candidates but with relets in the picture and the window reaching end-month dates rates won’t be under pressure. Bad weather due in the region might yet have some influence.
