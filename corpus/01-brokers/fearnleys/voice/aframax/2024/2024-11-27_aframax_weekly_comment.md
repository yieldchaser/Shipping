## Market Commentary

Bad weather caused some issues at the beginning of the week but seems to have settled a bit more now. Activity in the first half of the week has been limited with some fixing/failing. Dates covering into the first 5 days and rates look like they will remain steady and in certain date ranges could come under some downward pressure. US Market not enticing much in the way of ballasting just yet. An interesting Mediterranean/Black Sea market where the intervention of bigger ships is not extensive, but attractive returns have put them in the mix occasionally. Fixing window is pushing well forward with finding safe itineraries now at the forefront of charterers minds. The North Sea remains weak and the USG not giving much support; owners will look to keep one foot in the Mediterranean and with some requirement will likely push for higher than last done, but with Suezmax watching closely.
