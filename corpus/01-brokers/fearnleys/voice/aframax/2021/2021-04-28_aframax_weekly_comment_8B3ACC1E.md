## Market Commentary

In the North Sea/Baltic market we have seen a slight improvement in rates this week. This has been mainly driven by a more balanced tonnage list coupled with the largest Urals schedule since April last year. We expect rates to stay under upward pressure, given the current activity will continue to feed owners’ optimism and while waiting for a busier 3rd decade again. Much of the same can be said about the Mediterranean/Black Sea market this week where rates have improved a tad mainly due to a healthy CPC program. Although there are still quite a few prompt ships in the area, owners have been showing some resistance. In the week to come we expect the market to move sideways with an upward potential.
