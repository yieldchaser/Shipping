## Market Commentary

The Nsea/Baltic market remained flat with rates at bottom level despite a few more cargo inquiries. The tonnage list in the area is still plentiful as owners pushing in order to at least secure positive returns on local voyages. Moving forward we expect rates to keep moving sideways. As expected, rates in the Med/Bsea market came off a couple of points more this week, mainly due to the lacklustre activity on the cargo side. We do not expect any significant improvement to this market in the short run, and going into next week we expect it to continue trading sideways.
