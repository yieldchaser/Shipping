## Market Commentary

The Nsea/Baltic Aframax rates improved slightly on the back of a heavier 3rd decade Urals loading schedule. The tonnage list is looking somewhat tighter and owners put up a bit more resistance right now. There is a firmer undertone in the market, and we expect rates to firm in the current fixing window. In the Med/Bsea, a decent level of activity has done its part in taking out a fair number of prompt ships the last few days. Although rates have taken a slight dip since last week and the tonnage list remains healthy, we may see a slight firming of rates in the week to come if current activity levels are maintained.
