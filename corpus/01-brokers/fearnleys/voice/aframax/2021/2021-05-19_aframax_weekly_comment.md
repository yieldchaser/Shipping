## Market Commentary

After a slight improvement in rates last week, owners in the Nsea/Baltic market again had to give in for the downward pressure they faced this week. Charterers marched forward and covered quietly almost all stems for May. Moving forward, unless surrounding areas can offer an alternative, we expect rates to remain under pressure for the week to come as cargo volumes will be scarce and more ships coming back into position. The Med/Bsea has trade sideways all week as expected. However, in the last two days we have seen the tonnage list tighten a bit, and as a result we are at the time of writing seeing rate pick up a few points. If owners are able to maintain the current pressure on rates, we expect a slightly firmer market towards the end of the week, especially with a healthy Bsea program for June.
