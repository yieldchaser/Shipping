## Market Commentary

An oversupply of vessels coupled with limited activity in the North Sea area have led owners fixing cross-Continent runs on negative returns just for re-positioning matters. On the other side, as we have got colder weather and new ice forming in some strategic Baltic load ports charterers prefer to fix in ice-class vessels giving owners hope for some slightly higher returns. We expect market to remain flat in the week to come with most of January’s liftings in the Nsea already covered. In the Med/Bsea we are counting more than 30 ships prompt open free of cargo this week. The few cargoes coming into the market is a far cry from satisfying the amount of available tonnage, which in turn is reflected in TD19 showing a loss of close to USD 2,000 pd on TCE. We do not expect a significant recovery in the week to come.
