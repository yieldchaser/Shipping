## Market Commentary

The Nsea/Baltic market didn’t show any signs of life last week as rates remained flat at the bottom. Activity in the area is not enough to make the tonnage list shorter and charterers still manage to fix their cargoes at very low rates. We don’t foresee any tight window soon hence we expect rates to move sideways next week. In the Med/Black Sea, owners trying to optimize, but with a slight downward correction in freight levels seen at the beginning of this week, owners currently finding themselves battling for cargo opportunities not offering much more than last done levels at best. With the prevailing tonnage availability in the area, we don’t expect to see any significant improvement in the near term.
