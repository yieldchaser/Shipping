## Market Commentary

The Nsea/Baltic market appeared bottomed out as current levels gave negative returns on some specific routes. Owners bravely put up resistance, but had to give in as charterers managed to push rates even lower on the back of low volumes and a long tonnage list for 2nd decade loadings. Moving forward, a busier 3rd decade for the Urals could give a glimpse of hope for the owners. In the Med/Bsea we have the seen the market trade sideways all week with current levels on benchmark routes being repeated a number of times. TD19 is currently hovering around high ws80-levels, giving owners a return of around USD 3000 pd on TCE. Although owners try to hold back on rates, it seems most of them expect a flat market in the week to come.
