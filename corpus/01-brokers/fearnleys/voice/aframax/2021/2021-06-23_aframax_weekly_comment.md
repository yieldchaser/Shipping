## Market Commentary

Nsea/Baltic Aframax rates increased significantly after several weeks of remaining at close to zero return levels. With some vessels floating with certain grades onboard coupled with more ships leaving the area in laden condition created a tighter position list in the current fixing window. This mixed with more cargoes that usual being quoted created a perfect momentum for the owners to capitalize on. Moving into July dates, we expect the market to stabilize around current levels, but with some downward risk as charterers trying to bring in alternative tonnage with Suezmaxes, shuttles and own relets coming into play now. Also, in the Med/Bsea activity on the cargo side combined with a tight tonnage list has resulted in a 15 ws point pick up in rates this week. However, as in the North, the Suezmaxes are a threat.
