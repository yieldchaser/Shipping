## Market Commentary

While fundamentals remain the same in the Nsea and Baltic Aframax market, it is correct to say that it was more sentiment that pushed rates slightly higher this week. Going forward the tonnage list is looking shorter and as the October lifting program looks busier, owners will try to put some upward pressure on rates. The market is looking steady but firmer. In the Med/Bsea we have seen a rush of cargoes enter the market this week with both cross-Med and ex-Bsea being active. Rates across the board have picked up 10 points or so, and the tonnage list is tighter than it has been in a long time. However, Suezmaxes are now coming into play, as well as Aframaxes open in the North, so it will be interesting to see how long owners in the Med can keep an upward pressure on rates.
