## Market Commentary

The Aframax market in the North Sea and Baltic looks like will face a downward adjustment, but we still need to keep an eye on the Mediterranean/Black Sea market which can offer an alternative for non-ice owners open on the Continent. The Ice season in the Baltic is slowly coming to an end and owners will try to take advantage of the last premiums paid before restrictions will be lifted. As we are now looking at early-April fixing window, we expect rates to move sideways in for the North Sea in the week to come, with a downward pressure on rates for cargoes loading ex Baltic. In the Med/Black Sea the market has moved more or less sideways the past week. This week started with a slight upward correction, mainly due to weather delays in a few key ports causing a tighter tonnage list. We expect the market to remain steady/firm.
