## Market Commentary

Following holidays in the UK, it returned a tedious opening for the Panamax physical market with fundamentals largely unchanged with rates drifting lower. This despite an active and positive move in the FFA markets which created some consternation in parts, but overall had little impact so far. The North Atlantic was still widely gapped between the grain and mineral trades whilst trade ex-South America remained slow. The outlook ex Asia from the outset looked tonnage heavy with the smaller/older units discounting still for the quick Indonesia round trips, whilst there was muted talk of better bids being touted around for the longer rounds, but the market remained a watching brief.
