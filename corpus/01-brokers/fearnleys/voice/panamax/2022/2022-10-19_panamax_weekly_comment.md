## Market Commentary

A slow but steady week in the Atlantic basin, fresh cargoes hitting the market with an underlying demand off market too helping to keep rates steady and firm for both the transatlantic and fronthaul trips. Asia returned once more something of a two-tier market according to some sources, the North still seeing strong demand ex NoPac with firmer rates being concluded, but again there lacked any tangible mineral demand from Australia which on some positions has kept a lid on rates for now.
