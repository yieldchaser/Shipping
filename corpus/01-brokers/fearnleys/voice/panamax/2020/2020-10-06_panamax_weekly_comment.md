## Market Commentary

With the Chinese away for holidays it was expected less activity, however the volume of fixtures has increased. The TA rates increased some USD 1000 w-o-w and currently yielding in the mid 13k’s while shorter Baltic rounds are being fixed in the 14’s. In the eastern hemisphere we also see firming rates with NoPac rounds being fixed in the 14k region bss North delivery while Aussie rounds are being prized in the 12k region.
