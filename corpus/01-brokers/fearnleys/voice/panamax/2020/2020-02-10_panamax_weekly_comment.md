## Market Commentary

This week the index has ticked up a bit, as many participants feels a bottom have been reached, and several rates fixed for fronthauls and Nopac-runs have been stronger than last done. There are, however, still a lot of uncertainty in the market. For a Transatlantic trip, Kamsarmaxes (82.500 DWT) still earn around the low USD 3000's, while short fronthauls from the UK/Continent-region are priced at around USD 13,000. For grains ECSA/China charterers are paying around USD 7000 DOP Singapore for a roundtrip. A Transpacific roundvoyage in the east pay shipowners around the low USD 4,000's, and the Baltic 4TC-Index is up 72 points since last week, now at 582 points.
