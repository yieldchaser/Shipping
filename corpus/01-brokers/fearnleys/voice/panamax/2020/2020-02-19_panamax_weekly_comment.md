## Market Commentary

Another positive week with numbers moving upwards in the Panamax-segment, especially in the ECSA region and in the east. For a Transatlantic trip, Kamsarmaxes (82.500 DWT) earn around the low USD 4000's, while short fronthauls from the UK/Continent-region are priced at high USD 13,000. For grains ECSA/China charterers are paying around USD 8500 DOP Singapore for a roundtrip. A Transpacific roundvoyage in the east pay shipowners around the high USD 5,000's, and the Baltic 4TC-Index is up 140 points since last week, now at 722 points.
