## Market Commentary

The Panamax market ended last week on a softer note which continued into this week. Rates have been somewhat stable, but sliding south in both hemispheres. Still a good number of NCSA and ECSA cgos, but rates remain at ard 14k aps + gbb. In the North Atlantic we see a growing fleet list coupled with less demand and expect rates to soften further.
