## Market Commentary

The Panamax market took a turn mid last week, and has experienced firmer rates in both hemispheres. TA’s are now paying excess 12k for the longer runs, while the shorter are being paid in the 14k’s. In the Pacific, we see Aussie rounds being fixed in the 12k’s. We do expect this firming tendency to remain short term with a short tonnage list – especially in the North Atlantic. ECSA grain cargoes are still in a roll and being fixed at around 15.5k+550k GBB, and we now also see the Black Sea grain market coming into play.
