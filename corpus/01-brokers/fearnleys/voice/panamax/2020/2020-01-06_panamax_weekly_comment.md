## Market Commentary

Weak sentiment has put rates in both basins under constant pressure in the last weeks, with no exceptions this week. Please take note that the rates quoted will from now on be basis a Baltic type 82,000 DWT Kamsarmax. A TA round voyage pays owners around the mid USD 8.00 per day. A fronthaul from the Continent yields around the mid USD 15,000's. In the Pacific basin, a round voyage pays owners around USD 4,500 per day. The BPI 4TC-index is currently at 803 points, 618 down since week 50, 2019.
