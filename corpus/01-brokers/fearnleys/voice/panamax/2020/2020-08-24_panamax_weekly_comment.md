## Market Commentary

Last week we saw the Panamax market taking a turn with a slower market and less demand, especially in the Atlantic. This week the demand has picked up, but the tonnage list is increasing day by day. ECSA TA’s are being fixed on APS bss and the P1A index is currently yielding ard 15k while the shorter Baltic rounds has been fixed at low 14k bss UK delivery. For the ECSA grain run to Feast there has been talks of better bids, and currently being reported at around 14k bss delivery SE Asia. In the Pacific in general we do see a more stable market with rounds being fixed in the 13,5-14k region depending on delivery.
