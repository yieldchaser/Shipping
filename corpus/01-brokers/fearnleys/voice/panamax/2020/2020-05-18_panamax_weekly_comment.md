## Market Commentary

The Panamax market continued its soft appearance from last week, and especially in the Atlantic. The index shows around 2k for TA rounds, however the shorter Baltic rounds are also yielding around same levels. In the Pacific, we can see some signs of improvement with bids being above last done. The period market which has been next to non-existing during these Covid-19 times, has regained some activity this week with modern Kmaxes being fixed at low 9k for a wider redelivery span.
