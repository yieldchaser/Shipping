## Market Commentary

After weeks of firming rates in both hemispheres, the Panamax market now seems to have taken a breather. Rates are slowly coming off on all trades, and p1 is currently yielding around USD 19,600. The tonnage list is still tight, but demand has come off this week. In the eastern hemisphere we see p3 fixing at 14k bss China delivery. The grain market is still active out of ECSA and Black Sea and ships are being fixed retro SE Asia at ard USD 13/14k.
