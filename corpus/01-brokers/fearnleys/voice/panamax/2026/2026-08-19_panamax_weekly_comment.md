## Market Commentary

Panamax sentiment has turned more cautious this week, with the index giving back part of its recent gains and both basins trading with a softer bias. The Pacific has led the correction: prompt tonnage has accumulated across the North and South while fresh NoPac grain and Australian and Indo coal enquiry has been slow to absorb it, leaving charterers in control and the market still in search of a floor. The Atlantic has held up rather better, with ECSA fronthaul remaining the firmer leg and Continent tonnage still reasonably tight, although bid/offer have widened and fixing volumes are thin as charterers wait for the north to settle. Period discussion remains active across both Kamsarmax and Panamax, but owners are resisting the softer nearby curve and bids have yet to close the gap, so relatively little is concluding. We would expect a sideways-to-softer tone into next week, with any recovery contingent on a fresh injection of grain and coal cargo in the Pacific; in the meantime the Atlantic should continue to offer owners the better alternative.
