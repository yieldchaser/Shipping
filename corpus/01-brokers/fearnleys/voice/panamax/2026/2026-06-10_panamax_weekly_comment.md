## Market Commentary

The Panamax market remains divided between a gradually improving Atlantic and a weakening Pacific.

**Atlantic**

 sentiment has turned slightly more constructive, supported by better transatlantic activity, firmer fronthaul demand, and increasing NCSA enquiry, although ample tonnage and wide bid-offer spreads continue to cap gains. In the Pacific, limited cargo volumes, a growing tonnage list, and cautious charterers are keeping pressure on rates, particularly on Indonesian business. While vessel tightness indicators still point to near-term softness, strong shipment volumes, supportive demand fundamentals, and a coal forward curve now moving into slight backwardation suggest the market could find better support as July approaches.
