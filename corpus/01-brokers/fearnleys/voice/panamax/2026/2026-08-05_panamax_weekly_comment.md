## Market Commentary

The Panamax market firmed steadily through the week, with momentum broadening across both basins and owners increasingly able to hold their ground. In the Atlantic, the fronthaul grain trade remained the principal driver – a healthy flow of South American and US Gulf cargoes against a tightening tonnage list kept sentiment constructive and lifted end-month levels, even against continued charterer resistance. The Pacific firmed in tandem, underpinned by tight prompt tonnage, a steadier stream of North Pacific grain alongside Australian and Indonesian coal, and some weather-related disruption that lent owners additional confidence. Period interest was active and the bid-offer gap narrowed over the week, though owners continued to price forward cover with a firm hand and a modest premium on shorter durations. With cargo replenishment healthy in both basins and the forward curve holding a shallow backwardation, we enter next week on a constructive footing.
