## Market Commentary

The Panamax market carried a generally softer tone this week, with the Atlantic showing some signs of stabilizing while the Pacific remained under clear pressure from an oversupplied tonnage list and limited fresh cargo demand. In the Atlantic, activity improved modestly, with firmer sentiment out of ECSA and better support for fronthaul business from the US, although transatlantic remained relatively thin and prompt tonnage, particularly in ECSA, continued to trade at discounted levels. By contrast, the Pacific stayed bearish throughout. As owners were forced to soften ideas further, charterers continued to pick off the cheaper tonnage, and the lack of any meaningful fresh enquiry kept values edging lower. Overall, the market still feels cautious and directionless heading into the Easter holiday period, with any stronger late-week push in the Atlantic still to be proven.
