## Market Commentary

Panamax sentiment has turned softer, with the recent rally now looking increasingly tired. The Atlantic is losing support as fronthaul activity and ECSA enquiry slow, while a fuller tonnage list in the Continent and Mediterranean is adding pressure on owners’ ideas. In the Pacific, the tone is also more defensive, with limited fresh cargo, weaker paper, and prompt tonnage beginning to build, particularly in the North. Owners are still trying to hold close to last-done levels, especially for better-positioned modern units, but charterers are cautious and bids are drifting lower. Overall, the market looks close to a near-term peak, and without a fresh injection of cargo in either basin, further downside feels likely.
