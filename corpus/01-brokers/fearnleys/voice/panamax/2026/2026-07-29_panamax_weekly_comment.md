## Market Commentary

The Panamax market softened over the course of the week, with the index easing and sentiment turning more cautious across both basins amid the seasonal summer lull. The Atlantic set the softer tone – transatlantic and fronthaul enquiry stayed thin, prompt tonnage was discounted to secure employment, and a widening gap between owners' ideas and charterers' bids kept activity subdued, even as the Continent and Western Mediterranean remained comparatively tight on prompt vessels. The Pacific was the more constructive corner late in the week, steadying off recent lows as North Pacific grain enquiry improved and charterers returned to cover weather-disrupted positions, though an ample tonnage list and still-elevated Chinese stockpiles capped the recovery. Period interest remained active, with charterers continuing to pursue Kamsarmax cover for one- to two-year employment, but bid and offer stayed apart and owners of modern eco tonnage were reluctant to chase the market lower. On balance we would characterise the tone as soft but stabilising into month-end, with near-term direction hinging on whether the Pacific's tentative firming can broaden and Atlantic cargo replenishment comes through.
