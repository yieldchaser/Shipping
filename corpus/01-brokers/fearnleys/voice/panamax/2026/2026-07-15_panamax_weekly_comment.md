## Market Commentary

The Panamax market held a firm but consolidating tone through the week, with the index trading broadly sideways at historically elevated levels after the sharper gains of recent weeks. In the Atlantic, fronthaul remained the engine - steady mineral and grain demand out of the US Gulf, North Coast South America and the northern basins kept owners confident on the longer east-bound legs, while East Coast South America held its ground and better-supplied transatlantic rounds left the North Atlantic waiting on fresh cargo to set the next direction. The Pacific was the steadier of the two basins, underpinned by a deep North Pacific grain book, a consistent flow of Australian and Indonesian coal, and a gradually tightening prompt list as tonnage was drawn south toward South American loading; sentiment stayed constructive and owners largely held their offers, leaving charterers to chase. Period enquiry remained active and reasonably well bid, though owners' ideas and charterers' bids stayed some way apart and the forward curve continues to sit in modest backwardation. Overall the tone is constructive-to-steady heading into next week, with firmer bunker prices and a quieter North Atlantic the principal watch-points.
