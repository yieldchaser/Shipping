## Market Commentary

The week started off with holidays in several countries which led to a slow start in both hemispheres. Tuesday showed a positive undertone but still a wide gap between bid/offer. The North Atlantic has experienced a lack of demand compared to available tonnage, and mid-week we saw some Baltic cargos entering, which got fixed immediately. We do however now see from our tonnage count that tightness have reached a level that preceeded upturns previously – thus we do expect rates to spike the next couple of weeks.
