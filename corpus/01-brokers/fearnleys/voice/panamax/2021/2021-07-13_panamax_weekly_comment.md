## Market Commentary

It would seem the “summer market” has hit both hemispheres the last week, both on physical and FFA. The slight flow of fresh requirements in the North Atlantic is also seeing competition from larger vessels cutting size, hence the positive effect of the fresh requirements seem to be minimal. In general it would appear that there is simply less demand in both the Atlantic and Pacific basins at the moment. As a consequence of this, and also the dive in FFA values, the period market has also put the brakes on, with very few vsls reported fixed for period.
