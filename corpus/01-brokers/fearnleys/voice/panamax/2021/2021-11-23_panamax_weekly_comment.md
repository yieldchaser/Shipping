## Market Commentary

We have experienced a sliding market the last couple of weeks, but this week we see a bit more optimism from owners’ point of view. Rates are slowly picking up and P1A is currently yielding 25k. In the eastern hemisphere we also see rates picking up, however at a slower pace. NoPac rounds are being fixed in region of 18k. Some period fixtures have also been reported after the FFA’s stabilized and gained some value.
