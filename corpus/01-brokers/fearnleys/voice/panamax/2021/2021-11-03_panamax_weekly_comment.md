## Market Commentary

This week, the Panamax market has taken a major dive. All routes are down due to lack of demand, and a growing tonnage list in both hemispheres. P1A is currently trading around 32k from last week’s 36k. In the Pacific, rounds are being concluded at around USD 25k which is down from last week’s 37/38k. All this is caused by lack of demand, but we do believe we will find a logical bottom very soon. The period market has taken a massive beating this week, also with the effect from the downward curve in FFA’s.
