## Market Commentary

The Panamax market continued its firming tendency this week with fresh demand in both hemispheres. In the Atlantic, we see mineral demand picking up, but also the tonnage list is building up – thus could be a bit toppish mid-week. In the Pacific, Indonesian rounds have picked up but also NoPac demand gives support to rates. The period demand is also active with a Kamsarmax reported at 28.5k for 9/11 months basis Far East delivery.
