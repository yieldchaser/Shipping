## Market Commentary

The firming tendency from last week continued into this week as well. In the North Atlantic we saw strong numbers being achieved on the Baltic rounds and USD 30k was reported done. It was hard to put the finger on the longer Atlantic runs but p1A is currently yielding in the 17k. Tuesday we saw some fixing and failing being done, especially out of ECSA which could reflect that some chrts feel the market is toppish, however the tonnage list for grain clean ships is still scarce. This might as well be a breather after several days with rising market. In the eastern hemisphere, we still see an active period market while the spot market is somewhat slower. P3a is currently priced in the 12-13k region depending on delivery.
