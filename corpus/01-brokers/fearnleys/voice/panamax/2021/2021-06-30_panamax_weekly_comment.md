## Market Commentary

The index has been lagging since end last week – however mid-week it seems to have caught up. With congestion in European ports and a scarse tonnage list, charterers has had to pay up in order to cover their requirements. We now see low/mid 40k being fixed for P1A while the long Fhauls are being fixed in region of mid 50k. Shorter Baltic/India runs has even seen a 7 in front. In the eastern hemisphere we see Pac rounds yielding low 30k. Period market is still active backed by paper values.
