## Market Commentary

Although a slow start to the week with holidays, the Panamax market has been sort of counter cyclic compared to the other segments. While the bigger Capes and the smaller sized markets has experienced declining rates, the Panamaxes are still seeing higher rates than last done. P1A is currently achieving 21/22k and although we see less demand for fronthauls, it is priced at a healthy mid 30k level. In the eastern hemisphere rates are slipping, but P3 is still valued at a healthy mid 20k level.
