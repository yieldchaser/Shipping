## Market Commentary

From a booming market when we left last week the trend has turned the opposite direction. With London closed on Monday the start was very quiet, but we see a growing tonnage list in the Atlantic, and less demand. P1A is currently trading in the 28/29k region. In the Pacific, we still see good activity and rates keeping up for now. Rounds are being concluded in the mid 30’s and period activity has been good. Mid-week we do however see some ships failing.
