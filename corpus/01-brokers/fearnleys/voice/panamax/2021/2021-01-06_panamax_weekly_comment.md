## Market Commentary

2021 kicked off with firming rates in both hemispheres. The Atlantic is leading on with fresh demand and confident owners raising their fixing ideas. Solid grain demand out of the Americas will most likely push up the Pacific as well and ballasters from Singapore are fixing in the low USD 12k basis delivery DOP for ECSA/Far east rounds. TA’s are currently yielding mid 15k. Period fixtures have also regained activity, and a modern Kmax was rumored concluded for 1 year at 13k basis SE Asia delivery.
