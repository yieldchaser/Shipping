## Market Commentary

The Panamax market is continuing its firming tendency in both hemispheres. We see fresh mineral demand in the Atlantic, but also grain shipments have picked up, especially from Black Sea. Charterers book tonnage in SE Asia and Indian Ocean in order to cover their grains out of Black Sea. P1A is currently priced in the low 30s while fronthauls are yielding almost 50k. In the Pacific we see rounds being fixed at region 30k.
