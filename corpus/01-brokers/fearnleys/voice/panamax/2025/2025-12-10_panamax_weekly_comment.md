## Market Commentary

The Panamax market eased again this week, with the Atlantic still outperforming but losing some momentum as charterers kept the pressure on. Looking ahead, the near-term trend remains soft into year-end, but the broader indicators we track still point to healthier conditions and a potential rebound as we move into January.
