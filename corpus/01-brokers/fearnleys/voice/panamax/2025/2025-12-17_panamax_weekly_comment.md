## Market Commentary

The Panamax market remained under pressure this week as thin fixing activity across both the Atlantic and Pacific continued to hand control to charterers, with owner resistance steadily softening. In the Atlantic, prompt demand has largely been absorbed, bids are slipping below last-done levels and increased ballaster supply from a weaker Pacific is weighing further on P6, while ECSA is mostly covered into early January. The Pacific remains seasonally soft, with ample prompt tonnage and limited fresh cargoes keeping sentiment firmly charterer-led. Overall, despite the current weakness, owner behaviour and forward signals suggest the market is moving closer to a base rather than facing a materially deeper correction.
