## Market Commentary

The Panamax market eased this week, with both basins showing signs of topping out. In the Atlantic, the North Atlantic and U.S. Gulf rounds lost steam as fresh inquiry dried up and tonnage lists grew, leaving charterers firmly in control. Fronthaul demand was patchy at best, with most players waiting on the outcome of the upcoming U.S.–China meeting before committing. South America held steady but failed to gain momentum despite some late November interest. In the Pacific, activity was reasonable midweek but rates edged lower as owners softened ideas against a growing list and limited fresh cargo. Overall, sentiment turned cautious, with the market looking due for a correction after recent highs.
