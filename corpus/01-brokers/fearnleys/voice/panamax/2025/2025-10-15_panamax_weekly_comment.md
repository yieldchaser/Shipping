## Market Commentary

This week, the Panamax market was characterized by a distinct disconnect between a rallying paper market and a subdued physical segment, a dynamic driven by the escalating US-China tariff situation. This was evident in both basins; in the Pacific, bullish FFA sentiment failed to translate into significant physical activity as charterers remained hesitant, though the market found a floor from steady intra-regional coal and mineral demand. A similar story unfolded in the Atlantic, where a positive start to the week gave way to slow activity and a wide bid-offer spread as participants globally adopted a cautious wait-and-see approach. Despite the geopolitical headwinds, underlying fundamentals remain stable, with the South Atlantic in particular showing signs of future vessel tightness heading towards the end of the month.
