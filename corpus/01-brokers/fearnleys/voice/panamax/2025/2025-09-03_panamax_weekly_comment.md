## Market Commentary

The Panamax market has entered September on a softer note, with both the Atlantic and Pacific struggling to find momentum. In the Atlantic, enquiry has been thin, particularly from ECSA, leaving charterers with the upper hand and owners increasingly willing to discount to secure employment. Transatlantic demand has slowed, while front-haul is offering only limited support, leaving rates under pressure across the basin. The Pacific story is much the same, with an overhang of tonnage against limited Australian and NoPac demand pushing values steadily lower. Despite some ballasting to the West, oversupply remains the dominant theme, and owners are finding it challenging to resist charterers’ pressure. Overall, sentiment is weak, and the market looks set to remain under strain in the near term unless volumes pick up.
