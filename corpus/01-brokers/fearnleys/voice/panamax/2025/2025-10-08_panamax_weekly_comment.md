## Market Commentary

The Panamax market firmed as the week progressed, overcoming the early-week slowdown caused by Asian holidays. In the Atlantic, a tightening tonnage list in the North has lifted sentiment and rates, with grain fronthauls commanding a premium over quieter mineral runs, while the South Atlantic held largely steady. The Pacific also strengthened, particularly on North Pacific rounds where limited spot tonnage forced charterers seeking prompt cover to pay above last-done levels. Although overall inquiry was muted early in the week, owners have regained confidence, and with fresh demand expected to return, both basins look set to maintain a firmer tone heading into mid-October.
