## Market Commentary

The Panamax market maintained a constructive tone this week, with both basins showing firmer sentiment and a sense of underlying support. In the Atlantic, trans-Atlantic activity gained traction on improved grain demand, while fronthaul business remained limited but balanced by a tightening tonnage list in the North. ECSA values held steady with early December demand slowly building, aided by a firmer Pacific. In Asia, momentum persisted on the back of healthy enquiry ex-Australia and Indonesia, a shorter tonnage list, and continued period interest lending further confidence. Paper markets have also been trending higher, reflecting a generally optimistic outlook as owners retained the initiative heading into the second half of November.
