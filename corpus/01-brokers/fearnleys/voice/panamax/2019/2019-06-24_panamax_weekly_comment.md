## Market Commentary

A positive week for rates in both hemispheres, mainly driven by a strong transatlantic market. A TA round voyage currently pays owners about USD 9,000 per day, which means rates have increased by more than 20 per cent since last Wednesday. A short fronthaul from the continent yields about USD 17,000. In the east, a Pacific round voyage pays around the USD 9,000's. The BPI 4TC-index is currently at 1191 points, up 81 points from last week.
