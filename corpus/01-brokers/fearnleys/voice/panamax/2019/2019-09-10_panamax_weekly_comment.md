## Market Commentary

The market has clearly taken a breather in the past week, seen from the softening of rates in both basins. In the Atlantic, few fresh cargoes have entered the market, resulting in a slightly more advantageous position for the charterers. The transatlantic round voyages have therefore traded down from around USD 20,000 to about USD 18,500 per day this week. A fronthaul from the Continent currently yields about USD 29,000. In the East, a Pacific round voyage pays around the USD 15,000-mark. The BPI 4TC-index is currently at 2,144 points, down 117 points from last week.
