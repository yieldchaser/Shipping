## Market Commentary

After ending last week on a negative trend and holidays in Greece on Monday, the trend has continued to be soft. The ECSA market was holding up end of the week, but we now see this market also loosing ground on the rates. The bid/offer gap has widened and fixtures are being done at below last done. Fhauls are currently paying low USD 16k. TA’s are mid week seeing some slight increase in rates due to less prompt tonnage. The Pacific rounds are continuing to slide and currently trading ard USD 9k.
