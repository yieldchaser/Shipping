## Market Commentary

A slow start to the week with few fresh fixtures reported, however the little information emerged has shown a market that remains firm in both hemispheres. Grains from East Coast South America has remained firm, as efficient 82,000dwt vessels have fixed around USD 19.000 per day, with delivery India and Singapore, or USD 19,000 + 900,000 with delivery APS. With few fixtures in the Atlantic, a Panamax which fixed a Bauxite-run from Jord Lasfar via Kamsar to Aughinish at USD 20.500 kept the market at similar levels to last week, as regular transatlantic round voyages pays owners around USD 20,000. A fronthaul from the continent now pays about USD 29,000. In the east, a Pacific round voyage pays above the USD 14,500-mark. The BPI 4TC-index is currently at 2163 points, up 74 points from last week.
