## Market Commentary

With the rates continuing upwards, the Panamax index has climbed an additional 294 points since last Wednesday, currently at 2085 points. Vale is back in the market, and a strong iron ore price combined with high Chinese demand is giving rates a solid push. Several strong fixtures have been concluded, with Kamsarmaxes being paid over USD 30,000 for fronthauls to China, and ECSA to Far East are closing in on the USD 18,000 per day + 800,000 GBB mark for similar vessels. A transatlantic round voyage for regular Panamaxes now yields ahead of USD 20,500 per day, and a fronthaul from the Continent pays shipowners in the low USD 27,000's. In the East, a Pacific round voyage currently pays around mid USD 13,000's per day.
