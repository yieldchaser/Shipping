## Capesize/Newcastlemax

The model below consists of factors like the global ballast/laden vessel ratio, West of Singapore ballasters, and a couple of other AIS indicators. Below the charts are tables showing where the model sits currently. It is only at extremes that these models give very clear suggestions about the near-term outlook.

The models are now in neutral territory, and thus do not give a clear suggestion for the near-term. However, as written last week, the bullish signals are "live" through the third week of November. Therefore, a rising market still seems more likely than a falling market.

In the last 6 weeks, total South Atlantic shipment volumes were up 4.7%% compared to the corresponding weeks last year, which is an improvement on the last weeks' growth rate. Brazil iron ore exports growth has been negative for six straight weeks, but Guinea bauxite exports increased last week, which explains most of the relative improvement in total South Atlantic flows growth.

## Panamax/Kamsarmax

Shipment volume growth continues to be lower than DWT supply growth, with a slight worsening seen over the last few weeks. Through week 43, growth was 2.9%, compared to DWT supply growth of 4.7%. Over the last 10 weeks, volume growth was 2.3% compared to the same period last year.

The average seasonal pattern suggests the market will trend down into Q1 now. How sharp this drop will be depends on fundamentals.

Near-term, the indicators below suggest a market bounce is overdue.

**Top left chart:** Shipment volumes were up 2.8% year on year through week 41. In the last ten weeks, volumes were down +0.3% compared to the same period last year. Supply growth was 4.2% at the latest reading.

**Top right chart:** The 2-month lead of the coal futures curve suggest the market will entered a topping phase now.

**Bottom left chart:** The market has corrected in line with what the indicator predicted. It now suggests a rebound is imminent.

**Bottom right chart:** Big divergence between P6 and this indicator over the last weeks, the biggest seen this year by far, as the Pacific indicator "won" in this respect... The indicator suggests the market will bounce now.

## Supramax/Ultramax

Through week 43, shipment volume growth was 3.4% year-on-year, whereas over the last 10 weeks, growth was 3.2% compared to the same period last year. DWT supply growth continues at 5.5% year-on-year.

The ballast/laden vessel reading remains at a level which suggests further upside is limited in the near term. The reading now is comparable to July this year and September/October/November last year. Further, the spread over the Kamsarmax market is close to the highest seen since S11TC index inception.

**Top left chart:** Weekly shipment volumes, 2026 ytd vs 2025.

**Top right chart:** S11TC vs the Ballaster/Laden vessel ratio. A lower ratio indicates a looser market balance, and vice versa. The index and the ballast/laden ratio have diverged from the index over the last month and a half

**Bottom left chart:** The industrial metals index is composed of copper, aluminium, zinc and nickel, and weighted according to market value. The 9-month change of this index has a high correlation with the 9-month change of the Ultramax 1-year TC rate, and leads by about 3 months.

**Bottom right chart:** The 9-month change of the price of copper has a high correlation with the 9-month change of the Ultramax 1-year TC rate. The copper price change leads by about 4 months.

## Handysize

Through week 43, shipment volumes grew by an estimated 0.7% compared to last year. DWT supply growth remains at 3.2%. The market remains firmer than the weak headline figures suggest. In the near term, the strength of the Ultramax segment suggests continued sideways/slightly up in this segment.

**Top left chart:** The ratio between Rio-Recalada / Skaw-Passero (HS3) and SEA / Singapore-Japan (HS5) is usually a good predictor of tops and bottoms of the HS3 route. A higher ratio suggests a market top, and a lower ratio suggests a market bottom.

**Top right chart:** Through week 41, Handysize shipment volumes were 0.7% higher year on year, declining from the 3%+ figures seen in the last months. DWT supply growth was 3.2% at the latest reading.

**Bottom left chart:** The correlation between the S11TC and HS7TC is over 90%. The correlation is even higher when the S11TC index is shifted forward a few days, which means that movements in the S11TC index often lead the movements of the HS7TC.

**Bottom right chart:** The change in the price of copper is usually an accurate leading indicator for asset values.
