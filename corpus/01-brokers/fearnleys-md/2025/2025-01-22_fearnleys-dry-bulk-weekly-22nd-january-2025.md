## Capesize/Newcastlemax

The count of VLOCs sailing toward Brazil and the ballaster/laden vessel ratio suggest a bottom (and subsequent rally) is approaching, but it's probably still a few weeks away. Iron ore volumes in the pacific is lower than last year, as China's inventories remain relatively high, and consumption is still in the low season. However, on the latter point, imported iron ore consumption in China ticked up last week for the first time in two months.

## Panamax/Kamsarmax

The market remains weak and as written in our previous reports this is not likely to change before mid or late February. Activity in pacific coal markets should increase after the Chinese New Year, but there are concerns about fundamentals as inventories in China are high. 
Argentinian and Brazilian Soybean volumes are forecasted to be higher than last year, but the harvest is progressing slower than usual.

## Supramax/Ultramax

The market remains weak and is likely to remain so until the middle of February. Coal trades in the Pacific are very slow, and the South American grain season is progressing slower than last year. 
Basis average seasonality, the upturn will begin during the second week of February.
