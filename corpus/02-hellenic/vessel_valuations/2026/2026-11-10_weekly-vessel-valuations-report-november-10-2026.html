Tankers

Tanker values held broadly stable. Suezmax values edged lower across all age bands as softer earnings and lengthening Atlantic tonnage weighed, while MR2 values firmed, with product tanker earnings holding up better than crude.

Suezmax Fleves (150,000 DWT, Feb 2012, Hyundai Heavy Ind Ulsan Shipyard) sold to BGN for USD 110.0 mil (SS/DD due), VV Value USD 113.68 mil.

LR2 Haima (105,000 DWT, Mar 2009, Mitsui Tamano) sold by Asyad Shipping for USD 65.0 mil, VV Value USD 61.25 mil.

MR2 Mayfair (50,000 DWT, Dec 2008, Hyundai Mipo) sold by Seven Islands Shipping for USD 27.0 mil, VV Value USD 25.51 mil.

Containers

Container values remained broadly stable, with strong buying enquiry across sizes and ages. Only value movement was to be found in Handy and Sub Panamax sizes as the mid-age vessels continue to firm.

No notable sales this week.
