Tankers

Crude Tanker values firmed further this week, extending last week’s surge as continued regional disruption around the Strait of Hormuz and the Middle East kept earnings at exceptional levels and buyers hungry for crude tonnage. The restart of Saudi Arabia’s East-West pipeline following the earlier attack began redirecting crude flows, and by the extension of the US-China port fee truce, removed a near-term cost overhang for Chinese-linked tonnage.

VLCC Atherina (319,500 DWT, Jan 2011, Hyundai Heavy Industries Ulsan) sold to MSC for USD 146.0 mil, VV Value USD 146.78 mil.

Suezmax Poros (150,200 DWT, Feb 2008, Universal) sold for USD 80.0 mil, VV Value USD 74.59 mil.

Suezmax Nordic Freedom (159,500 DWT, Mar 2005, Daewoo) sold by Nordic American Tankers for USD 65.0 mil, VV Value USD 66.29 mil.

MR2 Pacific Onyx (50,000 DWT, Mar 2013, Onomichi) sold DD passed to Asian buyers for USD 35.0 mil, VV Value USD 30.53 mil.

Containers

Container values firmed for smaller tonnage and otherwise held broadly stable this week. The secondhand market continues to see plenty of buyers but a shortage of available ships, with owners in the 2,000 – 3,000 TEU segment remaining bullish.

No notable sales this week.
