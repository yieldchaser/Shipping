Bulkers

Values firmed for older tonnage across the Bulker market this week.

Capesize Ever Ability (180,100 DWT, Oct 2011, Qingdao Beihai Shipbuilding Heavy Industry) sold to H Line Shipping for USD 39.7 mil (SS/DD passed), VV Value USD 36.44 mil.

Kamsarmax Taho Australia (81,900 DWT, Oct 2019, Oshima) sold by Ta Ho Maritime for USD 38.0 mil, VV Value USD 38.42 mil.

Supramax Tony Smith (57,000 DWT, Mar 2011, Qingshan Shipyard) sold by Allseas Marine for USD 15.5 mil, VV Value USD 14.85 mil.

Handysize Danae (40,000 DWT, Sep 2022, Shin Kochi) sold by KN Maritime for USD 34.5 mil, VV Value USD 35.41 mil.

Tankers

Crude tanker values edged lower as spot earnings corrected from October’s exceptional peak, with sentiment shaped by hopes of de-escalation in the Middle East and Ukraine. Attacks on shipping in the Gulf, Red Sea and Black Sea nevertheless continue, keeping these regions high-risk, and Brent remains well below October’s average as Hormuz flows recover. MR2 values edged higher as secondhand prices held firm.

MR2 Pyxis Karteria (46,700 DWT, Mar 2013, Hyundai Mipo) sold by Pyxis Tankers for USD 37.0 mil (DD passed), VV Value USD 35.41 mil.

MR2 Tokyo (46,500 DWT, Jul 2009, Naikai Setoda) sold to Velos Tankers for USD 27.5 mil, VV Value USD 23.38 mil.

Containers

Handysize and Sub Panamax values edged higher as the container S&P market stayed active, with a shortage of vessels for sale keeping buyers focused on older tonnage and Chinese built resales.

Handysize Hull 357 and Hull 358 (1,800 TEU, Feb and Mar 2027, Huanghai Shipbuilding) sold en bloc by Cosmoship for USD 42.0 mil each, VV Value USD 40.78 mil and USD 40.49 mil respectively.
