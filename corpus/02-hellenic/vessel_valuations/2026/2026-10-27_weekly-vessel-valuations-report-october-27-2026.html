Bulkers

Handysize values firmed, with Union Taylor selling at a premium as buyers continued to pay firm prices for modern, high-specification tonnage.

Ultramax Libra Confidence (63,500 DWT, 2019, New Dayang Shipbuilding) sold to Asia Star for USD 33.5 mil, VV Value USD 32.55 mil.

Ultramax Diamond Queen (61,400 DWT, 2013, Shin Kasado Dock) sold by Taiyo Kaiun Kaisha for USD 24.0 mil, VV Value USD 23.89 mil.

Supramax Ever Progress (56,600 DWT, 2012, Jiangsu Hantong Ship Heavy Industry) sold by Chun An Shipping for USD 16.2 mil, VV Value USD 17.00 mil.

Supramax Saltwind Explorer (55,600 DWT, 2011, Hyundai Vietnam Shipbuilding) sold to Transworld Shipping Lines for USD 16.5 mil, VV Value USD 16.49 mil.

Handysize Union Taylor (37,700 DWT, 2014, Shimanami) sold by Union Commercial for USD 22.0 mil, VV Value USD 20.30 mil.

Tankers

Crude tanker values rose again, led by Suezmax and Aframax, as prompt tonnage commanded a premium and Middle Eastern buyers paid top prices. Strong earnings, with Hormuz disruption continuing to support employment for older tonnage, also underpinned values.

Suezmax Zouzou N (150,000 DWT, 2010, New Times Shipbuilding) sold by Dynacom Tankers for USD 103.0 mil, VV Value USD 98.89 mil.

LR2 Afra Mare (105,300 DWT, 2008, Hyundai Heavy Industries Ulsan) sold to Chinese buyers for USD 59.0 mil, VV Value USD 69.33 mil.

LR1 Kamome Victoria (74,900 DWT, 2011, Minami Nippon) sold to Velos Tankers for USD 39.5 mil, VV Value USD 42.71 mil.

Panamax Asean Forwarder (74,600 DWT, 2006, Sasebo) sold by Erato Shipping for USD 26.8 mil, VV Value USD 25.63 mil.

MR2 Prive Angel (51,200 DWT, 2008, STX Offshore) sold by Prive Overseas Marine for USD 24.5 mil, VV Value USD 23.05 mil.

Containers

Post Panamax values moved higher, while Sub Panamax edged up after the Evridiki G sale. Buying interest continued to exceed the available tonnage.

Sub Panamax Evridiki G (2,556 TEU, 2001, Hyundai Heavy Industries Ulsan) sold by Euroseas Shipping to MSC for USD 26.0 mil, VV Value USD 24.21 mil.
