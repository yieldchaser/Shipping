Bulkers

Handysize and older Supramax values moved higher as firmer freight rates fed into the S&P market, with several deals concluded above VV Market Value

Capesize Nightsky (183,000 DWT, Nov 2019, Namura) sold to Seanergy Maritime Corp for USD 73.0 mil, VV Value USD 71.92 mil.

Post Panamax Indus Triumph (93,000 DWT, Nov 2012, Taizhou Sanfu Shipbuilding) sold by MSPL Diamond for USD 16.6 mil, VV Value USD 17.06 mil.

Supramax SW Mistral I (58,100 DWT, May 2011, Tsuneishi Zhoushan) sold to ADNOC Logistics and Services for USD 20.0 mil, VV Value USD 18.57 mil.

Handysize Obe Dinares (33,400 DWT, Mar 2008, Shin Kochi) sold by OBE Ships Maritime for USD 12.4 mil, VV Value USD 11.32 mil.

Tankers

Older Aframax values moved lower as spot earnings dropped sharply on ample Atlantic tonnage, while Suezmax values edged lower across all age bands and VLCC and MR2 values held broadly stable.

VLCC Rising Sun 1 (297,400 DWT, Apr 2005, Dalian New Yard) sold by Beyond Shipmanagement for USD 127.0 mil, VV Value USD 107.17 mil.

Suezmax Sponde (158,500 DWT, Jul 2012, Samsung) sold to ADNOC Logistics and Services for USD 110.0 mil, VV Value USD 114.84 mil.

LR2 Raffles Harmony (105,400 DWT, Jan 2013, Hyundai Heavy Ind) sold to Mercuria for USD 60.5 mil (inc TC), VV Value USD 75.06 mil.

MR2 Virgen Del Quinche (48,000 DWT, Jul 2010, Iwagi Zosen) sold by Marflet Marine to an undisclosed buyer for USD 26.0 mil, VV Value USD 26.48 mil.

MR1 Helene Maersk and Henning Maersk (38,000 DWT, Jan and Apr 2010, STX Offshore) sold en bloc by Maersk Tankers AS for USD 30.0 mil each, VV Value USD 29.48 mil and USD 30.11 mil respectively.

Containers

Older Feedermax values moved higher as the container S&P market stayed busy with lots of interest and limited vessels available.

Handysize Hull 16 & Hull 17 (2,029 TEU, 2026, Jiangsu Sentosa Marine) sold for USD 75 mil, VV Value USD 76.1 mil.

Feedermax Mirror (803 TEU, Mar 2007, Damen Galati) sold by Ernst Russ AG for USD 11.5 mil, VV Value USD 8.81 mil.
