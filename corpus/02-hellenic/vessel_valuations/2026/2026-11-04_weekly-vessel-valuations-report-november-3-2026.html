Bulkers

Supramax values firmed at the younger end of the curve, with Kristinita and Charade both selling at premiums to VV Value amid positive Asian sentiment and tight prompt tonnage. Capesize values edged higher as the earnings rally gathered pace, with Cape Alliance selling well above VV Value. Panamax and Handysize values drifted marginally lower on the older age bands as softer Panamax earnings weighed on sentiment.

Kamsarmax Panorama (81,500 DWT, Feb 2012, Hyundai Samho Heavy Ind) sold by F Maritime for USD 20.0 mil, VV Value USD 22.03 mil.

Ultramax Delmax 66 (66,600 DWT, May 2026, Tsuneishi Cebu) sold by Drydel Shipping for USD 47.0 mil, VV Value USD 46.40 mil.

Supramax Spar Gemini (53,000 DWT, Feb 2007, Chengxi Shipyard) sold by Spar Shipping AS for USD 12.4 mil, VV Value USD 11.50 mil.

Tankers

Tanker values held broadly stable, as buyers continued to transact despite historically high price levels. Tightening VLCC tonnage and strong secondhand activity continued to support sentiment, although softer Suezmax and Aframax earnings limited upward pressure.

Containers

Container values remained broadly stable, with continued buying interest for a limited number of sales candidates supporting firm prices.
