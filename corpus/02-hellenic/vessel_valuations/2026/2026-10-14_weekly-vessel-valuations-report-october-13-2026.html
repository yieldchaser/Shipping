Bulkers

Bulker values held broadly stable this week, with small softening for Supramax and the younger Panamax bands and slight gains for Handysize.

Kamsarmax Kirribilli (82,200 DWT, 2011, Tadotsu Tsuneishi) sold to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 22.75 mil, VV Value USD 21.7 mil.

Kamsarmax World Diana (81,200 DWT, 2020, Oshima) sold to Greek buyers for USD 41 mil, VV Value USD 42.1 mil.

Supramax YC Aequor (57,000 DWT, 2012, Zhejiang Zengzhou) sold for USD 13.8 mil, VV Value USD 15.6 mil.

Supramax SSI Avenger (52,900 DWT, 2004, Oshima) sold to Chinese buyers for USD 10 mil, VV Value USD 10.6 mil.

Tankers

Crude tanker values rose further as record earnings kept buyers focused on the secondhand market, with newbuilding slots now stretching into 2029 and 2030. Prompt delivery tonnage, particularly in the Middle East, commands a clear premium, and sellers are reluctant to name prices.

VLCC Vieira (307,000 DWT, Sep 2002, Hyundai Heavy Ulsan) sold by Viera Group for USD 75.0 mil, VV Value USD 85.29 mil.

Suezmax Nicolaos (164,800 DWT, Jan 2010, Hyundai Samho) sold by Andriaki Shipping for USD 90.8 mil, VV Value USD 90.66 mil.

Suezmax Tataki (150,000 DWT, Jan 2010, New Times) sold by Dynacom Tankers for USD 97.0 mil, VV Value USD 81.8 mil.

Suezmax Tianlong (159,000 DWT, Jan 2009, Bohai) sold by Dynacom Tankers for USD 88.0 mil, VV Value USD 77.33 mil.

Suezmax LXH (151,200 DWT, Apr 2004, Samsung) sold by China Overseas Energy Development Group for USD 61.5 mil, VV Value USD 67.98 mil.

MR2 Aurea Glory (48,700 DWT, May 2006, Iwagi Zosen) sold by Chartworld Shipping for USD 17.5 mil, VV Value USD 16.07 mil.

Containers

Container values were broadly stable overall, with gains concentrated in the older Sub Panamax and Handysize bands. Secondhand sentiment stayed positive on firm demand and restricted supply, with newbuilding slots stretching into 2030.

No notable sales this week.
