MMI Daily Iron Ore Index Report November 25 2026

Hellenic Shipping News  |  25 November 2026  |  https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/mmi-daily-iron-ore-index-report-november-25-2026

Iron ore futures, most Qingdao port spot products and seaborne quotes pulled back slightly in tandem today. DCE most-traded closedat 657.0 yuan/mt, down 0.23% from Nov 24. Seven of the eight Qingdao spot products softened while one rose: Newman Lump dropped 20 yuan to 778 yuan/wmt, Carajas Fines fell 6 yuan to 780 yuan/wmt, and PB Fines, Mac Fines, BRBF, SSF and PB Lump each slipped 3 yuan, whereas Newman Fines rose 3 yuan to 636 yuan/wmt. Port 61% and 65% indices declined to 640 and 795 yuan/wmt (-2 and -5 yuan), while the 58% port index held flat at 601 yuan/wmt. Seaborne 61% and 65% composite indices eased to USD 89.20 and 105.45/dmt (each down USD 0.10/dmt) and all 12 seaborne brands declined USD 0.05-0.20/dmt; the Friday updated Domestic Ore Composite Price Index retained its Nov 20 quote of 792.40 yuan/mt (down 1.06% from Nov 19 on that date). On the fundamental side, annual winter maintenance halts at northwest steel mills drove a further decline in hot metal output, while ocean freight extended gains. Latest surveyed daily hot metal across 242 mills fell to 2.3359 million mt on Nov 25 (down 12,000 mt WoW, -0.51%), while the BF operating rate edged up 0.23 percentage point to 87.09%. On Nov 24, C5 freight rose another 3.22% to USD 15.69/mt and C3 gained 0.58% to USD 34.98/mt. In the week to Nov 20, global iron ore shipments stood at 35.2588 million mt (+10.88% WoW) and Chinese port arrivals at 27.0447 million mt (+1.04% WoW); total ten-port inventory was 109.2582 million mt on Nov 19 (up 1.3027 million mt, +1.21% WoW). Iron ore prices may trade within a narrow range in the short term. Consecutive freight gains and baseline blast furnace demand continue to provide floor support, but declining hot metal output amid northwest mill winter halts, high overseas shipments and elevated port inventory keep upside room constrained. The scope of winter maintenance and the pace of winter raw material restocking remain key to track; under a relatively loose supply demand balance, ore prices may stay range-bound.
