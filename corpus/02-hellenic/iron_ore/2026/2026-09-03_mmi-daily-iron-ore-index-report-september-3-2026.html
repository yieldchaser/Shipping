MMI Daily Iron Ore Index Report September 3 2026

Hellenic Shipping News  |  03 September 2026  |  https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/mmi-daily-iron-ore-index-report-september-3-2026

Iron ore futures traded firmer on Wednesday. The DCE most-traded I2701 contract settled at 726.5 yuan/mt, up 1.32% fromthe previous session. Qingdao port spot prices rose by an average of 12–14 yuan/mt, with traders showing only moderate enthusiasm in offering and mills buying on an as-needed basis. Overall spot trading activity was lacklustre. Current market structure points to signs of a structural inflection in iron ore demand, with mills favouring cost-effective fines over higher-premium pellet and lump. SMM survey data on major port inventories showed fines destocking for a second consecutive week, while destocking in concentrate and lump slowed and pellet stocks edged higher. Total sample inventories stood at 104.82 million mt, down 20,000 mt week-on-week. Meanwhile, with coke prices continuing to climb, mills in some regions have begun considering output cuts on tight coke supply and adjusting production schedules accordingly, which could weigh on near-term iron ore demand. Overall, gains in iron ore prices are likely to slow, with the market shifting into a range-bound pattern in the near term.
