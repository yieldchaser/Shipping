MMI Daily Iron Ore Index Report November 13 2026

Hellenic Shipping News  |  13 November 2026  |  https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/mmi-daily-iron-ore-index-report-november-13-2026

Iron ore showed selective price recovery today. The seaborne 61% and 65% indices rose to USD 90.35 and106.70/dmt, all 12 brands gained, and the Domestic Ore Composite Index reached 800.72 yuan/mt. However, DCE most-traded closed at 669.5 yuan/mt, down 0.22% from the previous close; the 61% and 58% port indices each fell 1 yuan while the 65% held steady. Improving quotations have not become synchronized market-wide strength. Physical balances remain central to sustainability. Latest surveyed daily hot metal increased 5,600 mt WoW, offering limited consumption support, while ten-port stocks rose 3.2766 million mt, mainly in fines and lump. Earlier higher arrivals still require mill purchases and port outbound volume to absorb them; higher seaborne quotes do not establish destocking. A cautious, range-bound near-term view remains appropriate. Recovering seaborne and domestic prices and broadly stable port products help ease pressure, while inventory accumulation and higher freight constrain sustained gains. Better orders and margins accompanied by steady restocking could strengthen support; mainly needs-based purchasing could leave prices fluctuating with stocks, trading and resource composition.
