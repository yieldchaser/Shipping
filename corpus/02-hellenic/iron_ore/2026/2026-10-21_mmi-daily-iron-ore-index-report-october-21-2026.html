MMI Daily Iron Ore Index Report October 21 2026

Hellenic Shipping News  |  21 October 2026  |  https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/mmi-daily-iron-ore-index-report-october-21-2026

Demand showed marginal improvement, but prices did not move consistently higher. Surveyed daily hot metal output rose to 2.3584 million mt on Oct 21, up6,400 mt, and the blast furnace operating rate increased to 87.16%. The 61%, 58% and 65% port indices each rose 1 yuan and DCE most-traded held at 681.5 yuan/mt. In contrast, the seaborne 61% and 65% indices eased to USD 90.40 and 107.40/dmt, with all 12 brands lower, reflecting different assessments of demand and resource availability across markets. Higher hot metal may increase baseline consumption, but sustained restocking still depends on raw-material stocks, finished-steel orders and mill profitability. Lower recent arrivals help ease pressure from incoming flows, while existing port stocks require actual collections for absorption. Meanwhile, higher C3 and C5 freight may alter cost conditions for some cargoes; ore prices and transport costs do not always move together. The near-term focus is whether improved production translates into persistent transactions. Buying that merely follows current consumption could leave prices range-bound, while stronger restocking and orders would provide firmer downside support. A single increase in hot metal does not establish a trend reversal; trading, production and inventories should be assessed together.
