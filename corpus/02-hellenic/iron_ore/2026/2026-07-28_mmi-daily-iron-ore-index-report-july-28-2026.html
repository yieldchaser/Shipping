MMI Daily Iron Ore Index Report July 28 2026

Hellenic Shipping News  |  28 July 2026  |  https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/mmi-daily-iron-ore-index-report-july-28-2026

Today, iron ore prices experienced narrow fluctuations on the futures market. The DCE main contract I2609 closed at 741 yuan/ton, down0.20% from the previous trading session. Spot prices at Qingdao Port remained unchanged from the previous day. Traders showed generally average enthusiasm for quoting, while steel mills purchased on a need-to-use basis, resulting in moderate overall spot transaction activity. At present, the fundamentals of iron ore itself show little change, with demand entering a plateau phase. According to SMM statistics, the blast furnace maintenance this week has reduced hot metal output by 1.5678 million tons, an increase of 140,200 tons compared to last week’s maintenance impact. Next week, the maintenance impact is expected to decrease by 207,500 tons week-on-week. On the supply side, as domestic mines gradually resume production, iron concentrate supply is expected to return to normal levels, and the concentrate premium may edge down slightly. In addition, the news front remains in a vacuum period, and the main contract is in its rollover phase, with no clear directional guidance for the near-term outlook. Market participants are closely watching the progress of negotiations and maintaining a strong wait-and-see attitude. Therefore, in the short term, iron ore prices are likely to continue their narrow range-bound oscillation.
