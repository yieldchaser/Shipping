MMI Daily Iron Ore Index Report October 14 2026

Hellenic Shipping News  |  14 October 2026  |  https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/mmi-daily-iron-ore-index-report-october-14-2026

Domestic prices remained under pressure while some seaborne brand quotations stabilised. DCE mosttraded closed at 674.0 yuan/mt, down 0.44%, and all eight Qingdao products fell 2 yuan/wmt. The 61%, 58% and 65% port indices eased to 656, 617 and 820 yuan/wmt. The seaborne 61% index held at USD 89.50/dmt and the 65% fell to 108.35. Meanwhile, nine of the 12 brand quotations rose and three were unchanged, showing that composite indices and individual brands do not always move together in the short term. Marginal changes in demand warrant attention. Surveyed daily hot metal output was 2.3520 million mt on Oct 14, down 1,200 mt from Oct 7: the decline narrowed but output had not recovered. The second coke cut and lower freight may ease costs, yet additional mill buying still depends on orders and margin repair. Average import trading margins narrowed to 8.40 yuan/mt, also showing that lower costs may not fully offset selling-price pressure. Firmer offers should be distinguished from a recovery in physical demand. Without sustained improvement in port transactions and mill restocking, strength in some seaborne brands may struggle to lift the broader market. Stabilising hot metal and stronger buying could improve downside support. A soft, range-bound outlook remains appropriate while watching for aligned improvement in trading, production and inventories.
