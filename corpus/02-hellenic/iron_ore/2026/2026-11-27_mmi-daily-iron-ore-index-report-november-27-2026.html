MMI Daily Iron Ore Index Report November 27 2026

Hellenic Shipping News  |  27 November 2026  |  https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/mmi-daily-iron-ore-index-report-november-27-2026

Iron ore futures retreated after early gains today, while most Qingdao port spot products, seaborne quotes and the domestic ore compositeindex weakened in tandem. DCE most-traded closed at 659.5 yuan/mt, down 0.53% from Nov 26. Six of the eight Qingdao spot products declined while two held flat: PB Fines, Newman Fines, Newman Lump, SSF, Carajas Fines and PB Lump each fell 3 yuan/wmt, whereas Mac Fines (638) and BRBF (688 yuan/wmt) were unchanged. Port 61%, 58% and 65% indices dropped to 639, 602 and 790 yuan/wmt (-3, -1 and -2 yuan). Seaborne 61% and 65% composite indices fell to USD 88.90 and 105.55/dmt (-1.00 and -0.20/dmt) and all 12 seaborne brands declined USD 1.00-1.35/dmt; updated on Friday, the SMM Domestic Ore Composite Price Index fell to 788.29 yuan/mt on Nov 27, down 3.95 yuan (-0.50%) from Nov 26. On the fundamental side, port inventories posted a modest phased drawdown and ocean freight stayed elevated, while lower hot metal output and high prior overseas shipments posed forward pressure. On Nov 27, 35-port iron ore inventory edged down to 153.45 million mt (down 300,000 mt WoW, -0.20%) as daily port outbound volume rose to 3.025 million mt (up 25,000 mt WoW, +0.83%); ten-port total inventory stood at 107.4695 million mt on Nov 26 (down 1.7887 million mt WoW, -1.64%). On Nov 26, C5 freight edged up 0.43% to USD 16.17/mt and C3 ticked up 0.06% to USD 35.20/mt. On the demand side, surveyed daily hot metal across 242 mills was 2.3359 million mt on Nov 25 (down 12,000 mt WoW) with the BF operating rate at 87.09%; on the supply side, global iron ore shipments stood at 35.2588 million mt (+10.88% WoW) and Chinese port arrivals at 27.0447 million mt (+1.04% WoW) in the week to Nov 20. Iron ore prices may continue to trade within a narrow range in the short term. Modest port inventory drawdowns, firmer port outbound volume and winter restocking expectations provide floor support, but declining hot metal output amid northern production controls and winter maintenance, expected arrivals from prior high overseas shipments and macro headwinds continue to cap upside room. Port arrival pace, hot metal declines and winter raw material restocking progress remain key to track.
