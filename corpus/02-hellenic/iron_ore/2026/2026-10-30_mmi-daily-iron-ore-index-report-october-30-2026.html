MMI Daily Iron Ore Index Report October 30 2026

Hellenic Shipping News  |  30 October 2026  |  https://www.hellenicshippingnews.com/mmi-daily-iron-ore-index-report-october-30-2026

The earlier recovery did not persist, with futures, port prices and seaborne quotations weakening together today. DCE most-traded fell 1.47%to 672.0 yuan/mt, while all eight Qingdao products declined 10 yuan/wmt. The 61%, 58% and 65% port indices were 650, 602 and 812 yuan/wmt; the seaborne 61% and 65% indices were USD 90.50 and 106.75/dmt. All 12 brands fell, showing that the rebound still lacks durable price support.

Production and inventories offer some marginal support. Latest surveyed hot metal increased for a second period and ten-port stocks fell 618,100 mt from the preceding survey. However, higher production is not equivalent to discretionary restocking, and inventories also reflect arrivals and unloading schedules. Actual buying still needs support from orders, profitability and stock management rather than isolated improvements establishing stronger aggregate demand.

Near-term attention should focus on the balance between higher costs and selling support. Rising C3 and C5 freight may reduce cost advantages for some cargoes. Needs-based mill buying could constrain a recovery, while stronger orders and profitability followed by persistent transactions would offer firmer downside support. A cautious, range-bound view remains appropriate rather than extrapolating a single price move into a trend change.
