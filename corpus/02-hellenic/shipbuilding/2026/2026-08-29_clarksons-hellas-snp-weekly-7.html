We are currently going through the third distinct phase of Middle East exports since the Hormuz crisis began.

After suffering an immediate loss of around 15 mb/d of export capacity in the spring months and then experiencing the short-lived reopening during the second half of July, we are now in a phase of increased tanker shuttling, which has become very prominent since early August with volumes estimated to have reached ~4.5 mb/d.

Middle Eastern producers, along with a handful of risk-tolerant tanker operators, have moved cargoes through the Strait of Hormuz to Ship-To-Ship hubs off Oman and Fujairah, where the volumes are transferred onto other tankers for onward delivery. The strategy has been more effective at getting stranded cargoes back into the market, even as attacks on vessels transiting Hormuz continue, as exporters are now assuming a greater share of the transit risk. All signs indicate a higher risk tolerance from producers, with UAE’s biggest crude exporters seeking pathways of bringing cargoes to market, relying on Ship-To-Ship shuttles to move trapped cargo.

Notably, average VLCC spot rates have climbed to their highest levels since the start of the US-Iran conflict, with modern vessels earning TCEs of USD 800,000 per day on voyages through the SoH. In terms of enquiries, we are observing increased interest in the larger tanker segments spanning Aframax upto VLCC, with units that can give prompt delivery being at the forefront of attention.

At the same time vintage tonnage is experiencing a notable revival in demand.

The Bulker sector continues to advance through the second half of the year on a solid footing, with fleet growth of 3.5–4% on track to be achieved this year, although the repercussions of the Black Sea and Middle East remain uncertain. This week ended on a positive note for the dry spot market, with the Capesize BDI closing at 5,536, up ~17% from last week’s close while we monitored notable activity in the Supramax and Ultramax segments, with enquiries primarily focused on units around 15 years of age.
