# Product Highlights

Iran Tensions Report

The new **Iran Tensions Report** on the Signal Ocean Platform provides a real-time view of shifting vessel activity in and around the Arabian Gulf. It includes traceable cargo flows, key chokepoint transits, supply and congestion trends, and market indicators like spot rates and bunker prices, giving you a clear picture of regional disruption, all in one place.

Signal Ocean data is now in Snowflake

Signal Ocean datasets are now available directly in **Snowflake**, enabling seamless integration, lightning-fast queries, and zero infrastructure hassle. Whether you're running vessel analytics or powering dashboards, you can access high-quality maritime data at scale, right where your analytics already live.

# Geopolitical Spillover: Dry Bulk

This report analyses how rising Iran-Israel tensions may affect dry bulk shipping, concluding that while oil flows face risk, dry bulk trade through the Strait of Hormuz remains largely resilient, with minimal market disruption expected under most scenarios.

# US Corn Trade Flows

U.S. corn exports are on the rise, boosting vessel demand and offsetting weaker soybean trade. With steady demand from Mexico and potential growth in Vietnam, U.S. corn could see continued export momentum in 2025 and beyond.

# Soybean Trade Flows

China's pivot away from U.S. soybean imports amid renewed tariffs is boosting South American exporters and shifting vessel demand from U.S. ports to Brazil and Argentina—reshaping Panamax and Supramax activity in global dry bulk.
