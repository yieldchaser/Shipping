# Product Highlights

*Published on 01 January 2026*

Discover the latest platform updates, including Baltic Rates integration in the Supply Trend chart, the new Group By functionality in Vessel List, the Vessel Benchmark Dashboard for Dry clients, and major accuracy improvements to Minor Bulk cargo estimation - plus key highlights on the evolving dynamics of the Dry and Tanker markets.


# Product Highlights

What shipped on the platform this month

Minor Bulk Cargo Estimation Model Upgrade

A major accuracy improvement to cargo estimation model for minor bulk commodities, powered by new lineup data and refined geofencing. Particularly impactful for Chrome Ore, Nickel Ore, Bauxite, Steel, and Colombian Coal.

Baltic Rates in Supply Trend

Overlay Baltic Rate data on the Supply Trend chart by selecting a route - spot correlations between vessel supply and freight rates in one unified view.

Group By in Vessel List

Organise your vessel list into collapsible swimlanes by zone, voyage state, pushed/market status, or commercial operator - faster workflows without juggling filters.

Vessel Benchmark Dashboard

Evaluate vessel speed and consumption against sister vessels and the Baltic Vessel, with sea margin, dry-dock history, and Signal-estimated consumptions. Two reports for comprehensive benchmarking.
