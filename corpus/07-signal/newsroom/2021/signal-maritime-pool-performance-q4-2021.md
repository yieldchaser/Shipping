# Signal Maritime | Our Story of the Month - December 2021

*Published on 03 December 2021*

## Signal Maritime Aframax Pool: Q4 2021 Performance Review


## Investing in Fleet Repositioning

Following detailed analysis on seasonality, covid effect and vessels’ supply-demand earlier in the year, [we](https://www.thesignalgroup.com) took a strategic decision to reposition a considerable part of the Signal Maritime Aframax Pool (SMAP) fleet towards West and specifically USG. From August to October, the Chartering team was working consistently towards the direction of repositioning vessels from East to West, increasing the fleet’s position value center-of-mass and getting ready for a West market jump in Q4 2021. This has been a bold move that would short-term affect performance but generate significant over-performance by the end of year.


## Implementing the Backhaul Strategy...

Finding the right cargoes and market levels to implement the strategy with the lowest possible hit on performance was not easy. Two highlights of the reposition strategy are the sequential backhauls of Chrysanthemum and Wonder Vega.


## … and Measuring the Impact

In the second decade of October, the West markets, with US being the flagship, were firming. Having a considerable West exposure resulted in strong fronthauls that boosted our [pool](https://www.thesignalgroup.com/signal-maritime#smartpool) partners’ earnings. At the same time we kept a sufficient local presence through x-USG voyages.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### What Is the Signal Maritime Aframax Pool (Smap)?

The Signal Maritime Aframax Pool (SMAP) is a commercially managed pool of Aframax tankers operated by Signal Maritime. Pool partners benefit from optimised voyage scheduling, market intelligence, and collective earnings that outperform single-vessel trading.


### How Did Fleet Repositioning Improve Q4 2021 Earnings?

By systematically moving vessels from East to West — specifically toward the US Gulf — between August and October 2021, SMAP built a strong West-of-Suez position ahead of seasonal market firming. When USG rates jumped in Q4, the fleet captured high-value fronthaul voyages that generated material earnings overperformance versus benchmark.


### What Is a Backhaul Strategy in Aframax Trading?

A backhaul is a cargo carried in the return direction of a vessel's primary trade lane, typically at lower rates. In SMAP's Q4 2021 strategy, sequential backhauls on vessels like Chrysanthemum and Wonder Vega were used to reposition the fleet westward at minimal cost, setting up higher-value fronthaul opportunities.

Signal Maritime applies analysis of seasonality patterns, supply-demand dynamics, and macroeconomic factors — including covid-related disruptions — to make strategic positioning calls ahead of the market.
