## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

Another week of significant activity, saw sixteen (16) units changing hands. Notably three (3) 
ultramax deals setting new benchmarks: buyers spent $22 mill for eco type Crown 63 blt 2014 named 
HAKO; in a Japanese style bid, similarly aged Japanese built SANTA MARIA (61k blt 2014 
Imabari/Japan, eco type m/e) went for slightly less (region low $ 21 mill) but basis forward delivery 
between April - June next year. In an off-market deal, GRACE HARMONY (60k blt 2015 
Onomichi/Japan) was committed for approximately $24 mill. Additionally, a rare animal, geared 
panamax bulker JAL KUMUD (76k blt 2008 Imabari/Japan Cr 4x30t) was acquired by Chinese 
interests for just over $13 mill, establishing a new market benchmark.

### Demolition

All markets are stable (more or less) with NO reported sales in Subcontinent this week.
