## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

One more very busy s+p week with supramax sales dominating the deals, having recorded 
nine (9) units changing hands; we understand that ten (10) eager buyers competed for the Cebu built 
2013 Tess 58 named "KN Future" which was finally sold for the strong level of either $18.3 or $18.75 
mill. One more Cebu 2011 built Tess 58 named "Eternity SW" went for the softer price of $15 mill to 
Chinese interests. In a private transaction, Vietnamese splurged $17 mill for "Super Saka" (55k built 
2011 Mitsui/Japan).
