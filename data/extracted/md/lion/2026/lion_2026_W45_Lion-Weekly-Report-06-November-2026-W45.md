## Market Commentary

Sale & Purchase: The second-hand market stayed busy, with forty-eight vessels reported sold across all 
sectors and values holding firm, particularly for prompt, charter-free tankers. 
In the bulker sector, the 2007-built Setouchi-max CAPE ALLIANCE (206,190 dwt, Imabari) changed hands for 
region USD 35 - 36.5 mill, against the USD 23.5 mill paid for the two-year-older CAPE MERLIN in December 
2025, showing how far values for older large tonnage have moved. The 2012-built Kamsarmax PANORAMA 
(81,504 dwt, Hyundai Samho) was sold for region USD 19.8 - 20.5 mill basis delivery in January 2027 with 
surveys due, compared with USD 22.5 mill for her one-year-younger sister DAEBO GLADSTONE in September. 
Bulgarian buyers paid USD 47 mill for the brand new, scrubber-fitted Ultramax DELMAX66 (66,565 dwt, 2026 
Tsuneishi Cebu). In the Supramax segment, clients of ADNOC acquired the Japanese-built BAHAMAS L (56,141 
dwt, 2012 Mitsui) for USD 22.25 mill, while the CHARADE (58,109 dwt, 2013 Tsuneishi Cebu) fetched region 
USD 22.5 - 22.8 mill. The scrubber-fitted MIAO ZHOU (58,595 dwt, 2013 Kawasaki) was sold for region excess 
USD 23 mill and the SW MISTRAL I (58,110 dwt, 2011 Tsuneishi Zhoushan) for USD 20 mill. Among the 
Handysizes, Greek buyers paid USD 21.5 mill for the 2016-built ASTRO CASTOR (37,347 dwt, Avic), and the 
box-shaped sisters PAIWAN WISDOM and DAIWAN WISDOM (2010 Hakodate) were sold en bloc for USD 
11.75 mill each. The OBE DINARES (33,440 dwt, 2008 Shin Kochi) achieved region USD 12.4 mill and the LUCKY 
VOYAGER (37,317 dwt, 2009 Saiki) is rumoured trading at region USD 14.5 mill. In total, 16 bulk carriers were 
reported sold: 2 Capesizes, 2 Kamsarmaxes, 1 Ultramax, 5 Supramaxes and 6 Handysizes. 
On the tanker front, 25 sales were reported, with buying interest concentrated on product tankers, although 
the VLCC sector also saw activity: the PHOENIX VIGOR (309,887 dwt, 2009 Imabari) is rumoured trading at 
region USD 150 mill and the RISING SUN 1 (297,439 dwt, 2005 Dalian) is committed at region USD 126 mill. 
The Suezmax FLEVES (158,517 dwt, 2012 Hyundai) achieved region USD 110 mill basis prompt delivery in the 
UAE, and Greek buyers paid USD 65 mill for the LR2 HAIMA (104,991 dwt, 2009 Mitsui). In the MR segment, 
four Chengxi-built newbuildings, the CS FUJAIRAH, CS ROTTERDAM, CS HOUSTON and CS SINGAPORE, were 
reported at region USD 53 - 55 mill each, while the 2008 HMD-built MAYFAIR (49,999 dwt) fetched USD 27 
mill, giving her sellers a profit of USD 10.5 mill on the USD 16.5 mill they paid in August 2025. In the smaller 
sizes, the MarineLINE-coated HELENE MAERSK and HENNING MAERSK (2010 STX Offshore) were sold en bloc 
for USD 60 mill, while the SEYCHELLES PIONEER (37,500 dwt, 2005 Lindenau) achieved USD 14.2 mill. 
In the container sector, five vessels changed hands. The Ice Class II SONGA PUMA (2,797 teu, 2009 Yangfan) 
was sold for region USD 40 mill, Indian buyers acquired the OEL VARUN and OEL SURYA (2,478 teu, 2005 & 
2006 Aker) en bloc for USD 20.75 mill and USD 22.03 mill respectively, and the 2,029 teu Jiangsu Sentosa 
newbuildings YANG LIAN HAI and HONG LAN GUO JI fetched USD 37.5 mill each. 
In the gas sector, Navigare Capital acquired the VLGC NAVIGARE GAIA (82,441 cbm, 2015 Hyundai Ulsan) for 
USD 96.5 mill against a two-year time charter to Shell, while the older GAS WARICH (82,026 cbm, 2008 DSME) 
fetched USD 81 mill. 
For a detailed review and additional information regarding this week's transactions, kindly refer to our 
"Representative Sales" section.


### Demolition

Bangladesh buyers have raised their numbers noticeably to retain their position as the best 
payers in the region, as reflected by the 1994-built OCEANIC STAR (69,043 dwt) at region USD 540 - 545 per 
LT including bunkers, while candidates remain scarce with owners keeping older tonnage trading in the 
strong freight markets. India remained calm, with the festive season weighing on local steel demand but 
specialist units still attracting interest. Pakistan was quieter, with local plate prices falling and interest rates 
raised to 12%, while Turkey held steady on limited supply.
