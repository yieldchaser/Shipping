## Market Commentary

This week (01 - 05 June) witnessed the largest Posidonia 2026 International Shipping Exhibition in Athens, 
Greece, in the event's nearly 60-year history. The city was energized by a dynamic atmosphere, combining 
global trade discussions with events, conferences, sports competitions, and seaside celebrations all over the 
Athens Riviera. The event drew over 40,000 participants, shifting focus away from S+P transactions and 
moving towards building and enhancing networking opportunities.


### Bulkers

Despite the "Posidonia lull", asset values stayed robust at historically high levels, supported 
by long-term positive outlooks and a limited availability of modern vessels for sale. This week, we 
recorded a total of 15 transactions, comprising 2 Capesize, 5 Kamsarmax, 2 Supramax, and 6 
Handysize bulk carriers. Several of these deals established new price benchmarks. For a more detailed 
review and additional information regarding these transactions, kindly refer to our "representative 
sales" section.
