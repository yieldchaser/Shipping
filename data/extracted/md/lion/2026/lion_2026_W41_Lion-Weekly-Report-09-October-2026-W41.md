## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

The Baltic Dry Index (BDI) continues its decline for 3rd consecutive week closing today at 
2,917 points, reflecting a decrease of 7.34 % compared to last Friday's closing value of 3,148 points: 

Although this week felt slow, the bulker S+P market is quite active (especially in larger tonnage) with a 
total of 12 transactions recorded, comprising 5 capes, 3 kamsarmax, 1 panamax, 2 supramaxes, 1 
handymax bulk carriers.  
 
For a detailed review and additional information regarding these week's transactions, kindly refer to our
