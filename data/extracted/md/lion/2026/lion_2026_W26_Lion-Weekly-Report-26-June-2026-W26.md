## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

As a record-breaking summer heatwave is currently affecting large parts of Europe this 
month, "the heat is on" also in the s+p market. This week, we recorded a total of 20 deals at firm 
levels, comprising 2 Newcastlemax, 1 post-panamax, 2 Kamsarmax, 3 Ultramax, 4 Supramax, and 8 
Handysize bulk carriers. For a detailed review and additional information regarding these transactions, 
kindly refer to our "representative sales" section.

### Demolition

Bangladesh maintains a cautiously stable position as local shipyards approach full 
capacity. Pakistan remains stable at elevated levels. In contrast, the Indian market continue to decline 
due to the rainy season, while the Turkish market has strengthened. For an overview of demolition 
market rates, please check our comprehensive demometer below:
