# Lion Shipbrokers Weekly Market Report — Week 44 (30 October 2026)

**Broker**: Lion Shipbrokers Limited (Athens, Greece)

>

## Market Commentary

Sale & Purchase: The second-hand market was busy this week, with fifteen vessels reported sold or 
committed for further trading, nine dry and six wet. In the bulker sector, Greek buyers paid USD 49 mill for 
the scrubber-fitted, 2017-built Capesize WILLIAM OLDENDORFF (179,120 dwt, Jinhai/China) basis forward 
delivery in Q1 2027, setting a new benchmark. The 2011-built, scrubber-fitted Post-Panamax CARL 
OLDENDORFF (93,028 dwt, Taizhou CATIC/China) changed hands for USD 16.5 mill, more than USD 1.5 mill 
above September's sale of the LC MILADY (93,758 dwt, built 2011 SWS/China) at high USD 14 mill. In the 
Panamax segment, Chinese buyers paid region high USD 13 mill for the Japanese-built AMOY SUNNY (76,598 
dwt, 2006 Imabari) with her special survey recently passed, a firm price for her age, while the 2001 DSME-
built BAO NENG 16 (74,492 dwt) fetched a strong USD 9.294 mill at an online auction. In the geared sizes, 
South Korean buyers acquired the 2012 HMD-built, Tier II Supramax UNITY MELODY (55,705 dwt) for USD 
18.5 mill, while the 2006 Tsuneishi Cebu-built VINCA (52,447 dwt) was committed for region low-mid USD 12 
mill. In the Handysize segment, Greek buyers paid USD 20 mill for the Japanese-built, open-hatch ASTRO 
POLARIS (37,054 dwt, 2014 Onomichi) basis charter-free delivery within the year, while the one-year-older 
CETUS TIGER (37,059 dwt, 2013 Saiki) was sold for USD 18.3 mill and the Chinese-built BAO SHINE (32,203 
dwt, 2013 JNS) for USD 13 mill. On the tanker front, the 2008 HMD-built LEON WISDOM (49,999 dwt) was 
sold for USD 26 mill, giving her sellers a profit of about USD 9.2 mill on the USD 16.8 mill they paid for her in 
January. The 2005 Shin Kurushima-built ROSE MAKIS (45,838 dwt) was sold for USD 19 mill. In the chemical 
segment, the stainless-steel FG ROTTERDAM (19,995 dwt, 2012 Usuki) changed hands for USD 24 mill. The 
deal of the week was the en bloc sale of three 18,500 dwt chemical tanker resales, GOLDEN OAKTREE, 
GOLDEN PINE and GOLDEN POPLAR, due for delivery from Fujian Southeast Shipyard in 2027, at USD 36.5 
mill each. 
For a detailed review and additional information regarding this week's transactions, kindly refer to our 
"representative sales" section.


### Demolition

The 1998-built geared Handysize SPARROW (24,176 dwt, 5,618 ldt) was sold for demolition to 
Pakistan at USD 500/LT. The Indian market softened slightly, although demand and pricing remain at similar 
levels to last week. Bangladesh and Pakistan are the strongest destinations, with firm levels, while Turkey is 
holding stable.
