## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

The Baltic Dry Index closed today at 3,224 points, rising from 2,991 points recorded last Friday.  
 
One might expect the sale-and-purchase market to ease ahead of Posidonia 2026, which start next week 
from June 1 to 5. Yet there's no sign of a "Posidonia lull" so far as activity stayed strong the past week, 
despite slight fluctuations in the freight market.  
 
We recorded an impressive 24 dry-bulk transactions this week - four more than last week: 3 x capes, 2 
x post- panamaxes, 1 x kamsarmax, 5 x panamaxes, 4 x ultramaxes, 5 x supramaxes and 4 x 
handysizes, as asset prices continue to show remarkable resilience, maintaining multi-year highs.
