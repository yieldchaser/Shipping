## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

Greek controlled Post Panamax sisters TRINITY I & ROZA (93k dwt built 2010 at 
YZJ/China) were sold together to Chinese buyers for $11.5 million each, matching the price of 
OCEAN VENUS (93k dwt built 2010 at Jinling/China) sold in December.  
Three kamsarmax vessels changed hands this week: Greek buyers acquired THREE SASKIAS (81k 
dwt built 2014 at JMU/Japan) from the Japanese market for $ 26.8 million, a notable increase 
compared to the November sale of NEW ASCENT (82k dwt built 2012 at Tsuneishi/Japan) for $19.9 
million. Greek interests also committed the eco main engine AQUAVITA SEA (81k dwt built 2020 at 
Hantong/China) for $ 30.5 million, significantly higher than the September sale of a similar vessel, 
CSSC TAI YUAN (81.5k dwt built 2020 Xingang/China), for $ 25 million. Tier-2 vessel 
EPIPHANIA (80k dwt built 2012 at STX/S. Korea) was acquired by European interests for $17.6 mill, 
consistent with the early February sale of ATHINA CARRAS (82k built 2012 Daewoo/S. Korea) for 
$17.5 million.  
In the handy segment, Greek-controlled ADVENTURE (33.7k built 2011 Samjin/China) found buyers 
at a price of $ 9.5 mill with surveys pending, a much softer level compared with sale of sister vessel 
EUROSKY (33.7k dwt built 2011 Samjin/China) for $ 11 million earlier this month.

### Demolition

Subcontinent shipping rates remain stable and positive, driven by strong demand for
