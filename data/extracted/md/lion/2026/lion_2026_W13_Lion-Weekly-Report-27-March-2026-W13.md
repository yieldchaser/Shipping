## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

The s+p market remained firm for one more week, with asset prices continuing to rise despite 
ongoing geopolitical tensions. Buyers (mainly from Far East) remained active despite increasing prices 
and the Middle East conflict, maintaining healthy transaction levels. The market showed a mixed trend: 
demand for larger vessels such as Capesize and Panamax increased, while interest in smaller segments 
like Supramax and Handysize declined. For example, on Tuesday 24th March, in a Japanese "auction" 
style sale, capesize FRONTIER GARLAND (181.5k blt 2011 Imabari/Japan) was sold for the firm 
price of $ 36.3 million basis charter free delivery May/June to Hong Kong buyers (clients of Jinhui). 
For comparison, the similar vessel FRONTIER KOTOBUKI (174k dwt, built 2011 Namura/Japan) 
was sold in January for $31.4 million, while last week, the smaller caper SQUIRESHIP (170k dwt, 
built 2010 Sundong/South Korea) was sold for $29.5 million.
