## Market Commentary

The Lunar New Year (LNY) effect refers to a persistent calendar anomaly where stock markets in 
China and surrounding regions typically experience higher positive returns around the holiday, driven 
by increased investor optimism. This trend appears to extend to the shipping market as well, with 
particularly strong demand from Chinese buyers for tonnage. Consequently, we can expect a busy 
February ahead.


### Bulkers

Japanese cape-size duo ALLY & MIKATA (both built in 2005) were sold together for $37 
million. The one-year younger, scrubber fitted caper ROBUSTO (174k built 2006 in SWS/China) 
achieved a higher price of $19.5 million, with delivery scheduled for Q2 this year in the Far East.  
The scrubber-fitted post-panamax JP CARETTA (88k built 2008 in Japan) set a new market 
benchmark, selling for approximately $13.5 to $13.8 million. 
The Kamsarmax vessel RIZE (82k built 2012 in S. Korea) sold for $17.7 million, while the Panamax 
BULK XAYMACA (76k built 2006 in Japan) was committed at around $9.2 to $9.5 million.  
Greek buyers acquired the ultramax MITSOS (63k built 2013 in China) for $20.5 million.  
Three supramaxes built in 2008 changed hands: Almi's ANASA (56k built in Mitsui, Japan, m/e B& 
W) at just over $13 million, Tess 58 FLORINDA (58k built in Zhoushan, m/e B& W) at $12.6 million, 
and Costamare's CLARA (56k built in IHI, Japan, m/e Wartsila) at $11.8 million, possibly reflecting 
engine preferences.  
Chinese buyers paid the firm price of $9 million for the Norwegian-controlled SPAR CANIS (53k 
built 2006 in Chengxi, China) and only $8.1 million for the younger, Indonesian-controlled MDM 
BROMO (53k built 2007 in Dayang, China).  
Greek buyers paid $18.4 million for the handy DARYA TAPTI (36k built 2015 in Shikoku, Japan), 
fitted with energy-saving features. The Korean-built handy JETSTREAM (34k built 2012 in SPP) 
sold for $13.5 million, while two Chinese-built handy sisters, YANGTZE GRACE and YANGTZE 
HAPPINESS (32k built 2012 in JNS), were sold domestically for $10 million each.
