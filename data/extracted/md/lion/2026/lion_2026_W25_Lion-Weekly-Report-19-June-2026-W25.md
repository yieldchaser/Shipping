## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

Although this week felt slow, especially with China closed today due to Dragon Boat festival 
holidays, we recorded a total of 12 deals, comprising 1 Capesize, 1 post-panamax, 1 Kamsarmax, 2 
Panamax, 2 Supramax, and 5 Handysize bulk carriers.  
 
Following the post-Posidonia surge in transactions, ships values have remained highly resilient at 
multi-year highs, but a softening freight market has induced some short-term caution. For a detailed 
review and additional information regarding these transactions, kindly refer to our "representative 
sales" section.

### Demolition

Bangladesh remains firm after local budget government announcement, Pakistan remains 
stable at high levels, India is down due to rainy season, Turkey has softened due to the seasonal 
slowdown during the summer period. For an overview of demolition market rates, please check our 
comprehensive demometer below:
