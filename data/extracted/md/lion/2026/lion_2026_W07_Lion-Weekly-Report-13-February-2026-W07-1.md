## Market Commentary

### Tankers

Sinokor's shopping frenzy continues as they are linked with three VLCC acquisitions this 
week: sisters INGRID & ILMA (314k dwt built 2012 Hyundai/South Korea) for $89 mill a piece and 
EAGLE VARNA (300k dwt built 2013 DSME/South Korea) for $ 86.5 million. These levels represent 
a substantial price increase compared to the en bloc sale of slightly bigger sisters DELTA GLORY & 
DELTA ANGELICA (320k dwt built 2012 at Hyundai/South Korea) which were sold for $80 million 
each in early January. 
Suezmax NORDIC POLLUX (150k dwt built 2003 at Universal/Japan) went for $ 25 mill, a price in 
line with en bloc sale of one-year younger sisters NORDIC LUNA & NORDIC SPRINTER (150k 
dwt built 2004 at Universal/Japan) for $25 mill each in early January.  
Aframax NORDSYMPHONY (106k dwt built 2008 at Tsuneishi/Japan) attracted $ 35.5 mill, same 
price as one-year younger sister IONIC ARTEMIS (107k dwt built 2009 at Tsuneishi/Japan) achieved 
in late January.

### Bulkers

One more active sale and purchase week with Chinese buyers again in the spotlight: Chinese 
interests acquired the capesize vessels STAR SCARLETT (175k dwt built 2014 at Jinhai/China) for a 
firm $ 36 million and SMYRNA (176k dwt built 2008 at Namura/Japan) for $ 25.3 mill. They are also 
negotiating the purchase of the scrubber-fitted kamsarmax sisters ANGLO BARINTHUS & ANGLO 
RED (81k dwt bult 2013 at Sainty/China) for $ 17 million each, either with balance of period index t/c 
attached to Klaveness or on a charter-free basis for delivery mid-late March. For comparison, the exact 
sister vessel, THERESA HAINAN, was sold in December last year for a price in the low to mid $16 
million range.  
 
Clients of Niovis have bought the supramax vessel LUMINA (56k dwt built 2015 at Mitsui/Japan) for 
$ 22 million establishing a new market benchmark. Clients of Lomar have disposed supramax LIMA 
TRADER (Dolphin 57 design built at 2012 Qingshan/China) for $ 13.2 million, while 
smaller LIANSON DYNAMIC (Diamond 53 design built 2006 at Chengxi/China) sold for a price 
region high $8 - $9 mill. In an auction sale organized last week by the High Court of Gujarat in India, 
supramax ELVITA R (53k dwt built at Zhejiang/China) went under the hammer for $8.95 mill.  
 
The highly sought-after design "B-Delta" Eco handysize POWAN (39.7k dwt built 2016 at 
Ouhua/China) was snapped by Greek buyers for a whopping $19.2 mill. Smaller handy SIGMA 
VENTURE (35k built 2012 at Chengxi/China) changed hands for $11.75 million.
