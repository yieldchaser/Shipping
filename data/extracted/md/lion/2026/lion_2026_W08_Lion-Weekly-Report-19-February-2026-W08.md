# Lion Shipbrokers Weekly Market Report — Week 07 (19 FEBRUARY 2026)

**Broker**: Lion Shipbrokers Limited (Athens, Greece)  
**Issue Date**: 2026-02-19 | **Report Week**: Week 07, 2026

>

## Market Commentary

### Tankers

Greek Owners have been active this week in both buying and selling: Clients of Thenamaris 
acquired the scrubber-fitted Suezmax sister vessels EMERALDWAY & SUNRISEWAY (158k dwt 
built 2022 at SWS/China) for $177 million en bloc. Meanwhile the older unit FAIRWAY (160k dwt 
built 2013 at HHIC/Philippines) was sold for $56.5 million to clients of Metrostar. Clients of 
Performance committed their aframax vessel P. SOPHIA (105k dwt built 2009 at Hyundai/South 
Korea) for $36.5 million basis forward delivery mid-May - mid June to undisclosed interests. Greek 
buyers also purchased two panamax vessels, AGILE (74k dwt built 2007 at New Times/China) & 
PGC ALEXANDRIA (75k dwt built 2006 at Onomichi/Japan) for $13.5 million & $15.75 million, 
respectively.

### Bulkers

Two Greek-controlled capesize units have changed hands this week; EPIC (182k dwt built 
2010 at Odense/Denmark) sold for approximately $32.2-$32.8 million to clients of Mercuria while 
CAPE BRAZIL (178k dwt built at SWS/China) was acquired from Far Eastern buyers for $31.5 
million. In the active Ultramax arena, Turkish buyers purchased the eco unit BERGE TATEYAMA 
(63.5k dwt built 2020 at Shin Kasado/Japan) for $34.25 million. Singaporean owners of ONLY YOU 
(60.5k dwt built 2017 at Sanoyas/Japan) made a significant profit, selling her for $28.3 million after 
buying her seven months ago for just $23.2 million. The one-year older sister vessel NORD 
CHESAPEAKE (60.5k dwt built at Sanoyas/Japan) commanded the price of $25.5 million, while 
EXPLORER AMERICA (61.7k built 2011 at Ohima/Japan) was purchased by Chinese interests for 
$18.2 million, both with surveys pending. TESS 58 FORTUNE TIGER (built 2013 at 
Cebu/Philippines) sold for $19 million and same age Crown 58 HAI JIN (built 2013 at Dayang/China) 
for just under $16 million. The box-shaped supramax SSI ERDOGAN BEY (51k dwt built 2010 
Oshima/Japan) achieved $15 million with surveys recently passed. Chinese buyers paid $9 million for 
Diamond 53 Supramax SPAR LIBRA (53k dwt built 2006 at Chengxi/China), same level as her sister 
SPAR CANIS was sold end of last month. Chinese-controlled OASIS CHAMPION (50k dwt built 
2002 at Mitsui/Japan) found buyers that spent $7.5 million, aligned with the recent sale of MARIA F 
(52k dwt built 2002 at Sanoyas/Japan) for $7.8 mill after surveys. Following last week's reported sale 
of POWAN, clients of Swire have also agreed to sell her sister vessel HUNAN (B-Delta design 39k 
dwt built 2016 at Chengxi/China, box, tier-2) for a similar price of $19 million to undisclosed buyers. 
Another B-Delta sister-ship SIDER ATHENA (39k dwt built 2013 at Chengxi/China, box, Tier-2) was 
sold for $16 million. Meanwhile RADIANT WEB (38k dwt built 2012 at Shimanami/Japan, open 
hatch) found Greek buyers that paid $15.3 million following recently passed dry-docking surveys. The 
vintage logger LIBERATOR (28k dwt built 2006 at Shimanami/Japan) achieved $6.7 million with 
surveys due, outperforming the December deal of similar vessel CSE PROSPERITY EXPRESS (28k 
dwt built 2006 at Imabari/Japan), which was sold for $5.5 mill, with surveys also pending.
