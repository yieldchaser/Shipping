## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

The charter market has recovered since last week following a short decline in late June, as the 
Baltic index closed today at 2717 points, 7.65% higher than last Friday's 2524 points. Second hand 
sales show no sign of fatigue, as we recorded a total of 14 deals at firm levels this week, comprising 4 
Kamsarmax, 2 Ultramax, 2 Supramax, and 6 Handysize bulk carriers. We have also gathered from 
market insights that: 
 
- M/V WF ARTEMIS (63,547 dwt, blt 2020 Iwagi/Japan, RINA ss due 04/2030 dd due 05/2028, 5 
ho/5 ha, 4 x 30.7t CR, B&W 6S50ME-B9) - offers were invited 30th June 2026, good ss/dd position, 
vsl is under negos in the region of $ 36.5 mill; The Sellers paid $ 29.8 mill for the ship in May 2025 
 
-M/V JNS PHOENIX (40,504 dwt, blt 2025 JNS/China, BV ss due 08/2030 dd due 08/2028, 5 ho/5 
ha, 4 x 30.5t CR, Everllence B&W 5S50ME-C9-HPSCR/TIER III) - offers were invited 30th June 
2026, Eco m/e, vsl is under negos in the region of low $ 34 mill. 
 
-M/V SOUTHERN CROSS (37,085 dwt, blt 2014 Saiki/Japan, NK ss due 02/2029 dd due 02/2027, 4 
x 30.5t CR, B&W 6S46MC-C8, 3 x gens) - offers invited 24th June 2026, vsl is under negos in the 
region between $ 18 to $ 19 mill. 
 
We also have received reliable information since last week indicating that DARYA KRISHNA 
(34,874 dwt, blt 2016 Namura/Japan) has commanded a price in the high $ 20 million range, rather 
than the $ 20 million previously reported. 
 
For a detailed review and additional information regarding these transactions, kindly refer to our 
"representative sales" section.
