## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

Second-hand sales have finally slowed down as we approach the middle of the summer 
holidays period, having recorded just 4 deals this week comprising 1 post-Panamax, 1 Kamsarmax & 2 
Supramax bulk carriers. We have also gathered from market insights that: 
  
M/V ROYAL HOPE (81,011 dwt, blt 2015 JMU/Japan, NK ss due 05/2030 dd due 05/2028, 7 ho/7 
ha, B&W 6S60ME-C) - vsl is under negos in the region of $ 31 mill.

propeller.
