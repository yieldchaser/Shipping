## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

The secondhand dry bulk market is fiercely tight. Panamax rates have been consistently 
improving, and Chinese appetite for overage tonnage is driving prices. The Chinese buyers are willing 
to pay up and seem to be setting new benchmarks week on week.  
 
It is important to note that asset prices for 15-year-old Japanese tonnage are even more inflated, which 
is pulling up the values of older ships - In the Eco segment the market has become extremely firm, 
hence smaller players are reluctant to invest in younger/significantly more expensive bulkers despite 
the need to increase capacity for Far Eastern trades. 
 
This week, we recorded a total of 12 transactions, comprising 1 Capesize, 1 post-panamax, 1 
Kamsarmax, 1 Panamax, 2 Supramax, and 6 Handysize bulk carriers. For a detailed review and 
additional information regarding these transactions, kindly refer to our "representative sales" section.
