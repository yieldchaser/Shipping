## Market Commentary

### Bulkers

The strength of the sale & purchase market persists, although the freight market experienced 
a minor correction, with the Baltic Dry Index decreasing to 2991 points today, down from 3151 points 
recorded last Friday, driven by reduced Capesize and Panamax spot rates. 
 
Secondhand vessel prices remain near multi-year highs, as shipowners continue to maintain healthy 
buying appetite, having recorded an impressive tally of 20 transactions this week: 1 caper, 4 x post- 
panamaxes, 3 x kamsarmaxes, 1 panamax, 2 x ultramaxes, 4 x supramaxes and 5 x handysizes.

### Demolition

The Subcontinent markets remain weak overall, with the exception of Pakistan. Pakistan's 
market is firm as it depends on recycled steel due to import restrictions caused by the ongoing conflict.
