### Bulkers

China is currently celebrating its National Day Golden Week holiday from October 1 to 
October 7, resulting in a slow week. The Baltic Dry Index (BDI) closed today at 3,148 points, reflecting

1 post panamax, 3 kamsarmax, 2 panamax, 3 Supramaxes, and 1 Handysize bulk carriers.
