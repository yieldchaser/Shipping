### Bulkers Market Commentary

KGJS have sold their Kamsarmax sister vessels "BTG Olympos" & "BTG Kailash" 81/2015 JMU, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 09/2023 & SS 02/2025, DD 05/2023 respectively, BWTS fitted both) to Greek buyers TMS for USD 35,6 mill each basis delivery May-August 2022 & June-August 2022 accordingly. For comparison, the one-year-older "Majestic Sky" 82/2014 Tadotsu, Japan (SS/DD 04/2022) was sold a month ago at USD 31,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Therese Selmer" 55/2006 Mitsui, Japan (SS 09/2026, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 17,5 mill basis delivery July-August 2022. Remind you that back in March, the one-year-older "Neraida" 55/2005 Oshima, Japan (SS 01/2025, DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 17 mill. Following the sale of their Handymax "Corona" 46/1999 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 03/2024, DD 06/2022, BWTS fitted) back in April at USD 10,2 mill, AM Nomikos have now committed the sister vessel "Aquata" 46/1999 Sanoyas, Japan (01/2024, DD 06/2022, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for mid USD 10 mill. Ionic Bulk Carriers have committed their Handysize "Ionic Huntress" 34/2012 Dae Sun, Korea (SS/DD 06/2022) for reportedly USD 19 mill. Note that back in March, same owners sold the sister vessel "Ionic Halo" 34/2012 Dae Sun, Korea at the same price basis SS/DD passed & BWTS fitted. Moreover, the vintage "Meray Glyfada" 28/2002 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 11/2022) from Janchart Shipping was sold to Syrian buyers for region USD 10 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the sister vessel "Mount Adams" 28/2002 Kanda, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 9,8 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

DHT Holding announced on Monday the sale of their VLCC "DHT Falcon" 299/2006 NACKS, China (SS 09/2026, DD 11/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) and one-year-younger sister "DHT Hawk" 299/2007 NACKS, China (SS/DD 07/2022, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) for USD 38 & 40 mill respectively basis delivery Q2 2022. Maersk Tankers have committed their MR2 "Maersk Messina" 48/2009 Iwagi, Japan (SS 06/2024, DD 06/2022, BWTS fitted) for USD 16,5 mill. Note that the Korean built "Forres Park" 47/2009 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 02/2024) was sold three weeks ago at mid-high USD 16 mill. The MR1 "Lugano" 37/2007 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 06/2022, Ice Class 1A) from NSB Niederelbe was sold for USD 11 mill, whilst the one-year- younger "Isolde" 37/2008 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 07/2023) was sold a week ago at low USD 11 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout were reported at the level of usd 33,000 /34,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 26,000/27,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 28,000/30,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 27,000/30,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 30,000/33,000 pd. West African trips via North Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 27,000 pd. West African trips via US East Coast into India were reported at the level of usd 45,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via South America were fixed around usd 31,000/33,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America into China were reported at the levelof usd 41,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 27,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.7M. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the usd 29,000 pd for 9/12 months employment basis delivery Persian Gulf.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 20,000/21,000 pd. US Gulf trips into East Coast South America with petcoke were reported at the level of usd 39,000/40,000 pd. North Coast South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 47,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

South East Asian trips into China with steels were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips with wood pellets were reported at the level of usd 25,000 pd. Continental trips into China with fertilizers were fixed around usd 23,000 pd. A Large Handysize was reported fixed at the level of usd 35,500 pd for 3/5 months employment basis delivery China.
