### Bulkers Market Commentary

Chinese owners have committed the Capesize "Rosebank" 177/2010 New Times, China (SS 08/2025, DD 09/2023) to UK buyers for USD 27,5 mill. For comparison, the two-years-younger "Stella Flora" 176/2012 Shanghai, China (SS/DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago basis SS/DD passed at USD 31,75 mill. The Panamax "Elim Joyce" 77/2004 CSBC, Taiwan (SS/DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to undisclosed buyers for USD 15 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Chailease Blossom" 77/2004 CSBC, Taiwan (SS/DD 08/2022) was sold three weeks ago at USD 13,5 mill. Moreover, the "Orient Prima" 76/2005 Imabari, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at region USD 17 mill, while the two-years-younger sister vessel "ES Sakura" 76/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 08/2022) was sold a week ago at USD 18,3 mill. Chinese buyers HNA Technology have acquired the Supramax "Van Fortune" 56/2009 Taizhou Sanfu, China (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 16,5 mill basis delivery July-August 2022. Note that the one-year-younger "Amoy Dream" 56/2010 Xiamen, China (SS 07/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at high USD 17 mill. On the Handysize sector, SMT Shipping committed their "Adriatic Pearl" 37/2012 Zhejiang, China (SS/DD 06/2022) to European buyers for high USD 19 mill basis delivery July 2022 with SS/DD passed & BWTS fitted. Furthermore, Hangang Global Shipping sold their "Golden Maple" 32/2009 Zhejiang, China (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 14 mill. Remind you that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-younger "Tan Binh 239" 33/2010 Zhejiang, China (SS 04/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at low USD 13 mill. In addition, the "Asomatos" 28/2003 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 04/2023) from Royal Maritime was sold for USD 11,5 mill, whilst the one- year-younger "Team Challenge" 28/2004 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS/DD 06/2022) was sold in May at USD 10 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Mitsui OSK Lines have sold their VLCC "Duqm" 310/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 39 mill basis delivery July-August 2022. For reference, the one-year-younger Korean built "Leonidas" 318/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 10/2024) was sold a month ago at region USD 42 mill. The MR2 "Angel No.6" 45/2005 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) of Zheng Yu Shipping was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 9,4 mill. Note that the one-year-older Korean built "Torm Horizon" 47/2004 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 06/2024, DD 06/2022) was sold a week ago at USD 9,5 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout around usd 24,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 22,000/24,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 21,000/23,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 25,000/27,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via North Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. Continental trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 31,000/32,000 pd. Inter- Mediterranean trips via East Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 22,000/24,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Chinese trips into WC Mexico with steels were reported at the level of usd 33,000/35,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 24,000/25,000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 21,000/23,000 pd. Philippines nickel ore trips into China were reported at the level of usd 25,000/26,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Mediterranean with petcoke were fixed around usd 36,000/38,000 pd. An Ultramax was reported fixed at the level of usd 31,000 pd for 1 year employment basis delivery China.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Continental trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 16,000/17,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into US East Coast- US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 22,000/24,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Continent-Mediterranean range were reported at the level of usd 23,000/25,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
