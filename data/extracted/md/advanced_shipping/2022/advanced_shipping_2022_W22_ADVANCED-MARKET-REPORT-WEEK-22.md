### Bulkers Market Commentary

Ever Stella Shipping have sold their Capesize "Stella Flora" 176/2012 Shanghai Jiangnan, China (SS/DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers Safe Bulkers for USD 31,75 mill basis SS/DD passed. Remind you that same buyers acquired the "Stella Anita" 180/2012 Dalian, China (SS 01/2027, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) back in April at USD 30 mill. The Kamsarmax "Majulah Harbourfront" 82/2014 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 11/2024, DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted) from Kambara Kisen was sold to Greek buyers for USD 31,6 mill, while the one-year-younger "Capricorn Moon" 81/2015 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 04/2025, DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) of Meiji Shipping was sold to German buyers for region USD 34 mill. On the Ultramax sector, Cido Shipping's "Fortune Bell" 61/2020 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 39 mill, while the three-years-older sister vessel "Pavo Bright" 61/2017 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to Greek buyers for mid-high USD 33 mill. The Supramax "Bao Chuan" 56/2007 Mitsui, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 17,8 mill. Note that the one-year-older sister vessel "Therese Selmer" 55/2006 Mitsui, Japan (SS 09/2026, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in May at USD 17,5 mill basis delivery July-August 2022. Moreover, the "Evnia" 53/2003 New Century, China (SS/DD 08/2023) was sold for USD 14,4 mill, while same owners sold the sister vessel "Nicolaos A." 53/2003 New Century, China (SS/DD 03/2023) in May at the same price. Stam Shipping have sold two Handysize vessels, the "Cetus Star" 33/2004 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 10/2024) to Chinese buyers for high USD 12 mill and the OHBS "Cassiopeia Star" 32/2005 Naikai, Japan (SS/DD 03/2025, DD 03/2023) to Turkish buyers for USD 13,3 mill. In addition, the OHBS "Conception Light" 32/2007 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 12/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold to undisclosed buyers at USD 15,7 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

RB Armatori have committed their Aframax "Rumford" 107/2012 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers Thenamaris for USD 32,7 mill. Moreover, the "Mitera Marigo" 105/2007 Sumitomo, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 08/2024) of Atlas Maritime was sold to Singaporean buyers for USD 24,95 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Banda Sea" 105/2007 Sumitomo, Japan (SS/DD 05/2022, Scrubber fitted) was sold back in April at USD 21 mill. The MR2 "Pacific Diamond" 48/2010 Iwagi, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) from Eastern Pacific Shipping was sold for USD 18 mill. Note that the one-year-older "Jal Sasvata" 46/2009 Naikai, Japan (SS 04/2024, DD 08/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 17 mill.
