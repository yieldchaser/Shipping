### Bulkers Market Commentary

Holger Navigation have sold their Capesize "Agia Trias" 185/2002 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 01/2023) to Turkish buyers Beks Shipping & Trading for USD 15 mill. For comparison, the "Cougar" 177/2002 Mitsui, Japan (SS 04/2025, DD 04/2023) was sold back in September at USD 14 mill. The Kamsarmax "Fiorela" 81/2011 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 11/2026, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) of Meadway Shipping & Trading was committed to undisclosed buyers for high USD 21 mill. Remind you that the Japanese built "Buenos Aires" 83/2011 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 12/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 21 mill. On the Supramax sector, Uglands Rederi committed their "Senorita" 58/2008 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted) for low USD 16 mill. For reference, the one-year-younger Zhoushan built "Sagarjeet"58/2009 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 05/2024) was sold in September at USD 16,15 mill. Furthermore, Orion Bulkers sold their "Teresa Oetker" 58/2010 Yangzhou, China (SS 11/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 16,1 mill, while the sister vessel "Genco Bourgogne" 58/2010 Yangzhou, China (SS 01/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at mid-high USD 15 mill. In addition, the "Jin Yao" 52/2004 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) of Jinhui Shipping & Transportation was committed for region USD 13 mill. Note that the two-years-older "Lian Xin" 52/2002 Kananashi, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in September at USD 11,5 mill. Erasmus Corp have sold their OHBS Handysize "ES Venus" 34/2014 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS/DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 19 mill, while Santoku Senpaku sold the one-year-older "Ansac Christine Nancy" 32/2013 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) for low USD 17 mill. Remind you that a couple of weeks ago, the "Dream Ocean" 33/2013 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 17,65 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Global Marketing Systems have sold the VLCC "Rowan" 300/2001 Kawasaki, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD overdue) to Chinese buyers for USD 29,8 mill. Note that same owners sold the one-year-older Korean built "Viki" 310/2000 Samsung, Korea (SS 06/2023, DD overdue) a couple of weeks ago at USD 29,5 mill. Arab Maritime Petroleum Transport have sold their LR2 sister vessels "Alburaq" & "Sea Legend" 112/2008 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 10/2023 & SS/DD 12/2023 respectively) for region USD 70 mill enbloc. For reference, the one-year-younger "Argo" 105/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS 04/2024, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in August at USD 34,5 mill.

West African trips via East Coast South America into Continent were reported at the level of usd 20,000/21,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via West Africa with bauxite were fixed around usd 17,000/18,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were reported at the level of usd 25,000/28,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Continent were fixed around usd 22,750 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K. South American into China were reported at the level of usd 21,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.1M. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Japanese trips into US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A number of West Coast Indian trips via Persian Gulf into East Coast India with aggregates were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Trips from US East Coast via East Coast Canada into US East Coast with aggregates were reported at the level of usd 28,000 pd. An Ultramax was reported fixed on a period at the level of usd 17,000 pd for 1 year employment basis delivery Indonesia.
