### Bulkers Market Commentary

Following the acquisition of the Capesize "South Trader" 181/2014 Koyo, Japan (SS/DD 01/2024) back in January, Greek owners Safe Bulkers have bought the Chinese built "Stella Anita" 180/2012 Dalian, China (SS 01/2027, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) from Ever Stella Shipping for USD 30 mill. Moreover, Ceres Shipping sold their "Aquaproud" 178/2009 SWS, China (SS 06/2024, DD 09/2022) to Greek buyers Alpha Bulkers for USD 26,5 mill. Chios Navigation Hellas have committed their vintage Panamax "Doric Arrow" 75/2001 Hitachi, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for region USD 13 mill. For comparison, the one-year- older "Evershine" 76/2000 Kanasashi, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 10/2023) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 12 mill. On the Supramax sector, Hsin Chien Marine sold their OHBS "New Able" 55/2014 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) to Japanese buyers for USD 26,5 mill. Furthermore, the conventional "Union Victory" 53/2010 Chengxi, China (SS 07/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed to Indian buyers for USD 17,5 mill. In addition, a second vessel sold from Chios Navigation Hellas, the "Doric Spirit" 52/2001 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 10/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) has gone at low USD 13 mill. Ellingsen Shipping Group have sold their Handysize "Seastar Empress" 35/2011 Nantong, China (SS 11/2026, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) to Norwegian buyers Vega Bulk for mid USD 16 mill. Note that a week ago, the one-year-older "Strategic Encounter" 33/2010 Zhejiang, China (SS 08/2025, DD 08/2023) was sold at low USD 14 mill. Moreover, the "Promise 3" 32/2010 Samho, Korea (SS 10/2025, DD 04/2023) from Eugene Group was committed for USD 17,2 mill, while the two-years-younger "Ionic Halo" 34/2012 Dae Sun, Korea (SS/DD 03/2022) was sold three weeks ago at USD 19 mill basis SS/DD passed & BWTS fitted.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Norwegian owners sold their Suezmax "SKS Skeena" 159/2006 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 08/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 23,5 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older "Bari" 159/2005 Hyundai, Korea (SS 04/2025, DD 11/2023) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 21,5 mill. SK Shipping have sold their MR2 "Pro Emerald" 46/2003 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS/DD 01/2023) to Far Eastern buyers for high USD 6 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the two-years-younger "High Priority" 46/2005 Naikai, Japan (SS 03/2025, DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at excess USD 9 mill. Furthermore, Yamamaru Kisen committed their "Sunlight Express" 46/2011 Shin Kurushima, Japan (09/2025, DD 09/2023) to undisclosed buyers for region USD 17 mill, whilst same owners sold a week ago the one- year-older sister vessel "Prime Express" 46/2010 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD 11/2023) for USD 15,5 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout were reported at the usd 12,000/13,000 pd range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 21,000/22,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 21,000/23,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 125K A number of Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 22,000/23,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 25,000/26,000 pd. South African trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 27,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1M. Inter-Continental trips via North Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 27,000/29,000 pd. South American trips via Red Sea into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 42,000 pd. On the period front, Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 31,200 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery Persian Gulf. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 26,000 pd basis delivery China for 11/13 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

Trips from China into Continent-Mediterranean range with steels were reported at the level of usd 30,000/33,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 19,000/20,000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 19,000/21,000 pd. Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 27,000/29,000 pd. Indian trips into US Gulf with pig iron were fixed around usd 31,000/33,000 pd. South African trips into India were reported at the level of usd 26,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 600K. Mediterranean trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 22,000/23,000 pd. North Coast South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 34,000 pd. An Ultramax was reported fixed at the level of usd 34,000 pd for 3/5 months employment basis delivery Persian Gulf.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Continental trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. Inter-Mediterranean trips with petcoke were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. South American sugar trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 33,000/34,000 pd. Trips from South America into Continent with steels were fixed around usd 39,000 pd. No period activity to report here. A Lange Handysize was fixed on a period at the usd 26,000 pd basis delivery Continent for 4/6 months employment.
