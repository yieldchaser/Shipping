### Bulkers Market Commentary

Franbo Lines Corp have committed their Supramax "Medi Bangkok" 53/2006 Imabari, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 17,5 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the "Hayama Star" 53/2006 Oshima, Japan (SS 07/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at the same price. Following the sale of the OHBS Handysize "Eva Bulker" 38/2012 Naikai, Japan (SS/DD passed, BWTS fitted) at USD 23 mill, Mssrs Lauritzen Bulkers have now committed the sister vessel "Milau Bulker" 38/2012 Naikai, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 23 mill also. Moreover, the "Omnia" 36/2011 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 08/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold for high USD 19 mill, while the one-year- younger sister vessel "Team Hope" 37/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 02/2027, DD 04/2025) was sold back in May at USD 19,85 mill. In addition, British Bulkers sold their "Lord Wellington" 32/2005 Hakodate, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025) to Chinese buyers for USD 14,3 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older "Cetus Star" 33/2004 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 10/2024) was sold a month ago at USD 12,8 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Viken Shipping have sold their Aframax "Kronviken" 114/2006 Samsung, Korea (SS 09/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A) to Greek buyers for USD 25 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the two-years-older "Carlton" 115/2004 Samsung, Korea (SS 10/2024, DD 04/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted, Ice Class 1A) was sold at USD 22 mill. The LR1 "Stena Paris" 65/2005 Brodosplit, Croatia (SS 12/2025, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1B) of Concordia Maritime was sold to Greek buyers for USD 15 mill. For reference, same owners sold back in March the two- years-younger sister vessel "Stena President" 65/2007 Brodosplit, Croatia (SS/DD 09/2022, Ice Class 1B) for USD 11,2 mill. Coral Shipping committed their MR2 "Explorer II" 47/2005 Onomichi, Japan (SS 07/2025, DD 10/2023) for USD 12,1 mill. Note that the "Angel No.6" 45/2005 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 9,4 mill. The MR1 "Alicudi M" 40/2004 Shina, Korea (SS/DD 02/2024) of Augusta Due Srl was committed for USD 14 mill, whilst the one-year-older "Seacrown I" 40/2003 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 01/2023) was sold in June at USD 8,5 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout around usd 11,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 19,000/21,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 18,000/19,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 19,000/21,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were reported at the level of usd 30,000/31,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Continent were fixed around usd 28,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K. East Coast South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 22,000/24,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.2M/1.4M. A Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 21,000 pd for 11/13 months employment basis delivery Japan.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 22,000/24,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 25,000/28,000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 25,750 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 575K. Continental trips into Black Sea with scrap were fixed around usd 19,000/20,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 29,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 450K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Continental trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 14,000/15,000 pd. Trips from Black Sea into US Gulf were fixed around usd 19,000/21,000 pd. US Gulf trips into East Coast South America with soda ash were reported at the level of usd 9,500 pd. No period activity to report here.
