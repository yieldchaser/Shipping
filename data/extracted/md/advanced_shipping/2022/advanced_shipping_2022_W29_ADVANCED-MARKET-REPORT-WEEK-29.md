### Bulkers Market Commentary

Jinyang Shipping have committed their Kamsarmax "Lila Tokyo" 79/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS 01/2025, DD 12/2022) for low-mid USD 19 mill. Note that the sister vessels "Thunderbird" 79/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS 11/2025, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) & "Bonneville" 79/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS 11/2025, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) were sold three weeks ago at USD 37 mill enbloc. SUMEC Group committed their Ultramax "Dayang Confidence" 63/2017 Yangzhou, China (SS 05/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) for USD 30 mill, while the one-year-older "Soho Mandate" 61/2016 DACKS, China (SS 08/2026, DD 09/2024) from Mid-Atlantic Bulk Carriers was sold to Chinese buyers for region USD 31 mill. On the Supramax sector, COSCO Shipping sold their "Shun Xin" 57/2010 COSCO, China (SS 01/2025, DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted) for high USD 16 mill. Note that back in June, the one-year-older "Van Fortune" 56/2009 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 16,5 mill basis delivery July-August 2022. Moreover, the Japanese built "Oreo" 55/2008 Kawasaki, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) of Diligent Holdings was sold to Greek based buyers for USD 19,35 mill. For comparison, the two-years- older sister vessel "IDC Falcon" 55/2006 Kawasaki, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in June for low USD 18 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Greek owners have committed their Aframax "Nicholas" 115/2007 Sasebo, Japan (SS/DD 08/2022) to Chinese buyers for USD 26,8 mill basis delivery August 2022 with SS/DD passed & BWTS fitted. Furthermore, the two-years- older "Songa Coral" 107/2005 Koyo, Japan (SS 01/2025, DD 01/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold for USD 25 mill. Note that a week ago, the Korean built "Matterhorn Spirit" 114/2005 Daewoo, Korea (SS 11/2025, DD 10/2023, Ice Class 1A) was sold at USD 24,75 mill. In addition, the "Super Sapphire" 100/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to Korean buyers for USD 26 mill, whilst the "Maran Sagitta" 105/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 27,6 mill. The MR2 "Sunny Bay" 50/2008 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 05/2023) of MSEA Tankers was committed to Chinese buyers for high USD 17 mill. Remind you that the sister vessels "Elandra Blu" & "Elandra Corallo" 50/2008 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 07/2023 & SS/DD 10/2023 respectively) were sold a week ago at USD 18,5 mill each. Thenamaris Ships Management have sold their MR1 "Seaexplorer" 40/2003 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 12/2022) to Turkish buyers Beks for USD 9 mill. For reference, the one-year-younger Japanese built "Hafnia Rainier" 40/2004 Saiki, Japan (SS 03/2024, DD 06/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold in June for USD 10 mill basis DD due.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 17,000/18,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 19,000/20,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via South America were reported at the level of usd 17,000/20,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 30,000/31,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 19,000/21,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 900K/1.1M. A Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 19,750 pd for 5/8 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 23,000/25,000 pd. Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 26,000/28,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips via Indonesia into West Coast India were reported at the level of usd 20,000/21,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into North Coast South America were fixed around usd 30,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of South East Asian trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 30,000 pd. Australian trips into South East Asia with salt were fixed around usd 22,000 pd. West African trips into India with phosphate were reported at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd. Inter-Mediterranean trips via Black Sea were fixed around usd 18,000/19,000 pd. Trips from US East Coast via East Coast South America into Continent with alumina were reported at the level of usd 17,000/18,000 pd. A large Handysize was reported fixed at the level of usd 22,500 pd for 3/5 months employment basis delivery China.
