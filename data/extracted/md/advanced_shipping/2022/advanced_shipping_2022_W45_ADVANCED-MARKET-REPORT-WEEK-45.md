### Bulkers Market Commentary

Owners have committed the Capesize "Aquafortune" 174/2011 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 04/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 27 mill. Remind you that the one-year-younger "Frontier Triumph" 181/2012 Imabari, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 06/2023) was sold back in September at region USD 30 mill. Bocimar's Post Panamax "CMB Van Mieghem" 95/2011 Imabari, Japan (SS 08/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to Swiss buyers for USD 21 mill. Note that three weeks ago, same owners sold the one-year-younger "CMB Chardonnay" 95/2012 Koyo, Japan (SS/DD 11/2022) for USD 21,5 mill. The Kamsarmax "Ocean Rosemary" 82/2013 Dalian, China (SS 04/2023, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold for region 21 mill, while the one-year- younger sister vessel "Ocean Thyme" 82/2014 Dalian, China (SS/DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in September at USD 21 mill also. On the Supramax sector, Jinhui Shipping sold their "Jin Feng" 52/2004 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) to buyers Xinfeng HK Shipping for USD 13,3 mill. For comparison, the "Jin Yao" 52/2004 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 13 mill. Moreover, Symphony Shipping committed their "Worldera-6" 52/2005 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 12/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) for low-mid USD 12 mill. Meiho Kaiun have sold their Handysize "Belle Etoile" 28/2014 I-S, Japan (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 14 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older sister vessel "Malto Hope" 28/2013 I-S, Japan (SS/DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 13,6 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Ridgebury Holdings have committed their Suezmax "Ridgebury Nicholas A" 159/2007 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 01/2023, Scrubber fitted) for USD 34 mill. For reference, the one-year-older Ice-Classed "Ridgebury Mary Selena" 146/2006 Universal, Japan (SS 09/2026, DD 01/2025, Scrubber fitted, Ice Class 1A) was sold back in August at USD 31 mill. The LR1 "Nordic Tristan" 73/2007 New Times, China (SS 03/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) of Nordic Hamburg Shipping was sold for USD 20,5 mill. Note that back in September, the one-year-younger "Amber" & "Azurite" 74/2008 New Century, China (SS/DD 08/2023 & SS/DD 09/2023 respectively) were sold for USD 18,4 mill each.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 12,000/14,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Indian trips via South Africa into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 11,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via South America were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via US Gulf into China were reported at the level of usd 19,000/21,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 18,000/19,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K/900K. A Kamsarmax was reported fixed on a period at the level of usd 17,000 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 8,000/10,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips into West Coast India were fixed around usd 5,000/6,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were reported at the level of usd 25,000/27,000 pd. An Ultramax was reported fixed on a period at the level of usd 13,250 pd for 16/18 months employment basis delivery Southeast Asia. Also, a Supramax was fixed on a period at the level of usd 16,250 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery Baltic.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Japanese trips into Southeast Asia with slag were fixed around usd 9,000/10,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into South Africa were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into West Africa were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Continental trips into India were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 22,000/23,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
