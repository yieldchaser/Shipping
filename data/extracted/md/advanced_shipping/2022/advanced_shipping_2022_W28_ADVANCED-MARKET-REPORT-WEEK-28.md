### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Kamsarmax "Ocean Scallion" 82/2013 Dalian, China (SS/DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) of Far East Horizon was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 23,5 mill. For reference, the "Sea Hermes" 81/2013 Xiamen, China (SS/DD 01/2023) was sold a couple of months ago at the same price. Golden Ocean Group have sold their Ultramax sister vessels "Golden Cecilie" & "Golden Cathrine" 60/2015 JMU, Japan (SS 02/2025, DD 02/2023 & SS 01/2025, DD 02/2023, BWTS fitted both) to Chinese buyers for USD 31,5 mill each. Remind you that the two-years-younger "Pavo Bright" 61/2017 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in late May at USD 33,7 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Neutrino" 58/2012 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 10/2022) was sold to undisclosed buyers for region USD 24 mill. Note that the smaller "Xin Xiang Hai" 56/2012 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 07/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold back in May for mid USD 23 mill. Furthermore, Costamare sold their "Merida" 56/2012 Taizhou, China (SS 01/2026, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 19,75 mill, whilst the one-year-older "Bao Progress" 56/2011 Jiangdong, China (SS 01/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 17,6 mill. Great Bulk Venture have committed their OHBS Handysize sister vessels "Venture Ocean" & "Venture Team" 39/2015 Jiangmen, China (SS 10/2025, DD 10/2023 & SS 11/2025, DD 11/2023 respectively, BWTS fitted both) for USD 25 mill each. Moreover, Chinese owners sold the "Yangtze Spirit" 35/2012 Nanjing, China (SS 01/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) for low USD 17 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older sister vessel "Jin Da" 35/2011 Nanjing, China (SS 09/2026, DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of months ago for the same price.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Teekay Tankers have sold their Aframax "Matterhorn Spirit" 114/2005 Daewoo, Korea (SS 11/2025, DD 10/2023, Ice Class 1A) to UAE based buyers for USD 24,75 mill. Note that earlier this month, the one-year-younger "Kronviken" 114/2006 Samsung, Korea (SS 09/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A) was sold at USD 25 mill. ICBC Financial Leasing have sold the MR2 sister vessels "Elandra Blu" & "Elandra Corallo" 50/2008 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 07/2023 & SS/DD 10/2023 respectively, BWTS fitted both) for mid USD 18 mill each, while the one- year-older sister vessel "Neutron Sonic" 50/2007 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 09/2022) from Interorient Marine Services was committed for USD 14 mill. In addition, the "Eastern Kalmia" 50/2007 Sls, Korea (SS/DD 10/2022) of Oriental Ocean Shipping Holding was sold for high USD 14 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

at the level of mid usd 10,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported at the usd 17,000/18,000 pd range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. East African trips into Persian Gulf with coal were fixed around usd 21,250 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 325K. Inter-Continental trips via US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via North Coast South America were fixed around usd 18,000/21,000 pd. Continental trips via East Coast South America into China were reported around usd 26,000/27,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1M. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the level of usd 20,000/21,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 25,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into West Africa with clinker were reported at the level of usd 20,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into US Gulf with cement were fixed around usd 20,000/21,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 30,000/31,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into China were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 600K/800K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize
