### Bulkers Market Commentary

Hokoku Marine have committed their Kamsarmax "Nord Pollux" 81/2016 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 01/2026, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for low USD 25 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Lowlands Comfort" 81/2016 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 02/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 26,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Eagle Bulk Shipping sold their "Jaeger" 52/2004 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 10/2024, DD 01/2023, BWTS fitted) to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 10,5 mill, while the sister vessel "Worldera-5" 52/2004 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at low USD 10 mill. Following the sale of the "Interlink Activity" 38/2015 Taizhou, China (SS 09/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1C) at USD 21 mill, Mssrs Ocean Yield have now committed the sister vessel "Interlink Sagacity" 38/2015 Taizhou, China (SS 08/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1C) to undisclosed buyers for USD 20 mill. Moreover, Anthony Giavridis Maritime sold their "Gant Grace" 28/2010 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 12,5 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-older sister vessel "Melina" 28/2009 Imabari, Japan (SS 08/2024, DD overdue) was sold basis DD passed & BWTS fitted at USD 10,9 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Ridgebury Holdings have committed their Suezmax "Ridgebury Mary Jane" 150/2008 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 05/2023, Scrubber fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 38,5 mill. Remind you that a month ago, same owners sold the one-year-older "Ridgebury Nicholas A" 159/2007 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 01/2023, Scrubber fitted) at USD 34 mill. The LR1 "Summit Africa" 73/2009 New Times, China (SS/DD 08/2024) was sold to European buyers for mid-high USD 24 mill. Note that back in November, the "Cape Tampa" 73/2009 New Times, China (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 20,25 mill. Stealth Maritime have committed their MR2 "T Rex" 50/2006 Spp, Korea (SS 12/2026, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) for USD 19,2 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger "Clean Imperial" 50/2007 Sls, Korea (SS/DD 11/2022) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 20 mill basis SS/DD passed & BWTS fitted.
