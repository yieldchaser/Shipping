### Bulkers Market Commentary

Nissen Kaiun have sold their Capesize "Navios Obeliks" 181/2012 Koyo, Japan (SS 07/2026, DD 09/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Greek buyers Synergy Maritime for high USD 29 mill. Moreover, following the acquisition of the "Aquafortune" 174/2011 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 04/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) a week ago at USD 27 mill, Mssrs Stealth Maritime have purchased the one-year-older Korean built "HL Shinboryeong" 179/2010 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 05/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 25 mill. The Kamsarmax "Darya Lok" 81/2012 Daewoo, Korea (SS 06/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) of Dry Bulk Services was committed for USD 21,5 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older "Fiorela" 81/2011 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 11/2026, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at high USD 21 mill. In addition, Yamamaru Kisen sold their "CMB Partner" 81/2016 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 11/2026, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers Minerva for USD 29 mill basis delivery February 2023. UK owners have sold their Panamax "Cerafina" 74/2005 Hudong-Zhonghua, China (SS 09/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 12 mill, whilst the one-year-younger sister vessel "Navios Symmetry" 74/2006 Hudong-Zhonghua, China (SS 06/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at the same price. On the Supramax sector, AM Nomikos Transworld Maritime committed their "Azzura" 52/2004 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for high USD 12 mill. Note that a week ago, the "Jin Feng" 52/2004 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 13,3 mill. Daido Kaiun have sold their OHBS Handysize "Ocean Echo" 37/2013 Saiki, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 17,5 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the two-years-older sister vessel "Royal Harmony" 37/2011 Saiki, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at high USD 16 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low/mid usd 8,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds continued rating primarily in the usd 13,000/15,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 10,000/12,000 pd range. Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Inter-continental trips via South America were fixed around usd 13,000/14,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic and Red Sea into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 21,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 19,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 900K/950K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 19,000 pd basis delivery Mediterranean for 11/13 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from South China into North China with clinker were reported at the level of usd 5,000/6,000 pd. Trips from North China into Vietnam with steels were fixed around usd 7,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 6,000/8,000 pd. On the period front, Ultramax was reported fixed at the level of usd 14,000 pd for 10/12 months employment basis delivery Continent. Also, an Ultramax was fixed on a period at the usd 13,100 pd basis delivery China for 18/20 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. Trips from Southeast Asia for 2 laden legs with redelivery China were reported at the level of usd 10,000 pd. A number of trips from Baltic into West Africa were fixed around usd 17,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
