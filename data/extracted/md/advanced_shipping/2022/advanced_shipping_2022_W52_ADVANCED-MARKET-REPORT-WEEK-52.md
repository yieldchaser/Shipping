### Bulkers Market Commentary

Noma Shipping have committed their Kamsarmax "Scarlet Albatross" 82/2015 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 07/2025, DD 06/2023) to Greek buyers for USD 31,7 mill. For reference, the two-years-older "Ibis Wind" 83/2013 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 07/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold back in October for USD 28,5 mill basis delivery End 2021 – Start 2022. Furthermore, Chinese owners sold their "Orient Union" 79/2011 Fujian, China (SS 08/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 17,5 mill. Remind you that back in October, the "Golden Endurer" 79/2011 Jinhai, China (SS 01/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 20 mill. Japanese owners have sold their Panamax "Coral Diamond" 76/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for low USD 16 mill. Note that a month ago, the one-year-younger "Yutai Ambitions" 77/2008 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 18,7 mill. Following the sale of the Handysize "Xing Ning Hai" 34/2015 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 07/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) at USD 23 mill back in October, Fortune Ocean Shipping have now committed the sister vessels "Xing Jing Hai" & "Xing Yuan Hai" 34/2015 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 07/2025, DD 07/2023 & SS 12/2025, DD 11/2023 respectively, BWTS fitted both) for excess USD 22 mill each.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Metrostar Management have sold their Aframax "Prostar" 115/2019 Daehan, Korea (SS/DD 01/2024) to Greek buyers for USD 52 mill. Remind you that the two-years-older "Antonis" 113/2017 Daehan, Korea (SS/DD 02/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 45 mill. The MR2 "Nord Gainer" 50/2011 Guangzhou, China (SS 04/2026, DD 09/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) from Norden was committed for low-mid USD 18 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Grazia" 50/2010 Guangzhou, China (SS 11/2025, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at region USD 15 mill. In addition, Nordic sold the MR1 sister vessels "Nordic Agnetha" & "Nordic Amy" 37/2009 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 05/2024, DD 08/2022 & SS 07/2024, DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted both) to German buyers for mid-high USD 11 mill each. Note that the two-years- older "Aiolos" 37/2007 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 03/2022) was sold back in October at USD 8 mill.
