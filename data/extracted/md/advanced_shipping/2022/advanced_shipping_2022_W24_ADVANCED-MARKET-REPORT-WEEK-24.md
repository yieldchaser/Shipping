### Bulkers Market Commentary

Chinese owners have committed their Supramax "Bao Proress" 56/2011 Jiangdong, China (SS 01/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 17,6 mill, while the two-years-older "Van Fortune" 56/2009 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 16,5 mill basis delivery July-August 2022. Moreover, Franbo Lines sold their "Hayama Star" 52/2006 Oshima, Japan (SS 07/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for low-mid USD 17 mill. Remind you that two-years-older "Prabhu Lal" 52/2004 (SS 03/2024, DD 05/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 16 mill basis DD freshly passed. On the Handysize sector, Lauritzen Bulkers committed their OHBS "Anne Mette Bulker" 38/2012 Naikai, Japan (SS 02/2027, DD 03/2025) to Turkish buyers Devel Shipping for USD 23 mill. Note that a week ago, the three- years-younger sister vessel "African Rook" 37/2015 Naikai, Japan (SS 02/2025, DD 04/2023) was sold at USD 27 mill. In addition, the "Canopus" 28/2006 Shimanami, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) of Universal Navigation was sold for USD 13 mill, whilst the three-years-older "Asomatos" 28/2003 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 04/2023) was sold a week ago at USD 11,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Nordic American Tankers have committed their Suezmax "Nordic Moon" 160/2002 Samsung, Korea (SS/DD 08/2022) to undisclosed buyers for USD 16 mill. For comparison, the Japanese built "Ridgebury Astari" 150/2002 Nkk, Japan (SS/DD 07/2022) was sold a week ago at USD 15,5 mill. German buyers Leonhardt & Blumberg have acquired the MR2 sister vessels "Cygnus" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 08/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) & "Sextans" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS/DD freshly passed, BWTS fitted) for USD 15 mill each. Remind you that back in May, the one-year-older "Falcon Nostos" 51/2006 Shina, Korea (SS 06/2026, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 13,2 mill. Hafnia Tankers have sold their MR1 "Hafnia Green" 40/2007 Saiki, Japan (SS/DD 08/2022) to Greek buyers Sea Hawk Maritime for USD 11,9 mill. For reference, the one-year-older Korean built "Miss Claudia" 40/2006 Shina, Korea (SS 05/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 11,5 mill.

at the level of high usd 12,00's fio range. North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 24,000/25,000 pd. #### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 25,000/26,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 19,000/21,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 24,000/25,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 25,000/27,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America and East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 32,000/34,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via West Africa with bauxite were fixed around usd 20,000/21,000 pd. North Coast South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 300K. East Coast South American trips into China was fixed around usd 25,000/27,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.5M/1.6M. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the level of usd 28,000/29,000 pd. South East Asian trips into China with tapioca chips were reported at the level of usd 25,000/26,000 pd. Indian trips via Indonesia into India were fixed around usd 32,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips from China into Persian Gulf were fixed around usd 24,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the level of usd 27,000/29,000 pd. Inter-Mediterranean trips were reported at the level of usd 18,000/19,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into East Coast South America with fertilizers were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd.
