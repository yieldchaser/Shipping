### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Capesize "Orient Angel" 176/2007 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 06/2023) was committed to Turkish buyers for region USD 19 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the two-years-older sister vessel "Shinyo Guardian" 177/2005 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 11/2024, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 17,25 mill. Navios Maritime have sold their Panamax "Navios Taurus" 76/2005 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2025, DD 04/2023) for USD 14 mill. Note that a week ago, the two-years-younger sister vessel "Blue Chip" 76/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 11/2022) was sold at low USD 15 mill. Moreover, the "Prabhu Puni" 76/2002 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 05/2027, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) from Tolani Shipping was committed for USD 11,5 mill, while the "Dooyang Jeju" 76/2002 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 11/2022) was sold a week ago at USD 10,2 mill. On the Ultramax sector, Nisshin Shipping sold their "Nord Yucatan" 63/2019 Nantong, China (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) for USD 28,5 mill. For reference, the three-years-older "Nautical Anne" 63/2016 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 12/2021, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold in August at USD 31 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Following the sale of the Suezmax "Lila Guangzhou" 159/2004 Bohai, China (SS 05/2024, DD 12/2022, BWTS fitted) back in September at USD 23,5 mill, Lila Global have now sold the sister vessel "Lila Shenzhen" 159/2004 Bohai, China (SS 12/2024, DD 01/2023, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 25 mill. In addition, Polembros Shipping committed their "Kimolos Warrior" 157/2013 Samsung, Korea (SS/DD 03/2023) to Turkish buyers for region USD 46 mill. Note that back in September, the one-year-older sister vessel "Vinga" 159/2012 Samsung, Korea (SS/DD 11/2022) was sold at USD 42 mill. Union Maritime have committed their LR1 "Houston Star"75/2004 Hyundai, Korea (SS 02/2024, DD 10/2022) to Turkish buyers for USD 13 mill. For comparison, the "Karadeniz Powership Anatolia" 72/2004 Samsung, Korea (SS 07/2024, DD 12/2022) was sold a month ago at the same price. The MR2 "Atlantica Bell" 50/2006 Stx, Korea (SS 03/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A) from Atlantica Shipping was sold to undisclosed buyers for USD 20,7 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older sister vessel "Northern Ocean" 50/2005 Stx, Korea (SS 05/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A) was sold a month ago at high USD 19 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid/high usd 7,00's fio range. A Capesize was fixed on a period at the usd 16,000 pd basis delivery Far East for 1 year employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

CIS Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 20,000/22,000 pd. North Pacific rounds continued rating primarily in the usd 15,000/17,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 12,000/14,000 pd range. Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Indian trips via South Africa into China were fixed around usd 18,000/19,000 pd. India chrome ore trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/12,000 pd. Inter-continental trips via North Coast South America were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. US Gulf trips via Red Sea into China were reported at the level of usd 16,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 650K. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 30,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K/800K. On the period front, Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 24,750 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery India. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 16,000 pd basis delivery Far East for 1 year employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment. A reposition fixture for a Supramax at the usd 12,000 pd on a backhaul. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 7,000/9,000 pd range. Indonesian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,000 pd. Australian trips into Continent with alumina were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

No fixture reported for this segment. No period activity to report here.
