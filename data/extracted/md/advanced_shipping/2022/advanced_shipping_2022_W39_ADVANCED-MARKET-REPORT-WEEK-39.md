### Bulkers Market Commentary

Greek owners Minoa Marine have acquired two Kamsarmax sister vessels, the "Cymona Gemini" 83/2006 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) from Alassia Newships Management for USD 15,85 mill & the "Pedhoulas Trader" 82/2006 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) from Safe Bulkers for USD 15,85 mill also. Moreover, FEH Shipping sold their "Ocean Thyme" 82/2014 Dalian, China (SS/DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 21 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Ocean Scallion" 82/2013 Dalian, China (SS/DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold back in July at USD 23,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Chinese owners sold the "Taurus Honor" 56/2009 Jiangsu, China (SS 11/2024, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 14,8 mill. Remind you that the one-year-younger "Shun Xin" 57/2010 Cosco, China (SS 01/2025, DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold in July at high USD 16 mill. In addition, Navios Maritime Holdings committed their "Navios Ulysses" 55/2007 Oshima, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 03/2025) for USD 14,5 mill. Note that a couple of months ago, the one-year-younger "Oreo" 55/2008 Kawasaki, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 19,35 mill. Daiichi Chuo Kisen Kaisha have committed their OHBS Handysize "Dream Ocean" 33/2013 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 17,65 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Super Valentina" 33/2013 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS/DD 02/2023) was sold back in June for region USD 22 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The VLCC "Maran Aquarius" 320/2005 Daewoo, Korea (SS 09/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) of Maran Tankers was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 52 mill. Remind you that Maran Tankers sold the sister vessel "Maran Andromeda" 320/2005 Daewoo, Korea (SS 01/2025, DD 09/2022, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) in early August at USD 37 mill. Ludwig Mowinckels Rederi have committed their Suezmax "Vinga" 158/2012 Samsung, Korea (SS/DD 11/2022) to Greek buyers Thenamaris for region USD 42 mill. Note that the one-year-younger sister vessel "Ridgebury Lessley B" 158/2013 Samsung, Korea (SS/DD 09/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 45 mill. Ridgebury Holdings have sold their MR2 sister vessels "Ridgebury Birch" & "Ridgebury Acacia" 53/2006 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 02/2024 & SS 11/2025, DD 03/2024 respectively, BWTS fitted both) to undisclosed buyers for USD 35 mill enbloc, while Braveheart Shipping sold their "Black Swan" 48/2007 Iwagi, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) for excess USD 19 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 16,000/19,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via US Gulf were fixed around usd 18,000/19,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were reported at the level of usd 26,000/29,000 pd. South American into China were fixed around usd 19,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 950K. A Kamsarmax was reported fixed on a period at the level of usd 18,000 pd for 4/7 months employment basis delivery India.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from China into India with fertilizers were reported at the level of usd 25,000/26,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips via Persian Gulf into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 12,000 pd. South African trips into East Coast India were fixed around usd 20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 200K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Indonesian trips into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. Continental trips into Far East were fixed around usd 16,000/17,000 pd. A Handymax was reported fixed on a period at the level of usd 20,500 pd for 6/8 months employment basis delivery China with redelivery Singapore-Japan range.
