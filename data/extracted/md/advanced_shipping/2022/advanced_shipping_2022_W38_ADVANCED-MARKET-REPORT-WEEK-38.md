### Bulkers Market Commentary

Sojitz Corp have committed their Kamsarmax "Buenos Aires" 83/2011 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 12/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 21 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Fortuna" 83/2010 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold earlier this month at USD 23,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Chinese owners sold their "Lian Xin" 52/2002 Kananashi, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) for USD 11,5 mill. Remind you that the two-years-younger "Anastasia S" 52/2004 Onomichi, Japan (SS/DD 09/2024) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 16,3 mill. Ortolan Strait have committed their Handysize "Ortolan Alpha Strait" 34/2010 Seko, Korea (SS 08/2025, DD 05/2023) to Chinese buyers for USD 15 mill. For comparison, the "Miss Simona" 34/2010 Spp, Korea (SS 06/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold in early August at USD 16,8 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

BAHRI have committed their VLCC "Hilwah" 316/2002 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 12/2022) to Chinese buyers for mid-high USD 37 mill. For reference, the Japanese built "Tsurusaki" 300/2002 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 09/2022) was sold back in August at USD 33 mill. The LR1 "Polar Unicorn" 74/2008 Onomichi, Japan (SS/DD 01/2023, Ice Class 1A) of Prime Marine Management was sold for mid-high USD 22 mill, whilst the two-years-older "Aristos" 75/2006 Onomichi, Japan (SS 08/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago for USD 22,5 mill. Moreover, Concordia Maritime sold their three LR1's "Stena Provence", "Stena Primorsk" & "Stena Performance" 65/2006 Brodosplit, Croatia (SS 03/2026 & DD 04/2024, SS 05/2026, DD 06/2024 & SS 06/2026, DD 09/2024 respectively, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1B) to European buyers for USD 20 mill each, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Stena Paris" 65/2005 Brodosplit, Croatia (SS 12/2025, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1B) was sold in early July at USD 15 mill. Following the sale of the MR2 "Ridgebury Saturn" 50/2007 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 08/2022) back in August at USD 18 mill, Ridgebury Holdings have now committed the one-year-younger sister vessel "Ridgebury Voyager" 50/2008 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 01/2023) for USD 19 mill. In addition, Thenamaris sold their "Seabright" 46/2006 Stx, Korea (SS 11/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 18 mill. Further to the sale of the "Seabright", Thenamaris sold their MR1 "Seamercury" 39/2003 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 06/2023, Ice Class 1B) for region USD 11 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Stefanie" 37/2003 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 07/2023, Ice Class 1B) was sold in early August at USD 10,8 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low/mid usd 9,00's fio range. South African trips into China with chrome ore were fixed around usd 25,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 18,000/20,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 22,000/24,000 pd. West African trips via North Coast South America into Continent were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via South America were reported at the level of usd 15,000/18,000 pd. Continental trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 24,000/25,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were reported at the level of usd 19,000/21,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 935K/1M. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into India were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. East African trips into India were fixed around usd 23,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 230K. South African trips into India were reported at the level of usd 17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 150K. Continental trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 15,000 pd. South American trips into South Africa were reported at the level of usd 27,000/29,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 17,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 750K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 100K. Indian trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 18,000/19,000 pd. South American trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 22,000/24,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
