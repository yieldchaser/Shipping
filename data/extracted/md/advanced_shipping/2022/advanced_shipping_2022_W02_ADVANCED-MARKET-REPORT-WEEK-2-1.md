### Bulkers Market Commentary

NYK Line have committed their Panamax "El Sol Sale" 75/2002 Kanasashi, Japan (SS 01/2025, DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 11,5 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older "Braveheart" 74/2001 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in November at USD 13 mill. Moreover, the vintage "HH Pioneer" 73/1997 Daedong, Korea (SS 12/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 9 mill, while the one-year-younger "Qin Fa 18" 73/1998 Halla, Korea (SS/DD 07/2022) was sold a month ago at USD 10,45 mill. Ehime Kisen's Ultramax "Ikan Senyur" 61/2010 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed to Vietnamese buyers for region USD 22 mill. Note that the one-year-younger "Sunleaf Grace" 61/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 08/2023) was sold back in November for USD 21,5 mill basis delivery March-May 2022. The Supramax "Sparrow" 53/2005 Imabari, Japan (SS 03/2025, DD 07/2023) was sold to undisclosed buyers for USD 13,5 mill. For reference, the two- years-older "Xiang Hua" 53/2003 Toyohashi, Japan (SS/DD 05/2023) was sold a month ago at USD 11,1 mill. Swire & Sons have committed their Handysize "Tientsin" 37/2016 Imabari, Japan (SS 07/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 26,5 mill. Remind you that the one-year-younger "New Face" & "New Days" 38/2017 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS/DD 03/2022 & 01/2022 respectively, BWTS fitted both) were sold back in December at USD 27 mill each. Furthermore, the Korean built "Longshore" 34/2010 Spp, Korea (SS 01/2025, DD 03/2023) was sold to European buyers for high USD 15 mill, whilst the one-year-younger sister vessel "AS Elbia" 34/2011 Spp, Korea (SS 05/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in December at USD 16,75 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low/mid usd 7,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 19,000/20,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into Japan were reported fixed in the usd 23,000/24,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd. South East Asian trips via ECSA into China were fixed around usd 20,000/21,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips via ECSA into China were reported at the level of usd 21,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were fixed around usd 35,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 20,000/21,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from Philippines into China with nickel ore were reported fixed around usd 14,000 pd. A number of Indian iron ore trips into China were fixed around usd 16,000/17,000 pd. Indian trips via Persian Gulf into India with aggregates were reported at the level of usd 24,000/26,000 pd. On the period front, a Supramax were reported fixed in the usd 25,000 pd for 3/5 months employment basis delivery India.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. US Gulf trips into West Africa were reported fixed around usd 22,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 38,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
