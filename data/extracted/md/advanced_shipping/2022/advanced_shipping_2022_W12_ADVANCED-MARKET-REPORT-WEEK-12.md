### Bulkers Market Commentary

Kumiai Senpaku have sold their Newcastlemax "Azul Libero" 203/2004 Universal, Japan (SS 09/2024, DD 09/2022, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for high USD 18 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the three-years-younger sister vessels "Baosteel Expedition" 203/2007 Universal, Japan (SS 12/2025, DD 12/2023) was sold at USD 19,5 mill. The Kamsarmax "Ocean Garlic" 82/2012 Dalian, China (SS/DD 06/2022) from Far East Horizon was committed to undisclosed buyers for mid USD 21 mill. Note that earlier this month, the "Galaxy" 81/2012 Qingdao, China (SS/DD 11/2022) was sold at USD 19,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Pacific Carriers sold their "Atlantic Tulum" 58/2008 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 03/2023) to Singapore based buyers Diamond Star Shipping for USD 17,4 mill, while the "Friendly Seas" 58/2008 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 02/2023) was sold a week ago at USD 18 mill. Furthermore, the "Orient Rise" 56/2010 Qingshan, China (SS 04/2025, DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed to Greek buyers for USD 17,2 mill basis prompt delivery. For comparison, the one-year-younger "Atlantic Mexico" 56/2011 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 10/2023) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 17 mill. In addition, Greek owners have committed their "Lucky Sea" 52/2005 Yangzhou, China (SS 01/2025, DD 02/2023) to Chinese buyers for USD 12 mill. Greek owners sold their Handysize "Ionic Halo" 34/2012 Dae Sun, Korea (SS/DD 03/2022) to undisclosed buyers for region USD 19 mill basis SS/DD passed & BWTS fitted. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-older sister vessel "Majesty" 34/2011 Dae Sun, Korea (SS/DD 03/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 15,5 mill. Moreover, the Japanese owned "Global Echo" 28/2012 I-S, Japan (SS 04/2025, DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 15,3 mill. Note that back in February, the two-years-older "Tramontana" 28/2010 Shimanami, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 14,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Tsakos Shipping & Trading have reportedly sold their Aframax "Proteas" 117/2006 Hyundai, Korea (SS 05/2026, DD 08/2024, Ice Class 1A) for USD 19 mill. For reference, the one-year-older "Stena Arctica" 117/2005 Hyundai, Korea (SS 11/2025, DD 10/2023, Ice Class 1A) was sold a week ago at high USD 14 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low usd 11,00's fio range. A Capesize was fixed on a period at the usd 32,000 pd basis delivery South East Asia for 20/22 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 26,000/29,000 pd. CIS Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 43,000/44,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 26,000/28,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 32,000/33,000 pd. Indian trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 31,000 pd. Transatlantic round voyage trips were fixed around usd 28,000/30,000 pd. Inter- Continental trips via US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 28,000/29,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 50,000/53,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 30,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.3M. On the period front, Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 32,000 pd for 5/6 months employment basis delivery South East Asia. Also, a Panamax was fixed on a period at the usd 24,500 pd basis delivery Japan for 12 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from China via Philippines into China were fixed around usd 39,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 43,000/45,000 pd range. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 27,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K. Mediterranean trips into West Africa with clinker were fixed around usd 28,000/29,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 46,000/47,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of Inter-Continental trips via West Africa were reported at the level of usd 21,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 44,000/45,000 pd. A Lange Handysize was fixed on a period at the usd 38,000 pd basis delivery Japan for 5/7 months employment.
