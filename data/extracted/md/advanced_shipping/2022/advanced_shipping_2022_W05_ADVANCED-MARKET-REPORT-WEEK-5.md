### Bulkers Market Commentary

Giuseppe Bottiglieri Shipping have sold their Post Panamax sister vessels "Bottiglieri Franco Vela" 93/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS 07/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) & "Bottiglieri Giulio Borriello" 93/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS 01/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 17 & 18,5 mill respectively. Remind you that a couple of months ago, the sister vessel "Mayfair Spirit" 93/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS 05/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 19 mill with T/C attached until Q2 2022. Japanese owners have committed their Panamax "Nord Fortune" 76/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS 07/2026, DD 10/2024) to Greek buyers Newport for region USD 16 mill. Note that a month ago, the one-year-older sister vessel "Coral Diamond" 76/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at low USD 16 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Magda" 58/2010 Yangzhou, China (SS 07/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed to Greek buyers Costamare for USD 16,85 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older "Nikolas III" 58/2009 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 06/2024) was sold back in December at USD 17,5 mill. Chiba Shipping have sold their Handysize "Royal Innovation" 28/2011 Imabari, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers Alma Shipmanagement for region USD 13 mill, while the two-years-older sister vessel "Targa" 28/2009 Imabari, Japan (SS 05/2024, DD 03/2022) was sold back in December at USD 14,2 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Pantheon Tankers have sold their VLCC "Sea Lynx" 318/2004 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 11/2024, DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 32,5 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Argenta" 319/2005 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 02/2025, DD 03/2023) was sold a month ago at USD 31,5 mill. The Aframax "Crudesun" 115/2018 Daehan, Korea (SS/DD 07/2023) was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 47,6 mill. Remind you that back in December, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Prostar" 115/2019 Daehan, Korea (SS/DD 01/2024) was sold at USD 52 mill. Yamamaru Kisen have sold their MR2 "Prime Express" 46/2010 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD 11/2023) to Greek buyers for low USD 16 mill. Note that back in November, the one-year-older "Angel 62" 47/2009 Onomichi, Japan (SS 07/2024, DD 07/2022) was sold at USD 13,8 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout usd 26,250 pd basis delivery China for 11/13 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 19,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into South East Asia were reported fixed at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 18,000 pd. Australian trips into China with iron ore were reported at the level of usd 17,000 pd. Trips from Red Sea via Black Sea into China were fixed at the level of usd 22,000/23,000 pd. Inter-Mediterranean trips via Black Sea were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Mediterranean were reported around usd 17,000/18,000 pd. South American trips into Continent-Mediterranean range were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K/925K. Period employments rated about usd 21,500 pd for 4/6 months basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Persian Gulf trips into India were reported at the level of usd 20,000/21,000 pd. South African trips into China were fixed around usd 25,250 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 525K. Mediterranean trips into West Africa with clinker were reported at the level of usd 18,000 pd. Inter- Mediterranean trips via Black Sea with pig iron were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. US Gulf trips into North Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 15,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 20,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from Black Sea via Red Sea into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 18,000/19,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 14,000 pd. South American trips into Continent with wood pulp were fixed around usd 24,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
