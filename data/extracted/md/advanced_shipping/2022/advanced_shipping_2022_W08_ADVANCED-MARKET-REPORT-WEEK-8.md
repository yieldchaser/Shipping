### Bulkers Market Commentary

Ever Stella Shipping have sold their Capesize "Stella Hope" 180/2016 Dalian, China (SS 09/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) to UK based buyers Zodiac Maritime for USD 42,5 mill with index linked T/C attached for 1+1 years. Moreover, Korean owners committed their "Dong-A Astrea" 179/2010 Hyundai, Korea (SS 04/2025, DD 12/2023), "Dong-A Eos" 179/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS 09/2024, DD 08/2022) & "Dong-A Oknos" 179/2010 Hyundai, Korea (SS 02/2025, DD 05/2023) to Greek buyers Minerva for USD 81 mill enbloc. The vintage Panamax "Lily Breeze" 74/1998 Nkk, Japan (SS/DD 01/2023) was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 8,6 mill. Remind you that back in January, the one-year-older Korean built "HH Pioneer" 73/1997 Daedong, Korea (SS 12/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 9 mill. On the Supramax sector, Tomini Shipping sold their "Tomini Majesty" 57/2010 Zhejiang, China (SS 12/2024, DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 15,9 mill, while the one-year-younger "Mandarin Grace" & "Mandarin Trader" 56/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS 01/2026, DD 12/2023 & SS 07/2025, DD 05/2023 respectively, BWTS fitted both) from Dasin Holdings were committed to Chinese buyers for USD 17,1 mill each. Note that earlier this month, the "Magda" 58/2010 Yangzhou, China (SS 07/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 16,85 mill. Futhermore, Korean owners sold their "WP Ambition" 55/2015 Mitsui, Japan (SS 03/2025, DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) to Turkish buyers for USD 25 mill. For comparison, the two-years- older "Tanikaze" 56/2013 Minaminippon, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold in January at USD 23 mill. Nissen Kaiun have committed their Handysize "Supernova" 36/2012 Shikoku, Japan (SS/DD 06/2022, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Russian buyers for USD 20,5 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the "Crimson Princess" 38/2012 Naikai, Japan (SS/DD 06/2022) was sold at USD 18,9 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Chemnav have committed their Suezmax "Rineia" 159/2004 Hyundai, Korea (SS 03/2024, DD 04/2022) to Middle Eastern buyers for excess USD 18 mill. Note that the sister vessel "Kaveri Spirit" 159/2004 Hyundai, Korea (SS 01/2024, DD 04/2022) was sold a month ago at USD 15,8 mill. Further to the sale of "Rineia", Greek owners Chemnav acquired the LR2 "Phoenix Hope" 105/2008 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 11/2023) from Phoenix Energy Navigation for USD 18,25 mill. The MR1 "Sky" 37/2007 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 12/2022) of Genel Denizcilik was sold to undisclosed buyers for USD 8,1 mill. For comparison, the "Gulf Moon", "Gulf Mews" & "Gulf Mist" 37/2007 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD due within 2022) were sold a month ago at mid-high USD 7 mill each.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low/mid usd 10,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported at the usd 17,000 pd range. Indian trips via South Africa into China were fixed around usd 22,000 pd. Period employments rated about usd 25,000/26,000 pd for 2year basis delivery China.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 25,000/27,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 23,000/24,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 24,000/26,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 25,000/27,000 pd. Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 22,000/23,000 pd. South African trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 22,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.2M. Inter-Continental trips via Murmansk were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 33,000/34,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via North Coast South America into Continent were reported at the level of usd 21,000/22,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 23,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.25M. South American trips into Continent with Bauxite were reported at the level of usd 32,000 pd. On the period front, Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 29,000 pd for 1year employment basis delivery Japan.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 35,000 pd range. South East Asian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 27,000/29,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into Caribbean were fixed around usd 20,000/22,000 pd. Us Gulf trips into Mediterranean with petcoke were reported at the level of usd 23,000/24,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Continental trips into US Gulf with fertilizers were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. Trips from US East Coast into China with nickel ore were fixed around usd 27,000/28,000 pd. Trips from US East Coast into Continent with wood pellets were reported at the level of usd 20,000/21,000 pd. US Gulf trips into EC Mexico with scrap were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Mediterranean with petcoke were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 28,000/29,000 pd. A small Handysize was fixed on a period at the usd 26,000 pd basis delivery Persian Gulf for 4/6 months employment.
