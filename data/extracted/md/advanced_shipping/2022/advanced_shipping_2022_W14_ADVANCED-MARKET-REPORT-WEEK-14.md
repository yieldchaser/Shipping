### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Post Panamax "CMB Pauillac" 95/2012 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 08/2022) was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 25 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the two-years-older sister vessel "Double Fortune" 95/2010 Imabari, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at region USD 21 mill. Redwood Ocean Shipping have sold their Kamsarmax "Rosco Litchi" 82/2011 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 25 mill. Note that the one-year-older sister vessel "MBA Liberty" 82/2010 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 01/2025, DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 22 mill. Furthermore, Chinese owners sold their "Yangtze Xing Hua" 81/2012 Guangzhou, China (SS/DD 05/2022, BWTS fitted) for USD 22 mill, while the one-year-younger "Sea Neptune" 81/2013 Xiamen, China (SS/DD 03/2023) of Golden Ocean Group was sold to Turkish buyers at USD 23 mill. For comparison, the "Ocean Garlic" 82/2012 Dalian, China (SS/DD 06/2022) was sold a couple of weeks ago at mid USD 21 mill. Navios Maritime have committed their Supramax "Titan II" 57/2009 Stx, Korea (SS 09/2024, DD 08/2022, BWTS fitted) for USD 17,5 mill basis delivery August 2022, while the one-year-younger sister vessel "Karimu" 57/2010 Stx, Korea (SS 12/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 19 mill basis delivery April-July 2022. On the Handysize sector, MT Maritime Management sold their "Strategic Encounter" 33/2010 Zhejiang, China (SS 08/2025, DD 08/2023) to undisclosed buyers for low USD 14 mill. Remind you that back in February, the "Emil Selmer" 32/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS 12/2025, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 14,5 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid usd 8,00's fio range. North Pacific round voyage trips were reported at the usd 19,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 20,000/21,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 20,000/22,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into South East Asia were fixed around usd 19,000/20,000 pd. Indian trips via South Africa into India were reported at the level of usd 32,000/34,000 pd. West African trips via North Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 35,000/36,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast and US Gulf into China with petcoke were reported at the level of usd 37,000/38,000 pd. Inter-Mediterranean trips via East Coast South America and Red Sea were fixed around usd 24,000 pd. North Coast South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 26,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 165K. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from China into Persian Gulf with slag were fixed around usd 34,000 pd. Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 27,000/29,000 pd. South African trips into India were fixed around usd 26,400 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 640K. Continental trips into Mediterranean with scrap were reported at the level of usd 23,000/25,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 29,000/31,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into US Gulf with clinker were reported at the level of usd 21,000/22,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 42,000/43,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of trips from China into South East Asia with steels were reported fixed around usd 20,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via Baltic were reported at the level of usd 18,000/19,000 pd. Continental trips via Murmansk into East Coast South America with fertilizers were fixed around usd 38,000 pd. A Lange Handysize was fixed on a period at the usd 26,000 pd basis delivery Continent for 4/6 months employment.
