### Bulkers Market Commentary

Fukujin Kisen have sold their Capesize "Lowlands Sunrise" 181/2011 Koyo, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers Stealth Maritime for USD 26 mill. Remind you that earlier this month, Vafias Group acquired the one-year-older "Aquataine" 181/2010 Imabari, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) at USD 26 mill also. Furthermore, IS Dongseo committed the sister vessels "Wisdom of the Sea 2" & "Wisdom of the Sea 1" 180/2011 Daehan, Korea (SS 11/2025, DD 12/2023 & SS 02/2026, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted both) for high USD 23 mill each basis T/C attached until March-June 2023. Following the acquisition of the Ultramax "Ultra Trust" 61/2015 Tadotsu, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted, Scrubber fitted) back in September, Mssrs Eagle Bulk have now purchased the Chinese built "Stony Stream" 64/2015 Chengxi, China (SS 04/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) for low USD 24 mill basis delivery February-March 2023.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Avin International have committed their Suezmax "Kriti Breeze" 151/2003 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 11/2023) for USD 30,5 mill, while the one-year- younger Japanese built "Crescent Moon" 150/2004 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted, Scrubber fitted) of Zodiac Maritime was sold for USD 33,5 mill. For reference, the "Concord" 159/2005 Hyundai, Korea (SS 06/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold earlier this month at USD 33 mill. The Aframax "Houston Star" 116/2007 Universal, Japan (SS 07/2027, DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) from Atlas Maritime was sold to Gardsea Shipping for USD 39,5 mill. Note that the sister vessel "Nordbay" 116/2007 Universal, Japan (SS 02/2027, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold three weeks ago at USD 34 mill. Moreover, same buyers acquired the United Maritime's "Minoansea" 108/2008 SWS, China (SS/DD 08/2023) for USD 39 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Gullit" 109/2008 SWS, China (SS/DD 03/2023) was sold a week ago at USD 34 mill. Pareto AS have committed their MR1 "Cape Gavi" 37/2008 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 02/2023) to Greek buyers Chemnav for low USD 17 mill. Remind you that the same owners sold the sister vessel "Cape Guinea" 37/2008 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 11/2023) a month ago at USD 16,4 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 9,000/11,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 9,000/11,000 pd. Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 8,000/9,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. West African trips via US Gulf into Continent were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/600K. A Kamsarmax was reported fixed on a period at the level of usd 17,250 pd for 8/10 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 8,000/9,000 pd. A number of trips from Philippines into China were reported at the level of usd 7,000/8,000 pd. South American trips into Persian Gulf were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 400K/500K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of trips from China into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 7,000/8,000 pd. Inter-Mediterranean trips via Black Sea were fixed around usd 13,000/14,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
