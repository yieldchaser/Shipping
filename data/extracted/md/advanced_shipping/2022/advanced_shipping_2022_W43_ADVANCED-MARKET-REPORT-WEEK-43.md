### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Post Panamax "Pellonia" 93/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 03/2025) was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 17,2 mill. Remind you that the sister vessels "Jin Lang" & "Jin Mei" 93/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS 04/2025, DD 04/2023 & SS 03/2025, DD 04/2023 respectively) were sold a week ago at USD 17,25 mill each. China Minsheng Trust have sold their two Kamsarmax "CCS Orchid" 82/2017 Jiangsu, China (SS 01/2027, DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) & "MSXT Hera" 81/2018 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 54 mill enbloc. Note that back in September, the younger "Nord Corona" 81/2019 Jiangsu, China (SS 09/2024, DD 09/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 29 mill. Pan Ocean have committed their Panamax "Pan Diva" 76/2004 Sasebo, Japan (SS 09/2024, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) for low USD 13 mill. Furthermore, Apollonia Lines sold their "Blue Chip" 76/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 11/2022) to Korean buyers for low USD 15 mill, while the one-year- older "Seawind" 75/2006 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 15,2 mill. For reference, the "Bellatrix" 77/2006 Oshima, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 14,6 mill. The Ultramax "Van Star" 61/2011 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 12/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold to undisclosed buyers for high USD 21 mill, while the "Ultra Wollongong" 61/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 22 mill. On the Handysize sector, Japanese owners sold their "Belle Ocean" 28/2014 I-S, Japan (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for region USD 15 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older sister vessel "Malto Hope" 28/2013 I-S, Japan (SS/DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold in September at USD 13,6 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Eurotankers have committed their Suezmax "Euroglory" 166/2005 Brodosplit, Croatia (SS 04/2025, DD 01/2024) for USD 26 mill. For reference, the two-years-older Chinese built "Da Ming Hu" 159/2003 Bohai, China (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 22 mill. Following the sale of the MR2 "Gotland Carolina" 53/2006 Guangzhou, China (SS 12/2026, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A Super) a couple of months ago, Gotland Rederi have now sold the sister vessels "Gotland Sofia" 53/2007 Guangzhou, China (SS 03/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A Super) for USD 18 mill and the one-year-younger "Gotland Aliya" (SS/DD 07/2023, Ice Class 1A Super) & "Gotland Marieann" 53/2008 Guangzhou, China (SS/DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A Super) for USD 18,5 mill each to Chinese buyers.
