### Bulkers Market Commentary

Michele Bottiglieri Armatore have committed their Kamsarmax "MBA Liberty" 82/2010 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 01/2025, DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 22 mill. Remind you that back in December, the two- years-younger sister vessel "King Barley" 82/2012 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (basis SS/DD passed, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 22,7 mill. Nisshin Shipping sold their Ultramax "Hanton Trader II" 63/2014 Jiangsu, China (SS 08/2024, DD 06/2022, BWTS fitted) to Hong Kong based buyers Jinhui for USD 25,5 mill, while the one-year-younger "Drogba" 63/2015 Chengxi, China (SS 10/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 28,7 mill. For reference, the sisters "Andros Island" 63/2016 Zheijiang, China (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024) & "Syros Island" 63/2015 Zhejiang, China (SS 07/2025, DD 10/2023) were sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 25 mill each. On the Supramax sector, Pacific Carriers sold their "Atlantic Mexico" 56/2011 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 10/2023) for USD 17 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the sister vessels "Mandarin Grace" & "Mandarin Trader" 56/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS 01/2026, DD 12/2023 & SS 07/2025, DD 05/2023 respectively, BWTS fitted both) were sold at USD 17,1 mill each. In addition, the two-years-younger "Nathan Brandon" 56/2013 Huatai, China (SS/DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed for USD 19 mill. Further to the sale of the "Atlantic Mexico", Pacific Carriers committed their Handysize "Atlantic Veracruz" 28/2009 Shimanami, Japan (SS/DD 07/2022) to Vietnamese buyers for low-mid USD 13 mill. For comparison, the one- year-younger sister vessel "Tramontana" 28/2010 Shimanami, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 14,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The VLCC sister vessels "Nautilus" 307/2006 Dalian, China (SS 12/2026, DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) & "Navarin" 307/2007 Dalian, China (SS/DD 07/2022) of Ridgebury Tankers were sold to Korean buyers Sinokor for USD 63,5 mill enbloc. Note that back in January, the younger "New Talisman" 296/2009 Bohai, China (SS 09/2024, DD 09/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 36 mill. Teekay Corp have sold their Aframax "Axel Spirit" 115/2004 Samsung, Korea (SS 04/2024, DD 05/2022) to Chinese buyers for USD 13,2 mill. Moreover, the smaller "Bunga Kelana 10" 105/2004 Samsung, Korea (SS 09/2024, DD 10/2022) was committed to Singaporean buyers for low USD 12 mill, whilst the sister vessel "Bunga Kelana 9" 105/2004 Samsung, Korea (SS 08/2024, DD 08/2022) has gone to Indonesian buyers for low USD 12 mill also. The MR1 "Duke I" 40/2002 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 09/2026, DD 12/2024) was sold to undisclosed buyers for USD 7,1 mill. The smaller but younger "Torm Tevere" 37/2005 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 07/2025, DD 10/2023) from TORM was sold to Nigerian buyers for USD 8,3 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid/high usd 11,00's fio range. A Capesize was fixed on a period at the usd 34,500 pd basis delivery China for 11/14 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 32,000/35,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 29,000/31,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 36,000/38,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 22,000/25,000 pd. Indian Coastal trips with coal were reported fixed at the level of usd 33,000/35,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via US East Coast were fixed around usd 20,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 26,000/29,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.4M/1.6M. On the period front, Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 30,000 pd for 1year employment basis delivery China. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 32,000 pd basis delivery Continent for 12/14 months employment

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 45,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 43,000 pd. Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 33,000 pd. West African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 26,500/27,500 pd. Mediterranean trips into US Gulf with cement were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd. South American trips into US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 34,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from Japan into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 60,000 pd. Trips from China into India were fixed around usd 38,000 pd. Indian trips into Red Sea were reported at the level of usd 52,000/54,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
