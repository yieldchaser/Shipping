### Bulkers Market Commentary

The vintage Panamax "Princess Jasmine" 73/1997 Sumitomo, Japan (SS/DD/BWTS due) was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 6 mill. Note that back in July, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Fortune Union" 73/1998 Sumitomo, Japan (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 8,5 mill. Diana Shipping announced the acquisition of nine Japanese Ultramax vessels built between 2015 and 2018 from Sea Trade Holdings Inc. for an aggregate price of USD 330 mill, of which USD 220 mill will be paid in cash and USD 110 mill is to be paid in the form of 18.487.395 newly issued common shares of the Company, at a price of USD 5,95 per share. The names of the vessels are "STH Athens", STH Chiba", "STH London", "STH Oslo", "STH Montreal", "STH Kure", "STH New York", "STH Tokyo" & "STH Sydney" and the deliveries are expected to occur during Q4 2022. German owners have committed their OHBS Handysize "Paxi" 28/2010 Imabari, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for high USD 15 mill. For reference, the conventional "Global Aquarius" 28/2010 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2025, DD 02/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 14,8 mill basis delivery October 2022.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The VLCC "Maran Andromeda" 320/2005 Daewoo, Korea (SS 01/2025, DD 09/2022, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) from Angelicoussis Shipping Group was sold to Singapore based buyers for USD 37 mill, while the Japanese built "Altair Trader" 311/2005 Mitsui, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 09/2023) was sold a week ago at USD 36 mill. LL Energy have committed their Aframax "Oberon" 106/2006 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS/DD 11/2022, BWTS fitted) for low-mid USD 25 mill. For comparison, the two-years-older "Blue Pride" 115/2004 Daewoo, Korea (SS 08/2024, DD 11/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 23 mill. Moreover, NGM sold their "Baroness" 105/2011 Sumitomo, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 34,5 mill. Ridgebury Tankers sold their three MR2 sister vessels "Ridgebury Rosemary E", "Ridgebury Alexandra Z" & "Ridgebury Cindy A" 50/2009 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 05/2024, SS/DD 01/2024 & SS/DD 01/2024 respectively) to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 22 mill each basis delivery September 2022. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-older sister vessel "Grand" 50/2008 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at low-mid USD 19 mill.
