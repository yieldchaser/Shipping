### Bulkers Market Commentary

Olympia Ocean Carriers sold their vintage Capesize "Arethousa" 169/2001 Sasebo, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 15,1 mill. Note that the one-year-younger "Agia Trias" 185/2002 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 01/2023) was sold a week ago at USD 15,5 mill. The Post Panamax "Lara Venture" 93/2011 Shanhaiguan, China (SS 09/2026, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) of Wah Kwong Shipping was committed to Middle Eastern buyers for low-mid USD 20 mill. For comparison, the "D Skalkeas" 93/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS 05/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 20 mill. Hisafuku Kisen have sold their Kamsarmax "Nord Gemini" 81/2017 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 02/2027, DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers Neda Maritime for USD 30,65 mill, while the sister vessel "Bulk Holland" 81/2017 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 04/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold to Greek buyers Newport for USD 29,65 mill. The Ultramax "Ultra Wollongong" 61/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) from Pacific Basin Shipping was sold to Hong Kong based buyers for USD 22 mill. Remind you that the Scrubber fitted "Ultra Dynamic" 61/2011 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 07/2026, DD 05/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold in September at USD 22,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Eurobulk sold their "Corinthian Emerald" 57/2012 Stx, Korea (SS 03/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 20,4 mill. Note that the Japanese built "Pacific Hero" 58/2012 Kawasaki, Japan (SS 01/2025, DD 01/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 20,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

COSCO Shipping Energy Transportation have sold their Suezmax "Da Ming Hu" 19/2003 Bohai, China (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 22 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Lila Guangzhou" 159/2004 Bohai, China (SS 05/2024, DD 12/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold in September at USD 23,5 mill. The Aframax sister vessels "Bluesea" & "Parosea" 113/2006 Samsung, Korea (SS 02/2026, DD 12/2024 & SS 05/2026, DD 04/2025 accordingly, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A) of United Maritime were sold for USD 62,5 mill enbloc. For reference, the Scrubber fitted sister vessel "Aristodimos" 113/2006 Samsung, Korea (SS 04/2026, DD 03/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted, Ice Class 1A) was sold in early September at USD 32 mill. Greek buyers United Overseas Group have acquired the MR2 "Swift Omaha" 46/2010 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 12/2025, DD 03/2024) for USD 25,5 mill, while same buyers bought a week ago the one-year-younger Japanese built "Dee4 Fig" 45/2011 Onomichi, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 26,5 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low/mid usd 9,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported in the usd 13,000/14,000 pd range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds continued rating primarily in the usd 17,000/19,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 15,000/17,000 pd range. Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. A Southeast Asian trip via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 17,000/18,000 pd. Indian Coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Inter-continental trips via US Gulf were fixed around usd 18,000/19,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were reported at the level of usd 27,000/28,000 pd. On the period front, Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 19,000 pd for 5/7 months employment basis delivery China. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 16,250 pd basis delivery China for 11/13 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A reposition fixture for an Ultramax at usd 18,500 pd on a Korean backhaul. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 15,000/17,000 pd range. Indonesian trips into Continent were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Philippines nickel ore trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd. Usd 24,000 pd on a US Gulf trip run into China. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. Trips from China into Southeast Asia were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 11,000/12,000 pd range. West African trips via Continent into Mediterranean were fixed at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
