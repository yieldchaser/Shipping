### Bulkers Market Commentary

Following the sale of the Kamsarmax "Rosco Litchi" 82/2011 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 25 mill, Chinese owners now sold the sister vessel "Rosco Palm" 82/2011 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 26 mill. Furthermore, the Korean built "Derby" 81/2011 Stx, Korea (SS 07/2025, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed for excess USD 24 mill. Remind you that three weeks ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Darya Kirthi" 80/2012 Stx, Korea (SS 02/2027, DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 25,25 mill basis delivery May-June 2022. Greek owners EuroDry have acquired the Panamax "Santa Cruz" 76/2005 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 07/2023) for USD 15,75 mill with T/C included until July 2022 at 14.800 usd/day. Note that the one-year-older "Maribella" 76/2004 Imabari, Japan (SS 12/2024, DD 02/2023) was sold three weeks ago at USD 14,9 mill basis delivery April-June 2022. In addition, the vintage "Shao Shan 1" 74/1997 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 06/2022) from COSCO was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 8,6 mill. For reference, the one- year-younger "Lily Breeze" 74/1998 Nkk, Japan (SS/DD 01/2023) was sold back in February at the same price. On the Supramax sector, Japanese owners sold their "GDF Suez North Sea" 55/2012 IHI, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 22 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older "Awesome Ashley" 55/2011 Mitsui, Japan (SS 07/2024, DD 07/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at region USD 21 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Idemitsu Kosan committed their VLCC "Nissho Maru" 300/2004 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 05/2024) to reportedly Greek buyers for region USD 30 mill. Note that earlier this month, the one-year-younger "Tokio" 306/2005 Mitsubishi, Japan (SS 02/2025, DD 02/2023) was sold at USD 31,5 mill. Eastern Pacific Shipping have committed their Aframax "Banda Sea" 105/2007 Sumitomo, Japan (SS/DD 05/2022, Scrubber fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 21 mill, while the one-year-younger "Berica" 115/2008 Sasebo, Japan (SS/DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at region USD 23 mill. Moreover, the "Astro Sculptor" 105/2003 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 01/2023) from Pantheon Tankers was sold at USD 15,6 mill. Remind you that the Japanese built "Blue Power" 106/2003 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 09/2023) was sold back in March at USD 13,5 mill.
