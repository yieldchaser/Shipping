### Bulkers Market Commentary

Neda Maritime have sold their Kamsarmax "Alexandra" 82/2006 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 12/2025, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) to Turkish buyers for low-mid USD 16 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Pedhoulas Trader" 82/2006 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 15,85 mill basis delivery December 2022. Chinese buyers have acquired the Panamax sister vessels "Agri Kinsale" & "Agri Queen" 77/2009 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 09/2024 & SS/DD 10/2024 respectively, BWTS fitted) for USD 16 mill each. Note that the two-years- older "Coral Emerald" 75/2007 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 10/2023) was sold in September at USD 14,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, GENCO sold their "Genco Bourgogne" 58/2010 Yangzhou, China (SS 01/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) to Bangladeshi buyers for mid-high USD 15 mill. For reference, the one-year-older Taurus Honor" 56/2009 Jiangsu, China (SS 11/2024, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 14,8 mill. Furthermore, German owners committed their "Cas Avanca" 55/2009 Hyundai-Vinashin, Vietnam (SS/DD 10/2024) for USD 14,3 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Clarke Quay" 55/2010 Hyundai-Vinashin, Vietnam (SS 11/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 17,1 mill. Japanese owners have committed their OHBS Handysize "Lodestar Pacific" 33/2015 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS 03/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers Evalend Shipping for high USD 19 mill, while the two-years-older "Dream Ocean" 33/2013 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 17,65 mill. Moreover, Korean owners sold the "Promise 2" 32/2010 Samho, Korea (SS 09/2025, DD 09/2023) to undisclosed buyers for USD 13,8 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the "Ortolan Alpha Strait" 34/2010 Seko, Korea (SS 08/2025, DD 05/2023) was sold at USD 15 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Phoenix Energy Navigation have sold their Aframax "Phoenix Beacon" 105/2011 Hyundai, Korea (SS 09/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers Performance Shipping for USD 35 mill basis delivery December 2022. Remind you that the Japanese built "Baroness" 105/2011 Sumitomo, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold in August at USD 34,5 mill. The MR2 "Jo Ask" 47/2007 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 11/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) of Nyhill Shipping was sold to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 20,5 mill. In addition, the one-year-younger "Energy Progress" & "Energy Puma" 46/2008 Sungdong, Korea (SS/DD 07/2023 & SS/DD 11/2023 respectively) were sold to Middle Eastern buyers also at USD 20 mill each.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low usd 9,00's fio range. Transatlantic round voyage trips were reported at the level of usd 28,000 pd. Trips from South East Asia via ECSA- West Africa into China were fixed around usd 24,250 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 18,000/20,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Indonesian Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 23,000/24,000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 23,000 pd. South African trips into Continent were fixed around usd 13,000/14,000 pd. Inter- Continental trips via North Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 22,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were fixed around usd 31,000/32,000 pd. On the period front, Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 18,500 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery China. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 17,750 pd basis delivery Japan for 8/10 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from China into India were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. US Gulf trips via Neo Panama into China were fixed around usd 19,600 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 960K. US East Coast trips into West Coast India were reported at the level of usd 24,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from China into South East Asia were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,000 pd. South East Asian trips via Australia into China were fixed around usd 15,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
