### Bulkers Market Commentary

Chinese owners have committed their Post Panamax "TW Manila" 93/2012 Jiangsu, China (SS 04/2027, DD 04/2025) for USD 19 mill. Remind you that in the end of October, the sister vessels "Great Glen" 93/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS 04/2025, DD 03/2023) & "Great Animation"93/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS 11/2025, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) were sold at USD 37 mill enbloc. On the Supramax sector, the "Sagar Moti" 58/2012 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 06/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) of NYK Line was committed to Far Eastern buyers for excess USD 17 mill. For reference, the three-years- older sister vessel "Sagarjeet" 58/2009 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 05/2024) was sold at USD 16,15 mill. Following the acquisition of the Handysize "Seastar Harrier" 40/2022 Hakodate, Japan (SS 11/2027, DD 11/2025) at USD 30,9 mill, Japanese owners Daido Kaiun purchased the sister vessel "Seastar Hawk" 40/2022 Hakodate, Japan (SS 08/2027, DD 08/2025) at the same price.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The VLCC "Syfnos" 298/2006 Universal, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 12/2023, Scrubber fitted) from Aeolos Management was sold to UAE based buyers for USD 56 mill, while the one-year-older Korean built "Seaking" 318/2005 Hyundai, Korea (SS 02/2025, DD 04/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 51,5 mill. Eletson Corp have committed their LR1 "Antikeros" 69/2004 Daewoo, Korea (SS 10/2024, DD 01/2023) for region USD 13 mill and the two-years-younger "Strofades" 69/2006 Daewoo-Mangalia, Romania (SS 11/2025, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) for USD 17,3 mill. For comparison, the "Freight Margie" 70/2004 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 06/2024) was sold a week ago at USD 14,6 mill. Norden have sold their MR2 sister vessels "Nord Magic" & "Nord Minute" 50/2009 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 09/2024 & SS/DD 03/2024 respectively, BWTS fitted) for USD 25 mill each. Note that a week ago, the "Pyxis Malou" 50/2009 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 24,5 mill. Moreover, the "Centennial Misumi" 47/2008 Onomichi, Japan (SS/DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) of MK Centennial Maritime was sold for USD 21,5 mill. Remind you that back in October, the "Starman" 46/2008 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 12/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 21 mill.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 10,000/12,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 11,000/13,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South African trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 12,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 200K. Inter-Continental trips via US Gulf were fixed around usd 16,000/17,000 pd. Continental trips into China were reported at the level of usd 23,000/24,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 17,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K/800K. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/19,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K/950K. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. South African trips into China were fixed around usd 16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 170K. Persian Gulf trips into East Coast India with urea were reported at the level of usd 13,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were reported at the level of usd 27,000 pd. South American trips into Continent with steels were fixed around usd 20,000/21,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/15,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 400K/550K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. South America Coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 22,000/25,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
