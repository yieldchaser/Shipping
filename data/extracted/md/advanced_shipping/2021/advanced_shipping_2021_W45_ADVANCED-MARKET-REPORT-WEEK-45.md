### Bulkers Market Commentary

Foremost Group have sold their Capesize "Bao May" 178/2010 SWS, China (SS 05/2025, DD 05/2023) to undisclosed buyers for USD 32 mill. Note that the one-year-younger "Cape Spring" 180/2011 Qingdao, China (SS 09/2026, DD 08/2024) was sold a couple of weeks ago at excess USD 30 mill basis delivery January 2022. Moreover, despite being falsely reported as sold back in October at USD 16,2 mill basis delivery Q1 2022, the "ASL Mars" 175/2004 SWS, China (SS 08/2024, DD 07/2022) is now committed for high USD 16 mill. The Kamsarmax "Ocean Rosemary" 82/2013 Dalian, China (SS/DD 04/2023) was sold at region USD 23 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year- older sister vessel "Xin Hua" 82/2012 Dalian, China (SS/DD 07/2022) was sold at USD 22 mill basis delivery December 2021 - January 2022. Furthermore, the "Majulah Harbourfront" 82/2014 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 11/2024, DD 10/2022) was committed to Far Eastern buyers for USD 31 mill. For reference, the two-years-older sister vessel "Ranger" 82/2012 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 07/2022) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 24 mill basis delivery Q4 2021. Nautical Bulk Holding have committed their Ultramax "Nautical Alice" 63/2016 Jiangsu, China (SS 06/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) for USD 28,5 mill basis delivery Q1 2022. Remind you that the same owners sold the sister vessels "Nautical Runa" 63/2015 Jiangsu, China (SS 09/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) & "Nautical Lucia" 63/2016 Jiangsu, China (SS 01/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) back in September for USD 55 mill enbloc. In addition, the "Ikan Senyur" 61/2010 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) from Ehime Kisen was sold for USD 22 mill. On the Handysize sector, the "Lake Dany" 28/2008 Shimanami, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold to her current Charterers for USD 11,9 mill. Note that the two-years-younger sister vessel "Paloma" 28/2010 Shimanami, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 08/2023) was sold back in September for USD 13 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Norden have sold their two MR2 sister vessels "Nord Skate" & "Nord Stingray" 51/2009 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 01/2024 & SS 05/2024, DD 06/2022 respectively, BWTS & Scrubber fitted both) to undisclosed buyers for USD 17 mill each. Moreover, following the acquisition of the "Ivy Express" 51/2009 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 02/2024) at USD 15 mill back in October, Greek buyers Spring Marine Management acquired the Japanese built "Angel 62" 47/2009 Onomichi, Japan (SS 07/2024, DD 07/2022) from Astar Marine for USD 13,8 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid/high usd 12,00's fio range. Pacific round voyage trips were reported at the level of usd 37,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 20,000/22,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 21,000/23,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into Malaysia were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. Trips from India via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 28,000/30,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips via Black Sea into China were reported at the level of usd 28,000/29,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via Baltic were fixed around usd 27,000/30,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into Continent were reported at the level of usd 28,000/29,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 30,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.17M. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 22,000 pd for 12/14 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

CIS Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 18,000/20,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into East Coast India were fixed around usd 32,000/34,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into South East Asia with fertilizers were reported at the level of usd 46,250 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean with scrap were fixed around usd 36,000/38,000 pd. Also, Continental trips into East Coast South America with fertilizers were reported at the level of usd 35,000/36,000 pd. North Coast South American trips with coal into East Coast South America were fixed around usd 28,000/30,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of trips from Black Sea into Continent were reported fixed around usd 37,000/38,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Continent with wood pellets were fixed around usd 26,000/28,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 36,000/38,000 pd. South American trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 38,000/40,000 pd. Also, South American trips into US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 38,000/40,000 pd. A large Handysize was fixed on period at the usd 24,000 pd basis delivery South America for 12 months employment.
