### Bulkers Market Commentary

Following the purchase of the Capesize "Frontier Phoenix" 181/2011 Koyo, Japan (SS 11/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted), Greek buyers NGM acquired the Chinese built "DS Charme" 176/2011 Jiangsu Rongsheng, China (SS 06/2026, DD 06/2024) from DS Shipping for USD 28 mill including index linked T/C at 89% of 5TC until July 2022. K Line have committed their Kamsarmax "Vijayanagar" 82/2010 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 02/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 24 mill. For reference, the "AM Express" 82/2010 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 01/2025, DD 09/2022) was sold a month ago at USD 21 mill. The Panamax "Navios Altair I" 74/2006 Hudong-Zhonghua, China (SS/DD 12/2021) of Navios Maritime was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 13,9 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Cabeiro" 74/2007 Hudong-Zhonghua, China (SS/DD 01/2022) was sold at USD 13,3 mill. Following the sale of their Ultramax "Nord Peak" 61/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS 03/2026, DD 05/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) from three weeks ago at USD 22,5 mill, Norden have sold the one-year-younger sister vessel "Nord Summit" 61/2012 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 08/2022, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 22,5 mill also. On the Supramax sector, Hsin Chien Marine sold their "New Pride" 58/2009 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 01/2022, BWTS fitted) for USD 18,8 mill basis delivery December 2021, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Belcargo" 58/2008 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 17 mill basis delivery October- November 2021. Moreover, Tolani Group sold their "Prabhu Gopal" 56/2003 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 03/2023) for USD 13,3 mill, whilst the "Bao Ella" 53/2006 Imabari, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed for USD 14,6 mill. The Handysize "Paloma" 28/2010 Shimanami, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 08/2023) of Diligent was sold to undisclosed buyers for USD 13 mill. Remind you that back in July, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Star Life" 28/2011 Shimanami, Japan (SS 03/2026, DD 01/2024) was sold at USD 11,25 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Note that the "Tmn Pride" 48/2006 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 10/2021) was sold a couple of weeks ago at low USD 8 mill also.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low usd 13,00's fio range. A Capesize was fixed on period at the usd 27,000 pd basis delivery South East Asia for 12 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 32,000/33,000 pd. Trips from China via US Gulf into China were fixed around usd 31,000/33,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 34,000/36,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 31,000/32,000 pd and into Persian Gulf at the level of usd 32,000/33,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 34,000/36,000 pd. Indian trips via Black Sea into China were fixed around usd 34,000/35,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via Baltic were reported at the level of usd 33,000/35,000 pd. Inter-Mediterranean trips via Black Sea were fixed around usd 31,000/33,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 40,000 pd. On the period front, Kamsarmaxes were reported fixed at the level of usd 30,500/31,750 pd for 6/8 months employment basis delivery South East Asia.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 35,000/36,000pd. Indonesian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 35,000/36,000 pd. A number of trips from Philippines with nickel ore into China were reported at the level of usd 35,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips via Persian Gulf into East Coast India with gypsum were fixed around usd 46,000/48,000 pd. South African trips into India were reported at the level of usd 31,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K/725K. Inter- Mediterranean trips via Black Sea were fixed around usd 40,000/41,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Indian trips into US Gulf were fixed around usd 38,000/39,000 pd. Trips from Black Sea into US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 34,000/36,000 pd. South American trips via River Plate into South America were fixed around usd 32,000/34,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 20,000/21,000 pd. A Large Handysize was fixed on a period at the usd 28,000 pd basis delivery ECSA for 6/8 months employment with redelivery Atlantic.
