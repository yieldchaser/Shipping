### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Panamax "Priscilla Venture" 77/2008 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 03/2023) was committed to Chinese buyers for USD 18,25 mill, while a week ago the "Lemessos Queen" 76/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS 10/2023, DD 10/2021, BWTS fitted) was sold at mid-high USD 18 mill. Asia Pacific Shipping sold three Ultramax sister vessels, the "Asia Ruby III" 63/2014 Jinling, China (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) for low-mid USD 22 mill including T/C attached, the "Asia Ruby II" 63/2014 Jinling, China (SS 04/2024, DD 04/2022, BWTS fitted) for low-mid USD 24 mill basis prompt delivery and the "Asia Ruby IV" 63/2014 Jinling, China (SS 07/2024, DD 08/2022, BWTS fitted) for high USD 23 mill. In addition, the "Nord Peak" 61/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS 03/2026, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold to Hong Kong buyers for USD 22,5 mill. The Supramax "Atlantic Ensenada" 55/2006 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 04/2023) of Pacific Carriers was committed to Bangladeshi buyers for USD 15,7 mill. Note that a week ago, the two-years-younger "Aditya" 55/2008 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 02/2023) was sold for USD 16,5 mill basis delivery September-October 2021. On the Handysize sector, the "Nereus Island" 38/2014 Imabari, Japan (SS 04/2024, DD 03/2022) from Yahata Kisen was sold to Pacific Basin for USD 21 mill, whilst Masumoto Shipping sold the one-year-younger "Pacific Bulker" 36/2015 Shikoku, Japan (SS 10/2024, DD 10/2022) for USD 21 mill also. Moreover, Premuda sold their "Four Diamond" 34/2011 Pha Rung, Vietnam (SS/DD 10/2021) to UAE buyers for mid-high USD 12 mill & the two- years-younger sister vessel "Four Emerald" 34/2013 Pha Rung, Vietnam (SS/DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) to Turkish buyers Deval Shipping for low USD 15 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Thenamaris have sold their Aframax "Seafalcon" 112/2002 Samho, Korea (SS/DD 02/2022) to Chinese buyers for USD 13,5 mill. Note that three weeks ago, the one-year-younger "Astro Saturn" 105/2003 Daewoo, Korea (SS 04/2023, DD 08/2021) was sold at USD 12,4 mill. The LR1 "Cabo Kamui" 74/2016 Sungdong, Korea (SS/DD 11/2021, BWTS fitted) & the one-year-younger sister vessel "Cabo Misaki" 74/2017 Sungdong, Korea (SS/DD 03/2022, BWTS fitted) of Nisshin Shipping were sold to Chilean buyers Ultratank for USD 31,5 mill each. Remind you that back in June, the sister vessel "Nord Lavender" 74/2017 Sungdong, Korea (SS/DD 07/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 31 mill.
