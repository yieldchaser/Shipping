### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Panamax "Yutai Ambitions" 77/2008 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed to Greek buyers for USD 18,7 mill. For reference, the "Okeanos Bliss" 76/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS 11/2023, DD 10/2021) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 18,75 mill basis delivery October-November 2021 with DD passed & BWTS fitted. Moreover, the vintage "Braveheart" 74/2001 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) from AM Nomikos was committed for USD 13 mill. For comparison, the "Banasol" 72/2001 Oshima, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 14 mill. Grieg Star AS sold to Greek buyers their Ultramax sister vessels "Star Crios" 63/2012 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 06/2022) for USD 21,5 mill basis prompt delivery Far East & the "Star Damon" 63/2012 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted) for USD 22,5 mill with T/C attached until March-May 2022 at 36.000 usd/pd. Japanese owners sold their OHBS Handysize "Royal Justice" 37/2012 Saiki, Japan (SS/DD 12/2022, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for region USD 19,5 mill. Note that the "Crest Navigator" 36/2012 Shikoku, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold three weeks ago at USD 21,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Maran Tankers have sold their VLCC "Maran Corona" 306/2003 Daewoo, Korea (SS 07/2023, DD 12/2021) for USD 28,5 mill. Latest similar sale was the one-year-younger "Gilos" 319/2004 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 10/2024, DD 08/2022), which was sold a month ago at USD 29 mill. The Suezmax "Densa Whale" 158/2012 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 03/2022, BWTS fitted) from Marinsa Denizcilik was sold to Greek buyers Thenamaris for region USD 32 mill. Hyundai Glovis committed their two MR2 sister vessels "PTI Rhine" & "PTI Amazon" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 02/2022 & SS/DD 03/2022 respectively) to undisclosed buyers for USD 9,9 mill each. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the sister vessel "Star Eagle" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 10/2022) was sold at USD 11,75 mill. Furthermore, the MR1 "Seaways Bodie" 37/2006 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 01/2022) was committed for USD 7,6 mill, while the "Aiolos" 37/2007 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 03/2022) was sold three weeks ago at USD 8 mill.
