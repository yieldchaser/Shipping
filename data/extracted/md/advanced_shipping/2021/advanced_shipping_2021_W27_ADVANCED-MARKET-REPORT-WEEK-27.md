### Bulkers Market Commentary

Transmed Shipping have committed their Capesize "Mangas" 174/2011 Boihai, China (SS 03/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 24 mill, while the one-year-older "Springbank" 177/2010 New Times, China (SS 11/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 26,5 mill. Moreover, the vintage "Leadership" 171/2001 Koyo, Japan (SS/DD 10/2021) of Seanergy Maritime was committed for USD 12,5 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-younger "King Sail" 177/2002 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 03/2022) was sold at USD 12 mill. The Kamsarmax "Sea Express" 79/2012 Cosco Dalian, China (SS/DD 07/2022) from Seatankers Management was committed to Greek buyers for high USD 18 mill. Note that back in June, the two-years-older "Xin Feng" 79/2010 New Century, China (SS 09/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 16,5 mill. On the Panamax sector, the "Qc Matilde" 76/2002 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 04/2025, DD 03/2023) was committed to undisclosed buyers for high USD 10 mill, whilst the one-year-younger "Koulitsa" 76/2003 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 13,6 mill. In addition, the Korean built "Aquaman" 75/2001 Samho, Korea (SS 08/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold to Far Eastern buyers for USD 11 mill. Byzantine Maritime sold their Supramax "Lara" 56/2008 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 15,3 mill and Belships committed their one- year-older "Belfri" 55/2007 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 06/2022) for USD 15,4 mill. For comparison reasons, the "Darya Lakshmi" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS 07/2024, DD 10/2022) was sold a week ago at USD 16,5 mill. The Handysize "Ming Yuan" 33/2010 Taizhou, China (SS 07/2025, DD 08/2023) was sold to Greek buyers for USD 11 mill. Note that the one-year- younger "Malen" 34/2011 Shanhaiguan, China was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 13 mill basis SS/DD passed & BWTS fitted.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid usd 11,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported around usd 30,000 pd levels. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 29,000/30,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 34,000/35,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 35,000/37,000 pd. Indian trips via Black Sea into China were fixed around usd 34,000/35,000 pd. Inter- Continental trips via Baltic were reported at the level of usd 43,000/44,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 47,000/48,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 26,000/27,000 pd. A number of trips from Philippines with nickel ore into China were reported at the level of usd 31,000 pd. Indian iron ore trips into China were fixed around usd 40,000 pd. South African trips into India were reported at the level of usd 33,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 550K. Continental trips into Mediterranean via Baltic were fixed around usd 35,000/36,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into South Africa were reported at the level of usd 41,000/42,000 pd. A Supramax was fixed on period at the usd 32,700 pd basis delivery Japan for 4/5 months employments with redelivery PG-Japan range.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. West African trips via South America into Mediterranean with pulp were fixed around usd 30,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via Baltic with steels were reported at the level of usd 25,000 pd. South American trips via River Plate into Continent were fixed around usd 42,000/43,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
