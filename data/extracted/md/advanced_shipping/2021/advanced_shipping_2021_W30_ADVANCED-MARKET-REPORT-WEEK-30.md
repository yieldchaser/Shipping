### Bulkers Market Commentary

Japanese owners committed their Post Panamax "Harvest Sky" 95/2013 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 23 mill basis delivery October 2021, Meanwhile, the "Dyna Camellia" 91/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 03/2022, BWTS fitted) from Hsin Chien Marine was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 17,5 mill basis delivery September-October 2021. Marubeni Corp sold their Kamsarmax "Glorious Wind" 83/2010 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 09/2023) to Greek buyers for excess USD 20 mill basis delivery November-December 2021. Note that the one-year-younger "Magnolia" 82/2011 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 22 mill basis delivery February 2022. On the Ultramax sector, the "Komi" 63/2015 Cosco Zhoushan, China (SS 01/2025, DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold to Greek buyers for USD 25 mill basis delivery September 2021 with T/C attached until November of 2021. Moreover, Big Fame Shipping sold their "Ocean Phoenix Tree" 62/2017 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 09/2022, BWTS fitted) for USD 29,5 mill basis delivery January 2022. In addition, Archangel Pacific sold their "Calico Jack" 61/2015 I-S, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) & "Phoenix Rising" 60/2015 Mitsui, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 55 mill enbloc including balance of T/C. AM Nomikos sold their Supramax "Dory" 55/2010 IHI, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 04/2024, BWTS & Grabs fitted) for USD 17,5 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older "Nord Trust" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS 10/2024, DD 10/2022) was sold a week ago at USD 16 mill. The Handysize "Ms Charm" 32/2010 Zhejiang, China (SS 07/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold to undisclosed buyers for USD 12,5 mill. For comparison, the "Ming Yuan" 33/2010 Taizhou, China (SS 07/2025, DD 08/2023) was sold earlier this month at USD 11 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

NYK Line have committed their VLCC "Tsuruga" 310/2009 Imabari, Japan (SS 10/2024, DD 10/2022) to Greek buyers for USD 39 mill. Note that the Korean built "Athenian Victory" 317/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS 09/2024, DD 08/2022) was sold back in June at USD 42,5 mill. Sovcomflot sold their Suezmax sister vessels "Scf Ural" & "Scf Caucasus" 159/2002 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 07/2022 & 03/2022 respectively) to Far Eastern buyers for USD 16,4 mill each. Remind you that the "Vladimir Velikiy" 160/2002 Samho, Korea (SS/DD 01/2022) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 16,2 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of high usd 13,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 28,000/29,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 28,000/29,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into China with iron ore were fixed around usd 31,000/32,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 30,000/32,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via West Africa with bauxite were fixed around usd 30,000/31,500 pd. Continental trips into China were reported at the level of usd 49,000/49,500 pd. Inter-Mediterranean trips via Black Sea were fixed around usd 33,000/35,000 pd. On the period front a Kamsarmax were reported fixed in the usd 31,000 pd for 3/5 months employment basis delivery in the Pacific.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment with limited fresh information. A number of Chinese trips into Philippines with steels were reported fixed in the usd 25,000/26,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 41,000/42,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 30,000/31,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into Mediterranean with timber were reported at the level of usd 22,000/23,000 pd. US Gulf trips via Mississippi River into West Coast Central America were fixed around usd 35,000/36,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
