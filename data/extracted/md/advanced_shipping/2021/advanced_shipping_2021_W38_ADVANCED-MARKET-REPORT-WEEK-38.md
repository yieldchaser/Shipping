### Bulkers Market Commentary

Carras have committed their Capesize" Aquabella" 177/2005 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 11/2024, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for high USD 19 mill. Alios Bulkers sold their Panamax "Bellamys" 76/2005 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 01/2025, DD 01/2023, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 17 mill. Note that a week ago, the "Great Talent" 76/2005 Sasebo, Japan (SS 01/2025, DD 02/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at the same price. On the Supramax sector, ORIX Corp sold their sister vessels "Million Bell" & "Glad Mark" 58/2012 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 10/2022 & SS/DD 07/2022 accordingly) for USD 44 mill enbloc basis delivery Q1 2022, whilst the "Trans Oceanic" 58/2012 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 07/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 23 mill. Moreover, Fujian Yongjia Shipping committed their "Gutian Loyal" 52/2004 Oshima, Japan (SS 11/2024, DD 07/2022, BWTS fitted) for USD 13,8 mill. Remind you that a couple of weeks ago, the two-years-younger "Bao Ella" 53/2006 Imabari, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at region USD 15 mill. The Handysize "Ocean Opal" 37/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 07/2022) was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 18,4 mill. For reference, the Japanese built "Nicoline Bulker" 38/2012 Naikai, Japan (SS/DD 01/2022) was sold a month ago at USD 20 mill. Furthermore, Yamamoto Kisen committed their "Cactus K" 31/2011 Hakodate, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 06/2024) to Greek buyers for USD 16,8 mill. Note that the OHBS "Maritime Faith" 33/2011 Kanda, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at high USD 16 mill. In addition, following their sale of the "Niki C" 30/2011 New Century, China (SS/DD 09/2021) at USD 12 mill, Messrs Cosmoship sold their "Serenity C" 31/2011 Fujian, China (SS/DD 09/2021) to Norwegian buyers Vega Bulk Carriers for USD 13,5 mill basis SS/DD passed & BWTS fitted.

### Tankers Market Commentary

AET Tankers have sold their Aframax sister vessels "Bunga Kelana 7" & "Bunga Kelana 8" 105/2004 Samsung, Korea (SS 01/2024, DD 02/2022 & SS 03/2024, DD 03/2022 respectively) for USD 13,3 mill each. For comparison, the two-years-younger "Sunny Atlantica" 114/2006 Samsung, Korea (SS/DD 11/2021) was sold a week ago at USD 15,5 mill. Greek buyers IMS have acquired the MR2 "Angel No. 5" 46/2009 Jinling, China (SS 05/2024, DD 05/2022) from Zheng Yu Shipping for high USD 11 mill, while Epic Gas sold their Japanese built "Justice Express" 46/2011 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 03/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 17 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid/high usd 19,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported around usd 68,000/69,000 pd levels. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 35,000/36,500 pd. Trips from China via US Gulf into China were reported at the level of usd 33,000/34,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 34,000/36,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 36,000/38,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 34,000/36,000 pd. Indian trips via Black Sea into China were fixed around usd 34,500/36,500 pd. Persian Gulf trips into India were reported at the level of usd 45,000 pd. On the period front, Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 34,000 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery South East Asia.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

CIS Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 34,000/36,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the level of usd 40,000/43,000 pd. A number of Indian trips into Continent were fixed around usd 38,000/40,000 pd. An Ultramax was fixed on a period at the usd 42,000 pd basis delivery South East Asia for 5/7 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of trips from China into India with logs were reported at the level of usd 34,000/35,000 pd. West African trips into India were fixed around usd 45,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into Continent were reported at the level of usd 34,000/36,000 pd. A number of Mediterranean trips into US Gulf with steels were fixed around usd 40,000/41,000 pd. South American trips into Continent with alumina were reported around usd 35,000/37,000 pd. South American trips into Caribs were fixed at the level of usd 47,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
