### Bulkers Market Commentary

Nissen Kaiun have committed their Capesize "Bulk Denmark" 181/2010 Koyo, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Greek buyers Thenamaris for USD 31 mill. Note that three weeks ago, the one- year-older "Ocean Clarion" 177/2009 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS/DD 03/2022) was sold at USD 24 mill. The vintage Panamax "Xing Bao" 72/1997 Hitachi, Japan (SS/DD 12/2022) was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 8 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-younger "Captain Stefanos M" 70/1998 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 07/2023, DD 10/2021) was sold at the same price. MK Centennial Maritime sold their Supramax "Centenario Forza" 56/2012 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 04/2022) to undisclosed buyers for USD 18,6 mill, while the two-years-younger "Nord Olive" 56/2014 Oshima, Japan (SS 07/2024, DD 08/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold three weeks ago at USD 22,5 mill. On the Handysize sector, the modern "Dry Beam Neo" 38/2019 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS 12/2024, DD 12/2022, BWTS & Log fitted) was committed to Swiss buyers Nova Marine Carriers for USD 26 mill. Moreover, the OHBS "Great Resource" 31/2010 Guangzhou, China (SS 05/2025, DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) of Sinotrans was sold to Greek buyers for mid USD 12 mill. For comparison reasons, the "Ming Yuan" 33/2010 Taizhou, China (SS 07/2025, DD 08/2023) was sold a week ago at USD 11 mill. In addition, Shoei Kisen's "King Rice" 28/2012 I-S, Japan (SS/DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold to Hong Kong based buyers Taylor Maritime for USD 12,7 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Greek buyers have acquired the VLCC "Takahashi" 314/2007 NACKS, China (SS 03/2025, DD 03/2023) from NYK Line for USD 34,5 mill. Furthermore, Onassis Group sold the Korean built "Olympic Legend" 309/2003 Samsung, Korea (SS 09/2023, DD passed) to Chinese buyers for excess USD 31 mill, whilst the "Maran Triton" 318/2003 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS/DD 07/2023) was sold a week ago at USD 29 mill. The Suezmax "Vladimir Velikiy" 160/2002 Samho, Korea (SS/DD 01/2022) was sold to undisclosed buyers for low USD 16 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the Japanese built sisters "Astra" & "Voyager" 150/2002 Sasebo, Japan (SS/DD 02/2022 & 01/2022 respectively) were sold at USD 16 mill each. Tsakos Shipping & Trading have committed their Aframax "Pericles" 114/2003 Samsung, Korea (SS 07/2023, DD 11/2021) to UAE buyers for USD 13 mill. Note that the "Maratha" 106/2003 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold back in May at USD 13,5 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low/mid usd 10,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported around usd 29,500 pd. Transatlantic trips were fixed around usd 33,000/34,000 pd. A Capesize was fixed on period at the usd 26,000 pd basis delivery China for 23/30 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 29,000/30,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into Japan were fixed around usd 28,500/30,000 pd. Trips from China via Black Sea into Singapore- Japan range were reported at the level of usd 31,000/32,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported fixed around usd 33,000/35,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South American into China were reported at the level of usd 39,000/40,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into Continent and West Africa were fixed around usd 41,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 44,000/45,000 pd. On the period front a Kamsarmax were reported fixed int the usd 24,500 pd for 1 year employment basis delivery in the Pacific.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

CIS Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 24,000/25,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 26,000/28,000 pd range. Persian Gulf trips via West Coast India into Continent with steels were reported at the level of usd 33,000 pd. South African trips into China were fixed around usd 30,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1M. Continental trips via Baltic into Mediterranean with scrap were reported at the level of usd 37,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Continent into West Africa with clinker were fixed around usd 39,000 pd. An Ultramax was fixed on period at the usd 36,000 pd basis delivery China for 4/6 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of West Coast Indian trips into Thailand were reported at the level of usd 36,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 30,000 pd with petcoke. No period activity to report here.
