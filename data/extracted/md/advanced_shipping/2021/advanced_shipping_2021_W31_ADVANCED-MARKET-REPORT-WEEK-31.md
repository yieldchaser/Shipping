### Bulkers Market Commentary

Greek buyers Dryships have acquired two Capesize sister vessels from Cara Shipping, the "Stella Lucy" 179/2015 Qingdao, China (SS 07/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) for a price of USD 38 mill including an index linked T/C (min Nov '21-max Apr'22) & the "Stella Laura" 179/2015 Qingdao, China (SS 06/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 36,5 mill including a fixed rate T/C (min Feb'22-max Jun'22). Yamamari Kisen have committed their Kamsarmax "AM Express" 82/2010 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 01/2025, DD 09/2022) for excess USD 21 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-younger "Magnolia" 82/2011 Tsuneishi Fukuyama, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 22 mill basis delivery February 2022. MG Shipping's Panamax "MG Sakura" 75/2006 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 08/2021) was sold to Greek buyers for USD 18 mill basis delivery November- December 2021 with SS/DD passed & BWTS fitted, whilst the one-year- younger sister vessel "Canea" 75/2007 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 06/2022) was sold a week ago at USD 17,5 mill. Moreover, after having spent for her USD 8,8 mill back in January, Primebulk sold the "Cabeiro" 74/2007 Hudong- Zhonghua, China (SS/DD 01/2022) to Chinese buyers for USD 13,3 mill basis delivery November 2021. The Supramax "Genco Provence" 55/2004 NACKS, China (SS 08/2024, DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted) from GENCO was committed to Turkish buyers for USD 13,25 mill, while the "Helena" 50/2002 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 07/2022) from Qingdao Sea Star Ships was sold to undisclosed buyers for USD 9,7 mill basis delivery November 2021. Good Harvest Shipping have committed their Handysize "Yunnan" 34/2015 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 16 mill including T/C attached at 9.500 usd/pd until December 2022. For reference, the "Dream Island" 34/2015 Hakodate, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 18,9 mill. In addition, Sinotrans sold the OHBS "Great Reward" 31/2011 Guangzhou, China (SS 01/2026, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 13,2 mill basis delivery October 2021.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Pan Ocean have sold their MR2 "Grand Ace2" 46/2006 Stx, Korea (SS 03/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers Aerio Shipmanagement for USD 9,3 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the two-years-older Japanese built "Jasmine Express" 47/2004 Onomichi, Japan (SS/DD 05/2022) was sold at USD 7,6 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of high usd 14,00's fio range. Indian trips via South Africa into China were fixed around usd 46,000 pd. A Capesize was fixed on period at the usd 33,500 pd basis delivery China for 3/5 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 27,000/28,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 32,000/33,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 30,000/31,000 pd. South East Asian trips via ECSA into China were reported at the level of usd 32,500/33,500 pd. South East Asian trips via Black Sea into China were fixed around usd 29,000/31,000 pd. Inter- Continental trips via Baltic were reported at the level of usd 31,000/32,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into India were fixed around usd 55,000 pd. Also, Continental trips via USEC into Japan were reported at the level of usd 46,500 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 25,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1,4M. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the level of ued 25,500/27,500 pd. A number of Indonesian trips via South Africa into China were reported at the level of usd 37,500/38,500 pd. Mediterranean trips into West Africa with clinker were fixed around usd 45,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment with limited fresh information. A number of Mediterranean trips into East Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 36,000 pd. A small Handysize was fixed on period at the usd 26,000 pd basis delivery Singapore-Japan range for 6/8 months employment.
