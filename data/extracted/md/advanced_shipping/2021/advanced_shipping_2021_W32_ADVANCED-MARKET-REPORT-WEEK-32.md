### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Capesize "Aqua Splendor" 175/2012 Jinhai, China (SS/DD 06/2022) was sold to undisclosed buyers for mid-high USD 20's mill. Note you that a month ago, the one-year-older "Mangas" 174/2011 Bohai, China (SS 03/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 24 mill. Safe Bulkers have committed their Panamax "Lemessos Queen" 76/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS 10/2023, DD 10/2021, BWTS fitted) to Middle Eastern buyers for mid-high USD 18 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older "Canea" 75/2007 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 06/2022) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 17,5 mill. Vietnamese buyers have acquired two Ultramax sister vessels, the "Lowlands Beacon" 61/2011 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 04/2022) from Hisamoto Kisen for USD 21 mill & the two-years-younger "Santa Barbara" 61/2013 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 01/2023) from Konlink Shipping for USD 24,5 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "New Aspiration" 61/2011 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 10/2021, BWTS fitted) was sold three weeks ago at USD 21 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Aditya" 55/2008 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 02/2023) was sold for USD 16,5 mill basis delivery September-October 2021, while the two-years-younger "Dory" 55/2010 IHI, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 17,5 mill. Japanese owners have sold their Handysize "Ocean Mercury" 37/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 03/2025, DD 03/2023) for mid USD 13 mill with T/C attached until May 2022 at 9.000 usd/pd, whilst the Japanese built "Maratha Promise" 37/2012 Saiki, Japan (SS/DD 03/2022) of Chowgule Steamships was committed for USD 16,5 mill. In addition, the OHBS "Sakura Kobe" 33/2011 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS 11/2024, DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold to Hong Kong based buyers Taylor Maritime for USD 16,2 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Thang Long Gas have sold their VLCC "Maya Vn" 318/2003 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 04/2023, DD 08/2021, Ldt: 44.131) to Chinese buyers for USD 27 mill. Note that the vessel was acquired a month ago at demo levels 560 usd/ldt basis "As Is" Batam including 600T of bunkers ROB. For comparison reasons, the "Olympic Legend" 309/2003 Samsung, Korea (SS/DD 09/2023) was sold back in July for USD 31 mill. The Aframax "Jag Lata" 105/2003 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS/DD 05/2023) from Great Eastern Shipping was committed to Chinese buyers for USD 13,5 mill. Remind you that the "Astro Saturn" 105/2003 Daewoo, Korea (SS 04/2023, DD 08/2021) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 12,4 mill.
