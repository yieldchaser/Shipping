### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Capesize "Cape Spring" 180/2011 Qingdao, China (SS 09/2026, DD 08/2024) was committed to undisclosed buyers for excess USD 30 mill basis delivery January 2022. Note that a month ago, the one-year-younger "Aqua Honor" 175/2012 Jinhai, China (SS/DD 05/2022) was sold at excess USD 28 mill. Kitaura Kaiun are rumored to have committed their Kamsarmax "Key Discovery" 82/2010 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 24,5 mill. Remind you that back in September, the sister vessel "Vijayanagar" 82/2010 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 02/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 24 mill. German owners have sold the Ultramax "Daniela Oetker" 61/2015 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 03/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) to Bangladeshi buyers Meghna for USD 29,8 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older sister vessel "Sailing Sky" 61/2014 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 06/2024, DD 04/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 27,25 mill basis delivery November- December 2021. On the Supramax sector, the "Pacific 08" 52/2004 Tsuneishi, Japan (09/2024, DD 11/2022) was sold to Chinese buyers for low-mid USD 15 mill basis delivery December 2021. Note that back in September, the "Gutian Loyal" 52/2004 Oshima, Japan (SS 11/2024, DD 08/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 13,8 mill. In addition, the one-year-younger Chinese built "Lucky Sea" 52/2005 Yangzhou, China (SS 01/2025, DD 02/2023) was sold to Egyptian buyers for USD 14,15 mill. Eiko Kisen have sold their Handysize "Crest Navigator" 36/2012 Shikoku, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) for mid USD 21 mill. Remind you that earlier this month, the one-year-younger sister vessels "New Inspiration" & "New History" 36/2013 Shikoku, Japan (SS/DD 05/2023 & SS/DD 02/2023 accordingly) were sold for USD 20,6 mill each. Furthermore, the sister vessels "Nina-Marie" 36/2012 Zhongchuan, China (SS/DD 08/2022) & "Renate" 36/2013 Zhongchuan, China (SS/DD 06/2023) from MST were sold for USD 17,5 & 18 mill respectively. For reference, the 2011 built "Teo" 36/2011 Qidong, China (SS 05/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 16,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Capital Product Partners have sold their MR1 "Aiolos" 37/2007 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 03/2022, Ice Class 1A, DPP) to undisclosed buyers for USD 8 mill. Remind you that a couple of weeks ago, the "Nord Highlander" 37/2007 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 01/2022) was sold at USD 8,8 mill with BWTS included in the price but not fitted.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low usd 12,00's fio range. CIS Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 35,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

A number of Australian trips into China with iron ore were reported fixed in the usd 36,000/37,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 34,000/36,000 pd. West African trips into Continent with bauxite were reported at the level of usd 34,000/35,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were fixed around usd 44,000/45,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via Atlantic were reported at the level of usd 31,000/33,000 pd. Inter- Mediterranean trips via Black Sea were fixed around usd 37,000/39,000 pd. A number of trips from Black Sea into China were reported at the level of usd 57,000/59,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 37,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.7M. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 53,000/54,000 pd. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 32,500 pd for 9 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 31,000/32,000 pd. A number of trips from China into Philippines were reported fixed at the usd 30,000/32,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 34,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips via Persian Gulf into East Coast India with clinker were reported at the level of usd 40,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean with scrap were fixed around usd 41,000 pd. US Gulf trips into South East Asia were reported at the level of usd 51,000/52,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

CIS Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 25,000/27,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China with alumina were reported fixed in the usd 33,000/35,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into US Gulf with steels were fixed around usd 38,000/40,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into East Coast South America with fertilizers were reported at the level of usd 34,000/35,000 pd. West Coast Central American trips into China were fixed around usd 36,000 pd and into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 30,000 pd. US Gulf trips into East Coast Mexico were reported at the level of usd 40,000 pd. West Coast South American trips into China were fixed around usd 40,000 pd. A small Handysize was fixed on period at the usd 32,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 550K basis delivery Continent for 5/7 months employment.
