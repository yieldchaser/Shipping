### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Capesize "Springbank" 177/2010 New Times, China (SS 11/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed to Greek buyers Kassian Maritime for USD 26,5 mill. For reference, the one-year-younger "Lowlands Tenacity" 180/2011 HHIC, Philippines (SS 04/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 29,5 mill. Safe Bulkers sold their Panamax "Koulitsa" 76/2003 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) to UAE based buyers for USD 13,6 mill. For comparison, the two-years-younger "Atlantic Hero" 75/2005 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 08/2023) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 14,9 mill. Moreover, the "Imperial Rose" 76/2008 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS/DD 08/2023) of Golden Union Shipping was committed to Greek buyers for USD 18 mill, while the one-year-older "Canea" 75/2007 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 06/2022) from Spring Marine was committed to Greek buyers also for USD 17,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Chellaram Shipping sold their "Darya Lakshmi" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS 07/2024, DD 10/2022) to Greek buyers for USD 16,5 mill, whilst the one-year-younger "YM Tradition" 55/2010 Kawasaki, Japan (SS 01/2025, DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted) from Yamamaru Kisen was sold for USD 16,3 mill basis delivery July-October of 2021. In addition, the "Imperial Fortune" 53/2006 Imabari, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) of Kenzan Kaiun was sold for USD 13,2 mill basis delivery September- October of 2021. Interlink Maritime have committed their Handysize sisters "Interlink Verity" & "Interlink Parity" 37/2012 Huatai, China (SS/DD 06/2022 & 03/2022 respectively) to Greek buyers Costamare for USD 14,75 mill each. Furthermore, Costamare is rumored to have acquired the similar "N Discovery" 37/2012 Zhejiang, China (SS/DD 10/2022) from Nord for low USD 14 mill and the Korean built "Cielo Di Capalbio" 36/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 03/2022) from d'Amico for USD 16 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Okeanis Eco Tankers sold their VLCCs "Nissos Antiparos" & "Nissos Santorini" 319/2019 Hyundai, Korea (SS 07/2024, DD 07/2022, BWTS & Scrubber fitted both) to Frontline for USD 90 mill each basis delivery in November of 2021. Meanwhile, Okeanis Eco Tankers acquired from Kyklades Maritime the resales "Nissos Kea" & "Nissos Nikouria" 299/2022 Hyundai, Korea (BWTS & Scrubber fitted) for USD 97 mill each basis delivery in 1st half of 2022. The MR2 "Jasmine Express" 47/2004 Onomichi, Japan (SS/DD 05/2022) was sold to Greek buyers for USD 7,6 mill. Remind you that back in May, the Korean built "Integrity" 46/2004 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 11/2024, DD 02/2023) was sold for USD 9 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid usd 11,00's fio range. A number of Indian trips via Black Sea into China were reported around usd 41,000 pd. A Capesize was fixed on period at the usd 34,500 pd basis delivery China for 7/11 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 31,000/32,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 34,000/35,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 32,000/34,000 pd. Indian trips via Black Sea into China were fixed around usd 37,000/38,000 pd. Indian iron ore trips into China were reported at the level of usd 45,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into West Africa were fixed around usd 39,000/40,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into Continent via Black Sea were reported at the level of usd 45,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 50,000 pd. On the period front a Kamsarmax was reported fixed int the usd 32,500 pd for 5/7 months employment basis delivery in the Atlantic.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

CIS Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 31,000/32,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 35,000/36,000pd. A number of trips from Philippines with nickel ore into China were reported at the level of usd 31,000 pd. Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 50,000 pd. West African trips via East Coast South America into Continent were reported at the level of usd 31,000/32,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 37,000/38,500 pd. US Gulf trips into Japan were reported fixed around usd 43,000/44,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean with sugar were reported at the level of usd 40,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

CIS Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 24,000/25,000 pd. A number of Continental trips into East Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 26,000/27,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Turkey into Caribs with clinker were fixed around usd 26,000/27,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 36,000/38,000 pd. A Handysize was fixed on a period at the usd 26,260 pd basis delivery Continent for 4/6 months employment with redelivery Atlantic.
