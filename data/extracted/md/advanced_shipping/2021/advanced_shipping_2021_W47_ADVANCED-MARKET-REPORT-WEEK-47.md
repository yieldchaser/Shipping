### Bulkers Market Commentary

Korean owners have sold their Kamsarmax "SM Aurora" 82/2012 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 08/2022) to Chinese buyers for USD 21 mill basis delivery Q1 2022. Note that the one-year-younger "Ocean Rosemary" 82/2013 Dalian, China (SS/DD 04/2023) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 23 mill. Stamford Shipping have committed their Ultramax "Drogba" 63/2015 Chengxi, China (SS 10/2025, DD 07/2023) to undisclosed buyers for USD 25 mill. Note that the one-year-younger sister vessel "Nautical Alice" 63/2016 Jiangsu, China (SS 06/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 28,5 mill basis delivery Q1 2022. In addition, Norden sold their "Nord Colorado" 60/2018 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 01/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to reportedly Greek buyers for region USD 32 mill. The OHBS Supramax "Cherry Dream" 51/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) from Kiku Kaiyo Kosan was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 17,8 mill. For reference, the conventional "Kaiwo Maru" 50/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD 09/2023) was sold back in September for USD 19 mill. The Handysize sister vessels "Dry Beam Neo" 38/2019 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS 12/2024, DD 12/2022, BWTS fitted) & "Fragrant Athena" 38/2020 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS 04/2025, DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) were sold for excess USD 30 mill each with T/C attached. Moreover, Suisse-Atlantique sold their "Charmey" 35/2011 Shinan, Korea (SS/DD 12/2021, BWTS fitted) to Middle Eastern buyers for high USD 15 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older "Dory" 34/2010 Spp, Korea (SS 05/2025, DD 09/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 16,2 mill basis delivery February 2022.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Pantheon Tankers have committed their Suezmax "Astro Perseus" 159/2004 Hyundai, Korea (SS 01/2024, DD 12/2021) to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 18,5 mill. Note that earlier this month, the two-years-younger "Seaways Saugerties" 162/2006 Daewoo, Korea (SS 04/2026, DD 06/2024) was sold at USD 21,8 mill. The Aframax "Antonis" 113/2017 Daehan, Korea (SS/DD 06/2022, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1C) of Enesel was sold to Turkish buyers Advantage Tankers for USD 45 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid/high usd 12,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 12,000/14,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. South East Asian trips via Black Sea into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 21,000/23,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via Murmansk were fixed around usd 21,000/23,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into China were reported at the level of usd 37,000/39,000 pd. Inter- Mediterranean trips via Black Sea were fixed around usd 22,000/24,000 pd. South American trips into Red Sea were reported at the level of usd 30,000/31,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 36,000/38,000 pd. On the period front, a Panamax was reported fixed at the level of usd 18,500 pd for 13/15 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of East Coast Indian trips into West Coast India were reported fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips via Persian Gulf and Red Sea into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 35,000 pd. South African trips into India were fixed around usd 25,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 480K. South American trips into US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 46,000 pd. A Supramax was fixed on a period at the usd 22,000 pd basis delivery Persian Gulf for 5/7 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from Baltic into East Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 39,000 pd. US Gulf trips into West Africa with grains were fixed around usd 26,000/28,000 pd. A Large Handysize was fixed on a period at the usd 27,000 pd basis delivery East Coast Central America for 3/5 months employment with redelivery Atlantic.
