### Bulkers Market Commentary

Kambara Kisen have sold their Kamsarmax "Lena B" 82/2017 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 01/2022, BWTS fitted) to Vietnamese buyers Hoa Phat for USD 35 mill basis delivery November-December 2021. For comparison, the two-years-younger "Zephyrus" 81/2019 Jiangsu, China (SS 03/2024, DD 03/2022, BWTS fitted, Scrubber ready) was sold a week ago at USD 36,5 mill basis same delivery dates. The Panamax "Orient Violet" 77/2015 Imabari, Japan (SS 04/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) from Mitsui was sold to Greek buyers TMS Shipping for low USD 30 mill basis delivery November-December 2021. Remind you that a month ago, the sister vessel "Sea Vision" 77/2015 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2025, DD 04/2022) was sold at region USD 30 mill basis delivery Q4 2021 – Q1 2022. Meiho Kaiun invited offers on Tuesday for their Ultramax "Kanoura" 61/2013 Iwagi, Japan (SS 04/2026, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) which was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 28,4 mill. Note that the one-year- younger "Sailing Sky" 61/2014 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 06/2024, DD 04/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold earlier this month at USD 27,25 mill basis delivery November-December 2021. On the Supramax sector, Uglands Rederi sold their "Carmencita" 58/2009 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 05/2024, DD 04/2022, BWTS fitted) for USD 21 mill, while the sister vessel "New Pride" 58/2009 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 01/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold back in September for USD 18,8 mill basis delivery December 2021. Furthermore, Japanese owners committed their "Ikan Selayang" 56/2013 Mitsui, Japan (SS 04/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) for USD 26,5 mill. Note that the two- years-older sister vessel "Medi Okinawa" 56/2011 Mitsui, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 22,5 mill. In addition, following the sale of the "Atlantic Ensenada" 55/2006 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 04/2023) back in August at USD 15,7 mill, Pacific Carriers have sold the sister vessel "Atlantic Yucatan" 55/2006 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 03/2023) for low-mid USD 17 mill. Japanese owners sold their OHBS Handysize "Global Passion" 33/2011 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 17,5 mill basis delivery November 2021 – January 2022, whilst the "Maritime Faith" 33/2011 Kanda, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at high USD 16 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The VLCC "Gilos" 319/2004 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 10/2024, DD 08/2022) was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 29 mill. Remind you that a couple of weeks ago, the two-years-older "Fos" 307/2002 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 02/2022) was sold at USD 28 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low/mid usd 14,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 39,000/41,000 pd. CIS Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 37,000/39,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 42,000/43,000 pd. A number of Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 41,000/42,000 pd. West African trips via US East Coast and Sweden into Continent were fixed around usd 35,000/36,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via Baltic were reported at the level of usd 34,000/36,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 40,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 2M. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 36,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.6M. On the period front, Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 33,000 pd for 11/13 months employment basis delivery India. Also, Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 37,500 pd basis delivery South East Asia for 6/8 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 42,000/43,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into Persian Gulf- Japan range were fixed around usd 40,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips via Persian Gulf into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 47,000 pd. Trips from Black Sea into Caribbean were fixed around usd 39,000/40,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were reported at the level of usd 60,000/62,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 42,000/43,000 pd. An Ultramax was fixed on a period at the usd 42,000 pd basis delivery South East Asia for 5/7 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of Continental trips into US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 37,000/39,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Continent with steels were fixed around usd 38,000/40,000 pd. West Coast South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 32,000/33,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
