### Bulkers Market Commentary

Noble Group have sold their Capesize "Aqua Honor" 175/2012 Jinhai, China (SS/DD 05/2022) to undisclosed buyers for USD 28 mill, whilst the sister vessel "Aqua Splendor" 175/2012 Jinhai, China (SS/DD 06/2022) was sold back in August at the same price. The Kamsarmax "Pedhoulas Fighter" 81/2012 Zhejiang, China (SS/DD 08/2022, Scrubber fitted) was sold to Chinese buyers for region USD 24 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the "Ranger" 82/2012 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 07/2022) was sold at USD 24 mill basis delivery Q4 2021. MC Shipping have sold their Ultramax "Sailing Sky" 61/2014 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 06/2024, DD 04/2022, BWTS fitted) to Vietnamese buyers for USD 27,25 mill basis delivery November-December 2021. Note that the two- years-older "Nord Summit" 61/2012 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 08/2022, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold three weeks ago at USD 25,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Tsurumi Kisen sold their "Spring Hawk" 55/2010 Mitsui, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 22 mill basis delivery November-December 2021, while the one-year-younger "Ivy Delta" 55/2011 IHI, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 20,4 mill basis delivery December 2021. The vintage Handymax "Jun Benefit" 45/1997 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 11/2022) was sold to undisclosed buyers for low USD 7 mill. Remind you that three weeks ago, the "Pacific Pamela" 49/1997 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 01/2022) was sold at USD 8 mill. D'Amico Dry sold their OHBS Handysize "Cielo Di Gaspesie" 37/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 07/2022) for low USD 19 mill. For reference, the "Ocean Opal" 37/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 07/2022) was sold a week ago at USD 18,4 mill. Moreover, ORIX Corp sold their two OHBS sister vessels "EOS Esperance" & "EOS Victory" 33/2012 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS/DD 05/2022 & SS/DD 08/2022 accordingly, BWTS fitted both) to Chinese buyers for high USD 34 mill enbloc. For comparison reasons, the one-year- older OHBS "Maritime Faith" 33/2011 Kanda, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at high USD 16 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Cosmoship Maritime sold their VLCC "Fos" 307/2002 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 02/2022) to undisclosed buyers for USD 28 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the two-years-younger "Starlight Venture" 291/2004 Hyundai, Korea (SS 11/2024, DD 01/2023) was sold at USD 30 mill.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 35,000/37,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 38,000/40,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 34,000/36,000 pd. A number of trips from Philippines with iron ore into Japan were reported at the level of usd 45,000 pd. Trips from India via ECSA into China were fixed around usd 35,000/37,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were reported at the level of usd 40,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K. US Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 37,000/38,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.6M. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 35,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.5M. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 31,500 pd for 10/12 months employment basis delivery in the Pacific.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 40,000/43,000 pd. Indian trips via Persian Gulf into India were fixed around usd 48,000/50,000 pd. Indian trips via South Africa into China were reported at the level of usd 35,000/36,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into Mediterranean with scrap were fixed around usd 35,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into West Africa were reported around usd 51,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Continent were fixed at the level of usd 40,000 pd. South American trips into US Gulf were reported fixed around usd 45,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
