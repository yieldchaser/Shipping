### Bulkers Market Commentary

Sojitz Corp have committed their Kamsarmax "Atlantic Legend" 83/2009 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 18 mill. The ship has T/C attached until April-July 2022 with a rate of 16.500 usd/pd. On the Supramax sector, Maritime Enterprises Management have sold their "Nikolas III" 58/2009 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 06/2024) to Chinese buyers for USD 17,5 mill. Meanwhile, Belships sold the two-years-younger "Belocean" 58/2011 Yangzhou, China (SS 03/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) to German buyers Oskar Wehr for USD 19,6 mill. Swire & Sons have sold their Handysize "Wuhu" 39/2014 Chengxi, China (SS 01/2024, DD 01/2022) for USD 21 mill, while the two-years-younger "Nordrubicon" & "Nordcolorado" 38/2016 Zhejiang, China (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024 & SS 08/2026, DD 08/2024 respectively) were committed to UAE buyers Tomini Shipping for USD 21,4 mill each. Moreover, the vintage OHBS sister vessels "UBC Sacramento" 31/2001 Saiki, Japan (SS/DD freshly passed, BWTS fitted) & "UBC Syndey" 31/2001 Saiki, Japan (SS 08/2026, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) from United Bulk Carriers were sold for USD 10,5 mill each. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-younger OHBS "AEC Diligence" 31/2002 Saiki, Japan (SS/DD 04/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 9,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The VLCC resales "Hyundai Samho 8052" & "Hyundai Samho 8053" 299/2022 Hyundai Samho, Korea were committed to Greek buyers for USD 97,5 mill each. Interesting note that the current sellers acquired the vessels back in March at USD 90 mill each. Salamon AG have sold their vintage Suezmax "Voyager" 150/2002 Sasebo, Japan (SS/DD 01/2022) to undisclosed buyers for USD 13,6 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Astra" 150/2002 Sasebo, Japan (Ldt: 22.560) was sold a month ago for recycling in Alang for 576 usd/ldt which translates to a price of USD 13 mill. The Aframax "Guanabara" 106/2007 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 06/2022) of Mitsui OSK Lines was sold to Greek buyers Castor Maritime for USD 16,5 mill. For reference, the three-years-younger "Silver" 107/2010 Tsuneishi, Japan & "Gold" 107/2010 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 07/2025, DD 07/2023 & SS 09/2025, DD 09/2023 respectively, BWTS fitted both) were sold a week ago at USD 24,5 mill each. Italian owners have committed on subs their MR2 "PS Houston" 51/2008 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 01/2023, Ice Class 1A) for mid-high USD 11 mill. For comparison, the two-years-older "AG Mars" 50/2006 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 02/2022) was sold a week ago at USD 10 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of high usd 8,00's fio range. A Capesize was fixed on a period at the usd 23,500 pd basis delivery China for 11/14 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 18,000/19,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 15,000/16,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 17,000/18,000 pd. Trips from China via US Gulf into China were fixed at the level of usd 19,000/21,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. West African trips via East Coast South America into Continent were fixed around usd 19,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via Murmansk were reported at the level of usd 20,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into West Africa were fixed around usd 22,000 pd. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 21,500 pd for 8/11 months employment basis delivery South East Asia.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 20,000/21,000 pd. Continental trips into US East Coast with scrap were reported at the level of usd 28,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

South American trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 34,000/36,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 36,000/38,000 pd. Also, South American trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 37,000/39,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
