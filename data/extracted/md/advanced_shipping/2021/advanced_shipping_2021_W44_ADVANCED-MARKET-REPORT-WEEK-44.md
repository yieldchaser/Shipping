### Bulkers Market Commentary

Fuyo Kaiun sold their Kamsarmax "Jiangsu Yangzi-Mitsui YZJ2015-2686" 82/2022 Jiangsu, China, which is set for delivery in January, to Greek buyers for USD 38,5 mill. For comparison, the resale of "Vorana Manx" 82/2021 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (BWTS fitted) achieved a month ago excess USD 40 mill basis delivery December 2021. Furthermore, the "Seacon Shanghai" 82/2019 Guangzhou, China (SS 06/2024, DD 06/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 38 mill, while the "Zephyrus" 81/2019 Jiangsu, China (SS 03/2024, DD 03/2022, BWTS fitted, Scrubber ready) was sold a month ago at USD 36,5 mill basis delivery November-December 2021. The Ultramax "Sunleaf Grace" 61/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 08/2023) from Toko Kisen was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 21,5 mill basis delivery March-May 2022. For reference, the two-years-younger "Kanoura" 61/2013 Iwagi, Japan (SS 04/2026, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 28,4 mill. On the Supramax sector, AM Nomikos sold their "Sophia N" 56/2009 Qingshan, China (SS 08/2024, DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted) for USD 17,75 mill. Remind you that back in September, the "Skylight" 56/2009 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 16,35 mill. Moreover, after having failed at USD 21,5 mill back in October, the "HTC Delta" 56/2014 Taizhou, China (SS 06/2024, DD 06/2022), BWTS fitted) was committed again for USD 20,5 mill. Nordic sold their Handysize "Nordic Malmoe" 35/2012 Nantong, China (SS/DD 01/2022) for mid-high USD 16 mill, while the "Nina-Marie" 36/2012 Zhongchuan, China (SS/DD 08/2022) was sold a week ago at USD 17,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Minerva Marine have sold their Aframax "Atalandi" 105/2004 Daewoo, Korea (SS 11/2024, DD 12/2022) for USD 13,6 mill. Remind you that back in September, the sisters "Bunga Kelana 7" & "Bunga Kelana 8" 105/2004 Samsung, Korea (SS 01/2024, DD 02/2022 & SS 03/2024, DD 03/2022 respectively) were sold at USD 13,3 mill each. Mitsui OSK Lines have committed their LR1 "Iris Victoria" 75/2010 Minami- Nippon, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) for mid-high USD 17 mill. Note that the sister vessel "Justice Victoria" 75/2010 Minami-Nippon, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 17,3 mill. The MR2 "Star Eagle" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 10/2022) was sold to undisclosed buyers for USD 11,75 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "High Venture" 51/2006 Stx, Korea (SS 04/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) from d'Amico Tankers was sold to Indian buyers for USD 10,7 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low/mid usd 11,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported around usd 28,000/29,000 pd levels. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 27,000/29,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed around usd 24,000/25,000 pd. A number of trips from India via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 30,000/31,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via North Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 32,000/34,000 pd. Continental trips via East Coast South America into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 28,000/30,000 pd. Also, Continental trips via Baltic into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 30,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 33,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.35M. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 42,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from China into West Coast Central America with fertilizers were fixed around usd 27,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 19,000/21,000 pd range. Indian trips into China with salt were reported at the level of usd 30,000/32,000 pd. US Gulf trips into EC Mexico were fixed around usd 40,000 pd. West Coast South American trips into Far East with concentrates were reported at the level of usd 44,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 43,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from North Vietnam via Australia into Chennai-Lumut range were fixed around usd 25,000/27,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Continent were reported fixed at the usd 34,000/36,000 pd. US East Coast trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 32,000/34,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
