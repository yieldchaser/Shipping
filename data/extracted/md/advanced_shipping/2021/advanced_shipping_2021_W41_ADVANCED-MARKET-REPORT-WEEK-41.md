### Bulkers Market Commentary

Japanese owners have sold their Capesize "Cape Garland" 178/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 32,5 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger "Rosco Maple" 181/2010 Sasebo, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 33,75 mill. Moreover, the Chinese owned "ASL Mars" 175/2004 SWS, China (SS 08/2024, DD 07/2022) was sold to compatriots for USD 16,2 mill basis delivery Q1 of 2022, while the two-years-younger "CHS Magnificence" 173/2006 Bohai, China (SS/DD 09/2021, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 19,15 mill. MC Shipping's Kamsarmax "Ibis Wind" 83/2013 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 07/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed for USD 28,5 mill basis delivery End of 2021 - Start of 2022. Remind you that the two-years-younger "Lowlands Nello" 82/2015 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold back in September at USD 32 mill. In addition, the "Zephyrus" 81/2019 Jiangsu, China (SS 03/2024, DD 03/2022, BWTS fitted, Scrubber ready) from Interocean Transportation was sold to Hong Kong buyers Chellaram Shipping for USD 36,5 mill basis delivery November- December 2021. Golden Ocean Group have committed two vessels, the Kamsarmax "Golden Endurer" 79/2011 Jinhai, China (SS 01/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) & the Panamax "Golden Opportunity" 75/2008 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 08/2023, Ice Class 1C) to undisclosed buyers for USD 37,2 mill enbloc. For reference, the "Elinda Mare" 79/2010 New Century, China (SS 12/2025, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in September at USD 20 mill. On the Supramax sector, following last week's sale of the "HTC Charlie" 56/2014 Taizhou, China (SS 02/2024, DD 01/2022, BWTS fitted) at USD 20,45 mill basis DD passed in November, the sister vessel "HTC Delta" 56/2014 Taizhou, China (SS 06/2024, DD 06/2022) is also committed for USD 21,5 mill. Fortune Ocean Shipping have committed their Handysize "Xing Zhi Hai" 34/2015 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 12/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 22 mill basis delivery January 2022, whilst the one-year-older sister vessel "ES Venus" 34/2014 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 04/2024, DD 02/2022) was sold for USD 20,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The MR2 "Ivy Express" 51/2009 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 02/2024) of Mitsui OSK Lines was sold to Greek buyers Spring Marine Management for USD 15 mill. Note that a month ago, the one-year-older "Hellas Explorer" 51/2008 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 01/2023) was sold at USD 12,8 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low usd 18,00's fio range. Pacific round voyage trips were reported at the level of usd 79,000 pd. Trips from Continent into China were fixed around usd 120,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 36,000/38,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 37,000/39,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 39,000/41,000 pd. Trips from South East Asia via ECSA into China were reported at the level of usd 34,000/35,000 pd. South East Asian trips via Black Sea into China were fixed around usd 36,000/37,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips via Black Sea into China were reported at the level of usd 42,000/43,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf and Neo Panama into China were fixed around usd 50,000 pd. Inter-Mediterranean trips via Black Sea and Red Sea were reported at the level of usd 35,000/36,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 38,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.45M. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 34,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.4M. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 33,000 pd for 1 year employment basis delivery Japan. Also, Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 40,000 pd basis delivery India for 4/6 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 41,000/43,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China with nickel ore were reported at the level of usd 33,000 pd. A number of trips from Philippines with nickel ore into China were fixed around usd 40,000/41,000 pd. South African trips into Indonesia were reported at the level of usd 32,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1,175,000M. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Inter-Mediterranean trips via Black Sea were reported at the level of usd 34,000/35,000 pd. Trips from Black Sea into West Africa were fixed around usd 46,000 pd. South American trips into Continent with sugar were reported at the level of usd 32,000/33,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
