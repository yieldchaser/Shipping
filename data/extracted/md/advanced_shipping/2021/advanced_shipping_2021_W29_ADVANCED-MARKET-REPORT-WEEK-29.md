### Bulkers Market Commentary

Cobelfret have sold their Post-Panamax "Lowlands Green" 95/2011 Imabari, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 21 mill basis delivery January 2022. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Double Providence" 95/2012 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 01/2022) was sold at USD 21,3 mill. The Kamsarmax "Magnolia" 82/2011 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) of Kawana Kaiun was sold to Greek buyers Diana Shipping for USD 22 mill basis delivery February 2022, while the "Navios Marco Polo" 80/2011 Universal, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) from Nissen Kaiun was sold to Taiwanese buyers Chailease International Leasing for USD 22,5 mill basis delivery October 2021. For comparison, the one-year-younger "Cecilia B" 82/2012 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 05/2022, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 23,5 mill. On the Ultramax sector, Kmarin sold their "Kmarin Ulsan" 63/2014 Jiangsu, China (SS 06/2024, DD 08/2022, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 22 mill basis delivery January 2022, whilst Norden sold their "Nord Hudson" 61/2014 NACKS, China (SS 08/2024, DD 06/2022) to undisclosed buyers for mid USD 24 mill. Moreover, the Japanese built "New Aspiration" 61/2011 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 10/2021, BWTS fitted) of Hsin Chien Marine was sold to Greek buyers for USD 21 mill. KT Marine's Supramax "Nord Trust" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS 10/2024, DD 10/2022) was sold for USD 16 mill. Remind you that a couple of weeks ago, the two-years-older "Belfri" 55/2007 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 06/2022) was sold at USD 15,2 mill. In addition, the smaller "Ken Sea" 53/2009 Iwagi, Japan (SS 01/2024, DD 10/2021) from Ikaros Shipping was committed for USD 15,5 mill. Following the acquisition of "Dry Beam Neo" 38/2019 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS 12/2024, DD 12/2022, BWTS & Log fitted) at USD 26 mill, Nova Marine Carriers sold their Handysize "Sider Faioch" 38/2015 Jiangsu, China (SS 06/2025, DD 06/2023) to European buyers for USD 21,2 mill basis delivery September 2021. Furthermore, Ultrabulk committed their "Ultra Calbuco" 38/2017 I-S, Japan (SS/DD 02/2022, BWTS fitted) to Indian buyers Chellaram Shipping for USD 23,5 mill basis delivery December 2021.

## Page 2

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

A number of Indonesian trips into Philippines were reported around usd 30,000 pd levels plus ballast bonus at the usd 50K. A number of Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 27,000/28,000 pd. Also, Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 30,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported around usd 30,000/31,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips via East Coast South America into China with sugar were fixed around usd 33,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into India were reported at the level of usd 63,250 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into China were fixed around usd 56,000 pd. North Coast South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 45,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported around usd 28,000/30,000 pd levels. A number of Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 28,500/30,000 pd. Indian bagged rice trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 38,000 pd. Trips from Black Sea into South East Asia were fixed around usd 59,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 24,000/25,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into China were fixed around usd 45,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into Continent were reported at the level of usd 26,000/27,000 pd. US Gulf trips with corn via Mississippi River into US Gulf were fixed around usd 29,000 pd. US Gulf trips into West Africa with petcoke were reported at the level of usd 30,000 pd. A Handysize open US Gulf was fixed for 2 laden legs with redelivery Singapore-Japan range at the usd 30,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
