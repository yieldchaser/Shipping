### Bulkers Market Commentary

Carras Hellas have sold their Capesize "Aquaprincess" 182/2009 Odense, Denmark (SS 11/2024, DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted) to other Greek buyers for USD 25 mill. Moreover, it is rumored that the two-years-older "Cape Treasure" 180/2007 Koyo, Japan (SS/DD 02/2022) from Keiyo Kisen was committed for USD 21,5 mill basis delivery January-February 2022. The Post Panamax "Mayfair Spirit" 93/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS 05/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to Greek buyers NGM for USD 19 mill basis T/C attached until Q2 2022. For reference, the sister vessel "CK Augusta" 93/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 11/2021, BWTS fitted) was sold back in September for USD 19,7 mill basis delivery January 2022. Grieg Star have sold their Ultramax sister vessels "Star Artemis" & "Star Eos" 63/2015 Yangzhou, China (SS 05/2025, DD 05/2023 & SS 10/2025, DD 09/2023 respectively) to Greek buyers Goldenport for USD 53 mill enbloc, while AM Nomikos sold their "Noni" 61/2015 NACKS, China (SS 06/2025, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 27,5 mill. Note that three weeks ago, the one-year-younger "Nautical Alice" 63/2016 Jiangsu, China (SS 06/2026, DD 10/82024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold at USD 28,5 mill basis delivery Q1 2022. On the Supramax sector, the "GDF Suez North Sea" 55/2012 IHI, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 21 mill. Remind you that the one-year-younger "Ikan Selayang" 56/2013 Mitsui, Japan (SS 04/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold back in October at USD 26,5 mill. In addition, the older "Fareast Hope" 55/2004 Oshima, Japan (SS 03/2024, DD 12/2021) of Jiangsu Fareast Shipping was committed to Chinese buyers for USD 14,75 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Nord Group have sold their LR1 sister vessels "Nordmerkur" & "Nordneptun" 75/2004 Hyundai, Korea (SS 05/2024, DD 06/2022 & SS 04/2024, DD 04/2022 respectively) to undisclosed buyers for USD 10 mill each, while Livanos Group sold the one-year-younger "Amazon Brilliance" 73/2005 Hyundai, Korea (SS 02/2025, DD 02/2023) for region USD 12 mill. Italian owners have committed their MR2 "PS Houston" 51/2008 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 01/2023, Ice Class 1A) to Greek buyers for USD 12 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-older "PTI Rhine" & "PTI Amazon" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 02/2022 & SS/DD 03/2022 respectively) were sold for USD 9,9 mill each. Furthermore, the "Arctic Bay" 48/2006 Stx, Korea (SS 03/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed to Norwegian buyers Atlantica Shipping for mid-high USD 11 mill.

## Page 2

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low usd 13,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported around usd 37,000/39,000 pd levels. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 17,000/20,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed around usd 19,000/22,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 22,000/24,000 pd. West African trips via North Coast South America into Continent were reported at the level of usd 36,000/38,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via Baltic were fixed around usd 34,000/36,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic and India into Singapore were reported at the level of usd 54,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were fixed around usd 40,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 41,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 24,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 29,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into India were reported at the level of usd 35,000 pd. South African trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 27,000/28,000 pd. South African trips via East Coast South America into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 31,000 pd. Trips from US East Coast into East Coast South America were fixed around usd 34,000/35,000 pd. On the period front, an Ultramax was reported fixed at the level of usd 35,500 pd for 3/5 months employment basis delivery US Gulf. Also, a Supramax was fixed on period at the usd 22,000 pd basis delivery Persian Gulf for 11/13 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. US Gulf trips into East Coast Mexico with petcoke were reported at the level of usd 30,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 39,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
