### Bulkers Market Commentary

German owners Blumenthal have acquired the Kamsarmax "King Barley" 82/2012 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 01/2022) from MK Centennial Maritime for mid-high USD 22 mill basis SS/DD passed and BWTS fitted. For comparison, the "SM Aurora" 82/2012 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 08/2022) was sold a couple of weeks ago for USD 21 mill basis delivery Q1 2022. Moreover, Kassian Maritime sold the sister vessels "Egyptian Mike" 81/2011 Guangzhou, China (SS 09/2026, DD 10/2024) & "George P" 81/2012 Guangzhou, China (SS/DD 08/2022) to compatriots Costamare for USD 39 mill enbloc. On the Supramax sector, Costamare also acquired the sister vessels "Belnor" 58/2010 Yangzhou, China (SS 05/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) & "Belstar" 58/2009 Yangzhou, China (SS 08/2024, DD 07/2022, BWTS fitted) from Belships for USD 33 mill enbloc. Furthermore, the sisters "Universal Bangkok" 56/2012 Qingshan, China (SS/DD 04/2022) & "Universal Bremen" 56/2010 Qingshan, China (SS 12/2025, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) were committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 33 mill enbloc. Note that a month ago, the older sister vessel "Sophia N" 56/2009 Qingshan, China (SS 08/2024, DD 10/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 17,75 mill. In addition, Tomini Shipping sold their "Tomini Infinity" 56/2010 Cosco, China (SS 01/2025, DD 03/2023) for USD 16,2 mill basis delivery January-February 2022. Taylor Maritime have committed their OHBS Handysize "Super Lydia" 37/2007 Saiki, Japan (SS/DD 01/2022) to Turkish buyers for mid USD 13 mill, while the OHBS "AEC Diligence" 31/2002 Saiki, Japan (SS/DD 04/2022, BWTS fitted) was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 9,5 mill. Moreover, Meadway Shipping & Trading committed their "Targa" 28/2009 Imabari, Japan (SS 05/2024, DD 03/2022) for low USD 14 mill. Remind you that the "Atlantic Veracruz" 28/2009 Shimanami, Japan (SS/DD 07/2022) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 12,7 mill basis delivery January 2022.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Following their purchase through a judicial sale of the VLCC "E Mei San" 318/2010 SWS, China (SS 03/2025, DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted) from Xihe Holdings at USD 36,75 mill, Aeolos Management have now acquired the "New Coral" 297/2010 Shanghai Jiangnan, China (SS 01/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) from China VLCC for USD 38,5 mill. Livanos Group have committed their MR2 "Evros" 47/2005 Stx, Korea (SS 01/2025, DD 12/2022) to Greek buyers for USD 9,4 mill, while the sister vessel "High Valor" 47/2005 Stx, Korea (SS 02/2025, DD 05/2023) from d'Amico Tankers was sold for USD 10,3 mill. For reference, d'Amico Tankers sold a month ago the one-year-younger "High Venture" 51/2006 Stx, Korea (SS 04/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 10,7 mill.

West African trips into Continent were fixed around usd 33,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean with scrap were reported at the level of usd 38,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Black Sea were fixed around usd 38,000 pd. On the period front, an Ultramax was reported fixed at the level of usd 30,000 pd for 10/12 months employment basis delivery US Gulf. Also, an Ultramax was fixed on a period at the usd 30,000 pd basis delivery Mediterranean for 3/5 months employment with redelivery Atlantic.
