### Bulkers Market Commentary

Greek owners have committed their Capesize "Corinthian Phoenix" 179/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) to clients of Hayfin Capital Management at USD 29 mill. Remind you that back in January, the sister vessel "Athenian Phoenix" 179/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 23,5 mill. The Kamsarmax "Valiant Summer" 81/2016 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 06/2026, BWTS fitted) was committed to clients of Blumenthal at USD 32,5 mill. Note that the same buyers acquired the one-year-younger sister vessel "The Prosperity" 81/2017 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 01/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) back in February at USD 31,5 mill. Dongguan Yinghao Shipping committed their Panamax "Ying Hao 03" 76/2012 Hudong-Zhonghua, China (SS/DD 05/2027, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at low USD 17 mill. Note that the "Ying Hao 02" 75/2012 Guangzhou, China (SS 06/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in March at USD 16,8 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Delta Avon" 56/2012 Cosco, China (SS 03/2027, DD 03/2026, BWTS fitted) was committed to Chinese interests at USD 14,3 mill. For comparison, the "Hony World" 56/2012 Xiamen, China (SS 06/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 14,7 mill. Furthermore, the "Super Bergkamp" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Greek interests at USD 15,5 mill. Remind you that back in February, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Konrad Schulte" 55/2010 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at low USD 16 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into China were fixed around usd 24,000/26,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K/1M. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 19,600 pd for 3/5 months employment basis delivery China. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 19,500 pd basis delivery China for 1 year employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/20,000 pd. South East Asian trips into India were fixed around usd 20,000/23,000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 22,000/25,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 220K/250K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of West African trips into East Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 6,000/7,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 16,000/17,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast Central America were reported at the level of usd 22,000/24,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
