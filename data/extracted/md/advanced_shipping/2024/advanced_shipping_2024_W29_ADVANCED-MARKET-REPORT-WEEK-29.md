### Bulkers Market Commentary

Oldendorff Carriers committed their Post Panamax "Claas Oldendorff" 95/2013 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 06/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Singaporean interests at region USD 27 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Van Gogh" 95/2013 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in late May at USD 25 mill. Following the acquisition of the Kamsarmax "Livia Rose" 81/2018 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 01/2028, DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) three weeks ago at USD 35,7 mill, clients of HMM have now bought the one-year-younger "BW Kobe" 81/2019 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 07/2029, DD 07/2027, BWTS fitted) at USD 37 mill. The Panamax "Glory Trader" 77/2004 CSBC, Taiwan (SS 08/2027, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Chinese interests at USD 11,7 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Pan Eldorado" 77/2004 CSBC, Taiwan (SS/DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in November at USD 8,8 mill. On the Supramax sector, Japanese owners committed their "Royal Samurai" 58/2010 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 17,3 mill. Note that the one-year-older "Rego" 58/2009 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 08/2029, DD 02/2027, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 16,5 mill. Moreover, Chinese owners committed the "Seacon Yantai" 57/2010 Cosco, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) to clients of PT Primatama Energi Mandiri at mid-high USD 13 mill. H Vogemann committed their Handysize "Voge Mia" & "Voge Emma" 36/2011 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 05/2026 & SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 30,6 mill enbloc, while the one-year-younger sister vessel "Darya Jamuna" 36/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 05/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) from Chellaram Shipping was committed at low USD 16 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low-mid usd 9,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 10,000/12,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 23,000/24,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K-900K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 18,000 pd for 8/10 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesia trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/18,000 pd. Trips from Philippines into China were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. South African trips into India were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 180K/200K. Mediterranean trips into US Gulf were fixed around usd 13,000/14,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K-600K. An Ultramax was reported fixed in the usd 14,500 pd for 3/5 months employment basis delivery India.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. Continental trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. Trips from US East Coast into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
