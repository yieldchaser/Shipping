### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Capesize "Highland Park" 174/2006 SWS, China (SS 03/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed to Chinese interests at low USD 15 mill. For reference, the three-years-younger "Mineral Ningbo" 178/2009 SWS, China (SS/DD 07/2024) was sold a month ago at region USD 20 mill basis prompt delivery in China. Star Bulk Carriers have sold their Ultramax "Star Bovarius" 61/2015 DACKS, China (SS/DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek interests at low USD 25 mill, while the one-year-younger sister vessels "Xing He Hai" & "Xing Hao Hai" 61/2016 DACKS, China (SS/DD 03/2026 & SS 03/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted both) were committed to Chinese buyers at USD 52 mill enbloc. On the Supramax sector, the "Ikan Parang" 56/2011 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) of Pacific Carriers was committed at high USD 11 mill. Remind you that back in September, the "Heilan Song" 56/2011 Tianjin, China (SS 12/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 13,1 mill. Moreover, the "Richmond Pearl" 53/2009 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 10,8 mill. Note that the one-year-older "RHL Clarita" 53/2008 Chengxi, China (SS 03/2028, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in October at USD 9,5 mill. Tide Line have sold their Handysize "Agali" 28/2013 Imabari, Japan (SS 07/2028, DD 10/2026, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for low USD 13 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Agia Irini" 28/2013 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in June at USD 13,8 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Glencore have committed their LR1 "Alpine Pembroke" 74/2010 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) & "Alpine Pioneer" 74/2011 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) to Turkish buyers Advantage Tankers for USD 65 mill enbloc. Note that a month ago, the older sister vessel "Leon Apollon" 75/2009 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 07/2024) was sold at USD 28,5 mill.
