### Bulkers Market Commentary

Hsin Chien Marine have committed their Capesize "New Delight" 181/2012 Imabari, Japan (SS 07/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) to clients of Jinhui Shipping & Transportation for USD 30,95 mill. Remind you that back in November, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Frontier Brilliance" 181/2013 Imabari, Japan (SS 12/2028, DD 10/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 30,5 mill. Furthermore, the "Big Bang" 174/2007 SWS, China (SS 04/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) of Star Bulk Carriers was committed to Chinese interests at region USD 20 mill. Note that the one- year-older sister vessel "Highland Park" 174/2006 SWS, China (SS 03/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at low USD 15 mill. The Post Panamax "AM Point Lisas" 93/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Chinese interests at region USD 14 mill, while one-year-younger sister vessels "AM Tubarao" & "AM Ghent" 93/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS 07/2026, DD 04/2024 & SS 06/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) were committed at USD 14,9 & 15,2 mill respectively. For comparison, the "GH Harmony" 92/2010 Yangfan, China (SS/DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 12,9 mill. The Panamax "Great Venture" 77/2008 Oshima, Japan (SS 03/2028, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Chinese interests at excess USD 14 mill. Remind you that back in October, the "Magic Phoenix" 76/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at region USD 14 mill. DTK Shipping have committed their Ultramax "Ocean Venus" 61/2012 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) at region USD 21 mill. Note that in the end of November, the "Santa Vitoria" 61/2012 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 19,8 mill. On the Supramax sector, Costamare committed their "Pegasus" 56/2011 Cosco, China (SS 01/2026, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 13 mill, while the "Rui Fu Kang" 57/2011 Xiamen, China (SS 09/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at high USD 12 mill. In addition, Wah Kwong Shipping Holdings sold the "Beijing Venture" 53/2010 Chengxi, China (SS 02/2025, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 11 mill. Clients of Costamare have also committed their Handysize "Alliance" 33/2012 Samjin, China (SS 01/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek interests at USD 12,2 mill. Note that the one-year-older sister vessel "Adventure" 33/2011 Samjin, China (SS/DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold in December at USD 11,4 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 10,000/12,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/13,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 12,000/15,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via North Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 17,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K/800K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 17,000 pd for 1 year employment basis delivery Japan. Also, a Panamax was fixed on a period at the usd 13,500 pd basis delivery China for 5/7 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips into China with salt were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into East Coast India were fixed around usd 22,000/25,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 600K/800K. An Ultramax was fixed on period at the usd 16,000 pd basis delivery China for 5/7 months employment. Also, a Supramax was fixed on a period at the usd 13,000 pd basis delivery China for 4/6 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

South East Asian trips into China were fixed around usd 5,000/6,000 pd. Indian trips into South East Asia were reported at the level of usd 6,000/7,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into US East Coast were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South American trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
