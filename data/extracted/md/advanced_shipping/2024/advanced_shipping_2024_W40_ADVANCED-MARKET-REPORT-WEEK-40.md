### Bulkers Market Commentary

Chinese owners committed the Capesize "Azure Ocean" 180/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 24,6 mill. For reference, the "Penelope T" 180/2007 Koyo, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 23,8 mill. Note that a month ago, the one-year-older "Eternal Hakata" 61/2014 Imabari, Japan (SS 02/2027, DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 24,75 mill. Remind you that back in May, the one- year-younger sister vessel "Sibulk Tradition" 53/2008 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 14,5 mill. For reference, the "Morges" 35/2011 Shinan, Korea (SS 12/2026, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in April at mid USD 14 mill.
