### Bulkers Market Commentary

For reference, the "Trust Shanghai" & "Trust Qingdao" 209/2021 SWS, China (SS/DD 04/2026 & SS/DD 02/2026, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) were committed a couple of months ago at excess USD 73 mill each. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Guo Tai Ping An" 56/2011 Qingshan, China (SS/DD 12/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 14 mill. ### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/13,000 pd. Australian trips into Japan were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. West African trips via North Coast South America into Continent were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via US East Coast into India were fixed around usd 22,000/24,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K/1M. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 16,750 pd for 8/11 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from China into India were fixed around usd 13,000/16,000 pd. Chinese trips into North Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Indian trips into China with salt were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

No fixture reported for this segment. No period activity to report here.
