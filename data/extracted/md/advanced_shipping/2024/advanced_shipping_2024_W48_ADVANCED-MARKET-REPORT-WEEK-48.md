### Bulkers Market Commentary

Japanese owners committed their Newcastlemax "Amber Horizon" 208/2010 Universal, Japan (SS 03/2028, DD 03/2026) to Chinese interests at USD 33 mill. Remind you that back in September, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Golden Gayle" 206/2011 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 12/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 40 mill. The Capesize "Nordboen Sun" 169/2010 Daehan, Korea (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to undisclosed interests at USD 23,5 mill. For reference, the two-years-older "Genco Hadrian" 169/2008 Sungdong, Korea (SS 12/2028, DD 11/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold back in July at USD 25 mill. Anglo-Eastern committed their Post Panamax "Pisces First" 93/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at high USD 12 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Potina" 93/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS 02/2026, DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at high USD 13 mill. On the Kamsarmax sector, Arcelormittal Shipping committer their "AM Contrecoeur" 82/2011 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 11/2026, BWTS fitted) at low-mid USD 17 mill. Remind you that back in October, the one- year-older "PS Cadiz" 82/2010 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 16,8 mill. Furthermore, the "Yangze 12" 81/2019 Jiangsu, China (SS 01/2029, DD 11/2026, BWTS fitted) was committed at excess USD 29 mill, while the one-year-younger "Vincent Triton" 81/2020 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in September at USD 29,95 mill. Three 2009-built Supramax vessels were committed this week. The "Aurora SB" 56/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS 09/2029, DD 10/2027, BWTS fitted) was committed at mid-high USD 15 mill, while the sister vessel "Atlantica Sun" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 15,2 mill. In addition, the "PPS Luck" 55/2009 Kawasaki, Japan (SS 01/2029, DD 02/2027, BWTS fitted) was committed at mid-high USD 15 mill.
