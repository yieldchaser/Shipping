### Bulkers Market Commentary

Following the acquisition of the Capesize "Corinthian Phoenix" 179/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) back in May at USD 29 mill, clients of Hayfin Capital Management have now acquired the "Nymphe" 180/2009 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) from Neda Maritime Agency at USD 29,3 mill. Globus Maritime have committed their Panamax "Moon Globe" 74/2005 Hudong-Zhonghua, China (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 11,5 mill. Remind you that a couple of months ago, the two-years-younger "W- Raptor" 76/2007 Jiangnan, China (SS 04/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 12,85 mill. The Ultramax "Velvet" 62/2018 Oshima, Japan (SS 11/2028, DD 09/2026, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) of Drydel Shipping was committed to Korean interests at region USD 36 mill. Note that a month ago, the one-year-older sister vessel "Ping Hai" 62/2017 Oshima, Japan (SS 09/2027, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 32 mill. On the Supramax sector, Hainan Guotai Shipping committed their "Guo Tai Ping An" 56/2011 Qingshan, China (SS/DD 12/2026, BWTS fitted) at USD 14 mill, while the one-year-younger "Panagia Kanala" 56/2012 Cosco, China (SS 06/2027, DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 16 mill. For reference, the sister vessel of the latter "Delta Avon" 56/2012 Cosco, China (SS 03/2027, DD 03/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold in May at USD 14,3 mill. Nova Marine Carriers have committed their boxed Handysize "Sider Eva Maria" 39/2014 Chengxi, China (SS 01/2029, DD 11/2026, BWTS fitted) at USD 21 mill. For reference, the one-year-younger OHBS "Cielo Di Valparaiso" 39/2015 Yangfan, China (SS/DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 21,8 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 17,000/19,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/20,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 12,000/15,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 18,000/21,000 pd. Inter- Continental trips via North Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 18,000/22,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 22,000/25,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K/1M. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 17,500 pd for 7/9 months employment basis delivery Japan.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 12,000/15,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into India were fixed around usd 14,000/18,000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/21,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 170K/210K. Continental trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. Trips from US East Coast into India were reported at the level of usd 26,000/28,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of trips from Japan into India were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 16,850 for 2-3 laden legs. US Gulf trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. North Coast South American trips into East Coast South America were fixed around usd 9,000/10,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
