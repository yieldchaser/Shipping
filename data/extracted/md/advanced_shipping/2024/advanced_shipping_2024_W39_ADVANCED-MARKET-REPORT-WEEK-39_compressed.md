### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 13,000/16,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/16,000 pd. A number of and Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 11,000/14,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Continent were fixed around usd 17,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 100K/200K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 17,750 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment. Trips from China into Continent via South Africa were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 19,000/22,000 pd. Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/15,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Continent were fixed around usd 18,000/21,000 pd. An Ultramax was reported fixed in the usd 17,000 pd for 5/7 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips from China into Indonesia were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A number of and Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Mediterranean trips Continent were fixed around usd 10,000/11,000 pd. US Gulf trips were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
