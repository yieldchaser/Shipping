### Bulkers Market Commentary

Japanese owners have committed the Kamsarmax "YMK Quartet" 82/2021 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 02/2026, BWTS fitted) to Greek interests at high USD 35 mill. Note that a month ago, the one-year-older "Aquavita Sol" 81/2020 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 33,5 mill. Furthermore, the "Scarlet Island" 81/2014 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) of Shikishima Kisen was committed to Greek interests also at USD 29 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "AOM Georgina" 82/2014 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 04/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold back in October at USD 24,7 mill. Yahata Kisen have committed their Ultramax "Maritime Prosperity" 61/2012 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 11/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek interests at region USD 22 mill basis delivery Jun'24. For comparison, the sister vessel "Ocean Venus" 61/2012 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in February at USD 20,8 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Daidan Mustikawati" 55/2005 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 01/2025) was committed to Chinese interests at USD 11 mill. Note that back in March, the one-year-older "Gant Muse" 56/2004 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 11,3 mill. ORIX Maritime have committed their Handysize "Susanoo Harmony" 37/2020 Saiki, Japan (SS/DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 29,5 mill, while the one-year-older "FW Excursionist" 34/2019 Hakodate, Japan (SS/DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to clients of Dadaylilar Shipping at USD 27 mill. Moreover, the Korean built "Morges" 35/2011 Shinan, Korea (SS 12/2026, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed at mid USD 14 mill. Remind you that back in December, the sister vessel "Vully" 35/2011 Shinan, Korea (SS 06/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 13 mill.

Libya's General National Maritime Transport have acquired the Aframax "Calypso" 111/2021 Sumitomo, Japan (SS 07/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) from Samos Steamship at a price of USD 79 mill. In addition, the "Izumo Princess" 105/2007 Sumitomo, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) of Tsakos Shipping & Trading was committed at low USD 37 mill. Remind you that the one-year-younger "Koro Sea" 105/2008 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 02/2028, DD 05/2026, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 39,5 mill. For comparison, the "Glenda Melanie" 47/2010 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 27,5 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of high usd 8,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 12,000/13,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/17,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/19,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were reported at the level of usd 26,000/28,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 21,000/23,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K/900K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 18,000 pd for 6/9 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesia trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/15,000 pd. A number of Indonesia trips into India were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. Trips from China via Philippines into China with nickel ore were reported at the level of usd 15,000/18,000 pd. Indian coastal trips were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 200K/220K. Mediterranean trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of South East Asian trips via Australia into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
