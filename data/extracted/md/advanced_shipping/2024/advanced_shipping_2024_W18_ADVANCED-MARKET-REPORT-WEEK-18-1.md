### Bulkers Market Commentary

Oak Maritime Canada have committed their Capesize "Heng Shan" 174/2007 SWS, China (SS 09/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 22 mill. Note that back in February, the sister vessel "Big Bang" 174/2007 SWS, China (SS 04/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold at region USD 20 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Gillingham" 58/2010 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 13,8 mill. Note that the sister vessel "Archagelos Michael" 58/2010 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 13,9 mill basis delivery June- July'24. Furthermore, the "FLC Longivity" 56/2009 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 11,8 mill. Remind you that in February, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Seamec Nidhi" 56/2010 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 10,8 mill. The Handysize "Taikoo Brilliance" 37/2015 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Turkish interests at USD 20,8 mill, while the "New Journey" 36/2015 Shikoku, Japan (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 20 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Following the sale of the Suezmax "Front Thor" 156/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 01/2025, Scrubber fitted) at USD 45,5 mill back in February, Mssrs Frontline have now committed the sister vessel "Front Loki" 156/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 03/2025, Scrubber fitted) to Norwegian interests at USD 47 mill. The Aframax "Claret Prince" 109/2010 Hudong-Zhonghua, China (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was committed to Chinese interests at USD 43,5 mill. Note that a week ago, the one-year-older "Afragold" 112/2009 New Times, China (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 41 mill. Moreover, the "Sona Star" 105/2003 Sumitomo, Japan (SS 11/2028, DD 04/2027, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 27,5 mill. Remind you that in early March, the one-year-younger "Sea Hazel" 106/2004 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 01/2029, DD 01/2027, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 29 mill. The MR2 "Seaways Niagara" & "Seaways Nantucket" 51/2008 Stx, Korea (SS 01/2028, DD 02/2026 & SS 06/2028, DD 07/2026, BWTS fitted) were committed to Middle Eastern buyers at USD 25,1 mill each. For comparison, the one-year-older sister vessel "Hansa Bergen" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 08/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 22,2 mill. In addition, clients of KSS Line acquired the "STI Manhattan" 50/2015 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) from Scorpio Tankers at USD 41 mill. Remind you that same clients acquired back in January the sister vessel "STI Tribeca" 50/2015 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) for USD 39,1 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/21,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were fixed around usd 30,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 900K/1M. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 16,500 pd for 6/8 months employment basis delivery China. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 17,150 pd basis delivery China for 1 year employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/20,000 pd. Trips from Philippines into China with nickel ore were fixed around usd 16,000/17,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. South African trips into China were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 180K/200K. An Ultramax was fixed on a period at the usd 20,000 pd basis delivery South East Asia for 3/5 months employment with redelivery PG-Japan range.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from US Gulf into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 9,000/10,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
