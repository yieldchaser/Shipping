### Bulkers Market Commentary

Ebe NV have committed their Capesize "El Grasso" 181/2012 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) to clients of Agricore at high USD 35 mill. Note that a month ago, the "Frontier Unity" 181/2012 Koyo, Japan (SS/DD 12/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold at USD 35 mill. The Kamsarmax "Vincent Talisman" 81/2020 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) of Nisshin Shipping was committed to Chinese interests at low USD 33 mill. Remind you that back in March, the sister vessel "Aquavita Sol" 81/2020 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 33,5 mill. Sea Pioneer Shipping have committed their Panamax "Bravery" 76/2004 Imabari, Japan (SS 06/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed interests at region USD 12 mill. For reference, the two-years-younger sister vessel "W-Galaxy" 76/2006 Imabari, Japan (SS 07/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in February at USD 13 mill. On the Supramax sector, Atlantic Bulk Carriers committed their "Desert Symphony" 53/2007 Kouan, China (SS 01/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 11,45 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Desert Unity" 54/2006 Kouan, China (SS/DD 10/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 10,1 mill. In addition, F-Maritime committed their "Magnum Force" 53/2008 Yangzhou, China (SS 11/2028, DD 10/2026, BWTS fitted) at USD 11,8 mill. Navigation Maritime Bulgare committed their Handysize sister vessels "Persenk" & "Belmeken" 30/2010 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 07/2025 & SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) at excess USD 21 mill enbloc. For comparison, the "New Legend Pearl" 32/2010 Jiangmen, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 10,2 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low-mid usd 10,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 16,000/19,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into Philippines were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into West Coast India were reported at the level of usd 22,000/23,000 pd. Continental trips into China were fixed around usd 37,000/40,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K/1M. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 17,500 pd for 6/8 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Trips from China into Persian Gulf or East Africa/Red Sea were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Indian trips into China via South Africa were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Indian Coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. A Supramax was reported fixed in the usd 17,600 pd for 6/8 months employment basis delivery China with redelivery PG-Japan range.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from Persian Gulf into East Coast India were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
