### Bulkers Market Commentary

HMM have committed their Capesize "FEG Success" 182/2010 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to compatriots clients of Korea Line at USD 28,2 mill, while the one-year-younger "Zampa Blue" 178/2011 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 30 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the "Cape Laurel" 180/2010 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 28 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Indigo Omega" 56/2012 Mitsui, Japan (SS 10/2027, DD 08/2026, BWTS fitted) was committed to Greek interests at low USD 17 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-older sister vessel "ND Armonia" 56/2011 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 18 mill. Furthermore, Densay Shipping & Trading committed the "SSI Nemesis" 56/2005 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 10,9 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Ionic Storm" 56/2005 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in July at low USD 13 mill. In addition, the "Zein" 52/2001 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 05/2027, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) of Allied Marine Serviced was committed at USD 7 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Sonya Blade" 52/2001 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 10/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in late April at USD 8,3 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Following the sale of the MR2 "Torm Platte" 46/2006 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) back in June at USD 23,5 mill, clients of TORM A/S have now committed the sister vessel "Torm Republican" 46/2006 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 03/2026, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed interests at USD 18,8 mill. Navigazione Montanari SpA have committed their MR1 "Valle Di Granada" 40/2005 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) to UAE interests at USD 13,4 mill. For reference, the one-year-older sister vessel "Valle Di Nervion" 40/2004 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in April at USD 15,3 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 5,000/7,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 5,000/7,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 6,000/7,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. South African trips into India were fixed around usd 12,000/13,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 200K/300K. Inter-Continental trips via US Gulf were fixed around usd 9,000/11,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America into Black Sea were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 200K/400K. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 11,000/13,000 pd. A number of. Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. South American trips into Arabian Gulf were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 200K/400K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of Continental trips via Baltic into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 8,000/10,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
