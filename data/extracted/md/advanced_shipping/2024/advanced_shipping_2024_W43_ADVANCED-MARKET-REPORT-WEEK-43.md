### Bulkers Market Commentary

Chinese owners have committed the Kamsarmax "Nova Optimus" 81/2012 Jiangsu, China (SS 09/2027, DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) to clients of Fujian Haitong at mid USD 16 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Theresa Guangdong" 81/2012 Jiangsu, China (SS 05/2027, DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in August at USD 19,2 mill. The Ultramax "Scorpio WQ" 76/2004 Sasebo, Japan (SS 02/2029, DD 07/2026, BWTS fitted) was committed at region USD 10,6 mill, while the one-year-younger "HC Victory" 75/2005 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 10,5 mill. For reference, the "Bravery" 76/2004 Imabari, Japan (SS 06/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in May at region USD 12 mill. Oldendorff Carriers committed their Ultramax "Alwine Oldendorff" 61/2014 JMU, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) & "August Oldendorff" 61/2015 JMU, Japan (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) were committed to Greek interests at USD 50,5 mill enbloc. Note that earlier this month, the "Lowlands Amstel" 61/2015 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at low-mid USD 26 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Virono Pride" 58/2009 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 01/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Chinese interests at low-mid USD 15 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the sister vessel "Titan I" 58/2009 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 16 mill. Moreover, Spar Shipping committed their "Spar Spica" 53/2005 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) at region USD 10,5 mill, while the sister vessel "Spar Lyra" 53/2005 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in July at USD 10,2 mill. Japanese owners committed their Handysize "Ken Mei" 29/2003 Shikoku, Japan (SS 09/2028, DD 10/2026, BWTS fitted) at USD 7,3 mill and the two- years-younger sister vessel "Ken Toku" 29/2005 Shikoku, Japan (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) at mid USD 8 mill. Note that the sister vessel "Ken Hou" 29/2004 Shikoku, Japan (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in July at USD 8,6 mill.

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 11,000/13,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/14,000 pd. South African trips into India were fixed around usd 12,000/13,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 200K/300K. Continental trips via US East Coast into Atlantic were reported at the level of usd 10,000/11,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/600K. US Gulf trips into South America were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 19,000/20,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 300K/500K. West African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
