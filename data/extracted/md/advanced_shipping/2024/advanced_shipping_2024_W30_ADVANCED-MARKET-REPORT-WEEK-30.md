### Bulkers Market Commentary

Tokei Kaiun have committed their Capesize "Cape Mathilde" 178/2010 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at region USD 30 mill. Remind you that a couple of months ago, the two-years- younger "El Grasso" 181/2012 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at high USD 35 mill. Furthermore, following the sale of the "Genco Maximus" 169/2009 Sungdong, Korea (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) & "Genco Claudius" 169/2010 Sungdong, Korea (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) back in March at USD 47 mill enbloc, clients of GENCO have now committed the sister vessel "Genco Hadrian" 169/2008 Sungdong, Korea (SS 12/2028, DD 11/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Far Eastern interests at USD 25 mill. Castor Maritime announced the acquisition of the Ultramax "Swansea" 63/2015 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) from Norbulk Shipping at a price of USD 25,5 mill. Moreover, Taiwan Shipping committed their "Tai Shine" 61/2012 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 10/2027, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) at region USD 22 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Maritime Prosperity" 61/2012 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 11/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed back in April at same levels. On the Supramax sector, Nanjing Ocean Shipping committed their "Heilan Cruiser" 56/2012 Shanghai, China (SS 12/2026, DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) at excess USD 14 mill. Note that earlier this month, the "Tristar Prosperity" 56/2012 Cosco, China (SS/DD 06/2027, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 15 mill. In addition, the "Ionic Storm" 56/2005 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) of Ionic Holdings was committed to Greek interests at low USD 13 mill. Remind you that the one-year-younger sister vessel "Ocean Glory" 56/2006 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in April at low USD 13 mill also. The OHBS Handysize "Coreleader OL" 37/2012 Saiki, Japan (SS 10/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) of Oceanlance Maritime was committed to clients of Manta Shipping at USD 17,4 mill. For comparison, the "Daydream Believer" 37/2012 Onomichi, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted, OHBS) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 17,6 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 13,000/15,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/15,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 15,000/18,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via US East Coast into China were fixed around usd 25,000/27,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 22,000/23,000. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 17,250 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery China. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 17,000 pd basis delivery South Korea for 2-year employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/19,000 pd. Indian Coastal trips were fixed around usd 9,000/10,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into India were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16.000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 10,000/12,000 pd. West African trips via South Africa into China were reported at the level of usd 20,000/23,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 25,000/27,000 pd. South American trips into India were reported at the level of usd 17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 19,000/21,000 pd. On the period front, a Handysize was reported fixed in the usd 14,000 pd for 5/7 months employment basis delivery South East Asia.
