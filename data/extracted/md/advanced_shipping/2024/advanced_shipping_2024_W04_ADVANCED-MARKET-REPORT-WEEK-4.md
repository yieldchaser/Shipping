### Bulkers Market Commentary

Oldendorff Carriers have committed their Post Panamax "Charlotte Oldendorff" 93/2010 Yangfan, China (SS/DD 02/2025, Scrubber fitted) to Greek interests at USD 15,5 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the sister vessel "GH Harmony" 92/2010 Yangfan, China (SS/DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 12,9 mill. The Panamax "Kerveros" 76/2003 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) of Titan Maritime was committed at USD 9,35 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger "Topaz" 75/2004 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 10,6 mill. Parakou Shipping have committed their Ultramax "CP Tianjin" 63/2016 Chengxi, China (SS 09/2026, DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) & "CP Guangzhou" 63/2015 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 23,8 mill each. Remind you that back in October, the sister vessel "CP Shanghai" 63/2015 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 23,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Isabella M" 56/2006 Mitsui, Japan (SS 07/2026, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold to Chinese interests at USD 12,5 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Advance" 55/2007 Mitsui, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 13,7 mill. The Handysize "Rui Fu Xing" 37/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 07/2027, DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted, OHBS) was committed at low-mid USD 16 mill. For reference, the Japanese built "Pacific Island" 38/2012 Shimanami, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold back in October at region USD 16 mill. In addition, Uni Ships & Management sold their "Uni Wealth" 29/2009 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 8,5 mill, while the "Seastar Tradition" 30/2009 Tsuji, China (SS/DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 9 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout China were reported at the level of usd 41,500 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 11,000/14,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/19,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 13,000/16,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via West Africa were reported at the level of usd 19,000/21,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into China were fixed around usd 18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K. Trips from US East Coast into India were reported at the level of usd 27,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 18,000/19,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K/900K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 15,750 pd for 6/8 months employment basis delivery Japan. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 19,250 pd basis delivery China for 1 year employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 8,000/10,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into East Coast India with limestone were fixed around usd 17,000/21,000 pd. South American trips into India were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 600K/800K. An Ultramax was fixed on period at the usd 15,500 pd basis delivery China for 5/7 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

No fixture reported for this segment. No period activity to report here.
