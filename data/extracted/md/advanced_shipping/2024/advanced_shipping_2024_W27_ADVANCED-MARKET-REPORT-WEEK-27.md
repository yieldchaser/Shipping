### Bulkers Market Commentary

Following the acquisition of the Capesize "El Grasso" 181/2012 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) back in May at high USD 35 mill, Mssrs Agricore have now acquired the "Iron Phoenix" 180/2012 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 11/2026, DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) from Nissen Kaiun at USD 35,5 mill basis delivery August-October'24. Furthermore, Kobe Shipping committed their "Ocean Courtesy" 178/2008 SWS, China (SS 03/2028, DD 01/2027, BWTS fitted) to clients of Jinhui Shipping at USD 24 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older "Heng Shan" 174/2007 SWS, China (SS 09/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 22 mill. The Ultramax "Luna Rossa" 61/2010 Oshima, Japan (SS 07/2028, DD 08/2026, BWTS fitted) of Drydel Shipping was committed to Chinese interests at USD 20,5 mill, while the one-year-younger "Almira" 61/2011 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS/DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) of Empros Lines Shipping was also committed to Chinese interests at USD 21,5 mill. Note that the "Maritime Prosperity" 61/2012 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 11/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in April at USD 22 mill. On the Supramax sector, Eships committed their "Tristar Prosperity" 56/2012 Cosco, China (SS/DD 06/2027, BWTS fitted) at USD 15 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Panagia Kanala" 56/2012 Cosco, China (SS 06/2027, DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in June at USD 16 mill. Moreover, the "Nordic Stavanger" 56/2011 Mitsui, Japan (SS 11/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed at region USD 18 mill, while the one- year-older sister vessel "Bright Hero" 55/2010 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 16,8 mill. The Handysize "Darya Ganga" 36/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 02/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) of Chellaram Shipping was committed at low USD 16 mill. Remind you that back in March, the one-year-older sister vessel "Omnia" 36/2011 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 08/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 15,2 mill. In addition, the OHBS "HG Darwin" 31/2002 Saiki, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to UAE based interests at USD 8,2 mill. Note that the sister vessel "Salvador" 31/2002 Saiki, Japan (SS 08/2027, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted, OHBS) was sold back in April at USD 7,7 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid usd 11,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Inter- Continental trips via North Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 19,000/21,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K/900K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 15,000 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

Trips from China into India were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Chinese trips via South Africa into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 16,000/17,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 180K. Persian Gulf trips into India were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. US Gulf trips were fixed around usd 16,000/17,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 14,000/15,000 pd. On the period front, a Handysize was reported fixed in the usd 12,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 370K for 4/6 months employment basis delivery South America.
