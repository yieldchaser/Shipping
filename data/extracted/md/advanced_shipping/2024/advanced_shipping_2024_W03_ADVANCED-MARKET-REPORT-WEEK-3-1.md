### Bulkers Market Commentary

Eagle Bulk Shipping have committed their Supramax "Crested Eagle" & "Stellar Eagle" 56/2009 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 04/2024 & SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) at USD 14,5 mill each, while the "Queen Kobe" 55/2009 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to Indonesian buyers at high USD 14 mill. Remind you that in early December, the one- year-older "Ermione" 56/2008 IHI, Japan (SS 09/2028, DD 06/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 14,2 mill. In addition, the "APJ Jad" 52/2002 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 05/2027, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) from Apeejay Shipping was committed at USD 7,2 mill. Note that a couple of months ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Star Theta" 52/2003 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 06/2028, DD 10/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 8,5 mill. The Handysize "Alam Suria" 29/2012 Shikoku, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) of Tsurumi Kisen was committed to undisclosed interests at USD 11,8 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger "Agali" 28/2013 Imabari, Japan (SS 07/2028, DD 10/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold three weeks ago at low USD 13 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Angelicoussis Shipping committed their VLCC "Elizabeth I.A." 306/2004 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 34 mill. Remind you that the Japanese built "Jessica D" 301/2004 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 07/2024) was sold in late November at USD 32,5 mill. On the Aframax sector, Active Shipping committed the "Fair Seas" 115/2008 Stx, Korea (SS 07/2028, DD 09/2026) for USD 43,5 mill. Note that a month ago, the one-year-older "Aegean Power" 115/2007 Samsung, Korea (SS 04/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 42 mill. Harren Tanker Management have commited the LR1 "Pataris "73/2009 New Times, China (SS/DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) to clients of Trafigura at USD 26 mill, while the two-year-older Korean built "Brook Trout" & "Lake Trout" 73/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 08/2027, DD 12/2025 & SS 07/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A) of Prime Marine Management were committed to Emarat Maritime at USD 26 mill each also. The MR1 "Dinah" & "Pluto" 37/2008 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 11/2028, DD 12/2025 & SS 10/2028, DD 11/2028, Ice Class A1) were committed at USD 41,75 mill enbloc. For reference, the two-years-older "Baltic Freedom" 37/2006 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 09/2026, DD 10/2024, Ice Class 1A) was sold back in September at region USD 18 mill.
