### Bulkers Market Commentary

Japanese owners have committed the Capesize "Cape Keystone" 179/2011 Hyundai, Korea (SS 12/2026, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 32,5 mill. Note that a month ago, the two-years-older sister vessel "Corinthian Phoenix" 179/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 29 mill. Lavinia Corp have committed their Panamax sister vessels "Icarus" & "Thisseas" & "Atlas" 75/2012 Penglai, China (SS 02/2027, DD 09/2025 & SS 11/2026, DD 11/2024 & SS 10/2027, DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek interests at USD 17,5 mill each. For comparison, the "Ying Hao 03" 76/2012 Hudong-Zhonghua, China (SS/DD 05/2027, BWTS fitted) was sold three weeks ago at low USD 17 mill. The Ultramax "Swansea" 63/2015 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) of Norbulk Shipping was committed to undisclosed interests at USD 25 mill. Remind you that back in March, the "Syros Island" 63/2015 Zhejiang, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 25,7 mill. On the Supramax sector, Golden Union Shipping committed their "Captain Andreadis" 58/2008 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 11/2028, DD 12/2026, BWTS fitted) at low-mid USD 16 mill. Note that the two-years-younger sister vessel "Marylaki" 58/2010 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in May at low USD 17 mill. Roussos Shipping have committed their Handysize "Aktea R" 28/2010 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) at high USD 10 mill. Remind you that back in March, the one-year-older sister vessel "Rin Treasure" 28/2009 Imabari, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 9,5 mill.

A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 16,000/19,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/20,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 16,000/20,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Indian trips via South Africa into China were fixed around usd 11,000/12,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were reported at the level of usd 23,000/27,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K/1M. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 19,400 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

Trips from Philippines into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 16,000/17,000 pd. Indian trips via South Africa into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. South African trips into China were fixed around usd 22,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 320K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of Indian trips via Australia into Persian Gulf were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,000 pd. Continental trips into US East Coast were fixed around usd 8,000/10,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
