### Bulkers Market Commentary

Ever Stella Shipping have committed the Capesize "Stella Hope" 180/2016 Dalian, China (SS/DD 09/2026, BWTS fitted) was committed to clients of GENCO at mid USD 47 mill. Note that back in July, the "Herun Global" 181/2016 SWS, China (SS 08/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 49,5 mill. Premuda's Kamsarmax "PS Cadiz" 82/2010 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to clients of Pioneer Marine at USD 16,8 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Oasea" 82/2010 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in May at USD 20,25 mill. The Ultramax "Beechgate" 63/2019 Iwagi, Japan (SS 03/2029, DD 04/2027, BWTS fitted) of Zodiac Maritime was committed to clients of Meghna at USD 35,6 mill. Remind you that back in July, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Jal Kamal" 63/2020 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 37,75 mill. In addition, the older "Nord Adriatic" 61/2016 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 04/2026, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 29,1 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "NPS Mosa" 53/2007 Iwagi, Japan (SS 06/2027, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed to Korean interests at high USD 12 mill, while the sister vessel "A Wisdom" 53/2007 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed a week ago at low USD 13 mill. Following the sale of the Handysize "Maple Fortitude" 32/2011 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 03/2026, BWTS fitted) at region USD 11 mill, Maple Leaf Shipping have now committed the one-year-older sister vessel "Maple Fortune" 32/2010 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 11 mill also.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 12,000/15,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/16,000 pd. A number of and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/15,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/700K. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/16,000 pd. Arabian Gulf trips into East Coast India were fixed around usd 16,000/17,000 pd. South African trips into West Coast India were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 150K/160K. Continental trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Continental trips into North Coast South America were fixed around usd 9,000/10,000 pd. Fronthaul trips from US East Coast were reported at the level of usd 20,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 12,000/13,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into US East Coast were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. West Coast Central American trips via West Coast Canada into Far East were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
