### Bulkers Market Commentary

Teh-Hu Steamship have committed their Capesize "Courageous" 181/2016 SWS, China (SS/DD 11/2026, BWTS fitted) to Greek interests at USD 50,5 mill, while the "Stella Hope" 180/2016 Dalian, China (SS 09/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) of Cara Shipping was committed to Greek interests also at USD 49,7 mill. Kyowa Sansho committed their Mini Capesize "Unta" 106/2009 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 18,5 mill. Remind you that the "Spring Samcheonpo" 119/2009 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed in early April at USD 18,4 mill. Japanese owners committed the Kamsarmax "Livia Rose" 81/2018 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 01/2028, DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) to clients of HMM at USD 35,7 mill. Note that the two-years-older sister vessel "Valiant Summer" 81/2016 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 06/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold in May at USD 32,5 mill. Furthermore, the "Sea Opal" 79/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) from Hanaro Shipping was committed at high USD 14 mill. For reference, the "Sterling Tora" 79/2010 Jinhai, China (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 14,25 mill. On the Supramax sector, Misuga Kaiun committed their "Maine Dream" 58/2012 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 19,3 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "ASL Fortune" 58/2012 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 04/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in late February at the same price. Moreover, the "Genco Warrior" 55/2005 NACKS, China (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) of GENCO was committed to Chinese interests at USD 12 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Seaboss" 55/2004 NACKS, China (SS/DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold three months ago at USD 11,7 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Following the sale of the "Front Loki" 156/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 03/2025, Scrubber fitted) back in May at USD 47 mill, Mssrs Frontline have now committed the sister vessel "Front Thor" 156/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 01/2025, Scrubber fitted) at excess USD 48 mill. The MR2 "Dignity" 50/2010 Guangzhou, China (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) of Sea Pioneer Shipping was committed to clients of Aerio Shipmanagement at USD 29 mill. Note that earlier this month, the two- years-younger sister vessel "Alhena" 52/2012 Guangzhou, China (SS 05/2027, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 32,2 mill. In addition, Aerio Shipmanagement committed their "Fos Energy" 46/2006 Stx, Korea (SS 03/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 20,5 mill. Remind you that back in April, the sister vessel "Grand Ace5" 46/2006 Stx, Korea (SS 04/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 21 mill.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 13,000/15,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/16,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Inter- Continental trips via North Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. On the period front, a Panamax was reported fixed in the usd 16,000 pd for 11/13 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

#### Handymax / Handysize

Continental trips into US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into West Africa were fixed around usd 14,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
