### Bulkers Market Commentary

Thenamaris have committed their Capesize "Sealink" 180/2010 Daehan, Korea (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) for USD 31 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-older "Athenian Phoenix" 179/2009 (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at region USD 23 mill. Moreover, the "Star Audrey" 175/2011 New Times, China (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) of Starbulk was committed to Greek interests at low-mid USD 27 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older "Seamate" 177/2010 SWS, China (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold in February at USD 26 mill. Following the sale of their Post Panamax "Christine Oldendorff" 93/2010 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) a month ago at USD 15,45 mill, clients of Oldendorff Carriers have now committed the sister vessel "Conrad Oldendorff" 93/2010 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Greek interests at USD 16,5 mill. Transocean Maritime Agencies have committed the Ultramax "Andromeda" 61/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) to Turkish interests at region USD 21 mill. For reference, the "Shimanami Queen" 61/2011 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS/DD 06/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold back in November at USD 18,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, clients of Abo Shoten have committed their "ASL Fortune" 58/2012 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 04/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 19,3 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Solar Africa" 58/2011 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 09/2027, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold three weeks ago at USD 17,8 mill. Furthermore, the "Konrad Schulte" 55/2010 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed at low USD 16 mill. Note that the one-year-younger sister vessel "Melia 1" 55/2011 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 07/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold in February at USD 17,8 mill. In addition, Densay Shipping committed their "SSI Avenger" 53/2004 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 10,5 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-older sister vessel "Rui Fu Sheng" 53/2003 Oshima, Japan (SS 11/2028, DD 11/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 8,5 mill. Chinese owners committed the OHBS Handysize "Lucky Glory" 32/2007 Kanda, Japan (SS 12/2027, DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) to Turkish interests at USD 10,3 mill. Note that the one-year-younger sister vessel "Lago Di Lugano" 32/2008 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 10/2025) was sold recently at USD 10,5 mill.
