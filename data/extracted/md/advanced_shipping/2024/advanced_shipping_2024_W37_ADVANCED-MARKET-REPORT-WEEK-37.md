### Bulkers Market Commentary

Chinese owners committed their Capesize "Azure Ocean" 180/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) to other Chinese interests at USD 25 mill. Remind you that back in March, the "Penelope T" 180/2007 Koyo, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 23,8 mill. Moreover, Norden committed their "Nord Magnes" 179/2011 HHIC, Philippines (SS/DD 11/2026, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 31,5 mill, while the sister vessel "Nord Ferrum" 179/2011 HHIC, Philippines (SS/DD 09/2026, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold back in May at USD 34 mill. The Ultramax "Amis Miracle" 62/2018 Oshima, Japan (SS 01/2028, DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) of Wisdom Marine Lines was committed to clients of Bahri at USD 34,35 mill. Note that back in June, the sister vessel "Velvet" 62/2018 Oshima, Japan (SS 11/2028, DD 09/2026, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold at region USD 36 mill. On the Supramax sector, Costamare committed their "Titan I" 58/2009 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) to clients of Gurita Lintas Samudera at USD 16 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Royal Samurai" 58/2010 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in July at USD 17,75 mill. Furthermore, Santoku Senpaku committed their boxed "Sparna" 54/2006 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at region USD 14 mill, while the sister vessel "Suzaku" 54/2006 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 07/2026, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1C) was sold a month ago at USD 14,5 mill. Kobe Shipping committed their Handysize "Globe Explorer" 28/2015 I-S, Japan (SS/DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 14,2 mill. Note that a week ago, the two-years-older "Ince Evrenye" 28/2013 Imabari, Japan (SS 10/2028, DD 10/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 12,7 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid-high usd 11,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 13,000/15,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/15,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. Indian Coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were reported at the level of usd 20,000/21,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 16,000/19,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/700K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 15,500 pd for 5/7 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from China into Red Sea were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd. Trips from Philippines into China were fixed around usd 16,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into India were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 170K/180K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. West African trips into Continent were fixed around usd 6,000/8,000 pd. US Gulf trips were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,000 pd. Trips from US East Coast into Continent- Mediterranean range were fixed around usd 16,000/19,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into North Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 13,000/16,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
