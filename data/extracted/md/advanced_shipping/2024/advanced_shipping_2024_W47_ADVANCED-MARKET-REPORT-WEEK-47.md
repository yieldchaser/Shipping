### Bulkers Market Commentary

Norden have committed their Capesize "Nord Energy" & "Nord Power" 179/2012 HHIC, Philippines (SS 03/2029, DD 03/2027 & SS 05/2029, DD 05/2027, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) at region USD 32 mill each. Note that back in September, the one-year-older sister vessel "Nord Magnes" 179/2011 HHIC, Philippines (SS/DD 11/2026, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold at USD 31,5 mill. Japanese owners committed their Post Panamax "Verdure Wave" 88/2005 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed interests at high USD 11 mill. Remind you that back in August, the one-year-older sister vessel "Corona Kingdom" 88/2004 Imabari, Japan (SS 11/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at the same levels. The Kamsarmax "Tomini Nobility" 81/2020 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) of Tomini Shipping was committed to Greek interests at USD 28,5 mill. Note that a couple of months ago, the "Vincent Triton" 81/2020 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 30 mill. Furthermore, the "Tristar Dugon" 79/2011 Cosco, China (SS 02/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed to Chinese interests at high USD 14 mill. For reference, the one-year-older "Sea Opal" 79/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in June at the same price. On the Supramax sector, Helikon Shipping Enterprises committed their "Mystras" 57/2013 Stx Dalian, China (SS 01/2028, DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) to clients of Vosco at USD 17,2 mill and the "Elikon" 57/2013 Stx Dalian, China (SS 01/2028, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) to other Vietnamese interests at USD 17,2 mill also. For comparison, the sister vessel "Olympus" 57/2013 Stx Dalian, China (SS 05/2028, DD 03/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold back in July at USD 17,1 mill. Moreover, the "Guang Fa 29" 57/2012 Stx, Korea (SS 01/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) of Taizhou Guangfa Shipping was committed to Chinese interests at USD 15,5 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Kibali"57/2011 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 08/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold in September at USD 16,7 mill. The OHBS Handysize "Wellpark" 37/2014 Oshima, Japan (SS 06/2029, DD 05/2027, BWTS fitted) was committed to undisclosed interests at USD 19,2 mill. For reference, the one-year-younger "Bunun Glory" 37/2015 Saiki, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted, OHBS) was sold in late July at USD 21,5 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 10,000/13,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/15,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 8,000/10,000 pd. West African trips via US Gulf into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. West African trips into Continent were fixed around usd 13,000/14,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 300K/400K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 15,700 pd for 1 year employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 9,000/11,000 pd. Arabian Gulf trips into East Coast India were fixed around usd 10,000/12,000 pd. South African trips into China or India were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 140K/160K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into Indonesia were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into South America were fixed around usd 8,000/11,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 9,000/10,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. US Gulf trips into South America were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/18,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
