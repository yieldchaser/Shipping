### Bulkers Market Commentary

Nicholas G Moundreas Shipping have committed their Capesize "Epic" 180/2010 Daehan, Korea (SS/DD 12/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 32,5 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Sealink" 180/2010 Daehan, Korea (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 31 mill. Furthermore, following the sale of the "Castillo De Valverde" 173/2005 Bohai, China (SS 12/2025, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 15,8 mill, Mssrs Empresa Naviera Elcano committed the sister vessel "Castillo De Catoira" 173/2005 Bohai, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at high USD 16 mill. In addition, GENCO sold their "Genco Maximus" 169/2009 Sungdong, Korea (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) & "Genco Claudius" 169/2010 Sungdong, Korea (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to clients of Golden Union at USD 47 mill enbloc, while the sister vessel "Enco Ommodus" 169/2009 Sungdong, Korea (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold earlier this month at USD 22,5 mill. The Panamax "Ying Hao 02" 75/2012 Guangzhou, China (SS 06/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) of Dongguan Yinghao Shipping was committed at USD 16,8 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Zhong Xin Pearl" 75/2013 Guangzhou, China (SS 09/2028, DD 06/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at the same price. On the Supramax sector, Anthony Giavridis Maritime committed their "Gant Muse" 56/2004 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) to Vietnamese interests at low-mid USD 11 mill. Note that a month ago, the sister vessel "SSI Challenger" 56/2004 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 10,7 mill. Briese Shipping committed their Handysize "BBC Pluto" 37/2010 Tianjin, China (SS/DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1C) to Turkish interests at USD 11,7 mill. For reference, the "Clipper Copenhagen" 37/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS 06/2025, DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1C) was sold back in December at USD 11,2 mill.
