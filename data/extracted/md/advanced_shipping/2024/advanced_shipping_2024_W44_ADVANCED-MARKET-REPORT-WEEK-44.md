### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Capesize "Hero" 178/2010 SWS, China (SS/DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed at region USD 26 mill. Note that back in September, the two- years-older sister vessel "Alpha Prudence" 178/2008 SWS, China (SS 01/2028, DD 04/2026, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold at USD 24,75 mill. Nisshin Shipping committed their Ultramax "Greenwich Pioneer" 63/2020 Nantong, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at excess USD 32 mill basis T/C attached till Mar'25 at 16.500 usd/pd. For reference, the one-year-older "Seacon Athens" 63/2019 Nantong, China (SS 07/2029, DD 07/2027, BWTS fitted) was sold back in August at the same levels. On the Supramax sector, Korea Line Corp committed their "Global Genesis", "Global Brave", "Global Frontier" & "Global Hope" 57/2010 Stx Dalian, China (SS/DD 09/2025, SS/DD 05/2025, SS/DD 08/2025, SS/DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) were committed to undisclosed interests at high USD 12 mill each. For comparison, the one-year-younger sister vessel "H Juno" 57/2011 Stx Dalian, China (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in July at USD 14 mill. The Handysize "Indian Ocean" 36/2011 Samjin, China (SS/DD 10/2026, BWTS fitted) was committed to Indonesian interests at USD 12,5 mill, while the sister vessel "Caroline Selmer" 35/2011 Samjin, China (SS/DD 07/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 12,6 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Thomas Selmer" 35/2011 Samjin, China (SS/DD 04/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold back in September at region USD 13 mill.
