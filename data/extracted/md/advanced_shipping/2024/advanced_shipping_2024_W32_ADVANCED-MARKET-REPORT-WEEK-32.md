### Bulkers Market Commentary

Wilmar International committed their Kamsarmax "Theresa Guangdong" 81/2012 Jiangsu, China (SS 05/2027, DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) and the sisters "Theresa Hebei" & "Theresa Jilin" 81/2012 Sainty, China (SS 03/2027, DD 04/2025 & SS 06/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) to clients of Fujian Highton at USD 19,2 mill each. For reference, the "Yu Qiang" 81/2012 PLA 4807, China (SS 05/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 17,5 mill. The Ultramax "Dolce Vita" 61/2012 Oshima, Japan (SS 09/2027, DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) of Drydel Shipping was committed to Chinese interests at USD 23,5 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Ultra Rocanville" 61/2012 Oshima, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in early May at low USD 23 mill. On the Supramax sector, Santoku Senpaku committed their OHBS "Suzaku" 54/2006 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 07/2026, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1C) at mid USD 14 mill. Moreover, Hubei Qintai Shipping committed their "Monica D" 52/2001 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 12/2026, DD 12/2024) at USD 7,8 mill. Remind you that three months ago, the "Sonya Blade" 52/2001 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 10/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 8,3 mill. Inui Global Logistics committed the OHBS Handysize "Ken Ryu" 31/2002 Saiki, Japan (SS 06/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 6,8 mill. Note that a month ago, the sister vessel "HG Darwin" 31/2002 Saiki, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted, OHBS) was sold at USD 8,2 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The VLCC "Apollo Harmony" 301/2010 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) of Idemitsu Tanker was committed on subs to Greek interests at region USD 58 mill. For reference, the one-year-older Korean built "C. Prosperity" 313/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 08/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold in late June at USD 53,5 mill. Navios committed their MR2 "Nave Orbit" 50/2009 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed interests at USD 25,9 mill basis forward delivery. Remind you that a couple of months ago, the sister vessel "Gunmetal Jack" 50/2009 Spp, Korea (SS 08/2029, DD 05/2027, BWTS fitted) was sold at excess USD 28 mill.
