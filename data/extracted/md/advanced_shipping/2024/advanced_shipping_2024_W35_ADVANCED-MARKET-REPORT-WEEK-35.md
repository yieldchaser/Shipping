### Bulkers Market Commentary

Ocean Freighters have committed their Capesize "Pontotriton" 178/2007 SWS, China (SS 07/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) to clients of Kaishun Shipping at excess USD 23 mill. Remind you that the "Heng Shan" 174/2007 SWS, China (SS 09/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in last April at USD 22 mill. In addition, Maran Dry committed their "Maran Prosperity" 174/2006 SWS, China (SS/DD 10/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 21,5 mill. The Kamsarmax "Nord Penguin" 81/2015 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted, Ice Class 1C) of Norden was committed to Greek interests at mid USD 30 mill, while the "Elsa-S" 80/2015 JMU, Japan (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold a week ago at region USD 31 mill. On the Supramax sector, Singaporean owners committed their "Sania" 57/2010 Qingshan, China (SS/DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at low-mid USD 12 mill. Note that the sister vessel "UM Elhanaya" 56/2010 Qingshan, China (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in July at mid-high USD 12 mill. The Handysize "Floriana" 33/2012 21st Century, Korea (SS 10/2027, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Greek interests at region USD 14 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older "Kouros Pride" 34/2011 Dae Sun, Korea (SS 07/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 13,8 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Turkish owners committed the MR2 "Elegant Grace" 50/2009 Spp, Korea (SS 03/2029, DD 06/2027, BWTS fitted) to Vietnamese interests at high USD 27 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Gunmetal Jack" 50/2009 Spp, Korea (SS 08/2029, DD 05/2027, BWTS fitted) was sold back in June at excess USD 28 mill. For reference, the "Jag Pranam" 48/2004 Iwagi, Japan (SS 12/2027, DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in May at high USD 14 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid usd 11,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 11,000/12,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/700K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 18,500 pd for 1 year employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesia trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/18,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. Indian coastal trips were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. South African trips into India were reported at the level of usd 19,000/22,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 190K/220K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. Mediterranean trips into Caribbean were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Trips from Black Sea into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
