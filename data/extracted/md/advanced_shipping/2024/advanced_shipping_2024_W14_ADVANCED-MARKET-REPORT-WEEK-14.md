### Bulkers Market Commentary

Japanese owners have committed the Post Panamax "Federico II" 92/2009 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 19,6 mill basis SS/DD freshly passed. Note that back in January, the one- year-younger "The Evolution" 88/2010 Imabari, Japan (SS 04/2028, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 17,2 mill. The Kamsarmax "Sammy" 82/2012 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 11/2026, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was committed to undisclosed interests at region USD 25 mill basis delivery May-July'24. Remind you that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-older "Key Guardian" 83/2011 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 04/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 23,7 mill. On the Ultramax sector, the "World Royal" 61/2022 DACKS, China (SS 04/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 35 mill basis delivery May-September'24. For reference, the one-year-younger Japanese built "Bulk Monaco" 63/2023 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 06/2028, DD 06/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 40,5 mill. Following the sale of the Supramax "Crested Eagle" 56/2009 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 04/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) a month ago at USD 14,95 mill, clients of Eagle Bulk Shipping have now committed the one-year-older sister vessel "Crowned Eagle" 56/2008 IHI, Japan (SS 11/2028, DD 02/2027, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) at USD 16,5 mill. Nova Marine Carriers have committed their OHBS Handysize "Sider Harmony" 38/2019 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 02/2029, DD 01/2027, BWTS fitted) to Middle Eastern interests at USD 28,5 mill. Note that the vessel was bought back in November from ORIX Corp at USD 26 mill. Moreover, the "Global Serenity" 32/2008 Kanda, Japan (SS 11/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 12,1 mill, while the one-year- older sister vessel "Lucky Glory" 32/2007 Kanda, Japan (SS 12/2027, DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 10,3 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

A number of Indonesian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Trips from China via Philippines into Japan were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 17,000 pd for 4/7 months employment basis delivery Japan.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from Philippines into China with nickel ore were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Trips from China via South Africa into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 17,000/18,000 pd. South African trips into China were fixed around usd 17,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 170K/180K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from US Gulf into UK-Continent with pellets were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
