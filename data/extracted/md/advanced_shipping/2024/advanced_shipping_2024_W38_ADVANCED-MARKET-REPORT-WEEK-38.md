### Bulkers Market Commentary

Okouchi Kaiun have committed their Ultramax "Queen Sapphire" 61/2011 Iwagi, Japan (SS 12/2026, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at region USD 21 mill. Note that a month ago, the one-year-younger "Dolce Vita" 61/2012 Oshima, Japan (SS 09/2027, DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 23,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Tolani Group committed their "Prabhu Mihikaa" 55/2005 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed interests at USD 11,85 mill. For comparison, the two-year-younger sister vessel "Tai Hunter" 55/2007 Oshima, Japan (SS 12/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in June at USD 14,8 mill. The OHBS Handysize "Elegant Emilie" 33/2008 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) of Taylor Maritime was committed at USD 12,5 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Graceful Gertrude" 33/2008 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS 08/2028, DD 07/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold back in April at USD 12,3 mill. Moreover, same owners committed the "Sassy Sofia" 32/2005 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) to Lebanese interests at USD 9 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Hainan Island" 32/2004 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in July at USD 8,8 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Great Eastern Shipping have committed their Suezmax "Jag Lalit" 158/2005 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 07/2028, DD 04/2026, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1B) to undisclosed interests at region USD 33 mill. Remind you that back in June, the "Seoul Spirit" 160/2005 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 34,5 mill.
