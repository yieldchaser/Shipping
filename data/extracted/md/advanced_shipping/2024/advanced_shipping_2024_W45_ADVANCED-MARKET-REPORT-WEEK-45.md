### Bulkers Market Commentary

Hokoku Marine have committed their Kamsarmax "Energy Sunrise" 81/2014 Tadotsu, Japan (SS 06/2029, DD 04/2027, BWTS fitted) to Greek interests at excess USD 23 mill, while the scrubber fitted sister vessel "Martha" 81/2014 Tadotsu, Japan (SS 09/2029, DD 08/2027, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold back in September at USD 26,2 mill. In addition, the "Pan Energen" 81/2012 New Times, China (SS 03/2027, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) from Pan Ocean was committed to Turkish interests at USD 16,4 mill. Remind you that the one-year-younger "AM Hamburg" & "AM Quebec" 81/2013 New Times, China (SS 01/2028, DD 02/2026 & SS 02/2028, DD 05/2026, BWTS fitted) were sold in September at low USD 18 mill each. The Panamax "Asia Graeca" 73/2004 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 09/2029, DD 09/2027, BWTS fitted) of Angelakos Hellas was committed to Indonesian interests at region USD 11 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the "Scorpio WQ" 76/2004 Sasebo, Japan (SS 02/2029, DD 07/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 10,6 mill. On the Ultramax sector, the "Ey Haydn" 63/2015 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Chinese interests at region USD 32,5 mill. Remind you that back in July, the "Swansea" 63/2015 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 25,5 mill. Moreover, Seacon committed their "Seacon Fuzhou" 63/2019 Nantong, China (SS 05/2029, DD 04/2027, BWTS fitted) to clients of Shenyuan Leasing at USD 31,8 mill, while the sister vessel "Seacon Athens" 63/2019 Nantong, China (SS 07/2029, DD 07/2027, BWTS fitted) was sold back in August at USD 32,1 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 11,000/13,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 9,000/12,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via US Gulf were fixed around usd 11,000/12,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/19,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean via Red Sea were fixed around usd 17,000/18,000 pd. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 16.900 pd for 9/12 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 9,000/11,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Arabian Gulf trips into East Coast India were fixed around usd 12,000/15,000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 160K/180K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/16,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via Baltic were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. Trips from US East Coast into Continent were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. South American trips into West or South Africa were reported at the level of usd 14,000/17,000 pd. A large Handysize was reported fixed in the usd 13,750 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery East Africa.
