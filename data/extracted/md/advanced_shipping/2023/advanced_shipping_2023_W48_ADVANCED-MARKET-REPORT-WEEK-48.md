### Bulkers Market Commentary

Pan Ocean have committed their Capesize "Mineral Noble" 170/2004 Hyundai, Korea (SS 03/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) to UAE interests at USD 12,8 mill, while the "Maran Innovation" 171/2004 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold a week ago at low-mid USD 13 mill basis prompt delivery. Following the acquisition of the Kamsarmax resale "AP Lovrijenac" 82/2024 Jiangsu, China at EURO 35 mill, Egyptian buyers NNC have now purchased the "Aurora Borealis" 82/2023 Cosco, China (SS 05/2028, DD 05/2026, BWTS fitted) for USD 38,3 mill. Moreover, Safe Bulkers sold their "Pedhoulas Cherry" 82/2015 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 26,6 mill basis delivery January-February 2024. Remind you that the one-year-younger "Western Monaco" 81/2016 Jiangsu, China (SS 02/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in early September at USD 26 mill. The Ultramax "Santa Vitoria" 61/2012 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) of Eiko Kisen was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 19,8 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Marlin V" 61/2013 Iwagi, Japan (SS 10/2028, DD 09/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold back in October at USD 21,1 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "ST George" 58/2012 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed at region USD 19 mill. For reference, the three-years-older sister vessel "Atherina" 58/2009 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at region USD 14 mill. Navios Maritime have sold their Handysize "Navios Lyra" 34/2012 Spp, Korea (SS 06/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 13,75 mill. Remind you that the "Ria" 34/2012 Dae Sun, Korea (SS 03/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in early November at USD 14,2 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Inter- Continental trips via East Coast Canada and Mediterranean were fixed around usd 27,000/29,000 pd. Continental trips into China were reported at the level of usd 35,000/37,000 pd. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 16,000 pd for 1 year employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/14,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into East Coast India were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 25,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Continent were fixed around usd 37,000/39,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. Continental trips into Mediterranean with scrap were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into US Gulf with steels were reported at the level of usd 23,000/25,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
