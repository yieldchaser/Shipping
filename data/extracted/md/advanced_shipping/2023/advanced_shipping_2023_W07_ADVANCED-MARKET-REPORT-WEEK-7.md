### Bulkers Market Commentary

German owners have committed their Capesize "Edward N" 176/2011 SWS, China (SS 01/2026, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 24 mill. Remind you that back in January, the Korean built "Aquanavigator" 179/2011 Daehan, Korea (SS 12/2026, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 25 mill. Greek owners Diana Shipping announced that they have signed a Memorandum of Agreement to acquire the Ultramax "Nord Potomac" 63/2016 I-S, Japan (SS 08/2026, DD 09/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) of Nissen Kaiun for a price of USD 27,9 mill. Goldenport Shipmanagement have sold their Supramax "Milos" 57/2010 Cosco, China (SS 10/2025, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) for mid USD 13 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "CN Journey" 57/2010 Cosco, China (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 13 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The VLCC "Adventure" 308/2005 Samsung, Korea (SS 10/2025, DD 01/2024, Scrubber fitted) from Eastern Mediterranean was committed for USD 52,5 mill, while the one-year-younger "Seahero" 306/2006 Daewoo, Korea (SS 03/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 56 mill. Moreover, Navios sold their "Nave Photon" 297/2008 Shanghai Jiangnan, China (SS/DD 10/2023) to Singaporean buyers Wirana for USD 58 mill. Concordia Maritime sold their three LR1 sister vessels, the "Stena Progress" 65/2009 Brodosplit, Croatia (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1B), "Stena Premium" 65/2011 Brodisplit, Croatia (SS 06/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1B) & "Stena Penguin" 64/2010 Brodosplit, Croatia (SS 10/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A) for USD 90 mill enbloc basis T/C back. Norden have sold their MR2 "Nord Stingray" 51/2009 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 05/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Turkish buyers for region USD 24 mill basis cancelling May'23. Note that earlier this month, the one-year-older "High Mercury" 51/2008 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 07/2023) was sold at USD 21 mill. In addition, United Overseas Group sold their "UOG Andros" 50/2009 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class II) for mid USD 22 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low usd 6,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported in the usd 3,000/4,000 pd range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds continued rating primarily at the usd 7,000/8,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 6,000/8,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into Persian Gulf were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. Indian coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 8,000/9,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 300K/450K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 13,500 pd basis delivery China for 9/11 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the level of usd 8,000/9,000 pd. South East Asian trips into India with clinker were fixed around usd 7,000/8,000 pd. South African trips into India were reported at the level of usd 10,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 100K. Continental trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 7,000 pd. An Ultramax was fixed on period at the usd 14,000 pd basis delivery China for 1 year employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

West African trips into Mediterranean with steel billets were reported fixed around usd 6,000/7,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 7,000/8,000 pd. A small Handysize was fixed on period at the usd 9,400 pd basis delivery China for 4/6 months employment.
