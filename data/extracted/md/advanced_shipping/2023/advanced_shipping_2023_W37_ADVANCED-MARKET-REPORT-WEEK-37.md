### Bulkers Market Commentary

Atlantska Plovidba have committed their Panamax "AP Libertas" 75/2008 Hudong-Zhonghua, China (SS 06/2028, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 12,5 mill. For reference, the one-year-older "Magic Rainbow" 73/2007 Jiangnan, China (SS 12/2025, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 12,6 mill. Greek owners EuroDry have announced the acquisition of three Ultramax sister vessels from Marine Capital, the "Galileo" 63/2014 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted), the "Giants Causeway" 63/2015 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) & "Sadlers Wells" 63/2015 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) for USD 65 mill enbloc. Remind you that back in July, the "Kambos" 63/2015 Cosco, China (SS/DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 24,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Meadway Shipping & Trading committed their "Wave Runner" 56/2008 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025) to Turkish buyers for high USD 12 mill. Note that the "Windsor Adventure" 56/2008 Mitsui, Japan (SS 03/2026, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in August at USD 13,5 mill. Moreover, Jinhui Shipping & Transportation sold their "Jin Feng" 52/2004 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 8 mill, while the one-year-younger "Ocean Reef" 52/2005 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) of Swiss Bulk Shipping was sold at USD 8,6 mill. Japanese owners have committed the OHBS Handysize "Global Effort" 37/2014 Onomichi, Japan (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for mid-high USD 16 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the "Nord Savannah" 37/2013 Saiki, Japan (SS 05/2028, DD 05/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 16,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Union Maritime have committed their Aframax "Fulmar" 115/2009 Hanjin, Korea (SS 03/2024, DD 05/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 39,5 mill, while the one-year-older Japanese built "Centennial Sapporo" 110/2008 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) of MK Centennial Maritime was sold to Turkish buyers Beks at region USD 35,5 mill. Pan Ocean have sold their MR2 "Grand Ace8" 46/2008 Stx, Korea (SS 02/2028, DD 03/2026, BWTS fitted) to Indian buyers at USD 23,8 mill and the two-years-older sister vessel "Grand Ace1" 46/2006 Stx, Korea (SS 02/2026, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) for region USD 19 mill. Remind you that back in June, the "Gulf Jumeirah" 46/2008 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 12/2023) was sold at USD 23,5 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low/mid usd 8,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 14,000/16,000 pd. CIS Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 9,000/10,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 10,000/12,000 pd range. A number of Indonesia trips into India were fixed around usd 12,000/13,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were reported at the level of usd 21,000/23,000 pd. Trips from US West Coast into China were fixed around usd 13,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 450K. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 630K/850K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 16,000 pd for 6/8 months employment basis delivery Mediterranean. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 13,500 pd basis delivery China for 6/8 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 13,000/16,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 13,000/15,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into India were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 170K. An Ultramax was fixed on period at the usd 15,500 pd basis delivery US Gulf for 1 year employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. Trips from US East Coast into Mediterranean with scrap were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
