### Bulkers Market Commentary

Foremost Group have committed their Capesize "Bay May" 178/2010 SWS, China (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) to Far Eastern interests for USD 25,5 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Mineral Ningbo" 178/2009 SWS, China (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 24 mill. In addition, Berge Bulk committed their "Berge Cristobal" 177/2003 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS/DD 07/2023, Scrubber fitted) to Chinese interests for low USD 13 mill. Note that a week ago, the one-year-younger "Tasik Melati" 180/2004 Koyo, Japan (SS/DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 15,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Ella" 52/2003 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) of Taylor Maritime was committed for USD 9,7 mill, while the one-year-older "Great Wenjie" 50/2002 Oshima, Japan (SS 09/2027, DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 7,5 mill. For reference, the "Worldera-1" 51/2001 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 8 mill. The Handysize "Caribbean Spirit" 35/2009 Nantong, China (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed for USD 10,8 mill. For comparison, the one- year-younger sister vessel "Ithaca Stockholm" 35/2010 Nantong, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold earlier this month at USD 11 mill. Moreover, German owners sold their "Alice Star" 32/2008 Hakodate, Japan (SS/DD 09/2023) for low USD 12 mill. Note that the one-year-younger sister vessel "Super Gunner" 32/2009 Hakodate, Japan (SS/DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 13 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid usd 8,00's fio range. A number of Indian trips via South Africa into India were reported at the level of usd 17,000/18,000 pd range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

A number of trips from China into India with metcoke were fixed around usd 7,000/8,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 5,000/7,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 8,000/10,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/11,000 pd. Indian Coastal trips were fixed around usd 10,000/12,000 pd. Inter- Continental trips via US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 10,000/11,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/625K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 15,500 pd basis delivery China for 5/7 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from China into Continent were reported at the level of usd 8,000/9,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 7,000/9,000 pd. Indian iron ore trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 525K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 21,000/22,000 pd. East Coast South American trips US Gulf were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
