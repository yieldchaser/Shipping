### Bulkers Market Commentary

Yahata Kisen have sold their Capesize "Gracious Eternity" 180/2009 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 21,8 mill. Note that back in July, the one-year-older sister vessel "Ocean Cobalt" 180/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS 07/2028, DD 04/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 19,5 mill. Moreover, the "West Trader" 175/2009 Jinhai, China (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) of Sinokor was committed to Greek buyers Danaos at high USD 18 mill. Remind you that back in June, the two-years-younger sister vessel "Aquaenna" 176/2011 Jinhai, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 22,4 mill. The Kamsarmax "Geneva Star" 81/2015 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Chinese buyers for USD 27 mill. For reference, the "Rikke" 81/2016 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 07/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in June at USD 27 mill. The vintage Panamax "Da Ying" 75/2003 Universal, Japan (SS 02/2028, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) of Qingdao Da Tong Int Shipping was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 7,3 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older "G R A" 76/2002 Imabari, Japan (SS 02/2027, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 6,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Schulte Group committed their "Hanseatic Eagle" 58/2010 Yangzhou, China (SS 01/2025, DD 08/2026, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 12,3 mill. Remind you that a couple of weeks ago, the "Rhine Confidante" 57/2010 Ningbo, China (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 10,8 mill. Nagashiki Shipping have committed their OHBS Handysize "Takeshio" 38/2012 Naikai, Japan (SS 08/2026, DD 07/2024) to Greek buyers for USD 14,5 mill. Note that a month ago, the one-year-older "Steady Sarah" 38/2011 Minami-Nippon, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 15 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 13,000/15,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 11,000/12,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Indian trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into Japan were reported at the level of usd 28,000/30,000 pd. Continental trips into China were fixed around usd 24,000/26,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 600K/850K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 15,250 pd for 6/8 months employment basis delivery China. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 14,000 pd basis delivery China for 10/13 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Trips from China into India with clinker were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd. A number of trips from Philippines with nickel ore into China were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Indian Coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A Supramax was fixed on period at the usd 17,000 pd basis delivery US East Coast for 4/6 months employment with redelivery Atlantic.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. US Gulf trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into India with petcoke were reported at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
