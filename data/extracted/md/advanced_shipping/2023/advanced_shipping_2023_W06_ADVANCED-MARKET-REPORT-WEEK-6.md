### Bulkers Market Commentary

Italian owners have committed their Kamsarmax "Liberty K" 82/2010 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 01/2025, DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 19,5 mill, while the one-year-older Japanese built "Hampton Bay" 81/2009 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to Greek buyers for USD 19,9 mill. The Supramax "Serenitas N" 56/2011 Yangzhou, China (SS 07/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed to Greek buyers for low-mid USD 12 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-older "CN Journey" 57/2010 Cosco, China (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 13 mill. German owners have committed their OHBS Handysize "Shanghai Pearl" 36/2011 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 01/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for region USD 14 mill. Note that a month ago the Japanese built "Galene M" 33/2011 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at the same levels. Furthermore, the conventional "AS Elenia" 34/2011 Spp, Korea (SS 09/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to Turkish buyers for USD 13,3 mill. In addition, Meiho Kaiun sold their "Belle Etoile" 28/2014 I-S, Japan (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) for high USD 12 mill. Remind you that back in November, the sister vessel "Belle Ocean" 28/2014 I-S, Japan (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 13,9 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Thenamaris have sold their VLCC "Seahero" 306/2006 Daewoo, Korea (SS 03/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 56 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older "Maran Aquarius" 320/2005 Daewoo, Korea (SS 09/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at mid-high USD 52 mill. In the Aframax sector, the "Atlantis" 105/2008 Sumitomo, Japan (SS/DD 09/2023) was sold to Greek buyers for USD 38 mill. Note that back in January, the two-years-older "Kriti Galaxy" 110/2006 Mitsui, Japan (SS 11/2026, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 38 mill. Super Shipping have committed their MR2 "Super Emerald" 50/2005 Shina, Korea (SS 12/2025, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) to Turkish buyers for USD 17,75 mill. For reference, the two-years-younger "Lila Amazon" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold three weeks ago at USD 20 mill. Moreover, Fletamentos Maritimos sold their "Markos I" 45/2005 Uljanik, Croatia (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for low USD 17 mill and the sister vessel "Loukas I" 45/2005 Uljanik, Croatia (SS 12/2025, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 17 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 7,000/9,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 7,000/8,000 pd. Indian iron ore trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. West African trips via North Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 13,000/14,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 9,000/10,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 200K/400K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 15,000 pd basis delivery China for 1 year employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 6,000/7,000 pd. Indian iron ore trips into China were fixed around usd 7,000 pd. South African trips into Persian Gulf with manganese ore were reported at the level of usd 11,300 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 50K. US Gulf trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 7,000/9,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Japan were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 10,000 pd. South American trips into South Africa were reported at the level of usd 9,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 4,000/6,000 pd. South American trips into Black Sea were fixed around usd 7,000/9,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
