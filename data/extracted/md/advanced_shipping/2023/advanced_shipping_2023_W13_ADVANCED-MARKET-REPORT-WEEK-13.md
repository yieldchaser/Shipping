### Bulkers Market Commentary

Following the sale of the Capesize "C H S Creation" 174/2006 SWS, China (SS 03/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 17 mill, Mssrs COSCO HK Shipping have now committed the Japanese built "C H S Splendor" 170/2006 IHI, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) to Middle Eastern buyers for mid-high USD 17 mill. Uglands Rederi have sold their Supramax "Carmencita" 58/2009 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 16 mill, while the two-years-older sister vessel "Iris Express" 58/2007 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 03/2027, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 14,4 mill. In addition, Hsin Chien Marine sold their "New Beginning" 56/2013 Mitsui, Japan (SS 01/2028, DD 02/2026, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 20,5 mill. JP Morgan have acquired three Handysize vessels from Swire Shipping, the "Erisort" 39/2014 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS fiited), the sister vessel "Erradale" 39/2014 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) and the "Wulin" 39/2014 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) for low- mid USD 20 mill each. Remind you that earlier this month, the one-year- younger "Cielo Di Angra"39/2015 Yangfan, China (SS 10/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) & "Cielo Di Valparaiso" 39/2015 Yangfan, China (SS 06/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) were sold at USD 20 mill each.

### Tankers Market Commentary

BW Group have sold their LR1 sister vessels "Hafnia Hudson" 76/2007 Dalian, China (SS 06/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) & "Hafnia Danube" 76/2007 Dalian, China (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) for excess USD 23 mill each. Note that back in February, the "Hafnia Columbia" 76/2007 Dalian, China (SS 01/2027, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at high USD 22 mill. The MR2 "PTI Danube" 50/2017 Spp, Korea (SS 01/2027, DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed for excess USD 40 mill basis delivery July 2023. Moreover, Nyhill Shipping & Trading sold their "Jo Kari" 47/2007 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 10/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) to Turkish buyers for excess USD 22 mill. For comparison, the "Atlantica Bay" 46/2007 Sungdong, Korea (SS 07/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold earlier this month at USD 21,8 mill. Ancora Investment Trust have committed their MR1 "Jemma" 38/2008 Guangzhou, China (SS/DD 06/2023) to Turkish buyers for USD 18 mill. For reference, the one-year-older sister vessel "Nordic Tatiana" 38/2007 Guangzhou, China (SS 09/2027, DD 10/2025) was sold back in January at USD 16 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of high usd 7,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 12,000/14,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 12,000/14,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. West African trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 22,000/23,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,500/18,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 650K/850K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 17,000 pd basis delivery China for 1 year employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from China into Singapore with aggregates were reported at the level of usd 10,000/11,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 11,000/13,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/15,000 pd. South African trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. Inter-Mediterranean trips were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into US Gulf with minerals were fixed around usd 10,000/13,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
