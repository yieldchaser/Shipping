### Bulkers Market Commentary

Diana Shipping have sold their Capesize "Boston" 177/2007 SWS, China (SS 06/2027, DD 07/2025) to Greek buyers for USD 18 mill. Remind you that back in July, the "Aquakatie"174/2007 SWS, China (SS/DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 15,9 mill. The Panamax "HC Pioneer" 75/2004 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 05/2024) of Dongguan Haichang Industrial was committed to Chinese buyers for high USD 8 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Navios Anthos" 75/2004 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in April at USD 11,25 mill. Parakou Shipping have sold their Ultramax "CP Shanghai" 63/2015 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) for region USD 23,5 mill. Note that back in July, the "Kambos" 63/2015 Cosco, China (SS/DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 24,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, RHL Reederei have sold their "RHL Clarita" 53/2008 Chengxi, China (SS 03/2028, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 9,5 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "RHL Marta" 53/2007 Chengxi, China (SS 10/2027, DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold in July at mid USD 10 mill. Furthermore, the "Star Zeta" 53/2003 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 11/2023) was committed to Hong Kong based buyers at USD 8 mill. Note that a month ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Jin Feng" 52/2004 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 8,1 mill. Following the sale of the Handysize "Purple Sea" 35/2011 Nantong, China (SS 06/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) a couple of weeks ago at USD 10,9 mill, Purple Sea Holding have now committed the sister vessel "Red Sea" 35/2011 Nantong, China (SS 08/2026, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 11 mill. Moreover, the "Leni Selmer" 35/2011 Samjin, China (SS 11/2026, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold to Greek buyers Roxette for USD 11,55 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Chemikalien Seetransport have committed their LR1 "Chemtrans Aegean" 76/2007 Dalian, China (SS 08/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 22 mill. For reference, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Hafnia Rhine" 76/2008 Dalian, China (SS 03/2028, DD 03/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold back in April at USD 26,5 mill. Dong-A Tanker have committed on subs their MR2 "Dong-A Themis" 50/2015 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) for USD 38 mill, while the one-year-younger "Lacerta" & "Bora Bora" 50/2016 Spp, Korea (SS 06/2026, DD 06/2024 & SS 03/2026, DD 07/2024 respectively, BWTS fitted) of TMS tankers were committed to Turkish buyers for USD 40,5 mill each.
