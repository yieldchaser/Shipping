### Bulkers Market Commentary

NYK Line have committed their Capesize "Atlantic Tiger" 180/2006 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 07/2024) to Far Eastern interests at USD 16,85 mill. For comparison, the "C H S Splendor" 170/2006 IHI, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in May at USD 16,3 mill. Furthermore, the younger "Zampa Blue" 178/2011 Mitsui, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) of Meiji Shipping Group was committed to Greek interests for region USD 30 mill. The Kamsarmax "Magic Twilight" 80/2010 Stx, Korea (SS 04/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) of Castor Maritime was committed to undisclosed buyers for high USD 17 mill. For reference, the one-year-older Japanese built "Thalassic" 81/2009 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in May at USD 21 mill. Japanese owners have sold their Panamax "Santa Barbara" 76/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS 09/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek interests for mid USD 17 mill. Note that the "Ultra Panache" 78/2011 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 20,4 mill. On the Supramax sector, Tsuneishi Group sold the "CF Diamond" 57/2016 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) for region 24,2 mill. In addition, Stove Shipping committed their OHBS "Stove Ocean" 55/2013 Oshima, Japan (SS 05/2028, DD 05/2026, BWTS fitted) to clients of Gearbulk for region USD 21 mill. Lauritzen Bulkers have sold their OHBS Handysize "American Bulker" 36/2016 Shikoku, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) for mid-high 22 mill, while the one-year-older "Maestro Diamond" 37/2015 Saiki, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 05/2023) was sold a month ago at USD 22,5 mill. Moreover, the Chinese built semi-boxed "Greenery Sea" 35/2012 Nantong, China (SS 06/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to undisclosed buyers at mid USD 12 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The MR2 "MTM Potomac" 51/2004 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) of MT Maritime Management was sold at region USD 18 mill. Remind you that the same owners sold the sister vessel "MTM Hudson" 51/2004 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) a month ago at USD 18 mill also. In addition, the "Eagle Bay" 47/2008 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 02/2028, DD 01/2026) was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 24,2 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 8,000/10,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 8,000/11,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 9,000/10,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. West African trips into Continent with bauxite were fixed around usd 8,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 14,000/15,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 13,000/16,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,800/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 480K/630K. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 5,000/7,000 pd. Indian iron ore trips into China were fixed around usd 6,000/7,000 pd. West African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into India with fertilizer were fixed around usd 11,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 9,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips from China into India were fixed around usd 6,000/7,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. Continental trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 7,000/9,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into US East Coast were reported at the level of usd 9,000 pd. Trips from Black Sea into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 5,000/6,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into US Gulf with alumina were reported at the level of usd 10,000/11,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast Central America were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
