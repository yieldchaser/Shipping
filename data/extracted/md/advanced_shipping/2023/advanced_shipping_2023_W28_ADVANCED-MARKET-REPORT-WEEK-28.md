### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Capesize "Ocean Cobalt" 180/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS 07/2028, DD 04/2026, BWTS fitted) of Daiwa Kisen was committed to Chinese buyers for USD 19,5 mill. Note that a month ago, the two-years-older sister vessel "Atlantic Tiger" 180/2006 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 07/2024) was sold at USD 16,85 mill. Novamaris have sold their Kamsarmax "Joy" 79/2011 Jinhai, China (SS 01/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) for region USD 14 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Sweet Irina" 79/2011 Jinhai, China (SS 08/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in May at mid-high USD 16 mill. Spring Marine have sold their Ultramax "Kambos" 63/2015 Cosco, China (SS/DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 24,5 mill. Remind you that back in April, the "Diomidis" 63/2015 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 25,5 mill. Furthermore, Pacific Carriers sold their "Ikan Pulas" 63/2016 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 10/2026, DD 11/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) for region USD 28,5 mill. Note that the one-year-younger sister vessel "Atlantic Monterrey" 63/2017 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in early April at USD 30 mill. On the Supramax sector, Taiwan Navigation sold their "Tai Honesty" 55/2007 Oshima, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for low USD 12 mill. For comparison, the two-years-younger "RHL Julia" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in June at USD 15,2 mill. Atako Tasimacilik have committed their Handysize "Katya Atk" 28/2009 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 09/2024) to undisclosed buyers for USD 10 mill. Remind you that back in April, the sister vessel "J-Harmony" 28/2009 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 06/2024) was sold at low USD 10 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Dong-A Tanker have committed their MR2 "Dong-A Triton" 50/2015 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) for USD 37,5 mill, while the two-years-older "STI Ville" 50/2013 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 09/2023) of Scorpio Tankers was sold to Pertamina PT for USD 32,5 mill. Moreover, following the sale of the "Beacon Hill" 47/2005 Onomichi, Japan (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 19 mill, Sinopec Hong Kong Petroleum have now committed the two-years-older "Phoenix Hill" 48/2003 Koyo, Japan (SS/DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted, Ice Class II) for region USD 16 mill. In addition, NYK Line committed their "Magellan Endeavour" 47/2006 Iwagi, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 17 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 6,000/7,000 pd. A number of trips from Japan via Australia into China were fixed around usd 8,000/10,000 pd. Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 5,500/7,500 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 4,000/5,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were fixed around usd 20,000 pd. Continental trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 20,000 pd. Inter-Mediterranean trips via East Coast Canada were fixed around usd 11,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 400K/600K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 12,750 pd basis delivery China for 4/6 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 6,000/7,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 10,000/12,000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 6,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into East Coast India were fixed around usd 7,000/8,000 pd. Persian Gulf into West Africa with clinker were reported at the level of usd 5,000/7,000 pd. South African trips into Far East were fixed around usd 14,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 140K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips from South China via North China into West Coast India-Persian Gulf were fixed around usd 6,000/8,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 5,000/6,000 pd. West African trips into South America were fixed around usd 3,000/4,000 pd. Inter- Mediterranean trips via Black Sea were reported at the level of usd 5,000/7,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into India were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
