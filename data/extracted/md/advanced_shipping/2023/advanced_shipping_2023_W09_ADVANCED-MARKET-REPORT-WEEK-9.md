### Bulkers Market Commentary

Globus Maritime have sold their Supramax "Sun Globe" 58/2007 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 08/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 14,1 mill. For reference, the one-year-younger "Navios Amaryllis" 58/2008 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 05/2027, DD 05/2025) was sold in December at high USD 14 mill. Furthermore, the "Supra Oniki" 57/2010 Qingshan, China (SS 07/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed for USD 12,9 mill. For comparison, the "Milos" 57/2010 Cosco, China (SS 12/2025, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 13,5 mill. The Handysize "Taibo" 35/2011 Zhejiang, China (SS 12/2026, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) of Costamare was committed to Norwegian buyers Pareto for USD 10 mill, while the smaller "Hong Kong Spirit" 32/2011 Taizhou, China (SS 01/2026, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) of Cetus Maritime was sold for USD 10,7 mill. Note that the one-year-younger "African Venture" 34/2012 Chengxi, China (SS 06/2027, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at mid-high USD 12 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

International Andromeda have sold their Suezmax "Yellow Trader" 158/2004 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 35 mill. Remind you that a week ago, the Croatian built "Kriti Diamond" 166/2004 Brodosplit, Croatia (SS/DD 01/2024) was sold at the same price. The LR2 "Four Sky" 115/2010 Samsung, Korea (SS 03/2025, DD 03/2023) of Premuda was committed for region USD 42,5 mill. In addition, the three- years-older Japanese built "Beks Indiana" 105/2007 Sumitomo, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025) was sold to Indian buyers Gatik for high USD 30's mill basis prompt delivery Med. Prime Marine Management have sold their LR1 "Sand Shiner" 73/2006 New Century, China (SS 12/2026, DD 11/2023, Scrubber fitted) for USD 23,25 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-younger "Hafnia Columbia" 76/2007 Dalian, China (SS 01/2027, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) & "Hafnia Kronborg" 73/2007 New Century, China (SS 03/2027, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) were sold at high USD 22 mill each. Greek buyers have purchased the MR2 "Spruce Express" 51/2006 Stx, Korea (SS 09/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) of Island Navigation for high USD 18 mill. Note that the one-year-younger sister vessel "Ibis Pacific" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 06/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 22 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low usd 7,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds continued rating primarily at the usd 13,000/15,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 12,000/14,000 pd range. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. West African trips via West and East Africa into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 16,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via North Coast South America were fixed around usd 11,000/12,000 pd. Continental trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 20,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 16,000/19,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 690K/900K. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 675K/850K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 16,000 pd basis delivery China for 6/8 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Indian trips into China were reported fixed around usd 12,000/13,000 pd. Indian trips via South Africa into China were fixed around usd 17,000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 170K. Continental trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 10,000/12,000 pd. An Ultramax was fixed on period at the usd 17,000 pd basis delivery India for 12/14 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 12,000/14,000 pd range. Continental trips into West Africa were reported fixed at the level of usd 8,000/9,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
