### Bulkers Market Commentary

Greek owners have sold their vintage Capesize "Agia Trias" 185/2002 Kawasaki, Japan (SS/DD 03/2023, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 12,5 mill. Moreover, Kitaura Kaiun sold their "Ocean Caesar" 180/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS 09/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers Winning Shipping for region USD 20 mill. Remind you that a couple of months ago, the one-year-older "Aquahope" 177/2007 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at mid USD 18 mill. Chandris Group have committed their Kamsarmax "Dona Tara" 81/2011 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 11/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 20,5 mill. For reference, the two-years-older Japanese built "Hampton Bay" 81/2009 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at high USD 19 mill. Following the acquisition of the Ultramax "Springfield" 63/2020 Cosco, China (SS 03/2025, DD 03/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) at USD 30,75 mill, Mssrs Eagle Bulk have now purchased the sister vessel "Aston" 63/2020 Cosco, China (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) for region USD 30 mill. Furthermore, Japanese owners sold their "Mutiara" 61/2012 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 04/2023, DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) to Hong Kong based buyers Pacific Basin for USD 21 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Ellie M" 52/2001 Kanasashi, Japan (SS 11/2026, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed for USD 7,5 mill, while the two-years-younger "Bulk Newport" 52/2003 Toyohashi, Japan (SS/DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at low USD 9 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Ridgebury Holdings have sold their Suezmax "Ridgebury Judith" 150/2008 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 07/2023, Scrubber fitted) for USD 40 mill. Remind you that back in December, the sister vessel "Ridgebury Mary Jane" 150/2008 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 05/2023, Scrubber fitted) was sold at USD 38,5 mill. The Aframax "Oaka" 106/2003 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 08/2026, DD 08/2024) of Cosmic Marine was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 24,1 mill. Note that the "S-Trooper" 105/2003 Sumitomo, Japan (SS/DD 10/2023) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 24,3 mill. Cedar Holdings have sold their MR2 "Ibis Pacific" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 06/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) for USD 22 mill, while the two-years- younger "Ridgebury Katherine Z" 50/2009 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 09/2024) was sold for USD 24,8 mill. For reference, the sister vessel of the latter "UOG Andros" 50/2009 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class II) was sold a week ago at mid USD 22 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout route were reported in the usd 5,000 pd. A Capesize was fixed on a period at the usd 17,500 pd basis delivery China for 11/13 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 7,000/9,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 8,000/10,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 10,000/12,000 pd. West African trips via Baltic into China were reported at the level of usd 27,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Inter- Continental trips via North Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 5,000/7,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 10,000/12,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 400k/725k. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 16,400 pd basis delivery China for 6/8 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/13,000 pd. Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. Continental trips via ARAG into Mediterranean with scrap were reported at the level of usd 8,000/9,000 pd. An Ultramax was fixed on period at the usd 14,500 pd basis delivery South East Asia for 4/6 months employment. Also, an Ultramax was fixed on period at the usd 15,500 pd basis delivery Persian Gulf for 4/6 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Australian trips into Japan were reported at the level of usd 13,000 pd. Trips from Black Sea into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Caribbean were fixed around usd 14,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
