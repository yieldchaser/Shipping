### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Kamsarmax "Ultra Tiger" 83/2009 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 05/2026, BWTS fitted) was committed to Indian buyers for USD 16,5 mill. Remind you that the one-year-younger sister vessel "IC Harvest" 83/2010 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 17 mill. Furthermore, Nisshin Shipping sold their "Presinge Trader" 81/2016 Jiangsu, China (SS 12/2026, DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) to clients of W Marine for high USD 25 mill. Note that the one-year-older Scrubber fitted "Pedhoulas Cherry" 82/2015 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold earlier this month at USD 26,6 mill. On the Ultramax sector, the "Xing Xi Hai" 60/2017 Mitsui, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Scandinavian interests at USD 29 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Xing Shou Hai" 60/2016 Mitsui, Japan (SS 10/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to Greek interests at USD 28 mill. For reference, the "Santa Francesca" 61/2016 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in October at USD 26,8 mill. The Supramax "Supra Oniki" 57/2010 Qingshan, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to undisclosed buyers at excess USD 11 mill. Remind you that back in September, the two-years-younger sister vessel "Supra Thessal" 57/2012 Qingshan, China (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 11,5 mill. Following the sale of the Handysize "Clipper Como" 37/2010 Huatai, China (SS/DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1C) earlier this month at USD 10,9 mill, Clipper Bulk have now committed the "Clipper Copenhagen" 37/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS 06/2025, DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1C) for low USD 11 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Great Eastern Shipping have committed their MR2 "Jag Prabha" 48/2004 Iwagi, Japan (SS 09/2027, DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for region USD 15 mill. Remind you that back in August, the "Scarlet Ibis" 46/2004 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at the same levels. For comparison, the one-year-younger "Baltic Mariner I" 37/2006 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 10/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1B) was sold in October at USD 18,5 mill.
