### Bulkers Market Commentary

Monaco based owners have committed their Capesize sister vessels "Aquasalwador" 180/2012 Daehan, Korea (SS 01/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) and "Aquanavigator" 180/2011 Daehan, Korea (SS 12/2026, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers Laskaridis Maritime for USD 27 & 25 mill respectively. Remind you that back in November, the "HL Shinboryeong"179/2010 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 05/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 25 mill. Greek owners sold their Panamax "Achilles II" 75/2004 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 10,3 mill. Note that a couple of months ago, the one-year-younger "LT Ocean Star" 75/2005 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 14 mill. The Ultramax "Petit Cham" 63/2013 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed to undisclosed buyers for region USD 20,5 mill, while the two-years-younger sister vessel "Stony Stream" 64/2015 Chengxi, China (SS 04/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 24,2 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "CN Journey" 57/2010 Cosco, China (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold to Indonesian buyers for USD 13 mill and Dasin Holdings sold the two-years-younger "Sunrise Rainbow" 56/2012 Jiangsu, China (SS 07/2025, DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 15,5 mill. For comparison, the "Anita N" 56/2010 Jinling, China (SS 02/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold recently at USD 14 mill. In addition, the "Kitakami" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold to Chinese buyers for region USD 15 mill. Note that the two-years-younger sister vessel "Royal Fairness" 55/2011 Mitsui, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 10/2023) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 16 mill. Taylor Maritime have committed their Handysize "Lovely Leah" 28/2012 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 11,4 mill. Remind you that the two-years-older sister vessel "Gant Grace" 28/2010 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 12,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Dynacom Tankers Management have sold their Suezmax "Ice Transporter" 146/2006 Universal, Japan (SS 10/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A) to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 47,5 mill, while the sister vessel "Mike R" 146/2006 Universal, Japan (SS 09/2026, DD 01/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted, Ice Class 1A) was sold to Middle Eastern buyers also for USD 50 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout 10.500 pd basis delivery China for 4/6 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 6.000/9.000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 6.000/8.000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 5.000/7.000 pd. South East Asian trips via ECSA into China were fixed around usd 10.000/12.000 pd. Indian trips via ECSA into China were reported at the level of usd 11.000/12.000 pd. Continental trips into China were fixed around usd 15.000/17.000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 15.000/18.000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 14.000/15.000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 400K/530K. On the period front. a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 13.000 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery China. Also. a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 16.500 pd basis delivery China for 1 year employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 3.000/4.000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 6.000/8.000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14.000/17.000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 140K/170K. Inter- Mediterranean trips with clinker were fixed around usd 10.000 pd. Mediterranean trips into South America were reported at the level of usd 5.000/7.000 pd. Trips from Black Sea into China were fixed around usd 15.000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean with scrap were reported at the level of usd 8.000/10.000 pd. US Gulf trips into India with petcoke were fixed around usd 20.000/22.000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

South East Asian trips via Australia into Japan were fixed around usd 8.000 pd. West African trips into South East Asia with rubber were reported at the level of usd 10.000/11.000 pd. Mediterranean trips into Continent were fixed around usd 6.000/8.000 pd. Inter-Mediterranean trips via Black Sea were reported at the level of usd 7.000/8.000 pd. Caribbean trips into Continent with alumina were fixed around usd 13.000/14.000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 11.000/12.000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 14.000/15.000 pd. No period activity to report here.
