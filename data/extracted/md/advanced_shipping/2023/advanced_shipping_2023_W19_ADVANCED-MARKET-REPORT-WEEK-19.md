### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Post Panamax "Claire Z" 93/2009 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed to Chinese buyers for low-mid USD 16 mill. For reference, the two-years-younger "Jupiter N" 93/2011 Taizhou, China (SS 04/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold three months ago at USD 16,5 mill. CDB Financial Leasing have committed their 2012-built Kamsarmax "Sweet Lydia" & "Sweet Venus" 79/2012 Jinhai, China (SS 06/2027, DD 07/2025 & SS 04/2027, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted both) for mid-high USD 17 mill each and their 2011-built "Sweet Irina" & "Sweet Melissa" 79/2011 Jinhai, China (SS 08/2026, DD 08/2024 & SS 09/2026, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted both) for mid-high USD 16 mill each. For comparison, the "DL Ivy" 81/2012 Jiangsu, China (SS 09/2027, DD 02/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold back in April at region USD 17 mill. On the Ultramax sector, the "Federal Iris" 63/2016 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 08/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) of Daiwa Kisen was committed to undisclosed buyers for region USD 30 mill. Note that a month ago, the one- year-younger sister vessel "Atlantic Monterrey" 63/2017 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 30 mill. Following the acquisition of the OHBS Handysize "Maestro Pearl" 37/2015 Saiki, Japan (SS 07/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) back in March at USD 22,3 mill, Turkish owners Devbulk Shipping have now purchased the sister vessel "Maestro Diamond" 37/2015 Saiki, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 05/2023) from Maestro Shipping for USD 22,5 mill. Furthermore, Dalex Shipping sold their "Ithaca Stockholm" 35/2010 Nantong, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) to Indian buyers for USD 11 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Pacific Spirit" 35/2009 Nantong, China (SS/DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at excess USD 10 mill. In addition, Sealestial Nagivation committed their "Mardinik" 34/2011 21st Century, Korea (SS 11/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) to Syrian buyers for USD 14,4 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the "Aphrodite M" 34/2011 Spp, Korea (SS 10/2026, DD 03/2025) was sold at USD 14 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low/mid usd 9,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported in the usd 25,000/26,000 pd range. A Capesize was fixed on a period at the usd 21,250 pd delivery China for 17/19 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 12,000/13,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 11,000/13,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South Africa were fixed around usd 15,000 pd. Indian trips via South Africa into India were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into China were fixed around usd 21,000/23,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,500/17,700 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 650K/775K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 15,000 pd basis delivery Japan for 8/10 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from China via Philippines into China with nickel ore were fixed around usd 10,000 pd. Trips from China into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 9,000/10,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 8,000/10,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips via Persian Gulf into East Coast India were fixed around usd 15,000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 190K/200K. Mediterranean trips into West Africa with clinker were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips Australian trips into Indonesia were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd. Indian Coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
