### Bulkers Market Commentary

Eastern Pacific Shipping have committed their Capesize "Mount Faber" 177/2008 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS/DD 03/2023, Scrubber fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 22,7 mill, while the one-year-younger Korean built "Golden Shui" & "Golden Feng" 169/2009 Daehan, Korea (SS/DD 05/2024 & SS/DD 03/2024 respectively, Scrubber fitted both) of Golden Ocean Group were sold for region USD 23 mill each. The Kamsarmax "Blumenau" 81/2012 Taizhou, China (SS 06/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) from German owners was committed to Greek buyers for USD 19,9 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger "Xin Hong" 82/2013 Dalian, China (SS 10/2027, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 20,5 mill. Following the sale of the Panamax "Magic Rainbow" 73/2007 Jiangnan, China (SS 12/2025, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 12,6 mill, Mssrs Castor Maritime have now committed their Japanese built "Magic Moon" 76/2005 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) to Korean buyers for USD 14 mill. Remind you that earlier this month, the two-years-younger sister vessel "ES Sakura" 76/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS 08/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 14,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Iris Express" 58/2007 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 03/2027, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 14,4 mill. Note that a month ago, the sister vessel "Sun Globe" 58/2007 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 08/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 14,1 mill. Moreover, Chandris Group sold their "Serene Jessica" 57/2011 Stx Dalian, China (SS 03/2026, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers 5 Ocean Shipping for USD 14,9 mill, whilst the one- year-older sister vessel "Ecopacific" 57/2010 Stx Dalian, China (SS 03/2025, DD 09/2023) was sold a week ago at USD 14,8 mill. Maki Kisen have sold their Handysize "Merchant Three" 28/2012 Imabari, Japan (SS 10/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) for low USD 13 mill and Dalomar Shipping sold their "Hadar" 28/2012 I-S, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) for USD 13,2 mill. Remind you that a couple of weeks ago, the two-years-younger "Galleon" 28/2014 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 14 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Chartworld Maritime Management have sold their LR2" Star Prosperity" 115/2009 Sasebo, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 12/2024) for excess USD 40 mill. Note that three weeks ago, the two-years-older "Beks Indiana" 105/2007 Sumitomo, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025) was sold at region USD 38 mill basis prompt delivery Med.

## Page 2

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout route were reported in the usd 14,000/16,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 13,000/15,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/16,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 12,000/15,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Continental trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 20,000/22,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via South America into Continent were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 21,000/22,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 625K/700K. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. Indian trips into South East Asia were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into East Coast India with aggregates were reported at the level of usd 27,000/30,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into India with metcoke were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from Black Sea into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
