### Bulkers Market Commentary

Diana Shipping have announced the sale of their Capesize "Aliki" 180/2005 Imabari, Japan (SS 03/2025, DD 04/2023) to undisclosed buyers for USD 15,1 mill, while the "Bulk China" 176/2005 Universal, Japan (SS 03/2025, DD 05/2023) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 13,3 mill. The Panamax "Ocean Domina" 76/2005 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 08/2023) was committed for mid USD 10 mill. For comparison, the one-year- older "Achilles II" 75/2004 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 10,3 mill. In addition, Navios Maritime sold their Korean built "Navios Prosperity I" 75/2007 Stx, Korea (10/2027, DD 12/2025) for USD 13,75 mill. Grindrod Shipping have sold their Ultramax "IVS Hirono" 60/2015 Onomichi, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 07/2023) to Hong Kong based buyers Pacific Basin for USD 24,5 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "ASL Grace" 60/2015 Onomichi, Japan (SS 07/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold earlier this month at USD 25,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Bulk Partners sold their "Bulk Newport" 52/2003 Toyohashi, Japan (SS/DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for low USD 9 mill. Note that back in November, the one-year-younger "Azzura" 52/2004 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at high USD 12 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Maran Tankers have reportedly sold their VLCC "Maran Aquarius" 320/2005 Daewoo, Korea (SS 09/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) for mid- high USD 52 mill. For reference, the Japanese built "Asia Dawn" 281/2005 IHI, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in January at region USD 50 mill. In addition, the vintage "Arcadia V" 299/2000 Kawasaki, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed for USD 40 mill. Polembros Shipping have committed their Aframax "Kythira Warrior" 115/2006 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 08/2026, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for region USD 38 mill. Furthermore, Avin International sold their "Kriti Galaxy" 110/2006 Mitsui, Japan (SS 11/2026, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 38 mill and the "Kriti Bastion" 106/2003 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 05/2023) for USD 30,5-31 mill. The MR1 "Nordic Tatiana" 38/2007 Guangzhou, China (SS 09/2027, DD 10/2025) was committed to Greek buyers for USD 16 mill, whilst the one- year-older "Delphi" 38/2006 Guangzhou, China (SS 01/2026, DD 04/2024) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 14 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

CIS Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 5,000/7,000 pd range. North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 7,000/9,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 6,000/8,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 10,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Persian Gulf were reported at the level of usd 15,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 13,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/600K. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 7,000/8,000 pd. Indian iron ore trips into China were fixed around usd 6,000 pd. South African trips into Far East were reported at the level of usd 11,000/14,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 120K/150K. West African trips into Far East were fixed around usd 12,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. South American trips into US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 10,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 9,000/10,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A Handysize was fixed for a trip from South Africa for inter South African business at the level of usd 8,250 pd. A number of US East Coast trips into Continent with petcoke were fixed around usd 11,000/12,500 pd. No period activity to report here.
