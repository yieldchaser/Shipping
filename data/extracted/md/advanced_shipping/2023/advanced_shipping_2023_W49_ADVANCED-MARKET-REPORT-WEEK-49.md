### Bulkers Market Commentary

Bocimar have committed their Capesize "Mineral Ningbo" 178/2009 SWS, China (SS/DD 07/2024) to Chinese buyers Agricore at region USD 20 mill. Remind you that back in July, the sister vessel "Aquaproud" 178/2009 SWS, China (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 18,9 mill. Furthermore, the "The Mothership" 177/2006 Mitsui, Japan (SS 03/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) of Kassian Maritime was sold to Chinese interests at USD 17,4 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Satori" 177/2007 Mitsui, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 18,5 mill. The Kamsarmax "IC Harvest" 83/2010 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 02/2024) was committed to Chinese interests for USD 17 mill, while the one-year-older "Cymona Galaxy" 81/2009 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) from Alassia Newships Management was sold to Greek interests at high USD 15 mill basis delivery March 2024. Note that the "ASL Neptune" 82/2009 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 16,2 mill. Navios Maritime have sold their Panamax "Navios Hyperion" 75/2004 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 02/2024) to Far Eastern interests at USD 9 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "HC Pioneer" 75/2004 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 05/2024) was sold a couple of months ago at high USD 8 mill. On the Supramax sector, Byzantine Maritime sold their "Ermione" 56/2008 IHI, Japan (SS 09/2028, DD 06/2026, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 14,2 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Wave Runner" 56/2008 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025) was sold back in September at high USD 12 mill. Suisse-Atlantique have sold their Handysize "Vully" 35/2011 Shinan, Korea (SS 06/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese interests at region USD 13 mill. Note that a week ago, the one-year-younger "Navios Lyra" 34/2012 Spp, Korea (SS 06/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 13,75 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The Aframax "S-Treasure" 106/2005 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) of Stalwart Management was sold to undisclosed buyers at USD 32 mill. Remind you that back in September, the scrubber fitted "Wonder Vega" 106/2005 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold at high USD 31 mill. The MR2 sister vessels "Nord Sustainable" & "Nord Supreme" 49/2015 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 05/2025 & SS/DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) were committed to Greek buyers for USD 39 mill each. Note that the two-years-older "Nord Steady" 50/2013 Stx, Korea (SS 06/2028, DD 06/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold back in October at USD 34,5 mill.
