### Bulkers Market Commentary

Noma Shipping have committed their Capesize "Mineral Shougang International" 180/2009 Dalian, China (SS/DD 07/2024, Scrubber fitted) for USD 22,7 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the "West Trader" 175/2009 Jinhai, China (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at high USD 18 mill. In addition, the "Cymona Iron" 176/2011 SWS, China (SS 01/2026, DD 08/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) of Alassia Newships Management was sold at USD 26 mill. The Kamsarmax "Royal Fukuyama "82/2013 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 07/2028, DD 04/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold to Greek buyers for region USD 23 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Navios Southern Star" 82/2013 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 04/2028, DD 04/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 21,6 mill. Pavimar SA have committed their Panamax "Magic Phoenix" 76/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) for region USD 14 mill, while the one-year-older "Boyang Garnet" 75/2007 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) of Boyang was sold at USD 13 mill. Furthermore, the "AOM Julia" 76/2009 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS/DD 08/2024) was sold at USD 15,5 mill and the sister vessel "Lady Marite" 76/2009 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 15,75 mill. The Ultramax "Santa Francesca" 61/2016 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed to Greek buyers for USD 26,8 mill. Remind you that the "Nord Everest" 60/2016 Oshima, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in early August at mid USD 24 mill. On the Supramax sector, Mingtai Navigation sold their "Ocean Glory" 56/2006 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) to Indonesian buyers for USD 11,1 mill, while Costamare sold their "Peace" 55/2006 Oshima, Japan (SS 03/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) to Vietnamese buyers for mid- high USD 11 mill. Remind you that back in July, the one-year-younger "Jenny M" 56/2007 Mitsui, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at mid USD 12 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout were reported in the usd 22,000/24,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 12,000/14,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/15,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via US East Coast and Mediterranean were fixed around usd 19,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 600K/800K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 15,500 pd basis delivery China for 1 year employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 13,000/15,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/15,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips into Persian Gulf were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. West African trips into China for 2-3 laden legs were reported at the level of usd 18,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 35,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips into US East Coast were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 25,000/26,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A large Handysize was fixed on period at the usd 13,500 pd basis delivery West Africa for 4/6 months employment.
