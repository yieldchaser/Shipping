### Bulkers Market Commentary

Lavinia Corp have committed their Panamax "Lambay" & "Nestor" 75/2011 Penglai, China (SS/DD 03/2026 & SS 09/2026, DD 03/2026 respectively, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for mid USD 16 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Hong Cheng" 75/2011 Penglai, China (SS 10/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in May at USD 17,2 mill. The Ultramax "Lowlands Mimosa" 64/2018 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 09/2028, DD 06/2026, BWTS fitted) of Fukujin Kisen was committed for USD 25,5 mill. The discount in price is due to the fire damage caused in June of 2022. Remind you that the sister vessel "KK Progression" 64/2018 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS/DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold back in June at mid USD 28 mill. On the Supramax sector, Chinese owners sold their "Seacon Dalian" 57/2010 Cosco, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) to Indonesian buyers Cakra Buana for USD 12 mill. For comparison, the "Rhine Confidante" 57/2010 Ningbo, China (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 10,8 mill. Furthermore, the "Nippon Maru" 55/2011 Mitsui, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) of Hayama Senpaku was sold to Greek interests at USD 17,3 mill. Remind you that back in August, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Pythagoras" 56/2012 Mitsui, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 18,2 mill. In addition, Star Bulk Carriers committed their "Star Theta" 52/2003 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 06/2028, DD 10/2026, BWTS fitted) for USD 8,5 mill, while the two-years-younger sister vessel "Bulk Trident" 52/2005 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 01/2026, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 9,8 mill. Dalomar Shipping have sold their Handysize "Ria" 34/2012 Dae Sun, Korea (SS 03/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 14,2 mill. Note that a week ago, the "Darya Jamuna" 36/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 05/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed a week ago at USD 16 mill. Moreover, the "African Ibis" 32/2004 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted, OHBS) from MUR Shipping was committed at USD 8,6 mill. Remind you that back in October, the "Sun Ruby" 32/2004 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 8,1 mill. Additionally, the "Lake Dany" 28/2008 Shimanami, Japan (SS/DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Greek interests at USD 9 mill basis prompt delivery in China, whilst the "Valor SW" 29/2008 Shikoku, Japan (SS 12/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed a week ago at USD 8,45 mill.
