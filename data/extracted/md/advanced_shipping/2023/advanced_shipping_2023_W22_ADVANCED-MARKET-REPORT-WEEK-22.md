### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Vintage Panamax "Great Wenchao" 75/1999 Mitsui, Japan (SS 06/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Chinese interests at USD 7,1 mill. For reference, the three-years-younger "Luck Fortune" 76/2002 Imabari, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 9,8 mill. RHL Reederei have committed their Supramax "RHL Julia" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) to European interests at high USD 15 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the sister vessel "Kitakami" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at the same levels. On the Handysize sector, Pacific Carriers sold the "Atlantic Altamira" 43/2017 Qingshan, China (SS 07/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 24,5 mill. As a comparison, the one-year-older sister vessel "Boreas Venture" 43/2016 Qingshan, China (SS 02/2026, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 23,2 mill. Furthermore, Tri-Mer services committed their "Brianna" 31/2009 Nanjing, China (SS/DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) to Turkish interests at USD 10,6 mill, while the Japanese built OHBS "Straits Breeze" 31/2009 Saiki, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was also sold to Turkish interests for USD 13 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The VLCC "Good News" 319/2002 Samho, Korea (SS 03/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) of New Shipping was committed at USD 43 mill basis prompt delivery Fujairah. Moreover, Dynacom Tankers sold their "Yio" 302/2005 Mitsubishi, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 11/2023) for region USD 54 mill. Note that back in April, the one-year-older sister vessel "Princess Mary" 306/2004 Mitsubishi, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 51 mill. Frontline have committed their Suezmax "Front Njord" 156/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS 08/2025, DD 11/2023) for USD 44,5 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Everbright" 156/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 03/2025, Scrubber fitted) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 43,5 mill. The LR1 "Amazon Beauty" 73/2004 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 01/2024) from Livanos Group was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 19,5 mill. As a comparison, the "Sauger" 72/2004 Samsung, Korea (SS/DD 02/2024) was sold back in April at USD 18,5 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 9,000/10,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 8,500/9,500 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 9,000/11,000 pd. Continental Round Voyage trips with minerals were fixed around usd 9,000/10,000 pd. Continental trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were fixed around usd 19,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 425K/650K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 13,500 pd basis delivery China for 4/6 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 4,000/6,000 pd. Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 5,000/6,000 pd. Indian trips via South Africa into China were reported at the level of usd 9,000/11,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 20,000/22,000 pd. US Gulf trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 13,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 310K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of East Coast South American trips into US Gulf- US East Coast were reported at the level of usd 14,000/15,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
