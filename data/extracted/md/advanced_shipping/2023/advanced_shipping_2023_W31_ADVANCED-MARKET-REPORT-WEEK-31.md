### Bulkers Market Commentary

Kitaura Kaiun have committed their Capesize "Shiosai" 176/2009 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 07/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 20,85 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older "Ocean Cobalt" 180/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS 07/2028, DD 04/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold in July at USD 19,5 mill. The Panamax "Despina D" 76/2004 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) from Oinoussian Diamond Shipping was committed to undisclosed buyers for mid-high USD 10 mill. Remind you that back in April, the "Navios Anthos" 75/2004 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 11,25 mill. On the Supramax sector, Deval Shipping & Trading committed their "Devbulk Gulten" 55/2015 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) for USD 22,6 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Nord Treasure" 55/2014 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 21,7 mill. The OHBS Handysize "Cielo Di Palermo" 37/2013 Saiki, Japan (SS 08/2027, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 17 mill. Remind you that the two-years-younger sister vessel "Maestro Diamond" 37/2015 Saiki, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 05/2023) was sold back in May at USD 22,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Oman Shipping have committed their VLCC sister vessels "Fida" & "Sifa" 316/2011 Hyundai, Korea (SS 05/2026, DD 05/2024 & SS 01/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted both) and their Japanese built "Saham" 300/2010 Universal, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 12/2023, Scrubber fitted) to Korean buyers Sinokor for USD 64 mill each. Remind you that the "Astro Chloe" 318/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 01/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold in July at region USD 62,5 mill. The Aframax "Tornado A" 105/2003 Sumitomo, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 10/2023) was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 24,75 mill. For reference, the one-year-younger "Sea Hazel" 106/2004 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 01/2024, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in May at excess USD 31 mill. Viken Shipping have sold the MR2 "Cassiopeia II" 50/2008 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) to Far Eastern buyers for USD 23 mill. Note that the sister vessel "Elandra Corallo" 50/2008 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold recently at the same price.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 8,000/10,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 7,000/9.000 pd. Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 5,000/6,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Indian trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/12,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via South America were fixed around usd 11,000/12,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 400K/600K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 9,550 pd basis delivery China for 6/9 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 8,000/10,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 9,000/10,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 200K. Indian Coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 9,000/11,000 pd. Indian trips into Japan were fixed around usd 9,000/11,000 pd. An Ultramax was fixed on period at the usd 14,000 pd basis delivery South East Asia for 3/5 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips from China into South Africa were reported at the level of usd 6,000/7,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 7,000/9.000 pd. Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 10,000/11,000 pd. South African trips into China were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
