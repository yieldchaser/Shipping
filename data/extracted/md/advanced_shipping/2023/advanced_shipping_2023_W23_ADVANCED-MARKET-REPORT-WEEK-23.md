### Bulkers Market Commentary

Chinese owners have committed the Capesize "Heruz Zhoushan" 181/2017 SWS, China (SS 09/2027, DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) to Greek interests for USD 41,5 mill basis delivery July-November 2023. The Ultramax "Belvedere" 66/2015 Mitsui, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) from Belships was committed to undisclosed buyers for excess USD 27 mill basis delivery Q3 2023. Remind you that the sister vessel "Bulk Electra" 66/2015 Mitsui, Japan (SS 12/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 27,2 mill. On the Supramax sector, Murakami Sekiyu sold their "Nord Treasure" 55/2014 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) for mid-high USD 21 mill. For reference, the one-year-older "New Beginning" 56/2013 Mitsui, Japan (SS 01/2028, DD 02/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 20,5 mill. The OHBS Handysize "Cielo Di Palermo" 37/2013 Saiki, Japan (SS 08/2027, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) of Nissen Kaiun was committed for USD 18,5 mill. Note that a month ago, the two-years-younger sister vessel "Maestro Diamond" 37/2015 Saiki, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 05/2023) was sold at USD 22,5 mill. In addition, the conventional "Seastar Endurance" 34/2011 Zhejiang, China (SS 09/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to European interests for mid-high 11 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Following the sale of the LR1 "Gulf Crystal" 75/2009 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 29,5 mill, Gulf Energy Maritime have now committed the sister vessel "Gulf Coral" 75/2009 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 09/2024) for USD 28,5 mill. Moreover, Prime Marine Management sold their "Golden Shiner" 75/2007 Onomichi, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 01/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted), the sister "Lake Sturgeon" 75/2007 Onomichi, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) and the "Bowfin" 75/2008 Minami-Nippon, Japan (SS/DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 79 mill enbloc. Super Shipping have committed their MR2 "Super Ruby" 50/2006 Shina, Korea (SS 02/2026, DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 20,9 mill. For reference, the one-year-older sister vessel "Neutron Ray" 50/2005 Shina, Korea (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 18,1 mill.
