### Bulkers Market Commentary

Imabari Senpaku have committed their Capesize "Ocean Corona" 180/2009 Koyo, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 20,8 mill. Remind you that back in September, the "Gracious Eternity" 180/2009 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 21,8 mill. Furthermore, the older "Cape Flamingo" 180/2005 Koyo, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 02/2024) of Zodiac Maritime was sold to Chinese buyers also at USD 15,4 mill. The Kamsarmax "ASL Neptune" 82/2009 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) of Agricore Group was sold to Greek buyers for USD 16,2 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "STL Miracle" 82/2008 Oshima, Japan (SS 09/2028, DD 09/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 15,3 mill. Japanese owners committed the Ultramax "Shimanami Queen" 61/2011 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS/DD 06/2026, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 18,5 mill. Remind you that a couple of months ago, the two-years-younger sister vessel "Ultra Regina" 61/2013 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS/DD 10/2023) was sold at USD 19,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Freedom Line" 56/2005 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) from Blue Seas Shipping and the one-year-younger sister vessel "Pride" 55/2006 Mitsui, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) from Costamare were committed at low USD 11 mill each. Note that a week ago, the sister vessel "Ocean Grace" 56/2005 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at the same levels. In addition, Japanese owners sold the "Tomorrow" 56/2013 Minaminippon, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers at region USD 20 mill. Shih Wei Navigation have sold their Handysize "Valor SW" 29/2008 Shikoku, Japan (SS 12/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) to Lebanese buyers for USD 8,45 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older "Uni Auc One" 28/2007 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS 06/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in September at USD 7,8 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Nordic Hamburg Shipping have sold their LR1 "Nordic Josephine" 74/2007 New Century, China (SS 05/2027, DD 06/2025) to Greek buyers for USD 23 mill. Remind you that the "Chemtrans Aegean" 76/2007 Dalian, China (SS 08/2027, DD 11/2025) was sold a month ago USD 22 mill. The MR2 "MR Euphrates" 50/2008 Guangzhou, China (SS/DD 12/2023) of Myrtle Marine was committed to Greek buyers for USD 20,8 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel "GH Austen" 50/2009 Guangzhou, China (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 23 mill. Moreover, the "STI Amber" 50/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 07/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) of Scorpio Tankers was committed to Greek buyers at USD 33,8 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 10,000/12,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/13,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South Africa into China were fixed at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips via South America into Persian Gulf were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via US East Coast were fixed around usd 17,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via South America were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Trips from Baltic into Continent were fixed around usd 13,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/13,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips into Persian Gulf were reported at the level of usd 9,000/10,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips via Persian Gulf into East Coast India were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. South African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 150K/180K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. US Gulf trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 20,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 15,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
