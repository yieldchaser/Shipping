## Page 1

### Bulkers Market Commentary

Mitsubishi Ore Transport have committed their Capesize "Santa Lucia" 176/2006 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 08/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) to Turkish buyers Beks for excess USD 16 mill. Remind you that back in June, the "Atlantic Tiger" 180/2006 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 07/2024) was sold at USD 16,85 mill. In addition, Chinese owners sold the "Xin Wang Hai" 174/2003 SWS, China (SS/DD 10/2023) to compatriots for USD 12,8 mill. The Ultramax "Lowlands Breeze" 61/2013 Iwagi, Japan (SS 07/2028, DD 07/2026, BWTS fitted) of Fukusei Sangyo was committed to Greek buyers for high USD 20 mill. For reference, the one-year-younger sister vessel "CL Ebisu" 61/2014 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in April at high USD 26 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Sea Aquarius" 53/2006 Imabari, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to Chinese buyers for high USD 9 mill. Note that the one-year-younger "Tai Honesty" 55/2007 Oshima, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in July at low USD 12 mill. The OHBS Handysize "Steady Sarah" 38/2011 Minami-Nippon, Japan (SS 05/2026, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed to UK buyers for USD 15 mill, while the two-years-younger "Nord Savannah" 37/2013 Saiki, Japan (SS 05/2028, DD 05/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 16,5 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel of the latter "Cielo Di Palermo" 37/2013 Saiki, Japan (SS 08/2027, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 17 mill. Moreover, the conventional "Tasman Spirit" 35/2010 Nantong, China (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 9,5 mill. Note that back in May, the sister vessel "Ithaca Stockholm" 35/2010 Nantong, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 11 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Lila Global have committed their Suezmax "Lila Orlando" 158/2005 Daewoo, Korea (SS 06/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for high USD 37 mill. Remind you that the "Classic" 159/2005 Hyundai, Korea (SS 10/2025, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold back in May at USD 36,75 mill.
