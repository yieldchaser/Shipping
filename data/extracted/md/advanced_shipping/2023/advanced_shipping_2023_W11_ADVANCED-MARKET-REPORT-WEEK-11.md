### Bulkers Market Commentary

Chinese owners have committed their Capesize "Xin Jin Hai" 180/2009 Dalian, China (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 23 mill. Remind you that back in December, the sister vessel "Amity" 180/2009 Dalian, China (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 19,2 mill. The Panamax "ES Sakura" 76/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS 08/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) of Erasmus Corp was sold for USD 14,5 mill. For reference, the two-years-older "Ocean Domina" 76/2005 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 08/2023) was sold back in January for mid USD 10 mill. Furthermore, the Chinese built "Magic Rainbow" 73/2007 Jiangnan, China (SS 12/2025, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 12,6 mill. Mid-Atlantic Bulk Carriers have sold their Ultramax "Soho Principal" 63/2016 Yangzhou, China (SS 04/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 26,5 mill. Note that back in January, the one-year-older "Steel Grace" 63/2015 New Times, China (SS 11/2025, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 24,5 mill. On the Handysize sector, MW Shipping committed their "Schelde Confidence" 38/2011 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers Arion Shipping for USD 17,2 mill. Remind you that a week ago, the "Octbreeze Island" 38/2011 Shimanami, Japan (SS 10/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at low-mid USD 15 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Aeolos Management have sold their VLCC "Kassos I" 319/2007 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 07/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to UAE based buyers for USD 60 mill. Moreover, Thenamaris Ships Management sold the two-years-older "Athina II" 318/2005 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 12/2023, Scrubber fitted) for USD 56 mill, whilst the Japanese built "Yufusan" 311/2005 Mitsui, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) from Mitsui OSK Lines was sold to Chinese buyers for USD 52 mill. The MR2 "Neutron Ray" 50/2005 Shina, Korea (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) of Interorient Marine Services was committed for USD 18,1 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger "Spruce Express" 51/2006 Stx, Korea (SS 09/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at high USD 18 mill. In addition, the Japanese built "Evian" 48/2006 Iwagi, Japan (SS 07/2026, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 18,5 mill. Viken Shipping have committed their MR1 sister vessels "Inviken" & "Utviken" 37/2009 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 03/2024 both) to European buyers for USD 20 mill each, while the two-years-younger "Atria" 37/2011 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 07/2026, DD 08/2024) was sold for USD 23 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low usd 9,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported in the usd 21,000/23,000 pd range. South African trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 16,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 1.2M. A Capesize was fixed on a period at the usd 25,000 pd delivery China for 5/7 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 19,000/21,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Indian coastal trips were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. West African trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 18,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 20,000/23,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Ukraine into China were reported at the level of usd 27,000/30,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 20,000/22,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,500/18,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 750K/850K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 18,750 pd basis delivery China for 1 year employment. Also, A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 17,350 pd basis delivery Japan for 5/7 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from China into West Coast India were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. South East Asian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Mediterranean trips into US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into Continent were fixed around usd 11,000/12,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. US Gulf trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 21,000/22,000 pd. A large Handysize was fixed on period at the usd 16,300 pd basis delivery Japan for 11/14 months employment.
