### Bulkers Market Commentary

Following the sale of the Capesize "Stella Ada" 180/2011 Dalian, China (SS 12/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) at USD 25 mill, Mssrs Ever Stella Shipping have now sold the one-year-older "Stella Cherise" 177/2010 SWS, China (SS 04/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for region USD 24 mill. Dong-A Tanker have committed their Kamsarmax "DL Ivy" 81/2012 Jiangsu, China (SS 09/2027, DD 02/2026, BWTS fitted) To Greek buyers for region USD 17 mill. Note that back in March, the one-year-younger "Xin Hong" 82/2013 Dalian, China (SS 10/2027, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 20,5 mill. The Panamax "Ying Hao 03" 76/2012 Hudong-Zhonghua, China (SS 05/2027, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) of Dongguan Yinghao Shipping was sold to European buyers for USD 18 mill. Remind you that the "Hui Xin 9" 75/2012 Shanghai, China (SS 05/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 17 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Oren" 56/2010 Jiangdong, China (SS 09/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold for USD 13,3 mill. Note that the "Sania" 57/2010 Qingshan, China (SS 10/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at region USD 14,5 mill. Shoei Kisen have committed their Handysize "J-Harmony" 28/2009 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 06/2024) to Vietnamese buyers for low USD 10 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Nord Setouchi" 28/2010 Imabari, Japan (SS 12/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 11,3 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The VLCC "Princess Mary" 306/2004 Mitsubishi, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Chinese buyers for USD 51 mill. Remind you that back in March, the one-year-younger "Yufusan" 311/2005 Mitsui, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 52 mill. ### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of high usd 8,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported in the usd 17,000/18,000 pd range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 13,000/15,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into Persian Gulf were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Indian coastal trips were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were reported at the level of usd 25,000/27,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via North Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 24,000/27,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 19,000/21,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 18,000/19,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 800K/875K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 16,500 pd basis delivery China for 5/7 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from China via Indonesia into Thailand were fixed around usd 11,000/12,000 pd. Indian trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. South African trips into Red Sea and Persian Gulf were fixed around usd 15,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 150K. Continental trips into Mediterranean with scrap were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China with petcoke were fixed around usd 23,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

No fixture reported for this segment. No period activity to report here.
