### Bulkers Market Commentary

Nissen Kaiun have committed their Kamsarmax "Lord Star" 82/2013 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 06/2027, DD 03/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Thenamaris for region USD 22 mill, while the two-years-older sister vessel "Santa Cruz" 83/2011 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at low USD 18 mill. Furthermore, Kambara Kisen sold their "Rikke" 81/2016 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 07/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek interests for USD 27 mill. Remind you that back in April, the one-year-older sister vessel "Valiant Spring" 81/2015 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 12/2025, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at low USD 28 mill. Avic International Leasing have committed their Ultramax sister vessels "Gemini Confidence" & "Virgo Confidence" 63/2019 New Dayang, China (SS/DD 07/2024 both, BWTS fitted) for low USD 27 mill each. Note that the one-year-older sister vessels "Taurus Confidence" & "Aries Confidence" 63/2018 New Dayang, China (SS/DD 12/2023 & SS/DD 11/2023 respectively, BWTS fitted) were committed a month ago at USD 27,9 mill each basis delivery Q4 2023. On the Supramax sector, RHL Reederei committed their "RHL Julia" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 15,2 mill. Note that a couple of months ago, the sister vessel "Kitakami" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at high USD 15 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

SONANGOL have sold their Suezmax "Sonangol Kassanje" 158/2005 Daewoo, Korea (SS 06/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) to Infinity Ships for USD 37 mill basis DD freshly passed. Remind you that back in May, the "Classic" 159/2005 Hyundai, Korea (SS 10/2025, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 36,75 mill. In addition, the "Melodia" 158/2011 Samsung, Korea (SS/DD 10/2024) of Horizon Tankers was committed to Gardsea Shipping at USD 47,5 mill. The Aframax "Minerva Zoe" 105/2004 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS/DD 03/2024, Ice Class II) from Minerva Marine was committed to Chinese buyers for region USD 31 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Seascout" 105/2004 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS/DD 01/2024, Ice Class II) was sold in April at USD 27 mill. Falcon Navigation have sold their MR2 "Falcon Sextant" 51/2009 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 09/2024) to Pacific Ocean for high USD 25 mill. For comparison, the "Adamas I" 50/2009 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 04/2024) was sold in early May at USD 24,5 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout were reported in the usd 14,000/16,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 6,000/7,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 9,000/11,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into Indonesia were fixed around usd 9,000/10,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Indian Coastal trips were fixed around usd 10,000/11,000 pd. South African trips into India were reported at the level of usd 12,250 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 220K. Continental trips into China were fixed around usd 17,000/20,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 13,000/16,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 400K/650K. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from Japan into Continent were fixed around usd 6,000/7,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 7,000/10,000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 8,000/10,000 pd. Indian iron ore trips into China were reported at the level of usd 7,000/10,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into East Coast India were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. South African trips into US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 11,000/12,000 pd. West African trips into China with manganese ore were fixed around usd 15,000/18,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 13,000/14,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 300K/400K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips from China into India were fixed around usd 6,000/8,000 pd. Inter- Mediterranean trips via Black Sea were reported at the level of usd 7,000/8,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 14,000/15,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 7,000/9,000 pd. A small Handysize was fixed on period at the usd 8,000 pd basis delivery South America for 3/5 months employment with redelivery Atlantic.
