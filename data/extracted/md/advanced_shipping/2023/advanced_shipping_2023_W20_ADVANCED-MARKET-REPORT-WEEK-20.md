### Bulkers Market Commentary

Bocimar International have committed their Capesize "Mineral Ningbo" 178/2009 SWS, China (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for region USD 24 mill. Note that back in April, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Stella Cherise" 177/2010 SWS, China (SS 04/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was also sold at USD 24 mill. Greek owners Capital have acquired the Kamsarmax "Xin Han" 82/2013 Dalian, China (SS 05/2027, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) of FEH Shipping for region USD 20 mill. Remind you that the same buyers purchased the sister vessel "Xin Hong" 82/2013 Dalian, China (SS 10/2027, DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) back in April at USD 20,5 mill. Ultrabulk have sold their Panamax "Ultra Panache" 78/2011 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 20,4 mill. Moreover, Chinese owners sold the "Hong Cheng" 75/2011 Penglai, China (SS 10/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 17,2 mill. For reference, the one-year-younger "Hui Xin 9" 75/2012 Shanghai, China (SS 05/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in March for USD 17 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Korean owners have committed their VLCC "Baltic Sunrise" 309/2005 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 11/2025, Scrubber fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 51,5 mill. Remind you that back in March, the "Athina II" 318/2005 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 12/2023, Scrubber fitted) was sold at USD 56 mill. The Suezmax "Agape Soul" 159/2001 Daewoo, Korea (SS 01/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 29 mill. Note that earlier this month, the "Amoroza" 159/2001 Hyundai, Korea (SS 12/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 30 mill. Castor Maritime have committed their Aframaxes "Wonder Bellatrix" 115/2006 Samsung, Korea (SS 04/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) & "Wonder Polaris" 115/2005 Samsung, Korea (SS 05/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) for USD 72 mill enbloc. In addition, the Japanese built "Aframax Riviera" 107/2005 Koyo, Japan (SS 03/2025, DD 06/2023) was committed to UAE based buyers for USD 35 mill. MT Maritime Management have sold their MR2 "MTM Hudson" 51/2004 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 18 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, same owners sold the sister vessel "MTM Colorado" 51/2004 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) at the same price.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout route were reported in the usd 18,000/20,000 pd range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 9,000/10,000 pd. Trips from China via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 11,000/12,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 8,000/10,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 9,000/11,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Trips from Black Sea into China were fixed around usd 19,000/20,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 475K/625K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 14,250 pd basis delivery China for 2 years employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 9,000/11,000 pd. Indian iron ore trips into China were fixed around usd 7,000/9,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 20,000/22,000 pd. South American trips into US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd. South American trips into East Coast India were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/675K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

No fixture reported for this segment. No period activity to report here.
