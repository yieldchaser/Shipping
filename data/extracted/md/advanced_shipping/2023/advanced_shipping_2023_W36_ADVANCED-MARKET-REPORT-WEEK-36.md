### Bulkers Market Commentary

Following the sale of the Capesize "Xin Wang Hai" 174/2003 SWS, China (SS/DD 10/2023) a week ago at USD 12,8 mill, Cosco Shipping Bulk have now committed the one-year-younger sister vessel "Tian Bao Hai" 174/2004 SWS, China (SS 04/2027, DD 06/2025) at USD 13,5 mill. Kobe Shipping have sold their Kamsarmax "Port Star" 82/2012 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 05/2027, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) to Singaporean buyers for USD 20,5 mill. For reference, the "Navios Southern Star" 82/2013 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 04/2028, DD 04/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold three weeks ago at USD 21,6 mill. The Panamax "Nenita" 76/2006 Sasebo, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) of Spring Marine Bulk was committed to Greek buyers for USD 12,3 mill. Remind you that back in July, the one-year-older sister vessel "Star Planet" 76/2005 Sasebo, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at low USD 13 mill. Shoei Kisen have committed their Ultramax "Ultra Regina" 61/2013 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS/DD 10/2023) to undisclosed buyers for USD 19,5 mill, while the "Lowlands Breeze" 61/2013 Iwagi, Japan (SS 07/2028, DD 07/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at high USD 20 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Rhine Confidante" 57/2010 Ningbo, China (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold to Greek buyers for USD 10,8 mill. For comparison, the "Star Globe" 56/2010 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) & "Sky Globe" 56/2009 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) were sold enbloc a week ago at USD 11,2 & 10,7 mill respectively. Keiyo Kisen sold their OHBS Handysize "Iris Sky" 28/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 11/2023) for USD 8,2 mill, while the one-year-younger conventional "Vantage Sword" 28/2009 Shimanami, Japan (SS 02/2024, DD 08/2026) of Vantage Shipping Lines was committed for region USD 9 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout were reported in the usd 14,000/16,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 10,000/11,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 20,000/22,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were reported at the level of usd 24,000/26,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 15,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were reported at the level of usd 19,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 900K. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 19,000/21,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 600K/700K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 13,000 pd basis delivery China for 5/7 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. A number of trips from Philippines with nickel ore into China were fixed around usd 11,000/12,000 pd. Indian Coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Indian trips via South Africa into China were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. South African trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Continental trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Continental trips via West Africa into India were reported at the level of usd 24,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. On the period front, a Supramax was reported fixed at the level of usd 12,250 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery Persian Gulf. Also, a Supramax was fixed on period at the usd 14,000 pd basis delivery West Africa for 4/6 months employment with redelivery Atlantic.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. East Coast South American trips into North Coast South America were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. A large Handysize was fixed on period at the usd 11,750 pd basis delivery China for 6/8 months employment.
