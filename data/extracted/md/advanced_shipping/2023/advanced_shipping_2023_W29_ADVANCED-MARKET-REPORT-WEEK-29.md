### Bulkers Market Commentary

H-Line have committed their Capesize "HL Passion" 179/2015 Dalian, China (SS 11/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for region USD 36 mill. Furthermore, the "Aquaproud" 178/2009 SWS, China (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) of GoodBulk was committed to Greek buyers for USD 18,9 mill. Note that a couple of months ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Bay May" 178/2010 SWS, China (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 25,5 mill. In addition, Danaos Shipping acquired the sisters "Bulk Ingenuity" 176/2011 Jinhai, China (SS 12/2026, DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) & "Bulk Integrity" 176/2010 Jinhai, China (SS 12/2025, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) & "Bulk Peace" 175/2010 Jinhai, China (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) & "Bulk Achievement" 175/2011 Jinhai, China (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) & "Bulk Genius" 175/2012 Jinhai, China (SS 06/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) from China Development Bank Leasing at USD 105 mill enbloc. For comparison, the sister vessel "Aquaenna" 176/2011 Jinhai, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 22,4 mill. Japanese owners have sold the Panamax "Nord Hydra" 77/2014 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) for excess USD 23 mill. Remind you that back in March, the one-year-younger sister vessel "TR Infinity" 77/2015 Imabari, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at mid-high USD 25 mill. Moreover, Kumiai Senpaku committed their "Delphinus" 77/2007 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 05/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 13,5 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the two- years-older "Star Planet" 76/2005 Sasebo, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 13,25 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Jenny M" 56/2007 Mitsui, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed for mid USD 12 mill, while the "Tai Honesty" 55/2007 Oshima, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 12,3 mill. The Handysize "Tomini Zonda" 38/2016 Zhejiang, China (SS 08/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 19,36 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Tomini Bora" 38/2016 Zhejiang, China (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold earlier this month at USD 19,65 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low usd 7,00's fio range. A number of round trips on the same route were reported in the usd 10,000/12,000 pd. South African trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 250K. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 5,000/7,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 6,000/8,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into China were fixed around usd 7,000/9.000 pd. Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 5,000/6,000 pd. Indian trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 10,000/13,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into China were fixed around usd 12,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via Baltic were fixed around usd 7,000/9,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 300K/500K. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 6,000/8,000 pd. Indian Coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 5,000/6,000 pd. South American trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 9,000/10,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips from China into South East Asia with steels were reported at the level of usd 4,000/5,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 4,000/6,000 pd. Continental trips into Black Sea were reported at the level of usd 5,000/6,000 pd. Continental trips into US Gulf were fixed around usd 5,000 pd. South American Coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 9,000/11,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
