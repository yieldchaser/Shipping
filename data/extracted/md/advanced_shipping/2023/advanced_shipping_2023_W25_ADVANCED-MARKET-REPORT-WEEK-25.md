### Bulkers Market Commentary

Ceres Shipping have committed their Capesize "Aquaenna" 176/2011 Jinhai, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) to Costamare for USD 22,4 mill. Remind you that back in April, the one-year-older "Rosebank" 177/2010 New Times, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 23,2 mill. Japanese owners have sold their Panamax "Coral Opal" 78/2012 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for low USD 19 mill. Note that the one-year-older "Ultra Panache" 78/2011 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 20,4 mill. The Ultramax "Hanton Trader III" 63/2014 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed at USD 23,6 mill. For comparison, the one-year- younger sister vessel "Diomidis" 63/2015 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in April at USD 25,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Corinthian Emerald" 57/2012 Stx, Korea (SS 03/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) from Eurobulk was committed to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 17,25 mill. Moreover, the "New Direction" 56/2013 Mitsui, Japan (SS 06/2028, DD 04/2026, BWTS fitted) of Hsin Chien Marine was sold at USD 19,6 mill. Note that the sister vessel "New Beginning" 56/2013 Mitsui, Japan (SS 01/2028, DD 02/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 20,5 mill. Newport have committed their Handysize "Agia Irini" 28/2013 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) to Florence Shipping for USD 13,8 mill. For reference, the one-year-older sister vessel "Merchant Three" 28/2012 Imabari, Japan (SS 10/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at low USD 13 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low/mid usd 8,00's fio range. Continental trips via East Coast Canada into Far East were fixed around usd 44,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 9,000/11,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 11,000/13,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 10,000/12,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via North Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 480K/675K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 13,000 pd basis delivery China for 9/11 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 9,000/11,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 10,000/12,000 pd range. South East Asian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 9,000/12,000 pd. Trips from Philippines into China with nickel ore were reported at the level of usd 10,000/11,000 pd. West African trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 10,000/13,000 pd. US Gulf trips into India were reported at the level of usd 14,000 pd. South American Coastal trips were fixed around usd 12,000/13,000 pd. South American trips into US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 12,000/15,000 pd. South American trips into India were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 450K/550K. South American trips into South East Asia were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 280K/300K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from Japan into Persian Gulf with steels were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. South East Asian trips into China were fixed around usd 7,000/8,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
