### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Post Panamax "Jupiter N" 93/2011 Taizhou, China (SS 04/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed to Greek buyers W Marine for USD 16,5 mill. Remind you that back in December, the one-year-younger "TW Manila" 93/2012 Jiangsu, China (SS 04/2027, DD 04/2025) was sold at USD 19 mill. Uglands Rederi have sold their Supramax "Bonita" 58/2010 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 09/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 15,7 mill. Note that the two-years-older sister vessel "Navios Amaryllis" 58/2008 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 05/2027, DD 05/2025) was sold in December at high USD 14 mill. The OHBS Handysize "Venture Ocean" 39/2015 Jiangmen, China (SS 10/2025, DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) was committed for low-mid USD 18 mill basis delivery February-March 2023, while the "TS Bravo" 39/2015 Shanhaiguan, China (SS 10/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 16,8 mill. Furthermore, the "African Venture" 34/2012 Chengxi, China (SS 06/2027, DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold for mid-high USD 12 mill. Note that the two-years-older "Mireille Selmer" 33/2010 Samjin, China (SS 05/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at high USD 10 mill. In addition, the sister vessels "Camila" & "Tia Marta" 34/2015 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 09/2025, DD 01/2024 & SS 09/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) were committed to Turkish buyers for USD 19,5 mill each. Remind you that the one-year-older "Kirishima Sky" 35/2014 Minaminippon, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 16,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The Suezmax "Amoroza" 159/2001 Hyundai, Korea (SS 12/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold to undisclosed buyers for USD 27 mill. Remind you that a couple of months ago, the one-year-older "Seatrust" 114/2004 Samsung, Korea (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1C) was sold at USD 35 mill. Diamond S Shipping have committed their MR2 "High Mercury" 51/2008 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 07/2023) to European buyers for USD 21 mill. Note that the one-year-older sister vessel "Lila Amazon" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at low USD 20's mill.
