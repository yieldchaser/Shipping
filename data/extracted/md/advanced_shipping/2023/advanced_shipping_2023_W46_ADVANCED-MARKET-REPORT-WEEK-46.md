### Bulkers Market Commentary

Daiwa Kisen have committed their Capesize "Frontier Brilliance" 181/2013 Imabari, Japan (SS 12/2028, DD 10/2026, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers at USD 30,5 mill, while the one-year-younger sister vessel "True Cartier" 181/2014 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was committed at USD 37 mill. In addition, the "Chow" 181/2016 SWS, China (SS 09/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) of Sinokor Maritime was sold to clients of Genco Shipping at USD 43,1 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Contamines" 181/2016 SWS, China (SS 06/2026, DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in early October at USD 42 mill. The Kamsarmax "Ultra Panther" 83/2008 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 06/2028, DD 06/2026, BWTS fitted) was committed to undisclosed buyers at USD 16,3 mill. Note that the "STL Miracle" 82/2008 Oshima, Japan (SS 09/2028, DD 09/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold in October at USD 15,3 mill. On the Supramax sector, Japanese owners sold their "Royal Knight" 58/2013 Kawasaki, Japan (SS 01/2028, DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 19,5 mill. Remind you that back in September, the one-year-older sister vessel "Bulk Patagonia" 58/2012 Kawasaki, Japan (SS 02/2028, DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at the same levels. Moreover, Jinhui Shipping committed their "Jin Quan" 52/2006 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 06/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 9,35 mill basis waiving inspection. For comparison, the sister vessel "Jag Rohan" 52/2006 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 01/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in early October at USD 9,25 mill. British Bulkers of Greece have sold their Handysize "Lord Nelson" 28/2005 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) to Turkish interests for USD 7,9 mill. Note that the two-years-younger sister vessel "Uni Auc One" 28/2007 Shin Kochi, Japan (SS 06/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in September at USD 7,8 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 12,000/14,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/14,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. Indian trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/13,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips via US Gulf into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. South African trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 14,750 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 475K. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were fixed around usd 24,000/25,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via West Africa were reported at the level of usd 23,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 17,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K/800K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed at the level of usd 17,000 pd for 11/14 months employment basis delivery Japan. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 17,250 pd basis delivery Continent for 4/6 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 12,000 pd. Inter-Mediterranean trips with slag were fixed around usd 12,000/13,000 pd. Trips from Black Sea into Continent were reported at the level of usd 11,000/12,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. East Coast South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,500 pd. East Coast South American trips into US Gulf were fixed around usd 14,000/15,500 pd. No period activity to report here.
