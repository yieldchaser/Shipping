### Bulkers Market Commentary

Following the acquisitions of the Capesizes "Aquahaha" & "Aquatonka" 179/2012 HHIC, Philippines (SS 02/2027, DD 05/2025 & SS 03/2027, DD 05/2025 respectively, BWTS fitted) at region USD 28 mill each, Mssrs Norden have purchased the one-year-older sister vessels "Star Borealis" & "Star Polaris" 179/2011 HHIC, Philippines (SS 09/2026, DD 02/2024 & SS 11/2026, DD 06/2024, Scrubber fitted) of Star Bulk Carriers at USD 32,5 mill each. The Vintage Panamax "Luck Fortune" 76/2002 Imabari, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) of Zhangjiagang Oceanicwit was committed for USD 9,8 mill. For reference, the three-years-younger sister vessel "Magic Moon" 76/2005 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 14 mill. One the Supramax sector, Pacific International Lines have sold their "Glovis Maine" 56/2013 Tianjin, China (SS/DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) for mid-high USD 15 mill. Remind you that same owners sold the sister vessel "Glovis Magellan" 56/2013 Tianjin, China (SS/DD 05/2023) in March at USD 15 mill. Moreover, the Japanese built "Super Odegaard" 55/2011 Mitsui, Japan (SS 10/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to Greek buyers for mid-high USD 18 mill, while the two-years-younger sister vessel "New Beginning" 56/2013 Mitsui, Japan (SS 01/2028, DD 02/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 20,5 mill. Union Maritime have committed their Handysize "Kalixenos" 36/2010 Samjin, China (SS 04/2025, DD 06/2023, BWTS fitted) to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 10,9 mill. In addition, the "Taizhou Pioneer" 32/2011 Taizhou, China (SS 09/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) from Danish owners was sold to Greek buyers for low-mid USD 11 mill. For comparison, the "Sea Plain" 32/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS 08/2026, DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at low 11 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The Suezmax "Everbright" 156/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 03/2025, Scrubber fitted) was committed to Turkish buyers Besiktas for USD 43,5 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older "Navigare Pars" 51/2012 Stx, Korea (SS 01/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in March at USD 32 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 12,000/14,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Indian trips via South Africa into India were reported at the level of usd 17,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into South East Asia were fixed around usd 20,000/21,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into China were reported at the level of usd 23,000/25,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 19,000/21,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 900K/1.1M. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 19,000 pd basis delivery China for 1 year employment. Also, A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 16,900 pd basis delivery South East Asia for 5/7 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from Japan via Indonesia into Continent were fixed around usd 8,000/10,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000 pd. Indian trips into South East Asia were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. South African trips into South East Asia were reported at the level of usd 16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 600K. Mediterranean trips into Far East were fixed around usd 17,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Continent were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Japan were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. South East Asian trips via Australia into Japan were reported at the level of usd 9,000/10,000 pd. Trips from Argentina into Brazil were fixed around usd 16,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
