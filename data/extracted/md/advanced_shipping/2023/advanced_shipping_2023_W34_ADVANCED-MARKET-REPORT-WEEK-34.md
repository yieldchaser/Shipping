## Page 1

### Bulkers Market Commentary

Shoei Kisen have sold their Newcastlemax "HL Imabari" 206/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 21,4 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Benitamou" 206/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS 05/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in early July at high USD 22 mill basis 1year BBHP. Joyway Shipping have committed their Capesize "Yuan Fu Star" 176/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS 12/2026, DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) to Middle Eastern buyers for region USD 23 mill, while the one-year-younger sister vessel "Mount Apo" 175/2012 (SS 04/2027, DD 09/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 24,75 mill. The Kamsarmax "Alam Kekal" 82/2018 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 10/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 31,8 mill. Moreover, AO Shipping committed their "Belo Horizonte" 81/2012 Taizhou, China (SS 07/2027, DD 09/2025, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers W Marine for mid USD 17 mill including T/C until December 2023 at 14.500 usd/pd. Note that back in March, W Marine acquired the sister vessel "Blumenau" 81/2012 Taizhou, China (SS 06/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) from AO Shipping at USD 19,9 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Windsor Adventure" 56/2008 Mitsui, Japan (SS 03/2026, DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to undisclosed buyers at mid USD 13 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older sister vessel "Jenny M" 56/2007 Mitsui, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold in July at mid USD 12 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The Suezmax "Namsen" 157/2016 New Times, China (SS 11/2026, DD 05/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) of Diamond S Shipping was sold to UK based Hayfin Capital Holdings for USD 67 mill. NYK Line have committed their MR2 "Scarlet Ibis" 46/2004 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) at region USD 15 mill. Note that a month ago, the two-years-younger "Magellan Endeavour" 47/2006 Iwagi, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at region USD 17 mill. In addition, Union Maritime sold their "Brunswick" 45/2010 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS/DD 01/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Vietnamese buyers at USD 24,5 mill. For reference, the one-year-older sister vessel "Celsius Rome" 46/2009 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 23,9 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

Trips from China into India with fertilizers were fixed around usd 9,000/10,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 5,000/7,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 8,000/10,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America into Japan were reported at the level of usd 24,000/26,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/660K. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/13,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Indian trips via Persian Gulf were reported at the level of usd 7,000/10,000 pd. West African trips into China were fixed around usd 17,000 pd. West African trips into India with fertilizers were reported at the level of usd 15,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into India were fixed around usd 12,000/13,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. An Ultramax was fixed on period at the usd 13,500 pd basis delivery China for 12 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A large Handysize was fixed for a time charter trip from Persian Gulf into Thailand with fertilizers at the usd 8,500 pd. No period activity to report here.
