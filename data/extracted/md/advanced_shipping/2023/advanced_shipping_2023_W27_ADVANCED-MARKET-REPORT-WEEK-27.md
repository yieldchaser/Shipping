### Bulkers Market Commentary

Sotirakou S have committed their Capesize "Agios Charalambos" 184/2003 Mitsui, Japan (SS 07/2026, DD 06/2024) to Indian buyers for USD 14 mill. Remind you that back in May, the one-year-younger "Tasik Melati" 180/2004 Koyo, Japan (SS/DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 15,5 mill. Moreover, following the sale of the "Aquaenna" 176/2011 Jinhai, China (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 22,4 mill, Mssrs Goodbulk have now committed their "Aquamarine" 182/2009 Odense, Denmark (SS/DD 07/2024) to New Shipping for region USD 18,6 mill and their "Aquarange" 179/2011 HHIC, Philippines (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) to Costamare for USD 23,5 mill. The Panamax "Star Planet" 76/2005 Sasebo, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) of Charterwell Maritime was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 13,25 mill. For reference, the "Magic Moon" 76/2005 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in March at USD 14 mill. On the Supramax sector, Stam Shipping have sold their "Chris" 56/2010 Cosco, China (SS 06/2025, DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) to Lomar Shipping for USD 12 mill. Note that back in May, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Daxia" 56/2011 Cosco, China (SS 06/2026, DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 14,2 mill. In addition, the "Marylisa V" 52/2003 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 09/2023) was committed to Chinese buyers for USD 7,5 mill including BWTS on board, while the sister vessel "Ella" 52/2003 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 11/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold in May at USD 9,3 mill. Lauritzen Bulkers have committed their OHBS Handysize "African Bulker" 36/2015 Shikoku, Japan (SS 11/2025, DD 03/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Korean buyers for low-mid USD 21 mill. Remind you that same owners sold the one-year-younger sister vessel "American Bulker" 36/2016 Shikoku, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 02/2024, BWTS fitted) in June at mid-high USD 22 mill.

Remind you that back in April, the "Odori" 45/2005 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 20 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low usd 8,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 8,000/10,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 6,000/8,000 pd range. A number of Australian iron ore trips into China were fixed around usd 10,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 210K. A number of Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 7,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 9,000/11,000 pd. South African trips into India were reported at the level of usd 12,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 200k. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were fixed around usd 17,000 pd. Continental trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/19,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via South America were fixed around usd 6,000/7,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/14,600 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 400K/460K. A Kamsarmax was fixed on period at the usd 11,000 pd basis delivery China for 5/8 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 7,000/8,000 pd range. Indonesian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 9,000/11,000 pd. Trips from Philippines into China with nickel ore were fixed around usd 7,000/8,000 pd. A number of Australian iron ore trips into China were fixed around usd 9,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 95K. Persian Gulf trips into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 8,000/10,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed at the usd 8,000/9,000 pd range. Continental trips into US East Coast were fixed around usd 8,000/10,000 pd. South American trips into US Gulf with petcoke were reported at the level of usd 13,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
