### Bulkers Market Commentary

Following the acquisition of the Capesize "Chow" 181/2016 SWS, China (SS 09/2026, DD 12/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted), clients of Genco Shipping have purchased the sister vessel "Comanche" 180/2016 SWS, China (SS 07/2026, DD 01/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) from Sinokor Maritime at the same price i.e. USD 43,1 mill. Moreover, Maran Dry Management committed their "Maran Innovation" 171/2004 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 03/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Chinese interests at low-mid USD 13 mill basis prompt delivery. Remind you that back in April, the sister vessel "Maran Pioneer" 171/2004 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 08/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold at USD 16,6 mill. Star Bulk Carriers have sold their Kamsarmax "Star Jennifer" 82/2006 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 03/2026, DD 08/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) at USD 14,5 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Restinga" 82/2006 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 09/2026, DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in July at mid- high USD 13 mill. The Panamax "Omicron Light" 76/2005 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold to Vietnamese buyers Hoanh Son Group at high USD 10 mill. The vessel was delivered to her new owners and renamed to "Hoanh Son Universe". Note that the sister vessel "Magic Moon" 76/2005 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed a week ago at USD 11,8 mill. On the Supramax sector, Highland Maritime committed their "NPS Mosa" 53/2007 Iwagi, Japan (SS 06/2027, DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 11,6 mill. In addition, India Cements committed their "Chennai Selvam" 52/2001 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 11/2026, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 6,5 mill, while the sister vessel "New Lotus" 52/2001 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 02/2026, DD 07/2024) was sold a week ago at USD 6,8 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The Aframax "Aegean Myth" 115/2006 Samsung, Korea (SS 08/2026, DD 09/2024, BWTS fitted) of Arcadia Shipmanagement was committed to Chinese interests at USD 37,5 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger "P. Kikuma" 115/2007 Samsung, Korea (SS 11/2027, DD 02/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 39,3 mill. Flynn Ventures have committed their MR2 "Ocios Ioannis" 47/2009 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 08/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 25 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the "UOG Helios" 46/2009 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 04/2024, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at region USD 25,5 mill.
