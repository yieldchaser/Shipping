### Bulkers Market Commentary

Ever Stella Shipping have sold their Capesize "Stella Ada" 180/2011 Dalian, China (SS 12/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 25 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the two-years-older sister vessel "Xin Jin Hai" 180/2009 Dalian, China (SS/DD 11/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at region USD 23 mill. In addition, the "Maran Pioneer" 171/2004 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 08/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) of Maran Dry Management was sold for USD 16,6 mill. The Ultramax "All Star Atlas" 63/2014 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 01/2024) of M/Maritime Corp was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 24,5 mill basis delivery Q3 2023. For comparison, the two-years-younger sister vessel "Soho Principal" 63/2016 Yangzhou, China (SS 04/2026, DD 03/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold in March at USD 26,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Singaporean owners have committed their "Roslyn" 57/2009 Qingshan, China (SS 10/2027, DD 10/2025) for region USD 14 mill, while the three-years-younger sister vessel "Universal Bangkok" 56/2012 Qingshan, China (SS 04/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed for USD 15,8 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Supra Oniki" 56/2010 Qingshan, China (SS 07/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold in early March at USD 12,9 mill. Costamare Shipping committed their Handysize "Miner" 33/2010 Taizhou, China (SS 03/2028, DD 03/2026, BWTS fitted) to Greek buyers for USD 10,5 mill. Note that a week ago, the one-year-younger "Taizhou Pioneer" 32/2011 Taizhou, China (SS 09/2026, DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at low-mid USD 11 mill. Moreover, Pacific Basin Shipping sold their "Black Forest" 32/2003 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for high USD 8 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older sister vessel "Crux" 32/2002 Kanda, Japan (SS 07/2025, DD 10/2023) was sold a month ago at USD 8 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

D'Amico Tankers committed their MR2 "Di Matteo" 46/2009 Naikai, Japan (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) to undisclosed buyers for USD 24 mill. Note that a week ago, the two-years-older "Ridgebury John B" 46/2007 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at region USD 23 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of low usd 8,00's fio range. A Capesize was fixed on a period at the usd 30,000 pd delivery Persian Gulf for 2/4 months employment.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 14,000/15,000 pd range. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A number of trips from China via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. A Panamax was fixed on period at the usd 15,800 pd basis delivery East Coast India for 4/6 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment. A number of trips from China via Vietnam into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Japan were fixed around usd 19,000/21,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of trips from Japan into South East Asia were fixed around usd 6,000/7,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America were fixed around usd 21,000/23,000 pd. South American trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
