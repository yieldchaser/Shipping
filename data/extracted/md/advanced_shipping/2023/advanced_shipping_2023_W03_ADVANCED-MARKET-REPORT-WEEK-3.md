### Bulkers Market Commentary

Hsin Chien Marine have sold their Post Panamax "Dyna Globe" 99/2006 Imabari, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) to Far Eastern buyers for USD 15,1 mill. The younger "AM Liberia" 98/2013 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 01/2023, BWTS fitted) of MK Centennial Maritime was sold to Greek buyers for USD 20,5 mill basis SS/DD freshly passed. Greek owners sold their Ultramax "Steel Grace" 63/2015 New Times, China (SS 11/2025, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) to compatriots for USD 24,5 mill. Remind you that back in December, the "Stony Stream" 64/2015 Chengxi, China (SS 04/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 24,2 mill. Taylor Maritime have committed their OHBS Handysize "Corkscrew" 33/2010 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) to Chinese buyers for USD 13,75 mill. Note that the one-year-younger sister vessel "Galene M" 33/2011 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a week ago at USD 14,1 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Thenamaris Ships Management have reportedly sold their Aframax "Seamagic" 116/2007 Hyundai, Korea (SS 03/2027, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A) for USD 47 mill. Note that a month ago, the non-Ice classed "Houston Star" 116/2007 Universal, Japan (SS 07/2027, DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 39,5 mill. The LR1 "Petali Lady" 71/2004 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 03/2024) was committed to Middle Eastern buyers for USD 18,75 mill. For comparison, the "Freight Margie" 70/2004 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 06/2024) was sold in early December at USD 14,6 mill. On the MR2 sector, the "Lila Amazon" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed for low USD 20's mill, while the one- year-older "T Rex" 50/2006 Spp, Korea (SS 12/2026, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 19,2 mill. Moreover, the "Challenge Passage" 48/2005 Iwagi, Japan (SS 04/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold for low-mid USD 17 mill. Note that back in December, the sister vessel "Nave Dorado" 48/2005 Iwagi, Japan (SS 08/2025, DD 08/2023) was sold at USD 15,6 mill. Chemikalien Seetransport have committed their MR1 "Chemtrans Mars" 37/2007 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1A) to undisclosed buyers for USD 20,1 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the sister vessels "Silent" & "Single" 37/2007 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 01/2023 both, Ice Class 1A both) were sold at USD 13,5 mill each.
