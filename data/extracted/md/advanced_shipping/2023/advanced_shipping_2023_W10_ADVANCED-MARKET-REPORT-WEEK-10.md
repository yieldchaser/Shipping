### Bulkers Market Commentary

Tsakos Shipping & Trading have committed their Capesize "Elizabeth II" 180/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2025, DD 08/2023) for USD 17,8 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Ocean Caesar" 180/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS 09/2026, DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at region USD 20 mill. The Supramax "Ignazio" 58/2010 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 04/2025, DD 05/2023, BWTS fitted) from Japanese owners was sold to Turkish buyers for USD 15,5 mill basis waiving inspection. Remind you that the "Bonita" 58/2010 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 09/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold a month ago at USD 15,7 mill. Moreover, the "Worldera-1" 51/2001 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 12/2024, BWTS fitted) was committed for USD 8 mill, while the "Ellie M" 52/2001 Kanasashi, Japan (SS 11/2026, DD 03/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 7,5 mill. On the Handysize sector, the "IVS Sentosa" 32/2010 Jiangmen, China (SS 06/2025, DD 07/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold to Greek buyers for region USD 11 mill. Remind you that back in January, the "Mireille Selmer" 33/2010 Samjin, China (SS 05/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold for high USD 10 mill. In addition, Kawasaki Kisen Kaisha sold their "Galleon" 28/2014 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 06/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 14 mill. Note that a month ago, the "Belle Etoile" 28/2014 I-S, Japan (SS/DD 10/2024, BWTS fitted) was sold at high USD 12 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Lila Global have committed their MR2 "Lila Rhine" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 02/2027, DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) for USD 22,5 mill. Remind you that a couple of weeks ago, the sister vessel "Ibis Pacific" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 06/2027, DD 11/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 22 mill. Furthermore, Atlantica Shipping sold their "Atlantica Bay" 46/2007 Sungdong, Korea (SS 07/2027, DD 10/2025, BWTS fitted) for USD 21,8 mill, whilst same owners sold back in January the sister vessel "Atlantica Breeze" 46/2007 Sungdong, Korea (SS 09/2027, DD 12/2025) for region USD 20 mill. Nordic have sold their MR1 "Nordic Humboldt" 37/2008 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 09/2023) to undisclosed buyers for USD 15 mill basis delivery June 2023. For reference, the Ice Classed "Style" 38/2008 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 04/2023, Ice Class 1A) was sold a couple of weeks ago at low USD 16 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout level of usd 17,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 15,000/17,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf into China were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 20,000/21,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 625K/725K. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of trips from China into Red Sea were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Indian coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd. Persian Gulf trips into East Coast India with limestone were fixed around usd 19,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/650K. A Supramax was fixed on period at the usd 14,600 pd basis delivery China for 4/6 months employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

No fixture reported for this segment. No period activity to report here.
