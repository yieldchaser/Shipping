### Bulkers Market Commentary

Chinese owners have committed their Capesize "Amity" 180/2009 Dalian, China (SS/DD 07/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 19,2 mill. Moreover, Ceres Shipping sold their "Aquahope" 177/2007 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 04/2023, BWTS fitted) to Turkish buyers for mid USD 18 mill. Note that a couple of months ago, Turkish owners Beks acquired the sister vessel "Orient Angel" 177/2007 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 06/2025, DD 06/2023) at USD 19 mill. The Ultramax "Ultra Bellambi" 61/2012 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 08/2027, DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) of Marugame Kisen Kaisha was committed to Far Eastern buyers for USD 21,9 mill. Remind you that back in October, the one- year-older sister vessel "Van Star" 61/2011 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 12/2025, DD 12/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 21,8 mill. On the Supramax sector, Pan Ocean have sold their "Pan Begonia" 57/2009 Stx Dalian, China (SS/DD 04/2024, BWTS fitted) for USD 12,5 mill, while same owners sold a couple of months ago the sister vessel "Pan Crocus" 57/2009 Stx Dalian, China (SS/DD 05/2024, BWTS fitted) at USD 14,7 mill. Furthermore, Japanese owners sold the "Royal Fairness" 55/2011 Mitsui, Japan (SS 10/2025, DD 10/2023) for USD 16 mill. Note that back in October, the two-years-older "Epic" 55/2009 Mitsui, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 04/2025) was sold at USD 18,5 mill. SMT Shipping have committed their Handysize "Baltic Pearl" 37/2014 Zhejiang, China (SS/DD 01/2024, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1C) for USD 19 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger "Interlink Sagacity" 38/2015 Taizhou, China (SS 08/2025, DD 08/2023, BWTS fitted, Ice Class 1C) was sold in December at USD 20 mill. In addition, the Japanese built "Kirishima Sky" 35/2014 Minaminippon, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS fitted) was sold to Turkish buyers for USD 16,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The Aframax "S-Trooper" 105/2003 Sumitomo, Japan (SS/DD 10/2023) of Stalwart Management was committed to undisclosed buyers for USD 24,3 mill. For reference, the two-years-younger "Sealoyalty" 106/2005 Universal, Japan (SS 05/2025, DD 07/2023) was sold a couple of weeks ago at region USD 32 mill. Following the sale of the MR2 "GWN 2" 50/2020 Samsung, Korea (SS 09/2025, DD 09/2023, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) a month ago at USD 38,5 mill, Mitsui & Co have now sold the one-year-younger sister vessel "GWN 3" 50/2021 Samsung, Korea (SS 01/2026, DD 01/2024, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Japanese buyers for USD 50 mill.
