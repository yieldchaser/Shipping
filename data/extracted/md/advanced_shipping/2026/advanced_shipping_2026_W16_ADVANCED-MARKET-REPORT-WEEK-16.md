### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds with redelivery in the Singapore-Japan range were reported at usd 16,000 pd, while trips with redelivery in China were fixed around usd 19,000/23,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into South East Asia were fixed at levels ranging from usd 18,500/22,000 pd. China trips via Indonesia into South China were reported around usd 16,000/19,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into the Singapore-Japan range were fixed between usd 18,000/23,000 pd. Additionally, India trips via East Coast South America into Singapore-Japan were reported at usd 20,000/22,000 pd. Continent trips via North Coast South America into Singapore-Japan were fixed at usd 25,500 pd. On the period front, Kamsarmaxes were reported fixed at usd 17,750 pd for 11/13 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

Indonesia trips into East Coast India ranged from usd 18,000 to usd 19,000 pd. Indonesia trips into West Coast India were fixed at usd 23,500 pd. South East Asia trips into the East Africa were reported around usd 21,000 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips via East Kalimantan into South China were fixed around usd 16,000 pd.
