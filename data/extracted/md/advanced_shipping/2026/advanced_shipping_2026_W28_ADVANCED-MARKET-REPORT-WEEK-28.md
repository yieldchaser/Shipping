### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route fluctuated upward, with routes from Dampier and Port Hedland to Qingdao settling between USD 12.70/13.35 fio. In the Atlantic, Tubarao to Qingdao routes secured between USD 31.60/32.50 fio, the higher end including West Africa options, while a transatlantic run from Tubarao to Taranto achieved USD 26.50 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac rounds from North China and Japan with Singapore/Japan and China redelivery secured between USD 15,250/20,000 pd. On Australian business, departures from North China, South China, South East Asia and Japan via Australia fixed between USD 15,000/19,000 pd to South Korea and Japan, between USD 18,250 pd to Singapore/Japan and between USD 16,000/23,000 pd to South China and China ranges. Indonesian coal runs from North China, South China and South East Asia via Indonesia saw redelivery to South China between USD 11,000/16,500 pd, to South Korea at USD 13,600 pd, to South East Asia between USD 13,500/15,000 pd and to East Coast India at USD 16,500/16,750 pd, while a Far East trip from North China via Russian Far East to Singapore/Japan fetched USD 18,000 pd. Further activity in the East included trips from South China and South East Asia via ECSA to Singapore/Japan range settling between USD 19,000/23,000 pd. From India, a West Coast India departure via ECSA to Singapore/Japan fixed at USD 22,000 pd, while East Coast India fixtures via East Coast India secured USD 30,000/30,750 pd for West Coast India redelivery, alongside trips via ECSA to South East Asia or Singapore/Japan range settling between USD 18,300/24,000 pd. In the Atlantic, an ECSA outbound trip to Continent/Med made USD 33,750 pd, while Continent departures fixed via US Gulf at USD 32,000 pd to Singapore/Japan and via NCSA at USD 32,750 pd to China. On period, a Kamsarmax achieved USD 16,850 pd for 3/5 months duration, while a Continent departure secured USD 30,000 pd for 4/6 months.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, trips to East Coast India secured USD 17,000 pd from North China, between USD 22,000/24,500 pd from South China and USD 20,500 pd from South East Asia. South East Asia coal runs via Indonesia to China fixed between USD 17,000/18,500 pd, while a Japan departure to ECSA settled at USD 15,000 pd. From India, a West Coast India departure via South Africa to Singapore/Japan achieved USD 19,500 pd, while a prompt iron ore run with Persian Gulf redelivery secured USD 45,000 pd. Additionally, an East Coast India spot trip fixed at USD 17,500 pd to the Med.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Far East, departures from Japan and South East Asia fixed between USD 14,000/17,250 pd with South East Asia and Singapore redelivery options. Moving to the Americas, ECSA grain activity saw departures from East Coast South America securing between USD 19,250/23,000 pd to NCSA and USD 19,500/20,000 pd to the US Gulf. Long-haul or local runs from the same area reached USD 20,000 pd to South East Asia, USD 22,500 pd for local ECSA redelivery, and USD 29,000 pd to West Coast South America.
