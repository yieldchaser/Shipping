### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route softened slightly, with Dampier to Qingdao fixtures settling between USD 16.25/16.75 fio. In the Atlantic, long-haul business from Tubarao to Qingdao, including West Africa options, achieved USD 43.50 fio. Transatlantic and regional Atlantic activity saw runs from Seven Islands to Praia Mole and Djen-Djen fixing between USD 26.50/27.25 fio, while a Narvik departure to Erdemir reached USD 23.75 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac trips with South China and Singapore-Japan options settled between USD 19,500/20,000 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, Japan, India and South East Asia ranges fetched between USD 19,000/26,000 pd. Indonesian coal runs to South China, Japan and India achieved between USD 13,000/25,500 pd. From East Coast India and West Coast India, trips via South Africa to India obtained between USD 23,000/25,250 pd, while trips via ECSA to Singapore-Japan and South East Asia range fixed between USD 18,250/22,500 pd. A direct ECSA front-haul to Singapore-Japan reached USD 23,000 pd alongside a USD 1,300,000 ballast bonus. In the Atlantic, a West Med departure via USG to China reached USD 27,500 pd, alongside NCSA runs to Continent, Mediterranean and West Africa agreed between USD 21,300/22,400 pd. On period, a fixture for 7 to 9 months duration was secured at USD 23,500 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, Indonesian coal runs to South East Asia and ECI ranges settled between USD 20,000/23,000 pd and USD 28,000/29,000 pd respectively. An Australian grain run back to South East Asia fetched USD 24,500 pd, a North China trip via NoPac to ECI reached USD 26,000 pd, and a South China trip via PG to Mediterranean obtained USD 26,000 pd. Out of South East Asia, a cement run to West Africa fixed at USD 17,500 pd, while an ECI departure via Indonesia to India made USD 22,000 pd. In the Atlantic, a Continent run via Murmansk to US Gulf achieved USD 29,000 pd, alongside a long-haul petcoke trip from USEC to China reaching USD 34,500 pd. On period, a long- term fixture for 20 to 21 months duration was secured at USD 18,000 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Atlantic, ECSA grain runs to WCSA range secured strong levels between USD 29,500/31,500 pd, alongside a long-haul petcoke trip from ECSA to China fixing at USD 21,500 pd. Out of US Gulf, transatlantic grain departures to Mediterranean and Spain achieved between USD 25,000/28,000 pd, while a Caribs trip via US Gulf to EC Mexico made USD 14,000 pd. From USEC, a grain run via St Lawrence to NCSA range reached USD 21,000 pd.
