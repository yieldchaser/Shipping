### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route settled between USD 13.95/14.70 fio across Port Hedland, Dampier and Port Stanley departures. In the Atlantic, long-haul business from Tubarao to Qingdao, including West Africa options, fixed between USD 34.20/35.40 fio. Transatlantic runs from PDM, Port Cartier and Narvik to European destinations achieved between USD 13.50/19.50 fio, while a TRMT departure to Qingdao made USD 10.40 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore-Japan and South East Asia options settled between USD 17,500/26,500 pd. An Australian round from South East Asia to Japan fetched USD 21,500 pd alongside a USD 300,000 ballast bonus. From West Coast India, trips via ECSA to South East Asia range fixed between USD 21,000/25,750 pd, while Indian Ocean runs via South Africa and Nacala obtained between USD 16,750/19,000 pd. Out of ECSA, front- haul fixtures to Singapore-Japan achieved between USD 22,650/24,500 pd with ballast bonuses from USD 1,265,000 to USD 1,400,000, whereas transatlantic grain runs to Continent and Mediterranean range reached between USD 33,650/38,500 pd. In the Atlantic, a Continent trip via USEC to Far East made USD 33,500 pd, alongside a scrap run to East Med securing USD 37,000 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, Indonesian coal trips to SE Asia and WCI ranges settled between USD 22,250/26,000 pd and USD 31,000 pd respectively. Nickel ore and clinker runs from South China to Far East and West Africa achieved between USD 18,500/26,500 pd, while an Australian nickel ore trip to China made USD 23,750 pd. Out of WCCA, a NoPac run to SE Asia fetched USD 23,000 pd with a USD 700,000 ballast bonus. Regional iron ore and limestone runs from ECI, WCI and PG ranges fixed between USD 12,000/20,000 pd, while South Africa coal to ECI reached USD 23,000 pd with a USD 230,000 ballast bonus. In the Atlantic, US Gulf coal to India reached USD 38,000 pd, with transatlantic grains fixing between USD 20,500/28,500 pd. A Continent scrap run to East Med achieved USD 37,000 pd and a Baltic trip to ECSA made USD 25,500 pd. ECSA front-haul grain and sugar trips to ECI and SE Asia ranged between USD 19,750/21,000 pd with ballast bonuses up to USD 1,100,000, alongside transatlantic grain to Med reaching USD 36,500 pd. On period, fixtures for 4 to 12 months duration settled between USD 21,000/23,500 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Pacific, regional runs from North China to South East Asia range fixed between USD 15,500/17,000 pd, while steel and project cargo departures from South China to WCCA and Continent range obtained between USD 21,500/22,000 pd. An East Australia trip to Japan achieved USD 19,000 pd and a WCCA grain run via Vancouver to Japan fetched USD 24,000 pd. In the Atlantic, USEC wood pellet runs to Continent range settled between USD 21,000/24,000 pd, an ECSA trip to Continent made USD 25,000 pd and a West Med departure to ECI reached USD 18,500 pd. On period, a Handymax delivering in ECSA fixed for 4 to 6 months duration at USD 20,000 pd.
