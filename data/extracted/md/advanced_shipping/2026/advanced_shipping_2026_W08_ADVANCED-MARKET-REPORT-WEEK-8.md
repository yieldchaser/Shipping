### Bulkers Market Commentary

Stealth Maritime have committed the Capesize "Cape Brazil" 177/2010 Shanghai, China (SS 10/2030, DD 08/2027) to Far Eastern interests at USD 31,5 mill. For reference, the "Eastern Freesia" 180/2010 Qingdao, China (SS/DD 10/2025) was sold back in September at USD 23,25 mill. Caravel Group have committed the Ultramax "Explorer America" 61/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 06/2026) to Chinese interests at USD 18,2 mill. Note that same owners committed a month ago the one-year-younger sister vessel "Explorer Africa" 61/2012 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 04/2027) at USD 19,2 mill. Moreover, Nissen Kaiun committed the "Nord Chesapeake" 60/2016 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 07/2026, Scrubber fitted) at USD 25,5 mill. For comparison, the "IVS Windsor" 60/2016 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 09/2026) was sold in December at high USD 25 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Fortune Tiger" 58/2013 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 07/2028, DD 07/2026) of Cido Shipping was committed at USD 19 mill. Remind you that back in October, the sister vessel "KN Future" 58/2013 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 10/2028, DD 11/2026) was sold at USD 18,75 mill. In addition, Densay Shipping & Trading committed the "SSI Erdogan Bey" 50/2010 Oshima, Japan (SS 01/2030, DD 04/2028, OHBS) at USD 15 mill, while the one-year-younger "Sun Master" 50/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 02/2026, OHBS) was sold in December at low USD 15 mill. Evalend Shipping have committed the Handysize "Liberator" 28/2006 Shimanami, Japan (SS/DD 04/2026) to undisclosed interests at USD 6,7 mill, matching the price achieved by her sister vessel "Shimanami Star" 28/2006 Shimanami, Japan (SS/DD 07/2026) back in December.

Remind you that the one-year-older "Pigeon Point" 48/2005 Minami-Nippon, Japan (SS 09/2028, DD 09/2026) was sold a month ago at USD 13,9 mill.
