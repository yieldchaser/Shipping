### Bulkers Market Commentary

Japanese owners have committed the Capesize "Orange Tiger" 181/2011 Imabari, Japan (SS 11/2030, DD 09/2028) at rgn USD 37 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Frontier Garland" 181/2011 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2029, DD 01/2027) was sold back in March at USD 36,3 mill. Moreover, following the sale of the "Aashna" 179/2012 HHIC, Philippines (SS/DD 10/2027) a couple of weeks ago at USD 37,5 mill, clients of Adani Shipping have now committed the sister vessel "Aanya" 179/2012 HHIC, Philippines (SS/DD 06/2027) at USD 36,9 mill basis delivery October-December 2026. The Kamsarmax "Aquavita Aim" 82/2019 Oshima, Japan (SS 11/2029, DD 01/2027) of Olive Shipmanagement was committed to European interests at USD 38,2 mill. For reference, the two-years-older "Mont Blanc Hawk" 81/2017 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 07/2027) was sold in June at USD 32,8 mill. Furthermore, Ciner Group committed their "Artvin" 81/2011 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 10/2026) & "Ordu" 81/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 05/2027) to Korean interests at USD 19,45 mill each. Note that back in May, the "Avalon" 81/2011 Sungdong, Korea (SS/DD 07/2026) was sold at high USD 17 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Viva Eclipse" 54/2009 Jiangsu, China (SS 01/2029, DD 01/2027) was committed at USD 11,75 mill. By way of comparison, the "Africa Hope" 53/2009 Yangzhou, China (SS 01/2029, DD 12/2026) was sold back in May at high USD 10 mill. Hsin Chien Marine have committed their Handysize "New Journey" 36/2015 Shikoku, Japan (SS 03/2030, DD 04/2028) to undisclosed interests at USD 19,8 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older sister vessel "KS Grace" 36/2014 Shikoku, Japan (SS 01/2029, DD 01/2027) was sold back in April at USD 16,9 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route strengthened, with Port Hedland and Dampier to Qingdao fixtures settling between USD 15.00/16.30 fio. In the Atlantic, Tubarao departures to Qingdao, including West Africa options, fetched between USD 34.25/36.10 fio, while a Boffa run to Yantai and Longkou reached USD 36.80 fio. Transatlantic activity saw a Tubarao run to Rotterdam fixing at USD 15.50 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, Australian rounds with redelivery in China, Japan, Taiwan, India and South East Asia fetched between USD 16,500/21,000 pd. NoPac trips with Singapore/Japan and South East Asia options settled between USD 16,250/17,500 pd, while a North America West Coast run to Malaysia made USD 15,000 pd. North China trips to Japan were agreed between USD 17,500/19,750 pd and Indonesian coal runs to South China, Japan, Taiwan and South East Asia ranges achieved USD 13,750/20,000 pd. From East Coast India, West Coast India and South East Asia, trips via ECSA and NCSA to Far East destinations obtained USD 18,500/24,500 pd, while a West Africa departure via NCSA secured USD 21,500 pd plus USD 550,000 ballast bonus. In the Atlantic, transatlantic and fronthaul fixtures via USEC and NCSA obtained between USD 24,000/35,000 pd. On period, short-term activity for 6 to 13 months duration settled between USD 19,950/23,000 pd, while a newbuilding vessel fixed for a longer 22 to 24 months period at USD 19,500 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

Out of India and the Persian Gulf, coastal and regional runs from West Coast India to East Coast India settled between USD 19,000/21,000 pd, while a long-haul trip via South Africa to China made USD 17,500 pd and a PG trip to ECSA fixed at USD 13,000 pd. Out of South Africa, coal trips to Far East and China options secured between USD 18,500/24,500 pd, alongside ballast bonuses from USD 185,000 to USD 245,000. In the Atlantic, an ECSA grain run to Singapore/Japan range achieved USD 29,000 pd, while a West Med trip via Continent to India obtained USD 24,000 pd. On period, an Ultramax fixed for 5 to 7 months duration at USD 21,000 pd with Atlantic redelivery.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Pacific, a local South East Asia trip with sugar fixed at USD 16,000 pd. In the Atlantic, transatlantic runs from USEC and US Gulf to Continent range fetched between USD 17,000/18,500 pd, while trips from ECSA and NCSA to US Gulf and West Africa achieved USD 18,250 pd and USD 16,500 pd respectively. Out of Continent, runs to WCSA and East Med settled between USD 15,250/17,000 pd. In the Med, transatlantic departures from East Med to USEC and US Gulf ranges were agreed between USD 12,000/14,000 pd, alongside extra ILOHC bonuses where applicable, while a return trip from USEC to East Med made USD 17,000 pd. On period, a Handysize open in South East Asia fixed for 4 to 6 months duration at USD 17,250 pd.
