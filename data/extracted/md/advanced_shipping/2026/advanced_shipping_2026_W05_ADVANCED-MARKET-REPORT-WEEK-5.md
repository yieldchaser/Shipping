### Bulkers Market Commentary

Minoa Marine have committed their Kamsarmax "Aya" 83/2006 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 06/2026) to Chinese interests at USD 10,8 mill. Remind you that back in November, the sister vessel "Yasa Pioneer" 82/2006 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 02/2026) was sold at USD 10,7 mill. Furthermore, the "Cretansea" 81/2009 Universal, Japan (SS 03/2029, DD 03/2027) was committed to Greek interests at USD 15,15 mill. Note that the two-years- younger sister vessel "Key Frontier" 80/2011 Universal, Japan (SS 06/2030, DD 05/2028) was sold in December at high USD 18 mill. Minoa Marine have also committed their Panamax "Anthea" 76/2006 Sasebo, Japan (SS/DD 04/2026) at USD 9,7 mill, while the sister vessel "Sfera" 76/2006 Sasebo, Japan (SS/DD 01/2026) was sold in December at USD 8,75 mill. Moreover, the younger "Global Bonanza" 74/2011 Sasebo, Japan (SS/DD 04/2026) was committed to Greek interests at USD 15,3 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "KT Birdie" 74/2011 Sasebo, Japan (SS 10/2030, DD 08/2028) was committed a week ago at USD 16,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Costamare have committed their "Clara" 56/2008 IHI, Japan (SS 06/2028, DD 09/2026) to Chinese interests at USD 11,8 mill. Remind you that the "Vega Stetind" 55/2008 Oshima, Japan (SS 02/2028, DD 12/2025) was committed back in November at USD 11 mill. In addition, following the sale of the "Spar Taurus" 53/2005 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 11/2025) back in September at USD 8,8 mill, Spar Shipping have now committed the one-year-younger sister vessel "Spar Canis" 53/2006 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 03/2026) to Chinese interests at rgn USD 9 mill. The Handysize "Jetstream" 34/2012 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 05/2027) was committed to undisclosed interests at rgn USD 13,5 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older sister vessel "Aphrodite M" 34/2011 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 10/2026) was sold in December at USD 12,5 mill.
