### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route throughout Week 24 reported at low-mid USD 12.00's/low USD 13.00's fio level. Brazil / China rates were noticed ta USD 35.00 fio level, while Atlantic trips into Continent were fixed at high USD 15.00 fio level.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds with redelivery in the China and Singapore-Japan range were mainly reported between USD 18,250/24,250 pd. A number of Australian trips from China and South East Asia into Japan and South China were fixed at levels ranging from USD 22,000/27,000 pd. South East Asian trips to China and Japan were noticed fixed between USD 15,500/20,500 pd. US East Coast trips to East Med were reported at USD 32,000 pd and to Japan at 18,500/19,000 pd, while one voyage to Singapore-Japan was reported at USD 20,000 plus a ballast bonus of USD 1,000,000. On the period front, Kamsarmaxes were reported fixed between USD 19,000/22,500 pd for 11/13 months durations.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

South East Asia trips round trips were mainly reported at a range of USD 19,500/21,000 pd. Far East trips with redelivery to East Coast South America were fixed at USD 26,000 pd and to West Africa were reported on USD 23,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips into China were fixed at USD 17,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips into Japan were fixed at USD 12,000 pd. US Gulf trips into East Med remained firm and were reported at USD high 32,000 pd. On the period front, there was limited data reported, with a Supramax reported fixed at mid USD 22,000 pd for 4/6 months at charterers' option.

#### Handymax / Handysize

East Coast South America trips into East Coast and West Coast South America were fixed at a range of USD 18,000/26,000 pd. US East Coast trips to Med were reported at USD 22,000 pd. Japan trips into South East Asia were reported at USD 14,000 and into China USD 14,500 pd. On the period front, Handysizes were reported fixed at USD 13,000 pd for 4/6 months.
