### Bulkers Market Commentary

Tsakos Shipping & Trading committed the Capesize "Irene II" 180/2006 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 04/2026) to Chinese interests at USD 21 mill. Note that a couple of months ago, the one-year-older sister vessel "Montecristo" 180/2005 Imabari, Japan (SS 06/2028, DD 06/2026, Scrubber fitted) was sold at low USD 20 mill. Furthermore, the "Robusto" 174/2006 SWS, China (SS/DD 09/2026, Scrubber fitted) of TMS Dry was committed to Chinese interests at USD 19,5 mill basis delivery Q2 2026, while the sister vessel "Pompano" 174/2006 SWS, China (SS/DD 06/2026, Scrubber fitted) from the same owners, was committed in December at high USD 17 mill basis delivery May'26. On the Ultramax sector, MPP Carriers committed the "Mitsos" 63/2013 Chengxi, China (SS 06/2028, DD 09/2026) to Greek interests at USD 20,5 mill. For reference, the two-years-younger "Beauty Jasmine" 63/2015 CSI, China (SS 04/2030, DD 03/2028) was sold back in November at USD 23 mill. Remind you that back in November, the one-year-older sister vessel "Atlantic Bulker" 36/2014 Shikoku, Japan (SS/DD 03/2027) was sold at USD 15 mill.
