### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route rallied significantly, with Dampier and Port Hedland fixtures to Qingdao settling between USD 15.80/16.50 fio, while a Dampier run to Kaohsiung fixed at USD 14.95 fio. In the Atlantic, long-haul business from Tubarao to Qingdao stood steady at USD 35.10 fio, whereas West African departures from Freetown and Nouadhibou to Qingdao achieved between USD 35.60/36.50 fio. In the transatlantic market, a Morebaya run to Gijon or Dunkirk made USD 13.10 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore-Japan options settled between USD 17,000/19,000 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, India, SE Asia and Singapore-Japan ranges fetched between USD 17,500/23,000 pd. Indonesian coal runs to South China, Japan and India ranges achieved between USD 14,500/21,250 pd. Out of West Coast India and Persian Gulf, coal trips via South Africa to India fixed between USD 24,000/27,500 pd. In the Atlantic, front-haul departures from East Coast India and West Africa via ECSA to Singapore-Japan range obtained between USD 21,750/26,000 pd, with the West African delivery also securing a USD 475,000 ballast bonus. On period, a Kamsarmax delivering in North China fixed for 11 to 13 months duration worldwide at USD 17,500 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, Indonesian coal trips from East Coast India and South East Asia settled between USD 14,000/19,000 pd, while an ECI iron ore run to China made USD 14,500 pd. A NoPac grain trip from North China to Singapore- Japan range reached USD 22,000 pd, whereas a South China bagged cement departure to WCSA fixed at USD 19,250 pd. In the Atlantic, US Gulf and USEC transatlantic grain, petcoke and wood pellet runs to Continent and Mediterranean ranges surged between USD 31,500/39,000 pd, while front- haul departures to China and Japan achieved USD 31,750 pd and USD 30,750 pd respectively. Continent scrap to Med obtained USD 34,250 pd. Out of ECSA, sugar trips to West Africa and Red Sea fixed at USD 32,000 pd and USD 20,500 pd, alongside a front-haul to Singapore-Japan at USD 19,500 pd plus a USD 950,000 ballast bonus. On period, two Ultramaxes in Asia fixed for 8 to 13 months on index-linked rates between 103% and 104% of BSI63.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Limited reported activity was recorded in the sector this week, centered exclusively in the Atlantic. Continent scrap via the Baltic to East Med achieved USD 32,000 pd, while ECSA grain departures to West Africa and coastal North Brazil settled firmly between USD 30,000/32,000 pd. On period, a Handysize delivering in West Africa fixed for 5 to 7 months duration worldwide at USD 14,500 pd.
