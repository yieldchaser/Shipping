### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route showed mild gains, with Dampier and Port Hedland fixtures settling between USD 12.85/14.25 fio. In the Atlantic, long-haul business from South Brazil, including Tubarao, Itaguai and Sudeste to Qingdao, fixed steadily between USD 31.00/31.90 fio, while a Ponta Da Madeira run to Isdemir achieved USD 16.75 fio. Out of South Africa, Saldanha Bay departures to Qingdao and Dangjin obtained between USD 23.25/25.75 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore-Japan options settled between USD 18,000/26,000 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, Japan and South East Asia ranges fetched between USD 19,750/24,500 pd. Indonesian coal runs to South China, Japan and India ranges achieved between USD 14,500/23,250 pd. From West Coast India and South East Asia, trips via ECSA to Singapore-Japan range fixed between USD 18,750/21,000 pd. A long-haul fertilizer trip from North China to Mediterranean range made USD 17,500 pd. In the Atlantic, transatlantic runs from Continent via NCSA to Europe range settled between USD 22,500/23,500 pd, while a US Gulf departure to Continent/Med range achieved USD 26,000 pd alongside a USD 600,000 ballast bonus. Front-haul trips from Gibraltar via USEC to India obtained strong levels between USD 31,000/35,000 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, Indonesian coal trips to West Coast India and East Coast India achieved USD 27,000 pd and USD 33,000 pd respectively, while a run to Japan range fixed at USD 19,500 pd. Long-haul departures from China maintained firm levels, with trips to Mediterranean from South China and North China securing between USD 27,500/31,000 pd, alongside a North China run to West Africa at USD 27,500 pd. Out of West Coast India, a coastal limestone trip to ECI range made USD 12,500 pd. In the Atlantic, an ECSA front-haul departure to South East Asia was agreed at USD 20,500 pd alongside a USD 1,000,000 ballast bonus.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Pacific, regional departures from Japan and North China to South East Asia and ECI settled between USD 16,500/22,000 pd, while a South China trip to WCI fetched a strong USD 30,000 pd. An ECSA front-haul run to South East Asia reached USD 22,750 pd. In the Atlantic, Continent scrap to Black Sea hit USD 41,000 pd, while steel and grain runs to USG and West Africa ranges fixed between USD 19,500/25,000 pd. Out of US Gulf, trips to NCSA, West Africa and India ranges obtained between USD 16,000/19,000 pd, while an ECSA run to East Africa achieved USD 25,000 pd. An East Med cement trip to Caribs made USD 12,500 pd. On period, a Handysize delivering in ECSA fixed for 5 to 7 months at USD 19,500 pd.
