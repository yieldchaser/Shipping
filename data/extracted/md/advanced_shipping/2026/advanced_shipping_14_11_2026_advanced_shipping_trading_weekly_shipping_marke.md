### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route firmed up, with Dampier and Port Hedland fixtures settling between USD 13.50/15.25 fio. In the Atlantic, long-haul business from Brazil to Qingdao, including Tubarao, Sudeste and Ponta Da Madeira departures with West Africa options, achieved between USD 36.10/38.00 fio. Transatlantic runs to Rotterdam fixed at USD 18.00 fio from Ponta Da Madeira and USD 14.88 fio from Saldanha Bay, while a coastal Indian trip from Visakhapatnam to Hazira made USD 7.30 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore-Japan options settled between USD 19,500/22,250 pd, while a WCCA wood pellet run to Continent made USD 14,750 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, India and Singapore-Japan ranges fetched between USD 17,250/25,000 pd. Indonesian coal runs to South China, Japan and SE Asia ranges achieved between USD 15,500/22,000 pd. From India and Persian Gulf, trips via ECSA to Singapore-Japan range fixed between USD 14,000/21,500 pd, while direct ECSA front-hauls reached USD 18,750/19,350 pd alongside ballast bonuses up to USD 935,000. In the Atlantic, front-haul departures from Continent and West Med via USG and USEC to China and India obtained between USD 21,000/27,350 pd. On period, fixtures from 4 up to 14 months duration settled between USD 17,500/20,500 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

Limited reported activity was noted in the Pacific, where a NoPac run from North China to South East Asia settled at USD 23,500 pd, while an Australian nickel ore trip to China made USD 21,500 pd. Regional runs from North China to Singapore and ECI via Indonesia to India fixed between USD 18,000/20,500 pd, alongside an ECI departure to China at USD 15,000 pd. In the Atlantic, Continent scrap to East Med achieved a strong USD 36,500 pd, while US Gulf grain and petcoke departures to East Med and India fetched USD 35,000 pd and USD 34,000 pd respectively. On period, an Ultramax delivering in North China was secured for 6 to 8 months duration worldwide at USD 24,500 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Pacific, a Japanese slag run to South East Asia fixed at USD 19,500 pd, while a WCI parcel cargo departure via ECI to South Africa settled at USD 13,300 pd. In the Atlantic, ECSA transatlantic runs to Continent achieved strong levels between USD 28,500/31,000 pd, alongside front-haul trips to Singapore-Japan and Persian Gulf reaching USD 24,000 pd and USD 32,000 pd respectively. Out of NCSA, coal runs to Continent fixed between USD 17,500/26,000 pd, while regional trips to ECSA made USD 13,500/14,000 pd. US Gulf wood pellet and grain departures to Continent and NCSA settled between USD 17,500/23,000 pd. From Continent and Mediterranean, grain runs to West Africa and Med achieved between USD 23,000/27,000 pd, whereas a steel trip to US Gulf and intra-Med grains fixed at USD 17,000 pd and USD 13,750 pd respectively.
