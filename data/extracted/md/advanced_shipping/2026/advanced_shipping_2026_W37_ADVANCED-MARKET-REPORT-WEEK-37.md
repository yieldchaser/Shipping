### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route maintained a strong footing, with Port Hedland and Dampier fixtures to Qingdao settling between USD 17.75/19.05 fio. In the Atlantic, long-haul departures from Tubarao to Qingdao achieved USD 41.88 fio, while transatlantic activity saw runs from Seven Islands to Rotterdam and Mobile to Praia Mole fixing at USD 16.00 fio and USD 24.00 fio respectively. Out of South Africa, a Saldanha Bay run to Qingdao fetched USD 30.69 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore-Japan and South East Asia options settled between USD 20,000/27,000 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, Japan and Singapore-Japan ranges fetched between USD 19,250/27,750 pd. Indonesian coal runs to South China and Japan achieved between USD 16,100/28,000 pd. From East Coast India and West Coast India, regional coal trips to West Coast India and South Africa options obtained USD 35,000 pd and USD 25,000 pd respectively. Trips via ECSA to Singapore-Japan and China ranges fixed between USD 22,250/34,650 pd, while a transatlantic grain run from ECSA to Continent and Mediterranean made USD 33,500 pd. Out of Continent, a trip via USG to India reached USD 35,500 pd, alongside NCSA runs to Continent and Mediterranean agreed between USD 20,750/22,000 pd. On period, a fixture for 5 to 7 months duration was secured at USD 22,000 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, nickel ore and coal runs from South East Asia to China settled between USD 17,000/17,500 pd, while a clinker trip from North China to Philippines fetched USD 19,000 pd. Out of East Coast India, an iron ore run to China achieved USD 15,500 pd, a Kandla departure via South Africa to Far East made USD 18,000 pd and a Salalah limestone run via PG to West Coast India secured USD 20,000 pd. In the Atlantic, a NCSA trip via USG to WCSA reached USD 29,000 pd. Transatlantic grain and coal fixtures from USG to Continent and East Med ranges obtained between USD 28,500/35,000 pd, while an ECSA front-haul to Singapore-Japan fixed at USD 20,000 pd alongside a USD 1,000,000 ballast bonus.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Atlantic, a Continent trip via West Med to USG fetched USD 10,000 pd, while a scrap run from Continent to East Med made USD 15,000 pd. Out of West Africa, a grain trip via ECSA back to West Africa range settled at USD 10,000 pd. On period, a vessel delivering in East Med secured USD 13,000 pd for 5 to 7 months duration with Atlantic redelivery options.
