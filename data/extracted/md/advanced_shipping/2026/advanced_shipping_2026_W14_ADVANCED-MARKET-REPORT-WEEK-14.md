### Bulkers Market Commentary

Dusung Shipping have committed their Kamsarmax "Donghae Star" 82/2012 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 05/2027) at low USD 17 mill, while the "John M Carras" 82/2012 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 01/2027) of Carras Hellas was committed to clients of Bright Navigation at USD 18,2 mill. Note that a couple of months ago, Carras Hellas sold the sister vessel of the latter "Athina Carras" 82/2012 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 02/2027) at USD 17,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Platon" 58/2011 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 08/2026) of Navina Maritime was committed at USD 15,4 mill. Remind you that back in December, the "Desert Glory" 57/2011 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 03/2026) was sold at high USD 14 mill. Moreover, Chinese owners committed the "Mercury Ocean" 53/2008 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 04/2026) to compatriots at USD 13 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Theodora" 53/2008 Iwagi, Japan (SS 12/2030, DD 02/2029) was sold a month ago at the same price. Following the sale of the Handysize "DL Lavender" 35/2014 Spp, Korea (SS 01/2029, DD 01/2027) at USD 15,8 mill, clients of Dong-A Tanker have now committed the one-year-older sister vessel "DL Olive" 35/2013 Spp, Korea (SS 12/2028, DD 11/2026) at USD 15,3 mill. In addition, Cetus Maritime committed the "Atlantic Spirit" 35/2013 Nanjing, China (SS/DD 03/2028) at mid-high USD 12 mill, after having sold the one-year-older sister vessel "Asia Spirit" 35/2012 Nanjing, China (SS/DD 04/2027) in February at mid USD 11 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds with redelivery in the Singapore-Japan range were reported between usd 14,00/17,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into China and India were fixed at levels ranging from usd 15,000/18,000 pd. China trips via Indonesia into South East Asia or India were reported around usd 19,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Singapore-Japan range were fixed at usd 19,200/19,500 pd plus ballast bonus ranging from usd 920k to usd 950k. Additionally, India trips via East Coast South America into Singapore-Japan were reported at usd 17,000 pd. West Mediterranean trips into Singapore-Japan were fixed around usd 23,000 pd. On the period front, Kamsarmaxes were reported fixed in the usd 19,000/20,000 pd range for 5/8 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

Indonesia trips into South China and Philippines ranged at the usd 12,000/15,000 pd level. A number of South Africa trips into India/China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd. US Gulf and US East Coast trips into China and Continent were fixed around usd 16,000/19,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Continent-Mediterranean range were fixed in a range of usd 17,000/22,000 pd. Additionally, East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America reached usd 26,500 pd. Continent and Mediterranean trips into West Africa were reported around usd 20,000/21,000 pd. On the period front, an Ultramax was reported fixed in the usd 17,000 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery North China.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips from East Mediterranean via Black Sea into East Coast South America and US East Coast were fixed around usd 11,000/12,000 pd. US East Coast trips into Mediterranean were reported at the usd 15,000 pd level. East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America, Caribbean, and US Gulf were fixed in a range of usd 16,000/20,000 pd. Additionally, South East Asia trips for Australia-Japan rounds were reported at usd 13,500 pd.
