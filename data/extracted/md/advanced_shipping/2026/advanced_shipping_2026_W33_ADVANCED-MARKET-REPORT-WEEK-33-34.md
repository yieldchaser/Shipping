### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route moved within a tight range, with Port Hedland and Dampier to Qingdao fixtures settling between USD 13.40/14.00 fio. In the Atlantic, long-haul departures from Seven Islands and Port Cartier to Qingdao and Kakogawa reached USD 43.00 fio and USD 49.25 fio respectively, while a West Africa run from Owendo to East Coast India fetched USD 45.75 fio. Out of South Africa, a Saldanha Bay trip to Qingdao secured USD 24.98 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore-Japan options settled between USD 15,500/16,100 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, Japan, India and Singapore-Japan ranges fetched between USD 15,500/19,000 pd. Indonesian coal runs to South China, Japan and regional destinations achieved between USD 14,100/21,250 pd, while a fertiliser run via Yangtze River to ECSA made USD 6,500 pd. From East Coast India, West Coast India and South East Asia, trips via ECSA and South Africa to India and Singapore- Japan range obtained USD 19,500/22,000 pd, alongside an ECSA departure fixing at USD 19,750 pd plus USD 975,000 ballast bonus. In the Atlantic, a Continent trip via USEC to South East Asia reached USD 30,750 pd. On period, activity was active with fixtures for 5 to 10 months duration agreed between USD 15,500/19,000 pd, while a 11 to 13 months period was secured at 112% of the P5TC index.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, a NoPac round settled at USD 16,500 pd, while an Australian run to Japan fetched USD 21,000 pd. Indonesian coal trips with options to India, Thailand and regional destinations achieved between USD 16,000/23,000 pd, whereas nickel ore and clinker runs made USD 19,000 pd and USD 16,000 pd respectively. Out of India, regional trips to Bangladesh and Chittagong secured between USD 25,000/25,500 pd, a West Coast India run to Japan fixed at USD 17,000 pd and a West Africa trip via Owendo to India achieved USD 27,000 pd. Out of South Africa, trips to China and India range obtained between USD 23,500/25,000 pd, alongside ballast bonuses ranging from USD 235,000 to USD 250,000. In the Atlantic, transatlantic grain and petcoke runs from USEC and US Gulf reached USD 28,000 pd and USD 34,000 pd respectively. On period, vessels secured between USD 20,750/21,000 pd for durations ranging from 6 to 10 months.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Pacific, a trip from South East Asia via Australia to Japan fetched USD 20,500 pd. In the Atlantic, an ECSA run to West Africa secured USD 16,750 pd. Out of the Mediterranean, a departure from East Med to NCSA and US Gulf range made USD 9,850 pd, while a long-haul trip from West Med via ECSA to Persian Gulf fixed at USD 9,500 pd.
