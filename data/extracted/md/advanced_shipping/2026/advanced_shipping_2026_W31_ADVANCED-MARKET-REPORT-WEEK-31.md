### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route showed a firmer tone, with Port Hedland and Dampier to Qingdao fixtures settling between USD 11.15/12.50 fio. In the Atlantic, Tubarao and Itaguai departures to Qingdao, including West Africa options, secured between USD 32.10/33.75 fio, while a long-haul run from Port Cartier achieved USD 46.70 fio. Out of South Africa, a Saldanha Bay trip to Qingdao fetched USD 24.12 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, Australian rounds with redelivery in China, South Korea, India and South East Asia fetched between USD 11,750/19,000 pd, alongside a long-haul trip from Continent via Australia to South Korea fixing at USD 34,400 pd. NoPac trips with Singapore/Japan, Far East and India options settled between USD 14,750/18,250 pd. Indonesian coal runs to China, India, Japan and local ranges achieved USD 11,100/18,000 pd, while a North China trip to South East Asia made USD 10,000 pd. From East Coast India, West Coast India and South East Asia, trips via ECSA and South Africa to India and Far East destinations obtained USD 16,000/21,000 pd and USD 19,000 pd respectively. In the Atlantic, Continent trips via USEC to India and South East Asia reached between USD 33,000/34,000 pd, while an intra-Continent run via Narvik made USD 22,000 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, trips from North China via Indonesia to East Coast India fixed at USD 18,000 pd, while a run to the Persian Gulf achieved USD 25,500 pd and a trip from Singapore to China settled at USD 15,500 pd. From East Australia, a trip to South East Asia fetched USD 22,000 pd plus USD 345,000 ballast bonus. Out of India, coastal and local round trips, including runs via South East Asia, settled between USD 17,750/20,000 pd. In the Atlantic, transatlantic runs from NCSA and ECSA to the Continent obtained between USD 16,000/25,000 pd. Out of South Africa, coal trips to West Coast India, Pakistan and Japan ranges secured between USD 22,000/27,000 pd alongside ballast bonuses ranging from USD 220,000 to USD 270,000.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Activity in the Handysize segment remained subdued for another week, with limited fixtures reported. In the Atlantic, an alumina run from the Caribbean to Continent fixed at USD 16,500 pd, while a NCSA trip to ECSA range fetched USD 15,200 pd. Out of Continent, a transatlantic grain run to NCSA settled at USD 9,500 pd, whereas a West Med trip via Safi to West Africa achieved USD 15,500 pd.
