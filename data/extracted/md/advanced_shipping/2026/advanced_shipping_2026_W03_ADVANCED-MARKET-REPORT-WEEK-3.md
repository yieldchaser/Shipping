### Bulkers Market Commentary

Norden have committed their Newcastlemax "Nord Palladium" 209/2021 SWS, China (SS/DD 04/2026, Scrubber fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 76,25 mill. Note that a month ago, the one-year-older "Atlantic Lion" 209/2020 SWS, China (SS 08/2030, DD 08/2028, Scrubber fitted) was sold at USD 73,6 mill. The Capesize "Mineral Honshu" 181/2012 Koyo, Japan (SS/DD 04/2027) was committed to Chinese interests at USD 37,7 mill, while the sister vessel "KM Osaka" 180/2012 Koyo, Japan (SS/DD 01/2027) was also committed to Chinese interests at USD 34,8 mill. For reference, the one-year-older sister vessel "Pacifist" 181/2011 Koyo, Japan (SS/DD 01/2026) was sold in early December at USD 32 mill. Bw Group committed the Kamsarmax "BW Matsuyama" 81/2019 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 09/2029, DD 10/2027) to Greek interests at USD 31 mill. Note that in November, the sister vessel "BW Japan" 81/2019 Tsuneishi Cebu, Philippines (SS 05/2029, DD 05/2027, Scrubber fitted) was sold at USD 32,5 mill. Moreover, the "Jag Aarati" 80/2011 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 02/2026) of Great Eastern Shipping was committed at high USD 14 mill. Remind you that back in September, the sister vessel "Silver Navigator" 80/2011 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 07/2026) was sold at USD 15,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Goldenport Shipmanagement have committed their "Pisti" 56/2011 Cosco, China (SS/DD 04/2026) at USD 12,8 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older sister vessel "Ince Kastamonu" 56/2010 Cosco, China (SS 04/2030, DD 04/2028) was sold in December at the same price.

News have emerged that Sinokor Maritime acquired approximately 30 VLCCs during the Christmas holidays. The LR3 "Eclipse I" 158/2006 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS/DD 08/2026) was committed to undisclosed interests at USD 33 mill. Remind you that the one-year-younger "Grand Ace9" 46/2008 Stx, Korea (SS 03/2028, DD 03/2026) was sold a month ago at USD 15,5 mill.
