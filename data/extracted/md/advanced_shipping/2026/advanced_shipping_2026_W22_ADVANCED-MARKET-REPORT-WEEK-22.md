### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds with redelivery in the Singapore-Japan range were reported between usd 20,000/23,000 pd. A number of Australian trips from South China into Singapore-Japan range were fixed at levels ranging from usd 23,000/25,000 pd. South East Asian trips to China and Singapore-Japan were noticed between usd 20,000/24,000 pd. East Coast South American trips to Singapore-Japan and Far East range were reported at usd 22,000/24,000 pd. On the period front, Kamsarmaxes were reported fixed between usd 20,000/21,000 pd for 5/7 months.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

South East Asia trips into East Coast India were reported at a range of usd 18,000/19,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips into Singapore were fixed at usd 17,000/18,000 pd. East Coast South American trips via Persian Gulf and East Coast South America into Continent-Mediterranean were fixed at usd 28,000/30,000 pd. US Gulf trips into East Coast India were reported at usd 20,000 pd. US East Coast trips into Mediterranean and South East Asia we reported at usd 25,000/28,000 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Australian trips into China were reported at usd 16,000 pd, while North China trips into South East Asia and East Coast India were fixed at a range of usd 19,000/20,000 pd. Continental trips into West Africa were reported at usd 12,000/14,000 pd. West Coast Central American trips into Singapore- Japan range were fixed at usd 17,000/19,000 pd.
