### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route moved slightly lower, with Dampier and Port Hedland fixtures settling between USD 13.25/13.80 fio. In the Atlantic, long-haul business from Tubarao to Qingdao, including West Africa options, held firm levels between USD 35.50/37.00 fio. A long- haul departure from Seven Islands to Qingdao reached USD 41.50 fio, while Saldanha Bay to Qingdao achieved USD 27.10 fio. In the transatlantic market, a Ponta Da Madeira run to Rotterdam fixed at USD 17.25 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore-Japan and Japan options settled between USD 19,250/24,000 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, Japan, South East Asia and Singapore-Japan ranges fetched between USD 17,500/25,000 pd. Indonesian coal runs to South China, India and Singapore- Japan ranges achieved between USD 14,000/21,800 pd. In the Atlantic, long- haul departures from Continent via USEC to India and Japan/South Korea ranges obtained strong levels between USD 25,000/26,250 pd. On period, a Kamsarmax delivering in North China fixed for 4 to 6 months duration worldwide at USD 19,100 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, regional departures from South China and North China to ECI and North China range settled between USD 20,500/29,000 pd, while a regional clinker run within SE Asia fetched USD 32,000 pd. Out of USWC, a NoPac trip to Far East reached USD 30,000 pd and a long-haul departure from North China to West Africa made USD 26,000 pd. An East Australia petcoke run to East Med obtained USD 28,000 pd. From West Coast India, regional salt and clinker trips to ECI and China ranges fixed between USD 14,000/19,000 pd, while a South Africa coal run to ECI achieved USD 22,000 pd alongside a USD 220,000 ballast bonus. In the Continent, scrap to East Med and wheat to West Africa settled between USD 24,000/33,000 pd. In the Atlantic, US Gulf front-haul petcoke and grain trips to India and China ranges ranged between USD 27,000/34,500 pd, while transatlantic grain and wood pellet runs from USEC and US Gulf to Med and Continent ranges fixed between USD 24,000/28,750 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Atlantic, US Gulf and USEC grain, pellet and coal departures to Continent, Mediterranean and regional destinations fixed between USD 16,750/21,000 pd. Continent grain runs to West Africa achieved USD 26,000 pd, while a Black Sea urea trip to Continent made USD 12,250 pd. Out of West Africa, fertilizer and grain runs via ECSA to Brazil and West Med range settled between USD 14,000/15,000 pd. From NCSA, a transatlantic departure to Continent range reached USD 25,250 pd.
