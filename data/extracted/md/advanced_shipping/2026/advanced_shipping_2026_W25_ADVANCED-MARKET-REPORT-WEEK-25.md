### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route throughout Week 25 fluctuated between USD 10.35/11.70 fio levels. In the Atlantic, activity resurfaced with a transatlantic run fixed at USD 15.90 fio and a long-haul trip from Itaguai to Qingdao settling at USD 31.00 fio, while South African trips into China were noticed at USD 22.48 fio level.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

NoPac rounds with redelivery Singapore-Japan were reported at USD 20,750 pd, while a North China trip via WCCA to India fetched USD 20,000 pd. On Australian business, East Australia (Newcastle) to South China secured USD 21,000 pd plus USD 195,000 ballast bonus, prompt runs via Australia to India settled at USD 20,500 pd, and South China via Australia to China trips made USD 20,750 pd. North China rounds to Japan fixed between USD 19,750/24,000 pd, while South East Asian and Indian trips redelivery China, India, and Singapore-Japan ranges settled between USD 17,250/25,000 pd, including SE Asia rounds at USD 19,000 pd and a discounted iron ore run from Japan via North China to SE Asia at USD 10,000 pd. In the Atlantic, West India departures via ECSA to Singapore-Japan fetched USD 20,500 pd, ECSA trips to Continent/Med reached a firm USD 35,000 pd, Continent trips via USEC to India fetched USD 30,750 pd, and West Med via NCSA to Continent/Med settled at USD 21,000 pd. On period, short durations fixed between USD 18,250/18,400 pd, a Post Panamax secured USD 23,250 pd for 2 laden legs, and longer periods of 7/9 to 11/13 months fetched USD 17,800/18,000 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

Pacific activity saw a Japan trip via NoPac redelivery ECSA fixed at USD 16,500 pd, while SE Asia round trips were reported between USD 15,500/19,000 pd. In the Indian Ocean, East Coast India trips to China and West Coast India fixed between USD 11,000/20,000 pd depending on specs, West Coast India trips via South Africa to Far East fetched USD 17,750 pd, and East African departures via South Africa to China were noticed at a firm USD 24,000 pd. In the Atlantic, Continent prompt scrap runs from Rotterdam to East Med fixed at USD 25,750 pd, while West Med transatlantic runs were reported between USD 10,000/13,500 pd. The US Gulf remained firm, with trips to Caribs yielding USD 25,500 pd and a front-haul trip to Singapore- Japan at a strong USD 32,000 pd. Further south, outbound ECSA trips to Continent secured USD 33,000 pd, while runs to Singapore-Japan settled at USD 30,000 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Far East activity saw Japan trips to SE Asia at USD 16,000 pd, while NoPac business included WCCA trips to North China and Japan at USD 17,000 pd. An East Med trip to Continent fetched USD 11,000 pd. Moving to the Americas, ECSA coastal and Caribbean runs fixed between USD 17,000/20,000 pd, coastal ECSA trips via Recalada settled at USD 18,500 pd, trips to US Gulf fetched USD 24,000 pd, and Recalada to WCSA secured USD 25,000 pd. Additionally, ECSA to Med trips were reported at USD 24,000 pd, and a long trip to Singapore-Japan settled at USD 19,500 pd. On period, a Handysize fixed at USD 14,250 pd for 6/8 months redelivery Far East.
