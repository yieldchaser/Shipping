### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates West Australia/China fluctuated, with Dampier and Port Hedland to Qingdao settling between USD 12.45/13.50 fio. In the Atlantic, Tubarao to Qingdao routes achieved USD 34.85 fio, while runs from Saldanha Bay and Nouadhibou to Qingdao secured USD 23.49 fio and USD 35.75 fio respectively. Transatlantic runs from Narvik to Rotterdam made USD 10.90 fio, and Tubarao to Eren fetched USD 29.85 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

Ιn the Pacific, NoPac rounds or multi-laden legs trips from Japan, North China and South China with Singapore/Japan or worldwide redelivery secured between USD 16,500/20,000 pd. On Australian business, departures via Australia settled between USD 14,900/20,000 pd to Singapore/Japan and USD 14,750/21,000 pd to China/India ranges. Indonesian coal runs from South China and South East Asia via Indonesia saw redelivery to South China between USD 11,500/22,000 pd, with further runs to South Korea, Singapore/Japan, and Japan ranges settling between USD 14,000/19,000 pd. Further activity in the East included trips from South China and South East Asia via ECSA to Singapore/Japan range settling between USD 17,000/22,000 pd. In the Atlantic, Continent departures fixed via US Gulf to Continent at USD 25,000 pd. On period, Kamsarmaxes achieved USD 16,500 pd for 5/7 months and USD 21,000 pd for 6/8 months, while another secured 97% of BPI for 12 months.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, NoPac rounds and local trips from North China with South East Asia and Singapore/Japan redelivery secured USD 16,000/22,000 pd. For longer runs from North China, a trip to the Med reached USD 26,000 pd, a run to West Africa achieved USD 23,000 pd and a trip to the US Gulf fixed at USD 14,500 pd. From South China and South East Asia, coal and nickel ore runs to China and South East Asia fetched USD 15,000/18,000 pd, while a barites trip from South China to the US Gulf achieved USD 15,500 pd. From India, a trip to China fixed at USD 17,500 pd, while PG and India departures to local ranges settled between USD 15,500/22,750 pd. In the Atlantic, US Gulf departures to NCSA and ECSA secured USD 25,000/27,000 pd, a USEC run to East Med made USD 31,500 pd and ECSA trips to NCSA fetched USD 26,000 pd. ECSA backhauls to Continent, India and Singapore/Japan ranges achieved USD 28,000/39,000 pd. From the Black Sea/Med, a Black Sea run to South East Asia settled at USD 28,000 pd and an East Med trip via Black Sea to South Korea made USD 21,000 pd. From South and East Africa, runs to China and Far East ranges obtained USD 18,000/24,000 pd. On period, an Ultramax fixed from South East Asia for 4/6 months duration at USD 22,000 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Atlantic, ECSA departures secured between USD 18,500/27,000 pd for grain trips to NCSA, US Gulf and WCSA ranges. Out of the US Gulf, wood pellets runs settled at USD 22,000 pd to the Continent and USD 23,000 pd to Japan, while a trip to China achieved USD 21,750 pd. From the Continent, a prompt grain run to West Africa achieved USD 14,750 pd.
