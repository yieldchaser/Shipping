### Bulkers Market Commentary

E Nomikos have committed the Kamsarmax "Epiphania" 80/2012 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 02/2027) at USD 17,5 mill. Remind you that earlier this month, the "Athina Carras" 82/2012 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 02/2027) was sold at the same price. The Ultramax "Only You" 60/2017 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 01/2027) of Alpha Omega Ship Management was committed to Danish interests at USD 28,5 mill. Note that the one- year-younger "Jetstream" 34/2012 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 05/2027) was sold a month ago at USD 13,5 mill.
