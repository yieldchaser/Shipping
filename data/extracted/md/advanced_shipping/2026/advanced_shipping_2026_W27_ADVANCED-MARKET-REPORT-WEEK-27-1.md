### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route fluctuated between USD 10.10/11.60 fio levels. In the Atlantic, transatlantic routes from Tubarao to Qingdao secured between USD 28.00/28.50 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac rounds fixed between USD 13,350/18,500 pd for Japan and North China departures with Singapore/Japan and China redelivery. On Australian business, trips via Australia settled at USD 15,500 pd from South China to China, and USD 18,500 pd from Japan to India. Indonesian coal runs from South China saw redelivery to South China between USD 10,500/12,000 pd and to South East Asia at USD 11,000/15,000 pd, while departures from South East Asia via Indonesia fixed between USD 13,500 pd (plus USD 120,000 ballast bonus) to South China, USD 18,000 pd to Singapore/Japan, and USD 20,500 pd to West Coast India. Further South East Asian departures via ECSA to Singapore/Japan range fixed between USD 16,000/24,000 pd. From India, Persian Gulf and West Coast India departures via PG or ECSA fixed between USD 18,250/30,000 pd to India and Singapore/Japan, while East Coast India trips via ECSA achieved USD 18,500 pd to South East Asia, USD 24,000 pd to Far East, and between USD 20,500/24,500 pd to Singapore/Japan. Local East Coast India iron ore runs via East Coast India to West Coast India secured a firm USD 35,500 pd. In the Atlantic, an ECSA outbound trip to Continent/Med made USD 33,750 pd, while Continent departures fixed via US Gulf at USD 32,000 pd to Singapore/Japan and via NCSA at USD 32,750 pd to China. On period, short duration business included a Kamsarmax fixing 2/3 laden legs at USD 19,750 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, a North China departure fixed at USD 22,000 pd for a trip to West Africa. From South China, a trip via Australia with logs to West Coast India settled at USD 20,000 pd. Indonesian coal and clinker runs from South East Asia secured USD 12,500/14,000 pd to China, USD 18,500 pd to South East Asia, USD 20,000 pd to East Coast India, USD 21,000 pd to West Coast India, and USD 22,500/23,000 pd to East Coast India/ECI ranges. From East Coast India, prompt trips fixed at USD 21,500 pd to West Coast India and USD 15,000 pd for a run via South East Asia with West Coast India redelivery. From the Americas, an East Coast South America grain run to Continent/Med achieved USD 28,500 pd, while a local West Coast South America trip secured USD 20,000 pd. On period, an Ultramax fixed from South China for 12 months duration at 106% of the BSI index.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Far East, South East Asia prompt runs via Japan or local South East Asia routes fixed between USD 11,000/17,000 pd with South East Asia, Malaysia, and Thailand redelivery options. In the Med, West Med departures settled at USD 12,750 pd to Continent and USD 10,000 pd to ECSA. Continent trips to West Africa made USD 16,000 pd. In the Americas, ECSA fixtures secured USD 24,000 pd to Med and USD 26,000 pd to West Africa.
