### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds with redelivery in the Singapore-Japan range were reported at usd 18,000/21,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into South East Asia and India were fixed at levels ranging from usd 17,500/23,000 pd. South East Asia trips via Indonesia into South China were reported around usd 20,000/23,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips into China were fixed at usd 18,000/19,000 pd. Continental trips via US Gulf were reported at usd 24,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into the Singapore-Japan range and continent-Mediterranean were fixed between usd 21,000/28,000 pd. Additionally, North Coast American trips into the Singapore-Japan range were fixed in a range of usd 20,000/21,000 pd. On the period front, Kamsarmaxes were reported fixed at usd 19,000/20,000 pd for 2 laden legs.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

Trips from East Coast India into India were fixed at usd 18,000/20,000 pd, while East Coast Indian trips into Continent were reported at usd 16,000/18,000 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Australian trips into the China were fixed at usd 20,000/21,000 pd. Japan trips into South East Asia were fixed at usd 13,000/14,000 pd.
