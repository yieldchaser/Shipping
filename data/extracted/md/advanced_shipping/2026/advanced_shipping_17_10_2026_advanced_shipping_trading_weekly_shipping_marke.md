### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route softened further, with Dampier to Qingdao fixtures settling between USD 11.25/13.40 fio. In the Atlantic, long-haul business from Tubarao to Qingdao, including West Africa options, fixed between USD 30.00/31.50 fio. Out of South Africa, Saldanha Bay departures to Qingdao achieved between USD 23.25/24.08 fio, while a TRMT run to Qingdao made USD 9.85 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore-Japan and West Coast India options settled between USD 20,000/23,500 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, Japan, India and Singapore-Japan ranges fetched between USD 19,000/27,000 pd. Indonesian coal runs to South China, China and Japan ranges achieved between USD 16,000/23,500 pd. From West Coast India and East Coast India, trips via South Africa and ECSA to India and Singapore-Japan ranges fixed between USD 25,500/28,750 pd. In the Atlantic, a Continent trip via USG back to Continent/Med range made USD 21,500 pd, an East Med run via NCSA achieved USD 27,250 pd, and a Continent departure via NCSA to Singapore-Japan reached USD 32,000 pd. On period, fixtures for 2 laden legs up to 7 months duration were secured between USD 19,000/27,000 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, Indonesian coal runs to South East Asia and ECI settled between USD 23,000/24,000 pd and USD 15,250 pd respectively. NoPac trips from North China and Japan to South East Asia fetched USD 25,000 pd, while South China clinker runs to ECI achieved strong levels between USD 30,000/31,000 pd. A long-haul departure from North China to West Africa fixed at USD 24,000 pd. Out of the Persian Gulf, a limestone trip to India obtained USD 16,500 pd, while a South Africa coal run to ECI reached USD 26,000 pd alongside a USD 260,000 ballast bonus. In the Mediterranean, salt to US Gulf and clinker to West Africa made USD 14,500 pd and USD 22,000 pd respectively. In the Atlantic, a US Gulf front-haul petcoke trip to China secured USD 34,000 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Pacific, regional departures from North China to South East Asia fixed at USD 19,000 pd, while a South East Asia coal run to Far East settled at USD 12,750 pd. A trip from South East Asia via Australia to China made USD 16,000 pd and a steel run from East Coast India to Continent obtained USD 13,500 pd. In the Atlantic, a US Gulf grain run to WCCA achieved USD 17,500 pd, an ECSA departure to Continent fixed at USD 15,000 pd alongside a USD 150,000 ballast bonus and an East Med trip to NCSA reached USD 11,000 pd.
