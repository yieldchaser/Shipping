### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds with redelivery in the Singapore-Japan range were reported at usd 18,000/21,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into South East Asia and India were fixed at levels ranging from usd 20,000/24,000 pd. South East Asia trips via Indonesia into India and South East Asia were reported around usd 21,000/23,000 pd. South China trips into India and Singapore-Japan range were fixed at usd 18,000/20,000 pd. North China trips via Russian Far East into India were reported at usd 20,500 pd. East Coast South American trips into the Singapore-Japan range were fixed between usd 18,000/22,000 pd. Additionally, North Coast American trips into the Singapore-Japan range were fixed in a range of usd 23,000/25,000 pd. On the period front, Kamsarmaxes were reported fixed at usd 21,850 pd for 2 or 3 laden legs.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

Trips from East Coast India into China were fixed at usd 18,500 pd, while South Africa trips into South East Asia were reported at usd 16,500 pd with a ballast bonus of usd 165,000. On the period front, an Ultramax was reported fixed at usd 19,250 pd for 4/6 months.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Atlantic, Continent trips into the US Gulf were fixed at usd 14,000 pd, while trips from the Continent into the Mediterranean were reported at usd 17,000 pd. East Mediterranean trips into West Africa were fixed at usd 12,000 pd.
