### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route fluctuated between USD 10.65/11.65 fio. From South Africa, trips to South Korea and China settled at USD 20.50/22.20 fio. In the Atlantic, a Nouadhibou to Qingdao run fixed at USD 34.35 fio, while Tubarao to Qingdao routes secured between USD 32.40/32.95 fio and a Tubarao to Eren run at USD 26.50 fio. Additionally, a long-haul trip from Port Cartier to Kakogawa achieved USD 43.35 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

NoPac rounds with redelivery in the Singapore-Japan range fixed between USD 14,000/20,000 pd. On Australian business, North China trips via Australia settled at USD 14,100 pd to South Korea and USD 16,000 pd to South China, while a Japan trip to South Korea fetched USD 12,900 pd and a run to Singapore-Japan secured USD 18,000 pd. Indonesian coal runs from South China or Hong Kong via Indonesia saw redelivery to South China at USD 12,000 pd and to South East Asia or India at USD 15,000 pd. South East Asia trips via Indonesia fixed at USD 13,000/16,500 pd to South Korea and South China, while a South China trip with South East Asia redelivery made USD 11,000 pd. Further South East Asia trips via ECSA to Singapore-Japan settled at USD 20,000/22,750 pd. From India, East and West Coast India departures via ECSA to the Singapore-Japan range fixed at USD 19,650/23,000 pd, while an East Coast India trip via ECSA to the Far East fetched USD 24,500 pd. In the Atlantic, ECSA front-hauls to Singapore-Japan reached USD 20,500 pd plus a USD 1,050,000 ballast bonus, ECSA outbound trips to Continent/Med settled at USD 32,000/34,000 pd and a Continent trip via France to China fixed at USD 27,500 pd. On period, a Kamsarmax secured a 10/12 months duration at 115% of the P5TC index.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, a North China departure via NoPac to Singapore-Japan secured USD 20,250 pd, while a North China trip carrying slag to East Coast India fixed at USD 24,000 pd. Outbound business from North China/Japan saw backhaul trips to the US Gulf fetching USD 16,000/17,000 pd, a run to the Caribs making USD 22,000 pd and long-haul trips to West Africa settling at USD 24,250/25,500 pd, while a Japan departure fixed at USD 25,000 pd for West Africa redelivery. Further South East Asian business included a trip via China with East Coast India redelivery at USD 18,000 pd and a trip via China to China at USD 13,500 pd. From East Coast India, trips via South East Asia made USD 16,000 pd to China and USD 21,000 pd to West Coast India. In the West Med/Continent, a West Med run via West Africa to India fixed at USD 20,000 pd, a Continent departure to India secured USD 24,500 pd and a Continent prompt scrap run to East Med achieved USD 27,000 pd. From South Africa, a trip to the Continent settled at USD 21,000 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Far East, a South East Asia prompt run via West Coast India with South East Asia redelivery fixed at USD 17,000 pd. In the Mediterranean, a West Med departure to the Continent settled at USD 12,750 pd, while a West Med run to ECSA fetched USD 10,000 pd. Moving to the Americas, ECSA front- haul activity saw an ECSA grain trip to the Med securing USD 24,000 pd, while an ECSA fixture to West Africa reached USD 26,000 pd.
