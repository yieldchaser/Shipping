### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route strengthened significantly, with Port Hedland and Dampier to Qingdao fixtures settling between USD 15.25/18.00 fio. In the Atlantic, long-haul departures from Tubarao to Qingdao, including West Africa options, achieved between USD 38.50/41.00 fio, alongside a Seven Islands run reaching USD 46.80 fio. Transatlantic activity saw runs from PDM and Port Cartier to Ijmuiden and Fos fixing between USD 20.00/21.00 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac trips with India and Singapore-Japan options settled between USD 16,750/23,750 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China, Japan and South East Asia ranges fetched between USD 18,000/24,500 pd. Indonesian coal runs to South China and Singapore-Japan achieved between USD 14,000/24,000 pd. From Persian Gulf, West Coast India and East Coast India, trips via South Africa to India obtained between USD 22,500/27,000 pd, while trips via ECSA to Far East destinations fixed between USD 19,000/24,500 pd. In the Atlantic, a NCSA departure via USEC to Continent range reached USD 23,000 pd. On period, short to medium-term fixtures for 5 to 12 months duration settled between USD 18,750/23,000 pd, while a longer 24 months period was secured at USD 19,500 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, an Indonesian coal run to Thailand achieved USD 22,000 pd, while a clinker trip from South China to Chittagong reached USD 28,000 pd and a cement trip to East Africa fetched USD 23,000 pd. Out of West Coast India, regional runs to West Coast India and Bangladesh fixed between USD 14,500/20,000 pd, alongside coastal India trips from East Coast India obtaining USD 21,000 pd, while a long-haul trip to China settled at USD 11,000 pd. Out of West Africa, long-haul ore and agricultural runs to China were agreed between USD 23,500/27,000 pd. From South Africa, departures to India and China secured between USD 18,000/24,000 pd, alongside ballast bonuses from USD 180,000 to USD 270,000. In the Atlantic, ECSA front-haul trips to South East Asia and Singapore-Japan range obtained between USD 19,000/19,500 pd, with ballast bonuses between USD 900,000 and USD 950,000. Transatlantic grain and woodpellet runs from USEC and US Gulf reached USD 30,000 pd and USD 27,500 pd respectively, while Continent departures to the Mediterranean range settled between USD 22,000/22,500 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Pacific, East Australia runs to South East Asia fetched between USD 19,500/20,000 pd, a USWC trip via NoPac to Singapore-Japan obtained USD 17,000 pd and a WCI departure to ECI settled at USD 15,000 pd. Out of South Africa, front-haul runs to Far East destinations achieved between USD 13,850/15,000 pd, a trip to WCI made USD 13,500 pd and a transatlantic run to Continent secured USD 16,000 pd. In the Atlantic, departures from USEC and USG to Continent and EC Mexico options fixed at USD 14,000 pd and USD 13,750 pd respectively.
