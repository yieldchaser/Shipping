### Bulkers Market Commentary

Moreover, Pacific Basin Shipping committed their "Swan River" 56/2005 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 07/2025, Scrubber fitted) at USD 10 mill. Remind you that back in March, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Strange Attractor" 55/2006 Mitsui, Japan (SS 10/2026, DD 07/2025, Scrubber fitted) was sold at USD 9,5 mill.
