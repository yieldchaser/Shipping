### Bulkers Market Commentary

OceanPal have committed their Capesize "Salt Lake City" 171/2005 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 09/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 16,2 mill. For reference, the one-year-older "Star Triumph" 176/2004 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold back in September at USD 20 mill. The Panamax "Fame" 75/2004 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) of Target Marine was committed at USD 8,6 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Argolis" 76/2005 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold recently at USD 8,2 mill. On the Supramax sector, Polforce Shipping committed their "Papayiannis III" 58/2010 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) at mid-high USD 11 mill. Remind you that back in September, the one-year-younger "Kibali" 57/2011 Stx, Korea (SS/DD 08/2026, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 16,7 mill. Furthermore, Sotirakou S committed their "Protector St. Raphael" 56/2010 Xiamen, China (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) at USD 11 mill, while the one- year-younger "Jag Rishi" 56/2011 Cosco, China (SS/DD 03/2026, BWTS fitted) of Great Eastern Shipping was committed to Chinese interests at high USD 11 mill. In addition, Tolani Group committed their "Prabhu Mihikaa" 55/2005 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) to Vietnamese interests at low USD 10 mill. Note that a month ago, the "SSI Nemesis" 56/2005 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 10,9 mill. Precious Shipping have committed the Handysize "Rojarek Naree" 29/2005 Shikoku, Japan (SS 10/2029, DD 09/2027, BWTS fitted) at USD 8,5 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Ken Toku" 29/2005 Shikoku, Japan (SS/DD 08/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in October at the same price. Moreover, Zodiac Maritime committed their "Woodgate" 28/2011 I-S, Japan (SS/DD 05/2026, BWTS fitted) to Vietnamese interests at USD 10,5 mill, while the one-year-younger "POS Oceania" 28/2012 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 07/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in October at USD 10,9 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 6,000/7,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 4,000/6,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 4,000/6,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 3,000/4,000 pd. Arabian Gulf trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 9,000/11,000 pd. West African trips via US Gulf into Continent were fixed around usd 7,000/8,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 14,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 400K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 13,000 pd for 5/7 months employment basis delivery China. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 14,100 pd basis delivery China for 1 year employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 6,000/8,000 pd. South East Asian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 2,000/5,000 pd. Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 4,000/6,000 pd. Indian coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. South African trips into China were fixed around usd 10,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 60K/100K. South African trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 6,000/7,000 pd. West African trips into China were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips from Japan into West Coast South America were fixed around usd 7,000/8,000 pd. South East Asian trips into Australia were reported at the level of usd 3,000/5,000 pd. Indian Coastal trips were fixed around usd 6,000 pd. West African trips were reported at the level of usd 4,000/5,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 6,000/8,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
