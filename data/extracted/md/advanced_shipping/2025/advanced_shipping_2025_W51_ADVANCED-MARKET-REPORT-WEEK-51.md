### Bulkers Market Commentary

Zodiac Maritime have committed their Newcastlemax "Cape Merlin" 206/2005 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 02/2026) to Chinese interests at USD 23,5 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Seacon Africa" 206/2006 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 06/2026) was sold at USD 22,7 mill. Following the sale of the Kamsarmax "Theresa Hebei" 81/2012 Sainty, China (SS/DD 03/2027) in November at USD 15,5 mill, Raffles Shipping have now committed the one-year-younger sister vessel "Theresa Hainan" 81/2013 Sainty, China (SS/DD 05/2028) at low-mid USD 16 mill. Oasis Ship Management have committed the Panamax "The Giver" 75/2006 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 03/2030, DD 06/2028) to Chinese interests at USD 11,2 mill. Note that the sister vessel "Yasa Unity" 75/2006 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 04/2026) was sold in November at mid USD 9 mill. On the Supramax sector, LA Maritime committed the "Captain Karam" 56/2006 Mitsui, Japan (SS 05/2029, DD 05/2027) to Chinese interests at USD 11,25 mill. Remind you that the one-year-younger sister vessel "Icarius" 56/2007 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 04/2027) was sold back in October at USD 11,75 mill. Moreover, Precious Shipping committed their "Wariya Naree" 53/2011 Hindustan, India (SS/DD 02/2026) at USD 9,85 mill, while the one- year-older sister vessel "Warisa Naree" 53/2010 Hindustan, India (SS 04/2030, DD 01/2028) was sold earlier this month at USD 9,4 mill. Empire Bulkers have committed the Handysize "Aphrodite M" 34/2011 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 10/2026) to undisclosed interests at USD 12,5 mill. For comparison, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Union Groove" 35/2012 Spp, Korea (SS/DD 01/2027) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 13,6 mill.
