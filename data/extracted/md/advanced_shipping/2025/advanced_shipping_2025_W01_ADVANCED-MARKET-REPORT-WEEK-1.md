## Page 1

### Bulkers Market Commentary

Minerva Marine have committed their Newcastlemax "Sikamia" 207/2008 Universal, Japan (SS 08/2028, DD 08/2026, Scrubber fitted) to Chinese interests at USD 29 mill. For comparison, the two-years-younger sister vessel "Amber Horizon" 207/2010 Universal, Japan (SS 03/2028, DD 03/2026) was sold in November at USD 33 mill. The Kamsarmax "Bettys Dream" 82/2008 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) of Zihni Shipping & Trading was committed to Chinese interests at USD 12,8 mill. Note that back in September, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Lily Atlantic" 82/2009 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 10/2029, DD 08/2027, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 16,3 mill. In addition, the "Ru Meng Ling" 81/2010 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) was committed to Greek interests at USD 15,4 mill. Tokai Shipping have committed their Handysize "Momo Glory" 28/2014 I-S, Japan (SS 01/2029, DD 01/2027, BWTS fitted) to Greek interests at USD 12 mill. Remind you that back in September, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Globe Explorer" 28/2015 I-S, Japan (SS/DD 02/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold at USD 14,2 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The MR2 "Kyra" 47/2006 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 01/2026, BWTS fitted) was committed to undisclosed interests at USD 17,1 mill, while the one-year- younger "Chiba" 45/2007 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 04/2025, BWTS fitted) of SR Navigation was committed at USD 17 mill. For reference, the "Fos Power" 47/2007 Onomichi, Japan (SS 04/2027, DD 12/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold back in September at USD 24 mill.
