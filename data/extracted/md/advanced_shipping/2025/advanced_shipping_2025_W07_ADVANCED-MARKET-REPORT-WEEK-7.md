### Bulkers Market Commentary

Zodiac Maritime have committed their Capesize "Cape Heron" 177/2005 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 02/2025) & "Cape Hawk" 176/2006 Namura & Sasebo, Japan (SS 10/2026, DD 03/2025) to Chinese interests at USD 30 mill enbloc. Note that a week ago, the "Cape Friendship" 185/2005 Kawasaki, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025) was sold at USD 16,1 mill. Diana Shipping announced the sale of their Post Panamax "Alcmene" 93/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS 01/2030, DD 12/2027, Scrubber fitted) to undisclosed interests at USD 11,9 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Pisces First" 93/2010 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 12/2025) was sold back in November at high USD 12 mill. Following the acquisition of the Ultramax "Beechgate" 63/2019 Iwagi, Japan (SS 03/2029, DD 04/2027) at USD 35,6 mill, clients of Meghna Group have now purchased the one-year-younger sister vessel "Nord Magellan" 63/2020 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025) from Tokei Kaiun at a price of low- mid USD 29 mill. Moreover, the "Sagar Kanta" 60/2013 Oshima, Japan (SS 03/2028, DD 06/2026) was committed to Indonesian interests at USD 19 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the "Omishima Island" 61/2013 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 09/2025) was sold at mid-high USD 19 mill. On the Supramax sector, the "Sagar Shakti" 58/2012 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 04/2027, DD 09/2025) was committed to undisclosed interests at high USD 14 mill. For reference, the sister vessel "Louisianna Mama" 58/2012 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 07/2025) was sold back in September at USD 19 mill. Polsteam have committed their Handysize "Mazury" 38/2005 Tianjin, China (SS/DD 04/2025, Ice Class 1C) to Far Eastern buyers at region USD 6 mill, while the sister vessel "Kujawy" 38/2005 Tianjin, China (SS/DD 01/2025, Ice Class 1C) was sold back in October at region USD 8 mill. Furthermore, IMECS committed their "Nord Nanami" 38/2012 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 06/2025) at USD 13,5 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older sister vessel "Blue Dragon" 38/2011 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 01/2025) was sold back in October at low USD 15 mill.
