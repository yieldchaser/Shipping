### Bulkers Market Commentary

Chinese owners have committed their Kamsarmax "ASL Venus" 82/2011 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 01/2026) to Greek interests at USD 14,4 mill. Remind you that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-older sister vessel "Key Action" 82/2010 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS 04/2030, DD 02/2028) was sold at USD 15,1 mill. Moreover, Nissen Kaiun committed the "AOM Sophie II" 81/2020 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 03/2030, DD 02/2028, Scrubber fitted) to Greek interests also at region USD 31,5 mill. For reference, the "Sentosa Spirit" 81/2020 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 02/2025) was sold back in February at USD 32 mill. The Supramax "Marigoula" 58/2013 Yangzhou, China (SS 10/2027, DD 11/2025) of JME Navigation was committed at USD 13,5 mill, while the two- years-older "Ingwar Selmer" 58/2011 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 03/2026) was sold a week ago at USD 11,7 mill. In addition, the "SFL Hudson" 56/2009 Jiangsu, China (SS 08/2029, DD 09/2027) was committed to Chinese interests at USD 11,3 mill. Note that a month ago, the two-years-younger sister vessel "Minoas" 56/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 04/2026) was sold at high USD 11 mill. On the Handysize sector, Chellaram Shipping committed their "Darya Ganga" 36/2012 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 02/2027) to undisclosed interests at USD 14,3 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older sister vessel "Stradion" 36/2011 Hyundai Mipo, Korea (SS/DD 01/2026) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 13,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 9,000/11,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/13,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Indian trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 18,000/21,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 20,000/22,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/600K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed around usd 12,500 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery China. Also, a Panamax was fixed on a period at the usd 10,100 pd basis delivery China for 12/14 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 9,000/11,000 pd. Trips from Japan into South East Asia were fixed around usd 8,000/11,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Trips from Red Sea into Far East were fixed around usd 10,000/12,000 pd. South African trips into Far East were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 130K/140K. An Ultramax was fixed on a period at the usd 13,000 pd basis delivery South East Asia for 1 year employment.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 9,000/11,000 pd. South African trips into China were fixed around usd 11,000/12,000 pd. South African trips into Continent-Mediterranean range were reported at the level of usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 7,000/9,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via Black Sea into Baltic were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
