### Bulkers Market Commentary

Mitsubishi Ore Transport have committed the Kamsarmax "Santa Graciela" 82/2013 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 03/2028, DD 03/2026) to Singaporean interests at high USD 18 mill. Remind you that back in March, the one-year-older sister vessel "Energy Hope" 82/2012 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 02/2027) was sold at USD 17 mill. Furthermore, the "Explorer Asia" 81/2016 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 01/2026) & "Explorer Oceania" 81/2015 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 12/2025) from Caravel Group were committed to Chinese interests at USD 41 mill enbloc. Note that the "Sea Marathon" 81/2015 Qingdao, China (SS/DD 09/2025) was sold in April at USD 18,4 mill. Kobe Shipping have committed the Handysize "Aries Sakura" 39/2020 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS 06/2030, DD 05/2028) to Greek interests at USD 25,5 mill. For reference, the OHBS "Nord Abidjan" 38/2020 Minaminippon, Japan (SS 01/2030, DD 02/2028) was sold in April at USD 25,5 mill. In addition, the "CS Crystal" 30/2010 Tsuji, China (SS/DD 06/2025) was committed to Korean interests at USD 10 mill, while the "Vantage Reef" 29/2010 Nantong, China (SS/DD 06/2025) was sold in April at USD 8,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Clients of Yasa Tankers have acquired the VLCC "C. Spirit" 314/2013 Hyundai, Korea (SS 04/2028, DD 01/2026, Scrubber fitted) from SK Shipping at low USD 67 mill. Moreover, New Shipping committed their "New Tinos" 300/2003 Universal, Japan (SS 04/2030, DD passed) to undisclosed interests at USD 37 mill. Remind you that back in May, same owners sold the sister vessel "New Naxos" 300/2003 Universal, Japan (SS 01/2027, DD 04/2025) at USD 33 mill. Teekay Corp have committed the Suezmax "Montreal Spirit" 150/2006 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 05/2026) to European interests at region USD 30 mill. Note that the two-years-older sister vessel "Nordic Castor" 150/2004 Universal, Japan (SS 07/2029, DD 02/2028) was sold in May at USD 23 mill. Teekay Corp have also committed the LR2 "Limerick Spirit" 105/2007 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 02/2027) to Greek interests at USD 27,8 mill. For reference, the two-years-younger Aframax "P. Sophia" 105/2009 Hyundai, Korea (SS 09/2029, DD 11/2027) was sold in April at USD 36 mill. The MR2 "Nord Oceania" 50/2018 Onomichi, Japan (SS 01/2028, DD 12/2025) of Norden was committed at USD 37 mill. For comparison, the "Nord Joy" & "Nord Jewel" 49/2018 JMU, Japan (SS 11/2028, DD 12/2026 & SS 01/2028, DD 11/2027) were sold a month ago at USD 37 mill each.
