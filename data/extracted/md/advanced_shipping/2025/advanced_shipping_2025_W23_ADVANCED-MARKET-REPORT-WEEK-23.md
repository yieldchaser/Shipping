### Bulkers Market Commentary

Nissen Kaiun have committed their Mini Cape "Bastions" 119/2011 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 01/2026, Scrubber fitted) at region USD 16,5 mill. For comparison, the one-year-older sister vessel "Brilliant Jupiter" 119/2010 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 07/2025) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 16 mill. The Kamsarmax "Shun Fu Da" 82/2006 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 06/2026) of Eastern Fortune Shipping was committed to Indian interests at USD 11,4 mill. Note that back in April, the two-years-younger sister vessel "Seaduty" 82/2008 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 04/2026, DD 11/2025) was sold at USD 14,25 mill. Pavimar have committed their Panamax "Toro" 76/2008 Imabari, Japan (SS 01/2026, DD 10/2025) at USD 12,2 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-older sister vessel "Aeolian Grace" 76/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS 07/2027, DD 11/2025) was sold at USD 11,2 mill. On the Supramax sector, Oskar Wehr committed their "Ingwar Selmer" 58/2011 Yangzhou, China (SS/DD 03/2026) at USD 11,7 mill, while the "Incredible Blue" 57/2011 Taizhou, China (SS/DD 10/2026) from Load Line Marine was committed at high USD 11 mill. Moreover, the "Annita" 53/2005 New Century, China (SS 11/2027, DD 10/2025) of Z&G Halcoussis was committed at USD 8,5 mill, the same price achieved a couple of weeks ago for the "Spar Virgo" 53/2005 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 06/2025). The Handysize "Sider Olympia" 38/2013 Imabari, Japan (SS 06/2027, DD 09/2025, Semi boxed) was committed to Greek interests at region USD 15,75 mill. For reference, the two-years-younger "Unity Star" 37/2015 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 08/2025, Semi boxed) was sold a month ago at USD 18,25 mill. In addition, ID Management committed the "ID Pioneer" 35/2012 Taizhou, China (SS 11/2027, DD 11/2025) to Chinese interests at USD 10,1 mill. Note that the "Celeste" 34/2012 Zhejiang, China (SS/DD 02/2027) was sold in May at USD 11 mill.
