### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout round trips on the same route were reported around usd 22,000/23,000 pd levels. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 14,000/16,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/16,000 pd. Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Indian trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 14,000/17,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/700K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 14,850 pd for 4/6 months employment basis delivery China. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 15,500 pd basis delivery China for 1 year employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the level of usd 9,000/12,000 pd. Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 6,000/8,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America were fixed around usd 17,000/18,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips from Caribbean into Continent were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. South American trips into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. Also trips from South America into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. South American trips into Far East were fixed around usd 13,000/14,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
