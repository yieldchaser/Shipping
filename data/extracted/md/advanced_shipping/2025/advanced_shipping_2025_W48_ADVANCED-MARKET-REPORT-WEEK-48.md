### Bulkers Market Commentary

NYK Line have committed the Mini Cape "NBA Rembrandt" 107/2012 Oshima, Japan (SS 10/2030, DD 10/2028) to undisclosed interests at USD 18,7 mill. Remind you that back in September, same owners sold the one- year-older sister vessel "NBA Rubens" 107/2011 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 01/2026) at USD 15 mill. Following the sale of the Ultramax "Beauty Lily" 63/2015 CSI, China (SS 01/2030, DD 01/2028) a month ago at USD 22,25 mill, the sister vessel "Beauty Jasmine" 63/2015 CSI, China (SS 04/2030, DD 03/2028) is now committed at USD 23 mill. On the Supramax sector, Vega Bulk committed the "Vega Stetind" 55/2008 Oshima, Japan (SS 02/2028, DD 12/2025) to Chinese interests at USD 11 mill. Remind you that the one-year-older "Icarius" 55/2007 Mitsui, Japan (SS/DD 04/2027) was sold in October at USD 11,75 mill. The Handysize "Thomas Selmer" 35/2011 Samjin, China (SS/DD 04/2026) was committed at USD 10,2 mill. Note that earlier this month, the "Pacific Ocean" 36/2011 Samjin, China (SS/DD 05/2026) was sold at USD 10,5 mill. Moreover, the "Trawind Dolphin" 33/2012 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS/DD 07/2027, OHBS) was committed at USD 13,8 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Trawind Roc" 33/2012 Shin Kurushima, Japan (SS/DD 08/2027, OHBS) was sold a couple of months ago at USD 13,6 mill. In addition, the "Yangtze Ambition" 32/2011 Jiangmen, China (SS/DD 04/2026) was sold at USD 9 mill, while the one-year-younger sister vessel "Yangtze Grace" 32/2012 Jiangmen, China (SS/DD 06/2027) was committed a week ago at USD 9,8 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

The VLCC "Oceanic Fortune" 320/2010 Hyundai, Korea (SS 01/2030, DD 04/2028, Scrubber fitted) was committed to Chinese interests at USD 57 mill. For reference, the one-year-younger Japanese built "FPMC C Knight" 301/2011 IHI, Japan (SS/DD 02/2026, Scrubber fitted) was sold in September at USD 55 mill. CMB NV committed their Suezmax "Sapphira" 149/2008 Universal, Japan (SS 07/2028, DD 04/2026) to undisclosed interests at USD 37 mill. Note that the sister vessel "Oceanus" 150/2008 Universal, Japan (SS 04/2028, DD 07/2026, Scrubber fitted) was sold in October at USD 38 mill. The MR2 sisters "PS Dream" & "PS Queen" 51/2006 Stx, Korea (SS 03/2030, DD 03/2028 both) were committed at USD 14 mill each, while the one-year- younger sister vessel "Star Eagle" 51/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 10/2027, DD 12/2025) was committed at mid-high USD 13 mill. In addition, the "Kyra" 47/2006 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 01/2026) was committed at USD 11,5 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-older "Jag Pooja" 48/2005 Iwagi, Japan (SS 08/2028, DD 08/2026, Ice Class II) was sold at high USD 10 mill.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 16,000/18,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 17,000/19,000 pd range. Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips via East Coast South America into Red Sea were fixed around usd 12,000/14,000 pd. East Coast Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. West African trips via North Coast South America into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were reported at the level of usd 25,000/26,000 pd. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 15,750 pd for 6/8 months employment basis delivery South China. Also, a Panamax was fixed on a period at the usd 15,000 pd basis delivery South China for 4/6 months employment.

West Coast India trips into South East Asia were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Arabian Gulf trips into East Coast India were fixed around usd 18,000/21,000 pd. West African trips into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize
