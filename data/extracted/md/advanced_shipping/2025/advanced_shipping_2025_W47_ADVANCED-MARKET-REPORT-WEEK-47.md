### Bulkers Market Commentary

Following the sale of the Newcastlemax "Bulk Sao Paulo" 208/2020 New Times, China (SS 06/2030, DD 06/2028, Scrubber fitted) a month ago at USD 72,75 mill, 2020 Bulkers have now committed the sister vessels "Bulk Santos" 208/2020 New Times, China (SS 06/2030, DD 06/2028, Scrubber fitted) & "Bulk Sydney" 208/2020 New Times, China (SS 01/2030, DD 01/2028, Scrubber fitted) to clients of GENCO at USD 145,5 mill en bloc. Ya Sa Shipping have committed their Kamsarmax "Yasa Pioneer" 82/2006 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 02/2026) at USD 10,7 mill. Remind you that back in September, same owners committed the one-year-older sister vessel "Yasa Neslihan" 82/2005 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 06/2030, DD 07/2028) at USD 10,6 mill. Furthermore, Taizhou Changxin Transport committed their "Chang Xin 66" 80/2012 Fujian, China (SS 06/2027, DD 11/2026, Ice Class II) to Chinese interests at USD 14 mill, while the two-years-older sister vessel "Adam I" 80/2010 Fujian, China (SS 06/2030, DD 03/2028, Ice Class II) was sold in September at USD 11,3 mill. The Panamax "Ionian Princess" 76/2007 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 01/2026) of Sea Trade Marine was committed to undisclosed interests at USD 10,3 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-older "Yasa Team" 75/2006 Sanoyas, Japan (SS/DD 02/2026) was sold at USD 9,3 mill. On the Ultramax sector, CMB FL sold the "Great Voyage" 61/2021 DACKS, China (SS/DD 04/2026) via online bidding at USD 30,5 mill. Remind you that the sister vessel "Great Vista" 61/2021 DACKS, China (SS/DD 03/2026) was sold a couple of months ago also via online bidding at USD 27,8 mill. Masumoto Shipping have committed their Handysize "Atlantic Bulker" 36/2014 Shikoku, Japan (SS/DD 03/2027) at USD 15 mill. For reference, the two-years-older sister vessel "Spring Breeze" 36/2012 Shikoku, Japan (SS 05/2030, DD 02/2028, Scrubber fitted) was sold a week ago at low USD 13 mill. In addition, Yangtze Navigation committed their "Yangtze Grace" 32/2012 Jiangmen, China (SS/DD 06/2027) to Chinese interests at USD 9,8 mill, which is the same price achieved a couple of weeks ago for the sister vessel "Yangtze Flourish" 32/2012 Jiangmen, China (SS/DD 05/2027).

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

at the level of mid/high usd 10,00's fio range. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 16,000/18,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 17,000/20,000 pd range. Indian trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. West African trips via US East Coast into India were fixed around usd 27,000/29,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via US Gulf into Continent were reported at the level of usd 20,000/22,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into China were fixed around usd 20,000/22,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/700K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 14,000 pd for 11/13 months employment basis delivery Japan. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 17,000 pd basis delivery South East Asia for 10/12 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment. Trips from North China into Red Sea were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 12,000/13,000 pd range. Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 27,000/28,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips from North China into South East Asia were fixed around usd 8,000/10,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean were reported at the levl of usd 17,000/18,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into US East Coast were fixed around usd 9,000/11,000 pd. North Coast South American trips via East Coast South America into Continent were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. Trips from US East Coast into Caribbean were fixed around usd 22,000/24,000 pd. US Gulf trips into China were reported at the level of usd 24,000/26,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
