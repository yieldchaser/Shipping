### Bulkers Market Commentary

For reference, the two-years-younger sister vessel "Lowlands Amstel" 61/2015 Iwagi, Japan (SS/DD 05/2025, BWTS fitted) was sold in late September at USD 26,3 mill. Remind you that the one-year-younger sister vessel "Diva" 56/2011 Jiangsu, China (SS/DD 05/2026, BWTS & Scrubber fitted) was sold back in September at USD 14,75 mill. ### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 8,000/10,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 4,000/7,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 4,000/7,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 9,000/10,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 10,000/12,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 8,000/11,000 pd. South African trips into India were fixed around usd 11,000/12,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 100K. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South American trips into US East Coast were fixed around usd 15,000 pd plus ballast at the usd 100K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 10,500 pd for 5/8 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 4,000/6,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 10,000 pd. South African trips into Arabian Gulf were reported at the level of usd 9,000/11,000 pd. South African trips into China were fixed around usd 10,000/11,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 100K/110K. US Gulf trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. US Gulf trips into South America were fixed around usd 15,000/16,000 pd. Trips from US East Coast into Continent were reported at the level of usd 19,000/21,000 pd. On the period front, an Ultramax was reported fixed in the usd 10,750 pd for 3/5 months employment basis delivery West Coast India.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Australian trips into China with logs were fixed around usd 9,000/10,000 pd. Trips from Singapore via Thailand into Indonesia were reported at the level of usd 7,000/8,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into North Coast South America were fixed around usd 13,000/14,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
