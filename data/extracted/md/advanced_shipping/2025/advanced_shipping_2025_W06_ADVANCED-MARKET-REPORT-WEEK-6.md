### Bulkers Market Commentary

Seanergy Maritime have committed their Capesize "Gloriuship" 171/2004 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 03/2027, DD 06/2025) to Chinese interests at region USD 15 mill. Note that a couple of weeks ago, the one-year-younger "Salt Lake City" 171/2005 Daewoo, Korea (SS/DD 09/2025, Scrubber fitted) was sold at USD 16,2 mill. Following the sale of "Volos" 82/2014 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 06/2029, DD 06/2027) a week ago at USD 24 mill, Chronos Shipping have now committed the two-years-older "Patra" 80/2012 Universal, Japan (SS/DD 10/2025) to Greek interests at USD 15,75 mill. On the Panamax sector, Costamare committed their "Rose" 76/2008 Shin Kasado, Japan (SS 04/2028, DD 04/2026) to Chinese interests at USD 10,8 mill. For reference, the two-years-older "Navios Sagittarius" 75/2006 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 11/2026, DD 03/2025) was sold back in November at the same price. Unity Marine committed their semi-boxed Handysize "Unity North" 37/2015 Oshima, Japan (SS/DD 04/2025) at USD 16,5 mill, while the one-year-older sister vessel "Wellpark" 37/2014 Oshima, Japan (SS 06/2029, DD 05/2027) was sold in November at USD 19,2 mill. In addition, Erasmus Shipinvest committed their OHBS "ES Kure" 33/2012 Kanda, Japan (SS 03/2027, DD 04/2025) to Vietnamese interests at mid-high USD 12 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Nord Copenhagen" 33/2012 Kanda, Japan (SS 09/2027, DD 11/2025) was sold in November at USD 14,2 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Vietmy Trans have committed their VLCC "Gold Pearl" 318/2005 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 02/2025, Scrubber fitted) at USD 29,5 mill, while the two-year- younger "Wafrah" 317/2007 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS 02/2027, DD 03/2025) of BAHRI was committed at region USD 40 mill. Remind you that same owners committed the one-year-older "Serene Sea" 105/2009 Sumitomo, Japan (SS/DD 01/2025, Scrubber fitted) back in October at USD 36,5 mill. Furthermore, IMS committed their "Nemo" 105/2008 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 03/2028, DD 08/2026, Eco M/E) to Chinese interests at USD 37,5 mill. Note that the one-year-older sister vessel "Sousta" 106/2007 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS 06/2027, DD 07/2025) was sold a week ago at USD 31 mill. In addition, the "Sofia II" 105/2008 Sumitomo, Japan (SS 09/2028, DD 12/2026) of Flynn Ventures was committed to Chinese interests at region USD 31 mill. The MR2 "Horizon Athena" 50/2008 Spp, Korea (SS 12/2028, DD 09/2026) of Horizon Tankers was committed to undisclosed interests at USD 20 mill. For reference, the "Bachata" 50/2008 Sls, Korea (SS 06/2028, DD 11/2026) was sold back in October at USD 27,2 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 6,000/8,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 5,000/9,000 pd. South East Asian trips via East Coast South America into China were fixed around usd 9,000/12,000 pd. Indian trips via East Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/11,000 pd. Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 7,000/8,000 pd. Inter- Continental trips via US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 7,000/8,000 pd. Mediterranean trips via US East Coast into Japan were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 13,000/15,000 pd plus ballast at the usd 300K/500K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 11,750 pd for 5/7 months employment basis delivery South Korea.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 6,000/8,000 pd range. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 7,000/9,000 pd. A number of Australian trips into Arabian Gulf were fixed around usd 5,000/7,000 pd. Arabian Gulf coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 7,000/8,000 pd. US Gulf trips into South America were fixed around usd 8,000/10,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were reported at the level of usd 8,000/9,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Arabian Gulf coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 3,000/4,000 pd. West African trips into East Coast Canada were fixed around usd 10,000/11,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 6,000/8,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into West Coast South America were fixed around usd 7,000/8,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
