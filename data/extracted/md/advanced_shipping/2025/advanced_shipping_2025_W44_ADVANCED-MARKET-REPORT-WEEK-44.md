### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 16,000/18,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 17,000/20,000 pd range. Australian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd. Indian trips via South America into India or China were fixed around usd 17,000/20,000 pd. India trips via South Africa into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/20,000 pd. West African trips via US Gulf into Continent were fixed around usd 19,000/21,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via US East Coast-US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 17,000/20,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were fixed around usd 25,000/27,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 16,000/18,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 600K/800K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 15,500 pd for 5/8 months employment basis delivery China

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A generally quiet week for this segment. North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 13,000/16,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 14,000/16,000 pd range. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

West African trips via Mediterranean into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,000 pd. US Gulf coastal trips were fixed around usd 19,000/21,000 pd. US Gulf trips into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 21,000/23,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 20,000/22,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America were reported at the level of usd 26,000/28,000 pd. A small Handysize was fixed on a period at the usd 11,100 pd basis delivery Singapore for 4/7 months employment.
