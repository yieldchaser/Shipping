### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Capesize "Hebei No. 1" 182/2009 Dalian, China (SS 10/2029, DD 09/2027, Scrubber fitted) was committed to undisclosed interests at USD 25 mill. Note that back in July, the sister vessel "Epic Trader" 56/2012 Jiangsu, China (SS 08/2027, DD 10/2025) was sold at the same levels. Remind you that the one-year-older sister vessel "Wooyang Queen" 37/2011 Saiki, Japan (SS/DD 08/2026, OHBS) was sold back in July at USD 13,8 mill.
