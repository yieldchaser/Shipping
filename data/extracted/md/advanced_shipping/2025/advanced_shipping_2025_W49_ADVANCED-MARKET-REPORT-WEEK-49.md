A generally quiet week for this segment. Trips from North China into Mediterranean were reported at the level of usd 13,000/14,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips via South Africa into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd. Arabian Gulf trips into West Coast India were fixed around usd 18,000/20,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast India were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 500K/700K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Trips from South China into South East Asia were fixed around usd 10,000/12,000 pd. Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. West Coast Indian trips via Arabian Gulf into China were fixed around usd 8,000/9,000 pd. South African trips via US East Coast into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 22,000/24,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into US East Coast-US Gulf were fixed around usd 12,000/15,000 pd. South American coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. South American trips into US Gulf were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. South American trips into West Africa were reported at the level of usd 19,000/20,000 pd. On the period front, a large Handysize was reported fixed in the usd 15,500 pd for 5/6 months employment basis delivery Continent with redelivery Singapore/Japan range.
