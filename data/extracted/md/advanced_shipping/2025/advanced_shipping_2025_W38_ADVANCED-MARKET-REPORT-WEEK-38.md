### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 12,000/14,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed in the usd 13,000/15,000 pd range. Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Indian trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 17,000/18,000 pd. India trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. India trips via South Africa into India were fixed around usd 14,000/17,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast into India were reported at the level of usd 28,000/30,000 pd. South American trips into Continent were fixed around usd 27,000/29,000 pd. South American trips into China were reported at the level of usd 17,000/19,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 700K/900K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 15,750 pd for 8/10 months employment basis delivery China. Also, a Kamsarmax was fixed on a period at the usd 22,800 pd basis delivery Continent for 4/6 months employment.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

Trips from Philippines into China with nickel ore were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd. A number of Indonesian into India were reported fixed in the usd 15,000/17,000 pd range. Arabian Gulf trips into East Coast India were reported at the level of usd 14,000/15,000 pd. West African trips into Continent with bauxite were fixed around usd 17,000/19,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

Arabian Gulf trips into West Coast India were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. Arabian Gulf trips via South Africa into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. West African trips via South America into South Africa were reported at the level of usd 15,000/16,000 pd. Continental trips via Baltic into Mediterranean were fixed around usd 17,000/18,000 pd. South American trips into US Gulf or Caribs were reported at the level of usd 21,000/22,000 pd. East Coast South American trips into West Coast South America were fixed around usd 22,000/23,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
