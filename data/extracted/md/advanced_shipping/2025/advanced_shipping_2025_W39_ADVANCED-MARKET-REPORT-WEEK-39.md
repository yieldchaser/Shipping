### Bulkers Market Commentary

The Kamsarmax "Nord Taurus" 81/2016 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 11/2026) was committed to Greek interests at USD 27,5 mill. Remind you that back in July, the "Ultra Puma" 81/2016 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 08/2026) was sold at USD 25,3 mill. Nissen Kaiun committed their Ultramax "African Cheetah" 66/2014 Mitsui, Japan (SS 03/2029, DD 04/2027, Scrubber fitted) to Bangladeshi interests at USD 24,5 mill. Furthermore, following the sale of the "Spar Virgo" 53/2005 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 06/2025) in May at USD 8,5 mill, Spar Shipping have now committed the sister vessel "Spar Taurus" 53/2005 Chengxi, China (SS/DD 11/2025) to Chinese interests at USD 8,8 mill.
