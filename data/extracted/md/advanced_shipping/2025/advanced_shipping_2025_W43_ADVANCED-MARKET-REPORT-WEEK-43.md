### Bulkers Market Commentary

2020 Bulkers have committed their Newcastlemax "Bulk Sao Paulo" 208/2020 New Times, China (SS 06/2030, DD 06/2028, Scrubber fitted) to clients of Ebe NV at USD 72,75 mill. Remind you that a month ago, same owners sold the three sister vessels "Bulk Sandefjord" 208/2019 New Times, China (SS 08/2029, DD 03/2027, Scrubber fitted, "Bulk Santiago" 208/2019 New Times, China (SS 09/2029, DD 03/2027, Scrubber fitted) & "Bulk Shenzen" 208/2020 New Times, China (SS 01/2030, DD 01/2028, Scrubber fitted) at USD 209 mill enbloc. The Capesize "Mineral Subic" 179/2011 Hanjin, Korea (SS/DD 05/2026) was committed to Chinese interests at USD 30,25 mill. Note that a couple of months ago, the one-year-older "Frontier Bonanza" 179/2010 Hyundai, Korea (SS/DD 10/2025) was sold at USD 26,2 mill. Moreover, the "AP Power" 173/2006 Bohai, China (SS/DD 09/2026) was committed at USD 14,5 mill. For reference, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Cape Veni" 173/2007 Bohai, China (SS 07/2027, DD 07/2025) was sold back in June at USD 17 mill. On the Supramax sector, Ever Prosperity Shipping committed the "Sea Dana" 57/2009 Jiangsu, China (SS 05/2029, DD 10/2026) at USD 11 mill. For comparison, the sister vessel "Marinor" 56/2009 Jiangsu, China (SS 07/2029, DD 05/2028) was sold a month ago at USD 10,8 mill. Furthermore, the "Xie Hai Yong Feng" 52/2001 Sanoyas, Japan (SS 06/2026, DD 07/2026) was committed at USD 6,8 mill, while the one-year-younger "JW Flora" 53/2002 Oshima, Japan (SS 07/2027, DD 10/2025) was sold a week ago at USD 6,8 mill also. The Handysize "Danship Bulker" 28/2009 I-S, Japan (SS 02/2029, DD 02/2027) was committed to Vietnamese interests at mid-high USD 8 mill. Note that the one-year-younger sister vessel "Bright Hope" 28/2010 Imabari, Japan (SS/DD 09/2025) was sold a couple of weeks ago at USD 9,8 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Following the sale of the VLCC "Monaco Loyalty" 307/2007 Dalian, China (SS 07/2027, DD 10/2025) in September at USD 42 mill, clients of Sinokor have now committed the sister vessel "Singapore Loyalty" 307/2007 Dalian, China (SS 10/2027, DD 11/2025) at USD 46,5 mill. Chemikalien Seetransport have committed the LR1 "Chemtrans Cancale" 73/2007 New Century, China (SS 06/2027, DD 03/2026) to undisclosed interests at USD 12 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the sister vessel "Fair Winds" 74/2007 New Century, China (SS 09/2027, DD 11/2025) was sold at USD 11 mill.
