### Bulkers Market Commentary

For reference, the one-year-younger sister vessel "Orion" 56/2007 Mitsui, Japan (SS 05/2027, DD 08/2025) was committed a couple of months ago at USD 10,6 mill. ### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout round trips on the same route were reported around usd 22,000/23,000 pd levels. No period activity to report here.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

North Pacific rounds ranged at the usd 13,000/15,000 pd. A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 11,000/15,000 pd. Australian trips into India were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/12,000 pd. Indian trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 11,000/13,000 pd. Continental trips via US East Coast or South America into China were reported at the level of usd 18,000/20,000 pd. South American trips into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 400K/600K. On the period front, a Kamsarmax was reported fixed in the usd 14,250 pd for 5/8 months employment basis delivery China.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

A number of Indonesian trips into China were reported fixed at the level of usd 13,000/15,000 pd. Indonesian trips into India were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Indian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 12,000/13,000 pd. South African trips into India were fixed around usd 13,500/14,500 pd plus ballast bonus at the usd 135K/145K. No period activity to report here.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 10,000/11,000 pd. West African trips into South America were fixed around usd 6,000/7,000 pd. Continental trips into Mediterranean via Baltic were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Mediterranean trips into Continent or South America were fixed around usd 6,000/7,000 pd. South American Coastal trips were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. South American trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 12,000/13,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
