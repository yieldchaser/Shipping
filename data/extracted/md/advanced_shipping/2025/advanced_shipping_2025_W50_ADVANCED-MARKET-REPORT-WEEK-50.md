### Bulkers Market Commentary

Alpha Bulkers have committed their Capesize "Antonis Angelicoussis" 177/2007 SWS, China (SS 11/2027, DD 02/2026) to Greek interests at USD 20,5 mill, while the one-year-older "Pompano" 174/2006 SWS, China (SS/DD 06/2026, Scrubber fitted) of TMS Dry was committed at high USD 17 mill basis delivery May'26. The Kamsarmax "Ursula Manx" 82/2021 Tsuneishi Zhoushan, China (SS/DD 08/2026, Ice Class 1C) from MX Shipping was committed to clients of Blumenthal at USD 33,85 mill. Note that the one-year-older "Grace Harmony" 60/2015 Onomichi, Japan (SS 07/2030, DD 05/2028) was sold in November at high USD 24 mill.
