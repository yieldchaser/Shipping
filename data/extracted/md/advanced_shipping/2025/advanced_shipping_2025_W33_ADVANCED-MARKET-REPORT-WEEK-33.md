### Bulkers Market Commentary

Primebulk Shipmanagement have committed their Capesize "Cape Aqua" 178/2009 SWS, China (SS 06/2029, DD 04/2027) to undisclosed interests at USD 24 mill. For reference, the two-years-younger "Sea Poseidon" 176/2011 SWS, China (SS/DD 01/2026) was sold back in March at USD 25,7 mill. Following the sale of the Kamsarmax "Pedhoulas Leader" 82/2007 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 02/2027) a couple of weeks ago at USD 12,5 mill, clients of Safe Bulkers have now committed the one-year-older sister vessel "Pedhoulas Merchant" 82/2006 Tsuneishi, Japan (SS/DD 03/2026) at USD 11,5 mill. On the Supramax sector, Eagle Bulk Shipping committed their "Star Goal" 56/2010 IHI, Japan (SS 01/2030, DD 02/2028, Scrubber fitted) to Vietnamese interests at mid-high USD 15 mill, while the "Cobra" 55/2010 Kawasaki, Japan (SS 01/2030, DD 05/2028) was committed at rgn USD 15 mill. For comparison, the "Mindanao" 55/2010 Mitsui, Japan (SS 01/2030, DD 03/2028) was sold a month ago at USD 15,5 mill. The Handysize "Bobic" 31/2006 Hakodate, Japan (SS/DD 10/2026) of Empire Bulkers was committed to Turkish interests at USD 7,1 mill. Remind you that a month ago, the one-year-older "African Swan" 32/2005 Kanda, Japan (SS/DD 08/2025) was sold at USD 6,5 mill.

### Tankers Market Commentary

Evalend Shipping have committed their VLCC "Halcyon" 299/2020 Hyundai Samho, Korea (SS/DD 11/2025, Scrubber fitted) to clients of International Seaways at USD 119 mill. Remind you that a couple of months ago, the two- years-older "Papalemos" 319/2018 Hyundai, Korea (SS 08/2028, DD 08/2026, Scrubber fitted) was sold at USD 108 mill. The MR2 "Nave Equinox" 50/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 12/2027, DD 04/2026, Ice Class 1A) of Navios Maritime was committed to Indonesian interests at USD 14 mill. Note that the sister vessel "PS Vancouver" 50/2007 Stx, Korea (SS 06/2028, DD 06/2026, Ice Class 1A) was sold in June at USD 13 mill.

### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia / China route continued throughout

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

A number of Indonesian and Australian trips into China were reported at the level of usd 13,000/16,000 pd. South East Asian trips via South America into China were reported at the level of usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Indian trips via South America into China were fixed around usd 14,000/16,000 pd. Continental trips via North Coast South America into China were reported at the level of usd 22,000/24,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via Narvik were fixed around usd 16,000/18,000 pd. Inter-Continental trips via US Gulf were reported at the level of usd 15,000/17,000 pd. No period activity to report here.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

Trips from China into East Africa were reported at the level of usd 16,000/17,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into China were fixed around usd 15,000/17,000 pd. A number of Indonesian trips into India were reported at the level of usd 12,000/14,000 pd. West Coast India trips via Arabian Gulf into East Coast India were fixed around usd 14,000/15,000 pd. West African trips into Arabian Gulf were reported at the level of usd 18,000/19,000 pd. US Gulf coastal trips were fixed around usd 20,000/22,000 pd. On the period front, an Ultramax was reported fixed int the usd 16,200 pd for 5/7 months employment basis delivery South East Asia.

#### Handymax / Handysize

A generally quiet week for this segment. Continental trips into West Africa were fixed around usd 10,000/11,000 pd. No period activity to report here.
