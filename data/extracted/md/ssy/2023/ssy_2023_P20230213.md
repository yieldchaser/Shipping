## Market Commentary

The SSY Pacific Capesize Index edged higher 14 points from last week to 3,663 in its first weekly increase this year. SSY’s assessed W.Australia-China iron ore spot voyage rate has lowered $0.20/t to $6.20/t. The round-voyage is down $150/day on the week to $2,500/ day, still at the lowest level since the week to 10 February 2020.
