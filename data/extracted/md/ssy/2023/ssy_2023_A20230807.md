## Market Commentary

The SSY Atlantic Capesize Index increased 151 points week-on-week to 8,289. SSY’s assessed fronthaul and round-voyage rate fell $4,500/day and $1,000/day to $33,000/day and $18,500/day, respectively. Although freight rates for certain routes are on a positive trend, the $70 increase in bunkers over the past fortnight has curtailed time charter earnings.
