## Market Commentary

Having witnessed the strongest week-onweek rally in the history of the SSY Atlantic Capesize Index, the Atlantic index strengthened 6,546 points in the past week to 18,382 points. SSY's assessed fronthaul earnings climbed $37,750/day to $85,000/day, while the trans-Atlantic rate increased $33,750/day to $75,000/day, both of which were the highest since October 2021.
