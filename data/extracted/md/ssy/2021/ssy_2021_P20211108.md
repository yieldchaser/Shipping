## Market Commentary

A fourth successive weekly decline, of 1,390 points to 7,975 points, pushed the SSY Pacific Capesize Index to its lowest level since July 2021. The W.Australia-China iron ore spot voyage rate fell $1.65/t to $10.85/t, while the round-voyage rate sunk $9,000/day to $24,000/day.
