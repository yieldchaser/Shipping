## Market Commentary

The SSY Pacific Capesize Index fell for the first time in 5 weeks, dropping by 3,323 points last week to 13,716. There was a steep $4.60/t decline in the W.Australia-China iron ore spot voyage rate to $16.75/t, while the round-voyage rate retreated by $22,900/day to $54,850/day.
