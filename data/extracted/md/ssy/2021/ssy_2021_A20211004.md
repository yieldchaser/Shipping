## Market Commentary

The SSY Atlantic Capesize Index surged 3,440 points to 22,198, marking the largest weekly increase since 2009 and the highest index since September 2008 (although the composition of the index has been updated since then). There were steep gains in the roundvoyage and fronthaul rates which rose by $15,500/day and $20,500/day to $84,750/day and $105,750/day, respectively.
