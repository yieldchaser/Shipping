## Market Commentary

The SSY Pacific Capesize Index rose by 1,196 points week-on-week to the highest level since 2008 at 17,037 (although the composition of the index has been updated over time). The W.Australia-China iron ore spot voyage rate increased by $0.15/t to $21.35/t, while there was a $1,400/day rise in the round-voyage rate to $77,750/day.
