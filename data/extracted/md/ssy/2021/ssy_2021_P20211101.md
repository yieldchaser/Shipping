## Market Commentary

A third consecutive weekly decline, of 1,976 points, saw the SSY Pacific Capesize Index fall below the 10,000 point mark for the first time since August, sliding to 9,365 points. There was a $1.75/t drop in the W.AustraliaChina iron ore spot voyage rate to $12.50/t, while the round-voyage rate declined by $8,500/day to $33,000/day.
