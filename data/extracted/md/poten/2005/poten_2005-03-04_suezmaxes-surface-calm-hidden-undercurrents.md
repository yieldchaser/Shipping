# Suezmaxes Surface Calm - Hidden Undercurrents

The volume of Suezmax spot fixtures has been around 2,100 since 2000 other than for a dip in 2001. But there have been changes in where Suezmaxes have been loading.

### Suezmax Spot Fixture Activity

### by Load Area

The number of fixtures from West Africa was roughly the same in 2004 as in 2000, but there were more fixtures from the Black Sea/Mediterranean and the Arabian Gulf. These have been more or less balanced by a decline in the number of UKC and Caribbean fixtures. ### Black Sea/Mediterranean Load Area in First Place

The increased number of liftings out of the Black Sea and North Africa is now greater than the number of liftings out of West Africa. West Africa, which held first place since 2000, is now in second place.

### Black Sea/Mediterranean Load Area

### Discharge Area

Other USA UKC Med While increased fixture activity in the Black Sea is good news to Suezmax owners, the average voyage length is shortening with fewer fixtures on the longer voyage to the UKC (down 87% in 4 years) and much greater activity on the shorter voyage within the Med (up 210% in 4 years). This decline in ton-mile demand is partly compensated by a rising long haul trade to the United States from 3 fixtures in 2000 to 50 in 2004. The completion of the BTC pipeline and the continuing decline in North Sea production should increase the number of Suezmax liftings from the eastern Med to northern Europe.

### West Africa in Second Place

The number of West Africa loadings for Suezmaxes slid from 2000 to 2003, but recouped nearly all its loss in 2004.

### West Africa Load Area

### Discharge Area

Other Carib ECSA UKC Med USA The United States has been picking up the slack from the fall-off in fixtures to the UKC and Mediterranean as Caspian crude backs out West African crude in Europe. Fixture activity to East Coast South America, primarily Brazil, has declined as Brazilian domestic (offshore) production moves the nation closer to self-sufficiency.

### Arabian Gulf Solid in Third Place

Arabian Gulf Suezmax liftings have also increased, but most of the gain is to ports in China suitable for Suezmax discharges.

### Arabian Gulf Load Area

### Discharge Area

Other W of Suez Australia SE Asia S Asia China&FE As China's economy continues to expand, Suezmaxes will see increasing business to move crude to ports that cannot accommodate VLCCs. Spot fixtures to South Asia, mainly India, and Southeast Asia do not display any consistent pattern, but fixtures West of Suez, the original purpose for Suezmaxes, are growing rather dramatically. Part of the explanation is transportation economics on occasions when oil companies can move a cargo into Europe at less cost with 2 Suezmaxes on a shorter voyage via the Suez Canal than around South Africa in a VLCC. Suezmaxes are also substitute tonnage when no VLCC is immediately available to move a cargo to Europe.

### Shrinking Trades

There are two- Suezmax liftings in the UKC are declining along with North Sea production as are liftings from Columbia, also suffering from declining production. Fortunately, other areas have picked up the slack.

### Workhorse Tankers

Suezmaxes are workhorses quietly doing their job while other tanker classes get the limelight. While the surface remains calm in terms of aggregate cargoes, changing undercurrents will continue to affect tanker demand. UKC and Columbia will present fewer trading opportunities, but the West of Suez trade may pick up if transient shortages of VLCC capacity continue to be a problem. AG liftings to China and Caspian will offer greater trading opportunities in the future.
