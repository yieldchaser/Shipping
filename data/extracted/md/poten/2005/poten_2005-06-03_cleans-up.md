# Cleans Up!

The clean up position on a baseball team is where the fourth batter has the opportunity to clean up the bases by hitting a home run. Product carriers may be hitting a home run with U.S. refineries operating at full utilization in the face of growing oil demand. Under these circumstances, not only should clean product imports be growing but their rate of growth should also be accelerating.

### So What Can We Conclude?

With U.S. clean product imports projected to rise, we can only conclude that demand for product carriers will continue to grow. Of course, there will be fluctuations along the way and spare gasoline refinery capacity in Europe will some day be exhausted. Then the United States will have to look even further afield for its import needs, which will benefit larger sized clean products carriers. As an example of longer-haul clean fixtures, on May 4th, a LR2 tanker was fixed to move 75,000 tons of gasoil from the Arabian Gulf to the U.S. Gulf
