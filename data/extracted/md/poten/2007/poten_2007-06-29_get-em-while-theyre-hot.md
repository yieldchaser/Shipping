# Get 'Em While They're Hot

Although it is only the first official week of summer, the time charter market is already hot. Over the past several months, time charter rates have trended upward. With an element of uncertainty always present in the tanker market, charterers are seeking the shelter of term coverage. The time chartering of vessels generally assures the charterer of quality tonnage for moving cargoes and can act as a financial hedge against the volatility of spot market rates.
