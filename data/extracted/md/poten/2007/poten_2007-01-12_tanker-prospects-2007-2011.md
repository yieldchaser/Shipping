# Tanker Prospects 2007 - 2011

### A Mid-Term Outlook

Over the past few years, the tanker industry has experienced what has been characterized as some of the most exciting times in its history. With worldwide crude oil demand at an all-time high, tankers have provided the critical link between producers and consumers. Shipping has been a key component not only in a physical sense, but as a key economic element in trading strategies.

In addition to increased demand, the tanker industry is facing several regulatory changes that significantly impact the supply side. Given the current uncertain landscape for freight rates and the ongoing structural changes in the market, the future will likely prove to be just as exciting.

Tanker Prospects (2007-2011) explores the most important issues facing the industry today and offers unique insights for future strategy development. Each vessel sector contains an in-depth analysis of historical trends and the projected macro and micro market drivers that shape future expectations.

As a senior executive planning your company's medium-term strategy, this analysis will be a critical component of a thoughtful plan.
