### Run-Up in Rates

Last week saw the largest one-day increase in WS rates in the past five years- a 50 WS point jump! Day rates on VLCCs in the Arabian Gulf - Far East are achieving $150,000 per day on a timecharter equivalent (TCE) basis. Those vessels fixed Arabian Gulf - US Gulf are earning near $100,000 per day, up from $25,000 per day a week ago.

Although WS rates have demonstrated significant one-day upside growth, it should be noted that the largest one-day drop in the VLCC spot market was 40 WS points seen in 2004. What goes up may eventually come down!
