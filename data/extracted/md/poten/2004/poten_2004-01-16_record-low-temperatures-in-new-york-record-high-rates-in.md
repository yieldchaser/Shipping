### There's More to Come

Two factors are at play here. One is the Bosphorus Effect where Aframaxes left the Caribbean to enjoy higher rates in the Mediterranean. As owners chase the end of the rainbow, ship supply dropped off in the Caribs, as seen in the number of available vessels. That came full circle when charterers seeking the fewer available ships caused rates to escalate. The second factor is greater lightering activity offloading VLCCs. That's why we think that there is more momentum left in this market.
