# Clean Is Good Handymax Activity Picks Up As Do Rates

Long-haul trans-Atlantic clean tanker rates are moving up faster than short- haul rates from the Caribbean to the US Gulf. The cause of this is a combination of rising U.S. import and falling clean products production in Venezuela. Moving more product from a source further away does wonders to rates. The following chart shows weekly assessed rates for two popular clean routes - UKC to US - and Caribbean to US. But these are weekly assessments, in terms of individual fixtures, some have been as high as WS340 on the UKC to US trade.

### Handymax Steps Up a Notch

Handymax voyages on the whole have been increasing during the past five years at an impressive average growth rate of 5.8% per year. The primary cause is refinery capacity growth lagging growth in demand. This tightens up refinery utilization and increases the seaborne movement of refined products. Note that 2004 data has been annualized.

### Where Do We Go From Here?

There is little expansion of refinery capacity in the United States, which means that imports of refined oil products will continue to grow. With Venezuela unable to operate its refineries at capacity, greater volumes will have to come from elsewhere and that elsewhere is further away. China is expanding its refinery capacity, but that growth is lagging the growth in demand. Thus we anticipate greater imports of clean products into China. Expanding imports to the United States and China should provide strong support for the underlying demand for product carriers.
