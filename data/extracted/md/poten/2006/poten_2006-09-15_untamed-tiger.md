# Untamed Tiger

When trying to assess the future of the world economy, crude oil demand and formulate an outlook for tanker rates, one of the key factors to consider is the state of the economy in China. For the second quarter, China's GDP grew at its fastest pace in more than a decade- over 11.3%. As demand for crude oil generally moves in line with economic growth especially in developing countries, the marked increase in crude oil imports is not surprising. What continues to be most interesting is that there are no looming downward pressures on the horizon for this giant economy.

### Caribs-East

Venezuela's recent announcement that it plans to increase exports to China to around 500,000 barrels per day over the next 5 years is a primary example of how evolving trade patterns will increase ton-mile demand in the future. China's demand shows no signs of stopping and incremental supply is inclined to come from all corners of the globe.

While the industry faces a growing orderbook, in the short-term the high demand for tonnage will remain strong largely due to emerging market economies like China. The second quarter stellar economic results for China support this view.
