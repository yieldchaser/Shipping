# Now Batting Clean Up....

### Caveat Emptor

The review each year of the largest charterers for both clean products and crude oil (see the market opinion of January 6th) generates some very interesting commentary from the industry. Owners are curious to know more about the locations, dates, and sizes of cargoes that are moving in the market. Charterers invariably comment that the report significantly understates the volume of business that they conduct in the spot market.

One must keep in mind that the spot market fixtures in our reports only take into account fixtures that are actually reported- those done in the "market" arena. We do not have access to information relating to fixtures that are done directly between charterers and owners, those that move under contracts of affreightment or with direct arrangements in a tanker pool. Charterers also fix ships on consecutive voyages where only the first voyage is of market knowledge and subsequent voyages are not recorded. We clearly understate the number of actual fixtures for charterers, but assess that their relative standing is still valid.

### Conclusion

The total number of clean spot fixtures increased from 2004 to 2005 by a relatively small number (about 70 fixtures). Looking ahead in 2006, the number of fixtures is expected to be well above 2005 levels as refining capacity in the United States remains at full capacity requiring increased imports to meet incremental demand. Moreover continued growth in clean products demand in India and China will account for more cargoes, although future expansion of refinery capacity may eventually have an impact on the product supply/demand balance.
