# Bienvenidos a Miami

This past week members of the energy industry convened for the 26 th Annual Fuel Oil/Energy Buyers' Conference in Miami Beach, Florida. The topics discussed ranged from macro drivers for worldwide energy consumption to the role of fuel oil in the energy mix. The variety of companies that were in attendance stimulated interesting discussions, both in the conference room and at the dinner table. ### No Fueling Around

The best part of this conference, besides the boat cruise and late night escapades, is listening to various views on how companies manage complex issues relating to fuel oil.

The conference was highlighted by an enlightening presentation by Mr. Carlos Barbosa, Fuel Oil and Feedstocks Manager for Petrobras. This international integrated oil company is a leader in Latin America and with financial strength and shipping expertise that allows for delivered sales of fuel oil, and other refined products. This company is a major player in the business. Petrobras' expertise in storing and blending in the Bahamas and Singapore allows them to stay focused on their customers. Mr. Barbosa confidently assessed that fuel oil will remain a key activity for Petrobras as they expand their presence in the emerging markets of Singapore and China.

The panel discussion, organized and moderated by Poten's fuel oil expert Larry Axelrod, allowed for several companies to share their views on the role of fuel oil in power generation from companies located in smaller markets such as Hawaii, Malta and Northern Florida to powerhouses serving Italy and Tokyo. All of the speakers gave enlightening perspectives on serving their markets; of particular interest was Floyd Shiroma of the Hawaiian Electric Company that addressed the challenges of supplying all of the islands with fuel oil.

Steve Levy of Sprague Energy addressed components of the Ultra Low Sulfur Diesel market and ways to avoid cost implications. Joe Gerstemeier of Baker Petrolite also spoke about changes in refined product specifications, focusing on heavy fuel oil.

Paul Smith, Vice President of Wartsila Americas, spoke about the use of diesel engines in ships and in power plants. He thoroughly addressed the ability for power plants to be duel-fueled.

The conference was an opportunity to learn from others with an interest in the fuel oil sector, and enjoy some warm Miami weather. With 26 years behind it, this conference has plenty of fuel for another 26!
