# The Tallest and the Strongest

Now that the 2005 National Championship College Football (American style) have been settled it's time to look at the Worldwide Chartering Championship for the oil and dirty petroleum (DPP) market. Surprisingly, the total activity in the sector is essentially unchanged from last year. Total dirty spot fixtures rose by just 19 fixtures from 10,412 in 2004 to 10,433 in 2005.

### Conclusion

Following robust fixture growth in 2004, spot market volumes remained largely unchanged in 2005 across major vessel sizes. With fixture volume at such strong levels, overall vessel demand is high and rates are up.

Many leading charterers changed places but did not replace each other over 2005 versus 2004, with major integrated oil companies holding onto strong rankings. With incremental demand forecast to grow in 2006, it is likely that spot market figures will be higher and a potential new Worldwide Chartering Champion crowned.
