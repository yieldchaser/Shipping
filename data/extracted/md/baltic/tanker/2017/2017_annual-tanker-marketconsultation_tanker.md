# Annual Tanker Market Consultation

**Date:** 02 January 2017  
**Publisher:** Baltic Exchange | **Category:** Tankers  
**Original URL:** [http://www.balticexchange.com/en/data-services/WeeklyRoundup/tanker/news/2017/annual-tanker-marketconsultation.html](http://www.balticexchange.com/en/data-services/WeeklyRoundup/tanker/news/2017/annual-tanker-marketconsultation.html)  

---

Tanker market participants are encouraged to join panelists for a tanker market consultation taking place on 13 September during London International Shipping Week at Skinners’ Hall.

This will be the main opportunity of the year for members to discuss the routes and vessels used within the current suite of Baltic tanker market benchmarks as well as upcoming changes and proposals.

The consultation takes place before the Baltic’s Freight and Commodities Forum at the same venue.

Email [marketing@balticexchange.com](mailto:marketing@balticexchange.com) to register.
