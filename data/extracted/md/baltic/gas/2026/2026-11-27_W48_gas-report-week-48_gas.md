# Gas Report - Week 48

**Date:** 27 November 2026  
**Publisher:** Baltic Exchange | **Category:** Gas  
**Original URL:** [http://www.balticexchange.com/en/data-services/WeeklyRoundup/Gas/News/2026/gas-report-week-48.html](http://www.balticexchange.com/en/data-services/WeeklyRoundup/Gas/News/2026/gas-report-week-48.html)  

---

### LNG

The LNG market softened further this week, with rates easing across all regions as hopes of a winter uplift continued to fade. The Atlantic remained particularly weak, with the arbitrage into Asia firmly closed and limited tonne-mile demand adding further pressure to freight.

On the BLNG1 Gladstone–Tokyo route, rates declined $3,100 week-on-week to settle at $46,000/day. East of Suez demand remained comparatively firmer, particularly in the Middle East, helping the Pacific maintain a premium to the Atlantic despite rates continuing to soften.

The BLNG2 Sabine–Isle of Grain route fell $4,800 to settle at $15,400/day. The Atlantic remained unusually quiet for the time of year, with limited firm requirements and plentiful available tonnage continuing to favour charterers.

Similarly, the BLNG3 Sabine–Tokyo route declined $3,500 to close at $35,200/day, reflecting the closed Atlantic-to-Asia arbitrage and continued weakness in long-haul demand.

Period rates also softened, with the six-month assessment falling $3,100 to $39,200/day, the one-year assessment easing $166 to $50,467/day, and the three-year assessment declining $700 to $69,600/day.

### LPG

The LPG market remained relatively quiet this week, with limited fixtures being done. Despite the slower activity, limited vessel availability continued to support freight rates, with activity expected to pick up following the Thanksgiving holidays.

On the BLPG1 Ras Tanura–Chiba route, rates settled at $254.20/tonne, with TCE earnings closing at $248,846/day. The BLPG2 Houston–Flushing route softened marginally, declining $0.17 week-on-week to settle at $175.50/tonne, while TCE earnings increased $1,627 to $209,503/day.

Meanwhile, the BLPG3 Houston–Chiba route edged higher by $0.43 to close at $315.43/tonne, with TCE returns rising $352 to settle at $193,761/day.
