## Capesize/Newcastlemax

Due to holidays in China, we got no data update from last week. Next week's update will be interesting as it will capture post-Liberation Day developments. The iron ore price has dropped in the last week, and this signals an incoming slowdown in China's iron ore consumption, unless the drop is purely sentiment-driven.

The outlook for fundamentals remains bearish, but low supply growth coupled with the fact that China's iron ore consumption is primarily driven by domestic construction means there should still be some market jumps here and there going forward.

## Panamax/Kamsarmax

But in this environment, will it counteract other weaknesses? Doubtful...

## Supramax/Ultramax

The vertical down move in commodity prices and stock markets tells you everything about how global markets assess Trump's Liberation Day policies. The outlook will remain bearish until there is a change to the ongoing turmoil.

The copper price lead shown below has taken a turn for the worse, and is telling us that the market will weaken during the second half of this year.
