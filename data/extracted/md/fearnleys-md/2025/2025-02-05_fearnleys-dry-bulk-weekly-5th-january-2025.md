## Capesize/Newcastlemax

Several indicators still suggest a market rally will occur soon. Ballaster/laden vessel ratios are at extremes, a high number of VLOCs will soon be laden and the iron ore price lead is pointing upwards. However, China's imported iron ore consumption is yet to suggest an upturn. So, ideally there should be a rebound over the next weeks in this data to be more certain that a rally will start this month.

## Panamax/Kamsarmax

The Soybean harvest in Brazil is 12% completed vs 40% completed by the same period last season. Export volumes are projected at record highs this year by the IGC,  which bodes well for the market over the coming months. 
However, fundamentals are likely to be suppressed by weakness in coal markets and developed economies, so the index average is likely to remain below last year's levels going forward.

## Supramax/Ultramax

On average, the market is at the seasonal low point now, and should improve in the coming weeks. JP Morgan's global manufacturing PMI edged into expansion in January, and industrial metals prices are showing some resilience. However, overall fundamentals does not look strong, so we still expect spot market levels to remain below that of last year.
