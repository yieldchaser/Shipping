## Capesize/Newcastlemax

Last week, we wrote the following: *Notwithstanding the last week's market softness, year-to-date spot market highs within the next month seem likely. Imported iron ore consumption in China has kept rising. VLOCs will be "out" of the market for a while still. We think C3 looks tight, and even though C5 activity was very low last week the market drop has not been significant. Activity on C5 has picked up this week.*

China's imported iron ore consumption rose further last week, and MySteel forecasts consumption to increase more this week and the next. This reaffirms our bullish near-term view on the market.

## Panamax/Kamsarmax

Last week, we wrote the following: *Average seasonality suggests the market could be close to peaking. However, the second wave of the ECSAM Soybean season is incoming, and the ballaster list looks thin. The Pacific also looks firm for the time being. So even though the market could be near a peak, we do not expect much, if any, downside from current levels in the next month.*
Last year, the "second wave" of the ECSAM grain season started on the 25th of March (defined as the bottom in the count of Pmx/Kmx sailing towards Brazil basis AIS). In 2023, the second wave started on the 5th of April. So it should be imminent now, and it will provide market support. Apart from ECSAM grains, coal shipments in the Pacific remain lower y-o-y, the US grain season will wind down, and the general macro-economic picture is not bullish. So, notwithstanding potential YTD spot market highs driven by ECSAM, the lower y-o-y market is likely to continue.

## Supramax/Ultramax

There is no change to our viewpoints given in the last weeks. The market often peaks around this time of year, and it seems we are on track for that development. Industrial metals prices have taken a tumble over the last weeks, and coal market fundamentals remain weak. With that backdrop, it is hard to expect much, if any, upside going forward. 
However, the second wave of the ECSAM grain season should provide market support, so we do not see any major drop coming in the near term.
