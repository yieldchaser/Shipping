## Capesize/Newcastlemax

We made a model consisting of factors like the global ballast/laden vessel ratio, West of Singapore ballasters, and a couple of other AIS indicators. Below the charts are tables showing where the model sits currently, as an attempt at an advanced "traffic light" type of outlook. It is only at extremes that these models give very clear suggestions about the near-term outlook. Since last week, the models changed from mildly positive to slightly bearish. The composite model remains barely in bullish territory, whereas the momentum model has changed to bearish.

In the last two months, iron ore volumes were unchanged on the same period last year, but bauxite volumes in the Atlantic were up by 50%, contrary to usual seasonal developments. China's bauxite imports are growing at a much higher rate than underlying Alumina consumption.

## Panamax/Kamsarmax

Shipment volume growth continues to be lower than DWT supply growth. Through week 35, growth was 3.5%, compared to DWT supply growth of 4.2%. Comparing the last 8 weeks to the same period last year, shipment volume growth declined by 0.9%. In the last three months, all volumes loaded in the South Atlantic, US Gulf and ECUS were down 0.6% compared to the same period last year.

The market has defied these weak headline figures, and also defied an August market peak suggested by the Pacific tightness indicator and the Newcastle coal futures curve indicator. This week is suggested by the South Atlantic indicator to be a peak.

Should the market continue surging from here, it would be a big surprise.

**Top left chart:** Shipment volumes were up 3.5% year on year through week 35. In the last eight weeks, volumes were down 0.9% compared to the same period last year. Supply growth was 4.2% at the latest reading.

**Top right chart:** The 2-month lead of the coal futures curve suggests the market could be past the peak.

**Bottom left chart:** The Pacific tightness indicator suggests market softness going forward. The index is a bit stronger now than what the indicator suggested.

**Bottom right chart:** The SATL tightness indicator suggests P6 could peak this week, then soften going forward.

## Supramax/Ultramax

Through last week, shipment volume growth fell further, to 3.6% compared with the same period last year. In the last 8 weeks, volume growth was 2.9%. DWT Supply growth remains at 5.2%. The near-term indicators still suggest a sideways-trending market.

**Top left chart:** Weekly shipment volumes, 2026 ytd vs 2025.

**Top right chart:** The chart indicates the tightness in the South Atlantic basin. A lower reading reflects tighter market conditions, but can also suggest a near-term market peak. Conversely, a higher reading suggests looser market conditions but can also suggest a near-term market bottom. The longer-term average is 247, and the latest reading is 239.

**Bottom left chart:** The price change of copper usually leads the change of the Supra/Ultra market. Towards the end of the year, the suggestion is for a sideways trending market. Some are attributing the rise in copper prices much more to supply than to demand, which, if that is the case, makes this indicator a bit less certain.

**Bottom right chart:** The chart indicates the tightness in the North Atlantic basin. A lower reading reflects tighter market conditions, but can also suggest a near-term market peak. Conversely, a higher reading suggests looser market conditions but can also suggest a near-term market bottom. The longer-term average is 287, and the latest reading is 272.

## Handysize

Revisions to week 35 shipment volume growth grew by an estimated 1.8% compared to last year. DWT supply growth remains at 3.2%. The market will most likely continue trending sideways in the near-term.

**Top left chart:** The ratio between Rio-Recalada / Skaw-Passero (HS3) and SEA / Singapore-Japan (HS5) is usually a good predictor of tops and bottoms of the HS3 route. A higher ratio suggests a market top, and a lower ratio suggests a market bottom.

**Top right chart:** Through week 35, Handysize shipment volumes were 1.8% higher year on year, declining from the 3%+ figures seen in the last months. DWT supply growth was 3.2% at the latest reading.

**Bottom left chart:** The correlation between the S11TC and HS7TC is over 90%. The correlation is even higher when the S11TC index is shifted forward a few days, which means that movements in the S11TC index often lead the movements of the HS7TC.

**Bottom right chart:** The change in the price of copper is usually an accurate leading indicator for asset values.
