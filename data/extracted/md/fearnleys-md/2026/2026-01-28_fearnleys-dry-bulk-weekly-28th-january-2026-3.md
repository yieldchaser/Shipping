## Capesize/Newcastlemax

Our expectations for a Jan/Feb low season comparable to 2024 have so far been exceeded. An index move like yesterday is extreme, considering we are in January.

We think the market could still cool off a bit due to the upcoming Chinese New Year, but in general, we expect firm levels.

**Top left chart:** The share of profitable steel mills in China ticked up last week, and profit margins for hot-rolled coils turned positive for the first time since early October. Hot metal output is 2% higher year on year.

**Top right chart:** The iron ore price complex is giving mixed signals. Spot prices remain stable, but the 1st month minus 3rd month futures spread has dipped into a slight contango. The price spread between higher-grade Brazilian ore and Australian iron ore is low. The latter is often bullish for freight as it incentivises longer-haul shipments.

**Bottom left chart:** The price of copper is a good barometer of global industrial activity, and leads asset values with high accuracy. The suggestion is for further upside in values in the next few months.

**Bottom right chart:** There has been an increase in the share of the active fleet in shipyards since the start of the new year. From 0.9% to 1.4% currently. We will see this rise above 2% frequently this year.

## Panamax/Kamsarmax

Indicators below still look bullish, but expect some flattening out or slightly downward move to occur around the Chinese New Year weeks. Then, we expect a resumption of the rally after that.

**Top left chart:** The price of copper is a good barometer of global industrial activity, and leads asset values with high accuracy. The suggestion is for further upside in values in the next months.

**Top right chart:** Since last week, the coal futures turned a bit more bullish again and are in a slight contango. Bullish suggestion to the P5 development going forward.

**Bottom left chart:** The net vessel change into the North Atlantic is positive, which more often than not correlates with a relative outperformance in the basin. That, along with positive indicators on the main routes should mean the basin will see a stable to firm development going forward.

**Bottom right chart:** The SATL tightness indicator suggests the market will trend up going forward.

## Supramax/Ultramax

We still expect a stable to firm market development over the coming weeks. A real rally will likely have to wait until after the Chinese New Year.

**Top left chart:** The three-month change in the price of copper looks very bullish for the coming months, and the three-month change of the S10TC index is very low.

**Top right chart:** The chart compares the ballaster/laden vessel ratio against the S11TC index. A low ballaster/laden vessel ratio reflects a tighter market balance.

**Bottom left chart:** An industrial metals index including metals like Aluminium, Zinc and Nickel. This index is now at similar heights to the peak in Q1 2022. Can market levels climb to the same heights as back then? We don't think so, but the highest *since* then is not unreasonable to expect.

**Bottom left chart:** The price of copper is a good barometer of global industrial activity, and leads asset values with high accuracy. The suggestion is for further upside in values in the next months.
