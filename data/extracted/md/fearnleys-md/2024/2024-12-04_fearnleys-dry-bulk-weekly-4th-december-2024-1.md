## Capesize/Newcastlemax

In December, the market either trends down or gets a late-month push due to the seasonal increase in Australian exports to China. We are unsure how it will develop this year, but it is clear that fundamentals are weak, so an eventual rally will not likely be major. 
According to Platts, China will hold its Central Economic Working Conference next week, which will set China's economic goals for 2025, including GDP growth and the fiscal deficit ratio. The intent to stimulate the economy is obvious, the question is whether it will work and if it does, when will dry bulk markets be lifted as a result? We think that at least the first half of next year will not see any benefit from Chinese stimulus measures.

## Supramax/Ultramax

Two weeks ago, we wrote the following:

*Overall it is not a strong picture. Demand growth both in terms of shipment volumes and ton-time has dropped significantly in the last months, industrial metals prices have trended down since late September, and the Brazil+US grain season is weaker than last year.
Seasonality suggests the market could bounce from current levels, but overall we expect the market to continue trending down until the beginning of February next year.*

The market is finding some stability here, around the time one usually sees a bounce. However, we expect the market to go sideways or slightly down until year-end.
