### Mediterranean
The end of last week was very busy with charterers fixing both ahead in dates and ahead of Posidonia. This week has remained active with a mixture of new business and some replacement business which has pushed the market up over 40 points very quickly. USD 15.85 16-18 June, last done USD 15.7 22-24 June.
The Panamax market opened the week on a softer note, with Posidonia and regional holidays keeping activity limited and many players still reassessing direction. The
Atlantic remains under pressure, with limited fresh mineral and grain enquiry meeting a growing prompt tonnage list in the Continent and Mediterranean, forcing earlier- position owners to trim expectations. ECSA is broadly flat but feels toppy for index dates, with spot tonnage still discounted due to limited cargo volume. In the Pacific,
Indonesia and Australian mineral flows continue to provide some support, but sentiment has turned more cautious as tonnage builds and fresh requirements remain thin. Overall, near-term indicators still point bearish, while coal demand and potential
Chinese weather disruptions remain the main upside risk.
Despite Posidonia & regional holidays around, market continued to exhibit a generally constructive tone. In the Atlantic, sentiment remained positive, particularly in USG where tightening vessel avails continued to support owners’ expectations. ECSA maintained firm underlying fundamentals, although fresh business remained limited.
In Asia, it was a mixed picture, with stronger demand evident in the north while ample prompt tonnage weighed on sentiment further south. The Handy sector showed comparatively firmer momentum across Asia, supported by tightening vessel supply and steady cargo demand, helping offset the subdued activity seen across much of the Atlantic basin. With the upcoming monsoon season in India, demand is expected to slowdown in the coming weeks. Period activity remains limited with very few fixtures reported in the region.
