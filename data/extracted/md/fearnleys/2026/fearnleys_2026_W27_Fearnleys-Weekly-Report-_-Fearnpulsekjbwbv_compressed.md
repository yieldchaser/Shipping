## 01 Tankers

### VLCC

Two steps forward and one step back. Iran and the US have been at it again trading airstrikes. And Washington has also reimposed crude sales sanctions on Tehran. You could have been fooled to think we were back to normal in the last few days with numerous enquiries and fixtures inside the MEG and rates have strengthened accordingly, but the exchange of fire underlines how fragile the situation still is. Players are likely to take a breather to see where we go from here. It will probably “blow over” but you never know after President Trump today indicated that the tentative ceasefire with Iran is over. Having been largely "dormant" lately, barring Brazilian export, focus could again change to the Atlantic for alternative supply.

### Suezmax

Outstanding West Africa volume for the third decade is likely nearing 10 mbbls, with 4–5 Guyana cargoes still to cover and likely a similar number from CPC. Further Brazilian volumes may also emerge, with VLCC availability remaining short in the Atlantic for July laycans. The front end of the Suezmax Atlantic list is fairly balanced; however, renewed Middle East tensions could see vessels waiting on MEG business ballast towards the Cape, offering charterers more end-July/early-August options. Conversely, if strikes persist, Asian buyers may turn to the Atlantic again, renewing forward buying and helping absorb any excess tonnage.

### Aframax

#### North Sea

The main headline this week has been vessels ballasting. Tonnage list in the North Sea has been trimmed with tonnage heading mainly to USG but by the middle of the week heading to the Mediterranean with the busy firming market becoming the destination of choice. North Sea has remained steady so far with not enough activity to be affected by the smaller tonnage list. Natural dates should start to push towards 3rd decade.

#### Mediterranean

We are slowly heading into 3rd decade of July and rates for local runs are close to the WS 200 mark. Multiple factors are positively affecting the market and the owners have built some proper summer sentiment for now. There are still outstanding cargoes for the 2nd decade and rates are expected to remain under further upward pressure.
