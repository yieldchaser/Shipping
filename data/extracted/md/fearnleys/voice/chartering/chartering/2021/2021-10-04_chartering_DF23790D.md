## Market Commentary

EAST A couple of Indian cargos being discussed for last decade of October, together with two Australian cargos fixed on subs for end October, sums up an otherwise muted week in the East. Public holidays in China and Korea have kept activity on the low, and most of the focus has been on the West where activity has picked up. Freight levels in the MEG seems to have stabilized around the mid USD 40s mark for now, and despite more ships being sent back West we are still left with a variety of open ships from the main owners and traders in MEG from last decade of October into early November. WEST As October positions are wiped out from the USG position lists, all eyes are now on first half November freight. Low USD 80 was done on a handful of occasions during early November, however, the market is now established in the mid USD 80’s Houston/Chiba with even a couple of fixtures just north of this level. Second decade November freight enquiries are increasing in frequency, and if owners’ get their way, we may see rates pointing north of mid USD 80’s.
