## Market Commentary

EAST September fixing is now starting to find its pace in the Middle East with the release of Adnoc acceptances on Tuesday evening. In addition, we understand there is a handful of first decade Australia cargoes in the woodwork, as well as an FOB tender from QP that was awarded yesterday. This month's last round of acceptances from Aramco are only expected to be published earliest on Sunday meaning quite a few charterers still need to wait for their dates. That said, charterers with a firm program at hand might consider making a move before high 40's levels potentially won't be on the table anymore. WEST The market has been far more active this week with rate levels, till now, hovering the mid-USD 80’s/ton for the transpacific run and mid-USD 40’s for the transatlantic run. Slowly, but surely, freight rates are picking up and entering into the mid/high USD 80's/40's with focus on second and third decade September cargoes. At these levels, ships are trading at low/mid and mid/high USD 30,000’s per day, respectively. Panama delays have come off to some extent, down to about six days each way at time of writing, however, it remains to be seen for how long this trend will last. If this does persist, we may see an opening for a further increase in freight rates going forward.
