## Market Commentary

EAST While the shipping market in the East has been fairly uneventful during the last couple of weeks, we have finally started to see more cargoes appearing in the market. A couple more fixtures were concluded for the last decade of March. Combined with more Owners sending their ships back West on speculation earlier, we are probably closer to a floor on shipping in the East than before, as the AG position list is slowly starting to look more balanced. WEST A couple of fixtures were concluded this week ex USG at rates hovering around OPEX levels. Based on the last done deals in the MEG and in the USG (mid/high USD 50’s H/C and high USD 20’s RT/C), the West market is trading at a small premium to the East but hopes are that more activity will surface on the back of the deals. Having said that, we do not expect any significant changes to the vessel balance versus cargo inquiries ex USG for some time. A West Africa FOB tender was floated this week as well and did receive some interest from various traders.
