## Market Commentary

EAST Adnoc acceptances were released yesterday, this has created some long-awaited vessel demand in AG. That said, most Charterers are in no rush prior to the long weekend coming up with CNY around the corner. Many of the vessels still open on Feb dates in the East are likely to go via Cape and end up in the US last decade March. The same could also be the case for those with potential early march positions, depending on demand and freight levels. WEST Following a couple of weeks with a fair amount of activity in the West and dominantly the US, the market has taken a bit of a breather this week. A few fixtures have been done, and it seems the freight level established last week in the mid-80’s pmt for the transpacific freight route, has prevailed this week. As we see it, US cargoes for the first decade of March seem to have been covered more or less, however, the Chinese New Year’s celebration seems to halt activity so we’re in for a quiet period until next week.
