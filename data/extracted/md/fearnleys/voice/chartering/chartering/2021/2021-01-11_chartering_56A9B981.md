## Market Commentary

EAST The acceptances from Adnoc were released over the weekend and since then we have seen a handful of deals concluded in MEG for 1st decade February loading. We have also seen some additional shipping length from traders being marketed, and as the majority of these are yet to be booked this has turned a climbing Baltic sentiment, for now. We are still also waiting for the Aramco acceptances, but it remains to be seen if these could have any significant effect on new shipping requirements as there are some talks of cuts from Saudi in February. WEST West of Suez we saw a range of deals concluded on shipping ex USG with freight rates peaking at some USD 110k per day or very high USD 180's per ton Houston/Chiba equivalent. Fixing activity has slowed down a bit this week compared to last, however, the balance of February is still looking relatively tight due to amongst others limited northbound Panama slots being available for pre-booking. Bids from would-be charterers are currently somewhat lower than the fixtures we saw concluded as of last week, but we have yet to see such bids hit.
