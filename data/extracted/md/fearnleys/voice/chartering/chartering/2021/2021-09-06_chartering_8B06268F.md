## Market Commentary

EAST Market East of Suez currently finds itself in a bit of a waiting mode prior to the next round of acceptances. Those with vessels on September dates in the East are hoping for some late running vessels or some last-minute Indian cargoes to avoid too much waiting time on their vessels. Any remaining potential September spot cargo in MEG would be much sought after by those with vessels open in this window hence competitive freight should be on the table. WEST Activity is halting in the West VLGC market. Not only is there a mismatch between bids and offers in the VLGC freight market just now, but there is an inherent lack of willingness to pursue what would make sense to conclude, simply due to what the arbitrage allows and last done fixture ex USG is too far apart. Freight and product will at some point find its feet again, but it seems time being that freight has to give. There are three vessels still left for the balance of September and first half October counts about fifteen or so VLGCs, meaning that currently, it is somewhat balanced and in control of largely two pure owners. Furthermore, we are yet to hear of any cancellations for October.
