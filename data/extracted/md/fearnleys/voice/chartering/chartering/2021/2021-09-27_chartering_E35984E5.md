## Market Commentary

EAST The East Baltic benchmark jumped up on the back of last done ex MEG at USD 44 Ras Tanura/Chiba which was concluded earlier this week. Seeing that the 'pure' owners are inclined to send their vessels in ballast towards the West in search for higher earnings, it may have a knock-on effect on the MEG market. Of a vessel line-up consisting of about ten vessels (give or take) left open for the balance of October, about half of these are relets. WEST In the western hemisphere, eyes are slowly but surely turning towards November enquiries and subsequently, what's in store. The typhoon currently passing the Pacific Ocean seems not to have any severe impact to the shipping supply both in the MEG and in the USG. End October has during the course of the week seen a handful of relets appearing on the horizon, however, these vessels have yet to affect the market significantly and the last concluded Houston/Chiba rate was in the very low USD 80's off early November dates. October has about six or seven vessels left and seeking employment opportunities and hopes are that after the US stats are released today, there will be an increase in activity.
