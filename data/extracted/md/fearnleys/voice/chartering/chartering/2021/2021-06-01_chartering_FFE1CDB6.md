## Market Commentary

EAST Rates have continued to drop in the East as we are yet to see any real activity in June, while still being left with a healthy amount of open tonnage. One trader was reported fixing a ship close to usd 50 Baltic for mid-June load, but it remains to be seen if this level will be repeated for current fixing window (last decade of June). Meanwhile we are starting to see more Indian enquiry in the market, and we also expect there to be uncovered cargos left from other players before we can close the month off. But it is still likely that some of the ships open for June will have to take waiting time and opt for July cargos after we start seeing more acceptances come out for next month. WEST The market has continued its somewhat slow pace from last week, as the charterers have more-or-less concluded their June lifting program, and both owners and charterers are getting ready for the next fixing window. We have already seen some vague inquiries for July, but so far nothing done. A market call cargo for first decade July loading, has attracted the attention of some owners, and are currently ongoing. There are only less than a handful of ships avail for June loading, and still some uncertainties on available ships for early July, so owners’ ideas have kept rather stable. As such, and with a market somewhat in the doldrums where the arb is allowing for far lower freight rates than the owners are willing to consider, we have as usual under such circumstances seen some proposals and requests for Baltic related deals. Last done at time of writing, is an end June lifting with freight calculated at an average July Baltic average. We have had another week with a discount for the transpacific route compared to the BLPG1 route, whereas the transatlantic route, although not as liquid, is trading at a healthy premium.
