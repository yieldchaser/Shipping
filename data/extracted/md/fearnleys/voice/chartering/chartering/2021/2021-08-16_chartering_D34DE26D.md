## Market Commentary

EAST The final acceptances for September were published on Monday and since then we have seen a handful of companies, including 3 Indian majors, checking the market for freight during 1H Sep. Prior to this the sentiment on freight was looking weaker in the AG (mainly due to a healthy supply of trader relets), but with more cargos in play we expect fixing ideas to be adjusted accordingly. Last reported deal in market (for non-India) was done in the usd low-mid 40s Baltic, and we expect next deal to be done around similar territory or higher WEST Last week we saw freight rates strengthening on the back of an improved arb US/East causing several Charterers to enter the market for shipping at the same time. A couple deals was concluded in the mid/high $80’s at the end of the week, but the unexpected draw in US inventories caused arbs to narrow and consequently put interest for freight at a standstill. That said, on an available ships vs. reported uncovered/available cargoes basis, one could argue that the market looks fairly balanced for September liftings.
