## Market Commentary

EAST With an increase in activity out of West African ports coupled with a continuing strong West market taking out a few vessels from the Middle East Gulf list of positions, we have seen Baltic climbing the last couple of days. We expect activity to pick up together with the release of Adnoc and Saudi acceptances later this week and early next week respectively. Meanwhile, there are still some ongoing discussions for end September dates in the MEG. WEST September US Gulf positions were finally absorbed during the course of the last week, and all eyes have shifted to October loadings. A large chunk of the first half October USG positions have already been cleared at rates in the mid/high 90’s Houston/Chiba, despite the product market trying to suggest otherwise. There are reportedly some FOB avails during first half October ex the US, however, opportunistic buyers seem time being to be halted by owner’s freight ideas.
