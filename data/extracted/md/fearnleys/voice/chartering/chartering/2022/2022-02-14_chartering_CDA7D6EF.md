## Market Commentary

EAST After a slower than usual month for February fixing with ‘only’ 13 deals concluded in the MEG, March has gotten off to a hot start and we currently count a dozen deals fixed (or still on subs) for 1st decade March loading. Nevertheless, last done rates still remain in the mid USD 40s Baltic region, as we are still left with a good variety of owners and traders relets even after more activity in market. Going forward the focus will be on the pending Saudi acceptances, and there are also a couple of fob tenders from Middle Eastern suppliers for mid and end March. WEST With freight rates seemingly stable for now we find the West premium comfortably around USD 10 pmt before waiting in the Panama Canal and last done fixture concluded at USD mid/low 90s Houston/Chiba range. It seems that February and first decade March enquiries ex USG is more or less spoken for, and for the first time in ages there is a prompt open vessel available in the US Gulf. With that said, it remains to be seen if there are enough cargos for the vessels coming open first half March in the USG.
