## Market Commentary

EAST Adnoc dates were published earlier this week with reports of some lifters getting advanced. Fixing activity for 1H Nov has been minimal so far but we have seen a couple deals concluded at high $80s RT/C. With some more delays in India and China the position list looks tight for first half Nov in East. How much OPEC+ oil cut from Nov onward would impact to the LPG production in MEG remain to be seen, but the sentiment in the East remains stable/strong so far. WEST The Western freight market also continues being strong now trading at parity with the East. At the time of writing we count approximately 25 spot fixtures out of the USG so far for November with last done deal concluded at high $130s Houston/Chiba and mid $70s H/F. The last remaining vessels for early Nov have been programmed and focus is now fully on mid and end month dates. Vessel availability this period is tight, especially 10-20 Nov range, and with the arb widening in addition to an increase in waiting days in the Panama Canal we remain bullish.
