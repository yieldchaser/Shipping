## Market Commentary

EAST With Singapore off for National day on Tuesday the market naturally took a small breather. So far just a few charterers have been showing interest in early September laycans, but with Adnoc acceptances out we expect more shipping requirements to follow. As the cargo lineups for next month starting to take shape we will eventually also get a more accurate picture for the shipping balance into September. So far this week the pace of activity has not followed as we have only seen one fixture concluded. Last done deal for August dates concluded at low 90s H/C while we see around 100 for September dates. There are still some ships left open in USG with two of them prompt open. The arb has narrowed a bit lately which has not been good for freight.
