## Market Commentary

EAST Even after a very active week in the MEG there are still a good number of vessels available on September dates. There are potentially a few more uncovered cargos left for September, including talks of two Indian majors being short end September. We have seen almost 30 spot fixtures concluded so far this month in the Middle East, which will be a ‘record high’ in terms of spot fixtures for the last few years. With the Qatar acceptances around the corner we expect fixing window to shift to next month in the coming week. WEST A bank holiday in London on Monday did not stop players from making a move in the Western market with three fixtures concluded in the USG - two of which from a big shipowner. The same tempo has continued further into the week, and we have seen a handful of fixtures so far with last done at around USD mid/high 100s H/C and high 50s H/F. The market is currently in the hands of few players as we on Tuesday saw the last remaining ships open in September get absorbed and we can now count 25 spot fixtures for the month, 3 more than August. Focus has now completely shifted towards early October dates and fixing is already well underway as charterers seem keen to secure a ship before it gets tighter.
