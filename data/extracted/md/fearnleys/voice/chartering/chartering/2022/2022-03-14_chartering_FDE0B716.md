## Market Commentary

EAST The momentum in Baltic has slowed down and for the first time in 2 weeks the BLPG1 print showed a small drop. This is partly due to less activity and more trader relets appearing in market for 1H April, but also due to bunker prices seemingly finding some ‘stability’ compared to previous weeks. As a result of more upstream production, we have also seen a couple of spot fob cargos for sale from Middle Eastern suppliers. But so far this has not triggered any significant activity on shipping yet, and we are (time of writing) still waiting for the Saudi acceptances for April. WEST A couple of leftover March vessels are being targeted for some final attempts to secure March FOB loaders at freight rates in the low USD 100’s Houston/Chiba and mid USD 50’s Houston/Flushing. First half April seems more or less done in terms of spot activity and all eyes are on second half April where there is a vessel requirement and FOB/CFR tender from an Indonesian major. Under the current environment, Houston/Flushing is trading at a mere USD 6,500 premium to that of Houston/Chiba and thus far in March there are six vessels already on the way towards North West Europe and it will continue to rise – that’s about the same number of VLGC liftings ex USG+USEC to NWE seen on average over the past three months. Although activity isn’t booming so far this week, we think there is definitely more to come.
