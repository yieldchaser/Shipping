## Market Commentary

EAST We have seen a handful of end May/early June cargos being worked in the Middle East this week. As the availability of open ships with firm schedules in end May is becoming less, owners’ ideas have increased and last done was concluded in the low USD 70s Baltic. With Adnoc dates published the focus has shifted to June, and we expect to see more activity in the days to come for 1st decade. WEST The vessel supply in the West is tightening as Panama waiting currently has increased to 15 days northbound and 8 southbound in the Neo canal. We are therefore seeing more vessels coming in ballast to the US via Singapore from the Far East to also have a peek in the MEG. Fixing 1H June seems to be as good as done and focus has now shifted towards 2H with last done concluded at mid high USD 120s H/C and low 70s H/F. With a tight position list, Panama delays and an improving arb we expect freight rates to increase.
