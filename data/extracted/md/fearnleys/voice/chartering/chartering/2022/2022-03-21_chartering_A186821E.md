## Market Commentary

EAST There’s been a handful of deals concluded or placed on subs for Middle East second decade April cargos this week, mostly in the high 50s freight range. East/West freight differential is again pointing in favor of the West and with less waiting in Panama (latest at 4 days both North and South bound). This will attract more ships away from the East. Foreseeing less vessels available coupled with VLSFO bunkers increasing some 6-7% since last Friday on the back of again rising crude - we expect freight to move back above the 60-dollar mark from here. WEST This week has already seen over twice as many fixtures compared to last week and the position list is now mainly dominated by pure shipowners. April cargoes are still in play and slowly but surely, early May is coming within fixing range. Although there have been over 30 spot fixtures for April thus far, we expect the last remaining handful of end April vessels to soon find suitable cargos. The last done deals ex USG/USEC were concluded at USD low 100s H/C and low USD 50 H/F, and owners are attempting to pull freight upwards from here. Time being, the arb is widening and vessels supply looks relatively tight and in control of a few owners for the front of May and it seems freight will increase gradually as time progresses.
