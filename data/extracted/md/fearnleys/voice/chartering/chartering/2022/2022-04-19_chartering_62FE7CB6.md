## Market Commentary

EAST Freight in the East remains firm and last done deal for non-India was concluded in the high $60s baltic. The fixing window is still mainly concentrated on 1st half of May, but there is one FOB tender for last decade of May that has received some attention. Time of writing there's an Indian cargo being worked for fairly prompt dates, and as competition is rather limited we expect this to be concluded at a premium. WEST The Western market continues to be strong with last done deal concluded at USD 125 Houston/Chiba range and USD 69 Houston/Flushing. Currently the freight is a bit stronger than the arb, but the vessel supply looks relatively tight so we expect the strong rates to maintain. The high demand we saw last month in Northwest Europe persist and the time of writing there are seven vessels heading this way so far in April. In all likelihood this will continue in the next months to come.
