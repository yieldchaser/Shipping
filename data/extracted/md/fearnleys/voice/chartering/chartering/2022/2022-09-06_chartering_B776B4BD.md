## Market Commentary

EAST We counted 29 spot fixtures ex MEG in August, which as of end August is the highest number of fixtures done in the last couple of years. September will however be even higher, and we currently count 31 spot fixtures concluded but with potentially a few more to be done. We are at the time of writing still counting about 4-5 open spot vessels available in the MEG for September, and the overhang into October is likely to be a bit less than that, if any at all as there still are some cargos being worked for September dates. WEST The week started with US off on Monday due to Labor Day, but that did not stop market players making deals in the West. So far this week there have been a handful of deals in the USG fixed between $110-112 H/C range and around $60 H/F. Fixing first decade October have seemingly come to an end, and with the latest fixtures included we count 13 deals with early October laycans, which is the most since May and June. Moving further into October the position list looks tight and traders already sniffing in last decade to secure a vessel. Bad weather makes scheduling and itineraries more uncertain and is helping an already strong Western market get even tighter. Freight is set to rise moving into the second half of October.
