## Market Commentary

EAST A couple of vessels were taken out in the East at the very end of last week in the mid $60s. Positions did slim down as a result, and with potentially a few late quotes from Indians or some ships running late - next deals could quickly climb toward high $60s in the East. However, this has yet to materialize. When comparing some 18 concluded spot fixtures for April with an average month, it is fair to say that the East market is currently looking well balanced as we gradually look towards May and the next round of acceptances. WEST With Easter just around the corner the US market has in the span of just two days seen more than a dozen cargoes quoted and above half of these already placed on subs. Most of the fixing is now focused in and around the second decade of May and we expect most market players would be counting on sorting their programs up until the beginning of third decade prior Easter. Freight levels in the first round of fixing this week has been largely hovering around 112 H/C and low 60 H/F. Owners ideas for fixing today, from those willing to show ideas, are at the time of writing being bumped up by at least 2-3 dollars on top of last done. Time will tell if the market is currently a bit overheated or if it’s a bad idea to gamble for the dust to settle.
