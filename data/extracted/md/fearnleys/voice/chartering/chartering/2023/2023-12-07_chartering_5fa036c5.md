## Market Commentary

EAST A so far dull week in the East with nothing much to report in terms of fresh fixtures or cargo enquiries. One deal from last week failed tech subs yesterday and this cargo will reportedly go back domestically meaning we at the time of writing actually count one less spot fixture compared to last week (14 down from 15). We still have some 3 and 1/2 weeks left on the calendar this year and we expect that some additional December cargoes will appear towards the tail end of the month before we start to focus on 2024 cargoes. That said, with the current sentiment in both East and West we see further downside on freight from Middle East right now and charterers seems to be taking their time. WEST The Western freight rates continue falling and the BLPG3 has dropped almost 25 dollars so far this week. It is the first time in over 40 days we find it below the 200-dollar mark. In total we have seen 28 spot fixtures out of the USG/USEC for December dates and 10 for January. The position list still showing a couple vessels left open in December while potentially seeing 7 open in 1H Jan – but a few ones could go into own program making actual count of vessels slimmer.
