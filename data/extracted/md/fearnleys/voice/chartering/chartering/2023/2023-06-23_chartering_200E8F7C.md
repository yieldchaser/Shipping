EAST Earnings are now getting into the USD 100,000 per day on a roundtrip basis in the MEG. Freight seems to be rather balanced in the East, and after seeing a robust upsurge lately it seems to be stabilizing slightly now. WEST The BLPG3 index route has increased with over 25 dollars so far this week, but we are still seeing the West trade at a small discount to the East. July fixing ex USG is seemingly concluded, and focus is now fully on August with last done at mid 170s H/C. At the time of writing the position list for the first half of August ex USG is looking tight with only a small handful of firm open ships available. In addition, the waiting time in the Panama Canal is expected to increase as new draft restrictions to save water will affect the number of daily transits. That being said, we believe todays strong rates to stay put unless the arb dictates otherwise.
