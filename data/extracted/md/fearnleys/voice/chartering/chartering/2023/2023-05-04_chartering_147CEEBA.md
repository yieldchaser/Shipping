## Market Commentary

EAST Despite many players being kept away from the office during Golden Week, the market in the East was very active yesterday - mainly thanks to one big supplier. So far at least five ships was placed on subs for 2H May dates ex AG on Wednesday, in addition to that there is now one India tender cargo that have been rolled over until to today. Sentiment is now looking strong on the back of a shorter position list and increased activity. There should still be enough ships available for the rest of May – but many of the remaining vessels are likely West candidates and the obvious East candidates is slimmed down quite significantly now. WEST Freight rates in the Western market has come off from mid-$130’s to $130 within the last week, but it now seems that freight rates are on the rise again on the back of a widening arb and relatively tight availability of ships. We continue to see large exports on VLGCs from the US and with April ending at close to 90 liftings and a consistent ratio of around 70% for East destinations, this increases the ton mile significantly for the sailing fleet and supports the bullish freight environment we have witnessed so far this year.
