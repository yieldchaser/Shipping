## Market Commentary

EAST With a descending West market the AG freight floor has followed and we are now seeing both regions trading closer to parity. It looks like we are more than half way through second decade of December fixing while the last decade likely will be the focus of next week. Seeing a good selection of both Owner controlled vessels and Trader re-lets available for second half of December might mean we still need freight to adjust a bit further before Charterers are tempted to move forward. WEST There are still a few open positions left in December USG although the majority of this weeks deals have been done for January dates. In total we now count 26 spot fixtures for December and 8 for January. After the arb recently narrowed about 30 dollars we have seen western freight rates drop almost 20 dollars on the BLPG3 index route this week. Despite this drop we are seeing Panamaxes trade at a huge premium of 40-50 dollars to Houston/Chiba via Pan route and three have been fixed the last few days - one of which almost two months ahead.
