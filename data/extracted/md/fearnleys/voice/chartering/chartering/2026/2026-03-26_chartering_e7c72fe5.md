## Market Commentary

After an initial softening in rates two weeks ago, Owners have since responded by decisively hardening their rate expectations, most notably in the USG where fixing activity has gathered pace. Any incremental supply has been swiftly absorbed as Charterers rushed to secure Far East cover. April has proven to be a record‑breaking month, with 45 spot fixtures concluded out of the USG, including 21 fixed for last‑decade April loading-underscoring the strength of demand and the firmly Owner‑led tone of the market.
