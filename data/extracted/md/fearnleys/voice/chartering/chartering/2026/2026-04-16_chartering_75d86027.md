## Market Commentary

Spot activity out of the USG accelerated further last week, with more than 25 fixtures reported. Rates have surged from around USD 170/t a week ago to as high as USD 225/t H/C via Panama Canal - the strongest level seen in over two years. At USD 225/t, earnings equate to approximately USD 115,000/day TCE for a modern conventionally fuelled VLGC, while current ask for freight is at $250/t, but we have not heard reports of any deals concluded at these record high levels yet. The recent intense fixing activity, combined with ongoing Panama Canal congestion forcing ships to sail via Cape of Good Hope, has effectively absorbed most available tonnage ex‑USG through early June. This tightening of supply is particularly notable given the absence of AG exports for the past six weeks.
