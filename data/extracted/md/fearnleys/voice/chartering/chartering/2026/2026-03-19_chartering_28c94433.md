## Market Commentary

Escalating tensions in the Middle East have effectively frozen MEG trading, with the Strait of Hormuz remaining largely inaccessible. While discussions persist around potential mitigation measures, including US‑led naval convoys, the ongoing uncertainty has driven a sharp spike in bunker prices. Tight supply conditions and limited availability have pushed VLSFO levels toward the $1,000 mark, prompting Owners to draw a firm line on rate expectations-most notably in the USG, where activity has accelerated markedly. The USG saw more than 21 fixtures concluded last week alone, pushing the fixing window into early May and driving rates from the low $130s H/C to the mid $170s today within last 7 days. Despite the possibility of additional tonnage ballasting from the MEG, the effective removal of MEG liftings from the supply chain has kept the arbitrage firmly open. Any incremental supply has been rapidly absorbed, as Charterers moved quickly to secure prompt tonnage to meet sustained Far East demand.
