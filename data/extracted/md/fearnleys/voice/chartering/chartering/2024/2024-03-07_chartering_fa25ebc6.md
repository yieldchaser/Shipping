## Market Commentary

EAST The Eastern market has been very quiet so far this week barring one previously fixed ship dropped by an India PSU. Tokyo conference would be the first subject to blame for the inactivity, but a well supplied position list in MEG puts charterers in a relaxing position. However, the sentiment seems to stabilize due to a shorter list and widening arbitrage West v East, this could potentially attract more vessels toward the West and at the same time balance things out in the East. WEST Fixing activity in the West has been slow this week with only one spot fixture reported, and we count 4 deals done for April ex USG/USEC so far. Vessel supply in 1H April is looking tighter as a handful of ships were also programmed under respective COAs this week, and we are now left with 8-9 ships open for this period. Last done concluded in the mid 110s H-C and mid 60s H-F, we expect activity to start picking up once people start to return from the Tokyo LPG conference.
