## Market Commentary

EAST US freight came up slightly this week while levels in the Middle East was moving in the opposite direction. Availability in the East generally looks longer than in the West, but with many of the end April vessels in the MEG also appearing on the West list we could possibly see some of them seek cargoes in the US. A total of 22 spot fixtures have so far been fixed in the Middle East on April dates versus an average so far this year at 23 (April last year we counted 25 spot deals on April dates ex MEG and Yanbu). WEST Fixing in the Western hemisphere looks to be more or less finished for April dates, counting 32 spot deals out of the US. For May we have already seen a few fixtures done and there are currently several players out looking for freight in the first half of the month. The vessel supply for first decade May is looking relatively tight seeing 5-6 available positions - half of them being relets. We see the tightness continue into the second decade as we find last done fixture concluded for May dates at mid 120s H/C.
