## Market Commentary

EAST / WEST Since the Easter break, the market has been asleep in the midst of various geopolitical developments relating to shipping tolls, tarrifs and general market directional factors. The much talked about arbitrage ex USG/USEC has yet to stabilise and as such received a lot of attention, which it normally does when the shipping market is quiet. The ship availability remains largely unchanged from last week. There have been few attempts to to fix vessels this week and last done remains at H/C via Panama at USD 103. One vessel was subfixed ex Soyo this week, however, it is still on technical subjects reportedly. Lately, we have seen a trend in the market of a few busy fixing days followed by a quiet week or more, when the Baltic suddenly rid any momentum gained during the busy days and numbers keep coming off, simply due to inactivity.
