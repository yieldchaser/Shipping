## Market Commentary

EAST Calm before the storm – and calm again.. While a few ships were taken out also in the East yesterday, second half of this week have been very much about the West. A late floating Indian PSU cargo 27-28 March ex MAA is expected to go at a high premium due to the timing of the cargo. Further fixing in the Middle East may be slowing down until we have Aramco acceptances expected early next week. By that time more early April positions should also have less blurry itineraries. WEST The week started out with freight ideas hovering around low USD 90s, however at the time of writing, Owners’ ideas are now above USD 100 dollar per ton and we have seen 15+ sub fixtures concluded since Monday. For the month of April we are now at 31 fixtures (excluding vessels committed under COA), and as such we are left with tight vessel supply for the balance of the month. The remaining open ships in the West are mainly Owner controlled tonnage, although there are still potentially a few traders able to give out freight.
