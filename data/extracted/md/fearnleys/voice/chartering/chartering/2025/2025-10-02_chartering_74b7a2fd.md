## Market Commentary

The VLGC shipping market activity has been subdued both east and west of Suez so far this week. Narrowing arb econs and the annoucement of CP hasn’t helped, however, following year-to-date fixing history there should be room for more business to be done. Whilst the MEG position list may look long at first glance, more than half of these available vessels are also potential US candidates via Cape from 1H November onwards. We currently count 12 spot fixtures ex MEG in October (4/6/2 by decade) and 20 spot fixtures ex US in October (8/9/3 by decade). November fixing is yet to begin, however, some questions surrounding freight have submerged for early November with bids hoovering the mid USD 120’s with no takers yet.
