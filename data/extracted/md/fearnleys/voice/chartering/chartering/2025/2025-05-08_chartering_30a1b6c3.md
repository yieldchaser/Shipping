## Market Commentary

EAST Two Indian PSU deals done yesterday at a small premium to Baltic. Another Indian cargo got rolled over and will be worked today together with a non-Indian cargo meaning there are two uncovered cargos currently in play. A major fixed a relatively prompt cargo today at mid-50s which we assess as being outside the fixing window and hence the premium. A small uptick in activity have so far managed to pull freight up slightly in the Middle East but how much further upside there is to this market remains to be seen. WEST The week started off with the ever lasting question of the arb being open or closed and parallel to freight, who dictates who. Knowingly or unknowingly, the US market have for the time being adopted a ‘one week on-one week off’ schedule whereby lack of activity both on product and freight pushes the Baltic downwards and same with the arb. Nevertheless, freight has yet again been able to withstand most downward resistance and the market remains flat despite the arb values trying to suggest otherwise. Last done HCvP deal is at USD 103 per ton, however, it feels as if further momentum is relatively lackluster. A handful of vessels were fixed this week off first half June dates at 3-digit freight, and there are still a few left by the looks of it.
