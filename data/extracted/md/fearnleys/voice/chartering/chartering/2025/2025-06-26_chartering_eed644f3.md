## Market Commentary

EAST The gap in earnings between East and West narrowed on Thursday after a few days with a more stable situation in the Middle East. A market tender was concluded some 5-6 dollars below last done levels despite a relatively tight shipping market illustrating the diminishing risk premium in the region. Looking ahead the freight market for mid July dates look set to stabilize around $80. WEST In July, we have recorded 27 spot fixtures, distributed as 11 in the first decade, 6 in the second, and 10 in the third. Additionally, one spot fixture has been registered for the first decade of August. Current arbitrage economics do not support the high $120s - $130s levels being sought by most Owners, but we did however see one spot fixture today on an ex DD ship at lower levels. Otherwise market is still in a holding pattern. While the remainder of July appears tight in terms of available tonnage, some of these vessels may ultimately roll into August. At present, we anticipate around 8 or more open ships for the first decade of August.
