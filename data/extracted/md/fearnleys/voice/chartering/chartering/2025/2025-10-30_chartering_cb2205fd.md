## Market Commentary

EAST Following the mutual suspension of trade penalty fees between the US and China, the VLGC freight market is showing renewed strength. With Chinese vessels no longer confined to the ME, shipowners have revised their freight ideas upward. Asking levels on the RT/C route are already into $60/ton and above - over $10 higher than yesterday’s last done. Where actual fixtures will settle remains to be seen at the time of writing. WEST Following a highly active close to last week, expectations were for a quieter start to this one. However, the market has defied those forecasts, with 11 USG fixtures already concluded this week. Rates have responded positively, climbing approximately $12/mt from recent lows. So far, 34 spot fixtures have been booked for November loading dates which is a notable increase from the 22 fixtures concluded in all of October, signalling a strong uptick in demand. Despite this surge in activity and firming rates, the USG still has 6-7 vessels open and ready to fix for November. This available tonnage is likely to cap further rate increases, keeping levels steady in the near term.
