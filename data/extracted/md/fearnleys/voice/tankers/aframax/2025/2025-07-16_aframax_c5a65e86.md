## Market Commentary

NORTH SEA The trend of relets and bigger vessels has continued into this week which has send both the sentiment and physical market soften. Very little market activity is pushing owners to look for alternatives but with the US markets also taking a hit on the Afra only the Mediterranean looks like a viable alternative at the moment. Very much a summer market at the moment. MEDITERRANEAN End month July dates are being worked quietly ex Libya with rates hovering around WS 130. The tonnage list has shortened and vessels with the right cargo and trade history are few and far between which in a busier market could give some upside pressure. But for now rates are moving sideways.
