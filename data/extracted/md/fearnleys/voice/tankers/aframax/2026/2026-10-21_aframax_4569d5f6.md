## Market Commentary

North Sea

North Sea activity has built through the week, with a lot of tonnage now on subs for end / early dates, in addition to vessel on subs, the available tonnage has been kept slimmer, with tonnage ballasting to fix in the Mediterranean and tonnage also being picked off for forward dates in the US. Paper was trending down due to lack of activity, but with a busy market a less populated list and alternative markets, it looks like we have found the bottom on the rates and owners' sentiment is looking to push again.

Mediterranean

Mediterranean remains strong and rates have bounced back a bit so far this week. Steady activity ex Sidi or STS Port Said is absorbing tonnage while the other local load ports should be sold-out for October. We have started to see early November dates for local runs and we expect rates to firm up again.
