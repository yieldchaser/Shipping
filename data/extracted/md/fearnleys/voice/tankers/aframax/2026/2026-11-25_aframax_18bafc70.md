## Market Commentary

North Sea

A shortening of available tonnage due to ballasting and vessels delaying especially in the Baltic has led to an active market for earlier dates than expected so far this week for end November/early December dates pushing rates up for those vessels with good itineraries. Early December on paper doesn’t look that busy stem wise but with some vessels remaining uncertain programming may not be as easy as it should be.

Mediterranean

Big discrepancy in rates between the different cross-Mediterranean runs with owners trying to push, activity however has been moderate for the 01-05 Dec window. Dates are pushing further now with a few cargoes asking quietly in the 5-10 window and tonnage list is looking balanced. Weather is still tricky so itineraries should be closely monitored.
