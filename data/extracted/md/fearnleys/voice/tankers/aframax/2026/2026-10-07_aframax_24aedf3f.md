## Market Commentary

North Sea The Aframax tonnage list in the North Sea is not looking too tight on paper. But the actual candidates for North Sea loadings in the current fixing window are more or less close to nothing. Surrounding markets are offering better returns for owners and will as such ballast out of the area. We expect further upward pressure on rates moving forward in October. Mediterranean Rates for the local Mediterranean/Black Sea market is at all time high levels. Despite plenty of vessels on the front end of the list and still quite a few of cargoes still outstanding, there is a Mexican standoff at the moment as charterers are trying to repeat levels around last-done while most of the owners are still pushing for higher. Cashing out on some positions seems to be a good choice though but sentiment prevails now over fundamentals.
