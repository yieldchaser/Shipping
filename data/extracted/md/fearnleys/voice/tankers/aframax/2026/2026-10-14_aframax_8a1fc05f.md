## Market Commentary

NSEA

A slow week so far with what looks like programming of tonnage taking out stems for the balance of the 2nd decade and pushing into the 3rd. There is a huge amount of tonnage coming from the US in October but we would expect a lot of that to head straight back rather than compete for Nsea business. So far this month we have seen less month on month ballast TA but expect this to pick up in the 2nd half of the month the list will be kept thinner in the Nsea with vessels leaving to the Med and US.

MED

Like the NSea Med activity has been muted so far this week but non compromised owners are still able to pick favourable cargoes and are holding back on just rushing in to offer which is still making it tricky for Charterers.
