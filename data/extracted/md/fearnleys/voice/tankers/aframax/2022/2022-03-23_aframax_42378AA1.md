## Market Commentary

Rates for the Nsea/Baltic Aframax market came off on the back of less activity. We still see quite high premiums for Russian trade despite a downward correction in rates. Some owners are not willing to fix current levels as the exposure for high bunker prices due to an unstable oil market is too high. Greater activity and firming rates in the USG/Caribs and the Euromed market might offer an alternative for those owners sitting with idle vessels on the Cont. Going forward we might see rates firm a bit.

**Med**

/Bsea: Over the course of the last week we have seen healthy cargo activity push the rates up about 20 points before it all slowed down again, and rates have come back down to where they were prior to the weekend. One to watch going into next week is further developments concerning Novo port and the CPC pipeline.
