## Market Commentary

Another week with lackluster activity in the Nsea and Baltic resulting in an oversupplied tonnage list for those cargoes being worked in the market. The current sentiment is soft with rates corrected downwards at levels perceived as the current bottom for now. Going forward we expect the market to move sideways with a soft undertone. We have seen yet another quiet week in the Med/Blsea area, with freight levels correcting downwards WS 20 point or so on benchmark routes. There is a soft sentiment prevailing in the region at the time of writing, this on the back of the revised and lighter CPC September Aframax programme and not seeing sufficient cross Mediterranean activity.
