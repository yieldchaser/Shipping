## Market Commentary

Yet another week in the Nsea/Baltic market without any positive changes to write home about. Some owners are desperately putting up some resistance, but slow activity and an abundant tonnage list neutralizes any attempts to push rates. However, a firm USG/Caribs market is offering an alternative for those owners willing to ballast. This might have a positive effect on the Nsea/Baltic market. Till then, rates are expected to move sideways moving into the next week. Much of the same can be said about the Med/Bsea market where there are enough prompt ships around to repeat the rate levels we have seen since last week. Any attempt from owners’ side to hold back on rates is replied by charterers taking ships out quietly off the market. Also, in the Med/Bsea we expect rates to move sideways in the short term.
