## Market Commentary

Rates in the Nsea and Baltic are firming on the back of continued activity ex Baltic where tonnage supply is minimal for any cargoes being lifted ex Russian ports. Rates in the Nsea are also under upward pressure for those looking for coverage for a lay can up to the 8th of April. Going forward, we expect the market to remain firm as we still expect more enquiries in the current fixing window. From a slight softening trend in the Med/Bsea last week, we have seen rates pick up again on the back of a tighter tonnage list. Increased cargo activity also supporting same. CPC outage still lagging, and quite a few owners still reluctant to entertain Bsea business. We expect the market to remain steady/firm in the short run.
