## Market Commentary

The NSea and Baltic market took another hit this week as some fixtures got done at new lows in the winter market. The abundance of vessels doesn’t help owners whose only choice is to sit idle or fix close to negative returns in order to re-position the ship and hope for an improved market on the next position. Moving forward, we don’t expect any dramatic changes, however rates might slightly move upwards as returns are currently close to nothing for some routes. As expected, rates in the Med/Bsea market came off a couple of points more this week, mainly due to the lackluster activity on the cargo side. We do not foresee a significant improvement to this market going into next week as tonnage has again started to pile up in the area.
