## Market Commentary

The Nsea market remained steady last week. Activity slowed down a bit as relets were used for own cargoes by charterers, but the thin position list preserved rates from falling. We expect market to remain strong moving forward in December and rates to move sideways. In the Med/BSea we saw high volatility on the rates last week as owners fixed well-below last done early in the week but managed to push back again. Market seems to be slightly oversupplied but healthy activity and a bunch of laden vessels stuck in the Turkish straits due to the P&I letter will help rates remain strong.
