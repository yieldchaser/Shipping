## Market Commentary

North Sea/Baltic took a step back this week on the back of weak cargo activity in the area. A few vessels ballasted out of the area to seek better alternatives but still this didn’t help the rates to improve. We expect rates to move sideways in the short term before we see some improvement later in August.

**Med**

/Bsea rates kept climbing for another week as Libya is back fully operational with a lot of prompt requirements. Supply of vessels in the area is still tight for cross-Mediterranean runs but also for CPC-approved vessels which helps owners to earn. Sentiment in the area is strong and we expect rates to remain steady for the week to come.
