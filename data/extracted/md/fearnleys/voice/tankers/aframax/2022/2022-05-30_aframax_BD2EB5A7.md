## Market Commentary

European Union leaders agreed in principle on a sixth package of sanctions against Russia preparing to finalize a ban on almost 90% of all Russian oil imports by the end of the year. This means refineries on the Continent will have to source oil in an even greater extent than before. As such we could see more intra-regional trade on some of the Nsea grades as more of the Urals could be shipped for East destinations. So far, the Aframax market across Europe is moving sideways and with some of the strategic export ports scheduled for maintenance we would need to see more volume before the market will improve.
