## Market Commentary

For Aframaxes in Europe we have seen another week where the market remained flat at bottom levels. Consistent oversupply of vessels is the key factor across the board as demand is not there to absorb the available tonnage. In the Baltic/Nsea we see a small number of ships floating with cargo on board, but nothing yet supporting a contango situation which could encourage floating storage. As we reach the second half of the monthly cargo program, rates are expected to remain flat. In the Med/Bsea, a few ships have been fixed out of Libya offering some optimism although not enough to put any pressure on rates. As it stands now owners are not seeing alternative markets to flee to, hence we don’t see the fundamentals change much in favour of owners in the natural fixing window.
