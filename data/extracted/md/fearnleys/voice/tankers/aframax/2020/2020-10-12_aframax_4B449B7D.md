## Market Commentary

Aframax owners trading in the North Sea and Baltic market have been in dire straits for the past few weeks. Rates have been hovering around bottom levels. For some owners it makes more sense to sit idle and wait than perform a voyage with negative returns. This is also the reason why some charterers have paid a couple points more than last done levels. The Mediterranean and Black Sea market remains low and steady, as benchmark routes have been fixed at repeat (bottom) levels for more than a month now. Apart from Libyan activity slowly recovering, there is little for owners to get excited about in the short run. We expect a soft market in the week to come.
