## Market Commentary

Aframaxes trading in the North Sea and Baltic area have seen rates hit rock bottom with earnings being close to nothing. With the current lack of activity and even oil company relets looking for employment we expect no improvements in the near future. In the Mediterranean and Black Sea rates have slowly crumbled down another 10 points or so during the course of the week. TCE’s on benchmark routes have fallen below OPEX levels and the general consensus in the market is that we are seeing bottom levels. In the week to come we expect the market to remain at same levels as we are still seeing a long tonnage list.
