## Market Commentary

In the North Sea and Baltic, we experienced a small uptick in rates mainly due to a couple of replacement jobs. However, this also added some upward pressure on rates for charterers working in the normal fixing window ex Baltic. Looking ahead the tonnage list looking a bit more balanced, and we expect rates to continue moving sideways unless we see any prompt replacement jobs. In the Med/Bsea we have seen a steady flow of activity continue this week. Rates have picked up a few points across the board, but benchmark routes are still only giving owners a few hundred dollars in returns. Albeit recent cargo activity the list of vessels FOC in the Med remains long, and we expect rates to move sideways over the holiday season.
