## Market Commentary

Rates in the Baltic and North Sea market bounced back this week and will continue its upward direction as we now move into the first decade of May fixing window. Increased cargo activity for both fuel and crude in the current fixing window coupled with numerous vessels that have and will leave the area for East discharge, delays in UK import terminals, and an increasing number of vessels tied up floating, are all factors creating a tight and firm market. Watch this space. Also in the Mediterranean and Black Sea the rates have taken a strong upwards leap this week as activity has been healthy. Fuel oil cargoes East bound have taken out ships and vessels coming open West Med have opted for participating in the firming markets in the North rather than try for cargoes loading East Med or Bsea. Going into next week we expect owners to hold back as the tonnage list gets tighter and rates continue upwards.
