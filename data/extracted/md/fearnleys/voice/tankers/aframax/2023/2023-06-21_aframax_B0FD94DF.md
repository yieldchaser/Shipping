## Market Commentary

NORTH Just as the sun never sets above the Arctic Circle during the summer months, the North Sea Aframax market seems like a prolonged journey repeating last done levels. Despite very good activity at the beginning of this week, rates have moved sideways. Some owners are looking to ballast to other load areas. But for typical North Sea players, it looks like next cargo won’t be till end month or early July dates at the earliest now.

**MED**

 More of the same in the Med/BSea as well, with activity picking up for end/early July but the owners didn’t manage to push rates higher. Tonnage list is pretty balanced at the moment, and we expect rates to move sideways unless 1st decade of July remains busy moving forward.
