## Market Commentary

Activity in the Nsea Aframax market has been quite decent, but rates have only moved slightly up on the back of this increased activity. The tonnage list is slowly thinning as ships also ballast or fix outside the Nsea basin. If the current activity level remains, then we could see rates firm a bit more. As a result, we see owners willing to fix premium cargoes ballasting to Baltic while others just looking for a local cargo before fixing window move too forward. We expect rates to move sideways in the short term with a potential to move higher if fixing activity increases.
