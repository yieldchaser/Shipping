## Market Commentary

Nsea has seen rates slip by around 15 ws points in the early part of the week. Surrounding areas also looking softer which has limited opportunities for ballasting. Available tonnage has started to thin especially for natural Nsea tonnage. Dates moving into 3rd decade. The Med has continued in a downward trend throughout the week. Stems for the month are all but covered with Owners on the lookout for early December stems. Natural tonnage in the Med lacks conviction off prompter dates but seems ready to pick up the influx of December supply of which we expect to see an increase. Suezmax market still weak and will have have further effect on Aframax freight levels. Sentiment is soft with additional corrections in this region in mind.
