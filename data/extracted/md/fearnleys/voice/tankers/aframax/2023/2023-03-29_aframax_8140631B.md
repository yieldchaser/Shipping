## Market Commentary

The North Sea Aframax market seem to have bottomed out at WS 180 for the time being and the tonnage list still looks tight in the front end, but then fairly balanced up to mid-month with a mix of oil company relets and independent owners in position. We have seen quite a few owners ballast away to fix in other load areas outside the North Sea basin. However, going forward we expect the market to remain around current levels depending on dates. It’s been a roller coaster week in the Med/Blsea area. The positive momentum took a U-turn end of last week following into this week. Charterers took a step back and managed to find alternatives offering them an escape. Even though the market has cooled off somewhat, owners still seeing nice returns on cross Med voyages. We foresee a soft undertone for the balance of the week.
