## Market Commentary

With a lack of activity in the North Sea and the tonnage list slowly building up, Aframax rates dipped further this week. Also, with surrounding markets not really offering a real alternative to ballast away, we might see rates hover around same levels for the near future. The Mediterranean market seems to be in an equilibrium state at the time of writing - with last done freight levels being achieved on benchmark routes. We expect the area to remain stable/soft in the current fixing window, unless activity levels pick up somewhat for the balance of 1st decade/mid-July dates.
