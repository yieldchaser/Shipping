## Market Commentary

North Sea market saw a busy start to the week with a few cargoes entering the market first thing Monday, but by the middle of the week activity has slowed. Dates pushing out well into to the 2nd decade with rates remaining relatively steady. With stem dates pushing out and limited activity the US markets has again attracted prompter tonnage to ballast. Owners are happy to repeat last done levels with dates now pushing out to mid-month continuing this sideways trend for now.
