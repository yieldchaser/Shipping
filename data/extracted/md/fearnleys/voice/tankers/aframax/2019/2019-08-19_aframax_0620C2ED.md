## Market Commentary

As the surrounding VLCC and Suez markets are currently picking up, the Aframax market in the Baltic and North Sea however is still hovering around bottom rate levels. Although we have seen a bit of activity the past week, such as Fuel Oil cargoes out of the area for Eastbound destination, and Baltic September dates in play, we are not expecting a noteworthy uptick in rates in the week to come. Following the flat market we have seen in the Mediterranean and Black Sea the past month or so, owners are regaining their optimism today as a spark in activity the last couple of days has resulted in a slight uptick in rates. Going forwards into September fixing dates we expect to see an upwards correction in rates as the Mediterranean and Black Sea market starts to recover.
