## Market Commentary

In the Nsea and Baltic markets, rates have firmed this week. A busier 3rd decade loading program in the Nsea and persisting ice conditions in the Baltic have helped owners gain more momentum to push for higher rates for end March loading. The tonnage list is opening up with more positions as we move forward, but in the short term we expect rates to remain steady/firm depending on dates and if non-ice or ice class vessels are required. Another active week has passed in the Med/Bsea with rates picking up another 30-40 points on main routes. As expected, the Suesmaxes have come into play now, however they are not capping the rate levels just yet. First decade fixing window out of Bsea looks healthy and we expect the market to remain firm in the short run.
