## Market Commentary

Aframax rates in the North Sea and Baltic moved sideways the last week. On a positive note, quite a few ships have ballasted out of the area heading to the US Gulf where higher TC returns offers a better alternative. Also, if the cold weather in Russia continues, some strategic export terminals might have ice restrictions coming into force. Moving into the 3rd decade December fixing window we might see an improvement in rates. In the Med/Bsea we have seen a slight firming trend over the last week that has given us another 10-point uptick in rates. That said, slower activity level the last couple of days has resulted in rates coming off again. A slim comfort to owners will be that the TCE of TD19 is still covering OPEX levels, however we expect the soft trend to continue towards the end of the week.
