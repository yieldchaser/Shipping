## Market Commentary

Finally, rates picked up in the North Sea and Baltic Aframax market with TCE returns at about USD 25k per day. The market is still looking interesting from an owner’s perspective on the back of a tighter position list coupled with a decent cargo activity in both the North Sea and Baltic. Going forward we call it steady/firm depending on load area and dates. In the Mediterranean/Black Sea we have seen rate levels drop 5-10 points so far this week. This is mainly due to less activity on the cargo side resulting in a healthier tonnage list. We expect the market to trade sideways the next couple of days, with a potential firming once we embark on December fixing dates.
