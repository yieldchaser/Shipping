## Market Commentary

As the tonnage list remains longer than the current cargo activity can satisfy, the Nsea/Baltic market has traded sideways at bottom levels yet another week. Going forwards we expect a bit more cargo activity in the 2nd half of September, however until then we expect the market to continue trading sideways. Much the same can be said about the Med/Bsea market, where the amount of ships outbalances the weak number of cargoes. We still need to see quite a few more ships be cleared away before we see any significant improvement of rates.
