## Market Commentary

As expected in the Nsea and Baltic market we had a downward correction as rates hit bottom level with earnings just about giving positive returns. Going forward we expect the market to continue hovering around bottom levels as the tonnage list is long and quite a few ships needs to be absorbed before we will see an uptick in rates again. Much of the same can be said about the Med/Bsea market. There is some optimism rate levels will pick up a few point in the next couple of days since 3rd decade fixing window ex Bsea looks decent and the tonnage list has tightened somewhat, however we are very close to Suezmaxes coming into play at these levels.
