## Market Commentary

As we head in to the Chinese Labour day holiday next week, it appears a lot of that business is being tidied up prior, with COA’s taking Chinese MEG tonnage up to the 20th May. The market has been on a steady climb this week and we have seen numbers for MEG/East creeping up to ws33.5 (cheaper older/handicapped tonnage are available). However, it is in the West where the majority of activity lies, with the Atlantic driving the market. Rates for West Africa coming on leaps and bounds to the ws40 level for East. The demand for modern, well approved tonnage in the USG is also drawing on the supply, meaning rates there creeping up to USD4.35m to China. There remains plenty of tonnage, but a little tightening has resulted in a firmer sentiment, and most VLCC owners are adept at driving sentiment when given the opportunity.
