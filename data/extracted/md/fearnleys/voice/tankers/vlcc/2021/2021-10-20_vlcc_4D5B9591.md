## Market Commentary

There is a palpable feel in the VLCC market that we are heading in to a firmer winter market, as sentiment amongst owners continues to build. So far, no major jumps, as the MEG has continued in the same vein, despite other areas pushing an upward trend. So far charterers have been able to find an alternative, be it an ex dd, or a vintage vessel etc. But, we have had 2 months of 120+ fixtures and November looks healthy. Still, the Atlantic is leading the charge. On the UKC, traders chasing bbls off ppt-ish dates (VL’s working same dates as Afra’s). If no VL’s are there at the right price, we could see Afra/Suez rates jump further as stems are split. More Atlantic volume will encourage modern ships to ballast from the East on speculation, giving the MEG a tighter feel it has not had in a while. An interesting week ahead.
