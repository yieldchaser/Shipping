## Market Commentary

There can be no denying a little uptick in activity, with several cargoes on the open market and more vessels finding employment. Maybe some green shoots of recovery, as reports of WS34+ MEG/China are doing the rounds which is likely not too wide of the mark. Modern owners holding for mid WS30’s, leaves the older ladies to compete at the WS30+ level, for cargoes that allow.

**West Africa**

 has been concluded at WS35 heading for India, in line with last done levels, and reports of multiple offers on uncovered enquiries require owners to hold their nerve. Elsewhere in the Atlantic, we've seen one fresh cargo N.Sea/Eastbound, but ships held on subs for over a week and cargoes trading and disappearing unlikely to add much strength for now. US export cargoes has been put on hold due to Hurricane Ida.
