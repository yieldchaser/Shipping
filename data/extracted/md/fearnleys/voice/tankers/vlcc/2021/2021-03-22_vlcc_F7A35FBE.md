## Market Commentary

A much better week for owners, as rates moved further in their favor. TCE’s are far from anything to celebrate, but coupled with slightly lower bunkers, returns are somewhat improved. In the MEG, the two tier market very evident, as older or ‘challenged’ tonnage struggle to ride the same wave as their modern equivalents. Charterers with slightly more relaxed vetting able to take ships at lower levels as was shown in Formosa taking an ‘05 built vessel at WS26.5, meanwhile the market was 6 points higher for modern. Today we are another half point up and owners will have the bit between their teeth for more. In the Atlantic, it was West Africa liftings that led the way, with rates climbing up through the WS30’s, as tonnage availability was squeezed off mid-April dates. Again, owners will be eyeing better numbers ahead.
