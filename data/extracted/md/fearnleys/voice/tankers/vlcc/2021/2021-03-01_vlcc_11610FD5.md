## Market Commentary

There is plenty of good news on the horizon regarding the volume and speed of the global vaccination process and Covid’s continued retreat (fingers crossed). Also expectations of an additional 1.5mbls injected in to the daily output after the OPEC+ meeting on Thursday, all point to good signs for our tanker market. Despite all this, a cursory glance at the fixtures achieved this week, certainly in the MEG, you would be forgiven for thinking otherwise. Charterers able to chisel a little more off last done, with MEG/East dipping to the WS30 level. However, some owners taking the approach of holding off, at least until after the OPEC outcome. In the Atlantic, the North Sea remains busy with a couple of deals ex Mongstad and Hound Point, drawing ships up on long ton-mile at slightly firmer numbers, latest at USD 4.3m to Korea.
