## Market Commentary

The sound of silence has been deafening in the VLCC market in the week gone by. Lack of MEG enquiry, a correspondingly long position list, galloping bunker prices, and charterers seeming well covered by own tonnage or COA arrangements. The Atlantic has offered a couple of opportunities, but every successive fixture has seen lower numbers concluded. All in all, a lacklustre picture, with daily returns yet again well below OPEX - and few if any signs of improvement in the short term.
