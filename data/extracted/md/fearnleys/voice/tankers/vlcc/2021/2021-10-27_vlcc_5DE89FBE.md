## Market Commentary

It has been a couple of weeks with steady increase in rates for the VLCCs. Driven initially by an active West market where charterers with long haul voyages to cover tapping up eastern ballasters, with ships open in the Atlantic hard to come by.

**East of Suez**

 also busy but with a position list filled with vintage ships or ex dd, the rates are not going through the roof quite yet. However, with owners’ sentiment strong, the rates are expected to increase as we move further into mid November laycan ex MEG. With the count now up to 50+ deals done, there is a lot of wood left to chop before we reach December, so upside potential in the days ahead.
