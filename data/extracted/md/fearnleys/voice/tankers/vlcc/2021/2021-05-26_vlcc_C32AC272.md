## Market Commentary

Last week was active with rates ticking in owners’ favour, but since Monday, charterers have been able to apply the brakes; holding off when possible and concluding deals off market. This air of inactivity usually plays on owners’ minds, and as such, without further volume, it is likely we are looking at a softer numbers in the near future. We ticked up to WS37.5 for MEG/East end of last week, but despite the best efforts of owners to hold the line, reports are ships are already firm at last done levels, with charterers aiming a few points lower (although it remains to test). June was expected to post the highest volumes of the year to date, so owners should not lose hope. With Iran coming back on line at some point in the near future, and potential OPEC production increases, Q3 looks a little brighter.
