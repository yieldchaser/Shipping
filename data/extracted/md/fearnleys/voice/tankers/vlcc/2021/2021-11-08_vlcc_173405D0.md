## Market Commentary

The sentiment has been waning in the VLCC segment in the week gone by. Rates have come under pressure as activity has gone quiet or under the radar, and MEG/Eastbound rates have been pushed below WS 45 for the best of them, with rebates to be had for tonnage not ticking all boxes. The Atlantic has fared a tad better, trading slightly north of the WS 45 mark for the benchmark Wafr/China route. The USG export sector has been off to a slow start for December with only a handful reported fixtures for the region. With worldwide crude demand almost back to pre-Covid levels longer term optimism remains strong, but short-term prospects are on the wobbly side, although the downside appears limited.
