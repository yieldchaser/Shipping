## Market Commentary

A busy week on the whole, but VLCC rates very much like an arm wrestle, edging to the owners’ side bit by bit as charterers fight for every inch. Most rate increases driven by the busier Atlantic market, as we have drifted firmly north of USD 5m in the USG to Ningbo and the latest West Africa deal able to squeeze a half point above last done to the WS 43 level. Similar in the MEG, as fractions of points are achieved on successive fixtures. No major swing just yet however and charterers may try to keep things under the radar, or hold off to keep a lid on things, but with 85 or so cargoes for MEG, still plenty to come 3rd decade. Worth noting some Japanese and Indian power utilities have boosted operating rates at their oil-fired units enabling them to limit the reliance on gas power generation and slow LNG-inventories.
