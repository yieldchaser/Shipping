## Market Commentary

"Much Ado About Nothing". Friday can’t come quick enough, in more ways than one. In the meantime, players are sitting on their hands in anticipation of the Strait of Hormuz opening. However, even if US and Iran put pen to paper we doubt we’ll see an immediate rush inbound any time soon. Seeing is believing and so on. Priority will likely be to get ships stuck in the Gulf out. The immediate urge for impulse oil buying may also be curbed as there are still tens of millions of barrels of crude stuck onboard those trapped ships in the Gulf, and refineries will need to process and digest this backlog first. There’s of course a rate out there for anything, money always talks, but the spread between what those owners willing to break the mould would want and what charterers would be willing to pay is going to take a broking job and a half to bridge. With the pot of gold at the end of the rainbow now becoming more tangible owners are naturally also pricing up all other less precarious alternatives as well. He who blinks first…
