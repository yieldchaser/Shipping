## Market Commentary

Activity has picked up this week, with several charterers having dipped their feet in for early December dates and a prompt end-Nov Fujairah/East cargo covered at WS 332.5 and end 1st decade Dec Fujairah/Korea at WS 335, up from last done. The front-end tonnage list has thinned considerably while uncovered cargoes continue to provide support, whilst still providing a cheaper alternative than Suezmaxes. While further upward risk is evident a Mexican standoff at the writing moment will test the resolve on both sides of the fence. The Atlantic has largely been left to Suez- and Aframaxes, with both segments having gained further momentum, and should charterers be tempted to scale up they’ll have few ships to choose from with natural positions in the current loading window, whilst potential ballasters from the East now benchmark a stronger Middle East market.
