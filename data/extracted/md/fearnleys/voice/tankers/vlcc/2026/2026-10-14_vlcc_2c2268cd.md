## Market Commentary

The VLCC market has gone a tad quiet on the surface but there is more going on than meets the eye. Earlier on in the week Saudi giant Bahri took a trio from Yanbu paying top dollar. Arguably the immediate pressure has eased from Fuj-GOO, partly as a result of Iran having ramped up attacks in the Strait of Hormuz but also due to a logistical backlog sorting out already concluded deals. Elsewhere Petrobras continues to be active having taken 3 ships so far this week for Brazil/east runs, chipping off a bit on last done for every successive fixture but still yielding euphoric returns close to $800k/day. Suezmax rates close to the W1000 mark Wafr/east continues to be a carrot for economy of scale and more ships will likely ballast west to seek their fortune in lieu of a slower pace from the Middle East. We may see a further downward correction, but it will likely be just that: a correction – and from historical highs.
