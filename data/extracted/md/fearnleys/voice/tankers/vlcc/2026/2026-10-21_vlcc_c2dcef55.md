## Market Commentary

VLCC rates have been under pressure in the week gone by, shedding 100-150 WS points depending on the voyages in question. The “weaker” sentiment is not helped by the composition of the position lists, littered with trader- and oil company relets. On the flip side we’re seeing more reports of oil supply increasing, with eg. MEG exports having surpassed prewar levels in periods over the last few weeks. Thus, the map is not exactly fitting the terrain currently, and something will have to give. We would not be too surprised to see some more “profit taking”, rates are still very, very good and risk/reward tilts in charterers' favour as they keep proceedings close to the chest and only drip-feed cargoes into the marketplace. A rumour doing the rounds suggests W650 has been concluded for a Fujairah/east run on another relet which will add fuel to fire if confirmed, but we should not be too far off the “sweet spot” where charterers start reengaging again in earnest.
