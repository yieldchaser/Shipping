## Market Commentary

Owners blinked first. The cycle seems almost predictable. Owners had their run past couple of weeks and were cruising around the WS 70 mark on TD3C. It is amazing what a few quiet days coupled with a bank holiday does to the sentiment. Charterers let the tonnage list build up and managed to take some wind out of the sails, with TD3C now in the low WS 60s. At circa 8.8m USG/East continues to attract ballasters from the East should help balance East and West. That and OPEC+ announcing that would unwind some cuts for June should put some positivity back in owners' camp and help.
