## Market Commentary

A combination of possible tightening of US sanctions on Russia, increasing OPEC oil production and being slightly behind the curve on the MEG end July program spurred owners to push rates up from WS 50 level MEG/East into the mid WS 50’s in the week gone by, despite ample tonnage supply suggesting otherwise. However, as US President Trump kicked the can down the road for 50 days on further sanctions and early August Saudi stems yet to have materialize, confidence within the owning community is showing signs of weakening. The West/East arb remain firmly closed and although the tonnage situation in the Atlantic is tighter than the MEG, inter-Atlantic movements have done little to add significant pressure.
