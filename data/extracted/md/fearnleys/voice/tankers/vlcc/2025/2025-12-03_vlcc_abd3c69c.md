## Market Commentary

The VLCC market has seen a downward rates adjustment in the week gone by from high W130’s to low-mid W120’s MEG/east as much of the activity has gone under the radar. But despite all the smoke and mirrors owners have shown a stoic restraint, fought “gravity” and held the line at now last done levels, and even trying for a bit more when an opportunity presents itself. The tonnage situation is still balanced, and tonnage is controlled on few hands.

**Baltic**

 estimates came in last night with very little change from the day before which suggests that the broking community is similarly inclined, despite maybe voicing the opposite. Another Brazil export cargo was quoted this morning, and quickly concluded at W107.5 (2.5 points down from last done) but other than that not very much to write home about in the Atlantic, and Suezmax rates are also on the defensive giving charterers more optionality .
