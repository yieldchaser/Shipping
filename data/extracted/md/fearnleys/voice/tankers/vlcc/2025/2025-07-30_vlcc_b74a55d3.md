## Market Commentary

Rates have continued to slip over the past week and are unable to find a bottom. The summer market continues and seems owners just want to get through it for now. Rates have slipped to a lever where shorter voyages are being preferred in all areas. Owners are shying away from committing to longer voyages and may start asking for small premiums. But for now, there is not enough volume to put an upward spin on the rates.
