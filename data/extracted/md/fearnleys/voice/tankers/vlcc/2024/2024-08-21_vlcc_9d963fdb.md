## Market Commentary

When the dust settled, August turned out to be a big month enabling rates to recover from the mid WS 40s in a matter of days, with WS 62.5 the highest paid for a MEG/Singapore run.

**MEG**

 1-10 September is far from done and owners will be trying to keep the momentum up. With Q4 around the corner, the shorter MEG/East runs are preferred over West Africa or Brazil, as owners want to keep short and are reluctant to lock in for longer voyages at the current levels (depending on the fleet size of course). As a result, West Africa, Brazil and the USG - heading East, are attracting a premium and it should be noted the tonnage situation in the West is also slim pickings. Owners went into September riding on the wave of WS 60s. But the usual tactic of drip feeding cargoes often played by charterers has been prevalent so far this week and this will strain owners confidence. Our view next week - East softer, West warmer.
