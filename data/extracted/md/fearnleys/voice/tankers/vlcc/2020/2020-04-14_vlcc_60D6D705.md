## Market Commentary

To paraphrase Hamlet; to cut or not to cut, that has been the prevailing question in the week gone by. We now know, and as a consequence, the VLCC community is hoping to get the last order in for that tray of shots before the bar closes. Suezmax owners have jumped the queue a bit, limiting V-owners choice of beverage, but by and large it looks like most will get served before April is over and probation kicks in. However, with oil prices continuing to fall, despite the cutback agreement – WTI currently trading at its lowest since 2002 – the contango is very much back in play, giving the owning community an extra outlet, and propping up spot rates at the same token. Reduced volumes will eventually take its toll, but the volumes to disappearing first will be the same as those which were about to disappear anyway, regardless of any OPEC+ agreement. In the meantime, there is life in the party yet.
