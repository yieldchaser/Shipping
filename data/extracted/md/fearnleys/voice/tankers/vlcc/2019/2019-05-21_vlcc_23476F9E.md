## Market Commentary

Owners persistency has paid off to some extent as VLCC rates are now in the low 40’s for Meg/East voyages. Earnings are still barely covering opex, however activity in the West and predominantly in the USG area has attracted a handful eastern ballasters for the next fixing window to come, early July. Market steady for now, but owners seem more optimistic as we are entering the summer months.
