## Market Commentary

The VLCC market has gone from strength to strength the last week, culminating at the writing moment with rates in the high W60’s from both MEG- and Wafr/east – yielding daily earning ast $40k or above. We are now at the last leg of first decade MEG cargoes, and when concluded it is not unlikely that rates will level out moving further into the month, as tonnage availability widens. The Atlantic has by and large been quiet, although Wafr stems have started to appear, whereas Brazilian export continues in an increasing tempo, keeping the firing burning I the west.
