## Market Commentary

A quiet end to last week and the same this week. The recent frenzy made charterers look well forward on dates , simply to secure tonnage. As it all turned out , it really was not necessary and now we have quiet days virtually all over to catch up to ‘normal volumes’. Some charterers are however out there and rates have inevitably softened , though to levels yielding earnings abt $80k/day. Levels only some weeks ago appeared we could only dream about. We do expect volumes both in Meg,Wafr and the Atlantic to come in to play in earnest and owners have far from forgotten what levels we saw at the ‘super peak’ we saw recently. Those levels may not be easily reached but hopes and expectations for the winter remains in full force in the shipowning community.
