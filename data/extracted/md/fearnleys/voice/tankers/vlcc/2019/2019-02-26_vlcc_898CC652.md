## Market Commentary

The brisk activity ex Usg for march has managed to pull the rates up for the other major VLCC-routes. Ships have ballasted on spec from the East towards Americas looking for increased tce’s which again has started to thin the lengthy tonnage-list for Meg and West Africa. Rates have corrected up , but one might argue that the latest sharp decrease in rates ex Meg and West Africa was A bit overdone. Therefore better balance of tonnage supply for the respective areas and rates found support at present levels.
