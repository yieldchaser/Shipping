## Market Commentary

With oil prices up, then down, it’s been a week where the bunkers dominate in determining the market levels, as opposed to any sentiment or supply and demand pressures. As owners refuel at the pumps, the calculated returns can dip dramatically after the (relatively) cheaper onboard bunkers are used up. But, as we see the bunker prices decrease, so does the rate. As for supply, by our count 180 vessels arriving Fuj in the next 30 days, speaks for itself, and with quoted MEG cargoes entertaining 15+ offers, owner’s sentiment will suffer. The Atlantic, only really the USG providing much nourishment for the VLCCs. On the flip side, OECD inventories at close to their lowest for the last 10 years, these will need replenishing, once OPEC are able/willing to lift production. It is up to Western leaders to convince them to do so.
