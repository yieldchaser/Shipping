## Market Commentary

Clear evidence over the past few days that around WS 60 MEG/East seems to be the level at which owners (scrubbers attached of course) are willing to profit take and fix away. Certainly, led by the relets, the MEG market has wobbled over the last few days, down to WS 57.5 MEG/Korea, despite continued enquiry and no real change in the fundamentals (apart from some cheaper bunkers). At the time of writing, there are a couple of cargoes working MEG/East, and we still have seen only 10 or so cargoes done in the 10-20 window. Owners should not panic. The US Gulf has seen successive deals higher than last done, up to USD 7.5m to Ningbo. However, West Africa has remained light on volume and Smaxes are also running out of steam. Without late August Wafr enquiry, or fresh September USG stems, owners will have to fight to hold recent gains.
