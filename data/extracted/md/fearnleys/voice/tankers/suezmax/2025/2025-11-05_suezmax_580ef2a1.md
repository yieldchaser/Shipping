## Market Commentary

The West remains extremely short on tonnage, with this tightness reflected in aggressive rates from the 

**US Gulf:** USD 7.25 million on subs for USG/WCI and WS140 on subs for USG/UKCM, both marking significant increases on last done. We have yet to see similarly aggressive movements in TD20 rates, but owners will be keen to replicate the gains seen across the pond and will undoubtedly use them as leverage. Adding to bullish supply fundamentals, demand is expected to accelerate, with very little covered from West Africa or Guyana and CPC December cargoes entering the natural window. This is without factoring in seasonal weather changes, which typically add further volatility to tanker markets as conditions cause vessel delays. The MEG remains very strong as well; however, VLCCs appear to be cooling slightly, which may weigh on sentiment. That said, ahead of the Bahri festivities next week, a potential surge in demand could prevent significant losses and may even trigger a rebound. Overall, a perfect storm is developing for owners as we approach year-end.
