## Market Commentary

Western markets remain piping hot with multiple cargoes still outstanding and supply that is extremely short and barely extends. Typically, when we reach these elevated levels, profit-taking tends to kick in from the owners’ side; however, so far they remain steadfast in pushing the market above and beyond the year’s highs. Additionally, several owners have opted not to extend subs, and with further questionable itineraries being taken, there’s potential for additional replacement cargoes to enter the market. We did see a brief flurry of VLCC cargoes for West Africa first decade November, but most appear to have been withdrawn - suggesting that using VLCCs as a release valve has not borne fruit. The MEG has overall been quiet, but sentiment should be healthy amidst strong Western markets and as a result further vessels exiting contention to the Cape of Good Hope.
