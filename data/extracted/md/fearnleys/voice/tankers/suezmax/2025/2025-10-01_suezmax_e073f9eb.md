## Market Commentary

So far this week the silence has battered sentiment despite supply and demand fundamentals that at the week's outset suggested some form of stability. 2nd decade West Africa appears to have been covered on the most part with very few reported deals, and Guyana seems to be heading the same way. However, we start today with 7 vessels on subs in the USG - that leaves 0 in ballast with just 3 sailing in the next 10 days. As a result outstanding USG/Guyana cargoes will have to drag vessels from this side of the Atlantic and will likely face renewed bullishness. Owners will be hoping this translates to corresponding markets. Aside from an Indian quote the MEG was quiet yesterday. Tonnage has grown and overall sentiment has declined as VLCCs have trended downwards.
