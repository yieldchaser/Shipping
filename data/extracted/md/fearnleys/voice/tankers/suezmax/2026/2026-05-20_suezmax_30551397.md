## Market Commentary

There has been sufficient activity across Black Sea-USG-Guyana-West Africa to keep lists fairly balanced for 1st decade cargoes. There should still be reasonable volume to come for 1st decade West Africa, given the lack of VLCC fixing, and as we move into next week, 2nd decade dates should also begin to work. Similarly, there has been fairly limited interaction from the larger units in the forward window as well. For West Africa, there will of course be the usual pragmatism from vessels coming from the East that are unable to fix elsewhere, which may weigh on the market. However, the wider Atlantic-Mediterranean-Black Sea market appears well supported.
