## Market Commentary

The market has resumed its upward trajectory this week, building on the momentum seen at the end of last week. CPC/Mediterranean rates have broken through the WS 250 barrier, and TD20 looks set to move above WS 240. Tonnage availability, both in the West and among vessels committed via the Cape of Good Hope, remains thin, and charterers look set to face increasingly emboldened owners, particularly given that there are still cargoes to cover for the second decade in both the Atlantic and ex-CPC.
