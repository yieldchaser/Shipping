## Market Commentary

Whilst the Middle East remains opaque supply & demand in the West are all working in owners favour presently, although with potential for seismic shifts down the line there is plenty of nuance. Multiple CPC stems versus what just 2 ships FOC or ETS within 5 days ex Mediterranean tilts TD6 towards the bulls. Supplemented nicely by Brazil enquiry, with 2-4 Liza working over the past 24H and 24MBBLS untouched in the 3rd decade ex West Africa, the Atlantic basin is also strong. However, VLCC trajectory on freight is not miles away from rendering them competitive on 1MBBL stems, utilisation of 3x VLCC this week ex West Africa in our window & potential for corrections in crude pricing that could bring back Chinese demand may all undermine Suezmax volumes. Over the past 6 decades we have averaged a colossal 25MBBLs per decade, which has been a significant market driver, particularly during this period when USG volumes are almost solely reserved for Aframaxes. This has halted further progress, and the risk of an Armada of ballasters ex East down the line (24-31 window) only serves to underline this. Charterers on the front end still have a tough job, but we are pretty much working 3 weeks out and with crude pricing, VLCC freight & Suezmax supply (both ex East and ex UKC if we have time to wait) potentially alleviating pressure there will be reluctance to work the forward window.
