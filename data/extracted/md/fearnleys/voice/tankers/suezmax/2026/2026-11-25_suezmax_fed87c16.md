## Market Commentary

With Suezmax impetus coming from USG spillover the market corrected up to align with TD25 which is now deflating. Suezmaxes are only competitive for USG/Transatlantic at WS 322 on a USD/BBL bss, the same TCE as a TD20 run bss circa WS 377. Whilst there was clear resistance on options cargoes ex West Africa, this changing dynamic brings UKCM tonnage back into play for TD20 for a window in which both West Africa & Brazil markets are fixed well forwards. Tonnage builds on VLCCs east of Suez undermine the prospect of Fujairah driving overall market resistance, and with circa 6 stems left up to the end of the 2nd decade ex West Africa and Thanksgiving pressing the pause button on Americas markets, charterers pouncing now on a market peak would be peculiar. Instead expect restraint. In the interim TD6 has remained in a WS 10 bandwidth with WS 450 last done, and Fujairah continues to work VLCC scraps prompt.
