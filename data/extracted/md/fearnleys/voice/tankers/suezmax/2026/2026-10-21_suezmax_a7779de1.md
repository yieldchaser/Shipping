## Market Commentary

* Finally, we have some activity in the Atlantic, albeit off forward dates.

* There should be earlier stems to fix, but we would not view the forward cargoes in the market as a signal for charterers to move, as it seems unlikely that prompt vessels will interact given the number of waiting days.

* Even if we do see an influx of prompter cargoes, it feels unlikely to have an immediate positive impact and would instead have more of a steadying effect.

* As such, it does feel like fixing patterns should shift back closer to two weeks out.

* Owners will be hoping for the tap to turn back on from Fujairah, as ultimately the lack of fixing has funnelled a large number of both VLCCs and Suezmaxs into Atlantic business compared to recent months.
