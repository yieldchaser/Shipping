## Market Commentary

On the one hand we have prompt replacements & 1-5 window cargoes working Suezmax, VLCC levels also in runaway mode with TD15 fixing circa 260KT x WS 200 and their USG list almost devoid of tonnage within the next 30-day. Thus expectations of strong West Africa demand to carry the Suezmax market in the 2nd decade could sustain this spike. On the other, we are fully priced out of the USG market with 2 enquiries withdrawing yesterday & Aframaxes USD 2.5/BBL cheaper, and basis latest cargo counts ex West Africa we may be down to our final 6 stems in the 1st decade once present enquiry is cleaned out. The list has 8 natural options FOC up to the 5th, 4 discharging presently, 3 at disport waiting and overall 12 natural ETS within the next 5 days/22 natural ETS within the next week. It's not hugely extensive & CPC players are not going to change their tune whilst TD6 is paying in the WS 500s either. Given the confidence a low-VLCC count should give owners in the 2nd decade, even if 1st decade demand falls off a little towards the end of the week, we are not anticipating owners to suddenly become pushovers.
