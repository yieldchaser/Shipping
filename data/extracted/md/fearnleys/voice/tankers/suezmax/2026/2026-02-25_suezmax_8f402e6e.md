## Market Commentary

All load regions from East to West have firmed, with the MEG and CPC leading the charge. The others appear undervalued and ready to pop, especially with prompt cargoes continuing to emerge – particularly out of West Africa driven by the abundance of unsold barrels. Tonnage remains very tight in both hemispheres and, with VLCCs continuing their Sinokor‑driven bull run, we should expect further split‑VLCC stems to explore Suezmaxes.
