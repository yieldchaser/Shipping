## Market Commentary

The Suezmax market is slowly waking from its holiday slumber, with an emphasis on "slowly". In the East, there has been a less than auspicious start with the only signs of life coming from short-haul Indian cargoes. TD23 "should" have been the big story to kick start 2024, with the Red Sea slap bang in the middle of the headline. But thus far, and despite the furore, rates are unlikely to reflect much upside (if any). We are pricing that run at circa WS 80 (2023 basis flatrates). Despite an overtly well stocked tonnage list, the Atlantic is a tad more positive with present TD20 rates holding up well (WS 107.5 basis 2023 flatrates). Support also comes from the USG where TA runs may yet bring some further New Year cheer.
