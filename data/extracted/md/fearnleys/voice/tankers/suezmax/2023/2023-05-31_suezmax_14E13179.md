## Market Commentary

After a calm conclusion to the previous week and an extended weekend, the Suezmax market has begun the current week at a sluggish pace. The list of available tonnage in the Western region continues to increase steadily each day, while cargo activity remains notably slow. Within the last 24 hours, TD20 rates have dropped from WS 115 to low WS 100's, with the possibility of the market further declining into double digits. In the Eastern region, the majority of cargoes this week have consisted of short-haul shipments between the Middle East Gulf (MEG) and India. There are still some fuel cargoes available for MEG/Spore, but overall, the Eastern market appears to be experiencing a weakened demand as well. Moving forward, we anticipate a further decline in the Suezmax market. However, one positive aspect we can derive from this market situation is the relatively swift fixing window, which could indicate more cargoes left for this week.
