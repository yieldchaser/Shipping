## Market Commentary

After news broke of a replacement cargo fixing TD23 135kt x WS 130, charterers appear to have been smoked into the MEG market en-masse. They have inexplicably interpreted this as a green light to jump in with four cargoes, straddling the last seven days of the month. What makes this even more curious, is that the morning after news of this juicy rate filtered through, the rate was universally dismissed as an aberration and owners were all talking maximum mid WS 60's, but those rates are no longer there thanks to the generous state of the cargo board and now likely start with a seven. In the Atlantic, there was much talk of VLCC's capping Suezmax, but rates have pushed on despite this, with TD20 likely to surpass WS 110 by the end of the week. With a number of unsold West African cargoes and a limited number of European ports that can accommodate VLCC's, charterers are unable to fully commit to VLCC's unless they know exactly where the cargo will end up.

**US Gulf**

 is looking positive, and we expect support from lightering and Aframax' to further underpin TA rates.
