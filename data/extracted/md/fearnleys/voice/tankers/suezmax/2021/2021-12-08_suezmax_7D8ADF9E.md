## Market Commentary

Just when it looked as if Q4 was turning into a total wipe out for owners, the Atlantic has granted them some relief. There are pockets of resistance in regions where laycans are being worked on a very prompt basis, which has caught some charterers by surprise. The US Gulf has been the main driver with tight lists and firmer rates enticing ships to ballast from the Western Atlantic. This has shorn the West African and Black Sea lists of a few ships and hindered the ability of early Wafr cargoes to repeat last done, resulting in firmer rates. The long East list is still of concern and with conversely lower enquiry levels, there is the probability that large numbers of modern ships, looking for employment, will set a course West. Td20 trades WS 67.5-70 firm whilst Td23 is WS 30 and flat.
