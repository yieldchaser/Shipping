## Market Commentary

Thus far, it's been a slow news week with nothing extraordinary to break the current trend. Unfortunately, long lists and low cargo volumes has meant charterers have come up against zero resistance in attaining last done levels, or less, and that is how the balance of the week will play out. A flicker of positivity comes in the form of the current oil price. This suggests demand is increasing which should reduce stockpiles and perhaps allow OPEC+ to free up more supply, but this is all subject to pandemic economics and the ability of countries to successfully roll out a vaccination program.
