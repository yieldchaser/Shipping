## Market Commentary

The Suezmaxes in the Atlantic have had a fairly busy couple of weeks, with West Africa and Brazil being the main drivers. Rates have been fairly steady around w70 on TD20, but closing in on Xmas now, we see Owners more nervous to get stuck with PPT ships over Xmas than Charterers are not to get their cargoes fixed. This has put some downward pressure on rates and TD20 is currently trading in the mid w60's. In the east the market has remained stable for some time now. Slow activity, with V's taking much of the volume ex MEG. We do not expect the market to improve going into next week, neither in the west nor in the east.
