## Market Commentary

The common aim of most owners this week has been to hedge by looking to go short, and therefore repeat market, or go long and be recompensed for their efforts. Since the beginning of the downtrend, Eastern ballasters have been a key depressor of West Africa/East moves, but turbulent bunker prices have made owners question whether last done rates justify a long haul gamble. Owners appear reluctant to forecast what their backhaul bunkers could be, and this is where a possible silver lining lies.

**West Africa**

/East was booked last night on what we believe was a no heat stem 130KT x WS63, which is up. Throw into the mix that it was a relet and we have further grounds for optimism.

**USG**

 is firm with TA trading 145KT x 60 whilst MEG/East has a more sombre feel with WS53 being repeated a few times. Td23 trades WS15.
