## Market Commentary

A deluge of Atlantic basin VLCC enquiry, coupled with a very interesting USG Aframax market, has created a potentially volatile road map for Suezmaxes. A key factor in assessing market trajectory, is the time it takes to fix a cargo, with one particular Black Sea stem reportedly still there after five days. Slow trading has been the order of the day with last done rates being rather difficult for charterers to attain, hence why we will soon see Td6 flirt with WS 80's levels.

**USG**

-ECM/TA is no longer back haul business and is 145KT x WS 57.5 with a firm outlook, which logically dictates that Td20 which will soon challenge the WS 70+ mark and likely to push on. In the East, rates have been moving at a slower pace, but there is room for further optimism with Td23 last done at WS 40 and MEG/East threatening WS 70.
