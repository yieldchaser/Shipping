## Market Commentary

Curiously, just as the situation in Ukraine looks like it’s slowly diffusing, the Joint War Committee has decided to add the Black Sea to their list of High Risk Areas. A few weeks too late maybe? Bizarrely, Black Sea rates have traded fairly flat during this period, which makes shipping the sole outlier, in global markets, not to have reacted to the threat of conflict. Elsewhere, US Gulf /TA has traded 145kt x WS 65 which underlines that the Atlantic market doesn't have a backhaul trade anymore as everything has become fronthaul. In the East, there's a fairly steady feel as TD23 is likely to repeat last done levels as fixing dates move into first decade March.

**MEG**

/East is still at the mercy of a very long list where rates will remain around the WS 60-62.5 mark for the balance of the week.
