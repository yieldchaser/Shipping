## Market Commentary

Russian grades continue to command a premium over the regular market ex Baltic and Black Sea with the curious exception of ESPO loadings from Kozmino. The wider market is still battling rising bunkers and falling freight with few signs of resistance other than some Indian market cargoes that have recently traded Wafr/India at circa 130kt x WS 88-90. Those with scrubbers have committed the dirty deed and allowed their consumption-advantage to be traded against them with Wafr/long East trading at WS 77.5-80.

**MEG**

/India enquiry has been particularly active, whilst conversely, long haul from this region has been quiet, allowing ships to recycle back onto the list quickly.

**MEG**

/East will trade WS 87.5-90 on modern whilst TD23 may start to threaten WS 30's if low cargo volumes persist.
