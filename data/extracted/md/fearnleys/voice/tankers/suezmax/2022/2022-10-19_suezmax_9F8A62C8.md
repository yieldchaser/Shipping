## Market Commentary

Suezmax's continue to benefit psychologically from a strong VLCC market. In the East, cargoes are working on a very prompt basis which has maintained momentum with owners. TD23 has been particularly busy with WS 95 reported on subjects whilst NPI has fixed 130KT x 160's for their MEG/Yingkou 1-4/11. In the Atlantic, West Africa has taken a backward step with Saras reported on subjects 130KT x WS 147.5 for a Dalia load, which is likely to price TD20 in the WS 150'S for the remainder of the week. In the Black Sea, the fixing window is firmly focused on the second decade where TD6 has twice been booked at WS 190, but those numbers are likely to ebb away as the weaker links have been taken out.
