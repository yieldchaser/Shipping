## Market Commentary

The Suezmax market is still the same old story. With the risk of sounding like a broken record, the market has a fairly decent cargo activity, but it is just too many ships. At the beginning of the week we had 5 cargoes working ex Wafr, giving owners an incentive to push for higher rates, but looks like 2.5-5 points is all that is in this. In the East, the market looks fairly steady. There are plenty of ships to choose from if you could take an older, or handicapped ship, but if you need a modern, the list is looking healthier. For the week to come we expect the market to keep on grinding at bottom levels unless something extraordinary happens the oil demand.
