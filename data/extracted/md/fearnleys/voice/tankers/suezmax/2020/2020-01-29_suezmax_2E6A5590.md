## Market Commentary

The Suezmax market have really taken a plunge this week. An increasing amount of ballasters from the East have put some serious pressure on the Atlantic market, and with Libya out of the question and VLCC digging in on the Suezmax bbls in West Africa, charterers had few problems pushing rates down. We do expect there is a couple of more points in this before we hit bottom, but owners are starting to tighten the grip already. In the East, the tonnage count is a lot lower than normal, and market is still coming off, so this will be interesting to see for next week. On the other hand, China's GDP will be affected by the Corona-virus, and trader are already cancelling contracts so we could see a dip in supply.
