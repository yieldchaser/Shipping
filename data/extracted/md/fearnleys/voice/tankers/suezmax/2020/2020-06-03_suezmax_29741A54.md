## Market Commentary

The Suezmax market is continuing the downward spiral from last week. The 30 day count in both WAFR and MEG is at the highest year to date. For Owners this means one thing - there is nowhere to hide. TD20 is closing in on $20k/day and Owners now need to focus on minimizing their waiting time and keep the ships sailing. As of now, we dont see any signs of improvement going forward either, so for this market to stop this downward spiral we would probably need rates to come down to a level where Owners would rather float than fix.
