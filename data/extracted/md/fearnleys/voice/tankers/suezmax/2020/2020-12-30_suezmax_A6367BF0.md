## Market Commentary

We should start referring to the week preceding New Year as "no quibble week". As expected, the market has been quiet with charterers and owners happy to fix variations of last done. This will continue until volumes hopefully increase next week. Above radar activity has been at a minimum, resulting in Wafr/Portugal trading at WS35 which will see Wafr/East find a home at about WS40. Bot/USG was last booked at WS17.5, albeit via Cape of Good Hope, which pitches a Suez/Suez Med run at WS10-12.5. Need to keep an eye on Td3c VLCCs as that list looks tighter for end 2nd decade dates. This should hold MEG/East Suezmax at a minimum of WS37.5 level for a modern vessel. There's very little else to say other than to wish you all a happy and prosperous 2021.
