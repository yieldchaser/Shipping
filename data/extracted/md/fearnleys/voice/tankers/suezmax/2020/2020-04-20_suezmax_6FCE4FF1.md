## Market Commentary

With May dates in the MEG finally coming into play, owners have enjoyed a bullish mix of VLCC cargoes splitting in two and big uncertainty in itineraries following weeks of lock-downs amid Covid-19. Ships have disappeared into TC's of various duration combined with charterers that are scrambling to secure the safe, free-of-cargo ships - a mass that is decreasing by the hour. Safe positions are like gold dust at the moment and owners will aim to make the most of it for as long as they can.
