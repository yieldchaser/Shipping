## Market Commentary

The West Africa market is still feeling the effects of the eastern ballasters, and we now see West Africa/Continent fixed below ws60. Front-end of the tonnage-list is still heavy, so no many signs of improvement this week. In the East, the 30 days tonnage-count is almost at the lowest this year, but we are still seeing ships willing around ws70 MEG/East. Cargo activity has been decent, so we could see some upward pressure in MEG the week to come.
