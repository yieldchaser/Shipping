## Market Commentary

Activity has been slow for Suezmaxes the past week, third decade dates in W.Afr were slow in showing and caused a slow decline in the rates as owners tried to secure business as close to their dates as possible. TD20 fell to ws70 and with a marked reduction in the Turkish Straits delays TD6 has steadied to ws95. Algerian barrels ex Arzew have kept the Med position list ticking over and sprinkling of cargoes in the North for East destinations have been welcome relief in an otherwise uninspiring market. A further softening of rates in the week ahead is on the cards, now is time for owners to tighten their belts in order to ride this trough out, Charterers are very much in the driving seat for now.
