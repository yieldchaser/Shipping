## Market Commentary

For the suezmaxes we have had decent activity for all markets last week. The tonnage-count in the Atlantic is looking better but we still have an overhang of tonnage preventing the rates from moving anywhere. The start of this week has been slow though, and we would need some end/ely activity to put Owners in a better position going into next week. In the MEG the market remains stable. The 30 day tonnage count remains steady just above 100 ships, and Owners are still getting around $18-19k for a round-trip MEG/Spore on a modern eco-ship.
