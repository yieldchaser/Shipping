## Market Commentary

Following a busy period, rates firmed up end of last week. This week have continued in the much same pace with healthy fixing activity in the Atlantic and in the East with rates creeping up even further - all be it in more healthy increments which bears hope for less rate volatility, which in turn can make this upturn last. Tonnage lists however look ample, so for this to last we need to see continued fixing activity in all areas.
