## Market Commentary

Suezmaxes still in the doldrums as the supply of tonnage in all markets have been ample. Hurricanes and bad weather in the Americas managed to rock the boat slightly last week, but it seems now that this market has topped as well as we see ships ballasting from UKC-GIB and over to compete with local tonnage. On a positive note, activity level overall is good, but there is an overhang of ships in the Atlantic and this needs to be absorbed before we start seeing any upward push in rates.
