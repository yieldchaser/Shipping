## Market Commentary

With sentiment in the tanker charter market looking flat, the S&P sector, despite the overall strong market, did little to provide a significant headline. Surprisingly, a 1999 blt Suezmax made the most noise, as WHITE TRADER (150K DWT 1999 NKK) was sold for USD 27M, representing almost double the price the unit would fetch at demolition. Elsewhere, in the very popular MR sector, Parakou sold MR PTI DANUBE (50K DWT 2017 SPP) at USD 41M, a price that initially seemed elevated when the ship reached the market but has quickly been realised as value. Viken sold the last of their HMD Handytankers, with NORVIKEN (37K DWT 2010 HMD) changing hands for USD 23M. Activity continues to increase in the dry sector with a total of 18 ships having been reported sold this week. JP Morgan purchased Handy trio of ERISORT/ERRADALE/WULIN (39K DWT 2014 CHENGXI) from Swire at USD 20.5M per unit, with MUR to manage the vessels going forward. In the Capesize sector, ROSEBANK (177K DWT 2010 NEW TIMES) was sold for USD 23M and MULAN (176K DWT 2005 TSUNEISHI) transacted for USD 16.25M, sales which will help price the spread of older units at this end of the size curve. Wisdom Marine sold Tess 58 Supramax AMIS ORCHID (58K DWT 2012 TSUNEISHI CEBU) for 18.7M, as Supramax values continue to be squeezed upwards.
