## Market Commentary

Much of the tanker story this week was to do with an attack on Norwegian owned STST tanker STRINDA 20K DWT FUKUOKA 2006, which was struck by a missile in the Red Sea. Fortunately, there were no injuries sustained but the narrative here is one of increased disruption to a very busy shipping lane, and further unpredictability in an already mercurial market. Aframax AEGEAN POWER 115K DWT SAMSUNG HI 2007 was privately sold at a very firm price of USD 41M, which will fuel speculation over whether this is a new benchmark, or whether there were other factors at play. Elsewhere ice 1B handy tanker BLUE TRADER 37K DWT HMD 2005 sold to Turkish Buyers at USD 17.4M. Eagle Bulk shares were the big winner this week in the dry market following the announcement of an all-stock acquisition by Star Bulk. The combined company will boast a total fleet of 169 bulkers and over USD 2BN in market cap. On the vessel side, 7 sales kept the market ticking over with the most significant being 3x Capesize units MINERAL TEMSE/DESTELBERGEN/BRUGGE 175K DWT NEW TIMES 2010/2011 bought by EGPN for total of USD 63.5M. With seasonal slowdown expected over Christmas/New Year and then Chinese New Year, market players will be keen to tie up as many deals as possible in the next week.
