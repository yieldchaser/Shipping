## Market Commentary

In what has otherwise been an uneventful week in the tanker S&P markets, UK buyers are reported to have acquired scrubber fitted Suezmax NAMSEN 157K DWT NEW TIMES 2016 for USD 67M. The last comparable sale was in June when EPS paid USD 75M for scrubber fitted ELANDRA OSPREY 157K HHI 2018. The downwards correction of dry values is gathering pace as the transaction liquidity begins to pick up . Capesize ARIADNE 180K DWT DSME 2009 was hotly contested, and eventually sold at USD 21.25M to UK buyers. Demand is growing in the 2008-2014 built range of Capesize as opportunistic owners look to find a bargain asset play. Capesize MOUNT APO 176K DWT RONGSHENG 2012 was bought by Peter Doehle for just under USD 25M, helping price similar assets in this sector. Elsewhere, MBA GIOVANNI/ROSARIA 93K DWT JIANGSU NEWYANGZI 2010/11 sold to Samudera for 33.5M en bloc.
