## Market Commentary

ATHENIAN FREEDOM (299K DWT 2013 HHI) to Chinese buyers (scrubber to be installed prior delivery) at USD 78M, and Tsakos fixing a VLCC on 3yr charter for USD 55k/day signals the underlying strength of the tanker story, despite the limp spot market and talk of lagging Chinese demand. Older large tankers still hold their value, with Chinese buyers paying USD 37.8M for LILA ORLANDO (158K DWT 2005 DSME). Elsewhere, Bahri offloaded 2x Zinc-coated MRs in the form of NCC NAJD/HIJAZ (45K DWT 2005 HMD) at USD 17.2M each. Across the bulk market, buyers are jostling for tonnage as some market participants speculate that we have either reached, or are about to reach, the bottom of the market. There will be a healthy flow of sales from now until the end of the year, not because owners are suffering, but because they will want to position themselves and structure their fleets to profit from improving conditions next year. Ultramax SANTA MARIA (61K DWT 2014 IMABARI) sold to Greek buyers at USD 23.5M, reinforcing the ongoing value correction. Enquiry has picked up dramatically in recent weeks in the handy sector, where we have seen 4 sales this week, most modern of which NORD SAVANNAH (37K DWT 2013 SAIKI) sold for USD 16.8M.
