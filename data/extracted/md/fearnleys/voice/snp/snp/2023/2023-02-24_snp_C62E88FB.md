## Market Commentary

Activity in the Bulker segment was muted this week in comparison to the recent acquisition of a sextet of H Line Newcastlemaxes by Golden Ocean for USD 291M. The vessels will be chartered back to H Line for 36 months at USD 21kpd. Elsewhere in the market, Winning bought Capesize OCEAN CAESAR (180K DWT IMABARI 2008) for USD 20.2M, and Supramax SUN GLOBE (58K DWT TSUNEISHI HI CEBU 2007) sold for USD 14.2M, once again showing that values in the dry sector have managed to hold despite recent negative sentiment. This will be welcome news to those dry sellers who have fended off opportunistic bids in hope for better prices. The Tanker market continues to read from the same script with premiums being paid for Ice Class units and vessels that would have been scrap candidates only a short while ago. Ridgebury Tankers, who have been proven sellers in this market, sold scrubber-fitted Suezmax RIDGEBURY JUDITH (150K DWT UNIVERSAL 2008) for USD 39.5M. They also sold MR RIDGEBURY KATHERINE Z (50K DWT SPP 2009) at USD 24.8M, into an MR market which seems to be bubbling as players eye healthy returns. Elsewhere, Euronav offloaded ice class 1C Suezmax CAP CHARLES (158K DWT SAMSUNG 2006) for USD 41.5M to Gardsea.
