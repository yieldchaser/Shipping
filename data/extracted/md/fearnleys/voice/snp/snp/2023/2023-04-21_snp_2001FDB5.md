## Market Commentary

Predictable unpredictability in the tanker charter market did not phase the S&P space as headlines continued to be written by high value transactions. Capital Maritime’s Ice class 1C Aframax ARISTOKLIS (113K DWT DAEHAN 2017) sold for a reported USD 68M, thus reaffirming the demand for modern second hand tankers. Ridgebury continued their tanker selloff with Suezmax RIDGEBURY LESSLEY B (158K DWT SAMSUNG HI 2013) being snapped up by Indonesian buyers for USD 54.5M. Elsewhere it was busy week on the MR front with a number of sales across the age curve. UK based buyers picked up MR KIRIBORA (50K DWT SPP 2013) for USD 33.5M and CELSIUS RANDERS (46K DWT HMD 2010) was sold for USD 26M, helping price units around the 10-year age range. A busy week for Supramaxes dominates the story in the dry market with 9 out of the 11 sales reported. Tess 58 AMIS LEADER (58K DWT TSUNEISHI ZHOUSHAN 2010) sold for a firm USD 18M, once again showing the value in good designs. 2010/11 blt Chinese units fetched considerably less, in the form of DAXIA (57K DWT COSCO DALIAN 2010), SANIA (57K DWT QINGSHAN 2010) and HAUT BRION (57K DWT TAIZHOU SANFU 2010) each being sold for region USD 14M. Elsehwere, Ultramax DIOMIDIS (63K DWT JIANGSU JINLING 2015) sold for USD 25.5M.
