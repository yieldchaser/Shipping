## Market Commentary

After a seasonal pause in our weekly reporting we are greeted back by promising snp markets which seem to have seen no sign of softening. Headlining the tanker space this week is the highly coveted product sector which has seen Ridgebury acquire a total of 6 vessels for an enbloc price of Usd 61m. Enquiry has indeed been high in the segment however with most players scouting for younger and scarcer sale candidates. Ridgebury therefore is offering a well needed benchmark in what has been a sustained illiquid deal environment. Elsewhere we continue to see vintage crude carriers achieving what can relatively be considered as healthy levels supported by a strong demolition market. The dry sector has lost no steam over the summer with ten units changing hands this week. Interest remains high from a range of buyers looking to lock in the returns offered by an ever established freight market. Notable activity this week is the sale of a 2017 Hudong built Kamsarmax achieving a staggering price of Usd 31m, “TRANS AFRICA”.
