## Market Commentary

Another week that has seen a series of dry cargo vessels being transacted at higher levels than their respective last done. Although this high weekly liquidity provides clear benchmarks for both parties to transact it can also become a moving target for buyers looking to meet the sellers’ fast changing expectations. The Supramax market has yet again seen a notable appreciation with the IKAN SELAYANG (56K DWT MITSUI 2013) being sold at Usd 26.5m. A comparable sale would be this of the sistership  “MEDI OKINAWA” (56K DWT MITSUI 2011) which was sold earlier this month at low Usd 23m. A notable dynamic remains the substantial ground gained by Japanese built vessels against alternative designs - this is particularly well illustrated by this weeks sale at Usd 21.75m of the WP BRAVE (58K DWT SPP 2012) which when compared to the sale of the Mitsui 56 highlight this growing rift. Volume in the Tanker snp markets remains subdued with buyers remaining cautious by taking what seems to be a standby approach by waiting for a pick up in liquidity before considering meeting the current offer. Headline transaction this week is that of the 2 x LR2s sold by Navig8 to JP Morgan. The vessels were both built in 2018 at New Times, scrubberfitted and each achieved a price Usd 48m.
