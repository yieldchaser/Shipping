## Market Commentary

As expected, we are indeed experiencing a relative slowdown in terms of volumes of sale on the Dry cargo fronts. However, with the weekly liquidity that we have been accustomed to over the past months, this slight loss of momentum is far from a complete halt in SnP activity with north of a dozen vessels changing hands this week. Values as a whole are shifting sideways for the time being despite the adjusted bids seen from buyers looking to take advantage of the dip in rates, to secure tonnage at less than last done. Signs of which can be seen in the more liquid of segments such as Supramax units that are seeing levels which are on average 10% lower than the most recent completed sales. Tanker markets continue to show growing signs of anticipation for a healthier rates environment. Buying enquiry is steadily rising along the recovering sentiment with product sector leading the charge showing a great number of enquiries for MRs and LR2. Well needed benchmark being set in the crude sector which has seen the Aframax “Antonis” (113k dwt, 2017 built Daehan) sold at Usd 45m. A sale that draws direction in a market that has strong levels of buying interest for units of such vintage, but which has seen no sales to provide some additional support for potential buyers to come forward with their bids.
