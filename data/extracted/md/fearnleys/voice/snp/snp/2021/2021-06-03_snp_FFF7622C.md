## Market Commentary

With the rally we are seeing both in the Dry cargo and container market, there is not much to write home about in the tanker market. The chartering market continues to struggle, with VLCC and Suezmax’s producing negative charter returns. SKS have sold their “SKS SATILLA” (2006, HHI, 158K DWT) for USD 23.25m. The price achieved falls in line with the sale of the one year younger sistership “SKS SPEY” (2007, HHI,158K DWT) which was sold for USD 24m a few weeks ago. Moreover, we understand that the LR2 “CHAMPION PLEASURE” (2008, Namura,105K DWT) has been sold in an off-market transaction to Union Maritime for USD 18.1m. The price represents a small downtick in values when comparing to the one year younger “CHAMPION PROSPERITY” (2009, Sasebo,115K DWT) which was sold end April for USD 19.7m. Another week with activity on all dry segments except for Capesizes, which seem to be taking a breather with lack of modern candidates being marketed for sale. China Development Bank Leasing steals the headlines acquiring the ex-Hayfin / Celsius fleet of 6 x Ultramaxes and the ex-Clipper controlled 11 vessel fleet of handysizes and Supra/Ultramaxes paying about USD 270 m for the two fleets in total. Noteworthy sales include new benchmarks for 15 year old Japanese Supramaxes setting the price tag at abt USD 13 m with the sale of the Olympic Oshima and Mitsui 2006 built Supramaxes “OLYMPIC PEACE” and “OLYMPIC PRIDE” at USD 25.5 m enbloc. For reference, the previous sale of a 2006 Japanese built Supramax, “CORAL ISLAND” (2006, Oshima, 55k dwt), was end February for USD 10 m. Lastly, as we have written in the recent reports, we are seeing an increase on value of Chinese tonnage with the sale of the Dolphin 57 “OLYMPIC PEGASUS” (2011, COSCO Guangdong, 57K DWT) at USD 14.8 m. Just a month ago, the “SILVIA AMBITION” (2011, Yangfan, 57K DWT) was sold for around USD 12.5 m. Costamare has reportedly purchased the “INTERLINK COMITY” (2010, Huatai, 37K DWT) for USD 12.8 m. The sister vessel, “SREDNA GORA” was sold for USD 11.6 m just few weeks back. We expect this trend to continue with the sharp increase of price of Japanese tonnage.
