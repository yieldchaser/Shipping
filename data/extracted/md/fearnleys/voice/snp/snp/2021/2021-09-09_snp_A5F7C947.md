## Market Commentary

Volume of transactions in the dry snp markets remains unhindered week after week with 15 vessels changing hands. The buying enquiries remain spread out across sectors with active buyers bidding across all sizes. The Ultramax/Supramax front is clearly leading the charge having captured 10 out of the 15 weekly reported sales. Although we continue to experience a relative climb in prices, the stream of potential sellers entering the market alleviates the upward pressure on values by diluting competition across a number of available candidates. Saying that the trend remains very much positive with TRF selling its two Hantong ultramax sisterships built 2016 and 2015 for an enbloc price of Usd 55m while Latsco achieved Usd 20.5m on its IVY DELTA (55k dwt built IHI 2011). Tanker SnP remains subdued in terms of market activity - headlines were however stolen by SFL announcement of its acquisition of 3 x 2019 Suezmax tankers believed to be contracted at New Times by Trafigura. The transaction combines a TC back agreement to the sellers for a minimum of 5 years.
