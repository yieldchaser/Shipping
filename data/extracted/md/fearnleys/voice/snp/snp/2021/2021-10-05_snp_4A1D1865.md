## Market Commentary

This week comes to a close with a number of fresh sales candidates being placed in the market continuing to drive liquidity across all dry segments. In terms of concluded business, the JP Morgan controlled capesize “TRUE ENDURANCE” (180K DWT HHI 2012) has now been sold to Turkish buyers at Usd 32.5m. The levels achieved fall in line with expectation as the last done in the sector is the “PROSPEROUS” (180K DWT SUNGDONG 2011) which was sold at Usd 31m last month. As expected from last week's negotiations, new supramax benchmarks have now been set with Japanese sellers committing the “MEDI OKINAWA” (56K DWT MITSUI 2011) at Usd 22.5m while the 2014 built Dolphin 57 “HTC CHARLIE” was sold at Usd 20.5m. The sale is pointing at a relative narrowing of the spread in value between Japanese and Chinese design which has recently been accentuated by the high volume of Japanese vessels changing hands. Growing anticipation is definitely the name of the game in the Tanker segments where a strengthening buying enquiry is becoming ever so apparent. Signs of increased bids suggest that buyers are gradually moving upwards in an effort to drive potential sellers to transact and secure tonnage from a very thin supply of sales candidates. The product sector has seen the sale of the “IVY EXPRESS” (50K DWT STX 2009) which was sold to Greek buyers at Usd 15m setting a well needed target for potential MR players looking to transact units of similar vintage. Elsewhere, Xihe holdings finalised the sale of the VLCC “E MEI SAN” (317K DWT SWS 2010) which achieved Usd 39m by Far Eastern buyers.
