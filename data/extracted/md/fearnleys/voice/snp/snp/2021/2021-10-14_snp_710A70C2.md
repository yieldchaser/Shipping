## Market Commentary

Following a series of headline announcements this week, the tanker space has for once joyed of most of the limelight and as such we thought we will give tankers the center stage of this weekly digest after being overshadowed by overwhelmingly active dry cargo markets. On Monday, Euronav publicly announced the acquisition of 5.5% of its stock by Fredriksen controlled entities. SEC filings have indeed confirmed that the Cyprus based Famatown Finance, a company beneficially owned by Mr. Fredriksen, has now increased its shareholding stake to 9.8% in the Belgian stock-listed owner Euronav. The move seems to be a welcomed sign for most tanker players as it firmly consolidates the current growing anticipation for a substantial market upturn. As mentioned throughout these previous weeks this growing sentiment was often made apparent by an ever increasing level of buying enquiry with owners struggling to acquire additional tonnage from a continuously thinning supply of sales candidates. In any case we expect this renewed momentum to spur several other parties into action which should see a strengthening of bids across the board in an effort to go meet the rising expectations of any potential seller. Other notable news in the product sector, is the sale of the two modern sisterships ECO LOS ANGELES & ECO CITY OF ANGELS (50K DWT HMD 2020) which were bought by Norden at Usd 36.5m each. The sale was made with the balance of the current charter to Trafigura attached, details of which remain undisclosed at this stage. Volume of transactions in the dry snp markets remains unhindered with 20 reported sales this week. The larger segments have seen a rise in activity with two modern VLOC being sold to Eastern Pacific shipping, namely the STELLA BELLA & STELLA IVY (250k dwt, Qingdao Beihai, 2016 & 2015) which have respectively achieved Usd 60m and Usd 58m basis a 5 years charter attached to BHP. Another three Capesizes built from early to late 2000s have changed hands providing additional support and benchmark for future transactions in the space.
