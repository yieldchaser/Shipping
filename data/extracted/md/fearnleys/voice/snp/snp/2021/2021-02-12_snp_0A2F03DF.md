## Market Commentary

Stock-listed dry companies are wasting no time monetizing their newly repriced shares as hard cash to scale their business. Eagle Bulk has acquired three 2011 Dayang built Supramaxes at region USD 29m financed with USD 21m in cash and 329k common shares which proves its financial flexibility as a listed company. We expect more such deals to materialize as long as P/NAV pricing remains favorable. Good activity in general on the dry side with several 10yrs benchmark sales to report this week. Lemos-backed Valhal Shipping picked up the scrubber fitted Capesize COLOGNY (180k dwt, 2011, TESS CEBU) for USD 22.9m. The last benchmark sale for a 10yrs old was the scrubber fitted CAPE ISTANBUL (176k dwt, 2011, HHIC-Phil) which went for USD 19m early this year. Albeit not directly comparable, the whopping USD 4m gap undoubtedly demonstrates the positive shift in sentiment. Another benchmark deal was the KIND SALUTE (96k dwt, 2011, Imabari) at USD 16.5m with docking due May 2021. To compare, SANGEET (96k dwt, 2012, Imabari) was sold for USD 15.5m late last year. Lastly, on Ultras we saw the ULTRA WOLLONGONG (62k dwt, 2011, Oshima) exchange hands at USD 13.8m. Four mid-2000 built Aframax have been sold this week reaffirming the downward trend in values witnessed so far this year especially for units of such vintage. Teekay has disposed of its two 2008 built sisterships EXPLORER SPIRIT & NAVIGATOR SPIRIT (105k dwt, 2008, Tsuneishi) at Usd 16m each while Castor Maritime has purchased DUBAI HOPE & DUBAI HARMONY (115k dwt, 2005, Samsung) at Usd 13.6m. The correction in values becomes particularly apparent when compared to the sale of the 2009 built STAVANGER FALCON & STAVANGER EAGLE (105k dwt, 2009 Sumitomo) which were sold earlier this year at USD18.25m each.
