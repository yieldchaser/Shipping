## Market Commentary

As expected and reflected throughout our previous reports, this week has seen an increased momentum in the capesize sector. With most fundamentals pointing towards a sustained period of increased rates the cape market is most definitely catching up in terms of SnP activity. We understand that 4 vessels have changed hands this week, partly driven by sellers looking to capture the upside on units purchased earlier in the cycle. An asset play made quite apparent by the ongoing negotiation taking place on the "CAPE LEGACY" ( 2011, Daehan, 180K dwt) purchased at USD 32.5 m in 2015 and now understood to be aiming for numbers in xs of USD 30 m. Understand the sale is yet to be finalised however illustrates particularly well the current drive witnessed in the market. Elsewhere, Cobelfret have sold their 2011 built HHIC "LOWLANDS TENACITY" at USD 29.5 m which suggests that the abovementioned targets should be achievable in the near future. Notable activity in the Tanker space lies on the overage front with a rise in steel prices driving the value on vintage front. Last week's reported sale of the “JUPITER" ( 2000, Daewoo, 300K dwt) is now confirmed at USD 27 m marking a substantial appreciation in value. The measure of this increase becomes particularly apparent considering that Mercuria bought the vessel in January this year for USD 19.5 m. Worth noting however, that post purchase Mercuria retrofitted the vessel for FSO activities applying a positive pressure on pricing. Nevertheless, we expect these increased scrap rates to drive the second hand value of these vintage units upwards and drive potential sellers out of the woods.
