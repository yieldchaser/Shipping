## Market Commentary

No major developments this week with unchanged sentiment across both sectors. The dry markets continue to see sustained liquidity with numerous deals to report each week and a steady stream of fresh sale candidates being placed in the market. This week has seen a particular pick up in activity on the modern front with a number of buyers now aiming for younger units. On the kamsarmax front, we have seen the Oshima built resale MV “RAMANA ROSE” being sold to Greek buyers at Usd 44m basis a prompt delivery from the yard while the Chinese built “SEACON SHANGHAI” (82k dwt 2019 Huangpu Wenchong) was sold at Usd 38m. A relative push in values for the modern units which becomes particularly apparent when compared against this weeks sale of the ten year old “KEY DISCOVERY” (82k dwt 2012 Tsuneishi Fukuyama) which achieved Usd 24m. The product space has captured all of this weeks tanker activity with 5 MR units changing hands. Similarly we have seen notable movement on the modern front with two resales being sold at Usd 37.8m. The vessels are currently being built at the ex STX facilities now operating under the name K Shipbuilding – both units are due for delivery in 2022. A transaction that offers a well needed benchmark in a pricing environment that has been hostage to soaring NB prices. Values on the older vessels remain under pressure despite the relative pick up in liquidity – a sentiment well illustrated by the sale of the D'Amico controlled “High Venture” (51k dwt 2006 STX) which was sold at Usd 10.7m with DD freshly passed and BWTS installed.
