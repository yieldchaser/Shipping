## Market Commentary

Another week for the history books in the crude market with fantasy rates across the board. The S&P market is now in a major vacuum with sellers impossible to pin down on price. In any event, buyers anchored to yesterday’s values will simply not succeed in enticing potential sellers - determined buyers need as such to mentally press the “ctrl/alt/delete” button. With what can only be described as perfect timing, Zodiac this week purchased at auction VLCC BRIGHTOIL GALAXY 320k dwt built 2012 HHI for usd 61.5 mill. It’s easy to predict that the next auction sale of VLCC BRIGHTOIL GEM 320k dwt blt 2013 HHI, which is scheduled for 19th-20th November, will attract a number of keen buyers and ditto strong price. Talking of history books – the last time we had VLCC TCE in excess of usd 300k pd was in December 2007. At that time prompt resale VLCC was valued at Usd 153 mill, five year old at Usd 130 mill and a ten year old at Usd 104 mill. Furthermore, any ships at that time had a bunker consumption considerably higher than today and the HFO price was 50% higher. Taking into consideration the lower finance cost (Libor 60% lower) of the already lower cost of assets - it’s CLONDYKE! Product tankers should pick up with crude shortly. Many LR2’s are rapidly going dirty plus crude tanker newbuildings are no longer stealing gasoil cargoes from product tankers. Bulker values are still moving sideways as seen by the sale of Tess-82 MANGARELLA built 2009 Zosen at region Usd 15.6 mill. Chances are we may see a spill-over effect from what’s going on in the crude markets whereby buyers who have missed the train there will increasingly put money to work for bulker purchases. Based on historical correlation between earnings and values, we believe particularly the 5-10 year olds are undervalued by Usd 2-4 mill. Dry will remain a robust earnings case.
