## Market Commentary

Reported earnings on crude tonnage continue to support the extreme valuations being seen in the tanker market, and without a peace deal between USA-Iran, there is no end in sight for this generational run. The market is seemingly coming to accept the constant shift in values and extraordinary premiums being paid for prompt tonnage. 17-year-old Scrubber fitted KAROLOS (149k dwt New Times 2009) sold this week for USD 91M basis a prompt delivery in the Mediterranean, a figure that comfortably eclipses what it would cost to order a brand-new ship for delivery in 2-3 years’ time. Tanker buying focus though, has shifted into the coated/products sizes this week as sustained higher refinery output and tightening supply of clean vs dirty LR tonnage has forced rates upwards. This week scrubber fitted PUFFIN PACIFIC (49k dwt STX 2020) was sold for USD 49M. Golden week in the Far East usually signals a seasonal slowdown in the dry market, but for now rates are holding at very healthy levels. It is worth keeping an eye on how this scenario progresses with the backdrop of a stagnating conflict in the Middle East and a consistent high oil price environment. Investors still have confidence in this capesize market though, with Norwegian buyers picking up GOLDEN MYRTALIA (177k dwt SWS 2011) for USD 40.5M.
