## Market Commentary

The tanker market continues to defy the broader geopolitical uncertainty, with sentiment underpinned by firm earnings and a steady flow of buyers prepared to meet owners’ price expectations. That said, crude buyers seem to have taken a breather this week as ever increasing pricing pushes more would be buyers into the yards. In the MR sector, Turkish buyers committed USD 20.8M for ice-classed ROYAL JASMINE (53K DWT GSI 2008), while AUTAN (51K DWT SPP 2009) changed hands at USD 23.5M. The pricing achieved for these vintage units further reinforces the depth of demand for mid-aged tanker tonnage, particularly where prompt trading flexibility or niche specifications are involved. A busy time indeed across the dry market as earnings remain firm. This week saw a cluster of modern handysize resales emerge from Ciner, with CLACTON, MARGATE, EASTBOURNE and PORTSMOUTH (40K DWT Jiangmen Nanyang 2024) reportedly sold to Norden at USD 30.5M apiece. The sellers in this transaction represent the target blueprint for buyers seeking modern units in this market, in the sense they have a modern fleet with a stacked orderbook, and are more likely to listen to potential suitors. Chinese buyers were particularly active this week, acquiring both JOY (81K DWT Chengxi 2019) for USD 31.5M and HUAYANG LILY (63K DWT China Shipping 2016) at USD 25.2M. Elsewhere, Greek buyers secured SUMAQ QUEEN (51K DWT Imabari 2017) for USD 25M.
