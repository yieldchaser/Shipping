## Market Commentary

Following an explosive start to the year, tanker activity has somewhat flattened this week. Rates remain firm, with owners confidently holding strong on their price expectations. Values for mid-aged MR tankers remain resilient, reflected in the sale of GREEN PLANET (50K DWT Dae Sun 2014), which achieved USD 31M. In the Handy tanker market, the HTM EVEREST (37K DWT HMD 2010) was sold for USD 20M establishing a welcomed benchmark in a relatively illiquid second hand market. On the dry cargo front, volumes remain high with FRONTIER KOTOBUKI (174K DWT Namura 2011) sold to Global Chartering at USD 31.4M, a correction from last done sale of the MIRACLE (180K DWT Tsuneishi Cebu 2011) at USD 32M, suggesting that Cape values of similar vintage may have reached their peak.
