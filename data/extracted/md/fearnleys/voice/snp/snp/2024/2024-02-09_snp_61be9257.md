## Market Commentary

Tanker rates remained firm this week, with little disruption to be expected as we enter into Lunar New Year celebrations which according to the South China Morning Post will see “a record high of over 9 billion trips” being made within China. The second-hand S&P market though failed to ignite any real excitement, with only a handful of transactions reported this week. Intermediate tanker PENINSULA XI 17K DWT HMD 2023 is the most notable of those deals, being bought by Union Maritime for USD 32.5M. A healthy amount of liquidity in the Dry sector this week, perhaps as players rush to complete deals before much of Asia celebrates, at the very least, a long weekend out of the office. Bullish sentiment at the largest end of the size curve sees 6 Newcs/Capes traded with market players seeking opportunity for the asset plays in the 10-15yo field. Scrubber fitted Newcastlemaxes QING MAY/LAN MAY 206K DWT SWS 2012/2011 were bought by Winning at USD 73M enbloc, exceeding previous price expectations. Elsewhere ArcelorMittal offloaded 3 post panamax bulkers.
