## Market Commentary

Tanker transactions dominate the wires this week with sturdier prices further emphasizing the latest trend. Although products have seen most of this action, the sale of the EUROCHAMPION 2004 (164K DWT 2005 HHI) for $40m shows that the trend is very much apparent for crude as well. Product tanker tonnage has seen stronger spot and term rates which has encouraged buyers and resulted in several transactions. One of which is the MT LR2 MARE ORIENS (110K DWT 2008 MITSUI CHIBA) sold to Chinese buyers for $42m. Even though tankers dominated the S&P market this week, dry has seen it’s fair bit of action as we are slowly approaching Chinese New Year. Asset pricing is mostly flat with a positive pricing bias for modern tonnage. This positive bias is prevalent in this week’s sale of the MV SSI PRIVILEGE and MV SSI SPLENDID (63K DWT 2019 JINLING) which sold en bloc for $30m each.
