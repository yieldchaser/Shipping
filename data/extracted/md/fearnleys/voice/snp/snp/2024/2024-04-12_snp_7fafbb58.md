## Market Commentary

A jolt of life in the tanker second hand market this week with a handful of transactions to report amongst the smaller sizes, otherwise, a fairly subdued week as Ramadan came to an end. In the standout transaction, D’Amico bought AMFITRION (50K DWT SAMSUNG HI NINGBO 2017) for USD 43.5M, compare that to the last sale of a similar Chinese MR SUNNY APATITE (50K DWT GSI 2016) at USD 34M, one can see just how much the market has shifted in the last two quarters. In the high value and tight STST market where levels continue to rise, Delos Shipping bought CHEM BULLDOG (21K DWT ASAKAWA 2010) at USD 23M. We are entering a critical period in this most recent dry market rally. Levels and buying interest have been ever increasing over the last quarter, however, as a consequence some buyers are now being priced out and others are perhaps pulling back to observe whether prices will remain high, or will correct in the short term. Scrubber fitted Kamsarmax SAMMY (82K DWT Tsuneishi 2012) sold at USD 24.6M, surprisingly softer than the initial market rumours pricing the deal closer to USD 26M. Capes continue to be in demand with the sale of scrubber fitted HL HARMONY (179K DWT DALIAN 2015) to Hong Kong based buyers at USD 43M.
