## Market Commentary

The start to Q2 has been somewhat disrupted with various holidays, however, it is not expected that it will mirror the explosive Q1 in the dry market. Asset prices rose sharply as buyers looked to acquire prompt market ships to take advantage of increasing rates, even enticing non-traditional dry players into the space. This sentiment appears to have shifted as those buyers who missed out on the rush have either been priced out, or indeed have turned to sellers themselves to try and capitalise on the inflated values. One could fall back on the underlying overall vessel supply argument, but it just feels that buyers are not comfortable at the current market levels, hence the downtick in sales. In the modern Ultramax space WORLD ROYAL (61K DWT DACKS 2022) sold for USD 35M, while NEW CHAMP (66K DWT MITSUI TAMANO 2018) was bought by Meghna for USD 33.85M. The reinvestment focus of the tanker market continues to be in Newbuildings, as the number of second-hand transactions stutters. In a rare modern tanker transaction, scrubber fitted Aframax AURVIKEN (112K DWT SAMSUNG HI 2019) was auctioned off and eventually sold to Greek buyers for USD 73M, this auction was a result of part of the ownership structure making the unit subject to sanctions against Russia. Elsewhere, Minerva offloaded Ice 1c MINERVA HELEN (103K DWT SAMSUNG HI 2004) for USD 30.35M.
