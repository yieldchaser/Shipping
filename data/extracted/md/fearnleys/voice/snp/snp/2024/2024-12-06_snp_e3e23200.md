## Market Commentary

Another muted, yet unpredictable, week in the tanker second hand market as reported sales vary in subsector from week to week and seemingly no longer follow any pattern. There remains a healthy level of enquiry behind closed doors, and owners are keen to take money off the table, however buyers are in the driving seat when it comes to discussing price. Saying that, more specialised sectors are holding firm with scrubber fitted J19 SONGA BREEZE (19K DWT FUKUOKA 2009) selling at USD 24M and WINTER (13K DWT 21st CENTURY 2009) at 13.5M. Sharply dropping earnings in the dry market could be the reason for lower volumes, but certain sectors are still proving attractive to buyers. Capesize particularly is falling under that banner with Far Eastern buyers securing 3 candidates this week. Now could be a smart time to move, so long as you are willing to ride out the slightly gloomy sentiment and likely slowdown over Chinese New Year, as competition could be fierce market units come Q1/Q2 25. As in any market correction, older ships are feeling the pinch with STRATTON (74K DWT HUDONG ZHONGHUA 2004) sold at USD 7.1M to Chinese buyers, a noticeable downtick on the USD 12M sellers were seeking earlier in the year for the vessel.
