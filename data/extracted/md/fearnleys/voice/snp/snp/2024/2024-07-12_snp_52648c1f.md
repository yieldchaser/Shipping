## Market Commentary

Capital is capturing the headlines this week with the purchase of Scrubberfitted Newcastlemax BERGE BOBOTOV (207K DWT BOHAI 2021) at $75m which marks an appreciation in values since last done “TRUST SHANGHAI/QINGDAO (209K DWT SWS 2021) at $73m - a price highlighting the continued strong appetite for larger tonnage. Elsewhere in the dry market, a flurry of Handysize transactions across all ages, most notably the MAESTRO EMERALD (39K DWT SAIKI 2020) fetching $30m from Turkish buyers, as well as Nisshin Shipping, who is rumoured to have offloaded 4 x handies, WESTERN DURBAN/LIMA/MIAMI/PARIS (39K DWT JIANGMEN 2015) at $19.75m each to Precious shipping. To no surprise, the activity in the tanker market continues to be subdued. Prices for older tankers remain resilient as the PGC COMPANION (72K DWT HUDONG-ZHONGUA 2005) fetched $18m. Buying enquiry remains high for product tankers with three transactions to report this week, most notably the ZEZE START (50K DWT HMD 2009), which is sold basis surveys due to Indonesian buyers for $27m.
