## Market Commentary

MRs, particularly older MRs this week, continue to be a popular investment despite the well documented rise in asset prices over the last 18 months. This is likely due to the low barrier to entry and the healthy returns on offer. The rise in pricing shows no sign in stopping as BAHRI sold DD Due NCC TABUK 45K DWT HMD 2006 for USD 20.7M, a slight rise on their last done DD Due NCC TIHAMA 45K DWT HMD 2006 at USD 20.5M. Several other sales have been or are close to being concluded, with Navios rumored having sold their NAVE EQUATOR 50k DWT, SPP 2009 at USD 26M to Soechi with slightly forward delivery and prompt dock due. A handful of similar aged MRs we understand being discussed with owners now aiming in the high 20s for both DD passed and due. Elsewhere, China continues to be a destination for older Afras, with Thenamaris’ DD Due SEAQUEEN 115K DWT SAMSUNG HI GEOJE 2004 sold to Victory Global Navigation at USD 30M. South Korean buyers continued their run on modern dry tonnage with two notable transactions this week. Scrubber fitted Newcastlemax FOMENTO TWO 206K DWT DAEHAN HAENAM 2017 was bought for USD 60M by Pan Ocean, while HMM provided Densay the opportunity for a rare modern asset flip, purchasing SSI DARING 36K DWT SHIKOKU 2017 for USD 2.7M more than they paid for it in March 2024, at USD 26.7M. We also observe this week the progress in pricing on the Kamsarmax front, XING DE HAI 82K DWT OSHIMA 2017 being bought by Tolani at USD 37M, versus the last notable comparable sale in August 2023 of ALAM KEKAL 82K DWT OSHIMA 2018 at USD 31.8M.
