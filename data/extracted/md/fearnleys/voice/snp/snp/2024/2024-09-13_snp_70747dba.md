## Market Commentary

Ice 1B JAG LALIT (158K DWT Hyundai Samho HI 2005) is the first suezmax tanker to be sold since June 24 as it was transacted this week at USD 33M. Not only suezmax, but tanker sales in general have stuttered this year, with historical high pricing giving potential investors much to consider. This sale will catch the attention of those owners who are placing the increasing number of ageing tanker candidates in the market. MR tankers continue to provide consistent liquidity to the market, with this week TENACITY (50K DWT GSI 2014) selling in line with other market candidates at USD 40M to Greek buyers. Dry liquidity is somewhat bolstered this week by a 7 ship en bloc transaction of the entire Dasin Holdings supramax fleet for USD 80M to Hai Tong of China. There continues to be a healthy amount of activity on second hand capesize units, as rates remain firm. ALPHA PRUDENCE (178K DWT SWS 2008) was bought for USD 24.75M, a slight increase on last done OCEAN COURTESY (178K DWT SWS 2008) which sold in July 24 for USD 24M. Overall, attitude on dry remains bullish, with a rapidly ageing fleet, despite some reported economic uncertainty in China.
