## Market Commentary

A predictably quiet week, with the majority of the market gathered in Athens for the latest instalment of Posidonia. Despite the slow week, the good times in the tanker market continue to roll on. MRs GUNMETAL JACK/PARADISE CITY 50K DWT SPP 2009 were both sold to Greek buyers at USD 28M, as this market continues to rise in terms of asset values. A more subdued, yet surprisingly active week in the dry market. In the standout deal, the Stefanou brothers (Sea Gate Navigation/Bright Navigation) took 3 Laskaridis Panamax units THISSEAS/ICARUS/ATLAS 75K DWT PENGLAI 2012 en bloc for USD 52.5M. Elsewhere, the Far East remains a likely place for older capes to end up as China’s Pioneer paid USD 32.5M for CAPE KEYSTONE 179K DWT HHI ULSAN 2011.
