## Market Commentary

With much of Asia taking holiday this week, one would have predicted a more subdued few days, however the increasingly volatile geopolitical landscape jolted the tanker market into life. The questions on the longevity of record asset prices have been put to one side, at least for now, as spot rates and tanker stocks both received sizeable boosts this week. Sentiment had been, that price ideas on second hand tanker tonnage would begin to soften, however owners and investors alike will be closely monitoring the earnings market as the situation in the Middle East unfolds. Appetite for older MRs remains firm as MR HAFNIA ANDROMEDA (50K DWT GSI 2011) was sold for USD 31M. A combination of extreme weather and Golden Week in China has not disrupted the liquidity in the bulk market, as demand for units remains strong. What continues to challenge investors though, is the disconnect between asset values and charter rates. Granted, there is cause for optimism on news of the incoming China stimulus, but overall demand and consumer confidence is still an issue in this major global market driver. Ultramax demand is high, and liquidity appears to be increasing, particularly on more modern units where BEECHGATE (63K DWT IWAGI ZOSWEN 2019) was sold at USD 35.6M.
