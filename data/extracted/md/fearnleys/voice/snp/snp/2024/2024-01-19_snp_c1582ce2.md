## Market Commentary

This week has seen a pickup in tanker activity with vintage tonnage leading the charge by capturing the bulk of the action. Geopolitical uncertainty along with ever increasing tensions in the Red Sea continue to apply upward pressure on rates which would suggest appreciating values in the near term that could trigger increased liquidity in the modern front. Noteworthy sales are the LR2 FAIR SEAS 155k DWT 2008 STX sold to Chinese buyers for a firm $43.5m. LR1 VOLANS 99K DWT 2009 HHI has also changed hands at reported price of $33m illustrating particularly well rallying values for units of that vintage considering she was bought by Sellers in July 2022 at a price of $26m. On the modern side, Scorpio taking current levels to their advantage by cashing in on the scrubber fitted MR2 STI TRIBECA 49K DWT 2015 SPP, which sold for $39.1m. Busy week on the Dry cargo front which Compared to last week has seen more modern units changing hands. NGM acquiring its third Capesize from Unisea with the purchase of CORONET 182K DWT 2011 Kawasaki at $27m. In the Ultramax segment Parakou Shipping sold their CP GUANGZHOU 63K DWT 2015 CHENGXI and CP TIANJIN 63K DWT 2016 CHENGXI en bloc for $47.6m. Another en bloc deal was reached on the Handysize EURUS VENTURE and NOTOS VENTURE 43K DWT 2017 QINGSHAN S. which sold at $46m.
