## Market Commentary

There is a feeling in the tanker market that beating last done is no longer the expectation for sellers, as buying interest wanes given a calming charter market and potentially impactive events to come in the near future (i.e. the US Election). That said, levels slightly below the last done will still provide owners with extremely healthy returns on their initial investments. A flurry of new candidates on the market coupled with a sharp correction on spot rates has dampened sentiment in the dry market. Buyers are being encouraged to either wait or are testing owners on levels very much at tomorrow’s pricing rather than yesterday’s. It remains to be seen whether owners will embrace this new pricing sentiment or endure the uncertain charter market and rely on the vessel supply story to support elevated values. There was healthy activity in the Capesize sector, particularly focused around the 15 year old mark, which will help price the increasing list of units in the market.
