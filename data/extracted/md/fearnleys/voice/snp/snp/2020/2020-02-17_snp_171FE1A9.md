## Market Commentary

Following days of speculations we understand that the Japanese controlled Aframax NANYANG STAR 105k dwt built 2012 Sumitomo has been committed at a price of Usd 33m. Although the sector has seen a healthy number of transactions since Q4 last year, most candidates changing hands have been on the older end of the spectrum. The levels achieved fall in line with market expectations in this uncertain market environment where bids are limited and where most sellers are holding on to their price ideas in view of the widely expected market recovery. In the product sector the Japanese controlled pumproom MR EAGLE MELBOURNE built 2011 Onomichi has invited offers this week and we understand firm negotiations are underway in the region of Usd 18.5m. A number of transactions to report on the dry sector with a pick up in activity on the Handysize and Panamax segments, with four reported sales in each sector. The consensus among market players seems to be bullish for the upcoming quarters leading to an increased buying enquiry with buyers looking to position themselves by taking advantage of the subdued freight markets in order to secure levels below last done.
