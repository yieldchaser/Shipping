## Market Commentary

Another week that has seen a flurry of activity across all segment of the Dry Cargo markets with close to 20 sales reported this week. In terms of values we continue to see an increasing number of more modern units being traded, setting consolidated benchmarks for assets of all vintage. Notable transaction in the Ultramax sector being the sale of the 2019 Oshima built Ultramax "ADVENTURE III" which was sold to clients of Meghna shipping at Usd 24.25 mill setting a clear picture for modern Japanese designs. Similarly in the Kamsarmax market we have more clarity on the modern Japanese front following the sale of the 2017 Namura built “SAKUZAYA JUSTICE” sold at Usd 25 mill. Generally although buying interest remains high, the steady stream of sale candidates is diluting competition giving buyers enough options to keep values relatively flat. Not much to report in the tanker sector except for the 2000 HHI built VLCC “BAG MEUR”, which we understand has been committed at region Usd 21.2 mill which is another confirmation of the erosion in values for vintage tankers. The same age "MARINA M" also HHI built, was in comparison sold for Usd 32 mill about 2 months ago, albeit with SS passed.
