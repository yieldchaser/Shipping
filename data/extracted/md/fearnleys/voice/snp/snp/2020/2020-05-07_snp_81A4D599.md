## Market Commentary

Thenamaris is reported to have acquired on NSF terms a VLCC newbuilding from Sinokor with delivery ex DSME in September 2020 for region usd 93.5 mill which is spot-on what Euronav paid for sister newbuildings also from Sinokor in early March. This is still a far cry from the Usd 106 mill paid for prompt delivery VLCC newbuilding, also from DSME, in January and confirms that recent spot earnings and values for brand new ships are decoupled. The answer is that no one believes the recent massive earnings spike is sustainable and we have indeed this week seen crude spot rates falling off. CM Lemos has booked two newbuilding Suezmax tankers at HHI for usd 59 mill each without scrubber, and as the year develops we will continue to see competitive pricing from Korean and Chinese yards. Dry values are dropping. The sale of Supramax ROYAL EPIC built 2008 Mitsui at Usd 7.8 mill is a telling example, but mind you we are still about 20% off what was done at the recent bottom in 2016. We have yet to see any tangible modern reference sales, but buyers are increasingly getting ready to move for bulkers. We understand the sale of Kamsarmax CERBA built 2010 STX at usd 13 mill has failed on Sellers’ subjects.
