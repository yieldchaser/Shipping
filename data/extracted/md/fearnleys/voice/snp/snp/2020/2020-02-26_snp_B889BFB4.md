## Market Commentary

Plenty of sales to report in the dry sectors with Oman Shipping making a swoop for three modern Ultramaxes. Another active buyer of Ultramax is Meghna Group of Bangladesh which proved an astute buyer also during the last down-turn in 2016. Values for this segment seem to have found a decent floor for transactions whilst the bigger sizes are certainly more in limbo. Tellingly, Kamsarmax BRIGHT PEGASUS built 2013 Zhoushan has failed to attract serious interest and sellers' price ideas of low to mid 20's (with scrubber retrofit prior delivery) seem far fetched at the moment. With non-scrubber fitted capesize earning close to zero it comes as no surprise that tangible deals are few and far between. We can but only hope for a rebound in markets come March/April. Slow steaming, anchored vessels, scrubber retrofit delays, vessel delivery delays, high scrapping, Vale iron ore shipments rebound, and pent-up China demand could hopefully create a “squeeze” like last year but as we speak it's all down to how the virus outbreak plays out.
