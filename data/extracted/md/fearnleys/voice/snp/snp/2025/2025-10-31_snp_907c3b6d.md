## Market Commentary

After the recent run on modern tonnage, the tanker second hand market has had a much more subdued week. Overall bullish earnings sentiment remains however, and VLCCs are leading the way with several fixtures reported well in excess of USD 100K/day. Owners will be hoping these levels sustain into a healthy winter market. In the clean space, Ice 1A SEAWAYS GATUN/HERUN (47K DWT HMD 2007) sold to Champion tankers for USD 12.7M each, the conservative price levels owing to strict KYC from the sellers. A US - China trade ‘truce’ will excite the dry market after talks between the two nations took place this week in Busan, South Korea. Tariffs will be trimmed but crucially for panamax owners, China have agreed to buy 12 million metric tonnes of soybeans through January and 25 million tonnes annually for the next 3 years. This soybean deal is one thing, but the wider market will welcome easing tensions and general stability between the two nations. In the standout transaction of the week, ultramax CMB BRUEGEL (63K DWT Shin Kasado 2021) was bought by Meghna for USD 32.8M, this follows up these buyers’ purchase of DRAFTDODGER (66K DWT Mitsui 2016) at USD 26.2M last month.
