## Market Commentary

Seasonally stronger crude markets, combined with an increasing US sanction effort against Russia has buoyed the tanker earnings market as selected owners are reporting VLCC rates breaching the USD 100K/day mark. This push in rates is expected to filter into the rest of the sector, which will likely keep second hand values looking strong towards the end of the year. ASYAD have offset their recent spending spree with the sale of scrubber fitted VLCC SAIQ (299K DWT Universal 2011) for USD 57M with TC attached until April at USD 41K/day. This is while another similar vintage VLCC scrubber fitted NAVE CONSTELLATION (296K DWT Dalian 2010) was sold for USD 52.5M, showing there is plenty of demand for this vintage of VLCC at these levels, despite their age. Elsewhere MRs PTI HUDSON/NILE (49K DWT SPP 2016) were sold to Chinese buyers with TC attached at USD 33M. A busy week across the dry market sees market players jostling for position and tonnage. Q3 rates remained consistent and there is widespread optimism for the years ahead, so one could speculate the bottom of the market from a values perspective is firmly in the rearview mirror. A notable variable that will need to be dealt with in 2026 however, is vessel deliveries. Certain subsectors are in line for high growth, particularly Kamsarmax and Ultramax, so buyers will be hoping for some opportunity here. In the standout transaction this week, EBE Belgium purchased scrubber fitted Newcastlemax BULK SAO PAOLO (208K DWT New Times 2020) for USD 72.75M. There has been a recent spike in demand for Newcastlemax units as owners look to take advantage of longer employment opportunities on offer for these vessels.
