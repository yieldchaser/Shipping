## Market Commentary

The VLCC space continues to provide liquidity to the tanker second hand market amid a strong spot market earnings environment. China’s buying appetite for VLCCs is also showing no respite, as LAYLA (317K DWT Hyundai 2007) sold for USD 46.75M. Chinese buyers are also being linked to at least 2 of the 3x scrubber fitted VLCCs sold from CMB.Tech this week, HAKATA/HAKONE (302K DWT Universal 2010) & IRIS (314K DWT HHI 2012) which generated a profit for the Brussels listed owner of USD 96.7M, though no official sale price has been disclosed. In the face of weakening, yet historically still above average, rates and an influx of newbuildings to come this year, there is an ongoing correction in MR values. In this space, CELSIUS PHILADELPHIA/PORTSMOUTH (50K DWT Jiangsu Newyangzi 2021) were sold to IMS with TC attached at USD 35.5M each. In the face of widely reported bearish sentiment, the dry market is delivering solid yet unremarkable returns. The all too familiar feeling that no one is pressed to sell remains a barrier to entry for opportunist buyers but will provide comfort to owners and investors with skin already in the game alike. That said, there is still a fair level of liquidity ranging across sizes and subsectors. This week, Turkish buyers were reported on the sale of ANSAC MOON BEAR (33K DWT Shin Kurushima 2017)*Open Hatch at USD 18.7M.
