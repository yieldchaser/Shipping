## Market Commentary

Tanker transactions gave some clarity to pricing this week, firstly with a significant suezmax transaction and a new benchmark on 15-year-old MRs. Scrubber fitted GOLDWAY/DIAMONDWAY (154K DWT SUNGDONG 2016) were sold for USD 136M en bloc to NAT, the first transaction on 2016 blt suezmax since 2023. Product sector continues to see increasing popularity, with buyers sensing an opportunity off the back of the recent price corrections. NH SIRI/ERLE (49K DWT HMD 2010) were bought en bloc at USD 45M, effectively pricing a 15-year-old MR at USD 22.5M, a far cry from Q3 last year where similar units would have been priced closer to thirty. The dry market is experiencing the usual seasonal bounce, however, owners and charterers are still unsure given the ever changing geopolitical landscape. This has resulted in an uncertain TC market, with few players willing to commit to extended employment in light of this increased uncertainty. Overall on a second hand basis, the situation remains the same, with the majority of activity limited to older tonnage, and modern tonnage very desirable for prospective buyers. In the standout transaction of the week, HMM picked up scrubber fitted Newcastlemax ORIENTAL DRAGON (207K DWT IMABARI 2014) for USD 49.2M.
