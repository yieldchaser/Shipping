## Market Commentary

The pre summer rush appears to have been short lived, with only a handful of reported sales this week. Standing out, however, is resale LR2 HESPERIA TIDE (115K DWT Zhoushan Changhong 2025), bought by Polembros for USD 70M, adding to the 4 other LR2 units they have on order at the same yard. Old VLCCs continue to see relatively high levels of enquiry, with CITY OF TOKYO (303K DWT Universal 2004) selling at a firm USD 41.5M, despite earnings being in the usual summer lull. Elsewhere, the LR1 KOI (74K DWT STX 2010) was sold to Greek interests at USD 23.3M, following a sharp correction in values in this sector. Usually, the excitement and volatility are reserved for spot cape earnings, however, this week all sizes across dry are enjoying a rush in spot returns. Rates particularly in the panamax sector are firming sharply, sitting at USD 15k/day, having spent the majority of the year closer to USD 10K/day. This will likely disincentivise sellers of tonnage, frustrating buyers who are now seeing the price asks of potential candidates rising. In the Kamsarmax sector, the attractiveness of yard is plain to see this week with sale of the Tess 82 ULTRA PUMA (81K DWT Tsuneishi 2016) selling at USD 25M versus a younger yet Chinese built AVICL ATERMIS (81K DWT Jiangsu Jinling 2019) being sold at USD 24.5M.
