## Market Commentary

A rare modern MR transaction took place this week, in turn highlighting the recent sharp correction that has taken place in this sector. Scrubber fitted EDEN (49K dwt HMD 2020)*SSDD due was sold for USD 40M, a correction particularly well illustrated against the scrubber fitted 2014 blt Torm-Sinokor vessels sold in Q3 last year at USD 42.5m. Otherwise, vintage VLCCs are still proving popular, with NEW NAXOS (299K dwt Universal 2003) being sold at USD 33.5M. An unremarkable week across dry, with the recently rallying capesize rates dipping to below USD 20k/day, a reminder to market players that despite some recent optimism, the outlook for 2025 is still very mixed. Sticking to the script of the recent times, mostly older ships are transacted this week, but with the exception of ATLANTIC BRAVE (33K dwt Shin Kurishima 2016) being sold for a below market USD 17.8M, even with a reported WW delivery range.
