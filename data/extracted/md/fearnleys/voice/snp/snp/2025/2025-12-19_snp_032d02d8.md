## Market Commentary

It was said in this report a few weeks ago that owners would be hard pressed to ignore buyers willing to transact at current levels, and it appears that some have caved to the extensive amount of enquiry in the market. Larger owners, for example Scorpio, have been wisely executing sales of sub 10 year old assets while simultaneously ordering new ships, ensuring fleet modernisation whilst cashing in on historically strong values of older units. This week they announced the sales of scrubber fitted STI GALLANTRY/GOAL (109K DWT GSI 2016) for USD 52.3M each. Elsewhere, SFL took money off the table with the sale of suezmax pair SFL OTTOWA/THELON (159K DWT Bohai 2015) for USD 57M each. A quieter than usual week in the second hand dry market, and for good reason. Robust earnings over the last few months have resulted in strong pricing, and with seasonal softening expected in Q1 2026, buyers are more likely to wait. That said, the market goes into the winter break in a strong position, leaving owners hoping for more of the same in the new year. This week, kamsarmax THERESA HAINAN (81K DWT Sainty 2013) was sold for USD 16.8M.
