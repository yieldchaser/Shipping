## Market Commentary

Aside from the unusually busy vintage Panamax sector, likely due to falling rates and abundance of older tonnage still operating, the story of this week is the benchmark set for modern Ultramax tonnage going forward. NORD MAGELLAN (63K DWT IWAGI 2020)*SS/DD due April 25, was sold to Bangladeshi buyers Megha for USD 29.2M, a correction from last done 2020 built ERIN MANX (63K DWT TSUNEISHI ZHOUSHAN 2020) at USD 32.5M late last year. There will be a number of prospective investors watching this sector closely, particularly with a high number of deliveries expected this year. The tanker market has thrown investors and owners into an interesting and unpredictable scenario, following the further sanctions introduced as one of outgoing president Biden’s final acts. It lit the touch paper for TC demand, as owners rushed to secure ships but much of this excitement seems to be short lived. The reality of the situation is that non eco tonnage will still be at the mercy of buyers, despite a flurry of transactions this week riding the positive sentiment. Euronav offloaded a quartet of suezmaxes, a shrewd move given ships of this age traditionally do not have much trading life left. Interesting to note all transactions this week are non eco tonnage. In the languishing product sector, NORD HIMALAYA (49K DWT ONOMICHI 2011) sold for USD 25.5M, a ship that would have been worth comfortably over USD 30M 6 months ago.
