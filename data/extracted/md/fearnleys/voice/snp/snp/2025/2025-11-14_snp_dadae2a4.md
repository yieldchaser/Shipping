## Market Commentary

High spirits this week as much of the tanker market gathered in Dubai for Bahri week, in a time where earnings are holding strong into the winter months. Unsurprisingly in the second-hand space, good spec suezmax units with prompt delivery are attracting a significant amount of market interest. A number of offers were seen on Scrubber fitted STENA SUNSHINE (159K DWT Samsung 2013), which eventually sold to Greek buyers at USD 58M. Despite this, it is worth pointing out that in a period of little over a month, there have been 30 new VLCC and nearly 20 Suezmax orders, showing that plenty of other buyers in the market prefer to focus on newbuildings, rather than compete at these premium levels in the second-hand market. Otherwise, it was the MR market which saw further liquidity, with Montfort purchasing pumproom pair MAERSK MARU/MISSISSIPPI (48K DWT Iwagi Zosen 2011/10) for USD 44M en bloc. A busy week, as ever, in the dry market. In an overall sense, the momentum is with this market despite the incoming usual seasonal slowdown. It will be interesting to assess whether this slowdown will affect asset values in any way, or whether the underlying optimism of the next year or so will carry values over this period. In the ultramax space, GRACE HARMONY (60K DWT Onomichi 2015) was sold for USD 24.8M.
