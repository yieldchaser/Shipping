## Market Commentary

The dry market had a bumper week in transactions, despite the overall uncertain sentiment that has been shared by most market players over the past few weeks. On the Supramax front, sisters GENCO BOURGOGNE & TERESE OETKER 2010 YANGZHOU DAYANG were sold at USD 15.75m and USD 16.1m respectively. Benchmarks which will be most welcome in a market that is seeing an increasing amount of similar tonnage been placed for sale. Capesize SHINYO GUARDIAN 2005 NAMURA was bought by Turkish buyers for USD 17.25m which also marks a new, lower benchmark for this vintage Capesize. The tanker markets were relatively subdued in terms of completed transactions with product tankers capturing most of the activity this week. The standout deal in this space was UOG picking up the DEE4 FIG 2011 ONOMICHI for USD 26.5m. To put this in perspective, the vessel was purchased by DEE4 in August 2020 for USD 16m. Performance Shipping added another Aframax unit to its fleet with the purchase of PHOENIX BEACON 2011 HHI for USD 35.0m.
