## Market Commentary

Dry cargo markets continue to see a flurry of transactions with north of twenty vessels changing hands across all segments. With strong level of enquiries being met by steady stream of sale candidates, values remain relatively flat and pricings fall in line with last done. Notable activity in the Capesize sector where Synergy has bought the 2010 Koyo built MINERAL HAIKU at Usd 34m providing further benchmarks for Japanese designed vessels of similar vintage. Panamax and Kamsarmax segments have also joyed of a pick up of activity with 6 units being sold this week, mostly driven by Chinese built vessels with pricing at relative highs for sellers but at levels that remain attractive for buyers weighing Chinese designs against their Japanese counterparts. Tanker sale and purchase markets seem to have lost steam again off the back of a relative softening on the chartering front. A freight volatility that continues to feed into a widening bid offer spread on the snp front with most potential sellers holding off for levels reflecting a largely expected recovery. As a result only four sales to report this week with most buying enquiries falling in the Aframax space where a number of discussions taking place which may translate into further sales in the near future.
