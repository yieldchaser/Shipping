## Market Commentary

A busy week in the dry market saw a rare sale of a modern asset, in the form of Ultramax BERGE TRONADOR 2020 DACKS built sold for USD 32.8m. Elsewhere, Post-Panamax D SKALKEAS 2011 Newyangzi sold for USD 19.0m to Hong Kong interests. Panamaxes AGRI KINSALE / AGRI QUEEN 2009 Oshima built were sold at auction for USD 16.0m each. Many units remain in the market across the size curve, and players are braced for an interesting Q4 as they jostle for market position. The tanker market shows no signs of cooling off as VLCC PRINCESS MARY 2004 Mitsubishi built sold for a huge USD 48.0m, granted she is SS freshly passed and BWTS fitted, but this total is well over previous market estimates. In the smaller sizes however, the S&P market is beginning to look crowded with a rumoured 50 plus MRs currently seeking new ownership. This is likely to apply pressure to values across in this sector, which saw 5 transactions this week. The pick of the sales were ENERGY PUMA / ENERGY PROGRESS 2008 Sungdong built, sold to Middle Eastern buyers at USD 20.0m each.
