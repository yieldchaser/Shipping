## Market Commentary

All eyes on a tanker market that seems to be going from strength to strength as demonstrated by this week’s reported transactions. The product markets as expected have drawn most of the interest, leading to substantial drive in values with new benchmarks being set across several vintages. The Japanese controlled “CHALLENGE PHOENIX” invited offers on Tuesday after having up to a dozen buyers registering interest; the vessel is now sold at a price in the region of USD 18m to Greek buyers. A jump in pricing which becomes particularly apparent when compared to last months sale of the “ENERGY PANTHER” (47k dwt 2008 Sungdong) which achieved Usd 16.1m. Similar dynamics on the more modern front with JP Morgan achieving Usd 35m for their 2016 SPP built unit “LARGO SUN”. A comparatively subdued Dry SnP market with the most recent market slow down gradually taking hold on the SnP front. We are indeed witnessing a weakening bid with buyers now looking to secure tonnage at levels below last done. We therefore expect a relative correction in values despite the resilience shown by potential sellers who would rather wait over these summer months rather than pulling the trigger at these lower levels.
