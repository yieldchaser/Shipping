## Market Commentary

Increased sale and purchase activity on the dry cargo front where the supramax segment has been capturing most of the buying enquiry with 6 units changing hands this week. A pick up in liquidity pointing to a relative correction in values with new targets being set. A dynamic led by Chinese and Korean designed vessels which in typical fashion are the first to dip with sellers more willing to catch a falling knife by showing less resistance against a bid at less than last done. Elsewhere the Japanese controlled Kamsarmax “ENERGY TRITON” (82k built Tsuneishi 2012) achieved Usd 25m which similarly sets a well needed benchmark in a market that has started the year with a degree of hesitancy as to where the asset values are heading. Signs of ongoing negotiations and inspections suggest further sales in the near future. The tanker market remains extremely subdued in terms of S&P with an underlying chartering market that offers no signs of respite. Noteworthy activity this week is the sale of the VLCC “ATHENIAN SUCCESS” which was sold to Sinokor at USd 42.5m. Levels that remain unsurprisingly flat with same owners having sold the exact sister “ ATHENIAN HARMONY” in December last year at Usd 42m.
