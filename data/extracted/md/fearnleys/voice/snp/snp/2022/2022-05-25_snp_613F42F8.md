## Market Commentary

A flurry of activity in the dry cargo markets with up to 20 vessels changing hands this week. From handysize to capesize the dry SnP space is joying of sustained liquidity providing great transparency on values which in turn facilitate further transactions. A dynamic that becomes particularly well illustrated by the ultramax/supramax sector which sees a consistent amount of units changing hands over the weeks, keeping levels in line with relative last done. This week the “FORTUNE BELL” (Abt 61k dwt built 2020 Shin Kurushima) has been sold at Usd 39m in line with last months sale of the “ULTRA INITIATOR” (abt 62k built 2019 Oshima) at Usd 37.5m while the “OSIOS DAVID” (Abt 55k dwt built 2012 IHI) achieved a price of Usd 22.5m paving the way for the several sisterships that are currently in the market solicitating interest. A pickup in activity in the handysize segment this with 7 vessels being sold this week. On the tanker front Sinokor continues this year’s shopping spree with the acquisition of the two VLCCs “NUCLEUS” & “NEPTUN” (Abt 300k dwt built 2007 Dalian) at a price of Usd 30.8m each. Notable activity continuing to take place in the Aframax sector with a number of region 10 year old units being under discussions – as previously reported the long standing bid/ask spread seems to be slowly closing up spurring potential buyers into action. A dynamic that we expect to hold in the near future translating into heightened liquidity and an appreciation in asset values. Similar activity witnessed in the product markets where the strong rate environment continues to drive interest upwards.
