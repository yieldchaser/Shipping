## Market Commentary

A slight cooling in the charter market was duly ignored this week as S&P activity ramped up in the tanker market. The first VLCC transaction of a 10 year old unit since Q1 was registered in the form of the C. PASSION blt 2013 HHI which was sold for USD 66m to European buyers. If viewed in regard to the old valuation adage that a 10 year old ship is worth roughly half a newbuild, this transaction will put a smile on the faces owners of modern VLCC tonnage – not that they need any excuse. MR owners should be advised not to get carried away by the sale of ATHLOS blt 2016 Samsung HI Ningbo for USD 41m, which was bought at a significant premium for US Naval purposes. Elsewhere, Pareto purchased LR1 Sisters CAPE TEMPEST/TAMPA blt 2007/08 New Times en bloc for USD 34m. Uncertainty continues to govern sentiment in the dry space, with market players watching the worsening COVID-19 situation in China with trepidation. A transaction of note was the sale of Newcastlemax sisters CHINA STEEL TEAM/ENTREPRENEUR blt 2006/07 China Shipbld en bloc for USD 35m to Greek buyers. Elsewhere down the size curve, there were a number of handy transactions including SEASTAR HARRIER blt 2022 Hakodate for USD 30.9m and TS Bravo blt 2015 Shanhaiguan for USD 16.75m.
