## Market Commentary

This week comes to a close with both sectors joying of an increased amount of sales; a liquidity however that reflects two very different underlying market sentiment. The long awaited tanker recovery seems to have become a long distant memory with a number of potential sellers now taking the decision to pull the trigger and dispose of assets that they have for so long resisted to sell at substantially corrected levels. A dynamic particularly well illustrated in the mid 2000 built Aframax front which has seen a great number of potential candidates being marketed over the past months, but by sellers hoping to capture the anticipation of a market rebound. Same sellers who have now decided to go meet the bid setting new benchmarks for other owners looking to catch the falling knife. Dry cargo snp markets seem to also have set its direction seeing buying interest strengthening amongst all sectors with buyers willing to now pay levels in line with last done, in order to secure the right candidates. Values therefore remain particularly well supported both by a strong level of buying interest but also by a pool of sellers confident in the longevity of this market.
