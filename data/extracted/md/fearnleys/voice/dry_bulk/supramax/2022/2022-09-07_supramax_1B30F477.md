## Market Commentary

The Supra and Ultra Atlantic markets remain pretty much unchanged from previous weeks. Though we can notice ECSA market was a bit more active compared to the previous week, levels remained unchanged. Supra 56,000 dwt was fixed at USD 20,000 pd delivery NCSA to China. Another Supra 52,000 dwt was fixed from Brazil to Lagos with sugar at USD 18,000 pd paying a premium for HRA.

**Black Sea**

 market expects some improvement after Ukraine started exporting grain. The market reacted positively and remains stable in the Black Sea and Mediterranean. Trips to Far East paying around USD 21,000 pd on Ultramaxes basis Canakkale delivery. The Pacific basin showed some signs of more activity and rates are stable. Round trips to China on Supra with delivery were fixed at USD 19,000 pd. Supramax with delivery SE Asia fixed USD 21,000 pd.
