## Market Commentary

Since last week there has been a drop in the number of vessels heading to the North Atlantic/Continent. Vessels heading to the major loading countries in Asia seems like reached a peak. Not much change in vessels heading to the Med/Black Sea or the South Atlantic. Nothing clear and obvious on near-term direction in other words. Looking at macro-factors it is hard to see how demand growth is going to push upwards enough to lift the market above the USD 17,000-20,000 pd range. We believe the risk is clearly to the downside.
