## Market Commentary

With bigger uncertainty on the Russian coal ban and further sanctions it’s hard to say what direction the market will take – but would say market will remain strong with a slight drop over the easter. Owners willing to call Russian ports still seeing huge premiums at rates around 70-80,000. FFA kept falling all week whilst South America FH remains thick firmer. It’s basically been a drop all over further markets and the gap between the bid/offer keeps spreading. Period market have also seen a heavy correction and is more or less not even present on the longer ones. Rate wise Continent front haul was fixed at lower 20s on a Supramax – from USG similar vessel where fixed low 30k.
