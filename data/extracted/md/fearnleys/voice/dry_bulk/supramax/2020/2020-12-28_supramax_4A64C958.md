## Market Commentary

The last week of 2020 and Christmas holidays last week added some rush activity from certain areas in the Atlantic, mainly USG and NCSA. The BSI maintained relatively good level and healthy period fixtures were concluded. Ultramax 60,000 dwt was able to obtain USD 11,000 for period delivery in China. The US Gulf is the main attraction area with Ultramax fixing USD 22,000s for trips to the East Mediterranean and in the USD 28,000s for trips to China. Trips from Brazil and Argentina was active, however rates for a 61,000 dwt were reported fixed in the mid teens. The Asia market was very much positional as some owners were lucky to obtain higher rates than others. A 55,000 dwt open Philippines fixing a trip via Indonesia redelivery China at USD 13,000, while others was fixing only in the low USD 11,000s. West coast Indian trips into China were reported at the level of USD 14,000.
