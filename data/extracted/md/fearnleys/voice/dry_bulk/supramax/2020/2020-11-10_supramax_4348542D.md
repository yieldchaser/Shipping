## Market Commentary

From the beginning of this week indexes kept heading south. Very little activity and in general we believe the market is fading away. There are a few grain cargoes recently surfacing in the USG to Far East destinations. Rates reported in the region of USD 21,000 for Ultramax and below USD 20,000 on Supra. ECSA also losing ground and rates reported on fronthaul run in the upper USD 13,000's plus USD 375,000 ballast bonus. Scrap from the Continent to Turkey was fixing in the high USD 10,000's. Tonnage building up in East Mediterranean putting further pressure on rates. Limited fronthauls with grain ex Black Sea made owners to adjust rates downwards. The Asian market was stable, but rates was also under pressure. Nopac and Australian round voyages was reported fixing Tess 58 index type at USD 8,500's with respective ballast bonus on APS load port.
