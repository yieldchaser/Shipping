## Market Commentary

Sliding rates across the board with most of the areas losing ground. In the Atlantic, still seeing healthy rates from Continent although it’s not as tight as previous weeks. Owners are fetching around USD 18,000 for scrap from Continent to East Mediterranean and around USD 14-15k to USG, while from USG to Far East are trading in the low 20k's and TA in the mid teens. From the Indian Ocean, ships are covering South Africa to SE Asia around USD 11,500+150k gbb, while further East Nopac rounds fixing at around USD 10,250 and Indonesia/China coal rounds at low USD 9k basis South China delivery.
