## Market Commentary

Less excitement from market this week. With paper dropping and period market remaining rather flat. From Atl basin most action came from USG, where fhauls were covered at USD 26000. Supra from USG to Egypt got covered at low USD 20 000.

**Continent**

 remained flat . Usual scrap runs on smaller supra covered at USD 17000 and on ultramax same was paying USD 20000. Some talks about scaling down panamax cargoes to ultra/supra might add some action, but rather for breachers. From ECSA usual soyabeans runs to SE ASIA got covered at ca USD 16000 + 550k ballast bonus. Ultramax from Durban to China was covered at low USD 14000 + 400k ballast bonus for end feb dates. Indo-India rounds lost most ground , where same was paying USD 9500. On the period front a Supra was placed at USD 14000 levels for 4-6 monts and Atl trading and an Ultra got secured for 4-6 mos at high USD 15000.
