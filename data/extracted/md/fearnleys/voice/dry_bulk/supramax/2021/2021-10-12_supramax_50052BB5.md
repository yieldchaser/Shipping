## Market Commentary

Slight uptick in vessels heading to the major loading countries in Asia. We could see this increase further going forward due to the desperate need for coal. In the Atlantic, sentiment remaining strong from USG. Grains from USG to Far East were fixed closer to USD 55,000 and trips to the Continent covered around mid 40s. Ultramax for Continent round was fixed at USD 43,000. Bauxite run from Kamsar to North Continent at USD 34,000. From the East, more Indonesian coal coming into market. A Supramax open Bohai Bay was rumoured fixed in the low USD 30,000s for trip to SE Asia. Bigger Handy was covered around USD 43,000 for Indo/China round. On the period front, Ultramaxes were discussed at low USD 30,000 for 1 year and low 40s for 4-6 months.
