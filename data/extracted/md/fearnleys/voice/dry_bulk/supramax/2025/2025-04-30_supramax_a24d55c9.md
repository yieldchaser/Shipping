## Market Commentary

The Supramax and Handysize markets remained largely subdued, with minimal fresh activity and a lack of clear direction across both basins. In the Supramax market, the South Atlantic continued to offer a glimmer of optimism, supported by steady demand, while the US Gulf and Asian markets remained flat amid limited enquiry. The overall tone was muted, reflecting a cautious sentiment from both owners and charterers. The Handysize market followed a similar pattern, with the South Atlantic standing out as the most stable region, backed by consistent transatlantic cargo flows. In contrast, the Continent, Mediterranean, and US Gulf faced downward pressure due to oversupply and weak demand, while Asia remained stagnant amid a build-up of tonnage and scarce fresh enquiries. Both segments showed marginal movements in their respective indices, reflecting the overall lack of momentum in the market.
