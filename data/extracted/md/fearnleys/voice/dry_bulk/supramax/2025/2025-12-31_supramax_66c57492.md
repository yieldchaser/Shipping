## Market Commentary

The Handy and Supramax markets remain under pressure as activity continues to slow ahead of the Christmas holidays, with prompt tonnage outweighing demand and charterers firmly in control. In the Handysize sector, sentiment is weak across both basins, with the Continent, Mediterranean, US Gulf and South Atlantic all seeing limited enquiry and softening rates, while Asia remains subdued as owners lower expectations to secure employment. The Supramax market reflects a similarly negative tone, particularly in the Atlantic and US Gulf where a lack of fresh cargoes and a growing tonnage list continue to weigh on rates, while the Mediterranean, South Atlantic and Asia lack momentum despite some minor interest from the Indian Ocean. Overall, sentiment across both segments remains cautious to bearish, with little indication of near-term improvement during the holiday period.
