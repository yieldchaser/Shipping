## Market Commentary

The market remained muted across most regions with limited fresh enquiry and growing caution among participants.

**Atlantic**

 rates were under pressure due to oversupply of tonnage and weak demand, particularly for backhaul business. The South Atlantic showed some balance, but owners were cautious and hesitant to drop rates further. In the Pacific, demand was weak, especially from Indonesia, keeping freight rates subdued. Owners resisted lowering rates in the Mediterranean and Indian Ocean regions, but overall sentiment stayed soft. The 11TC average declined steadily over the week, falling from USD 12,429 on April 1st to USD 12,058 by April 8th. Period market remains quiet.
