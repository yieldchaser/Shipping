## Market Commentary

It has been a rather quiet start to 2024 on the cargo side. However, lots of activity on period front and operators are rushing to take in tonnage on the Handy and Supra market. 60/61k dwt vessels being fixed at around USD 14,000-15,000 levels for 1 year duration and Handy market hovers around high USD 11,000/12,000 for 1 year. Meantime, the usual Indonesia-India Supra being fixed around 8k levels, Indonesia-China on a Supra around USD 12,000.

**Pacific**

**Far East**
