## Market Commentary

The Supra/Ultra market is weak with rates coming down across basins on. Cargo volumes in the USG & Continent have been low, and cargoes are covered at below last done levels in view of ample tonnage availability. The West Africa market has been active with a handful of stems in the market for fronthaul direction and all covered away. Indian Ocean & MEG markets have been fairly quiet. Meanwhile in the Pacific markets, the usual Indonesia-India coal stems have been covered well below last done levels. At this stage, there is a bearish sentiment in the market and period activity has been dull lately on the geared sizes.
