## Market Commentary

The Supramax was mixed. In the Atlantic, activity remained positional, with differing views on whether the US Gulf had reached its bottom. However, the USG seems to be picking up slowly. The Mediterranean-Continent region saw some activity, but fresh inquiries were limited, while the South Atlantic stayed balanced but quiet. In Asia, sentiment was more positive, though demand remained weak however there has been a supply of prompt tonnage. Overall, the market didn’t see much change. The Handysize was similarly quiet, with limited activity in the Atlantic. Some fresh demand surfaced in the South Atlantic, while the US Gulf held steady. The Pacific was more active, with tight tonnage availability supporting decent rates.
