## Market Commentary

There is not much action in the market on the first trading week after the New Year celebrations. The soft tone presents, where USG seems to be most affected. Oversupply in the Atlantic and slow demand in the Pacific decreased the rates. Escalating conflict in the Red Sea and the Black Sea adds to the uncertainty in the market direction. The evidence of less demand in USG and South Atlantic putting pressure on rates. The Baltic BSI routes on S1 and S4 dropped by approx USD 2,000 pd a day. In the Pacific, a similar situation with weak demand and a slow start of the year pushed owners to accept lower rates. Inter Pacific trading around USD 10,000 pd.
