## Market Commentary

The Supramax and Handysize markets showed mixed conditions with a generally softer tone in the Atlantic and steadier activity in Asia. In the Continent and Mediterranean, both markets remained relatively balanced with stable demand. However, the US Gulf and South Atlantic faced increasing pressure due to a growing tonnage list and limited cargo demand, leading to weaker sentiment. In contrast, the Asian market remained comparatively firmer, supported by steady trading activity and tighter vessel availability in some areas, although rising bunker prices and fuel constraints made some charterers more cautious.
