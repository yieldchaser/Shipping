## Market Commentary

Supramax and Handy markets remained under pressure over the past couple of sessions, with sentiment largely subdued across both basins. Limited fresh enquiry and steady to growing tonnage availability continued to weigh on rates, particularly in the Atlantic where demand remained thin. The Continent–Mediterranean region showed little change, with muted activity and largely stable conditions. In Asia, sluggish trading persisted as weaker cargo volumes and cautious charterer activity kept momentum in check. Elevated bunker costs and uncertainty further dampened sentiment across regions. Overall, the market continues to drift with softening fundamentals and no clear signs of recovery in the near term.
