## Market Commentary

The Capesizes are experiencing a rally this week, supported by a range of factors but increased iron ore prices being the main driver as iron ore prices have reached its highest value since 2013. Activity from Brazil and Australia is therefore healthy, and rates are increasing healthy day by day. The average index broke 20,000 at the beginning of this week and is expected to strengthen further.
