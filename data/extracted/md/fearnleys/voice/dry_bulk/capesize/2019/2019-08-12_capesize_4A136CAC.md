## Market Commentary

Confidence regained for the big ships after a brief nervous period, average 180000-tonners now enjoying daily spot earnings of close to usd 28k. Considerable geographical bunker differentials and weather issues cause tonnage supply disruptions. whilst iron ore prices are up and all major miners needing/taking prompt units. The West Australia/China trade is stable in volume with resultant gains in daily earnings of 23 pct for pacific rounds, coming in at usd 27k. With assistance from increased fronthaul activity, transatlantic levels are up 18 pct to print at usd 25k. General outlook is positive as reflected in FFA support and a hectic period ahead for extensive scrubber retrofits and IMO 2020 compliance works.
