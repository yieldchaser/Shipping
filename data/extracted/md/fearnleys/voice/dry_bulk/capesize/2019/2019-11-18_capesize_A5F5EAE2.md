## Market Commentary

Nervous and softening, with average daily earnings down 10 pct w-o-w to come in at USD 18,500 as Brazil iron ore exports again fail to live up to expectations and Atlantic coal is in limbo. Although hardly significant in relative terms, a 25 pct drop in transatlantic earnings has considerable effect on sentiment. Fronthaul volumes however expected to increase, and with a short list of ballasters towards ECSAM there is quite some optimism amongst players.

**Far East**

 volumes are stable and just enough to withstand further negativity, the Dampier/Qingdao conference trade hovering around USD 8.75 pmt and Pacific rounds for modern 180,000-tonners just a fragment down to pay a present USD 22-22,500. FFA's give little support for considerable period activity, and recent long-term conclusions appear limited to 177,000 dwt, built 2006, done for about 2 years at USD 15k or touch more.
