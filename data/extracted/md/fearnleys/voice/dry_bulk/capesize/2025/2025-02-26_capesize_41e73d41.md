## Market Commentary

On the West Australia front, we see a significant uptick in enquiries from operators and tenders alike for 10 March onwards dates. Some were seeking forward tonnage for end March and few enquired for full April dates primarily on an index linked basis. Enquiries for East Australia coal and other fronts of the Pacific have ticked up slightly as well, adding to the overall optimism of the market as we approach mid-week. On C3 ex Brazil to China and West Africa, we see a majority of enquiries for second half of March dates.

**Far East**
