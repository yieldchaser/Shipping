## Market Commentary

The Capesize market began the week on a soft note before gaining momentum midweek, though this improvement tapered off amid lighter activity in both the Atlantic and Pacific. Early pressure on rates was offset from midweek onward as increased cargo inquiry and firmer fixing on key routes, particularly C3 and C5, helped stabilize sentiment and lift levels. In the Pacific, steady demand from the miners supported gradual rate improvement, while the Atlantic experienced tighter tonnage lists, which in turn bolstered both Transatlantic runs and fronthaul employment. Market confidence was further supported by reports of a one-year trade truce between the U.S. and China.
