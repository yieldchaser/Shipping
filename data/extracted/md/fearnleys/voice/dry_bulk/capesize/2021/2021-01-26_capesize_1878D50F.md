## Market Commentary

As congestion have eased and some of the activity slowed down for seasonal reasons, the time charter index fell by USD 7767 during the last week. This is obviously quite dramatic, but it has also been expected by most market participants as February is usually a slower period for capes . The C3 route, which represents iron ore from Brazil to China is down by 13%, and the C5 West Australian loaded iron ore to China is down by 18%.
