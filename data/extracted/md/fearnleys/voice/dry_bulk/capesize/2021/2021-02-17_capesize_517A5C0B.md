## Market Commentary

The Capesize market have seen a tremendous recovery over the last week. The two main routes with iron ore from Brazil and Australia to China is up by 23% and 46%, and the total time charter basket of all routes is up by 48%. This is backed by a very firm Panamax market and an increasingly strong belief amongst market participants in the Dry Bulk segment.
