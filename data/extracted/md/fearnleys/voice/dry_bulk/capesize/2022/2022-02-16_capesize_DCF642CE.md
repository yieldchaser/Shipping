## Market Commentary

Winter doldrums not yet over for the big ships, and spot values presently stuck at close to half that of Supras/Panamaxes. Average daily earnings flat w-o-w at USD low 12k's as both major-volume trades West Australia/China and Brazil/Feast fail to meet expectations. Brazilian miners still battling weather-related production- and shipping delays, causing concern for a growing number of ballasters looking for March fronthaul alternatives - and owners discounting heavily quick Far East rounds in anticipation of better times to come. Only bright spot being the West Africa/Far East trades, compensating for part of the missing Atlantic commodity flows. Period interest and activity lives its own life as most experienced takers well aware this is the time of year when bargains are secured. Levels reflect present forward views, and representative fixtures include 180,000 dwt, built 2012, fetching USD 27,300 basis 9-13 months and 179,000 dwt, built 2011, done for 19-22 months at USD 26,500.
