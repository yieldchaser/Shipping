## Market Commentary

After a positive development last week, this week started with uncertainty for the Indonesian coal export after the government put a ban during the weekend. Though most of the drama has calmed down, both charterers and owners for this trade has avoided new business before the situation is clearer. As options for owners now are limited, charterers for other routs seen more interest coming their way and market started to slide. West Australia to China is down by USD 1.20 per metric ton w-o-w. On a more positive note, Atlantic basin is holding and as the congestion in China increasing, we might see a change in sentiment over the coming days and week.
