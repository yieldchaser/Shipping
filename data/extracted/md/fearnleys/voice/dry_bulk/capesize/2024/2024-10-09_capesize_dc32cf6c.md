## Market Commentary

On the West Australia front, we see enquiries from miners, operators, and tender primarily for last October dates and some operators enquiring for forward November dates. Volumes out of East Australia remained flat from last week and picked up slightly as we approach mid-week for late October to early November dates. Some activity on the ex Indonesia and North Pacific front for late October dates. On C3 ex Brazil to China, a majority of operators were seeking for November dates while some still enquiring for late October dates.

**Far East**

 spot tonnage is abundant with availability of prompt and early vessels. Ballasting tonnage is thinning for late October and heavily weighed on first half of November. On C5, the week started with fixtures concluding at mid-high USD 10 pmt levels and retreated to low USD 10 pmt levels and subsequently to mid-high USD 9 pmt levels by mid-week. On C3, we see fixtures concluding at low USD 26 pmt levels for early November and sub USD 26 pmt levels for end November.
