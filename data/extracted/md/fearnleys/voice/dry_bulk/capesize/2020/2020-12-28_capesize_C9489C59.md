## Market Commentary

Activity fading at healthy levels towards year-end, with average spot earnings hovering around USD 15-16k for standard type tonnage. Major miners enjoy historically high iron ore prices, and keep being busy stacking up tonnage for up to mid January loading from West Australia where spot rates have at times gone in excess of USD 8.00 pmt. Similar pattern from Brazil, where a generally tight Atlantic market see anything between USD 15.00 and USD 16.00 pmt done for the Brazil/China route depending on terms and dates. This situation expected to continue into the new year. Limited period activity as ffa levels not supporting expectations – representative fixtures include mediocre 177,000 dwt/built 2010 concluded for 16-10 months at USD 14k.
