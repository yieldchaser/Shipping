## Market Commentary

The heavy drop continued this week with the Brazil-China route still suffering an oversupply of ballasters. The routes for this segment are down by 19% on voyage basis (c3) and down by 62% on time charter basis. The lack of activity in other segments gives no support to the basket pricing, which is down by 60% to USD 2,000 per day. We now see levels stabilizing as it is simply no margins left before we go into negative hire levels, however there is still a lot of available tonnage so it’s unlikely to see a significant increase in rates within the next week(s).
