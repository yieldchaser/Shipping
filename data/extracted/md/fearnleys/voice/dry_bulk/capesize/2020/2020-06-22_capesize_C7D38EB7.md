## Market Commentary

The Capesize rally continues with similar gain in the average rates as previous week, from 19,000 daily last week to close to 29,000 daily today. Iron ore is still the key factor, linked to restocking and increased infrastructure spending in China. Of the key iron ore routes, Australia c5 is up USD 2 to present high 9s pmt, and Brazil’s c3 is close to USD 22 from low 19s. The period activity is firm, with short period being the main interest as first quarter 2021 is still valued relatively low, sub USD 10,000.
