## Market Commentary

We see the market continuing its move upwards despite an expected quiet week with holidays in China. The Brazil to China route with iron ore is the main driver where it’s been a lot of activity with levels up 18% from last week. However, in terms of increase the north Atlantic is the most impressive with the fronthaul rate being up 50% and transatlantic up 69%. The Pacific is more stable, but still with good amount of activity is up 3%. For now, the market seems very solid and with a thin available tonnage list for Brazil it is expected to see further near-term improvement.
