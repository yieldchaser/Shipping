## Market Commentary

Over the last week the market has come off by 26% for all routes, and the sentiment have been very gloomy. However, the market finally found some support and today we see better freight for both Brazil-China and West Australia-China, the two major routes for iron ore. This has turned the sentiment positive and the cargo list has increased especially in the South Atlantic region. That said, not everyone are convinced we are set for a new upturn so it’s still vessels pricing decent freight levels though at higher prices.
