## Market Commentary

Capesize market under pressure with the basket of all routs down 19% week on week. The activity on West Australia has calmed down and in the Atlantic we see Panamax pricing cheaper for the same trade. Thus, charters are splitting their Cape stems into Panamax and Cape list of tonnage increasing. On a positive note, the activity in South Atlantic still fairly high and Cape freight is now getting competitive compared to Panamax.
