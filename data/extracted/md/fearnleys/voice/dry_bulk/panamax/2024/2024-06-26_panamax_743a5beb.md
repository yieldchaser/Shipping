## Market Commentary

This week, the Panamax market saw a continued downward trend in rates across both the Atlantic and Pacific basins. Limited activity, particularly in the Atlantic, and an underwhelming South American market contributed to the decline. The Pacific arena remained lethargic, with owners discounting shorter runs to minimize exposure to depressed rates. Despite firm fundamentals, including ton-time growth outpacing fleet growth, the market lacked sufficient cargo influx to halt the slide. The Kamsarmax index dropped below the Supramax index, suggesting limited downside from current levels. Holidays in Greece added to the subdued market sentiment, with little indication of an immediate turnaround.
