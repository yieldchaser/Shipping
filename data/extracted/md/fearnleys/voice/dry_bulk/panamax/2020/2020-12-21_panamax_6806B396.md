## Market Commentary

We were optimistic and cheerful about the dry cargo market. How drastically that took us by surprise in March with a new phenomenon called Covid19. In January wearing a mask in a store would get you arrested, now in December it’s the new normal. Market at a glance, January vs December if you look at fuel difference 586-393=193 USD. On a transatlantic average of 700 mt x193 USD diff=135,100 USD/50 days duration=2,702 pr day difference just on fuel January vs December. The market continued to ease as we close in on the final full working week prior to Xmas holidays. Many started the week talking of a slower market as activity levels waned. This was in fact the case as many of the operator’s books appear complete for the year. This has been adding some additional pressure and the indices appeared to reflect same with a full week of losses. All Panamax indices is red this week. The market was weaker across all regions, as a lack of activity owing in large to a lack of bids with charterers stepping back and seemingly happy to wait prior the holiday break.
