## Market Commentary

This week the Panamax market has experienced a slight uptick in rates in both hemispheres. There are however signs of slight weakening in the Pacific market the last couple of days. TA’s are currently yielding in the 10-11k region, while Pacific rounds are being fixed at 12k. We do see more demand for minerals in the North Atlantic and a quite tight tonnage list for now. This can turn quickly when available tonnage will re-appear.
