## Market Commentary

The market has continued its negative trend since last week, with rates sliding in both hemispheres. We did see Atlantic voyage fixtures being concluded with TCE to low 9,000 already end last week, thus we do feel the index has been lagging somewhat as p1A is being priced at high 10,000 mid-week. The slow demand compared to the growing tonnage list is what we are up against. In the Pacific, there is a higher volume of concluded business, however rates are down some USD 2,000 w-o-w and currently yielding 10,000.
