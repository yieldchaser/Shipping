## Market Commentary

After weeks of declining markets in both hemispheres we now seem to be at a turning point. It is more positive talks out in the market and the Baltic index is gaining values. However, it is not directly visible on rates being fixed - just yet. TA’s are currently yielding high USD 9k while a NoPac round fixed USD 12,500 bss N.China delivery. We do see more demand in the market, but also a long tonnage list, thus we do not expect a big rally to happen.
