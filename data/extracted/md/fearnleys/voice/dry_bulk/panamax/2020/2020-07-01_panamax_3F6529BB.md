## Market Commentary

With holidays in China end of last week, the market took a breather with little business done. The gap between bid/ask was significant, and mid-week we finally see that a common ground has been found. The Atlantic market has seen a daily increase in rates where p1A currently yields around the upper USD 12k, and a general optimistic view for further increase across the market for the coming weeks. In the Eastern hemisphere we see a slow growing list of available tonnage and lack of fresh requirements to absorbe same. This is again evident in the rates achieved where p3A slowly coming off and at time of writing yields around USD 9,600.
