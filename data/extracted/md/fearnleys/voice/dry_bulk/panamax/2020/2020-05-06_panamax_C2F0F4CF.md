## Market Commentary

The softening trend from last week continued into this week. Allthough some Indian players re-entered the mrkt on Monday with coal from Sth Africa and Indonesia - it has not relieved the pressure on tonnage comitted for ECSA. Rates keeps softening and ships are being reported fixed at ard Usd 8,5k retro Spore or ard 11,5k +150k GBB bss APS delivery. In the North Atlantic we also face fixtures at below last done, and owners tends to book the cgos in order to delay positions for short durational business. In the Pacific we also see rates tumbling with Aussie rounds being concluded at 5,5-6k bss delivery SE Asia. With most Asian countries (except Japan) back after holidays mid week – it could lead to some more activity going forward.
