## Market Commentary

The market continuing its negative sentiment in both hemispheres. For a transatlantic trip, Kamsarmaxes (82,500 DWT) now earn around the low USD 4,000's, while short fronthauls from the UK/Continent-region are priced at around USD 13,000. From ECSA chrtrs are paying around USD 13,000 + 300,000 gross ballast bonus for a trip to China, or around USD 7,000 DOP Singapore. A transpacific round voyage in the East pay shipowners around USD 4,000 in the current market, and the Baltic 4TC-Index is down 133 points since last week, now at just 627 points.
