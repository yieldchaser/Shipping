## Market Commentary

This week the Panamax market has turned firmer after a quieter start, with sentiment improving across both basins. In the Atlantic, early pressure from a longer Continent tonnage list and limited transatlantic demand has eased as NCSA grain activity picked up, giving owners more confidence and pushing fronthaul levels higher, while ECSA remains supported by steady demand and tighter prompt tonnage.

**USG**

 activity is still relatively flat and transatlantic volumes remain thin, but the tone is clearly more constructive. In the Pacific, the market remains firm despite some Golden Week disruption, with Australia and Indonesia coal/mineral demand providing the main support, prompt tonnage looking tight, and modern/premium units in particularly short supply. With China back in the market and Japan returning, activity is picking up and owners are again showing stronger resistance.
