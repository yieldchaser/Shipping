## Market Commentary

The Panamax market has shown a steadier and more constructive tone this week, with the Atlantic improving on the back of better fronthaul and Transatlantic enquiry, allowing owners to push firmer ideas despite a tonnage list that is still by no means tight. The North Atlantic has benefited from improved replenishment and better support out of USG/USEC, while ECSA remains the softer pocket, particularly for prompt ships, although forward April positions appear more stable. In the Pacific, sentiment has also strengthened, helped by a shorter spot list, decent demand out of Indonesia and Australia, and a touch more period interest, but bunker volatility continues to cast a shadow over negotiations and remains the key variable shaping sentiment across both basins.
