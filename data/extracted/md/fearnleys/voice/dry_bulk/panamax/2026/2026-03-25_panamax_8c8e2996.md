## Market Commentary

The Panamax has opened the week on a softer note, with pressure in both basins as cargo flow has yet to match the growing tonnage count. In the Atlantic, transatlantic remains largely flat, fronthaul continues to lack real momentum, and ECSA is drifting as prompt ships build against limited nearby demand, forcing owners to show more flexibility. In the Pacific, sentiment has also weakened, with charterers holding back, bids slipping below last done, and owners facing a longer list in a market still short of fresh cargo. For now, the overall tone remains cautious, and without a clearer pickup in volume, rates are likely to stay under pressure in the near term.
