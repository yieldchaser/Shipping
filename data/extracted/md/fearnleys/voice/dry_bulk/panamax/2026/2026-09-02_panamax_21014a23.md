## Market Commentary

The Panamax market has firmed steadily through the week, with the gains broadening across both basins as the index pushed higher over successive sessions. In the Atlantic the tone is constructive: transatlantic business remains well bid on the back of a replenished cargo book and a tightening nearby tonnage list, while fronthaul has been slower to follow, with owners holding above charterers' ideas on US Gulf and South American grain. The Pacific has been the stronger of the two, where a steady flow of Australian and Indonesian coal alongside North Pacific grain has left prompt tonnage short and charterers chasing owners' levels rather than the reverse. Period interest has picked up markedly, with a good spread of owners collecting for both short and twelve-month cover and bids increasingly obliged to follow last-done rather than set it, although the forward curve continues to price modest backwardation beyond the prompt. We expect the tone to remain sideways-to-firmer into next week, with the main question being how much of the recent move reflects genuine incremental demand rather than positioning ahead of the autumn grain programme.
