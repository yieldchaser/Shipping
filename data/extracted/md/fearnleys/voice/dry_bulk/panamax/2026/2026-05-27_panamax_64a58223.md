## Market Commentary

The Panamax/Kamsarmax market opened the week cautiously after the holidays, with sentiment still lacking conviction despite some firmer undertones. The Atlantic appears broadly balanced, with prompt tonnage largely absorbed, slimmer lists in the Continent/West Mediterranean and ECSA supported by firmer paper, while the Pacific looks more constructive on stronger Australian minerals and steady Indonesia/China demand, with owners showing more resistance. That said, the broader indicators remain bearish near-term: shipment volumes are only modestly ahead of fleet growth, coal contango has widened, and both Pacific and South Atlantic tightness indicators point to potential softening, suggesting any upside may remain fragile unless fresh cargo volumes build quickly.
