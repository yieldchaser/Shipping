## Market Commentary

The Panamax market has firmed steadily through the week, with the index posting consecutive gains and sentiment improving across both basins. The Pacific has been the clearer engine: NoPac and Australian grain and coal demand has absorbed the prompt tonnage that had been weighing on the market, and owners are now comfortable holding their levels while charterers chase. In the Atlantic the North has been the steadier of the two, supported by a healthy coal and minerals programme into the Continent that has kept fronthaul well bid, while the South Atlantic has traded more sideways as the market looks toward the next grain window and owners show little urgency to discount. Period enquiry has picked up noticeably, with a wider range of owners and operators willing to engage on one-year business, although bids continue to sit modestly behind owners' ideas. With paper firmer and tonnage tightening into September, we expect the tone to remain sideways-to-firmer in the near term.
