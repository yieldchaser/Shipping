## Market Commentary

The Panamax market presents mixed signals this week. On the one hand, the coming months might see an upward trend, aided by the US grains season. On the other, vessel activity to Asia, especially China, is declining. European holidays have limited market exchanges, but the market remains robust. The North Atlantic tonnage is tight, with notable gains in the Baltic area due to August demands. Furthermore, a strong September demand in ECSA has shipowners confidently asking for higher rates, even amidst an overall slow market and a dip in the Baltic Exchange Index for Panamax.
