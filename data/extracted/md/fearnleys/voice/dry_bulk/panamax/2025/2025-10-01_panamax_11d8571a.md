## Market Commentary

The Panamax market has struggled this week with sentiment firmly on the back foot. In the Atlantic, a lack of fresh mineral demand and thinning trans-Atlantic volumes have weighed on rates, while fronthaul interest out of the US East Coast has not been enough to absorb the growing tonnage list across the Continent and Mediterranean. East Coast South America continues to slide as charterers push levels down despite owners’ resistance. In the Pacific, Golden Week has drained activity with most Chinese requirements already covered, leaving limited fresh demand and spot ships struggling to find employment. With FFA support also absent, fixtures are being concluded below last done and the near-term outlook remains soft.
