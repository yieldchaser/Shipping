## Market Commentary

The Panamax market showed signs of weakening this week, with limited upside as seasonal grain exports from the US and Canada began to decline and vessel counts to Indonesia fell. In the Atlantic, the North saw a modest pickup in transatlantic activity on fresh spot demand from NCSA, while the South remained quiet, partly due to the Geneva dry conference. Tonnage in the Mediterranean is building, though sentiment remains cautiously firm. In the Pacific, the market continued to soften amid an oversupplied tonnage list and slow cargo replenishment, with rates easing further across NoPac and Australia trades. Support from ECSA grain volumes remains a key stabilizing factor.
