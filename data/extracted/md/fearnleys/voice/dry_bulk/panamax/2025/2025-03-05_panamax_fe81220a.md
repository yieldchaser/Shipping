## Market Commentary

The Panamax market remained subdued this week across both Atlantic and Pacific basins, facing persistent headwinds from an oversupply of tonnage and limited fresh cargoes. In the Atlantic, despite some uptick in grain activity from NCSA, the continued build-up of available vessels in ECSA weighed heavily on rates, now dipping below USD 10k/day amid sluggish fixture activity for 2H March. In the Pacific, the market faced persistent downward pressure as grain round voyages were scarce, compounded by reports of cancelled Indonesian cargoes due to pricing volatility, further eroding sentiment. Although Australian coal cargoes from Cape splits offered minor relief in volumes, they failed to halt the overall downward trend, with rates steadily declining and little immediate upside anticipated.
