## Market Commentary

The Panamax market started the week firmly supported by tight tonnage and strong grain-driven enquiry, particularly in the North Atlantic and ECSA regions, though overall fixture volumes remained limited. However, by mid-week sentiment softened globally, with the Atlantic basin seeing widening bid-offer spreads and subdued activity, as charterers pulled back amid uncertainty and weaker FFAs. In the Pacific, rates also turned softer despite steady demand, as negative paper sentiment and rising tonnage availability pressured owners into narrowing their ideas. Market conditions are currently positional, and much depends on the forthcoming grain activity from South America, which will heavily influence both Atlantic and Pacific directions in the short term.
