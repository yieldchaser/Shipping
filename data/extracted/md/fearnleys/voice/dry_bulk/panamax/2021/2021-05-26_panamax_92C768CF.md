## Market Commentary

This week has been influenced by holidays in most European countries beginning week, and Singapore mid-week. It is not a clear direction but rather a standoff between charterers and owners. The firm underlying sentiment is still there and P1A is currently yielding around 20k. Grain volumes out of ECSA is certainly picking up with several June stems entering the market. On the other hand we see volumes out of USG evaporating as US grain is currently priced out. In the eastern hemisphere we see index levels come off, much lead by early positions reducing their offers in order to cover before Singapore holidays.
