## Market Commentary

Even though rates have been lagging behind in the East, the week overall has shown a continued recovery in the Panamax market. A transatlantic round voyage currently yields about USD 8,000 per day, while a short fronthaul from the Continent yields about USD 15,500. In the East, a Pacific round voyage pays around the mid USD 7,000's. The BPI 4TC-index is up 94 points to 1056.
