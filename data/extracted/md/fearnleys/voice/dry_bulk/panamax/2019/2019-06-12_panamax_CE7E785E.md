## Market Commentary

The market has turned down in both basins this week, with the TA-rates especially taking a major hit. A transatlantic round voyage currently yields around USD 7,000 per day, while a short fronthaul from the continent yields something the middle of USD 16,000. In the east, a Pacific round voyage now pays around the USD 10,000's per day. The BPI 4TC-index is down 155 points for the week, currently at 1149.
