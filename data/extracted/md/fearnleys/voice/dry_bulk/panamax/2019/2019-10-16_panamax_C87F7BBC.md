## Market Commentary

A rather flat week, but with a hint of softening rates going forward. Currently, rates are quite similar to what was reported last week, but the sentiment has turned slightly down in the last couple of days. A TA round voyage currently pays owners around the mid USD 15,500 per day on a standard Panamax, while a fronthaul from the Continent now yields around USD 25,000. In the East, a Pacific round voyage pays around the USD 13,500-mark. The BPI 4TC-index is currently at 1888 points, 36 up since last week.
