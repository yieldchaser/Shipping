## Market Commentary

A much more positive outlook in most areas resulted in a strong increase in both hemispheres. Having been lagging slightly behind the general market, the North Atlantic has looked firmer and has increased the most. A transatlantic round voyage currently yields about USD 3,500 per day, while a short fronthaul from the continent yields about USD 12,000. In the east, a Pacific round voyage pays around the low USD 7,000's. The BPI 4TC-index is up 103 points to 752.
