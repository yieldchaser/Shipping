## Market Commentary

With rates continuing to improve, especially in the Atlantic, the BPI 4TC-Index has risen this week, now at 1177 points. A transatlantic round voyage currently yields about USD 10,000 per day, while a short fronthaul from the Continent yields around low USD 17,000. In the East, a Pacific round voyage pays around USD 8,000.
