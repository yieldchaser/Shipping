## Market Commentary

After a volatile February, the shipping market has shown signs of stabilization in recent weeks. Both the Atlantic and Asia regions are experiencing a slowdown in spot market activity, with rates holding steady. However, it's important to note that availability continues to increase during this time of year. In terms of the cargo market, we've observed a softening in TTF prices, with European storage remaining well above the average for this time of year, while JKM prices have seen an increase relative to TTF., This has presented some limited opportunities for an inter-basin arbitrage and as a result, we have seen several cargoes heading east. Given the availability lists, this dynamic hasn’t stressed the freight market yet, but may help explain the steady charter rates. Market activity continues to lean towards more multi-month requirements, although levels are remaining steady in this segment, too. There haven’t been any fresh newbuilding orders placed this week.
