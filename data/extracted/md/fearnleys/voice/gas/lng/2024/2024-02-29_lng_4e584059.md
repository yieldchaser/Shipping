## Market Commentary

The LNG shipping market has experienced a sluggish week, with limited activity observed across both basins. While some developing cargoes have emerged, the lack of firm requirements have result in the softening of spot rates. Prompt vessel availability remains high compared to the demand seen this week, and ships opening end March to 1H April 2024 may see a discount against the current spot rates. The premium between the spot and term markets continue to persist, but as period-flexible vessels move closer to the spot fixing window, the bid-offer spread is expected to narrow, and a new benchmark awaits. On product, the current low-price environment has spurred recent activity in China, driving demand for more cargoes ahead of the Labor Day Week. 1H April appears to be tighter, with buyers ready to transact at levels above the spot JKM price. As a result, optimistically, we hope for an open west-east arbitrage, leading to an increase in spot shipping activity for April and May. In the LNG newbuilding segment, a Greek Owner has reportedly placed an order for four vessels with Korean shipbuilder Hyundai Samho Heavy Industries at approximately US$270 million per ship. The newbuildings will be scheduled for delivery within 2028.
