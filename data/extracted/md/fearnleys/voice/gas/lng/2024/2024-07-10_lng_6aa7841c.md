## Market Commentary

Following last week’s charter rate rally in the Atlantic, it is the Pacific's turn this week. Several requirements were hitting the market for 2H July and 1H August laycans. To this end, we are seeing a slow, but steady increase in rates and consolidation of RT econs in the Pacific basin, yet the delta between Atlantic rates remains notably wide. Activity in the Atlantic is continuing albeit at a slightly slower pace, as there is an expectation of more 2-stroke tonnage emerging through August and the arbitrage remains volatile. The short-term market has remained relatively quiet, as Charterers weighing their options. The arbitrage may test the market in coming weeks, but for now the landscape remains flat. Newbuilding prices have remained stable this week.
