## Market Commentary

Following the relatively quiet spell last week, there has been a significant upturn in spot chartering enquiries issued across both basins for May and June cargoes. Spot rates are experiencing a slight increase in the Atlantic but remain unchanged in the East. While there is an abundance of vessels in the available tonnage list, finding the perfect match for specific requirements can pose a challenge, particularly with some of the older vessels warming up. Interest in multi-month charters is also picking up and with periods through the upcoming winter, there is increasing visibility on market sentiment for the traditionally stronger months of the year. All of these market developments are happening under the shadow of more geopolitical uncertainty in the Middle East, and although this unlikely to have a direct impact on the LNG shipping market (since the Suez Canal is already a no-go area) the wider impact on energy markets remains a risk. This week saw no newbuilding orders, with prices holding steady.
