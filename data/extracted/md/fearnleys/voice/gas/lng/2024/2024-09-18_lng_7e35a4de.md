## Market Commentary

As Gastech draws the attention of players in the LNG industry this week, the LNG shipping market remains in a fairly muted state. There have been relatively few firm requirements and there is an extensive availibity list across both basins. However, there have been sporadic signs of activity with several fixtures being reported last week and into this week, triggering a slight tick up in charter rates for larger tonnage in the Atlantic. Outside of the flutters of optimisation demand, a multi laycan requirement in the Atlantic will keep market players engaged this week. With the opening of the November fixing window and the prospect of cooler weather in the northern hemisphere on the horizon, both Owners and Charterers will be eagerly watching on for an indication of which direction charter rates will develop and whether we see an improved demand for winter shipping coverage typical for this time of year. There have not been any LNGC newbuilding orders this week and prices remain stable. Separately, it is reported that Golar LNG has entered an EPC agreement with CIMC Raffles to convert Fuji LNG (148,000 cbm) into a MK II FLNG production unit with a liquefaction capacity of 3.5MTPA for delivery in Q4 2027 for approximately $2.2bn.
