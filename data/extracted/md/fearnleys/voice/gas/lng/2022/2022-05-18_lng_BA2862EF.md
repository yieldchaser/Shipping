## Market Commentary

This week has delivered on the premise that the shipping market is indeed strengthening, for which the signs were already starting to be visible a few weeks back. The latest levels for concluded spot fixtures have not only made us increase the headline rates across the board, but also required a notch-up on our Ballast Bonus sentiment, since charterers might now have to absorb more idle time to secure vessels unless one manages to find a perfect matching delivery/redelivery dates and location. Modern vessel availability also continues to slim down, although it is to be noted that many of the recent spot fixtures are for relatively short single voyages, meaning that the forward availability still have some length. Term markets have also been active, as new modern vessel positions are met with considerable interest, although term levels seem to be more stable, with slight upward pressure. Last but not least, the general newbuilding market remains red hot with ordering interest from several shipping segments. Newbuild prices remain firm and shipyards are receiving interest on their next available slots, even if it’s getting bit far out in time.
