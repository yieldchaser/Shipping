## Market Commentary

In our first Weekly Report of 2025, we brazenly asserted that “we can be sure that 2025 will be just as interesting as was 2024”. In what turned out to be our most accurate forecast to date, this year has indeed had it all. As we head into Christmas Day, however, it isn’t armed conflicts or trade wars that that are exerting the most influence on our market, rather it’s the reminder that shipping is a derived demand, and when there’s more LNG to be shipped, there’s more LNG carriers needed to ship it. Like most of the market, we were caught out by the dramatic jump in Atlantic exports in Q4, and the resultant strain on LNG shipping fleets which took us into the six-figure winter fixing mode. Whilst these rates had been par for the course in many previous years, they weren’t meant to appear this year, and the fact that the market still hasn’t properly corrected underlines the structural nature of this winter’s rate rise. This root cause has significant ramifications for 2026, with many charterers carrying recent reminders that being short shipping in LNG can be costly. So, whilst most owners won’t get to unwrap a clean term TCP tomorrow morning, those with available 2-strokes coming up on their books will be feeling considerably more Christmas cheer than they would have only two months ago. There’s no shortage of festivities at the yards either, with Hudong-Zhonghua securing a hat-trick of Newbuilds for a producer’s fleet renewal needs, whilst Samsung have reportedly bagged a brace on the back of more long-term tender business. Merry Christmas, everyone!
