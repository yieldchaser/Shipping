## Market Commentary

The tighter market conditions in the Atlantic persists still driven by uncertainties on discharge schedules, and 1H November is now effectively sold out. Bids have continued to improve to meet the offer and interestingly, sentiment remains strong enough to support a number of more forward fixtures at prevailing levels, including some rumoured for longer period closing out the remainder of the year. The pressing question for both Owners and Charterers now is whether these levels are maintained through year-end of whether the market eases again; for now, we're likely faced with a wait and see approach for liftings further out.

**East of Suez**

, charter rates have also been lifted on the back of this shift, helped too by improved fundamentals with more supply online from Australia and a steadier flow of Canada volumes. Lots of opportunities for owners to assess although it remains to be seen if there will be sufficient to keep all available TFDE's moving.
