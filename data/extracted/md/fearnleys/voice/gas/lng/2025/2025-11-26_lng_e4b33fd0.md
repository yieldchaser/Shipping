## Market Commentary

The spot market gains seen in recent weeks continue with significant increases across all basins, although trying to report any of these regions under a single headline rate may give a false impression of a smooth and steady rise. The reality has been a much more volatile climb that may surge one day then soften the next, and although this has appeared mildly chaotic at times we do now see an overarching curve through winter. In the Atlantic, the lack of 2-stroke tonnage has led to some very tight periods in which majors are allowing ships to be pried from their portfolios…at a premium. When there has been competition among owners and sub-letters, this has merely acted as a fluttering brake rather than an abrupt cliff edge. And yet, as we look ahead to the end of December and beyond, rates are clearly easing from the mid-December highs but do remain at elevated levels and this suggests a soft landing into 2026. The Pacific basin has also seen more activity and at higher levels, but with considerably less volatility than in the West. This may be because ST and TFDE vessels offer more of a relief valve when 2-strokes disappear (something we address in our recent LinkedIn post), but there is also much more opportunity to optimise shorter voyages. Amidst these two major basins, the Middle East has again asserted itself as a strong demand source and has been a focal point with a spate of new requirements and fixing levels that are attractive enough to pull ships in from Asia.
