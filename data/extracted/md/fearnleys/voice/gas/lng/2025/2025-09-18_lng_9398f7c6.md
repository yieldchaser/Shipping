## Market Commentary

We’re normally strapping ourselves in for spot rates’ annual trip to the moon at this time each year, but it appears as though the shuttle has been grounded in 2025. With an endless stream of uncovered cargos to be shipped, the hope for Owners is that vessel supply starts to dry-up as portfolios horde their tonnage as an insurance policy against any weather driven (or geopolitically driven) spikes. Unfortunately for those who are long tonnage, this has not happened. To focus on the positives, the spot market is working exactly the way it’s meant to - and this is great news for the handful of Charterers who are not also Owners. For everyone else (which is actually everyone else…) who is reliant upon a winter premium to balance the books in 2025, there’s just way too much vessel supply on offer to make this a plausible outcome for now. Of course, this is the cyclical nature of LNG shipping and whilst we need to brace for a couple of years of low spot rates, as mentioned in last week’s circular, the long-term outlook for the industry is very bullish. Reviews from Gastech have highlighted the sheer number of portfolios looking for long-term tonnage and this means that there’s more than enough work to keep Owners and Charterers busy for now. And whilst it might only be jam tomorrow for Owners, it at least provides some line of sight to better times ahead for all of us.
