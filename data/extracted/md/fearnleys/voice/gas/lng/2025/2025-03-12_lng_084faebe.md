## Market Commentary

After a very busy but soft start to the year, the LNG shipping market is gaining momentum and rates are rising across the basins. There isn't one single game-changing event that one can point to, but rather an accumulation of several factors that in themselves may not be decisive but combined, are creating a lot of headaches for traders and schedulers. These include the commencement of commercial cargoes from Calcasieu Pass, the potential arbitrage opening through summer, accelerated ramp up from Plaquemines and positive developments in the Ukrainian ceasefire talks. The strengthening prompt market is looking like hitting a very tight pinch-point in mid-April, and there are several cargoes uncovered. Against this bullish sentiment though, there remains a lot of geopolitical and economic uncertainty ahead and the fundamental oversupply of tonnage remains, so it's unclear how the markets will look in a few weeks' time let alone a few months. There have been no newbuild orders placed this week.
