## Market Commentary

The spot market has continued to drift lower, confounding any thoughts of revival off the back of a number of Atlantic requirements towards the end of August. The relatively high level of activity across all basins has applied a brake to more significant downward pressure on rates; nonetheless, fixing levels across the key benchmarks of 2-strokes West of Suez and TFDEs East of Suez are down week on week. The Pacific premium is unusual this time of year, and is a sign of a challenging trading environment that hasn’t really seen much of an arbitrage pulling ships out of the Atlantic for some time. Those hoping for a recovery won’t be seeing many encouraging signs, other than increased energy demand due to above average temperatures, so for now “utilisation” remains the name of the game. If there is a silver lining for owners, it is the growing interest in multimonth/multiyear tonnage. Rate expectations will be muted, but securing business through this weaker market will hold some appeal. On the cargo side, Venture Global will supply 2 MTPA of LNG from its Calcasieu Pass 2 project to Eni over 20 years. This deal marks Eni’s first long-term LNG contract from the U.S. On the Newbuilding front, there were no reported conventional LNGC orders over the last week and prices remain stable.
