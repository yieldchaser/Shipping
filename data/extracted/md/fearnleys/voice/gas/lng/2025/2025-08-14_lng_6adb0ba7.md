## Market Commentary

Spot activity has eased off slightly over the past week, as could be expected after breakneck speed with which requirements were covered during July.

**Atlantic**

 fixtures have continued to trickle through with little direction or movement on charter rates and whilst availabilities look patchier further forward, charterers are in no hurry expecting redeliveries to repopulate position lists in due course. The Pacific has remained quiet, and rates have come under further pressure with a single spot fixture for early September seeing plenty of offers. Whilst we may have finally reached holiday mode on the spot, the long-awaited summer lull has not killed longer term discussions. Preparations are being made for some structural shorts next year, against growing availability profile from independent owners looking to take short term coverage before anticipated tightening later in the decade. Fresh newbuilding orders remain muted year-to-date though yards are comfortable with large orderbooks and a healthy pipeline of prospective requirements from the LNG space and other segments alike.
