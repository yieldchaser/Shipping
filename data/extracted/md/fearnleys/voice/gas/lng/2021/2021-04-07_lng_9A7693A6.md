## Market Commentary

LNG shipping market activity has picked up right where it was before the short Easter break as new spot requirements emerged from both basins boosted by the strengthening LNG cargo prices, which are currently rendering the threat of US cargo cancellations to minimal at least in the near term. Furthermore, there are handful of ongoing term-requirements, which together with the fresh spot requirements, are giving available Owners plenty of options to choose from. Accordingly, spot rates are cautiously climbing upwards as vessel availability shortens, however the cap between spot and term rates still stays relative wide as expectations of stronger market conditions for the second half of the year persists. It definitely feels like the overall shipping market is a bit more volatile this year and this uncertainty might not disappear anytime soon. Notwithstanding the recently launched long-term tenders from a few big industry parties, there were no new LNG carrier orders placed this week.
