## Market Commentary

It’s been a volatile week in the LNG market, but prices seem to have recovered to just below last weeks highs. During this period of volatility, shipping rates have held up except the West TFDE rate. Traders and Charterers are marketing sublet positions for intra Atlantic voyages at attractive rates, whiles Charterers looking for tonnage to take product out east, may struggle, pending dates.  Prompt availability is dominated by subletters, some of which have pockets of availability and others with more flexibility. The fact of the matter is that vessel availability continues to be date sensitive and limited.  In the newbuilding segment, Celsius has been linked to the two latest orders at SHI. The positions are said to have been options executed at high USD 190s mill per vessel, which is below our current price guidance for a new order placed «today».
