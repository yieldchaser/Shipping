## Market Commentary

The LNG shipping market remains active, primarily driven by charterers seeking term tonnage through to winter and beyond. Several fixtures have been concluded so far and with vessels disappearing, term rates have risen and are expected to strengthen further. Conversely, the spot market remains relatively flat despite a handful of fixtures during the week. Prompt vessel availability has reduced but further ahead redeliveries and sublet tonnage are adding to the position list. Whether this strong activity in the term market will last is yet to be seen, as some are taking a wait-and-see approach hoping for some softening in summer. There haven’t been any new orders on conventional tonnage recorded so far this week, however Newbuilding prices showing some small gains.
