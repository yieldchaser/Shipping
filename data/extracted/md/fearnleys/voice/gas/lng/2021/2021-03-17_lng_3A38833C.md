## Market Commentary

Ireland doesn’t play a significant role in LNG, yet St Patrick’s day is well known globally and the celebrations this year will be very different to the famous Guinness or two in the company of good friends. There isn’t too much for Owners to celebrate today though, with rates still languishing below breakeven. However, there is good reason to believe there’s more demand for LNGCs ahead including prompt multi-month deals that have emphasized the delta between steam propulsion, TFDE and 2-stroke. The steams are presenting good value for charterers, particularly given the tender for 24 cargoes into Argentina that can accommodate the smaller parcels sizes of most steam vessels. As a further boost, the warming weather hasn’t held back cargo pricing; LNG is strengthening to an extent that may see more Atlantic cargoes flow East, all good reason for optimism for Owners. Although no new orders this week, newbuild prices experience a modest increase, which may prove persistent. In short, the prompt market and into Q2 2021 may not appear too interesting, but looking further forward the market looks set to rebalance
