## Market Commentary

There remains a sense of separation in the LNG shipping market as spot remains relatively inactive, and term continues to be the main focus for many. Requirements are picking up prompt vessels though, and this is helping rates gradually increase week on week, seemingly typical of the shoulder months. Term enquiries are more abundant with plenty of charterers in the market, and there’s a definite rise in sentiment with healthy levels being discussed. LNG shipping suffered little during the Suez disruptions, as cargoes were replaced, ships diverted and panic averted, highlighting the other side to the volatility we often see within the industry. Delays for traffic already waiting in line remains to be seen, but there’s little to suggest anything other than business as usual. The current spread of prompt tonnage across the basins suggests that if vessels were needed quickly, they could be found. No conventional Newbuilding orders have been placed this week, but we continue to see prices increasing.
