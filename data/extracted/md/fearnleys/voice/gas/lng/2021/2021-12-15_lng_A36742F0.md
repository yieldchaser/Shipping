## Market Commentary

The emergence of more relets and a lack of fresh cargoes has further softened the spot market, and with Christmas festivities little over a week away this might be how we see out the year. This is 2021 though, and the surge towards a record high gas price in Europe yesterday reminds us just how volatile this market is today. At the time of writing, JKM pricing hadn’t responded to the same degree so the arbitrage is very firmly slammed shut. This is a very unusual situation for December, and although it is bearish shipping the conditions are ripe for a monumental rebound if Asia tries to outbid Europe for LNG and relets are withdrawn. There were no fresh orders confirmed this week.
