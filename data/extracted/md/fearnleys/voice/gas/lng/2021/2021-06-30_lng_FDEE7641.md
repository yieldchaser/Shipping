## Market Commentary

The market continues to see action in the multi-month-plus timeframe, and this is making up for a lack of activity in the spot market. The latest term discussions have piqued the interest of several owners and charterers, but we do see a growing reluctance from both sides to work terms that are too far away from their initial expectations. All this activity does mean the balance of availability this winter will be held by sublets rather than independent owners, and those curious to see how the peak season could play out may have been granted a glimpse in the past week: a recent spot requirement in Asia saw a number of sublets being offered and these clearly weighed on the levels achievable. Will this be the pattern for the rest of the year? Perhaps the opposite; with stronger cargo economics and longer voyages in winter, we may well see the same volatility but in entirely the opposite direction.
