## Market Commentary

Multi-month activity dominates the focus of most discussions which continue to successful conclude despite the lacklustre prompt market. Yet it should be noted that freight levels remain bullish compared to historical levels so it is not surprising that a temporary correction to the downside is likely as available tonnage looks for utilisation through July and early August. Beyond is less certain, yet most views agree with a contango into September. However, views do differ on how deep winter will eventually be. Regardless of individual views most are in agreement that the lack of independent tonnage continues to be an overwhelming factor and weighs heavily on decision making. The cargo market has been volatile this week and may encourage a reorganisation of portfolios and a flurry of activity as cargo destinations are altered, something that would be welcomed by those with prompt availability. The number of desponent owners with availability on the prompt continues to increase in the east yet west remains sparse, should intra-Atlantic flow increase then this should lead a balancing of the available tonnage globally. In the new build market Owners continue to order to lock in newbuilds before prices escalate further yet it does impact the number of speculative orders available for charter from 2024.
