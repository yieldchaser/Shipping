## Market Commentary

Although we’ve seen a correction in spot shipping rates over the last few weeks, we are now seeing signs of the market leveling off. However, there isn’t much activity in the spot market overall. On the term side, market participants seem to be approaching owners earlier than in previous years in trying to line up term coverage for next winter. At this stage, owners appear to be holding on to some of the gains in term rates achieved in November and December 2020. Despite vessel utilization risk in the spot market for the upcoming periods, owners looking ahead with one eye on winter past. No LNG newbuild orders recorded this week either.
