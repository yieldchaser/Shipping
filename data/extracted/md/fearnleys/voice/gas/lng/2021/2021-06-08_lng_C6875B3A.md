## Market Commentary

Activity in the spot market remained fairly muted this week. With firm commodity prices & freight rates, discussions around ship positions, cargo trades and optimisation of both were taking place, but few deals were concluded. However, the Far East thirst for 3Q deliveries are now dragging vessels from the Pacific into the Atlantic for US-sourced cargoes back to Asia. Spot Pacific requirements, offering owners shorter charter durations, would normally warrant a premium in these circumstances, but sublet vessels with pockets of availability have dampened this effect. LNG shipping availability may however tighten as we look ahead. With the current arbitrage opportunities, there is a clear risk for more and longer voyages West to East. Scheduling may then become challenging with potential waiting time in Panama versus taking the long route via Cape. With yard prices firming across shipping segments, the newbuild market saw 2 interesting orders being concluded. JOVO place an order for a rare mid-sized 79,800cbm vessel at Jiangnan. Over at KSOE (HHI) 4x 200,000cbm LNGC were ordered and these vessels are apparently to be fitted with the new ME-GA two-stroke engines.
