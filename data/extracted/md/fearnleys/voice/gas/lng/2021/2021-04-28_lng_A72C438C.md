## Market Commentary

The term LNG charter market continues to heat up, with large week-on-week gains across all vessel types, and spot shipping rates are feeling the warmth spill over. Charterers seeking spot coverage now need to compete for Owner’s attention as Owners seems to be more interested in evaluating and securing term coverage. Availability is tight both in Atlantic and Pacific and coverage in the Atlantic have in some instances needed to source tonnage from the Pacific, which is uncommon considering that it’s still April. With more vessels chartered for term over the last few weeks, we could see more subletters showing positions for spot over the coming months. It has been quiet on the LNG newbuilding front this week, but yard quotes on almost all vessel types are firming up.
