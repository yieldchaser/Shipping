## Market Commentary

The LNG shipping market has awoken right in time for the bi-annual Nor-Shipping Conference in Oslo, following a period of slumber through the European and Singapore end-of-May holidays. Movement into the July pricing month and the emergence of an arbitrage has triggered a number of developing and firm spot requirements west of Suez, as offtakers seek shipping coverage especially in light of potentially longer voyages into Asia; as such, headline rates have begun to firm up in the West. It’s a different story in the East as activity is quite muted; however, there is a similar outcome as modern tonnage is difficult to source hence an uptick in levels. On the term side, a fresh 1-year requirement released this week confirms the lasting demand from players eager to mitigate risk of coming out short over the winter months. There are no reported orders in the newbuild market this week, however, levels remain firm. To those that are attending Nor-Shipping, enjoy the sun and we look forward to seeing you over the coming days, if we have not already.
