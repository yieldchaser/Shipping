## Market Commentary

After a few weeks of relative calm in the shipping market, things got pretty busy pretty quickly last week. Many of the leading indicators of a tight shipping market have been flickering to life recently, such as increasing ballast speed and more FOB/DES and swapping activity on the cargo market, so it’s not surprising that some sublets are being pulled and the availability list is shrinking. This hasn’t led to an increase in fixing levels yet, and it may not do at all while LNG storage levels are high and pricing remains subdued, but it has led to more shipping enquiries as participants look for opportunities in the spot market. Likewise for term charters, there are more enquiries as traders and portfolios look to take advantage of what has hitherto been considered a buying opportunity next year. That being said, Panama Canal disruption, geopolitical tensions, uncertainty around the future of steam ships and the start of the EU ETS are all theoretically bullish 2-Strokes. Conversely, the wave of redelivering tonnage from Q1 next year should provide commercial opportunities for those who are more agnostic when it comes to propulsion, size and vintage. All in all, it’s hard to do anything other than sit on the fence this week! There were no newbuilding orders placed within this reporting cycle.
