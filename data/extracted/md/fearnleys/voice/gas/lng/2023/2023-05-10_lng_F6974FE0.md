## Market Commentary

We have finally had a somewhat "normalised" week as Japan and China both returned from their lengthy holidays. However, any expected boost for LNG demand was short-lived, as buying remains muted and as a result, JKM prices continue to test new lows. On the LNG shipping side, the spot market continued to soften despite some new activity and regular tender cargoes. We even witnessed a spot fixture going below "roundtrip econs", which until now has been a strong support level. The softening can be attributed to the ongoing slump in LNG prices (and constantly regenerating sublet availability). However, it's a completely different story in the term market, as demand remains very healthy and new requirements appear every week. Unfortunately, closing the Bid-Ask spread is now more challenging due to the wide gulf between spot and term rates. Looking ahead, we expect spot market activity to pick up once we get the contango back into play. As ever, it will be a volatile road up to winter! Turning our attention to the yards and no fresh newbuilding orders were placed this week - which has provided the sector with a well deserved breather.
