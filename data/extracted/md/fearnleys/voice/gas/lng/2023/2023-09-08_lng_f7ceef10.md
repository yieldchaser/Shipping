## Market Commentary

Gastech offered the industry plenty of opportunities for discussion this week in Singapore. As the week draws to a close, sentiment looks to have the edge with another charterer paying up to cover a Q4 cargo. A reminder to all that the fundamentals (an increasing of available prompt tonnage) and the sentiment don’t need to be aligned for rates to escalate. A key theme of the conference has of course been new business and this week was no different, many new players offered interesting new opportunities in the near term but also from 2027 and beyond. It is clear ambitions, for many, are grand in scope! No newbuilds again this week but let's see if any announcements are made on the back of the remaining meetings this week.
