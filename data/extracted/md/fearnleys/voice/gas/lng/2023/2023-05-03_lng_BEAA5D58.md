## Market Commentary

The LNG shipping market has seen a quiet week resulting in low demand for spot charters. However, there is interest in securing tonnage for longer periods, as more Charterers are seeking to prepare for the upcoming winter season. While spot rates have remained unchanged in all basins, multi-month and multi-year period deals have been observed, with more expected to follow. This trend is likely to reduce term availability and increase sentiment for term charters. It is always a question of where winter spot rates will be, and this will eventually be factored into longer-term rates as Charterers look to compromise on longer periods at the right price to cover this winter. At present, many Independent Owners are reluctant to fix for short periods and are instead enticing Charterers with better rates for longer-term charters. However, for now, some are struggling to find a bridge between the spread, but it is expected that either parties will bite soon. Despite the current subdued state of the spot LNG shipping market, the increased interest in longer-term charters suggests a positive outlook in the coming months. On the newbuilding front, prices remain firm with reports this week that an Owner have secured two 200,000m3 LNG carriers at around US$277M a vessel from Hyundai Heavy Industries for delivery in late Q4 2027.
