## Market Commentary

While rates remained relatively stable last week, the dynamics have shifted significantly this week due to a surge in vessel requirements in both basins. Although there are vessels available, Charterers are showing a clear preference for larger vessels to lift the larger cargo parcels especially in the West, leading to a perceived scarcity of LNG vessels available for charter. In the East, there has still been a market for the smaller steam turbine vessels. As a result, charter rates have increased from last week's levels, and this upward trend is expected to continue throughout the week, as vessel requirements remain unfulfilled as of today. Floating storage taking advantage of the contango situation are still occupying shipping capacities. There have been no reports of newbuilding orders this week, as ship prices show no signs of weakening and demand for shipbuilding slots continues to be robust.
