## Market Commentary

General news flows in recent weeks have had an air of pessimism but the mood may be changing, with countries reopening and an expectation that things are starting to look a little brighter and the same can be said for the LNG industry. Despite low product prices and LNGC rates, the industry continues to its forward progress. The 19.2 billion US dollar shipbuilding deal agreed last week by Qatar Petroleum with three South Korean yards offer Owners a well needed distraction from today’s spot market. New areas and sectors of demand is also bearing fruit, with further LNG bunkering vessels being launched. And whilst operating on the open seas has seen new challenges raised through the restrictions on movement of personal and quarantine measures, MAN dual-fuel engines passed one million operating hours this year, which reflects a level of experience and operating history that should offer improved reliability and a greater understanding of the technology meaning smoother sailing for Owners and Charterers alike. All we need next is a sustainable arbitrage to reduce what is a slightly bulging availability list.
