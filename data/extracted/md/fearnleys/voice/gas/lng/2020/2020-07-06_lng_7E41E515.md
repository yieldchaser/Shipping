## Market Commentary

Over the past week we’ve seen a few signs of life in the spot market, with those vessels fixed or placed on subs being at or slightly above recent spot rates. The focus of the market continues to be winter coverage, with reports of several Charterers securing additional tonnage through the winter. These fixtures have established a forward view that is not necessarily as weak as the current sport market would have it. Furthermore, independent owners with open winter tonnage are growing increasingly selective on which opportunities they will entertain. It’s almost mythical how these things tend to come together around week 27 every year. One thing does seem certain, and that is that the market may have bottomed out…although this does not mean that we’ll see a sudden increase in spot rates this side of the summer holidays.
