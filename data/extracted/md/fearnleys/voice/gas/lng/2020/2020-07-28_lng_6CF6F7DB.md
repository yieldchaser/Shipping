## Market Commentary

Voyage economics remain broadly flat this week, but the sense of a firming shipping market is growing. Prompt business is still dominated by spot cargoes and tender-driven enquiries, and this continues to churn through the availability list without reducing it much. Looking ahead though, sublets are being withdrawn and the bid / offer spread is finally converging, giving hope to owners seeking to salvage something of the year. It's now a question of timing: the best cargo economics span roughly October to December, but owners are reluctant to risk this much idle time prior to delivery, so few multimonth fixtures have been concluded. As the weeks roll on and owners get more visibility of their fleet schedules, we expect a flurry of these fixtures. This has not been a normal year, but despite the disruption of recent months it's reassuring to see the familiar dance of owners and charterers as we look towards winter. No newbuildings reported this week and prices remain flat.
