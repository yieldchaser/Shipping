## Market Commentary

Owners and charterers have had to respond quickly and aggressively in some instances to the pandemic that continues to effect the global markets. The response has varied but generally we have seen the emergence of a two-tier market over the past couple of weeks. In one camp, and reflected in the reductions in headline rate in our weekly report, are the owners and disponent owners seeking coverage and utilisation for their fleet; in the other camp are the owners who remain firmer, in anticipation of a tighter market due to the disruption to fleet schedules, delays in discharging and in project maintenance, and in quarantine measures requiring set periods between port calls. With such distinct views and limited depth to the market, there is considerable volatility at present. This may subside as the fixing period moves into June, when the possibility of cargo cancellations and the potential for vessel availability to grow could have an impact on the shipping market. On a separate note no new orders have been placed, as one would expect and new build prices take a small decline, as do second-hand prices.
