## Market Commentary

Weak end user demand mainly coming from North East Asia has held the product price on the floor this past week with the LNG supply overhang still apparent in both basins. However, we have seen more creative chartering opportunities, as spot rates climbed despite the amount of available tonnage, especially from the Pacific. Those Charterers in Europe who are observing the forward pricing curve, are perhaps now considering to float cargoes in the area, which could tie up some tonnage in the basin as we head into the winter months. Owners still remain bullish for winter and multi-month chartering still seems to be the most attractive option for them with healthy appetite for this type of business holding rates strong. However, with the product price remaining low compared to this time last year, it may be unlikely that Charterers will be able to justify the dizzy heights gained by Owners last year if they have a say. Yard pricing remained unchanged since last week, and no new orders have been reported.
