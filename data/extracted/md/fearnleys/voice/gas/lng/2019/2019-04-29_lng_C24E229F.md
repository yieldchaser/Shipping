## Market Commentary

Firstly, thanks to all readers for completing the information request sent out last week, it’s great to have such a strong response and an active audience. Enough of the admin, the LNG freight market has more than enough going on to fill this column despite numerous public holidays worldwide. Live tenders from CFE and Gail this week have caused a spur of activity in and around the Americas. Across the Atlantic, West Africa has also had its fair share of available cargoes just as the first production from Argentina looks to be hitting the market. When considered with the Algerian and Egyptian volumes seen over the recent weeks and the imminent US commissioning volumes due, the Atlantic has never been so dynamic in terms of production. It’s not quite as positive on the demand side with the Atlantic to Pacific arb struggling to support consistent cross basin trade flow. The Pacific remains a little muted with limited fixtures being reported and as we increase the ballast bonus sentiment, it is the Pacific that weighs on the assessment advancing further this week. No reported new build orders this week but some long term tenders are progressing well which should see the total tally of uncommitted vessels on order book reduce. Happy holidaying if you are out of the office this week.
