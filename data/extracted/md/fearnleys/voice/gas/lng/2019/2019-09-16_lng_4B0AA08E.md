## Market Commentary

What some thought was to be a quiet week on the chartering front with Gastech in Houston taking many players away from their desks was proved wrong as the spot shipping market turned rapidly with an influx of shipping requirements being issued early this week. The predominant wave of activities are Pacific-driven as vessels find employment with a significant increase in charter rate sentiments compared to the previous week. Spot prices for LNG have increased in both basins as well, and may have contributed to the recent flurry of activities on the spot shipping market. While charter rate levels spiked up across all vessel sizes as indicated, no newbuilding orders reported this week and the indicative newbuilding price level remains.
