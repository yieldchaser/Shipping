## Market Commentary

As Easter Holidays is coming closer, it is safe to say that activity is slowing down. Spot fixing interest has now shifted to May-June cargos in both basins and quite a few fixtures has been concluded already.

**East of Suez**

, availability list has shortened significantly, rates, however, haven’t seen any drastic change so far, but Charterers should make notes. West of Suez, availability consists mainly of sublet tonnage which means that Atlantic might at any stage become short of shipping, should those players with extra tonnage optimise their portfolios. For now, main shipping interest is related to cargoes out of the Mediterranean and should this continue, things might become more active after Easter. In Newbuilding market, Capital Gas has been reported to have exercised their second option for a vessel with HHI giving them a total of six vessels.
