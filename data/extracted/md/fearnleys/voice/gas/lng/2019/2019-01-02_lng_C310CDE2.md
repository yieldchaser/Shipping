## Market Commentary

While many of the market participants are still enjoying the Festive Period, market activity has just slowly started to pick up. A few fresh requirements, one from an Eastern Major, will keep things busy this week, but as vessel availability in the East is increased, it will not be a surprise if we witness a further correction on East of Suez rates as Charterers are focusing on February cargoes. In the Atlantic, availability is limited and consists mainly of portfolio sublet tonnage as activity remains muted and with the West to East arbitrage closed it will be interesting to see the next market direction here. On the newbuild front, a few more orders were added just as we got into the festive mode. A record high 60 LNG vessels were ordered in 2018. Will this activity continue in 2019? As this is just the first week of the year, we expect more activity to emerge as we move ahead and we looking forward to a great year for LNG in 2019! Wishing you all a Happy New Year!
