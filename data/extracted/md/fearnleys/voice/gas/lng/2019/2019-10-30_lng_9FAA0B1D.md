## Market Commentary

Some cause for a pause in the chartering market as sanction news and its effects dissipate and chartering requirements pull back from the recent sprint. This news couldn't come soon enough for some, yet a couple of prompt cargoes remain effected by its wake and those holding the cargoes continue to weigh up the choice between taking a ship at year highs or selling FOB at slightly better numbers. This challenging market has seen some creativity which is great to see. There aren’t too many Picasso’s out there and the LNG chain doesn’t provide a white canvas backdrop to go crazy but there are still some individuals/companies making grounds with ideas that should be common place. Creativity does face a challenging compromise, in a tight market the chartering equivalent to a silent assassin normally comes out on top but right now chartering departments are just one cog of a complex string of adjustments that takes time to align. This inability to move quickly carries its own risks. The result is lots of activity, questions and requirements and bigger differences between gains and losses. The biggest movement in shipping economics this week is to the ballast bonus sentiment whilst further out one year and multi-year rates take a slide, thus rates relatively stable this week but overall economics are starting to drift downwards. New build prices are stable yet under pressure whilst second hand prices continue their downward trend.
