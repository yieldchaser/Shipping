## Market Commentary

It has been quite an active week for LNG Shipping. Spot hire rates have increased on the back of several cargoes / requirements in both basins. In addition to the rise in rates, there is a significant push from Owners to cover fuel cost on positioning, as well as improvements on ballast bonus figures. The availability list has also seen a reduction; however, we are far from being “short” shipping, but rather «balanced» as we currently do not foresee any big challenges for Charterers looking to secure a ship for 1H June. Also, due to a weak JKM index versus a steadier NBP, the arbitrage to the East has been closed for quite some time and with most cargoes trading intra basin, ships that are fixed are likely to return to the market shortly. Nonetheless, the current situation looks to favor Owners and we may see the spot market firming further. Newbuild prices remain unchanged and no further reports of any new orders this week.
