## Market Commentary

For a week that saw relatively few fixtures and broadly static rates, there sure were enough intriguing developments to keep everyone on their toes. The Atlantic saw a combination of factors disrupt trade flows, including continuing long queues for cargoes into the UK, severe storms in the Bay of Biscay and industrial action in France impacting some discharges. The additional time on water for laden cargoes and slower ballast passages raised concerns among charterers with tight schedules, and with issues continuing through the week coupled with the first exports out of Freeport we may see an uptick in fixing levels. The Pacific hasn't seen the same excitement, but continues to tick over with a steady flow of requirements for owners to work. It hasn't all been rosy for owners though, as charterers can take comfort in the amount of interest generated by a multi month requirement from the spring. As we approach the final week before Christmas and the last fortnight of the year, we hope both owners and charterers can find some festive cheer.
