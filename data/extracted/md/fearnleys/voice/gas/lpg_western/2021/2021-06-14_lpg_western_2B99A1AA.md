## Market Commentary

NWE Both propane and butane are tight in the region due to an issue restarting Karsto after it’s planned maintenance. CIF ARA propane values have been rising have been rising though the propane / naphtha spread also widened and is at -$75pmt for July, as naphtha was dragged up by a jump in Brent. Butane lies at 86% Naphtha which is very strong for the time of year, especially with strong naphtha values. This strength has not translated to an open arb from the US which has been strong as well. TOT window has been quiet this week, though still assessed at a premium, with many traders waiting for the dust to settle after crudes moves.

**MED**

 Butane in the Med has been tracking naphtha, showing strength. Prompt strength remains for 1H July with June trading all but done. Delivered buying interest is being seen both in the East and West Med for July arrival. AMERICAS A weekly EIA propane stock build of 1.544mmbbls was met with trepidation as the previous few weeks have been riddled with corrected data. And while there remains the expectation that this week’s data contains yet more of the same, it was perceived as directionally accurate. There remain concerns as to the ability of US stocks to build to sufficient levels, but to move from present totals of 55mmbbls to a desired level of 75mmbbls over the space of the next 16 weeks requires a weekly average build of just 1.25mmbbls, which (from a historical perspective) is entirely achievable. With just one exception, the mid-Jun to mid-Oct average build rate has increased each year over the past 5 years, moving up from 1.3mmbbls in 2016 to an average weekly build of nearly 1.7mmbbls last year. Therefore, it is well within reason that US stocks will rise to sufficiently comfortable levels. The pricing structure, meanwhile, reacted to the EIA data in a bullish manner, moving from ~95cts/gal to over 96cts/gal. By Thursday morning the market had realized additional gains and was traded at 96.5c/gal. Terminal fees have been waffling in the low/mid single-digits, with East Coast transactions occurring just under 4 cpg , and a recent Gulf Coast deal was reported at the “low 4’s” per gallon.
