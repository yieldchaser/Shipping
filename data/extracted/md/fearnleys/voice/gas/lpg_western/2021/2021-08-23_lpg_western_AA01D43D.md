## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: Strong US overwater export volumes in July will very likely be overshadowed by record-level volumes in Aug – despite the buyback scheme embarked upon by Enterprise earlier in the summer. The September roster appears solid, as well, with only two cargo cancellations known. The robust outbound volumes are the primary reason that domestic US propane stocks have been unable to rise to desired levels. This week the EIA reported overall stock levels to be 68.7mmbbls – up by 1.9mmbbls week-over-week. Still, this is just 75% of both last year and 2019 as domestic production simply cannot hold pace. In 2019 production averaged 1.148mmbbls/day. By 2020 it had risen to 1.179mmbbls/day, and on a YTD basis 2021 has so far improved to 1.235mmbbls/day – but still, exports reign and will likely continue to disallow stocks from satisfactorily improving. Crop drying expectations for this Fall, however, call for minimal demand as a lasting, dry and “scorching” Midwest heat has allowed crops to “wither” in the fields. This should allow stock levels to build at a mildly better rate, but the hope of reaching 80+ mmbbls is fading, or perhaps already dissipated. Spot terminal fees in the US in September, while sentimentally up, remain on either side of the 5ct range for Sep, though little is being accomplished. 2022 contract terminal fee values, depending on the ratio, frequency, volume, etc., range from the upper 4cts/gal level to well over 6cts/gal. North West Europe: The physical market in the region is tight, window premiums assessed at September CIF ARA +$4.5pmt. Propane has once again switched back to a discount to naphtha. Though at a mere -$6pmt in September it is not nearing the -$30pmt needed to switch back to cracking propane instead of naphtha. The build in US inventories is hoped to widen the arb to NWE though, in this low demand environment it is unlikely a significant volume of US tons will find their way to the region. CIF ARA butane is at 104% naphtha showing uncharacteristic strength for the time of year.

**MED**

: A quiet week though trends are the same. The region remains tight all grades. Butane particularly with values slightly above those in NWE at 106% naphtha.
