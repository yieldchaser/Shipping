## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Discussions and activity on the Gulf Coast were mostly muted, in part due to fog-and in part due to the ambiguity in the freight market, where rates on a Houston/ Chiba basis, were pegged as low as USD127 per metric ton- a far cry from the 180-200 pmt discussed just a week ago. The negative propane/naphta spread in Europe has led to a handful of European players to source tons from the US, both from Marcus Hook and US Gulf, preferably on a delivered basis, in efforts to avoid the volatile spot freight market. In Mt. Belvieu, front month propane prices have come off considerably due to warm weather and falling international prices and Propane now stands att roughly 65 percent of WTI compared to the 75 percent level seen last week. . A handful of February and March FOB cargos are now on offer at around 4 cents, though buyers have been unwilling to achieve these levels quite yet. On paper the Feb/March stands at -115/ ton. The big question today, is the market oversold, or has the high for 2021 already been achieved. Stats delayed due to MLK Day and the inauguration will shed more light Friday.
