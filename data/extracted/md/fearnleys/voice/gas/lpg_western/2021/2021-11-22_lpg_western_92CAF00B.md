## Market Commentary

NWE The TOT window saw a propane deal done at strong levels as a trader purchased a 1H Dec delivered cargo at a fixed price equivalent to December CIF ARA +$34pmt. Multiple VLGCs are set to arrive in the region at the end of December and early January. As was seen by an oil major offering 15-20 December arrival VLGC in the window. A petrochemical buyer tendered for a full propane VLGC delivery 1-5 January arrival, looking to get the best offer from multiple potential sellers. LPG production in the region has been down by around 35% in the last two weeks of October and the first two weeks of November. The reduced production occurred just as the demand duo PDHs returning to the market with December Propane / Naphtha spread at +$21pmt, and winter demand picking up. The region has absorbed all of the arb cargoes headed to the region and that looks set to continue with the window trading at hefty premiums for prompt cargoes and small premiums for January arrivals.

**MED**

 The region is quiet as traders wait for Algerian contract prices for December to be published next week. Freight tightness has been hampering prompt cargo trades. The region has seen imports from the US which has covered the most urgent demand. USA HAPPY THANKSGIVING!
