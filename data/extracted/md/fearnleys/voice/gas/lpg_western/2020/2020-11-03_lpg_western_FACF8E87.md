## Market Commentary

AMERICAS As the election continues oil prices have drawn back after an initial surge. Mt Belvieu propane has remained strong and lies just below 60% WTI. A larger than expected 2.6 million barrel draw in US inventories for the week ending October 30th was bullish Mt Belvieu. The draw was largely due to massive exports through October, and averaging 1.425 million barrels per day for the week. Some believe with 95.8 million barrels in domestic inventories and already well into draw season Mt Belvieu should not be at such a high % WTI. The question is, with terminal expansions and export volume records set to be broken this winter, could the inventory doubters be proved right? Unseasonable demand from South America and prompt demand from Latin America continues to be seen.
