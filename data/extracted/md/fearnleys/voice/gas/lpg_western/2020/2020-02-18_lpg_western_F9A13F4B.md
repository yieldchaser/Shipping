## Market Commentary

Mt Belvieu propane prices rose to above 33% WTI this week as a result of the news of tariff exemptions for US LPG imports to China. This Mt Belvieu strength narrowed the arb East and cooled buying interest. The fog which impeded Houston Ship Channel traffic earlier in the week has dissipated, and these delays are not expected to have a big effect on the barrels per day exported. Less supply seen coming out of the AG has pushed US FOB sellers ideas up, and although the arbs East and West are open, the bid offer level remains wide. Sellers for March tons are still asking for double digits as buyers are slightly below. Volume to be Exported from the US East Coast terminal at Marcus Hook is expected to find its stride beginning April as the winter demand season comes to an end. Pipeline capabilities on ME 2 are 300,000 barrels per day but we expect the actual figure to be more like 175,000 barrels per day which will mean about 10 VLGC’s per month.
