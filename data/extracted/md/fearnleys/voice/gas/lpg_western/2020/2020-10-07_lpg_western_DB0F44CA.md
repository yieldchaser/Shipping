## Market Commentary

AMERICAS A draw of 100,000bbls left US propane inventories effectively flat at 101.842mmbbls. Market participants were anticipating anywhere from a 1mmbbl draw to a 1mmbbl increase. Domestic US propane market values moved up from a close on Monday of 50.125cts/gal, to a close of 51.333cts/gal on Tuesday and then closed Wednesday at 50.500cts/gal – leaving the pricing structure, while still mildly positive, rather unchanged as of midweek. Export volumes are deemed to be stronger, but Hurricane Delta now threatens the US Gulf Coast and in one case a rather precautionary Force Majeure was declared for a cargo which was slated to load on the weekend. Depending on the actual path taken by the (now Category 2) storm, a temporary closure of the Houston Ship Channel is presently unlikely. Crude oil prices are up at present on oil shutdowns in the US Gulf, as almost 1.5mmbbls/day of production has been shuttered. Meanwhile, arbs for Q4 have seen significant improvement as Asian values extended gains, propping up the likelihood that the West-to-East trade will be bolstered.
