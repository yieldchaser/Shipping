## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The front month propane price at Mt. Belvieu softened slightly today (Thursday) following another strong US Inventory build (2.2 Million barrels) Freight remains in the 90's basis Houston/Chiba and has done little to support netbacks- however the arb has improved week on week and now stands at approx. $135/ton basis Oct/Nov LST/FEI.  Very end October cargoes are now on offer at MB+5.875cpg with buyers coming from a 4 handle. The overbought product glut in Asia is showing signs of finally dissipating and this will hopefully allow for more concrete spot discussion basis November arrival. On the US East coast, the few remaining Butane parcels are being worked end September and 1h October before all the volume will be consumed by blending season.
