## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The FOB US Gulf Propane resale discussion remained thin for January cargos- as freight continued to eclipse the entirety of the Jan /Feb LST/FEI arb, (which now stands at $175/ton). On Wednesday the EIA reported a sizeable draw of 4.1 million barrels, exacerbating the negative pressure on the arb. An easing of propane production, coupled with another strong week of exports (1.3million barrels/ day) was the catalyst for the considerable 3 million barrel draw in padd 3. The East coast inventories drew down just shy of 1 million barrels while the midcontinent realized a small build. It’s beginning to feel a lot like Christmas.. Activity feels muted and while there are a sparse few January cargos on offer both from Marcus Hook and Houston, it will take a trader/ end user who already has a vessel if there’s to be any chance to mount a competitive bid.
