## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: Discussions in the US Gulf today are focused around the number of cancellations which have or will occur for April loading cargoes. Understandable when Mt Belvieu propane prices have been relatively strong compared to crude (although at 20 year lows on flat price) while delivered markets weak. The seventh consecutive draw in US inventories of 1.8 million barrels was larger than expected and did not help. The effect has been USGC FOB market is cancellations. Rumoured between seven and ten VLGCs for April. The hope is that these cancellations will build inventories, bearish Mt Belvieu, widening the arb. The dampener could be new bearish propane production estimates for 2020-2021 in this new ultra-low crude price environment. Leading to traders asking themselves if the arb will widen as hoped, and if so when?
