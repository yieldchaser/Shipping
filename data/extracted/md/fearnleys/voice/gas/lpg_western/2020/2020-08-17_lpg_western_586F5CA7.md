## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: The front month propane price at Mt. Belvieu was buoyed Wednesday, following the EIA's surprising flat Inventory report that effectively decreased the arb even further-to roughly $117/ton. Freight in the 90's basis Houston/Chiba has done little to support netbacks and the last concluded September cargo out of the US Gulf is said to have sold at MB+3.75cpg- reflecting the poor economics East. The overbought product glut continues to loom over Asia which in turn has led to as many as 12 September US cargos to have been cancelled, though that number remains up for debate. Despite the large number of cancelations, these cargos continue to be resold, allowing for exports to remain strong, along with a number of September cargos still up for sale from both Marcus Hook and the Gulf where buyers are said to be coming from the high 3's and sellers in the low 4's. A number of traders seem to think the discussion for October cargoes from the US gulf will be more competitive as the east oversupply clears.
