## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: The EIA reported PADD 3’s (US Gulf) first significant inventory build of the season on Wednesday. Despite the echo of production cutbacks, US propane output remains above 2 million barrels/day though further cuts are likely on the horizon. As it stands, the arbitrage is shut. A weeklong US crude rally, on top of a cautious supply outlook has crushed netbacks to 2.875cpg to Asia and 0.250cpg to NWE. Cancelation levels. With that said, no US cargos have been cancelled yet on the belief that the substantial Mid-East propane cutbacks, and continued strength in India will result in physical premiums upon cargos arrivals. A tender issued by a Chilean Importer was awarded on Wednesday to a competitive offer of MB +15cpg, catching the market off guard.
