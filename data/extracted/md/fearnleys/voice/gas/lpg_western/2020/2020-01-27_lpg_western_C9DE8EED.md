## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: Confusion reigns. Crashing arb and terminalling fees has participants scratching their heads wondering if it really is January or have we actually morphed into a summer month. Netbacks for US Gulf cargos have come off close to 75 percent since their peak in just over 3 weeks leaving buyers and sellers worlds apart. Adding to the confusion is a CP price which seems way over valued and leaving US terminal operators wishing they could sell CP related. US inventories came off 3.7 million barrels for the week and this allowed propane prices to edge back over the thirty percent of WTI value. Their were fog delays last week but exports showed up at a record 1.569 million barrels per day. We realize this takes into consideration pipeline , rail and truck exports as well as waterborne exports but we cant help but feel this level is a bit overdone.
