## Market Commentary

US With much volatility and uncertainty, Mont Belvieu propane has, since the start of June, seen a 10cpg swing in pricing but seems now to be finding some footing in the mid 70cpg range. It was immediately assumed with the release of this week’s EIA inventory which showed a 5.1mmbbl increase in propane stocks, that further relaxation would occur. But considering all of the other factors currently competing with one another the market remains essentially flat. The larger-than-expected inventory build brought the overall total stock level at 72.60mmbbls, or 99% of last year’s 2H Jun level, and puts the U.S. inventories in a position to reach well in to the high 90mmbbl level by the end of Oct – the conclusion of the traditional ‘build’ season. Meanwhile, the spot FOB terminal fee market continues to compete with freight and has been held largely to either side of the 5cpg level.
