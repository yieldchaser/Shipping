## Market Commentary

The EIA this week reported yet another propane inventory build which has overall US stocks resting at 78.40mmbbls, which is effectively on par with last year’s stock level, but more importantly, well beyond the 5yr average for the 27th week of the year. It was only a few months ago when serious doubts were expressed as to whether 2025 inventories would rise to sufficient levels. However, if historical averages are employed to calculate the balance of the inventory build season (which concludes 31 Oct) for 2025, stocks should then be at 99mmbbls – well above the 5yr average of 92.3mmbbls. However, it is believed that operations have now begun at Energy Transfer’s Nederland terminal which should elevate export volumes, thus impacting overall propane inventory levels. As for the Gulf Coast terminal fee market, where July is largely concluded, the August market is pretty well pegged at between 5-6cpg, depending on timing, ratio, etc.
