## Market Commentary

US Asking levels for a spot FOB USGC cargo have been hovering in the low double-digit range for the past few weeks, with sentiment swaying back / forth based on a host of factors, including freight, FEI premiums, tariff uncertainty and the usual uncertainty of the conclusion of winter. The last known transaction was at 11cpg, but sellers for the moment have the upper hand and are asking 12s for April. With the recent uptick in freight, however, this may be short-lived as buyers are now pointing to the growing vessel availability on the Gulf Coast. Meanwhile, the EIA this week recorded a larger-than-expected draw of 3.4mmbbls, where a decline of some 1.5mmbbls had been anticipated. This brought US propane stock levels to 87% of last year’s early March inventory level, and 74% of two years ago. The EIA data also revealed a 2.697mmbbl/day propane production level, just off the high-water mark of 2.823mmbbls seen in April 2024. Exports suffered a bit due to lingering, persistent fog on the Gulf Coast over the past week (or so), and at 1.718mmbbls/day was a decline from the previous week’s export level of 2.248mmbbls/day. For perspective, the highest export levels were achieved in Apr 2024 when the EIA reported export volumes at 2.335mmbbls/day.
