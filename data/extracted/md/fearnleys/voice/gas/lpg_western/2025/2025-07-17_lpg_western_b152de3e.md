## Market Commentary

US Mont Belvieu propane values have softened on the heels of yesterday’s EIA report which showed a larger-than-expected inventory build. Where a +2.5mmbbl increase was forecast, the EIA reported a 4.40mmbbl build for an overall stock level of 82.80mmbbls. That, in turn, puts the US in position to potentially reach over 100mmbbls at end-Oct, but the startup of Energy Transfer’s expanded marine terminal capacity will likely have an impact on the outcome. Meanwhile, the August market remains at between the 5.00-6.00cpg terminal fee level, depending on timing and ratio, etc. The declining MB market, though, has allowed the arb to improve but whether terminal fees will rise or the freight market will capture that differential is presently in limbo. The freight market has been quick and nimble and largely able to have remained on top of the changes in the market, but some FOB sellers have begun to (proverbially) draw a line in the sand and are offering at no less than 6cpg.
