## Market Commentary

US: Mont Belvieu propane continues in the upper 80cpg range, bolstered by ongoing weather related demand and crude which remains in the low $70/bbl range. With freight rates languishing, terminal fee transactions have been seen in the 9s for March, and in the 10cpg range for April. Sentiment is in favor of sellers, as ‘strip’ buyers have emerged as of late. The EIA today released its findings for the week ending 14 Jan, and where the market was expecting a draw of some 2.5 to 3.0mmbbls, the data showed a 3.6mmbbl draw. Export levels were remarkably high considering the fog which enveloped the southeast Texas Gulf Coast last week, but nonetheless were recorded at 1.988mmbbls/day against a YTD average of 1.850mmbbls/day In the news, Targa just announced "a new 150 MBbl/d fractionator in Mont Belvieu (“Train 12”). Among other announcements, Targa also announced a new expansion of LPG export capabilities at Targa’s Galena Park Marine Terminal (“GPMT LPG Export Expansion”) which will increase capacity to approximately 19 million barrels per month (“MMBbl/month”)". Targa’s current nameplate capacity is 450k b/d, and will rise to 634k b/d by Q3 ’27.
