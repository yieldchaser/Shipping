## Market Commentary

US As anticipated, Mont Belvieu market levels continue to taper now that the (formerly) raging winter storm has subsided. This is because while at the same time demand is in decline, production from the Bakken, the Permian and the Eagle Ford have, at 2.59mmbbls/day resumed (as of the week ending 31 Jan) their approach towards the YTD 2025 average of 2.6mmbbls/day. Meanwhile, and also as expected, export volumes have improved as the EIA report a 2.137mmbbl volume for the week ending 31 Jan which compares to the YTD average of 1.861mmbbls/day – so around 275,000bbls/day greater than average. With fog in/around Houston this week the export number for next week may well decline. Terminal fees these past couple of weeks have very slowly increased, having earlier dipped – notionally, at least – into the upper single digits, but as Mont Belvieu pricing softened and the arb fell a low 10’s/gal was seen fixed, and at present asking levels have moved up in to the low teens. Very little is being accomplished, though, as the market has gone quiet with the now ongoing uncertainty surrounding the question of tariffs.
