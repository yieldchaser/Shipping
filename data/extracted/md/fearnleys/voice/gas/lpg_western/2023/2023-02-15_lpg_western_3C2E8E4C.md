## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Market levels in March have remained robust, particularly for the first half of the month, but begin to taper by the second. April, as well, softens and beyond that May, as well, dropping as far as the mid single-digits by June. As yet, however, only limited activity is being noted due to the backwardation that exists, the downward curve preventing much enthusiasm in the out months. The arb both to the East and the West remains open – and widely – despite the weak PDH margins in the East and the nearing conclusion of winter in the West. The EIA yesterday reported a draw of 2.6mmbbls, mildly more than the anticipated 2.25mmbbl – the average that the industry at large had predicted. Still, though, stock levels remain nearer to the 5-year highs, than the averages, so even with a relatively large draw there remain sufficient stocks. Talk of summertime brine containment persists. LatAm demand has been noted but transactions at this point have not been confirmed. Southern hemisphere winter demand should, however, begin to appear shortly. NWE The staggering large cargo premiums seen in January on the back of resurgent petrochemical demand and competition from the East’s re-emerging appetite for LPG are now ancient history. Premiums have plummeted from +$50/ton in January, to premiums in the mid-teens now in Feb. The cause? Propane has maintained its strength in relation to naphtha thus disincentivizing consumption at crackers, along with continued mild weather throughout the region. Further still, Its estimated Europe will import approximately 320,000 tons this month of LPG -from both the North Sea and the US, leaving the continent very well supplied and subsequently pressuring prices to the downside.

**MED**

 The Mediterranean region is focused entirely on the search, rescue, and aid of those affected by the recent earthquake. With that said, the LPG import terminal in southwest turkey has seen 2 LGC’s discharged since the disaster, leaving the region comfortably supplied for the time being.
