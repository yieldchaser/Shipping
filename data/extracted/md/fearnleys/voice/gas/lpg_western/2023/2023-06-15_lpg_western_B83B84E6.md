## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Netbacks to the US have improved in recent days, having climbed (in the case of the European market) from the depths of less than a penny in early June to around a nickel at present. The East, however, continues to be rather heavily favored, but the gap has narrowed this week. This is in spite of the freight market which continues to confound and is related in part to the growing inventory levels in the US. Overall propane stocks were reported by the EIA to have risen to 75.50mmbbls as of 9th June, an increase of 3.2mmbbls, which is the second-largest build for the 23rd week since 2018 (which grew by 3.82mmbbls). Nonetheless, propane inventories are now 145% on a YOY basis, and 137% of two years ago. Terminal fees for July are seen at between 5.00-6.00cpg, depending on timing/location, etc., but are more in the low 6cpg range for Aug, followed by mid/upper 6s for Sep and onwards to the 7cpg range for Oct. Very little is being accomplished as yet, however, as the summer doldrums seem to be setting in. Latin American demand is satisfied in the northern tier, and best described as “pending” in the southern tier. There are potential requirements through the summer, but it is dependent upon weather-related demand in the lower latitudes. NWE An early July full propane VLGC offered in the window was equivalent to a small discount to CIF ARA, and failed to garner any interest. The vessel’s AIS data continues to show South China as the destination, so the offer is testing European demand. A TOT bid in the window also for early July dates increased to July CIF ARA +$4.5 for the contract 19.4-21KT volume, failed to find a counterpart. The import volume for the region remains at 385,000MT in June, a long way off the 600,000MT received in May. Butane remains under pressure with a large cargo offer in the window failing to find a home at 57% bal June Naphtha. Butane has seen a rise in demand from petchem buyers as the deep discount has it replacing propane in the feedstock slate where possible.

**MED**

 The region has seen a large increase in import volume in June, 500,000MT will be imported, a four month high. This has the market taking a breather and looking for demand opportunities.
