## Market Commentary

AMERICAS MB propane market levels have improved alongside the gains seen in the crude arena, but the percentage of propane-to-WTI continues in the 34-35% range, giving some cause to believe that propane is undervalued – particularly going into the winter. Prior to that, however, crop drying demand is not expected to reach average consumption levels due to the general lack of rain in the MidWest and the scorching heat which continues to plague most of North America. Meanwhile, in consideration of the OPEC crude cuts which have limited LPG output in that region of the world, much of the focus is now on the US Gulf Coast as an alternative source of supply. However, with limited marine terminal capacity and burgeoning demand – not to mention ongoing limitations related to shipping availability – terminal fees have ramped up solidly. Transactions are known to have occurred at above 15cts/gal for 1H Oct spot FOB deals, with talk of any remaining volumes at still higher levels. As for propane inventories, the EIA report for the week ending 25 Aug stocks built by a surprising 3.2mmbbls for an overall total of 95.5mmbbls – well beyond what was achieved last year in late August when stocks were recorded at 72.2mmbbls. With yet another four weeks in the traditional build season, it is well within reason that propane stocks will easily exceed 100mmbbls prior to what is now expected (by some) to be a colder-than-normal winter period. NWE The TOT window has been consistently offered with no bids corresponding bids for a 2H September propane cargo. Leaving the physical discount at September CIF ARA -$7.50/MT. Propane naphtha lost almost $10/MT as propane gained whilst naphtha came off. Now at -$106/MT for September propane remains the favoured feedstock. The current issue is the continued reduced run rates and maintenance at petchem plants, which is shrinking the size of the European market. The September East/West spread has widened to +$107/MT. Though with Panama Canal transit costs and waiting times some traders see Europe as a safe option keeping US tons flowing across the Atlantic. Butane values tracked naphtha down and are assessed at 89% September Naphtha quotes.

 Sonatrach prices were posted at $530/$550 for propane and butane respectively. Split ratio VLGCs continue to head to the West Med from the US as Turkish demand remains high.
