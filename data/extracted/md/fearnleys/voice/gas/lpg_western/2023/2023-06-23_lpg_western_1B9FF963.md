## Market Commentary

AMERICAS MB propane prices began the month at just over 57.25cts/gal, and as of this writing are off by about a penny at 56.25cpg, which is generally in line with anticipation which called for prices to see a decline under the weight of the rapidly growing inventory levels. Stock level growth over the summer should continue to put downward pressure on the pricing structure. This week’s EIA data, however, did not help that effort as an expected build of some 3.2mmbbls turns out to have been a 1.5mmbbl increase for an overall total of 77mmbbls. The 5-yr average build for week 24 is 2.44mmbbls. Meanwhile, though, freight is the word of the day as Hou/Chi rates have risen by over $20/mt (from just over $156/mt at the start of June to current levels approaching $180/mt. Terminal fees have not yet seen any reaction and remain in the mid 5cpg to perhaps 6cpg range for July, modestly higher in Aug and a bit higher, still, in Sep. NWE The East vs West spread has widened to $78/MT which will make it hard for the region to draw US tons. Additionally with the rapid rise in freight rates, freight offers favour going East from the US. The constant bidding for a 1H July TOT cargo finally drew out a corresponding offer, and although nothing was concluded, this left the TOT cargo premium at July CIF ARA +$6/MT. Petchem propane demand is down with the Propane / Naphtha spread at $124/MT due to reduced run rates and butane being the favoured feedstock as the Butane / Naphtha spread is $244/MT. Butane remains at 56% Bal June Naphtha.

**MED**

 Imports to the region from the US have dried up, although the 490,000MT set to arrive in June is large. The region is well supplied and any demand is being rapidly filled.
