## Market Commentary

Exports from the US have maintained above 1.3million barrels per day. Market participants sees this level as the new normal at least until the EPD expansion comes on line with 7 more cargos per month in September. The arbs remain wide enough to sustain strong freight levels both to Europe and the East. South American demand continues it’s seasonal rise.   On the domestic front the build of 1.1million barrels was smaller than the expected 1.5 range though overall inventories are still over 22 million higher then this time last year. Most of the builds recently have been in the Midwest which may cause some more downward pressure there as the spread between Midwest and USGC is about 6cpg but some of the other products are 10cpg or higher. The outright price has not moved very much this week on the front with 1cpg change week on week from the high to the low. The average for the week on front month has been 58.8125-59.0625, with the high being after stats came out. Also of note the prices are at or close to the lows for the last 11 months. On the Butane front the price had largely been following crude oil and has seen a little more volatility with a weekly range on the front of 63- 66.0625 for the daily average. Volumes have been fairly lackluster on the front trades.
