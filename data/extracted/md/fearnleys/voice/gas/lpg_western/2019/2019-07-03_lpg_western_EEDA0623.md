## Market Commentary

The 4th of July traditionally marks the middle of the summer and the market seems to be reflecting this as well. Inventories are building as expected, and with this weeks build of 1.3 million barrels it seems almost certain that inventories will peak at over 100 million barrels when the winter season officially starts on October 1st. Production is maintaining over 2 million barrels a day and exports are maintain over 1 million barrels per day. In South America, Gasco, the owner of Gasmar, and Enlasa announced their plans for a new fully refrigerated floating storage has been approved. Construction is scheduled to start in March of 2020 and the project should be completed in the 2nd quarter 2022. The tank will be 45000 cubic meters and the imports will be handled by Gasmar.
