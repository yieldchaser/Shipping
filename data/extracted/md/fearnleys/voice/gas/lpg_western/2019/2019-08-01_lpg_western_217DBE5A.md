## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: Due to unprecedented delays in the US Gulf during the late Winter and Spring of this year the Arb market enjoyed a very good few months. Many traders pondered whether this was precursor to going back to the future of the export Arb or simply a short term outcome of unusual circumstance. As the market now has returned to a more normal summer market, it seems it may be more the latter. In the US inventories built at close to the expectation of 1.4 million barrels. However this did not stop the wacky markets between LST and Enterprise. With LST losing a substantial amount of price on the last day of the month, they now start August at parody. We do not expect this to last but only time will tell. Ethane reached single digits on the week for the first time but that was also just for the July barrel. In August Ethane may test the single digits again.
