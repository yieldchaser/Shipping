## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: The lower than expected draw in US inventories of 100,000 bbls took the total inventories to 99.8 million barrels. The draw was bearish against expectations but Mt Belvieu went up. Last week the inventory number was bullish and Mt Belvieu went down. Go figure, but with that said the first real cold weather of the season has arrived and this domestic demand may be the real reason for the domestic price strength. This has coupled with rising freight rates has brought netback values down and traders FOB bid ideas down into single figures for December. We seemed to have reached a stalemate of sellers not willing to go below double figures FOB premiums and sellers not willing to pay up. Cargoes have been being worked a long way in advance to the usual 30-40 days prior laycan due to tight freight. The question is who will blink first for the few remaining FOB cargoes available this year? Buyers paying up or sellers coming down?
