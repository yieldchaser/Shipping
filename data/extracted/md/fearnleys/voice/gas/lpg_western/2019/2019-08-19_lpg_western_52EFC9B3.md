## Market Commentary

AMERICAS US inventories grew 4 million barrels last week which was above market expectations but the market managed to not lose any further ground from prior to stats. Current inventories stand at 90.5 million barrels a large 22 million barrels over last year. This has traders looking at the weekly propane production. The 4 week average of 2.253 million barrels a day is high. This production coupled with high inventory levels has been keeping Mt Belvieu propane around 30% of WTI. Export capacity from the US has increased this year and if Mt Belvieu remains cheap we could see a large amount of that export capacity being utilised sooner than expected. Traders are wondering if we will see multiple cancellations before the end of the year. Or if the market will continue to support elevated freight rates, and FOB trades slightly above cancellation levels.
