## Market Commentary

Propane inventories resumed their seasonable build , increasing this week by 2.3 million barrels. At this pace inventories should peak at over 100 million barrels by the end of September. This level should ensure more than enough inventory to support a cold winter, as well as maximum exports, even with the export expansion at Enterprise which will come on line in the third quarter. This high inventory level is also effecting prices, pushing propane levels down to less than 35 percent of WTI. This normally is good for the Arb however high freights are hampering US Gulf exports. In South America, Brazilian demand continues at their normal winter pace.
