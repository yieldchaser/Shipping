## Market Commentary

This winter in the US has been deified by smaller than expected draws in US inventories. This week the 1.2 million barrel draw was again lower than expected. The reason behind these lower draws has been the domestic production which has only dipped under the 2 million barrels per day a few times. Even through very cold weather for a sustained amount of time in many parts of the US the production is just too great. This very high production is only set to increase and exports will be expected to make up for this additional production, ie product will have to be priced to move. The recent fog delays have had a large part in pushing up delivered markets with high cash diffs in the East and West windows, and is set to continue through the week. Traders right now are trying to assess how long these premiums will continue, and if they can justify paying through netback value for US FOBs. A tender from Chile was heard to be won on a delivered basis at around 17cpg.
