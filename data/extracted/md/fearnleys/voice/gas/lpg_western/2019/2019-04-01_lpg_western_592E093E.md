## Market Commentary

Mt Belvieu propane prices weakened Wednesday following a larger than expected build of 1.6 million barrels. Domestic production remains above 2 million barrels a day whilst exports were above 1 million barrels a day for the second week running. At the Houston IHS conference much of the talk has been about new US export capacity coming on at the end of this year and in 2020, aligning well with new US fractionation capacity. Both of which will be substantial. The belief is still that all of the new US propane to hit the water will find a home by pricing itself into the international market.
