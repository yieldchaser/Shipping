## Market Commentary

AMERICAS: Enterprise Products issued a press release this week stating that in addition to their 175,000 barrel expansion coming on line in September of this year, they would have another expansion of 260,000 barrels per day commencing third quarter 2020. When completed this would give Enterprise a capacity to load 1.1 million barrels per day of LPG or 60 VLGC cargoes per month. It seems hard to imagine where all this product will go but one thing that seems certain in LPG is the product always seems to find a home. Closer to the present the Gulf Coast is gearing up for its first storm of the season, Current forecasts have the storm missing the Texas export Terminals and going into Louisiana but precautions are being made none the less. With Enterprise and Targa finally recovered from the winters disruption they are holding their breath and hoping this storm does not hit.
