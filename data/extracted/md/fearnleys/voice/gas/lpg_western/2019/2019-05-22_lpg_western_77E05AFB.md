## Market Commentary

The propane market for sure did not like the stats this week and combined with falling crude oil, made for an ugly day losing over 2.25 CPG. Overall propane inventories are getting rather high @ 65.8 million 48 of which is in the Gulf Coast. FOB USGC terminalling fees are bid at 7.5cpg while offers are in the double digits for June cargoes. This wide bid/offer level is attributed to window premiums in the East market. These cash premiums remain in the single digits at the moment. This means that traders are reluctant to sell FOB US at a flat netback US/FEI levels. One thing is for , sure as long as market remains in backwardation l, stock building will not start and the entire market knows it must before the winter season.
