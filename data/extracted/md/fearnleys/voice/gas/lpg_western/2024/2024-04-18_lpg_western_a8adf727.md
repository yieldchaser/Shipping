## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Where inventory data expectations ranged widely from a build of around one million barrels to a high of as much as three million barrels, most of the market was clustered around the 2mmbbl build range. The EIA yesterday, however, posted a build of 4mmbbls – well above most (if not all) expectations. The initial shock soon gave way, however, to the generally accepted view that the large build likely included corrective data from weeks past, versus the idea that there might be a correction in the coming weeks. In any case, stocks as of 13 April 2024 stand at 55.7mmbbls, which is almost exactly 100% of last year’s level – but still 150% of two years ago. The MB pricing structure reacted immediately and sharply increased the rate of decline for non-TET propane values. Currently MB c3 is pegged at the mid 78cpg range with the expectation that further price erosion is likely on the horizon. In looking towards the end of the build season – typically the first week of Oct – most calculations put stock levels at around the 95mmbbl range, which is based on robust production levels, generally capped export volumes, flat(ish) chem consumption, warmer weather and (therefore) limited summertime res/com demand. NWE At present, Europe is projected to conclude april having imported just shy of 500kt- half of which will discharge at Terneuzen. The regions resilient demand comes on the back propane's utilization versus naphtha at Europeans petrochemical plants- The East /west spread continues to widen, and econs are becoming less advantageous- but for now, cargoes especially from Marcus Hook, continue to sail toward NWE.
