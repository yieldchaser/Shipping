## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Propane values at Mont Belvieu have with modest exception been slowly on the rise since mid Q4 ’23, though WTI has not entirely kept pace which has – in turn – led to a YTD average propane-to-WTI ratio of 48%, or 10% over the calendar 2023 average of 38%. Meanwhile, as the days and weeks progress in 2024 the prospects of a meaningfully significant winter season in the U.S. continue to erode, and at over 60mmbbls as of early Feb, stock levels and product availability are not an issue. Various models have Feb concluding in the low/mid 50mmbbl range, which is entirely sufficient even in the event of a major weather event. Furthermore, there is little unused or lingering export capacity at this point, but production is expected to simultaneously continue rising which should put additional downward pressure on pricing. As such there are a rising number of market participants who are forecasting that MB propane values will decline. The question, however, is when, and by how much? As for terminal fees, the high-water market was set at 13cpg for early March, but since then a host of sellers have emerged and asking levels have declined by around half. Buy-side interest is limited, though, as the pricing backwardation has pushed some players to the sidelines.
