## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The EIA this week reported a modest draw of 300,000bbls which brought overall US stocks to 101.5mmbbls, or almost exactly100% of the same period in 2023, and 115% of the total achieved in 2022. As before, there are sufficient stocks of propane in the domestic US market. Production is most recently listed at 2.754mmbbls/day, which is well above average for the year (and the quarter), and exports are, for the most recent reporting period, actually below average at 1.578mmbbls/day (against a YTD average of 1.724mmbbls/day). While those numbers are being absorbed, propane at Mont Belvieu continues to outperform crude, the evidence being the sharp rise in the c3/WTI ratio, which moved from 42% at the front of October to 50% at the close of business yesterday. Terminal fees, meanwhile, are solidly robust through Nov, but there is very little remaining on offer. It is then that the market softens, and rather suddenly. Where Nov transactions were solidly in the mid 20cpg range, December is bid at a dime less, though offers are – for the moment - still in the upper teens.
