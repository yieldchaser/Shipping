## Market Commentary

AMERICAS As of this writing Mont Belvieu propane values have declined nearly 13cts/gal since the first of April, and 3.50cpg since the start of May. This is largely due to the weight of the inventories, which continue to rise – most recently by 2.2mmbbls, which brought overall US total propane stocks to 56.90mmbbls, or 96% of the total recorded at the same time last year. This year, however, production is higher, and exports are effectively capped which, alongside static domestic res/com and generally flat petchem-related demand should result in stock builds throughout the summer. Furthermore, with a 36-37% relationship to crude, it appears likely there is further downside potential. As for terminal fees, they are robust, but little is being accomplished. 13cts/gal is being offered for (at least) a trio of 2H Jun parcels but buy-side ideas remain significantly lower. 1H Jul interest is beginning to emerge but remains in the low double-digits.
