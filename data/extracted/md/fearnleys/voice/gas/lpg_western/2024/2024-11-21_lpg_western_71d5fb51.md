## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The EIA this week reported an as-predicted draw of 700,000bbls which brought overall propane stock levels to 97.70mmbbbls as of 15 Nov 2024. This compares to the 99.30mmbbls in mid Nov 2023 (which equates to 98.39% YoY) and 89.00mmbbls in mid-Nov 2022 (or 109.78% YoY). On a Days of Supply basis, there are presently 96 days of supply, which is below the Q4 ‘24 value of just over 100 days, but well above the Cal ’24 average of 82.65 days. Meanwhile, Gulf Coast propane terminal fees remain generally soft, having come off the 20 (plus) cts/gal range last week to the mid teens at present. Butane fees would nominally be around half of that. Otherwise, while the US/FarEast arb is waffling around the $200/mt mark, many regular market participants have already begun their retreat to the sidelines in order to preserve 2024 profits – and year-end bonuses.
