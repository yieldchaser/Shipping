## Market Commentary

AMERICA All eyes are on the US export market, but with tightness at the terminals (which is the combined result of back-to-back-to-back storms in/around Houston as well as reduced load rates due to maintenance) it is the re-sellers who are in control at present. And with a lengthy Gulf Coast freight market, offers for 1H Aug range from 13cpg (for a 2:2) to ‘mid’ teens for a 4:0. The market did seem to sigh in relief with the release of yesterday’s EIA data which showed a solid 2.1mmbbl build. The market had been anticipating something like 1.75mmbbls, but in hindsight it is thought that perhaps this week’s data included corrections from weeks past, during which time lower-than-expected builds were recorded. At present US propane inventories are listed at 73.5mmbbls, which is 92.5% of last year, and 136% of two-years ago. Current estimates for the end of the 2024 ‘build’ season (which traditionally concludes the first week of Oct) have total overall stocks pegged at a about 99mmbbls – which would be sufficient for the winter season and anticipated export volumes. Meanwhile, the Mont Belvieu market continues to be priced at stubbornly high levels. Non-TET MB propane today is marked at ~84cpg, or just over 43% of crude oil which – in its own right – continues to make upward strides
