## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Generally speaking the domestic US market has seen a slow start to 2024 as market participants have returned to the desk but have so far been engaged in finalizing their respective 2023 books. While there has lately been in North America some weather-related demand, and export volumes are at a respectably healthy 1.720mmbbls/day (off from 1.882mmbbls in the previous EIA report), overall US stock levels for the week ending 29 Dec were given at 82.6mmbbls, or 2.1mmbbls lower than the previous week’s reported data. Market expectations varied widely, but in the end a pragmatic prediction of an approximate 2.5mmbbl decline was deemed reasonable, and thus the 2.1mmbbl drop was considered appropriate. As such, Mont Belvieu propane prices for Jan have not reacted much and remain in the upper 60cpg range. Terminal fees, though largely untested so far this year, are expected to have remained in the mid/single digit range of around 6cpg. NWE The TOT window has been quite to start the year, the reduction of US exports transiting the region as they avoid the Suez Canal has reduced window offers. Also causing the East West spread to narrow from $165/MT yesterday to $158/MT today. An increase in petchem demand in the region from 340,000MT in November to 420,000MT in December is a welcome boost of demand as mild winter weather has kept seasonal demand low. An increase in North Sea LPG production, due to LPG’s premium to Natural Gas halted rejection, has accounted for part of this new petchem demand. Large cargo butane trades have been absent from the market leaving value assessed at 81.5% January Naphtha.

**MED**

 The mild temperatures for the most part this winter have kept stocks in the region at healthy levels. While the large volume US imports have been accounting for any new demand.
