## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Happy Thanksgiving! NWE US imports to Europe continue at high monthly volumes as North Sea production remains low. The European window has been active for the last week and is assessed at December CIF ARA +$11pmt. The large unexpected build in US inventories could see more volume exported from Marcus Hook, the majority of these tons find their way to European shorts. With the East market softening slightly, traders could once again try to place their cargoes into European shorts particularly if they need a vessel back promptly. Cold weather is starting to creep into the region which will soon start increasing demand, for now stocks are high. Butane demand in the region has calmed slightly and values have come off to 92.5% Naphtha which has taken a dive related to the drop in crude oil values.

**MED**

 Crude price volatility has LPG traders in the region waiting for the dust to settle until showing interest in the open market. Weather related demand is yet to be seen.
