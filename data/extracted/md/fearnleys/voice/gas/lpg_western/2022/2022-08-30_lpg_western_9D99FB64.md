## Market Commentary

AMERICAS The domestic U.S. propane market was described as dangerously directionless which has given cause for many to retreat for the sidelines; the winning strategy being to do nothing, avoid any pitfalls and reduce the chances of making mistakes. Stock levels of propane in the U.S. rose by 4.2mmbbls to 72.20mmbbls, per the EIA report for the week ending 26 Aug. It is widely believed that over the course of September there should be very little problem in reaching sufficient pre-winter inventory levels and will likely end the month at 76-78mmbbls. Terminal fee levels have softened and the limited buy-side interest that does exist now starts in the 3cpg range. Sellers ideas, while earlier commencing with a 5-handle, are now encroaching upon cancellation economics. The U.S. Labor Day holiday will be observed on this next Monday, and as a result very little is expected to occur in the coming days. NWE The September Propane / Naphtha spread has flipped into positive territory, now at +$10pmt. So far there has not been any talk by petchems of switching feedstocks. Rather reducing run rates, with most European petchems having already reduced propylene output to 70% of capacity or less. So far now petchem traders have resold any volume in the window. There is disruption in North Sea supply and this could lead to higher import demand. So far this has not been seen and with the lack or window activity but the market looking long any offers are likely to go at a small discount to September CIF ARA. Butane continues to firm in this shoilder month before blending season begins, now at 95.5% September Naphtha.

**MED**

 Sonatrach postings at $630pmt for propane and $612pmt for butane are 12 and 14 month lows respectively. The region continues to work through the summer supply. Market activity has not yet picked up prior to blending season.
