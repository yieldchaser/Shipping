## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Volatility in the crude arena has allowed WTI prices over the past week to range from $96.30/bbl to $104.22/bbl, only to trade presently at $95.87/bbl as of this writing. That has translated to volatility in the domestic U.S. propane market where prices have ranged from over $1.14/gal down to just over $1.10/gal and now to current values just over $1.12/gal. The less-than-robust EIA report this week has allowed propane to retain some of its value as the stock data this week revealed a build of just 1.4mmbbls where the market was anticipating something above 2.25mmbbls. Current inventory levels are 94% of the same time last year, but just 70% of those recorded two years ago. Sentiment is that inventories are veering towards being insufficient for the winter. Terminal fees have shown modest buoyancy and asking prices for Aug are in the upper 4s, perhaps 5cpg. Buy-side interest, though, remains subdued. 2H 2022 is being talked in the 6cpg range. LatAm demand remains generally flat, though it is anticipated that modest volumes of spot demand may emerge for Sep. NWE The run of record LPG import months look like they will come to an end in August. The region is well supplied and demand remains low as expected in summer. The buyers with large storages could be incentivised to buy if they have the capacity take more product, to take advantage of the CIF ARA contango. August/September CIF ARA at -$5pmt and September/October CIF ARA-$6pmt. The July East West spread is +$62pmt, so all spot cargoes should be heading East, although term volumes continue to flow to Europe. The butane market in the region has been very quiet since being well offered for end July and early August arrival. Butane value notionally 82% Naphtha, this value is firming up as the butane product overhang is being worked through.

**MED**

 The summer continues to progress in the region without much activity. Although a VLGC loaded in the US is heading to Dortyol for an early August arrival.
