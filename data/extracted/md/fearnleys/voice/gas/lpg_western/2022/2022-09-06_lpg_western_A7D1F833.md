## Market Commentary

AMERICAS Poor economics continue to plague the US export market amid unattractive arbs. As such, and alongside swelling freight rates, terminal fees remain depressed with buy-side interest in the 3cpg range and sellers offering at around 4cpg; hovering just at/above cancellation economics. At least four cargoes are believed cancelled for Sep, with potentially more on the horizon due to persistently limited demand – particularly in Asia and Europe. There is, however, potential demand from LatAm as winter nears end and consumption increases. The EIA today released its findings for the week ending 02 Sep 2022 amid a wide range of expectations which spanned from 1mmbbls to as much as 3mmbbls, effectively revealing the uncertainty in the market. The data showed a 1.9mmbbl build for an overall total of 74.1mmbbls, or 106% of the same time last year but just 74% of two years ago. This effectively propels expectations for end-Sep stocks to exceed earlier expectations and broach the 80mmbbl mark. NWE A drop in Brent Crude pulled European Naphtha values down with it. This has pushed the September Propane / Naphtha spread in the region to +$27pmt, which is likely to further reduce petchem run rates in the region. Margins for Spot ethylene is now as low as a 25% discount to the monthly term price in August and spot propylene reached a discount of 30% to the monthly term price, Imports have slowed to the region, though planned maintenance at Karsto and Kollsnes could push up the need for imports as LPG production decreases by about a third during this time. To meet the expected maintenence demand, the number of US Origin propane cargos has continued to climb from already record levels. In Poland, an interesting supply demand dynamic is playing out as LPG demand for autogas has surged, against the restriction in accepting russian Product. Poland has sourced 50 percent less LPG from Russia and has looked to Norway and Sweden along with other sources to fill the requirment. Butane remains at 94% September Naphtha, though the C4 market has not seen activity in some time.

**MED**

 Vitol returned to the window to bid for 6KT butane FOB Lavera, September 14-16, which went unmet at a final bid of $605pmt. A butane heavy VLGC cargo loading STS off Estonia is expected to be discharged in Morocco in September.
