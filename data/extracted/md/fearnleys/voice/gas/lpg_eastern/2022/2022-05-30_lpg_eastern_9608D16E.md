## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco cut Jun CP to $750 for both grades, down by $100 for C3 and $110 for C4 respectively. The price was slightly lower than market anticipations. Lukewarm demand from traditional heating segment into summer season and soaring freight dampened the buying interest. Cash differentials for July FOBs still remained in negative territory, with one evenly split cargo for 1h Jul lifting was reported done at CP minus mid-teens level. Jul CP was traded at $735 at the time of writing.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium for Jul cargos softened this week. 23kt propane for 1h Jul delivery was bid at Jul FEI minus $3 while offer at flattish level. Similar cargo for 2h Jul delivery was offered at FEI plus $2. Several 46kt propane for 1h Jul arrival were changing hand outside window. One SEA importer was reported to buy a end Jun/early Jul delivered evenly split cargo at CP plus 40ish. One EChina cracker user awarded its buy tender for 23kt propane delivered during 5-15th Jul into Ningbo reportedly. Another EChina based refinery was also reported to procure one or two 1h Jul delivered 46kt propane cargo for cracking use.
