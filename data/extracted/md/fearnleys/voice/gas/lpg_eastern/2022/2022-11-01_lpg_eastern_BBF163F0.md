## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco announced Nov CP to $610 for both C3 and C4, whose propane was within expectation, but butane was surprised to be set at parity. And it’s been a rollercoaster ride for swap number this week. Dec CP was traded high to $636 on Wed from Monday’s $604 and plunged to $609 at the time of writing. With additional spot demand from Indonesia, discussions for evenly split cargo have improved, and one end Nov lifting cargo was traded at Dec CP minus single digit.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium improved considerably with worry of tightness on front market, as of rising freight and Panama delays. Bid for 23kt propane parcel for 1h Dec delivery was at Dec FEI plus $7 and for 2h Dec slightly less. Off window several Chinese petchem players are in the market tendered for Dec delivered propane, with only two managed to award one full propane cargo each, at FEI plus mid low teens into Ningbo and plus nearly $20 into Panjin. While the other PDH user based in Qingdao didn’t award their buy tender for similar volume as eroding margins against soaring costs. Also another Schina PDH is also reckoned that might not award their buy tender for half cargo propane for 1h Dec arrival for regular origins. Price for evenly split cargo was also on the fly. One SChina traditional importer was said to award their buy tender for a mid dec delivered 11/11 parcel at Dec CP plus freight which reckoned at 70s min in view of current Baltic. And another similar parcel for 1h Dec delivery was also reported concluded at 70s to CP into SEA delivery. Pertamina also called another spot tender to procure upto 69kt evenly split cargo for end Nov lifting AG or early dec delivery. Meanwhile, they’ve also called a term tender to secure a min 24 evenly split cargo for 2023 delivery.
