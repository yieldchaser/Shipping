## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco released their acceptance, with most said as per nomination and some reported minor delays. 1st round CP idea was recommended at $560-570 for C3 and $565-580 for C4. At the time of writing, Jan C3 swap was assessed at $561 and C4 at $570, and Jan/Feb C3 traded at $19 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 Discussions for Jan delivery cargoes are less active comparatively, with most buy positions covered before holiday. But off window a couple of 23kt propane parcels for 2h Jan delivery was changed hand at Jan FEI plus mid-teens. On Thursday 46kt propane for 1h Feb delivery was bid in window at Feb FEI plus $23 basis Ningbo, yet to see offers. Though the holiday is at the corner, we are seeing quite a flurry of buying demand for late Jan and Feb delivery this week. Last Friday one NChina PDH bought one 2h Jan delivered full propane cargo at Jan FEI plus around $20 reportedly. And one Vietnam importer was said to purchase a heavy propane cargo for 10-31st Jan delivery at Jan CP plus 50s. One Australia heavy propane cargo for early Feb lifting was also reported placed into Far East at Feb FEI plus 20s to 30s. Meanwhile, Fei/MOPJ spread widened and we’ve seen cracking units emerged for feedstock. One EChina cracker user tendered privately to procure 46kt propane for 10-28th Jan delivery at Jan FEI plus high teens reportedly. And HTC also issued buy tender for 23kt propane CFR Daesan end Jan to early Feb. Apart from propane inquiries, butane are in good demand as well. Taiwan FPCC awarded their tender to buy one 22/22 for 1-10th Feb delivery into Mailiao at Feb MOPJ minus high teens. India BPCL tendered to buy one evenly split cargo for 1-23rd Feb lifting or 11-28th Feb delivery. Indonesia PERTAMINA also procured two evenly split cargoes for early and late Feb delivery via tender, with one awarded on FOB AG at CP minus mid-teens and the other on CFR delivery at CP plus 40s reportedly.
