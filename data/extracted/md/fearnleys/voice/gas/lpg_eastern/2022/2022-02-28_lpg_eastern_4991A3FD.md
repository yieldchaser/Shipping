## Market Commentary

MEG Apr CP reach a multiple year high at $940 for C3 and $944 for C4 respectively, along with the surge in crude. Apr/May was traded at nearly $50 backwardation. Qatar Petroleum released their Apr loading acceptance with minor delays heard. Players are waiting for other producers’ acceptances and availabilities in spot sales. And this week Saudi Aramco was reported to sell one late Mar lifting heavy propane cargo at fixed price.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium keep soaring up as only bids seen in window. 23kt propane for 1h Apr delivery was bid at Apr FEI plus mid 50s on Thursday. Outside window seller for such parcels is heard asking at Mar FEI plus 50s range, whereas Mar/Apr FEI traded at high $40s range. One EChina cracker was reported that they didn’t award their buy tender, which asked for two parcels of 23kt or 46kt propane during late Mar-1h Apr delivery into Ningbo. Another PDH user also tendered to buy 23kt propane for similar delivery range. PDH users are planning for turnarounds or rate cuts due to the negative margins, as some which under turnaround at the moment is reselling propane stock to capture the profits in residential sector, and some is purchasing propylene to offset the less output from own units.
