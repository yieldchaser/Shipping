## Market Commentary

MEG Aug C3 CP was traded at $690 and C4 at $678, up by $20 from last week as crude rebounded. Aug/Sep C3 CP was traded at flattish or slight backwardation as front CP surged up. 1st round CP idea ranged from $670-685 for C3 and $660-670 for C4. However, 2nd round recommendation is expected to be refreshed higher as oil extended gains. KPC issued tender to sell a 2-3 Sep lifting evenly split cargo.

**FAR EAST**

 Window activities are muted, only earlier this week one 23kt propane parcel for 2h Aug delivery sold at Aug FEI minus $3.5. Off window several similar parcels were traded at Aug FEI minus $3-4. Meanwhile, a couple of full propane cargos for 1h Sep delivery were changed hand at equivalence to Sep FEI minus high single to low teens. Due to squeezed margin in, several PDHs in East and North China are having turnrounds or cutting down rates, refraining from spot purchase. Indian IOC issued term tender to secure one evenly split cargo monthly in Q1 2023 and believe the remaining demand for Q2-Q4 will come out consecutively. Earlier BPCL also tendered to procure in total 1.7 mmt LPG for 2023 delivery, which is still in validity but offers with good discount to CP heard. Indonesia PERTAMINA also issued spot tender to buy one evenly split cargo for late Aug lifting ex AG or mid Sep delivery.
