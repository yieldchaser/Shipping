## Market Commentary

MEG 1st round Nov CP idea was recommended at $590 for C3 and $580-585 for C4. At the time of writing, Nov C3 CP was traded at $603 and C4 at $598. Nov/Dec C3 was traded at $10 contango. Discussions for evenly split cargo has improved from mid high teens to low mid teens to CP, with Indian and Indonesia players out for spot cargos. Qatar Petroleum was reported to sell a mid Nov lifting evenly split cargo at 50% FEI 50% CP link price to a Chinese major.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium for prompt delivery market improved further this week with prompt buying demand emerging. 23kt propane for 2h Nov delivery was changed hand at flattish level to Nov FEI. And similar parcel for 1h Dec delivery was bid at Dec FEI plus $6 while offer heard at plus high single digit. PERTAMINA tendered to buy two evenly split cargo for Dec delivery, in either FOB AG or CFR basis. IOC was reported to buy two similar cargos for Nov and Dec liftings ex AG. Chinese players are also actively seeking cargos for Nov and Dec delivery. One EChina PDH player issued prompt tender to secure a late Nov delivered 23kt propane, which was reported concluded at Nov CP plus 50s. One NChina PDH user was said to cover a 46kt propane for 2h Nov delivery at similar price. One new PDH player in Schina purchased a 10-25th Dec delivery 46kt propane at Dec CP plus 50s reportedly. Another EChina cracker issued tender to procure a 1h Dec delivered 46kt propane into Ningbo. Apart from spot discussion, negotiations for term 2023 are ongoing. We shall expect more players petchem users call term tenders to secure next year supply, especially for heavy propane feedstock.
