## Market Commentary

MEG Mar CP edged up this week and traded at $782 at the time of writing, and C3/C4 was traded at flattish level. Backwardation into Apr narrow down to $32. A few FOB AG cargoes were changing hand recently. One evenly split cargo for late Feb/early Mar lifting was sold at Mar CP minus mid-teens reportedly. One end Feb lifting full propane cargo was reported to sell at CP minus high single digit.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium improved slightly this week. 23kt propane parcel for 2h Mar arrival was reported traded at FEI plus mid-20s. And 1h Mar value were assessed higher due to the previous delays caused by fog and Panama congestion. However petchem demand are not very satisfying. PDH margins eroded as end products failing to catch up the rise on the costs. Several PDH was said to delay restart after TAR or lower down run rates to cut loss. One SChina PDH was reported to shut down for one month maintenance due to glitch. This week only one NChina PDH user secured 46kt C3 for Mar delivery reportedly. Another EChina PDH user is exploring 23kt propane parcel. Taiwan FPCC issued buy tender to purchase flexible ratio for 23kt or 46kt LPG for 2h Mar delivery.
