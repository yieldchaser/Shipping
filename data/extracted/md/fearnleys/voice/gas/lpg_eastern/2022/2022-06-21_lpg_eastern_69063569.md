## Market Commentary

MEG Oil tumbled amid fears over recession and swap market is full of uncertainties. Both Jul C3 and C4 were traded below $700 but rebounded on Thurs at $707. Saudi Aramco released the July acceptance with some advanced liftings reported, and it was said to have several spot cargos for sale, but will be less compared to Jun spot availabilities. One trader is bidding last decade Jul lifting evenly split cargo at Jul CP minus $15 but no offers in window. 1st round CP idea for C3 ranged from $695 to $710, and C4 at $5-10 differentials to C3.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium strengthened this week as less sellers outstanding. 23kt propane for 2h Jul delivery was traded from Jul FEI flat to plus $4. And similar cargo for 1h Aug delivery was offered at Aug FEI plus $5 and 2h Aug at flat. Off window a handful of cargos been placed and sentiment improved despite the volatility in swap market. At least one 23/23 cargo for 2h Jul delivery were changed hand between traders, likely into NEA market. One 9-13th Jul lifting ex Australia, heavy butane cargo was placed into Japan and Korea reportedly. One SEA importer was said to purchase a heavy propane cargo for 2h Jul delivery, and it was said to have bought one heavy butane cargo previously for late Jun/early Jul arrival. One Korean trader procured a 16-25th Jul delivered 23kt propane at Jul FEI plus low mid single digit via tender reportedly. And one NChina PDH issued private tender to buy a July arrival full cargo propane, and the result is yet to know at the time of writing.
