## Market Commentary

MEG Another week of volatility, Apr CP C3 plunged to $835 from last week’s peak. Apr C4 maintained at $20 higher than C3, and Apr/May CP was still traded at $38 backwardation. At the time of writing, market players are waiting Saudi acceptance and worrying if any delays occur. Some Apr lifting FOB deals were concluded this week reportedly. One early Apr lifting heavy propane FOB was changed hand at CP minus mid high teens, while another mid Apr lifting evenly split FOB was done at CP minus mid high single digit.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials softened this week as of limited buying sentiments for pure propane, partly due to China PDH’s refrained purchases. In window one 46kt propane for 2h Apr delivery was concluded at Apr FEI plus $38, and 23kt parcel was bid at Apr FEI plus around $40. Demand for butane cargos improved this week, especially from India and SEA regions. One 3/1 ratio cargo for Apr delivery was placed into Thailand. And some other players are still exploring evenly split cargos for Apr delivery. Other than this, Taiwan CPC issued buy tender to procure 22kt propane for 21-30th Apr delivery. One Korean imported tendered to buy 23kt propane for end Apr delivery. One SEA importer is reported to buy 46kt propane for 2h Apr delivery at CP plus 20s for PDH use. One Ningbo-based cracker bought a 2h Apr delivered full propane cargo at FEI plus mid-40s reportedly.
