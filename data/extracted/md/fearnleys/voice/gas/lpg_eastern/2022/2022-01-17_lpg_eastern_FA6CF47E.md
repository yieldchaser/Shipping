## Market Commentary

MEG Earlier this week Feb C3 CP stabilized around $740 only, despite the crude rally. And C3/C4 spread enlarged to $9 from last week’s $3, reflecting the ample supply on the front market amid lukewarm demand especially on evenly split ratio. A couple of cargos in such grades for early Feb lifting were available for sale from some AG term lifters. However, on Thursday Feb C3 CP was traded up to $748 under a prompt support from cracking demand. Saudi Aramco released their acceptance with some cargos reported shifted to Ras Tanura loading as Yanbu jetty might have a planned turnaround from 2h Feb. Qatar Petroleum was said to sell two mid Feb lifting FOB cargos, and the price assessed to be equivalent to CP minus 20s.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium for Feb arrival cargos have softened this week, as most petchem players have secured their feedstock via tenders in the last two weeks, and traders covered their shorts. 2h Feb discussion is muted and most focus shifted into Mar arrival market. 23kt propane parcel for 2h Mar delivery was traded at Mar FEI plus $16 off window reportedly, while similar cargo for 1h Mar period was bid at similar level against offer at low 20s on FEI link. Off window buying inquires appear thinner compared to last week but still we are still seeing petchem users emerging to cover alternative feedstock demand under a better economy. Two PDH users have secured in total 69kt C3 for 2h Feb delivery, with one at Feb CP plus 30s for full cargo parcel into NChina and the other at Feb FEI plus less $20 for half cargo parcel into SChina. One Taiwanese cracker was rumoured to procure some Feb arrival cargos for cracking at decent discounts to MOPJ promptly. And Taiwan CPC also issued tender to buy 11/11 parcel for 19-28 delivery. One Korean importer was reported that didn’t award their buy tender for either 23kt C3 or 23kt C4 during 2H Feb delivery as offers not attractive for cracking yet. Another Korean cracking user also issued buy tender for 23/0 or 0/23 or 23/23 during end Feb to early Mar delivery.
