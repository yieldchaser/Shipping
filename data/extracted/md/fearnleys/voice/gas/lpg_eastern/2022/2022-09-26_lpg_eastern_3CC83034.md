## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco cut Oct propane CP by $60 to $590 and butane CP by $70 to $560, which is higher than swap value but amid the recommendation range. Given ample supply against limited demand especially for butane heavy cargos, Nov C3 CP was traded at $585. But some players believe that the flat price will rebound later with the winter demand anticipation, and slightly fewer spots available from middle east producers for Oct liftings.

**FAR EAST**

 With the APPEC in SG, activities are limited this week. 23kt propane for 2h Oct delivery was changed hand at Oct FEI minus mid-single digit, and similar cargo for 1h Nov delivery was bid at Nov FEI minus high single digit. Outside window a couple of 1h Nov delivered 46kt propane cargos were traded at Nov FEI minus low-mid teens. PDH margins improved as of the decline in feedstock costs, however downstream propylene and PP price remained steady or weakened a bit. Several PDHs, which are off for maintenance earlier, have resumed operation from last week. Hence the feedstock demand for Nov delivery are expected to increase significantly. One NChina PDH user was said to procure a propane parcel for 2h Oct to 1h Nov delivery. Two other Chinese PDH players have also tendered to buy similar parcel for 1h Nov and mid Nov delivery respectively.
