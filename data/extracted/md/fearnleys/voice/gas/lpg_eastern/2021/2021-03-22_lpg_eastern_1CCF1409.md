## Market Commentary

MEG 1st round CP recommendation ideas ranged from $560-$595 for propane, and butane at $30-40 less. At the time of writing, Apr CP was traded at $570, and backwardation into May enlarged further to $47. FOB discussions are still under deep discounts, amid sluggish demand for evenly split cargo, against the rising freight. Qatar Petroleum was reported to sell their third Apr lifting FOB, which is a full cargo propane for late Apr loading.

**FAR EAST**

 Active bids in the window this week. One 23k propane parcel for 2h Apr delivery was traded at Apr CP plus $2, equates to Apr FEI plus high 20s. And there are still other buyers bidding similar parcels for such period delivery. Meanwhile, same cargo for 1h May delivery was bid at May FEI plus low 20s. Off window we’ve seen a handful of cargos being placed into China. One EChina petchem user awarded their buy tender to secure 4 lots of 23/0 parcels for Apr to Jun delivery. And following this, another NChina major importer secured one to two heavy propane cargos via tender for May delivery. We expect more petchem buying interests will emerge after several PDHs completing their turnarounds. One SChina importer also issued buy tender for one 11/11 parcel for May delivery. Other than this, Indonesia PERTAMINA tendered to buy one evenly split cargo for early May lifting ex AG or alternatively for mid/2h May delivery.
