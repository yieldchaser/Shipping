## Market Commentary

ASIA Traders and end-users are trickling back to their desks after ushering in the year of the Ox. Western suppliers and traders are hoping this will spark some buying, which has been slow to materialize despite what appears to be low inventory throughout the region. Maybe it is the high absolute levels, (although low as a percentage of Brent), or the hope of increased Middle East availability based on the Saudis recent announcement, or the belief that US Tons have nowhere else to go, but whatever the reason, the lack of buying interest has definitely caused a few sellers to reexamine their thinking. Whatever the reason what now seems clear is that exports from the US to Asia will be reduced at the current arb levels.
