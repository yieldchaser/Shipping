## Market Commentary

MEG Feb Propane CP has soared up to $596 following the crude jump, but Mar CP was traded at heavy backwardation of over $40. As Saudi pledged to cut oil output, Feb CP gained more support with expectations on potential shortage of propane. Meanwhile, Feb C3/C4 enlarged to $30, as propane is more favorable than butane in winter season. Qatar Petroleum issued tender to sell 24-25th Feb lifting equal ratio cargo.
