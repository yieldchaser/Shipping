## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco released their Apr liftings with some cuts and shortage on propane reported. Apr C3 CP was traded at $584, while C4 CP at $25 less, reflecting the length on butane. Apr/May CP backwardation enlarged to nearly $40, foreseeing more supply out of AG onwards. Despite the Saudi’s shortage on propane, a plenty of Apr Fobs are available from other AG producers. KPC issued tender to sell its late Apr lifting 2/2 split cargo, previously it was reported to award its Q2 sale tender of three similar ratio cargos. Qatar Petroleum also issued two tenders this week to market one full propane and one evenly split cargo for Apr lifting.

**FAR EAST**

 Buying sentiments for Apr delivery slightly improved this week, amid potential delays ex US and the rebound on petchem demand. Propane parcel for 2h Apr delivery was bid at Apr FEI plus high teens. Off window several parcels was placed into Japan at similar level, and one sold into EChina at Apr plus 30s with deviation counted. However, Apr FEI/CP was still in negative territory, traded even lower this week at minus 30s, partly due to the anticipation on higher CP as of Saudi cuts. Apart from this, several buy tenders are running this week. Taiwan FPC secured another 22kt butane at Apr MOPJ minus mid-40s via tender. One Schina PDH user also awarded their buy tender for 23k propane in May delivery into Dongguan. Another EChina PDH user issued a buy tender to purchase 23kt propane delivered into Ningbo monthly from Apr to Jun.
