## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco set Oct CP at $800 for propane and $795 for butane, up by $135 and $130 respectively, in view of tight gas supply against strong winter demand. This being said, the value is higher than paper swaps ever traded and beyond most players’ expectations. Nov CP was traded at $815 at the time of writing, reflecting a bullish outlook into forward markets. Evenly split cargo for Nov lifting was traded into India at CP plus low single digit premium reportedly.

**FAR EAST**

 A plenty of deals were concluded this week in window, trading period ranging from 2h Oct delivery into next Mar. 23kt propane for 2h Oct delivery was traded at Oct FEI minus $10 and 46kt parcel was at minus $14. 23kt lot for 1h Nov delivery was traded at Oct FEI minus $2, equates to Nov FEI minus $7. And similar parcel for 2h Mar delivery was traded at fixed price of $818, while Mar FEI was assessed at $742.5. Off window a handful of tenders were under discussion. One EChina Petchem player awarded their buy tender and secured two parcels of 23kt propane for Nov delivery at Nov FEI minus low teens reportedly. One Korean importer was reported to buy 23kt butane for 1h Nov delivery via tender at FEI minus mid-single digit. One Japanese importer also issued buy tender to secure 6 or upto 12 lots of 23kt propane for monthly delivery in 2022.
