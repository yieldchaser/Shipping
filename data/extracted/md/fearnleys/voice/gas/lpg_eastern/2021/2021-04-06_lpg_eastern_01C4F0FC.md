## Market Commentary

MEG May CP was traded at $509, and backwardation into June was assessed at $17. May C3/C4 spread narrowed down to $17 from previous $30, as the summer demand recovering for Butane. Netbacks for May FOBs are improving due to the delays on US supply against better demand driven by petchem sector. KPC was reported to sell one 4/5 May lifting heavy propane cargo at May CP minus 10s. Previously it sold one end Apr lifting evenly split cargo to one NChina petchem importer via tender.

**FAR EAST**

 A plenty of bids in window this week has propped up the cash premium for May delivery cargos. Bids on 23k propane parcel for 1h May delivery were edging up to May FEI plus $28, however no offers seen yet. Similar cargo for 2h May delivery was bid at May CP plus $40, which equates to May FEI plus $20, and offered at May FEI plus mid-20s. 1H/2H spread is assessed at $10 or more, caused by the delays on Panama canal under foggy weather. Off window buying sentiment is also active. One EChina petchem user managed to secure a 23k C3 parcel for later May delivery at May CP plus low 40s reportedly via tender. However, another SChina PDH user who aimed for similar cargo for early May delivery, didn’t award the tender due to high price charged for prompt delivery. Taiwan CPC also issued tender to secure one 11/11 parcel for May delivery and the result is yet to know. Other than this, Indian IOC tendered to purchase one evenly split cargo for mid/2h May delivery after quieting down for months.
