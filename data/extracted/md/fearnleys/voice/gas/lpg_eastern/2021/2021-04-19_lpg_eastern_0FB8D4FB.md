## Market Commentary

MEG Flat price is volatile this week. May propane CP rebounded to $485 and butane at $20 less. Earlier this week, May/June CP spread narrowed down to $3 but quickly enlarged to $7 by the time of writing. Saudi Aramco released their May acceptance with no cuts reported, but supplier is said to have rich butane supply as previous months. KPC was reported to sell their late May lifting heavy propane cargo at Jun CP minus around $10. And following this, QP was said to award their sale tender for late May loading full propane.

**FAR EAST**

 Off window a handful of deals were concluded this week. 23kt Propane for 2h May delivery were traded actively, ranged from May FEI plus mid to high teens, and cash premium is expected to edge further high. One reason being is the prompt cracking demand due to the improved economy on FEI/MOPJ. Earlier this week it went to minus $80 and several petchem users have emerged to procure LPG for cracking feedstock. However the spread narrowed down quickly to minus low 50s afterwards. Taiwan FPCC procured one full cargo propane for late May delivery at May MOPJ minus low 50s via tender. And they’ve also issued term tender to secure seven to twelve cargos for June to Dec 2021 delivery or July 2021 to June 2022 delivery. Another Chinese Major also issued buy tender to secure 12 cargos for July 2021 to June 2022 delivery.
