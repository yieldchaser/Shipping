## Market Commentary

MEG Another week of volatility. Nov Propane swap retreated to $810ish in the mid-week from previous $850ish, but rebounded to $840ish at the time of writing. Nov Propane/Butane spread was narrowed to $26 from last week’s $38 as more inquiries on evenly split cargo emerged. Nov/Dec CP was traded at $2 contango only.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium for Nov delivered parcels improved amid active trades this week. 23kt propane for 1h Nov delivery in window were traded at FEI minus $18 earlier this week, while bids for similar cargo on 2h Nov delivery improved to minus $10 from previous minus mid-teens range. Full cargo for such period was bid at FEI minus $12 while offer at minus $8. Off window a plenty of tenders were floating in the market. On inquiry for propane, three PDH users in China issued buy tenders and secured in total 92kt propane for Nov delivery, with some reported done at FEI minus low-mid teens. One Vietnam PDH also purchased 46kt propane for Nov delivery at Nov CP plus mid 20s reportedly. On evenly split cargo, two Indian PSUs issued buy tenders for one 22/22 cargo for Dec delivery each. One SChina importer also tendered to buy a 11/11 parcel for 2h Nov to 1h Dec delivery.
