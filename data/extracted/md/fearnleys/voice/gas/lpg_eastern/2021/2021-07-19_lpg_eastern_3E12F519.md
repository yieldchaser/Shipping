## Market Commentary

MEG Following the crude movement, Aug C3 CP retreated to $633 but later rebounded to $646, and C4 was traded at $8-10 less. Aug/Sep CP was traded to flattish at the time of writing, reflecting the uncertainties on forward market length. Saudi Aramco was reported to have cut some volumes on their Aug acceptances, mainly for Indian clients.

**FAR EAST**

 A handful of deals were concluded in window this week. 23k propane for 2h Aug delivery was traded at Aug FEI plus $1 to $1.75, but the cash premium was softening as most buyers have completed Aug program, coupled with the soaring flat price on Aug FEI and CP. Despite the significant build in US propane inventory, MB stands firm and therefore FEI/CP further widened to $30 with concerns on the shortage supply on Sep arrival cargos. Off window spot discussions are muted into China as of sluggish domestic residential market in summer season. One SChina PDH buyer tendered to buy monthly 22.5k propane delivered into Dongguan from Oct 2021 to Feb 2022. Meanwhile, One Korean importer issued prompt tender to purchase 23k propane for 21-27th Aug delivered into Daesan.
