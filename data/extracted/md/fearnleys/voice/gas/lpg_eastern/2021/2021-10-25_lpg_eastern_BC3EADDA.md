## Market Commentary

MEG Nov C3 swap value dropped heavily to $830 on the time of writing, down by nearly $30 compared to last week, trigged by the decline in crude and the plunge in China DCE-LPG market. 1st round CP idea ranged widely from lowest to $835 to highest $860 for C3, and C3/C4 spread was recommended from flattish to $20.

**FAR EAST**

 A flurry of deals was concluded in the window this week, with majority for 2h Nov deliveries. 23kt propane for this period was traded at Nov FEI minus mid-single digit, or at flat price $845 on Thursday, and similar cargo for 1h Dec delivery was at $844 on the same day. Three Chinese importers were reported to award some term volumes for next year feedstock use, with 23kt parcel reported done at CP plus low mid 40s into SChina, and 46kt parcel reported at partial FEI plus near $10 and partial CP at 30ish into NChina, which to be confirmed. Another two Chinese importers also issued term tenders to secure next year supply. One SChina traditional importer tendered to buy 11/11 split monthly from Jan to Dec, the other EChina-Based new petchem player tendered to buy 9 to 10 parcels of 23kt propane monthly from Mar/Apr to Dec. One Australia stem were reported to split into two 11/11 parcels and sold into Japan at a discount to Dec FEI for mid/2h Dec delivery.
