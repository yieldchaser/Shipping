## Market Commentary

**MEG:** CP swap fluctuates this week, followed the volatile crude amid bearish market. July CP was traded at $323, slashed by over $20. A plenty of FOB cargos are offered at discounted level yet to attract much interests. Qatar Petroleum was said to sell one early Jul lifting 2/2 cargo at Jul CP minus mid-20s. KPC has closed their sale tender for a 3/1 loading 9/10th July with the result remaining sketchy. ADNOC released their July lifting schedule with several liftings being advanced and is also reported to have spot availability.

**FAR EAST**

: A bunch of tenders flowing in the market. Indonesia Pertamina have issued both term and spot tenders this week. The term tender explores two equal ratio cargo monthly on CFR basis from Jan to Dec 2021. While the spot tender inquires for two equal ratio FOBs ex US from 25th June to 10th Jul by fixed price. Taiwan FPCC awarded their buy tender to secure 22kt butane for 2h July delivery at Jul MOPJ minus low 40s. Taiwan CPC also tendered to buy 11/11 for 16/25th Jul delivery. Propane for Jul delivery are traded under downward pressure with limited buyers emerged. Earlier this week one 2h Jul arrival full cargo propane was reported concluded at Jul FEI minus $10. During mid-week, 23kt Propane for 1h Jul delivery was traded in window at Jul FEI minus $10.5 and on Thursday seller offer further down at minus 13.5 but not concluded. One SChina PDH bought 23k Propane for last decade Jul delivery at Jul CP plus mid-single digit last Friday, while the other EChina PDH user bot similar cargo at MB pricing plus 80ish.
