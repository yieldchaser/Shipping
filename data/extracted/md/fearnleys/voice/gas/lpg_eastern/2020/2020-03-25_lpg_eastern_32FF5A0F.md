## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Swap prices are on a roller coaster ride this week again. Apr C3 CP has plunged to 150ish mid-week and rebounded later to $200, with C3/C4 traded flattish. Apr/May CP was once in small contango but quickly returned to backwarded structure when Apr CP surging up. 1st round CP recommended idea varied significantly, ranging from $150-$250 reportedly. Some players do not favor a super-duper cheap CP to cause huge inventory loss. One trading house issued semi-term buy tender for one equal ratio cargo monthly from Jun to Dec lifting.

**FAR EAST**

: Despite the bearish number and non-economic ARB, sentiment is gradually turning positive. Extra spot volumes out of AG are slowly being absorbed into East. A handful of deals are concluded this week. 23kt propane parcels for 1h May delivery have been traded at May FEI minus $2 to $4. We think some are advance purchases with the anticipation of tighten supply in 2h May CFR market, as 8 to 10 Apr liftings ex US were reportedly cancelled. Chinese importers are also actively buying 2H Apr to May delivered cargos lately, with some deals concluded at the premium over $40 and few equal ratio buyers remaining. One SChina PDH user issued tender to buy 22kt C3 for 8-16th May delivery. Other PDH users are also inquiring for May arrival propane for feedstocks. Taiwan CPC issued buy tender to secure one 11/11 delivered during 3st to 12rd May, and the tender is still in validity. Indonesia tendered promptly to buy one equal ratio cargo for 19-21 Apr delivery, and the result is yet to know.
