## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Swap values edged up this week, following the crude jump with anticipation on production cuts. May CP was traded at $260ish, however refrained gains later this week amid uncertainties. As expected, C3/C4 spread has enlarged to $20, due to the robust prompt demand from India. FOB discussions varied widely as bid idea at small premium while seller targets at double digits. The sole support on premium lies in the consistency of India purchase, which remains a question for forwarding months.

**FAR EAST**

: Earlier this week 23kt propane for 1h May delivery was traded at May FEI plus $13, which equates to May CP plus $20. Despite active bids at FEI plus teens in window, sellers are keener to sell cargos into China. As FEI/CP spread narrowed down within $10 only whilst landed price into China for May arrival at May CP plus low 40ish, weakening but still more economic. Several Indian tenders were awarded with CFR price ranging from CP plus 40s to 80s reportedly, relating on exact laycan/laytime requests and other conditions. This week BPCL issued buy tender again to secure another 90k tones for May to early June delivery. Indonesia Pertamina was reported to award one evenly split cargo for May delivery in last tender, and this week issued new tender for another cargo in June delivery. Deals into China recently are mainly driven by petchem feedstock demand. One Schina-based PDH closed their tender to secure 23kt propane delivered in last decade May. Meanwhile, at the time of writing, two more PDH users have tendered to buy total up-to two full propane cargos from mid-May to 1h June delivery.
