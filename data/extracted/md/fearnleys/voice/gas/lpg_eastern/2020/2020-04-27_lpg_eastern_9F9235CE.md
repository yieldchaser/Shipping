## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi raised up May CP for propane by $110 to $340, and for Butane by $100 to $340, which in line with some recommendation idea. That being said, the value is higher than paper swap. June CP is traded at $277 at the time of writing, which is $63 less than May CP. Apparently the anticipation on cuts from AG exports is unable to offset concerns over demand loss. One evenly split FOB for late May lifting ex Kuwait was offered at $336 for both propane and butane, while another June lifting ex QP was offered at $275 for propane and $245 for butane.

**FAR EAST**

: Earlier this week one full propane cargo for 1h June was traded at June CP plus $27. However, the premium discussing level quickly reduced to teens as of ample supply in 1h June arrival. Despite the narrowing arbitrage, Us tons keep sailing to east, especially for equal ratio cargos. China spot demand is mainly driven by petchem this week. One EChina PDH didn’t award its buy tender for propane delivered in 1st decade June due to program changes. Another SChina PDH purchased one 23k propane parcel for mid-June delivery at June CP plus mid low 40s via tender. One NChina PDH of 600k/y capacity is still offline till mid May and yet to purchase any June spot. Apart from spot discussion, one China Major importer issued term tender to secure one cargo per month from July 20 to June 20, ratio to be evenly split or heavy propane.
