## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Despite the weak sentiment on physical purchases, Jul CP rebounded to $337 this week, with the anticipation on Saudi cuts. Aug CP is traded at $4 less, still in backwarded structure. One early July lifting full propane was offered at mid-teens discounts to July CP, and two 1st decade Jul lifting equal ratio cargos were offered at minus high single digit on Thurs. Both are yet to trigger buying interests as netback value assessed with 20ish discounts at least. KPC issued sale tender to market a 12/13 Jul lifting 3/1 cargo, which is reported awarded at Jul CP minus 20s.

**FAR EAST**

: A couple of deals were concluded in the window, with 23kt propane for 1h Jul delivery traded at Jul FEI minus $16, and 2h Jul done at minus $8.88. Off window discussions for Jul arrival tons were muted as in lack of buyers. We are expecting several July unsold cargos to roll into Aug, with $5 contango on Jul/Aug FEI against low floating costs. Taiwan CPC was reported to purchase one 11/11 for 2h Jul delivery at Jul CP plus mid high single digits via tender, following one similar cargo was reported sold into China at Jul CP minus. A Chinese Major issued annual term tender to secure one full propane for monthly delivery from Oct 2020 to Sep 2021, which is due to close by Friday.
