## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco has set Mar CP at $430 for Propane and $480 for Butane, down by 15% and 12% respectively compared to Feb. April CP tumbled to 330s after Mar CP set, but edged up afterwards and at the moment traded at $370, with Butane at $45 higher. Whether April CP can continuously stand firm remains a question, as some Chinese has acquired tariff exemption to import US origin cargos. Qatar Petroleum has released April acceptance with over two weeks’ delay being reported. One early April equal ratio cargo is said to change hand at April CP plus small premium.

**FAR EAST**

: Active window this week with plenty of bids for April delivery propane. One 23kt propane for 2h April delivery was traded at Apr FEI plus $19, equivalence at Apr CP plus $16. 1H April appears short as delays caused by foggy weather, and some sellers are holding back till have clear picture on shipping program. Biggest news this week is Chinese has started applied for US tariff exemptions and at least 12 major players in China have already granted on such approval. We expect the discussing premium level to come off as of flexible import origin and less origin swap demand, except for freight saving otherwise. Importers especially PDH users have started to inquire for April propane, which exerts the tightness on 1h April supply. One SChina PDH has bought 23k propane for mid-April delivery at Apr CP plus high 50s reportedly, and one EChina PDH tendered to buy 23k propane for early April delivery, which tender is yet to close. Indonesia Pertamina has awarded buy tender for 22/22 mid-April delivery heard at April CP plus low 40s, and India IOC also tendered to buy two parcels of 22/22 for April delivery.
