## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Swap price is riding a roller coaster, as new year starts with uncertainties between Iran and US. Earlier this week, Feb CP spiked up to $565 but rapidly plunged to $505 on Thurs, following the crude. Qatar Petroleum has released Feb acceptances with delays reported from 5 to 12 days. Since most lifters nominated for early Feb laycan to avoid heavy backwardations, which assessed at $50 at the time of writing. QP has tendered to sell a 24/27th Feb lifting full propane cargo, which due at 14th Jan. Other than this, limited FOB offers were seen, as lifters concerning acceptances for Adnoc and Saudi. The FEB FOB value ranges from small discounts to teens premium, subject to the loading laycan and ratio.

**FAR EAST**

: A handful of propane delivered in Feb into Japan/Korea are concluded this week. Premium starts higher early this week, with deals traded at Feb FEI plus 53$ for 1H Feb and $45 for 2h Feb, later declined to $40 mid-week. Despite the softening premium, Far East market still appears strong with MB/FEI arb opens wide, which props up the discussions on terminal fee premium for US fob liftings. Moreover, continued tightness in Non-Us origin supply has buoyed up market sentiments. Several Chinese PDHs have carried out or going to have maintenance, partly due to negative margins and partly in lack of feedstocks. Offers for equal ratio cargos during 2h Feb delivery into China were heard at Feb CP plus 90s, whilst propane offers barely seen. Indian HPCL has a term inquiry for 6 plus 3 optional equal ratio cargoes for 2020, which is reportedly still under negotiation. BPCL has issued buy tender to secure 800kt 50/50 ratio cargos from Mar 2020 to 2021, close by 20th Jan. Indonesia PERTAMINA issued buy tender to secure two equal ratio cargos delivered in mid FEB several times, with nothing being awarded reportedly.
