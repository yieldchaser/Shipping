## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco finally released acceptance with 7 cargos being cut for May lifting reportedly, which in line with expectation as oil cut in motion. Despite US crude turned negative during this week, May Propane CP stands firm and edges up to $320, with butane at $10 less. With front strength, May/June CP backwardation enlarged once to $40 and narrowed to mid-30s on Thursday. June CP might experience a decline after May CP set. However, due to the volume cuts from AG producers and potential India residential demand, June CP swap shall still gain support fundamentally.

**FAR EAST**

: Limited activities this week before May CP set. Active bids in window but few deals are concluded. 23kt C3 for 1h June delivery were bid at June FEI plus high teens or June CP plus high 20s. This is yet to attract sellers’ interests, who are keener to ship tons into China instead. One Korean petchem player was reported awarded their buy tender for 23kt propane delivered in end May to early June. This week a couple of June arrival propane were placed into China at June CP plus 40s. PDHs plants still enjoy a considerable margin, but the weakening end products price VS rising costs are eroding the margins. Most plants are still operating at high rates, apart from two N.China units are offline due to glitches this week.
