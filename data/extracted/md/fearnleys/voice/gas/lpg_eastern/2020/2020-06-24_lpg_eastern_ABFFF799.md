## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Despite the crude rally and fall, Jul CP swap stabilized due to the coming CP announcement. Jul CP was traded at $341 but backwardation into Aug widened to $15 from previous $5. 1st round CP ideas varied widely. Majority recommend at $340-350 for C3 and $15-20 less for C4, with high end at $370 for both grades. Qatar Petroleum was said to sell one end July lifting full propane at CP minus $25. One early Jul lifting full propane was offered at $320 on Thursday, equates to $20 discounts to CP but yet to be traded.

**FAR EAST**

: Residential demand is still sluggish due to summer season, whereas cracking demand slowly recovered with a couple of butane parcels have been absorbed into Korea. One Korean petchem user secured an end Jul delivered 2/2 with propane at Jul FEI minus $10 and butane at Jul Mopj minus 50s via tender reportedly. Other than this, one full propane for 1h Aug delivery was placed into NChina for PDH feedstock. Indonesia PERTAMINA issued buy tender to purchase one equal ratio cargo for mid Jul lifting ex US or to be delivered during end Aug, which is closed by this Friday. PERTAMINA had bought one early Jul lifting US 2/2 in its last tender.
