## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco raised up June CP Propane at $350 by $10 and lowered down Butane by $10 to $330, which was amongst the recommendation range. Afterwards Jul CP has rapidly surged up to $345 from last week’s $305, with the concerns over escalating China-US tensions. Qatar Petroleum has released July loading acceptance with some liftings being advanced, as lifters tend to nominate later loading to avoid cargo landing in bearish July market. It was reported that KPC failed to award their 1-2 Jul lifting 4/0 cargo with bids received at CP minus 20ish or more.

**FAR EAST**

: Limited activities this week as in lack of buying inquiries. 23kt propane for 1h Jul delivery were traded at Jul FEI minus $5, while 2h Jul with small contango at minus $2. Asian Importers are still under high inventory and barely have appetite for 1h Jul arrival cargos. Only one EChina PDH user shows interest to take a parcel of 23kt propane for such period. One Chinese Major has sold a full cargo propane for 1h Jul into Tianjin at Jul FEI plus small digits. One SChina PDH user issued buy tender to secure 23kt propane for last decade July delivery, which is still in validity with abundant offers received.
