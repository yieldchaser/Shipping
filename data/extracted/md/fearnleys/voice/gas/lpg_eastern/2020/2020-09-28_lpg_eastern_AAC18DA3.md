## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco set Oct Propane CP at $375, up by $10 and Butane at $380, up by $25. Such number is in line with most recommendations. Nov and Dec CP are traded at healthy contango structure, with expectation of better winter demand. QP sold its 24/25 lifting full propane cargo, at the level of Nov FEI minus high 40s reportedly. India BPCL is reported to purchase one Nov delivery evenly split cargo at Nov CP minus $8, much higher than other spot discussion on double digit discounts for Nov liftings.

**FAR EAST**

 Front market gains momentum due to the US cargos delays caused by panama congestion. Propane for 1h Nov delivery was bid up and one full cargo was reportedly traded at Nov FEI minus mid high single digit. One Korean petchem user secured a parcel of 23k propane for end Oct/early Nov delivery at flattish to Nov FEI. Before the coming Golden Week and mid-autumn festival in China, importers are actively inquiring for heavy propane cargos for Nov delivery. Future players on DCE also closed their Nov contract position before holiday and some rolled positions into forward months, actively trading FEI intermonth spread simultaneously.
