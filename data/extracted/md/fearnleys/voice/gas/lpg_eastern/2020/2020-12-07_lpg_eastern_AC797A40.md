## Market Commentary

MEG Jan Propane CP is traded at $458, ticking up by $12 this week. However, butane price is deteriorating as plenty of evenly split cargos were shown in the market this week. At the time of writing, Butane CP is traded at $3 lower than Propane, and we expect it to go further lower as of tepid demand. Qatar Petroleum is reported sold one late Jan lifting evenly split cargo, and it also tendered to sell another equal ratio cargo for mid Jan lifting. Meanwhile, one similar cargo for 10/11 Jan lifting was sold but yet to hear details.

**FAR EAST**

 We’ve seen prompt demand for 2h Dec delivery cargos this week, and some prompt swap inquires due to the latest Panama delays. Half cargo propane for Jan delivery was discussed at Jan FEI plus teens level, but cash premiums softens a bit as foreseeing more cargos loaded ex US shall sail to east after the Panama backlog cleared. The freight is soaring up due to short availabilities on vessels, either east or west, and Jan FEI/CP spread expands widest at $60 and now at $50, compared with last week’s 30ish range. One EChina PDH importer tender to secure one 4/0 for early Feb delivery into Ningbo. Other than this, Chinese buyers refrain from buying interests and would like to wait before taking further moves.
