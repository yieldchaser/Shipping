**FAR EAST**

: Freight is soaring and FEI/CP has enlarged wildly from last week’s minus $3 to $32! We’ve seen one bid for 23/0 2h Dec delivery at $500 in window with no sellers showing up. Off window, one Jan delivery full propane was reported sold at Jan FEI plus low single digit earlier this week. But other sellers with Jan delivery cargos are holding back positions, seeing freight is surging up and expecting premium coming up further. Indonesia Pertamina issued tender to purchase one end Dec lifting AG 22/22 or alternatively for mid Jan delivery. One SChina PDH user awarded buy tender to secure 5 parcels of 22k propane monthly delivery from Feb to May 2021 at CP plus high 20s level. There are two more Petchem players are tendering for full propane as feedstock for next year delivery, and one more traditional importer is exploring equal ratio cargos via term tender as well.
