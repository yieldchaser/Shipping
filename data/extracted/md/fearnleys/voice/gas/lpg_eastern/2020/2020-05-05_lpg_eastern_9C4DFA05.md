## Market Commentary

**MEG:** June Propane swap is traded at $290, slightly rebounded however yet to rise further as in lack of support fundamentally. The slowdown of India and Indonesia demand has exerted pressure on butane price, which swap is traded at $35 less than propane. Qatar Petroleum has released June lifting acceptance with no cuts but some minor delays. And they’ve issued sale tender to market one 18/20th June lifting equal ratio cargo. FOB with same ratio but for end May/early Jun lifting was offered at small discount to June CP but yet to attract bids.

**FAR EAST**

: A slow start in Golden week holiday. 1h June arrival propane was bid at June CP plus mid low teens or June FEI plus mid-single digit, yet to entice sellers’ interests. On the contrary, a handful of equal ratio cargos including US origin which will land in June, are struggling to find home. Chinese importers have finalized 1h June program and now exploring for 2h June arrival tons, propane heavy mainly, driven by petchem demand. One NChina PDH has restarted its unit of 600k/y capacity today, which was offline for last two weeks. China imports has slashed by 21% in Q1 with average monthly import below 1.3 mmt. However, import in April has recovered to 1.5mmt (VLGC tracking) and May also expects with a slight increment. With two new PDHs of total capacity over 1mmt coming online in June, we expect from June onwards there will be additional 100k feedstock demand monthly at least.
