## Market Commentary

MEG Thin discussions for evenly split cargos this week and C3/C4 spread narrowed to $3. Oct C3 CP was assessed at $570, Oct/Nov Cp was traded at $15 dollars contango. Qatar Petroleum was reported to sell a 6-11 Oct lifting full propane cargo on FEI netback. Freight is soaring due to tight fleet and FEI/CP enlarged to $110s from last weeks’ $70s.

**FAR EAST**

 Inquiries in window are muted this week as market participants are attending APPEC and Gastech meetings. Off window 23kt propane parcel was bid at Oct FEI plus 3-4 while offered at mid high single digit. Apart from such, 46kt propane for 1h Oct delivery were changed hand between traders at Oct FEI plus mid single digit basis NChina reportedly. A couple of tenders were reported cancelled due to high offers, one is for 23/0 parcel for late Sep/early Oct delivery into Qinzhou, while the other for 11/11 delivered into Shenzhen during Oct. One Cracker user issued tender to buy 22kt or 44kt LPG with butane ratio for Oct delivery. However other Petchem users are hesitant to inquire Oct feedstock, with some considering rate cuts or extend planned turnaround durations. China PP market revived this week as into demanding season, coupled with the impact of stimulus plan on property market over the weekend.
