## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi released the May acceptance with most reflected as per nominated, and limited impact was seen on Opec cuts for now. Qatar Petroleum was reported to sell their end May lifting heavy propane cargo at May CP minus high 20s. And discussions for evenly split cargo softened this week with bid discounts at CP minus 30s range. At the time of writing, May C3 CP was traded at $558, and C4 CP was traded at $3 less, further narrowed down from last week.

**FAR EAST**

 Quiet window this week as most players have done with May program. 23kt propane for 2h May delivery was bid at May FEI flat and similar parcel for 1h Jun delivery was bid at Jun FEI minus low single digit. Off window more sales tenders emerged this week, especially for evenly split cargos. One Australia stem for end May lifting was reported sold into Japan for mid Jun delivery. And a couple more for May delivery were also placed into China. Last CPC tender for late May delivered 11/11 was reported traded at CP plus 70s, whereas other evenly split parcel into Schina were at CP plus around 40 or less. Apart from split ratio, 46kt propane parcel for 2h May delivery was sold into China at some discounts to FEI reportedly, and believed there are still some room for petchem plants to procure additional cargos. Taiwan FPC awarded their buy term tender and secured monthly 44kt propane from July 2023 to Jun 2024 delivery. And one Chinese Major also tendered to secure monthly or quarterly 45kt LPG for same delivery period, which still under discussion at the time of writing.
