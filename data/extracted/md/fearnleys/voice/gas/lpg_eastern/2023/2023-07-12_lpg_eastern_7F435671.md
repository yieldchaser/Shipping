## Market Commentary

MEG FOB differentials improved further this week especially for evenly split ratio. Qatar Petroleum was reported to sell two FOB cargos for 6-8th Aug lifting via last week’s tender, with one said to be 22/22 concluded at Aug CP minus mid-single digit. KPC was also said to award their 4-5th Aug 22/22 sale tender at similar level. Off window similar grade was also traded at CP minus small discounts. Apart from such, QP also tender to sell a 15-17th Aug lifting full propane. Aug C3 surged to $460 and C3/C4 narrowed to $5-8. Aug/Sep CP was assessed at $20 contango.

**FAR EAST**

 In contrary to FOB AG, cash differentials for Aug delivery LPG softened and dived deep on Thurs window. Offers for 23/0 parcel on 1h Aug delivery declined to Aug FEI minus $9.5 and similar parcel for 2h Aug at minus $1, whilst no bids for 1h Aug seen and 2h Aug bid at minus $3 only. 11/11 parcel for 1h Aug delivery was offered at Aug FEI minus low teens. Off window 46kt propane parcels for 2h Aug delivery basis NChina and Echina were traded at Aug FEI plus mid single and flat respectively. Quite a handful of buy tenders floating in market this week, especially for butane heavy grade. One Schina importer withdrew their butane buy tender for 11-22 Aug delivery due to no offers reportedly. Taiwan CPC also issued similar tender for 2h Aug delivery, yet to know the result. One SChina cracker user issued tender for Aug delivery LPG in flexible ratio. One oil major covered a butane related cargo for 2h Jul to early Aug delivery into Malaysia at CP plus 20s. other than spot inquires, BPCL issued annual term tender to secure 42 cargos of 22/22 for 2024 delivery on FOB or CIF basis. Additionally, several PDH users emerged to secure propane feedstocks. One NChina PDH procured one and half cargo propane for Aug delivery at FEI plus low mid single digit from last tender, and still has additional demand for one more Aug delivery propane. One SChina based PDH also purchased a 2h Aug delivered full propane cargo at Aug CP plus 60s. A few other petchem players are still in wait and see mood amid the eroding margins and high inventory.
