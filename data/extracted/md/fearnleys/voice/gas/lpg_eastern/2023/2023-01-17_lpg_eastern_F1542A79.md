## Market Commentary

MEG ADNOC and Saudi have announced their Feb lifting acceptance with some cuts in exports reportedly due to the planned maintenance. As of the decline on MEG supply, the Feb C3 CP surged up to $688 with C4 traded at $5 higher. Feb/Mar CP backwardation also enlarged further to $41. Last but not least, Feb CP/FEI widened from last week’s minus $3.5 to minus $17 at the time of writing. Freight have dropped significantly with less liftings ex MEG and more fleets available to relet.

**FAR EAST**

 Concerning the delays on Feb arrival cargos, caused by the unpredicted fog, sentiment in window remain strong, especially traders bidding up to cover positions. 23kt propane for 2h Feb delivery was bid at Feb FEI plus $75 and similar parcel for 1h Mar delivery was at Mar FEI plus $50. As lunar new year holiday coming near, activities are scarce except a few tenders running in the market. Off window one Malaysia producer was reported to award their sale tender for a evenly split cargo for 21-23th Feb lifting ex Tanjung Sulong. And one NChina PDH issued buy tender to secure a propane cargo for Mar delivery.
