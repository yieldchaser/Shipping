## Market Commentary

MEG CP recommendation was reported at $555 for C3 and C4 at flattish or $5 cheaper, and it is expected to be announced by this Sunday. At the time of writing, May C3 CP was traded at $554 and C4 at $551. Jun CP was traded at $6 backwardation to May. Discounts for evenly split cargo lifting in Jun were assessed at minus 20s to CP.

**FAR EAST**

 Quite thin discussion in window this week. 23kt propane parcel for 1h Jun delivery was bid at FEI plus $4 while offer at plus $9, and similar parcel for 2h Jun delivery at FEI bid at flat vs offer at $4.5, reflecting the front tightness caused by the Panama congestion delays. Differ from the quiet window, prior to holiday next week, off window a handful of buy tenders were floating in the market, and some even for prompt May delivery. Through tenders, 46kt propane parcel for 1h Jun delivery into East and North China were reported done at FEI plus high single to low teens level, while into South China at Jun CP plus high 30s. And there are still a couple of tenders seeking for May and June delivery propane ongoing, for petchem use. Butane wise, one oil major also secured via tender for a 2h May delivery evenly split cargo into Malaysia. Apart from spot tenders, last week FPC and one Chinese major both managed to cover their term demand on delivery basis. The former one was reported to secure monthly 46kt propane at MOPJ minus 70s, while the latter one at FEI plus low mid single digit for monthly 46kt LPG at different ratio subject to delivery period. And there is another PDH user also tendered to buy monthly 46kt propane for 2H of the year delivery.
