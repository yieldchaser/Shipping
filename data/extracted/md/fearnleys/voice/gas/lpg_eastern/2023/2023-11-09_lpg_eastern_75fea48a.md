## Market Commentary

MEG While we are waiting for Dec lifting acceptance, we’ve seen Dec CP rose from $600 to $616. Dec C4 was assessed at $12 higher, reflecting the better demand on evenly split cargos driven mainly by India and Indonesia residential segments. And it’s believed that the premium over Dec loadings will stand firm as Pertamina issued tender to buy two evenly split cargos for 16-25 Dec liftings ex AG or CFR during 1-10 Jan. Dec/Jan CP was traded in $10 backwardation. There are rumors circulating on one AG producer might carry out maintenance during Q1 but the exact timeline not clear yet.

**FAR EAST**

 The recent Panama transit restrictions have disrupted the delivery program and lifters or owners are diverting the vessels for other routes. The increased ton miles and rising auction fees undoubtedly support the FEI value, hence widen the arbitrage. Due to negative margins, a bunch of PDHs users are advancing maintenance or cutting run rates, which resulted in cargo resales or book-out for propane delivered in 2h Nov to 1h Dec. This being said, the cargos have been well absorbed, to traders covering shorts, who faced with cargo delays; and to Japanese or Korean importers for stockpiling on winter demand. With the rising feedstock costs eroding margins, we might not expect any active spot buying inquiries from China petchem plants. Such might prevail until Panama situation eased or when lifters have clear program with less uncertainties. Nevertheless, the petchem players are issuing term tender to secure term supply for next year, with concerns over volatility on spot market. Apart from above, a handful of 23kt propane for 2h Dec delivery changed hand at Dec FEI plus $2-3, softening from earlier at $5-6 as of limited buying interest.
