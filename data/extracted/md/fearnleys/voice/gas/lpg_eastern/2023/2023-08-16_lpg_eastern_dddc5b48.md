## Market Commentary

MEG Following the crude, Sep C3 was traded lower this week at $517 and C4 at $5 higher than C3 by the time of writing. Sep/Oct CP enlarged rapidly from Monday’s flattish to $13 contango on Thursday, partly reflecting the softening front as several buyers have covered their demand for evenly split cargos from AG producer’s trading arm, which reported at Sep CP plus high 20s-30s range.

**FAR EAST**

 Premium for 23kt propane on 2h Sep delivery fluctuates. Bids softened to low single digit in window on thur, while similar parcels was concluded at FEI plus $5-6 earlier this week , following the volatile time spread. One Oil Major issued sale tender for a mid Sep lifting evenly split cargo from Dampier and result is yet to know by the time of writing. And Taiwan FPC withdrew their buy tender for an 22/22 cargo during 20-30 Sep delivery, as offers too high. Buying interests from other petchem units are muted owing to the squeezed margins. Several China PDHs shut down due to glitches or annual scheduled turnarounds, and some expected to extend the duration in view of the poor margins.
