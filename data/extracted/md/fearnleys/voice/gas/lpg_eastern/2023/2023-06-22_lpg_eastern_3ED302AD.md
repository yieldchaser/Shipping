## Market Commentary

MEG At the time of writing, Saudi Aramco hasn’t announced Jul CP. 1st round Jul CP was recommended at $420-430 for C3 and $400-410 for C4, both lower than curve value. QP was reported to award their last tender for both 3/1 and 2/2 ratio on 17-21st Jul lifting, yet to hear details. A producer trading shop is fixing vessel for their 5th Jul lifting cargos, who reckoned less loadings than previously in Jun but still lead to lengthy supply.

**FAR EAST**

 Bids for 2h Jul 23kt propane parcels are active and cash premium edged up from flat to mid single digit to Jul FEI. With the soaring freight, costs plus deviations on half cargo climbs, along with traders bidding in window to cover shorts. A couple of 46kt propane parcels for 2h Jul delivery were changed hand, reported at FEI minus high single digit basis Chiba and plus low mid single digit into North/East China. Similar cargo for 1h Aug delivery was also said to concluded at FEI minus high single digit basis Chiba. Indian BPCL was reported to award their Aug buy tender for evenly split cargo at Aug CP plus high 20s at CFR basis from one producer trading shop. And the seller was said to place a similar grade cargo for 1h Jul delivery into China at Jul CP plus mid teens, and still have a or some more cargo with butane available for sale. A few tenders were floating this week, however most have been cancelled due to limited and high offers. Only one Chinese player was reported to procure 11/11 for Jul delivery at Jul CP plus mid high 20s via tender. It’s believed that these petchem buyers will have to secure the feedstock for Aug delivery again, especially for those who have skipped Jul purchases. As several PDHs returning from maintenance, coupled with new PDHs commissioning in Jul/Aug, we expect general operating rates to grow stably given current marginal profits. However, it is noted that some Chinese funds eye on the Propane/PP profits hence doubted if sustainable. Jul and Aug FEI/CP widened rapidly from last week’s $43 to $73, effected by the combination of strong FEI bids against weakening CP, surging freight, and the unpredictable Panama congestion delays.
