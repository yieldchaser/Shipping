## Market Commentary

MEG Qatar Petroleum was said to award two cargos, one 22/22 and the other 3/1, through their sale tender for 2-5th Jul lifting, at CP minus high 30s reportedly. At the time of writing, Jul CP was traded at $446 for C3 and C4 at $20 less, as of plentiful butane supply in the front market. Jul/Aug CP was traded at $10 contango.

**FAR EAST**

 Premium for 1h July delivery propane edged up this week. 23kt propane parcel for such period was traded at Jul FEI plus low teens while similar cargo for 2h Jul done at plus $3 on Thurs window. Off window a handful of 46kt propane parcels for 2h Jul delivery were placed at Jul FEI plus low mid single digit into North and East China. And one EChina PDH issued tender to procure upto 69kt propane for Jul delivery. Other PDHs users are expected to come out for July/Aug procurement as well. However the high inventory and slow consumption refrain them from speculative buying or stockpiling. Taiwan CPC issued tender to buy 22-31th Jul delivered 11/11 parcel yet to hear result. One Korean petchem importer issued term tender to procure monthly 3 lots of propane from Sep 2023 to Jun 2024 delivery.
