## Market Commentary

MEG AG supply still appears tight with Indian PSUs asking for optional cargos. Evenly split cargo was bid at Oct lifting at CP plus $25 but no offers seen in window. At the time of writing, Oct C3 CP was traded at $570 and C4 at $5 higher. Oct/Nov C3 widened further to $16.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium for 23kt propane parcels softened for Oct delivery. Earlier such parcel for 2h Oct delivery was changed hand at Oct FEI plus $5, however offered down to flattish level on Thurs. Similar parcel for 1h Nov delivery was offered at Nov FEI minus $4. The Oct FEI/CP enlarged to $120, highest surpassed $130, partly owing to the freight, has refrained the buying interest. Off window a handful of tenders floating. One EChina PDH user secured 23kt propane parcel at Oct FEI plus low teens reportedly. One Korean player also tendered to buy 23kt butane for 2h Oct delivery for cracking. One heavy propane cargo of Australia origin for 2h Oct delivery was reported sold into Japan at FEI plus mid-high teens. Another oil major also tendered to procure evenly split cargo for Oct lifting or Oct delivery into Port Klang. FPC also tenders to buy Oct delivery LPG at flexible ratio, max 23kt for each grade. China PDHs are still reluctant to move as negative margins on soaring feedstock price, with some adjusting rates or plan to advance scheduled maintenance. Despite such, several new PDHs are still expected to start up in Q4 with indispensable feedstock demand.
