## Market Commentary

MEG Premium for butane grade softened as a few evenly split cargo shorts covered, which earlier reported dealt or offered at CP plus 30s for Sep lifting but bid interest subdued to mid-teens only this week. Sep C3 CP was traded at $535 and C4 at flattish level. However forward market still appears strong especially with one Indian major inquiring for Nov and Dec butane cargos. Sep/Oct C3 CP was traded at $17, also reflecting the coming winter demand. Producers are said to have delayed some butane loadings, resulted by the increased liftings into India with optional cargos, plus max tolerance.

**FAR EAST**

 Limited activities in window this week, 23kt propane parcels for 2h Sep delivery was traded at Sep FEI flattish level reportedly. Similar parcel for 1h Oct delivery was bid at Oct FEI minus low-mid single yet to attract offers, especially with uncertainty in Panama transits on skyrocketing costs. Off window a handful of propane for Sep delivery were changed hand between traders, indirectly placed into China, at Sep FEI plus mid/high single digit basis EChina or mid teens basis NChina. One SChina cracker covered a 2h Sep delivered 46kt propane at an average of Sep CP and Oct FEI plus 40s via tender. Another EChina cracker secured 23kt propane at Sep FEI plus low single digit for late Sep delivery basis Ningbo. One NChina PDH player called tender to procure two full propane cargos for 2h Sep and 1h Oct delivery, respectively. Similarly, one Vietnam based PDH user also tendered to secure propane for 2h Sep to 1st decade Oct delivery. Other than spot inquiries, one SChina based PDH user tendered for monthly 23/0 from Oct to Mar delivery which due to close on this Friday.
