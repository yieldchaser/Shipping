## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco set Oct CP at $600 for C3 and $615 for C4, up by $50 and $55 respectively. This is higher than curve value and players’ expectations. This being said, the strong crude, robust demand against tight supply on AG FOBs, together with the high FEI/CP spread all lend supports to the CP. Additionally, premium for evenly split cargos for Oct liftings was assessed earlier at Oct CP plus 40s, especially with Indian majors emerged for spot inquiries continuously. At the time of writing, Nov CP was traded at $606.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium inched up this week on 23/0 for both 2h Oct and 1h Nov delivery in window. Such parcel was bid at Oct FEI plus $5 for 2h Oct, and Nov FEI plus $2 for 1h Nov. Outside window some 46/0 parcels for 1h Nov delivery were changed hand at price reported at Nov FEI plus high single digit basis NChina. Buying inquires for feedstock are active this week. FPC awarded their buy tender for mid Nov delivery full propane cargo at Nov FEI plus mid-single digit into Ningbo, and their 600kt/y PDH unit is expected to do trial run soonest. One Vietnam based PDH user reported to buy Nov delivery propane at Nov CP plus $100s, while the other SChina PDH user cancelled similar tender due to high offers. One NChina PDH managed to secure a Nov delivered full propane cargo at Nov FEI plus high single digit reportedly. Another EChina cracker also reported have bought 23kt propane for 1st decade Nov arrival into Ningbo. Apart from propane, Indian IOC retendered to procure in total 4 evenly split cargos for Nov and Dec delivery, after they failed to award their last one.
