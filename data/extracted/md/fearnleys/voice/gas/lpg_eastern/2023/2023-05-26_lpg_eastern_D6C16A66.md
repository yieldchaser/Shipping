## Market Commentary

**MEG:** KPC was reported to award their sale tender for end Jun lifting 3/1 ratio at Jun CP minus low 40s. And there are a couple of late Jun and July lifting evenly split cargos were offered via window. Given the soaring freight, players are hesitant to take FOB positions and the FOB netback is much lower than recent tender levels. Jun C3 CP was traded at $497 at the time of writing, and C4 CP at $10 less. Jun/Jul CP was traded at $8 backwardation at the moment.

**FAR EAST**

: Cash premium surged up for 23/0 delivered in 2h June, and a few traders covered such shorts at Jun FEI plus mid-teens, which mostly from Japan or Korean importers’ resales. Similar cargo for 1h Jul delivery was bid at Jul FEI plus $2, yet to entice interest. 46/0 for 1h Jul delivery was reported changed hand at Jul FEI minus small digits basis Chiba, or high single digit basis Ningbo. One Korean importer was said to procure two propane cargos for 2h Jun and 1h Jul delivery, respectively. Another Korean importer also tendered to buy 23kt butane for 6-12 Jul delivery. One evenly split cargo was said placed into SChina at Jun FEI flat by a producer trading arm. Petchem margins eroded as downstream consumption demand yet to recover, hence concerns over the PDH run rate cuts and demand for Jul delivery looming in the market. Some PDH was said to resell their Jun arrival propane, amid high inventories even.
