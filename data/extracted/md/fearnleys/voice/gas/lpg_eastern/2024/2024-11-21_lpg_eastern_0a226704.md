## Market Commentary

MEG Spot differentials for Dec FOBs edged lower, assessed at Dec CP minus small single digit for evenly split grade, as in lack of buying interest. Saudi Aramco released their Dec lifting acceptance, with some reported to be cut on propane tolerance and some evenly split ratio changed to heavy butane. This is quite surprising as Nov butane was restricted on plus tolerance. Qatar Energy tendered to sell an evenly split cargo for 19 to 22 Dec lifting. With such additional butane length, Dec C4 quickly fell and Dec C3/C4 traded at $13 vs last week’s $4.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium improved as cargo length slowly been absorbed. Despite the slim buying interests from China, a couple of 46/0 parcels for 2h Dec delivery basis Chiba were changed hand at Dec FEI minus mid-single digit. And in the window 23/0 parcel for 2h Dec delivery was bid at $1.5 to Dec FEI while offer at $3.5. Similar parcel for 1h Jan delivery was offered at $7.5 to Jan FEI. Dec FEI/CP was relatively volatile, traded from minus $12.5 on Monday and narrowed down to minus $3.5 by the time of writing. HTC tendered to buy 23/0 for last decade Jan delivery but result remains sketchy. One NChina PDH issued buy term tender for 46/0 monthly delivery into Tianjin. Despite the weakening flat price, China PDH overall run rates still at below 65%, due to scheduled maintenance and unexpected glitch. We expect rate to be improved by end Nov, as a couple of PDHs will resume operation. Other than petchem, disappointingly residential demand yet to rebound in China, and the market is overwhelmed with a handful of unsold special origin stems for Dec delivery.
