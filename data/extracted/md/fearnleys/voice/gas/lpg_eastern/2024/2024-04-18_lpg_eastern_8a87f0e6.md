## Market Commentary

MEG Fob discounts for May liftings further deepened as of ample supply amid limited demand, especially evenly split cargos. KPC was reported to sell their mid May lifting heavy propane cargo at May Cp minus mid high 30s. Saudi Aramco released their May lifting acceptance and its trading company is fixing vessels for early May loadings. May C3 Cp was traded at $584 and C4 at $588, May/Jun Cp was traded at $20 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 Unlike the Fobs, premium on 23kt propane parcel for 2h May delivery was bid up to May FEI plus high single digit. And similar parcel for 1H June was bid at Jun FEI plus $7, while May/Jun FEI traded at $6. A handful of tenders were floating again this week. One NChina PDH user awarded their buy tender for 46/0 2h May delivery at May FEI plus low teens reportedly. One SChina PDH also secured similar parcel at the mixture of FEI and Cp plus teens level. There are another two tenders ongoing for propane delivered into Hainan and Tianjin in 2H May to 1H Jun periods. One SChina cracker also issued two tenders for April and May LPG at flexible ratio but were reported canceled, perhaps ended up taking in special origin cargos, which was reported done at May Cp minus level for prompt delivery and mid high single level for May delivery. Pertamina tendered to buy evenly split cargo for 8-12 Jun delivery. IOC issued mini term tender to secure similar ratio cargo for May to Jul delivery. HTC also called term tender to procure 23kt propane from Jul 2024 to Jun 2025 delivery.
