## Market Commentary

MEG Spot differentials for evenly split grade edged down to flattish level for Dec lifting. KPC was reported to sell two cargos via tender at CP plus flattish to low-mid single digit, for early Dec and end Dec lifting respectively. And BPCL was also reported to award their buy tender for FOB Dec at similar level. By the time of writing, Dec C3 CP was traded at $620 and C3/C4 widened to $4.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials fell further for Dec cargos, amidst limited demand. 23/0 parcels for 1h Dec was bid at Dec FEI plus $3 while similar parcels for 2h Dec were changed hand at Dec FEI flattish to small premium. Dec FEI/CP has fell into negative territory at minus $3 by the time of writing. Despite the decline of propane flat price, propane buying interests are thin in view of bearish downstream consumptions. One PDH user secured 46/0 for 2h Dec delivery into NChina at Dec FEI minus low mid single, with 8 offers received reportedly. Another Schina importer awarded their buy tender for 46/0 delivery during 3-31 Dec at Dec FEI minus $3.5, which equates to Dec CP minus mid-single digit at the time of trading. Formosa issued buy tender for 46/0 2h Dec delivery into Ningbo, expects with good discounts. CPC also tendered to buy 11/11 or 2/2 for end Dec/early Jan delivery. Apart from spot discussion, two Chinese importers were reported to procure some 46/0 parcels for next year at FEI plus mid high single digit bss Ningbo and Tianjin respectively.
