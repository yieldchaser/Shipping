## Market Commentary

MEG FOB strength extended further this week, with IOC keep re-tendering buy tender for monthly evenly split cargo in Q4. Assessment for such ratio is at CP plus mid-teens to $20s but barely see offers. Thanks to the robust demand, Oct CP barely moved despite the crude loses. By the time of writing, Oct C3 CP was traded at $602 and C4 was assessed at $5 higher than C3.

**FAR EAST**

 With the APPEC conference ongoing, market players kicked off next year term negotiation, with the concerns over limited supply expansion against foreseeable demand increment. Petchem players addressed the worries on the resilient LPG feedstock price and olefins oversupply weighing on slow downstream consumptions, whilst traders are sensitive on next year petchem performance. In terms of spot discussion, Oct FEI swap softened this week, alongside the crude losses and Oct FEI/CP narrowed down from $37 to $23. In the window we’ve seen bid for 2h Oct 23/0 basis Chiba at Oct FEI plus $5.5 whereas offer at $8.5. Outside window a flurry of buy tenders ongoing. Indonesia PERTAMINA tendered to buy 2/2 or 1/1 parcel for 6-25 Oct delivery. CPC also sought 1/1 for last decade Oct delivery via tender. Other than butane, one NChina PDH also issued buy tender to secure 46/0 for 2h Oct delivery and we’ve seen a couple inquire the same privately.
