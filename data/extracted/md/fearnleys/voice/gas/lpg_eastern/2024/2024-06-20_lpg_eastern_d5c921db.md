## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco released their Jul FOB acceptance with max 0% tolerance on propane as of tightness, but butane remains in ample supply. Qatar Petroleum issued tender to sell 22/23 Jul lifting heavy propane cargo. AGT also offered in window for Jul FOB sale, asking Jul C3 CP minus $5 for C3 and Jul C3 CP minus $35 for C4. Eveny split cargo for Jul lifting was assessed at Jul CP minus low teens but in lack of discussions this week. At the time of writing, Jul C3 CP was traded at $585, up by $16. Jul C3/C4 enlarged to $20 and Jul/Aug C3 was traded at $5 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premiums softened further this week as of weakening freight and subdued buying interests. A couple of 23/0 parcels for 2h Jul delivery were traded at Jul FEI plus $7.5 this week but Thursday's premium declined further to $6. Outside the window a handful of tenders were awarded this week, however, volume wise less than expected as a couple of buy tenders were only awarded in 23kt lot rather than 46kt lot. Flat price has surged up with Jul FEI traded at $636, up by $26 WOW. The increasing costs and squeezed margins have suppressed some buying interests. FPC was reported to award an Aug delivered 2/2 cargo at Jul MOPJ minus hi 50s into Mailiao, which equates to Jul FEI flattish level or Aug FEI plus low-mid single. It’s also reported to procure 23/0 for 2h Jul delivery into Ningbo at Jul FEI plus high single. Similarly, one SChina PDH user secured only 23/0 for a similar range into Qinzhou at Jul CP plus low 60s. Another 2/2 cargo for Aug delivery was also traded into Schina at the level of Aug FEI minus hi single or equates to Aug CP plus low 40s reportedly. Indonesia Pertamina also called a tender to secure one 2/2 for late Aug delivery.
