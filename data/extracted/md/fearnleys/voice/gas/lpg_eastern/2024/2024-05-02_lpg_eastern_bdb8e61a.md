## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco cut May CP to $580 for C3 and $585 for C4, down by $35 each. The price is in line with expectation, but the announcement time is earlier than what most expected. As of the drops in flat price and attractive discounts on FOBs, we are seeing buying demand from Indian major recent. HPCL tendered to buy two evenly split cargos for 2h May and Jun liftings ex AG and was reported to award on CFR basis to a producer trading arm, with netback at CP minus mid high teens reportedly. At the time of writing, Jun CP was traded at $545 and C3/C4 turned into positive at $5. Jun/Jul CP was traded at $7 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 Active bids for 23kt C3 parcel, especially for 2h Jun delivery into FAR EAST. On Thur, such parcel for 1h Jun delivery was bid at June FEI plus $7 and similar parcel for 2h delivery bid at plus $6, whereas few offers seen. 11/11 or 0/23 for 2h Jun delivery (including Us origins ) were offered at Jun FEI plus $5.5. Off window discussions slow down as of the ongoing labor day holiday. However before the holiday, a handful of 46kt C3 were already placed into China at Jun FEI plus low teens basis Ningbo, and a 23kt C3 parcel also traded via tender at similar price. With the new PDH expansion and the marginal but positive profits we expect more feedstock inquiries after the holiday for 2h Jun arrival onwards. The China latest PDH run rates have improved close to 70%, with some units returned from maintenance and new startups. Apart from PDHs, cracking demand continued its strength, we’ve seen FEI/MOPJ spread in minus 90s and 80s for May and June respectively. One Korean importer issued term tenders to secure 23kt butane for monthly delivery from Aug 2024 to Jan 2025 (6 lots) or to Jul 2025 (12 lots), and 23/23 or 23kt butane for Feb 2025 to Jan 2026 delivery.
