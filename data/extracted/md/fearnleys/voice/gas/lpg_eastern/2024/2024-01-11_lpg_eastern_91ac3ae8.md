## Market Commentary

MEG Discussions on evenly split cargo for Feb lifting softened this week with offer heard at CP minus mid-single digit. A Feb FOB was rumored to change hand at CP minus 20, which is yet to be confirmed. At the time of writing, Feb C3 CP was traded at $578 and C4 at $10 higher. Feb/Mar CP was traded at -7$ backwardation. ADNOC released their term lifting schedule with no cuts heard but some got advanced. And Qatar Petroleum tendered to sell a late Feb lifting 22/22 cargo.

**FAR EAST**

 The decline in flat price finally enticed the buying interests. A flurry of tenders from China have been floating in the market this week. At least five lots of 46kt propane have been sold into China at the range of Feb FEI minus high single to low teens level basis East to NChina. There is another buy tender for Feb propane into SChina ongoing and we are expecting at least one more PDH player to procure Feb feedstock. As Feb FEI/CP narrowing down, the CFR east value is in equivalent to CP plus around $20 only, which is more favored now compared to Iranian cargo with less price advantages. In addition to the Chinese purchase, several traders were bidding in window for 23kt propane parcel basis Chiba of 2h Feb delivery at Feb FEI minus mid single digit. Apart from spot tenders, the drastic drop in freight have also attracted the attention on term discussions. We’ve seen three term buy tenders, with one inquiring for monthly 46kt propane from Feb to Jun delivery into NChina, one for monthly 23kt or 46kt propane from this Mar to next year Mar delivery into SChina, and the last for monthly 11/11 from Mar to Dec delivery basis SChina also.
