## Market Commentary

MEG Despite limited inquiries this week, cash premium for Nov lifting evenly split cargo maintained at CP plus $18-20. Saudi Aramco was said to cut the plus 5% tolerance on butane for Nov lifting while no changes for propane. At the time of writing, Nov C3 CP was traded at $628, and C3/C4 was traded at $7.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials softened further this week with a couple of 46/0 for 2h Nov delivery offered in window at Nov FEI plus low single digit basis Yantai or minus low single basis Chiba, but no bids seen. 23/0 parcel for 1h Dec delivery was bid at Dec FEI plus $3.5 while offered at $5.5. Outside window a handful of 46/0 for 1h Dec delivery was placed into EChina and NChina at Nov FEI plus mid single digit. At the same time, a flurry of tenders was concluded this week. Taiwan Formosa was reported to procure a 23/0 into Mailiao at Nov MOPJ minus teens, and a 46/0 into Ningbo at Nov FEI flattish, both for Nov delivery respectively. Huayi was reported to procure two parcels through their tender for 10 Nov to 1h Dec delivery, with one at Nov CP plus mid high 20s. Inpex was reported to sell via tender, a 3/1 for 11/12 Nov lifting on CFR Chiba basis at Nov FEI plus mid high single digit to a trader. Another 23kt butane for early Dec lifting ex Port Bonython was offered via Santo tender this week. Other than normal stems, there are 4 Iranian cargos available for Nov delivery, offered via tender, among which 2 for heavy propane and 2 evenly split ratios. Last tender for heavy propane delivered in Nov was reported concluded at Nov CP plus low teens. However since Nov FEI/CP narrowed to $25, plus limited buying appetites, we believe the differential will have to decline significantly to award.
