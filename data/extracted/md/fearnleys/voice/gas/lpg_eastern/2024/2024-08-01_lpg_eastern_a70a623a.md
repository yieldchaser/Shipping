## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco raised Aug propane CP by $10 to $590 and butane CP by $5 to $570 respectively. Despite the heated discussions on evenly split cargo, Aug C3/C4 widened from $15 to $20, partially due to Saudi’s tight supply on propane in August. However, it was said that AG producers might have more propane available for Sep liftings and Sep C3/C4 narrowed down to $8. BPCL was reported to awarded one or two Sep lifting evenly split cargo at Sep CP plus mid high single digit. At the time of writing, Sep C3 CP was traded at $597 and Sep/Oct was traded at $5 contango.

**FAR EAST**

: 23kt propane parcel for 1h Sep delivery basis Chiba was bid at Sep FEI plus $6 while similar cargo for 2h Sep delivery bid at plus $3. 46kt full lot basis Tianjin was bid at $5 yet to see offers in window. Nevertheless, off window a handful of propane cargos for early/1h Sep delivery were concluded into China, with price ranging from FEI plus high single to low teens based on different locations. One EChina importer reckoned current premium higher than expectation hence cancelled their buy tender of 46/0 for 1h Sep dleivery, while another Echina cracker user still issued buy tender for 4/0 des Ningbo 6-15 Sep. despite the eroded margins, we are seeing some PDHs plan to restart up in Aug, in the hope of stronger downstream demand in coming Sep and Oct. Apart from propane cargos, premium over butane related cargos also remained strong, regardless the softening freight. One Australia stem of evenly split cargo for 8-22th Sep delivery basis Chiba was heard sold at Sep FEI plus high single. However, a couple of tenders were cancelled reportedly due to high offers. PERTAMINA was said to have cancelled their buy tender for an evenly split cargo for 19/23 Sep delivery as offers asking Sep CP plus 40s. Similarly, one SChina player cancelled their buy tender for late Aug to late Sep delivery evenly split cargo. Other than normal stems, latest special origin cargo for late Aug delivery into SChina was heard done at Aug CP plus 40s, which barely have any price advantage over normal stems. As per our shiptrack, China July VLGC import reached record high hitting 3.8mmt, up by 14% MOM and 20% YOY. According to China Custom data, China import in 1H increased by 1.8 mmt YOY, and we believed the increment from 2H will be higher, due to new demand from the released of new capacity in petchem.
