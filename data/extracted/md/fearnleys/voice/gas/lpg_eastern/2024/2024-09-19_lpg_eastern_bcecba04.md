## Market Commentary

MEG Spot differentials for Oct evenly split fobs remained strong, due to the Indian continued robust demand for Q4 delivery. Such fob was assessed at Cp plus 20s but offers barely seen. We’ve hearing much higher number shown into Indian PSU tenders but yet to be awarded. By the time of writing, Oct C3 Cp was traded at $611 and C4 at $7-9 higher.

**FAR EAST**

 A handful of deals concluded in window this week, mainly trader shorts covered. Premium for 23kt C3 for 2h Oct delivery into far east softened, traded from FEI plus $5 earlier and declined to 3ish. 46kt lot for 2h Oct delivery basis Yantai was offered at Oct FEI plus $10 while similar parcel for 1H Nov delivery was bid at Nov FEI plus $6.5. Comparatively, propane demand from end user outside window is less active, mainly resulted by the squeezed margins. FIC awarded their buy tender for 4/0 delivered during 1-20 Oct basis Ningbo at Oct FEI plus high single. However Tianjin Bohai was said to cancel their buy tender for 4/0 2h Oct delivery as offer received at similar high single range. Well butane is another story. Taiwan CPC and SChina Sinobenny both issued buy tender for late Oct to early Nov delivery evenly split cargo respectively, which reported concluded at Cp plus $60s but details are sketchy. Other than spot inquiries, Huayi issued term buy tender to secure monthly 46kt C3 into Qinzhou in 2025. Pertamina called 2025 term buy tender for 2/2 split volumes. Equinor also tendered to buy 2/2 into Port Klang for 1H 2025 delivery.
