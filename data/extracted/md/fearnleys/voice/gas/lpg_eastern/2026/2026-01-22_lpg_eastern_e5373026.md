## Market Commentary

Spot differential for Feb lifting 2/2 split cargo was assessed at $20s to Feb CP, in lack of fresh deals. Indian HPCL was reported to have awarded one Mar FOB 2/2 ratio at Mar CP plus mid-single digit. With more US butane expected to flow into EAST in Mar, cash premium for AG Fobs has since softened. QatarEnergy also issued a sale tender for a 2/2 split loading 22-27 Feb. By the time of writing, Feb CP was assessed at $540 for C3 and $531 for C4. Feb/Mar C3 CP was assessed at $17 backwardation. US FOB resale prices were reportedly surged to 14ish cpg for late-February loading and 10 cpg for early March loading, underpinned by strong cash premiums into the Far East and softening freight rates. However notional netback to Asia fell sharply as Arbitrage to EAST narrowing, driven by higher LST prices due to the cold snap in US. On Thurs, Feb FEI/Lst plunged from $200s to $180s and Mar narrowed from $170s to $160s. Cash premium for Feb and 1h Mar delivery into FAR EAST remains firm, supported by extended front-end tightness due to US delays. On Thurs window, 23/0 parcel for 1h Mar delivery basis Chiba was bid at Mar FEI plus $49 while 2h Mar at plus $33. One 46/0 parcel for 2h Feb delivery basis Chiba was bid at Feb FEI plus $22. Outside window earlier this week 46/0 parcels for 1h Mar delivery were changed hand at Feb FEI plus high teens to low 20s reportedly. A flurry of buy tenders from Chinese players emerged this week, with most seeking feedstock amid insufficient term supply. While some were cancelled amid high or no offers, at least 250kt propane was concluded reportedly. Deals were heard at plus 80s on Non-Us stem, to Feb and Mar delivery month CP respectively; and Feb FEI plus low 30s for 1h Mar delivery on Us stems, which equates to Mar FEI plus around 60. General inventory in China remains low as only 2.5mmt LPG tracked for Jan delivery, which is around 500kt less than last Dec or monthly average in Y2025.
