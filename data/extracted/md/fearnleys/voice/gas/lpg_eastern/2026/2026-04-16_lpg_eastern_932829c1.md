## Market Commentary

Uncertainties over US-Iran talks, coupled with worsening supply disruptions after US blockade over SoH, continued to heighten market volatility. By the time of writing, May CP was traded at $680 for C3 and $720 for C4. Arbs crashed this week as of declining landed price against soaring freight. The FEI/Lst swap narrowed to $326 for May and $269 for Jun, down by roughly $50 and $30 respectively week on week. Terminal fee declined to mid 20s cpg to low 30s cpg for Jun lifting propane, however one end May evenly split cargo was reported traded at high 30s cpg, reflecting the butane shortage. U.S. propane/propylene inventories rose by 301,000 barrels to 77.9 million barrels in the week ended 10th Apr, leaving stocks remained 75.6% higher year on year. Window trades are relatively active this week, with 4 deals concluded for 23/0 parcels delivered in 2h May basis Chiba, at the price of May FEI plus $128/$130 or Balmo Apr (15-30) Apr FEI plus $11. Outside window, a number of tenders failed to conclude. FPCC procured one 46/0 for 2h May delivery into Mailiao via tender, which reported at May FEI plus around $130 for US stem. A Canada origin 46/0 for May delivery was reported sold into Schina at May FEI plus $190s. However, butane inquiries are difficult to cover with limited alternatives outside AG. Several tenders seeking evenly split or butane cargo for May/Jun delivery were reported cancelled due to a lack of offers.
