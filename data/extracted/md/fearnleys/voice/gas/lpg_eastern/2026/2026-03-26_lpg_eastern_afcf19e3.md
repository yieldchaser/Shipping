## Market Commentary

Heightened uncertainty and price volatility have dampened activities in both physical and paper market. Despite muted activities ex Middle East, bids for Apr lifting evenly split cargos ex Yanbu were seen at $830 and $840. 1st round CP recommendation ranged widely from $500s to $800s reportedly, reflecting significant market divergence. By the time of writing, Apr CP was assessed at $638 for C3 and $633 for C4. Spot terminal fees declined this week as of Us-Far East ARB narrowing down to $400s for Apr and $300s for May. Terminal fee for May liftings has fell sharply to 30s cpg, vs last week’s 50s cpg. U.S. propane/propylene inventories rose by 478,000 barrels to 73 million barrels in the week ended 20th Mar, leaving stocks remained 69% higher year on year. In the window, 23/0 parcel 1h May delivery was bid at Apr FEI plus $15, equates to around May FEI plus $107, while similar parcel for 2h May delivery was bid at May FEI plus $100. 4/0 parcel for 2h May delivery basis Ningbo was also bid at Apr FEI plus $15. Earlier this week, some 2/0 for 2h May delivery was reported traded at Apr FEI plus mid high teens. Outside window, Pertamina was reported to have procured a Australian 3/1 stem for Apr delivery. HTC was heard to award 23kt butane for 1st decade May delivery at Apr MOPJ link. FPCC also awarded their buy tender for one 4/0 in 2H May delivery. However, one NChina PDH player cancelled their propane buy tender for late Apr/1h May delivery as offers are higher than expected. Separately, bids from Indian players softened this week, as one Iranian cargo was reported sold into India market at CP plus $200s after US issued Iran General License for permitting the trade. Traders focused on Iranian market are holding off from forward purchase as monitoring the prospects of a US-Iran ceasefire.
