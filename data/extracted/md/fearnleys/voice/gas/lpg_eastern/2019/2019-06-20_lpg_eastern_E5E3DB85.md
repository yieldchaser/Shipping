## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco released their July acceptance over the weekend, with some Butane liftings being advanced. It is reported that the producer is offering equal ratio cargos for July lifting with ample Butane stock. July CP swaps remained flat most of the week, until Thursday afternoon when it rose to $360, shadowing the crude rebound. Not sure if freight has reached the ceiling at $80 but the continuous increment on shipping costs have squeezed the margins, more so when considering current weak demand from India and China. Buyer are asking mid-teens or even higher discounts on FOB.

**FAR EAST**

: Plenty of deals were concluded in the window during this week. 23kt propane for 2h July delivery was traded at Jul FEI plus 3-6$, which equates to July CP plus low mid 50s’. FEI/CP still maintains at 50ish, thanks to the soaring freight. Off window inquiries into China are quite sluggish, thus the spread between US and Non-US origin has been narrowing quickly. Apart from window deals, a handful of tenders are ongoing. SP Chemical awarded their buy tender to one Chinese major importer at July CP plus 60s for 23k propane, SP Chemicals are reported to be looking for another cargo for end July delivery, for use in their cracker. Following that, Dongguan Juzhengyuan was also reported to have secured Propane from the largest Chinese importer at July CP plus 50s. FPCC awarded their buy tender for 11/11 during 16-20th July delivery into Mailiao at July MOPJ minus mid 80s. Indonesia Pertamina has closed their term tender for 2 full cargos for delivery January to December 2020, result is yet to known. Having said this, the majority of spot demand is driven by petchem for Propane, buying interest from traditional importers is pretty slim, with only one Chinese importer reported to have purchased an 11/11 cargo at July CP plus around 50$.
