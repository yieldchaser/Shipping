## Market Commentary

**MEG:** The incident in Saudi Arabia over the weekend turned the whole market upside down. Tuesday late night Saudi Aramco finally released their October liftings acceptances, this was with only around two weeks delay being reported and minimal cuts heard, this soothed the market sentiment somewhat. October CP initially surged to $450, up by $100 compared to the previous Friday, only to later plunge to $400 after the acceptance announcement. We still believe that with the impact of such incident, October CP will hover above the $400 level. In addition, the prompt demand from especially India and Indonesia, means Butane undoubtedly will be maintained at $10-15 higher. At the time of writing, October CP is traded at $410. However, the front strength has slightly changed forward CP swaps into a backwardated structure. Qatar Petroleum awarded their mid October lifting of full Propane to a Chinese importer at October CP plus small premium.

**FAR EAST**

: The supply disruption in Saudi has helped support the US origin price. 23k Propane for 1h October delivery was traded at October FEI minus $15.5. A similar cargo for 2h October delivery is being bid at October FEI minus $12 versus offer at minus $5. With ample supply from the US, October FEI/CP has narrowed to a $5 spread, makes US origin tons even more attractive to Japan and Korean importers. The US-Feast arbitrage is still wide open, and players are keen to secure equal ratio cargoes ex US, either to cover shorts in own system into SEA, or for better optimization on cargo origin swap. Off window market, discussions was in a stalemate situation. VLGC freights are increasing due to friction in the MEG and potential increase in additional war risk premium etc. Meanwhile, some lifters are cautiously programing bearing in mind the Saudi delay in liftings and continually monitoring the loading schedules out of the MEG before taking further decisions. There are still some buying inquiries for October delivery tons, not only from China but also prompt demand from India and Indonesia. Some traditional importer still have room to take in middle October delivery Propane heavy cargoes, while the others are now exploring November delivery tons. Indonesia’s Pertamina issued another buy tender, this time to buy an even ratio cargo to be delivered into Teluk Semangka during flexible October dates which is closing this Friday with validity till following Monday. India BPCL has issued prompt buy tender for a 22/22 delivered during end Sep into early Oct arrival.
