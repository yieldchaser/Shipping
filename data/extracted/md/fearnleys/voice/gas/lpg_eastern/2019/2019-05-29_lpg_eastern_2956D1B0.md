## Market Commentary

**MEG:** The highlight of the week was the announcement of Saudi Contract Prices for June of $430 for Propane and $415 for Butane. At the time of announcement, this was more or less in line with expectations. However, as the week have moved on, it become obvious that there are/were substantial unsold FOB volumes still to be placed. These volumes are from producers and from traders as well Some of these have now been cleared but at large discounts to CP, ranging from high single digit to high teen’s. This price dive has been aided by the continued strength in the VLGC freights. Arriving in the office Monday morning, we were presented with a near contango situation for the balance of the year but this quickly changed whereby backwardation has once again reared it’s ugly head, especially so for Butane. It is reported that Iran LPG export volumes this month will reach 550 kt, or more.

**FAR EAST**

: It has not really been an active trading week, tough some deals were concluded. A WAF origin cargo was sold into East China for 1st half July arrival and a window deal for 23 kt c3 1st half June at June FEI plus 8 was done. These deals are more or less representative of the market developments this week. While these deals point to a CP netback of lower than minus 20, it should not surprise anyone that this downward pressure on CFR values more than likely will continue for some time. The realization that slowing increase in demand from the 2 biggest import markets, combined with continued strong Iranian exports and new volumes from US, Canada and Australia, mean that Eastern markets are very well supplied.
