## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Price shoots up this week amid robust demand. Jan CP has traded at $475, up by $30 WoW and butane is traded at $25 higher. ADNOC has announced their Jan lifting with 7-10 days delays reportedly, and some ratio being changed from equal ratio to heavy propane. Qatar Petroleum has issued a sale tender for a full propane loading 29/31th Jan, which ratio is very attractive to Chinese Petchem buyers. FOB discussions are active with India and Indonesia buy tenders flowing in the market. One late Jan lifting equal ratio cargo was reported traded at Jan CP minus small discount, as Jan/Feb CP falls into over $25 backwardation. Whilst other earlier liftings, which can make within Jan arrival, are assessed at the value of at least $5-10 premium.

**FAR EAST**

: Very heated market with bullish sentiment for whatever origins. 23kt propane for 2h Jan delivery have been traded from Jan FEI plus $13 Monday, to plus $29 on Thursday, which equates to Jan CP plus around $55. And off window still many buyers exploring 2h Jan arrival tons. Non-Us origin cargoes also in strong demand from not only SEA, but also China. India IOC has issued tender to buy two equal ratio cargoes delivered in Jan and Indonesia Pertamina has also tender to buy 29/31th Jan 22/22 CFR into Kalbut. Chinese importers are bidding aggressively for Jan and even Feb arrival propane. Earlier this week one Chinese importer managed to secure 23kt propane for 1h Jan arrival at Jan CP plus high 60s. Another SChina Petchem buyer has just closed tender to buy 23kt propane for 16-25th Jan delivery, which still in validity. Players assessed the cash premium will easily exceed $70 as front market too tight, against too many propane inquires from petchem rigid demand. One Chinese player is reported that awarded on the latest Sonangol tender for 7/9th Jan lifting heavy propane and has placed the propane parcel into EChina.
