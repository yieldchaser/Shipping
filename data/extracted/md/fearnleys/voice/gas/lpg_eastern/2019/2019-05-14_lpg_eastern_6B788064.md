## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco released their June acceptances, and it appears that there are some shortage on C3 supply. Lifters who have full C3 liftings get delays while the rest with equal ratios are more comfortable with dates as per nominated. There are plenty of equal ratio cargoes are available in the FOB market and the offer level for 2h June lifting has come off to discount level. KPC issued a sales tender for a 4/5 June lifting 2/2 c3/c4, result is not known yet. Indian importers are also reported to postpone/resell some June lifting FOBs. Paper swap values have been volatile this week. US stock buildup and higher production figures than expected has smashed the market values. June CP once traded at $480 earlier this week, only to plunge below $450$ and then slightly rebound to $455 at time of writing. Butane is assessed $22 cheaper but with the length on Butane we expect the spread to expand.

**FAR EAST**

: Due to the minor delays caused by the collision in Houston ship channel over the weekend, the June CFR market has tightened, buyers has been buying in/off window to cover front shorts. 23kt propane for 1h June was traded in window at June FEI plus $53, last time such a high premium was achieved is two years back. 2h June CFR market is less tight but Japanese buyers are actively covering in at levels of June FEI plus $25-30. Comparatively, China importers have been less active this week, main buyers have been PDH operators. Sluggish demand from the domestic sector is killing extra speculative interest. Traditional importers are cautiously monitoring their inventories and sales before deciding whether to buy additional tons, taking into consideration the front premium and intermonth spread between June/July.
