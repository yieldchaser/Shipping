## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Despite the Brent stands above 60$, LPG paper values doesn’t follow anywhere near closely this week. By time of writing, Sept Propane CP traded at $338 whilst Butane is $10 higher at $348. Saudi Aramco has announced Sept lifting acceptances with some liftings reported to be deferred into 2h Sept and later month lifting, with less supply due to oil cut. Therefore supports front CP and front contango narrowed from $5 to almost flattish. Other than this, a couple of 1st decade Sept lifting FOB spots have been traded this week at single digit discounts, against the rising freight. This week Baltic has risen amid plentiful shipping inquiries from Indian players and tight supply in 1st decade Sept.

**FAR EAST**

: CFR prices for delivery into Korea/Japan still appears weak, as 23kt Propane for 2h Sep delivery traded at Sep FEI minus $16, which equates to a CFR price of Sep CP plus high single digit this Wednesday. Similar cargo for 1h Oct delivery is offered at Oct FEI minus $12. With ample supply from US and limited buying interest for US origin, Sep FEI/CP has narrowed rapidly from $28 earlier this week to $20. During the Thursday window, One Chinese major was bidding a 3/1 cargo for 2h Sep delivery at Sep CP plus $44, meanwhile they are bidding one 3/1 loading 22/23 Sep at Sep CP minus $2 and one 2/2 loading 17/18 Sep at Sep CP minus $3. Another Chinese major bids a 3/1 for Oct delivery at Oct CP plus 43$. However such bidding ideas are yet to attract seller’s interest, considering current rising freight. One Chinese major is reported to have covered a 33/11 Sep delivery at Sept CP plus 40ish this week. discussions from traditional importers remain tepid due to high stocks and concerns over weakening domestic prices with the possibility of cheaper Sept CP. Pertamina issued buy tender for two equal ratio cargoes delivered into Indonesia at 4-6 Oct into Teluk Semangka and 8-10 Oct into Kalbut, FOB alternative are also considerable.
