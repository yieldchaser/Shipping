## Market Commentary

MEG： Qatar Petroleum released their May acceptances with some lifters experiencing 2-3 weeks’ delays, mainly for equal ratio cargoes which were nominated for early month lifting. QP is reportedly selling a 45 kt Propane cargo for 1st decade lifting at June CP plus around $10 premium, they may still be able to squeeze a full Propane spot cargo for end May or early June loading. The Baltic VLGC freight has soared by over $5 during the last few days (WOW) and is still climbing. With limited vessels open in the East until 1st decade May. Traders with cargo length and not enough shipping, tends to resell their FOB tons to collect the premium instead of selling CFR delivery. Saudi Aramco announced April CP over the weekend at $515 for Propane and $535 for Butane, up by $25 and $15 respectively. Since the April CP was announced, May CP has gained quickly and is $494 at the time of writing. However the spread between May/June CP have widened from last week’s 20ish to $40. May FEI/CP also widened from previous -$8 to -$26, which reflects the front strength against forward length.

**FAR EAST**

: The tightness in the front market didn’t see any relief during this week. Although the Houston ship channel slowly started to resume to normal operation, lifters are still waiting for clearance on previous backlogs. Limited or no sellers can commit cargoes with fixed arrival until the vessel has departed from US Gulf. 23kt Propane for 1H May arrival is bid at May FEI plus $48 while the offer is at $21 higher. 22+22 kt c3/c4 cargoes is in even shorter supply with buyer’s bidding at May CP plus $60 with no sellers to be seen. 23kt Propane for 2H May arrival is less tight with value being about $25 less. Off window discussions mostly ended up in deadlock. Japanese and Korean importers are not willing to release AG origin cargoes without in return getting US origin cargoes back because of low inventory. Very few trades was concluded within this week, as a consequence inquiries are outstanding ranging from 2h April to May delivery. Indian IOC issued a buy tender for 22+22 kt c3/c4 with 2h May delivery into Ennore, and optional parcels for 15/15 kt or 20/20 kt delivered in June to Aug.
