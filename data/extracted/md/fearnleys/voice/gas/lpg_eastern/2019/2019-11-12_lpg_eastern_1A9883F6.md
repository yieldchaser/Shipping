## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Swap market value edged up this week. Dec Propane rebounded to $415, and Dec FEI/CP spread has further enlarged from mid 30s last week to mid/high 40s. With acceptance for Dec lifting being released, players starting to engage actively in FOB discussion, but bids are at discount level as of soaring freight. KPC issued a sale tender to offer a 14/15 Dec lifting 22/22, the result is yet to know at the time of writing. Qatar Petroleum has officially announced a ten-year agreement with Wanhua Chemical to supply roughly 800,000 tons LPG annually. Just in last Nov, Wanhua also signed a ten-year purchase agreement to ADNOC to secure 1 million tonne LPG per year.

**FAR EAST**

: Front window is strengthening this week. During mid-week, 23kt Propane for 2h Dec delivery is traded at Dec FEI plus $6. As Dec FEI/CP keep widening, the US/Non-Us origin spread has rapidly narrowing down. We might soon see players deliver non Us origin into Japan/Korea range instead of swarming into China. Spot inquiries from China remains slim, compare to recent term discussion. One EChina PDH issued a mini term tender to secure 23/0 or 46/0 in Jan and Feb but later withdrew as offers not attractive. One 11/11 for 1h delivery into Schina was reported done at Dec CP plus around $60 only. Indonesia Pertamina was reported to awarded their tender for a equal ratio split cargo delivered mid Dec at Dec CP plus high 40s, which is roughly Dec CP flattish or small discount on FOB netback. CPC was reported awarded their buy tender at Dec CP plus low mid 60s for the purchase of one 11/11 delivered in 2h Dec. And they’ve issued a FOB/DES term tender to swap a volume of 176k-264k AG FOB cargos for 176k cargos Des into Taiwan. Considering 20 Cal freight level, there might be limited parties can commit such.
