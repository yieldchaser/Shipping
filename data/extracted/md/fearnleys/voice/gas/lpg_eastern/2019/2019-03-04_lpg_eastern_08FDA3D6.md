## Market Commentary

**MEG:** It all started middle of the week, April CP moved from $460 to $490s. Tightness in the front market have pushed up the price. Saudi Aramco appear to have little to nothing when it come to spot availabilities till June. Term lifters are cautiously waiting for April lifting acceptances, also thinking about confirmed and possible future US fog delays. The spread between Propane and Butane has narrowed a bit with anticipation that more US butane will come to East. There are however some uncertainties due to the weather.

**FAR EAST**

: The Asian market has been quiet with the Tokyo conference being this week. Nevertheless, in the window one Korean importer aggressively bid for 23kt propane for prompt 2h Mar delivery. No sellers appeared in the window until Thursday, to offer 23kt propane for 1h April, this show a premium of low 40s over April CP. April CP gained strength from the tightness in front month, with April FEI less support. With expectation that ample supply from US will sail to east, Apr FEI/CP turned into negative again and traded at minus $8 at the time of writing. Off window there are active inquiries from either China or SEA. But with slim margins in both residential and PDH petchem, importers might restrain their spot activities and cover volume on rigid demand basis. Indonesian Pertamina is reported to have awarded their last tender for 11/11 only arriving early April. Another noticeable phenomenon is more Iranian cargoes have been tracked sailing to China from Mar. So far we’ve seen around 300k tonnes is arriving/will arrive in first half period.
