## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Despite the crude rise earlier this week and improved demand, August swaps was traded under pressure at $360, $15 less than our last report. September CP still maintains at $3-4 contango for the time being. FOB market appeared quiet this week, both for cargo and freight trades. Some players with shipping length are reportedly looking for possible spot cargoes to utilize their ships. With softening freight and improving premia in Eastern market, FOB discounts have narrowed and today one trader was seen offering their late Aug Yanbu lifting 2/2 ratio asking Aug CP plus $5 (Yanbu discount to apply), while the last deal for similar cargo was done at CP minus single digit.

**FAR EAST**

: Window bids/offers still roam at discount levels. On Wednesday a 23k Propane for 2h August was traded at August FEI minus $4. A similar cargo for same period, today was offered at minus $2 and cargo for 1h Sep offers at minus $5 basis Sep FEI, yet no bids was seen. Earlier this week, the strong MB and firm freight squeezed the arbitrage value but latest stock data with a considerable build has enable the economy to return again for Eastern trades. Off window enquires have been rather active. One EChina PDH user awarded their buy tender for 23k Propane delivered in 2h Aug at Aug CP plus mid high $60s, in addition, an other EChina cracker user was reported to have awarded their buy tender for 22k Propane for delivery end Aug into Taixing at Aug CP plus $60s, even if their original tender demand was for upto 44k Propane. FPCC has issued a spot tender to buy a 33/11 or 44/0 for delivery during 11-15th Sep, the result is not yet to known. Pertamina reportedly awarded one 2/2 for their mid Sep demand into Teluk Semangka, and are still looking for one more.
