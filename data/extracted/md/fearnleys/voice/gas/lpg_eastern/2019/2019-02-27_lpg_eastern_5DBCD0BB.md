## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco announced Mar CP at $490 and $520 respectively for propane and butane, up by $50 each, which is much higher than player’s expectation. The tightness on AG spot supply didn't get relieved and traders can scarcely squeeze out tons for resale. Apr CP swap is heavily backwardated, but slowly edged up at $466 at the time of writing. Apr C3/C4 spread has narrowed down from Mar at $20 at the time being.

**FAR EAST**

: Tightness in 2h Mar extends into 1h Apr. The foggy weather in US continues and incurs further delays, which exert upward pressures on price in short term. On Wed a 23kt propane delivered in 1h Apr was traded at Apr FEI plus $15, which is $12$ higher than other bid. And we barely see any offers for this period even off window. 2h Apr is slightly at ease with expectation that the cargo will move smooth later on. Price of non-US origin is also hitting rooftops, with outstanding tenders from Indian and Indonesia for equal ratio cargoes delivered ranged from late Mar to Apr. Propane cargo within Mar arrival is reported done at low mid 40s into China, and premium of for 1h Apr arrival cargos hover over 40$. One North China petchem user has issued a private tender to secure one full propane cargo delivered in Apr. And other Chinese are actively inquired for propane or heavy propane cargo delivered in 1h Apr as well.
