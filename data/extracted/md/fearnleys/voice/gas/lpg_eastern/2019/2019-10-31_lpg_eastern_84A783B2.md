## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Saudi Aramco has set the November Propane CP at $430 and Butane CP at $445, each up by $10 from October price. The Propane CP is in line with latest swap value, yet some end users/importers reckon it is higher than expectation. Also, the spread between Propane and Butane is wider than latest trade which was at $10. At the time of writing, December CP is traded at $410, $20 less than the just announced November CP. December FEI/CP spread is traded at around $35, partly driven by the sustained strong freight. Qatar Petroleum is reported to have awarded their December lifting of a full cargo Propane at December CP plus a small digit premium to a Chinese Major.

**FAR EAST**

: A flurry of deals was concluded in the window this week. 23kt Propane for 2h November delivery was traded at November FEI plus $14, and one equal ratio cargo for end November delivery into Chiba was done at November CP plus $77, which equates to November CP plus $5 on FOB netback. Off window discussions on Propane for December delivery are also active with a few trades concluded at December FEI plus mid-high single digit during mid-week. However, the levels currently under discussion are weakening. One S.China PDH operator issued a buy tender to secure a 1-10 December delivery of 23/0, the result is yet to be known by the time of writing. Other Chinese importers are cautious and want to think twice before taking spot cargoes in December before CP is set, importers also se sluggish demand from domestic customers combined with surplus supply from refineries. Nevertheless, importers still expect strong rising demand and are starting inquire for next year’s term supplies but some discussions are still pending with uncertainties on US-China tariffs and strong freights in 2020.
