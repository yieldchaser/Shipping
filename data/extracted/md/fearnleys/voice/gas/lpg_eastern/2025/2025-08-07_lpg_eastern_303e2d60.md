## Market Commentary

MEG Spot differentials improved as of better butane demand. Indian IOC was reported to award one Sep lifting 2/2 at Sep CP minus high 20s, while Oct and Nov remained uncovered as offer price not favorable. Market players are waiting for producers’ acceptances to have full scheme on FOB supply. By the time of writing, Sep CP was traded at $520 for C3 and C3/C4 at $23, Sep/Oct CP at $15 contango.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials for Sep delivery propane are still in heavy discounted territory, with 1H being discussed at Sep FEI minus 20s and 2H at minus 10s respectively. During mid-week one 23/0 parcel for 2H Sep delivery basis Chiba was changed hand at Aug FEI flat, which equates to Sep FEI minus mid-teens. At the time of writing, 23/0 for 1h Sep delivery was offered at Sep FEI minus $18 while bids at minus $23. Similar parcel for 2h Sep delivery was bid at Sep FEI minus $20. Earlier this week, Sep FEI/MOPJ was once widened to over $60, following strong crude, which attracted a handful of cracking demand. FPCC issued tender to secure 23kt or 46kt LPG at a flexible ratio for Sep delivery but awarded only 23/0. HTC called tender to procure 23kt butane for 16-22nd Sep delivery. Chinese importers still prefer non US stems for Sep delivery, awaiting the US-China tariff talk outcomes. One Canadian origin propane cargo for Sep delivery was reported sold into EChina at Sep CP plus 20s, while other non Us stem traded at Sep CP plus 30s basis Schina. One evenly split cargo for 1h Sep delivery was sold into SChina at Sep CP plus low 30s reportedly.
