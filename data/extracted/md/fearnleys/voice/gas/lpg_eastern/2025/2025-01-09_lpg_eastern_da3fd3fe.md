## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco lowered Jan CP by $10 for propane to $625 and $15 for butane at $615, reflecting the weaker demand on delivered market, especially Jan FEI/CP being traded in negative values. Qatar Energy issued sale tender for a 3/1 cargo loading 16-23 Feb. FOB for feb lifting was assessed at mid/high teens discounts amid muted discussions. By the time of writing, Feb CP was traded at $627 and Feb/Mar CP traded at $12 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 Very lively window this week with three deals concluded. 46kt propane for 2h Feb delivery basis Yantai was traded at Bal Jan FEI minus $1.28, and 2 lots of 23kt propane for 1h Feb delivery were done at Feb FEI plus $9 and $10. Similar parcel for 2h Feb delivery basis Chiba was bid at $7ish, yet to see offers. Outside window a flurry of 46kt propane parcels were also placed into China for Feb delivery at Feb FEI plus mid high single digit, in terms of the new startup PDHs, completion of maintenance and anticipated demand recovery after holiday. HTC also issued tender to buy 23kt butane for late Feb delivery. One of the heated topics this week is China’s Shangdong Port Group banned US-Sanctioned tankers to call its ports and players are worrying if same would affect in LPG carriers. As per our tracking, around 1mmt Iran cargos was imported into Shandong, accounting for 11% among 8.8 mmt total imports into China. And only 9 VLGCs are listed in SDN list, which means other fleets can still be alternatives to continue the flows.
