## Market Commentary

MEG Due to the Us-China trade escalation, non-Us stems’ value strengthened and evenly split cargo for May lifting improved from CP minus teens last week to single digit premium but yet to hear trades. May FEI/CP spread widened to over $100 due to the slash on FEI swap, coupled with the volatile crude. By the time of writing, May CP was assessed at $568 for C3 and C3/C4 at $25. May/Jun CP was traded at $12 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 A week of volatility amidst uncertainties and fears over US-China trade tensions. Propane before 13/May (inclusive) arrival into China, which was under tax waiver, was once traded at May FEI plus $100. Whilst all the later delivery without tax exemption have to seek home somewhere else. Hence discussion over 2h May delivery plunged to discounted level, and 23/0 for 2h May delivery into FAR EAST (outside China) was bid at May FEI minus $10.88. Market players are cautiously monitoring the situation. Inquiries on non-US cargo or Swap from Chinese players spiked up, as roughly 50% of China import are driven from petchem feedstock, which heavily rely on US origin, with monthly imports amounted to nearly 1.5 mmt. However, the swap fee (swinging following the tariff) are too far to breach and yet to hear much origin swap happened. This being said, limited heavy propane grade available from AG etc increased the swap difficulties. We are expecting some petchem plants to cut rates, or advance or extend their turnaround after using up feedstock. Nevertheless, the widening FEI/MOPJ enticed the cracking demand and FPCC was reported to secure two 46/0 parcels for 2h May delivery at MOPJ minus 80. One AG origin 23/0 parcel for 2h May delivery basis Dongguan was traded at May CP plus mid hi 30s, which equates to CP plus mid-single digit on netback. Other than this, there is a sale tender from special origin offering three cargos for 1h May delivery basis Schina, with two 3/1 and one 2/2 ratio.
