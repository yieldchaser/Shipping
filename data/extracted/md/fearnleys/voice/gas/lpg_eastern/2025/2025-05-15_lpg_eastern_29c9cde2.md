## Market Commentary

**MEG:** Slim discussions on evenly split cargos for Jun loaders this week and cash differentials deepened further as lack of buying interests. KPC was reported to sell their 0/4 cargo for 6-10 Jun lifting at Jun CP minus 30s to a SEA player. Indian IOC tendered to buy monthly one evenly split cargo for Q3 period on FOB or CFR basis, which might lend support to Q3 FOB discussions. By the time of writing, Jun C3 CP was traded at $584 and C3/C4 assessed at $25. Jun/Jul CP C3 are in $25 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

: Cash premium for 23/0 1h Jun delivery basis Chiba improved to Jun FEI plus $7.5, as of a lack of offers for 1H period. Similar package for 2h Jun delivery was traded outside window at Jun FEI plus $6/7 reportedly. With the US-China tariff agreement, Chinese importers have reconsidered to import US cargos, by comparing costs with additional 10% tax, vs Non Us origin. However, due to the eroding margins, a handful of petchem plants have cut rates, refrained from additional buying for Jun cargos. Cash differentials therefore softened, with 46/0 non Us origin reported sold into SChina at Jun CP plus $40 only, a dramatic drop from last week’s 60s . And a couple of US origin 46/0 parcels for Jun delivery basis EChina and NChina were changed hand at Jun FEI plus mid high single digit. Compared to propane, butane was heavily discounted vs propane, with offers slashed to CP plus teens level basis delivery into China. Traditional importers struggled with high CP costs vs slow distribution. Other than normal stem, another special origin cargo sale tender is available, offering two evenly split and two heavy propane grades for 2h Jun delivery basis SChina.
