## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco cut Aug CP to $575 for C3 and $520 for C4, both by $55, reflecting the front length. One evenly split cargo for 2h Aug lifting was offered at Sep CP minus mid-teens, and similar ratio cargo for Sep lifting was also offered at Sep CP minus $10, which barely attract buyer interest in terms of netbacks. By the time of writing, Sep C3 CP was traded at $530 and C3/C4 at $15.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash differentials for Sep delivery are still discussed in heavy discounts. 23/0 for 1h Sep delivery basis Chiba was offered at Sep FEI minus $11, and similar parcel for 2h Sep delivery was bid at Aug FEI minus mid-single digit or Sep FEI minus $20. Outside window, a couple of 46kt C3 was reported changed hand for 1h Sep delivery, with one traded at Aug FEI minus mid-single digit basis Ningbo. As US-China tariff trade talks are still ongoing, Chinese importers are cautiously taking non-Us stems, avoiding the potential tariff hikes. One EChina importer procured a 23/0 (non US) for Sep delivery into Ningbo at FEI plus 10s via tender reportedly. Following the crude rise, Aug and Sep FEI/MOPJ widened rapidly and cracking demand improved with a handful of buying inquires emerged for butane. One Korean petchem player was reported to award a 0/23 for 7-13 delivery at Aug MOPJ minus 40s. Another Korean importer also issued butane buy tender for Sep delivery. Two Schina petchem buyers also issued butane buy tender but cancelled in the end as offer prices are not favorable.
