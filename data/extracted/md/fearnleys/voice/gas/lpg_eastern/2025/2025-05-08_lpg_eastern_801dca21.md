## Market Commentary

MEG Butane surplus has deepened the FOB netback discounts to teens level for evenly split grade, while propane stands firm in premium. By the time of writing, Jun CP was traded at $574 for C3 and $547 for C4. Jun/Jul C3 CP was traded at $28 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 Propane demand remains strong amongst active stockpiling and cracking buying from Japanese and Koreans importers, together with traders covering shorts. A handful of 23/0 for 1h Jun delivery actively was changed hand at Jun FEI minus $6 and $7, and 4/0 basis China was traded at minus $8. On Thurs, bids for 23/0 parcel for 1h Jun and 2h Jun delivery improved to Jun FEI minus $4 and $5 respectively, while few offers seen. China markets are relatively slow compared to last week, as players are waiting for upcoming US-China trade talks. Petchem players refrained purchase by cutting run rates, as soaring costs on CP and cash premiums eroding margins. Despite such, Jun delivered propane are still traded at CP plus 50s/60 into China reportedly, in view of tight supply on non-US stem. However, butane was heavily discounted vs propane, as of ample supply against limited buying and high inventory from receivers. On special origin, there are two heavy propane and two evenly split cargo cargos for end May to mid-Jun delivery being offered via tender.
