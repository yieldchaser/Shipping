## Market Commentary

MEG Spot discounts for Feb FOBs still stay in teens level. One evenly split cargo loading in Feb was heard traded at Feb CP minus mid-teens, to one producer trading arm. Qatar Energy was also reported to award their sale tender for a 3/1 of 16-23 Feb lifting to a Chinese player but price was sketchy. At the time of writing, Feb CP was traded at $636 and C3/C4 spread at $9. Feb/Mar CP was assessed at $7 backwardation.

**FAR EAST**

 Cash premium eased off but flat price stands firm, following the strong swap prices especially the surge on MB price due to the cold weather and big inventory draw. 23kt propane parcel for 2h Feb delivery was offered in window at Feb FEI plus $10, and off window such parcel was traded at plus $8 reportedly. Meanwhile, a couple of 46kt propane parcels for Feb delivery was placed into China at Feb FEI plus mid high single digit, to different regions. Butane price also inched up owing to the sharp price increase on MB, narrowing the ARB to FAR EAST. CPC issued buy tender for late Feb delivered 11/11 cargo. On special origins, one evenly split cargo and one heavy propane cargo, both for 1st decade Feb delivery basis SChina, were reported concluded at Feb CP minus 20s. Other than spot, Tianjin Bohai called a mini term tender to secure 46kt propane monthly from Mar to Jun delivery into Tianjin, whose PDH is currently under maintenance and expected to restart after CNY. The PDH margins eroded further due to the rising cost, but we still expect the run rate to improve slightly with some units returning from glitches. However, the sluggish downstream demand still refrains plants from ramping up rates.
