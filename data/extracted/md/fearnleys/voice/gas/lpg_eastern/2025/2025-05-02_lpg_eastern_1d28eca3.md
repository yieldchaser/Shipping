## Market Commentary

MEG Saudi Aramco announced May Cp at $610 for propane and $590 for butane, which is surprisingly above market expectation, especially C3/C4 spread is not as wide as expected amid the long butane supply. Fob netbacks for Jun loaders vary widely depending on ratios. As in lack of discussions, cash differentials for heavy propane are reckoned at high single to teens premium while evenly split at discounts only. By the time of writing, Jun Cp was traded at $565 for propane and $543 butane respectively.

**FAR EAST**

 Trading activities slowed down as of labour holiday, coupled with uncertainties on US-China trade talks. 23/0 parcel for 1h Jun delivery was offered at Jun FEI minus $8 on Friday window but no bids seen. Outside window similar cargo was traded at Jun FEI minus $5 earlier. Despite the deviation cost, full cargo 4/0 are now reckoned with higher premium vs 2/0 from 2H May delivery onwards, said to be supported by Japanese and Korean buyings. No official announcement from China yet, but likely product like ethane might be granted with tariff exemptions, and China is also evaluating the tariff impacts in different industries. Most Chinese importers have managed to sell their US Stems/buy non US stems or bookout with their suppliers for May cargos. For Jun delivery, premium for non-Us propane extended the strength, and was assessed at Jun Cp plus 60s, as of rigid petchem demand. However, discussions for non-Us butane are in stalemate as in lack of demand. Ningbo Huatai cancelled their buy tender for 1st decade Jun delivered propane as of few offers. One petchem player cancelled their butane buy tender for May-Jun delivery as offers not attractive. One NChina PDH importer was reported to award their Aug to Dec mini term buy tender for monthly one heavy propane cargo at Cp plus 40s.
