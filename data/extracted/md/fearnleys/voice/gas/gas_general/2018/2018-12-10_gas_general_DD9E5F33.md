## Market Commentary

The VLGC market in the west really had a boost in activity this week, as Charterers seems to utilize the window of opportunity between a couple of weeks of festivities and before the actual holidays starts. Several cargoes were floated on the market, both for end of December and as well as January loading, and they were all snapped up rather quickly, more or less eliminating the expected overhang of vessels from December to January. Most recent deals have been concluded at rates in the mid-60s per ton for the bench-mark route from Houston to Japan via the Panama Canal.
