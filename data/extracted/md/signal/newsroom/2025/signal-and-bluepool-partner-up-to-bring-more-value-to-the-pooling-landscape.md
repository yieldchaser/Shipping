## The Partnership at a Glance {#partnership}

**Signal Maritime Services**, a leading force in tanker pooling, marks its entry into the dry bulk segment by investing in **Bluepool**, a rapidly growing dry bulk pool operator.

This is a strategic collaboration between two companies that share a common vision: leading performance, transparency, and technology-driven pooling. Bluepool currently manages a growing fleet of **21 vessels** across a Panamax and a newly established Supramax pool — built on the same commercial principles that have defined Signal Maritime Services' tanker pooling model since 2018.


## What This Means for Vessel Owners and Investors {#vessel-Owners}

This partnership expands the commercial options available to owners and investors across both the tanker and dry bulk segments. By combining Signal's analytical infrastructure with Bluepool's dry bulk expertise, the two companies will deliver:


- **Broader market intelligence** across tanker and dry bulk markets
- **Diversified commercial structures** for vessel owners
- **Expanded investment opportunities** through a unified industry network

The bunkering and operational intelligence that has defined Signal's tanker pooling model now extends into dry bulk — giving Bluepool partners access to data-driven decision support previously out of reach for smaller pool operators.


## Leadership Perspectives {#leadership}

“This is not just an investment; it’s the joining of two like-minded organizations committed to bringing greater efficiency and value to their respective shipping segments.” commented Panos Dimitracopoulos, CEO of Signal Maritime Services. “Bluepool's deep expertise in dry bulk, combined with our proven technological backbone, creates a formidable force ready to unlock new opportunities for our partners.”

This strategic move will drive growth and strengthen both companies’ ability to serve their partners. By combining their deep commercial knowledge in the tanker and dry segments with cutting-edge analytical resources, Signal and Bluepool will deliver broader market insights and stronger performance capabilities across both markets. And by pooling their industry networks and offering diversified commercial and investment structures, the partnership will open expanded opportunities for vessel owners and investors alike.

“We are excited to welcome Signal as a strategic investor. Collaborating with such a strong and diverse team sets the bar really high. Both teams very much look forward to joining forces towards pool growth and technology enhancement with an aim to provide superior, flexible and transparent pooling services. The industry is moving toward consolidation, and we plan to be the commercial managers serving that consolidation." commented Nikolas Gavriilidis, Managing Partner of Bluepool.
