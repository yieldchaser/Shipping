# Relet Tonnage Doubles

*Published on 16 June 2020*

## Surge in Relet Vessels Impacting the Tanker Market

In the last six months, the number of relet vessels on the spot market has doubled. "Relets" are vessels professionally operated by oil companies that trade on the spot market.

So far in 2020 we have seen remarkable conditions in the tanker market.  Covid-19 caused drastic changes to supply and demand levels, from [delays at ports](/newsroom/heres-how-quarantines-could-impact-the-tanker-shipping-market) to [significant demand to oil storage](/newsroom/opec-cuts-and-floating-storage-requirements-3-scenarios), and there was a drastic collapse in oil pricing.  When demand for floating storage spiked, oil companies hedged their exposure through time charters.
