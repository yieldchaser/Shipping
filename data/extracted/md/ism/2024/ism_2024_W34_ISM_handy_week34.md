## Market Commentary

Despite the fact that Atlantic freight rates have not changed much by the end of the week, the sentiment has still improved slightly on the Supramax/Ultramax and Handy markets. The offer of cargoes with September laycans has grown both in ECSA and USG , but rather long

Thus, shipment of 25-30,000 t of wheat from Upriver to Bejaia is still assessed by ISM at $47-48/t given 7,000x/3,000x l/d rates, while that of 25-30,000 t of fertilizers from Tampa to N.Brazil is estimated at $24/t.

Supramax/Ultramax rates are generally flat also in the Asia - Pacific region despite a slowdown in Indonesian coal imports by India and China. “We see less back-haul requests and offers for steel shipments from the Far East to SE Asia; owners with vessels open in Australia prefer to ballast them to SE Asia in order to find cargoes,” a broker mentions negative aspects of the APAC Handysize/ Handymax sector's demand side. Nevertheless, the cargo/ tonnage ratio stays relatively balanced, and Handysize/ Handymax rates are stable.

The Indian Ocean market still lacks positive impetus amid flat cargo exports from PG/WCI and a slowdown of ore traffic from ECI.

Next week may well bring a rise in Atlantic freight rates as the supply/demand ratio is firmly on the side of ship owners. “We are seeing a lot more encouragement, with deals being discussed at evaluated levels,” a broker

In this situation, taking advantage of the impulse from the Atlantic market, Supramax/Ultramax owners will probably manage to raise rates also in the Continent and the Mediterranean Sea. There are few other preconditions for higher activity on the European market, unless we mention the gradual end of the vacation season. The situation in the Handysize segment is unlikely to change in the coming week.

“We expect lack of firm cargoes as well as excessive tonnage list to persist in the Indian Ocean,” a broker dealing with Supramax/Ultramax segment claims. As for the APAC region, players so far note few signs of improvement in coal imports by China as well as in Chinese steel exports by Handysize-Ultramax fleet.
