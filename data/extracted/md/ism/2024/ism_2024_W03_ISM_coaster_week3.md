## Market Commentary

The cargo flow has perked up slightly in the Black and Mediterranean Seas after January 15 amid brisker shipments of various cubic size agri products from Ukraine, as well as steel products from the EU countries and fertilizers from Egypt. However, this slight upturn

in volumes is still insufficient to compensate for the growing list of vessels opening for late January and early February dates. Players note only a slight increase in freight rates from the Danube ports ($1-2/t up) amid increased downtime due to weather, while owners working on other routes struggle to keep rates stable or even make further concessions (within $0.5/t). «We see wider gap in ideas and therefore less fixing activity, with many cargoes to stay on the market for next week,» a Turkish broker commented. At the moment, rates for shipment of 5-7,000 t of corn from Izmail or Reni to Alexandria are hovering at $38-39/t, while those for transportation of 3-4,000 t of wheat from Varna to Marmara at $15-15.5/t. Note that current RV TCEs for 5-12k dwt fleet are still 20-50% above the OPEX, but only the year 2020 showed lower mid-January owners' earnings over the last 5 years.

The downtrend is getting clearer on the Azov sea- river market , but the relevant tonnage list is growing slowly and remains limited (due in part to downtime in Kerch and weather conditions), allowing ship owners to restrain the rate drop. Over the week, freight rates have sagged by mere $1-2/t, which still allows ship owners to earn at least 2.5 times more than OPEX. Shipment of 3,000 t of wheat from Rostov/Azov to Marmara now costs $59- 60/t.

In North Europe , the cargo traffic is recovering very slowly after the holiday lull. As before, only weather conditions make adjustments to the freight rates. «It's absolutely quiet cargowise in Continent/Baltic, but owners are trying to raise rates amid adverse weather,» an EU broker commented.

Most players are confident that any growth of activity and freight rates can hardly be expected in the next couple of weeks. Among positive moments for ship owners, we can note only possible further growth of Ukrainian
