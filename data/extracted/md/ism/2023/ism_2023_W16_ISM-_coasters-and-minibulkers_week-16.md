## Market Commentary

The sentiment is worsening on the small-tonnage shipping market. Despite the end of Easter holidays, the market has not shown any hint on the recovery this week. Moreover, activity has expectedly decreased in the Black and Mediterranean Seas by the end of the week amid the Eid al-Fitr holidays in Muslim countries. Spot and late

Coaster freight rates keep sliding down both in the north and south of Europe

April tonnage list is increasing, especially in the Marmara Sea and EMed. «Charterers are easily finding vessels, especially for voyages within Med,» a broker commented to International Seaborne Market. Freight rates have dropped by $1-2/t in the Black Sea and within $0.5-3/t in the Mediterranean Sea this week. Thus, rates for shipment of 5-6,000 t of wheat or corn from Reni or Izmail to Mersin have decreased to $52-53/t, while those for transportation of 7-8,000 t steels from Novorossiysk to Marmara to $23.5- 24.5/t.

The downtrend continues in the Azov Sea as well. Over the week, sea-river rates have dropped by $3-4/t. A couple of deals for shipments of 3,000 t of coal from Rostov to TBS have been signed at $39/t.

The Black and Mediterranean Seas may show some activity upturn after Ramadan, but we should not expect the market growth. Moreover, the return of import duties on grain in Turkey (scheduled for 1 May), as well as low demand for steel products in MENA countries, will most likely force ship owners to make new concessions in early May.

There are no preconditions for any improvements in North Europe either. «Warehouses of many large charterers full in receiving end, so we expect a further drop in activity and freights soon» - a local broker commented to  International Seaborne Market.
