## Market Commentary

Activity remains extremely low on coaster market, with only slightly healthier demand for 5-6k dwt vessels in Black Sea

The small-tonnage shipping market is still waiting for the new grain season. The vast majority of operators are only thinking about how to minimise losses and hold out until the market picture normalizes. Among positive factors, we should note only a barely noticeable upturn in shipments of 5-6,000 t grain stems from Black Sea ports, but even the decline in relevant tonnage list in the Black and Marmara Seas (by about 20%) did not allow owners to raise rates from the bottom. Thus, shipment of 5-6,000 t of soybeans or corn from Ukraine to Alexandria still costs low $20s/t. In the Mediterranean Sea, the cargo traffic is maintained only by a rather regular flow of fertilizers, while shipments of steel, grains and minerals are sporadic. Ship owners struggle even to keep rates stable. Rates for transportation of 5-6,000 t of urea from Damietta to SpanMed are holding at mid-$20s/t.

Despite plenty of vessels laid up in the Azov Sea , sea-river owners are not able to push rates up in the area as new grain and coal offers are disastrously few.

The North European market is not showing any improvement either, if not for the fact that owners have at least managed to stop the rate decline due to a slight drop in the number of open vessels. Trade is extremely slack in the region due in part to the transport and logistics exhibition in Munich.

The freight market is not expected to improve in the next couple of weeks, as negative sentiment reigns on main commodity markets. For example, spot grain prices are under strong pressure as traders are only discussing new crop purchases. Turkey's demand for square billets is minimal, as are exports of finished steel products. Meanwhile, demand for Egyptian urea has also declined to a minimum after a slight recovery. In addition, Eid al-Fitr is celebrated in Muslim countries at the end of this week and early next week, which will minimize the already low activity.
