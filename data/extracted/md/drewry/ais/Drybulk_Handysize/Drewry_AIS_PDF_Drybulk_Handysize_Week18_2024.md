## Elevated utilisation

* Drewry's utilisation index for Handysize vessels remained 1% higher YoY. As the inactivity of vessels has reached its highest level in 2024, the supply stretch available has improved.
* The tonnemile index hovered around the same level for three weeks after moderating from the high levels seen last month.
* Market activity continues to be high in the East where the share of laden tonnage has reach its highest level in 2024, leading to a rise in freight rates.
* At the same time, congestion levels have squeezed in the East, with tonnage waiting at the anchor dropping to historically low levels.
