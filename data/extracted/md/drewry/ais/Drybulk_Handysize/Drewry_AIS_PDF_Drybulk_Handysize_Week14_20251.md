## Market Commentary

### Handysize Tonne-miles index declined

- The Handysize tonne-mile index experienced a sharp decline in the first two weeks of March. Although it recovered to some extent in the second half of March, it further declined 3%, reaching 91.7 in week 14.

- Baltic benchmark rates mirrored this trend, aligning with utilisation patterns. Global laden speed dropped significantly by 3.6%, with ballast speed following the same .

- The cross-Atlantic region remained quiet, marked by a substantial decrease in laden tonnage underway. More vessels gathered at ports, contributing to increased congestion at anchor in the Atlantic.
