## Handysize Tonne-Miles Index Surged Amid Rising Speed

* The Handysize tonne-miles index surged by 10.5% in week 17. This was in tandem with sharply rising ballast and laden speed.

* Laden speed in the West reached its highest levels since August 2022, accompanied by an increase in the percentage of laden vessels in the region. In the East, vessels at anchor increased by 18% MoM, leading to higher congestion of ballast vessels.

* Ballast tonnage in the Atlantic declined due to the Easter holidays, resulting in stagnant market sentiments. In accordance, Spot rates on the Europe-Brazil route fell by 2.5%.
