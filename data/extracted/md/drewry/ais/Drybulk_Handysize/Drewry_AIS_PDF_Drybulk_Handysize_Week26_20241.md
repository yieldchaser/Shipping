### Marginal decline in Handysize utilisation

* The utilisation of Handysize vessels declined WoW by a marginal 0.1%, from 93.2% to 93.1%. As the global ballast speed increased last week, vessel utilisation dampened.

* The laden tonne-mile index declined 1.4% WoW as more vessels are stuck in congestion, particularly at load ports.

* As the vessels underway continue to be on a downward trend in the East as well as the West regions and more vessels are at the ports, the supply-stretch available in the market is likely to squeeze, providing buoyancy to the rates.
