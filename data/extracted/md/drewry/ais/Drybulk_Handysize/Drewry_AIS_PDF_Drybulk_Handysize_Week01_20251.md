## Stability in utilisation

* The global Handysize utilisation remained stable at 92.3% in week 51 and 52 of 2024, while continuing to be substantially lower YoY.

* The global ballast speed was on average 9 knots last week, lower by as much as one knot in the same period last year.

* Additionally, a slower start to the year is expected due to lowermarket activity with reduced congestion levels YoY observed across key regions such as East Asia, China, the Indian subcontinent, the Pacific, Oceania, and the Atlantic.
