### Improvement in WoW utilisation

- The Handysize vessel utilization improved last week from 92.2% to 92.6%, reaching close to last year's level. The utilisation trend continues to be volatile every week, with the speed of the vessels following the trend to respond to the changing demand.
- Meanwhile, the tonnemile index moderated WoW. As China's hydropower generation has picked up, its coal demand has moderated MoM despite being elevated. As China imports thermal coal from Russia's Far East ports in Handysize vessels, the vessel demand is impacted as trade tapers.
- The ballast tonnage at China's load ports has dropped significantly, indicating tepid coastal trade. Meanwhile, the laden tonnage at anchor in China, waiting to unload at discharge ports, has peaked for 2024 and is seasonally higher compared to last year.

## Glossary

- **Handysize vessels:** small dry Bulk vessels, and are in the size range 10,000-40,000 dwt.
- **Trading status:** Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen.
- **Laden dwt miles:** Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight (or capacity)
- **Bunker price:** Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port
- **% Laden:** Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair
- **East:** East to Suez Canal, comprising China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania
- **West:** West of Suez Canal, comprising Atlantic region
- **HS7_38:** Spot rates from Baltic Exchange for North China/South Korea/Japan to South East Asia route
- **HS1_38:** Spot rates from Baltic Exchange on the Skaw/Passero-Rio de Janerio/Recalada route
- **At anchor:** Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area
- **Moored:** Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging
- **Underway:** Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination
