### Decline in weekly utilisation

* The utilisation of Handysize vessels moved slightly downwards from 93.4% to 94.1% last week but remained above the level in 2023.

* Meanwhile, the global tonne-mile index expanded last week after remaining stable in the week before, in contrast with the trend observed last year.

* Buoyant grain trade supported Handysize demand in the otherwise seasonally lean period, pushing congestion levels higher in discharge ports of major regions.

* The tonnage waiting at the anchor at discharge ports in China swelled by 0.5 mdwt approximately, crossing the level seen in 2023.
