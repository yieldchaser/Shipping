## High Congestion in China and India led to record tonnage waiting at anchorage in the East

* The global Handysize utilisation rate surged to 93.7% in the last week of January, recording a 1.1 percentage point increase compared to the same period last year.

* The rise in utilization was primarily driven by an enhanced ballast speed, a trend observed within this vessel segment only, defying the usual trend. Additionally, the tonne-mile index saw a WoW growth of 8.1%.

* The tonnage waiting at anchorage in the East reached historically high levels of 12.61 mdwt, possibly due to increased congestion of ballast vessels at anchorages in China and India.

Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet.<br/>Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |
