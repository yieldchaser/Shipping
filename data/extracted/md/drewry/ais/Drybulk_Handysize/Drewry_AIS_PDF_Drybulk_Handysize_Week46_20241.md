## Marginal Decline in Handysize Utilisation

1 content_summary

* The utilisation of Handysize vessels declined WoW by 0.7 percentage point, from 93.0% to 92.3%, and accordingly rates followed. The Baltic's HS7TC also showed a downward trend over the week

* Ships at anchor decreased in the East as well as in the western part of the globe

* There was a substantial fall in the laden tonnage waiting at anchor in the Pacific, mostly in China. Similarly, there was a significant decline in ballast vessels at anchor in the Atlantic as many vessels loaded grain and soybean from the US Gulf and the East coast of the US and sailed to Europe and Asia. There were some shipments coming to the Middle East as well, might be because of some hasty imports in anticipation of some trade restrictions in future.
