## Utilisation on an uptrend

1 content_summary
* The utilisation of Handysize vessels expanded for the second consecutive week, while being substantially higher YoY for the fourth week.

* Meanwhile, the global tonne-mile index moderated WoW but continues to be high YoY. Restart of Ukraine's seaborne grain exports to the EU and buoyant trade of steel products on the China-India route have been aiding Handysize demand.

* The tonnage underway in the East rose in March juxtaposed with a substantial decline in congestion in the region.

* The congestion at the load and discharge ports eased significantly in India and China, adding to the supply in the market. However, higher demand has been soaking up supply, aiding rates in the market.
