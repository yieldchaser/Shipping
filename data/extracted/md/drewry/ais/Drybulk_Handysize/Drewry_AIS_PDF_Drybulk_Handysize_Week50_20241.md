## Market Commentary

### Decrease in laden tonne-mile index

- The tonne-mile index declined marginally last week after rising for two consecutive weeks. Meanwhile, the utilisation of Handysize vessels continued its downward trend in week 49 to reach 91.8%.

- The laden tonnage at anchor in India for Week 49 dropped by 0.4 mdwt as the import of grains, primarily from Australia, declined in tandem with the seasonal trend.

- Meanwhile, congestion at discharge ports also contracted due to a sharp decline in coal shipments from South Africa and Indonesia, attributed to weaker seasonal demand for thermal coal.
