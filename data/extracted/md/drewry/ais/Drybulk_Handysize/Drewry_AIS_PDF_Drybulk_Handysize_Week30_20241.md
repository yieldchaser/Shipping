### Expansion in utilisation of Handysize vessels

1 content_summary

* The utilisation of Handyszie vessels expanded last week to reach 93.2% from 92.6% the week before.

* Meanwhile, the tonnemile index continued to be on a downward trend, declining for the second consecutive week.

* The laden tonnage at anchor in China was buoyant as Russia continued to ship coal from its ports in the Far East to China.

* China's coal inventory has been high MoM but lower YoY. With buoyant demand for electricity, the trade on this route is projected to be significant in the near-term.
