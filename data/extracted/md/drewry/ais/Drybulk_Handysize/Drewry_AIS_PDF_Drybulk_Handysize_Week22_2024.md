### Ample ballasters in the West
1 content_summary

* Drewry's tonnemile index edged up WoW but remained much below last year's levels.

* While the share of laden tonnage increased WoW in the East, the share dropped to its weakest level in 2024 in the West.

* Buoyant demand for minor bulk is supporting Handysize demand, leading to expansion in tonnage waiting to discharge cargo at China's ports.

* The tonnage underway in the West continues to be buoyant as in the first quarter, with improvement in the intra-EU grain trade as well as in exports from Ukraine. But with ample ballast vessels in the West, rates continue to be dampened.
