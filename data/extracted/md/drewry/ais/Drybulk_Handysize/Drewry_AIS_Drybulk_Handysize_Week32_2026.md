### Handysize rates remain under pressure as tonnage increases across both basins

- The Handysize tonne-miles index edged down 5% to reach 116.2 in week 32, with rates continuing to move differently across both basins. Limited fresh enquiry and an increasing pool of vessel availability kept charterers in control, while the Atlantic saw further weakness in ECSA and softer activity in the US Gulf. The Continent and Mediterranean remained subdued, with only limited scrap and cement-related demand. The speed also slowed down across the east and the west.
- The US Gulf faced the sharpest correction as a lack of new cargoes and a growing number of vessels weighed on fronthaul and transatlantic rates. The Continent also softened amid weaker Turkish scrap and fertiliser demand, while the East Mediterranean remained comparatively resilient, supported by new grain cargoes and stronger premiums for owners willing to trade via the Red Sea.
- The Pacific lost some momentum as enquiries from North Asia eased and vessel availability increased across Southeast Asia and East Coast India. However, steady Chinese demand for Indonesian coal, continued South African cargoes in the Indian Ocean, and improving Australian export activity helped in limiting the further downside.

## Glossary

- **Handysize vessels:** Small Dry Bulk vessels, and are in the size range 10,000-40,000 dwt.
- **Trading status:** Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen.
- **Laden dwt miles:** Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight (or capacity)
- **Bunker price:** Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port
- **% Laden:** Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair
- **East:** East to Suez Canal, comprising China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania
- **West:** West of Suez Canal, comprising Atlantic region
- **HS4_38:** Spot rates from Baltic Exchange on the US Gulf or north coast South America to Skaw-Passero route
- **HS7_38:** Spot rates from Baltic Exchange for North China/South Korea/Japan to South East Asia route
- **At anchor:** Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area
- **Moored:** Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging
- **Underway:** Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination
