## Market Commentary

### Atlantic strength offsets softer Asian sentiments in Handysize market

- The Drewry's Tonne-miles index rose WoW to 119.2, with sentiment turning more positional as September cargoes emerged. The Atlantic led the improvement, particularly ECSA and the US Gulf, while the Continent and Mediterranean remained quiet but showed firmer rates towards the end of the week. The global laden speed also increased in tandem.

- The South Atlantic remained the strongest area, supported by continued grain demand and tightening available tonnage. The US Gulf also improved towards the end, with fixtures around $20,000/day for transatlantic business, while stronger demolitions enquiry helped lift sentiment in the Continent and Mediterranean.

- The Pacific market started slowly but gained some momentum in the Far East, where a relatively balanced tonnage supported rates. However, Southeast Asia remained subdued, and the Far East continued to face limited cargo availability, keeping charterers in a strong position. Period enquiry remained healthy, with increased interest in employment of up to one year.
