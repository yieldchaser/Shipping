## Market Commentary

### Weak demand and high tonnage supply to soften freight rates

- The Aframax tonne-miles index fell over the week ending at 89.9 points, weaker by 10 points YoY. Markets were generally on the quieter side which left Aframax vessels having to compete with even the larger sized VLCCs and Suezmaxes for employment.

- The vessels at anchor in the East and the West showed an 11% and 14% MoM increase respectively as tonnage demand waned further towards the end of the year.

- Freight rates were generally softer in the Med due to an oversupply of tonnage. Conditions are likely to remain similar in the next week with freight rates likely to soften further.
