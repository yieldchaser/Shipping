## Market Commentary

### Charterers seek coverage prior to Libya's resolution to drive rates

- Aframax rates will go up in the coming weeks as the charters are demanding coverage ahead of Libya's resolution. The rates on cross-Med and Black Sea-Med routes increased last week due to the same reason.

- The activity was stable in the Atlantic region, with a sufficient number of tonnage available, pressuring the rates in the region, while the rates on AG-East (TD8) declined due to low trading activity.

- Global ballast speed decreased by 3.6% in week 37, while global laden speed experienced an uptick. The eastern market remains stronger than the western market, with increased vessel movement. As we approach winter, rates are expected to strengthen further.

Glossary

| S No. | Terms           | Define                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1     | Aframax vessel  | Crude tanker vessels which have dwt in the range of 80,000 - 120,000.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |
| 3     | Laden dwt miles | Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight(or capacity) for that fleet                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 4     | Bunker price    | Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 5     | % Laden         | Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 6     | East            | Region East of Suez Canal and comprises China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 7     | West            | region West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
| 8     | TD7             | Spot rates on North Sea–UK Continent route and data have been taken from Baltic Exchange                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 9     | TD8             | Spot rates on Kuwait-Singapore route and data have been taken from Baltic Exchange                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 10    | At anchor       | Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 11    | Moored          | Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 12    | Underway        | Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
