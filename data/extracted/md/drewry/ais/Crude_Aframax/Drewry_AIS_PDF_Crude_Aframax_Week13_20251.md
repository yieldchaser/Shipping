## Market Commentary

### Aframax market dwindles amid lack of tonnage demand

- The Aframax tonne-miles index declined by 6.3 points ending at 88.5 points in week 10. Though the week started steady in regions such as the Med and the UKC, market activity waned as the week progressed resulting in a softening in freight rates.

- The Aframax market remained quiet on the cross-Atlantic, with a deep fall in the tonnage underway and more vessels gathering at the ports.

- Some fresh demand might be seen next week which could move up freight rates in the East. While an uptick in activity in Libya towards the end of the month remains hopeful, freight rates in the Med market are likely to remain weak next week.
