## Market Commentary

### Gains in the USG-Caribs offsets weak activity in the Med

- The tonne-miles index improves in week 24, ending the week at 122.3 points. The short-haul runs around the Med showed weaker activity driving down rates in the region with more ballast vessels being available in the region.

- Off the Med, there was tighter activity towards the UKC while most gains for Aframaxes came from the USG to Caribs route. Week 25 is likely to see a continuation of firmness in Aframax demand in the region which could see spot rates flattening. An increase in Canada's oil exports could also push up tonnage demand in the near term.

- Meanwhile, tonnage demand towards the East has not been active, as spot rates also saw a decline. This could result in more tonnage accumulating in the MEG next week.
