## Market Commentary

### Aframax tonnage demand to be firm next week

- The Aframax market ended on a high in week 40 with rates increasing on most routes towards the end of the week. However, as markets were slow in the previous week, the tonne-miles index ended the week at 99.4 points.

- Recent developments in the Middle East caused many vessels to depart for other routes, spiking up rates in the region. Meanwhile, the reopening of Libyan oil ports is expected to drive up the tonnage demand in the region.

- The markets in the USG saw some gains in week 40 as most vessels were able to find employment. However, incoming ballast tonnage could curb the growth in freight rates next week.

| S No. | Terms           | Define                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1     | Aframax vessel  | Crude tanker vessels which have dwt in the range of 80,000 - 120,000.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |
| 3     | Laden dwt miles | Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight(or capacity) for that fleet                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 4     | Bunker price    | Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 5     | % Laden         | Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

| 7     | West            | region West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          |
