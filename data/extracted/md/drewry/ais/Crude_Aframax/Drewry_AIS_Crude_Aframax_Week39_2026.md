### Aframax market softens amid lower congestion and weak tonne-miles

- Aframax utilisation eased to 89.9% during Week 39, down 1 percentage point MoM and YoY, while the tonne-mile index remained weak at 90.7, down 12.1% YoY. Lower tonne-mile demand continues to weigh on market, although utilisation remains close to the key 90% level, suggesting fleet employment is still relatively healthy.
- East of Suez remained the stronger market, with the share of laden vessels rising by 3.6 percentage points MoM, reflecting steady cargo activity. However, declining anchored capacity and higher ballast speeds suggest vessel availability is improving as fleet repositioning outpaces demand growth, reducing market tightness across key loading regions.
- Congestion continued to ease across major trading regions, supporting faster vessel turnaround and higher fleet productivity. Lower anchorage levels, particularly in Asian discharge areas, combined with elevated sailing speeds despite higher bunker prices, effectively increased available tonnage and limited support for spot freight rates.

## Glossary

- **Aframax vessel:** Crude tanker vessels which have dwt in the range of 80,000 - 120,000.
- **Trading status:** Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen.
- **Laden dwt miles:** Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight(or capacity) for that fleet
- **Bunker price:** Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port
- **% Laden:** Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair
- **East:** Region East of Suez Canal and comprises China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania
- **West:** region West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region
- **TD7:** Spot rates on North Sea–UK Continent route and data have been taken from Baltic Exchange
- **TD8:** Spot rates on Kuwait-Singapore route and data have been taken from Baltic Exchange
- **At anchor:** Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area
- **Moored:** Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging
- **Underway:** Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination
