## Market Commentary

### Aframax demand holds firm as Atlantic strength counters Eastern weakness

- Aframax utilisation strengthened to 92.4%, confirming that vessel demand remains robust supported by recent correction in spot earnings. Tonne mile demand increased WoW but it remained lower as compared to last year.

- Transatlantic crude movements continued to support Aframax employment, particularly for regional and short-haul cargoes. Meanwhile, easing tensions between the US and Iran have helped restore confidence in Middle East trading routes, encouraging some vessel repositioning back into the region. However, owners remain cautious as geopolitical risks and elevated insurance costs continue to influence routing and deployment decisions.

- Softer demand in China and parts of the East is being balanced by healthier cargo activity in the Atlantic and resilient Indian import requirements.
