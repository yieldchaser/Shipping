## Market Commentary

### Firm trade in the UKC supports Aframax demand

- The tonne-miles index saw a slight decline in week 31 ending the week at 104.9 points. Despite the fewer tonne-miles, the highlight of last week's activity was towards the UKC with freight rates gaining WoW. Rates on Med to Black Sea route also saw some firmness in the rates as tonnage tightened towards the end of the week. Week 31 might however see some weakness due to the arrival of more ballast vessels.

- Towards the East, markets remained quieter due to low activity, but rates did not show much movement as tonnage supply remained weak. Market activity is likely to remain subdued next week.

- Prevailing monsoon conditions have further dampened the demand for refined products and is likley to keep trade low especially towards Asia for the next few weeks.
