## Aframax utilisation remained resilient despite softer spot rates.

1 content_summary

* Aframax utilisation remained firm at 92.4%, supported by a strengthening tonne-mile index that rose to 100.5, despite spot rates easing from the peaks recorded in recent months.

* Cargo movements within the Atlantic Basin remained the primary driver of demand, supporting fleet utilization and partially offsetting weaker long-haul activity in other regions.

* Laden tonnage at anchor remained elevated across key eastern discharge regions, particularly India and the Pacific, reflecting sustained crude inflows into major consuming markets and underpinning demand for Aframax tankers.

* Despite bunker prices rising to around $785/tonne, vessel speeds remained largely unchanged suggesting owners are prioritizing cargo acquisition over fuel cost optimization, supported by expectations of robust earnings.
