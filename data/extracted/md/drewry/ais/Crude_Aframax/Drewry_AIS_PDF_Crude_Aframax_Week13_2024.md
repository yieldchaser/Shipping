## Market Commentary

### Firm markets activity in the Med expected to support tonnage demand next week

- Week 12 saw an uptick in the tonne-miles index even as tonnage demand in the Aframax market remained relatively quieter. The index rose to 122.5 points, up by 1.9 from the previous week.

- Rates towards the East of Suez especially in the Med increased further last week as the market activity in the region remained relatively firm. However, this has attracted several ballasters towards the region which could weigh on freight rates next week.

- Market activity towards the East was relatively muted causing freight rates to remain somewhat stable. However, China has been a top importer of Russian oil this year, which will continue to keep market activity active next week.
