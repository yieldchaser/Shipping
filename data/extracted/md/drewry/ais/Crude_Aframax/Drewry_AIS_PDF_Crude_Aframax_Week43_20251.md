## Market Commentary

### New US sanctions on Russia push up freight rates

- The Aframax tonne-miles index increased to 97.3 in week 43, continuing to rise from the previous week. While market activity in the Aframax market started slowly, it picked up pace by the end of the week, supporting freight rates.
