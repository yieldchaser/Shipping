## Market Commentary

### Weakness in Western CPC demand weighs on freight rates

- The Aframax tonne-miles index ended the week at 83.8 points, declining for another week since the start of the year. The market lull also pulled down the global average speeds of both ballast and laden vessels as utilisation declined due to weak tonnage demand.

- Freight rates towards the UKC region softened as demand continued to be weak. The rates on Med, however, started off the week strong but dwindled towards the end of the week as weakness in demand in the larger tanker segments also trickled down to Aframaxes.

- only the MEG to East held up throughout the week, as freight rates increased WoW, resulting in tighter tonnage in the MEG. Freight rates on this route will likely remain steady next week, while the number of ballast vessels headed towards the MEG will likely see an uptick.
