## Market Commentary

### High tonnage demand to support freight rates in the East

- The LR2 tonne-miles index weakened for the second week, ending at 117.6 points. Though freight rates mostly firmed up, the tonnage underway fell in both the East and the West.

- Towards the West of Suez, there was steady market activity; however, the tonnage supply outpaced the demand. If market conditions remain the same next week, freight rates are likely to weaken.

- The markets towards the East will likely remain firm next week, supported by an increase in vessel loadings, which will keep freight rates firm.
