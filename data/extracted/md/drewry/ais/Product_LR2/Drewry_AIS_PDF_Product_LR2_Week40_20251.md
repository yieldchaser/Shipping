## Market Commentary

### Increased Eastern demand pushes up freight rates

- The LR2 tonne-miles index improved in week 40, ending the week at 120.8 points, supported by firm market activity in the previous weeks. The % laden as a result improved by around 3% in both the East and the West.

- Towards the West, it was a quieter week despite resulting in freight rates dampening. Ballast speeds have also slowed down as a result, and the momentum is likely to continue next week.

- Freight rates increased in the East as market activity firmed up, especially on the MEG-Japan route. Current market conditions are likely to prevail next week as well, supporting freight rates.
