## Market Commentary

### Subdued LR2 demand keeps freight rates steady

- It was a quieter week in the LR2 market, with the tonne-miles index ending the week at 108.8 points. Vessels sailed slower last week in both the East and the West as tonnage demand also weakened. Rates remained more or less stable throughout with slight gains on TC8.

- The tonnage supply exceeded the cargo list towards the end resulting in the tonnage in port increasing in both the East and the West. A slight uptick in tonnage underway in the West resulted from a relatively healthy tonnage movement off the West Coast of Africa.

- LR2 activity towards the East of Suez is likely to continue having a meek outlook in week 29 with tonnage demand remaining subdued. Ongoing maintenance in several plants will keep LR2 demand dampened.
