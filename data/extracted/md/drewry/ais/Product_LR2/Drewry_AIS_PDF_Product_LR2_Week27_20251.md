## Freight rates weaken while vessel speeds decline

* The LR2 tonne miles index remained stable at 107.7 points last week as the LR2 markets remained lackluster in both the East and the West. The global vessel speeds have been on the decline for both laden and ballast vessels indicating weaker market conditions.

* The markets remained soft throughout the week in the East as well and the West. The East of Suez could however see some firming up of trade next week as tonnage supply is likely to decline with more vessels heading elsewhere.
