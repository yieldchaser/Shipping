## Market Commentary

### Weak market activity dampens freight rates

- It was a slow week for the CPP market, resulting in declining rates among various product tanker segments. However, the LR2 tonne-mile index gained four points, ending the week at 106.4 owing to an increase in tonnage underway.

- The tonnage demand was weak in both the East and the West, which resulted in freight rates declining on most routes.

- Market sentiments for next week remain positive despite the softness in week 37. This is expected to support freight rates next week, especially in the East.
