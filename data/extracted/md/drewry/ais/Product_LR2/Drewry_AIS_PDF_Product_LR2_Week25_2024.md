## Market Commentary

### LR2 market activity to remain firm in the West

- The tonne-miles index declined in week 24 to 106.1 points as the week started off with weaker market activity. There was an increase in tonnage demand towards the latter half of the week which is likely to continue in week 25.

- The market activity towards the West was generally firmer than the East, despite freight rates weakening on both sides. A positive outlook among charterers in the MEG for Westbound cargoes is likely to support the LR2 demand in week 25.

- The laden speed increased in both the East and the West, though tonnage demand is likely to remain soft on Eastbound routes due to weaker demand.
