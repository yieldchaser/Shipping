## Market Commentary

### Weak LR2 demand dampens sentiments

- The LR2 market had a slow week crushing the positive sentiments at the start of the week. Despite the weakness in the tonnage demand, the tonne-miles index saw a minute increase of 1.2 points ending the week at 117.4 points.

- Demand was generally weak, with few enquiries and low activity. Towards both the East and West, the CPP markets remained quieter, with much of the vessel movement coming from the previous weeks.

- There remains much uncertainty regarding next week, but markets towards the East seem more likely to recover as tonnage seems to be heading Eastward.
