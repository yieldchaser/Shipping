## Weak Asia demand to continue next week

\* It was a softer week for the LR2 market though the tonne-mile index saw an uptick ending the week at 117.3 points. The CPP market was quite subdued overall as tonnage demand was weak amid the low availability of cargoes.

\* Prices in the MEG-Japan route saw a downward revision which could remain weak in week 31. Typhoon Gemini in northern Tawan could continue to cause some congestion in the region, however rates are likely to remain unaffected due to the seasonal lull in the market.

\* Week 31 has a faint hope for a revival in tonnage demand towards the West but freight rates are likely to remain subdued.
