## Market Commentary

### LR2 demand softens amid improving vessel turnaround

- LR2 utilisation stood at 89.7%, weakening on MoM basis by 2.5 percentage points and YoY basis by 1.8 percentage points, indicating that fleet supply has slightly outpaced demand. While the share of laden vessels increased by 4 percentage points MoM in the east, supporting utilisation in the key Middle East loading regions, a 2.8 percentage points decline in the west points at softer Atlantic market conditions

- Congestion trends provide limited support to freight rates, with laden anchor days down 22.5% MoM, indicating faster vessel turnaround and improved operational efficiency. Congestion eased notably in China, while the Indian Ocean and Atlantic remain the main areas of laden vessels at anchor, increasing effective fleet supply as vessels spend less time in queues.

- Global tonnage at anchor rose 0.8 mdwt on a four-week moving average, while ballast tonnage remains concentrated in the Atlantic, where anchored ballast vessels are well above 2024 levels and recently reached around 6 mdwt. Healthy positioning in the Indian Ocean and Pacific contrasts with the Atlantic build-up, suggesting vessel supply is accumulating faster than demand.
