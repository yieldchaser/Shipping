## Market Commentary

### LR2 market to remain soft

- The LR2 tonne miles index increased by close to 7 points in week 24, ending at 109 points. It was a slow week for the LR2 market in both the East and the West, as freight rates softened due to the higher tonnage supply.

- The global vessel speeds for ballast and laden vessels showed an uptick, which could indicate an improvement in market activity for LRs in the next week. Freight rates will likely remain sideways in week 25, but there will be potential for improvement towards the West.
