## Market Commentary

### Healthy loing-haul flows sustain LR2 market strength

- LR2 market conditions strengthened further during the week, with utilisation rising to 92.8%, up 1.4 percentage points MoM, while the tonne-mile index climbed to 130.6, up 9% YoY. Strong long-haul product flows continued to absorb available tonnage, keeping fleet employment elevated and supporting a tighter supply-demand balance across the sector.

- In the East of Suez, vessel activity stayed firm despite a marginal decline in the laden vessel share. Ballast tonnage in the Indian Ocean remained elevated while vessel speeds increased, suggesting active repositioning towards cargo-rich regions. Meanwhile, Atlantic laden congestion continued to build, suggesting stronger discharge activity and localised supply absorption.

- Rising anchorage volumes and sustained cargo employment offset the gradual increase in vessel availability across some western markets. Global tonnage at anchor increased on a four-week moving average basis, while utilisation remained above 92%, suggesting that congestion and healthy cargo demand continue to limit effective fleet supply and underpin LR2 market.
