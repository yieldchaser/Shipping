## LR2 market supported by strong tonne-mile demand despite fall in utilisation

* LR2 utilisation declined by 1.8 percentage points to 90.3% WoW and by 1 percentage point on YoY basis, while the tonne-mile index rose by 8.5% to 122.3 YoY, suggesting vessel demand being driven more by voyage length than clean products volume growth.

* Laden anchor days rose by 14.5% on a 4-week moving average basis, while the share of laden vessels rose by 7.4 ppercentage points in the east and 5.8 percentage points in the west, suggesting elevated congestion across the Atlantic and Pacific basin.

* Despite a 12.8% MoM fall in bunker price to $691/tonne, the ballast speed in the east fell by 3.9% MoM, though it rose in the west by 7.3%.
