## LR2 market tightens as utilisation strengthens and congestion limits effective supply

* LR2 market strengthened in Week 36, with fleet utilisation rising to 91.8%, up 1.2 percentage points MoM and above 2024 and 2025 seasonal averages. The tonne-mile index reached 125.6, up 6.7% YoY, reflecting strong long-haul flows and a relatively tight supply-demand balance.

* Supply tightened further as congestion increased. Global tonnage at anchor rose by 0.8 mdwt on a four-week average, while laden anchor days increased 4.6%. Atlantic congestion remained elevated, with laden tonnage above 4 mdwt and ballast tonnage near 6 mdwt, limiting effective spot-market vessel availability.

* Fleet productivity remained constrained despite a 0.8% MoM decline in bunker prices to $838/tonne. Ballast speeds rose 3.5% MoM in the East but fell 6.4% in the West. Higher waiting times and congestion continued to restrict effective fleet supply, supporting LR2 freight earnings.
