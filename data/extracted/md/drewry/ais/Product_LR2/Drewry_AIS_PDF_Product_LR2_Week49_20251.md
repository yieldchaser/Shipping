## LR2 market shows recovery signs with rising utilisation

* The LR2 tonne-miles index held nearly steady at 118.7 in Week 49, continuing its recovery after the dip in Week 46.

* Ballast speeds rose to 9.78 knots, while laden speeds softened to 10.87 knots, indicating a stabilising market with expectations of higher utilisation ahead. The share of laden LR2 vessels increased in the East, with a modest rise also observed in the West.

* In the LR2 market, MEG–Japan rates slipped and the widening rate gap versus LR1s is expected to draw more charterers towards LR2 tonnage.
