## Market Commentary

### LR1 freight rates likely to weaken next week

- The LR1 tonne-miles index increased for the fourth week, ending week 32 at 108.2 points. Rates firmed up despite being a quieter week overall, owing to a push from other segments.

- The week started firm in the East but weakened towards the end. Due to low tonnage demand, freight rates could weaken next week.
