## Market Commentary

### Freight rates climb higher despite weaker market activity

- The tonne-miles index fell in week 12 to 109.2 points as market activity remained weak towards the West of Suez. Spot rates however climbed up on most routes due to lower tonnage supply and a firming market towards the East.

- The global ballast and laden speed declined in the East and the West, however ballast speed in the East showed a slight uptick. The CPP markets are likely to remain muted next week towards the West but could soon bounce up as some refineries in Europe have face outages.

- Trade activity towards the East will likely remain robust for the next few weeks, continuing to support freight rates on Asia routes.
