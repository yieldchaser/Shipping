## Market Commentary

### LRs freight rates continue to slip due to low demand

- Though shaded by weakness in tonnage demand, it was a mixed week for LR2s. Freight rates declined overall; however, the tonne-miles index improved by 11 points, ending the week at 105.6.

- Towards the East, the market was slow as speeds declined while the tonnage at anchor surged. Towards the West, some tonnage was absorbed by stronger markets towards West Africa; however, rates continued to decline. Market conditions are likely to remain unchanged next week.
