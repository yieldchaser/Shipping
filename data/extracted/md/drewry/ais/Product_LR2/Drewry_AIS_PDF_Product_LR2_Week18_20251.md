## Market Commentary

### A slow week for LR2s due to low tonnage demand

- Week 18 was a week of sluggish demand in both the East and the West. As a result, the tonne-miles index fell to 116.2 points after having peaked in the previous week. Both laden and ballast speeds also declined in the East as well as the West.
