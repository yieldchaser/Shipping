## LR2s continue to be plagued by weak demand

* The LR2 tonne-miles Index continued to decline in week 36 ending the week at 107.6 points. The CPP market continued to be quiet due a to a lack of cargoes as indicated by the WoW decline in the %laden in both the East and the West.

* Despite the slow week, LR2s fared better than most of their counterparts. The tonnage demand towards the West remained flat with a short holiday in the US delaying cargo movements. Freight rates also continued to decline further and are likely to remain under the same conditions next week.

* The CPP market towards the East fared better but was not enough to ramp up rates. Nevertheless, it allowed for some pressure to be taken off LR1 and MRs. Next week has the potential for an upturn as the seasonal demand is ought to increase soon.
