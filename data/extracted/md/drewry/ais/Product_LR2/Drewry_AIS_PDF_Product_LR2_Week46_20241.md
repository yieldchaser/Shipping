## Market Commentary

### LR2 market likely to remain weak

- The LR2 market had a slow week with the tonne miles index falling to 110.1 in week 45. The weak tonnage demand led to rates falling WoW on most routes. LR2 demand might be further impacted in week 46 due to the Bahri week.

- There has been close to a 50% increase in the vessels in port in November due to weak tonnage demand which has caused more ballast vessels to remain at the ports. The short haul trade has been more prominent in recent weeks causing voyage days to remain shorter on average.

- The LR2 market is likely to remain weak as the refined product demand continues to be on the softer side. It is likely that rates decline further in week 46.
