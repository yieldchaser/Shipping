| 1     | LR2 vessel      | Long range coated vessels above 80,000 dwt                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO numbers have not been included in the fleet.
