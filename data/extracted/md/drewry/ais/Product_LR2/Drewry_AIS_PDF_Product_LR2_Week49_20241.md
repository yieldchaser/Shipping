## Market Commentary

### Tight tonnage supply pushes up freight rates

- The LR2 tonne-mile Index declined further in week 48, ending the week at 99,7 points. It had been previously declining since week 43. While tonnage demand was not too strong, the lack of available tonnage and a firm LR1 market amid sparse repopulation aided the LR2 freight rates to increase. The USG market quietened down due to the Thanksgiving holiday but is expected to become more active from week 49, boosting LR2 rates towards the West.

- On the East side, the MEG-Japan route also saw an uptick in the spot rates. Tonnage demand for LR2s was firm but next week might see some dampening due to lack of cargo flow. Freight rates are likely to move in tandem as more vessels move Eastward.
