## Market Commentary

### Tonnage demand in the East to support freight rates

- The LR2 tonne-miles index ended the week at 115.1 points, rising for the 4<sup>th</sup> consecutive week. Meanwhile vessel utilisation has also been picking up despite being lower MoM.

- It was a quiet week towards the West as tonnage demand remained weak, though the markets towards the East were mostly positive, though freight rates firmed up on both sides.

- The %laden grew by 5% in the East, while rates on TC1 increased from WS142 to WS165. However, market sentiments weakened towards the end of the week, which could result in lower gains in freight rates in week 13.
