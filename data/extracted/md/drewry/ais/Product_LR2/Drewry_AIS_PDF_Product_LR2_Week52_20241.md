## Market Commentary

### An influx of ballast vessels headed East could dampen rates next week

- With the end of 2024 approaching, the market activity seems to be on the weaker side. The tonne-miles index declined further, ending the week at 112 points. Limited tonnage demand, especially in the West, is also likely to keep utilisation between the 86-87% mark in the next week.

- The East of Suez saw relatively steadier markets accompanied by an uptick in both laden and ballast speeds. However, some tightness in the market could be loosening up soon as more ballast vessels head to the ports, increasing the tonnage supply.

| S No. | Terms           | Define                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| ----- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | LR2 vessel      | Long range coated vessels above 80,000 dwt                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO numbers have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |
| 3     | Laden dwt miles | Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight (or capacity)                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 4     | Bunker price    | Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 5     | % Laden         | Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| 6     | East            | East of Suez Canal, comprising China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 7     | West            | West of Suez Canal, comprising Atlantic region                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 8     | TC1             | Spot rates from Baltic Exchange on ME Gulf-Japan route                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 9     | TC8             | Spot rates from Baltic Exchange on ME Gulf-UK Continent route                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 10    | At anchor       | Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
| 11    | Moored          | Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 12    | Underway        | Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     |
