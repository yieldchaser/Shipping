## Market Commentary

### Weakness in the CPP market continues in week 33

- The tonne-miles index rose in week 33 ending the week at 120.5 points. Despite the stretched voyages due to the Red Sea crises, the clean products market has been facing a weakness in demand. Much of it is also due to seasonal factors such as monsoons, which dampen the demand for transportation fuels.

- The soft demand for tonnage has weighed on the freight rates in both the East and the West. The laden speed has however been increasing in both the East and the West while an uptick in the ballast speed towards the West after several weeks of decline could suggest some upcoming improvements in the market next week.
