## Lower utilisation reflects softer demand and expanding fleet availability for LR2 segment

* LR2 utilisation declined by 0.8 percentage points to 90.7%, while the tonne-mile index fell 12.3% YoY to 109.7, suggesting moderation in vessel demand compared to the elevated levels last year.

* The share of laden vessels fell by 8.6 percentage points in the east and 1 percentage point in the west MoM, while global ballast tonnage at anchor continued to be at high level suggesting growth in available tonnage, contributing to modest increase in vessel availability.

* Despite bunker prices rising by 11.9% MoM to $785/tonne, ballast speeds increased by 7.7% MoM in both regions, expanding effective fleet supply and adding pressure on utilisation.
