### LR2 market tightens as speeds diverge and rates rise

- The LR2 tonne-miles index slipped to 109.4 in week 46. During the same period, ballast speeds increased to 9.65 knots, while laden speeds eased to 10.70 knots reflecting softer market activity and steadily declining utilisation.

- LR2 freight rates posted a week-on-week gain, supported by a tight balance between supply and demand and robust crude market fundamentals. This strength was evident in the firming of MEG–Japan route rates.

## Glossary

- **LR2 vessel:** Long range coated vessels above 80,000 dwt
- **Trading status:** Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO numbers have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen.
- **Laden dwt miles:** Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight (or capacity)
- **Bunker price:** Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port
- **% Laden:** Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair
- **East:** East of Suez Canal, comprising China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania
- **West:** West of Suez Canal, comprising Atlantic region
- **TC1:** Spot rates from Baltic Exchange on ME Gulf-Japan route
- **TC8:** Spot rates from Baltic Exchange on ME Gulf-UK Continent route
- **At anchor:** Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area
- **Moored:** Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging
- **Underway:** Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination
