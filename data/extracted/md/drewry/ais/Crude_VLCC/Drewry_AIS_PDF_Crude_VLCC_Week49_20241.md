## Market Commentary

### Freight rates weaken over increased tonnage supply

- The VLCC tonne-mile index improved slightly in week 48 as the %laden towards the West saw an uptick with several tonnage being fixed in the previous weeks.

- Freight rates on the other hand saw a big dip on most major routes especially in the AG as surplus tonnage gathered in the region. The ex-USG routes also saw a decline in activity with Thanksgiving week dampening trade in the region. West Africa and Brazil to China saw a relatively busier week.

- The market activity next week is likely to remain soft, weighing on the freight rates. Some improvement might be seen towards the West as the markets in the West Coast Atlantic regions resume activity.
