## VLCC utilisation stable but momentum weakens on idle build-up

1 content_summary

* VLCC utilisation stood at 89.2%, up slightly by 0.6 percentage points week-on-week, though still 1.1 percentage points below last year, indicating the market remains somewhat softer on a yearly basis. Spot earnings on the benchmark TD3C route hovered around 476 WS, showing that while rates have eased from recent peaks, they continue to hold at relatively firm levels.

* Even though bunker prices have dropped sharply, VLCC speeds have only adjusted modestly and vary by region, suggesting that subdued crude demand and excess vessel capacity—not fuel costs—are currently shaping the movement of ships.

* Rising ballast tonnage at anchor in the West and increasing idle vessels highlight a build-up of prompt tonnage near load regions (US Gulf, West Africa).

* The increase in ballast vessels in regions like the Atlantic and China suggests that ships are struggling to find their next cargo, leading to longer idle times and a sustained excess of available tonnage in the market.
