## Positive week for VLCCs despite despite a fall in tonne-miles

* The VLCC tonne-miles index fell further in week 33 ending the week at 112 points. Despite the decline, the freight rates increased on most routes as plenty of cargo was available, boosting the tonnage demand.

* The crude trade towards the East was robust, especially in the second half of the week. The Arabian Gulf to India/China routes saw some big gains in rates as the week went by. The positive sentiments are likely to remain in week 34 as well.

* Firm crude demand in Asia has also had a positive impact on US Gulf to East routes. Even ex-Brazil rates saw a rise which is likely to attract more ballasters in week 35 along the Eastrn Coastlines. VLCC demand as a whole is likely to remain firm next week.
