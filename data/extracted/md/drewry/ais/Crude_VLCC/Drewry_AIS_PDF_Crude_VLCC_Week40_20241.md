## Market Commentary

### VLCC markets remain subdued but hopeful

- The VLCC market started off with high sentiments, which phased out towards the end of the week. The tonne-miles index weakened, ending the week at 101.3 points. The markets in the Far East could see further softening next week as the Golden Week celebrations are already around the corner. However, they could see a revival once the celebrations end.

- Firm demand was observed in the US-Latin America routes, but hurricanes have remained a deterrent to trade in the US Gulf regions. Meanwhile, West African markets remain positive despite a subdued week, as winter demand is expected to soon drive up tonnage demand.
