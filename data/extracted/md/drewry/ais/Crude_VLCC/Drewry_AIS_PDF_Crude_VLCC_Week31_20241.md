## Market Commentary

### VLCC demand continues to be buoyed by China

- The VLCC tonne-miles index ended the week at 108.5 points. Apart from a slight dip in week 25, it has been on a continuous rise as demand especially towards the East has been positive. Following the positive trend, week 30 also saw the market strengthening, bumping up rates on the MEG-China route.

- There was great enthusiasm for VLCC tonnage at the start of the week but waned towards the end as markets in the Atlantic showed progressively weaker interest. Week 31 is likely to retain these weakened sentiments in the Westbound trade.

- The West Africa to China trade also seems hopeful for next week which will further support freight rates in the East.

Glossary

Week 31 | 2024

| S No. | Terms           | Define                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1     | VLCC vessel     | All Crude tankers which are above 200,000 dwt.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |
| 3     | Laden dwt miles | Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight(or capacity) for that fleet                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 4     | Bunker price    | Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 5     | % Laden         | Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 6     | East            | East of Suez Canal and comprises China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 7     | West            | West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 8     | TD1             | Spot rates on the ME Gulf-US Gulf route; the data has been taken from the Baltic Exchange.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| 9     | TD3C            | Spot rates on the ME Gulf-China route; the data has been taken from the Baltic Exchange.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 10    | At anchor       | Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 11    | Moored          | Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 12    | Underway        | Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
