## Market Commentary

### Seasonal weakness in demand weighs on VLCC freight

- The VLCC tonne miles index fell in week 27 ending at 97.1 points. The VLCC market seems to have been affected by the seasonal weakness in demand as freight rates declined on most routes. After the brief surge in rates during mid-June due to the Israel-Iran conflict, rates have been on the decline. With a seasonal lull ahead, weak tonnage demand will continue weigh on freight rates.

- The %laden in the East declined while it increased marginally towards the West. However, with tonnage underway declining on both sides, market activity will remain weak in week 28.
