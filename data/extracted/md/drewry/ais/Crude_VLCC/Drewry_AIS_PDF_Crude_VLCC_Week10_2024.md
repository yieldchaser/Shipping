## Market Commentary

### Subdued market activity could persist next week

- The tonne-miles index continued to increase last week from its upturn in week 4 to end the week at 10.5 points. The crude market had a quiet week leading to the softening of rates on most routes. AG-China route declined by $3/day in week 9.

- Weak tonnage demand in the East led to a few vessels ballasting West in the hopes of higher activity next week. Muted demand in the USG is likely to continue next week while more ballasters are travelling Westbound. This could lead to rates softening further in week 10.

- The global ballast and laden speed declined in both the East and the West as activity in the crude market waned. Due to more vessels heading westward, congestion eased up in Chinese ports, leading to tonnage gathering in the Atlantic.
