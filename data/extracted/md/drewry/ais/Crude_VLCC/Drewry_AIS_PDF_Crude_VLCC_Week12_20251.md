## Market Commentary

### Low tonnage supply to keeps freight rates high

- The VLCC tonne-miles index reached a new high this year, ending week 12 at 106.4 points. Rates have been firming up overall, especially towards the West, as new geopolitical events unfold and the US clamps down on more vessels involved in trading sanctioned crude.

- An uptick in vessels at port in the East could keep rates sideways next week while ballast vessels heading towards the MEG increase.
