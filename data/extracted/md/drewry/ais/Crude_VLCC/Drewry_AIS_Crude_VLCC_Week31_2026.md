### Atlantic Basin congestion continues to absorb prompt VLCC tonnage and support freight rates.

- The VLCC market remained firm, as utilisation stood at 90.8% despite slowdown in tonne-mile demand. With the tonne-mile index falling to 82.2, the persistence of strong vessel employment highlights the role of congestion, vessel positioning inefficiencies and tighter effective supply in underpinning freight market performance.
- Effective VLCC supply remained constrained by persistent congestion particularly across the Atlantic Basin. A sharp increase in western anchorage volumes, combined with higher global tonnage waiting at anchor, continued to absorb available capacity and support spot freight markets.
- Asia continued to underpin global VLCC activity, with China, India and the broader Pacific Basin accounting for the largest concentration of both laden and ballast tonnage. While congestion at key discharge hubs has eased from the elevated levels seen earlier in the year, anchorage volumes remain above historical averages, reflecting sustained crude import demand across the region.

## Glossary

- **VLCC vessel:** All Crude tankers which are above 200,000 dwt.
- **Trading status:** Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen.
- **Laden dwt miles:** Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight(or capacity) for that fleet
- **Bunker price:** Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port
- **% Laden:** Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair
- **East:** East of Suez Canal and comprises China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania
- **West:** West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region
- **TD3C:** Spot rates on the ME Gulf-China route; the data has been taken from the Baltic Exchange.
- **TD22:** Spot rates on the US Gulf-China route; the data has been taken from the Baltic Exchange.
- **At anchor:** Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area
- **Moored:** Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging
- **Underway:** Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination
