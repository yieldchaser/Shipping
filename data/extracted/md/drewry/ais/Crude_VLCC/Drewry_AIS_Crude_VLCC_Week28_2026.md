## Market Commentary

### Resilient Asian import demand and Atlantic exports support VLCC activity

- VLCC utilisation stood at 89.6% indicating stronger vessel employment as more ships moved from waiting positions into active trading. Underlying demand remained supported by long-haul crude movements from the Atlantic Basin to Asia. Tonne-mile index remained above the annual baseline at 103.3 and was 7.3% higher than a year ago, indicating that voyage distances continue to support vessel demand.

- Asian demand remained relatively resilient. Chinese discharge activity stabilised after recent weakness, while India continued to demonstrate steady crude import requirements. The Pacific region maintained healthy levels of laden vessel activity, reinforcing Asia's role as the primary demand centre for VLCCs.

- Continued crude exports from the US Gulf, Brazil and West Africa, combined with stable Asian import requirements, helped maintain a favourable demand environment for VLCCs.

VLCC

Glossary

Week 28 | 2026

| S No. | Terms           | Define                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1     | VLCC vessel     | All Crude tankers which are above 200,000 dwt.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |
| 3     | Laden dwt miles | Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight(or capacity) for that fleet                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 4     | Bunker price    | Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 5     | % Laden         | Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 6     | East            | East of Suez Canal and comprises China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 7     | West            | West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 8     | TD3C            | Spot rates on the ME Gulf-China route; the data has been taken from the Baltic Exchange.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 9     | TD22            | Spot rates on the US Gulf-China route; the data has been taken from the Baltic Exchange.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| 10    | At anchor       | Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 11    | Moored          | Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 12    | Underway        | Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
