## VLCC demand to improve soon
1 content_summary

* The VLCC tonne-miles Index declined further in week 36 after falling since week 31 except for a slight upturn last week. The index ended the week at 104.2 points, just about a point higher YoY. Nevertheless, the crude market is at the eve of a comeback as the winter demand will soon drive up tonnage demand.

* Asian demand especaialy on ex-MEG routes improved in August and is expected to continue rising. This will help boost VLCC demand improving tonne-miles and utilisation alike.

* There seems to be a positive sentiment in the market which is likely to be reflected from next week onwards with an uptick in rates. The vessel movement has also been increasing which will increase the underway tonnage in week 27.
