## Market Commentary

### VLCC rates decline as tonnage builds up

- The VLCC tonne-miles index ended at 100.8 points, down 2.5 points from the previous week as market activity waned as the week progressed. Despite the week starting out firm, rates fell as activity levels declined, likely due to the International Energy week drowning out trade sentiments.

- As a result, there seems to be a buildup in tonnage, which is likely to keep rates flattened next week. The speed for both laden and ballast vessels increased last week, however, which could see even more buildup towards the Atlantic West as more ballast vessels head West.
