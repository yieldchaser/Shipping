## Market Commentary

### Asian demand leads trade in week 17

- It was a softer week in the VLCC segment with the tonne-miles index falling by 1.2 points to reach 113 in week 17. Trade was weaker in the West of Suez causing freight rates to fall marginally on most routes.

- While vessels had a hard time fixing cargoes from West Africa, MEG to Asia trade remained buoyant which helped in cushioning freight rates on most MEG to Asia routes.

- The global ballast and laden speed dipped last week mainly as a result of vessels slowing down in the East. This could indicate a softening of trade next week especially towards the Indian West Coast regions. Trade in the West could firm up next week supporting freight rates.
