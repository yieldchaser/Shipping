## Market Commentary

### VLCC utilisation strengthens despite weak tonne-mile dmeand

- VLCC utilisation improved to 92.3% in week 40, up 1.6 percentage points MoM and YoY, suggesting stronger fleet employment despite subdued demand indicators. However, the tonne-mile index remained weak at 87.5, down 2.2% YoY, suggesting that reduced voyage distances and softer long-haul crude movements continue to weigh on underlying transport demand.

- Market activity showed signs of regional divergence, with vessel capacity at anchor declining by 4.1% MoM in the west and 0.5% in the east. Higher ballast speeds in the east point to active fleet repositioning and improving vessel availability, while the recovery in the tonne-mile index from recent lows indicates some improvement in cargo flows heading into the fourth quarter.

- Easing congestion and faster vessel turnaround continued to lift effective fleet supply. Lower anchorage levels, combined with elevated ballast and laden sailing speeds, increased tonnage availability across key trading regions, offsetting support from stronger utilisation and limiting the upside for VLCC freight rates despite firmer bunker prices.

Define

VLCC vessel
All Crude tankers which are above 200,000 dwt.
2
Trading status
Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet.
Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an
AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen.
3
Laden dwt miles
Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight(or capacity) for that fleet
4
Bunker price
Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port
5
% Laden
Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair
6
East
East of Suez Canal and comprises China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania
7
West

TD3C

TD22
Spot rates on the US Gulf-China route; the data has been taken from the Baltic Exchange.
10
At anchor
Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area
11

12
Underway
Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination
