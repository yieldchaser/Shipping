## Market Commentary

### Pre-Christmas rush firms up activity in the MEG

- The VLCC tonne-miles index declined in week 51, ending the week at 103.4 points. Fleet utilisation has also been declining in recent weeks as the holiday season dampened market activity, especially in the West of Suez. The East side markets look brighter, with some vessels being fixed on ex-MEG to Asia, likely due to a pre-Christmas rush before the year ends. The relatively stronger activity in the MEG could see rates firm up next week as tonnage supply has contracted in the regions.

- Softness in the market has slowed the average speed of laden vessels while the speed of ballast vessels remained intact. A lack of a year-end rush across markets is likely to weaken the vessels underway in the next week.

| S No. | Terms           | Define                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1     | VLCC vessel     | All Crude tankers which are above 200,000 dwt.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet.
