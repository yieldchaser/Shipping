## Market Commentary

### Muted demand across the board weighs on freight rates

- it was another lacklustre week for VLCC as freight rates continued to decline on most of the major routes. Nonetheless, the stretched voyages pushed up the tonne-miles index to 110.4 points, gaining 4 points over the week.

- The global laden and ballast speed declined further in week 22 as tonnage demand weakened in the Middle East and the US. VLCC activity in West Africa also remained muted and is likely to remain weak in the short term as market indicators do not point towards any signs of a recovery in activity.

- The tonnage underway in the East saw an uptick amid a declining %laden likely indicating that market activity will remain soft as more ballast vessels await to be fixed. Meanwhile, the charter rates will likely continue to follow the downward trend.
