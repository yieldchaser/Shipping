## Market Commentary

### VLCC freight rates reach an unexpected peak in week 44

- The VLCC tonne-miles index surged to a new high in week 44, reaching 113.8 points. It was a phenomenal week for the tanker segment as freight rate gains reached new peaks.

- The AG-China route, in particular, saw rates surge as tonnage demand increased throughout the week. The utilisation for VLCCs also firmed, and the momentum is expected to continue next week as well.

- The recent US sanctions on major Russian oil producers will bode well for the Middle Eastern oil suppliers, boosting the MEG to Asia tonne-mile demand.
