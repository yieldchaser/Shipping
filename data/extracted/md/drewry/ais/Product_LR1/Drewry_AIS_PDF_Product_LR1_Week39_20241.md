## Market Commentary

### Spur in product demand for winter sees LR1 demand tightening

- The LR1 tonne-miles index continued to rise for a third consecutive week as markets have been faring better for the LRs. Overall, it was a positive week for the segment, with rates also inching higher WoW. The subtle increase in the global laden and ballast speeds also shows market enthusiasm and an increase in tonnage underway, especially towards the East.

- Tonnage lists seem to be tightening as demand for LR1 increases. While the markets towards the West of Suez were mixed with some softness throughout the week, the markets towards the East were in full swing. Some tightening in tonnage also helped push up rates. The positive sentiments are likely to continue next week.
