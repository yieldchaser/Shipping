## Market Commentary

### LR2 sees gains towards the East amid mixed activity levels

- It was a somewhat mixed week for the clean products market; however, the tonnes-miles increased to a new high for the year, ending the week at 103.3 points. Sentiments were weaker towards the West compared to the East, but overall, tonnage has been building up more in the West than in the previous weeks.

- Towards the West, market activity started strong, but an increase in tonnage supply, even from the other segments, capped any gains on freight rates. Despite steady rates in week 14, it will likely see a decline next week.

- Meanwhile, freight rates firmed up towards the East as tonnage demand remained ample. However, more ballast tonnage will be attracted next week, which will weigh on freight rates.
