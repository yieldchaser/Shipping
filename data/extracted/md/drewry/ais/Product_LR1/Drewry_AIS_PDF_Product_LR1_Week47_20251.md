## Market Commentary

### LR1 freight rates firm on tight cargo flow

- The tonne-mile index climbed to 99.3 in week 47, accompanied by week-on-week increases in both laden and ballast speeds.

- Despite subdued activity in the LR1 segment, freight rates continued to edge highe, driven primarily by date-sensitive fixing. While prompt options remain available, later-month UKC positions are increasingly limited.

- The steady inflow of LR1 cargoes is generating brief periods of tightness despite adequate vessel availability. Owners remain confident pointing to limited LR supply and more cargoes competing for tonnage than ships ballasting toward the Persian Gulf. We expect this supportive momentum to persist in the near term.
