## Market Commentary

### Weak markets weigh heavy on LR1s

- The tonne miles index declined for the first time after rising consecutively for five weeks, ending at 92.8 points in week 15. The lull of the Easter holidays followed by Eid continued to weigh heavy on freight rates as well as rates declined on most routes.

- The %laden vessels in the East saw an uptick of 2% last week, declining conversely in the West. The declining ballast speed in both the East and the West point towards more vessels waiting near the ports amid limited cargoes.

- There is some hope for improvement in week 16 especially towards the Med-UKC regions if tonnage supply tightens due to fewer vessels ballasting to the region. Markets opening up after the Eid holidays could help push up rates in the region. However, market activity towards the East is likely to remain weak next week.
