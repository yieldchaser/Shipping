## Market Commentary

### Uptick in LR1 rates likely to remain firm next week

- The LR1 market started out soft with the tonne-miles index falling slightly to reach 77.9 points in week 2. Towards the West, markets were on the weaker side with the demand for clean tankers being generally muted. To the East of Suez on the other hand, the market activity had been gaining pace especially on the MEG to Japan route as spot rates climbed higher WoW. The demand for naphtha is likely to continue pushing up rates next week.

- There was a slight drop in the tonnage underway in the East and the West. However, anchorage in the East saw an uptick. With naphtha demand bound to improve further, the tonnage underway especially towards the East is likely to rise next week while it remains firm towards the West.
