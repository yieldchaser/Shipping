## Market Commentary

### LR1 freight rates receive support from Eastern markets

- The LR1 tonne-miles index declined once again and ended the week at 64.5 points. The decline however slowed down compared to the sharp dips in the previous weeks.

- The markets towards the West saw subdued activity throughout the week but managed to keep rates firm. The markets in the East saw much better sentiments as many vessels found prompt tonnage. This is likely to continue in the next week as well with more vessels clearing out which could push up rates slightly.

- The % laden in the East and West both saw a slight increase in week 42, with ballast vessels gaining speed. As a result, next week is likely to see an uptick in activity in both the East and the West.
