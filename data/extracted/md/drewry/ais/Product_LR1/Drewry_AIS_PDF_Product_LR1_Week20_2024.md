## Market Commentary

### Tight tonnage supply to help support freight rates

- The tonne-miles index fell slightly last week ending the week 78.4 points as quieter markets in the West and tight tonnage supply in the East curbed the market activity for LR1s.

- Freight rates firmed last week, especially in the East where the tightening of tonnage pushed up rates amid fresh requirements towards North Asia. This is likely to continue for the next couple of weeks as freight rates continue to inch higher in tandem.

- The vessel speeds declined in both the East and the West except for ballast speed in the West which showed an increase. As a result, week 20 could see some uptick in market activity in the West, keeping freight rates buoyant.
