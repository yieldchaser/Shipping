## Market Commentary

### LR1 market sees a firming of freight rates

- It was a firmer week for the LR1 market; however, the tonne-mile index did see some softening, ending the week at 68.4 points. There was a MoM decline in the vessels at port and anchor in both the East and the West. The clean products market seems to be doing slightly better this month as we get closer to colder winter temperatures.

- The weakness in tonnage supply towards the East helped rates firm up slightly; however, tonnage demand also remained stable for the week. Market conditions are likely to remain stable next week, but rates might see further firming up.
