## LR1 market eases as utilisation and tonne-mile demand moderate

1 content_summary

* LR1 utilisation stood at 88.5%, down by 4.7 percentage points MoM and 1.7 percentage points below a year ago suggesting a moderate easing in market tightness.

* Tonne-mile index declined to 90.8, remaining below 2025 levels and signalling softer fleet absorption, suggesting reduced support from long-haul products trade, limiting demand growth.

* The share of laden vessels in the West increased by 6.9 percentage points MoM, suggesting stronger employment in the Atlantic basin, while the East saw a 3.7 percentage points decline. This divergence suggests relatively stronger demand strength in Western markets than in the East.
