## Market Commentary

### Weak tonnage demand weigs on freight rates

- The LR1 tonne-miles index continued to rise in week 20, gaining 0.9 points over the week. The global vessel speeds saw opposite trends among ballast and laden vessels as ballast speed gained 0.2 knots while the laden speed fell by close to 1 knot over the week.

- The market was rather slow towards the West due to tonnage demand being weak. Towards the East however, the freight rates increased by close to 30% over the week due to low tonnage supply amid high demand. Market conditions are likley to remain the same next week.
