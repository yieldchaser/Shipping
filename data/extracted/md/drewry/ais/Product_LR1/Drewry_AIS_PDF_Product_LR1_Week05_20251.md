## Market Commentary

### LR1 market likely to remain sluggish

- The LR1 tonne-miles index continued to decline in week 5 since the start of the year apart having temporarily risen in week 3. The index ended at 67.7 points as fleet utilisation has declined in the past several weeks as clean product demand remained sluggish throughout January.

- The markets remained quiet in both the East and the West which pushed down freight rates further. As a result, a dearth of tonnage remains, waiting to be employed while vessel speeds also seem to have slowed down globally. Next week does not look too bright either for LR1s, freight rates are likely to weaken further.
