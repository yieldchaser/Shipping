## Market Commentary

### Tonnage underway to increase in week 11

- The LR1 tonne-miles index declined in week 11, ending at 93 points as more vessels were at the port while the tonnage underway declined in both the East and the West.

- Market activity was strong, on the other hand, especially in the East, with firm tonnage demand on the MEG-Japan route pushing up rates.
