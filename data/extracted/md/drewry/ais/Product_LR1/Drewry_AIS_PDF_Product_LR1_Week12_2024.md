## Market Commentary

### Bright week ahead for LR1s as trade activity picks up in MEG

- The tonne-miles index for LR1 increased to 84.4 in week 11 as fresh demand for tonnage emerged towards the West of Suez, especially for the UKC to WAF route.

- Freight rates increased on most routes with large gains towards the East on the MEG-Japan route. There will likely be an increase in ballast tonnage headed towards the MEG in week 12, due to the robust rise in freight rates on TC5 which could cause tonnage supply in the UKC to contract over the week.

- The ballast tonnage in China and India increased last week but would likely lower over week 12 as more tonnage heads towards the Suez. With trade activity in the CPP market expected to firm next week, freight rates will also remain supported.
