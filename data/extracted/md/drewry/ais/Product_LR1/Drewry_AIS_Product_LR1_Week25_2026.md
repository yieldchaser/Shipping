## Easing utilisation and rising availability weigh on LR1 outlook

1 content_summary

* LR1 utilisation softened WoW, falling from 93% to 91%. The decline is primarily driven by weaker activity in the Northeast Asia (China), where the share of laden vessels fell sharply and congestion eased, which led to accelerated vessel turnaround, effectively releasing additional tonnage back into the market.

* The global anchored tonnage edged slightly higher while laden anchor days fell significantly by 16% on a 4-week moving average basis suggesting smoother discharge operations but slower re-employment of vessels post-discharge.

* Ballast speeds declined in the East by 9.1% MoM indicating softer demand whereas it rose in the west by 8% MoM. The sharp drop in bunker prices from $748 per tonne to $666 per tonne WoW did not fruitify into uniform sppeed increases, suggesting fuel-cost incentives being outweighted by uncertain demand outlook.

* LR1 tonne-mile index fell from 86.6 to 77.1 WoW, with a continued YoY decline by 11.9% suggesting a decline in long-haul trading and persistence of short-haul and regionally concentrated flows, particularly within Asia into nerby deficit regions.
