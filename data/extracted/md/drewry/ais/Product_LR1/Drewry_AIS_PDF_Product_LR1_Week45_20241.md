## Market Commentary

### Weak product demand weighs on LR1 freight rates

- The LR1 tonne-miles index saw an uptick in week 44 after reaching its lowest point this year. Despite an overall weakness in the clean products market, LR1s saw some support from the UKC/Med markets. As a result, week 45 is likely to see a slight improvement in the %laden towards the West.

- Market activity and tonnage demand towards the west remained muted. It was a slow week, resulting in a decline in freight rates on the MEG-Japan route. This softness is likely to prevail in week 45 as well, leaving more ballast vessels to remain in port towards the MEG. The winter demand seems to have not picked up yet which will likely keep utilisation low as well.
