## Market Commentary

### The LR1 market braces weak demand

- The LR1 tonne miles index showed an uptick in week 29 ending the week at 74.4 points. It was a lacklustre week for the LR1s as not enough cargo was available, especially towards the East, which resulted in the freight rates adjusting downwards on the MEG-Japan route.

- Towards the Med and the UKC, there was a firming in the market for MRs and LR2s leaving LR1 with fewer trades to carry out. However, next week remains hopeful for LR1 owners as tonnage demand is likely to go up with more cargoes anticipated to join the lists which could further support the freight rates in the region.

- While the global ballast speed saw a steady increase, there was a marked decline in the laden speed, which could show an upturn next week, provided that more cargoes will be entering the market. There might be some congestion towards the US Gulf leading to delays or longer transits.
