## Market Commentary

### LR1 market tightens on stronger fleet absorption and rising laden activity

- LR1 utilisation rose to 89.2%, up by 1.7 percentage points MoM, indicating improved fleet absorption and a firmer market, while the tonne-mile index declined by 10.4% YoY at 100.1, suggesting demand remains softer than in 2025.

- Fleet employment strengthened across both basins, with the share of laden vessels increasing by 4.5 percentage points MoM in the east and 2.7 percentage points in the west. Combined with a 13.7% decline in laden anchorage days, this points to improved vessel utilisation and faster vessel deployment.

- Regional vessel availability remains mixed with anchored capacity rising 26.7% MoM in the west and falling 9.3% in the east, suggesting relatively tighter conditions in the east of Suez. Despite bunker prices rising by 17.9% MoM to $815/tonne, vessel speeds increased, suggesting resilient freight economics and active fleet deployment.
