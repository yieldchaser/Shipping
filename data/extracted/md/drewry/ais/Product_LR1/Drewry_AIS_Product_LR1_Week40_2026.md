## Market Commentary

### LR1 demand remains resilient as rising vessel availability caps market tightness

- LR1 tonne-mile demand remained supportive, with the tonne-mile index at 107.6, up 4.5% YoY, suggesting resilient long-haul product flows despite softer market conditions. However, utilisation fell to 88.9%, down 2.5 percentage points MoM and 3.4 percentage points YoY, suggesting vessel supply growth and improving fleet productivity are outpacing demand gains.

- Vessel availability increased across key trading regions, with anchored capacity declining by 9.9% MoM in the west and 23.7% MoM in the east. The share of laden vessels rose by 8.2 percentage points in the west, reflecting healthy cargo activity, but higher ballast speeds in both regions indicate prompt tonnage remains readily available as owners reposition vessels more aggressively.

- Market tightness continued to ease as congestion levels moderated and voyage turnaround improved. Lower anchorage volumes, coupled with a rise in sailing speeds despite bunker prices increasing to $866 per tonne, effectively expanded the active fleet supply. As a result, strong tonne-mile demand and cargo movements provided support to LR1 earnings, but growing vessel availability limited the scope for a sustained rate rally.

LR1 vessel
Long range coated tanker vessels which have dwt in the range of 60,000-80,000
2
Trading status
Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet.
Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an
AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen.
3
Laden dwt miles
Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight (or capacity)
4
Bunker price
Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port
5
% Laden
Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair
6
East
East to Suez Canal, comprising China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania
7
West
West of Suez Canal, comprising Atlantic region
8
TC5
Spot rates from Baltic Exchange on Middle East Gulf–Japan route
9
TC8
Spot rates taken from Baltic Exchange on Middle East Gulf-UK Continent route
10
At anchor
Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area
11
Moored
Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging
12
Underway
Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination
