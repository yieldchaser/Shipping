## Market Commentary

### Tonne-miles index sees firmness amid weak demand

- The LR1 tonne-miles index increased by 5.4 points, ending the week at 96.4, increasing for the second week in a row. Despite the higher tonne-miles covered, the LR1 market did not fare so well as fewer cargoes were available in the East and the West, leading to a decline in freight rates.

- The vessels in port increased by over 20% in both the East and the West. Even the global ballast and laden speeds declined last week with a slight decline in tonnage underway. Market conditions are likely to remain similar next week.
