## Market Commentary

### Outlook for LR1s for next week remains optimistic

- The LR1 tonne-miles index continued to rise in week 8 ending the week at 87.4 points as a slight firmness in the tonnage supply aided tanker rates especially towards the East. Towards the West, the lack of tonnage demand dampened some possible freight rate gains.
