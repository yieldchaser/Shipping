## Market Commentary

### July likely to start off with a softening in the market

- The tonne-mile index continued to rise for the sixth week in a row ending the week at 87.7 points. The LR1 market in week 26 leaned closer towards being soft accompanied by some correction in rates happening in the market. Despite that, the transits via the COGH continued to add miles to the voyages.

- The demand for CPP towards the East in particular was weak as the laden speed showed a decline over the week. More vessels ballasted Eastward might see some improvement in trade next week as cargo lists lengthen.

- The rising threat of a category 5 hurricane in the West Coast Atlantic could disrupt the trade in the coming week, pushing up rates.
