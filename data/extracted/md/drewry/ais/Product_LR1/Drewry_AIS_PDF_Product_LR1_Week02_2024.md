## Glossary

- **LR1 vessel:** Long range coated tanker vessels which have dwt in the range of 60,000-80,000
- **Trading status:** Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen.
- **Laden dwt miles:** Refers to the average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight(or capacity) for that fleet
- **Bunker price:** Refers to price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port
- **% Laden:** Refers to share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair
- **East:** Region East refers to the region East to Suez Canal and comprises China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania
- **West:** Region West refers to the region West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region
- **TC5:** Refers to Spot rates on Middle East Gulf–Japan route and data have been taken from Baltic Exchange
- **TC8:** Refers to Spot rates on Middle East Gulf-UK Continent route and data have been taken from Baltic Exchange
- **At anchor:** Refers to the vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area
- **Moored:** Refers to the vessels moored in port for loading or discharging
- **Underway:** Refers to the vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination
