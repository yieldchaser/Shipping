## Market Commentary

### Steady demand for LR1 in week 2

- The vessel utilisation was 91% in week 2, increasing approximately by 2% from the previous week.

- The %laden in the East started significantly higher than it did in 2023, however, it was lower YoY in the West. Ports in the West seem to be busier than their counterparts in the East.
