## Market Commentary

### Weak activity in the LR1 market likely to continue next week

- The LR1 tonne-miles index increased by almost 7 points in week 7 to end at 98.5 points. It was a quieter week in the LR1 market despite the rise in the tonne-mile index which was about 15 points higher YoY.

- The global vessel speed in the East declined as tonnage enquiries were low in the previous weeks with activity in the market expected to remain on the lower side in week 8 as well. However, as markets in the Far East have started to become more active after the lull of the Lunar New Year celebrations, demand for LR1 could show an uptick from the end of the week.

- Nigeria's new Dangote refinery put out its first LR1 naptha cargo in the market last week which could see LR1 demand on ex-WAF routes increasing in the long run.
