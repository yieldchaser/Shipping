## Market Commentary

### Ample supply weighing down rates

- The utilisation of Capesize vessels decreased after reaching substantially high levels last month. The tonnage underway in the West has been historically as well as unseasonally high in April.

- The expansion in the supply stretch avaialble in the market rose primarily due to the rise in ballast vessels that are drifitng in the sea.

- The ballast speed in the West remained low amid ample supply in the Atlantic. As a result, the spot rates have started to normalise.

- Meanwhile, the tonnemile index remains significantly high YoY despite slight moderation last week. Buoyant demand in China has pushed the employability of vessels, as a result of which, 13.4 mdwt tonnage is waiting to unload at the country's discharge ports.
