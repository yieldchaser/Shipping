## Steady production of iron ore keeps the utilisation up

1 content_summary

* The Capesize utilisation edged up slightly to 92.5% in week 44, with a 27.2% fall in vessel capacity at port MoM in the West. Alongside this, vessel capacity at anchor is falling, leading to reduced congestion.

* Vale reported its strongest quarterly output in 3Q25, driven by record production from the S11D project and continued ramp-ups across other operations. YTD performance keeps the company on track to meet the upper end, supported by higher sales volumes.

* Strong Brazilian exports to long-haul Asian markets are expected to sustain tonne-mile demand and increase Capesize activity through remianing year.

* In the Pacific, steady demand from miners supported gradual gains in the latter half of the week, while in the Atlantic, a tightening tonnage list provided support to fronthaul trades. The announcement of a one-year US-China trade truce further lifted market sentiment.
