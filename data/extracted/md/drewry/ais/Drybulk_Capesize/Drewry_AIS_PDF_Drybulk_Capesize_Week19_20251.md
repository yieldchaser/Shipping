### Capesize tonne mile index drops amid declining vessel speed

1 content_summary

* The Capesize tonne-miles index declined by 4.3% in week 19 in tandem with reduced ballast and laden speeds; a decrease in Baltic rates followed.

* The percentage of laden vessels in the West decreased in accordance with declining spot rates on the Brazil-China route.

* Vessels at Anchor in the East decreased by 20% MoM, accompanied by reduced laden congestion in the region.

* China's laden tonnage has decreased due to a reduction in coal imports. This decline is attributed to slower growth in power consumption and increased coal inventories within the country.
