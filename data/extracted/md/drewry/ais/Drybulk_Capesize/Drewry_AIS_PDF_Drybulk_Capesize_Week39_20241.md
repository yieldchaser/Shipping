### Capesize Utilisation Drops Amid Slowdown in Laden Speed

1 content_summary

* The laden speed declined in the Western and Eastern hemispheres for the second consecutive week

* The ballast tonnage at anchor in the Indian Ocean fell significantly within a week, from 2.0 mdwt to 1.5 mdwt

* This is attributed to reduced iron ore trade on the India-China route due to low demand in China, which resulted in fewer loading activities in India

* On the contrary, the global tonne-mile index for Capesizes improved last week and remained significantly high YoY. Buoyant bauxite demand in China has pushed the employability of vessels, resulting in about 12.5 mdwt of cargo waiting to unload at the country's discharge ports
