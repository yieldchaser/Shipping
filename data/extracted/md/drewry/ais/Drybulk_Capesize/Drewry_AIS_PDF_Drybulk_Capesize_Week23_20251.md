## Market Commentary

### Capesize markets soars amid robust iron ore trade

- The Capesize tonne-miles index experienced a strong upward trend, rising by 26% over the past two weeks. Increased iron ore trade volumes from Brazil and Australia drove this growth. This led to an increase in Baltic 5TC rates.

- The Laden Tonnage at Anchor for China showed an uptick in Week 23, signalling increased demand from the Far East, particularly China. Higher Capesize loadings from Australia primarily fueled this surge, as iron ore exports to China remain robust.

- This trend is further supported by increased ballast speeds in the east. Meanwhile, the rise in Australian iron ore shipments to India has led to growing congestion at Indian ports during Week 23, reflecting logistical challenges amid the higher cargo volumes.
