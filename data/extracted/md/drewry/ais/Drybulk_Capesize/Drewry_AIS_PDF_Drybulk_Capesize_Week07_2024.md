## Weakness in utilisation persists

* ● The utilisation of Capesize vessels stayed on a downward trajectory despite a strong start to the year.
* ● The global tonne-mile index complemented the weakness in the market, registering a WoW decline. However, the index remained much higher than the levels achieved last year in the same period.
* ● As China's Lunar New Year Holidays begin, activity in the market has slowed down, particularly affecting Capesize demand.
* ● The congestion in China's load ports has eased while the congestion at the country's discharge ports remains modest as compared to last two years.
