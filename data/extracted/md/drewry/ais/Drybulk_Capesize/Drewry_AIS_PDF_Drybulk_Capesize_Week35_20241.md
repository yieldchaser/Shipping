## Decline in utilisation

1 content_summary

* The Capesize vessel utilization declined last week, reversing the upward trend of the previous three weeks. The drop in global ballast speed squeezed the supply in the market, dampening utilisation.

* The tonne-mile index rose marginally but continues to be much higher than last year's level. Iron ore sockpiling activity by China has been aiding Capesize demand as iron ore prices continue to be subdued. The expansion in the share of laden vessels in the East compared to last year corroborates the high demand.

* Meanwhile, the proportion of laden vessels is subdued in the West as more vessels load at Brazil and Guinea and head towards the East. The global moving average of the tonnage waiting at the anchor has been subdued lately and is likely to ease freight rates in the short-term with the ease in congestion.
