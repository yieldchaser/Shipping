## Surge in vessel utilisation

1 content_summary

* The utilisation of Capesize vessels surged, registering a rise of one percentage point in a week. The resulting level of utilisation was much higher compared to last year.

* Despite the high supply of ballast vessels in the Atlantic, high demand due to strong export volumes on long-haul routes such as Brazil-China and West Africa-China, raised employment of vessels.

* The tonnage underway in the West surged to historically high levels, with approximately 70 mdwt moving in the region.

* Meanwhile, the global tonne-mile index moderated WoW but continues to be high YoY. China’s iron ore inventories remain low YoY, with import for restocking aiding demand.
