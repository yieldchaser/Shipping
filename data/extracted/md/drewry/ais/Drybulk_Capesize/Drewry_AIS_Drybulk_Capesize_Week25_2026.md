## Market Commentary

### Atlantic weakness offsets Pacific demand in Capesize market

- The Capesize segment weakened during the week, with tonne-miles index falling slightly WoW to 95.6, while Baltic C5TC rates fell in tandem. Ballast speed also declined alongside falling laden speed. Spot rates also fell on major Capesize routes, including the Brazil-China route.

- Capesize activity improved in the Pacific with Ballast congestion increasing as major miners remained active in the market, helping to support cargo volumes. However, despite firm cargo demand in the Pacific, the Atlantic market softened with laden congestion falling to YTD lowest levels as vessel availability increased. A growing tonnage list weighed on freight rates, limiting upside momentum and creating a divergence between the two basins.

- The combined impact of Middle East-related vessel delays and congestion at West African ports has removed part of the fleet from active trading. Although a very small capacity is directly affected, these inefficiencies continue to tighten effective vessel supply.
