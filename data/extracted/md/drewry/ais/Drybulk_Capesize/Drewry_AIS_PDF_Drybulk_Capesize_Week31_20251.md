## Market Commentary

### Capesize market shows signs of strength in 2H25

- Capesize utilisation has begun to recover in 2H25, rising by 2 percentage points since mid-July after a subdued 1H25. This rebound aligns with a softening in Baltic rates earlier in the year, which are now improving alongside utilisation. The gains have been driven by increased activity on the Brazil-China and Transpacific routes.

- Despite temporary disruptions from cyclones and flooding in early 2025, Australia’s miners maintained robust iron ore exports, with nearly 85% of shipments heading to China which supported Spot rates on the Brazil-China route to surge 27% MoM in 2H25.
