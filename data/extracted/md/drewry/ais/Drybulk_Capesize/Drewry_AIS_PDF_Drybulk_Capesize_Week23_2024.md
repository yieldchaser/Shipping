### Capesize utilisation inches up

1 content_summary

* The utilisation of Capesize vessels rose to 93.3% from 92.7% the previous week due to heightened industrial activity in India and China, driving steady demand for iron ore and coking coal.

* Additionally, the Capesize tonne mile index has surged to 108.1, an increase of 7.5 basis points W-o-W, as the demand for Capesize vessels on the Guinea-China bauxite route rose, driven by a substantial expansion in Chinese domestic aluminium consumption. Overall, the industrial surge in key regions has significantly boosted the demand and utilisation of Capesize vessels, reflecting a robust market for bulk commodities.
