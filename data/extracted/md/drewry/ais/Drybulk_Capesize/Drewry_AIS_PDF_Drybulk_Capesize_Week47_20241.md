## Rise in utilisation as speed increases
1 content_summary

* Capesize vessel utilisation rose marginally last week from 91% to 91.2%, though it remains much lower YoY. The increase in global ballast speed contributed to WoW improvement in utilisation.

* Additionally, the tonne-mile index expanded, driven by robust iron ore shipments from Brazil and Australia.

* Amid the growing demand for coal, Australian exports to China have edged up, further supporting the steady employment of Capesize vessels on this route. This resulted in higher tonnage waiting at the anchor in Oceania's load ports compared to last week.
