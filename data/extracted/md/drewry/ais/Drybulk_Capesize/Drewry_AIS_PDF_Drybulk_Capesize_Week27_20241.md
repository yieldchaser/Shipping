## Higher YoY demand for Capesize vessels
1 content_summary

* The utilisation of Capesize vessels continued on the downward trend for the second consecutive week, reaching close to the lowest levels seen in last two months.

* The dampening of utilisation levels can be attributed to the upswing in the global ballast speed, which reached its highest level seen in the last two months.

* The share of laden vessels continued to be buoyant in the East as well as the West regions, signalling higher employment of vessels due to expansion in iron ore and bauxite demand.

* Guinea's rising bauxite exports are a critical driver for the upsurge in Capesize employment. The exports surged by 22% YoY in June, a notable increase despite the typical summer decline caused by the rainy season. This has also contributed to the buoyant tonnemile index in 2024 compared to 2023.
