### Utilisation remained stable, Capesize ballast speed plunged

1 content_summary

* Global Capesize utilisation increased a marginal 0.2 percentage points WoW to 92.1%, but it was down 1.5% YoY for week 9. In contrast, the tonne-miles index remained stable at 100.4, marking a 13% YoY increase.

* Laden tonnage at anchorage in China declined nearly 30%, possibly due to the country's plans to cut steel production which will weigh on iron ore demand. Market estimates indicate that iron ore imports fell to a record low in February, the lowest since 2019, which has potentially led to reduced loadings on Capesize vessels.

* Ballast speed crashed 15% compared to two weeks prior when it was at a record high. Laden speed also declined in tandem, with 4.3% WoW.
