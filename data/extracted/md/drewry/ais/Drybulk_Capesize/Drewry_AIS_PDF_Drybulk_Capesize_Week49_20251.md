## Capesize market tightens as Utilisation and rates strengthen

* Capesize utilisation strengthened notably, rising to 92.5% from 92.2% WoW, reflecting firmer trade activity. This uplift was accompanied by a sharp rally in freight rates on the benchmark C5TC index, marking the strongest increase since December 2023.

* The surge was primarily driven by robust front-haul demand, including higher iron ore shipments from Brazil and increased bauxite exports from Guinea, which collectively absorbed more tonnage and supported higher utilisation and spot rates.

* Vessel operating patterns also signalled tightening supply, with laden speeds remaining steady, ballast speeds edged higher, reaching their highest level this year, indicating owners repositioning to capture stronger market opportunities.
