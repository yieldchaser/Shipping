## Decline in Capesize utilisation

1 content_summary

* The utilisation of Capesize vessels shrank 0.2 percentage point from the previous week, but still remained substantially lower than the level in 2023.

* The ballast speed in the West as well as in the East reduced significantly due to lower iron ore loadings from Brazil and Australia and fewer coal cargoes into Europe.

* Meanwhile, laden tonnage at anchor in China plunged this week, from 12.6 mdwt to 11.4 mdwt, due to reduced iron ore and coal imports amid sufficient stockpiles.
