### Pacific weakness and Atlantic oversupply driver rates decline in Capesize market

- The Capesize vessel utilisation fell WoW in tandem with falling tonne-miles index in week 41, while the Baltic benchmark C5TC rates also declined to $35,860/day. The market remained under pressure as limited cargo and ample vessel availability outweighed increased fixing activity along with, the Singapore public holiday and weaker FFA sentiment.
- The C2 spot rates also declined to around $16.23/tonne, from $18.26/tonne the previous week, as available tonnage outpaced demand for early-October loading dates. Despite active fixing and elevated bunker costs at Chinese ports, owners competed aggressively for cargoes.
- The South Atlantic remained under pressure, amid growing vessel availability and demand shifting towards November dates. The North Atlantic also softened despite fresh transatlantic and fronthaul enquiries.

## Glossary

- **Capesize vessel:** Dry bulk vessels above 100,000 dwt
- **Trading status:** Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen.
- **Laden dwt miles:** Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight (or capacity)
- **Bunker price:** Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port
- **% Laden:** Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair
- **East:** East to Suez Canal, comprising China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania
- **West:** West of Suez Canal, comprising Atlantic region
- **C2:** Spot rates from Baltic Exchange on the Tubarao-Rotterdam route
- **C3:** Spot rates from Baltic Exchange on the Tubarao-Qingdao route
- **At anchor:** Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area
- **Moored:** Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging
- **Underway:** Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination
