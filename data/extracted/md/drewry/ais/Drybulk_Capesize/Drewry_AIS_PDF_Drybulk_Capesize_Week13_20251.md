## Market Commentary

### Global Capesize recovery continues

- Capesize utilisation continues to recover, marking its fourth consecutive week of improvement. It increased by 0.3 percentage points, reaching 92.5%. The Tonne-miles index also showed this positive movement in tandem.

- In March, ballast vessel days at anchor fell by almost 9%, while global ballast speed increased in the Western regions, suggesting that more vessels are actively seeking employment, with tonnage underway rising WoW in the west.

- Laden Tonnage at anchor in China and India has been trending downward due to a decrease in their seaborne imports of coking coal over the past month. This decline is further supported by a recent reduction in India's thermal coal imports.
