## Holiday season impacts Capesize earnings. Tightening tonnage provided some boost.

* The global Capesize utilisation increased to 95.2% compared to 2024.

* The Capsize tonne-miles index experienced a 1.6% WoW decline, reaching 109 points. This decline can be attributed to the seasonally low market activity as the Pacific region enters the Lunar New Year holidays. This seasonal slowdown is further evidenced by reduced laden tonnage at anchor in China.

* The share of laden vessels in both East and West regions declined WoW. In tandem, reduced enquiries for iron ore shipments from Brazil to China translated into spot rates declining by 3% WoW, reaching $17.4 per tonne on the benchmark Brazil-China route.
