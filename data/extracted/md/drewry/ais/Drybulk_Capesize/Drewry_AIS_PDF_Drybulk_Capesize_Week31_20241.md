### Moderation in utilisation of Capesize vessels

1 content_summary

* The utilisation of Capesize vessels declined marginally last week to reach 91.7% from 91.9% the week before.

* Meanwhile, the tonnemile index continued to be on a downward trend, declining for the third consecutive week. However, the market continues to perform well YoY.

* The ballast tonnage at anchor in the Atlantic has been modest as the onset of monsoon disrupts loading activity in Guinea, dampening bauxite trade in this season.

* China's iron ore inventory for 2024 peaked in end-June, which has moderated additional imports in July, reducing the ballast tonnage wiating to load in Australia and Brazil.
