## Market Commentary

### Capesize Tonne-Mile weakness reflects dwindling Chinese demand

- Tonne-mile demand for Capesize vessels dipped 1.3% in Week 27, largely due to a fall in Chinese iron ore imports driven by weakening steel demand.

- Ballast tonnage in the Atlantic continued its downtrend, nearing historically low levels since 2023. This reflects softer loadings from Brazil to China due to reduced manufacturing activity in China.

- Spot rates on the Brazil-China route dropped 13.7% WoW, further pressured by Australia's forecast of declining iron ore export revenues amid China's weak steel demand and lower production levels.
