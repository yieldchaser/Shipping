## Market Commentary

### Strong demand meets tighter effective supply in the Capesize market

- The Drewry's Tonne-miles index for Capesize is on an uptrend, rising 2.8% WoW, reaching 98 in week 37. C5TC faced some correction WoW as the early-week Pacific rally lost momentum. C5 (Australia-China), however, remained firm, supported by strong miner participation and healthy demand for vessels. The willingness of miners to cover multiple vessels has kept the market well supported.

- North China port closures due to strong winds and ongoing operational disruptions have delayed vessel schedules and reduced fleet efficiency. At the same time, higher bunker prices are encouraging owners to factor in higher fuel costs in voyage economics.

- C3 levels (Brazil-China route) held around $41–42 per tonne, supported by limited available vessels and fresh October demand, with owners maintaining firm offers. However, elevated freight rates are increasingly challenging the economics of commodities. This could limit further upside if Atlantic demand softens, although the relatively short vessel list continues to put a cap.
