## Market Commentary

### Asian strength leads steady gains amid tightening tonnage in Supramax market

- The Supramax vessel utilisation increased WoW to 93.7% in tandem with rising tonne-miles index and Baltic benchmark rates to $20,300, with firm NoPac grain and backhaul demand supporting the Far East. Indonesian coal remained a key driver, with Indonesia-India, while South Africa-India/Pakistan business reached as also reached new highs.

- US Gulf fronthaul and transatlantic demand improved, with Ultramaxes achieving rates in the $30,000s/day range. The Continent saw strong activity as fertiliser and grain absorbed available tonnage, while expanded Black Sea requirements further tightened vessel availability and pushed scrap rates higher.

- Strong fixing activity across both basins, combined with fewer available vessels and firm backhaul demand, has encouraged owners to hold for higher rates. Period activity has consequently picked up, while the FFA remains supportive heading into Q4.

| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |
