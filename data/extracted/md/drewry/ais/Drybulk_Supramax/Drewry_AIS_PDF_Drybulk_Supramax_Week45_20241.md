## Market Commentary

### Increase in laden tonne-mile index

- The laden tonne-mile index increased by 2.1% WoW, primarily driven by steady demand for tonnage in the ECSA and the USG. Soybean exports from the US have expanded rapidly ahead of the election results due to the fear of tariffs. Since more than 20% of the shipments to China are on Supramax vessels, the demand has risen over the last week and is likely to sustain in the short term.

- Increased demand resulted in a surge in congestion in the Atlantic, leading to higher ballast tonnage waiting at load ports.

- Meanwhile, congestion in China has increased over the week driven by increased shipments of soybean as well as coal.
