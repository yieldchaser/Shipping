## Market Commentary

### Buoant coal imports in India

- The tonnemile index moderated WoW, reaching 98.7 last week from 100.1 the week before. While the share of laden vessels moderated in the East, it continued to be on an uptrend in the West. The share continues to be much higher compared to the last year in both regions.

- The significant rise of 1.3 mdwt laden tonnage at anchor in India indicates a surge in import activity due to buoyant demand for coal from Indonesia.

- Meanwhile, the Atlantic, particularly the US Gulf, experienced lower tonnage availability due to increased demand for wood pellets from the US Gulf to Japan and grains from the US Gulf to North Coast South America.
