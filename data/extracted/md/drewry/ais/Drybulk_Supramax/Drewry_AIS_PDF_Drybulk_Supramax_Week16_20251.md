## Market Commentary

### Weakness prevails in the Supramax market

- The Supramax tonne-miles index weakened slightly in week 16, down by 0.4, as market tonnage demand remained soft. This was in tandem with falling utilisation, ending the week at 93.2%.

- Ballast speed in the west dropped to its lowest levels since 2022. There was also a sharp decline in the percentage of laden vessels in the west. In accordance, Spot rates on the USG-Europe route fell by 4.6%.

- Vessels at anchor in the East increased by 17% MoM, leading to higher congestion of both laden and ballast vessels at anchor. Laden tonnage at anchor in the Pacific increased sharply by 36% WoW due to early May orders from the west, mainly for salt and grains.
