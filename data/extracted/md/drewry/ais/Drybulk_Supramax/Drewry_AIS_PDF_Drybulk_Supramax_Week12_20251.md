## Rates increased in tandem with Utilisation

1 content_summary

* In week 12, the Supramax Utilisation increased by 0.8 percentage points, reaching at 93.1%.

* Concurrently, Spot rates on the Indonesia-India route increased 25% WoW while spot rates on the USG-Europe increased 12% WoW highlighting growing trade between the regions.

* Global ballast speed saw a WoW increase attributed by increased speed in both east and west, while global laden speed experienced a WoW decline.

* Ballast tonnage at anchor in China recovered significantly from the lows it reached since the beginning of the year indicating recovering Chinese demand for the vessel segment.
