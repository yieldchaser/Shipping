## Decline in Utilisation

1 content_summary

* The Supramax vessel utilisation declined last week to 92.2%, reversing the upward trend observed in previous weeks. A drop in global laden speed squeezed the vessels availability, dampening utilisation.

* The tonne mile index also declined from 94.1 to 93.1 points.

* This decline is attributed to reduced grain shipments from the US Gulf to Europe and South America due to lower seasonal demand, which resulted in fewer loading activities for Supramax vessels in the Atlantic region.
