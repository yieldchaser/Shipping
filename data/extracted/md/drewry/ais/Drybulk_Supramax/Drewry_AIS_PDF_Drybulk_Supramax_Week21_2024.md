## Surge in congestion in Indian region

* The utilisation expanded last week to reach the highest level this year but it still remained below levels attained in 2023.

* On the other hand, Drewry's tonnemile index was stable but continues to be elevated compared to last year.

* The share of laden tonnage moderated WoW in the East leading to stability in the spot rates. However, the rates remain elevated as the share of laden tonnage is substantially high YoY.

* Buoyant coal demand from India has led to a WoW rise in congestion levels in the Indian region, with 6.4 mdwt waiting at discharge ports to unload. The congestion levels peaked last week compared to last two years
