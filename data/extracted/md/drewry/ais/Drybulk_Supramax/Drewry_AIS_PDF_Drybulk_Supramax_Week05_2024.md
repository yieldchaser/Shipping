### Brake on global speed

* Supramax utilisation improved last week helping freight rates to recover, particularly on Transatlantic routes.
* Global speed, ballast as well as laden, has slowed down this year to address the regulatory issues, in all likelihood.
* Meanwhile, congestion in the West has increased over the week; there has been a surge in Supramaxes at anchor in the US Gulf carrying Russian fertilisers
* Additionally, there has been a substantial increase in Supramaxes discharging Indonesian coal at the Chinese ports.
