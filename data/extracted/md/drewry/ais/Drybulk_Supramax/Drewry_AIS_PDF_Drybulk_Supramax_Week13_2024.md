## Market Commentary

### Coal trade supporting Supramax demand

- The utilisation of Supramax vessels rose marginally WoW, reaching last year's level.

- Meanwhile, the tonnemile index moderated last week but remained much higher compared to last year.

- The Supramax employability increased as the YTD coal exports from Indonesia expanded. Substantial expansion in imports from India, where the government has urged power producers to import 6% of total coal requirements for blending, has contributed to the vessel demand.

- The laden tonnage at anchor in the Indian ports has reached the highest levels in 2024, with approximately 5.2 mdwt waiting to unload.
