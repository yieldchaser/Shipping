## Market Commentary

### Atlantic leads gains in supramax sector

- Supramax utilisation rose 1.4% in week 45 to 93.5%, supported by a continued uptrend in tonne-mile demand in 2H25 and a MoM increase in vessel capacity at port in the East.

- At the same time, capacity at anchor has been declining, thereby easing congestion. Baltic benchmarks tracked the higher utilisation, with spot rates holding firm across both East and West.

- In the Atlantic, sentiment remained positive, with tight vessel availability and stronger enquiry from the US Gulf, following China’s commitment to purchase 12 million tonnes of US soybeans this season and 25 million tonnes annually over the next three years.
