## Supramax utilisation continues to be on an uptrend

* Supramax utilisation remained stable in week 33, rising by just 0.1 percentage points, baltic rates firmed in tandem. Vessels at port declined 8.2% MoM in the West and 1.1% MoM in the East as more vessels moved underway.

* With ballast congestion increasing in China, Spot rates on China-India route surged reaching around $19,800/pd. Spot rates in the West on US-Europe route also followed the upward rates trajectory in August.

* In Asia, steady demand kept sentiment positive, with healthy activity across most regions. The Indian Ocean remained relatively subdued.
