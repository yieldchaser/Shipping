## Market Commentary

### Surge in Supramax shipping demand

- Drewry's tonnemile index for Supramax vessels remained on an uptrend for the third consecutive week, with a 10.7% rise YoY.

- This is juxtaposed with the lowest inactivity levels in the Supramax segment after 2021, resulting in ample supply in the market, keeping utilisation levels subdued.

- The tonnage underway in the West has reached historically high, with 40 mdwt underway.

- Meanwhile, ample coal demand in Asia has also kept the share of laden vessels in the East high. As a result, the spot rates on the Indonesia-China route have been rising.
