## Market Commentary

### Supramax markets faces diverging regional trends

- Supramax utilisation posted a modest gain of 0.6 percentage points over the past week, reflecting a market split between regions.

- The Atlantic basin gained momentum, with the US Gulf standing out as the key driver of positive sentiment. Supporting this trend, the number of vessels in port in the West rose by nearly 4% MoM.

- In contrast, the Pacific market continued to face headwinds from vessel oversupply, leading to reduced congestion among ballasting ships.

- Vessel speed trends were mixed, although global ballast speeds trended higher overall.
