## Supramax market remains under pressure as utilisation and rates slide

* The Supramax market remained under pressure, with vessel utilisation slipping to 92.5% amid muted trade activity, while benchmark S10TC rates and ballast speeds both edged lower, reinforcing the softer market tone.

* Within the Atlantic, laden tonnage at anchor declined as market conditions remained uneven, with steady demand persisting for US Gulf trans-Atlantic business, while fronthaul activity showed signs of softening.

* Across Asia, sentiment also remained under pressure with rates edging lower, as limited demand, mainly from the south, was met with ample tonnage. Meanwhile, the Indian Ocean held a broadly neutral tone, with steady activity supporting relatively stable rates.
