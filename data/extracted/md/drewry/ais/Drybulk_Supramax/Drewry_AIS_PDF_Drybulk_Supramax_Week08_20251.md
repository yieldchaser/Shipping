## Supramax utilisation starts to increase along with improving tonne-mile index

\* In week 7, global Supramax utilisation increased 0.1 percentage point for the first time this year, reaching 93%. Concurrently, the Supramax tonne-miles index exhibited a marginal rise to 86.4, driven by Egypt’s higher wheat imports from Russia.

\* Ballast tonnage at anchorage surged 9% in the Atlantic, driven by higher grain loading. In contrast, laden tonnage at anchorage in China declined almost 50% WoW as Shenhua, China’s major coal importer, halted spot coal imports.

\* Although ballast speed decreased almost 3% YoY in week 8, it is on a recovery trajectory, showing a slight WoW uptrend in response to increased time charter fixtures.
