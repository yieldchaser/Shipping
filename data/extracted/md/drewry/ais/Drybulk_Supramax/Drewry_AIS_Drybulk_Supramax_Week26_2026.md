### Atlantic resilience offsets Holiday-driven weakness in Asia

- The Supramax market softened during the week, with the Baltic benchmark S10TC declining to $19,050/day alongside falling vessel Utilisation. Trading activity slowed considerably due to May Day holidays and the Geneva dry bulk events, while enquiries in the Pacific also dwindled. Indonesian coal activity remained subdued, and high Chinese coal inventories continued to cap demand, resulting in weaker sentiment across Asian routes.
- The Atlantic continued to outperform the Pacific, supported by firm demand from the Continent and Mediterranean for ECSA and the US Gulf. Although the US Gulf came under pressure from an increase in ballasting vessels and fewer fresh cargoes, the Mediterranean, Black Sea and West Africa remained relatively firm.
- While sentiment remained mixed, specific regional trades continued to underpin the market. Strong Pakistani coal demand lifted South Africa's exports, and the Indian Ocean market improved modestly. Meanwhile, firm steel demand supported Far East–West Africa fixtures, and expectations of higher demand for ECSA and US Gulf cargoes helped maintain positive sentiment in the Atlantic.

## Glossary

- **Supramax vessel:** Medium sized dry Bulk vessels in the size range 40,000-70,000 dwt.
- **Trading status:** Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen.
- **Laden dwt miles:** Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight (or capacity)
- **Bunker price:** Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port
- **% Laden:** Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair
- **East:** East to Suez Canal, comprising China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania
- **West:** West of Suez Canal, comprising Atlantic region
- **S8_58:** Spot rates from Baltic Exchange on China–East Coast India route
- **S4A_56:** Spot rates from Baltic Exchange on US Gulf-Europe route
- **At anchor:** Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area
- **Moored:** Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging
- **Underway:** Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination
