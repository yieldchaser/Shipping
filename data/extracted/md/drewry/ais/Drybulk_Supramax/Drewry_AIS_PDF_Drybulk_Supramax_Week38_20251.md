## Market Commentary

### Supramax market sentiment strengthens

- Supramax utilisation rose 0.7 percentage points WoW in week 38, with ballast speeds and Baltic benchmark rates also edging higher.

- The loading and discharge activity at port in the West rose 11.5% MoM in September while it improved 5.5% in the East. Spot rates also imporved in the West on US-Europe route further implying the improved market sentiments.

- While the ballast congestion in China remained low this week, it saw an sharp increase in Oceania. Laden tonnage in Atlantic also remanied high, constantly above than 2024 levels.
