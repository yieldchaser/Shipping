## Decline in Utilisation

1 content_summary

* The utilisation of Supramax vessels declined marginally from 91.9% to 91.7 % this week, however, it has fallen by 1.3 percentage points over the past month.

* Meanwhile, the laden tonne-mile index also decreased by 0.3 percentage points WoW primarily because of low seasonal trade.

* However, there is an increase in congestion in the Atlantic, mostly in the US East Coast to load soybean and grain before the new government takes control in the US.
