## Substantially high fall in utilisation

* The Supramax vessel utilization declined last week to the second-lowest level in 2024 so far, reaching 91.9%, attributed to the declining laden speed.

* Meanwhile, the tonnemile index reached its highest level in the last month but continued to be subdued YoY. As grain exports from ECSA expands in the ongoing season, Supramax demand is projected to improve, pushing congestion at load ports higher.

* Meanwhile, the tonnage waiting at the anchor at the load ports in Asia has moderated and remains much below compared to the last two years for the ongoing season. In fact, congestion at the load ports in the Indian subcontinent region has dropped to its lowest levels in the last two years.
