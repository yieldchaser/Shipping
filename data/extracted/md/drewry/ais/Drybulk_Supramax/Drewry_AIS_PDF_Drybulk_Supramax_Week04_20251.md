## Market Commentary

### Aggravated seasonal lull in Supramax demand

- The utilisation for Supramax vessels declined marginally WoW but remain substantially lower than last year due to the aggravated seasonal lull in market activity.

- The ballast speed continued to be on a downtrend in response to subdued fixtures, dampening the supply utilisation. Correspondingly, the tonnemile index remained weak, ending at 87.1 points last week, down from 92.2 in week 1.

- As the market is engulfed with bleak activity due to the Lunar New Year Holidays in China, the congestion at major Chinese ports is subdued. Additionally, China secured most of its soybean stocks for 1Q25 ahead of Trump assuming the office in January 2025, which has aggravated the seasonal lull in the recent weeks.
