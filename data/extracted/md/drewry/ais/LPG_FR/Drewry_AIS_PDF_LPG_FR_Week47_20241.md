## VLGC spot rates improved with improved supply and rising inquiries from China and India

* The tonne mile index improving, backed up robust US and Middle East supply and increasing demand from India and China

* Spot freight rates rose 2% WoW on AG-Japan and 8% WoW on US-Japan routes.

* Low residential LPG prices in India raised laden tonnage in the Indian Ocean, and strong petchem demand despite negative margins did same in the Pacific.

* Rates are expected to improve over Nov-Dec, with the majority of hurricane activity in USG now over. Vessel surplus to curb growth.
