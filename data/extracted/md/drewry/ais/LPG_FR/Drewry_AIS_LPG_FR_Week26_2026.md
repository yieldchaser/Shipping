## VLGC tonne-mile demand rises despite subdued spot market activity

* Drewry's tonne-mile index rose further from the previous week, indicating increasing share of laden VLGCs in both the East and West.

* Spot market activity remained subdued as weak Asian buying interest limited enquiries and fixtures.

* Meanwhile, stronger Middle East demand tightened Atlantic vessel availability and brought additional July US Gulf cargoes to market.

* Freight rates in the East continued to command a strong premium over ex-US Gulf routes, supported by robust Middle East loading activity.

* Meanwhile, regional oil companies increasingly used their own vessels for Strait of Hormuz transits.
