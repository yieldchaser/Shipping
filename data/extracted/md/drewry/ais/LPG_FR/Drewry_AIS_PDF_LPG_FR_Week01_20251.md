## VLGCs start 2025 with rates firming as vessel availability tightens

1 content_summary

* Higher loading in both- the US and the Middle East eases the available tonnage list; 15 VLGCs loaded from the US in the first week of 2025

* VLGC spot rates on the AG-Japan and US-Japan recovering from the holiday lull, yet down 4% and 1% WoW, respectively; Tonne-mile index up 2.6%

* Robust Asian demand and fall in LPG prices to support trade. Expected increase in vessel loadings from the Middle East to lure shipowners to divert vessels to the East.

* The US-Asia price arbitrage supported trade, but prices will be volatile as the US terminal capacity nears its limits.
