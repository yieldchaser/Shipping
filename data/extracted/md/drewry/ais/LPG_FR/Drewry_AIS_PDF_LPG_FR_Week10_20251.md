## VLGC earnings to improve slightly on improving Indian demand

* VLGC earnings showcased stability on the benchmark US-Japan and AG-Japan routes, despite a 4.3% increase in tonne miles demand.

* The tonne miles demand increased because of robust Indian demand. However, vessel surplus dampened recovery in rates.

* Congestion at Indian terminals rising due to the higher imports, created delays as indicated by the increase in laden tonnage at anchor.

* Conversely, the indicator showcased decline in China as the country’s LPG imports remained subdued.

| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |

| 7     | West            | Region West refers to the region West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 8     | BLPG1           | Spot rates on Arabian Gulf–Japan route and data have been taken from Baltic Exchange                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |

| 10    | At anchor       | Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 11    | Moored          | Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
