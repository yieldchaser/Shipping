## VLGC spot market holds firm despite declining tonne-mile demand

* The tonne-mile index continued to decline, reaching 109.

* At the same time, the share of laden VLGCs fell in the East but increased in the West.

* Tight vessel availability in the West kept the VLGC spot market firm throughout the week.

* Meanwhile, rates in the East remained strong despite renewed uncertainty following the resumption of hostilities in the Middle East Gulf.

* Although demand for vessels willing to enter the Middle East Gulf stayed healthy, the pool of eligible ships continued to shrink.
