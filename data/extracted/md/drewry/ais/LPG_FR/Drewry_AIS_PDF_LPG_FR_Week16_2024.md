## LPG trade stable amid a volatile arbitrage

1 content_summary

* Fixing activity to remain robust in the next week as the US-Asia price arbitrage gradually opens.

* Congestion increased with more laden tonnage at anchor in China.

* China's petchem sector to support LPG demand amid the start of new PDH plants. Further boost will be provided with improving PDH margins.

* Meanwhile, seasonality will affect the Asian LPG demand from the rest of the sectors, pressurising rates.

* The tonne-mile index rose to 124.1 points from 118.7 last week and has been increasing consecutively for the last six weeks with increased transits from COGH and the Panama Canal.

| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |
| 3     | Laden dwt miles | Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight(or capacity) for that fleet                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
