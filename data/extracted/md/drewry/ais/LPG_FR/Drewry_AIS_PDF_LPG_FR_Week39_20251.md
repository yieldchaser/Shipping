## VLGC rates ease but maintain good levels

* Spot rates on the AG-Japan and US-Japan routes declined by 6% and 2% WoW, respectively, amid increased fixtures absorbing vessels

* China's LPG demand is projected to fall, leading to increased vessel availability. Japan and South Korean import demand muted despite open arbitrage.

* Drewry's laden tonnage at anchor- China indicator showcases a rise in vessels waiting to discharge following the second tariff pause between US and China.

* Meanwhile, Drewry's LPG tonne-mile Index improved due to the surge in US-China LPG trade.

* The impact of the USTR regulations on LPG shipping remains insignificant with under 10% of VLGCs affected, already being reprogrammed away from US trade.

| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |
