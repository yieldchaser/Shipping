## VLGC earnings to remain under stress amid vessel oversupply.

* VLGC earnings on the benchmark US-Japan route fell 5% WoW while on AG-Japan route declined 13% WoW.
* The recent winter storm in the US disrupted loadings at terminals, causing buildup of tonnage and exerted downward pressure on rates.
* As vessels ballasted towards the Middle East to mitigate the situation, an oversupply near the Gulf pressurised spot rates on AG-Japan route.
* This shift in export source impacted tonne-miles demand, resulting in a 7% WoW drop in the tonne-miles index to 116.6 points.
* Laden tonnage at anchor in China decreased sharply this week as the country entered the Lunar New year holidays.
