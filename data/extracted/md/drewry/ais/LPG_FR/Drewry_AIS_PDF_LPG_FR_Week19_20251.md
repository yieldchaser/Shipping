## VLGC earnings improve as trade pattern changes

* Spot rates on the benchmark US-Japan and AG-Japan route rose 2% and 9% WoW, respectively, reaching $104 and $53 per tonne in week 19.

* Rates on AG-Japan surged as China switched to the Middle East amid US-China tariff escalations.

* The recent de-escalation involving retaliatory tariffs on LPG falling to 10% from 125% earlier likely to boost US-China trade.

* Drewry's LPG tonne miles index registered a 7% WoW rise as Asian demand showed sighs of recovery.

* Drewry's laden tonnage indicator showcases vessels moving towards US for loading, indicating another trade pattern change.
