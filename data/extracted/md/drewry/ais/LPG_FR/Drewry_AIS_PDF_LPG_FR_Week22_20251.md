## VLGC TCE at $50,000 pd but demand uncertainty to break the rally

* Spot rates on the benchmark US-Japan and AG-Japan declined 2% and 7% WoW, respectively, after peaking at $113.5 and $67.2 per tonne in week 21.
* Repositioning of vessels from the Middle East to the USGC created temporary tightness, impacting rates.
* Uncertainty in the market keeps buyers in a "wait-and-see" mode, fixtures lacklustre out of the US.
* Despite volatility in rates, Drewry's LPG tonne miles index remained stable.
* Meanwhile, Drewry's Laden tonnage at anchor indicator for the Pacific Basin showed an uptick, reflecting an increase in demand from Far East Asia, particularly China.
