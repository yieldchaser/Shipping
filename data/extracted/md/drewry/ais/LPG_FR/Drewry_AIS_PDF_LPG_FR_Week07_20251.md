## VLGC earnings to remain under stress amid vessel oversupply and weak demand

* VLGC earnings on the benchmark US-Japan route fell 4% WoW while on AG-Japan route declined 6% WoW.
* Increased vessel availability amid weak demand from India and China exerted downward pressure on rates. Low Laden tonnage at anchor in China confirms this trend.
* Accordingly, tonne miles index declined 4.5% reaching 113.6 points, indicating reduction in long-haul trade.
* Normalisation of red sea crisis will have minimal impact on LPG trade. However, petchem trade on Europe-Asia route can see marginal improvement.
