### Falling tonne-mile demand deflating VLGC spot rates despite bump in Indian inquires

- Declining laden tonnage across the Indian, Atlantic, and Pacific oceans indicates a reduction in LPG trade along major routes.
- Spot rates on AG-Japan fall 17% WoW while US-Japan fall 7%; Rates in the West could ease further as vessels compete for limited cargo.
- Higher Indian inquiries for November deliveries to provide support; Delays at East coast ports due to cyclone Dana may lead to congestion.
- Rising propane prices, driven by Israel-Iran tensions, are expected to decrease LPG demand east of Suez as petrochemical industry, especially in China, is likely to shift to naphtha.
