### Panama Canal delays and rising Western rates drive VLGC spot market

* The tonne-mile index dropped to 92.3 in Week 35 after rising to reach 99.6 the previous week.

* Meanwhile, the share of laden vessels shifted slightly, declining in the East and rising in the West, though both week-over-week changes were marginal.

* The spot market in the West stabilized slightly after the previous week's gains, which were driven by fewer available ships and growing Panama Canal delays tied to Super El Nino expectations.

* Higher canal auction costs and reduced transit slots from September continued to push rates up from Week 34.

* Despite these Western gains and a minor drop in the East's laden vessel share, Eastern spot activity remained quiet, apart from Vadinar fixtures.
