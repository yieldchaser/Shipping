## VLGC rates collapse on weak Asian demand

* Subdued demand and narrowing US-Asia arbitrage slowed fixing activity lowering shipping rates
* VLGC spot rates dropped 37% WoW on US-Japan route to an average $65.3 per tonne in the week ending 19 January
* Weak petchem margins led to unplanned shutdowns in China, declining the operating rates to 67%
* The number of laden vessels in China and India is lower, even after accounting for seasonality
