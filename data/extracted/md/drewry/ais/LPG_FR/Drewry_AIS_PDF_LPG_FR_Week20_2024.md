## VLGC rates rise on favourable arbitrage

* VLGC spot rates rise 6% WoW on the US-Japan route to an average of $140.17 per tonne in the week ending 10 May

* Improved US-Asia arbitrage and lower Saudi LPG prices aiding fixing activity and demand

* Competition for VLGCs rises at the Panama Canal as LNGCs and containerships increase transits

* Asian LPG demand is still under pressure given the persisting negative PDH margins

* Ballast tonnage volume rises in the Atlantic as VLGCs opt to move to the West through COGH for higher earning; also squeezing vessel availability
