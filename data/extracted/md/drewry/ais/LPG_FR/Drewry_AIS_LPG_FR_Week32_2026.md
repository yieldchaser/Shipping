## VLGC market activity remains muted as tonne-miles index falls

* The tonne-miles index continued its downward trend this week, falling to 95.1, while the share of laden vessels declined in both the East and West.

* The VLGC spot market was relatively quiet, with challenging trading conditions and the summer holiday period limiting fixture activity.

* In the West, rates eased slightly but remained firm for some routes, while charterers remained cautious due to volatile arbitrage economics.

* Although the end-August and early-September vessel positions remain tight, elevated Panama Canal auction costs have encouraged charterers to postpone fixing.

* Meanwhile, the Eastern market saw very little activity this week as uncertainty surrounding the ongoing Middle East conflict continued to weigh on sentiment.
