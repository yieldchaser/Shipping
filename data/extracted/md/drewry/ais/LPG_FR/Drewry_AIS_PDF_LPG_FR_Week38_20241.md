### VLGC rates weaken on lower Middle Eastern exports, increasing vessel availability in West

* VLGC transits rise through Cape of Good Hope; decline at the Panama Canal.

* Fall in Middle Eastern exports incentivise owners to ballast vessels to the West.

* Higher COGH transits improving tonne-mile demand; vessel build-up likely in the West.

* No significant impact on US Gulf loadings from Hurricane Francine.

* Spot rates declined 30% and 17% in the first half of the month on AG-Japan and US-Japan benchmark route.
