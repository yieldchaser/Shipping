## VLGC spot rates slump amidst weak Asian demand.

* Spot freight rates fell by 3% WoW on the AG-Japan route and by 2% WoW on the US-Japan route due to weak downstream demand in China.

* Accordingly, the tonne miles index dropped by 3% WoW with a dismal trend expected over December.

* Rising congestion at Indian LPG terminals to provide boost to VLGC rates in December.

* US-Asia arbitrage could face volatility with High US terminal fees and lower Chinese petchem production easing Asian prices.
