## VLGC earnings to dwindle amid weak demand, higher vessel availability

* VLGC spot rates on the benchmark US-Japan route and AG-Japan route fell 1% and 8% WoW respectively.

* Drewry's LPG tonne mile Index also registered a 6% WoW fall to 117.6 points, indicative of reduced trade

* Ballast tonnage indicator for China surged, suggesting a rise in 'for order' vessels in the region

* Meanwhile, laden tonnage at anchor indicator for India declined, signalling easing congestion amid easing LPG demand
