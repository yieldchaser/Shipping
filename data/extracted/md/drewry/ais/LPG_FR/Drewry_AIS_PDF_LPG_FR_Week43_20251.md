## VLGC spot rates normalise amid easing demand and increased tonnage availability

* Spot rates on the AG-Japan and US-Japan routes declined by 7% and 4% WoW, respectively, amid fading demand and increased vessel availability.
* Demand concerns have again hit the LPG market as China's LPG imports decline due to weak petchem margins. Meanwhile, Japan and South Korea's weak residential demand impacted their imports.
* Buyers remain cautious on 2026 term volumes amid weak fundamentals, leading to lower contract renewals and inspiring decline in Saudi CP benchmarks. Elevated spot rates in prior months have further compressed petchem margins due to high landed cost, dampening downstream demand.
* Drewry’s laden tonnage at anchor (China) indicator fell, indicating the end of China’s restocking phase and the onset of a low-demand 4Q25.
* Additionally, proposed China's port fees on US-linked vessels and potential USTR regulations could bifurcate the VLGC market, forcing US- or China-linked tonnage to reallocate away from the US-China trade.
