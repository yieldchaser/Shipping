### VLGC rates normalises amid easing Panama congestion and rising vessel availability

- Spot rates on the AG-Japan and US-Japan routes declined by 7% and 5% WoW, respectively, as increased vessel availability reversed the prior bullish trend.
- Easing Panama Canal congestion is resulting in increased VLGC transits, increasing vessel availability.
- Low Asian delivered prices and narrowing US-Asia arbitrage also pressurise spot rates.
- Meanwhile, Drewry's laden tonnage at anchor in China indicator rose, signalling LPG import recovery amid rising PDH demand.
- Other factors—reduced war risk premium and 90-day extension of US- China tariff pause—has reasserted supply-demand fundamentals and led to downward movement in rates.

## Glossary

- **fr vessel:** Fully refrigerated tankers built to carry liquefied gases at low temperature and atmospheric pressure in onboard fully refrigerated storage tanks, and are in the size range 15,000-100,000 cbm.
- **Trading status:** Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen.
- **Laden dwt miles:** Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight(or capacity) for that fleet
- **Bunker price:** Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port
- **% Laden:** Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair
- **East:** East of Suez Canal and comprises China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania
- **West:** Region West refers to the region West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region
- **BLPG1:** Spot rates on Arabian Gulf–Japan route and data have been taken from Baltic Exchange
- **BLPG2:** Spot rates on US-Europe route and data have been taken from Baltic Exchange
- **At anchor:** Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area
- **Moored:** Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging
- **Underway:** Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination
