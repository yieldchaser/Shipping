## VLGC rates rises on improving arbitrage

* US LPG prices decline as propane inventory build-up due to increased production

* Widening arbitrage supports a rise in US VLGC fixtures for Asia

* China's LPG demand improves with conclusion of lunar new moon holidays and favourable propane-naphtha spread

* Spot rates increased 7% WoW on US-Japan route to an average of $94.12 per tonne in the week ending 16 February

* VLGC passage through Panama Canal increases MoM in February
