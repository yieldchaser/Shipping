## Rates improve on higher loading in the US

1 content_summary

* VLGC spot rates improving with easing congestion at US Gulf; backlog created due to hurricane delays clearing.

* Limited activity in the East of Suez but fixtures look to increase on Indian demand ahead of festivals.

* No major impact on LPG shipping so far due to the escalating Iran-Israel tensions in the Middle East.

* A longer fixing window and narrowing US-Asia arbitrage expected to curb the rise in VLGC rates.

* LPG demand in the Asian petchem sector expected to decline due to negative margins and competition from naphtha.
