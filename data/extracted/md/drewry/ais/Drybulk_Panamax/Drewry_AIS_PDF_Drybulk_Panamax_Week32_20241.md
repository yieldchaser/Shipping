## Panamax utilisation rises

1 content_summary

* There was a slight increase in Panamax vessel utilisation, which rose marginally from 91.6% to 92.0% WoW. This rise is attributed to the consecutively rising ballast speed, indicating positive sentiments in the market.

* The tonnemile index reversed its downward trend last week and is likely to expand further with the growing soybean exports.

* Simultaneously, the tonnage waiting at the load ports in the Atlantic has revived. However, it remains low YoY. However, due to the substantial decline in the laden tonnage at anchor in the Atlantic, the tonnage in port in the West has dropped to historically low levels.
