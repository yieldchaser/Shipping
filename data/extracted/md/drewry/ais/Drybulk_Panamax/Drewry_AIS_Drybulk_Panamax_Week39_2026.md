## Market Commentary

### Atlantic market leads gains in the Panamax market as coal demand supports the Pacific

- The tonne-miles index rose 2.7 to reach 108.4 at the end of week 39, with tighter available tonnage and stronger cargo enquiry in the North Continent and West Mediterranean lifting Skaw-Gibraltar transatlantic route rates to $20,380/day. Fronthaul demand was even stronger.

- East Coast Australia round-trip rates also increased, while Indonesian coal activity strengthened later in the week, with an 82,000-dwt fixing via Indonesia to India at $25,500/day. A relatively less tonnage list helps owners to maintain firm rates.

- China’s August coal production fell 7% YoY, extending the contraction to 14 consecutive months, while coal-fired power generation has continued to outpace domestic coal supply. This widening supply-demand gap could support near-term coal imports and Panamax demand, although softer coal-fired generation may limit the upside.
