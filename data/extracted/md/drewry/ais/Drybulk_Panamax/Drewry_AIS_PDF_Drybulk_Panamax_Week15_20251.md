## Panamax utilisation dwindled
1 content_summary

* The Panamax tonne-mile index experienced a 3% decline WoW in the second week of April. Concurrently, utilisation rates decreased by 0.3 percentage points, reaching 93.2%, leading to a softening of freight rates.

* Spot rates on key routes saw significant drops, with the transatlantic and transpacific routes plunging by 18% and 23% WoW, respectively. A reduction in the percentage of laden tonnage in both eastern and western regions accompanied this decline.

* Laden tonnage in India declined due to declining non-coking coal imports, which fell by almost 21% YoY in the past four weeks.
