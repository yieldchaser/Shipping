## Market Commentary

### Surge in Panamax demand

- Drewry's tonnemile index for Panamax vessels stayed elevated after surging last month, with a 13% rise YoY.

- This is juxtaposed with the lowest inactivity levels in the Panamax segment after the end of Covid lockdowns, resulting in ample supply in the market, squeezing utilisation levels.

- The tonnage underway in the East has reached historically high. With more than 20 mdwt laden tonnage already waiting at the anchor at the discharge ports in the East, the congestion is likely to exacerbate in the region.

- Despite ample coal trade in the Asian markets aiding vessel demand, the Baltic P5TC rates have been on a downward trend as vessel utilisation remained subdued.
