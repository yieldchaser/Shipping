## Surge in Panamax utilisation

* The utilisation of Panamax vessels rose from 91.4 % to 92.3 % last week but remained below the level in 2023.
* Additionally, the tonne-mile index rose marginally, exceeding last year's level, driven by robust grain exports from the US Gulf and Brazil to Asia.
* Meanwhile, the congestion increased in the Pacific due to high coal shipments from Australia and Indonesia.
