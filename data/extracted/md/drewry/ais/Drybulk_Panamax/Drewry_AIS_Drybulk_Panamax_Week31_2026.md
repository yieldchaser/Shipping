## Panamax utilisation remains high despite softer market indicators

* Panamax utilisation has eased from its 2026 peak but remains high. Utilisation reached a yearly high of 93.6% in week 26 before declining to 92.7% in week 30. Despite the recent easing, it remains above the 92.2% recorded in the same period last year.

* Port congestion is becoming less supportive of the market, although conditions remain mixed by region. Laden congestion declined sharply in the East, China and Indian Ocean, while congestion increased in the Pacific, Oceania and Atlantic. Ballast congestion also declined in China and the Indian Ocean but increased in the Pacific and Atlantic.

* Overall, easing congestion in key loading and discharge regions, combined with higher speeds, could continue to put pressure on utilisation if demand does not strengthen.
