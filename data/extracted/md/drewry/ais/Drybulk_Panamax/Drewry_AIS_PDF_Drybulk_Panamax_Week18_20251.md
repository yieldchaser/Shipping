### Panamax Index drops amid vessel speed changes

1 content_summary

* ● The Panamax tonne-miles index declined by 4.4% in week 18 in tandem with falling ballast and laden speed.

* ● Laden speed in the West dropped, accompanied by a decrease in the percentage of laden vessels in the region as vessels sought employment.

* ● Vessels at anchor in the East increased 15.5% MoM, followed by increased congestion of both ballast and laden vessels in the East.

* ● Laden tonnage in India increased due to increased thermal coal imports, which hit a 10-month high, led by a surge in shipments from Indonesia, resulting in improved market sentiments.
