## Low seasonal demand impacts Panamax employability.
1 content_summary

* The global Panamax utilisation witnessed a substantial fall, declining to 93.7% in the second week of 2025.

* The Panamax tonne-miles index experienced a 2.6% WoW decline, reaching 96.8 points, attributable to seasonally low coal demand from Far East Asian market owing to Chinese new year holiday.

* The tonnage waiting at the anchorage at China's discharge ports dropped to 3.4 mdwt, significantly lower compared to the same period in last two years.

* The decline in tonnage underway in the east also corroborates the seasonal lull in China's markets.
