## Market Commentary

### Panamax utilization rises

- There was a slight increase in Panamax vessel utilisation, which rose marginally from 93.1% to 93.4% WoW. This 0.3% uptick is primarily due to robust activities in the coal market.

- The heightened demand for coal in Asia, especially from China and India, driven by increased power consumption during the summer months, has significantly boosted coal imports. As a result, this seasonal surge in demand has led to a higher need for Panamax vessels, thereby boosting utilisation.

- The laden tonnage at anchor in China has increased by 0.6 mdwt. This surge in congestion is attributed to substantial imports of soybean from Brazil and corn from Argentina. The influx of these agricultural commodities has resulted in a buildup of laden vessels at anchor, indicating heightened demand and seasonal peaks in import activities.
