## Panamax Pacific market recovered after Indonesian coal weakness
1 content_summary

* The Panamax market improved during the week, with the tonne-miles index reaching 114.8, an 11% YoY increase, and the Baltic benchmark P5TC rising to $19,825/day alongside increasing vessel Utilisation. The Atlantic remained the main driver, while Pacific gains were supported by improving demand from Australia and the North Pacific.

* The Pacific started from a softer base as Indonesian round-voyage demand declined due to high coal inventories and weaker buying from end-user power plants. However, with a large number of spot vessels covered last week and fresh cargoes gradually returning, the market began to recover. Indonesia-South China rates improved, while tighter vessel availability in Australia supported overall sentiment.

* The North Atlantic continued to strengthen as cargo demand outpaced available supply, particularly in the North Continent and the West Mediterranean. Transatlantic rates moved to over $21,700/day, while fronthaul and US East Coast- India business continued to firm. The South Atlantic also remained supported, with tight end-July tonnage.
