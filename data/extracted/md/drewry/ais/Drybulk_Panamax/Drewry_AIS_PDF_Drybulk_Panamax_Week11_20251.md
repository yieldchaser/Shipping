## Market Commentary

### Panamax Tonne-miles index rises

- The Panamax Tonne-miles index increased 7 points over the week, reaching 103. The tonnage at the port and anchor declined MoM in the East with more vessels underway, pushing up the tonne-miles in the previous week.

- The rise in tonnage underway supported the firming up of freight rates in week 11. Utilisation also increased marginally in tandem with the tonne-miles index.

- Laden tonnage at anchor dampened WoW for China due to a decline in seaborne coking coal imports, attributed to increased overland imports from Mongolia.

- Conversely, laden tonnage at anchor in India inched up WoW after two weeks of decline, due to the country's increased focus on domestic steel production which could potentially drive up coking coal demand.
