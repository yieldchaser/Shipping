## Panamax utilisation falls aas China cuts its coal imports

1 content_summary

* The utilisation of vessels in the Panamax segment dropped to 92% in week 22, aligning with drop in the tonne-mile index. This moves in tandem with falling Baltic rates on major Panamax routes.

* A key driver of this reduced utilisation is China’s decision to curb coal imports and encouraging power plants to stockpile domestically mined coal instead. This move comes amid falling domestic coal prices and an effort to support local miners after a significant output expansion.

* However, increased Australian coal cargoes to India has contributed to growing congestion at Indian ports in week 22.
