### Ballast speed improves, indicating gradual market recovery

* The global Panamax utilisation remained steady at 93.5% in week 6. However, the Panamax tonne-miles index declined 1% WoW, reaching 92.1.

* Global laden speed increased by 2.5% WoW. Although global ballast speed is down YoY, it has increased marginally WoW and is in tandem with laden speed, indicating slow but gradual market recovery and increased vessel employment.

* Increased days at anchorage for ballast vessels, in alignment with rising ballast tonnage at anchorage in the Pacific and Atlantic, will put pressure on vessel supply in the short term. However, it is not expected to impact rates in the immediately .
