## Market Commentary

### Surge in tonnemile index

- The Panamax vessel utilisation increased last week from 92.8% to 92.9%. However, the rise was marginal due to the contrary impact of change in laden and ballast speeds. While the global laden speed increased from 10.58 knots to 10.84 knots, the ballast speed dropped from 11.46 knots to 10.83 knots during the week, counterbalacing the impact on the vessel utilisation.

- The tonne-mile index has risen significantly and reached its peak last week in 2024. The buoyant coal and grain trade continues to support Panamax demand, with the surge in the export of steel products aiding additional demand.

- The tonnage underway in the East has been historically high in July 2024. With consistently strong coal trade between Indonesia and China, coupled with the surge in steel exports from China, Panamax demand in the region is high. The congestion at China's load ports reached the second-highest level in 2024 last week, indicating that exports from the region will rise in near-term.
