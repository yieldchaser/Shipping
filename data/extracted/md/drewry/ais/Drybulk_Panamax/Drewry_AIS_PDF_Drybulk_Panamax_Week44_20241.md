## Stability in utilisation

* The global Panamax utilisation remained stable at 92.1% in week 41 and 42 of 2024, while continuing to be substantially lower YoY.

* Meanwhile, the global tonne-mile index remained elevated as ample corn exports from the Atlantic have aided Panamax employability. US' corn exports have been on an uptrend despite lower imports from China.

* Congestion at discharge ports declined in all major regions, except in the Indian subcontinent, where buoyant coal imports kept congestion high.
