## Rise in Utilisation as speed increases

* The Panamax vessel utilisation expanded last week from 91.6% to 92.5%, after having declined for three consecutive weeks. This rise is attributed to consecutively rising ballast speeds, indicating positive market sentiment.

* Meanwhile, the tonne-mile index rose sharply, reaching a higher level than last year, driven by buoyant soybean trade from ECSA, particularly Brazil.

* Additionally, longer vessel routes due to Panama Canal congestion have boosted vessel demand of this segment.

* As congestion at China's discharge ports has reached the lowest levels in 2024 so far, we project it to tread upwards in the upcoming weeks as China's soybean imports expand.
