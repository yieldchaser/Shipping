## Softness in tonnage demand weighs on freight rates

\* The Suezmax tonne-miles index continued to improve in week 8, ending the week at 100.3 points. However, this week's rise was slightly flatter than the previous week, with a sharp rise in the number of vessels at ports in the East. Activity was relatively subdued this week due to the lesser availability of cargo, pushing down rates.

\* Rates declined on most routes except for the Black Sea to Med, which remained intact. Market activity next week is likely to remain on par with week 8, which could result in current rates remaining firm, especially towards the East.
