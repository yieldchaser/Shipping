## Market Commentary

### Firm Eastern demand to support freight rates next week

- The Suezmax tonne-miles index continued to rise in week 11, ending the week at 116.9 points. It has been continuously rising for the past seven weeks, though utilisation of the Suezmax fleet has seen slight variances.

- Freight rates firmed up on most routes especially on ex-MEG routes, as Middle Eastern crude continues to grow in popularity in Eastern markets this year.

- Market sentiments for next week are likely to remain positive as firm Suezmax demand in the East seems to have had spillovers in the Western markets as well which will likely continue to push up freight rates overall.
