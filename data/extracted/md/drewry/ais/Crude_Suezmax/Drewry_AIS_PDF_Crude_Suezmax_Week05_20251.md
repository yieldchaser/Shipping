## Market Commentary

### Eastern Suezmax demand sees a rise ahead of the Lunar New Year

- The Suezmax tonne-miles index improved close to five points ending the week at 89.1 points as the pre-lunar New Year rush caused an uptick in the tonnage demand ahead of the holidays. As a result, week 6 is likely to continue seeing a slight squeeze in the tonnage availability especially for Eastbound cargoes.

- A 38% MoM increase in the tonnage at anchor in the West has also been accompanied by a decline in the vessel speeds of both ballast and laden vessels with the tonnage underway declining in tandem. Market activity at the moment remains sluggish but rates continue to be firm. Next week could see some improvement in tonnage demand but will most likely remain on similar levels as in week 5.
