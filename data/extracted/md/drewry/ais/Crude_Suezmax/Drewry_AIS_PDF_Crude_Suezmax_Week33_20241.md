## Market Commentary

### Suzmax employment remains subdued due to weak seasonal factors

- The Suezmax tonne-miles index declined in week 32, ending the week at 93.4 points. The decline has been continuing since week 27, caused by an overall weakness in oil demand. This has also been reflected in the fleet's utilization, which has been declining since week 25.

- There is also strong competition for cargo from the VLCCs. There has also been minimal movement in the markets in the Arabian Gulf region heading Eastward.

- The Suezmax market in West Africa remained steady but sentiments for next week are not very optimistic. Freight rates declined on most routes except on routes via the Suez Canal. The Suezmax market is likely to remain subdued in week 33 as well.
