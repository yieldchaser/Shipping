## Market Commentary

### Firmness in the Suezmax market likely to weaken

- The Suezmax tonne-miles index fell in week 47 ending the week at 95.1 points. Despite the index weakening, rates firmed up as market activity improved in the Middle East post the Bahri week. Many vessels headed towards the East, especially to India with some upward push coming from the Med. The tonnage towards the East is likely to increase next week, keeping rates firm.

- Activity towards the USG remained buoyant, aided by a steady Aframax market. However, next week might see dullness in the market due to a lack of tonnage demand.

- Overall, most of the gains in spot rates came from tonnage demand stemming from the East. This might improve the tonnage underway next week. However, there is likely to be some slowdown in activity in the other regions, keeping more tonnage at the ports, especially the Atlantic and AG.

| 7     | West            | Region West refers to the region West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 10    | At anchor       | Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 11    | Moored          | Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
