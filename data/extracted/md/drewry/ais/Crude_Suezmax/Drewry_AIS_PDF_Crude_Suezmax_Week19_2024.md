## Market Commentary

### Tonne-miles index climbs up for the third consecutive week

- The tonne-miles index continued to rise in week 18, from its uptick in week 16 to reach 120.7 points. Robust demand for tonnage on long haul routes helped in pushing up the tonne-mile demand last week as well as freight rates on routes in the West.

- Next week is likely to see a positive continuation of the demand for Suezmax vessels especially on the longer haul routes.
