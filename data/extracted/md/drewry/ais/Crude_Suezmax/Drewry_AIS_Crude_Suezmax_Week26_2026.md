## Stable utilisation and strong tonne-miles keep Suezmax earnings supported

* Suezmax utilisation remained stable at 90%, although slightly lower MoM, but above last year (89.7%), while the tonne-mile index grew by 8.4 point, suggesting improving vessel demand.

* Despite an 11.6% MoM fall in bunker prices, vessel speeds have not changed significantly, particularly in the East, preventing lower fuel costs from translating into higher fleet supply.

* Recent Iran-US developments have likely added short-term volatility to vessel positioning, particularly in the Middle-East. The brief de-escalation followed by a rapid breakdown, may have influenced regional fleet movements, contributing to the observed tightening in the East. However, the impact appears temporary, with no clear disruption to tonne-mile demand.
