## Market Commentary

### Robust tonnage demand in WAF keeps freight rates high

- The Suezmax tonne-miles index ended the week at 123.4 points, reaching a new high for the year as freight rates have been gaining steadily over the past few weeks. Freight rates increased on several routes towards the West, while they were steadier towards the East.

- Market activity towards West Africa was robust throughout the week, which helped push up tonne-mile demand. There was also high tonnage demand in the larger VLCC, which had trickle-down effects on its smaller counterpart. Market sentiments are likely to remain similar next week.
