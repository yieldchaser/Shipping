### Firm utilisation persists despite lower tonne-miles and rising eastern availability

1 content_summary

* Despite suezmax utilisation remaining firm at 91%, the tonne-mile index fell from 118.3 to 104.8 as a number of long-haul Atlantic to Asia voyages reached their discharge destinations. This increased vessel availability in the east and reduced the share of laden tonnage, indicating that voyage completions outpaced fresh long-haul cargo fixing .

* Atlantic loading regions appeared to remain the primary source of demand, with vessels continuing to be absorbed from waiting positions and re-entering active trading.

* Despite the increase in Eastern vessel presence, the share of laden vessels in the East fell 5.4 percentage points MoM, suggesting many recently arrived ships had already discharged cargoes and transitioned into ballast status.

* Port congestion across key eastern regions remained relatively limited, indicating that operational delays had little impact on effective vessel availability.
