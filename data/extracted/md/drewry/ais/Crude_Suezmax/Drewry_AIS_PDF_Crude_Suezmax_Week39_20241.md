## Market Commentary

### Freight rates likely to remain firm next week

- The Suezmax markets saw a firming up of freight rates despite a soft week. The tonne-miles index showed a slight increase of 0.7 points ending the week at 108.2. Freight rates firmed up on most routes and sentiments are high as winter demand is expected to boost tonnage demand in the coming weeks.

- China recently released new oil export quotas to boost trade of refined products which is expected to support Suezmax freight rates in the coming weeks. Meanwhile there was some firming of rates in the Med/Black Sea region and the USG to UKC routes which will likely attract more ballast tonnage next week.

- Towards West Africa, sentiments remained high but it did not translate to any uptick in trade activity. However, this leaves much potential for Suezmax demand to grow in week 39.
