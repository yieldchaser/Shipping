## Market Commentary

### Freight rates gain on Med and UKC routes

- The Suezmax tonne-miles index declined by close to 3 points in week 10 to finish at 106.4. It was a mixed week for the segment as trade activity rose and waned in various regions however, rates on TD20 gained $3 by the end of the week.

- The global ballast speed rose last week by around 0.5kn inching closer to 9kn mostly due to increased speed towards the East. Some activity could likely increase Eastward, especially China as indicated by the strong growth in %laden towards the East. Though, as a result, we could see an increase in tonnage ballasting back towards the Suez as well.
