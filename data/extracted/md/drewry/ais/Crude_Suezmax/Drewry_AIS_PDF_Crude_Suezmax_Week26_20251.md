## Market Commentary

### Ceasefire in the Middle East causes rates to weaken

- It was an overall soft week for the Suezmax market. The easing of tensions in the Middle East due to the ceasefire has caused rates to dwindle, especially for the ex-MEG routes.

- The tonne miles index did show an uptick amid all the new developments in the Middle East. While the risk of an oil supply cut due to the possible closure of the Strait of Hormuz briefly spurred oil demand, increasing the underway laden vessels, freight rates will likely weaken next week.
