## Market Commentary

### Trans-Pacific trade leads the Suezmax demand in week 14

- The Suezmax tonne-miles index softened in week 14, ending the week at 104.2 points. It was a quieter week, especially towards the East with sentiments for week 15 not being very bright.

- Tonnage was well supplied in the AG and WAF regions softening rates especially on Eastbound routes. Market in the USG and Brazil were most active last week both showing an uptick in their trade activity.

- The ballast speed in the West increased by 1 kn over the week as the firm market activity is expected to continue into the next week, causing more ships to ballast Westward. However, Eastbound activity is expected to remain soft in week 15 which will also cause freight rates to weaken.
