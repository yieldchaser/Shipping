## Market Commentary

### A forward looking week for Suezmaxes ahead

- The Suezmax tonne-mile index saw minimal change in week 29, ending the week at 108.8 points. While markets were generally quieter, demand saw an uptick towards the end of the week. However, the lull throughout the majority of the week caused freight rates to lower, which might prompt cargo owners to continue exerting pressure to keep freight rates down even in the next week.

- The markets in the WAF region are likely to remain the most active next week, which could continue to cause rates to weaken. Meanwhile, the laden speed in the East and West showed an increase, pointing towards a more hopeful week for Suezmax tankers.
