## Market Commentary

### Subdued Suezmax demand to remain the trend

- The Suezmax tanker market has been facing an underwhelming demand this year, which will likely remain weak as we enter the mid-year seasonal lull. The tonne-miles index did see a rise in week 25, ending at 111.9 points as route diversions continued to stretch the Suzmax voyages.

- The crude oil demand in Asia has been weak this year, but the %laden in the East saw a slight uptick last week. There was an increase in tonnage underway in the East, which could also include tankers that have newly entered the CPP market.

- The markets in the West have also witnessed similar lulls and will likely remain subdued next week, affecting freight rates negatively.

Glossary

Week 26 | 2024

| S No. | Terms           | Define                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| ----- | --------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1     | Suezmax vessel  | Crude tanker vessels which have dwt in the range of 120,000 - 200,000.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               |
| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet.<br/>Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |
| 3     | Laden dwt miles | Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight(or capacity) for that fleet                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 4     | Bunker price    | Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 5     | % Laden         | Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| 6     | East            | East of Suez Canal and comprises China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| 7     | West            | Region West refers to the region West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 10    | At anchor       | Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 11    | Moored          | Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 12    | Underway        | Vessels currently at sea and are currently underway towards their destination                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
