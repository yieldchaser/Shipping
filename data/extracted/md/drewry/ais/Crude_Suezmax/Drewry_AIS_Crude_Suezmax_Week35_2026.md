### Atlantic strength underpins utilisation amid muted tonne-mile growth.

1 content_summary

* Suezmax utilisation remained firm at 91.6% while tonne-mile index declined slightly to 104.2 . The gap between rising utilisation and softer tonne-mile demand indicates that a larger proportion of Suezmax employment is being generated by shorter regional trades rather than long-haul inter-basin crude movements.

* Tonnage underway in western regions increased notably, pointing to stronger cargo execution and vessel employment across Atlantic-loading areas including West Africa, the Mediterranean, Brazil and the US.

* Indian Ocean laden volumes remained elevated, reflecting continued crude movements through traditional Middle East to Asia trade corridors, although not at levels sufficient to significantly boost tonne-mile demand.
