## Market Commentary

### Lackluster week in the Suezmax market

- The Suezmax tonne-miles index declined for the third week, ending at 102.1 points. Meanwhile, there was an uptick in vessels at anchor MoM with a corresponding decline in vessels underway.

- it was a quiet week for the Sezmax market as rates declined WoW. Tonnage demand towards the UKC was weak causing rates on WAF to UKC to decline further.

- There were some trickle-down effects from the relatively firmer demand in the VLCC markets, supporting rates on ex-USG.

- Freight rates are, however, likely to weaken further in week 37 due to waning tonnage demand in the VLCC segment.

| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |

| 7     | West            | Region West refers to the region West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| 8     | At anchor       | Vessels with navigation status ‘At Anchor’ in the waiting zone near port area                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| 9     | Moored          | Vessels moored in port for loading or discharging                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
