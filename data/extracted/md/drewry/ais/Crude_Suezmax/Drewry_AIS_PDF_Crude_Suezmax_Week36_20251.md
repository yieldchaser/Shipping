## Market Commentary

### Suezmax tonnage demand to remain stable

- The Suezmax tonne-miles index gained 2 points in week 36, ending at 117.4 points. It was a typical week, and freight rates remained stable overall. Much of the market activity is aimed towards end-Sept and early October dates, and minimal activity could be seen in the coming weeks.

- The average speed for both ballast and laden vessels increased in the East and West. In comparison, the %laden has weakened WoW. This will likely result in more vessels at anchor and fewer vessels underway next week.

- Next week, the WAF-UKC trade will likely remain positive with higher gains on freight rates.

Glossary

| S No. | Terms           | Define                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1     | Suezmax vessel  | Crude tanker vessels which have dwt in the range of 120,000 - 200,000.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |
