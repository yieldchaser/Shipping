## Market Commentary

### Freight rates decline as tonnage demand weakens

- It was a quieter week in the Suezmax market as freight rates declined on most routes. The tonne-miles index ended the week at 108 points. The markets started off firm towards the East but dwindled as the week went on with tonnage at anchor being more than 20% higher MoM.

- Towards the Med, the CPC controversy caused some market disruptions weighing down freight rates. Meanwhile, the market towards West Africa was more positive, attracting more ballast vessels from the Med. Rates are likely to remain sideways next week.
