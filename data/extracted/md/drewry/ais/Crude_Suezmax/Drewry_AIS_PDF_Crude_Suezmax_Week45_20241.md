## Market Commentary

### Winter demand is expected to boost Suezmax activity this week

- The Suezmax tonne-mile index ended the week on the softer side at 105.9 points after declining by around 5 points over the week. Utilisation, on the other hand, seems to have headed for a peak for a third time this year. Despite October ending off mild, an uptick in seasonal demand is likely to push up vessel utilisation as well as rates in the coming weeks.

- Week 44 started off soft, but more ballast vessels arrived at the USG, which eased rates in the USG-UKC region. It was a slow week overall as vessel speeds declined in both the East and the West. The markets towards the West had shown more enthusiasm as the Chinese demand remained on the weaker side. Week 45 will, however, see more activity with the highest gains on ex-USG routes.
