## Market Commentary

### Mixed market sentiments keep freight rates buoyed

- The Suezmax tonne-mile index ended the week at 111.8 points after declining by around 4 points. The week ended with mixed sentiments in the Suezmax market as hurricanes continued to disrupt trade in the West Atlantic regions.

- The markets in the UKC also witnessed some softening, with most of the week's gains going towards the MEG, which saw slightly firmer rates. Libyan exports coming back on track also supported the region, attracting more Aframaxes.

- While the Westward markets seem likely to be plagued by limitations from weather conditions, markets towards the East remain hopeful for next week.
