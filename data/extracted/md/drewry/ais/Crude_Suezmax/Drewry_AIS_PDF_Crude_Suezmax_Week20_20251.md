## Market Commentary

### Positive sentiments likely to push up freight rates next week

- The Suezmax market had a slow week with freight rates declining on most routes. Despite this, the utilisation of vessels for this segment has been continuously rising as activity continues to be stable with more vessels being fixed at lower rates.

- The market towards the MEG showed some improvement last week with more enquiries expected to push up rates next week. Similarly, a good level of enquiries towards West Africa for transatlantic voyages are expected to push up rates next week due to a decline in the tonnage supply.

## Section 8: Detailed Indicators (Page 9)

Suezmax Glossary Week 52 | 2024

| S No. | Terms           | Define                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| ----- | --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1     | Suezmax vessel  | Crude tanker vessels which have dwt in the range of 120,000 - 200,000.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| 2     | Trading status  | Status of vessels has been derived primarily from the navigation field within the AIS message. Vessels which do not have IMO number have not been included in the fleet. Furthermore, vessels that have not returned AIS message for at least 30 days, have been excluded from the analysis of fleet status while vessels that have not returned an AIS message for up to 30 days are assumed to have the same trading status as when last seen. |
| 3     | Laden dwt miles | Average distance travelled per week across the laden target fleet multiplied by the average vessel deadweight(or capacity) for that fleet                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 4     | Bunker price    | Price of the VLSFO at Singapore Port                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 5     | % Laden         | Share of laden vessels in total fleet excluding vessels under repair                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |
| 6     | East            | East of Suez Canal and comprises China, Pacific, Indian Ocean and Oceania                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        |
| 7     | West            | Region West refers to the region West of Suez Canal and comprises Atlantic region                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                |
