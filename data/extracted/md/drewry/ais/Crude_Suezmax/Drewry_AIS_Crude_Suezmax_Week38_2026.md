## Suezmax market remained balanced as stronger vessel supply offset trading activity.

1 content_summary

* Suezmax utilisation remained relatively resilient at 89% during the week, even as the tonne-mile index eased to 106.3 from 109.0. The modest decline in demand was largely offset by ongoing crude trading activity; however, improving vessel availability, easing congestion and faster vessel turnaround times increased effective fleet supply and prevented utilisation from strengthening further.

* Vessel availability increased in the East, with anchored capacity rising 12%, pointing to a growing pool of prompt tonnage across Middle East and Asian loading regions.

* Congestion dynamics were mixed across regions. Laden tonnage waiting in China declined noticeably, signalling faster discharge operations and quicker vessel recycling back into the market. Meanwhile, congestion involving laden vessels in the Indian Ocean increased during the week, although the tighter regional conditions were not strong enough to offset broader improvements in vessel availability elsewhere.
