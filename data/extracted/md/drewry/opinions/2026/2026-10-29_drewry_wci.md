## Market Commentary & Analysis

The Drewry World Container Index (WCI), the benchmark widely referenced by procurement teams, decreased 3% to $4,429 per 40ft container, mainly due to a decrease in rates on the Transpacific trade route.

On the Transpacific trade route, rates from Shanghai to Los Angeles fell 6% to $7,514 per 40ft container, and those from Shanghai to New York decreased 5% to $9,631 per 40ft container.

On the Asia–Europe trade route, rates from Shanghai to Rotterdam inched up 1% to $3,686 per 40ft container, and those from Shanghai to Genoa remained stable at $4,151 per 40ft container.
