# Ports & Terminals Financial Insight - October 2026

*Published on 14 October 2026*

Global port throughput growth slowed in 2Q26 amid Middle East disruptions, while aggregate revenue and EBITDA rose year-on-year. The Drewry Port Equity Index outperformed the S&P 500 in 2026, despite weakening throughput momentum.

We forecast slower throughput growth in 2026, followed by a recovery in 2027 as Middle East conditions improve. Valuations vary across operators, with geopolitical risks remaining as a key concern. For more details, please read our latest edition of Financial Insight – Ports & Terminals.
