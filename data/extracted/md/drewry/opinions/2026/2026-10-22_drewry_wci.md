## Market Commentary & Analysis

The Drewry World Container Index (WCI), the benchmark widely referenced by procurement teams, increased 2% to $4,585 per 40ft container, driven by higher rates on the Asia to Europe and Transpacific trade routes.

On the Transpacific trade route, rates from Shanghai to Los Angeles rose 2% to $8,007 per 40ft container and those from Shanghai to New York inched up 1% to $10,166 per 40ft container.

On the Asia–Europe trade route, rates from Shanghai to Rotterdam increased 3% to $3,632 per 40ft container and those from Shanghai to Genoa grew 5% to $4,152 per 40ft container.
