# Drewry’s Ship Operating Costs Annual Review and Forecast Adds Cpi-Adjusted Opex Figures

*Published on 11 November 2026*

London, UK, 11 November 2026: Persistent price inflation across the shipping industry is making independent benchmarking of vessel operating costs more important than ever, according to the latestShip Operating Costs Annual Review and Forecast 2026/27report published by global shipping consultancy Drewry.

For the first time, this year’s edition presents costs in real (CPI-adjusted) terms as well as nominal dollars, (at the shipping sector level) allowing owners, managers and investors to understand how their savings effort perform in an inflationary price environment. While the global Consumer Price Index is not a perfect representation of how prices of equipment and services change in shipping, it is available across most countries of the globe on a long-term basis. The CPI-adjusted opex figures tell an interesting story about how vessel operators make every dollar spent count.

Assessments cover vessels in the container, dry bulk, crude, product, chemical, LNG, LPG, general cargo, reefer, ro-ro and car carrier sectors, and are based on 10-year-old vessels (younger where appropriate), with analysis by vessel age. Drewry’s estimates draw on direct opex data contributions from ship managers through its Ship Operating Cost Steering Group, data from ship operators, specialist expert input and more than four decades of cost data collection.

Looking ahead, operational expenditure is expected to increase steadily, with seafarer availability, decarbonisation regulation and yard capacity among the key factors shaping owners’ cost base over the forecast period. Accompanied by a full Excel dataset, the report enables owners to benchmark their own fleets, while creditors and equity investors can feed Drewry’s estimates directly into cash flow and valuation models.

Organisations purchasing theShip Operating Costs Annual Review and Forecast 2026/27receive 10 complimentary credits to OpCost IQ, Drewry’s digital vessel operating cost benchmarking tool, delivered via Drewry OnDemand. OpCost IQ benchmarks individual vessels or entire fleets against industry averages and uses AIS-enabled operational profiling to put cost performance in the context of each ship’s deployment and utilisation, helping users pinpoint cost irregularities and what drives them. One credit equals one vessel benchmark report.
