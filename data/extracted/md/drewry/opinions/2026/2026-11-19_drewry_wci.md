## Market Commentary & Analysis

The Drewry World Container Index (WCI), the benchmark widely referenced by procurement teams, decreased 3% to $4,176 per 40ft container this week due to declining rates on the Transpacific trade route. Despite the decline, the WCI remains 126% higher YoY.

On the Transpacific trade route, rates continued their downwards trend for the seventh consecutive week. Rates from Shanghai to New York decreased 9% to $8,194 per 40ft container, the lowest in 15 weeks, and those from Shanghai to Los Angeles fell 5% to $7,026 per 40ft container, the lowest in 12 weeks. In response to the weak demand, carriers are reducing capacity through blank sailings.

On the Asia-Europe/Med trade route, rates from Shanghai to Genoa increased 6% to $4,436 per 40ft container after remaining stable for three consecutive weeks, while Shanghai to Rotterdam rates remained unchanged at $3,496 per 40ft container.
