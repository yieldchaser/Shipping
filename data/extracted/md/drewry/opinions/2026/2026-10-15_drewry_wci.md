## Market Commentary & Analysis

The Drewry World Container Index (WCI), the benchmark widely referenced by procurement teams, rose 3% to $4,485 per 40ft container, mainly due to an increase in rates on the Asia-Europe trade route.

On the Transpacific trade route, rates from Shanghai to Los Angeles inched up 3% to $7,884 per 40ft container, while rates from Shanghai to New York fell 1% to $10,075 per 40ft container.

On the Asia–Europe trade route, rates from Shanghai to Rotterdam increased 6% to $3,522 per 40ft container, and those from Shanghai to Genoa grew 7% to $3,945 per 40ft container.
