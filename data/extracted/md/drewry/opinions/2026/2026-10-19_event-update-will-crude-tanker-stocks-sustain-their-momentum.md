# Event Update - Will Crude Tanker Stocks Sustain Their Momentum?

*Published on 19 October 2026*

Crude tanker stocks have emerged as the best-performing shipping segment in 2026, supported by geopolitical uncertainty, trade dislocations, and increased market inefficiencies and have considerably outperformed the S&P 500.

The Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index has surged 110.9% YTD (vs S&P 500 up 11.9%). In this thought piece, we explore the key drivers behind this strong rally and share our outlook for the sector.
