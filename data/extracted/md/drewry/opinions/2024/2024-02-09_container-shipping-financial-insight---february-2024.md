# Container Shipping Financial Insight - February 2024

*Published on 09 February 2024*

The disruption in the Red Sea boosted spot rates temporarily but this has failed to fortify investor sentiment regarding container shipping equities which continued slipping.

In YTD 2024 (ending 8 February), the Drewry Container Equity Index has fallen 5.1% despite WCI being up 127.9%. Read on to find key reasons driving this movement.
