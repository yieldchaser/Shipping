# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 17 September 2024

*Published on 17 September 2024*

For the week ending 13 September, majority of Drewry Equity Indices rose except LPG which declined 3.3% due to fall in the TCE rates.

Container Equity Index rose 2.6% due to major announcements made by carriers regarding changing alliance structures. Meanwhile, Crude and Product Tankers rose 2.1%, 1.6% respectively due to rise in the TCE rates.
