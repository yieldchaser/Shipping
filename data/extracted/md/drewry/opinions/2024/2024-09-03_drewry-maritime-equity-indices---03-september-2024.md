# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 03 September 2024

*Published on 03 September 2024*

For the week ending 30 August, Drewry Equity Index exhibited a mixed performance.

Crude and Product tanker rose 0.9% and 0.6% respectively on the news that OPEC+ production could rise in October. Dry Bulk and LNG rose 0.9% and 0.2% respectively. LPG Index plunged 3.0% due to a fall in the BW LPG. Meanwhile, the Container index declined 1.5% as spot rates continued their downtrend for the sixth consecutive week.
