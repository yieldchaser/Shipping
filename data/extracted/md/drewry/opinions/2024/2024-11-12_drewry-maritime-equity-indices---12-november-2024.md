# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 12 November 2024

*Published on 12 November 2024*

For the week ending 8 November, while major global equity indices surged (S&P 500 up 4.7% and Russell 2000 up 8.6%), Drewry Equity Indices exhibited a mixed performance. Global equity indices gained due to market expectation of the newly elected government being favourable to the business environment.

The Dry Bulk Index rose 2.7% due to an increase in TCE rates. Similarly, LNG Index increased 1.5% mainly driven by a 5.6% increase in Golar LNG stock price. Conversely, the container index declined by 1.8%, following investor expectations of the newly elected government’s increased tariffs on imports into the US (particularly from China). Meanwhile, the Crude and Product Tanker Index continued their downward trend, declining 2.9% and 6.8% respectively.
