# Tanker Shipping Financial Insight - April 2024

*Published on 26 April 2024*

Amid the ongoing Middle East conflicts and OPEC+ production cuts, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index rose 6.3% YTD (as of 24 April). Meanwhile, the Drewry Product Tanker Equity Index surged by 14.7% YTD, outpacing the S&P 500 which increased by 6.3%.

Read on for detailed commentary to know the factors driving the upswing.
