# Container Shipping Financial Insight - November 2023

*Published on 23 November 2023*

Driven by weak 3Q23 financial results, the Drewry Container Equity Index decreased 3.7% in the last one month (as of 22 Nov 2023). Additionally, asset prices continue to fall due to the supply-demand imbalance. Read on for detailed commentary on the aforementioned topics and factors driving them.
