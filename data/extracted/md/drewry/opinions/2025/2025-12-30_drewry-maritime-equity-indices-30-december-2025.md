# Drewry Maritime Equity Indices - 30 December 2025

*Published on 30 December 2025*

For the week ending 26 December 2025, Drewry equity indices largely posted gains (vs. the S&P 500 up 1.4% and the Russell 2000 up 0.2%). Infrastructure-related stocks gained, except for the logistics sector which declined, while bulk shipping stocks showed mixed performance.

The Drewry Port Equity Index led the gains, rising 2.3%, driven by broad-based strength across major port operators, followed by the Container Equity Index, which increased 1.7%. In contrast, the Drewry Logistics Equity Index declined 1.1%. Within bulk shipping, the Dry Bulk and Crude Tanker equity indices rose 2.9% and 0.6%, respectively, while the LNG Shipping and Product Tanker equity indices declined 0.7% and 0.2%, respectively.

On a YTD basis, infrastructure-focused indices have delivered positive returns (Logistics +10.6%, Ports +22.8%, Container +4.9%). Within bulk shipping, the Drewry Crude Tanker Equity Index remains the top performer, up 41.3%, outperforming other segments (Dry Bulk: +27.3%, LNG: −0.7%, LPG: +9.1%, Product: +4.9%).
