# Drewry Extends Global Coverage in Flagship Container Freight Rate Insight Service

*Published on 29 April 2025*

London, UK, 29 April 2025 – Drewry Supply Chain Advisors, the ocean freight cost benchmarking and procurement support division of Drewry Shipping Consultants, is pleased to announce a significant expansion to the lane (port pair) coverage within its flagship spot market ocean freight rate benchmarking service,Container Freight Rate Insight(CFRI), effective from 30 April.

The culmination of an 18-month data development and sourcing project has resulted in the addition of 5,500 new lanes to the service across Asia, Europe, the Americas and Africa, providing clients with even deeper, more granular insights into global container shipping markets.

“This major enhancement reflects Drewry’s ongoing commitment to supporting shippers, carriers and logistics providers with the most comprehensive, accurate and actionable freight rate intelligence available,”said Philip Damas, Drewry’s Managing Director and Head of Supply Chain Advisors.“Recent market volatility and heightened uncertainty has heaped pressure on ocean freight procurement and logistics teams, elevating the importance of timely and reliable benchmarking data,”continued Damas.“With this transformational development, we are pleased to be able to give our clients the expanded visibility they need to optimise and strengthen their negotiation position and manage costs at this challenging time.”
