### Dry S&P Activity Commentary

On the dry S&P activity, Ultramax and Supramax sectors were on the top of buying charts. Greek buyers acquired the “Great Venture” - 61K/2019 Dalian for USD 30.03 mills through auction. On the same sector, clients of Belships have entered into agreement to sell the “Belvedere” - 67K/2015 Mitsui for excess USD 27 mills basis delivery within Q3 2023. The Supramax “Nord Treasure” - 56K/2014 Mitsui was sold for excess USD 21 mills. Finally, the Handysize “Seastar Endurance” - 34K/2011 Zhejiang changed hands for USD 11.6 mills to European buyers.

The wet S&P activity was subdued with only a handful sales to report. Clients of DHT Holdings acquired the Scrubber fitted VLCC “Maria P. Lemos”- 319K/2018 HHI for USD 94.5 mills. The Suezmax “Front Njord”- 157K/2010 Jiangsu Rongsheng was sold for USD 44.5 mills. Finally, the MR2 “Super Ruby” - 50K/2006 Shina changed hands for USD 20.9 mills.
