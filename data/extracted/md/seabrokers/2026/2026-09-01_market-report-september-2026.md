# OSV Market Round-Up

## **NEW KID ON THE BLOCK: 2020 BULKERS ENTERS OFFSHORE MARKET WITH A SPLASH**

The offshore market has a new entrant, and that new player has joined the industry with a significant show of strength. 2020 Bulkers Ltd. has entered into a binding share purchase agreement with the shareholders of AHTS AS regarding the acquisition of all the outstanding shares in the company. Specifically, AHTS AS commenced operations in 2026 and, as of mid-September, owns seven large AHTS vessels with further agreements in place to purchase three additional large AHTS vessels. Furthermore, as part of the transaction, the key shareholders of several other companies, namely UMAS 42 AS, UMAS 49 AS, Anchor Launcher AS, Anchor Challenger AS and Anchor Australia AS, intend to sell their shares in each of those entities to AHTS AS. Each of those companies either owns a single AHTS or has a contract in place for the purchase of an AHTS vessel.

That takes the total number of vessels to be acquired by 2020 Bulkers to 15 large AHTS units. Those vessels are the Aurora Challenger, Emerald (ex Skandi Emerald), GH Challenger, GH Columbia, GH Pathfinder, Launcher (ex Navvik/Maersk Launcher), MH Atlantis, MH Discovery, MH Enterprise, MH Explorer, MH Freedom, MH Liberty, MH Navigator, MH Voyager and Skandi Peregrino.

Aurora Challenger (c/o G. Saunders) c/o G. Saunders

Those 15 vessels are plying their trade all around the globe, with three in the Caribbean, two in South America, two in Northwest Europe, two in West Africa, two in India, one in the Black Sea, one in the Middle East, one in Southeast Asia and one in Australia.

The aggregated purchase price for all of the shares in the companies to be acquired, assuming a 100% uptake, is approximately USD 406 million on a debt-free cash-free basis.

Having completed the full divestment of its six-vessel strong Newcastlemax dry bulk fleet in April 2026, 2020 Bulkers has since “explored various strategic opportunities” to create value for its shareholders. Describing the rationale for this specific venture, the company has identified that the market for large AHTS vessels is “attractive with an ageing fleet leaving supply structurally constrained” with just one vessel in this market segment actually delivered since 2018. With limited available vessel capacity, and an expectation for future demand to increase driven by a resumption of exploration drilling and the development of new basins, 2020 Bulkers is of the opinion that large AHTS asset values are lagging upward day rate momentum, and the segment “is priced at approximately 40% discount to estimated newbuild parity with no orderbook.”

2020 Bulkers will be backed with this investment by a sponsor group of key shareholders consisting of Uthalden AS, MH Capital AS and Songa Capital AS, experienced industrial investors with a strong track record of building and managing global shipping and offshore companies. Under the ownership of 2020 Bulkers, AHTS AS will “establish an organisation to operate and grow the business through further consolidation.” Tom Babinski has been appointed as the company CEO.

# OSV Market Round-Up

## NORTH SEA AHTS SUPPLY INCREASES BUT RATES FOR LARGER VESSELS REMAIN ELEVATED

There has been a noticeable increase to spot market supply within the North Sea AHTS sector following the recent arrival of multiple vessels from other regions, however fixture rates for the larger vessels in the market have remained elevated, much to the dismay of charterers.

The list of arrivals over the last three or four months includes the Aurora Challenger, Bear, Boka Fulmar, Fram Prince, Olympic Zeus, Sea Banckert, Sea Evertsen and Skandi Laser. While there have been some vessels migrating in the opposite direction, the net supply change has undoubtedly been in charterers' favour.

That has resulted in a scenario where several owners have failed to generate desirable levels of utilisation on the spot market because of the increased competition for work. The last couple of days of September saw as many as 15 vessels sitting prompt available for charter throughout the region.

Nevertheless, fixture rates for the higher specification vessels have been maintained at lofty levels. The average charter rate for a large vessel (>22,000 bhp) in September remained north of GBP 110,000 (USD 145,000). That represents the harsh nature of the regional AHTS market today; many owners are struggling from a utilisation perspective while charterers remain firmly exposed to highly elevated rates.

## MULTI-YEAR EQUINOR CONTRACT FOR TIDEWATER

**<u>MULTI-YEAR EQUINOR CONTRACT FOR TIDEWATER</u>**

Tidewater has secured a multi-year contract for one of its large PSVs in Norway. Equinor has awarded a five-year firm charter to the Sygna Tide with five further one-year options available. This contract is scheduled to commence circa late October. This is a positive development for Tidewater in relation to that specific vessel. The Sygna Tide had potentially been lined up earlier this year for a one-year firm charter with Adura (Equinor's UK joint venture with Shell) however that contract never came to fruition; in hindsight, the owner will be delighted to have finalised a five-year firm fixture with Equinor instead. This latest contract follows a similar five-year firm charter that Equinor had awarded to another Tidewater PSV, the North Barents, earlier this year. The Sygna Tide is a 12 year-old VARD 107 PSV while the North Barents is a nine year-old ST-216 Arctic PSV.

In relation to its incumbent fleet, Equinor has also recently extended its contract with Skansi Offshore PSV Sjøborg for an additional year. The 14 year-old vessel, built to the Havyard 833 design, is now firmly committed until October 2027. The Sjøborg has been working continuously for Equinor (and predecessor Statoil) dating back to the fourth quarter of 2017.

## HAVILA's HAVYARD PSVs IN DEMAND IN THE NORTH SEA

In addition to the contract awards and extensions detailed above, Equinor has also made the decision to retain the services of the Havila Clipper in Norway. The Havyard 832 CD PSV, built in 2011, is now firmly committed until January 2027. The Clipper has been almost continuously engaged on multiple Equinor contracts dating as far back as 2022.

## ISLAND CONTENDER JOINING YANTAI SUPPORT FLEET

A third PSV has been selected to provide additional support for DNO's drilling campaign with the Deepsea Yantai semisubmersible offshore Norway. At the start of 2026, Well Expertise (now Elemental Energies) awarded five-well firm contracts to Eidesvik and Østensjø Rederi for the Viking Princess and Edda Ferd; those contracts were awarded on behalf of DNO, Sval Energi (a subsidiary of DNO) and Wellesley Petroleum to support each of their drilling campaigns with the Deepsea Yantai. Now that the rig has progressed to operations with DNO, Island Offshore has secured a contract for the Island Contender PSV to join the support fleet; the Yantai is expected to continue on its current deployment with DNO until early 2027.

# REPSOL TAKES TIDEWATER DUO FOR DECOM SUPPORT

Pacific Discovery (c/o G. Saunders)

Repsol has awarded contracts to Tidewater to charter two of the owner's D-Class AHTS vessels to support its decommissioning campaign with the Borgland Dolphin semisubmersible offshore Spain. Tidewater has eight D-Class AHTS vessels in its fleet: the Pacific Duchess is currently in the Mediterranean and the Pacific Dispatch is in the North Sea, while four of the vessels are in West Africa (Pacific Defiance, Pacific Discovery, Pacific Dolphin and Pacific Dragon) and two are in Southeast Asia (Pacific Diligence and Pacific Dove). The first vessel selected for the campaign is the Pacific Discovery, which has joined the Borgland Dolphin in Las Palmas after mobilising south from the North Sea; the second support vessel is expected to be the Pacific Duchess. The vessels will be supporting the Borgland Dolphin for a seven-well firm plug & abandonment campaign with a minimum period of 220 days; the rig is scheduled to commence operations in November.

# TWO TERM ASIA-PACIFIC CHARTERS FOR SOLSTAD

Solstad Maritime has been awarded firm contracts for its AHTS duo Normand Saracen and Normand Scorpion with a combined duration of 475 days. The drilling support contracts, with an undisclosed charterer in the Asia-Pacific region, are expected to commence in the second or third quarter of 2027 and come with 400 days of options beyond the end of the firm periods. Both vessels are currently working for Woodside in Australia. For the new contracts, Solstad has described the combined monetary value at the upper end of its "Substantial" range, which is defined as contracts with a value between USD 10 and 30 million.

# MONSOON HEADING FOR SOUTH AMERICA

The vessel has been rechartered by Saipem with her next fixture to commence in direct continuation of this current contract, however the next deployment will be offshore Guyana. # BRAZIL OSV MARKET CONSOLIDATION CONFIRMED

The significant market consolidation that had been planned for the Brazilian OSV market has proceeded as planned following the finalisation of two merger/acquisition agreements.

Tidewater has confirmed the completion of its acquisition of Wilson Sons Ultratug Participações S.A. and its affiliate Atlantic Offshore Services S.A. (collectively WSUT). Via this transaction, Tidewater has added 22 WSUT PSVs to its fleet, strengthening the company's presence in the strategically important Brazilian market, and raising its Brazil exposure from six vessels to 28. Significantly, 19 of WSUT's PSVs were constructed in Brazil, establishing Tidewater as one of the main providers of Brazilian-built PSVs which receive priority to operate in the domestic market.

Meanwhile, OceanPact and Grupo CBO have completed their business combination following final clearance from CADE, Brazil's antitrust authority. As part of the integration, CBO has become part of OceanPact and the Navigation business unit has been renamed as OceanPact CBO. The combined company now operates a fleet of 70 vessels.

# Feature vessel

ACE Aristotelis (c/o Capital Offshore)

## ACE ARISTOTELIS

Capital Offshore has accepted delivery of its first newbuild PSV from the Fujian Mawei Shipyard in China. The ACE Aristotelis, built to the Breeze Z4423 design, is the first in a series of a dozen vessels that Fujian Mawei is building for Capital as the Greek owner rapidly expands its profile and market share within the offshore industry. The ACE Aristotelis, an 88m PSV with a deadweight of 4,700t and a deck area of 1,000m², is currently in the final stages of her maiden voyage from China to Singapore.

One more vessel (the ACE Agamemnon) is being built for Capital to the Breeze Z4423 design, while ten vessels are being built to the MMC 897 CD design. Of the ten MMC vessels, six are being built as PSVs (ACE Achilleas, ACE Aias, ACE Anikitos, ACE Atrotos, ACE Aktoras and ACE Alexandros) while four are being built as enhanced MPSVs with 100t subsea cranes and moonpools (ACE Alkiviadis, ACE Avax, ACE Apollonas and ACE Attalos).

Based on the current schedule, Capital Offshore is expected to take delivery of two more PSVs before the end of 2026 while there will be staggered deliveries of the remaining nine vessels over the course of 2027. All 12 vessels will operate under the management of UOS Capital GmbH, a 50-50 joint venture between Capital Offshore LLC and Hayfin Capital Management LLP.

## STARNAV ACCEPTS DELIVERY OF BRAZIL-BUILT PSV

Starnav Serviços Marítimos held a naming ceremony on September 17th for the Starnav Elektra, the first delivery in a newbuild cycle where several Brazilian owners will supply domestically-built vessels for long-term contracts with Petrobras.

The Starnav Elektra is the first in a series of six sister PSVs being built at the Detroit Brasil Shipyard in Itajaí. Starnav emerged victorious from a Petrobras tender in December 2024 where the company won the rights to construct six newbuild hybrid PSVs to fulfil 12-year contracts with Petrobras. Bram Offshore/Edison Chouest is also building six newbuild PSVs off the back of that same tender. The Starnav Elektra is a 92.1m vessel with a deadweight of 5,500t; the PSV sailed from Santa Catarina to Rio de Janeiro for her maiden voyage under the command of an all-female crew of 16.

## NEWBUILD UNICORE PSV EN ROUTE TO NIGERIA

The Jiangsu Zhenjiang Shipyard has described this particular delivery as the lead ship in a series of newbuild PSVs; a second PSV being built for Unicore was launched in late May.

## WINDWARD'S FINAL CSOV DELIVERED

Windward Hamburg

Windward Offshore has taken delivery of the **VARD 4 19**-designed Windward Hamburg, the fourth and final vessel in its commissioning service operation vessel (CSOV) newbuild series with VARD.

VARD's shipyard in Vietnam delivered the vessel in early September. Windward's fleet now includes the 2025-built Windward Athens, and the 2026-built CSOVs Windward Paris, Windward Munich and Windward Hamburg.

The vessels all have a length of 87.5m and beam of 19.5m, and they are equipped with a height-adjustable, motion-compensated gangway, an electric motion-compensated crane, a height-adjustable boat landing and battery-hybrid propulsion compatible with green methanol. The vessels have accommodation for up to 120 persons and helidecks for personnel transfer.

The Windward Athens, Windward Munich and Windward Paris are all operating in Northwest Europe in the offshore wind market.

# DAJIN HEAVY INDUSTRIES EXPANDS INTO INSTALLATION

Offshore wind foundation supplier Dajin Heavy Industry is moving into the installation market by selecting the Ulstein HX122 design for an offshore installation vessel.

The vessel will be equipped to install monopiles, jacket foundations and offshore substations to tackle the shortage of specialised vessels needed to install next-generation offshore wind foundations.

The newbuild will be capable of installing future 4XL monopiles and will feature a large working deck, advanced lifting equipment and dynamic positioning. The design can also be used for offshore oil & gas construction and decommissioning activities. The Ulstein U-STERN and X-BOW hull forms improve vessel motion and operability, which will allow the company to offer a more integrated service across the offshore wind foundation supply chain.

# SKYBORN REACHES FINANCIAL CLOSE ON GENNAKER

Skyborn Renewables has reached financial close for the 976.5 MW Gennaker offshore wind farm offshore Germany, with more than EUR 3 billion secured.

The project has now moved from development into construction as the largest offshore wind farm in the German Baltic Sea.

Gennaker will deliver 976.5 MW of capacity from 63 turbines, each with a maximum capacity of 15.5 MW. Construction is under way, with turbine installation scheduled for late summer 2027 and commercial operation targeted for 2028.

The developer has already selected Dajin Heavy Industry for the transition pieces, Seaway7 for the foundation installation, and a consortium of TKF and Boskalis for the supply and installation of the inter-array cables.

## TULLOW CHARTER FOR NOBLE DRILLSHIP

Noble Corporation has picked up a new contract for the Noble Faye Kozack drillship in West Africa.

The rig is currently working for Petrobras offshore Brazil, with that charter scheduled to come to a conclusion in January 2027. Following the completion of the Petrobras contract, the Noble Faye Kozack will be mobilised across the Atlantic Ocean although she may incur some idle time prior to commencing her new West Africa contract in mid-2027.

The drillship has been chartered by Tullow Oil to drill up to ten wells offshore Ghana. According to Westwood Global Energy, Tullow Oil had tendered to source a deepwater rig for a five-well firm campaign offshore Ghana, with an estimated duration of around 250 days, with five further optional wells available.

## WEST AFRICA CONTRACT FOR CONQUEROR

Transocean has secured a new contract for the Deepwater Conqueror that will see the ultra-deepwater drillship relocated from the US Gulf to West Africa next year.

The rig has been awarded a two-well contract offshore Equatorial Guinea with an undisclosed operator. With an estimated duration of 170 days, this new fixture has added approximately USD 80 million to Transocean's contract backlog, excluding additional services and compensation for mobilisation and demobilisation. That would equate to a day rate of circa USD 470,000.

While the charterer has not been named, it is expected to be Chevron. The Deepwater Conqueror is currently committed to Chevron in the US Gulf until January 2027, and is expected to continue with the same operator following her relocation to West Africa.

## MULTI-YEAR NORWAY FIXTURE FOR DEEPSEA BERGEN

Odfjell Drilling has received a Letter of Award from Vår Energi for a long-term charter with the Deepsea Bergen semisubmersible offshore Norway.

The proposed scope of work is for a three-year firm contract with scheduled commencement early in 2028 following the completion of the rig's current charter with Equinor. This will extend the rig's firm backlog until the first quarter of 2031.

Odfjell has indicated that the financial backlog for the rig's new contract will be circa USD 518 million, including mobilisation fees but excluding annual escalation, integrated services, performance bonuses and fuel incentives. That would equate to a day rate in excess of USD 470,000.

Odfjell acquired the Deepsea Bergen from Northern Ocean Ltd in December 2025 for a price of USD 480 million. The rig, which was known as the Deepsea Bollsta at the time, had already been trading under the management of Odfjell prior to that transaction.
