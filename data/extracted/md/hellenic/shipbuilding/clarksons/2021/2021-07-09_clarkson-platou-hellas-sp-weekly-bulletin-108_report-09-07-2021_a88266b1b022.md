# Clarksons Weekly Sale & Purchase Report - 2021-07-09

**Publisher**: Clarkson Hellas Ltd. | **Issue Date**: 2021-07-09 (Week 27)

## Desk Commentary

### Tankers
In dry bulk this week, Aston Enterprise are reported to have ordered two firm 82k dwt Kamsarmax at Chengxi Shipyard, with deliveries set for 4Q 2022 and 1Q 2023. Chengxi Shipyard confirmed taking order for a firm 82k dwt Kamsarmax from Chellaram Shipping, with delivery expected within 2023. In the gas carrier market, Samsung announced taking order for three firm 180k CBM LNG carriers from an unknown buyer, with the first vessel to be delivered in 4Q 2023 and the remaining two in 1Q 2024. Sovcomflot declared options to add two further vessels in their series of 174k CBM LNG carriers at Hyundai Samho. The vessels, which are set to be delivered in 4Q 2023 and 1Q 2024, bring the series to three overall. Hudong Zhonghua announced that CSSC Leasing declared an option in their series of 174k CBM LNG carriers, with the latest vessel set to be delivered in 2024 and brings the series to three.

### Demolition
Is it coming home? The feel-good factor has not just been felt here in England after the football teams heroics, but also across the strong buying appetite being felt across the sub-continental markets. Despite many predicting a fall, this is yet to materialise and with the lack of workable tonnage looking set to continue until at least the end of the year, demand should remain strong resulting in continued aggressive rates. Surely, it is only a matter of time for the much anticipated USD 600 per ldt level to be reached for a conventional ship which would be a remarkable recovery from this time last year where the market was in a depressed state due to the Covid restrictions in place. The Pakistani recyclers are making a sterling effort to compete with their counterparts in Bangladesh, however, are still a little short in their indications, whereas the Indian recyclers are still unable to mount any competitive levels.
