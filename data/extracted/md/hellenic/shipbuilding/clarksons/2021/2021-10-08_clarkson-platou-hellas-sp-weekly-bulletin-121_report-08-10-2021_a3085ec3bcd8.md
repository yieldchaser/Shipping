# Clarksons Weekly Sale & Purchase Report - 2021-10-08

**Publisher**: Clarkson Hellas Ltd. | **Issue Date**: 2021-10-08 (Week 40)

## Desk Commentary

### Newbuilding
In the gas carrier market, Qatar Petroleum announced orders for four firm 174k CBM LNG Carriers at Hudong Zhonghua, with delivery of the first two vessels expected in 2024 and the remaining two vessels in 2025. Meanwhile, in containers, Hanjin Heavy Industries announced taking order for four firm 5,700 TEU Containerships from an unknown European Owner, with deliveries expected throughout 2023, with the final vessel to be delivered by November 2023.

### Demolition
<u>**One Way?**</u> A quiet week has concluded with little activity being witnessed in the recycling sector. All reports from the analysts suggests that the dry and wet sectors in the shipping community look set to continue its current firm stance and therefore, the current lack of tonnage for recycling being experienced will continue towards the end of the year. With this in mind, and on the back of positive steel markets, the expectation is that price levels will move one way, ie northwards, given the diminishing stockpiles from the actual yards and few new candidates replacing them. In addition, demand to buy tonnage should remain in place as the infrastructure led post-Covid recovery in most countries will drive the requirement of construction material. This therefore should ensure that price levels remain positive for certainly the foreseeable future and into 2022.
