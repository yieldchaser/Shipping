# Clarksons Weekly Sale & Purchase Report - 2021-07-23

**Publisher**: Clarkson Hellas Ltd. | **Issue Date**: 2021-07-23 (Week 29)

## Desk Commentary

### Tankers
In the tanker market, Hyundai Mipo announced taking order for two firm 50k dwt MR’s from an unknown owner, with delivery expected in 1H 2023 and 2H 2023 respectively. Dalian Shipbuilding announced Shanghai North Sea Shipping declared an option for a further 155k dwt Shuttle, with delivery in 4Q 2023 bringing the series to two. In dry bulk, the major news this week came from Japan. It was announced that against long term employment for JFE steel, MOL, K-Line and NYK each ordered one LNG Dual-fuel 210k Newcastlemax at Nihon Shipyard (The JMU and Imabari JV), with the vessels set for delivery in 1Q 2024, 1H 2024 and 1Q 2025. In other news, clients of Safe Bulkers announced ordering three firm 82k dwt Kamsarmax at an as yet unknown Japanese yard, with delivery of the first vessel set for 4Q 2023 and the remaining two in 1Q 2024. ### Dry Bulk
In the gas carrier market, it was another busy week. Hyundai HI announced taking order for three firm 174k CBM LNG carriers from an unknown owner, with delivery of two vessels set for 2H 2024 and the final vessel with January 2025. Hyundai Samho announced contracting two firm 174k CBM LNG carriers, also from an unknown owner, with delivery of both vessels slated for 1Q 2024. The yard also announced taking orders for another two firm 174k CBM LNG carriers, again from an unknown owner, with delivery of the vessels expected in 1H 2024 and August 2024 respectively. In containers, Briese Schiffarts declared two options in their series of gearless 1,800 TEU boxships, bringing the series to six, with delivery of both vessels expected within 2023. Huangpu Wenchong announced contracting a firm gearless 1,900 TEU vessel with TS Lines, set to be delivered within In the ferry market, Stena Roro announced ordering two firm 1,400 passenger, 195m LOA Ropax ferries at CMJL Weihai. They are the 11th and 12th in the series of LNG dual fuel vessels and delivery will be in 2024 and 2025. Stena Roro also announced ordering an 1,100 passenger, 203m LOA ice classed Ropax, again ordered at CMJL Weihai with delivery expected within 2024. Rosmorport announced ordering two LNG dual fuel Icebreakers at Onego Shipyard, with delivery of both vessels expected with 2025.

### Demolition
With the religious holiday of Eid being celebrated this week, the market basically ground to a halt as end recyclers had little appetite and willingness to acquire new tonnage, leaving cash buyers and industry players unable to build up any momentum or traction on the little available tonnage that was proposed by some puzzled Owners. With this sparse activity, it is safe to say that prices held firm as there was no mitigating factor to cause disruption to the local market. However, with the upcoming summer holidays and travel disruption over the past year, many Owners will be taking a welcome break over the next few weeks which could see a drier summer period than normal and one that will leave cash buyers empty handed if they do not grab the slender units that come to the The nationwide lockdown restrictions in Bangladesh have been extended further until August 5th which may also weaken sentiment for the time being. We would like to wish those celebrating Eid, good health, happiness and success.
