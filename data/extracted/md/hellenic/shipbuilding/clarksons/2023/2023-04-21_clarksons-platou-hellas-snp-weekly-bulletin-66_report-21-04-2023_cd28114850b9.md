# Clarksons Weekly Sale & Purchase Report - 2023-04-21

**Publisher**: Clarkson Hellas Ltd. | **Issue Date**: 2023-04-21 (Week 16)

## Desk Commentary

### Newbuilding
In tankers this week, Clients of Metrostar are reported to have ordered two firm 75k dwt LR1's at Yangzijiang, with both vessels slated for delivery in 2H 2025. Hyundai Mipo announced contracts for two firm 50k dwt MR's with an unnamed owner, understood to be Simatech, with the vessels both set to deliver by July 2025. Marex Maritime announced ordering two Stainless Steel 12.5k dwt Chemical tankers at Kurinoura Dock, with the vessels both vessels expected to deliver within 2024. In dry bulk, Wisdom Marine announced orders for three firm 40k dwt Handysizes at Onomichi, with all three vessels expected to deliver in 2026.
