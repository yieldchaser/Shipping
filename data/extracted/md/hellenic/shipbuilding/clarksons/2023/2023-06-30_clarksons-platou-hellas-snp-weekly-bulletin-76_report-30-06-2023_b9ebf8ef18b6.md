# Clarksons Weekly Sale & Purchase Report - 2023-06-30

**Publisher**: Clarkson Hellas Ltd. | **Issue Date**: 2023-06-30 (Week 26)

## Desk Commentary

### Newbuilding
Klavness Comination Carriers announced ordering three firm 83k dwt CABU carriers at Yangzijiang, with the vessels set to deliver in 1Q, 2Q and 3Q 2026 respectively. In containers, it has been reported that CMA CGM ordered ten firm LNG dual fuel 24,000 TEU Containerships at Yangzijiang, with the vessels delivering throughout 2026 and 2027. Maersk announced orders for six firm 9,000 TEU containerships at Yangzijiang. The vessels will have Methanol Dual Fuel propulsion and will deliver throughout 2026 with the final vessel delivering in 2027. Finally, in Chemical tankers this week, Zhoushan Changhong announced contracting a firm 3.5k Stainless Steel Chemical Tanker for Ningbo Ganglonf Shipping, with the vessel expected to deliver in 2025.

### Demolition
There is no change to the rhythmic pattern experienced over many weeks from the regular reporting of slow activity, lack of tonnage, etc as the Eid Al-Adha festivities that are taking place this week have created even quieter market conditions. Despite the odd trickle of container units rippling into the market, which is evidenced by the sale reported below, this lack of available tonnage certainly looks set to continue for the next few months whilst Owners focus their attention on their summer holidays. One positive news generates around the entry into force of the Hong Kong International Convention for the Safe and Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships (HKC). With Bangladesh, and thereafter, Liberia, ratifying the Convention, we must now wait for 24 months under the IMO regulations (after the entry comes into force) that the HKC will become maritime law. A truly momentous occasion! Meantime, we wish all those currently celebrating this weeks festivities, a Happy Eid Al-Adha.
