# Clarksons Weekly Sale & Purchase Report - 2022-09-03

**Publisher**: Clarkson Hellas Ltd. | **Issue Date**: 2022-09-03 (Week 35)

## Desk Commentary

### Newbuilding
In Dry this week, Shanhaiguan have announced an order for 4 firm 37,000dwt lakes fitted handysize bulkers from existing client Polish Steamship. The vessels are expected to deliver in 2025. U-Ming meanwhile have announced an order for a pair of 64,000 dwt Ultramax bulkers at New Dayang Shipyard, these are their 3rd and 4th in the series and will deliver in Nov 2024 and April 2025 respectively. Finally, CITIC leasing are reported to have returned to Dalian for a further 5 x 65,000dwt bulkers all for delivery in 2025, bringing their total number of vessels on order at the yard up to 15 vessels now. In Gas, HMD have announced an order from an un-named European owner for a pair of LPG carriers. The vessels are understood to be 45,000 cbm in size and the first of these is expected to deliver in 4Q 2024, with the second in 1Q 2025. In Containers, Wuchang have announced an order with clients of Cosmoship for 2+2 x 1,180 TEU Container carriers. Both vessels are expected to deliver in 2024. Lastly, in the passenger carrier market, CMJL (Weihai) have announced an order for one 194 passenger RoPax placed by China-Japan International which again is expected to deliver in 2024.

### Demolition
**Speculative Thrill!** With China implementing new lockdown regulations, the dry freight market was sent into further negative spins and therefore, we may now see more vintage dry units circulated into the market. A capesize bulker achieved a very impressive number this week, as per the sale listed below, with a good quantity of bunkers RoB. However, there are rumours the buyer may attempt to fix on a short trade, despite the poor sentiment in the dry sector, which could then justify the premium paid. If the sale is aimed towards a resale into Bangladesh and the hope of the Government once again allowing larger L/C's to be opened (from the current limit of USD 3.0 mill), then unfortunately information obtained from Bangladesh towards the latter part of the week will not aide the Buyers resale structure. Information became apparent that new taxations may come be implemented as the Government aim to set reasonable prices of nine products, including flour, edible oil, lentils, sugar, rice, flour, coarse flour, cement and rods, within the next 15 days in an effort to bring stability to the country's economy. There are even discussions of lawsuits being filed against anyone disregarding the designated prices. Pakistan is currently undergoing horrific flooding from the monsoon rains and the industry is really at a standstill for the time being. Our thoughts and prayers go to those currently suffering from these tragic floods. With the country still suffering from their currency woes, and the current flooding, little activity is expected for the foreseeable future..
