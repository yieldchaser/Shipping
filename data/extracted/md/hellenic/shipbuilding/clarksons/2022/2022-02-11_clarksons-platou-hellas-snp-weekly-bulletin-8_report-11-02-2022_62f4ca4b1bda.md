# Clarksons Weekly Sale & Purchase Report - 2022-02-11

**Publisher**: Clarkson Hellas Ltd. | **Issue Date**: 2022-02-11 (Week 6)

## Desk Commentary

### Newbuilding
In tankers this week, Mitsui OSK Lines announced ordering one firm 50,000 dwt Ice-breaking MR at GSI Nansha. The vessel is Ice Class Arc7 and will be delivered in 2024. In the gas carrier market, Hyundai Samho announced contracting two firm 174k CBM LNG Carriers from an unknown owner, with delivery of both vessels expected in 1Q 2025. Meanwhile, it was reported that Wanhua Chemical Group ordered two firm plus two optional 99k CBM VLEC's at Jiangnan, with the firm vessels set to be delivered in 2025. In the Car Carrier market, Eastern Pacific Shipping ordered two firm LNG dual-fuel 7,000 TEU PCTC's at CMJL Weihai, with delivery of both vessels expected in 2025.

### Demolition
**Attracting Interest!** The firming rates being witnessed in the recycling market is starting to gain attraction for some ship owners as we are now hearing of several more sales being concluded, as evidenced in the sales collated below, with more units being circulated into the marketplace. There has certainly been more activity this week than previous weeks and maybe Owners are now biting the bullet and accepting that these high levels on offer are worth considering as the scrap value of their vessels rises. On reflection, some are questioning the current price levels on offer as they reportedly are not being supported by the local recyclers and that some cash buyers are trying their luck on the back of the recent lack of tonnage availability. There has been a Capesize bulk carrier concluded for recycling this week, which is possibly a 'one off' as the dry freight market is showing signs it has bounced back from its recent correction. Therefore, the majority of new tonnage can only be expected to come from the Tanker and Offshore sectors.
