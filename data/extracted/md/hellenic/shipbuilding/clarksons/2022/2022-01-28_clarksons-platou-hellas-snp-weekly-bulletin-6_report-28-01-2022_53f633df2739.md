# Clarksons Weekly Sale & Purchase Report - 2022-01-28

**Publisher**: Clarkson Hellas Ltd. | **Issue Date**: 2022-01-28 (Week 4)

## Desk Commentary

### Newbuilding
In dry bulk this week, COSCO Zhoushan announced taking orders for two firm 63.6k dwt Ultramax from KC Maritime, set to be delivered in 2024. CSSC Dalian Shanhaiguan announced taking orders for four firm 37k dwt Handysize bulkers from Polish Steamship, with deliveries expected in 2024. In the gas carrier market, Hyundai Samho announced taking order for a firm 174k CBM LNG Carrier from an unknown owner, with delivery expected in 2Q 2025. In containers, Dalian Shipbuilding announced taking orders for six firm LNG Dual-Fuel 16,000 TEU Containerships from MSC. The vessels will have Ammonia Ready class notations and will be delivered throughout 2024 and 2025. Loitek Ergonomic Technology ordered a firm 1,800 TEU Containership at Huanghai Shipbuilding, set to be delivered in 1Q 2023. Schoeller Holdings declared two optional 2,000 TEU MPP's at Huangpu Wenchong, bringing their series to six at the yard, with the latest two vessels to be delivered in 1H 2025.

### Demolition
**Upwards Momentum!** Market levels continue to firm without any significant sales to report in lieu of the oncoming Lunar holidays in the Far East. The lack of tonnage availability is the perfect storm to create aggressive buying prowess and current price indications seemed to have gained a USD 20-30 per Idt profit margin from last week. There are certainly not too many expectations of improved activity in the next couple of weeks as the holidays in the Far East take effect. Several reports during the week also suggest China wishes to lower its steel production/exports during this year, although many predict this could prove difficult. In the first half of 2021, Chinese steel mills have churned out nearly 12% more crude steel compared to the same period in 2020 however, should this slowdown materialise, we could expect continued firm price levels for ship recycling in the Indian sub-Continent.
