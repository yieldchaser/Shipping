# Clarksons Weekly Sale & Purchase Report - 2026-03-21

**Publisher**: Clarkson Hellas Ltd. | **Issue Date**: 2026-03-21 (Week 12)

## Desk Commentary

### General
The S&P market remains active, with asset values in both the tanker and dry bulk sectors remaining firm. Modern eco-design vessels are drawing particularly strong interest and achieving premium levels, while older, non-eco tonnage is also maintaining liquidity, successfully attracting buyers and securing solid prices.

### Dry Bulk
In the Kamsarmax segment, after inviting offers on Monday, the **MG MERCURY** (ABT 85K DWT, 2016, IMABARI, BEAM 35 M) was acquired by European interests at levels in the low USD 28's m. On the Panamax front, the **BARWON** (ABT 78K DWT, 2015, SASEBO, ECO ME) invited offers earlier this week, attracting interest from up to 5 buyers, and has now been reported sold for xs USD 26 m. On the geared segment, we understand that a 2021 Japanese Ultramax was sold for region USD 37 m, whilst the Supramax **FIGEAC** (ABT 53K DWT, 2011, CHENGXI) was sold for a price close to USD 12 m, basis surveys promptly due. Meanwhile, the older **SOMNATH** (ABT 55K DWT, 2005, OSHIMA) was sold at region USD 9 m.

### Tankers
The Handy MR **CHRYSOPIGI** (ABT 38K DWT, 2006, GUANGZHOU) was sold at USD 9 m, basis surveys promptly due.
