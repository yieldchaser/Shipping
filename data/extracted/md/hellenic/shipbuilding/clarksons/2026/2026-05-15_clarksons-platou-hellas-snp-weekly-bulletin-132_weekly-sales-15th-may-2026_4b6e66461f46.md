# Clarksons Weekly Sale & Purchase Report - 2026-05-15

**Publisher**: Clarkson Hellas Ltd. | **Issue Date**: 2026-05-15 (Week 20)

## Desk Commentary

### General
This week's SnP activity has primarily centered around large tankers and mid-sized bulk carriers, with various negotiations progressing across sectors and several transactions materializing. Dry Cargo vessels have continued to attract strong interest, as seen over recent weeks, supported by firm prices and limited availability.

### Dry Bulk
In the Ultramax segment, **DOMINATOR** (ABT 64K DWT, 2021, SHIN KASADO) is committed at USD 38m basis surveys promptly due in July 2026. Further activity was recorded on the Supramax side, with Greek buyers emerging behind the eco-Supramax **SUMAQ QUEEN** (ABT 51K DWT, 2017, IMABARI, OHBS) sale, reported committed at levels of excess USD 25m. Furthermore, the Supramax **SETY** (ABT 55K DWT, 2010, I.H.I, WARTSILA) was sold for USD 16.5m. The **PLANET TEAM** (ABT 53K DWT, 2009, SINOPACIFIC ZHEJIANG) was sold to undisclosed buyers at mid-USD 10m, whilst the older **JALMA TOPIC** (ABT 52K DWT, 2006, TSUNEISHI CEBU) achieved a price in excess USD 12m.

### Tankers
In the VLCC space, clients of Trafigura are reported to have acquired the scrubber-fitted resale VLCC **LAS PALMAS** (ABT 306,000 DWT, BLT 2026, HENGLI SB DALIAN) at levels in excess USD 160m basis September 2026 delivery, highlighting the appetite for modern eco tonnage with prompt delivery. Furthermore, Greek interests are understood to have committed the resale Suezmax-pair **OLYMPIC STAR** and **N/B DH SHIPBUILDING 5111** (ABT 157,000 DWT, BLT 2027, DH SHIPBUILDING) at an en-bloc price of USD 190m, with both vessels scheduled for delivery within 2027.
