# Clarksons Weekly Sale & Purchase Report - 2026-07-31

**Publisher**: Clarkson Hellas Ltd. | **Issue Date**: 2026-07-31 (Week 31)

## Desk Commentary

### General
Events in the Middle East are continuing to shape shipping dynamics. With disruptions now affecting Saudi Arabia's two key export routes—the Strait of Hormuz and the Bab el-Mandeb—the effective voyage duration for Middle Eastern crude destined for Asia has increased by approximately one month and tanker earnings have now climbed at a weighted average of USD 75,000/ day, highest number since early May. Market outlook appears to be overall positive, supported by fundamentals such as the continued growth in long-haul exports, increasing Venezuelan exports now carried on “mainstream” tankers, and oil restocking margins. S&P tanker activity was further bolstered this week, with several fairly modern VLCCs understood to have been transacted to Middle Eastern interests at firm price levels, supporting sentiment across the rest of the tanker market. We also understand that several eco MR2s ranging from circa 10-year-old units up to resales are currently under firm negotiations. Furthermore, buying appetite continues to extend also on the older units, within the 15–20-year-old range – in which we still monitor a number of buyers, mostly from the Middle East and Far East markets, being on the look-out for MRs & LR1s as well as crude takers, more specifically on Aframaxes & Suezmaxes. Dry market has held to a robust first half of the year, underpinned by strong iron ore exports from Australia and Brazil, alongside resilient Chinese import demand despite elevated inventories and softer domestic steel production. Additional support has come from steady minor bulk trade and healthy Atlantic grain export volumes. Looking ahead, the Capesize segment is expected to continue outperforming the smaller size classes, driven by sustained growth in long-haul Atlantic iron ore exports and favorable ton-mile demand. In terms of activity, market sources indicated a number of approximately 15-year-old Korean- and Japanese-built Kamsarmax units as being currently in advanced negotiations, while the focus of Dry Tonnage buyers seems to be in the 15-20-years-old Supramax-Ultramax segment, driven by Far Eastern market players

### Dry Bulk
After inviting offers last week the Post Panamax **PONT ROUGE** (ABT 99K DWT, 2021 TSUNEISHI ZHOUSAN) is now sold at mid-upper USD 30m's. Moreover, the Panamax **IVESTOS 8** (ABT 75K DWT, 2008, HUDONG ZHONGHUA) was sold for USD 11.7m with surveys due to undisclosed buyers. In the Supramax segment c/o Unity Maritime has now sold the **VIVA ECLIPSE** (ABT 54K DWT, 2009, JIANGSU EASTERN, CR: 4X36T) for USD 11.75 m.

### Tankers
Although we understand several negotiations to be ongoing, there are no new tanker sales to report this week.
