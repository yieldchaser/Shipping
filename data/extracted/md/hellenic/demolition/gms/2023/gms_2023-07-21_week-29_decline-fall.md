# Market Commentary

## DECLINE & FALL!

Prices continue to struggle across all of the major ship recycling markets, as Bangladesh further declined off of the back of depreciating currencies and sustained L/C struggles, while Turkey suffered similarly with declining steel plate prices, the Lira, and finally, its vessel prices.

The supply of tonnage seems to have increased just slightly over the last couple of weeks (particularly from the Chinese market) and as such, many Owners and Cash Buyers have been left chasing down various recycling destinations amidst weakening prices.

As forecasted a few weeks ago, Pakistan finally seems ready to return into the game and has even overtaken an extremely lackluster Indian market wherefrom, lowball offers of below USD 500/LDT seem to be coming regularly in, with little success for all parties involved. As such, it may take some time to work through the L/C issues in Pakistan, but it is certainly encouraging to see various offers come back close to currently competitive levels.

On the supply side, it has mostly been vessels from the Dry Bulk sector that continue to be introduced to the recycling markets, especially older Panamax and Handy units built in the 90s that are perhaps overdue for retirement from their respective fleets.

Post budget in Bangladesh has certainly been more difficult to get Central Bank approval on fresh L/Cs, but things seem to have eased up a touch this week as local Buyers are emerging once again, just as the tonnage flow is also increasing.

Finally, the Turkish market seems to have taken a turn for the worst, as import and local steel prices, the Currency, and even vessel prices have all taken a tumble during this week, with some vessel indications reportedly coming in even below USD 300/MT.

Overall, it will certainly take some time to absorb many of the unsold vessels not only being freshly proposed into the markets, but also those units in Cash Buyer hands and as such, sentiments and demand may remain somewhat muted for some time – at least until the monsoons start to subside.

# Bangladesh

## EASING!

*Margin notes: Buyers emerge. | Bulkers sold.*

A lot of the L/C difficulties post-Eid seem to finally be easing this week as an increasing number of Buyers emerged at the tables to offer on and acquire new units, be it at these lower overall levels of today.

Vessels continue to be proposed from the Chinese market (in particular) and there are several Dry Bulk vessels (Handy and Panamax sizes in particular) backed up and waiting for L/Cs to open so that vessels can arrive locally and eventually beach.

Fresh sales this week saw the Chinese controlled ZHENG HAO and ZHENG JIE (10,613 LDT) committed en bloc, for a decent USD 495/LT LDT basis an ‘as is’ China delivery, in addition to another Bulk Carrier YANG FAN (10,934 LDT) that was committed with various removals, a conversion, and installed cranes, for a decent USD 540/LT LDT basis an ‘as is’ Batam delivery and with sufficient bunkers included on board for the voyage to the sub-continent.

Meanwhile, fundamentals continue to face their share of troubles as the Bangladeshi Taka continues to trade at a record-breaking BDT 108.50 levels against the U.S. Dollar, all while local steel plate prices continue to flatline.

# India

## ON EDGE!

*Margin notes: Low levels. | Below 500.*

Indian prices have sunk to their lowest levels for quite some time now and there are even opportunistic offers forthcoming from Alang Buyers that are below USD 500/LDT.

These lowball offers are not resulting in much success for Indian Recyclers - even for HKC green tonnage - and with Pakistan coming back into the picture recently, firmer prices will be needed in the near future, in order for domestic Recyclers to secure their share of tonnage.

Domestic fundamentals continue their dance as the Indian Rupee continues trading into the high Rs. 81s against the U.S. Dollar, all while local steel plate prices extended their volatility once again, ending the week only USD 1/Ton above those from last week.

**NO MARKET SALES REPORTED**

# Pakistan

## LEAPFROG!

*Margin notes: Enquiries emerge. | Above India.*

News of the USD 3 billion injection of IMF funding is set to ease the financial crisis that Pakistan has crushingly endured for nearly all of this year, and this has seemed to finally bear some fruit this week as several offers and End Buyers were seen emerging from the abyss and enquiring to buy tonnage once again.

Having been out of the picture for the better part of a year, it will take some working through the ongoing L/C issues with domestic banks, in order to see some form of business normality resume moving forward.

For now, fundamentals continue to deteriorate as the Pakistani Rupee weakened drastically from PKR 277 to nearly PKR 286 this week, all while local steel plate prices remain horizontal for months on end.

Notwithstanding, despite the weakening fundamentals, Pakistani Buyers have jumped above India in the price rankings and are encouragingly within striking distance of competing with Bangladesh once again.

As such, we can only hope a quick small LDT deal or two should help set the local financial gears cranking again, making it smoother for further deals moving forward.

**NO MARKET SALES REPORTED**

# Turkey

## DECLINE CONFIRMED!

*Margin notes: Fundamentals drop. | Finally accepted.*

Last week, imminent fears of an upcoming correction seem to have played out this week as declines in key fundaments (i.e., plate prices and the Turkish Lira) resulted in vessel prices declining by about USD 20/Ton this week.

Not only did import and local steel plate prices decline by about USD 15/Ton and USD 20/Ton respectively, but the Lira also declined further as it approached TRY 27 against the U.S. Dollar – ending the week at TRY 26.95.

Sentiments have gotten worse and on the few units being offered, all levels in the low USD 300/MT (some even reportedly below) came forth this week from nervous Aliaga Buyers.

As such, even though supply may seem better than before, these new lower levels might put off fresh Sellers and Cash Buyers, at least until these prices are finally accepted.

**NO MARKET SALES REPORTED**
