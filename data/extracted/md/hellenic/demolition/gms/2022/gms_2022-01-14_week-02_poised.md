# Market Commentary

## POISED?

Recycling markets (especially in the sub-continent) seem poised for some positive movements this week, as both Cash Buyers and End Buyers seem increasingly willing to compete on any available tonnage.

There have been several specialist units, Stainless Steel Tankers & Fish Factories - being the flavor of the recent supply into India of late - as both Pakistan and Bangladesh watch stranded on the sidelines, due to the ongoing paucity of vessels that has been suffocating the markets of late.

Even the Turkish market endured a period of resilience this week, as all of the essential fundamentals remained in sync with last week’s levels, giving Aliaga Buyers the continued sense of stability that this market has endured of late.

There are very few large LDT vessels in the market to discuss at present, especially as talks of a recovery in Tanker rates at some point this year continue to make the rounds and as Dry Bulk and Container rates continue to soar. Indeed, those vessels that may have been scrap candidates not too long ago, are now seeking to pass surveys and continue trading for better returns - such is the state of the trading markets at present.

There also seems to be a degree of optimism in the markets moving forward (both in terms of steel and currencies), hence the upward scramble in prices that is being witnessed of late,, especially when a vessel is introduced for a recycling sale.

The overall preference does seem to be for smaller LDT vessels since the markets are indeed at historically highs and levels can fluctuate wildly from week-to-week. As such, we have not seen such positivity-&-demand for some months now as we are currently witnessing in the market.

As the Chinese / Lunar new year holidays approach in the Far East, it may be a slower period in terms of supply for sub-continent markets, and this in turn may further lead to a firming of demand / prices.

# Bangladesh

## STRANDED!

*Margin notes: Frustrating. | L/C issues.*

It has been a frustrating period in Bangladesh, as local Recyclers have witnessed vessels being sold into competing markets – even on favoured large LDT tankers – at levels Chattogram Buyers could have easily competed at.

India and Pakistan have both been securing their share of the tonnage of late, as the Bangladeshi market has largely remained stranded, waiting to make some serious moves, especially as Q1 2022 is upon us.

Demand remains good, yet there has been concerns of late on L/Cs, with banks not meeting required limits on vessels to open and release in a timely manner – especially prior to the vessel’s arrival at the Bangladeshi waterfront. As usual, this leaves the Cash Buyer potentially exposed on incoming deliveries, especially if the markets take a dip.

# India

## ALIVE!

*Margin notes: Roaring back. | Fish factory sales.*

The Indian market has come roaring back into the picture - especially since the turn of the year - with some extraordinarily bullish numbers on those vessels that are currently available for sale.

Local steel plate prices firming by USD 15/Ton this week certainly added to the aggression currently on show from Alang Recyclers. Even the Rupee has been safely bouying around the low Rs. 74s against the U.S. Dollar this week.

As such, two Stainless Steel Tankers have been sold from Glory of Singapore, as the PROSPERITY (5,314 LDT) fetched a handsome USD 830/LT LDT - given the 580 Tons of solid SUS 304 on board) - and the EXPRESS (2,998 LDT) received an even firmer USD 930/LT LDT - with 282 Tons of solid SUS 316 on board.

Russian fish factories appear in vogue at the moment and the USSR built 1982 HELSINGFORS (3,639 LDT) received an extraordinary USD 725/LT LDT in the latest sale from this sector this week.

# Pakistan

## CREEPING UP!

*Margin notes: Tanker sales. | Looking up?*

The Pakistani market has been creeping up since the start of this year and they have certainly secured their share of (particularly geographically positioned) tonnage that would have been destined for Bangladesh at certain points of last year.

This includes a slew of MR and Aframax Tankers that were taken away from the hungry clutches of Chattogram Recyclers.

With the currency stabilising of late and steel plate prices showing a firming trajectory, the future is indeed looking bright for Gadani Buyers, should they choose to step up to the plate.

**NO MARKET SALES REPORTED**

# Turkey

## STEADY AS SHE SHAKES!

*Margin notes: Fundamentals even. | Shaky December.*

After vigorously shaking local sentiments, Turkish Fundamentals spent a relatively quiet week, with the Turkish Lira buoying around the TRY 13.5X mark – similar to last week. Even import and local steel prices remained relatively steady this week, with little change being reported.

Amidst the ongoing scarcity of tonnage, no new vessels have reportedly arrived Aliaga’s waterfront this week as Aliaga Buyers remain eager to procure vessels slated for a Spring delivery.

Overall, as levels remain firm, Turkey does appear to be stabilizing despite traversing a shaky December.

**NO MARKET SALES REPORTED**
