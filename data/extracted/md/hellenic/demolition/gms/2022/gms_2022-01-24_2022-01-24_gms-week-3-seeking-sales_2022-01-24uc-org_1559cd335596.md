It has been an increasingly static week in the Indian sub-continent ship recycling markets,
especially after some early 2022 optimism displayed by a resurgent India in particular. 
Sales on various specialist units soared this past week in Alang – with several stainless-steel
chemical tankers, Russian fish factories, and a barge being committed at some extraordinary
levels. 
Competing markets in both Pakistan and Bangladesh could only sit by and watch, with very 
few favored large LDT units to work on, and as the holiday season approaches in China and 
much of the Far East. 
Even in Turkey, it remained another relatively stable week, with no material change reported
in the severely-depreciated-but-now-stable Lira or even in import / local steel prices. 
Overall, demand is certainly ripe across sub-continent locations, but there are still a few vessels
needed in the market to satisfy the recent onset of demand, and sub-continent Recyclers usually
find themselves competing at above market prices on any of the select units that do become 
available.  
Even steel prices and currencies remain stable-to-positively poised as well, certainly indicating
a bullish first quarter of the year (as has historically been the case). Tanker charter rates are still
in the doldrums, and it remains to be seen how much longer the ongoing Dry Bulk rally can
last.  
As such, it is rather peculiar that there is not a greater volume of ongoing negotiations on
recycling candidates at the moment. It appears as though most owners will have to wait-and-
watch until after the lunar New Year holidays before making any moves and it could well be a
slower start to 2022 – especially in terms of sales leading up to mid-February.

MARKET COMMENTARY

BANGLADESH 
FEW AND FAR BETWEEN! 
Bangladeshi Buyers have had very few vessels to work on this week, especially as Alang 
shoots ahead and Pakistan likewise comes back into the picture. Yet, Chattogram Buyers
have managed to take deliveries of the most number of units and LDT in the market this 
week.  
Prices have been somewhat softer in Chattogram for the last month or so, and despite 
steel prices previously firming, they have flatlined of late and it may take a little more
time before prices catch up on the ground. 
As such, it has been a somewhat frustrating week for local buyers, who have the capacity 
and desire to acquire units, but are still to see some viable candidates to really sink their 
teeth into yet.

INDIA 
Further sales. 
 
RUN CONTINUES! 
After the sale of two stainless steel tankers and a fish factory last week, this week has been 
another fantastic one of surging levels on specialist vessels, with two more deals being 
confirmed to Alang Buyers. 
A favoured USSR built fish factory was concluded to local Buyers’ as the MOSKOVSKAYA 
OLIMPIADA (3,916 LDT) managed to fetch an extraordinary USD 755/LT LDT, whilst the 
Canadian built Barge AHTS KIGORIAK (4,855 LDT) fetched an equally firm USD 760/LT 
LDT due to the significant amount of valuable and saleable machinery on board, including 
four generators and three thrusters. 
The Indian Rupee has been trading in the mid Rs. 74 against the U.S. Dollar of late, whilst 
local steel plate prices have also inched up by about USD 6/LDT over the course of the 
week. 
However, it is not possible to compare the sale of specialist units to more standard Tankers 
/ Bulkers and the saleable machinery, non-ferrous (being USSR built) and favourable size 
have all contributed to current levels by at least USD 150/LDT above what a standard 
Bulker or Tanker would be in India today.

GMS Weekly 
UNDER RADAR! 
Pakistan has been creeping up of late and has also managed to secure a couple of vessels 
(largely) under the radar and out of the spotlight of its competitors, whilst India has been 
capturing select and specialist units at some serious show stopping numbers. 
Pakistani Buyers remain keen to buy but are still a fraction off of what Bangladeshi Buyers 
are currently paying. 
However, if the lack of supply continues, it may only be a matter of time before local Buyers 
firm up their offerings, in order to ensure they are securing their share of the tonnage.

ANOTHER STEADY ROUND! 
After watching the Lira / U.S. Dollar nosedive off the precipice, the recent Turkish market 
steady dance seems cautiously welcome as despite astronomical vessel prices, the Turkish 
market seems jittery whilst Aliaga Buyers remain cautious. 
The Lira continues to bounce around the TRY 13.5X mark against the U.S. Dollar as the 
Central Bank has decided to maintain the interest rate at current levels for the time-being. 
Even import and local steel plate prices were relatively unchanged during the course of 
the week. 
Overall, it certainly remains quieter in Turkey (from a market tonnage perspective) despite 
Turkish yards being relatively full and gearing up for later Q1 deliveries.
