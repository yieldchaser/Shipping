## 1. Market Overview

### Highlights
- Big numbers.
- Tanker flavor.
- Steel firm.
- Charter hopes?
- China back.

### Editorial: Market Commentary

We have seen several Capes sold for HKC recycling in the mid USD 600s/LDT in recent weeks, especially as chartering rates in this sector have taken a bit of a dip. Tankers keep being introduced into the recycling markets and several deals have reportedly been concluded at levels in excess of USD 650/LT LDT in Bangladesh, as the Chattogram market heats up to some increasingly unprecedented levels.

Steel prices remain firm across all sub-continent locations and even the Indian market has started to come back into the picture, such is the demand emanating from all markets at present.

Finally, the Turkish market remains impeccably poised to place some record levels of their own, if only the dearth of tonnage wouldn’t have kept the market so exquisitely stranded.

Overall, given some of the numbers on show, it is indeed surprising that not more candidates are being introduced for a recycling sale. Yet, there are likely enough inklings in the wet sector to suggest a turn in sentiments, which may lead even more Owners to hold on to their tonnage for now.

With Chinese production set to ramp up again now that Chinese New Year holidays and the winter Olympics are over, perhaps there is scope for further improvements on price as well.

## 3. Country Market Intelligence

### Bangladesh

- **Headline**: NO CEILING!
- **Key Notes**: *Leading way.* | *How high can go?*

Bangladesh leads the way once again, with some of the best numbers in the sub-continent on any tonnage on offer or those units already in Cash Buyer hands.

With one HKC cape being sold to Bangladesh last week, India secured another HKC only Cape this week at even higher levels (albeit with over 1,000 Tons of bunkers on board) – to show that all markets are viable and are willing to strongly compete on available tonnage, at firm levels once again.

With several vessels being concluded well over USD 650/LT LDT, there is now the (worrying) speculation as to just how high this market can go. Notwithstanding, as long as steel plate prices (that are up another USD 12/Ton this week), the Bangladeshi Taka remains firm, and supply painfully sparse, there (hopefully) seems little to be worried about thus far!

### India

- **Headline**: REBOUND!

India has rebounded recently after a few nervy weeks following steel reversals and have even got back into the action, with Alang Recycling reportedly securing a couple of units.

Of course, there seems to be no competing with the rampant Pakistan and Bangladeshi markets at present, so firmly placed to secure tonnage are Buyers at those locations.

As such, rather than competing on standard Tankers and Bulkers, Indian Buyers are having to make do on a diet of specialist units such as fish factories, stainless steel tankers and HKC green vessels only.

On the local fundamentals front, local steel plate prices jumped a massive and nearly USD 20/Ton this week, boosting local sentiments. Even the Indian Rupee has firmed against the U.S. Dollar, to Rs. 74.3X during the course of the week, keeping Alang Buyers eager for tonnage.

*Market Sales Reported*: **NO MARKET SALES REPORTED**

---

### Pakistan

- **Headline**: COMPETING!
- **Key Notes**: *Rivalling Bangladesh.* | *Delivery concerns.*

Pakistan remains closely competitive with the resurgent Bangladeshi market, particularly for those geographically positioned vessels that may be operating or being delivered to Cash Buyers on ‘as is’ basis in the UAE / PG region.

Steel plate prices remain firm and demand is certainly ripe in Gadani, as local Recyclers look to fill plots that have reportedly been vacant for some time.

News about some rough deliveries at the start of the year is creating some disturbance amongst the recycling community and it may be that Sellers / Cash Buyers are looking to deliver their vessels elsewhere for now, rather than risk facing headaches at the final stages of delivery.

*Market Sales Reported*: **NO MARKET SALES REPORTED**

---

### Turkey

- **Headline**: FIRM…YET SLOW!

The Turkish market traveled through yet another slow week, despite local steel plate prices reportedly registering a minor improvement. Even the Lira, while egregiously weaker, remains stable at its worst.

No reports of fresh arrivals or news of market units working, emanated from Aliaga this week as this has overall been a silent week.

Yet, demand remains firm, prices remain at record highs, and Turkey remains the only viable destination for units opening up in EU waters.

*Market Sales Reported*: **NO MARKET SALES REPORTED**
