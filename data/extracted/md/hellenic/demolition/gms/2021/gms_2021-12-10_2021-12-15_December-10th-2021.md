## 1. Market Overview

### Highlights
- Reversals
- India down.
- Limited supply.
- Suezmax sale.
- Pakistan rupee.

### Editorial: Markets remain tentatively poised this week, as days of constant reversals have left most end  Buyers without any motivation or appetite to acquire units at yesterday’s levels.

India has been the most affected by the recent falls, with all offers on vessels now coming in around the mid USD 500s/LDT and below, having seen well above USD 600/LDT just last month.

The decline in local steel plate prices in India have been so severe (in excess of USD 60/Ton in a matter of a weeks) that many Alang Buyers are continuing to wait-and-watch further market developments, rather than offering on any available units.

The ongoing dearth in the supply of tonnage has stretched into this week as well, as an increasing number of owners in the wet sector are looking set to bank on market improvements going into 2022.

One Suezmax sale was concluded into Bangladesh at decent numbers this week – and this shows that the falls have not been that pronounced in neighboring markets (certainly not as bad in Chattogram at least).

Gadani has been struggling with currency depreciations of late, and the week even started with a dramatic level of PKR 178.20 against the U.S. Dollar – the highest EVER!

Turkey too has not been spared from collapsing currency fiasco as the Lira teeters on the edge just shy of TRY 14.0 against the U.S. Dollar, while local and import steel respectively registered declines of their own.

As we get closer to the holidays and Owners the world over gear up for time with family and friends, we anticipate supply could further weaken and deliveries further delayed in light of increased travel restrictions due to the new Omicron Covid variant.

## 3. Country Market Intelligence

### Bangladesh

- **Headline**: TALES OF WOE!
- **Key Notes**: *Doom and gloom.* | *Cautious.*

The talk in Bangladesh this week has been of even more doom and gloom as even local steel plate prices fell by about USD 8/Ton, but the one market sale concluded in some time to Chattogram Buyers suggested a different story.

The Winson Oil suezmax tanker OCEAN RUBY (22,029 LDT) was committed for a firm USD 620/LT LDT, to belie a lot of the woeful tales emerging from the market.

Many end users remain cautious and determined to talk down the market, but a lack of tonnage may ultimately decide that any further falls may not be as dramatic.

---

### India

- **Headline**: DOLDRUMS!

The Alang ship recycling market has remained in the doldrums for some weeks now and is rapidly losing touch with competing Bangladeshi and Pakistani markets.

Local steel plate price declines have rocked Alang, with levels now over USD 60/LDT below their recent highs and end Buyers understandably abstain from any serious negotiating / buying, while they assess the true damage as a result of the seemingly unending collapse.

The INR too has been trading at a marginally weaker Rs. 75.5X against the U.S. Dollar, but the ongoing lack of supply and far firmer competing neighbours may eventually drag Indian levels up closer to the 600/LDT levels again, as the market gears up for 2022.

*Market Sales Reported*: **NO MARKET SALES REPORTED**

---

### Pakistan

- **Headline**: WORRYING WEEK!
- **Key Notes**: *Currency collapse.* | *Demand low.*

It has been a worrying week in Gadani with the currency touching the highest level it has ever been against the U.S. Dollar (currently at PKR 178.20).

This has led to Gadani Buyers scaling back offers on any of the scarce units currently available, as prices tumble below USD 600/LDT, mirroring the Indian collapse.

Steel prices have also cooled off by about USD 11/Ton this week, whilst demand for finished product is running low as there is a reported slowdown in construction projects and electricity tariffs increase (thereby increasing cutting costs).

For the time being, however, it is expected to be a quieter end to the year in Pakistan whilst local fundamentals settle.

*Market Sales Reported*: **NO MARKET SALES REPORTED**

---

### Turkey

- **Headline**: IN RETREAT!!!

The Turkish market seems to finally be in retreat this week, as both import steel fell by about USD 8/Ton and local steel plate prices dropped by about USD 15/Ton. Even the Turkish Lira is breaking records week-after-week as this week, it briefly breached TRY 13.9X.

Understandably, local sentiments are high on caution mode and while demand still persists, levels are likely to start retreating in the coming week(s), especially if these fundamentals continue to drop.

Turkey too hasn’t escaped the pinching shortage of tonnage, as the dearth has managed to keep levels up so far.

Just how this market will react as we head into 2022 remains to be seen. For now however, levels are still strong and Owners can certainly negotiate a high-priced deal at this time.

*Market Sales Reported*: **NO MARKET SALES REPORTED**
