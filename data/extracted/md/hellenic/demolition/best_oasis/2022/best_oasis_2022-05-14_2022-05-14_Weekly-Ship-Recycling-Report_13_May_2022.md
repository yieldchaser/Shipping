Demand yet to pick up 
The market participants are back after long Eid holidays, but the enquiries 
have not seen an uptick as the recyclers have sufficient inventory on hand 
and they are willing to wait and watch for the global scrap prices to 
stabilize. 
 
The depreciation of BDT against USD has kept the market sentiments 
subdued this week. The demand for the US currency increased recently 
due to higher prices of essential items following the ongoing Russia-
Ukraine war. The currency is trading at BDT 86.67/USD. 
 
Imported scrap prices have softened by USD 35-40/MT after the market 
reopened following Eid holidays.
