Leading the Subcontinent Market 
Bangladesh is experiencing an increase in demand for Ship plates due to 
the resumption of construction activities and favorable steel prices. With 
improving tanker freight rates, recyclers are bidding competitively to 
secure tonnage before it is too late.    
 
As a result of rising demand and imported scrap prices, domestic rebar 
offers have increased by USD 25/MT week on week. The ongoing supply 
concerns and the upcoming holy month of Ramzan are being seen as 
reasons for this spike in demand.
