Stable Market conditions 
 
The domestic steel prices remained volatile this week but ended with an 
upward trend as the end user industries in India continue to remain on the 
upswing, maintainaing stable demand for ship plates. There is a demand 
for tonnage, but most recyclers are hesitant to finalize deals at these levels 
in fear of any price decline in future. 
 
Domestic scrap prices moved up by USD 10/MT as limited availability 
forced buyers to raise their bids to procure requisite volumes.

Market maintains strong momentum 
An upward trend is evident in the market with continual rise in offer prices 
from end buyers for the available tonnage. The buying interest for mid-
sized tonnage is quite high whereas for larger vessels there are very few 
offers due to already elevated price levels.  
 
As was expected since last week, major steel mills have raised their re-bar 
offers by about USD 15/MT due to increased costs and end-user demand. 
 
Imported scrap increased by about USD 5/MT this week backed by 
supportive domestic demand.
