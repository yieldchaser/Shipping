# Best Oasis Weekly Ship Recycling Report - 2024-04-26

## Weekly Market Overview

In the ship recycling markets across four key destinations, distinct trends are observed. In India, the market shows positive momentum with stable rates, and prevailing conditions suggest continued market favorability, assuming stable supply levels and no major geopolitical disruptions. Bangladesh's market has reactivated post-Eid holidays, experiencing a slight increase in activity and a rise in inquiries, with clearer market trends expected to emerge next week.

Contrarily, Pakistan's recycling market remains quieter, with lower offers from recyclers compared to other subcontinental regions. Meanwhile, in Turkiye, the market maintains stability with an increase in container supply, which may influence future pricing and demand dynamics in the recycling sector.

The European Union, alongside the U.K., U.S., and Canada, has announced a series of tightened sanctions on Iran in response to its recent attack on Israel.

These coordinated sanctions specifically target Iran's drone and missile industries, focusing on individuals and companies integral to Iran's drone production. This move represents a unified Western response to Iran's aggressive actions.

The global economy is projected to grow at a steady rate of 3.2% through 2024 and 2025, matching the pace set in 2023, according to the IMF's latest World Economic Outlook. While advanced economies are expected to experience a slight acceleration in growth—from 1.6% in 2023 to 1.8% by 2025—this will be balanced by a modest deceleration in emerging markets and developing economies, which will see growth decrease from 4.3% in 2023 to 4.2% in the following years. The long-term global growth forecast stands at 3.1%, marking the lowest in decades. Meanwhile, global inflation is anticipated to decrease from 6.8% in 2023 to 4.5% by 2025, with advanced economies likely achieving their inflation targets before their emerging and developing counterparts. Core inflation is expected to decline more slowly over the period.

### Bangladesh

**Post-Eid reopening sees slight uptick in recycling market activity and inquiries.**

- The  market  has reopened following the Eid holidays and there is a noticeable uptick in interest from recyclers.
- Post-Eid, it was anticipated that market activity would increase; this expectation has been met with a marginal rise in market engagement. There has also been a noticeable rise in inquiries within the market.
- A clearer understanding of the market's direction is anticipated by next week and the upcoming week will be interesting to monitor how the market develops.
- Bangladesh is on the brink of a significant economic milestone, as it is projected to contribute over 1% to the global gross domestic product by 2028, according to the latest IMF World Economic Outlook. However, the World Bank notes that the country's real GDP growth for the current fiscal year remains relatively subdued at 5.6%, which is below the average annual growth rate of 6.6% observed in the decade before the Covid- 19 pandemic.

### Pakistan

**The market remains subdued relative to its subcontinent Counterparts.**

- The ship recycling market in Pakistan is notably quieter compared to other regions in the subcontinent. Offers from Pakistani recyclers are consistently lower than those from recyclers in other parts of the subcontinent.
- Pakistan's central bank is anticipated to maintain the key interest rate at a record high of 22% during the upcoming policy meeting. This would mark the seventh consecutive meeting without a change in the rate. The decision comes as Pakistan prepares for critical discussions with the International Monetary Fund (IMF) regarding board approval and negotiations for a longer-term program.
