### Pakistan

**Market inactivity coupled with severe criticism from the IMF**

The construction industry is currently experiencing a dearth of business activity, leading to a significant reduction in demand for steel. The limited demand that does exist is being met through the smuggling of steel from the border with Iran.
- The IMF slammed Pakistan's proposed new yearly budget, stating the country's administration had neglected to include a more equitable tax structure. Concerns were renewed over the progress of months of discussions between the cash-strapped country and the lender over a blocked bailout tranche after harsh condemnation from the IMF's official for Pakistan.
- The Pakistani government is touting its first Russian oil imports as a transformative development for the struggling economy. However, experts caution that it is premature to determine the extent to which this inscrutable agreement will provide assistance.
