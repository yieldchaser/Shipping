## Market Overview

The Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD) predicts that India's economic growth will surpass that of China in the current and next
years. The OECD's recent global economic outlook report forecasts that India, China, and Indonesia will surpass gross domestic product projections for the years
2023 and 2024. The organization anticipates a 2.7% global growth for the current year. The report stated that, excluding the year of the Covid pandemic in 2020,
the projected annual rate would be the second-lowest since the global financial crisis.
Chinese steel rebar prices reached a three-year low this week. The anticipated revival for the current year seems to be delayed. Global steel prices have declined
as a result of reduced demand from China, thereby impacting countries such as India. Meanwhile in 2022, steel production decreased by 4.2% compared to the
post-pandemic rebound of 1,960 million tonnes in 2021, which was the largest drop since 2009 and 1991.
Oil prices increased and remained stable throughout the week due to apprehension regarding the growth of oil demand, which was offset by reductions in Saudi
output. Anticipated increase in demand during the summer holiday season in the United States, due to higher driving activity, is being counterbalanced by
concerns regarding China's sluggish fuel demand. Analysts anticipate a potential increase in oil prices in the event that the US Federal Reserve decides to forego
an interest rate hike during its upcoming meeting on June 13-14.
