# Best Oasis Weekly Ship Recycling Report - 2023-06-12

## Weekly Market Overview

The Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development (OECD) predicts that India's economic growth will surpass that of China in the current and next years. The OECD's recent global economic outlook report forecasts that India, China, and Indonesia will surpass gross domestic product projections for the years 2023 and 2024. The organization anticipates a 2.7% global growth for the current year. The report stated that, excluding the year of the Covid pandemic in 2020, the projected annual rate would be the second-lowest since the global financial crisis.

Chinese steel rebar prices reached a three-year low this week. The anticipated revival for the current year seems to be delayed. Global steel prices have declined as a result of reduced demand from China, thereby impacting countries such as India. Meanwhile in 2022, steel production decreased by 4.2% compared to the post-pandemic rebound of 1,960 million tonnes in 2021, which was the largest drop since 2009 and 1991.

Oil prices increased and remained stable throughout the week due to apprehension regarding the growth of oil demand, which was offset by reductions in Saudi output. Anticipated increase in demand during the summer holiday season in the United States, due to higher driving activity, is being counterbalanced by concerns regarding China's sluggish fuel demand. Analysts anticipate a potential increase in oil prices in the event that the US Federal Reserve decides to forego an interest rate hike during its upcoming meeting on June 13-14.

### Bangladesh

**The market's pace is slow, Lc troubles grow, Inflation lurks around the horizon.**

- There exists a degree of ambiguity regarding the opening of LCs for the purpose of recycling the vessel. The persistent challenge of opening Lc has been a significant issue of concern within this geographical area for a prolonged time.
- The current state of the local market is characterized by a softness that is reflected in the low levels of demand being observed. The buyers are meticulously scrutinizing both the domestic and global scrap markets.
- The current market value of steel in the local market has remained fairly constant over the past two to three weeks, with only minor fluctuations of approximately 100 to 200 Taka observed.
- In May, Bangladesh's inflation reached a record-high level of 9.94 percent, providing further evidence of the country's worsening economic situation. According to the Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics, the monthly inflation rate in May was greater than 9.52 percent for the first time since August 2022.

### Pakistan

**A new budget for Pakistan, Will it bring change, Or remain in range?**

- The market for ship recycling has exhibited a prolonged period of inactivity and its future trajectory remains uncertain, contingent upon the IMF's bailout and its potential impact on both the industry and the broader economy.
- The Pakistan government aims to balance economic growth with IMF-imposed conditions to revive a bailout program by presenting a carefully prepared budget for the upcoming fiscal year.The Pakistani government aims to reduce the fiscal deficit to 6.54% of GDP in the 2023-24 fiscal year, which is slightly lower than the revised estimate of 7% for the current year. The GDP growth rate target is set at 3.5%. The proposed expenditure plan allocates 14.46 trillion rupees ($50.45 billion) in total, including 1.8 trillion rupees for defence.  Additionally, the country is scheduled to hold a general election by November.
