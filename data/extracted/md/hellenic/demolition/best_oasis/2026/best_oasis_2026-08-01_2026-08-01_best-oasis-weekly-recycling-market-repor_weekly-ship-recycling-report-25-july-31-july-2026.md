## Country Market Intelligence

### India

- The Alang ship recycling market showed some improvement in sentiment during the
- week, with buyers actively seeking tonnage after losing several recent opportunities to
- Chittagong and Gadani.
- Although local market fundamentals remain relatively weak and overall activity is still
- below the levels seen in Bangladesh and Pakistan, some buying interest continues to
- be observed.
- Recent sales involving dark fleet vessels have provided limited support to market
- activity, while buyers remain selective on available tonnage.
- The Indian government's plans to develop port, ship repair, and shipbuilding
- infrastructure in and around Gujarat may provide support to the regional maritime
- economy over the longer term.

### Bangladesh

- The Bangladeshi ship recycling market remained firm during the week, with buying
- interest continuing to strengthen.
- Chittagong recyclers maintained a strong appetite for tonnage despite a recent decline
- in local melting scrap and steel plate prices.
- With the monsoon season nearing its end and an increasing number of HKC-compliant
- recycling yards becoming operational, demand for recycling tonnage is expected to
- rise further.
- These positive market fundamentals are expected to support steady and gradually
- improving price levels in the coming weeks.

### Pakistan

- Market conditions remained healthy during the week, supported by firm demand
- from local recyclers.
- The continued absence of steel imports from Iran has helped sustain domestic
- demand and positive buyer sentiment.
- Recyclers remain interested in available tonnage, with conditions expected to remain
- steady in the near term.
