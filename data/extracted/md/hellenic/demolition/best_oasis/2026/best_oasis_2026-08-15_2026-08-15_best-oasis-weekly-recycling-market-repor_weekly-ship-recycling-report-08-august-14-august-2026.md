## Country Market Intelligence

### India

- The Indian market remains active, particularly for specialized tonnage such as reefers
- and passenger vessels, while local steel prices have declined from the previous week.
- Recent sales of a passenger vessel and a reefer have achieved strong levels,
- reflecting continued interest for such specialized units.
- With Pakistan around USD 30–40/LDT higher and Bangladesh approximately USD
- 80–90/LDT above India, conventional tonnage remains difficult for India to attract.
- India continues to receive specialized tonnage as well as sanctioned vessels.

### Bangladesh

- The Bangladesh market remains active, although overall sentiment has moderated
- from the stronger levels witnessed around two weeks ago, with local prices also
- easing slightly during the week.
- Recycling prices are being maintained at present, although the softer local levels
- could become more evident in the coming week.
- Demand continues to remain strong, particularly for mid-to-large-sized vessels, with
- buyers actively seeking suitable tonnage.

### Pakistan

- The Pakistan market continues to witness strong demand, with buyers keen to
- secure additional tonnage as requirements remain high across the recycling market.
- With steel imports from Iran having reduced drastically, the requirement for steel
- from ship recycling has increased considerably, further strengthening buyers’
- appetite for available vessels.
- Buyers are expected to remain active in the coming week as they continue to seek
- suitable tonnage to meet their requirements.
