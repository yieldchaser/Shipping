## Country Market Intelligence

### India

- The Indian ship recycling market remained subdued during the week, with weak local
- demand for end products continuing to weigh on overall sentiment.
- Local steel prices have shown some stability; however, current levels are expected to
- be temporary as demand for end products remains weak.
- Market activity may soften further in the coming weeks as weak domestic demand for
- end products is expected to continue limiting market momentum.
- Two Indian recycling yards have been recommended for inclusion in the EU List. Priya
- Blue was not included in the latest recommendations; however, there is optimism that
- the facility may be considered in the next round.

### Bangladesh

- The Bangladeshi ship recycling market remained firm during the week, with buyers
- continuing to show strong interest in acquiring additional vessels.
- Local market activity remained relatively subdued; however, buyer appetite for
- recycling candidates continued to be healthy.
- Prices remained firm, supported by sustained purchasing interest despite limited
- momentum in the local market.

### Pakistan

- The Pakistani ship recycling market remained buoyant during the week, with buyers
- actively seeking suitable recycling candidates.
- Mid-sized vessels in the 7,000–10,000 LDT range continue to be the most preferred
- among local buyers.
- While Pakistani buyers remain unable to match the pricing levels offered by
- Bangladesh, market sentiment remains optimistic.
- Confidence is supported by limited steel availability, as reduced supplies from Iran are
- expected to provide support to the local recycling market.
- However, concerns are growing over the broader economic outlook, as a sharp
- increase in fuel prices over the past 8–10 days is expected to fuel inflation and could
- weigh on market sentiment in the near term.
