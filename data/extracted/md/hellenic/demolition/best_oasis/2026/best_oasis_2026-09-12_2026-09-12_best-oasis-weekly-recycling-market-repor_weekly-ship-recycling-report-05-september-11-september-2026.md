## Country Market Intelligence

### India

- The Indian recycling market remains quite buoyant. After a strong run, prices have
- stopped increasing further and remain close to last week's peak, with only a very
- minor downward trend.
- The recent strengthening of the US dollar over the last two days may encourage a
- slightly more cautious buying approach. However, there have been no vessels
- available for sale to Alang, India, throughout the week.
- Buyers continue to purchase sanctioned tonnage, keeping the Indian market alive.
- With steel from Iran still no longer coming, there is further scope for ship-recycled
- steel to continue its good run.

### Bangladesh

- The Bangladesh recycling market remained stable during the week, with local prices
- showing moderate movement and no clear upward momentum.
- Buying interest remains present, although buyers are not showing a strong
- willingness to chase prices aggressively.
- Buyers continue to show interest in bulk carriers and normal tonnage at moderate
- market levels, while maintaining a measured approach.
- Overall, the market remains steady and moderate, with no significant change in
- either buying activity or local price sentiment.

### Pakistan

- The Pakistan recycling market remains buoyant, with several buyers actively looking
- for tonnage, but no vessels are currently available.
- Buying interest remains firm, while the continued absence of Iranian steel provides
- further support for ship-recycled steel.
- Overall, the market remains well-supported, with demand outweighing the limited
- availability of vessels.
