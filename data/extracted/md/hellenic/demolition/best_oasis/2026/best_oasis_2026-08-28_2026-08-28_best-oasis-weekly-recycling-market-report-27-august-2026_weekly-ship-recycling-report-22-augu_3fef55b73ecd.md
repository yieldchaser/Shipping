## Market Overview

MARKET SUMMARY
INDIA
The Indian market remains firm, with keen interest from buyers for fresh tonnage.
Local market conditions have strengthened during the week, providing positive
support to recycling sentiment.
Recent enforcement action against scrap transactions conducted outside the formal
GST framework has resulted in a significant crackdown on cash-based trading. With
scrap increasingly required to be sold through GST-compliant channels, demand
within the formal market has improved.
This shift has supported a pickup in local scrap demand and prices, keeping recycling
fundamentals healthy and buyer appetite for new tonnage active.
BANGLADESH
The Bangladesh market has softened slightly at the local level during the week,
although overall demand for recycling tonnage remains present.
Price levels for vessels have remained broadly unchanged; however, buying interest
is no longer as firm as previously witnessed.
Buyers continue to show interest in available units, but the slight easing in local
market conditions has resulted in a more measured approach to acquisitions.
PAKISTAN
The Pakistan market remains healthy, although some correction has been witnessed
following the sharp increase seen during the previous week.
Buyer interest continues to remain positive, with demand for suitable recycling
tonnage still evident despite the adjustment in local market levels.
Overall fundamentals remain supportive, with the recent correction viewed as a
moderation from last week’s elevated market rather than a significant change in
sentiment.

The Türkiye market remained largely unchanged during the week, with no significant
movement witnessed in overall market conditions.
Domestic steel prices have remained stable, resulting in recycling fundamentals
staying broadly in line with the previous week.
With no meaningful change in steel prices or underlying market dynamics, buyer
sentiment and activity continue to remain steady.
