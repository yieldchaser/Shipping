Reporting week: 14 MARCH - 23 MARCH 2026

# MARKET SUMMARY

## INDIA

The Indian ship recycling market remained at a standstill this week, with sentiment coming under pressure from the extraordinary increase in the USD/INR index and the lack of corresponding improvement in local steel prices.

Market participants are also closely watching developments surrounding the government’s ship recycling scheme, where active work on the broader structure remains ongoing and a standardized process is expected to be introduced in due course.

Overall sentiment remains negative; however, the scarcity of available tonnage has continued to attract some keen buyers who may still be willing to pay reasonable levels, even as profitability remains under question.

# BANGLADESH

Activity in the Bangladesh ship recycling market improved this week, with buyers showing increased urgency to secure vessels ahead of the upcoming budget and particularly targeting units that can be delivered before the new measures take effect.

Prices have remained firm, although the local market has not fully moved in line with the stronger levels being offered by buyers, reflecting expectations that market conditions may turn more favorable in the near term.

Sentiment in the region has also been supported by the presence of a newly elected business-friendly political environment, which has added a more positive undertone to the market.

# PAKISTAN

The Pakistani ship recycling market remained largely unchanged from the previous week, with sentiment showing little movement overall.

Market activity was limited as Eid holidays continued over the past few days, with most participants away from the market during this period.

Looking ahead, a clearer direction may begin to emerge in the coming week as normal activity resumes.

# TURKIYE

The Turkish ship recycling market remained quiet this week, with little local activity reported due to the holiday period.

Import levels, however, moved up by around USD 8 over the course of last week, providing a somewhat firmer underlying indication for the market.

With participants expected to return after the holidays, there is potential for improved activity and sentiment in the week ahead.
