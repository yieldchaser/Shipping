Reporting week: 31 OCTOBER - 06 NOVEMBER 2026

# MARKET SUMMARY

## INDIA

The Indian ship recycling market remains firm during the week, with buyers showing strong interest in acquiring suitable vessels for recycling.

Local steel markets have softened, although the decline in the USD/INR exchange rate has provided some support to buying sentiment.

A sanctioned LPG carrier was reportedly sold for recycling into India during the week, with no other sales reported.

Overall, market sentiment remains firm, supported by sustained buying interest and continued demand for suitable recycling tonnage.

# BANGLADESH

The Bangladeshi ship recycling market continues to remain firm, with buyers actively seeking suitable vessels for recycling.

Buying interest remains steady, with prevailing price indications largely unchanged from the previous week.

Overall, market sentiment remains positive, supported by firm buying interest and stable pricing levels.

# PAKISTAN

The Pakistani ship recycling market remained largely unchanged this week, with subdued buying interest amid sluggish domestic market conditions. Continued pressure from Iranian imports, including unofficial cross-border trade, has further dampened breakers’ appetite for acquiring vessels.

Vessel price indications remain broadly in line with the previous week, while market sentiment continues to range from moderate to subdued, with limited buying activity amid persistent pressure on domestic markets.

# TURKIYE

The Turkish ship recycling market remains cautious, with limited vessel availability and weak purchasing activity.

No new recycling sales were reported during the week, as yards continued to face pressure from weak domestic steel demand, high financing costs and currency depreciation.

Buying prices remain relatively uncompetitive compared with India, Bangladesh and Pakistan, limiting Türkiye's ability to attract conventional recycling tonnage.

Nevertheless, Türkiye maintains a competitive position in recycling EU-linked vessels, supported by its proximity to Europe and established environmental compliance standards.

Overall, market sentiment remains selective, with limited expectations of a significant recovery in recycling activity in the near term.
