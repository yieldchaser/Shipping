Reporting week: 13 JUNE - 19 JUNE 2026

# MARKET SUMMARY

## INDIA

Market sentiment in India remains subdued, with recyclers increasingly concerned that prevailing price levels are not supported by underlying local market fundamentals.

Despite weak domestic demand and soft steel market conditions, a pronounced shortage of available recycling candidates continues to underpin pricing across all vessel segments.

Ship recyclers remain active in acquiring tonnage at elevated levels, largely to sustain yard operations and retain workforce capacity rather than on the basis of improved market economics.

The disconnect between local fundamentals and offered prices persists, although limited vessel supply is expected to prevent any significant correction in the near term.

# BANGLADESH

Following several weeks of aggressive bidding activity, Bangladeshi recyclers have stepped back from the market, resulting in a correction of approximately USD 25– 30/LDT across all vessel categories.

Prior to the national budget announcement, breakers actively secured inventory amid concerns over the potential introduction of heavier taxation measures.

Budget-related fears have since eased, with the government implementing only a modest VAT increase from BDT 1,200 to BDT 1,500, a significantly milder outcome than initially anticipated.

While recyclers with open yard capacity remain interested in fresh acquisitions, buying appetite is now being expressed at notably lower and more realistic price levels.

# PAKISTAN

Market sentiment in Pakistan remains unchanged from the previous week, with no major movement reported across pricing or demand.

Local recyclers continue to maintain the same stance as last week, with market activity remaining steady but without any fresh developments.

The coming week may provide clearer direction, depending on fresh tonnage availability and any new market developments.

# TURKIYE

Market sentiment in Türkiye remains soft, with recycling prices continuing to trend lower amid weakening demand.

The market has moved further away from the stronger levels witnessed earlier in the year, reflecting a gradual deterioration in buyer confidence.

Recyclers are exercising increased caution and remain reluctant to commit aggressively to fresh purchases until clearer market direction emerges.
