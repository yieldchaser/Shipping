# MARKET SUMMARY

## INDIA

The Indian ship recycling market has weakened this week, primarily influenced by currency fluctuations, resulting in softer buying indications across vessel categories.

Local steel demand remains subdued, with limited buying activity and relatively slow movement in the domestic market.

The coming months are traditionally a stronger period for steel demand, raising expectations of improved market activity and renewed buying interest.

Overall, market sentiment remains cautious in the near term, although a seasonal recovery in demand could support greater activity in the coming weeks.

# BANGLADESH

The Bangladeshi ship recycling market remains firm, with buyers continuing to demonstrate strong interest in acquiring suitable recycling tonnage.

Local market conditions have shown some improvement, providing additional support to prevailing buying indications.

Overall, market sentiment remains positive, supported by sustained buying interest and relatively stable pricing.

# PAKISTAN

The Pakistani ship recycling market remains relatively quiet, with no significant changes in overall market activity.

Buying indications have softened slightly compared with the previous week, although buyers continue to express interest in acquiring suitable recycling candidates.

Overall, market sentiment remains cautious, with limited activity and softer price indications.

# TURKIYE

The Turkish ship recycling market remains largely unchanged this week, with no significant developments in local market conditions.

Buying indications remain broadly stable, with limited movement in overall recycling activity.
