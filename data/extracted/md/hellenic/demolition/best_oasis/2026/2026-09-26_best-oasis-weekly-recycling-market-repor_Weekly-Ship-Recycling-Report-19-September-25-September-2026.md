Reporting week: 19 SEPTEMBER - 25 SEPTEMBER 2026

# MARKET SUMMARY

## INDIA

The Indian recycling market came down sharply during the week, with local levels retreating significantly from the recent highs, in line with our earlier view that the upward movement could prove temporary rather than develop into a sustained recovery.

Local steel prices have also weakened, putting pressure on recyclers’ workable levels and making buyers more cautious in their approach to fresh tonnage. At the same time, the new policy framework introduced by the Gujarat Maritime Board is a positive development for the industry, with the authorities continuing to show strong support toward strengthening and developing the ship recycling sector.

Overall, the immediate market has weakened considerably, although the broader regulatory direction remains supportive for the industry.

# BANGLADESH

The Bangladesh recycling market remained largely stable during the week, although local steel prices have softened and placed some pressure on recyclers’ buying levels.

The limited availability of suitable recycling candidates continues to support vessel prices, with a few keen buyers still prepared to pursue attractive tonnage at relatively firm levels where their buying calculations remain viable.

Overall, the market remains steady, with softer local fundamentals being partly offset by the continued shortage of vessels available for recycling.

# PAKISTAN

The Pakistan recycling market remained under pressure during the week, with local plate prices weakening further, continuing to weigh on recyclers’ buying appetite.

Despite the softer domestic steel market, vessel prices have remained broadly around last week’s levels, with no notable fresh recycling sales reported during the week.

The PKR has remained relatively stable in the recent period, while regular import activity continues across the wider market. Availability of alternative steel products, including HR Coil, is also helping meet local demand and limiting the impact of reduced ship-derived scrap supply.

Overall, the market remains cautious, with softer local steel prices continuing to influence buying sentiment, while the currency environment remains relatively stable for the time being.

# TURKIYE

The Turkish recycling market remained largely stable during the week, with no significant movement in vessel prices.

Imported scrap and local steel prices both recorded modest improvements during the week; however, these gains have yet to translate into stronger recycling vessel prices.

Overall, the market remains steady, with recyclers maintaining broadly similar buying levels while awaiting a clearer direction from the underlying steel and scrap markets.
