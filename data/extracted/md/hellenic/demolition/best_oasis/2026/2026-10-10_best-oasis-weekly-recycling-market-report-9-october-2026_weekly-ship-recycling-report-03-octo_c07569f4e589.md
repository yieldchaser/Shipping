# MARKET SUMMARY

## INDIA

The Indian recycling market remained broadly similar to last week, with little change in overall market conditions. However, the strengthening of the US dollar against the Indian rupee has exerted some downward pressure on prices.

Buying interest remains strong, with buyers continuing to show keen interest in acquiring suitable vessels despite the limited availability.

Vessel supply remains extremely tight, with virtually no new vessels entering the market apart from one or two smaller ships.

Overall, demand continues to significantly exceed available supply, with buyers remaining highly competitive for the very limited tonnage available.

# BANGLADESH

The Bangladeshi recycling market has become somewhat more active this week, primarily due to the continued shortage of available vessels rather than a significant improvement in local market conditions.

Buyers continue to pursue available tonnage, with some acquisitions driven by the need to secure vessels for recycling to meet Hong Kong Convention (HKC) compliance requirements.

Other buyers are also proceeding with purchases due to the lack of alternative vessels available in the market.

Overall, market activity has strengthened amid the ongoing shortage of ships, with buying decisions increasingly influenced by limited availability and HKC compliance requirements.

# PAKISTAN

The Pakistani recycling market remained broadly similar to last week, with buyers continuing to show interest in acquiring vessels.

Despite the continued buying interest, Pakistani buyers remain unable to compete effectively with Bangladesh for the limited vessels available in the market.

Overall, market activity continues to be constrained by the shortage of available tonnage and the competitive buying environment in Bangladesh.

# TURKIYE

The Turkish ship recycling market continues to demonstrate stability despite the ongoing depreciation of the Turkish lira, with overall market conditions remaining largely unchanged.

Recycling prices are holding steady, with no significant fluctuations observed, and price levels remaining relatively firm.

The supply of vessels available for recycling remains limited, while Turkish recycling yards continue to adopt a selective approach, focusing primarily on good-quality vessels.The main sources of tonnage remain vessels located in close geographical proximity to Turkey, as well as vessels required to be recycled in accordance with European Union regulations.

These factors continue to drive the limited flow of vessels toward Turkish recycling facilities.
