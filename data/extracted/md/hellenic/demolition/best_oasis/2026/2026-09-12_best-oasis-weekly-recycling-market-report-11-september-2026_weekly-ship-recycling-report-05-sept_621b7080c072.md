Reporting week: 05 SEPTEMBER - 11 SEPTEMBER 2026

# MARKET SUMMARY

## INDIA

The Indian recycling market remains quite buoyant. After a strong run, prices have stopped increasing further and remain close to last week's peak, with only a very minor downward trend.

The recent strengthening of the US dollar over the last two days may encourage a slightly more cautious buying approach. However, there have been no vessels available for sale to Alang, India, throughout the week.

Buyers continue to purchase sanctioned tonnage, keeping the Indian market alive. With steel from Iran still no longer coming, there is further scope for ship-recycled steel to continue its good run.

# BANGLADESH

The Bangladesh recycling market remained stable during the week, with local prices showing moderate movement and no clear upward momentum.

Buying interest remains present, although buyers are not showing a strong willingness to chase prices aggressively.

Buyers continue to show interest in bulk carriers and normal tonnage at moderate market levels, while maintaining a measured approach.

Overall, the market remains steady and moderate, with no significant change in either buying activity or local price sentiment.

# PAKISTAN

The Pakistan recycling market remains buoyant, with several buyers actively looking for tonnage, but no vessels are currently available.

Buying interest remains firm, while the continued absence of Iranian steel provides further support for ship-recycled steel.

Overall, the market remains well-supported, with demand outweighing the limited availability of vessels.

# TURKIYE

The Turkish recycling market remained largely stable this week, with no notable change in either demand or pricing. Market activity continues at a controlled pace, with vessel availability shaped mainly by regulatory requirements and positioning.

Yards remain comfortably covered with existing commitments, limiting the need to compete aggressively for fresh tonnage. Buyers continue to show preference for wellmaintained, quality vessels, while taking a more cautious approach towards less attractive units.

The continued weakness of the Turkish lira remains a factor in market sentiment and pricing. Nevertheless, the overall market direction remains steady, with buyers and sellers largely holding their current positions.
