Reporting week: 26 SEPTEMBER - 02 OCTOBER 2026

# MARKET SUMMARY

## INDIA

The Indian recycling market remained tight during the week. Prices, which had declined last week, have recovered to some extent, although the weaker exchange rate has partly offset the improvement.

Vessel availability continues to be the key constraint, with very limited tonnage currently being offered for recycling. As a result, buyers remain highly competitive for whatever suitable units become available.

The market continues to depend significantly on opportunities involving sanctioned vessels or specialised assets, including drilling and other non-conventional units, due to the continued shortage of mainstream recycling candidates.

Overall, buying interest remains strong despite the tight supply environment, with recyclers actively competing for the limited tonnage available.

# BANGLADESH

The Bangladeshi recycling market remained relatively quiet during the week, with very few vessels available for recycling and limited market activity.

Buyers continue to show interest in acquiring tonnage, although buying appetite remains comparatively more measured than in India and Pakistan.

Market sentiment has softened somewhat, with recyclers maintaining a more moderate approach while continuing to look for suitable opportunities.

Overall, Bangladesh remains moderately positioned, with vessel availability continuing to be the principal constraint on market activity.

# PAKISTAN

The Pakistani recycling market remained active in terms of buying interest, although vessel availability continues to be extremely limited, similar to the situation in India.

Very few actual sales have been reported. One approximately 17,000-tonne tanker, which had previously been hit by a drone, has reached Gadani, while otherwise there has been little meaningful tonnage entering the market.

The market now appears increasingly saturated, with buyers having already acquired tonnage and some feeling that earlier purchases were concluded at comparatively high levels. As a result, vessel prices have moved lower.

Local steel market conditions have also remained subdued, with pressure from imported HR coil from China and, more recently, competitively priced material from Brazil restricting improvement in domestic market sentiment.

Overall, buying capacity remains present, but the combination of limited vessel availability, softer vessel prices and a weak local steel environment has made buyers more cautious.

# TURKIYE

The Turkish recycling market showed a slight improvement during the week, with vessel prices edging higher in line with the modest seasonal firming typically observed at this time of year.

Imported scrap and local steel prices also recorded some gains, providing additional support to market sentiment, although the upward movement in recycling vessel prices remained limited.

Overall, market conditions remain relatively stable, with recyclers maintaining broadly similar buying levels while allowing for a small seasonal improvement in pricing and continuing to await clearer direction from the underlying steel and scrap markets.
