Reporting week: 07 NOVEMBER - 13 NOVEMBER 2026

# MARKET SUMMARY

## INDIA

The Indian ship recycling market has started to regain momentum following the five-day Diwali holiday break, with market activity gradually picking up.

Local steel demand is expected to improve as markets return to normal operations, supporting renewed buying interest among recyclers.

Buying indications are expected to strengthen further in the coming week as demand improves and market activity gathers pace.

Overall, market sentiment remains positive, with expectations of firmer prices and improved buying activity in the near term.

# BANGLADESH

The Bangladeshi ship recycling market continues to attract strong buying interest, with recyclers remaining keen to acquire suitable tonnage.

However, local market conditions have softened over the past few days, putting some downward pressure on price indications.

Despite the recent softening, buyers remain keen to secure suitable recycling candidates, with buying interest continuing at competitive levels.

Overall, market sentiment remains cautiously positive, supported by sustained buying interest despite the recent weakness in local market conditions.

# PAKISTAN

The Pakistani ship recycling market remains largely unchanged this week, with limited movement in local market conditions and demand.

Although buyers remain present, the availability of suitable recycling tonnage continues to be limited.

Several recyclers who were previously keen to acquire vessels have now adopted a wait-and-see approach, anticipating a correction in vessel prices before re-entering the market.

Overall, market sentiment remains subdued, with buying activity expected to remain selective until more competitively priced tonnage becomes available.

# TURKIYE

The Turkish ship recycling market remains stable but subdued, with limited vessel availability and cautious purchasing activity.

Recyclers continue to face pressure from weak domestic steel demand, elevated financing costs and ongoing currency depreciation.

Buying prices remain relatively uncompetitive compared with India, Bangladesh and Pakistan, limiting Türkiye's ability to attract conventional recycling tonnage.

Nevertheless, Türkiye retains a competitive position in recycling EU-linked vessels, supported by its proximity to Europe and established environmental compliance standards.

Overall, market sentiment remains cautious, with recyclers adopting a wait-and-see approach as they await an improvement in domestic steel demand. Buying activity is expected to remain limited in the near term.
