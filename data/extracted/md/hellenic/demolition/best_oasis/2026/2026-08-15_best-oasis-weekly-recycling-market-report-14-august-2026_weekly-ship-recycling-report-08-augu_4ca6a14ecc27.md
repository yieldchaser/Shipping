Reporting week: 08 AUGUST - 14 AUGUST 2026

# MARKET SUMMARY

## INDIA

The Indian market remains active, particularly for specialized tonnage such as reefers and passenger vessels, while local steel prices have declined from the previous week.

Recent sales of a passenger vessel and a reefer have achieved strong levels, reflecting continued interest for such specialized units.

With Pakistan around USD 30–40/LDT higher and Bangladesh approximately USD 80–90/LDT above India, conventional tonnage remains difficult for India to attract.

India continues to receive specialized tonnage as well as sanctioned vessels.

# BANGLADESH

The Bangladesh market remains active, although overall sentiment has moderated from the stronger levels witnessed around two weeks ago, with local prices also easing slightly during the week.

Recycling prices are being maintained at present, although the softer local levels could become more evident in the coming week.

Demand continues to remain strong, particularly for mid-to-large-sized vessels, with buyers actively seeking suitable tonnage.

# PAKISTAN

The Pakistan market continues to witness strong demand, with buyers keen to secure additional tonnage as requirements remain high across the recycling market.

With steel imports from Iran having reduced drastically, the requirement for steel from ship recycling has increased considerably, further strengthening buyers’ appetite for available vessels.

Buyers are expected to remain active in the coming week as they continue to seek suitable tonnage to meet their requirements.

# TURKIYE

The Türkiye market remained at relatively low levels during the week, although overall conditions continued to remain stable with no significant change in sentiment.

Buyer activity and market fundamentals remained broadly unchanged from the previous week, with little movement witnessed across the market.

The coming week will be watched for any signs of improvement in activity or a shift from the prevailing stable sentiment.
