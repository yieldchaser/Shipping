## Country Market Intelligence

### India

- The Indian market strengthened during the week due to the limited availability of steel
- plates, as slow cutting activity and several vessels remaining away from shore
- restricted supply.
- This improvement is expected to be temporary, with conditions likely to change over
- the coming weeks. Demand for steel remains very weak at the end-product level.
- Buyers remain keen to acquire vessels but continue to face difficulty competing with
- offers from other recycling destinations.
- Dark-fleet vessels continue to be recycled at Indian shores. As India remains the only
- destination accepting such vessels, it holds a significant advantage over competing
- markets.

### Bangladesh

- Local market conditions weakened over the week, although healthy inquiry for
- recycling candidates remains.
- Buyers are showing greater interest in bulkers and other vessels without compliance-
- related concerns.
- Compliance risks remain a major challenge after a tanker was sanctioned following its
- arrival and completion of clearances, just before beaching. Such developments place
- significant pressure on both ship recyclers and cash buyers.

### Pakistan

- Market conditions remained largely unchanged, with recyclers actively seeking vessels
- but very few candidates being offered.
- Local markets remain short of scrap, supporting healthy conditions and good demand
- from buyers.
- Pakistani buyers remain unable to compete with Bangladesh but are currently in a
- position to compete with India.
