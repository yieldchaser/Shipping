Reporting week: 01 AUGUST - 07 AUGUST 2026

# MARKET SUMMARY

## INDIA

The Indian ship recycling market continues to see consistent demand from end buyers, with local buying interest remaining relatively firm during the week.

Contrary to the softer expectations indicated over the past few weeks, market prices have moved upward, supported by renewed local demand amid limited availability of steel from recycled vessels.

Tide-related constraints have also contributed to tighter availability, helping the market maintain its current elevated levels.

Non-ferrous prices have remained firm, providing additional support to recycling values and keeping Indian buyers keen to acquire suitable tonnage.

# BANGLADESH

The Bangladeshi ship recycling market remained under pressure during the week, with weak demand dragging local steel plate prices lower for another consecutive week.

Despite the prevailing bearish sentiment, the market is not entirely quiet, with a few recyclers actively pursuing high-quality recycling candidates.

Buyers are willing to pay healthy premiums for prompt-delivery vessels that are well maintained and carry a substantial quantity of spares, keeping selective demand alive for the right assets.

Market participants will be closely watching the coming weeks for signs of a broader return in recycler demand, which would be required to support an improvement in baseline price levels.

# PAKISTAN

The Pakistani ship recycling market continues to remain strong, with buyer appetite improving further and recyclers becoming increasingly keen to secure available tonnage at prevailing market levels.

Buyers are showing greater confidence in the market, with firm local demand and positive sentiment encouraging more active interest in suitable recycling candidates.

The overall market tone remains healthy, and with buyers becoming keener by the day, Pakistan is expected to continue offering strong competition for available vessels in the near term.

# TURKIYE

The Turkish ship recycling market remained largely unchanged from the previous week, with no notable movement observed in overall market conditions.

Buyer activity and market sentiment continued at broadly similar levels, with recyclers maintaining a cautious approach toward available tonnage.

With limited fresh developments during the week, attention now turns to the coming weeks for any signs of renewed activity or a shift in prevailing market sentiment.
