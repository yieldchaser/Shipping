## Country Market Intelligence

### India

- The Indian recycling market remained tight during the week. Prices, which had
- declined last week, have recovered to some extent, although the weaker exchange
- rate has partly offset the improvement.
- Vessel availability continues to be the key constraint, with very limited tonnage
- currently being offered for recycling. As a result, buyers remain highly competitive for
- whatever suitable units become available.
- The market continues to depend significantly on opportunities involving sanctioned
- vessels or specialised assets, including drilling and other non-conventional units, due
- to the continued shortage of mainstream recycling candidates.
- Overall, buying interest remains strong despite the tight supply environment, with
- recyclers actively competing for the limited tonnage available.

### Bangladesh

- The Bangladeshi recycling market remained relatively quiet during the week, with
- very few vessels available for recycling and limited market activity.
- Buyers continue to show interest in acquiring tonnage, although buying appetite
- remains comparatively more measured than in India and Pakistan.
- Market sentiment has softened somewhat, with recyclers maintaining a more
- moderate approach while continuing to look for suitable opportunities.
- Overall, Bangladesh remains moderately positioned, with vessel availability
- continuing to be the principal constraint on market activity.

### Pakistan

- The Pakistani recycling market remained active in terms of buying interest, although
- vessel availability continues to be extremely limited, similar to the situation in India.
- Very few actual sales have been reported. One approximately 17,000-tonne tanker,
- which had previously been hit by a drone, has reached Gadani, while otherwise there
- has been little meaningful tonnage entering the market.
- The market now appears increasingly saturated, with buyers having already acquired
- tonnage and some feeling that earlier purchases were concluded at comparatively
- high levels. As a result, vessel prices have moved lower.
