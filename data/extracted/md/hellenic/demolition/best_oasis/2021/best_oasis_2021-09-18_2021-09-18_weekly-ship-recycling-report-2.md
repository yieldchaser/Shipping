# Best Oasis Weekly Ship Recycling Report - 2021-09-18

## Weekly Market Overview

WEEKLY   SHIP  RECYCLING  REPORT 10th September to 17th September 2021

Iron ore prices continue their free fall and are being a sentiment dampener  for the subcontinent recycling market, making the end buyers reluctant to  place high offers due to uncertainty of price trajectory and demand for  steel. Among the three major players of subcontinent, Pakistan is leading  the price board followed closely by India and Bangladesh.    The container shipping market is experiencing one of its best years as a  perfect storm of COVID-driven purchasing demand, port congestion and  typhoons have boosted freight rates but many industry participants are  now of the opinion that the supply chain disruptions which caused these  record-breaking prices may ease soon and the freight rates will start to see  some corrections shortly.
