Leading the price charts 
The end buyers of Bangladesh are leading the price charts yet again as the 
impact of rising price of scraps globally is being felt in the local market. 
Steel prices have been on an upward curve due to pickup in demand 
especially from construction sector. 
 
All major steel mills in Bangladesh have raised their re-bar offers by USD 
20/MT backed by continual rise in imported scrap prices. 
This constant rise in steel prices is hurting the real estate sector as the 
builders do not have the scope to pass on the rising costs to the customers 
since the housing agreements are signed very much in advance.
