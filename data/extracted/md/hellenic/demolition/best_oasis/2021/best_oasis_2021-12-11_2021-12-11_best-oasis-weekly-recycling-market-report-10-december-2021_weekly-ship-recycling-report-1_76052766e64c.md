Demand curve remains flat 
The end buyers have maintained their prudent approach as they are 
looking for signs of increase in demand before finalizing deals for securing 
tonnage. With the recent heavy rainfalls and halting of ongoing 
construction projects, the demand has slowed down to a great extent 
leading to a dull market sentiment discouraging buyers to make further 
purchases. 
 
Imported scrap offers have recorded a further decline of about USD 10-
