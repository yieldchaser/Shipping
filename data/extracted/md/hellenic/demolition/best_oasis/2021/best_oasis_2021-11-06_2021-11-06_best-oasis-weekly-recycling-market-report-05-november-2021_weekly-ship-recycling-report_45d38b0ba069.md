availability, and possibility of replacing fossil fuels completely in the years to 
come.

Strong buying interest 
The impact of rising prices of scrap globally in the previous weeks is now being 
reflected in the market as the end buyers have shown increasing interest to 
offer competitive prices leaving behind the resistance which was being shown 
in the previous month after the iron ore prices nosedived. 
 
The soaring raw material prices have forced major Steel mills to increase rebar 
prices further by about USD 20-25/MT.  
  
The imported scrap prices have witnessed a minor correction of about USD 
5/MT this week after a series of continual rise since past two weeks.
