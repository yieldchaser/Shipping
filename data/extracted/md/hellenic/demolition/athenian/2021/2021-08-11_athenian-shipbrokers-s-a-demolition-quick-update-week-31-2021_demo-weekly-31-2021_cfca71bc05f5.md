# Athenian Shipbrokers Demolition Quick Update - Week 31

**Publisher**: Athenian Shipbrokers S.A. | **Issue Date**: 2021-08-08 | **Period**: 2nd- 8th August

## Market Commentary
The demolition prices continued to rise, driven by scarcity of available tonnage. Despite the concerns and the lockdown restrictions from the surge of Covid-19, the upturn of the recycling markets seem will be continued in the rest of summer/monsoon months. Bangladesh maintained its uplifting course with prices above the barrier of USD 600/LTD. The same upward trend had been followed by Pakistani recyclers. Meanwhile, India’s prices had been subdued mainly by transport strike in Alang.
