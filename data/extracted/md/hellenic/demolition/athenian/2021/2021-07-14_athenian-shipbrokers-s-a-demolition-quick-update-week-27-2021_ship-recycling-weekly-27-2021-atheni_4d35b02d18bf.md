# Athenian Shipbrokers Demolition Quick Update - Week 27

**Publisher**: Athenian Shipbrokers S.A. | **Issue Date**: 2021-07-09 | **Period**: 5thJune- 9nd July

## Market Commentary
Firm prices continued to prevail in the ship recycling market, primarily owed to the lack of available tonnage, with the End Buyers giving rates of high 500/LDT. Pakistan remained a strong competitor in the market, but lost the lead to an incredible Bangladeshi market. India’s supply of specialist vessels was steady, along with some green vessels, however that was not able to move them from the last place of the market.
