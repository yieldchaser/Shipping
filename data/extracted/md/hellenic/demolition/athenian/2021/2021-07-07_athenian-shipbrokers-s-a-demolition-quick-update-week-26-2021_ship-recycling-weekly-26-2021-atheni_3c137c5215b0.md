# Athenian Shipbrokers Demolition Quick Update - Week 26

**Publisher**: Athenian Shipbrokers S.A. | **Issue Date**: 2021-07-02 | **Period**: 28thJune- 2nd July

## Market Commentary
Sub-continent market pricing continued its healthy course offering mid and high 500/LDT for the right ships. Pakistan stayed on the leading position for another week, despite Bangladesh’s recovery on the market and the impressive high priced deals. In addition, India’s steel prices were declined last week, due to the Indian Rupee depreciation over the past weeks.
