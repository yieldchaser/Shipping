# Athenian Shipbrokers Demolition Quick Update - Week 29

**Publisher**: Athenian Shipbrokers S.A. | **Issue Date**: 2021-07-23 | **Period**: 19th- 23rd July

## Market Commentary
Bangladeshi market continued its impressing course, despite the period of Eid holidays, in addition with Pakistani market which lost several well placed vessels. For Indian market is a very positive time, with the increasing rates of steel plate prices and Indian Rupee rates against US Dollar. Turkey remained closed for the entire working week due to the Eid celebration, so there were no offers or indications.
