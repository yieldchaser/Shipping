# Athenian Shipbrokers Demolition Quick Update - Week 28

**Publisher**: Athenian Shipbrokers S.A. | **Issue Date**: 2021-07-16 | **Period**: 12th- 16nd July

## Market Commentary
Sub-Continent market steel prices improved over the past week continuing its uplifting course. Bangladesh pricing improved further, with few sales over USD 600/LDT. Pakistan remained closed to its Bangladesh counter parts, in the race of the market pricing. India on the other hand had a rough past weeks due to declining local steel prices, but India’s market progressed forward last week and tried to compete with her counter parts.
