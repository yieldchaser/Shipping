# Athenian Shipbrokers Demolition Quick Update - Week 30

**Publisher**: Athenian Shipbrokers S.A. | **Issue Date**: 2021-07-30 | **Period**: 26th- 30th July

## Market Commentary
At the end of the EID holidays, the supply of tonnage was shortened. Bangladesh had a difficult time to secure vessels despite the fact of the outstanding numbers excess 600/LDT. Pakistan started to understand that they have to step up their game to compete with their counterparts. On the other hand, India regained ground on market pricing and tried to get as many vessels from their rivals as they could.
