PBF at Tangshan port dealt 995 yuan/mt; decreased 15-20 yuan/mt over yesterday. As the Spring Festival approaches, some steel mills that have not completed replenishment have accelerated their pace. Overall, the daily average port clearance volume has slightly increased, with a more significant increase in port clearance in North and East China regions; Overall, due to a significant increase in arrivals, port inventory remains in a state of accumulation. Next week, on the eve of the Spring Festival, steel mills will end restocking and market transactions will soon come to a halt. Mineral prices may continue to fluctuate narrowly.
