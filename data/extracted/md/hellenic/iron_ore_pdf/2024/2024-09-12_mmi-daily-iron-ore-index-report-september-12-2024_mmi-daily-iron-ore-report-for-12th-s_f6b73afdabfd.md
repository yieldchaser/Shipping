# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Iron ore futures surged today. The most-traded contract I2501 finally closed at 707 yuan/mt, with a daily increase of 3.97%. Traders actively sold their stocks; steel mills remained cautious and restocked as needed. The market trading atmosphere was average today. In Shandong, PB fines were traded at around 690-710 yuan/mt, up 10-20 yuan/mt from yesterday; in Tangshan, PB fines were traded at around 700-720 yuan/mt, up 10-15 yuan/mt from yesterday. Today's rebar apparent demand data showed a significant increase, with a notable decline in the inventories of the five major steel products and a substantial rise in end-user demand. The overall market sentiment improved, driving ore prices sharply higher. Given that recent changes in steel mill profits have been negligible and the blast furnace resumption has been slow, there is insufficient drive for further increases in ore prices and limited room for them to rise further.
