## Desk Commentary
DCE iron ore futures tight by 0.1%; the main contract I2405 closed 989.5. some traders were negative to sell, Some steel mills tended to be wait-and-see, and purchasing enthusiasm is not high.  total transactions remained insipid.  PBF at Shandong port dealt 1026-1030 yuan/mt; decreased 5-10 yuan/mt over yesterday. As of January 26th, the total inventory of 35 ports tracked by SMM was 124.62 million tons, an increase of 1.35 million tons month on month and a decrease of 9.02 million tons year-on-year; The daily average port clearance volume of imported ore is 2.933 million tons, a decrease of 127000 tons compared to the previous week. There has been a slight decrease in port clearance this week. Firstly, due to the completion of pre holiday replenishment by steel mills, procurement has significantly slowed down; Furthermore, some ports along the river are affected by strong winds, resulting in significant reductions in port clearance; With the end of pre holiday storage in steel mills, the volume of port clearance will continue to decline. Considering that the macro outlook remains optimistic in the later stage, it is expected that mining prices will continue to fluctuate at high levels. COPYRIGHT METALS MARKET
