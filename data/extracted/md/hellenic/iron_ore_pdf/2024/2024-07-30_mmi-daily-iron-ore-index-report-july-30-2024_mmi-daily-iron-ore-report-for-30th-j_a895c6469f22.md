## Desk Commentary
DCE iron ore futures market falling sharply trends, the main contract closed 756, an decrease of 2.77% throughout the day; Traders are feeling indifferent about shipping; steel mills are cautious and mainly purchasing on demand. The overall market atmosphere today is average. In Shandong, the transaction price of PB powder is between 755-760 yuan/ton, a decrease of 15-20 yuan/ton compared to yesterday; in Tangshan, the transaction price of PB powder is between 770-775 yuan/ton, a decrease of 20-25 yuan/ton compared to yesterday. According to SMM statistics, the impact of blast furnace maintenance on iron water this week (July 27th to August 2nd) was 109.91 million tons, an increase of 7.24 million tons compared to last week. The current trend of iron water is declining, weakening the support for iron powder demand, and exacerbating the losses of steel mills. Some steel mills have gradually increased their blast furnace maintenance plans. It is expected that short-term iron ore prices will predominantly fluctuate weakly. COPYRIGHT METALS MARKET
