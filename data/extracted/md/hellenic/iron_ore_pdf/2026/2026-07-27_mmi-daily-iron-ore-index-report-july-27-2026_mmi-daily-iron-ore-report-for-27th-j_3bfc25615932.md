# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Today, iron ore price fluctuations on the Dalian Commodity Exchange (DCE) were relatively narrow. The DCE benchmark contract i2609 closed at 741 yuan/ton, down 0.27%. Spot prices at Qingdao Port remained essentially flat compared with the previous trading day. Early morning saw active quotations from traders, while steel mills purchased on an as-needed basis, resulting in generally moderate spot trading activity.

Recently, iron ore supply has shown an increase. According to SMM statistics, global iron ore shipments totalled 27.82 million tonnes, down 10% month-on-month but up 1% year-on-year on a cumulative basis. Shipments from Australia and Brazil both declined slightly; shipments from non-mainstream countries also decreased month-on-month, though those from India and Peru rebounded noticeably. Meanwhile, iron ore arrivals at Chinese ports totalled 30.32 million tonnes, surging 54% month-on-month and up 5% year-on-year cumulatively, with part of the increase driven by the discharge of vessels delayed by previous typhoons. On the demand side, blast furnace pig iron production continued its off-season downward trend, moving steadily lower. On the sentiment front, the US and Iran engaged in ceasefire negotiations, causing oil prices to fall, but the iron ore market showed little reaction. Overall, short-term iron ore prices are likely to remain in a range-bound pattern.
