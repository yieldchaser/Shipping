# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Today, DCE iron ore futures trended weakly, with contract I2609 closing at 746 yuan/mt, up 0.88% from the previous trading day. Port spot prices rose by about 5-7 yuan/mt from the previous day. Trader activity was average, and steel mills purchased on an as-needed basis. As of now, spot trading sentiment has been mediocre. The latest SMM survey data further confirmed the weakening trend in iron ore demand. Statistics show that the national blast furnace operating rate fell to 89.78%, and daily average pig iron production dropped to 2.4406 million mt, down 15,400 mt MoM. Additionally, affected by commemorative events in Tangshan, local environmental protection-related maintenance is expected to intensify, and the weakening trend in hot metal output is likely to strengthen further. In the short term, the bearish fundamental backdrop has deepened. However, given the currently high proportion of short positions in the futures market, selling pressure is nearly exhausted, and the downward momentum for prices has begun to fade. Short-term ore prices are likely to consolidate and move sideways. Going forward, attention should be paid to whether a reduction in short positions could drive a price rebound.
