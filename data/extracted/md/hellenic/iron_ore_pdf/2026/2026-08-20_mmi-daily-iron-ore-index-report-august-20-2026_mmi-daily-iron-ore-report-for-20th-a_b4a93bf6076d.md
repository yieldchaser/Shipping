## Desk Commentary
Iron ore futures traded weak today. The DCE most-active contract I2701 settled at RMB 707/mt, down 1.19% from the previous trading day. Spot prices at Qingdao Port fell RMB 4-5/mt. Traders showed low enthusiasm in offering, while mills restocked on an as-needed basis at a measured pace, leaving overall spot transactions light. SMM's latest inventory data for the 10 key ports show total stocks at 105.40 million mt, down 1.55 million mt week-on-week. Apart from a slight build in concentrate, lump ore, pellets and fines all posted drawdowns. In the near term, iron ore demand remains stable. On fundamen- tals, iron ore prices are likely to trade rangebound in the near term, with any directional move depending more on how news flow transmits through the market. COPYRIGHT METALS MARKET
