# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Today, the DCE iron ore futures trended weaker today, with contract I2609 closing at 751.5 yuan/mt, up 0.87% from the previous trading day. Port spot prices rose 2-5 yuan/mt from the prior trading day. Trader activity was moderate, and steel mills mainly purchased for essential restocking. As of now, spot trading volumes were moderate. SMM's latest statistics showed that total iron ore inventory at 35 main ports nationwide was 147.16 million mt, down 1.14 million mt WoW. Over the same period, the daily average port pick-up volume edged down by 23,000 mt to 3.275 million mt. This week, affected by typhoons and persistent rainy weather, port operations were disrupted, vessel berthing and unloading eﬃciency dropped significantly, and port congestion intensified. On the demand side, as hot metal production of steel mill blast furnaces continued to decline, the daily average port pick-up volume pulled back slightly. With increasing blast furnace maintenance at steel mills, hot metal production is still expected to decline further. Therefore, from a fundamental perspec- tive, short-term support for iron ore prices is expected to continue weakening. COPYRIGHT METALS MARKET
