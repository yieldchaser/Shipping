# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Today, the iron ore market showed a slightly weaker trend on the futures side. The DCE main contract i2609 closed at 713.5 yuan/ton, down 0.35% from the previous trading session. Spot prices at Qingdao Port declined by an average of about 0-2 yuan/ton from the previous day. Trading activity among merchants was subdued, while steel mills remained cautious and preferred to wait and see, resulting in relatively limited spot transaction volumes so far this week.

Iron ore supply remained largely stable this week. According to SMM statistics, global iron ore shipments totaled 32.24 million tonnes last week, holding steady week-on-week; iron ore arrivals at Chinese ports reached 29.68 million tonnes, down slightly by 2% month-on-month.

On the news front, the strike at Port Hedland continues to progress, with approximately 150 workers having joined the action so far. However, the strike's short-term impact on iron ore supply has been limited, and the market reaction has been relatively muted. Taking all factors into account, apart from the backdrop of ample supply and a weak fundamental picture, there is currently no strong unilateral driver in the iron ore market. Therefore, iron ore prices are likely to continue their range-bound and slightly weak pattern in the near term.
