# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Iron ore futures traded firmer on Friday. The DCE most-traded i2701 contract settled at 727 yuan/mt, up 1.04% from the previous session. Qingdao port spot prices rose by an average of 1 yuan/mt, with traders showing only moderate enthusiasm in offering and mills buying on an as-needed basis. Overall spot trading activity was lacklustre.

Iron ore port inventories drew down modestly this week, though demand turned softer. Total stocks across 35 major Chinese ports stood at 143.91 million mt, down 1.7 million mt week-on-week, with overall inventory levels flattening out; average daily port outbound volume rose by 55,000 mt to 3.145 million mt. Some domestic mills have scheduled equipment maintenance on tight coke supply, leaving them less willing to procure iron ore, and growth in blast furnace hot metal output is expected to slow. Elsewhere, mills in Indonesia are facing industrial water shortages linked to El Niño, while market talk points to a blast furnace outage at a Vietnamese mill following a fire. Taken together, these factors point to weaker iron ore demand in the near term, leaving prices likely to come under pressure.
