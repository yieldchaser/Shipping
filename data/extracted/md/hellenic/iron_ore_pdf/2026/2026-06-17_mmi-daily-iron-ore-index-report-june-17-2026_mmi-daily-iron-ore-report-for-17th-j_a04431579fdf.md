# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
The DCE iron ore futures continued to decline today, with the benchmark i2609 contract finally closing at 747.5 yuan/ton, down 2.61% from the previous session and extending losses markedly. Portside spot prices also fell in tandem, dropping 10-15 yuan/ton from a day earlier. Traders showed diverging approaches—some refrained from quoting and stood on the sidelines, while others offered cargoes in line with market conditions; steel mills remained cautious, with strong intentions to push prices down and a slower procurement pace, dampening overall market sentiment. Trading was subdued, and spot transaction volumes remained limited as of now. According to SMM survey data released on June 17, the blast furnace operating rate among 242 sampled steel mills stood at 90.67%, up 0.20 percentage point week-on-week, while the average daily hot metal output of the sample mills reached 2.465 million tons, an increase of 5,300 tons from the prior week. However, looking ahead to next week, as steel mills face growing margin pressure, maintenance plans are increasing, which may mark a near-term turning point for hot metal production. Demand is expected to weaken marginally, while persistent supply-side pressure and the off-season effect in the industry jointly weigh on iron ore prices, exacerbating the decline. In the short term, pessimistic sentiment dominates the market, and DCE iron ore prices are likely to continue their bottoming-out process.
