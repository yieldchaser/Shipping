# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Today, the DCE iron ore futures futures consolidated on a subdued note. The most-traded DCE i2609 futures contract closed at 746 yuan/mt, down 0.13%. Spot prices at Qingdao Port fell about 2-3 yuan/mt from the previous trading day. Trader activity was moderate, with relatively few inquiries from steel mills. The current spot trading atmosphere is sluggish. From the current fundamentals, according to SMM statistics, total iron ore inventories at major ports nationwide were 144.92 million mt, down 210,000 mt MoM, with the overall inventory continuing to destock. Over the same period, the daily average port pick-up volume edged down 15,000 mt to 3.205 million mt. Despite a sharp decline in port arrivals, iron ore inventories still saw a slowdown in destocking, reflecting the current weak demand for iron ore. Looking ahead to next week, affected by a new round of environmental protection-driven production restrictions in Tangshan, the decline in hot metal output may widen, and iron ore prices will remain under pressure in the short term. Watch for any bullish policy signals from the Politburo meeting next week.
