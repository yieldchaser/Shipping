# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Today's iron ore market performance on the Dalian Commodity Exchange (DCE) remained weak. The DCE benchmark contract I2609 closed at 698 yuan/ton, down 2.85% from the previous trading session. Qingdao port spot prices fell by 9-12 yuan/ton from the previous day. Traders showed low willingness to quote, while steel mills purchased only for rigid needs, resulting in sluggish overall spot transactions. At present, there is no change in the fundamentals of the iron ore market, and supply is expected to remain relatively loose within the visible timeframe. Last week, SMM reported global iron ore shipments of 32.26 million tonnes, up 16% month-on-month. Meanwhile, iron ore arrivals at Chinese ports totaled 30.29 million tonnes, down slightly from the previous week but up 6% year-on-year on a cumulative basis. On the news front, some traders have recently become entangled in disputes over invoices and trade documents. Market participants expect that banks and trading houses will tighten credit lines and raise margin requirements to reduce risk exposure. As a result, some participants have been forced to sell physical cargo or hedge on the exchange, amplifying the downside movement in the short term. Therefore, iron ore prices are likely to show a weak trend in the near term. COPYRIGHT METALS MARKET
