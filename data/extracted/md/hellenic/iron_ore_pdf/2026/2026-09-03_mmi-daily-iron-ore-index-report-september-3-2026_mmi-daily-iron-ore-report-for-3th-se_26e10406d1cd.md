# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Iron ore futures traded firmer on Wednesday. The DCE most-traded I2701 contract settled at 726.5 yuan/mt, up 1.32% from the previous session. Qingdao port  spot prices rose by an average of 12-14 yuan/mt, with traders showing only moderate enthusiasm in offering and mills buying on an as-needed basis. Overall  spot trading activity was lacklustre. Current market structure points to signs of a structural inflection in iron ore demand, with mills favouring cost-effective fines over higher-premium pellet and  lump. SMM survey data on major port inventories showed fines destocking for a second consecutive week, while destocking in concentrate and lump slowed  and pellet stocks edged higher. Total sample inventories stood at 104.82 million mt, down 20,000 mt week-on-week. Meanwhile, with coke prices continuing to  climb, mills in some regions have begun considering output cuts on tight coke supply and adjusting production schedules accordingly, which could weigh on  near-term iron ore demand. Overall, gains in iron ore prices are likely to slow, with the market shifting into a range-bound pattern in the near term. COPYRIGHT METALS MARKET
