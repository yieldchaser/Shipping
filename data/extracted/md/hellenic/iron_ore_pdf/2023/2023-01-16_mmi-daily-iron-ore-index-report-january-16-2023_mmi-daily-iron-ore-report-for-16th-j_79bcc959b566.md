# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
DCE iron ore futures market falling sharply trends，the main contract closed 832.5，an decrease of 4.31% throughout the day; some traders were active to sell, steel mills purchased on demand. Today the overall market transaction atmosphere is acceptable. PBF at Shandong port deal 835-840 yuan/mt, decrease 30-40 yuan/mt; PBF at Tangshan port dealt 837-840 yuan/mt, decrease20-26 yuan/mt. According to shipping data tracked by SMM, global iron ore shipments totalled 29.45 million tonnes last week, up 1.1% from the previous month. Australia sent 15.22 million tons to China, up 3.6% from the previous month; Brazil shipped 1.34 million tons to China, down 56.8% from the previous month. The total amount of China's iron ore arriving at port was 27.5729 million tons, down 1.27% from the previous month, and the volume of iron ore arriving at port this week was relatively stable. At the demand end, some steel mills resume production plans or delay the Spring Festival, in general, the average daily stick water is still rising slightly. However, considering the steel mill replenishment space narrow, expected this week the shock of the mine price. COPYRIGHT METALS MARKET
