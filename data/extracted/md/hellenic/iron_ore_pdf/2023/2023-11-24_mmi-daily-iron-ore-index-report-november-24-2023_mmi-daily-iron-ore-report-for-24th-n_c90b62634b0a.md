## Desk Commentary
DCE iron ore futures fall today by 0.61%.the main contract I2401 closed 986.5. some traders were active to sell, Some steel mills tended to be wait-and- see, and purchased  on demand. today's overall market transaction atmosphere in general. PBF at Shandong port dealt 993-997 yuan/mt; increased 2 yuan/mt over yesterday. As of November 24th, SMM has tracked a total of 110.49 million tons of inventory in 35 ports, a total of 540000 tons compared to last week and a decrease of 18.94 million tons compared to the same period last year. The daily average port clearance volume of imported mines in this period decreased by 14000 tons to 2.907 million tons on a weekly basis compared to last week. Although entering the off- season, steel mills still maintain a high demand for iron ore to support the dredging volume. But aŌer entering winter, some ports were affected by strong winds and low temperatures, resulting in a slight decrease in port clearance this week. In the future, overseas shipments will remain stable, and according to SMM statistics, the impact of blast furnace maintenance next week will decrease. It is expected that the demand for iron ore will still support the volume of port clearance; Considering that regulatory pressure may continue, it is expected that mining prices will mainly fluctuate in a narrow range.
