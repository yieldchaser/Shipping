# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
DCE iron ore futures rose by 2.39% today, the main contract closed at 963.5. The steel mills are not active to purchase. The overall trading sentiment of the market is general. PBF at Shandong port deal 1010 yuan/mt. PBF at Tangshan port deal 1018 yuan/mt. As of December 8th, SMM has tracked a total of 115.1 million tons of inventory in 35 ports, with a cumulative increase of 3.02 million tons compared to last week. The price of domestic iron ore is high, speculative demand is weak, and steel mills have a poor willingness to purchase goods at high prices. However, the low inventory strategy of the steel mill remains unresolved, and there is an expectation of restocking in the future. From the perspective of iron ore fundamentals, overseas mines will start to surge at the end of the year, and it is diﬃcult for steel mills to see a significant increase in daily iron production. It is expected that inventory at Port 35 will continue to accumulate in the future. Currently, aƩention needs to be paid to the trends of the Politburo and Economic meetings in the later stage, as well as changes in market sentiment. It is expected that mining prices will continue to fluctuate widely. COPYRIGHT METALS MARKET
