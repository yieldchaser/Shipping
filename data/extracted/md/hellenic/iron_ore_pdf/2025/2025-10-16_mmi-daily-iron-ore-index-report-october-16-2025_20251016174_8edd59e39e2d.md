# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Iron ore concentrate prices in the Tangshan area of Hebei have remained relatively stable in recent days, with ex-factory prices for 66% grade iron ore concentrates (dry basis, including tax) at 920-925 yuan/mt. Supply remains tight, as the local governments in Qian'an and Zunhua recently issued written documents regarding land use regulations for iron ore beneficiation enterprises, leading to production suspensions and rectifications at some non-compliant enterprises, along with production restrictions and power rationing. According to SMM tracking, most of the suspended enterprises are beneficiation plants with relatively small capacity, having a limited impact on local iron ore concentrate supply. Demand side, local steel mills are mainly purchasing as needed, with a strong overall desire to bargain down prices. Overall, it is expected that local iron ore concentrate prices will continue to fluctuate at highs in the short term.
