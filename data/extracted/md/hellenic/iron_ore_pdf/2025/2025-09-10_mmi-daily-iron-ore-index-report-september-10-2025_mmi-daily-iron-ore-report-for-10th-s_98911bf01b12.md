## Desk Commentary
Today, the iron ore futures market opened weak but then strengthened, with the most-traded contract IO61 closing at 805, up 0.25% slightly from the previous trading day. Traders sold goods in line with market conditions; steel mills gradually accepted higher prices and purchased as needed. The overall market trading atmosphere was moderate. In the Shandong region, the mainstream transaction prices for PB fines were 795-800 yuan/mt, flat from yesterday's prices; in the Tangshan region, transaction prices for PB fines were around 800-810 yuan/mt, also flat from yesterday.
