# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
DCE iron ore futures were in the doldrums today. The most-traded contract I2601 closed at 790 yuan/mt, down 1.74% WoW. Traders showed moderate willingness to sell, while steel mills' wait-and-see sentiment intensified, with some mills restocking at low levels. Market transaction activity remained moderate. In Shandong, mainstream transaction prices for old PB fines were around 780-785 yuan/mt, down 10-13 yuan/mt from the previous day; in Tangshan, PB fines traded at 790-795 yuan/mt, up 10-15 yuan/mt from the previous day. As of September 26, SMM data showed that iron ore inventories at 35 ports nationwide fell by 540,000 mt WoW, continuing the destocking trend. Meanwhile, daily average port pick-up volume for imported ore increased by 102,000 mt WoW to 3.215 million mt, indicating that steel mills accelerated restocking ahead of the National Day holiday. However, as the holiday approaches, restocking by mills has largely been completed, reducing support for ore prices. Coupled with rising risk-averse sentiment in the market ahead of the holiday, iron ore prices plunged in the afternoon session. Overall, iron ore prices are expected to fluctuate rangebound next week.
