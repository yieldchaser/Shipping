# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Today, DCE iron ore continued to weake trend today, with the most-traded contract I2601 closing at 788.5 yuan, down 0.32% from the previous trading day. Traders demonstrated a moderate willingness to sell, while steel mills purchased as needed. Overall market trading sentiment remained subdued. In Shandong, transaction prices for PB fines ranged between 785-790 yuan, down 0-2 yuan/mt from yesterday's levels; in Hebei, transaction prices for 60.8% PB fines stood at 793-797 yuan/mt, down 2-3 yuan/mt from the previous day. Due to extended maintenance at steel mills, total production of major steel products remained high this week. Although apparent consumption saw a slight rebound and port inventories edged lower, current steel inventory levels remained elevated on a YoY basis, leading to widespread pessimism in the market regarding future inventory pressure on steel mills. Against this backdrop, iron ore prices remained under pressure and traded in the doldrums. However, as hot metal production did not decline as expected, actual demand continued to provide some support to ore prices, limiting further downside room. Looking ahead, given expectations of a weakening fundamental outlook for iron ore, ore prices are expected to remain under pressure and exhibit a fluctuating trend. COPYRIGHT METALS MARKET
