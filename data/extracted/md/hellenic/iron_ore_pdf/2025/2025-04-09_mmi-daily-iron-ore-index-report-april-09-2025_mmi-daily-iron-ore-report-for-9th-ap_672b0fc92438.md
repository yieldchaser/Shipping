Today, The DCE iron ore futures opened lower and then rebounded slightly. The most-traded contract i2509 finally closed at 689, with a daily decline of 2.68%. Traders showed poor willingness to sell at low prices; steel mills cautiously waited and watched, with weak inquiries; market trading volume significantly decreased compared to the previous two days. In Shandong, the mainstream transaction price of PB fines was around 741 yuan/mt, down 4 yuan/mt from yesterday; in Tangshan, the transaction price of PB fines was near 750-755 yuan/mt, down 0-5 yuan/mt from yesterday.
