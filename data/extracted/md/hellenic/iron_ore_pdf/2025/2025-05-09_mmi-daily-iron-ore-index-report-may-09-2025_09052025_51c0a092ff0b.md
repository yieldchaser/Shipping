## Desk Commentary
Today, the DCE Iron Ore futures fluctuated rangebound during the day, with the most-traded contract i2509 closing at 696, down 0.57% for the day. Some merchants showed moderate enthusiasm for shipping goods. As the weekend approached, the purchase willingness of steel mills weakened somewhat, with some steel mills conducting tenders based on demand. The market transaction atmosphere was average. In the Shandong region, the mainstream transaction prices of PB fines were around 753-757 yuan/mt, up 2 yuan/mt from yesterday's low. In the Tangshan region, the transaction prices of PB fines were around 760-765 yuan/mt, stable compared to yesterday's prices. The daily average port pick-up volume of imported ore was 3.019 million mt, a decrease of 129,000 mt from before the holiday.
