Today, DCE iron ore futures continued to decline, with the most-traded contract I2509 closing at 706, down 2.21% for the day. Traders' willingness to sell was average; steel mills adopted a wait-and-see attitude, with relatively low procurement enthusiasm. The market transaction atmosphere was average. In Shandong, the mainstream transaction prices of PB fines were in the range of 740-745 yuan/mt, down 10-15 yuan/mt from Friday last week.
