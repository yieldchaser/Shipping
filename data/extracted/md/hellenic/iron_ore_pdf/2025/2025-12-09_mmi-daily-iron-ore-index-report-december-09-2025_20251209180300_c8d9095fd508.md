# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Today, iron ore continued its weak and fluctuating trend. The main contract I2605 closed at 757.5, down 0.72% compared to the previous trading day. Traders sold goods  in line with market conditions, while steel mills made purchases based on demand, with a relatively positive procurement sentiment. The overall market trading  atmosphere was moderate. In the Shandong region, the transaction price for PB fines ranged from 778 to 783 yuan per ton, down 0-3 yuan per ton compared to  yesterday. In the Hebei region, the transaction price for PB fines ranged from 785 to 790 yuan per ton, also down 0-3 yuan per ton compared to yesterday. According to SMM statistics, during this week (December 6 to December 12), the impact of blast furnace maintenance on hot metal production was 1.666 million tons,  an increase of 121,000 tons compared to last week's maintenance impact. Next week (December 13 to December 19), the impact of blast furnace maintenance on hot  metal production is projected to be 1.7156 million tons, an increase of 49,600 tons compared to this week. Hot metal production is expected to decline to its lowest  point this month, further weakening the fundamentals and putting pressure on iron ore prices. Tomorrow, the Federal Reserve's interest rate decision is set to be  announced, which may cause some disruption to iron ore prices. However, in the short term, iron ore prices are likely to remain in a narrow range with limited  fluctuations. COPYRIGHT METALS MARKET
