# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
Today, the DCE iron prices fluctuated within a narrow range, with the main contract I2605 closing at 760.5, down 0.33% compared to the previous trading day. Traders adjusted their sales strategies based on market conditions. As the week approached Friday, steel mills showed limited purchasing interest, with some lowering prices to procure according to their immediate needs. Overall market sentiment remained subdued. In Shandong, the transaction price for PB fines ranged from 778 to 782 yuan per ton, up 0-3 yuan from the previous day. In Hebei, the transaction price for PB fines ranged from 792 to 800 yuan per ton, also up 0-3 yuan from the previous day.

The new port storage fee regulations introduced today sparked market discussions. If implemented, these rules are expected to significantly impact some foreign-funded enterprises and non-mainstream traders, potentially triggering short-term selling pressure and a pessimistic market sentiment. Considering the macroeconomic narrative has largely concluded, iron ore prices have returned to fundamentals. However, with fundamentals remaining relatively weak, it is anticipated that ore prices will continue to exhibit weak and volatile trends in the coming week.
