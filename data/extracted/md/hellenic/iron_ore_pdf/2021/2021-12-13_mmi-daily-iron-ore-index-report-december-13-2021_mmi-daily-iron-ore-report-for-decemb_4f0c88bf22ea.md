## Desk Commentary
DCE iron ore futures rose largely today, and the main contract closed 668.5, increased more than 5% for the day, the quotation in the morning was increased by 10-20 yuan/mt from last Friday. Most traders tended to negotiate on the price separately, and some steel mills purchased on demand. Today the overall market transaction atmosphere is acceptable. PBF at Shandong port dealt 710-725yuan/mt, increased 15-25 yuan/mt from last friday;SSF at Shandong port dealt 465-470, increased 10-30 yuan/mt from last friday; PBF at Tangshan port dealt 720-730 yuan/mt; increased 25-35 yuan/mt from last Friday.
