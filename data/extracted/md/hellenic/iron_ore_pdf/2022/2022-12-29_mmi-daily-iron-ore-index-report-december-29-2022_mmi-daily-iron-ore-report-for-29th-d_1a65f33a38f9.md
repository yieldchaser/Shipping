## Desk Commentary
DCE iron ore futures rose by 1.56% today, the main contract closed 845. The traders' willingness to ship is general.The steel mills are wait and see.The overall trading sentiment of the market is general.PBF at Shandong port dealt 830-837 yuan/mt,increase 2-14yuan/mt; PBF at Tangshan port dealt 842-845 yuan/mt,increase 4-10 yuan/mt. According to the inventory data of the top ten ports tracked by SMM, the total inventory of the top ten ports fell by 1% month on month to 89.25 million tons this week. In terms of varieties, the crude powder inventory declined significantly, and the refined powder, lump ore and pellets were accumulated to varying degrees. The pre holiday replenishment is still in progress, and the demand for iron ore remains high. In terms of market funds, there is still a good expectation for the first quarter of 2023. It is expected that iron ore prices will mainly fluctuate before New Year's Day. COPYRIGHT METALS MARKET
