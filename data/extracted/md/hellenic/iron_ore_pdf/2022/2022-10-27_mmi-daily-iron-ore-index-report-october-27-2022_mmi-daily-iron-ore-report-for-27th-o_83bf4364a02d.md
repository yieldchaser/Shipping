# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
DCE iron ore futures declined by 4.1% today, the main contract closed 643.5. The traders' willingness to ship is general . The steel mills are purchase on demand. The overall trading sentiment of the market is less. PBF at Shandong port dealt 695-696 yuan/mt,increase 10-20yuan/mt. PBF at Tangshan port dealt 695-700 yuan/mt. According to the inventory data of the top ten ports calculated by SMM, the iron ore inventory of the top ten ports in China decreased slightly by 1.1% this week to 87.55 million tons, of which fine powder and pellets were accumulated slightly, and lump ore was removed most significantly. As the heating season is approaching, steel mills and traders should prepare their warehouses in advance to cope with the upcoming environmental restrictions. The decline in inventory this week is mainly due to the reduction in the number of arrivals. However, the terminal demand in the later period is light and the steel plant maintenance may lead to the decline of molten iron production. The port inventory has a certain risk of accumulation. It is expected that iron ore prices will remain weak and volatile in the short term. COPYRIGHT METALS MARKET
