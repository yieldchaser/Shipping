# Benchmark Price Indices & Domestic Concentrates

## Desk Commentary
DCE iron ore futures rose by 2.8% today, the main contract closed 863. The traders' willingness to ship is general. The steel mills are wait and see. The overall trading sentiment of the market is general. PBF at Shandong port deal 847-855 yuan/mt, increase 25 yuan/mt, PBF at Tangshan port deal 854-870 yuan/mt, increase 15-18 yuan/mt. As of December 30, the inventory of 35 ports tracked by SMM totaled 128.47 million tons, down 1.54 million tons from last week and 23.34 million tons from the same period last year. The average daily port dredging volume of imported ore in this period increased by 30000 tons to 2.957 million tons month on month. This week, the profits of the steel plant were repaired slightly, and some steel plants' blast furnaces were prepared to resume production, which, combined with the small replenishment of warehouses in the early New Year's Day, made the port more active in dredging. The new year has a "good start". It is expected that in January 2023, molten iron production will increase significantly, supporting iron ore demand again. At present, the price of raw materials continues to rise, and the steel mills may jointly expect the coke purchase price to rise and fall, so as to repair profits and improve
