## Desk Commentary
DCE iron ore futures rose by 2.28 % today, the main contract closed 629. The traders' willingness to ship is more. The steel mills are purchase on demand. The overall trading sentiment of the market is general. PBF at Shandong port dealt 650 yuan/mt, increase 2-10 yuan/mt. PBF at Tangshan port dealt 650 yuan/mt, decrease 5-7 yuan/mt. Recently, news about the maintenance of the blast furnace has been reported frequently.
