## Desk Commentary
DCE iron ore futures rose by 2.37% today, the main contract closed 755.5. The traders' willingness to ship is general .The steel mills are purchase on demand. The overall trading sentiment of the market is general. PBF at Shandong port dealt 745-750 yuan/mt,increase 13-18yuan/ mt;SSF at Shandong port dealt 610 yuan/mt .PBF at Tangshan port dealt 762 yuan/mt,increase 17 yuan/mt. According to SMM tracking, global iron ore shipments totaled 32.07 million tons last week, up 3.5% month on month. Among them, 14.63 million tons were shipped from Australia to China, an increase of 0.5% month on month; Brazil sent 2.61 million tons to China, a quarter on quarter decrease. The total amount of iron ore in China reached 26.94 million tons, up 3.8% month on month. At present, overseas supply is still in a loose cycle, and it is expected that Australia Pakistan shipment will continue to increase. On the demand side, some blast furnaces may resume production this week, leading to a small recovery in hot metal output. On the whole, there is liƩle contradiction in the fundamentals.
