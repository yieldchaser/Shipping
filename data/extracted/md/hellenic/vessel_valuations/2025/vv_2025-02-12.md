## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Mid age values down slightly on last week, whilst newbuild values for larger tonnage firmed

### Tanker
Tanker values have remained stable however S&P activity is under pressure with a number deals failing due to uncertainty surrounding buyers

### Container
Midage Sub Panamax are up this week following recent sales, modern Feedermaxes are down.
