## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker Values have firmed for larger tonnage in the last week owing to sales of vessels such as Frontier Garland

### Tanker
Older tonnage Suezmax VV values firming slightly owing to a recent en bloc deal while LR1 and MR values have declined across the board

### Container
Values for older Sub Panamax vessels are continuing down, with smaller more modern vessels rising slightly
