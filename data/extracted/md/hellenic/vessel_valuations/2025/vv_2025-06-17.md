## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker values have remained stable this week, with a small decrease in younger Supramxesdue to sales such as CMB Rubens

### Tanker
Tanker values have fluctuated this week owing to some recent secondhand deals and orders. S&P markets were once again focused on the MR segment, with values and earnings largely holding steady after consistent downward pressure in recent weeks.

### Container
Modern Sub Panamax vessels are nudging up and older ones down following recent sales, older Post Panamaxes continue to make small gains
