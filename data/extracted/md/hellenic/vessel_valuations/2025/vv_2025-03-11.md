## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker Values firming slightly further this week for larger tonnage,whilst older smaller tonnage values have decreased owing to sales of vessels such as Iyo Sea.

### Tanker
Fluctuation observed for VV tanker values last week mainly owing to a number of recent sales with smaller, older tonnage seeing a significant decrease

### Container
Stability reigns for most sectors this week, smaller New Panamaxes are up slightly whilst ULCV NBs are nudging down
