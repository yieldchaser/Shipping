## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker values have remained stable for larger tonnage this week, with a decrease in smaller tonnageSupramaxes and Handysize due to sales such as Pythias and NY Trader III

### Tanker
Tanker S&P activity has quietened this week as the recent ceasefire has led to less risk in the market, buyers are being more careful and making fewer speculative deals

### Container
Second-hand container values remain stable this week
