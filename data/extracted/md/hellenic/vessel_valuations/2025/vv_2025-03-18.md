## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker Values firming across the board this week, owing to improved freight ratesand sales of vessels such as Kmarin Oslo and Achilles Bulker

### Tanker
Tanker values have remained stable on a negative trend over the last week with a number of smaller, clean vessels sold to unknown Chinese buyers.

### Container
Stability continues for the larger sectors this week, whilst mid-age Panamaxes increased and smaller vessels crept up too.
