## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
VV Bulker values have mostly increased this week. With mid aged Capesize, Panamaxes and olderSupramax vessels all seeing an increase due to sales such as Mineral Cloudbreak

### Tanker
S&P activity remains high across many of the tanker sectors, with values for most sectors on the rise.

### Container
Second-hand values remain stable this week, the only notable change being in modern Handy and Sub Panamax Containers.
