## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker values have remained stable this week, with a small decrease in older tonnageHandysize due to sales such as UBC Tokyo

### Tanker
Tanker values have remained stable across the board this week as S&P activity is low except for a number of en bloc MR deals that have recently been confirmed

### Container
Older Post Panamaxes continue to rise this week, and midage Panamaxes are following suit on the back of recent sales
