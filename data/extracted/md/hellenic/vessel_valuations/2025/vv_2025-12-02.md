## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Second-hand values largely firmed in the bulker sector, with mid-age Panamax and Handysize bulk carriersshowing substantial gains. Supramax values dipped slightly, following a week of softer sale prices

### Tanker
VV MR Tanker values have firmed this week especially as S&P activity has increased including a number of MR2 en bloc deals

### Container
Older Feedermax values are up this week, alongside Handy containers as second-hand demand for smaller vessels remains strong
