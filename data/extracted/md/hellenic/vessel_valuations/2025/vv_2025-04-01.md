## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker Values have firmed this week, with Midage Handysizes seeing the most significate increase again this weekowing to sales of vessels such as Atlantic Brave and Nimble Nicky.

### Tanker
Tanker values have remained mostly stable over the last week with older tonnage Suezmax and Aframax values firming owing to recent deals

### Container
Older Panamaxes are continuing up this week, smaller second-hand vessels nudging up too
