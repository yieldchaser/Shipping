## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker values have remained stable this week, with a decrease in mid age Panamaxes and Supramaxesdue to sales such as Baby Cassiopeia and Star Runner

### Tanker
S&P activity has increased in the last week as we have seen deals confirmed from all Tanker sectors with values remaining stable for the most part. Older tonnage VLCCs are achieving lower prices and there is a clear discrepancy between age of vessel and sale price achieved as seen from the recent sales of Papalemos and C Spirit

### Container
Recent sales are nudging down older Handysize and Sub Panamax vessels this week and bolstering older Feedermaxes
