## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker values have remained stable this week, with a small decrease in older tonnage Capesizesdue to sales such as Tradership and Thalassini Agatha.

### Tanker
Older and smaller tonnage Tanker values have softened over the last week owing to recent deals while we mainly see stability for large tonnage values

### Container
Older Feedermaxes are continuing to firm whilst modern vessels remain fairly stable
