## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker values have remained mostly stable this week, with an increase in older smallertonnage due to sales such as Rijn Confidence and Jin Gang

### Tanker
Tanker values remained stable for the larger tonnage with some downward movements for Suezmax and LR1's coinciding with a drop for Suezmax rates

### Container
Post panamax midage values down following a recent sale, and handy container values up in older vessels. Other sectors remain stable with limited sales candidates this week
