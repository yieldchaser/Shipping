Bulker Values have firmed slightly this week, owing to sales of vessels such as Sea Charm,Sea Marathon and 134 (Hull) Nantong Xiangyu

Tanker values have remained mostly stable over the last week with older tonnage Suezmax and Aframax values firming further owing to recent deals

### Container
