## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
VV Bulker values have mostly remained stable this week. With Capesizes and Supramaxesboth seeing an increase due to sales such as Mineral Shougang and Forever SW

### Tanker
S&P activity has slowed this week and so the majority of VV Tanker values remain stable. The market seems cautious with buyers being selective and owners holding back.

### Container
Midage and older Handysize and Sub Panamax values on the rise this week, alongside an increase in newbuilding values for the same. The rest of the sector holding steady.
