## Bulkers

Bulker secondhand activity remained active on a week-on-week basis, though the broader Marchpicture tells a more cautious story. Sales are down 68% against the same month last year, as ongoing Middle East uncertainty has prompted many owners to adopt a wait-and-see approach. Values remained relatively stable across the Bulker sector. Exceptions to this included modern Panamax BCs, where values softened somewhat, and midage Supramaxes, where values firmed in response to strong pricing.

The smaller segments have dominated sales and orders this week. With rates remaining stable, there is little impetus to sell, and older handy container values continue to benefit. Newbuilding values for the same have eased slightly following recent orders.
