## Tankers

Tanker values firmed across the board this week, with owner appetite for modern tonnage continuing to build against the backdrop of ongoing Strait of Hormuz disruption. Evidence of persistently elevated war-risk premiums comes in the form of the recently reported Pinios resale for USD 200m, basis prompt Middle East delivery through the Strait.
