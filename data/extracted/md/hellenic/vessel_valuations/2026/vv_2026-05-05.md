## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Older Panamaxes have come up this week following a flurry of recently sold vessels.

### Tanker
8-year-old Aframaxes are currently selling at parity with newbuild values, driven by its versatility and sustained demand across the Tanker sector.

### Container
Container values have come up almost across the board this week, the only exception being older Feedermaxes which have come down slightly following recent sales. A steady stream of newbuilding orders has pushed values up across a variety of sizes and sectors.
