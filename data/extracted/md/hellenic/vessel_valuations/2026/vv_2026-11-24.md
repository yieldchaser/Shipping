## Tankers

Crude tanker values edged lower as spot earnings corrected from October’s exceptional peak, with sentiment shaped by hopes of de-escalation in the Middle East and Ukraine. Attacks on shipping in the Gulf, Red Sea and Black Sea nevertheless continue, keeping these regions high-risk, and Brent remains well below October’s average as Hormuz flows recover.
