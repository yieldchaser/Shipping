# Weekly Vessel Valuations Report, March 24 2026

## Bulkers

It was a mixed week for the Bulker market, with the larger vessel sizes improving while the smaller sizes softened.The Bulker freight markets have remained relatively unaffected by the conflict in the Middle East. However, recent attacks affecting global gas supply, raised concerns about an economic slowdown. This uncertainty spilled over into the Bulker FFA markets, where expectations for 2026 softened.

## Tankers

Large Tanker values continued to firm. The acquisition of Suezmax Silverway (157,800 DWT, Jan 2017, Sungdong) by MSC appears to signal growing appetite for the sector, with rumours emerging that the Owner is actively seeking additional Suezmax vessels, pointing to the early stages of a potential buying spree
