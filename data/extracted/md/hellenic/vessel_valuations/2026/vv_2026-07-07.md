## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Midage Supramax values dipped in response to soft pricing for the Livita,while older tonnage firmed in response to the sale of the VW Trust to Chinese buyers.

### Tanker
VV S&P values were broadly stable on a one-week basis, with MR values ticking up modestly across the board. Sentiment continues to be supported by easing Middle East tensions and continued growth in U.S. crude exports

### Container
A stable story across container values this week with little movement across most sectors, older Feedermaxes continue their upward trend. Port congestion and no notable changes in the Strait of Hormuz keeping vessel utilisation high.
