## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker sale and purchase activity was healthy this week, with ten vessels transacting across the Kamsarmax,Panamax, Supramax, and Handysize segments, reflecting continued buyer appetite across the size spectrum. Evidence of soft pricing among mid-age and vintage Panamax and Handysize vessels resulted in dips to our values.

### Tanker
Despite the fluctuation in earnings this week, Tanker values held their firming trend across all segments. S&P activity has slowed down in the past number of weeks with only midsize/larger tonnage reported this week.

### Container
Second-hand values remain stable this week across all size segments. Handy container newbuilding values have picked up a little this week after a couple of new orders have been announced.
