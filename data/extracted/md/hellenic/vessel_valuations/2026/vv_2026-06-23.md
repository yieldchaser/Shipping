## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
VV Bulker values remained mostly stable across all bulker sectors. Newcastlemax and Capesize values drifted marginallylower while Handysize saw positive movement especially for resales.

### Tanker
VV S&P values were broadly stable on a one-week basis. Modern Aframax values saw slight softening while older tonnage continues to appreciate.

### Container
No new sales this week, but demand remains high, with values still coming up for older Feedermaxes.
