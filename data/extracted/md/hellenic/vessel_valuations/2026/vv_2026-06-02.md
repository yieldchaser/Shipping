## Tankers

The secondhand Tanker market remained subdued with downward pressure on value movements. VLCC transactions were particularly subdued, totalling just USD 664.3 mil across nine vessels over the past three months. Torm was the standout buyer, acquiring 10 vessels for approximately USD 420.0 mil, primarily from Singaporean sellers. Scorpio Tankers remained the dominant seller, disposing of 12 vessels totalling USD 543.6 mil.
