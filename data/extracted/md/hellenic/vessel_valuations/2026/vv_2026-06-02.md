## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker S&P activity was solid, with 16 vessels changing hands across all segments.Broader market sentiment remains underpinned by cautious optimism following the US-China tariff truce, which has improved near-term trade flow expectations for dry bulk commodities. Strong pricing was observed across vintage tonnage, leading to our values for older vessels firming.

### Tanker
The secondhand Tanker market remained subdued with downward pressure on value movements. VLCC transactions were particularly subdued, totalling just USD 664.3 mil across nine vessels over the past three months. Torm was the standout buyer, acquiring 10 vessels for approximately USD 420.0 mil, primarily from Singaporean sellers. Scorpio Tankers remained the dominant seller, disposing of 12 vessels totalling USD 543.6 mil.

### Container
Sales in the Handysize sector have supported firming across all ages.
