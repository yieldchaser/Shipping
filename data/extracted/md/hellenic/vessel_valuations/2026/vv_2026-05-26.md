## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker S&P activity was strong this week, with 18 sales reported across all major size segments.The standout deal was an en bloc transaction involving four 2024-built Handysize vessels; Clacton, Margate, Eastbourne and Portsmouth sold by Bank of Communications to Norden at USD 30.5 mil each. Values firmed in response to strong pricing, observed among both new and vintage tonnage

### Tanker
The secondhand Tanker market was quiet this week against a backdrop of broader sector-wide slowdown

### Container
With no new second-hand sales coming to light this week, values remain stable across most sectors and ages
