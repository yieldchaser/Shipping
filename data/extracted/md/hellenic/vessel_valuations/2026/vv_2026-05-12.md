## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
The secondhand Bulker market remained active this week. Modest value increases occurred across Bulker segments,as sales revealed more evidence of strong pricing in the sector

### Tanker
The secondhand market was active in the Handy Tanker sector this week, where lower sale prices and softening earnings have shifted values down for older tonnage. In contrast, older Suezmax values continued their upward trajectory

### Container
Container values remain stable this week, the only exception being older Feedermaxes which continue to come down
