## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker secondhand activity remained busy this week, with transactions spanning Panamax to Newcastlemaxtonnage and Chinese buyers featuring prominently across multiple segments. Resale appetite also remained evident, with two 64,100 DWT Ultramax newbuilds from New Dayang Shipbuilding sold to Niovis Shipping, though soft pricing resulted in a dip to our Ultramax resale values.

### Tanker
The secondhand tanker market is showing broad strength this week, with values firming across mid-size and older tonnage in particular.

### Container
Second-hand values have remained flat this week as no new sales have come to light
