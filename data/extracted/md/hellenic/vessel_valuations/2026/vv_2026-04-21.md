## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker secondhand market activity remained healthy this week, with transactions recorded across a spread of sizes.Appetite from Chinese buyers dominated the week's activity, with Supramaxes proving particularly liquid. Despite uncertainty surrounding the Strait of Hormuz, investor appetite for quality secondhand tonnage has shown little sign of cooling. Values were mixed; firmer pricing drove an upward revision in the Panamax and Handysize sectors, while mid-age Supramax values softened in line with evidence of weaker pricing.

### Tanker
VV Tanker values remained broadly stable this week amid a slowdown in S&P activity. Older LR1 tonnage continued to firm following a recent en bloc deal.

### Container
Majority of container values remained stable this week, some small adjustments for handy containers following the most recent orders.
