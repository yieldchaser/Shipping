# Weekly Vessel Valuations Report, April 21 2026

## Bulkers

Bulker secondhand market activity remained healthy this week, with transactions recorded across a spread of sizes.Appetite from Chinese buyers dominated the week’s activity, with Supramaxes proving particularly liquid. Despite uncertainty surrounding the Strait of Hormuz, investor appetite for quality secondhand tonnage has shown little sign of cooling.
