## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker values have held largely steady this week, with modest gains seen in older Capesize and Panamax vessels.Sustained buyer appetite across segments continues to underpin a well-balanced sale and purchase market.

### Tanker
VV Tanker values firmed again this week for mid age tonnage. Secondhand activity was slower this week and mainly dominated by the handy sector

### Container
Values remain stable across the board this week. Minor increases in Feedermax resales and a slight drop in older Panamax values
