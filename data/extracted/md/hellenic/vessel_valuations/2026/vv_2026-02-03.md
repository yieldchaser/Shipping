## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
The market showed continued appetite for modern tonnage, with Greek and Chinese buyersbeing particularly active

### Tanker
VV values for mid-age and older product Tankers have firmed this week following an influx of secondhand deals. The VLCC S&P market stays active with another recorded Sinokor deal

### Container
Second-hand Handy Containers have come up across the board following some recent sales of modern and older tonnage. Post Panamax resales are down this week following a recent en bloc sale and values across other sectors hold steady
