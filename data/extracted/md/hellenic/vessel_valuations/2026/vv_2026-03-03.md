## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Evidence of very strong pricing for modern Panamax tonnage, including the sale of the Three Saskias for USD 27.1 mil,has led to a substantial firming of values. Modern Supramax tonnage has also continued to show strong pricing. Softer pricing for Capesize newbuild orders and resales contributed to a dip in our values.

### Tanker
Secondhand activity ramped up this week across all sectors as we see values continuing to firm, further inflated by the recent boost in earnings

### Container
Midage Handy Containers are up a little this week following a couple of recent sales in the sector. Modern New Panamax values have fallen slightly
