## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Bulker secondhand activity this week was dominated by Chinese buyers. The concentration of Chinese buyinginterest across multiple size segments points to sustained regional demand. Bulker values remained stable this week.

### Tanker
Values firmed across crude sectors this week, with older tonnage Aframax Tankers leading gains despite the recent decline in earnings. A Hormuz resolution is expected to sharpen appetite for modern tonnage, with fleet renewal demand set to accelerate as sanctioned and ageing vessels are phased out.

### Container
A few more sales have come to light this week, but with only a small number to choose from buyers are taking vessels with TCs and for forward delivery to ensure they have the capacity they need.
