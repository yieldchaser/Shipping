## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Value movements were subdued this week, as achieved prices held broadly steady,with the exception of a slight correction in mid-age Supramax vessels.

### Tanker
The Tanker S&P market was quiet this week, with mid-age MR values continuing to correct as a result of previous sales.

### Container
Feedermax values continued to move this week with two notable sales, seemingly the only segment where charter-free tonnage is still available.
