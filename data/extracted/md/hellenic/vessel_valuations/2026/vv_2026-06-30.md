## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
Panamax values rose in response to high prices achieved by last week's Mont Blanc Hawk and Marinicki C sales.Supramax values dipped in response to the soft pricing of the Ocean Bright.

### Tanker
VV S&P values saw positive movement across older tonnage this week, with older VLCCs, Suezmaxes and MR2s all appreciating. Older MR2 values being the standout performer. Asset values for older VLCCs remain well supported, with the Lila Kochi sale landing against a backdrop of tight fleet supply and healthy tanker earnings.

### Container
The S&P market remains quiet and demand remains high, values are still nudging up for older Feedermaxes but no notable movements across any other sectors.
