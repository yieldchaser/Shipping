## Sector Market Commentary

### Tanker
Tanker values remaining stable last week with older tonnage LR1s seeing an increase due to recent sales while older tonnage VLCCs see a decline in values

### Container
Slight shifts for Container values this week, Feedermax newbuilds are still on the increase and mid-age Handys and Sub Panamax have come down
