## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
S&P Values increased for Panamax vessels, most notably for older tonnage owing to sale of vessels such as Bravery

### Tanker
Tanker values mainly stable across the board with older tonnage MRs firming once again

### Container
Secondhand values firmed this week for most types and ages, the exception being mid-age Handy and Sub Panamax vessels declining c.6%
