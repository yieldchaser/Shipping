## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
S&P Values Bulker values increased for Capesize and Panamax vessels owing to sales of vessels such as Courageous and Livia Rose

### Tanker
Tanker values remained mostly stable apart from a decrease for older tonnage VLCC, owing to the sale of C Prosperity

### Container
Secondhand values continued to spike from firmed earnings, with impressive gains for 15YO Panamax in response to sales
