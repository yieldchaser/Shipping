## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
S&P Values for mid-age Ultramax and Supramax increased last week owing to sales such as Florentine Oetker

### Tanker
Tanker values remained mainly stable with older tonnage LR1s firming

### Container
Container values firmed across the board from improved earnings, except for vintage Feedermax that lost up to 1.2%
