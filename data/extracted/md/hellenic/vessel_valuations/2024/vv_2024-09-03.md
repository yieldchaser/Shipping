## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
S&P Values for older Capesize increase owing to sales such as Pontotrition and modern Panamax,older Surpamax and Handysize bulkers have all seen values decrease

### Tanker
Tanker values remained stable across the board last week

### Container
Market values for Post Panamax and Panamax marginally depreciated across all ages whilst a minor uptick was observed for Feedermax from resale to twenty years of age.
