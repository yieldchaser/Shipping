## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
S&P Values remain mostly stable aside from increase for mid-age handy owing to sales of vessels such as Cielo Di Tampa

### Tanker
Last week saw softening of older tonnage VLCC values owing to the sales of Apollo Harmony and PNS Serena

### Container
Container values remain stable, small gains for Feedermaxes and the larger segments, whereas Handy and Sub Panamax values soften slightly this week
