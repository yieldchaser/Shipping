## Sector Market Commentary

### Bulker
S&P Values remain mostly stable aside from increase for older Supramax owing to sales of vessels such as Suzaku

### Tanker
Further softening of older tonnage Aframax and LR2 values as earnings continue to decline

### Container
Marginal gains for Panamax and larger segments across all ages. However, values softened for Sub Panamax and Handy's in response to recent sales and softer earnings
