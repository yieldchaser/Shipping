### Bulker
S&P Values Bulker values increased for Supramax and Ultramax vessels owing to sales of vessels such as Beate Oldendorff and Velvet

### Tanker
Tanker values remained mainly stable across the board again despite the observed fluctuations in earnings

### Container
Secondhand values appreciated across the board, with impressive gains for 15YO Panamax in response to sales
