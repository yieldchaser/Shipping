# Clarksons Weekly Sale & Purchase Report - 2026-09-25

**Publisher**: Clarkson Hellas Ltd. | **Issue Date**: 2026-09-25 (Week 39)

## Desk Commentary

### Tankers & Dry Cargo Desk Talk

Tanker rates have hit record highs this week. VLCC earnings are now averaging over USD 600,000 p/d globally, with rates near Hormuz reaching as high as USD 800,000-1.5 million p/d. This is being driven by a surge in oil shuttled through the Gulf of Oman, which has effectively tripled ton-mile demand for crude carriers. At the same time, the broader geopolitical situation and port delays in the East have left the market critically undersupplied with modern tonnage.

On the dry bulk side, activity remains steady with Capesize earnings continuing to show resilience above USD 50,000 p/d, driven by strong Pacific mineral flows and steady Brazilian exports. S&P transactions across both sectors reflect significant premium valuations for modern, scrubber-fitted eco units.
