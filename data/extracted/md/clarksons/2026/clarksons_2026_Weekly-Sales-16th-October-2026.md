# Desk Talk

This week, S&P activity has maintained the same healthy momentum across all segments which has been enduring for the past months. On dry, rates have been softer, showcasing a 43% lower m/m end of the week for the Capes at 29,500/day, a decline which is attributed to the reduced Chinese steel import demand, as mills are faced with thin margins and iron ore port inventories remain elevated. At the same time, data suggests congestion at Chinese discharge ports has been reduced, resulting in greater supply of tonnage. Despite the softening of the dry market, buying appetite continues: Cape and Mini-Cape present high activity sectors with attention being concentrated mainly on 10 -15-year-old units, as well as on modern eco M/E vessels, with prompt delivery being favored. In the non-geared segment, we are seeing notable interest in Ultramax tonnage, particularly modern Japanese-built vessels, as well as non-eco Handysize and Supramax units.

In the tanker space, there has been considerable discussion surrounding the possibility that US may lift certain sanctions in an effort to counteract the much-increased price of tanker freight. It is still ambiguous how this could be effectuated and to what degree the sanctioned fleet would be allowed to re-enter the mainstream market. At the moment, an estimated 168 of the 934 VLCCs in the global fleet are subject to US OFAC sanctions, the majority of which are linked to the illicit transportation of Iranian oil. Given the ongoing attacks in the Strait, a lifting of these sanctions appears more unlikely than that of vessels sanctioned for ties with Russian exports, amid the recent US-Russia oil deal reports. For another consecutive week, we are monitoring heightened activity on all tanker segments, with cleanproduct trading LR and MR tonnage being at the forefront of attention.

## Dry Cargo

In the Supramax sector, the Chinese built **EVER PROGRESS** (ABT 56,592 DWT, 2012, JIANGSU HANTONG HI, C:4X36T) was sold via auction at USD 16.24, while the **JIN XING** (ABT 55,496 DWT, 2007, OSHIMA SHIPBUILDING, C:4X30T) was acquired by Chinese interests at USD 14.6m.

Moreover, in the Handysize space, following an invitation for bids last week, the **ASL CRYSTAL** (ABT 38,582 DWT, 2019, TSUNEISHI CEBU, C:4X30T) has been concluded to clients of Thoresen Thai at USD 27.7m. Additionally, **STELLAR ISABELA** (ABT 37,993 DWT, 2017, SHIMANAMI SHIPYARD, C:4X31T) was committed at excess USD 24m, basis prompt delivery in the Atlantic. Finally, **ZBB SERENITY** (ABT 37,302 DWT, 2010, HUATAI HVY. IND., C:4X30T) was sold at a price in the region of USD 12m.

## Tanker

In the MR2 segment, the **PRIVE ANGEL** (ABT 51,246 DWT, 2008, STX SB (JINHAE)) was committed to U/D interests at USD 24.6m.
