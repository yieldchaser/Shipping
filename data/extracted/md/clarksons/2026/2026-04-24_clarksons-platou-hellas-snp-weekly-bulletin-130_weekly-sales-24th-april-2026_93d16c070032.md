## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

#### Tankers

This week, tankers received attention across all segments. On the larger sizes, increased interest has been observed in the Suezmax space, while notable appetite for approximately 10–15-year-old Aframaxes and MR2s has also been monitored.

#### Dry Cargo

The Dry Bulk sector has maintained its healthy trend as well, with modern ECO Ultramaxes being in the forefront, along with notable transactions taking place in the Post-Panamax and Supramax spaces. Meanwhile we observe numerous enquiries for vintage Panamax vessels in the market.

### Dry Cargo S&P

In the Post-Panamax segment, after inviting offers earlier this week, the CORONA BRAVE (ABT 88K DWT, 2006, IMABARI) was acquired by Chinese interests for USD 12.9m. Furthermore, in the Ultramax space, c/o United Marine Egypt have sold 2 x Newbuildings 2027 ex-New Dayang namely the N/B 'NDY1315/1316' (ABT 64K DWT, 2027, NEW DAYANG) for an enbloc price in the region of USD 73m to Greek interests. Finally, the Supramax CHAKRAVATI (ABT 55K DWT, 2006, KAWASAKI) was sold at mid-USD 10m, basis surveys promptly due.
