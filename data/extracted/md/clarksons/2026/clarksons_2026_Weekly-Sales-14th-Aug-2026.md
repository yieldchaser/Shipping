# Desk Talk

Even with the expected seasonal slowdown we are seeing healthy activity in the S&P market, with several transactions recorded and ongoing negotiations.

With the Strait of Hormuz tensions continuing to shape market sentiment. Transits through the Strait remain near record lows, and violence has resurfaced, with further Houthi attacks on commercial vessels in Bab el-Mandeb and U.S. forces striking a container ship that attempted to breach Washington’s blockade on Iranian ports in the Gulf of Oman, underscoring the security risk and risk premium pricing across Gulf routes.

Drybulk sentiment remains firm, led by Capesize gains, but strong across sizes, supported by steady iron ore and coal demand. Capesize Buyers are still active for vessels around 15 years old and, at the same time we have recorded a number of recent Kamsarmax sales. The majority of enquiries we are seeing, however, are concentrated in the geared segment, predominantly modern Handy Bulkers, 15-20 years old Supramaxes and modern Ultramaxes.

On the tanker side, Suezmax spot rates jumped to an assessed global average of USD 108,500/day (from USD 81,000/day last week) after a burst of USG activity pulled tonnage away from W.Afica and Med. Activity is also rotating between segments, VLCCs, quiet last week, are now more active in the USG, while Suezmax demand is shifting Worldwide. This is also reflected in the increased purchase enquiries for VLCC, Suezmax and Aframax tonnage, although in general, sentiment continues to suggest that Buyers are not yet willing to pay the premium Sellers are asking. On the product tankers side, Buyers are actively looking for MR tonnage of around 15 years of age and younger.

## Dry Cargo

As mentioned, the Kamsarmax sector has seen particularly strong activity recently, with a number of around 10 years old vessels changing hands. The **MEDI POSITANO** (ABT 82K DWT, 2015, TSUNEISHI ZOSEN, SCRUBBER) was sold to Greek Buyers for around USD 32m. Meanwhile, the **PRESINGE** (ABT 82K DWT, 2015 TSUNEISHI ZHOUSHAN) has been acquired by Indian interests for USD 29.25m. The **BBG WUZHOU** (ABT 82K DWT, 2016, TSUNEISHI ZHOUSHAN) was sold via auction at xs USD 29m, which was the opening price, as Buyers did not face any competition. Meanwhile, the **ROYAL HOPE** (ABT 81K DWT, 2015, JMU TSU), was sold at levels around USD 31m after inviting offers on the 5th of August.

On the Supramax side, the **GLOBAL ORIOLE** (ABT 59K DWT, 2012, NACKS) was sold to Chinese interest for USD 19.5m, whilst the vintage **GLORY BRIDGE** (ABT 50K DWT, 2001, MITSUI CHIBA) was acquired by Chinese interest for USD 7.5m.

## Tanker

C/o CMB.TECH have sold their very modern Korean-built scrubber-fitted Suezmax **BRISTOL** (ABT 157 DWT, 2024 HD HYUNDAI SAMHO), for an undisclosed price, generating a capital gain of approximately USD 56.9m with delivery by the end of this year.

On the MR segment, Clients of Onex DMCC have committed their two Hyundai 2026 resale newbuilding MR tankers **ON PROMISE** & **ON PROSPER** (ABT 50K DWT, 2026, HD HYUNDAI MIPO) in the very high USD 50m’s each basis delivery within Q3 and Q4 of this year.
