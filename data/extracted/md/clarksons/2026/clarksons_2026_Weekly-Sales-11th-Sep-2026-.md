## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

#### Dry Cargo

As spot rates remain on a strong trend across both Wet and Dry spaces, we ran through another week where we observed all-time-high tanker, and very strong dry cargo tonnage prices, while appetite for secondhand vessels is continuing to absorb the elevated price levels.

### Tanker S&P

In the VLCC market, the NISSOS HERACLEA (ABT 313,525 DWT, 2009, HD HYUNDAI HI (HHI)) was sold to Middle Eastern interests for USD 112m. Furthermore, on MR2s, the GRAN COUVA (ABT 47,128 DWT, 2008, HD HYUNDAI MIPO) was concluded at USD 19.25m.
