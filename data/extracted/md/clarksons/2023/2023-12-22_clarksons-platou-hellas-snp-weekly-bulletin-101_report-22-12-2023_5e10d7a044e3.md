## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

Plenty of room at the Inn!

Market conditions remain similar to recent weeks as we head into the Christmas and New Year celebrations, with the lack of available tonnage continuing to frustrate the recyclers and leaving their facilities depleted. This may be a good thing as sentiment in India is muted with daily downward corrections in the domestic steel markets, thus this lack of supply may help to stabilise the situation and stop any negative response to price levels for any tonnage. Bangladesh and Pakistan remain limited in activity, continuing to be restricted by their financial implications that they have battled with throughout this year.
