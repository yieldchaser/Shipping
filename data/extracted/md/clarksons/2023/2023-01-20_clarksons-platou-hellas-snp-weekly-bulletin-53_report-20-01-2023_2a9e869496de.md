## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

Another busy week in Car Carriers market, with CSSC Shanghai Waigaoqiao contracting five firm plus two conventionally fuelled 9,000 CEU PCTC's for Grimaldi. The vessels will hold an Ammonia ready class notation and will be fitted with Air Lubrication, with the first three firm vessels to deliver in 2025 and the remaining two in 2026. SAIC Anji placed two separate orders for conventionally fuelled 8,900 CEU PCTC's, with four delivering from CMJL Nanjing and three delivering from CSSC Jiangnan. At CMJL Nanjing, the first two vessels will deliver in 2025 and the remaining two in 2026. At Jiangnan, the first two vessels will deliver in 2025 and the remaining vessel in 2026. All the vessels will hold a Methanol ready class notation.
