## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

The 'possible positive' report circulating this week was the EU considering once again to put some selected yards in Alang (India) on to the approved EU list of recycling facilities. This would be a huge step to help capacity for EU approved facilities as currently, we are heavily relying on Turkey to absorb the larger tonnage that comes for sale requiring EU approval. The aim is that by 24th March, 2024 there will be some progression in talks, but many still believe this will take 1-2 years with efforts from the Indian Government and EU organisations to provide some breakthrough. Whether the NGOs accept this cleanly remains to be seen but, in the meantime, we are yet to see an official announcement by the EU.
