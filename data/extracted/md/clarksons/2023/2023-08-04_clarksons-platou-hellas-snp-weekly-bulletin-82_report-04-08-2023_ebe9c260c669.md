## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

Just like the wet weather being experienced in the UK at present, the recycling market has seen a trickle of tonnage come into play. With units steadily flowing out of the Chinese owners, this is giving the market a slight heartbeat in the season lull of the summer months. Interestingly, some surprise container candidates have been circulated from clients of MSC despite the holiday season firmly upon us. As evidenced below, the vessel achieved a firm price due to the end recycler having previously taking the sister vessel.
