# New Building

DH Shipbuilding are also reported to have also won an order for an LR2, this a single ship with MJL Bangladesh Ltd for delivery in 1H 2026. Chengxi Shipyard have announced an order for 4 x 50K MR product tankers with Schoeller Holdings for delivery in 2026. On the smaller end of the sector, Wuhu Shipyard have announced and order for 4 x 6,600dwt Methanol DF ready, ice 1A, chemical tankers. These have been signed with RFOcean and are due to deliver from 2H 2025 into 2026.

In Dry, just the one order to report, with clients of Cardiff Marine having placed an order at Chengxi Shipyard for a pair of 82,600 dwt Kamsarmax bulkers, both for delivery in 2026.

# Recycling

## Hit and Miss!

Certain vessels achieve a certain price sometimes in this market and quite clearly, this week portrays why each sale can be individual, and not down to market conditions. It is reported a small container vessel of about 4,600 ldt has been committed basis delivery Bangladesh for region USD 622.00 per ldt, creating a huge spread across all tonnage types. If this is indeed correct, then some reasoning unbeknown to many must be the answer as it would seem this far exceeds current pricing sentiment. The only possible explanation is that for such a small unit, a Letter of Credit can be opened from Bangladesh.

Why are we surprised? There have been reports of many units now sitting outside Chattogram (some for 4-6 weeks) waiting for L/C’s to be opened and as such, several being diverted to India where they are offering lower rates which clearly shows the difficulties recyclers are facing from their Banks.

The talk generally this week, is that of price levels weakening further from both Bangladesh and India and many doubt any rebound will be seen until after the holiday season, which is very much upon us, leaving most industry players, still scratching their heads with this latest Container sale.

Some good news arrived from Pakistan this week as it is felt that local recyclers could return to the bidding table soon. This is on the back of the IMF loan provided over recent weeks, which should result in offers finally emanating from Pakistan, although realistically, we do not expect this to happen for at least a couple of months.
