## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

An important announcement was made this week with the news emanating from Bangladesh confirming that the Bangladeshi government has approved ratification of the Hong Kong Convention for the Safe and Environmentally Sound Recycling of Ships (HKC). This is such a huge step towards the entry into force of an international recycling convention for shipping and is the second destination, after India, in the Indian sub. Continent to ratify the HKC.
