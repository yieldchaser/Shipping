## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

In Containers this week, it was reported that CMA CGM ordered six firm 15,000TEU containerships at CSSC Dalian Shipbuilding (DSIC). The vessels will be fitted with methanol dual-fuel propulsion with the first vessel set to deliver in 4Q 2025 and run throughout 2026.
