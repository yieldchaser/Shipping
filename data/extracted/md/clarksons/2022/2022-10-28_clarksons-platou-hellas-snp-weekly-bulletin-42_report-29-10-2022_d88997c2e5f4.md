## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

With Diwali celebrations in full swing this week, observants in the industry were taking this period to spend time with their families without the interruption of tonnage circulation. It was an especially poignant one this year with the UK announcing yet another Prime Minister (its 3rd one this year alone), with Rishi Sunak being the first Asian leader of the house of commons, making it a particularly special coincidence. It's hoped that he will now provide some much-needed stability to the home economy and this will help support what is wildly a chaotic global state of monetary affairs.
