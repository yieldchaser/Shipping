- **Date**: 2022-04-29 (29/04/2022)

## Desk Commentary

### NEW BUILDING

This week, in the gas carrier sector, CNOOC announced ordering six firm 174k CBM LNG Carriers at Hudong Zhonghua, with deliveries of the first and second vessel set for 2025, the third, fourth and fifth in 2026 and the final vessel to be delivered in 2027. Hyundai Samho announced contracting a firm 174k CBM LNG Carrier with an unknown owner, with delivery of the vessel set for October 2025.

### NEW BUILDING

In containers, Huanghai SB announced taking orders for ten firm gearless 1,800TEU Containerships from SITC, with deliveries expected to run throughout 2024.
