## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

As we enter the season of Advent, it is encouraging to see the arrival of an improved sentiment from India where the domestic steel prices have rebounded this week. This in turn, has brought slightly stronger price indications from this destination with India now offering a better showing than their counterparts in Bangladesh and Pakistan.
