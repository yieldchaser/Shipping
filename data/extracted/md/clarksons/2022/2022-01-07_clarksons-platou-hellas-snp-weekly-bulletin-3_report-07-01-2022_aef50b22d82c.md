## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

Hellas S&P Weekly Bulletin 07 January 2022

### NEW BUILDING

In the gas carrier market, Hudong Zhonghua announced a joint venture between CNOOC and Mitsui OSK Lines ordered six firm 174k CBM LNG Carriers, with deliveries of the vessels are expected to run from 2024 through to 2026. The yard also announced that CSSC Shipping Leasing declared an optional 174k CBM LNG Carrier, bringing their series to four, with delivery of the latest vessel set for 2024. DSME announced that clients of Maran Gas Maritime declared two optional 174k CBM LNG Carriers, with delivery of both vessels set for 2H 2025 bringing their series to four at the yard. Hyundai Samho announced taking order for a firm 174k CBM LNG Carrier from an Asian owner, understood to be SK Shipping, with delivery of the vessel expected within September 2024. Nantong CIMC SOE announced taking orders for three firm 7.2k CBM LPG Carriers from Hartmann Schiffarts, with the vessels all slated for delivery within 2024.

### NEW BUILDING

In Containers this week, Hyundai Samho announced that MSC ordered six firm LNG dual-fuel 15,000 TEU Containerships, with delivery of the vessels expected to be delivered in 2H 2024. Hyundai Mipo announced taking orders for three firm 1,800 TEU Containerships from an unknown owner. The vessels are "LNG Ready", with the first two vessels to delivered in 3Q 2023 and the final vessel in October 2023.

### NEW BUILDING

In the Ferry market, Aulong Shipbuilding announced that HFerry ordered a firm 700 passenger Catermaran, with delivery of the vessel set for 2H 2023.
