## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

#### Dry Cargo

This question is aimed at the sales of larger dry bulk units that have recently been concluded as recycling transactions. With a slight pickup in the dry freight market, until these units arrive to the recycling yards will we know whether they can be removed from the world fleet list. The higher rates that were offered do still seem for these vessels to be either for trading further by the cash buyer or flipped to trading buyers. Time will tell!
