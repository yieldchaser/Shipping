## Desk Commentary

### NEW BUILDING

In tankers this week, Nanjing Yangyang Chemical Transport announced ordering two firm 13k dwt Chemical tankers at Dayang Offshore, with both deliveries expected within 2023.

### NEW BUILDING

In dry bulk, Wisdom Marine announced an order for two firm Ultramax at Imabari Shipbuilding, with delivery of both vessels expected within 2024.

### NEW BUILDING

In the gas carrier market, Hyundai HI (Ulsan) announced taking orders for two firm 174k CBM LNG Carriers from an unknown owner, with delivery of vessels set for 1H 2024. Hyundai Samho took order for a firm 174k CBM LNG Carrier, also from an unknown owner, with delivery slated for 3Q 2024.
