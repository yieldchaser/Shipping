## Desk Commentary

### NEW BUILDING

In the gas carrier market, DSME announced taking orders for two firm plus two optional 174k CBM LNG Carriers from Clients of Maran Gas Maritime, with delivery of the firm vessels expected in 2024.

### NEW BUILDING

In the Ferry market, Scandlines announced ordering a 1,200 LM, 140 passenger Ropax at Cemre Shipyard. The vessel is Hybrid-battery powered and will be delivered within 2024.
