## Desk Commentary

### Desk Talk

The sluggish market remains with the dearth of tonnage continuing, and the Indian recycling industry particularly in the doldrums with slim demand from the steel mills for ship steel. Some serious question marks are being raised from Alang with many of the rerolling mills closing and very little appetite for the recyclers to procure tonnage at this current time. Maybe the Indian election in April, will provide some much-needed stimulus to the industry and hopefully kickstart sentiment again!
