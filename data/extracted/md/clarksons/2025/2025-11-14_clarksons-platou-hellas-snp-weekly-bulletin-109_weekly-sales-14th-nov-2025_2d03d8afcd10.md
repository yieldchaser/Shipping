## Tanker

Similar pattern on the wet side as well, where UAE-based buyers acquired this week a couple of pumproom-type MR2s from Maersk Tankers. Specifically, the **MAERSK MISSISSIPPI** and the **MAERSK MARU** (abt 48k dwt, built 2011 and 2010 Iwagi) changed hands for USD 44m enbloc.

For comparison, a couple of weeks ago the 2011-built sistership **YOSEMITE TRADER** was reported sold for around USD 21.5m, basis SS/DD due in March 2026.
