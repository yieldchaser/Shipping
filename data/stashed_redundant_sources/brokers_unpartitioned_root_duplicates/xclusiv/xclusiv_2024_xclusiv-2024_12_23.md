## Market Overview

### In a Nutshell

- The tanker market demonstrated sustained profitability in
- 2024 despite significant market volatility across different
- vessel segments. (Page 1)
- VLCC and Suezmax tankers experienced peak earnings early
- in the year, followed by a decline. (Page 1)
- Aframax rates experienced a sharp correction but subse-
- quently rebounded strongly. Atlantic MR segment is now
- significantly outperforming the Pacific MR. (Page 1)
- MR2 vessels drive the S&P activity in 2024, being responsi-
- ble for nearly more than one-third of all sales. (Page 1)
- Tanker NB activity was very firm in 2024, however a decline
- in the number of orders was observed since Q3. (Page 1)
