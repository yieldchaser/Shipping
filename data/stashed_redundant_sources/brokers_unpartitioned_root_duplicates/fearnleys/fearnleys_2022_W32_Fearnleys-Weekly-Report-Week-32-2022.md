## 01 Tankers

### Market Commentary

#### Vlcc

A clever game played by charterers this week, as strong activity is kept in the shadows and vessels are picked off the list. We are up to 135 or so deals ex MEG for August, with a little under half of the 20-31 window to cover. Rates have flattered to deceive with MEG/East remaining pegged in the high WS 50's. Owners should maintain some hope as we swing towards the Autumn loading program, as the list remains balanced and those requiring modern ships will find them a little scarcer. A point of note, WS 65 achieved off very prompt dates, but given the lack of competition not a reflection on the market, and the vessel had been sat in the MEG for a while, so the TCE was close to USD 0.

The Transatlantic activity increased with a number of ships fixing USG/UKC at USD 3.3m, and we expect to see more of this trade going forward.

#### Suezmax

The Suezmax market have been absolutely nothing to talk about the last week. Activity has been almost non-existing and the whole market have basically been waiting for a downward correction. This all changed yday with activity picking up in the USG on Afras, in addition to V's looking a bit better. The USG market is more attractive for ships open Cont/Med and the only ship willing to look at Wafr will be eastern ballasters. All of this will limit available tonnage in these markets, hopefully keeping rates close to last done levels. In the MEG, it's still rather quiet, except from short voyages into India. A lot of ships still looking Wafr or Bsea instead, so we expect the MEG market to remain around current levels.

#### North Sea

The North Sea/Baltic market been moving sideways this week with rates for a cross UKC paying around WS 185 level. However, with quite a few Panamax enquires looking at Aframax tonnage due to a tight tonnage list on Panamax this we could see an upward pressure on rates moving into the 3rd decade fixing window. The Med/Blsea market continued the downward spiral throughout last week. There's been some under the radar activity this week, but not sufficient to support the prompt and available tonnage hence freight levels taken a further dip this week. We don't foresee any real improvements for the balance of the week unless activity bounces back. However, some owners now ballasting away eyeing more promising returns in the USG.

## 02 Dry Bulk

### Market Commentary

#### Capesize

After last week's drop with an activity blackout, market have found some ground as all the miners are back again for West Australia. However, though market is active and ffa bounced a bit the physical levels are not dramatically higher as there still is a lot of availed tonnage around as congestion has eased. The expectation for next week is some improvements as levels have been so hard smacked down, however with summer doldrums it's hard to imagine a very big movement.

#### Panamax

A further slow week activity wise with limited activity emerging. Sentiment consequently appeared weaker in both basins largely owing to a lack of activity according to some but across the board the tonnage profile continues to grow for now.

Some representative fixtures this week: The Great Triumph (77,835 2015) San Ciprian 13 August was heard to have fixed Cobelfret's latest Kamsar to San Ciprian bauxite lift at $16.10 fio.

Yasa Fortune (82,849 2006) Gibraltar 14 August was said to have fixed for a front haul trip at a rate in the mid-upper $20,000s.

Sifnos (81,084 2015) Yeosu 6-9 August was fixed for a trip via EC Australia redelivery India at $18,500. Amalia (81,783 2019) Gib 11-12 Aug fxd subs tct via NCSA redel Skaw-Gib (intn Jorf Lasfar) $20,500 - Norden.

Black Pearl (79,600 2012) Hong Kong 12 Aug tct via Indonesia redel India $14,000 - cnr. W-Original (81,874 2012) Putian 9/10 Aug tct via Indonesia redel S China $14,000 - cnr.

ASL Venus (82,153 2011) Masinloc 13 Aug tct via Indonesia redel India $18,000 - Seatrans.

#### Supramax

The Supra market in still lucking activity across all trading areas. Slow start of the week with declining sentiment. ECSA and USG are keeping steady momentous tough rates are not impressive. North Atlantic, Continent and Mediterranean markets suffering luck of inquiry.

Supramax dely Brazil TCT to Algeria fixed at USD 25,000 pd. Trips from Continent to Med with scrap fixing below USD 20's dop on modern Supramax. Trading in Russian ports still paying premium.

## 03 Gas

### Market Commentary

#### West

Last week we saw an uptick in activity after a couple of weeks with slow pace in the Western market. So far this week the pace of activity has not followed as we have only seen one fixture concluded. Last done deal for August dates concluded at low 90s H/C while we see around 100 for September dates. There are still some ships left open in USG with soon two of them prompt open. The arb has narrowed a bit lately which has not been good for freight. A 1.75 million build would help widen it and maybe give freight some support.
