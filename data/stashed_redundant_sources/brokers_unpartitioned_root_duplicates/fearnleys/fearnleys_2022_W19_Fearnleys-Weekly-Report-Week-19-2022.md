## 01 Tankers

### Market Commentary

#### Vlcc

On the surface, a slowly softening market with a drip of enquiry, as MEG/East hovering just north of WS 40 for modern ships which tick all boxes, and high WS 30's for those which do not. TCE's return on the cusp of loss making, even for the most economical of units. But, there remains a flow of 'off market' deals, with owners happy to fix as close to last done as they can. A line in the sand will be drawn at some point, so further softening will be strongly resisted.

At the time of writing, the Atlantic market has a couple of enquiries from both Brazil and the USG, and with Suezmaxes slowly turning upwards, there is hope for VLCC owners heading for the Cape, or indeed already hunting those waters. Reports of a 130 mt stem Wafr-UKCM doubling up to a VLCC as economies of scale kicked in to play will bring some cheer.

#### VLCC

A sense of modest optimism pervades the Atlantic Suezmax market this week, but we have to emphasize the word "modest" as the tonnage supply looks fairly adequate, especially in West Africa, suggesting this recent upturn is based on sentiment, not fundamentals. Reports of a couple of TD20 cargoes struggling to repeat last done levels has brought the prospect of VLCC co- loads into view, which may cap proceedings at WS 95 level. A further limiting factor remains the East Suezmax market which has been the least exciting region of late. There is a lot of expectation that we will see increased MEG/West requirements in the medium term, but until that translates into real world fixtures TD23 is going to trade high WS 40's/low WS 50's. MEG/China needs proper testing, but we rate it WS 87.5-90 for a straight run today.

#### North Sea

Aframax owners in the North Sea have had a week with very few cargo enquiries. A great portion of cargoes in the North Sea basin have been covered with own tonnage. On the back of this charterers tested the market by quoting the market and managed to push down the rates for an Ecuk load off 17-18 May dates. Going forward we expect rates to move sideways till activity picks up again.

#### Mediterranean

The Med/Bsea market has been trading sideways all week with a softening trend. This is due to a lack of cargo activity and a tonnage list which is getting longer. The status is quo in Libya for the time being, and since surrounding markets are not offering any firmer alternatives, we expect the market to remain soft going into next week.

## 02 Dry Bulk

### Market Commentary

#### Capesize

Capesize rates are increasing in the East and in the West, with greater expectations as well as actual activity out of Brazil being the main driver. The West Australian iron ore rates are consequently following up day by day. C5 is presently at 14,50, up from USD 12 pmt one week ago. The overall index is up from 19,000 to 31,000 over the same period. This is happening after a long period of time where the Brazilian exports have been affected by heavy rains in the beginning of the year. There is still room for a further positive trend in rates.

#### Panamax

The market in the Pacific region remained buoyant, levels continuing to improve with levels of enquiry from both Australia and North Pacific remaining strong. There is more enquiry in the US Gulf however felt that levels of enquiry further south from East Coast South America had reduced in recent days leading to an easing of levels.

Some representative fixtures this week: BBG Guigang (81,567/2020) open CJK 14-20 May was rumored to have been placed on subjects for 6 to 8 months around USD 30,750-31,000.

Medi Palmarola (81,874/2018) open Ghent 16 May was rumored to have been placed on subjects for a transatlantic round at USD 32,000.

Glory Amsterdam (77,171/2006) open Swinoujscie with prompt dates was rumored to have been placed on subjects for a transatlantic round at USD 30,000 but further details were not yet available.

The Sea Gemini (81,716/2014) open Dafeng 12-17 May was rumored to have been placed on subjects for a trip via East Coast Australia to India between USD 27,000 and 28,000.

Great Hope (75,480/2012) open Hansha 9 May was also rumored on subjects for a trip via Indonesia to India at USD 25,000 with Tongli.

Km MT. Jade (81,487/2008) open Kashima 9 May was rumored to have been placed on subjects for a trip via Indonesia to Japan at USD 27,000 to Asahi.

#### Supramax

Supramax market holding firm momentum both in the Atlantic and Pacific basin, South Asia sees good improvements, despite recent holidays in Australia. The rates for TC from SE Asia to China around USD 27,000 pd. From North China and Japan seems market is quieter. Supra rates for round trips to the Far East and NOPAC still at stable level around USD 27,000 pd. India market was moving fast after the holidays that was ending last week and the rates was reported between USD 30,000 for east direction and USD 35,000 for trip into Atlantic. Supramax rates for Atlantic RV estimates about USD 34,500 pd, whole Pacific still lacking behind and estimates about USD 27,000 pd. The Atlantic still missing big volume from the Baltic and Black Sea area due to the Russia - Ukraine situation. The US Gulf/ECSA market still remain firm with sign of further improvements.

## 03 Gas

### Market Commentary

#### East

We have seen a handful of end May-early June cargos being worked in the Middle East this week. As the availability of open ships with firm schedules in end May is becoming less, owners' ideas have increased and last done was concluded in the low USD 70s Baltic. With Adnoc dates published the focus has shifted to June, and we expect to see more activity in the days to come for 1st decade.
