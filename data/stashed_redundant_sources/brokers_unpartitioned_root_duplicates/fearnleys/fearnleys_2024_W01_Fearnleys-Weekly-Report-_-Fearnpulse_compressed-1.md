## 01 Tankers

### Market Commentary

#### Vlcc

A shorter week as 2024 kicks in and rates continue to drift further south. At the time of writing, MEG/China clings to the WS 50's (on 2023 flats), but this line in the sand will likely be tested as the week draws on. Tonnage is plentiful and TCE's remain decent, so there is scope for owners to consider below last done. Charterers continue to pick targets from the shadows, so deals going unreported, but January is already up to 100+ deals and the 3rd decade yet to be touched.

#### Aframax

2024 WS rates. The smaller sizes need to move up a bit quicker to help out their bigger sisters.
