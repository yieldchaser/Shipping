The SSY Atlantic Capesize Index rose by a further 1,666 points over the past week to 15,376, an increase of almost 5,000 points since the start of the month. There were sharp rises of $9,000/day and $12,500/day in the round-voyage and fronthaul rate to $32,250/day and $61,750/day, respectively.
