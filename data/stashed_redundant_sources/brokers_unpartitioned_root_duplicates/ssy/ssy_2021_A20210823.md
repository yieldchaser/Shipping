## Market Commentary

At 16,688 points, the SSY Atlantic Capesize Index jumped 2,258 points in the week to 23 August, reaching the highest level since December 2009. Both the Atlantic round-voyage and fronthaul rate surged over the past seven days, up $12,000/day and $11,150/day to $50,000/day and $75,000/day, respectively.
