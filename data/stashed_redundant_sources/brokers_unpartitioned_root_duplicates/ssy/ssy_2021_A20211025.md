## Market Commentary

The SSY Atlantic Capesize Index plunged by a further 2,469 points weekon-week to 18,210, although it remains more than 10,500 points above the year -ago level. There were declines of $13,500/day and $11,850/day in the round-voyage and fronthaul rates to $65,500/day and $83,000/day, respectively.
