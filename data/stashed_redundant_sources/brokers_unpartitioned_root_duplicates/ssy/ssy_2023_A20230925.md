## Market Commentary

The SSY Atlantic Capesize Index increased 1,043 points week-on-week to 8,884 points, the highest level since May. Fronthaul and trans-Atlantic earnings continued their upwards momentum, with SSY’s fronthaul earnings up $4,750/day to $38,500/day, while the round-voyage charter rates rose $5,150/day to $17,150/day.
