## Market Commentary

The SSY Pacific Capesize Index weakened throughout the holidays to be down 44 points over the past three weeks to 7,164 points. With slight weakening in iron ore demand as a result of the upcoming Chinese New Year, SSY’s assessed Australia-China iron ore spot voyage rate is $1.20/t lower over the past three weeks at $10.00/t. Despite being down, $4.90/t since mid-December, the voyage rate is up $2.85/t from same time last year. Correspondingly, the Pacific roundvoyage rate is also down $6,000/day to $20,750/day.
