## Market Commentary

The SSY Pacific Capesize Index rallied 2,587 points week-on-week to 9,607, at the strongest pace since 2009. Benefitting off of the stronger commodity flows in the Pacific, SSY’s assessed Australia-China iron ore spot voyage rate jumped $2.00/t higher to $13.00/t, while the Pacific round-voyage rate soared $10,000/day to $35,000/day, strongest since May 2022.
