## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures declined by 2.13% today, the main contract closed at 897. The traders' willingness to ship is less. The steel mills are wait and see . The overall trading sentiment of the market is worse. PBF at Shandong port deal 894-910 yuan/mt, decrease 11-31 yuan/mt; PBF at Tangshan port deal 918 yuan/mt, decrease 12-17 yuan/mt. At the weekend, the data released at the important meeting held by the state was lower than the market expectation, and the opening trend on Monday was weak. On the supply side, according to SMM statistics, the total global iron ore shipments of SMM last week were 30.87 million tons, a decrease of 1.6% on a month-on-month basis; The volume of shipments from Australia and Brazil decreased. In the current period, the arrival volume of China's main ports decreased significantly, and the output of blast furnace molten iron of steel plants at the demand end increased slightly. The current ore price is still greatly affected by the macro policy news. Under the support of fundamentals, the falling space of the current price is relatively limited, and the short-term iron ore price will be mainly subject to broad fluctuations.
