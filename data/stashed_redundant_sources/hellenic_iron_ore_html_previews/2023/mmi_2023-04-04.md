## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures declined by 2.06% today, the main contract closed at 881.5. The traders' willingness to ship is less. The steel mills are less active to purchase. The overall trading sentiment of the market is negative. PBF at Shandong port deal 888-898 yuan/mt, decrease 4-14 yuan/mt ; SSF at Shandong port deal 765 yuan/mt .PBF at Tangshan port deal 900 yuan/mt, decrease 15-18yuan/mt. The current market sentiment is still pessimistic due to the demand for finished products. First, the rainy weather before and after the Tomb Sweeping Day may affect the terminal construction. Second, the holidays also affect some demand. From the perspective of imported iron ore fundamentals, the current pattern has not changed much, but with the recovery of overseas supply in the later stage, the supply-demand contradiction may gradually accumulate. In the short term, iron ore prices continue to fluctuate widely.
