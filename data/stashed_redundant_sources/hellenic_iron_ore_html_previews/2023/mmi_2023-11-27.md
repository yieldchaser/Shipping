## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures open higher and go lower. the main contract I2401 closed 980.5. an increase of 0.36% throughout the day. some traders were negative to sell, Some steel mills tended to be wait-and-see, and purchasing enthusiasm is not high. total transactions remained insipid. PBF at Shandong port dealt 993-995 yuan/mt; the same as last Friday. PBF at Tangshan port dealt 1000 yuan/mt; decreased 5 yuan/mt from last Friday. SMM shipping data shows that the total global shipment volume is 33.52 million tons, an increase of 8.7% compared to the previous month. Mainly from the shipment increment in Brazil. The total arrival volume at the port was 25.6211 million tons, an increase of 13.45% compared to the previous month. The supply is expected to increase both in the near and long term, which weakens the support for mineral prices. The news surface is also relatively calm, with narrow fluctuations in the iron market. In the later stage, we will mainly focus on macro news and data on off-season finished products in terminal demand.
