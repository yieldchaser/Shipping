## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures rose by 1.98% today, the main contract closed 873.5. The traders' willingness to ship is more. The steel mills are active to purchase. The overall trading sentiment of the market is better. PBF at Shandong port deal 878-883 yuan/mt, decrease 2-5 yuan/mt; PBF at Tangshan port deal 887-890 yuan/mt. The futures market opened after the holiday, and the market sentiment was more optimistic with the guidance of the market. On the basic side, according to SMM tracking, the global iron ore shipments totaled 26.91 million tons, down 11.5% month-on-month. Among them, 13.3 million tons were sent from Australia to China, down 16.8% month-on-month; Brazil sent 770000 tons of iron ore to China, a decrease of 57.9% on a month-on-month basis, while the total amount of iron ore delivered to China was 22924900 tons, a decrease of 13.72% on a month-on-month basis. On the demand side, the molten iron of steel mills slowly rebounded, supporting the ore price to rise but not fall. Looking back on the frequent supervision of the relevant departments on the ore price in recent years, this has become the primary risk point of the ore price, and it is expected that the current iron ore price will still fluctuate.
