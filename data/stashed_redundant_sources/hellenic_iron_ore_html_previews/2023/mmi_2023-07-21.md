## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures declined by 0.12 % today, the main contract closed at 846.5.The traders' willingness to ship is general.The steel mills are not active to purchase.The overall trading sentiment of the market is nor- mal.PBF at Shandong port deal 875-879yuan/mt,decrease 11yuan/mt. SSF at Tangshan port deal 743 yuan/mt .The recent production restrictions and production capacity control information have continued to disrupt market sentiment, but the current profit situation of the steel plant has given rise to strong driving force. However, the restrictions on sintering machines caused by environmental factors are still continuing, and there may be further tightening in the future. At present, market sentiment has undergone significant changes and the impact of fundamentals is relatively small. It is expected that iron ore will continue to operate in a volatile manner in the short term.
