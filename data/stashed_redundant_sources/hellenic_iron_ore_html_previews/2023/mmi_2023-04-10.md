## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures market falling trends by 1.83%, the main contract I2309 closed 777. some traders were active to sell, steel mills purchased on demand. today's overall market transaction atmosphere in general. PBF at Shandong port dealt 885-890 yuan/mt; decreased 5-10 yuan/mt from last Friday. SSF at Shandong port dealt 760 yuan/mt; decreased 10 yuan/mt from last Friday. PBF at Tangshan port dealt 900-902 yuan/mt; the decreased 5 yuan/mt from last Friday. SMM shipping data showed global shipments fell 750,000 tonnes month-on-month to 30.27 million tonnes this week, with both Australia, Pakistan and China down. In addition, due to the weather, the volume of arrivals decreased by 5.5 million tons to 19.55 million tons. Considering the recent wind and rain weather in Australia, it is expected that the shipment volume may continue to decline next week. On the demand side, molten iron is already at a high level, and blast furnaces are scheduled to resume production this week, which is expected to reach a high level. But considering the building materials demand is weak, policy supervision is more intensive, expected this week mine prices are still mainly shock operation.
