## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures continued to rise largely today , the main contract I2401 closed 817, an increase of 3.68% throughout the day; some traders were active to sell, and there is a demand for some end of month delivery resources; The steels procurement more cautious, The overall market transaction atmosphere has weakened today. PBF at Shandong port dealt 899 yuan/mt; the same as yesterday. PBF at Tangshan port dealt 899 yuan/mt; the same as yesterday. The operating rate of blast furnaces in SMM research remained unchanged month on month, but the utilization rate of production capacity and molten iron production slightly improved. The rigid demand procurement of steel mills still provides strong support for iron ore prices. In the off-season of terminal demand, the shipment of finished products is average, and the price increase of finished products is not as high as that of iron ore. Steel mills' profits shrink, and negative feedback pressure increases. However, in the short term, policies can stimulate market sentiment, and mining prices are prone to rise but difficult to fall. In the short term, they may continue to be strong and volatile.
