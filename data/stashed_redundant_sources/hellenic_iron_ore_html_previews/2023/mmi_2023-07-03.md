## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures declined by 1.68 % today, the main contract closed at 819. The traders' willingness to ship is less. The steel mills are not active to purchase. The overall trading sentiment of the market is general. PBF at Shandong port deal 865-870 yuan/mt, increase 3 yuan/mt. PBF at Tangshan port deal 865-886 yuan/mt, decrease 10 yuan/mt. SSF at Tangshan port deal 740 yuan/mt, decrease 2 yuan/mt. Today, the market reported another production restriction on crude steel, causing panic in market sentiment. The outlook fell in response, but according to the steel mills tracked by SMM, there is currently no confirmed information within the factory. Fundamentally speaking, the total global iron ore shipment volume of SMM is 36.83 million tons, an increase of 11% month on month; The total arrival volume of major ports in China is 22.0172 million tons, a decrease of 11.21% compared to the previous month. Currently, overseas shipments are in a relaxed cycle, but the port volume has still decreased this week. On the demand side, according to SMM maintenance, there may be a slight decrease in the average iron production this Sunday. Overall, the fundamental contradiction in iron ore is not significant. The market is greatly affected by news and demand for finished products, and the ore price is still mainly fluctuating at high levels.
