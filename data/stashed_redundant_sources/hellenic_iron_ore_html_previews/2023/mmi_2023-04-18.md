## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures rose by 2.08% today, the main contract closed at 784. The traders' willingness to ship is better. The steel mills are active to purchase. The overall trading sentiment of the market is better. PBF at Shandong port deal 883-895 yuan/mt, increase 11 yuan/mt. PBF at Tangshan port deal 902-908 yuan/mt, increase 7-18 yuan/mt. The macro data released today showed that the GDP data of the first quarter exceeded the market expectations, the property data performed generally, and the market sentiment strengthened. As the May Day holiday approaches, many steel mills have a need to replenish inventory in advance. However, according to SMM statistics, the impact of blast furnace maintenance this week on molten iron was 587200 tons, an increase of 1000 tons compared to last week's maintenance. The production of molten iron from steel mills has decreased, which has loosened support for ore prices. The current negative feedback risk has been delayed due to the pre holiday terminal reserve. Considering the relatively active iron ore market, it is expected that iron ore prices will operate in a volatile trend in the short term.
