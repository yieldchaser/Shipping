## Market Commentary

>DCE iron ore futures rose by 0.26 % today, the main contract closed at 965.5. The steel mills are not active to purchase. The overall trading sentiment of the market is general. PBF at Shandong port deal 983 yuan/mt. Yesterday's post market release of social finance data showed a positive trend and market sentiment was moderate. According to SMM statistics, the impact of blast furnace maintenance this week on molten iron was 1.4582 million tons, a decrease of 154500 tons compared to last week's maintenance. Therefore, this week's blast furnace molten iron production slightly rebounded, supporting the iron ore demand. Considering that iron ore has been fluctuating at high levels for a long time and lacks upward momentum, the upward pressure continues to increase. It is expected that iron ore prices will mainly operate in a narrow range of fluctuations in the short term.
