## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures rose by 1.36% today, the main contract closed 855.5. The traders' willingness to ship is general. The steel mills are active to purchase .The overall trading sentiment of the market is better .PBF at Shandong port deal 848-855 yuan/mt, increase 5-10 yuan/mt; PBF at Tangshan port dealt 861 yuan/mt, increase 10-13 yuan/mt. According to the news, the relevant national departments held a meeting to release the work related to stabilizing prices, and the market's fear of falling was aggravated. However, rumors of riots in Brazil from overseas have once again raised concerns about iron ore supply in the market. In terms of fundamentals, according to the data of the ten ports tracked by SMM, the volume of port inventory of the four major varieties has accumulated to 89.95 million tons. With the completion of replenishment of some steel mills, the speculative atmosphere in the market is relatively strong. However, the negative feedback of the terminal off-season results in poor profits of steel mills, and the willingness to purchase iron ore has declined. In the short term, iron ore prices are still volatile.
