## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures rose by 1.34% today, the main contract closed at 909.5. The traders' willingness to ship is less. The steel mills are wait and see .The overall trading sentiment of the market is worse.PBF at Shandong port deal 915-916 yuan/mt,increase 5-20 yuan/mt;PBF at Tangshan port deal 933 yuan/mt,increase 17yuan/mt. Recently, the relevant state departments concerned about the problem of iron ore prices rising too fast, which has continued to cause hot discussion in the market, and the market mentality is different. According to the data of the General Administration of Customs today, China imported 19.4196 million tons of iron ore and its concentrate in January-February, up 7.3% year on year, with a slight increase in the supply side of iron ore. The increase in the output of molten iron of blast furnace gives certain support to the ore price. The terminal demand of the industrial chain is flat, while the current macro news is mainly stable, and iron ore prices are expected to remain volatile.
