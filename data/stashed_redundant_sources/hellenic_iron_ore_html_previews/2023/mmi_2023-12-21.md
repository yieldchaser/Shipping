## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures market rose by 3.53%. the main contract i2405 closed 968, Some traders adopt separate quotations ; Some steel mills tended to be wait-and-see, and purchasing enthusiasm is not high. total transactions remained insipid. PBF at Shandong port dealt 996-1000 yuan/mt; increased 5-10 yuan/mt over yesterday. PBF at Tangshan port dealt 1015-1028 yuan/mt; increased 10-18 yuan/mt over yesterday. The inventory of SMM's ten port sub varieties is still in a slight destocking state. The destocking in this cycle is mainly due to a significant decline in arrivals last week due to port congestion. From the perspective of variety structure, many regions in Hebei have once again experienced environmental production restrictions. Although the cost-effectiveness of lump ore and pellets is not high, there has been a slight increase in demand. In addition, there are rumors in the market today that the deposit interest rate is about to be lowered. The market interpretation is that the deposit interest rate is about to be lowered, boosting market sentiment and driving up mining prices significantly.
