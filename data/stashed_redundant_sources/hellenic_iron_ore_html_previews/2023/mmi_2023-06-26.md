PBF at Shandong port dealt 835-845 yuan/mt; decreased 0-10 yuan/mt over yesterday. PBF at Tangshan port dealt 865-870 yuan/mt; decreased 5 yuan/mt over yesterday. he total global iron ore shipment volume of SMM is 33.18 million tons, a decrease of 1.7% compared to the previous month. Among them, 18.58 million tons were shipped from Australia, a decrease of 14.3% month on month; Brazil's shipment volume was 8.48 million tons, an increase of 23.3% month on month; The total amount of iron ore delivered to port by SMM China was 24.797 million tons, an increase of 6.08% compared to the previous month. Overseas shipments are stable, and the arrival volume continues to increase. After the consumption of iron ore inventory during the Dragon Boat Festival, just needed replenishment still forms support for the spot price of iron ore, which is easy to rise but difficult to fall, and maintains a volatile trend in the short term.
