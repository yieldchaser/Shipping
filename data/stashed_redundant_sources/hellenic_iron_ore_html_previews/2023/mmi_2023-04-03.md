## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures declined by 2.04% today, the main contract closed at 890.5. The traders' willingness to ship is less. The steel mills are less active to purchase. The overall trading sentiment of the market is negative. PBF at Shandong port deal 900-909 yuan/mt, decrease 12-14 yuan/mt. PBF at Tangshan port deal 915-918 yuan/mt, decrease 13-16 yuan/mt. According to SMM global shipping data, this week, SMM's global iron ore shipments totaled 31.02 million tons, an increase of 3.4% month on month; Among them, the traffic volume in Australia has decreased, while the traffic volume in Brazil has increased slightly; The total number of arrivals from major ports in China has declined slightly. On the demand side, a few steel mills have resumed production of blast furnaces, and the growth rate of molten iron has narrowed compared to last week. At present, there are many rumors of interviews with relevant national departments in the market, and the market is pessimistic about the demand for finished products. market sentiment has been affected to some extent. In the short term, iron ore prices are expected to fluctuate widely.
