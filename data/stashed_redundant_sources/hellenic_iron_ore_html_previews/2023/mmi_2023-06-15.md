## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures rose by 1.43% today, the main contract closed at 815.5. The traders' willingness to ship is general. The steel mills are not active to purchase. The overall trading sentiment of the market is general. PBF at Tangshan port deal 875 yuan/mt, increase 5-9 yuan/mt. From the news perspective, the investment growth rate is still negative after the release of real estate data today. However, the Federal Reserve temporarily suspended interest rate hikes and the market mentality remained weak. Fundamentally speaking, there is currently some support for mineral prices, but considering the continuous growth of mineral prices, the market still has some concerns about the national outcry. In the short term, iron ore prices are mainly fluctuating at high levels.
