## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures rose by 0.06% today, the main contract closed at 844.5. The traders' willingness to ship is high. The steel mills are active to purchase. The overall trading sentiment of the market is more. PBF at Shandong port deal 914-918 yuan/mt, increase 4-8 yuan/mt. PBF at Tangshan port deal 910-919 yuan/mt, increase 6 yuan/mt. According to SMM shipping data, the total global iron ore shipment volume of SMM is 35.33 million tons, an increase of 18% compared to the previous month; Among them, the shipment volume from Australia and Brazil has increased, while the arrival volume from major ports in China has increased by 1.74% month on month. On the demand side, the production of molten iron from blast furnace steel mills fluctuated narrowly this week, and the fundamentals of iron ore may show a dual strong pattern of supply and demand. Entering the Golden Nine Silver Ten stage, attention should be paid to the changes in steel mill profits and the expected fulfillment during peak seasons. In the short term, ore prices may fluctuate strongly.
