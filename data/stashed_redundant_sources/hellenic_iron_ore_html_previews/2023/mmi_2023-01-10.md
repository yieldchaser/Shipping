## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures rose by 1.21% today, the main contract closed 839.5. The traders' willingness to ship is general. The steel mills are active to purchase. The overall trading sentiment of the market is better. PBF at Shandong port dealt 828-840 yuan/mt, increase 8-20 yuan/mt; PBF at Tangshan port dealt 840 yuan/mt, increase 10-15 yuan/mt. According to SMM data, iron ore shipments in Brazil last week decreased by about 1.5 million tons month-on-month. At present, the southern hemisphere is in summer, and the impact of the Brazilian rainy season is still expected to be from January to March. The weekly arrival at Chinese ports will have a reduction of about 1 million tons. On the demand side, the impact of blast furnace maintenance by SMM this week was 184.4 tons, which was 73300 tons lower than that of last week. The iron ore demand is supported by the steel mills' replenishment before the Spring Festival. However, in view of the increased national regulation, it is expected that the iron ore price will still fluctuate in the short term.
