## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures market rose by 0.06%. the main contract I2309 closed 807. Most traders adopt separate quotations. Some steel mills tended to be wait-and-see, and purchasing enthusiasm is not high. total transactions remained insipid. PBF at Shandong port dealt 845-850 yuan/mt; decreased 5-10 yuan/mt over yesterday. PBF at Shandong port dealt 852-860 yuan/mt; increased 10-15 yuan/mt over yesterday. In the traditional off-season, the demand for steel continues to decline, but production remains high, inventory pressure increases, and high production may not be sustainable; The demand for iron ore will significantly decrease in the medium to long term due to the combination of environmental protection and production restrictions, as well as the suppression of coarse steel leveling policies. However, the current high production of molten iron provides strong support for ore prices, leading to wide fluctuations in iron ore prices.
