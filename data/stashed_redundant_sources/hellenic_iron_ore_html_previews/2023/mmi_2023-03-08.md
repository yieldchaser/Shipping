## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures rose by 0.83% today, the main contract closed at 912.The traders' willingness to ship is less.The steel mills are wait and see .The overall trading sentiment of the market is worse.PBF at Shandong port deal 918-920 yuan/mt,increase 1-4yuan/mt;PBF at Tangshan port deal 930-935 yuan/mt,increase 2 yuan/mt. As of today, according to SMM, the operating rate of blast furnace has remained stable on a month-on-month basis, the utilization rate of blast furnace capacity has increased on a month-on-month basis, and the daily average iron output of steel mills has increased slightly. The profits of the blast furnace steel plant have recovered and the enthusiasm for production has been supported to some extent. At present, the downstream demand continues to recover, the raw material inventory of steel mills is low, and the iron ore price just needs to support the ore price, and the iron ore price is still strong in the industrial chain. In general, iron ore prices will still be volatile.
