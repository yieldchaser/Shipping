## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures declined by 1.88 % today, the main contract closed at 836.5. The traders' willingness to ship is high. The steel mills are active to purchase. The overall trading sentiment of the market is more. PBF at Shandong port deal 900 yuan/mt, decrease 16 yuan/mt. SSF at Shandong port deal 760 yuan/mt, decrease 15 yuan/mt. PBF at Tangshan port deal 920 yuan/mt. Today's news fluctuated sharply, with reports of a meeting between relevant national departments and key futures companies to analyze and judge the iron ore market and price trends. The market is experiencing panic, and the iron ore market continues to weaken. But currently, there is no specific information flowing out of the meeting, and the pessimistic attitude may be digested in the short term. The current market is gradually entering the peak season, and the finished product table needs to see marginal improvement compared to the previous month, with average year-on-year improvement efforts. In the future, it is still necessary to pay attention to the changes in steel mill profits and finished product demand, and it is expected that ore prices will fluctuate strongly in the short term.
