## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures fall today. the main contract I2405 closed 955. an decrease of 0.37% throughout the day. some traders were negative to sell, steel mills purchased on demand. today's overall market transaction atmosphere in general. PBF at Shandong port dealt 998 yuan/mt; decreased 12 yuan/mt from last Friday. PBF at Tangshan port dealt 1009-1015 decreased 3-10 yuan/mt from last Friday. SMM shipping data shows that the total global shipment volume is 33.05 million tons, a decrease of 2% compared to the previous month. There has been a slight increase in shipments to Australia this cycle, but a significant decline in shipments to Brazil has dragged down global shipments slightly. The total arrival volume at the port was 27.1197 million tons, an increase of 2.77% compared to the previous month. The supply remains relatively loose. Adding to the poor performance of weekend CPI, PPI, and real estate data, market sentiment has weakened and iron ore prices have moved downwards. In the later stage, there will be little change in the fundamentals of iron ore, mainly focusing on macro policy news and regulatory risks.
