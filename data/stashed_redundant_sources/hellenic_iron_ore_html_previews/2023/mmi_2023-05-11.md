## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures declined by 3.46 % today, the main contract closed at 698.5. The traders' willingness to ship is general. The steel mills are not active to purchase. The overall trading sentiment of the market is less. PBF at Shandong port deal 765-778 yuan/mt, decrease 24-27 yuan/mt. PBF at Tangshan port deal 810 yuan/mt. Last night, the US CPI data was released below market expectations, but sentiment dissipated during the trading session last night. Today's macro data shows that the increase in social financing scale has boosted market confidence, and there was a slight but limited recovery in the end of the day. At present, the fundamentals of iron ore have not changed much. As of yesterday, the operating rate and capacity utilization rate of blast furnaces according to SMM statistics have decreased month on month, while the daily average iron production of sample steel mills has slightly decreased. Although iron ore has experienced a rebound in the early stage, considering the poor sustainability of demand for finished products, it may drive the resonance of raw materials downwards. Overall, iron ore prices will still fluctuate and operate.
