## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures open lower and go higher, an increase of 2.99% throughout the day, the main contract closed 895. The spot quotation in the morning almost kept steady. some traders were active to sell, and adopt separate quotations; but steel mills purchased on demand. today's overall market transaction atmosphere in general. PBF at Shandong port dealt 975-990 yuan/mt; increased 10 yuan/mt over yesterday. SSF at Shandong port dealt 700-715 yuan/mt; increased 10-15 yuan/mt over yesterday. PBF at Tangshan port dealt 1000-1010 yuan/mt; SSF at Tangshan port dealt 715-720 yuan/mt; Yesterday, six regions without the epidemic in Tangshan were unsealed, and other regions are expected to be unsealed in the near future. At that time, the shortage of raw materials in steel mills caused by traffic control may ease. The market is expected to focus on replenishment of inventory by steel companies after the end of the epidemic, boosting the price of ore. But given the recent continued low steel profits, iron ore prices will be held back.
