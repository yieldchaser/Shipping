## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore was was shocked sharply all the day.The main contract closed at 695.5,decreased of 2.59%. Traders are motivated to ship, steel mills are mainly on the sidelines, and there are more transactions in the market. PBF at Shandong port dealt 730-760 yuan/mt;decrease 10-40 yuan/mt than yesterday; SSF at Shandong port dealt 648-650 yuan/mt .PBF at Tangshan port dealt 765-780 yuan/mt,decrease 8yuan/mt than yesterday. The United States announced that the CPI in June rose by 9.1% year-on-year, and the economic recession was expected to increase. There were rumors of loan suspension in the domestic real estate sector, market confidence was hit, and iron ore futures fell again. According to the SMM survey, the blast furnace operating rate this week was 78.15%, up 0.22 percentage points from the previous week and down 3.19 percentage points from the previous month. The centralized maintenance since mid-June has ushered in the first batch of resumption of production, but since July 4th, 3 blast furnaces have actually resumed production. Due to the sluggish market conditions, some steel mills even plan to increase the number of blast furnaces to stop production, and the demand has not improved.
