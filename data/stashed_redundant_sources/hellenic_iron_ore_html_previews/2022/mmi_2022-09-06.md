## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore was was shocked and keep upward all the day. The main contract closed at 691.5, increased of 1.77%. Traders are generally motivated to ship. Steel mills are mainly on the sidelines, and market turnover continues to pick up. PBF at Shandong port dealt 730-743 yuan/mt, keep stable compare with yesterday. PBF at Tangshan dealt 749-753 yuan/mt, increase 3 yuan/mt than yesterday. Today spot prices follow futures rose. Fundamentals, iron ore is still in a strong situation of supply and demand, demand to follow the beginning of the improvement. At present, the daily output of molten iron has recovered to 2.2728 million tons, and according to SMM statistics, there are still 15 blast furnaces waiting to resume production in September. The market for the terminal demand is expected to turn better, according to the current fundamentals, the mine price rise has a certain support. But steel mill profit or limit iron ore price upside space, iron ore price is expected to be strong in the short term shock operation.
