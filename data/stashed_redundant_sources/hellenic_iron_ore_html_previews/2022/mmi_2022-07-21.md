## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore was was shocked sharply all the day.The main contract closed at 657,decreased of 0.3%. Traders are general motivated to ship, steel mills are mainly on the sidelines, and there are few transactions in the market.PBF at Shandong port dealt 660-665 yuan/mt;decrease35-45 yuan/mt than yesterday;55F at Tangshan port dealt 575 yuan/mt. According to the inventory data of the top ten ports tracked by SMM, the total inventory of the ten ports this week accumulated another 1.3% to 82 million tons, with the arrivals at the ports decreased by 150,000 tons compared with the previous period. And according to SMM statistics, the daily average impact of hot metal during blast furnace maintenance in steel mills has decreased by 11,000 tons compared with the previous day. A few steel mills are preparing to resume production, but there will be 4 blast furnaces scheduled for maintenance in the next week, and iron ore demand is still weak. It is expected that the price of iron ore will be weak in the short term.
