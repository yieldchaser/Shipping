## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore was was shocked and keep downward all the day. The main contract closed at 714, decreased of 1.38%. Traders are generally motivated to ship. Steel mills are more mainly on the sidelines, and market turnover continues to pick up. PBF at Shandong port dealt 745-760uan/mt, decrease 15-20 yuan/mt than last week; SSF at Shandong port dealt 645yuan/mt, decrease 5 yuan/mt than last week .PBF at Tangshan dealt 760 yuan/mt, decrease 15 yuan/mt than last week. From a macro perspective, the Fed's further interest rate hike is bad for commodity prices, affecting market confidence, and the iron ore futures market is down slightly. Basically speaking, SMM's global iron ore shipments totaled 28.03 million tons, a month on month decrease of 5.4%. Among them, the volume sent by Australia to China increased by 7.8% month on month, and the volume sent by Brazil to China decreased by 39.6% month on month. The supply side was narrowed, and there was a certain support from the ore price. This week, there are still some steel mills expecting to resume production, and the average daily molten iron output may rise slightly.
