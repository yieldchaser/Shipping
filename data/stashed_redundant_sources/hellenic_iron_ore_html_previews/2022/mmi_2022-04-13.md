## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures market falling trends by 1.45%. the main contract 2209 closed 885. The spot quotation in the morning almost kept steady. some traders quotation is still stronger, steel mills purchased on demand. total transactions remained insipid. PBF at Shandong port dealt 980-1000 yuan/mt; decreased 10-30 yuan/mt over yesterday. SSF at Shandong port dealt 710-730 yuan/mt; prices are mixed today over yesterday. PBF at Tangshan port dealt 990-1000 yuan/mt; SSF at Tangshan port dealt 730-735 yuan/mt. the same as yesterday. In the morning, policy supervision again out of the news, iron ore futures fell. Fundamental aspects at present as two big steel mills center Tangshan and unlock all domain of Binzhou, superposition state for transportation logistics implementation of the new policy, because the transportation lead to shortage of raw materials, is expected to become useful outbound problems such as difficult or will ease, steel mills returning, drive the steady increase of iron ore demand, gives the ore price must support, is expected to ore price shock run tomorrow.
