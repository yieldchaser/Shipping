## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures continued to fluctuate rangebound with a stronger bias today, with the 12601 closing at 788.5 yuan, up 1.81% from the previous trading day, and the increase expanded. Traders showed good willingness to sell; steel mills purchased as needed, and some mills with better steel sales had stronger restocking intentions, resulting in a moderate overall market transaction atmosphere. In Shandong, PB fines traded at 785-795 yuan, up 10-15 yuan/mt from last Friday; in Hebei, the mainstream transaction price for PB fines was 795-805 yuan/mt, also up 10-15 yuan/mt from last Friday. Last week, SMM global iron ore shipments totaled 33.3 million mt, down 190,000 mt WoW, a decrease of about 0.5%. SMM China iron ore arrivals amounted to 28.52 million mt, down 1.84 million mt WoW, a drop of about 6%. The simultaneous decline in shipments and arrivals slightly eased short-term iron ore supply pressure. In addition, recent pessimistic sentiment was released, and market sentiment improved, driving a rebound in futures. However, the pattern of strong supply and weak demand for iron ore is difficult to change, and iron ore prices remain under pressure.
