## Market Commentary

On October 20, DCE i2601 Iron Ore Futures Contract closed at 767 yuan/mt, down 0.58% from the previous trading day. Spot cargoes at major ports fell by 1–5 yuan/mt, with PB fines transaction prices in Shandong region ranging between 775–778 yuan/mt, down 1–5 yuan/mt WoW from last Friday, while transaction prices of PB fines at major ports in Hebei region stood at 780–785 yuan/mt, also down 1–5 yuan/mt WoW. Steel mills mainly procured based on demand for restocking, and market trading sentiment remained moderate. Fundamentally, narrowing steel mill profits, coupled with expectations of tighter environmental protection-driven production restrictions in some regions, have strengthened mills' willingness to conduct maintenance, weakening support for iron ore demand. Supply side, both shipments and port arrivals increased significantly this week, and supply is expected to remain relatively ample in the near term. In the short term, with fundamental support weakening, iron ore prices are projected to fluctuate.
