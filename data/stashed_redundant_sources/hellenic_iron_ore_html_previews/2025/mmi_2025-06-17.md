## Market Commentary

Today, DCE iron ore futures pulled back from highs, with the most-traded contract I2509 closing at 699, down 0.07% for the day. Traders sold goods in line with market conditions, while steel mills adopted a wait-and-see attitude and purchased as needed. The market transaction atmosphere was average. The mainstream transaction prices of PB fines in Shandong were around 715 yuan/mt, down 5 yuan/mt from yesterday's prices. The transaction prices of PB fines in Tangshan were around 730 yuan/mt, down 5 yuan/mt from yesterday's prices. According to SMM's weekly data on the impact of blast furnace maintenance, the impact of blast furnace maintenance on pig iron production this week was 1.1184 million mt, a decrease of 14,500 mt WoW. Pig iron production this week is expected to stop falling and stabilize, providing short-term support for ore prices. However, it is also necessary to be vigilant about the rising expectations of negative feedback triggered by high production levels during the off-season.
