## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures fluctuated in the doldrums throughout the day, with the most-traded contract I2509 closing at 702.5, down 0.43% for the day. Traders' willingness to sell was moderate. Steel mills adopted a cautious wait-and-see attitude, with a moderate amount of low-level inquiries. The market transaction atmosphere was moderate. The mainstream transaction prices of PB fines in the Shandong region were around 700 yuan/mt, basically stable compared to yesterday's prices. The transaction prices of PB fines in the Tangshan region were around 710-715 yuan/mt, also basically stable compared to yesterday's prices. According to the SMM survey, on June 25, the operating rate of blast furnaces at 242 steel mills surveyed by SMM was 87.96%, up 0.26 percentage points MoM.
