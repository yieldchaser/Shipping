## Market Commentary

Today, iron ore futures continued to hold up well, with the most-traded contract I2601 closing at 777 yuan, up 0.39% from the previous day. Spot cargoes at ports rose by 3 yuan compared to the previous working day, with PB fines in Shandong transacted at 777-783 yuan, unchanged from yesterday, and PB fines in Hebei transacted at 793-796 yuan, up 3 yuan from the previous day. Overall market transactions were moderate, with steel mills purchasing mainly for rigid demand and traders selling at appropriate prices. Industrial data today showed that SMM hot-rolled coil inventories continued to build up, while construction materials saw slight destocking, though overall inventory pressure remained. On the news front, the meeting is expected to conclude soon, and the upcoming release of content may impact market sentiment. In the short term, ore prices are likely to fluctuate rangebound.
