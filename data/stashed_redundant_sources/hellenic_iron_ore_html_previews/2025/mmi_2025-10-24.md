## Market Commentary

Today, iron ore futures prices experienced sideways weakness, with the main contract DCE I2601 closing at 771, down by 0.58% compared to yesterday. Port spot prices declined by 2-5 yuan from the previous trading day. In Shandong, PB powder transactions ranged between 775-780 yuan per ton, a decrease of 2-5 yuan/ton from yesterday. In Hebei, transaction prices for PB powder ranged between 790-795 yuan per ton, down by 5 yuan/ton. Steel mills' procurement activity has weakened, and traders are primarily selling at reasonable prices. Overall trading volume remains moderate.
