## Market Commentary

Iron ore futures showed slight strength today, with the most-traded contract I2601 closing at 771.5, up 0.06% from the previous trading day. Traders sold at market prices, while steel mills purchased as needed, with inquiries declining from yesterday. Market trading sentiment remained moderate. In Shandong, mainstream transaction prices for PB fines stood at 765-770 yuan/mt, up 0-3 yuan/mt from yesterday. In Tangshan, PB fines traded around 780-785 yuan/mt, up 5-10 yuan/mt from yesterday.
