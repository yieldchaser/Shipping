## Market Commentary

Today, Dalian iron ore futures opened higher but moved downwards, fluctuating in the doldrums during the day. The most-traded contract I2509 eventually closed at 789, with a daily decline of 0.44%. Traders sold goods according to market conditions; steel mills adopted a wait-and-see attitude, with some purchasing at lower prices. The market transaction atmosphere was average. The mainstream transaction prices of PB fines in Shandong were around 774 yuan/mt, down 6 yuan/mt from yesterday's price; the transaction prices of PB fines in Tangshan were around 785-790 yuan/mt, down 5-10 yuan/mt from yesterday's price.
