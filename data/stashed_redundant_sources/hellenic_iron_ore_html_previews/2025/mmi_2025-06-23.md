## Market Commentary

After opening, DCE Iron Ore futures rose slightly and then pulled back slightly during the session. The most-traded contract I2509 eventually closed at 706, up 0.5% for the day. Traders sold goods according to market conditions. At the beginning of the week, steel mills mainly adopted a wait-and-see attitude and made fewer inquiries. The market transaction atmosphere was average. The mainstream transaction prices of PB fines in the Shandong region were around 710-713 yuan/mt, basically flat compared to last Friday. The transaction price of PB fines in the Tangshan region was around 720 yuan/mt, also basically flat compared to last Friday.
