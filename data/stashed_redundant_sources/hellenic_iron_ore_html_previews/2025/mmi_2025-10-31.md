## Market Commentary

Today, iron ore futures were in the doldrums, with DCE I2601 contract closing at 800 yuan, down 0.56% from the previous trading day. Mainstream spot cargoes at ports fell 0-5 yuan/mt compared to yesterday. In Shandong, transaction prices for PB fines were around 800 yuan/mt, down 0-5 yuan/mt from the previous day; in Hebei, transaction prices for PB fines were 805-810 yuan/mt, down 5 yuan/mt from the previous trading day. Trading activity in the spot market was mediocre today. Steel mills mainly procured based on rigid demand, with inquiries being cautious; traders showed moderate enthusiasm in offering, and trading volume was relatively small.
