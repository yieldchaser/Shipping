## Market Commentary

Today, iron ore prices shifted from weak to strong, with the main contract I2605 closing at 769, up 1.85% from the previous trading day. Traders showed strong willingness to support prices, while steel mills continued to press for lower procurement prices. Market trading activity remained subdued. In Shandong, the transaction price for PB fines ranged from 780 to 790 yuan per ton, up 3-8 yuan per ton from the previous day. In Hebei, the transaction price for PB fines ranged from 795 to 798 yuan per ton, up 8-10 yuan per ton from the previous day. According to an SMM survey, as of December 10, the blast furnace operating rate at 242 surveyed steel mills was 85.23%, down 0.68 percentage points from the previous period. The daily hot metal output of the surveyed steel mills was 2.3619 million tons, a decrease of 18,100 tons from the previous period. Demand for iron ore continued to decline. However, today's macroeconomic factors, such as the real estate interest subsidy policy, stimulated market sentiment and drove futures prices higher. Considering the ongoing expectations surrounding recent macroeconomic meetings, it is anticipated that iron ore prices may maintain a relatively strong and fluctuating trend within the week.
