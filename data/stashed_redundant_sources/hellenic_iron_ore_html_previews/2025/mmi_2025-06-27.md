## Market Commentary

Today, Dalian iron ore futures continued to rebound, with the most-traded contract I2509 closing at 716.5 yuan/mt, up 1.99% for the day. Traders were actively shipping goods. As the weekend approached, purchase willingness of steel mills weakened, but better sales of finished steel in the morning drove some demand growth. The market transaction atmosphere was average. The mainstream transaction prices of PB fines in the Shandong region were around 706-710 yuan/mt, rising slightly by 10 yuan/mt compared to yesterday's prices. In the Tangshan region, the transaction prices of PB fines were around 720-725 yuan/mt, up by 10-15 yuan/mt compared to yesterday's prices. This week, the expansion of steel mill profits drove a rebound in pig iron production, and iron ore demand was robust, providing rigid support for ore prices. However, affected by nationwide rainfall, the demand for finished steel may weaken.
