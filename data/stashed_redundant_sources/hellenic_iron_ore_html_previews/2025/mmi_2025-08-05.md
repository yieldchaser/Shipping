## Market Commentary

After opening, DCE iron ore futures first fell and then rose. The most-traded contract i2509 eventually closed at 790.5, with a daily increase of 0.76%. Traders were enthusiastic about selling, while steel mills adopted a wait-and-see attitude, with weak purchase willingness. The market transaction atmosphere was average. The mainstream transaction prices of PB fines in the Shandong region were around 773-776 yuan/mt, up 5-10 yuan/mt from last Friday. The transaction prices of PB fines in the Tangshan region were around 780-790 yuan/mt, rising slightly by 5-7 yuan/mt from last Friday.
