## Market Commentary

Today, the most-traded iron ore futures contract I2601 continued to be in the doldrums, closing at 769, down 0.19% from yesterday. Traders showed a moderate willingness to sell; steel mills purchased as needed, with few inquiries. The market transaction atmosphere was average. Due to the extended vehicle control period, some steel mills have increased their transportation of materials from ports to their premises. In Shandong, the mainstream transaction prices for PB fines were around 765-768 yuan/mt, down 2-5 yuan/mt from yesterday; in Tangshan, the transaction prices for PB fines were around 775-780 yuan/mt, down 5 yuan/mt from yesterday.
