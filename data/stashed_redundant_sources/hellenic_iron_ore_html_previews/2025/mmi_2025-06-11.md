## Market Commentary

Today, the Dalian Commodity Exchange iron ore futures market held up well, with the most-traded contract l2509 closing at 707, up 1% for the day. Traders showed good enthusiasm for selling, while steel mills adopted a cautious wait-and-see attitude, with most conducting tenders as usual. The market transaction sentiment was moderate. In Shandong, the mainstream transaction prices of PB fines were around 725-730 yuan/mt, rising slightly by 5-10 yuan/mt from yesterday's prices. In Tangshan, the transaction prices of PB fines were around 740-745 yuan/mt, also rising slightly by 5-10 yuan/mt from yesterday's prices. Optimistic signals from the China-US-London negotiations boosted market sentiment, driving the futures market to rise amid fluctuations. SMM data showed that the decline in pig iron production narrowed this week, and the blast furnace operating rate remained resilient.
