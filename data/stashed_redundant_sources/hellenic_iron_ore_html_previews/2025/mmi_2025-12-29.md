## Market Commentary

Iron ore prices experienced strong and volatile movements today. The main contract I2605 ultimately closed at 796.5, marking an increase of 2.58% compared to the previous trading day. This represents the largest gain of the fourth quarter. Traders demonstrated moderate enthusiasm for shipments, while steel mills showed limited willingness to procure, with some only purchasing as needed. Market transactions remained subdued. In the Shandong region, the transaction price for PB fines ranged from 795 to 803 yuan, reflecting an increase of 5-10 yuan per ton compared to last Friday. In the Hebei region, PB fines transacted between 812 and 820 yuan per ton, also up by 5-10 yuan per ton from last Friday. According to SMM shipping data, arrivals this week continued to rise by 2.2%. The supply pressure on iron ore remains on an upward trajectory. However, news emerged today regarding potential production cuts at a Brazilian pellet plant. Additionally, the Ministry of Finance's meeting over the weekend indicated that national subsidies will continue in 2026, boosting market sentiment. Coupled with increased market expectations for a rebound in hot metal production in January and pre-holiday inventory restocking, these factors collectively drove a notable rise in iron ore prices.
