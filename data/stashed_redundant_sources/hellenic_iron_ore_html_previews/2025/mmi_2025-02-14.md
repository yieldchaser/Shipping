## Market Commentary

Today, iron ore futures turned from strong to weak, with a significant decline in the afternoon. The most-traded i2505 contract finally closed at 810.5 yuan/mt, down 0.98% for the day. Traders sold goods based on market trends, while steel mills restocked as needed. Market transactions were moderate. In Shandong, mainstream transaction prices for PB fines were at 800-805 yuan/mt, up 0-3 yuan/mt from yesterday; in Tangshan, transaction prices for PB fines were around 820-825 yuan/mt, relatively stable compared to yesterday. Over the weekend, the impact of the cyclone in Australia temporarily ended, and iron ore shipments are expected to return to normal next week. Short-term supply support for iron ore has weakened, and a loose supply pattern is expected to continue in the future. Considering the current high valuation, the short-term upward momentum is expected to remain weak, and the market may continue to fluctuate rangebound next week.
