## Market Commentary

Today, Dalian iron ore futures were in the doldrums today, with the most-traded contract I2509 closing at 802.5, down 1.11% for the day. Traders showed moderate willingness to sell, while steel mills restocked as needed. Market transaction sentiment remained tepid. In Shandong, mainstream transaction prices for PB fines were around 780-784 yuan/mt, down 0-5 yuan/mt from yesterday. In Tangshan, PB fines traded at 795-800 yuan/mt, also down 0-5 yuan/mt.
