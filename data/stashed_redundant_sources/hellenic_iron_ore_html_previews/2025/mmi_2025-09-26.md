## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures were in the doldrums today. The most-traded contract I2601 closed at 790 yuan/mt, down 1.74% WoW. Traders showed moderate willingness to sell, while steel mills' wait-and-see sentiment intensified, with some mills restocking at low levels. Market transaction activity remained moderate. In Shandong, mainstream transaction prices for old PB fines were around 780-785 yuan/mt, down 10-13 yuan/mt from the previous day; in Tangshan, PB fines traded at 790-795 yuan/mt, up 10-15 yuan/mt from the previous day.
