## Market Commentary

Iron ore futures traded firmer today. The DCE most-active contract i2701 settled at RMB 710.5/mt, up 0.42% from the previous session. Spot prices at Qingdao Port rose RMB 5-9/mt from the previous trading day. Traders were active in offering, while mills showed limited enquiry, leaving overall spot transaction volumes moderate as of press time. According to the latest SMM statistics, total iron ore inventories across China's 35 major ports stood at 146.78 million mt, up 390,000 mt week-on-week — a modest build. Average daily port outbound volumes edged down 7,000 mt to 3.088 million mt. The data show that while both arrivals and port outflows declined during the period, the expected increase in arrivals outpaces the extent of any recovery in outbound shipments. Meanwhile, pig iron output remains unsteady amid fluctuating blast furnace maintenance, and under a pattern of strong supply against weak demand, iron ore inventories are likely to tilt towards accumulation. Accordingly, with the news flow in a vacuum and capital largely on the sidelines, iron ore prices are likely to trend weaker.
