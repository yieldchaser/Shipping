## Market Commentary

Today, the DCE iron ore futures trended higher today. DCE's most-traded I2609 contract closed at 706 yuan/mt, up 0.93% from the previous trading session. Spot prices at Qingdao Port rose 3-8 yuan/mt from the prior trading day. Traders quoted actively, while steel mills purchased as needed, leaving overall spot trading moderate. The latest SMM survey data showed blast furnace operating rates at steel mills at 89.04%, up 0.11 percentage points MoM. Blast furnace capacity utilization rate was 88.80%, down 0.1 percentage points MoM. Daily average hot metal production at sampled mills stood at 2.4058 million mt, down 2,900 mt MoM. Due to increased maintenance at steel mills, pig iron production next week is expected to edge down, but given the short maintenance cycles, short-term iron ore demand remains fairly stable. On the news front, as the false invoice incidents continue to unfold, credit institutions are expected to tighten risk exposure, potentially hampering liquidity in short-term iron ore trade. On balance, short-term iron ore prices are likely to move sideways.
