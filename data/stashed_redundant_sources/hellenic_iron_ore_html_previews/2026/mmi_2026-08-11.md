## Market Commentary

Today, the iron ore market showed a relatively strong performance on the futures market. The DCE main contract 12609 closed at 721.5 yuan/ton, up 1.26% from the previous trading session. Spot prices at Qingdao Port rose by an average of about 3-6 yuan/ton from the previous trading day. Traders were generally active in quoting prices in line with market conditions, while steel mills purchased according to their needs, with overall spot trading volume remaining moderate so far. In the short term, changes in the fundamental supply-demand balance of iron ore itself are relatively small, though there is an expectation of a modest uptick in demand. According to SMM statistics, the hot metal impact volume from blast furnace maintenance this week is 1.4057 million tons, down 34,400 tons from last week's maintenance impact. Next week, the hot metal impact volume is expected to be 1.3498 million tons, down 55,900 tons from this week. At the same time, coke and coking coal futures performed strongly today, with market sentiment turning optimistic toward the ferrous metals complex, which in turn boosted iron ore futures prices in tandem. Based on a comprehensive assessment, iron ore prices are likely to continue their range-bound fluctuation with a modest upward bias in the short term.
