## Market Commentary

Today, the iron ore market showed strong performance on the Dalian Commodity Exchange (DCE), with the benchmark contract i2609 ultimately closing at 762 RMB/ton, up 1.13%. At Qingdao port, spot prices edged up by about 0-2 RMB/ton from the previous trading day, as trading volumes remained thin. Traders' offer enthusiasm was generally moderate, while steel mills showed weak purchasing interest. Spot transactions so far today have been limited. SMM survey data confirm continued weakness in iron ore demand. According to the survey results, the blast furnace operating rate across 242 Chinese steel mills this week stood at 89.79%, and the average daily hot metal output of sampled mills reached 2.4382 million tons, down 2,400 tons from the previous week. The fundamental support for iron ore prices continues to weaken. On the news front, some mining firms are reportedly set to implement vessel loading restrictions at ports as early as tomorrow, sparking intense market sentiment that drove up futures prices. However, such factors are unlikely to provide sustained support for iron ore prices over the longer term. Considering supply-demand dynamics and sentiment, iron ore prices are likely to remain firm in the short term until sentiment fades and prices revert to fundamental drivers.
