## Market Commentary

Iron ore futures firmed modestly today. The DCE most-active contract I2701 settled at RMB 714/mt, up 0.85% from the previous session.Spot prices at Qingdao Port rose RMB 2-3/mt from the previous trading day. Traders were reasonably active in offering, while mill purchases were largely restocking on a just-in-need basis, leaving overall spot transaction volumes moderate.Iron ore fundamentals remained steady this week, with demand likely to contract slightly in the near term. According to SMM statistics, hot metal output affected by blast furnace maintenance came in at 1.3317 million mt this week, an increase of 20,600 mt from the previous week. Next week, the volume of hot metal affected by blast furnace maintenance is expected to reach 1.3682 million mt, up 36,500 mt from this week. On the news front, the two sides in the Port Hedland strike have yet to reach agreement on pay. The strike has now run for a month; although the impact on overall shipments has been limited, the unfolding situation has lent some bullish support to market sentiment. On balance, iron ore prices may lean bullish in the near term under the guidance of sentiment, though the bearish pattern over the medium to long term remains unchanged.
