## Market Commentary

Today, the iron ore futures moved steadily today. The most-traded DCE 12609 contract closed at 720.5 yuan/mt, up 0.14% from the previous session. Spot prices at Qingdao Port fell by 0-2 yuan/mt from the previous trading day. Traders mostly priced based on market conditions, while steel mills mainly procured on a rigid demand basis, leaving total spot volumes relatively low so far. Iron ore demand support weakened slightly this week. SMM data showed the blast furnace operating rate across 242 steel mills at 88.91%, down 0.13 percentage points WoW. The sample mills' daily average hot metal output was 2.4005 million mt, down 5,300 mt WoW. On the news front, rumors remain frequent and hard to verify. The market focused on progress in long-term contract negotiations with mines, with most funds staying on the sidelines awaiting further confirmation of direction. Overall, ore prices may continue to consolidate in the near term, but iron ore's own fundamentals currently cannot drive a trending move, leaving the overall market still dependent on news-driven catalysts.
