## Market Commentary

The continuous iron ore contract opened with a gap up today, showing strength in the early morning but weakening in the afternoon. The main contract, I2605, eventually closed at 811.5 CNY/ton, up 2.33% from the previous trading session. Meanwhile, spot prices increased by 5-10 CNY/ton compared to the previous trading day. Traders' enthusiasm for quoting was average, while steel mills restocked based on immediate needs, with fewer inquiries. Overall, spot market transactions were sluggish. According to an SMM survey, total iron ore inventory at 35 major ports nationwide reached 155.41 million tons this week, a week-on-week increase of 610,000 tons, indicating an improvement in the pace of inventory accumulation. Meanwhile, the average daily port throughput was 2.53 million tons, down 20,000 tons from the previous period. Looking ahead, the moderating pace of iron ore inventory accumulation suggests a pick-up in demand. Concurrently, due to structural adjustments on the supply side, rigid demand for iron ore has concentrated on certain varieties, causing localized supply shortages and forming strong bottom support. Coupled with persistent bullish market sentiment, and with top-side pressure easing alongside robust support from below, iron ore prices are expected to trend stronger in the short term.
