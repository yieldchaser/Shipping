## Market Commentary

Iron ore futures traded firmer today. The DCE most-active contract i2701 settled at RMB 734.5/mt, up 1.17% from the previous trading day. Spot prices at Qingdao Port rose by an average of RMB 1-9/mt. Traders were only moderately active in offering, while mills were largely on the sidelines, leaving overall spot trading activity subdued. On fundamentals, iron ore supply edged higher. According to SMM observations, global iron ore shipments totalled 34.45 million mt as of the end of last week, up 14% week-on-week and broadly flat year-on-year on a cumulative basis. Shipments from Australia, Brazil and other non-mainstream countries all posted modest gains, with the exception of South Africa. Over the same period, China's total iron ore arrivals came in at 26.91 million mt, up 19% week-on-week and up 4.2% year-on-year on a cumulative basis. Elsewhere, SMM understands that trading in iron ore call options has been fairly active in overseas markets, reflecting continued confidence among funds in the outlook. On balance, iron ore prices in the near term are likely to be driven more by capital flows and to extend a firmer trend on sentiment, though developments in the negotiations still warrant attention.
