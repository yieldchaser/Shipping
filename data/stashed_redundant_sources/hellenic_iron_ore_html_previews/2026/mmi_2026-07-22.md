## Market Commentary

Today, the DCE iron ore futures weakened today. The most-traded DCE i2609 contract closed at 739.5 yuan/mt, down 1.00%. The spot price at Qingdao Port fell around 4-8 yuan/mt from the previous trading day. Traders showed moderate enthusiasm in offering prices, while steel mills remained largely on the sidelines. As of now, spot trading volumes were moderate. According to this week's SMM survey, the capacity utilization rate of blast furnaces at sampled steel mills was 89.49%, down 0.50 percentage point WoW. Daily average hot metal production at sampled steel mills was 2.4247 million mt, down 13,500 mt WoW. Although this round of environmental protection-driven production restrictions was weaker than expected, hot metal production is expected to continue its downward trend. Meanwhile, environmental and safety inspection factors have not yet dissipated, and some steel mills may have temporary maintenance plans. Overall, blast furnace hot metal production is likely to keep declining in the short term, and iron ore demand is expected to remain weak. Therefore, iron ore prices are expected to consolidate with a bearish bias in the short term
