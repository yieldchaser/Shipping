## Market Commentary

The DCE iron ore futures market strengthened in a volatile manner today, with the main contract 12609 ultimately closing at 783.5 RMB/ton, up 0.45% from the previous trading session. Port spot prices rose slightly by 3-5 RMB/ton compared to yesterday. Traders showed moderate enthusiasm for quoting; steel mills purchased on a need-to basis; as the weekend approached, overall market trading activity was subdued. The transaction price for PB fines at Shandong ports was 754 RMB/ton. At Caofeidian Port, PB fines traded at 760 RMB/ton, while Jimblebar fines traded at 723 RMB/ton.
