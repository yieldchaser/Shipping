## Market Commentary

Today, the DCE iron ore futures moved steadily today. The most-traded DCE I2609 contract closed at 716.5 yuan/mt, up 0.35% from the previous session.Spot prices at Qingdao Port averaged a decline of about 0–2 yuan/mt from the previous trading day. Trader activity was moderate, steel mills purchased as needed, and overall spot transactions were modest. This week, iron ore exhibited a supply-demand weakness trend. SMM's latest statistics show that iron ore inventory at 35 major ports nationwide totaled 146.39 million mt, down 420,000 mt WoW. Meanwhile, the daily average port pick-up volume edged down 45,000 mt to 3.095 million mt. The supply side, affected by earlier shipping pace and weather factors, saw port arrivals edge down. On the demand side, some blast furnaces underwent maintenance this week, and SMM's daily average pig iron production dropped to 2.4058 million mt, dragging iron ore demand lower. In terms of fundamentals, shortterm iron ore prices may lean toward consolidation.
