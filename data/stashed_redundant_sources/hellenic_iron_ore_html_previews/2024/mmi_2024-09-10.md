## Market Commentary

Today, iron ore fluctuated upward and plunged at the close. The most-traded contract I2501 finally settled at 675 yuan/mt, dropping 0.07% throughout the day. Traders were fairly active in offloading their stocks. After the steel mills lowered prices, their cautious sentiment grew stronger, leading to a reduction in purchase willingness. Today's market trading atmosphere was average. The transaction price of PB fines in Shandong was around 678-690 yuan/mt, down 0-2 yuan/mt compared to yesterday; in Tangshan, the transaction price of PB fines was around 692-705 yuan/mt, down 0-8 yuan/mt compared to yesterday.
