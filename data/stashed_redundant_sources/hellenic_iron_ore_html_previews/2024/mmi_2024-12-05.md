## Market Commentary

Today, iron ore futures opened higher but moved downwards, continuing to fluctuate downward throughout the day. The most-traded contract I2501 finally closed at 800.5 yuan/mt, down 1.17% for the day. Traders' enthusiasm for selling has somewhat declined; steel mills' purchase willingness is moderate, mainly purchasing as needed. Today's market trading atmosphere was average. In Shandong, the mainstream transaction prices for PB fines were 785 yuan/mt, down 5-10 yuan/mt from yesterday; in Tangshan, the transaction prices for PB fines were 800-810 yuan/mt, down 0-10 yuan/mt from yesterday.
