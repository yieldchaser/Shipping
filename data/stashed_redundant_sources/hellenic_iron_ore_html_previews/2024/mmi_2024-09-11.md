## Market Commentary

Today, iron ore opened low and moved higher, fluctuating upward throughout the day. The most-traded contract l2501 closed at 694, up 1.09% over the day. Traders showed a good willingness to sell, while speculative demand increased; some steel mills had restocking needs, enhancing their purchase willingness. Market transaction sentiment improved today. In the Shandong region, PB fines traded at around 690-695 yuan/mt, up by 5-10 yuan/mt from yesterday; in the Tangshan region, PB fines traded at around 700-710 yuan/mt, up by 5-10 yuan/mt from yesterday.
