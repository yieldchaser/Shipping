## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures continued to fall in a day , the main contract closed 825 , an decrease of 1.84% throughout the day; some traders were negative to sell; Some Some steel mills tended to be wait-and-see, and purchasing enthusiasm is not high. total transactions remained insipid. PBF at Shandong port dealt 823-830 yuan/mt; decreased 5-10 yuan/mt over yesterday. PBF at Tangshan port dealt 850-855 yuan/mt; decreased 5 yuan/mt over yesterday. According to SMM research, on June 5th, the operating rate of blast furnaces according to SMM statistics was 93.91%, an increase of 0.3% compared to the previous month. The daily average iron production of the sample steel plant was 2.2821 million tons, an increase of 4500 tons compared to the previous period. The production of pig iron in this cycle is still showing an upward trend. But the increase is relatively small. The overall demand for iron ore is still acceptable, which provides some support for ore prices. However, the current market sentiment is weak, and it is expected that short-term mining prices will face upward pressure and downward support, with narrow fluctuations running.
