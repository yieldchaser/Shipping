## Market Commentary

Today, Dalian iron ore futures continued to decline sharply, with the most-traded contract I2501 closing at 678.5 yuan/mt, down 2.58% for the day. Some traders halted offers. Steel mills restocked as needed. Market trading sentiment was relatively average today. In Shandong, PB fines traded at around 680-685 yuan/mt, down 10 yuan/mt from yesterday; in Tangshan, PB fines traded at 700 yuan/mt, down 5-10 yuan/mt from yesterday. The apparent demand for the five major steel products announced today was almost flat, with demand recovery falling short of expectations, while supply increased. This deepened market concerns about future demand decline, further worsening market sentiment. In the short term, market sentiment appears overly pessimistic, and fundamentals have not shown significant improvement. It is expected that iron ore prices will fluctuate at low levels.
