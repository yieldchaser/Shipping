## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures fall today , the main contract closed 831.5 , an decrease of 2.23% throughout the day; The short-term bearish sentiment in the market is strong, and the mentality of merchants in quoting has weakened, often following the market trend; Steel mills have fewer inquiries and mainly focus on on-demand procurement.
