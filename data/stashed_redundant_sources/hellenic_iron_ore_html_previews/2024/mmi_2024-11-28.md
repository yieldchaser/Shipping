## Market Commentary

Today, iron ore futures moved downwards after a higher opening, with the most-traded contract I2501 finally closing at 786.5 yuan/mt,maintaining stability throughout the day. Traders sold goods according to market trends, while steel mills restocked as needed. The market trading atmosphere was average today. In Shandong, mainstream transaction prices for PB fines were 775-780 yuan/mt, unchanged from yesterday; in Tangshan, transaction prices for PB fines were 785-790 yuan/mt, down by 0-5 yuan/mt from yesterday. Today, the apparent demand for rebar slightly decreased, while inventory increased slightly. The demand for construction materials has entered the oﬀ-season, and with the impact of future weather conditions, demand is expected to weaken further. Additionally, rumors of production control at a certain steel mill have led to market expectations of weaker iron ore demand, suppressing ore prices at low levels. Given that some steel mills have already started stockpiling for the Chinese New Year, the forward spot market remains active, providing some support for iron ore prices. Iron ore prices are expected to strengthen again after a short-term consolidation.
