## Market Commentary

DCE iron ore futures rose largely today, the main contract closed 908, an increase of 1.68% throughout the day; Today's overall transaction atmosphere is still good, with PB powder prices in Shandong region at 880-895 yuan/ton, an increase of 5-10 yuan/ton compared to yesterday. According to SMM statistics, the impact of blast furnace maintenance on iron production this week (May 18-24) is 1.0622 million tons, a decrease of 33,200 tons compared to last week. The impact of blast furnace maintenance on iron production next week (May 25-31) is expected to be 1.0554 million tons, a decrease of 6,800 tons compared to this week. Iron production continues to rise, which may continue to drive demand for iron ore, leading to strong fluctuations in iron ore prices in the short term.
