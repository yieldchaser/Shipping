## Market Commentary

Iron ore futures fluctuated strongly, with the most-traded contract I2501 closing at 710.5 yuan/mt, up 1.21% for the day. Traders followed the market trend to sell. Steel mills were cautiously observing, mainly making small and frequent purchases. The overall market transaction atmosphere was relatively average today. In Shandong, PB fines traded at 705-710 yuan/mt, basically flat compared to yesterday's prices; in Tangshan, PB fines traded at 720-725 yuan/mt, up 0-5 yuan/mt from yesterday's prices. Recently, although market pessimism has eased somewhat and the market has rebounded slightly, pig iron production remains on a downward trend, and end-user demand has not improved, resulting in insufficient momentum for iron ore prices to rise. Under the pressure of negative feedback, iron ore prices fluctuated at low levels.
