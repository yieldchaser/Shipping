## Market Commentary

Today, iron ore futures opened low and closed high, with a significant increase throughout the day. The most-traded contract I2501 closed at 699.5 yuan/mt, up 4.64% for the day. Traders were moderately active in selling, and speculative transactions slightly increased; steel mills were more cautious, with some restocking as needed. The market transaction atmosphere was average today. In Shandong, PB fines traded at around 685-705 yuan/mt, up 20-35 yuan/mt from yesterday; in Tangshan, PB fines traded at 710-720 yuan/mt, also up 20-35 yuan/mt from yesterday. According to SMM statistics, blast furnace maintenance reduced production by 93,300 mt this week, a significant decrease WoW. The reduction is expected to continue expanding next week, boosting iron ore demand. Additionally, most steel mills needed to restock before the holiday, providing strong support for ore prices. Meanwhile, the central bank announced a series of growth-stabilizing real estate policies today, including lowering down payments and existing mortgage rates, which boosted market macro sentiment and led to a significant rise in futures. In the short term, ore prices may continue to fluctuate upward.
