## Market Commentary

Today, iron ore futures initially rose and then fell back from highs, experiencing wide fluctuations throughout the day. The most-traded contract I2501 finally closed at 754 yuan/mt, down 0.53% for the day. Traders sold goods according to market trends. Steel mills were cautious and their purchase willingness weakened. The overall market transaction atmosphere was average today. In Shandong, PB fines traded at 745-748 yuan/mt, falling back 0-7 yuan/mt from yesterday's highs; in Tangshan, PB fines traded at 760 yuan/mt, falling back 0-5 yuan/mt from yesterday's highs. As of August 30, the total inventory of 35 ports tracked by SMM was 150.47 million mt, up 3.34 million mt MoM and up 32.27 million mt YoY. The daily average port pick-up volume of imported ore was 2.87 million mt, down 30,000 mt MoM and down 150,000 mt YoY. Recently, ore prices have started to rebound, putting pressure on steel mill profits, leading them to restock as needed, which caused a slight decline in port pick-up volume. Port inventories have surged again, suppressing the rise in ore prices. However, considering the expected improvement in future end-user demand, iron ore demand may increase, and ore prices are expected to strengthen again next week.
