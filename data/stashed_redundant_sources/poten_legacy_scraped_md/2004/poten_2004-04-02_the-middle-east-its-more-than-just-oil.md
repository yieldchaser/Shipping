# The Middle East It's More Than Just Oil!

### The Middle East

It's More Than Just Oil!

The Middle East has always been an important and strategic trading region throughout world history. Today the region remains a critical part of the world economy. In addition to its large consumer base, the region contains significant natural reserves - particularly oil and gas. The world consumes 78 million barrels of oil per day and The Middle East produces about 30% of that total. Giant reserves found in the early 1950s in Saudi Arabia and surrounding nations have rendered the Middle East and oil synonymous in energy parlance. Increasing Russian and West African supplies to Europe and North America have recently shifted some of the Middle East supply focus to the Far East with incremental exports to China and India growing regionally, but the west is an important customer base. In total, the world relies on the Middle East for 20-25% of its daily crude oil imports and with 65% of the world's estimated reserves, it will remain a huge component of future energy requirements.

Causes, Consequences and Tanker Owners The tanker business is a consequence of a cause. The economic well being for tanker owners, in particular VLCC owners, whose aggregate deadweight capacity is about half of the world tanker fleet, is dependent on Middle East exports. Middle East exports are also a consequence of a cause: U.S. and Asian oil import demand. The demand for crude is there, and the good news for owners has always been that the supply is a long way from the market.
