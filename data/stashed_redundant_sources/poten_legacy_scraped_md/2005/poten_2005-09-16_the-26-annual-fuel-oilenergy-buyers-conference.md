# The 26 Annual Fuel Oil/Energy Buyers' Conference

### The 26 th  Annual Fuel Oil/Energy Buyers' Conference

On October 23-25, the 26 th Annual Fuel Oil/Energy Buyers' Conference will be held at the Eden Roc Renaissance Resort & Spa at Miami Beach, Florida. The Conference is an annual event that explores the international heavy fuel oil, natural gas, and electricity markets. Attendees are representatives from a wide variety of companies in North and South America, Europe, and Asia.

The principal areas of interest to be covered at the conference are:

### ¾  the impact of current and proposed environmental legislation and

### international regulations on heavy fuel oil and bunkers

¾ the changing usages of fuel oil in the world, with an outlook for the global

### economy, crude oil, and heavy fuel oil prices

### ¾  hedging's role in risk management and its principal techniques

¾ the changing character of today's heavy fuel oils in terms of properties and

### specifications

In addition to providing a forum for heavy fuel oil buyers, sellers, and service providers to meet one another and discuss issues of mutual concern, there is also a planned conference golf tournament at the Miami Beach Golf Club. Further information on the Conference is in the accompanying file.
