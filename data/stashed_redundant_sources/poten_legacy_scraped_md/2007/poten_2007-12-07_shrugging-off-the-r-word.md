# Shrugging-off the R-word

In the international businesses of oil and shipping, there is certainly a great deal of international travel. The weakening U.S. dollar takes on a somewhat personal significance as travelers encounter exchange rates on a day-to-day basis. While the strength of the currency is just one component of the economic picture, other drivers have led prognosticators to believe the U.S. economy could enter a recession in 2008. While many vital signs of the U.S. economy have remained quite healthy, the sub-prime lending crunch and a more sluggish housing market are two signals that pundits consider when determining the prognosis for the coming year. ### Taking It To The Bank

The past several decisions of the Federal Reserve Bank (the Fed) have led to reductions in interest rates. The Fed meeting next week, seems to be carrying a high degree of uncertainty with respect to the decision. Many contend that continued cuts in interest rates have pumped cheaper and cheaper money into the hands of the American public, something that may be considered by many to be an unwise policy. December will likely prove to be a significant month with respect to economic indicators in the United States and may serve set the tempo for the year to come. Higher consumer spending this season, in the face of high energy prices may be one sign that the U.S. consumer, and in turn the economy, to sustain through the coming year.
