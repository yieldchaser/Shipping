Dry bulk

Baltic Exchange dry bulk assessments are made on a dollar per day time-charter or dollar per tonne voyage basis. 

Baltic Dry Index

The Baltic Dry Index (BD) is a composite of the dry bulk timecharter averages of the Capesize (40%), Panamax (30%) and Supramax (30%) indices.

Air Freight

The Baltic Air freight Indices (BAI) reflect the weekly transactional rates for general cargo. The data includes a headline index - the BAI Index – a weighted average of the 17 underlying destination basket routes, as well as six outbound indices – a weighted average of the destination baskets from each of the six origin airports.

Tools for shipping investors

Baltic Operating Expenses Index - Dry (DOPEX)

A quarterly index tracking the daily vessel operating expenses for Capesize, Panamax, Supramax and Handysize vessels.

DOPEX is calculated using crewing, technical and insurance costs.

A five year old dry dock cost, amortised over five years, is published separately.



Baltic Operating Expenses Index - Tanker (TOPEX)

A quarterly index tracking the daily vessel operating expenses for Aframax and MR product carriers.

TOPEX is calculated using crewing, technical and insurance costs.

A five year old dry dock cost, amortised over five years, is published separately.

Baltic Operating Expenses Index - Gas (GOPEX)

A quarterly index tracking the daily vessel operating expenses for LPG and LNG carriers.

GOPEX is calculated using crewing, technical and insurance costs.

A five year old dry dock cost, amortised over five years, is published separately.

New Building

Since 2022 the Baltic has been producing monthly Newbuilding price assessments for various vessel sizes and classes, built in China.  Vessels range from Newcastlemax to Handysize, VLCC to MR and LPG and LNG carriers.  There are four different container vessels, from large 15,000 TEUs to 1,900TEU feeder vessels.  The Panellists are experts in the Chinese Newbuilding market.

Baltic Chemical and Agricultural Oil Assessments 

Assessments are priced in USD per metric ton and basis 5,000 mt or 10,000 mt shipments (and 40,000 mt for vegetable oils).
