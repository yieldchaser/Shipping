### Dry Bulk Freight Commentary

#### Capesize

Iron ore rates on the West Australia to China route softened, with Port Hedland and Dampier fixtures to Qingdao settling between USD 13.70/15.65 fio. In the Atlantic, long-haul departures from Tubarao, including West Africa options and Owendo to Far East destinations achieved between USD 36.50/40.88 fio, alongside a TRMT run to Qingdao fixing at USD 9.75 fio. Transatlantic activity saw runs from Seven Islands and Sept-Iles to Rotterdam and Ijmuiden agreed between USD 16.70/19.60 fio.

#### Kamsarmax / Panamax

In the Pacific, NoPac trips with Singapore-Japan options settled between USD 17,300/22,000 pd. Australian rounds with redelivery in China and Singapore-Japan ranges fetched between USD 20,000/22,500 pd. Indonesian coal runs to South China, Japan and South East Asia achieved between USD 15,000/24,250 pd, alongside an ECI trip via Indonesia to India fixing at USD 14,000 pd. From East Coast India, West Coast India and West Africa, long-haul trips via ECSA to Singapore-Japan range obtained between USD 18,500/28,750 pd. Out of West Coast India, a run via South Africa to India made USD 19,850 pd, while an ECI trip via East Africa to India reached USD 26,300 pd. Coastal iron ore and coal runs from ECI to WCI range settled at USD 25,000 pd.

#### Ultramax / Supramax

In the Pacific, an Indonesian coal run to China settled at USD 16,000 pd, while a steel trip from South China to WCI fetched a strong USD 29,000 pd. Regional grain and bagged cargo runs from North China via NoPac and Japan to ECI and India ranges obtained USD 21,000 pd and USD 24,000 pd respectively. Out of West Coast India, salt runs to Far East and Japan ranges fixed between USD 14,500/16,000 pd, alongside a trip via South Africa to Far East securing USD 20,000 pd. Red Sea departures with fertilizer and rock phosphate to ECI, WCI and South East Asia ranges achieved between USD 16,000/27,000 pd. From South Africa, runs to WCI and Singapore-Japan range obtained between USD 22,000/25,750 pd alongside ballast bonuses from USD 220,000 to USD 257,500. On period, a Supramax fixed for 5 to 7 months duration at USD 19,100 pd.

#### Handymax / Handysize

In the Pacific, regional departures from South East Asia and North China to Japan, WCI and South East Asia ranges settled between USD 14,500/22,000 pd, with a trip via China to WCI including a USD 240,000 ballast bonus. A USWC grain run via Vancouver to Japan fetched USD 18,250 pd, while a West Australia grain departure to Far East achieved USD 20,000 pd alongside a USD 150,000 ballast bonus. In the Atlantic, an ECSA front-haul trip to South East Asia was agreed at USD 26,500 pd with a USD 265,000 ballast bonus.
