## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2025-10-08 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

A relatively muted week on spot in the Atlantic as a gap between bids and offers has emerged for 1H November loadings, with most owners not in a rush to lock in vessels for what might be their final rotation before the year’s end. The potential for seasonal demand spikes and for further geopolitical disruptions to European gas supplies during the winter, such as the TTF uptick following the Qatari disruptions this week (later confirmed to have no effect on LNG shipping), may explain some of the hesitation ahead of December TTF/JKM pricing next week; however, the fundamentals are still winning out and longer periods are priced largely flat through to Q1 and even beyond well into 2026. There has been an uptick in activity in the Pacific this week with a number of vessels on subs for prompt employment, and although levels remain higher than in the Atlantic they are under downward pressure.   Long-term sentiment is underpinned by a good number of structural requirements from fleet renewal and the huge wave of supply coming towards the end of the decade, including further progress on pre-FID projects. However, the immediate outlook is hampering discussions for multiyear periods starting in the next 6-12 months.
