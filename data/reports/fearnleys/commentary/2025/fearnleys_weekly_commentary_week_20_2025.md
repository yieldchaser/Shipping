## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2025-05-14 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The wheels keep turning in the LNG shipping spot market as fresh requirements flow in steadily and plenty of fixtures placed on subjects in the last week. Rate levels have softened somewhat in the Atlantic, although this seems to be driven more by well-matched cargo dates and motivated sub-letters with very tight vessel availability than by a generally weaker trading environment. As such candidate vessels are fixed out the market, rates are likely to stabilise again especially as fresh requirements are emerging for the second half of June. There remains plenty of interest on longer periods through 2025-26, with a slight uptick in sentiment also observed in small part due to improved spot conditions but underpinned by the widely anticipated fundamental tightening in years to come. With a couple of recent fixtures being reported, there may be a little momentum building in this segment and owners and charterers will be considering their next steps quite carefully.  There were no newbuildings ordered in the past week, but with further positive news on US project developments and some softening in the rhetoric and implementation of tariffs, it has broadly been a “good news” week for LNG shipping.
