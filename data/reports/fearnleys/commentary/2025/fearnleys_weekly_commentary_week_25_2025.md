## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2025-06-18 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The LNG shipping market is rarely immune from the influence of geopolitical events, though with Middle East tensions boiling over into further direct conflict, the risk posed by the potential closure of the Strait of Hormuz affecting more than 20% of global LNG supply, brings a degree of uncertainty rarely seen in the industry. The reality of weak supply/demand fundamentals has kept rates subdued for the year to date, but sentiment has continued to firm this week with security of supply on the shipping side paramount in case of significant disruption to trade flows. On an operational level, owners face increased AWRP and greater vigilance when transiting high risk areas, and some additional logistical measures to limit time within the region, but it is business as usual for now for loadings out of the Middle East. Further international involvement would of course bring additional complexity to an already fragile situation.   Chartering activity remains robust with numerous fresh spot requirements being worked in all three major hubs, East, Middle East and West, and there remains a premium for vessels West of Suez. The end of July is looking a little tighter on paper, and combined with the backdrop in the Middle East sentiment is firming. Whilst charterers have been comfortable with spot exposure through the year thus far, there is now more incentivise to lock in tonnage for multimonth period against the backdrop of firming rates on the prompt and further forward
