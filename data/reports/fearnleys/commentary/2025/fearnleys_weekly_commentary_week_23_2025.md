## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2025-06-04 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

It’s starting to feel very Summery in this market, although perhaps we can lay some of the blame at the feet of Nor Shipping, which has lured a number of chartering protagonists into Oslo this week. Whatever the reason, there’s been a noticeable reduction in spot market activity, and this has in turn seen us reduce rates in the East for both 2-Strokes and TFDEs (we won’t comment on Steam ship rates…). With a growing number of disponent owners now marketing their July availability, one assumes that the next done in the Pacific will be hotly contested, especially when factoring in the independently controlled tonnage that’s currently idling within the basin. In the Atlantic, and on term, we’ve decided to keep everything on hold and whilst this does leave one-year 2-Stroke rates trading at a ~25% premium to spot, there’s no sign of any MEGI/MEGA/XDF owners discounting their tonnage for 12 months of coverage just yet. This is partly explained by the low base we’re starting from (there’s little incentive for portfolios to release their best ships at these levels) but it’s also the case that there are a number of term requirements for owners to choose from (we count ~15 for delivery in 2025 and at least the same again basis delivery from 2026). Given this smorgasbord of charterers, periods and trades on offer, it’s little wonder that Owners are ignoring current spot rate levels and placing their hopes in the various suppliers of jam for tomorrow. Of course, not all ships are created equally so whilst it’s been difficult to close the bid-ask spread for the newest 2-strokes, those charterers who are able to make use of TFDEs for their trade have been shown some competitive offers and this is likely where the best value sits for now.   Turning to the Newbuild market, there’s been no new conventional orders placed these past seven days, but there has been further growth in the SSLNG fleet with United Bunkers confirming a second 20k LNGBV order from CIMC SOE at the end of last week.
