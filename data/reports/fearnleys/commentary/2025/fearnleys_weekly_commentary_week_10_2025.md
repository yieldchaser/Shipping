## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2025-03-05 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The spot market has continued to firm across all basins this week thanks to sustained pressure from several short-term requirements against more limited supply, particularly in the Atlantic where Venture Global off-takers are no longer able to give the flexibility sought by charterers beyond April. A small number of March lifters have had to stomach significant jumps relative to last week in order to cover some prompt loadings, but rates are still depressed as compared with previous years. The spot fixing window is growing wider with charterers trying to cover more forward dates, but uncertain fleet schedules are not making this easy. As such, offers continue to show slight increases on last done and momentum is currently with owners, although the underlying fundamentals remain challenging and with a number of ships redelivering from spot business it remains to be seen whether the recent gains will be maintained.  Several mid-term requirements have also emerged for vessels delivering even further forward and into 2026, with optional structures remaining a key target for charterers. Interest in the same has been highly dependent on the length of the firm period, as owners are naturally reticent to lock in redelivering vessels at significant discounts from prevailing long-term rates. Nevertheless, we expect plenty of interesting discussions soon, and the emergence of term charterers is generally a sign that players are anticipating a market revival.  Newbuilding activity remains subdued when compared with recent years, there have been no new LNGC orders to report this week.
