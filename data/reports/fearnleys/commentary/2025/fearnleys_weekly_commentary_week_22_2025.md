### Gas Market Weekly Comment - Western Market
**Date:** 2025-05-29 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

US Sellers heavily outnumber buyers in the US FOB market.  Offers of all varieties litter the market, both in June and into July, and are priced accordingly.  Most sellers have reduced their asking prices to the low 5cpg range, but the last known transaction was accomplished for a late-Jun 4:0 at 4.75cpg, nearer to the calculated netback level, and setting a new bar.    Freight rates are playing a crucial role in the matter and seem to have the upper hand at the moment as a rise in spot shipping levels results in a corresponding decline in terminal fees.  Some market participants had this week sidelined themselves after the holiday on Monday but were eagerly awaiting the data revealed today by the EIA.  A large propane inventory build was both hoped and anticipated as a higher build would likely allow Mont Belvieu c3 values to relax and thus open the arb and improve netbacks.  However, despite exceedingly robust production levels, exports were also strong and the EIA reported a 2.00mmbbl propane inventory increase to an overall total of 55.1mmbbls.
