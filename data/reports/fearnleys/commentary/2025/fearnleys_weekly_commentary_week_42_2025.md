However, a significant overhang of October tonnage is expected to carry into November, keeping freight rates under pressure and limiting upside potential in the short term. Looking ahead, any further developments in the US-China trade landscape could quickly reshape market dynamics. That said, given the inherently supply-driven nature of the VLGC market, we expect a swift adjustment to any new trading norms as players adapt to evolving conditions.
