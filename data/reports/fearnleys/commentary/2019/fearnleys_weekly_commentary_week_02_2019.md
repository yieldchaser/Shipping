### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2019-01-07 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The first proper trading week of 2019 is underway and although we’ve recorded a good number of fixtures so far this year, it hasn’t been enough the get the shipping market out of the softening trend we’ve seen through the holiday season. The shipping rates have come off and so has other elements such as the ballast bonus sentiment. This weakening can mostly be associated to an increase in availability, amongst which, some are sublets, which indicates that the underlying demand is also coming off. Although the spot market is coming off near term, the sentiment and Owners remain optimistic for 2019 and term rates for a 1yr and 3yrs TC’s are relatively stable.
