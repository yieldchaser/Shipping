## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2019-08-28 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

As August comes to a close with the LNG cargo market continuing to underwhelm, owners and charterers circle the dancefloor waiting for each other to make the next move. Portfolio players and traders with length in their fleets, either through trading shorter routes or with pre-secured winter coverage, are growing reluctant to sub-let but continue to be well-placed to pick up the cargoes that do come to market. The result is a general lack of demand for independent tonnage; however, the good news for owners is that vessel availability remains tight particularly in the west, and the east is not awash with vessels despite the usual seasonal build-up. With the imminent start-up of Freeport and the approaching winter demand, the enduring strong sentiment is readily understood.    On the newbuilding front, there were two further orders last week: one fresh order placed by NYK at SHI and an option exercised by Maran Gas at DSME. This brings the YTD total for 2019 to 31 conventional LNGCs, versus 37 for the same period in 2018 and 62 for the full year.

### Gas Market Weekly Comment - Eastern Market
**Date:** 2019-08-26 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

MEG: Saudi Aramco today announced September Propane CP at $350, this being $20 less than August, Butane September CP came at $360, flat to August.  Paper swaps had been traded higher this week, due to prompt demand from both India and Indonesia for September liftings of equal ratio cargos.  After the September CP announcement, October CP traded at up to $357, October FEI/CP quickly narrowed to $21 from a previous $30ish.  One European trader reportedly sold a 20-25 September lifting of full cargo C3 to a Chinese importer at discount to Oct CP.    FAR EAST: A few deals were concluded in the window this week as traders had to cover shorts.  23k Propane were traded at September FEI minus $11ish. A similar cargo for 1h October delivery were offered at Oct FEI minus $16, buyers are still to come forward.   Indonesia’s Pertamina awarded an evenly split 2/2 delivery into Kalbut during 4-6th October, reportedly at October CP plus high 30s. And new tender for same ratio cargo at 8-10th October delivery into Teluk Semankga has closed but result is yet to be known. India’s HPCL also issued prompt tender for end Aug and Early Sep delivered equal ratio cargoes, which they didn’t manage to award. Later they reissued buy tender for 45kt equal ratio cargo to be delivered during 11 Sep to 5 Oct, and a 21k equal ratio cargo for 21-31 Sep delivery.   Comparatively, the Chinese market appeared quiet this week as most Buyers have already covered their September positions and waiting for September CP announcement before taking October positions. One end September/early October arrival of full Propane is reported to have been sold to China at October CP plus low mid-40s. One E.China petchem user is reported to have purchased 23kt Propane for 2h September delivery at September CP plus low 50s level.  China custom data shows August imports at close to 2mmt, which is the highest monthly import level in 2019 so far.

### Gas Market Weekly Comment - Western Market
**Date:** 2019-08-26 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

USGC: The market mostly shrugged off the 3.7 million barrel build with some assistance from crude. However we are now 23 million barrels over last year and for sure on pace to break the 100 million level. Volumes are still somewhat lack luster as it has priced most of this in and traders are still nursing summer hangovers. With three weeks of over 3 million barrels built per week in US inventories, this has become the new normal. The FOB market in the US continues to be active for end September and early October although more offers have recently been seen than bids. Despite this FOB discussions continue around the 6cpg mark.
