## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2019-08-19 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

During the past trading week we’ve seen a firming sentiment in all basins, but this has yet to translate into firming rates. The stronger sentiment is mainly driven by a reduction in available tonnage, a number of cargoes to work/ offer on and expectations of strong winter shipping market. ### Gas Market Weekly Comment - Eastern Market
**Date:** 2019-08-19 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

MEG: Despite the Brent stands above 60$, LPG paper values doesn’t follow anywhere near closely this week. By time of writing, Sept Propane CP traded at $338 whilst Butane is $10 higher at $348. Saudi Aramco has announced Sept lifting acceptances with some liftings reported to be deferred into 2h Sept and later month lifting, with less supply due to oil cut. Therefore supports front CP and front contango narrowed from $5 to almost flattish.  Other than this, a couple of 1st decade Sept lifting FOB spots have been traded this week at single digit discounts, against the rising freight. This week Baltic has risen amid plentiful shipping inquiries from Indian players and tight supply in 1st decade Sept.    FAR EAST: CFR prices for delivery into Korea/Japan still appears weak, as 23kt Propane for 2h Sep delivery traded at Sep FEI minus $16, which equates to a CFR price of Sep CP plus high single digit this Wednesday. Similar cargo for 1h Oct delivery is offered at Oct FEI minus $12.  With ample supply from US and limited buying interest for US origin, Sep FEI/CP has narrowed rapidly from $28 earlier this week to $20.   During the Thursday window, One Chinese major was bidding a 3/1 cargo for 2h Sep delivery at Sep CP plus $44, meanwhile they are bidding one 3/1 loading 22/23 Sep at Sep CP minus $2 and one 2/2 loading 17/18 Sep at Sep CP minus $3. Another Chinese major bids a 3/1 for Oct delivery at Oct CP plus 43$.  However such bidding ideas are yet to attract seller’s interest, considering current rising freight.  One Chinese major is reported to have covered a 33/11 Sep delivery at Sept CP plus 40ish this week. discussions from traditional importers remain tepid due to high stocks and concerns over weakening domestic prices with the possibility of cheaper Sept CP.    Pertamina issued buy tender for two equal ratio cargoes delivered into Indonesia at 4-6 Oct into Teluk Semangka and 8-10 Oct into Kalbut, FOB alternative are also considerable.

### Gas Market Weekly Comment - Western Market
**Date:** 2019-08-19 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

AMERICAS US inventories grew 4 million barrels last week which was above market expectations but the market managed to not lose any further ground from prior to stats. Current inventories stand at 90.5 million barrels a large 22 million barrels over last year. This has traders looking at the weekly propane production. The 4 week average of 2.253 million barrels a day is high. This production coupled with high inventory levels has been keeping Mt Belvieu propane around 30% of WTI. Export capacity from the US has increased this year and if Mt Belvieu remains cheap we could see a large amount of that export capacity being utilised sooner than expected. Traders are wondering if we will see multiple cancellations before the end of the year. Or if the market will continue to support elevated freight rates, and FOB trades slightly above cancellation levels.

### Chartering Weekly Comment
**Date:** 2019-08-19 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

West  This week has seen a weakening in the Western premium which built up previously. In the west, this was driven mostly by an abundancy of relets through which players looked to benefit from the higher Western rate. Despite limited vessel availability in 1H Sep, there are a larger amount of ships in the second half of the month.
