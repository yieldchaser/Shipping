### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2019-02-20 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

Amid the bearish sentiments circulating since the start of the month, we did see improvements in terms of shipping requirements this week.  Activity has picked up and mainly in the East of Suez. However, spot shipping rates are still under pressure as demand is unable to support the current number of vessels available. Furthermore, we have seen a continuous dip in the Far East LNG prices, which in turn continues to keep the West-to-East arbitrage opportunities low. Going forward, and as we are about to enter the “shoulder months” in the gas market, the market expects current shipping levels to remain relatively flat. However, as the current spot market is in favour towards the Charterers, we may start seeing more interest for multi-month requirements to cover the second half of the year and the next winter. On newbuildings, we understand that Maran Gas has exercised their option and has placed another order for a 174,000cbm MEGI with DSME. Reports indicate that the vessel will be delivered in the first half of 2021.
