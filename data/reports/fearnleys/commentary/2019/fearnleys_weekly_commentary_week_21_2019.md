## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2019-05-21 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

Rates continued to edge upwards this week with a small yet persistent increase. Demand for cargo appears to have waned again and it’s become quite apparent that freight rates have begun their own journey and delinked from cargo prices. This lack of correlation between the cargo and freight market is highly irregular and has many market players perplexed when assessing multi-month indications. A small yet significant shopping spree for Q3 and Q4 cover has occurred over the past couple of weeks and has left less than a handful of different Owners with flexible tonnage available for the course of 2019. Although the number of multi-month fixtures this year is less when compared to 2018, it is worth noting that this time last year marked the commencement of Charterers taking winter coverage and what followed was a rapid change in the freight market. Cargo prices last summer where very different to today’s but with European regasification slots filling up fast it could be ton-time that dominates the headlines this summer. Availability remains fair with sufficient vessels to serve anticipated demand on the prompt but with more commissioning cargoes coming online in the near future this landscape is likely to change. New build prices take a tick up to 189 this week although orders through Q2 have been slower than the past two quarters.
