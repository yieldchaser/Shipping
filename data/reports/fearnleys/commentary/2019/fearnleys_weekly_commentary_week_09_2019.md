## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2019-02-25 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

We are almost done and dusted with February and that also means the official end of the Northern Hemisphere “winter months”. From a market perspective there is little change, as the cargo demand has stayed relatively flat for a while now. There are however signs of improved cargo activity, which has in turn rippled into new shipping requirements for March and April. Still, LNGC spot rates remain under pressure, since the availability list is rather long and includes sublets that in general can fix at competitive levels to compensate for their reduced flexibility or period availability.  It’s IP week in London, and among other discussions, there are still a wide range of views on how the rest of the 2019 will pan out on commodity pricing. The only thing certain is that no one has a reliable crystal ball.  On the newbuilding side, it was another active week with Maran Gas ordering two more 174,000cbm LNGC newbuilds from DSME for late 2021 delivery. More to come next week?

### Gas Market Weekly Comment - Western Market
**Date:** 2019-02-27 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

This winter in North America has been a monster. The cold weather started early and it has lasted long. None the less the propane production has been an even bigger monster. Averaging just over 2 million barrels a day, the question of whether the US could handle record exports and cold weather has been answered with a resounding YES. Export Terminals and mid-stream companies are gearing up with expansions, new fractionators, and new pipelines to handle the production going forward, but the question will no longer be is there enough product,  but where will the product go? This week US C3 inventories decreased by 1.2 million barrels which was less than expectations. This and declining freight rates has allowed the arb to widen to levels not seen in several months and is pointing towards stronger terminalling fees. It certainly seems that this who locked in terminalling under 6 cpg for 2019 are currently on the right side for now.
