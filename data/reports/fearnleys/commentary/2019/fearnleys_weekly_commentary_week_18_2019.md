### Gas Market Weekly Comment - Eastern Market
**Date:** 2019-04-29 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

MEG: Saudi Aramco set their May Contract Prices for Propane at $525$, up by $10 and for Butane at $530, down by $5. Although it was in line with the swap values, some players now say the contract price is higher than their expectations, more so as crude has softened this week.  Qatar Petroleum released their June acceptances with only a few delays, most lifters got dates as per nomination. June CP has seen a mini crash, so now standing at $450 at the time of writing, equal to a 14 pct drop as compared to May. Butane saw a even larger drop in prices. June CP may yet rebound, as Iran sanctions starts to take effect but so far the slow demand from India and perceived sufficient supply from US have suppressed the price.   FAR EAST:  The market is quiet due to Japan Golden Week and China Labour Day celebrations.  Few bids and offers are shown in the window, with 23kt C3 for 1h June bid at June FEI plus $13/14 and no offers seen, at the same time 2h June Sellers are asking May FEI level minus $1.  With the US terminal backlocks being slowly cleared, players anticipate that the 2h June market will be sufficiently supplied with cargoes and premiums will come off.  China imported 4.67mmt LPG in Q1, slightly less by than same period in 2018 by 2 pct, this is despite China now has one additional PDH plant which commenced operations during Feb 2019. Currently China’s domestic market price is under pressure with disappointing margins from both residential and petchem sectors is seen. In addition the summer season is soon upon us, combined with disappointing Propylene demand and lower Gasoline additives margins. Even so, China importers have started exploring the June market with expectations that premiums coming off with softening freight and overall ample supply.  China based LPG Buyers still have to cope with the uncertainty on US-China tariff talks, positive signals have been released in recent talks and people expects something progressive will come out soon.

### Gas Market Weekly Comment - Western Market
**Date:** 2019-04-29 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The US market has shifted into Spring and Summer mode leaving the fears of running out of product behind. Now the sentiment has shifted to  the discussion of can the rest of the world take all the US exports?  During the Enterprise conference call yesterday their CEO referred to how high prices create supply and low prices create demand, and the US market seems to be in the sweet spot of creating supply and demand. This,  we agree with,  and with the Mt Belvieu propane price around 40 percent of WTI, we see the arb being open and exports continuing at a very high level. In fact exports for last week were seen at record levels.  Also during the conference call  Enterprise confirmed their expansion is on schedule and additional exports will commence during the 3rd quarter of this year.
