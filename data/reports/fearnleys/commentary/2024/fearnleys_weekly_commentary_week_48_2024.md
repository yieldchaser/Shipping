## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2024-11-27 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

There has been little change to rates and sentiment in spot LNG shipping over the past week, with a handful of fixtures spread between the two basins at levels largely unchanged week-on-week. Although as a word of caution for those claiming winter has already passed us by, storage levels in Europe are being drawn down quickly and Korea is experiencing record snowfall for this time of year. There has also been some moves on the FSRU front, with reports that the Energos Eskimo is heading to Egypt for a 10yr charter to EGAS once she finishes up duty in Aqaba, Jordan in mid-2025. EGAS is also looking for another FSRU in the near-term to support a widening gap between domestic gas production and expected growth in power consumption.
