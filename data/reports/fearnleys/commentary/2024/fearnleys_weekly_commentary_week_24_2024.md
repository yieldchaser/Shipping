So far this week, various spot requirements have emerged to cover July loadings out of the Atlantic basin, and several fixtures are currently being worked. AMERICAS Volatility is the key descriptor of the MB propane market this week.  Crude strength and exterior influences such as hedge funds and price-shavers (i.e., not fundamental factors) have conspired and lifted values to near 80cts/gal, and propane/WTI ratios have reverted up to the low 40-percentile range.    How long this will remain the case is as-yet undetermined, but the present situation was exacerbated by the data revealed by the EIA for the week ending 7th June which showed a rather paltry 1mmbbl week-over-week increase to a total of 69.80mmbbls, or 92.5% of last year.
