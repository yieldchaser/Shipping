## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2024-11-13 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The LNG shipping market continues flush with activity in both basins, with the number of spot fixtures in the past seven days reaching double digits whilst several others look set to be concluded before the end of this week. Looking a little further out, both East and West regions are showing uncovered spot requirements spanning end-November to end-December, and plenty of Charterers seem relaxed enough to leave vessel shorts in their Winter program this year. With healthy vessel supply keeping a lid on rates thus far, such charterers have been in the driving seat; however, we could still see sentiment start to build again if more of the prompt length gets tied up.  One silver lining of the unfortunate and untimely downfall of LNG freight seems to be the arrival at a sweet spot where DES trades can rival FOB economics, the strength of which had previously been eating away at spot shipping requirements. Add to this the low levels encouraging some tentative speculative demand for term tonnage into 2025, and we may be seeing the early signs of the multi-month market re-emerging.   On the newbuilding front, Maran has recently ordered two firm 174,000 cbm MEGI carriers at Hanwha Ocean, with the option of two additional vessels. Both vessels are expected to be delivered in 2027.
