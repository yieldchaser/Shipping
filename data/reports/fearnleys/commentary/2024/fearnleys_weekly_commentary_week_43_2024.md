## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2024-10-24 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

Little change in sentiment on LNG shipping this week. Spot requirements in both basins continue to be covered for prompt liftings at levels below last done, with charter rates for all conventional vessel types converging at historical seasonal lows. Weak downstream demand for LNG combined with tonnage oversupply has led portfolios to aggressively bid on FOB cargoes to keep their vessels moving, resulting in negative implied freight economics. Nonetheless there is a reasonable amount of spot activity, and we expect further fixing opportunities throughout the remainder of Q4 albeit amongst strong competition.   As the steady flow of newbuildings continues to add pressure on overall fleet utilisation in the near term, the multi-month space is likely to remain muted. However, discussions around a redistribution of next year’s length are ongoing, as a handful of players show appetite to take on tonnage for additional trading flexibility, although it may be some time before we reach alignment on hire rate and structure.  Newbuilding activity has been limited this week although Mitsui OSK Lines (MOL) have announced the long term charter of their ordered newbuilding FSRU with storage capacity of 200,000m3 to Singapore LNG.
