## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2024-03-06 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

After a brief pause in activity, the shipping market came back to life this week with a handful of spot and term fixtures reported. The spot activity has been evenly spread across the main production centres and whilst it hasn’t resulted in firmer rates (yet) the good news is that this tempo should persist right into winter. This is due to the growth in FOB cargoes on offer, stemming from the number of long-term buyers who have exercised DQT under their SPAs (spot is significantly cheaper than a traditional oil slope at present). For those taking term tonnage, it is - as ever - about paying over the market in summer in exchange for more competitively priced winter coverage. In our new LNG price environment, however, these ships can also be the difference between a portfolio winning and losing a cargo tender; in short, if the best alternative to buying the cargo is to sit idle, it’s difficult for sub-award Charterers (buyers) to compete. On this basis, we expect term activity to continue as it is the best hope for owners to gain employment at present.   Turning to Newbuilding activity, following the 4 x LNGCs inked last week, we add an FSRU to the list this week with HHI securing the impressive $364 million order.
