## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2020-08-19 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

Sentiments picked up over the last few days as demand and enquiries for shipping increases due to the anticipation of winter demand for gas. Despite the apparent long list of available vessels, proposals for vessels with compliant specifications and available periods come far and few in between. Portfolio players and projects are constantly having to optimise their fleet schedules in order to offer their vessels to charterers’ requirements, clearly indicating there is not many vessels to choose from which are readily available. It may not be a good time to be short shipping going forward as availabilities change on a daily basis. Both basins have seen enquiries for the months of September, October and November deliveries. Charters for inter-basin trades could be revived in the next weeks or so, which would soak up shipping even more. Charter rate ideas have generally been far apart between owners and charterers, and it may continue to be the case as shipowners stand firm on their commercial expectations.   Brace yourselves, winter is coming.
