## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2020-01-01 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

Firstly, Happy New Year! We wish everyone a prosperous 2020.   The week leading up to New Year has been quiet as LNG chartering experts (who planned ahead) took a well deserved break, the consequence has been limited vessel requirements West of Suez and the same can also be said for East of Suez.  Availability remains tight in the West with portfolio players running the show on prompt vessel tonnage, which will result in levels remaining stable. On the contrary, in the East there seems to be some added availability which is a cause for concern for ship owners.    Looking back, we can certainly say that 2019 was a year with seasonal rate fluctuations, reaching an unexpected low during Q2, to then firm up through Q3 and Q4. LNG prices were however «flattish» with limited to non existent West to East arbitrage in the main, but did see a mini-armada set sail in September for discharges in November.  LNG trading pattern - we saw more intra-basin activity as LNG stayed «local». 2019 was also a busy year for the few remaining LNG building Yards, as new orders remained robust throughout the year with a total count of 42 orders. What 2020 will bring is yet to be seen...but we at Fearnleys see another year with strong volume growth, more focus on «greener» shipping and some interesting trends worth monitoring. Hence, we are looking forward to the new opportunities of this New Decade! Once again we wishing you a Prosperous and Happy New Year!
