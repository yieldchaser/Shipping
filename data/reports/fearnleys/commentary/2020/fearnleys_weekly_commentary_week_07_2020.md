## 2. Tanker Sector (Crude & Products)
### VLCC Weekly Comment
**Date:** 2020-02-10 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

As with much else these days it’s all about the Corona virus in the VLCC market. With oil trading reduced for the lack of buying interest, charteres are by and large left with fixed contact liftings, thereby tilting the supply/demand picture further in disfavour of the owning side. However, with increasing oil surplus without a ‘home’ we have seen a boost in floating storage interest. Although yet to materialize in earnest, this could become a saving grace balancing things to a degree. Bunker prices have also seen a downward pressure contributing to daily earnings, which at the writing moment are lingering at or below OPEX for ships burning compliant fuels.

## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2020-02-10 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The downward trends seen in recent weeks have continued, and by some measures have even accelerated in the LNG markets. Turning first to the cargo market, two of the major importing regions have seen large price falls, with JKM and TTF down ~20% and ~12%, respectively, week-on-week; set against a far more modest decline in HH pricing, trading margin are squeezed further. Uncertainty continues in the Far East, with cargoes delayed into or diverted away from China; the capacity for other importers to absorb the excess is being tested and the full impact of Force Majeure is yet to be seen. In response, the shipping market continues to cool. The competition for charters and in particular for utilisation is pushing rates lower. The lower cargo pricing is also narrowing the ST/TFDE/MEGI-XDF spread. It is not easy remaining optimistic in the current market, but with the latest reports showing coronavirus infection rates being in decline and if production remains strong, we could see interest in floating storage short term and a bounce in vessel demand and cargo pricing in the medium term.

Deal of the year to date being Euronav’s purchase of three scrubber fitted VLCC newbuildings from Sinokor with delivery ex DSME at the end of 2020 and early 2021.
