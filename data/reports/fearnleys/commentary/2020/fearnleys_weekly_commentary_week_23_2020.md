## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2020-06-03 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

As prompt LNG trading and shipping conditions continue to underwhelm, most market players are busy making the best of things today while looking ahead for hints of change. The bare facts are that LNG pricing remains low across the globe, US cargo cancellations are biting and vessel availability is creeping up. But if these pessimal conditions are the harshest for survival they’re also a great driver of ideas and innovation, and as we enter the second half of the year there are increasing signs of clever thinking and smart trades. High inventory levels and low pricing in Europe combined with fewer US cargoes is creating a novel dynamic in the north Atlantic, and demand continues to return to markets in the east as economies gain momentum emerging from lockdown. The prospect of European reloads to capture value either in localised contangoes or in inter-basin trade are encouraging for tonne-time and tonne-mile demand, and in turn ship owners are entertaining creative ideas on rates such floating structures. There will be some fascinating moves in the months ahead.   Although no newbuildings have been ordered this week, Qatar Petroleum have moved to reserve berths for more than 100 LNGCs at the three main Korean yards.

## 4. Sale and Purchase (S&P) & Corporate Activity
### SnP Weekly Comment
**Date:** 2020-06-04 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The sense of urgency to buy tankers is waning and values are slowly correcting downwards. Two 2000 built VLCC’s have been sold for region Usd 24 mill each which is Usd 6 mill lower than the recent peak at Usd 30 mill for the same age LUCKY TRADER concluded at the end of April. However it should be noted that LUCKY TRADER had her Special Survey passed and gave prompt delivery. Values for vintage tankers will be most exposed going forward but across the board we see the bid/ask spread widening in line with falling newbuilding prices and expectations. Judging by history we are likely to enter a period of limited sales activity with a wait-and-see attitude prevailing.  For bulkers we see a clear buying momentum building up and we may indeed be getting closer to a more robust floor on values which should trigger increased S&P activity. Sales volume YTD has been dominated by 10 year olds and older which is where we expect such a floor first to be established.  The more modern ones should then follow suit, perhaps with another 10-15% correction downwards.
