## 2. Tanker Sector (Crude & Products)
### VLCC Weekly Comment
**Date:** 2020-07-01 | **Subtype:** Tank Weekly Comment | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

It's been slim pickings for VLCC owners in the week gone by. Activity picked up a tad in the last 24 hours, mostly from the Atlantic with a couple of West African cargoes to go with some North Sea and USG inquiry. Rates have continued south across the board, although charterers' efforts to break the WS30 mark for the benchmark MEG/China route have so far proven overambitious, as owners have dug their heels in closer to WS35. ## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2020-06-29 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

It is hard to believe that we are already in Q3 this year and it definitely has been an extraordinary first half of 2020. With the start of Q3, we have also seen more movements and fixtures for multi-month charters, all aiming to cover the next winter period, as Charterers and Owners were finally able to converge on commercial levels. However, there seems to be a market consensus that this ongoing summer will not see a significant increase in rate levels and that only September is likely to be the swing month, during which some are already predicting a firmer shipping market, while others believe this to happen only in October. Regardless of what will happen at the end of the quarter, right now the prompt market is relatively calm and as such we have kept our spot rates stable. Finally, a somewhat of a rarity to report these days; China's Hudong-Zhonghua shipyard has confirmed an order this week for 174kcbm LNG carrier newbuilding trio for delivery between 2022-2023, which was placed by a joint venture between Petrochina and Cosco Shipping Energy Transportation.

One Korean importer reportedly bought one 2/2 for 1h Aug delivery this week, and meanwhile it also issued purchasing tender for monthly 23kt butane parcels delivered from Sep onwards to Nov or next Feb. One Japanese Oil Company issued close tender to sell an Australia origin heavy propane cargo for Aug delivery.  Indonesia Pertamina issued new buy tender to secure another equal ratio cargo for Sep delivery or 24th Jul to 22th Aug lifting ex US, valid by this Friday. China May LPG import exceeded 2mmt, up by 20% from April. However, June arrival is estimated with a big draw upto 460kt due to high inventory and slow consumption. 1H 2020 import is expected to decline by 12% or 1mmt.

### Gas Market Weekly Comment - Western Market
**Date:** 2020-07-02 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

AMERICAS A larger than expected 3.3 million barrel build was seen in US inventories. Part of this increase was attributed to US refineries coming back online and running at 75.5% the highest since early April. Domestic production has been more resilient than initially expected and with WTI at $40 a barrel some wells are coming back online. The improved production picture will put downward pressure on Mt Belvieu as a percentage of WTI which today lies at 50%. This should in turn open the arb if we see demand recovery form the East market. These MT Belvieu price moves are strengthening US FOB bid/offer levels and keeping cancelations talks in the rear-view.
