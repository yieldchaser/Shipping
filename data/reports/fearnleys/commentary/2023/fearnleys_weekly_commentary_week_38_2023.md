While rates remained relatively stable last week, the dynamics have shifted significantly this week due to a surge in vessel requirements in both basins. Although there are vessels available, Charterers are showing a clear preference for larger vessels to lift the larger cargo parcels especially in the West, leading to a perceived scarcity of LNG vessels available for charter. There have been no reports of newbuilding orders this week, as ship prices show no signs of weakening and demand for shipbuilding slots continues to be robust.

AMERICAS Mont Belvieu propane market levels are in retreat after the EIA inventory data for the week ending 15 Sep showed a 1.6mmbbl build which pushed overall stocks to 100.7mmbbls – which was entirely expected, but nonetheless is an important milestone, if only from a psychological perspective.
