## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2023-03-08 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

As the spot market continues gaining momentum, we’re seeing a focus back in the Atlantic. The long list of available prompt tonnage maintains pressure on rates in the basin, but most of these ships only have short windows of availability. The news of strikes at LNG terminals in France could have some impact as most requirements from the US Gulf had Europe in their sights. Combine this with European LNG prices recently falling to a near 20-month low, we could start to see Charterers look for alternative destinations for their cargo as more demand from the Middle East and India emerges. It is still tight if you need to find a vessel in the Pacific, especially if it needs to be cold and modern. We’re also seeing the fixing window stretch out to May, and rates within the basin remain firm but are likely to increase if we see more enquiry. Disparity on fixing levels between basins remains. Term discussions are ongoing with short bursts of activity on multi-month and multi-year, and we do anticipate rates maintaining. On Newbuild activity, NYK was reported to have ordered three 174,000cbm vessels to be built at HSHI. The price is reported at $255m per vessel with delivery in late 2026 & 2027. Today we also wish a happy International Woman's Day to all our colleagues, customers, and all women in the industry.
