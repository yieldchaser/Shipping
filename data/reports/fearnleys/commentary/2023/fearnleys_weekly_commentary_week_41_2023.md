## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2023-10-12 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

Spot shipping rates have come off again, reflecting bearish sentiment during the week.   The softening of shipping rates are very much linked to a lack of firm requirement in the market. As vessel availability exceeds the demand, there would be significant discount for prompt ships. With the decline in the spot market, a potential opening arises for charterers to secure term tonnage. Several charterers are actively exploring opportunities for early next year, seeking short-to-medium-term coverage at levels below last done for such period. Thus, taking prompt action to charter for an extended term can prove beneficial at the current levels. Although the market looks quite different this year to last year, there is a cautionary tale in that the majority of vessels are currently under control of subletters, and they can withdraw rapidly if market conditions change. And unfortunately, global events are only adding to these risks and we have seen the LNG price strengthening in recent days.   On the newbuilding front, prices remain firm with reports this week that a far east buyer is linked to an order for one LNG carrier at around US$260M from Samsung Heavy Industries for delivery in September 2026.
