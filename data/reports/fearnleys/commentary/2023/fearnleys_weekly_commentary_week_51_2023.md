## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2023-12-20 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The winter spot market this year has proven to be notably subdued when compared to recent years, lacking any significant spikes.  Sentiments have softened again, marked by spot fixtures concluded at levels lower than the preceding week. The current lack of demand and limited arbitrage opportunities has contributed to an increase in vessel availability, exerting downward pressure on spot rates. Subletters with shipping capacity are actively pursuing opportunities and vying for cargoes. LNG prices have also decreased, primarily attributed to ample inventories despite declining temperatures in the North Asia region.  In contrast, the escalating daily situation in the Red Sea has encouraged an increasing number of companies to redirect their vessels away from the Suez Canal and sail via the Cape of Good Hope. This heightened risk of further attacks may result in a temporary surge in tonne/miles and might create additional demand for shipping. With a weaker spot market, rate projections for multi-month and up to one-year term periods have also softened. As the year draws to a close, market participants remain on the sidelines, anticipating decisions for the upcoming year. No new orders were reported this week, however newbuilding prices remained stable.  In closing, for those celebrating Christmas, wishing you a celebration filled with joy and happiness.
