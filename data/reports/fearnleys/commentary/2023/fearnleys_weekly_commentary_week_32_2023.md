## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2023-08-09 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

As Singapore celebrates its 58th birthday, it’s testament to the role the country now plays in the LNG market that things have been very quiet in the East today. Of course, this is naturally a quiet time in the market anyway, as the holiday season continues across the Northern Hemisphere, gas storage continues to fill up in Europe and LNG tanks remain near their tops in NE Asia. The upshot of this supply build-up is that the contango that had previously kept 2H 2023 2-Stroke term fixtures ticking over has now contracted, and we return to a very wide bid-ask spread between Owners and Charterers. Turning to the prompt, we’ve seen fixtures in both basins this week and the main take-away is that owners are expecting rates to double in October. With the fixing window now in September, this has proven a sticking point in spot negotiations – especially when the charter period strays into October. Consequently, a handful of October requirements remain uncovered because even though Charterers have been keen to fix before rates shoot up, Owners (primarily portfolio players) see no benefit in locking in a fixture now when the market could roof again when we hit Q4. In any event, we’re printing higher rates this week as we close in on Q4 and we wait with interest to see who gives first on the next October fixture – Owners or Charterers? Newbuild prices remain flat this week and we have no new orders to report.
