## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2023-11-29 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The spot LNG shipping market has been relatively subdued of late, with only a slow trickle of firm requirements and fixtures in the past week. Despite the reduced activity in the market and an abundance of available vessels, the sentiment regarding rates remains resilient. We are almost at the time of year when the market dropped in the last couple of years, yet there are no discernible signs of a market collapse this year. Disponent owners of available vessels have maintained offers at market levels when responding to the limited vessel requirements that have been issued, and numbers out to January suggest a healthier tale end to the winter than we've seen in recent years. The current week has seen a little more demand for ships, primarily driven by prompt deliveries and January loadings. This uptick in demand raises the possibility of a "double-peak" in rate levels for this winter season, or at least a steady plateau.   Simultaneously, there are no indications suggesting any softening in the shipbuilding market. Consequently, the rate expectations for a long-term charter remain robust among owners with vessels available. Given that shipyards are operating at near-full capacity for the foreseeable future, the industry is constrained to working with existing vessels or those already under order. Notably, there have been no new orders this week, as newbuilding prices continue to hold firm.
