## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2022-04-13 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The spot market again saw quite a lot of activity, with a number of ships fixed in both basins. The disparity that has been seen in recent weeks between the softer market for loadings East of Suez versus those loading West of Suez does still remain, but has narrowed due to steady gains in the Pacific. Otherwise, it has been a familiar pattern of sublet vessels being marketed for relatively narrow pockets of availability, and either fixing or being withdrawn for internal use. Thus, the churn within the availability list is still outpacing the number of enquires to be worked. Term business continues to attract the few independent owners with tonnage remaining, as well as those charterers with enough length to play with. Stepping back from the microcosm of the LNG shipping market, the ongoing consequences from the terrible situation in Ukraine is behind a renewed attention on energy supply and security. The LNG markets will come into sharp geopolitical focus and although the implications are yet to be fully understood, we anticipate a lot of activity on LNG import and export projects.      In the newbuilding front, there were six orders at Hudong, placed as one order by COSCO for Sinopec needs.
