## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2022-07-04 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

Prompt demand from Japan for LNG cargoes continues, as the nation is looking at restocking inventories after the heatwave of last week. Importers from Europe and Asia keep battling for cargoes amidst the global natural gas supply shortage, but current record high seasonal prices have deterred some buyers. The West to East arbitrage is still closed, hence there are few signs of reduction of the sublet tonnage oversupply in the near future.  Spot market activity in Atlantic remains subdued, as portfolio players with tonnage keep winning prompt cargo tenders. In the East, there has been some more activity but interest has mainly been focusing on smaller Steam Turbine tonnage putting further pressure on rates. Looking further ahead, the Freeport outage continues to be a concern over the next months, but for the time being most subletters are reluctant to release any modern tonnage over winter in anticipation of potential cargo opportunities or arbitrage openings. There have been no new NBs orders reported so far this week, however both NB and SnP markets remain active with prices firming.

AMERICAS Volatility has overtaken the crude oil markets which have been reeling from fears of a deepening global economic recession (not to mention a strengthening U.S. Dollar), the former causing governments to sharply raise interest rates in a bid to slow the economy.  As such, WTI saw highs of over $122/bbl in June, but new lows were posted this week at $98.53/bbl.   Correspondingly, the MB propane market has reacted, not only in terms of the flat price but as it relates to WTI on a percentage basis.  Propane at Mont Belvieu is currently valued at ~$1.14/gal, or about 48% of WTI, where at one point the relationship had dipped in to the low 40 percent range. The EIA today released its findings on US propane stocks which, for the first time in some weeks, was expected to reveal a more accurate set of data.  Most in the market were expecting a build but expectations ranged widely from under 1mmbbls to over 3mmbbls.  As of 01 July 2022, however, the Overall Total propane stock level in the US is 54.9mmbbls, or 95% of last year, but 70% of two years ago. Terminal fees were earlier in June seen fixed in the upper 5cpg range, but buyers retreated to the low 4s until recently when the market turned and two separate transactions are widely believed to have been fixed for August at 5cpg.  2H 2022 values are at/above 6cpg.  NWE Butane Storages in the region remains full and multiple cargoes are looking for homes with no luck so far. In the propane markets, Europe is on track to import close to 625,000 tons In July, (a new record  high) , prompting a handful of unmet window offers, and a decidedly weak market sentiment to begin the short week. That said, LPG Avails from the North Sea will likely tighten further still, as liquids continue to be re-injected into the Nat Gas stream.   MED Butane is long in the region as traders look to the region to release some of the North West European butane pressure.
