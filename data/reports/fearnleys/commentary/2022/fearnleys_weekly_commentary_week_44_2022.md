## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2022-11-02 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The LNG freight market continues to hold firm this week as we see an uptick of activity from cargo owners seeking prompt shipping in both basins coupled with various term opportunities that have come to market, sustaining the demand for term tonnage both prompt and as far out as 2026. Despite fluctuating LNG prices and the continued absence of a West-to-East arbitrage, the appetite for spot shipping remains strong and there is still a lack of vessels being shown from subletters holding on to their winter tonnage. As such, when availability does appear rates remain high and command three-way economics. Such an environment has prompted the question amongst market participants of whether freight rates have reached a zenith? The answer is not yet clear, with the potential for floating plays to unwind on the one hand, set against the potential for colder weather or the emergence of an inter-basin arbitrage on the other hand. On the Newbuild front, there is continuing interest in ordering at both Chinese and Korean yards with at least 3 more orders placed since our last report.
