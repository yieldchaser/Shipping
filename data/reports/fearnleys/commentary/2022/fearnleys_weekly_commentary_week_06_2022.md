## 2. Tanker Sector (Crude & Products)
### VLCC Weekly Comment
**Date:** 2022-02-07 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

Last week provided little cheer for owners, but what little there was, ebbed further away this week. Lows of WS 16 for MEG/West and low WS 30’s for East doesn’t make for healthy returns. A glance down the position list shows the scrubber/eco vessels taking most of the available cargoes, as simply making more sense of the returns. This week comes to a close with both sectors joying of an increased amount of sales; a liquidity however that reflects two very different underlying market sentiment.
