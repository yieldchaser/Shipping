## 2. Tanker Sector (Crude & Products)
### VLCC Weekly Comment
**Date:** 2022-12-28 | **Subtype:** Tank Weekly Comment | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

A week shortened by the festive period, with London back in play today. Last week finished on a slow slide, as rates for MEG/East slipping down to the low WS 70’s for a Taiwan run. ## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2022-12-27 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

As the year draws to an end, we look back and reflect on another rollercoaster year for the spot market. 2022 has been a year of highs and lows; rate and commodity price records, geopolitics & diversification adding yet another layer of complexity and a newbuild market so active, the yards had to slow down order intake and take a breather.  Market dynamics are often unpredictable in LNG, but it has once again highlighted the importance of the commodity in the global energy mix, cementing its role as we continue moving towards a “greener” future. In shipping, we also have to consider new regulations coming into force from the IMO, and how these will affect our markets over the next 12 months. As expected, the short term market was mainly wrapped up in the lead up to the end of the year, so activity has been limited over the past days and rates remain at similar levels last seen for both spot and term. Activity will likely resume over the next few weeks as many return to desks, it’s still winter and pre-holiday conversations will re-ignite.  After a record-breaking year at the yards, another 2 newbuilds were added to the tally late December with Oman LNG securing slots at KSOE. We also saw ENI place an order at Wison Heavy Industries for a 2.4MTPA FLNG unit which is planned to be deployed in the Republic of the Congo. From all of us here at Fearnley LNG, thank you for all your support over the last 12 months and we look forward to another exciting year 2023.
