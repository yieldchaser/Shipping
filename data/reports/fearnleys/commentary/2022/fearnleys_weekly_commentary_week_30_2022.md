Clear evidence over the past few days that around WS 60 MEG/East seems to be the level at which owners (scrubbers attached of course) are willing to profit take and fix away. ## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2022-07-27 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

With the summer holiday period in full flow across most of Europe, spot shipping activity has remained light with a focus still towards multi-month business, as indicated by a growth of requirements from off takers seeking coverage across the upcoming winter period and the front months of 2023. Consequently, spot rates continue to stagnate and there has not been a material shift in the number of available vessels in either basin as various subletters seek employment in the near term. With the divergence between spot and multi-month interest, there continues to be a disconnect between spot and terms rates, as has been the theme for much of this year. It was announced this week that two 174,000 m3 were ordered at DSME shipyard, as newbuild prices continue to firm up.

**Date:** 2022-07-25 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

AMERICAS As of the middle part of this week, few deals have been done in the US Gulf FOB market attributed to unpredictable, sharp crude swings along with a general summer holiday market. At present, bids for US GULF FOB cargos sit at 4 cpg, with August FOB sellers targeting slightly higher in the mid 4's. Numbers in line with netback levels to the East, but failing to account for costs canal delays etc. The stagnant resale market has left freight feeling long, and predictably lead to notable drops (close to 5 bucks in past couple days)  in freight rates on a Houston/Chiba basis. As of today, the Baltic assessed Houston/Chiba freight at $92 pmt. On Wednesday, the EIA showed a larger than anticipated build of 2.4 million barrels for the week ending 22nd of July. As it stands total stocks now sit at 62 million barrels, (4 percent lower than the same period last year) Though propane exports increased week on week, so has propane production- which now sits at roughly 2.37 million barrels per day.  NWE  Propane market feels particularly week at the moment, following a record month of US origin cargos arriving in the region. The total imported volume exceeded storage capabilities alluding to the high run rates at flexible steam crackers both in and out of the ARA.  In eastern Europe, the ban on Russian origin LPG cargos has been lifted into Poland, however, there have yet to be deals done as supply current supply seems adequate to meet summer demand.   MED In the med, the spot market has continued to sit idle as demand has been more than adequately fulfilled by term contracts. Very dead summer market.
