## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2022-03-16 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

High level of activity in the term LNG shipping market continues, as the week brought a number of fresh new period requirements for Owners to consider. Although most of these requirements are still ongoing, the early onset of provided levels suggest a new anchor point for especially 1-year term rates going forward. In all term charter markets, the story is more or less unchanged, as more modern (and lower boil-off rate) vessels command a hefty premium, due to a predicted strong LNG price environment for the foreseeable future, although on a week-to-week basis, the prices for LNG and oil have come down from their recent highs, mainly due to the increased concern of extended lockdowns in China caused by the reappearance of COVID-19, and potential return of Iranian oil to markets. However, the narrative is a bit more complicated in the spot market, as the wide array of spot requirements have shown that despite the fact that the current LNG prices – at least in theory - create a massive disparity between the various vessel types, in reality not all requirements are equal, and many of them have factors and/or limitations that could, for example, favor smaller vessels in this type of commodity price environment. This has in turn created quite a mixed bag of fixture levels seen in the spot market and thus finding a fitting rate for each vessel type really depends on the requirement itself. However, generally speaking, the spot market has stayed relatively flat, since added availability has been matched with new emerging spot requirements. In the newbuilding market, we did not record new orders for this week, but the market is anticipating that QatarEnergy will announce further new orders any time soon.
