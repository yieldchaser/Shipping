## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2026-05-27 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

After many weeks of static rates in the West and a slow softening in the East, there is now clear momentum building in the spot market. A modest yet sustained arbitrage for cargoes heading out of the Atlantic into Asia has put a little more pressure on the fleet, and although there are not a vast number of requirements there are even fewer ships to meet them. The upwards pressure on rates is thus supply driven, and will remain sensitive to trade flows and the availability of sublets. Traders and schedulers are being kept busy by the continuing uncertainty in the Middle East, fuelled by repeated contradictory hype of an impending “deal” and an unusual ceasefire in name only. It is clear the LNG and LNG shipping markets will be impacted well into 2027, and after a brief pause there has been more activity in the multi-month space. Control of tonnage will be key to navigating what could be a strong winter, and we expect more appetite for charterers to take on tonnage for longer periods.
