## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2026-07-01 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

As many had feared, hopes for resumed safe passage through the Strait of Hormuz in the near future were once again dented as further attacks on commercial shipping were followed by retaliatory strikes on Iran over the weekend. The brief window of free transit enjoyed by LNG Carriers last week is back on hiatus and there was an immediate reaction in the LNG market, with pricing into Asia ticking higher and the arbitrage widening. It is clear now if it wasn’t before, that there will be no quick fixes and there remains a lot of risk heading into the second half of the year and through winter. For now though, despite continued uncertainty, the summertime lull in the spot market is apparent: availability lists are quite thin but there are also a relative lack of requirements, so there is a slight oversupply of tonnage putting downward pressure on 2-stroke and TFDE rates. This is more pronounced East of Suez than West where there is considerable premium, although Atlantic rates dipped below $90k/d for the first time in months off the back of a rush of QatarEnergy sublets. Looking further ahead, there has been little change for multimonth periods and rates have remained fairly flat through to 1Q 2027.
