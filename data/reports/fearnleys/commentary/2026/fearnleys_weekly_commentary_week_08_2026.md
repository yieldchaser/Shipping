## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2026-02-18 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

The momentum reported last week has continued in both basins, albeit in fits and starts, with spot rates in the Atlantic basin now edging higher than in the Pacific and Middle East. The primary driver in what is still a fundamentally over-supplied Atlantic market has been scheduling disruptions, mainly linked to cold weather impacting cargo operations as well as storm and adverse sea conditions. The markets East of Suez are a little quieter as expected through the holidays, although recent fixing levels are struggling to hold steady due to a healthy availability list.
