## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2026-03-18 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

Market volatility has eased this week, perhaps as the reality of a prolonged conflict in the Middle East has sunk in and the opposing bull and bear factors surrounding the curtailment of Middle East supply is now beginning to shape up. A lack of liquidity in the Atlantic spot market and mystery shrouding a handful of Qatari fleet fixtures towards the end of last week saw implied levels pare back considerably before finding some support in the low-mid $100kpd's. Fresh fixtures have been few and far between as elevated cargo and freight costs jostle for position, keeping bid/offer spreads wide. East of Suez, fixing activity has been higher, led by continued Omani flows and strong volumes from other regions producing excess cargoes. Whilst levels have trended lower overall, TFDE and 2-stroke rates remain healthy and there is even life for some steamers finding spot employment. Whether, or not, the reappearance of previously underutilised or idle tonnage and the arrival of newbuildings will outweigh tonne mile increases amidst trade flow disruptions, remains to be seen. However, uncertainty and volatility on high LNG prices leave no doubt as to the value of flexible modern tonnage in the short term.
