## 3. Gas & LNG Markets
### LNG Market Report
**Date:** 2026-01-21 | **Subtype:** Market Commentary | **Desk:** Fearnleys Research

This week has seen a bit of an ebb after the deluge of spot fixtures seen last week, when the number of ships placed on subjects hit double digits in the Atlantic Basin alone. Despite that fixing frenzy, spot charter rates have plummeted further into levels many had not expected to see in January, but such is the unpredictability of the LNG shipping market that this is just a mirror image of the spike seen in Q4, which also caught many by surprise. Market fundamentals may not always tell the full story, but the oversupply of modern tonnage has been fuelled by European gas prices soaring and a prevailing sense that tonne mile demand will take a hit in the short term. Such is the price disparity that options to deliver cargoes originating East of Suez into Europe have been sought by a number of players looking to capture value in a game of increasingly fine margins. Whilst this may be great for those managing to achieve it, there is not likely to be a significant impact for the overall fleet balance and now, more than halfway through January, the steady churn of new buildings from the yards is picking up again which is weighing further on sentiment for short multimonth periods. And regarding newbuilds, two fresh orders have been placed this week and the early signs suggest this may be a strong year for the shipyards.
